想报告, 但完全忘记了. 现在我找到了时间, 那天晚上我独自一人在我的房间里. 我是高中三年级的学生, 我居住的城镇那天完全干燥, 由于某种原因, 唯一的解决办法是用另一种药物代替, 所以我决定用Dimenhydrinate代替, 我过去曾几次服用过这种药物, 因为我预计这片水会让我生病. 但这并没有阻止我服用大量的药片. 我喝了24片 (1200毫克) 口服与水, 而我通常会服用12片. 由于那天下午我有点丧, 我认为我可以加倍剂量, 这是一个坏主意. 我等待效果到来, 坐在我的房间里, 坐在电脑前, 听音乐. 下面的时间表开始于晚上8点半左右, 我感觉到那种舒适的感觉像一个温暖的毯子一样覆盖着我的身体. 我停止在电脑上玩牌游戏, 只听音乐, 音乐,正如我从过去的经历中学到的, 对于高剂量二酸旅行来说非常重要, 因为它使我平静下来, 我继续播放音乐, 看到一些小幻觉在我面前开始出现. 这些轻微的幻觉很有趣, 由于药物尚未达到顶峰, 我也注意到舒适的感觉开始消失, 取而代之的是一种奇怪的不稳定感, 在T+0:45 我觉得有些不对. 我认为双重剂量开始影响我, 我觉得自己与地面不平坦, 房间的地板,天花板和墙壁都像一样摇. 我自己站在地球上,但我的房间绝对疯了. 我知道它们只是幻觉,所以我迷恋地看着, 记下我眼前发生的所有效果. 如果不是因为不舒服的身体旅行, 我会在子里搞得很开心, 我只是站在那里像一个尸, 幻觉变得越来越强烈. 我感知到的一切似乎是跨越幻觉和现实之间的界限. 我在和那些不在场的人交谈, 仿佛在现实生活中, 实际上我感觉自己在握手, 感觉到对方手握住我的手. 我从来没有经历过如此真实的幻觉, 但我对所看到的和发生的事情没有任何控制. 我决定不能等待, 我不得不冒险走进黑暗的走廊去所. 我走在走廊上, 经历过最可怕的药物经历. 走廊上挤满了我不知道他们是谁的人. 这不可能是真实的, 因为在我服用药前, 房子里只有3个人睡着了. 走廊里的人不可能是真实的, 我会碰到一个人, 打他. 我会被击倒,那个人会转向我,他的脸会发光, 他们用一种我从未听说过的方言咒骂我, 我从来没有听说过最卑的亵话. 我估计每一个人都能用眼皮我的股. 我尽可能谨慎和礼貌地穿过活死巨人群, 尽量不惹他们, 这不是我愿意冒的风险. 但不可能不碰到他们中的任何一个, 因为大厅是那么多人! 每次我遇到一个人的脸都会转过来, 我终于走进了浴室, 打开了灯. 我在洗手间的逗留令人惊地没有任何事件, 我当时想起, 照明需要稍微暗淡, 浴室的照明非常好, 我对此感到高兴. 为了避免幻觉, 我决定不能在浴室睡觉. 于是我鼓起勇气打开浴室的门,回到自己的房间. 我发现我的父母站在那里等待解释我在走廊里所做的所有噪音和我正在和谁说话. 我知道我被搞了,所以我没有回答,回到我的房间,因为我的父母跟着我. 为了向父母解释发生了什么事, 我开始和父母谈话,试图找出最好的借口, 我知道它们只是另一个幻觉, 我觉得这一切太真实了, 才知道这是幻觉. 现在我看到的东西, 食着我的恐惧和不安全感, 没有办法阻止它们. 这次有趣但有趣的旅行现在变成了梦. 由于某种原因, 这种药物让我无法入睡, 我的整个房间已经被邪恶的灵魂所困扰和占据. 所有的家具都在移动, 变成了荒谬的形状. 他们中的一些人长大了腿, 在我的房间里走来走去, 在其他生活家具面前嘲笑我, 他们都笑了. 如果视觉效果像LSD或那样明亮而美丽, 但我的旅程是黑暗的,沉的,充满了邪恶. 我觉得自己在这个世界上很孤单, 现在我躺在床上, 我把桌面灯照向房间的其他部分, 试图让幻觉消失. 我仍然能听到那些已经消失的声音. 我还能听到那些已经消失的声音. 我面对墙壁,把床单拉到头上. 我仍然能听到所有声音在说服我回来. 我感觉到他们的手通过毯子摸着我. 我尽我所能忽略了, 留在那里一整晚, 幻觉还在继续. 时间表的结束 我还躲在床单下, 我完全没有睡觉, 我对幻觉的真实性感到惊, 这就是唯一一个我无法区分现实和想象的例子. 这是我有史以来最可怕的药物体验. 我感到非常幸运, 我从中学到的一课是要保留并尊重这些物质的使用目的, 我现在在无名吸毒者计划中, 已经清理了所有控制/改变思想的物质76天了. 甚至是狂喜和酸! 那些是我选择的药物, 即使这意味着我的生命, 现在我生活的新篇章开始了, 我终于恢复了自己的生活, 其他: 双化物 (17):实体/生物 (37),不好的旅行 (6),独自 (16)

Jul 21, 2006. erowid.org/exp/31342 DOSE: 1200 mg oral Dimenhydrinate (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 158 lb About 3 months ago I had experienced a bad trip on dimenhydrinate and wanted to report it but completely forgot about it. So now that I have found the time I figured better late than never. I was in my room by myself that evening. I am a junior in high school and I was shit out of hallucinogenic drugs. The town I lived in was completely dry that day for some reason and I was unable to locate any of my hallucinogens of choice. Being an addict of three years, the only answer to this predicament was to substitute with another drug because after all, what’s an evening without trippin’! So I decided to substitute with Dimenhydrinate which I conveniently have access to 24 hours a day, any day. I have taken this drug a couple of times in the past with pleasant results but didn’t do it regularly because I liked LSD better. My mindset wasn’t the best one I could have been in because I expected that the dimenhydrinate was going to make me sick. But that didn’t stop me from taking a whole lot of those tabs. I downed 24 tabs (1200 mg.) orally with water whereas I would have normally taken 12. Since I was kind of bummed that afternoon I thought I might as well double the dose for a stronger trip and to make up for my lack of acid. BAD IDEA. I waited for the effects to come on sitting in my room at my computer just listening to music. The following timeline started at around 8:30 pm, about the time I dropped the tabs. T+0:30 I feel that comforting feeling coming over my body like a warm blanket. I stopped playing the card games on my computer and just listened to the music because my coordination was leaving me and the games were becoming difficult to focus on. Music, as I learned through past experiences, is very important to a high dose dimenhydrinate trip because it settles me down, keeps me grounded, and prevents me from freaking out if the trip gets too strong. So I kept the music going as I watched a few minor hallucinations start to materialize in front of me. These minor hallucinations were fun to watch but not very pronounced. The major ones were yet to come, as the drug has not yet reached its peak. I also note that the comfortable feeling was starting to leave and was being replaced by an odd unstable feeling of not being grounded. T+0:45 Something doesn’t feel quite right. I think the double dose is starting to take its toll as I slip deeper into that dream-like dimension. The body trip at this time is fairly uncomfortable as I feel as if I am not level with the ground; like I’m standing on an angle. The floor, ceiling, and all of the walls in my room have taken on a rubbery shaking motion. I myself was standing still relative to the earth but my room was absolutely going crazy. I knew that they were just hallucinations so I just watched on in fascination making mental notes of all of the effects taking place before me. If it weren’t for the uncomfortable body trip I would be having a shit-my-pants fun ass time trying to capture all of the visuals. But instead I just stood there like a zombie, a very unpleasant effect. T+1:00 Oh my god the hallucinations are becoming intense. Everything that I perceived seemed to be crossing the boundary between hallucination and reality. I was talking to people who weren’t there but looked to be realer than real. As if there was actually a person there in real life. At some point in time I shook the hand of one of the people and actually felt myself doing so as I felt the resistance of the other person’s hand grasping my own. Never in my life had I had hallucinations so real that I could touch and feel them. But I didn’t have any control over what I saw and what took place. T+1:30 I had to take a leak very badly at this time so I decided that it could not wait and I was going to have to take the chance of going into the dark hallway to go to the bathroom. I was walking through the hallway and the most frightening experience I have ever had on drugs happened. The hallway was crowded with people that I had no idea who they were. It was impossible that this could have been real because there were only 3 other people in the house who were all asleep before I took the pills. I figured that since the people in the hallway couldn’t be real that I would just walk through them. But when I tried this I would run into a person and actually hit them. I would be knocked back and the person would turn to me and his face would light up revealing the grotesque face of a human that had died decades ago, the image of a common living-dead stereotype. As this happened they would curse me out using a dialect that I had never heard before but could somehow understand and speak fluently. This language contained some of the vilest profanities that I have ever heard in existence. All of the walking dead were large in size and I evaluated that each one could whoop my ass blindfolded. I was as careful and courteous as possible as I made my way through the crowd of living-dead giants trying not to piss any off because I feared that if one went to kick my ass that it would be so real that I could feel every bone in my body being broken. Not the kind of chance that I would take. But it was impossible not to collide with any of them because the hall was so damn crowded! Every time I ran into one the face spun around and lit up, as I would be cursed out again in the harshest language there is. I finally made my way into the bathroom and turned on the light. My stay in the bathroom was surprisingly uneventful because the light was too bright for me to see any visuals. I then remembered that the lighting needs to be kind of dim for visuals to be seen on dimenhydrinate. And the bathroom being so well lit that it didn’t allow any visuals to be in sight. I was happy about this. I finished up and decided that I couldn’t sleep in the bathroom just to avoid the hallucinations. So I mustered up all of my courage and opened the bathroom door to head back to my room. As I opened it I found my parents standing there waiting for an explanation for all the noise I made in the hallway and to whom I was talking to. I knew I was fucked so I didn’t answer and walked back to my room as my parents followed me. I got to my room and turned off the music so I could explain to my parents what was going on. I started to talk to my parents trying to make up the best excuse possible when suddenly they just vanished into thin air before my eyes! I then knew that they were just another hallucination manifested into reality by my own mind. All of it seemed too real for me to know it was a hallucination. This freaked me out hardcore because now I was seeing things that gnawed at my fears and insecurities with no way of stopping them. T+2:15 This somewhat fun but interesting trip is now turning into a nightmare. The dimenhydrinate for some reason isn’t letting me fall asleep to escape the horror. My entire room has become haunted and possessed by evil spirits. All of the furniture is moving and morphing into ridiculous shapes. Some of them are growing legs and walking around my room laughing at my expense. Everything had a face on it that mocked me in front of the other living furniture and they all laughed. I might have been able to turn this into an entertaining experience if the visuals were bright and beautiful like they are on LSD or shrooms. But the trip I am on is dark, dreary, and full of evil. I feel very alone in the world and horribly detached from it at the same time. I am lying in my bed at this time and have decided that I have seen enough. I shined the desk lamp toward the rest of the room to try to make the hallucinations disappear. Most of the visuals dissolved from the light but some persistent ones still remained visible; I still heard all of the voices from the ones that disappeared as well. I face the wall and pull the bed sheets over my head. Through the covers I still heard all of the voices coaxing me to come back out. Even scarier I felt their hands touching me through the blankets. I ignored to the best of my ability and remained there for the rest of the night with the hallucinations still going at it. END OF TIMELINE I was still hiding under my sheets as mourning came. I didn’t sleep at all and was physically and mentally drained. It utterly amazed me how real the hallucinations were both spatially and in their duration. This was the only instance where I couldn’t possibly tell the difference between reality and a figment of my imagination. This was the number one most scariest drug experience that I have ever had. I feel extraordinarily lucky that I still have my sanity today (so I hope). A lesson I learned is to reserve and respect these substances for their intended uses because even a minor anti-emetic drug such as dimenhydrinate can produce effects that can leave me hanging on the frayed ends of sanity. I’m now in the Narcotics Anonymous program with 76 days clean of all mind-controlling/altering substances. Even ecstasy and acid! Those were my drugs of choice that I was just unable quit even if it meant my life. I am on a new chapter in my life where I am working to make ends meet. I’m finally getting my life back and I am feeling myself once again.

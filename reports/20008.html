准备工作 通过在网上阅读的其他经验, 我想试一试, 我把大约10个种子加热, 大约4克, 在里, 其他经验表明要将种子与或粉混合. 据说这可以增强效果. 不管怎样,我也没有. 我把种子成最细,最干的粉末, 我做了四行,每行由2个种子组成. 剩下的我直接吸烟后我吃完了烟草. 经验 虽然我不能说它和我以前经历过的任何化学冲动一样好, 这是我从烟草中得到的感觉: 虽然不如狂喜, 但类似于在大麻上享受愉快的时光, 过度活跃和敏感. 我想要走路和和人们交谈. 这次旅行大约持续了1个半小时, 这种食物的缺点是味道和气味. 这种东西很臭, 诚实地说, 我不认为我会再做一次, 也许这描述了这次旅行? 吸烟和吸烟的味道非常糟糕. 单独使用 (16), 制备/食谱 (30), 一般 (1)

With guidence of other past experiences read online, i thought i'd give this a try to relieve my bordem! I heated around about 10 seeds, which was around 4g's, in a pan, in a way that I would cook popcorn. Other experiences stated to mix the seeds with either baking soda or either lime powder. This has been stated to enhance the effects. Anyway, i didnt have either. So i cooked and crushed my seeds into the finest, dryest powder that i could manage and snuffed them straight, in lines. I made four lines, each consisted of aound 2 seeds each. The rest i smoked straight after i had finished the snuff. Experience I was very surprised at the effects of this, although i cant say that it is as good as any chemical rush that ive had before but i certainly got a rush. These are the feelings that i got from the snuff: Euphoria, not as much as ecstacy but simliar to that of a good session on cannabis with out the heavy headiness. Hyper-activity and sensitivity. I wanted to go walk and talk to people. The trip lasted around about an hour and a half, it was pleasantly surprising and nice. Downside to this was the taste and smell. The stuff stinks and to be honest with you and dont think id do it again just because of this. Maybe that describes the trip? It tastes horrible to snuff and to smoke.

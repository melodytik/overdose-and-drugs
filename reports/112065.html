我上周停止服用Adderall rx (每天两次20毫克内射),因为我越来越耐受,不愿增加剂量或更换药物,我已经这样做多年了. 我订购了Mucuna pruriens1000毫克的囊. 几乎5天来, 我每天早上服用1个囊, 下午早些时候服用第二个. 我曾经研究过它作为一种补充剂来缓解戒断症状, 这通常持续2天至1周. 我注意到一种快速兴奋的感觉, 在很短的时间内, 我感觉很舒服, 焦点很清醒, 情绪有所提升 (不是高兴, 我在下午2点左右又服用了一剂 (我没有发现它消失了,我想服用推剂量,因为我在第一剂量中做得很好,所以我去服用了.) 快乐,警觉继续下去, 我在一个拥挤的购物中心, 不是最喜欢的地方. 那天晚上我睡得很好. 接下来的四天, 包括今天, 我重复了剂量和时间, 没有任何快速的感觉, 没有任何感觉是在某个东西上. 吃零食是没有想到的. 我正在打字时吃饼干, 但比平时慢一些. 这种草药让我惊叹不已! 我需要更多的研究是否可以每天的事情或是否最好用于阿德拉尔/安非他命重置. 感觉比RX药的最佳时光更好, 因为我真的不能说我已经服用了任何东西, 这太好了吗? 其他: 在经验时的年龄:47 发布时间:2018年7月3日

Jul 3, 2018. erowid.org/exp/112065 DOSE: T+ 0:00 1 g oral Mucuna pruriens (capsule) T+ 6:00 1 g oral Mucuna pruriens (capsule) BODY WEIGHT: 135 lb I stopped taking my Adderall rx last week (20mg IR twice a day) because of growing tolerance and reluctance to up the dose or switch meds, which I’ve been doing for many years. I ordered Mucuna pruriens 1000 mg capsules. For almost 5 days now I’ve been taking 1 cap early in the morning, and second during early to mid-afternoon. I had researched it as a supplement to ease the discontinuation symptoms, which, for me, are mainly sluggish fogginess and a constant need to snack. This usually lasts from 2 days to a week. On the first day when I took it at around 8am, I noticed a quick pick-me-up feeling which was, for a very short time, joined by a slightly jittery caffeinated feeling. This subsided after about 30 minutes at the most, leaving me with a very comfortable sense of alert focus, mood lift (not euphoric but a kind of happy, spring-in-my-step.) I had zero fogginess and my appetite was normal to slightly reduced. I took another at around 2pm (I did not detect it wearing off, I wanted to take the recommended amount and since I did well with the first dose I went for it.) The happy, alertness continued, and I had sustained energy for the rest of the day. There may have been a slight crash at around 9pm when I noticed some irritability but I was also in a crowded shopping mall which is not a favorite place to be to begin with. I slept well that night. For the next 4 days, including today, I repeated the dosage and timing- I did not experience the jitters again, nor any discernable crash. I have had energy like a teenager, no racing, no speedy feelings, no sense of being “on something.” I think there is definitely an appetite suppressant quality but I’m not averse to food. Snacking just doesn’t come to mind. I’m eating cookies right now as I type, but more slowly than I normally would. I am blown away by this herb! I need to research more on whether it can be a daily thing or whether it’s best used for an Adderall/ amphetamine reset. It feels better than the best days on the rx meds- because I can’t really tell that I’ve taken anything and because there is no crash or irritability at the end of the day. Too good to be true?

Jun 16, 2021. erowid.org/exp/90734 DOSE: T+ 0:00 0,5 mg insufflated Pharms - Triazolam (pill / tablet) T+ 0:45 0,25 mg insufflated Pharms - Triazolam (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 245 lb Ahhh… The refreshing smell of a benzo in my nose yo. So I am avid with benzo I have yet been able to take Xanax that will actually make me buzz. I have maxed at 13 mg of Xanax with no effect besides post amnesia very mild. I was prescribed generic Diazepam for almost a year with a script for 200 10 mg pills a bottle every 3 – 4 months. It takes at least 70 mg for Diazepam to actually work as anti-anxiety on me. I was suggested to take one before school and two with supper. That turned into 8 before school and usually one with supper to help me beat insomnia. Well to my disappointment I was taken off the drug because the doctor did not think my insomnia was related to anxiety. I was sent to a psychiatrist and he immediately after listening to me and looking wrote a prescription for Triazolam and told me I was having what is called dental anxiety because of the directions my wisdom teeth grow. That was it I heard (Triazol“am”) the am gave away the benzo for me. So tonight I got into the bottle I get 30 a month for once a daily use. Tonight we start off with two. 0.50 mg of Triazolam to most Benzo users sounds like nothing. Me personally I think it has one of the best buzzes a benzo can give. I pull out two to start off take out a gift card and grind them to a fine powder. Pulled out my straw and snorted my first dose for the night. (Triazolam is water soluble and safe to snort) T=0:15: I feel a similar Elavil mixed with Diazepam feel. Nice head relaxation and yet also am racing with thoughts. T=0:30: I love these things personally much more potent than any benzo I have touched (Xanax, Diazepam, Lorazepam, Clonazepam, and Clorazepate) T=0:45: At this time my brain seems to want to tell me what I'm going to do. Alters my decision making. I would probably light a blunt in my dad’s face at this state. I took another .25 mg just to up it. Very similar to about the buzz of 70 mg of Diazepam. Best part my jaws are so relaxed I usually have them clenched tight but this pill allows me to have a lot of upper body relaxation. T=1:35: Pretty tired but I ain't got shit to do so I can’t sleep???? Thinking about walking to my friends and hitting the bong. Cloudy thoughts, can’t remember ever couple of minutes what I am yapping about, dizziness in a small portion, and euphoria is very present. This is the last log of this trip. Good mixes I have found for Triazolam go as followed: 30 mg Amphetamine salt (Adderall) x 1 mg of Triazolam: Takes the sleep edge off of the triaz and gives me a nice body high with the addi stimulant. I was able to eliminate my surroundings and feel like I was normal not ADHD like usual. I consider having another .25 mg when i'm coming down from the addi so I don’t start getting depressed from it. 3-6 beers, 30 mg Diazepam, .50 mg Triazolam: This is by far the most unstable type of trip I have had. This combo had me jump out a window at 2 in the morning and walked to Walmart to buy beer when we already had a full case. The Triazolam once again has a bad tendency to make me do things I wouldn’t naturally do even when intoxicated. Cannabis x Triazolam: gives me a very nice Promethazine feel to the high. Nods are frequent but euphoria is on ten.

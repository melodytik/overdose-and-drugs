在过去的两年里, 我服用了7次麻醉药, 我没有耐受性. 很有趣的是,我从来没有服用过不需要两种药来达到完全效果的药. 于是四个月前我打电话给一个朋友,我们叫他Rico Suave, 我有一些MDMA在我的财产, 但计划在下降很快我从来不喜欢干,所以我需要支持. 所以我给里科打电话,他说他必须看看. 他打电话回来说他可以得到一些, 但他们要35美元! 我问他们是什么类型, 里科随便说"欧米茄". "色"的意思? "是的"我几乎来到了那里. 我知道这些药的声誉! 他们是纯粹的MDA晚些时候我去接三个. 邀请我的朋友,我们叫他约翰, 在即将到来的" Buzzfest"上和我一起. 他对几乎所有物质都是处女, 但对实验非常感兴趣. 我向他介绍了. 这个孩子本月刚刚尝试了大麻. 我一直想让他认识X, 因为我觉得他是最能欣赏的朋友. 庆祝活动的第一天来了, 一整天的音乐会, 幸运的是我的侯爵变成了黑人, 我们将一天浪费在等待中, 直到7点15分左右的日落. 我让约翰把他的欧米茄大约15分钟之前我做我的,因为当我们做他来得晚,更高峰. 这首歌开始: 现在我们要做什么呢? 其他药物 任何药物我都会注意到意识的变化, 这种情况也没有什么不同. 我感觉到药在我的喉下滑动时, 我感觉不同. 我们坐在那里,紧张不安地等待着事情发生. 这里不会发生什么. 约翰嘲笑一些肥胖的人, 后来他们会回来困扰他. 咖啡因在我们和离开中不断涌现. 为了消磨时间, 我们在场地周围走一圈, 约在+:40左右,约翰和一个熟女聊天. 她的衫非常粉红. 像我从过多的安非他命中下降, 和轻微的在我的头和身体. 约翰和我再次坐下来. (T+00:45-1:00) 现在肯定发生了改变. 我怀疑它是否真的会起到作用, 就像我总是这样做. 约翰和我表现得很奇怪, 打哈欠,伸展, "我希望我没有这样做",约翰说. 我很高兴, 因为我知道他们会有所作为. 我提醒他,那天我至少告诉他三次, 他深呼吸, 我看着他的眼睛像孩子一样大. "我从来没有感觉到这么好"典型的东西. 他的出现让我开始了. 几分钟后,我感觉到'窗户打开'. 焦虑的蝶变成了兴奋的蝶. 一个微笑从耳朵传播到耳朵和温暖传播通过我. 我又一次惊地发现, 它们似乎有比正常的更厚的标记物, 我回到了家. (T+1:00-1:30) 现在我们都感觉很好. 我告诉约翰X的最好部分是遇到新朋友,我们走了. 身体的高度明显不同. 尤其是肩膀肌肉有显著的紧张. 人们说MDA更快,但我觉得更多的负担. 我们从一个地方跑到另一个地方, 让你想谈论E和我通常问人们的第一件事是他们是否滚动. 一对夫妇说是的. 我提到我们在Omega和一个家伙给我说"你不知道你在为什么. 约翰和我去买水. 这是有史以来最美味的水. 饮酒让我脑海中出现了水晶,布和纯洁的画面. 非常有想象力! 饮酒让我心情不好, 我们就坐下来, 我很快就上升了. 这两个小时是模糊的. 约翰变得很口, 用酒广告的风格说'我爱你', 抱怨他嘲笑胖子. 在他的世界里,一切都是完美的. 让我爱上了这种化学物质. 奇怪的是,这是他第一次,第二次做任何改变心态的事情,而我发现它太激烈了. 接下来是我的世界:生活变成了一场感官和情感输入的暴风雨. 约翰向我讲述他的人生故事, 周围有各种各样的人, 激光和灯棒. 我早些时候向约翰展示了我的眼睛如何在X上动 (他没有). 现在它们摇得很厉害, 就像在我的脑海里翻回一样. 我没有注意到它,直到约翰说'你吓坏了我'. 他每次发生这件事都会这么说, 当它停止时, 事实上,感官输入使得这种兴奋变得如此强烈. 乐队演奏时, 听起来太多了, 但在休息时, 我变得很, 我在草地上坐了两三个小时, 我对此感到麻木, 并同意约翰所说的一切. 如果我着草, 它会突然向右移动一脚. 我的皮肤感觉像真的是柔软的皮革. 在这段时间里, 约翰离开去和别人交谈, 我还经常告诉约翰我很好,很开心, 最终,高潮的效果消失了, 兴奋感也降到了我使用MDMA的正常水平. 我立即打开门,告诉他过去的两个小时是地狱, 他似乎受伤了,我撒了谎,但我告诉他这是为了破坏他的第一次. 他感谢我. 我们继续谈论暴力是多么愚蠢, 乔治·布什如何需要E. 我们站起来走路, 我觉得自己真的是开明了. 这种MDA让我想要放松, 沉浸在宇宙中. 听着我说. 我们谈论和玩光棍. 追踪器让我想起戴夫·马修毁的音乐录像. 完全令人敬畏. 我遇到了一个喜欢绿色微笑的人. 这是一个我通常会讨厌的人. 有一个白色的哥哥, 尽管如此, 我们使用E似乎使我与他团结起来, 并接受他作为一个人. 我们休息了一会,然后继续前进. 后来我有幸躺下看星星. 完全令人敬畏. 这种美丽和力量的感觉. 晚上的云层似乎以一种不自然的速度. 那些在我的外围滑冰在几秒钟内穿过天空. 我看着月亮时,约翰和我都说三. 一个普通的白色的黄色, 和蓝色的光环. 月球似乎正朝着地球飞来. 这让约翰感到紧张. 我们走路聊了一会儿. 在这个点上, 几乎全程下降. 约翰和我吃了一些普罗扎克,维生素和100毫克的5-HTP. 实际上,这是一个完美的时机, 我离开了音乐会能够开车. 仍然有一些沉默的和平感和视觉静态. 尽管有更多有意义的对话, 不是那么皮和更真实. 我回家看了卡通电视, 在那个房间里有一只, 我们仍然不知道它是否真实到今天. 到了凌晨3点左右, 我突然昏昏欲睡. 然而约翰整晚都没有睡觉. 总体而言,MDA和MDMA非常相似, 如果不是大剂量, 我认为最好的比较方法是GBL:GHB. 似乎更粗暴,更逼人,更强大. 我觉得它几乎同样有同理心. 也许少了一点欣喜感. 它们甚至没有触及能做的事情. 另外,MDMA似乎比MDA更能降低我的抑制力, 尽管如此,MDA是一种美妙的药物, MDA (34):关系 (44),一般 (1),节日 / Lg. 人群 (24)

Jul 22, 2007. erowid.org/exp/19376 DOSE: 1 tablet oral MDA (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 185 lb Alright, in the last 2 years I have taken MDMA 7 times, once every 3-4 months. I have no MD tolerance. Interestingly enough I have never had pills that didn’t require two for full effects. So about 4 months ago I call a friend, we'll call him Rico Suave, looking for some tabs. I had some MDMA in my possesion, but was planning on dropping soon and I never like to be dry, so I needed back up. So I give Rico a call and he says he'll have to look around. He calls back and says he can get some, but they’re gonna be $35! So I ask what type they are and Rico casually says omegas. 'Orange?'. 'Yeah' I almost came right there. I knew the reputation of these pills! They were pure MDA Later that night I go pick up three. So I get it all planned out, and invite my friend, we'll call him John, to take one with me at upcoming 'Buzzfest'. He is a virgin to almost all substances, but very interested in experimentation. I introduced him to shrooms. This kid just got around to trying weed this month. But he is a very emotional person and I had been wanting to introduce him to X as I feel he is THE friend who would appreciate it the most. So the day of the event comes, an all day concert, happily my marquis turned black, I had no doubts! We waste the day sitting in anticipation, till the sunsets at around 7:15. I let John drop his Orange Omega about 15 minutes before I do mine, cuz when we did shrooms his came in later and peaked harder. It begins: ~*MDA*~ 125-150mg (T+ 0:00-:30) Upon taking any drug I notice an immediate shift in consciousness, this was no different. The minute I felt the pill slide down my throat I felt different. We sat around with nervous anxiety waiting for something to happen. Nothing much would happen here. John makes fun of some fat people which would later come back to haunt him. (T+ 0:30-:45) Strange caffeine energy keeps springing up in us and the leaving. To pass time we walk laps around the venue to pass time, then sit down, then 5 min. later feel the need to do it again. Sometime around +:40 John talked to a girl he knew. I found I had a hard time following the simple conversation and that her shirt was VERY pink. At this point there was a minor disassociation present, like when I am sketched from the comedown of too much amphetamine, and a slight tingling in my head and body. John and I sit down again. (T+ 00:45-1:00) Alteration definitely happening now. Butterflies in the stomach and I’m wondering if its really gonna kick in, just like I always do. John and I are acting strange, yawning, stretching, rambling nonsense. 5 min later, 'Oh my God' 'I wish I hadn’t done this' says John. This actually made me happy, as I knew they were kicking in. I reminded him that I has told him at least 3 times that day that when it came on, he would get some anxiety, and that this was normal. Apparently my comment was very reassuring as he took a deep breath, and I literally watched his eyes widen like a child. 'Oh my God, this is amazing' 'I've never felt this good' typical stuff. His coming up pretty much got me going. Within minutes I felt the 'window open'. In an instant I felt the confusion and disassociation disappear, and the butterflies of anxiety turn to butterflies of excitement. A grin spread from ear to ear and warmth spread thru me. I busted out the glowsticks and was amazed once again. They seems to have thicker tracers than normal, but still barely noticablely different from MDMA. I was back home. (T +1:00-1:30) Well now we were both feeling it good. I tell John the best part of X is meeting new people, and we're off. The body high is noticably different. Not nearly as 'floaty' and there is significant muscle tension, shoulder especially. People say MDA is more speedy, but I felt more weighed down. We run from place to place, talking to anyone and everyone who will listen. E makes you wanna talk about E and the first thing I usually asked people was if they were rolling. A couple said yes. I mentioned that we were on Omegas and one guy gives me that 'You don't know what you in for.' I didn't. John and I go buy water. It is the best tasting water ever. Drinking it sent images of crystals, lush waterfalls, and purity thru my mind. Very imaginative! All this drinking though suddenly put my stomach in a bad mood, so we went to sit down, where I'd be spending most the night. (T +1:30-3:00) I shot up very quickly, too quickly. These two hours were a blur. John became very talkative, saying things like 'I love you man' in the beer commercial style and lamenting his making fun of fat people. In his world everything was perfect. He was having the E high that made me fall in love with the chemical. Strange, it was his first time, 2nd time doing anything mind altering. and I was the one finding it too intense. On to my world: Life had become a blizzard of sensory and emotional input. There was loud rock music playing in my face, John pouring out his life story to me, and a variety of people, lasers, and glowsticks all around me. I showed John earlier how my eyes wiggle on X (his didn’t). Now they were shaking so hard they would roll completely back in my head. I wouldn’t notice it until John said 'Your scaring me'. He said it everytime time it happened, and when it stopped, he'd go back onto to his ecstatic ranting. In fact sensory input is what made the high so intense. When the bands were playing it was too much, but during intermissions I became talkative and loved up. I spent most of these 2-3 hours sitting in the grass changing postions from indian style to leaning back. There was so much stimulus that I just ended up going numb to it and agreeing with everything John said. If I stared at the grass it would suddenly shift a foot to the right. My skin literally felt like really soft leather. Sometime during this John left to talk to other people, but came back 'cuz it just doesn’t feel right without you, man'. Also I repeatedly tell John I’m fine and having fun, so I wouldn’t cause him to panic. (T +3:00-5:00) Finally the peak wears off and the high moves down to my normal level with MDMA. I immediately open back up and tell him that the last 2 hours were hell, but I’m fine now. He appears hurt that I lied, but I told him it was so I would ruin his first time. He thanked me. We went on to talking about how stupid violence is, and how George bush needs to take E. We get up and walk and I am presented with a feeling of total peace with myself. I feel truely enlightened. Instead of running my mouth like on MDMA, this MDA just made me want to relax and soak in the universe. And listen. We talk and play with glowstick. The tracers bring to mind the music video for Dave Matthew's crash. totally awe inspiring. I meet up with a guy who’s rolling on 'green smileys'. This is a guy I’d normally hate. Ghetto white dude with a platinum grill. Yet our use of E seemed to unify me with him and accept him for a person. We chilled for a few and went on our way. Later I got the privalege of laying down and looking at the stars. Totally awe inspiring. Such a sense of beauty and power. The night clouds seemed to speed by at an unnaturally high pace. The ones in my periphial skated across he sky in seconds. When I looked at the moon, John and I both say three. The normal white one with a yellow, and blue one haloing it. Also it looked as if the moon was shooting towards the Earth. It made John nervous. We walked and talked for a while. (T +6:00-9:00) Pretty much came down all the way at this point. John and I popped some prozac, vitamins, and 100mg 5-htp. Actually it was timed perfect and I left the concert able to drive. There were still lingering feelings of peace and visual static here and there. Still had yet more meaningful conversations, that weren’t as sappy and more truthful. Went home and watched cartoon network that seemed more realistic than normal. There was a fly in that room or so we thought. We still don’t know if it was real to this day. Sometime around 3:00am I went to bad and fell instantly asleep. John however didn’t sleep the whole night. Overall MDA and MDMA are very similar, and if it wasn’t for the large dose I wouldn’t have been able to tell. I'd say the best comparison for MDA:MDMA is GBL:GHB. MDA seemed rougher, more pushy, and stronger. It had less social qualities and more introspective, but I feel it was almost every bit as empathetic. Maybe a little less euphoria. Definitly more visual, yet they don’t even touch what shrooms can do. Also MDMA seems to lower my inhibitions just a tad more than MDA, so its better for parties and such. Still MDA was a beautiful drug and would love to reexperience it at perhaps 2/3 the dose.

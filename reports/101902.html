我有一段时间前做了一次非常奇怪的旅行... 这一切都是因为我服用了一剂. 没有效果,我又试了一次. 没有效果,然后我被酒打了一下, 一个小时的时间都在. 所以这次旅行很快. 我看着电视机和立体机的光线穿过它们向我走来. 这一切都让我... 旅行中我第一次看到和听到,整个存在是无限的数字. 我看到无限的可能性和无限的不同生活. 我们都会成为无限数的生物. 在那之后, 我在宇宙中冲浪了一点点. 我看到了不同的星系,甚至黑洞. 声音开始回响,并曲线. 我发现黑洞有其用途. 当然,这都是比喻, 但在旅途中, 这些地方是为那些在无限存在中不被喜欢的人. 他们的'灵魂'将被送入'黑洞', 这将是他们存在的终结. 然后当然我的旅程带我到地狱. 我感觉到自从我的""与旅行中发生的事情相呼应. 感觉不像我的身体被挤压, 而是我的意识,我的灵魂, 地狱是旅程中的一个地方, 那些做过坏事的人会独自一人去的地方, 除了自己的意识之外, 这当然也是一种比喻, 但这就是我看到的地狱. 在地狱之后当然是天堂. 在天堂里,我被压迫的意识转向了我感到高兴的现实. 我在座椅上看着整个旅程, 最后我看见了神的眼睛. 无所不在的上帝眼睛, 带着光线从它发出的. 在电影"第一接触"中, 乔迪·福斯特遇到了外星人, 他们来了作为角色的父亲, 我也是一道光,一个光环,在我上面,看到天堂在我的左边,地狱在我的右边. 之后我有两次回忆. 我有两次回忆. 另一个是我乘飞机从埃及返回芬兰. 我听到"撒旦"对我说话, 这很怪异. 我还听到有一天的喇声, 呼应着某种审判的东西. 我不知道它们应该是什么, 但我明白整个精神存在只是乐趣和游戏. 我也想到了我经历过的所有旅程. 旅行让我觉得我并没有完全掌控它们, 它们更像是与一个单一的来源进行对话, 我知道这是一个很奇怪的方式看待它, 我甚至在思考这只是这次旅行, 我们是否都接触到同一个源头在我们的迷幻之旅酸和等等... 是否能够联系到唯一的源头, 我只是在思考,如果上帝是迷幻的,对于一些人来说,这可能会为他们打开不同的视角, 我完全赞成宇宙意识, 我们与更高层次的东西相互联系, 我也想知道为什么这么多人觉醒了光明会和上帝无所不见的眼睛, 我只是在旅行后生活在一个上帝是一切和无处不在的地方 我只是在旅行后生活在一个上帝是一切和无处不在的地方 大多数人根本看不到它. 对于那些认为这可能只是从我的脑海里出来的人来说, 好吧,我的脑海会在潜意识中对各种东西有所了解. 虽然有人可能会说我的潜意识只是在玩弄我, 但整个旅程就像一本书, 我希望人们能开始理解旅行可能只是与更高层次存在的对话. 我认为,可能有数十亿不同的神,可能有数十亿不同的世界,可能有数十亿不同的死后生活, 在某种意义上,上帝可能是印度教的三位一体, 这就是我. 发布时间:2016年7月20日 虫 (349):未知背景 (20),实体/生物 (37),一般 (1)

When it didn't work I took another one and when it didn't work I took a third one. It didn't work and then I had a hit from a bong and was tripping very hard for an hour. So the trip was pretty fast. In the start of the trip there came voices and I saw when I watched the stereos and tv's lines of light going through them towards me. And it all hit to me... First in the trip I saw and heard that the whole existence is endless by numbers. I saw that there is endless possibilities and endless different lives. We all would be endless beings in this endless sequence of numbers. After that I surfed little bit around the universe. I saw different galaxies as sitting in my bench and even black holes. When I went to a black hole the voices started to echo and curve. I saw that the black holes has a usage. Of course it was all metaphors but in the trip it became clear that those places were to people who would go to damnation. Those places were to people who wouldn't be liked in the endless existence. Their 'souls' would be sent to 'black holes' and that would be the end of their existence. Then of course my trip took me to Hell. I felt Hell since my 'cramps' echoed with the things happening in the trip. It didn't feel like my body was squeezed but it was my consciousness, my soul, that was squeezed harder and harder so that the pain amplified more and more. Hell was a place in the trip where those people who have done bad things would go to be alone in a place where the pain just amplifies more and more. There isn't anyone else there except the own consciousness. Of course this would also be a metaphor but that is how Hell was shown to me. After Hell of course came Heaven. In Heaven my consciousness of being squeezed changed towards the fact that I was feeling euphoric. I had tears of joy and tears of agony while I was sitting in the bench watching the whole trip through which didn't feel to come from my head. In the end I saw the eye of God. The All-Seeing Eye of God with rays of light coming from it. It was kinda like the same thing when in the First Contact movie Jodie Foster met the extra terrestrials and they came as the Father of the character, well, for me the Godhead chose to come as the All-Seeing Eye of God. I also was a light, a halo, above me and saw Heaven going to the left of me and Hell to the right of me. After it I have had two flashbacks. I have had two flashbacks. One was when I was coming from Egypt back to Finland with a aeroplane. I heard 'Satan' talking to me which seemed to be something of a MicroKorg XL devil vocoder. It was pretty freaky. Also I heard one day trumpets going which echoed of some sort of Judgement stuff. I don't know what they are supposed to be but I understand that the whole spiritual existence is just fun & games. When I have pondered this trip which I wrote about, well, I have also pondered all the trips which I have been through. All the trips kinda feel that I wasn't completely in charge of them but they rather seem as conversations with a single source that would be the Godhead. I know that it is pretty weird way of seeing it and I'm even pondering was it just this trip which might have been a close call with the cramps and stuff to death or could all of my trips been conversations with the same source. Do we all contact a same source in our psychedelic trips of acid and mushrooms and so on... Did the Shamans back in the old days be able to contact the single source which is everything and saw many things because of it? I'm just pondering what if God is Psychedelics and for some people this might open different ways to see it since they might test are the trips conversations with a one single source that would be the Godhead. I'm all for the Cosmic Consciousness and of us being interlinked to some higher stuff even if I can't explain why it is so. Also I have pondered why so many people have awakened to the Illuminati and to the All-Seeing Eye of God without them being aware of the fact that it is real? I just live after the trip in a place where Godhead is everything and everywhere I just live after the trip in a place where Godhead is everything and everywhere and most people will never see it. And for those who think that it might have just come from my head, well, would my head have knowledge about all sorts of stuff like that in the sub-consciousness. Of course someone might tell that my sub-consciousness was just playing with me but the whole trip went through like a book that was written for me to see.

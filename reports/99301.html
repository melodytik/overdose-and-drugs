接收了500毫克的25C-NBOMe, 我在上嘴唇和牙之间放了牙膏,10分钟后牙就麻木了. 我感受到的感觉与牙医使用的诺瓦坎相似. 我发现一种新的思维方式开始占据主导地位. 我看着我的天花板轻微扭转. 我在整个旅行中感觉很好, 在我把纸条放进嘴里后大约7个小时, 两天后我去吸了一碗大麻. 我开始咳, 视力开始变暗, 我在外面的时候, 听说我开始抽. 我在30秒内就昏倒了, 并且指责当时的脱水. 他告诉我,在持续使用10天后, 他出现了一种无法消失的震动. 我开始担心可能是NBOMe造成这些事情. 我尝试了5毫克左右的5-MeO-MIPT. 两天后使用5-MeO-MIPT我想我会尝试一些大麻再次停止使用它后昏. 我服用了两次,大约150毫克的植物物质. 接着我的腿开始非常. 我无法控制它, 它会在波浪中出现, 强烈的震动会持续10分钟, 我没有测量心率, 我当时非常担心, 认为这可能是某种恐慌发作, 我不禁觉得这种化学物质可能会引起某种反应, 另一方面,它可能与5-MeO-MiPT或只是恐慌发作和脱水有关. 总体而言,我只是想把这些信息作为潜在的健康问题, 其他: 发布时间:2016年8月8日 25C-NBOMe (540),5-MeO-MIPT (287):不适用 (38),旅行后的问题 (8),健康问题 (27)

Aug 8, 2016. erowid.org/exp/99301 DOSE: T+ 0:00 1 mg buccal 25C-NBOMe (blotter / tab) T+ 48:00 smoked Cannabis T+ 216:00 5 mg oral 5-MeO-MIPT BODY WEIGHT: 165 lb After receiving some blotters of 500mcg 25C-NBOMe I decided to give them a try. I had put the blotter between my upper lip and gum and within 10 minutes my gums became numb. The sensation I was feeling was similar to novacane used by dentists. Within an hour I began to notice visuals and noticed a new mindset taking hold. I watched my ceiling twist ever so slightly. I felt good the whole way through the trip and was very pleased with my experience. It ended roughly 7 hours after initially putting the blotter in my mouth. Now 2 days later I went to smoke a bowl of cannabis. I ended up going into a coughing fit after which my vision began to darken leading me to ultimately pass out. While I was out I was told I began to convulse. I was Down and out for no more then 30 seconds and blamed it on dehydration at the time. I spoke of this to a friend who also had taken 25C-NBOMe and he told me that after 10 days of constant use he had developed a sort of shaking that would not go away. I began to get worried it may be the NBOMe causing these things at this time. A week went by and I tried some 5-MeO-MIPT around roughly 5 mg. 2 days after using the 5-MeO-MIPT I thought I would try some cannabis again after ceasing all use from it after passing out. I took 2 hits, about 150mg of plant matter. My legs then began to shake very strongly. It was no longer under my control and I could not stop. It came in waves, it would be intense shaking for 10 minutes then stop for 5 minutes only to begin again. I didn't take my heart rate although it felt very elevated. I was very worried at the time and think this may have been some sort of panic attack but then remembered what my friend said about his shaking after the use of 25C-NBOMe. I can't help but feel this chemical may have caused some sort of reaction that affects my brain whenever I smoke cannabis. On the other hand it could have been related to the 5-MeO-MiPT or just a panic attack and dehydration. Overall I just wanted to get this information out as a potential health problem in case any one was experiencing similar problems after use.

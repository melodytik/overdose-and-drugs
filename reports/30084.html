我和两位无畏的探险家 (L&F) 曾计划去另一个严的psilocybin旅程一段时间, 在徒劳地寻找一段时间后, 尽管我有疑虑, 我还是冲动停下来, 走到树中一个孤单的罗德树. 在我的眼睛之前是一个巨大的标本完美的色. 它们绝对美丽,有些是巨大的,波浪般的, 我选择了大约75% (留给其他人去做他们精彩的繁殖任务) 然后离开了. 我认为这是想要被发现并吞的标志. 我们花了一天时间穿过雪覆盖的山脉,山谷和森林, 讨论这个, 缓解彼此的日常压力和担忧, 给彼此信心去解开这些被认为是强大的的. 走过后, 我们找到了适合繁殖的息地 (木片的灌木), 我们回到小屋,为旅程做好准备. 我们用和茶 (空腹) 喝下, 等待警觉. 经过十分钟的时间, 我在半小时内被迫躺在黑暗的卧室里, 我从来没有经历过这种快速而强烈的毒药体验. 经过相当长的时间, 迅速演变的地貌变成了堡垒般的结构, 覆盖这些建筑物上有成千上万的相同的像, 这些人物是小的腰和重看,无情,但活跃. 他们似乎向我前进, 发出雷般的, 几乎是部落的鼓声. 浮在这些上出现了看起来像一些银色半透明的, 戴冠的昆虫形天使精灵. 它们在游行的军队的后面, 那些天使精灵似乎向我腰, 但他们没有沟通, 我几乎迷失在一片充满强烈形象的海洋中, 我能感觉到我的自我正在消逝...不是我自己, 在我的提交天使精灵似乎点头赞同,从那时起我可以回忆起很少精确. 我似乎在一个深的蓝色语言海洋中漂浮, 我觉得自己是一颗圆形彗星, 我记得一个绝对的幸福. 很快我意识到L&F惊地喘着口气,笑着,但几乎是遥远的, 我们穿上外套和手套,走到外面, 寒冷的山谷被新月所带来的星光所淹没. 星座的颜色是彩虹的颜色. 我觉得自己是古老的,永恒的, 经过一段快速的散步, 我们回到了小屋, 准备了一些热巧克力, 开始互相分享我们的经历. 总的来说, 这对我来说是一次突破性的经历, 另外,请注意Gartz,Stamets等说的,P.cyanescens是一种极其强大的. 我可以说3.5g比3.5g墨西哥更强, (我估计至少相当于5g) 其他: - (67):小组 (2-9) (17),自然/户外 (23),烂的体验 (4)

Preparation Pays Off Mushrooms - P. cyanescens by bluey Citation:   bluey. "Preparation Pays Off: An Experience with Mushrooms - P. cyanescens (exp30084)". Erowid.org . Jun 14, 2004. erowid.org/exp/30084 DOSE: 3.5 g oral Mushrooms - P. cyanescens BODY WEIGHT: 190 lb This was to be my first try with wild collected Psilocybe cyanescens. myself and two fellow intrepid explorers (L& F) had planned to go for another serious psilocybin journey for some time, and after searching in vain for quite a while for cyanescens, I was elated to stumble on them (after nearly giving up) in a coppice on my way to work. Something had 'told' me to stop and look in this relatively uninviting spot and yet despite misgivings I gave into the impulse pulled over and walked to a lone rhodedendron bush in the beech coppice. BINGO! there before my very eyes was an enormous patch of specimen perfect cyanescens. They were absolutely beautiful, some were huge and wavy capped with deep blue margins others small and flying saucer like. I picked around 75% (leaving the others to do their wonderful task of procreation) and left. I took this as a sign of the mushrooms intent to be 'discovered and ingested'. That weekend my two friends and I headed of into the mountains where we had hired a cabin in a deeply forested valley. we spent the day walking through the now snow capped mountains, valleys and forest discussing the mystery, easing one anothers daily stresses and concerns away and giving one another the confidence to unravel the mystery of these supposedly potent mushrooms. After our walk we found some suitable habitat for the mushrooms to flourish (wood chipped shrubbery) and deposited a small bag full of mulch riddled with the mycelium from the mushroom discovery site, hopefully creating a future patch for the mushroom to flourish. Feeling high on nature, and now on equal terms with the mushroom after helping it to further its range, we returned to the cabin, and prepared for the voyage. After admiring the mushrooms and making a toast for a sucessful journey, we ingested our mushrooms in ginger and lemon tea (on empty stomachs) and waited to alert. Within ten minutes I could feel the tingling and stomach knotting starting and by its rising intensity there was no going back. CEV's of turquoise hued lanscapes started shortly after the physical effects and within 30 minutes I was forced to go and lie down in the darkened bedroom and submit to the almost overwhelming landscapes washing over me. I have never had a psilocybin experience that kicked in this fast and intense before. After quite some time the rapidly evoloving lanscapes turned into fortress like structures that pulsed, throbbed and changed structure at an amazing pace. Covering these structures were thousand of identical mushroom effigies similar to the aztec and mayan sculptures. These figures were small squat and heavy looking, emotionless and yet animate. They seemed to march forward at me in formation, emmiting a thunderous almost tribal drumming technoid sound. Flaoting above these emerged what looked like some kind of silvery translucent, crowned insectoid angel elves. They hovered at the back of the marching mushroom army as though orchestrating their movements. The angel elves seemd to bend near to me yet they did not communicate, they seemed distanced from the experience as though observing from afar. Almost lost in this sea of intense imagery I could feel my ego draining away...not my self, I knew who I was and where I was but I felt different,dissolved and free, at one with everthing, floating in the same space as the indifferent relentlessly marching army plodded on. The angel elves seemed to nod in agreement at my submission, and from there on in I can recall little precisely. I seemed to float bodyless in a deep turquoise ocean of language that changed rapidly like the fuits on a slot machine whilst bodyless. I had the sensation of being a spherical comet like pink aura that swam around like a contented tadpole. I recall absolute bliss. Shortly after I became aware of L & F gasping in amazement and laughing but almost distantly as though we were in a huge hall together. we enjoyed seemingly this mind space together, I recall a shared and felt ecstacy between us. Eventually the images and sensations subsided and we gathered our selves together, put on our coats and gloves and walked outside where the cold mountain valley was awash with the starlight afforded by a new moon. The constellations dripped with rainbow colours. I felt at this point ancient, timeless and blessed to be alive - feelings that have come to typify my psilocybin experiences. After a brisk walk we returned the cabin and prepared some hot chocolate and began to share our experiences with one another. All in all this was for me a breakthrough experience, in exploring this mushroom, and hopefully an indication of what may come in future experiences.....If they are prepared for as thoroughly as this one. *Furthermore heed what Gartz, Stamets etc. say, P.cyanescens is an extremely potent mushroom. I can say that 3.5g is far stronger than 3.5g of mexican cubensis, (I would estimate equivalent to at least 5g) Take heed and care, pay respect and they should share their magic with you.

我刚刚发生了一件非常不寻常的事情, 我不知道是什么引发了这种情况, 我是个有经验的人 (我强调这一点, 只是因为这类报告的人通常认为作者对精神病药有点新鲜) - - 我已经服用了很多以下所有东西, 没有特定的顺序:狂喜,,LSA,DXM,各种药物,,可卡因,水晶, 这就是这个奇怪的小报告的内容. 我刚刚被诊断出患有双极性情绪, 我今天早些时候服用了第一个25毫克的拉米克塔尔 (lamotrigine,一种治疗双极性障碍的情绪稳定剂), 我昨天晚上睡觉前也服用了1毫克的克萨克斯, 醒来时感觉比平时好得多. 我刚刚从做一些事情回家几分钟前, 走到后院, 我不想上,只是得到一个轻微的情绪提升和放松一个从几次. 我把房间点燃了, 接下来我打了两次大枪, 我回去收到一个朋友在手机上发送的令人痛苦的消息, 我突然想起自己正好看着前方, 无法移动身体, 完全被在床上, 我突然变得极度偏执, 我开始脑海中发生一些荒谬的想法, 我试图移动,发现这是不可能的,我的眼半开,我不能再打开他们. 我试图坐起来, 但仍然不动. 忽然有什么东西了, 我把整个身体摇了起来. 之后,就好像什么都没有发生过, 我实际上得到一个大笑容在我的脸上, 这就是所有那些认为自己吸了PCP的人所说的. 现在,大约十分钟后, 我感觉很好, 我绝不是一个没有经验的吸烟者,我已经吸了很多年了, 如果我知道怎么做, 我会再做一次. 虽然很可怕, 但它确实是不寻常和有趣. 我只是想分享. 其他: 药物 - 拉莫特里金 (432):一般 (1),单独使用 (16)

Aug 9, 2007. erowid.org/exp/42540 DOSE: 25 mg oral Pharms - Lamotrigine (pill / tablet) 2 hits smoked Cannabis (plant material) BODY WEIGHT: 145 lb Something entirely unusual just happened to me that I thought was worth sharing. I don't know what provoked it, but it may have had to do with a number of factors. For some quick background, I'm a somewhat experienced tripper (I stress this point, only because this is the type of report where people often assume the author is somewhat new to psychadelics) - I've taken plenty of all of the following things, in no particular order: ecstasy, shrooms, LSA, DXM, various pharmaceuticals, nitrous, cocaine, crystal, and of course, my favorite, cannabis. Which is what this odd little report is about. Some factors that may have influenced it is that I was just diagnosed with being bi-polar and was a little shaken by the news - not upset, just not sure how to take it. I took my first 25mg tablet of Lamictal (lamotrigine, a mood stablilizer for bi-polar disorder) earlier today, and don't know if that has any bearing on this at all. I also took 1mg of xanax last night right before going to bed and woke up feeling much better than normal. Anyway, I had just gotten home from running some errands a few minutes ago, and stepped out to the back yard to take a hit or two of my brother's joint that I knew he had left there the day before. I didn't want to get high, just get that slight mood lift and relaxation one gets from a couple hits. I lit the joint up and took two giant hits and held them in for as long as I could each time, trying to get the most out of them. I went back in and got sent some distressing news by a friend on my phone, and laid down on my bed. It gets a little shaky here, but I suddenly remember looking straight ahead and not being able to move my body, feeling completely sucked to the bed, and I got extremely paranoid suddenly, my mind screaming suddenly that I was about to die. Ridiculous thoughts about my medicine perhaps clashing with the weed, the weed being laced, etc, began to run through my head. I tried to move and found it impossible, my eyelids were half-open and I couldn't open them any more. I was seeing a blurry view of everything and tried to sit up but was still immobile. Suddenly, something snapped, and I jerked my whole body around. After that, it was like nothing had ever happened, and I actually got a big grin on my face and thought, 'Holy shit. That's what all those people who thought they smoked PCP were talking about'. Now, about ten minutes later, I feel great yet vividly remember the temporary terror I was in. I'm by no means an inexperienced smoker, I've been doing it for many years, but nothing like this has ever happened before. Frankly, if I knew how, though, I'd do it again. Albeit terrifying, it was certainly unusual and fun. Just thought I'd share.

好吧,首先我被诊断为严重的抑郁症/失眠症. 我已经连续两个晚上了起来, 现在大约是早上7点半. 没有必要浪费一天,所以我早上服用了300毫克的Wellbutrin. 是的,我已经在30分钟内起身了. 后来那天我很无聊没有毒品. 于是我在晚上8点服用了40毫克的普拉诺洛尔 (没有处方), 然后在8点半服用了300毫克的韦尔布特林. 我在45分钟内开始出汗, 像我喝了8杯咖啡, 这种出汗开始刺激我,所以我脱下衫,打开风扇. 我开始玩一些安慰的东西, 希望能改变心情. 我很难集中注意力, 因为我的手臂动, 心率跳. 我把这首歌改为伊的"星期六开车" 我把它和伊的曲子在一起很久了. 晚上10点左右我感觉有点好些, 但仍然有点, 我的头部感觉温暖, 现在让这个长篇故事简短一下; 失眠的第三天发生了. 我抽了一包红酒, 弹吉他, 洗, 其他: 2007年6月19日发布

I had been up for two nights in a row now, and it's about 7:30am. Why waste the day, so I pop my prescribed 300mg wellbutrin for the morning. Yep, I'm up and runnin' within 30 minutes now. Later that day I was bored with no narcotics at hand. So I pop 40mg propranolol at 8:00pm (not prescribed), then 300mg more of wellbutrin, prescribed, at 8:30. Within 45 minutes I am sweating horribly, and tweakin' like I've downed 8 cups of coffee and took some amphetamine. This sweating is starting to irritate me so I take my shirt off and turn the fan on. Spiders and Vinaragoons is starting to wig me out, so I play something soothing hoping for a change in mood. It's hard to concentrate because of my tweaking arms and pulsing heart rate. I change the song to Bowie's 'Drive In Saturday'...aahhhh. I keep it with the Bowie tunes for a long while. About 10:00pm I feel somewhat better, but still tweaking and my head feels warm, but a bloodrush loving type of warm. Well to make this long story short now; day three of the insomnia occurred. I smoked a pack of Reds/played guitar/took a shower/ and cleaned most of my room, (which was a wasteland).

Those areas feel all pinched and grey, while the rest of me is undulating with an rainbow of prismatic everbursting light. I can actually feel myself from the inside out sorta, through the energy. Sorta like how yer hand fits inside of a rubber glove. So close a relationship, yet separate things entirely. It is weird.

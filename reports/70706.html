我从来没有尝试过多克斯化合物, 我等不及了. 我有6个结果, 这是一篇关于旅程事件的长篇报告, 以及药物如何让我对它们产生反应, 晚上七点左右, 我开始吃了一块彩虹彩的糖块. 我花了这段时间和一个朋友在房间里玩吉他, 听音乐, 晚上9点左右, 我感到一种充满和平的感觉. 不管发生了什么,我都觉得我可以冷静下来. 这就像感觉到一切都是在那个完美的时刻. 我和我的朋友走到他的宿舍, 我可以坐着,几乎被音乐包围, 大约在9:30我吃了另一个立方体 我们坐了好一个小时, 然后我们决定去我朋友家, 这将是一个冒险. 我们都没有车,所以我们必须乘坐公共交通工具, 现在我开始看到视觉效果. 我们在烟店停下来买香烟, 我看到地毯在我的下面移动, 这次火车行程似乎是永无止境的, 我觉得忙是不可能的 我们花费太多的时间担心我们要去哪里, 到了另一位朋友要接我们. 我在那儿再抽一支烟, 感觉自己和世界完全和. 到了11:35我又吃了两块 我的朋友接我们,我们到了派对. 在20至30人聚会中, 我坐在那里抽烟, 和人们聊天, 我的旅程仍然在一个和平的宁静状态. 看到周围的人逐渐陷入醉酒的状态, 刚好大家在凌晨2点35分开始昏倒, 我坐在外面抽烟了大约45分钟, 看着云层移动和流动, 虽然我没有感觉到, 情况也开始变得更加激烈. 接下来的五六个小时非常激烈, 我开始退出现实如果我没有直接与某人订婚. 我会离开我的身体, 因为我不再适应周围的环境. 在我的脑海里, 每个记忆都与新想法和环境联系在一起. 我觉得它是完全抽象的. 这就像在完全清醒的时候做着生动的梦. 我在另一个吉他上弹奏, 我在自己的心灵空间里完全迷失了一两个小时, 完全没有什么可怕的, 我已经准备好了, 几乎没有什么动作, 但我仍然处于一种精神状态. 我可以吃一个巨大的早餐在Hopp,但我没有吃任何前一天. 午后两三点左右我已经回家了. 眼睛的视觉已经消失了, 但我的瞳孔仍然是黑色的大盘子, 大约下午3点半,我吃了一点酸. 我真的没想到酸会有这么大的影响. 我开始从前一天晚上没有人想要的酒中喝酒, 但确实在一个小时内我开始感觉到这种LSD魔力, 两小时内我得到了很好的酸视觉,我必须仍然在DOM上倒,视觉和我处于最低状态肯定仍然有点. 晚上八点左右,我又吃了两块糖. 晚上9点左右, 我和一些朋友前往一家聚会, 我们到达那里正如他们准备. 我在聚会上喝了六七杯酒. 终于有七十个人在一个小房间里跳舞, 现在是晚上11点左右, 我感觉到酸开始减弱. 尽管如此, 我仍然处于一种令人难以置信的和平状态. 我能感觉到一切都带来了良好的振动. 尽管我已经睡不着了, 我在凌晨2点左右回到我的房间, 事情真的开始平静下来. 我终于听音乐, 在凌晨3点左右昏倒了. 我第二天早上九点左右醒来, 仍然感觉有点奇怪, 总的来说,DOM是一种令人惊叹的药物, 开幕之夜是一次令人难以置信的精神体验. 我发现唯一具有精神价值的化学物质是2C-T-2. 它具有一种内省的和平特质. 尽管如此,第二天晚上, 没有别的方法可以描述它, 它真的值得称之为宁静,宁静和和平. 其他: LSD (2),DOM (20):各种 (28),烂的经历 (4),多天的经历 (13)

When some came around I could not wait. I had 6 hits which came on rainbow colored sugar cubes. This is a long report of the events of the trip and how the drug made me react to them, there is a summary at the end. I began by simply eating one of the rainbow colored sugar cubes at around 7:00PM I spent this time hanging around in my room with a friend playing guitar and just listening to music, looking at stupid shit on youtube. At around 9:00PM I was feeling a great sense of peace filling me. No matter what was happening or what was going on I felt like I could just be chill. It was like having a feeling that everything was the perfect moment at the time. Me and my friend walked over to his dorm and ended up having an acoustic jam out in the hall in his building. I was able to just sit and almost be enveloped by the music, again all I could feel is peace. At around 9:30 I ate another cube We sat around and jammed for an hour when we decided to head over to my friends house who was having a jungle juice party. This was going to be an adventure. Neither of us owned a car so we would have to jump on the public transit and ride that for an hour and a half. At this point I was beginning to see visuals. We stopped off to pick up some sish and cigs at the tobacco shop and I could see some shifting and moving of the carpet below me if I focused. The train ride seemed to take forever and no time at all. I felt like hurrying was not possible. We spend so much of are time worrying about where we are heading that we forget to enjoy the moment, can describe the state of mind I was in. It was 11:30 and we reached the end of the line where another friend was coming to pick us up. I chilled there smoking a cigarette again just feeling a sense of total and utter peace with me and the world. At 11:35 I took two more cubes My friends picked us up and we got to the party. I have no problem tripping in party situations of 20-30 people. I sat around smoking hookah and kind of chatting with people as I was the only one tripping and everyone else was just getting shit faced drunk. My trip was still at a peaceful state of tranquility. It was interesting to watch the people around me fall apart as they slowly descended into drunkenness. Just around when everyone starting passing out at around 2:35 am my trip really started to hit me. I was sat outside for around 45 minutes smoking and watching the clouds shift and flow like an incredibly complex lava lamp in the sky. It was after this that I decided to go inside as it was probably around 15 degrees even though I did not feel it. Also things began to get much more intense. The next five or six hours were very intense, I began to come down at around 6am. I began to withdraw from reality if I was not directly engaged with someone. I would leave my body because I ceased to function in my surroundings. In my head it was like every memory was being linked with new ideas and surroundings. It was like complete abstraction in my mind. It was like having vivid dreams while completely awake. I jammed on another guitar but kept losing focus unless I really concentrated or was talking to someone. For probably an hour or two I was completely lost inside my own mind space, it was in no way scary, I was all ready so at peace that the slow build up had given me that I was completely ready to let go into my own mind. The next morning at around 8am things had mellowed out, things were barely moving but I still was still definitely in a tripping state of mind. I was able to eat a huge breakfast at ihop, but I had not eaten anything the day before. At around two or three in the afternoon I had made it back home. I was no longer having visuals but my pupils were still big black saucers and I was still in a different mind state. At around 3:30pm I ate a hit of acid I was not really expecting the acid to really have that much of an affect. I had started drinking from a jug of wine no one had wanted the night before and having passed on alcohol I was ready to mellow out a little bit. But sure enough about an hour in I was starting to just feel that lsd magic, and mind state. Two hours in I was getting great acid visuals, I must have still been tripping on the DOM and the visuals and mindstate I was in was definately still somewhat dom. At around 8pm I ate two more sugar cubes At around 9pm me and some friends headed over to a house party where there would be a live reggage/rock band playing. We got there right as they were setting up. I ending up drinking about six or seven beers over the course of the party. There ended up being like seventy people in a tiny room dancing out to reggage. It was around 11PM and I had felt the acid start to wear off. But still I was in an incredibly peaceful mindset. I could just feel the good vibes coming off everything. I still had all the energy in the world despite being up for forever. I was back in my room at around 2am and ended up eating a little more. Things were really starting to die down. I ended up listening to music and ended up passing out at around 3am. I woke up the next day at around 9am and still felt a little strange, but fell asleep fine the next night after just sitting around my room all day. Overall I can say DOM is an amazing drug, it is by far my new favorite research chemical. The first night was an incredibly spiritual experience. The only other research chemical I have ever found to have any spiritual value was 2C-T-2. DOM is definitely not a party drug, it has a very introspective peaceful quality to it. Yet the second night with the acid it gave a feeling of just being able to have a great time. There is no other way to describe it, it is truly deserving of the title Serenity, Tranquility, and Peace.

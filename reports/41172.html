这句话听起来很简单,很无辜, 我认为它是无辜的,直到它遇到了我的鼻子. 两年前我被处方里塔林. 我当时30岁,我对 (当时正在恢复酒精) 非常熟悉.起初,我每天服用5毫克两次,在口服剂量下,我减轻了一点体重,并能够真正专注. 我是一个新人! 我有能力集中精力和动力, 我可以用微笑完成两倍的工作. 现在让我回顾一下大学. 只是回想起可卡因的一些小事. 我做了可卡因两次,第二次我意识到它会使人上,因为第二次我们得到了可卡因我的想法是,'他的线更大. 幸运的是, 我在一周后毕业, 进入一个没有毒贩的世界 (我自己选择了). 甲基酸盐对我来说是完美的化合物, 直到那天, 五毫克和,我就是"男人". 它不像可卡因那么强烈, 触摸任何能让我感到高兴的东西就像爱上我一直喜欢的女孩一样. 我说谎是为了让我的剂量增加到每天40毫克, 大多数时候一切都很好. 然后回家吃午饭, 再做同样的事情. 工作进展顺利,我最好的工作发生在那些日子. 经过大约三个月的时间, 除了那天早上从床上起床. 快速前进到现在. 我每周都要扔60粒20毫克的药. 没有人会把我的嘴扔进去. 在我写这篇文章时, 甲基化物已经不再像以前那样起作用. 我是一个隐士. 我是我以前在工作和社交圈子中所处的男人的一半, 即使吸了20毫克. 经过30个多小时的清醒, 我有过精神崩. 我注意到,即使我在跑出后几天没有想到这个合体, 虐待已经改变了我. 我的想法似乎超越了我的想法和现实. 刚刚在写这篇文章时, 我打了"exmle"而不是"example", 每次我去买药的时候, 我都说,'我不会吸药', 现在我做的时候真的觉得很昏昏欲睡, 我刚刚在工作中收到我的第一份报告, 因为我对某人发出了毒品/失眠引起的愤怒. 他们对我做了毒品检测, 结果没有发现任何东西, 现在该怎么办? 无论如何我都不高兴. 我又开始喝酒了. 我不记得爱情是什么样的. 其他: 药物 - 甲基 (114):各种 (28), 丧失魔力 (34), 旅行后的问题 (8), 和习惯性 (10), 健康问题 (27), 回顾 / 摘要 (11)

The word seems so simple and innocent, like any prescription compound out there. I guess it was innocent really until it met my nose. About two years ago I was prescribed Ritalin. I was 30 at the time and I was well well versed in addiction (alcohol - recovering at the time.) At first, I was on 5mg twice a day and at that oral dose I lost a little weight and was able to actually focus. I was a new man! I had the power of focus and drive for the first time in probably a decade. Everything was going wonderful as I was able to get twice the work done with a smile. Now let me backtrack to College. Just a little flashback on Cocaine. I did Cocaine twice and the second time I realized that it addicts a split second after the first euphoric rush because the second time we got coke my thoughts were, 'His line is bigger.' That is a thought I've never had with a drug before. I now see that I can buy love if only for a few minutes and luckily I graduated college a week later to a world of no drug dealers (by choice.) Methylphenidate was the perfect compound for me until that day I crushed a pill and put it up my nose. 5mg and BAM I was 'the man.' It wasn't as strong as coke or even close to the high really. The thing is when I am leading a sober and boring 9-5 life touching anything that creates a sense of euphoric well being is like falling into mutual love with the girl I always liked. I lied to get my dose upped to 40mg a day after just one month of abuse. Most of the time everything was fine. Wake up and snort 20mg then come home for lunch and do the same. Work was going great, my best work ever happened during those days. After about three months I got to the point where I would run out a week early. It didn't bother me except that first morning out of bed. Fast forward to now. I go threw 60 20mg pills every week or so. None of them go threw my mouth. As I write this Methylphenidate has stopped working like it did. I'm a recluse. I'm 1/2 the man I used to be at work and in social circles even after snorting 20mg. A few times, after being up 30 hours plus, I've had mental breakdowns. I've noticed that even though I don't think about the compound a few days after I run out that the abuse has changed me in some way. My thoughts seem to get ahead of my thoughts and reality. An example of this just happened as I write. I typed 'eaxmle' not 'example' like my mind had skipped forward on it's output. Every time I go to get the pills I say, 'I'm not going to snort them' and I get home a bust out the Mortar and pistil. I actually get sleepy now when I do it but sleep is impossible. I just got my first write up at work for going off on someone in a drug/sleepless induced rage. They drug tested me and found nothing so I played it off as agoraphobia. What to do now? I'm not happy either way. I'm drinking again. I don't remember what love fells like.

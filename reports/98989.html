Dec 28, 2022. erowid.org/exp/98989 DOSE: 7 mg smoked AM-2201 (powder / crystals) Damiana (dried) BODY WEIGHT: 235 lb I have tried several synthetic cannabinoids, including JWH-018, JWH-210, and JWH-250. However, I have found a very practical application for AM-2201. The following experience has been replicated several times quite consistently. I always take a very moderate dose when it comes to research chemicals; quite frankly they scare me a little bit. However after a few times of using it, I realized its potential. I sourced my AM as well as JWH compounds froma now defunct vendor, and they were purported to be domestically produced. They arrive in round metal tins with the compound and weight written on the top. The AM-2201 is a light smooth powder, kinda like powdered sugar, which in contrast to the more granular powders of the JWH's. T+00:00 It is a weekend evening, I begin sober and alert, and I intend on cleaning. I apply a small eyeballed amount, about a quarter the size of the match head of a paper match, onto a substrate of damiana (though I have used other herbs or hops before) and smoke it in one hit. The taste is somewhat chemically and tastes somewhat bitter in my mouth. T+00:01 The onset is very quick. I begin with a heightened sense of awareness. My pulse quickens and I can feel it in my chest. T+00:05 The awareness has turned into a mild paranoia. I go around, check the door locks and windows, make sure all the drapes are closed, and turn down the music I am listening to a bit. I also go and stow all of my paraphernalia in a safe. My upstairs neighbors have narced on me before with weed, so I never feel truly safe in my apartment. T+00:10 After checking the security of my surroundings, I sit down and rest for a moment. I can feel my heart beating more strongly now. I take a few hits of marijuana to mellow this effect, with some success. T+00:20 I got distracted watching YouTube videos. I finally get to cleaning. I try to start with the dishes but it seems like it's too much standing around, and I decide to put them off. I start picking up trash and dishes around my apartment in a whirlwind. I operate very efficiently, and feel accomplished as I go. I am also talking to myself, out loud, and then questioning my sanity a little bit. T+01:00 I have gotten a large part of the cleaning done, except the dishes. I find myself becoming more and more distracted, but not much more than my usual self. I decide to watch a little TV before tackling the dishes. T+01:45 The effects are mostly wearing off but I still have an urge to be productive. I finally get around to the dishes. T+02:25 Other than a very mild paranoia and the heightened awareness, the effects are pretty much gone. I experience no hangover or other side effects. I know I was on the low end of dosage, but since I cannot find a source anymore I am trying to stretch out my stash for cleaning purposes. I am a fairly pragmatic person and I would much rather have some motivation to clean a few dozen times than trip my balls off and go insane a few times. I know this isn't a very exciting trip report but if you knew how much I hated cleaning it might put it into some perspective.

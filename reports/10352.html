在万圣节的精神下, 我决定尝试一些新事物, 这种未知的糖果果会是个可怕的诡计还是美味的小吃??? 这种化合物是提莱胺, 基胺的结构亲属, 也被用作动物麻醉剂. 兽医品牌Telazol是提莱胺和佐拉泽帕姆的混合物 (在手术期间添加的佐平可减轻肌肉硬). 泰泰胺不像胺一样被批准用于人类. 泰勒胺在许多国家被列为受管制药物, 包括美国, 我得到的材料是纯的提莱胺粉末 (而不是兽医品牌的特拉混合物添加了代亚), 适当地混合在盐水中, pH 调整, 然后通过0.02 微米的注射器过器进行消毒. 患者每天接受15mg的药物. 我没有看到任何经历报告, 除了一位同行的第一手报告. 根据动物数据和我多年前向朋友提供样本的回忆. 我认为它大约是基他胺的4倍. 我记得我的朋友告诉我它类似于胺, 他没有提供太多关于这次经历的细节, 他当时对胺胺产生了极大的耐受性和习惯性, 总结一下, 这种药物是可恶的!!! 身体和心灵的感觉更加固化,更不那么迷幻, 但绝对是脱离的, 与K有一些相似之处, 在高峰期, 我的意识完全完整, 身体部分不动. 这段旅程很有趣,有点愉快, 这种化合物远远优于胺. 恢复阶段似乎持续了*永远*. 我的头像是经过一个奶酪器, 然后放在一个欢乐的轮回. 运动恶心,头痛,整体不舒服的感觉, 峰值/平原似乎持续了大约1.5 - 2小时,恢复/重新进入阶段持续了大约4 - 6小时或更长时间. 吸了一些大麻试图帮助重新进入,但毫无用处. 在康复过程中我有时能够把旅程引向一些有趣的地方, 但总体而言, 我在心理上并不是处于一个糟糕的状态, 没有魔鬼或类似的东西, 在某个时候我考虑通过添加更多的胺来进行流产, 很高兴我做出这个决定, 我不敢相信这些东西留在我身边多久. 我在舌头后面有一种奇怪的状化学味道, 我终于能够入睡了几个小时, 整整第二天我都感到恶心和头, 我躺在沙发上一整天和晚上, 并不能工作. 我发现自己在重复一个熟悉的咒语: 这就是... 没有更多疯狂的实验药物! 从现在开始,我会坚持经过考验的... (观众评论) (是的,对,我会相信当我看到它先生. 青. 无论如何, 青声称他肯定会从所有有潜在可疑品质的实验性药物中休息一段时间. 我为那些被提莱胺毒剂的可怜的子感到难过! 这是一个合物被认为"不适合人类食用"的案例. 了解该药物的历史, 确定是否曾经在开发和评估阶段对人类进行过试验和测试, 这可能是个个人的特殊负面反应, 然而, 一个试验T先生是我需要知道的. 就我而言,提莱他的章节已经关闭了. 其他: 药品 - 泰莱坦胺 (167):单独 (16),第一次 (2),一般 (1)

Trick or Treat? Tiletamine by Toad Citation:   Toad. "Trick or Treat?: An Experience with Tiletamine (exp10352)". Erowid.org . Nov 2, 2001. erowid.org/exp/10352 DOSE: T+ 0:00 15 mg IM Pharms - Tiletamine (powder / crystals) T+ 2:59 -999.0 mg smoked Cannabis (plant material) BODY WEIGHT: 180 lb In the spirit of Halloween I decided to try something new to me and previously untested... Would this unknown candy apple be a terrible trick or a tasty treat??? The compound in question was tiletamine, a structural relative of ketamine that's also used in animals as a veterinary anesthetic. The vet brand Telazol is a mixture of tiletamine and zolazepam (benzodiazapine added for to lessen muscle stiffness during surgery). Tiletamine is not approved for use in humans like ketamine is. Tiletamine is scheduled as a controlled medicine in many countries including the US, however it's not regulated or controlled in my country of residence. The material I had obtained was a pure tiletamine powder (not the vet brand telazol mix with added benzodiazapine), properly mixed in saline and ph adjusted, then sterilized by passing through a .02 micron syringe filter. 15mg IM was administered. I had not seen any experience reports other than a first hand report from a fellow traveller. The dosage levels were based on animal data and recollections from a friend that I had provided samples to many years back. I figured it was about 4 times the potency of ketamine. I recalled my friend telling me it was similar to ketamine but with much greater potency as we had predicted and somewhat longer acting. He didn't provide too many details about the experience but did mention that if given the choice he preferred ketamine. He had developed a very large tolerance and habit with ketamine at the time, which i suspect is why he was not able to provide more details and a better qualitative evaluation of the effects. To sum it up in a nutshell... the stuff is vile !!! it has an undesirable receptor binding profile with poor kinetics if you ask me. The ride up was easy going and interesting, less floaty than K, more solidified and blocky feeling in the body and mind, less psychedelic but definitely disassociating, some similarities with K but very unique, some insights and ideas immerging, the degree of disassociation was similar to a 50mg IM dose of K. During the peak my mind awareness was completely intact, body partially immobile. This part of the trip was interesting and somewhat enjoyable, although not as euphoric as ketamine. The ride down was where it really became obvious that this compound is far in-superior to ketamine. The recovery phase lasted what seemed like *forever*. My head felt like it had gone through a cheese grater and then placed on a merry go round. Motion sickness, headache, general dysphoric malaise that just kept going and going. The peak/plateau seemed to last for perhaps 1.5 - 2 hours and the recovery/re-entry phase lasted some 4-6 hours or more. Smoked some cannabis to try and help with re-entry, but it was to no avail. During recovery I was able to steer the trip into some interesting places at times, but overall it could be characterized as a very unpleasant feeling which dominated the last 3/4 of the experience. I was not in a bad place psychologically speaking, no demons or such, mostly just thoughts like 'ok we got our answer, now how much longer must we endure?' At one point I contemplated aborting by adding more ketamine to launch back into hyperspace while the tiletamine wore off, decided against it however due to the unknown factors involved. Glad I made this decision since I was later to find out just how long acting the tiletamine residual is. I couldn't believe how long the stuff stayed with me. My body felt toxified, with strange rubbery chemical taste on the back of my tongue along with considerable level of general physical malaise. I was finally able to fall asleep for a few hours and then woke up with a very nasty hangover, complete with painful headache. I felt sick and dizzy all the next day, couldn't even look at the computer screen without feeling overwhelmed. I layed on the couch all day and evening and was not able to work. Found myself repeating a familiar mantra : 'That's it... no more crazy experimental drugs! From here on out I'm going to stick with the tried and true...' (spectators commentary) (yeah right, I'll belive it when I see it Mr. Toad. In any case, Toad claims that he'll definitely be taking an extended vacation from all experimental drugs with potentially dubious qualities for awhile.) I feel sorry for the poor apes who get a load of tiletamine! such treatment is nothing short of animal abuse in my opinion. This is one case where a compound has been deemed 'not for human consumption' for good reason. It would be interesting to find out more regarding the history of this drug to determine if human trials and tests were ever performed during its development and evaluation stages. Of course this could be a case of one individual's idiosyncratic negative response, however one trial with Mr T. was all I needed to know. As far as I'm concerned the tiletamine chapter is closed.

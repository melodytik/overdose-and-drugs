中文译文 兴奋剂

我决定去看看植物园在门口里藏着什么. 我走下去, 发现一棵树上写着"大草". 我只是向一个朋友指出了这件事,然后离开. 他和另一个同伴跑进门口, 脸上表现出兴奋的表情, 所以我很想去. 着长约20厘米的味茎, 我感觉到一个五分钟的, 五分钟后, 我开始感觉超活跃, 发现自己像个白痴一样拍手. 我在第一次后大约35到40分钟就感觉到"跳动"的高峰, 我发现自己在这个阶段对什么都不笑. 在大约一个小时后, 我突然感到梦幻般的衰退与戏剧性的能源结束. 我确实感觉自己可以跑400米比世界上任何人都快, 尽管如此, 接下来晚上大部分时间都头疼. 思考模式几乎没有显著变化 (请记住我当时只尝试和气), 没有足够的享受与健康相关的风险. 虽然植物园的植物叶子是免费的, 味道也不是很好. 我认为滥用这种阴毛形状的植物很简单. 我妹妹也曾在某个时候着说它有刺激的方面. 我会证明自己是对的. 如果您想把它从列表中勾选掉,请继续, 其他: 2007年11月7日发布 第一次 (2), 一般 (1), 小组 (2-9) (17)

Nov 7, 2007. erowid.org/exp/35671 DOSE: 8 in oral Ephedra sinica (plant material) BODY WEIGHT: 65 kg So one boring day when there was no homework to complete before the end of lunch I decided to see what the botanic gardens kept in their freely passed gates. I strolled down there and sure enough I found a bush labeled 'ephedra major'. As there were many tourists taking photos of near by Wollemi pine I simply pointed out the thing to a friend and left. The next day, him and another mate came running into the gates with an excited look on their faces, showing me a shamefully large bundle of stems. So sure enough I wanted a go. Chewing the shit tasting stem of about 20cm in length, I felt a five minute kick and had the urge to run. When swallowed, 5 mins later I began to really feel hyperactive and found myself clapping my hands like an idiot. It was about 35-40 minutes after first chewing that I felt the peak of the 'jumping aroundness' and then settled into a shaky sit with mild euphoria. At this stage I found myself laughing a fair bit at nothing. At around an hour after I felt a sudden dreamy slump with a dramatic end to the energy. I did, however, feel like I could run a 400m race faster than anyone on earth for alot of the previous hour. It was disappointing though. There was then a headache for most of the following evening. There was little significant change to thought patterns (take into account I had only tried pot and nitrous at the time) except for a bit of jumble while attempting to do all ten maths questions at one time. Not enough enjoyment for the health risks involved. It was a bit dicey to rack plant foliage from the botanic garden even if it was free. The taste wasn’t good either. Being personally interested in conifers and having read about a strange genus called ephedra I thought it would be quite a simple task to abuse the pubic hair shaped plant. My sister had also muttered at one point that it had its stimulating aspects. I would turn out to be right. So if you want to tick it off your list then go ahead but it has its potential health risks and could be quite boring for an experienced drug user.

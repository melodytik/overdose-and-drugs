I do have experience with cannabis, truffles (psilocybin), 4HO-MET, alcohol and 2-FDCK. Preparation, set & setting: I’m researching alone in my room, I lit some tealights to create a cozy vibe. I also have some snacks and drinks. I feel a bit nervous about taking a new drug, but that’s normal for me, and I have control over the feeling. I acquired the substance via a Dutch research chemical company, as a free sample alongside my order. This morning I smoked a small amount of weed using a bong in, less than a tenth of a gram. Its (minor) effects have worn of hours ago. It is in the late afternoon now. I read online to get an idea of what to expect. Goal: Relaxing, maybe going on a short walk, or even do some work for uni if I feel like it. Experience: T0:00 – I split the 60mg pill in half, ingested one half, so 30 mg, orally with herbal tea. T0:05 - Slight feeling of a come up, I think this can also be nervousness around researching a new chemical. T0:15 - I feel a somewhat stimulated. Both relaxed and in the mood to do something productive. Also a but euphoric, but not much. T0:30 – The mild euphoria has worn off. My favorite infotainment YouTube channel uploaded a new video, which I watched. My senses felt a little more stimulated, especially the music sounded enhanced. So far it feels natural, and I feel like I can have normal interactions and act sober if I had to. T0:45 – After the video I watched I listened to some new music. It felt enhanced, but not completely comparable to what I experienced on cannabis or psychedelics. I can’t articulate why. I had 2 pieces of dried fruit. I wouldn’t say my taste felt very enhanced, only very mildly. It’s about an hour from my regular dinner time, but I’m not hungry. I have also decided to not go on a walk outside, because it’s dark outside. Every 15 minutes or so I feel a headache that disappears in seconds. T1:00 – I’m having a nice time just relaxing so far! I noticed that I don’t feel the pressure to be being productive, which is something I oftentimes struggle with. It helps with just being in the moment and enjoying the Sudanese music I found on my YouTube homepage (Noori & His Dorpa Band, if anyone is interested). It’s interesting, I feel like I can just switch between being productive and being relaxed without too much effort. This is quite unique to me, and only something I have experienced while I was on vacation and when I was on 2-FDCK. [Later added note: This possibly has to do with me having autism (diagnosed). I suspect I may also have inattentive type ADHD, but I was never tested for it.] I think I may currently be peaking. T1:10 - My sense of time is distorted. I feel like the moment of ingestion was about two hours ago, and that I have been watching this live video of at least half an hour, while I barely passed the 20 minute mark. T1:25 – The peak has passed. I think about T0:50-T1:10 more or less was when I peaked. I’m not fully at baseline, but getting there. I think about T0:50-T1:10 more or less was when I peaked. I’m not fully at baseline, but getting there. I feel motivated to have a productive evening once it has fully worn off, but I’ll have to see if I keep feeling like that. Still listening to live performances on YouTube (Sophie Nzayisenga now) and having a good time. T1:50 – Just had a cup of decaffeinated coffee. I think I’m at baseline. T2:00 – Baseline. Reflection: Would I try 2-FEA again? Yes. My main goal of relaxing has definitely been reached. As stated, I didn’t go for a walk, which is fine. During the experience I didn’t do uni work, but felt motivated to do so after relaxing. After returning to baseline I did some productive work, but nothing big. I recall I was quite concentrated on writing the trip repost, contrary to my experience with 2-FDCK and 4-HO-MET. This was probably because I wrote it in word and was on my laptop anyways, but the chemical probably also had a helping hand in concentration, albeit mildly so. For these reasons I will take the other half of the pill in a few weeks from now to research its effects on the mind while studying.

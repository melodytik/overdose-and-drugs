作为一种源, 我非常尊重它, 但并非没有担心它的毒性. 它已经在欧洲和中东使用了几千年, 甚至在圣经中提到 (一个女人用她的丈夫换取一些杏仁, 它是达图拉,汉班,贝拉多纳的亲属,含有高达0.4%的类化合物 (斯科波胺,阿特罗宾,阿托特罗宾,素,素,库斯科希格林,索兰德林,曼德拉戈林). 这些可以导致精神活动和催眠状态. 较高剂量可以诱导妄想并导致通过呼吸道的死亡. (阿特罗平以Atropa,死亡女神命名). 所以要小心. 作为其他旅行的重点. 我已经成功地使用了它与psilocybin,LSD,MDMA,damaiana和大麻. 吃下来, 杏仁会有相当大的清洗作用. 这会导致吐和腹持续三天, 我最喜欢的摄入方法是将一些杏仁粉混合在关节中, 我还用曼德拉和酒制成液. 我把混合物放在一个封闭的容器里三周, 然后把一些香烟浸入其中. 当他们干燥,我有一些迷幻香烟. 我发现曼德拉的灵魂是一种古老而完全神奇的意识, 给人留下了无数万年的印象, 我没有对其毒性产生抵抗力. 我确实对其精神作用产生了抵抗力, 这很危险,因为有些化物可以在体内停留几天. 如果达到临界水平, 一周内少于1克就足够了, 特别是仅用于加强其他药物. 如果它喜欢你,它就喜欢你. 时间表: 27172 曼德拉 (33):组合 (3),回顾 / 摘要 (11),未知背景 (20)

Mandragons Mandrake by panaceam Citation:   panaceam. "Mandragons: An Experience with Mandrake (exp27172)". Erowid.org . Nov 28, 2003. erowid.org/exp/27172 DOSE: smoked Mandrake BODY WEIGHT: 135 lb I have been using mandrake for several years and have a great respect for it as an entheogen, but not without fear of its quite toxic effects. It has been used throughout europe and the middleeast for thousands of years, even being mentioned in the bible (where a woman trades her husband for some mandrakes, curious since you can't use them for anything else). It is a relative of datura, henbane, belladonna, and contains up to 0.4%alkaloids (scopolamine, atropine, apotropine, hyoscamine, hyoscine, cuscohygrine, solandrine, mandragorine). These can lead to psychoactive and hypnotic states. Higher doses can induce delerium and lead to death by respiratory parplysis. (atropine is named after Atropa, a death goddess). So be careful. I have chosen to use it only in small doses, as an accent on other trips. I have used it successfuly in combination with psilocybin, LSD, MDMA, damaiana and Cannabis. Ingested orally, mandrake has some pretty substantial purgative effects. It leads to vomiting and diarrhea that can last up to three days, even if taken in too small a dose to get high. My favorite method of ingestion is to mix some mandrake powder into joints to be smoked once the other drugs start to peak. I have also made tinctures out of mandrake and rubbing alcohol. I Let the mix sit in a closed container for three weeks, then dip some cigarrettes into it. When they dry, I have some psychedelic cigarettes. I have found the spirit of mandrake to be an ancient and utterly magical consciousness that gives the impression of having existed for countless aeons, while showing panoramas of images like witch's sabbaths; supersexualised and sadomasochistic views of hell, or heaven depending on your viewpoint. Contrary to things I have read, I DO NOT develop a resistance to its toxic effects with constant use. I do however develop a resistance to its psychoactive effects, which may be tempting to take more in order to get the same high. This is dangerous, because some of the alkaloids can stay in the body for several days. If you reach a critical level, you overdose and lose the ability to breathe. Less than a gram in a week is plenty, especially is used only to accentuate other drugs. Approach mandrake with caution, but that said, if it likes you, it likes you.

我拿了一棵我拥有的植物, 我开始把所有的土壤都洗掉, 我砍掉了整个植物, 甚至是叶子 (取出中间部分), 留下了根和松散的叶子. 我只剩下大约60个叶子和根. 然后把它放进一个咖啡清器里, 我把它放回里面,重新造, 再次,再次,再次,总共造了五次. 咖啡的颜色差不多是咖啡的颜色. 茶叶的好处在于:它很美味,很香,喝起来就能让人平静. 它的味道很大胆,几乎是坚果和一点水果. 这将是令人愉快的, 只是有味道造. 我喜欢喝非常强烈的茶, 我现在正在喝第一杯. 我决定去散步, 听"自称乌"一遍, 我坐在当地一所小学的长椅上, 我带着一个小热瓶充满茶. 科勒乌斯玩的是光. 这几乎总是我最初的效果. 一切都开始看起来像印象派的绘画. 我听说阿卜辛对视力和光线有类似的作用. 每个东西都有一个闪闪发光的湖景效应, 我开始深深地欣赏学校旁边的街道. 这条街是用泥土和石头建造的 (我住在一个无处可见的小镇) 直到大约5年前, 这条路上很少有车. 这使得路上闪闪发光的处女美. 我无法形容它, 但上帝让我爱上了一切...... 科莱斯真的是爱的植物. 萨尔维亚总是让我感觉回来了. 总是有机会有可怕的时间与它, 和结束比以前更糟糕的情绪. 但负面的副作用总是超过积极的. 科勒乌斯是微妙而美丽的, 我意识到我的iPod已经死了. 它可能已经死亡大约15分钟. 我真的不在乎. 无论如何,我也没有太喜欢音乐. 我一直享受着湿的感觉, 我开始思考我的生活. 茶使我几乎完全脱离了自己. 或许把根加入酒中, 我的思想没有流动. 他们觉得自己不存在. 我坐在这里多久了?我对那个房子有什么感觉? 我喝完最后一杯茶. 我是你能想象到的最幸福的情绪. 我担心我可能会从学校走回家的路上到我的子 (上帝保佑...) 我觉得我的内在本质,我的内在存在,我的内在光, 最后一句话应该注意的是, 科勒乌斯从来不是强烈的, 给我生活中的每一件事都添加了一点美好. 我注意到我有点倒,可能是因为强烈的酒让我有点昏昏欲睡. 我开始走回家. 我觉得与我走过的道路有很强的联系. 我几乎可以感觉到我脚步带到这个地方的重量, 我几乎可以看到他们. 几乎触摸他们. 我之前一定是那么紧张. 为什么要担心? 生命是否应该如此激烈, 以至于无论我走到哪里, 我都会留下隐形的滑行痕迹? 当然没有. 没有人应该. 但重要的是永远不要失去对下流的意识. 这里确实存在着某种力量. 它可能没有智慧, 但它将永远无处不在. 无论发生什么事, 爱情总是会胜利. 这是我们生命的时刻. 所有的事情都在流动. 所有的事情都相结合. 科莱斯对我的身体没有太大的影响, 脑子就是它所打的位置. 我无法想象将所有东西放在现在的位置需要多少工作. 工作太多了. 我希望我能欣赏到我周围的随机美丽, 我认为如果我这样做,我可能会死. 我在沙发上醒来. 电视已经关了,我面前还有一个空杯. 我觉得我睡了一下. 我意识到科莱斯想让我睡觉, 我很快就睡着了. 我几乎不记得我的梦, 虽然我记得它是非常自然的感觉. 也许我在山上 - - 我想也许? ,好吧. 经历过的夜晚让我在至少一周后的时刻保持了极高的情绪. 我不确定它是否完全消失了. 这次旅行让我更接近自己的存在. 我不认为这次旅行改变了我. 我认为这次旅行只是一个工具, 带我去更高更完整的东西, 没有人知道. 总的来说,这是我向植物说再见的好方法. 科莱斯一直爱着我. 他总是抱着我. 在我最需要他的时候总是照顾我 (是的,科莱斯是男性). 我可能永远会关心至少一棵花, 因为他给了我如此多的爱和温暖, 毕竟,为什么这一切都应该是关于工厂. 我应该更多地尊重爱的潜流, 而不是我发现它的方法. 希望我永远不会忘记那天晚上学到的东西. 其他: 科勒乌斯 (168):准备/食谱 (30),神秘经验 (9),实体/生物 (37),治疗意图或结果 (49),烂的经验 (4),独自 (16)

Sep 5, 2009. erowid.org/exp/68806 DOSE: 60 leaves oral Coleus (tea) BODY WEIGHT: 110 lb Before I write this report, I would like to note that I have been taking around 1.2 g piracetam a day in conjunction with soya lecithin. TIME: 0:00 I took a plant that I had, one of the most healthy as well, and said a quick prayer for it. I then proceeded to wash out all of the soil, and take just the plant with the roots to my sink. I de-stemmed the entire plant, even the leafs (took out the mid section) and left the roots and the loose leaf material. I was left with around 60 leaves and roots. I then put this into one coffee filter *just BARELY fit* and proceeded to brew an 8 cup load through. Once it was brewed I put it back in and rebrewed. and did that again and again and again for 5 total brews. The brew was now down to about 5 cups, and was about the color of coffee. The nice thing about coleus tea: it is tasty. it is fragrant. it is calming just drinking it. Its got a very bold flavor to it, thats almost nutty and a little fruity. It would be enjoyable just having the flavor to brew. Its a great end of the day tea at any strength, however when I am drinking it for its effects rather than its flavor, I like to have it considerably strong. time: 00:30 I am drinking the first cup now. I decided to go for a walk and listen to Self Titled - tortoise all the way through, since it had been a while. I was sitting on a bench at a local elementary school at about 11pm, smoking cigarettes, and just relaxing. I had brought a small thermos with me full of the tea. time: 00:45 Coleus plays with light. Thats almost always been the initial effect me. Everything begins to look like an impressionist painting of itself. I hear absinthe shares a similar effect on vision/light. Everything has a shimmering/reflection on the lake effect added to it, and becomes played out on itself just a tad. I begin to gain a heavy appreciation for the street running next to the school. This street was made out of dirt and rocks (I live in a sort of small town sort of out in the middle of nowhere) until about 5 years ago, when they re-modeled the school. Few cars drive on this road. It was raining slightly a few hours before, and this causes the road to just shimmer in its virgin beauty. All of the lights loose brightness and gain something else, I cannot quite describe it, but god does coleus make me feel in love with everything....Coleus is truly the plant of love. Salvia always makes me feel taken back. Just bewildered by everything, and theres always a chance of having a horrible time with it, and ending up in a worse mood than before. Datura shares a few traits when smoked with coleus, but the negative side effects always overpower the positive ones. Coleus is subtle and beautiful, and truly speaks to the undercurrent of light and love that the entire world is pulsing with. Time: 1:00 I realize that my ipod has died. It had probably been dead for around 15 minutes. I don't really care. I wasn't enjoying the music much anyways. I had been enjoying the feeling of the wet bench against my legs and the sound of the crickets. I begin to think about my life in general. The tea has made me removed from myself almost completely. Most times this amount of plant material is not this intense, if you could even call coleus intense in any respect. Maybe adding the root to the brew helped punch it up a little bit. My thoughts aren't flowing. They feel non existant. How long have I been sitting here? how do I feel about that house? Time: 1:15 I finish my last cup of tea. I am the most blissful mood you can ever imagine. I fear I might just cum my pants walking home from the school (god forbid...) Nothing external or internal seems to matter right now though. I feel as if my core essence, my core being, my core light, has been stripped raw and is left in a plain of darkness illuminating the emptiness around me. It should be noted with that last statement that coleus is never intense, it just wraps its arms around everything. Adds a nice little touch to everything in my life. I notice I am stumbling a bit, probably due to the fact that the strong brew has made me slightly sleepy. I begin to walk home. I feel strongly connected with the path that I took to get here. I can almost taste the heaviness of my footsteps leading to this place, and I can almost see them. Almost touch them. I must have been so uptight before. Why the worry? Should life be so intense that I have to leave a metaphysical skid mark wherever I go? Of course not. No one should. Sometimes we get wrapped up in things and thats okay, but its important to never loose awareness of the undercurrent. There IS a force at work at here. It may not be intelligent, but it will be forever omnipresent. I feel extremely content with the realization that no matter what happens, love will prevail. time: 1:30 This is the hour of our lives. All things flow. All things mesh. The coleus has not had too much effect on my body, other than making me slightly tired and sleepy. The mind is where it has hit. I cannot imagine how much work it would have taken to put everything where it is right now. So much fucking work. I wish I could appreciate just the random beauty that is going on around me, for just one second, in its entirety. I think if I did that, I just might die. time: 1:45 I wake up on the couch. The tv is off and an empty cup is sitting in front of me. Guess I dozed off. I realize that the coleus wanted me to sleep, and was trying to show me something in my dreams. I fall back asleep quickly. Time: ----- I remember almost nothing from my dreams, although I do remember it being highly natural in feel. Maybe I was on a mountain - I think maybe? Oh well. The night that I experienced left me with an incredibly glowing mood for at least a week afterwards. I'm not sure if it has ever completely faded. Something about this 'trip' brought me so much closer to my own existence. I do not think that it was the trip that changed me. I think that the trip was just a vehicle to bring me to something higher and more complete, and something that is not chemically induced, which I why I think that it never really faded, and I don't think it ever will. ----------- All in all, this was a great way to say my good byes to a plant. Coleus has always loved me. Always held me. Always cared for me when I needed him most (yes, coleus is a male). I will probably always care for at least one coleus plant, seeing as he has given me so much love and warmth I could never repay him even if I grew him and propagated him amoung friends for the rest of my life. And after all why should it all be about the plant. I should pay my respects more to the undercurrent of love than the method with which I discovered it. I hope that I never forget what I learned that night.

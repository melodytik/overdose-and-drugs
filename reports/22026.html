我写这篇文章是因为我相信任何服用像Geodon这样的抗精神病药物的人都应该知道大麻的风险. 我已经服用Geodon只有7天了. 这是一种抗精神病药物, 用于精神分裂症患者, 七天时间. 在第六天, 我真是太傲慢了! 我决定在晚上九点半左右, 继续抽几碗的老植物只是为了看看... 这是一个可怕的错误. 到了9:45,一切顺利,我被石头. 我心情很好,没有什么不对. 但在10点左右, 一切都改变了. 我开始有呼吸困难. 我的思想是阴暗的. 我的心跳很快. 我很难让自己平静下来. 我确信我会死. 我叫醒了 (我18岁,还在家里), 开始她很生气 (这是可以理解的), 我直到凌晨12:40才下来 有趣的,不是吗? 我睡着了,电视还在,胸部还在疼. 现在我知道用极强的抗精神病药不该怎么做了. 我幸运地还活着.

Aug 10, 2005. erowid.org/exp/22026 DOSE: 20 mg oral Pharms - Ziprasidone (daily) 2 bowls smoked Cannabis (plant material) BODY WEIGHT: 167 lb I'm writing this the day after my experience because I believe anyone out there taking an anti-psychotic like Geodon should know of the risks he or she is taking when marijuana is involved. I've been taking Geodon for only 7 days now. It's an anti-psychotic prescribed to schizophrenics and what not to lower the amount of dopamine in the brain. 7 days. And how cocky I was on that 6th day! I decided, at about 9:30 PM, to go ahead and smoke a couple bowls of the ol' plant just to see...wow. What a horrid mistake. At 9:45, things were smooth and I was stoned. My mood was decent and nothing was wrong. But at around 10:00, everything changed. I started to have trouble breathing. My mind was cloudy. My heart was racing. (At 10:18 I checked my pulse - 126 bpm...) I had enormous trouble trying to calm myself down. I was sure that I was going to die. I woke my mom up (I'm 18, still in the house), and explained the situation to her. At first she was angry (understandable), but then she got me to calm down a bit. I didn't come down until 12:40 AM. Interesting, eh? I fell asleep with the TV on and my chest still aching. Of course, I'm still going through weird aches and pains today, but hopefully they're nothing serious...Well, now I know what not to do with an EXTREMELY potent anti-psychotic. I'm lucky to be alive.

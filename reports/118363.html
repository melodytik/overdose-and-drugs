First dose: Saturday evening around 6-8pm ROA: I was using a bottle of 10mg/ml solution of Bromazolam, which tasted terrible so I would put 2 drops at a time in the food I was eating to avoid the taste. Roughly 30 drops would be 1ml or 10mg. After 11 drops and two hours passed I decided to go out skating. I noticed being slightly relaxed and motor function slightly impaired. Coming back from that I decided to take roughly 10 more drops since I didn’t feel much. A few hours after that I'm noticeably clumsy and very relaxed. I looked through my phone and found that I had texted my boss to call out of work Monday. I fully expected to sleep through Sunday and be good for Monday. But I don’t remember if I even woke up Sunday, I somehow texted my boss Monday and I have somehow acquired a half gram THC cart. I found this particularly odd since I never buy half gram carts but I don’t even know where I got it from or when. Even after becoming fully conscious again on Tuesday, my motor functions were heavily impaired, skating was difficult, bumping into things etc. My mental capacity also was severely limited, I had noticeably slow speech and difficulty finding words for things. These effects didn’t go away until Thursday. So, obviously I had taken way too big of a dose. I didn’t realize at the time how big of a dose it was because I didn’t feel much to begin with. I don’t know if it's a better experience at a lower dose, but I’m not going to risk trying that again seeing how easily I lost days of my life.

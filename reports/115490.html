Jun 16, 2021. erowid.org/exp/115490 DOSE: 1 mg oral Flualprazolam (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 86 kg Exactly as expected : an anti-tank missile I was looking for nitazene class products but somewhat disappointed by price or scarcity. Instead I went for a more conventional depressant, a benzo. There are only so many benzos that are sold and still legal but one is all I need. I get those 100x1mg packages which imo gives 200 standard doses for medical purposes. But I wasn't looking for medical so I went to 1mg. It's a mini pill so I thought great for sublingual but actually it's not. Idk what they put in it after 5 mins there was still a fair bit of powder and it has a bad taste. Swallowed the remaining half. Effects some quick 10 minutes maybe although they are limited they seem like the ones coming from the sub route. Within 30min I am almost hammered completely and that's AFTER a triple-cheese burger menu. Which leads me to two conclusions. The product is a knock off from triazolam, and at the same time a alprazolam on steroids. 0.5mg is the limit for keeping consciousness and not forgetting too much. 1mg is a maximum in order to keep a sane mind and a little physical control. More than that is anesthetic. It's not gonna kill me but I feel more impaired than with products like nitazenes, even phenobarbital. Physically for sure. Mental state is ok I guess I am ESL but still type as I should :p Arguably a lot of booze could get me to that place but not with the mental state only physical impairment. Don't think I've been more incapadotated as much in life! Quantities they sell them in are jokes no one makes a 0.5mg pill which would be on par with strong triazolam. Effects are obviously benzo-like so nothing new but because of flualpra's methylated structure it is among the fast acting ones. Duration of action should still be rather short because even though I slept for 11 hours there was no after effect in the morning.

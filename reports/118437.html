Nov 3, 2025. erowid.org/exp/118437 DOSE: 300 ug oral 1D-LSD (blotter / tab) vaporized Hexahydrocannabinol BODY WEIGHT: 80 kg Trapped in Time: A Reflection on My First Bad LSD I took 1D-LSD, consuming 2 blotters, each containing 150 µg, for a total of 300 µg. I felt weird but fascinated by how things moved, aware that I was high, though the effects were mild. My problems began after I vaped some HHC. A confusing message read, "That was the most pure get ready I’ve ever had in my entire life," which triggered intense feelings of dissociation. I started trying to figure out why that sentence had a structure like that, and then I just froze. I felt really weirded out, as I had no idea what my purpose was. After about 3 minutes, I got a small idea about how to fix a little thing about my personality. I immediately went to my notes to start writing it, but it didn’t make sense. I fell into a literal frozen stance, with my head moving from left to top like the sides of a triangle. I couldn’t move; my heart rate was well over 200 bpm, and I was having a panic attack. I had never had a panic attack in my life. I felt like I was stuck in a loop, like a robot in this universe, and that the universe had stopped moving, with every living thing frozen and controlled by an external entity. I spent 10 minutes trying to figure out how to escape this feeling. Somehow, I managed to overcome it and walked home, with multiple thoughts about time stopping. Once home, I started typing on a Discord server for tripsitting, desperately trying to find answers about whatever those triangles were. Looking back at my messages, I saw I was typing "3 triangles," "7:41," "traumatic," and other nonsensical phrases. I wrote over 700 messages, and after an hour and a half of typing, I kind of "escaped" it. I then went outside to smoke, and after calming down, I listened to some music and thought about what the hell had happened. Note that I did not have a trip sitter, I was alone, and while the environment was somewhat good for me, I did not take my diazepam to kill the trip. Overall, this experience was really scary, but I felt better the next day and moved on from it without any lasting effects.

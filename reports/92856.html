我以前曾经用过Vicodin十多次 (从10mg到50mg), 我喜欢每一秒钟 (除了每一秒钟都需要!) 我也经验丰富的其他药物如大麻,DXM,DPH,酒精... 最近我偶然发现了一些Nucynta (Tapentadol) 并服用了3-50毫克的药丸. 我喜欢在晚上喝酒, 听着我最喜欢的说唱音乐, 我已经吃了三粒50毫克的药, (这似乎是一个时代后!) 晚上11点15分 - - ! 这让我感觉像一个半车. 让我进入我爱的"片幸福", 但这次没有预警, 我在玩"使命召唤"游戏, 感觉很舒服, 我喜欢它. 我父亲和哥哥对我说话 (他们不知道我在喝酒), 我发现我的言语有点不同, 我从来没有从片这样感觉. 我决定让我们放松一下,在躺着看电视, 我在客厅呆了好一个小时, 开始昏昏欲睡, 我躺在床上很尬30分钟, 眼睛闭着, 几乎没有意识. 这绝对是最好的部分. 电视在后台播放, 我没有注意, 在12:35我昏倒了. 总的来说, 我很喜欢这种体验. 我喜欢这么多我做了几次, 但没有经验是我第一次一样好. 为了让我的身体能容忍这种情况, 周三或周四我会做我最喜欢的片的事情--去上学. 这听起来很疯狂, 但它让一天过得很慢, 这使我变得更有社交性, 人们似乎觉得我更有趣/有趣. 美国: 发布时间:2016年1月9日 单独使用 (16) 一般 (1)

Jan 9, 2016. erowid.org/exp/92856 DOSE: 150 mg oral Tapentadol (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 165 lb Ahhh opiates, how delightful they are. I've previously experienced Vicodin a good 10+ times (anywhere from 10mg-50mg) and I loved every second of it (minus the need to itch every second!) I've also done Oxycontin once. I'm also experienced when it comes to other drugs like Marijuana, DXM, DPH, Alcohol...but these are by far my favorite high. Recently I stumbled upon some Nucynta (Tapentadol) and took 3-50mg pills. It was about 9pm, and I love taking them at night where I end up passing out while listening to my favorite rap music, to often wake up a few times throughout the night and still under the influence, feels great. 9:05-I popped three 50mg pills (what seemed like ages later!) 11:15pm-BOOM! It hit me like a semi. Usually when opiates begin to kick in they start out slow, and move me into the 'opiate bliss' I've come to love, but this had no pre-warning, the son of a bitch hit me like that! 11:20-I'm playing some Call of Duty, chilling, and I felt so warm and fuzzy I loved it. My father and older brother were talking to me (not knowing I was under the influence) and I could tell my speech seemed a bit different, I was slurring and my voice seemed somewhat more scratchy and I had to force words out more. I never felt like this from opiates. 12:00am-I was getting somewhat tired from these, and decided lets chill out and watch some T.V. while laying down, and boy did I feel like a million bucks. I stayed out in the living room for a good hour when I was starting to nod off, and decided that it was time to go to bed. 12:30am-I laid in a pretty awkward position on my bed for a good 30 minutes straight, eyes closed and barely conscious. This was by far the best part. The T.V. was playing in the background, and I wasn't paying any attention to it, my mind was just wondering off into blissfulness. 12:35am-At around this time I passed out. All in all. I loved the experience. I loved it so much I did it a couple more times, but neither experience was as good as my first. However, I did them in a close time-frame (two days apart) so my body had a tolerance to it. I haven't taken any in a few weeks, and on Wednesday or Thursday I'm going to do my favorite thing with Opiates...Go to school. It sounds crazy, but it makes the day go by so slow, but so worth it. It makes me more sociable and people seem to find me more interesting/funny while on them.

中文译文 致幻剂

我碰巧发现了一棵被认定为圣佩德罗的盆栽仙人掌. 经过阅读有关圣佩德罗的各种报道和过去经验, 我把它磨在咖啡机里, 这种方法产生了96个囊. 我读过类似的摄入方法, 人们吃了100个'00'囊, 我听说最薄弱的圣佩德罗没有梅斯卡林, 而最强大的圣佩德罗却是皮约特的两倍. 我获得的仙人掌是后者 (我摄入囊时我不知道). 在15分钟内我感觉到一个强烈的警报. 在30分钟内, 我开始体验强烈的CEV和OEV. 我决定需要躺下. 这没有帮助. 对于已经过去的时间来说, 我觉得自己得太厉害了. 进入"恐惧". 我正在考虑诱导吐. 没有必要这样做, 在一个小时的标记, 我的身体已经决定 清洗是我经历过的最糟糕的一次. 我觉得我的肚子被扭曲了, 实际上,最后我甚至吐出一点血. 所以接下来的事件可能并没有按时间顺序进行. 我经历过最强烈的视觉体验. 一切都被"几何"幻觉所取代. 我决定要躺下. 我躺在那儿时, 时不时地失去意识. 绝对是过度放大. 在三小时左右, 我还在上升. 我注意到我必须立即去所. 后来我出现了最糟糕的腹. 我记不起所的情况, 我记得有一次我从所掉下来. 冲洗. 洗完水后, 我意识到我的胃实际上并没有被空. 没有人知道我在哪里. 这次是"大清洗". 杯子和杯子的未消化的仙人掌. 这味道很可怕. 我坐在所上, 我从特伦斯·麦肯纳的DMT报告中得到了一个经验. 我坐在那里,在所,我的眼睛关闭. 我感觉到自己心中存在着某种"其他"智能. 我看到了实体. 通过在我的脑海中形成不同的多维物体, 我明白它在说什么. 实际上是多维符号, 类似于日语/克林贡文字. 然后是时候去骑车了. 我觉得我们正穿过某种虫洞或超空间运输系统. 在道的尽头是一个星系团. 我们选择了一个星系, 找到了一颗恒星,并前往它的系统. 然后到一个星球. 整个星球都活着. 字面上的意思. 那里有一个文明. 这座城市大概覆盖了地球的四分之一. 这就像"星球大战"或"联系"之类的东西. 这就是事情变得奇怪的地方 (不,真的!). 实体告诉我,它可以向我展示更多城市,但要做到这一点,他必须使用/居住在我的大脑中. 所以我同意了. 我再次同意了. 由于我不喜欢这部电影, 但这一次我意识到,我给予的越多, 感知的越少. 我相信这是一个陷或无限循环的种类. 我怀疑,如果我没有"醒来", 我可能会完全失去了理智. 这是一个非常激烈和可怕的经历. 我认为这是峰值. 我已经坐在上两个小时了, 我在接下来的5个小时内不断地失去意识, 这比我有过的任何,,LSD,,AMT,2C-T-7或2C-I体验都更激烈. 我吃过和吸过这些物质的"英雄"剂量. 整个体验持续了18个多小时. 我认为我吸收了超过1克的素. 我以前吃过圣佩德罗, 并且从来没有失去它. 我已经发誓不再吃任何含有梅斯卡林的食物. 需要15到20个生命来理解发生在我身上的事情. 在圣佩德罗小心. 有些是超级弱, 但有些我的股一直到阿尔法·星. Harsh Learning Experience Cacti - T. pachanoi by BobBob Citation:   BobBob. "Harsh Learning Experience: An Experience with Cacti - T. pachanoi (exp27988)". Erowid.org . Jul 26, 2005. erowid.org/exp/27988 DOSE: 24 in oral Cacti - columnar (dried) BODY WEIGHT: 200 lb Last year I had a strange dream. This is the recollection of that dream: I had happened across a potted cacti that was identified as San Pedro. Having read various reports on the strength of San Pedro and from past experiences, I decided to ingest two feet, dried. I ground it in a coffee grinder and capped it into '0' capsules. This produced 96 capsules. I've read similar methods of ingestion where people ate 100 '00' capsules with great success. I've heard that the weakest San Pedro contains no mescaline while the most potent San Pedro is twice the potency of peyote. The cacti I acquired were the latter (unbeknown to me when I ingested the capsules). Within fifteen minutes I felt a strong alert. Within 30 minutes I was starting to experience strong CEV and OEV. I decided I needed to lie down. This didn't help. At forty-five minutes I decided that I was tripping way too hard for the amount of time that had passed. Enter 'the fear.' I was pondering inducing vomiting. There was no need for this, at the one hour mark, my body had already decided that the contents of my stomach must be purged. 'La purge' was the worst I've ever experienced. It felt like my stomach was being twisted like water being wrung from a washcloth. In fact, at the end I even spit up a tiny amount of blood. At this point in time, time itself kinda lost all meaning, so the follow events probably are not in chronological order. I was experiencing the most intense visuals I've ever seen. Everything was replaced by 'geometric' hallucinations. I decided that I should lie down. As I lay there, I was passing in and out of consciousness. Definitely over amping. At about the 3 hour mark I was still going up up up. I then noticed that I had to go to the bathroom immediately. I then had the most nasty diarrhea that I've ever had. I don't remember much on the toilet as I was passing in and out of consciousness. At one point I remember falling off the toilet. Flush. After flushing, I realized that my stomach had not in fact been emptied. Thus, I threw up again. This time it was 'La Grande Purge.' Worse than earlier. Cups and cups of undigested cacti matter. The taste was horrid. I then sat back on the toilet to try to get a grip. I then had an experience straight out of the pages of a Terrence McKenna DMT report. I was sitting there, on the john, with my eyes closed. When I felt the presence of some 'other' intelligence in my mindspace. I saw the entity. It then began to attempt to communicate by forming different polydimentional objects in my mindscape. I understood what it was saying. It then attempted to teach me it's 'written' language which was actually multi-dimensional symbols that resembled sort of a Japanese/Klingon script. Then it was time to go on a ride. It appeared to me that we were traveling through some sort of wormhole or hyperspacial transit system. At the end of the tunnel was a galactic cluster. We singled out one galaxy and traveled to it. Singled out one star, and traveled to it's system. Then to a planet. The whole planet was alive. Literally. It had a civilization living on it. The city covered probably 1/4th of the planet. Kinda like on Star Wars or Contact or something. This is where things gets weird (no, really!). The entity communicated with me that it could show me more of the city, but to do so, he would have to use/inhabit a larger portion of my mindscape. So I agreed. When that limit was reached, it made the proposal again, 'give me more, and I'll show you more.' I agreed again. But this time it occurred to me that the more I gave the less I perceived. I believe this was a trap or an infinite loop of sorts. I question that if I hadn't have 'snapped out of it' that I could have lost my mind completely. This was a very intense and frightening experience. I take it this was the peak. I had been sitting on the pot for about 2 hours now and decided to go lay back down. I kept passing in and out of consciousness for probably the next 5 hours or so before I began to reach some sort of level 'normal' for psychedelic exploration. This was more intense than any yage, Salvia, LSD, shrooms, AMT, 2C-T-7, or 2C-I experience I've had. I've eaten and smoked these substances at 'heroic' doses. The whole experience lasted over 18 hours. I figure I must have absorbed over one gram of mescaline. I've eaten San Pedro before this, and never lost it. But after this experience, I have vowed never to eat anything that contains mescaline again. It will take 15-20 lifetimes to integrate what happened to me. Beware the San Pedro. Some is super weak, but some kicked my ass all the way to Alpha Centauri. (And aliens attempted to take over my mind!)

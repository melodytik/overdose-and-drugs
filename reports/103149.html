为了阐述 Acacia confusa 的精神活性, 进行了一项实验, 确定其根皮的口服活性, 没有任何添加剂. 程序 在2杯水中煮沸3大匙碎碎的Acacia confusa根皮, 加入汁, 15分钟. 然后把树皮在另一杯淡水中, 煮25分钟, 再一滴汁. 接下来的提取物与第一个结合并煮成3⁄4杯. 然后让冷却. 生物试验 0:00 这种酒在几乎空腹时被消耗. 味道非常苦, 虽然不像亚华沙那么糟糕. 0:03 轻微的身体. 0:08 惰/中度的身体高开始设置. 0:14 心灵很容易被无生命物体所吸引. 精神高开始. 开始出现恶心. 0:23 试图吐. 不可能. 0:27 周围视觉幻觉和抽象的想法是显而易见的. 0:35 身体疲但精神清醒. 0:54 高度正在消散. 下来了 完全清醒. 开始打字报告. 结论 这种植物的根皮确实有轻微的精神作用. 总体而言,它的诱导效应与中度大麻高类似,尽管伴有恶心. 然而,它并不是 (如果单独服用) 一种迷幻药. 已使用的证券 发布时间:2018年5月6日 混树 (664):一般 (1), 准备/食谱 (30), 单独 (16)

Procedure Boiled 3 tbsp of shredded Acacia confusa root bark in 2 cups of water with a squirt of lime juice for 15 minutes. Then collected the water and boiled the bark in another cup of fresh water for 25 minutes with another squirt of lime juice. The next extract was combined with the first and boiled down into ¾ cup. Then let cooled. Bio-essay 0:00 The brew was consumed on a near empty stomach. Very bitter tasting, although not as bad as ayahuascha. 0:03 Slight body chills. 0:08 Lethargy/moderate physical high began to set in. 0:14 Mind is easily transfixed on inanimate objects. Mental high begins. Nausea begins registering. 0:23 Attempt to vomit. Cannot. 0:27 Peripheral visual hallucinations and abstract thoughts are apparent. 0:35 Feel physically exhausted yet mentally clear and awake. 0:54 High is dissipating. Coming down. 1:15 Completely sober. Begin typing report. Conclusion The root bark of Acacia confusa was indeed mildly psychoactive. Overall its induced effects were similar to a moderate Cannabis high, albeit with nausea. However, it was not (when taken by itself) a psychedelic.

我是一名20多岁的男性, 想尝试一种ED药物. 我可以勃起,但我经常自慰和/或做爱,所以需要很多时间才能勃起, 我从来没有服用过伟哥或西阿里斯, 药丸是20毫克,一个名牌药丸. 据说需要一个小时才能起作用, 然后给你一个16小时的"窗口". 我很担心, 因为我十几岁的时候, 伟哥还很新, 我听说过一些可怕的故事 (言,但可信), 制造商的网站上说10mg (每天最多一次) 是大多数男性的起始剂量. 所以在我这个年龄, 服用两倍的剂量是有点可怕. 我想要把药丸打成两半, 但它不是像Xanax或Adderall这样的固体药丸. 它更像是一个OC - 它有这个时间释放 (我假设) 涂层,然后半固体半粉末的白色化学物质. 我擦掉了一小块的涂层来发现这个. 由于我无法打破它, 我咬了一下大约1/4的药丸 (4或5毫克). 之前我故意自慰很长时间, 确实是如此! 很快就开始了, 大概在一分钟之内. 只是为了纪录我相信它是更安全的得到一个药丸与你想要的剂量,所以你可以留下涂层. 几乎立即开始硬起来, 没有任何精神/身体刺激. 我发现我的性欲没有改变. 如果我投入其中,勃起是强大的和愉快的, 但如果我平静下来它开始消失. 我完成了,然后在接下来的三小时里, 每次我都能勃起, 比平常更强,更容易, 现在已经有9个小时了, 效果已经完全消失. 这是一个有趣的经验, 但阴茎不是冒险伤害的东西通过采取太多的这些东西. 已使用的证券 瓦尔德纳菲尔 (741):第一次 (2),单独 (16)

May 15, 2016. erowid.org/exp/50949 DOSE: oral Vardenafil BODY WEIGHT: 190 lb First Experiment With Levitra I'm a male in my early 20s who got curious about trying an ED drug. I can get erections, but I masturbate and/or have sex a lot, so it takes a lot to get it up and it's hard to maintain. I've never had Viagra or Cialis, but I understood this was somewhat in the middle of those two in terms of potency/length. The pill was 20mg, a name-brand tablet. It's supposed to take an hour to kick in, then give you about a 16 hour 'window'. I was worried because as a teen, when Viagra was new, I heard horror stories (gossip, but believable) about guys our age trying it and getting painful throbbing erections that didn't go away for hours. Moreoever the maker's website says 10mg (once per day maximum) is the starting dose for most men. So twice the dose at my age was a little scary. I wanted to break the pill in half, but it's not a solid scored tablet like Xanax or Adderall. It's more like an OC - it's got this time-release (I presume) coating, then the half-solid half-powdered white chemical inside. I rubbed off a tiny piece of the coating to discover this. Since I couldn't break it, I nibbled off about 1/4th of the pill (4 or 5mg). Beforehand I purposely masturbated for a long time so I would know if the Levitra was responsible for any erection. It sure was too! It kicked in extremely fast, within probably a minute. Just for the record I'm sure it's much safer to get a pill with the dose you want so you can leave the coating on. But it worked out pretty well - I almost immediately started getting hard and it was throbbing within another minute. That was without any mental/physical stimulation. I then masturbated for a while and found that my libido was unchanged. If I got into it, the erection was powerful and enjoyable, but if I calmed down it started to go away. I finished that, then over the next three hours repeated that process twice. Each time I got a good erection, far stronger and easier than normal, but each time less intense than before. It's been about 9 hours now and the effect is completely worn off. Its was an interesting experience, but the penis isn't something to risk hurting by taking too much of this stuff.

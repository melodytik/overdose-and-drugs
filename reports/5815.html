我应该注意,我不会解决我如何收到的药物,我做,然而,知道一个事实,它是纯粹的和积极的DOET. 毫无疑问,这是我一生中最可怕的夜晚, 我服用了12到15毫克, 从星期六晚上9点到星期天晚上11点, 我只能把它描述为从现实世界转移到简短 (实时) 的, 却又生动而清晰的虚构插曲, 其中许多是怪诞而痛苦的. 我做了,说,看到了那些根本没有,和时刻后醒来在同一个地方通常只是着太空. 后来有人告诉我, 我着镜子, 发出一个高音, 显然不像人类的尖叫声. 我怀孕了想法,图像,词语和关系, 我在许多方面都担心自己的生命, 事实上,我不确定我有没有. 我尝试了几次, 剂量从3毫克到15毫克. 虽然我经常去聚会, 周围都是清醒的人, 尤其是那些我知道会很难交往的人 (父母,前女友等), 我知道其他三人也尝试过DOET. 一个几乎没有感觉到任何东西,只报告说"无法入睡".然而,另外两个人也经历了类似于我的感觉. 他告诉我,他的生活在很多方面都变得更好了. 这似乎简单地扩大了他的世界. 我曾经告诉过很多人我的经历, 我甚至写信给舒尔金, 但没有得到回复. 我很想和某人谈谈这个问题, 时间表:5815 DOET (135):未知背景 (20),一般情况 (1)

Mar 22, 2001. erowid.org/exp/5815 DOSE: 12 mg oral DOET (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 150 lb Even Shulgin's notes on DOET surprise me, because based on my experiences, I believe the potential of this chemical has been regrettably overlooked. I should note that I will not address how I received the drug--I do, however, know for a fact that it was pure and positively identified as DOET. My first experience was without a doubt the most terrifying night of my life and certainly a night to which I can trace several key changes in my world. I took between 12 and 15 mg, and tripped from about 9 pm on Saturday until 11 pm on Sunday, when I finally drank several beers and some rum, threw up, and went to sleep. The experience was incredibly intense, with hardcore visuals including prolonged episodes of what I can only describe as departures from the real world into brief (in real time) yet vivid and lucid fictional interludes, many of which were grotesque and painful. I did, said, and saw things that simply were not there, and moments later woke up in the same place having usually just stared off into space. At one point, this kind of episode overcame me while (I was told later) I stared into a mirror and emitted a high-pitched and remarkably non-human sounding scream. I conceived of ideas, images, words, and relationships that I never would have been capable of in any other condition. I feared for my life at many points, and could not be convinced that I would 'return to normal.' In fact, I'm not sure if I have. I tried it several times since, ranging doses from 3 mg to 15 mg. Nothing has been as intense as that first night, though I would often go to parties and surround myself with sober people, especially those I knew would be a challenge to associate with (parents, ex-girlfriends, etc.) I had some unbelievably twisted and complex experiences. I know of three others who have tried DOET. One felt practically nothing, and merely reported 'not being able to get to sleep.' Two others, however, experienced effects much like mine. One tripped for two days and tells me that his life has changed in a multitude of ways for the better. It seems to have simply expanded his world in a tremendous way. I have told many of my experience but have met no one who has tried the substance. I even wrote to Shulgin but received no response. I'd love to be able to talk to someone about this or get some more information, since none seems to be available.

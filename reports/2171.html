美国 我没有参与酒的制备工作, 我没有服用任何药物或维生素补充剂, 经历了一整天没有禁食或过度进食, 旅游指南和厨师杰德把四个囊的碎哈马林 (叙利亚路) 放在我手掌里. 服用这些药物50分钟后, 开始出现几乎无法察觉的模糊感, 不是在视觉领域, 而是在触觉领域: 具体来说, 我身体与周围世界之间的界限变得模糊了, 这种感觉可以描述为把世界吸收到我身边, 我们开始吸食一种轻微的5-MeO-DMT和大麻 (Kosh) 混合物, 足以产生即时的明确的DMT反应, 玻璃管是以这种方式抽烟的几小时. 哈马林/5-MeO-DMT组合可以延长行程数小时,使其可以很快地建立,在20分钟内达到顶峰,然后保持在顶峰,或在它的周围调节上下,波浪和低谷 (或主题的变化). 这是一次"干净"的旅行. 我在Kosh喝完之后立即感到清醒. 然后又回到精神运动的波浪模式, 精神"噪音"的增加和思想的扩散是其特点. 我无法理解声音的语言, 我自己的语言和使用它的能力, 我觉得自己很稳定,我没有发生任何变化, 唯一的视觉幻觉 (世界变化) 是无形的:物体不移动或扭曲, 一个纯粹的概念幻觉过器被放置在我的感觉地图上, 就像一张酸盐和明亮的颜色被放置在投影机上的图片上:过器上的颜色填补了原始图片上的空白;我猜,但我认为我看到的是动词. 我还有一种无法抗拒的笑容. 只是充满空气的行为, 在烟雾的房间里和外面, 足够让我欢笑. 我在这里所做的区别与下降有很大关系, 我从LSD和MDMA中体验到的"快乐"是让我在下降时想要更多的东西, 这种5-MeO-huasca的效果完全不一样, 似乎只能用10到40分钟的DMT/5MeO急性旅行, 并用Harmaline延长到几个小时... 它扩展, 它就像旅行中的显微镜. 值得注意的是, 哈马林对LSD有软化和扩张的作用, 5-MeO-huasca: 强烈推给寻求快乐的人和探险家. 对于未知者来说, 我注意到自从我开始服用DMT/5-MeO以来, 我不称之为回忆, 因为我的意识并没有突然扭曲 (除了一个显著的例外, 导致一些有趣的小事情, 比如扩大语言的定义, 或他们的猫. 没有大问题. 5-MeO-DMT (58), 叙利亚路 (45), 药水 (822):小组 (2-9) (17),组合 (3),一般 (1)

Ayahuasca Lite Pharmahuasca (Syrian Rue & 5-MeO-DMT) by el Lelande NiMH Citation:   el Lelande NiMH. "Ayahuasca Lite: An Experience with Pharmahuasca (Syrian Rue & 5-MeO-DMT) (exp2171)". Erowid.org . Jun 26, 2000. erowid.org/exp/2171 DOSE: T+ 0:00 4 oral Syrian Rue (capsule) T+ 1:30 smoked 5-MeO-DMT (powder / crystals) T+ 1:30 smoked Cannabis BODY WEIGHT: 190 lb I was not involved in the preparation of the ayahuasca, so unfortunately do not have dosage details. I was on no medication or vitamin supplements, had gone through a regular day with no fasting or excessive meals at the end of a week during which I'd not ingested anything psychoactive. Jade, guide and master trip-chef, dropped four capsules of crushed up Harmaline (Syrian Rue) in my palm. Fifty minutes after taking these, there was an onset of barely detectable fuzziness, not in my visual field but in my tactile field: specifically, the boundary between my body and the world around it were blurring, the sensation can be described as either absorbing the world into myself or the world absorbing me. An hour and a half after taking the Syrian Rue we began smoking a light concoction of 5-MeO-DMT and marijuana (Kosh), enough for an immediate and definite DMT reaction, not a dose high enough for space flight or warp drive, but the crackly increase of frequency of the body, the epidermis, followed by a scattering of vision and a wave of mental hallucinations/noise. The glass pipe was smoked this way on and off for a few hours. The Harmaline/5-MeO-DMT combination extends the trip for many hours, allowing it to build very quickly, reaching a peak within 20 minutes, then remain at that peak, or modulating around it up and down, waves and troughs (or variations on a theme). It was a 'clean' trip. My head felt clear immediately after puffing on the Kosh. My awareness was suspended in this clarity for a few seconds, and would then be dropped back into the wave pattern of mental motion, characterized by an increased mental 'noise' and a diffusion of thoughts. Vocal language escaped me -- my own language and my ability to use it would not do justice to the information that was delivering itself to me and transubstantiating in my mind. 'I' felt stable, 'I' underwent no change, but 'I' was swept up in the drastically chaotic world around me. The only visual hallucinations (the changing world) were invisible: objects were not moving or warping, but I watched layers upon layers of action, or energy, or frequencies, existing around and between everything. A purely conceptual hallucination filter placed over my sensory map, like a sheet of acetate with light colors placed over a picture on a projector: the colors on the filter filled in some blanks on the original picture; I'm guessing, but I think I was seeing verbs. There was also an irresistible urge to grin. The mere act of filling my lungs with air, both in the smokey/misty room and outside, was joyful enough to give me the giggles. It wasn't *pleasure* so much as joy ... the distinction I make here has a lot to do with the come-down, which was effortless other than exhaustion from so much mental activity. The *pleasure* I experience from LSD and MDMA is one that tends to make me want more when I'm coming-down, like a slight withdrawal. There is none of that with this 5-MeO-huasca, which really seems to do no more than take the 10 to 40 minute DMT/5MeO accute-trip and stretch it out with Harmaline to many hours ... it expands, it slows way down and drifts you along with great big feathery wings rather than strapping rocket boosters to your ass. It work like a microscope on the trip. It seems worth noting that Harmaline has a softening and expanding effect on LSD, and possibly others (that I haven't tried mixing it with yet). 5-MeO-huasca: highly recommended for pleasure-seekers and explorers alike. For the uninitiated, I'll say that I have noticed a change in my daily pattern of thoughts since I've begun taking DMT/5-MeO. I don't call these flashbacks because I don't have sudden distortions in consciousness (with one notable exception during which a quadrant of my visual field disappeared, bending the remaining three quadrants together, for a very distorted hour-and-a-half), but I do take a lot with me back from a trip. Mostly I call it the breakdown of conceptual barriers resulting in funny little things like expanding the definition of language to the point where some folks are gonna look at you funny when you're talking to them. Or their cats. No biggie.

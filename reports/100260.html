作为一个热衷于研究化学品的用户, 我认为它会类似于醇, 产生轻微的嗜睡感, 第一个错误. 我把手指进袋子, 第二个错误 接下来的几天发生了什么是模糊, *去银行试图开设一个账户. 后来因为我不记得的原因被拒绝. 我和女朋友因为它而发生了争. 在一天的时间里, 摄入了一克乙酸. 我用它来对抗我心灵的迷雾, 我相信这是疯牛病或另一种痴呆症. 我没有像预期的那样感到昏昏欲睡或醉, 我被禁止在凌晨3点独自去看邮件. 尽管如此,我还是去看邮件. 一个人. * 经过处理一些抹纸后意外摄入了一剂未知数量的25i-NBOMe. 我不确定它是进了我的眼睛,吞下了它还是什么. 开始有强烈的视觉开放眼睛和常见的狗的感觉我从迷幻药. 我知道我在当2Pac的嘴唇开始可疑地在我的音乐播放器. 这是我从任何药物中得到的最生动,最强烈的视觉效果. 在心理病的第一个迹象上睡着了. 总的来说,我失去了大约3天的工作记忆, 我相信法纳泽帕姆会与那些可怕的药物相匹配. (BZ和迪特兰让我想起了) 它与其他二类药物完全不同. 我会沉睡几个小时, 然后醒来不知道我是谁或在哪里. 芬纳西帕姆就像那些"黑衣男士"棒一样, 这种药物是我永远不会再接触的药物. :没有一次. 其他: 药品 - (505): 糟糕的旅行 (6), 火车事故和旅程灾难 (7), 第一次 (2), 各种 (28)

Three-Day Dementia Phenazepam by Mercury in Aries Citation:   Mercury in Aries. "Three-Day Dementia: An Experience with Phenazepam (exp100260)". Erowid.org . May 30, 2013. erowid.org/exp/100260 DOSE: oral Pharms - Phenazepam (powder / crystals) oral Ethylphenidate (powder / crystals) 25I-NBOMe (blotter / tab) BODY WEIGHT: 120 lb As an avid research chemical user, I wanted to test out this phenazepam everyone was yapping about. I assumed it would be similar to benzos and produce a mild sleepy feeling, so I took it after a stimulant binge to help me sleep. Mistake #1. I dipped my finger in the bag and proceeded to eyeball a very 'small' unknown dose. Mistake #2. What happened for the next few days was a blur, but I'll summarize what I remember: *Went to the bank to try and open an account. Was denied for some reason I couldn't recall later. Ended up in an argument with my girlfriend over it. *Ingested a whole gram of ethylphenidate over the course of a day. (Or was it two days?) I took it to combat the mental fog I was feeling, which I convinced myself was Mad Cow disease or another form of dementia. I was not sleepy or drunk feeling like expected--I was completely delirious, trapped in a waking-dream state like a zombie. *Fell face-first into the bed after being told I shouldn't go check the mail alone at 3:00 a.m. Went and checked the mail anyway. Alone. *Accidentally ingested an unknown dose of 25i-NBOMe after handling some blotters. Not sure if I got it in my eye, swallowed it or what. Proceeded to have INTENSE open-eye visuals and the usual shitty feeling I get from psychedelics. Knew I was tripping when 2Pac's lip began quivering suspiciously on my music player. These were the most vivid, intense visuals I've gotten from any drug. Managed to go to sleep at the first signs of psychedelia. Overall, I lost about 3 full days of my working memory to a tiny dab of powder. After reading up on MK-ULTRA mind control experiments, I believe phenazepam would've fit right in with the nightmarish drugs used in those tests. (BZ and ditran come to mind). It is nothing like other benzodiazepines. I would fall asleep soundly for a few hours, but then wake up not knowing who or where I was. Phenazepam is like those Men In Black sticks that erase your memory in a single flash. It's on a very short list of drugs I'll never touch again. Phenazepam: Not Even Once.

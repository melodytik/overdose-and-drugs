我今天三小时前服用了25毫克的5-MeO-DPT. 在旅行之前, 我对半个月前100毫克的lysergamide 药物有过耐药性. 在服用5-MeO-DPT前2小时, 我服用了5毫克的科雷格和12.5毫克的梅克利辛 (一种通用运动恶心药丸). 我还在早上喝了一小部分D8-THC液. 我的秤可能错了5毫克, 因为它是旧的和过度使用, 我确实打算服用25毫克 (在内心争论是否服用28毫克或40毫克之后). 根据报道, 我预计会有轻微的视觉, 非常沉重, 然而, 在我的5-MeO-DPT旅行中, 虽然头部动, 但不是旅程的亮点. 我可以表现得清醒, 欺骗一些人只要我不需要聊天或呆在附近很长时间. 让人分心的感觉会让人很难用这种物质欺骗人太久. 如果我等待更长时间, 我会得到更强烈的视觉效果. 我发现对于更微妙的效果的耐受性, 可能需要2周到一个月才能像以前那样强烈地感知它们. 吸食大麻可以增强视觉效果. 视觉效果非常不明显. 我在YouTube上看PC游戏, 我在半小时内感觉到它,并在一小时内达到顶峰. 首先引起我的注意的是我脑海中的触觉, 脉从我的想象中开始, 慢慢地穿过整个身体. 有时甚至在中途也会发生. 它们的脉冲非常频繁, 间隔3-10分钟. 我学会了如何在某种程度上"潜入海浪", 在旅途中我看到了这种物质的巨大潜力. 如果我让海浪来, 感觉就很棒. 波浪是多彩的, 他们有基督的精神. 吸食大麻增强了我的触觉. 如果我允许的话,脉冲可以与音乐和声音协同工作. 整段时间,我的头脑感觉60-80%的能力,像清醒. 虽然头部动的确存在, 我不能说我发现5-MeO-DPT像DMT或它的亲属一样"深度" (我以前使用过5-MeO-DMT,这是深度). 实际上,5-MeO-DPT 确实有一点超现实光, 想到DMT时, 我会想到很多东西, 但"明亮"并不是我用来描述DMT的词; 相比之下, 作为我尝试的第一个替代DPT分子,5-MeO-DPT也具有相同的"明亮"感. 虽然我能感觉到声音,但音频基本上没有受到影响. 我确实觉得我可以服用更高的剂量, 根据报道, 我准备服用一种可能令人恶心和紧张的物质. 虽然我确实相信我可以保持这种物质下来没有努力不管运动恶心药. 对于紧张感, 我在上升过程中经历了一点紧张感, 显然,您的行程可能会有所不同, 因为5-MeO胺经常被指责感到紧张, 5MeO-DPT 25毫克口服是一种相对功能性的,新的,共感的,非常触觉的旅行, 我感觉到物质,但我慢慢下来脉已经消失了. 时间表: 116815 发布时间:2022年11月28日 5-MeO-DPT (391),药物 - 梅克利辛 (247),大麻 (1):未知背景 (20),组合 (3),第一次使用 (2),一般 (1)

Nov 28, 2022. erowid.org/exp/116815 DOSE: T+ 0:00 12.5 mg oral Pharms - Meclizine T+ 2:00 22 - 28 mg oral 5-MeO-DPT T+ 0:00 smoked Cannabis BODY WEIGHT: 140 lb I took 25mg of 5-MeO-DPT three hours ago today. Before the trip, I had a psychedelic tolerance from a 100ug lysergamide trip 6 days ago. I have extensive experience with substances. I took 5mg of Coreg and 12.5mg of meclizine (a generic motion sickness pill) 2 hours before taking the 5-MeO-DPT. I also had a tiny amount of D8-THC tincture in the morning. My scale might be off as much as 5mg because it's old and overused, but I do have plenty of experience weighing out small quantities of powder and I'd say it looked like 22-28mg. I definitely aimed to take 25mg (after an internal debate with myself over whether or not to take 28mg or, jokingly, 40mg). Based on reports, I was expecting a slightly visual, very heavy, sedating head trip with a significant body load. However, this was mostly not true in my 5-MeO-DPT trip. The head trip was present, but not the highlight of the trip. I could have acted sober and fooled a number of people as long as I didn't have to converse or stick around for very long. A distracting feeling would make it hard to fool someone with this substance for too long. If I had waited longer for my psychedelic tolerance to reset, I would have gotten stronger visuals. Psychedelic tolerance whatsoever is said to take at least 3 days, but I find that for tolerance to the finer effects to reset, it may take 2 weeks to a month to be able to perceive them as strongly as before. Smoking cannabis enhanced the visuals. The visuals were very unobvious. I was watching PC gameplay on YouTube as I came up. I was feeling it at half an hour and reached the peak at one hour. The first effect to catch my attention was a tactile sensation in my head that tingled the whole body in slow, powerful pulses. The pulse started in my imagination and moved slowly down through the whole body. When the pulses began, they would sometimes fall flat, even in mid course. The great pulses came very regularly, like 3-10 minutes apart from each other. I learned how to be able to "dive into the waves" to some extent partway into the trip and I saw great potential in this substance. The waves were an awesome feeling if I let them. The waves were colorful and they had the spirit of Christ. Smoking cannabis enhanced the tactile sensation for me. The pulses could synergize with music and sounds if I let them. All the while, my mind felt 60-80% capable, like sober. Again, the head trip was present but certainly nowhere as powerful as I expected. I couldn't say I found 5-MeO-DPT "deep" like DMT or its relatives (I have used 5-MeO-DMT before, which was deep). 5-MeO-DPT did have a bit of that surreal light that base DPT has. When I think of DMT, I think of a lot of things, but "bright" just isn't a word that I would use to describe DMT; in contrast, I would describe DPT as something "bright". So being the first substituted DPT molecule I tried, I would say 5-MeO-DPT also carries that same "bright" feeling. While I could feel sounds, audio was mostly unaffected. Health wise, I do feel like I could have taken a significantly higher dose without feeling substantially worse. Based on reports, I was prepared to take on a substance that was possibly nauseating and tight. The half of a motion sickness pill I took before dosing helped eliminate nausea, though I do believe I could have kept this substance down without effort regardless of the motion sickness pill. As for tightness, I experienced a small amount of tightness during the come-up but everything else and afterward felt fine. Apparently, your mileage may vary, as 5-MeO tryptamines are often accused of feeling tight, so I was better safe than sorry this first time. 5-MeO-DPT 25mg orally was a relatively functional, novel, synaesthetic, very tactile trip, modest but present in other senses, and next time, I will take much more! 4 hours after dosing and I feel the substance, but the pulses are gone as I come down slowly.

Jul 28, 2015. erowid.org/exp/106314 DOSE: 20 mg oral U-47700 (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 200 lb To start off, I've had experience with a wide variety of psychoactive substances ranging from the hardest of psychedelics like DMT, LSD, 60x Salvia to the bliss of opioids like Opium (tea), Tramadol, Oxycodone, and just about everything in-between. I recently ran across a novel opioid substance by the name of U-47700 in search of a legal and easily accessible alternative to traditional opioids. From my understanding, it is a mu receptor agonist, suggesting that effects should closely resemble other opioids. Anyway, to the trip report! My very first experience, I took 20mg which is a somewhat conservative dose. Since it was my first time, and there wasn't very much information on the substance, I figured 20mg would be a good place to start. I have no opioid tolerance whatsoever, am in pretty decent shape, and generally have a pretty good overall mindset. 20mg down the chute. This stuff tasted awful - very bitter! I sat waiting anxiously for the first effects to start coming on. No more than 20 minutes later, I start feeling a warm sensation in my stomach and a mild euphoria coming on. 'Man!' I thought to myself, 'I like this stuff already!'As the title suggests, the effects were in fact very similar to tramadol; a substance I've had at least a few dozen experiences with. I sat there for an hour or so playing video games with my friend, and felt completely content with everything - a feeling I usually get when on opioids. It wasn't a powerful 'rush' of euphoria, if you will, but rather it's just more of a feeling of being in a wonderful mood. Another thing I noticed is that it (albeit unsurprisingly) makes you feel very relaxed and tired, and that your breathing slows down and breaths are deeper. You could easily nod off if you took a higher dose, say 40-50mg. Perhaps a dose that high may produce slight nausea, but nothing overwhelming by any means. The effects lasted for probably two or three hours before tapering off, and probably an additional hour or two before baselining completely. I had absolutely no trouble sleeping that night. Overall, U-47700 definitely has some recreational value to it. I enjoy it as more of an end-of-the-day relaxer, but opioids by nature have an addictive property to it, so be mindful of your habits! This definitely felt safer than a lot of other things I have tried; I never noticed any feeling of toxicity to it. I would definitely recommend this to anybody looking for a good RC opioid; you won't be disappointed.

Took 1.5 grams when it got to me and nothing happened. Two days later, took 2.5 grams at once, and 45mins later, felt a bit of stimulation, but no noted euphoria just a bit tingly. Researched about MAO-B inhibition and found cats claw (uncaria tomentosa) for this attempt. Took it for rest of week. Then Saturday (tonight) decided to go big or go home. I read that caffeine also inhibits MAO, so decided to take dosing into my own hands. 6:50pm - took 2.5gs PEA...took 4 capsules (500mg ea) with strong coffee and 3 capsules of cats claw and tried to insufflate the 5th cap... 6:50pm - took 2.5gs PEA...took 4 capsules (500mg ea) with strong coffee and 3 capsules of cats claw and tried to insufflate the 5th cap... WOW that burns... Didn't even make it through the line. Ended up dissolving the rest of it in water and drinking. Sniffing PEA is 10x worse than any other drug I've done. Cocaine is like air compared... Even MDMA or meth are not half as bad as this stuff! 7:10 - take 1 gram PEA with the same coffee -no noted change in state 7:20 - take another gram PEA with same coffee - at 7:25 feel a small rush of tingling and blood pressure increase 7:30 - take 3rd gram PEA -by now first rush is pretty much gone 7:40 - take 4th and last gram of the night - felt a bit of a rush, but nothing too crazy Now waiting for effects to start... 8:03 - 'Oh god here it comes' feeling in my brain - first of many rushed starts not too strong but definately there. Tiny bit of euphoria and lots of tingling around circumference of my head Then on it goes.. Rush kind of settles, then next thing you know BAM another wave of tingling and a bit more euphoria. Tried to get up and walk, body feels heavy. 8:20 approx. - waves are coming every 5 minutes each one getting stronger and stronger.... More 'pulsing' from huge increase in blood pressure each time a wave hits then settling. Along with the pressure feeling is a very strong tingling sensation on skin. Euphoria is becoming stronger almost to that of a mild MDMA high, or the feeling when I first notice I am getting high while smoking weed. Blood pressure is through the roof... Feels like my face is red as blood along with my whole body. At about 8:40 the strongest wave is ramping up - I'm sitting on the floor against the wall reading a forum post about PEA on my blackberry and all of a sudden I realize that I went blank almost like I passed out for a short bit and that I'm reading the same line over a second time now... I look at the clock and it's been 5 minutes!!!... I notice that my hand holding the Blackberry is funny looking... WTF from the knuckles to the tips of my fingers on the back of my hand is blood red like a rash.. Palm is same with red stopping just past the wrist... Look at my other hand and its the same... So I start freaking out a little and getting fearful. I go to the washroom to examine myself in the mirror with my shirt off and my shoulders are beet red like a sunburn...and from my spine tapering off outward is beet red... My face and neck are completely bloodshot. My heart is pumping a million miles a minute. And I'm still having these rushes every couple minutes that are gradually getting less and less, but more scary each time. My heart is pumping a million miles a minute. And I'm still having these rushes every couple minutes that are gradually getting less and less, but more scary each time. The color of red in my hands normalizes just a tad by the end of each wave just in time for the next one to come...redness brightens up again... Heart is still racing. 8:55 -finally my fear is becoming less after realizing that this occurance is repeating and I'm still alive and breathing normally... Now I just wait for the rushes to end... and the hands and body to turn back to normal color. Pea is not really a 'drug' high, but more of a body-revving substance that makes your body almost burst from the inside out. Yes there is a feeling of euphoria during the bursts, but the feeling of uncontrollability over my body's reaction is too much to have to think about, because my brain is still working as if I'm sober... There is no feeling of being lost in the high... It's as if I am completely normal but my heart is going nuts, my body is turning beet red under pressure from the interaction of the PEA with brain chemicals to create adrenaline and noradrenaline. I get the bonus of having a dopamine release during all this to create the euphoria, but a high is supposed to be a thing that you can like without boundaries, not being able to have a fearful mindset of 'am I gonna make it through this alive??'. Compared to other drugs like coke and e, when I'm on those drugs, my mind is lost in some place unexplainable... I don't even have the ability to think about my body's response or how my heart is doing or if I'm about to stroke. Overall, I did enjoy the euphoric feeling associated with the PEA effects, but weighing that against the thoughts I had to overcome to enjoy it?? Not worth it to me. This was a learning experience for me, at first I was looking for something to get high on that was legal... I thought PEA would do that for me, but I've learned that it's not my kind of thing.

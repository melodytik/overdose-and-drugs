我得到了一个免费的4-MeO-PCP样本. 我打算写一篇关于我对这种物质的实验的报告. 今天早上我服用了300毫克的德克斯特罗米多芬 (纯). 这对我来说是很正常的. 我平均每周服用3至4次. 它在我的生活中扮演多个角色, 其中两个主要的角色是:我的DOC, 和我的情绪稳定剂. 我对DXM有很高的耐受性,即使服用300毫克也能正常运作. 到了晚上23:40我摄入了大约100毫克的4-MeO-PCP. 我的体重表往往偏差5到10毫克. 这味道像我预计燃烧过渡电路的味道. 像大多数药物一样非常痛苦. 我觉得我的精神正常, 但我觉得我的身体开始发生变化. 可能是血压变化,不知道. 打电脑游戏或其他东西. 我发现我有一种轻微的感觉, 大脑开始有点奇怪. 我正在服用125毫克多来弥补我认为的分离性耐受性. T+2:30 - 强度更高,感觉很奇怪. 身体上的感觉像我焦虑或许. 我以为我需要小便,但什么都没有出来. 我觉得很累,但这可能只是因为我今天早起. 开始有这样的感觉, 失去了平衡,视力开始"滞后". 我感到越来越奇怪. 我洗手的时候发生了一些奇怪的事情. 我开始听到一个女人的声音告诉我'我知道你在那里'伴随着一个冲动的声音. 我的子抽一次相当暴力. 我没有做太多. 我和我的室友聊了一会儿, 玩过一场"星际战斗2"的比赛, 结果非常糟糕. 我发现我的火车总是被什么东西打断. 基本上我会说话而失去一部分句子, 在星际飞船我仍然可以很好地控制但我不能很容易地制定战略. 我不小心撞到家里的东西, 只有深度感知和自我意识正在消失 尤其是在我的手臂和腿部. 这非常不愉快. 奇怪的想法没有意义. 眼睛闭着, 我进入一个充满活力的梦境世界, 我觉得我的身体被拉到地面, 这很奇怪. 现在回想一下:将来我可能会多试一试. 在达到高峰之前, 这可能是基因组胺释放. 下次我一定会尽量休息,100%清醒, 另一个因素是我在肚子满的时候服用. 这种经历并非令人高兴, 另一份报告描述了类似于中性偏执的情况. 这种药物让我听到声音和看到影子的人, 但它并不可怕. 我没有感觉到有人想抓住我, 但我一直感觉到不存在的东西试图与我互动. 其他: 4-MeO-PCP (498):第一次 (2),实体/生物 (37),一个人 (16)

Tense Nerves 4-MeO-PCP by bujmiv Citation:   bujmiv. "Tense Nerves: An Experience with 4-MeO-PCP (exp99012)". Erowid.org . May 28, 2013. erowid.org/exp/99012 DOSE: T+ 0:00 100 mg oral 4-MeO-PCP T+ 2:00 125 mg oral 4-MeO-PCP BODY WEIGHT: 125 lb I got a free sample of 4-MeO-PCP. My intention is to write a decent report on my experimentation with the substance. This morning I took 300 mg dextromethorphan (pure). This is fairly normal for me. I take this amount 3-4 times on an average week. It plays multiple roles in my life, the two main ones being: my DOC, and my mood stabilizer. I have a high tolerance to the DXM and function fairly normally even on 300 mg. At 23:40 I ingested about 100 mg 4-MeO-PCP. My scale tends to be off by 5-10 mg. It tasted like I would expect a burnt transistor to taste. Very bitter like most drugs. T+0:40 - I feel normal mentally but I think I'm starting to sense a change in my body. Blood pressure change maybe, don't know. Going to play a computer game or something for a bit. T+1:00 - Laid down for a few minutes and started noticing patterns in the dark. When I opened my eyes I realized I feel kind of light and I'm getting slight tracers. T+2:00 - Weak feeling like I'm coming up on DXM or nitrous maybe. Brain is starting to act a little odd. I am taking 125 mg more to make up for what I perceive to be a dissociative tolerance. T+2:30 - Stronger CEVs, feel weird. Sensation in body like I'm anxious maybe. Thought I needed to pee but nothing came out. Feel tired but it might just be because I got up early today. Starting to get those sensations where you temporarily feel lost/confused. Losing balance and vision starting to 'lag.' T+2:50 - Comeup has never stopped. I keep feeling more strange. I was washing my hands and something weird happened. I started hearing a female voice telling me 'I know you are there' accompanied by a rushing sound. At the same moment my vision dimmed heavily for a second and my neck twitched one time quite violently. I have not been doing much. I talked to my roommate for a bit then laid down longer. Played one game of Starcraft II and did terribly. While playing or talking I found that my train kept getting interrupted by nothing. Basically I'd talk and lose part of a sentence but still be able to recall what I meant to say. In Starcraft I could still control fine but I couldn't strategize easily. T+3:00 - It keeps feeling stronger. I am accidentally bumping into things around the house even though I'm not wobbly. Just depth perception and self awareness are disappearing so far T+4:00 - I am quite dissociated. When I sit completely still there is a growing uncomfortable sensation in my body, particularly my arms and legs. It is very unpleasant. Strange disconnected thoughts that don't make sense. T+5:00 - With my eyes closed I go to a vivid dream world and unlike DXM or NO2 I can remember what I experience after I come out of it. T+6:00 - My body feels like it's being pulled to the ground but I feel weightless. Odd. Retrospect: I might test this more in the future. A bit before I peaked I felt very uncomfortable. It might have been histamine release. Next time I will definitely try to be well rested, 100% sober and take a dose more like 150mg. Another factor was that I dosed on a full stomach. The experience was not euphoric at all, it was not dysphoric either though. Another report described something like neutral paranoia. This drug made me hear voices and see shadow people but it wasn't scary at all. I didn't feel like anyone was out to get me but I kept sensing nonexistent things trying to interact with me.

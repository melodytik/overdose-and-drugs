我在2003年有过一些偏执和焦虑问题, 这种东西很好, 直到我的医疗保险用完, 作为替代品, 精神病医生每天给我开300毫克的Thorazine, 吃这些东西时,我总是感到疲倦. 我没有任何能量. 我和一个朋友在外面染色我的车窗 (我们正在做的一个小项目). 我记得最后一件事就是走到垃圾桶扔东西. 我在公寓的阳台上醒来, 远处听到救护车的警报. 几秒钟内, 一切都是黑白, 医护人员到达, 但违背妻子和朋友的建议, 他们离开了. 我刚刚从医院住了一周回来, 后来我妻子告诉我我有一个全面的发作. 我去看了我的精神病医生, 他把它归因于托拉. 他立即将我从药物中解脱, 并将我放入缓解剂, 以防未来的发作. 托拉是一件糟糕的事情! 其他: 药品 - 原 (318):小组 (2-9) (17),健康问题 (27)

Feb 16, 2007. erowid.org/exp/49656 DOSE: 300 mg oral Pharms - Chlorpromazine (daily) BODY WEIGHT: 280 lb Back in 2003, I was having some problems with paranoia and anxiety, and was prescribed Seroquel. This stuff worked ok, until my health insurance ran out, and I could no longer afford it. My psychiatrist prescribed 300mg of Thorazine daily as a substitute, since Thorazine is an older medicine with a generic (chlorpromazine), and I could easily afford it. When taking this stuff, it made me tired all the time. I had no energy whatsoever. Anyway, after a week of taking it I was outside with a friend tinting the windows on my car (a little project we were working on). The last thing I remember was walking to the trash can to throw something away. I woke up on the balcony of my apartment some time later, with the sounds of ambulance sirens in the distance. Everything was in black in white for a few seconds, eventually, I got to my feet and ran inside. The paramedics arrived, but against my wife & friend's advice, I told the paramedics to 'Get the fuck out of my house!'. They left. I had just gotten back from a week in the hospital, and had no desire to go back. My wife later told me that I had a full blown seizure. I went to see my psychiatrist, who attributed it to the thorazine. He promptly took me off the stuff, and put me on depakote, to guard against future seizures. Thorazine sucks!

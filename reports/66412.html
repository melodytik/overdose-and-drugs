我发现了一些GBL, 并决定我应该测试水. 我刚刚完成了一次中期考试, 几位朋友可能会乘火车从纽约到蒙特利尔见我, 但他们到达的时间已经过去了, 我有5毫升的瓶子,决定从2毫升开始. 我检查了时间,以注意到影响,晚上8点. 服用药物后, 我坐下来等待效果. 经过大约15分钟的时间, 我觉得音乐很愉快, 但这是我最喜欢的乐队之一, 经过15分钟的时间 (现在是晚上8点半), 我发现效果相当令人失望, 我把另外1.5毫升放进一杯, 喝下有毒的品味液. 在接下来的30分钟里, 我很难协调, 发现自己在家里走路时碰到家具和墙壁. 我很容易闭上眼睛, 当我闭上眼睛时,会有道效应, 我刚刚看到的图像会在1到2秒的时间内从我的视野中消失, 我很喜欢摇摇头. 我很喜欢摇头. 整个身体都有一种化学毒性的感觉. 我决定一些大麻可能有助于恶心,所以我打破了一块和吸了一碗. 不幸的是,恶心持续了. 我听到有人敲门. 我很兴奋,很紧张,很后悔我喝醉了, 我地走到门口,我们互相拥抱,说话,我邀请他们进来. 我告诉他们我已经服用了GHB, 不想向他们解释GBL在体内分解成GHB. 我期待着参与毒品的社交方面, 我觉得很好, 但没有明显的高兴感, 我认为我的高兴情绪很大程度上是由于我的朋友的存在, 我确实注意到我的触觉有一种缓慢. 如果我触摸我的脸, 我仍然可以感觉到我手的压力, 我们走下圣. 劳伦特去一个小脏的酒吧, 他们可以得到几杯酒后, 这么长的旅行. 知道GBL和酒精不应该混在一起. 走到那里, 我一直在摇摇欲, 碰到我的两个朋友, 我觉得自己的身体比心灵更醉. 我知道自己看起来有多可笑, 我迫切需要一些水, 我觉得这可能有助于缓解我的强烈恶心. 我们到了酒吧. 时间大概是10点15分或10点30分, 我觉得自己很生病. 我的朋友说他们会给我一些水, 我找到了一个展位,躺下. 我立刻开始睡着了. 我醒来时,我的朋友们在旁边, 我还没有注意到. 我们离开了酒吧, 看到我显然太疲了. 我在回程中一直喝水, 在家里,我们吃了一些南和胡姆斯,看了Seinfeld. 我立刻昏倒了. 我在凌晨1点被朋友们唤醒, 他们试图让我离开沙发, 我倒在床上睡着了. 我不会再服用这种化学物质. 整个经历中显然存在毒性. 效果似乎与酒精相似, 但不那么好. 虽然酒精似乎能促进对话, 由于GBL引起的嗜睡,恶心和缺乏运动能力, 两个指向下. 其他: 总体情况 (1), 困难经历 (5), 其他 (28)

Oct 11, 2009. erowid.org/exp/66412 DOSE: T+ 0:00 2 ml oral GBL (liquid) T+ 0:30 1.5 ml oral GBL (liquid) T+ 1:00 1 bowl smoked Cannabis - Hash (tar / resin) BODY WEIGHT: 145 lb Being continually interested in altered states, I came across some GBL and decided that I should test the waters. This particular evening was well suited for experimentation, seeing as I had just finished a midterm and had nothing to do. There was the possibility of a few friends taking a train from New York to see me in Montreal, but it was well past their time of arrival and I figured that they must be coming up another day. I had a 5mL vial at my disposal, and decided to begin with 2 mL. I checked the time to take note of the effects, 8 PM. After dosing, I sat back and waited for the effects. After about 15 minutes, I got up to walk around. I found my legs wobbly and my vision somewhat “choppy.” I found the music enjoyable, but it was one of my favorite bands so I’m sure a sober appreciation would have been just as good. After about another 15 minutes of this (it is now 8:30 PM), I found the effects quite underwhelming, and decided that perhaps an extra 1.5 mL would give me a better idea of the effects for the substance. I put another 1.5 mL into a shot glass, and downed the toxic tasting liquid. Over the next 30 minutes, I felt all of the afore mentioned effects escalating. I had very little coordination and found myself running into furniture and the sides of walls as I walked through the house. It was very easy to close my eyes, and I’m sure I could’ve fallen asleep if I had tried. When I would shut my eyes, there would be a tunneling effect, where the images I had just seen would disappear from my vision over the course of about 1 or 2 seconds, by the black outer edges of the image pushing inwards till the last thing I saw was whatever it was I was focusing on. My whole head felt very loose and rubbery, and I enjoyed rocking m head from side to side. My stomach felt upset, and there was a kind of chemical toxicity feel to my whole body. I decided that some hashish might help the nausea, so I broke off a chunk and smoked a bowl. Unfortunately, the nausea persisted. An hour and a half had passed from initial dosing when I heard a few knocks on the door. I got up, excited, nervous, regretful of my inebriation, knowing that my friends from New York had arrived. I stumbled to the door and we exchanged hugs and words and I invited them in. I informed them that I had taken GHB, not wanting to explain to them that GBL is broken down into GHB in the body. I was looking forward to engaging in the social aspect of the drug but found that my speech and thoughts were incredibly slow and drawn out. I felt good, but there was no distinct sense of euphoria, and I think my elevated mood was largely due to my friends’ presence, especially because they are, under sober circumstances, incredibly funny. I did notice, however, a type of slowness to my feelings of touch. If I were to touch my face, I could still feel the pressure of my hand once I had taken my hand away, similar to, but in no way as immensely pleasurable, the body high of MDMA. We went walking down St. Laurent to go to a small sleazy bar where they could get a few drinks after such a long trip. I would refrain from drinking, knowing that GBL and alcohol shouldn’t be mixed. The whole walk there, I kept wobbling, running into both my friends, nearly running into people on the street. My body felt much drunker than my mind. I knew how ridiculous I looked, but could not help stumbling along. I desperately needed some water, and felt that that might help alleviate some of my intense nausea. We got to the bar. It was probably 10:15 or 10:30, around 2 – 2.5 hours after my first dose. I felt very sick by this point. My friends said they’d get me some water, and that I should just take a seat. I found a booth and lied down. I immediately began to fall asleep. I woke up a bit later with my friends by me and some water near me. One of my friends had been trying to wake me, shaking me, and I hadn’t noticed. We left the bar, seeing how I was clearly too exhausted to stay much longer. I kept drinking water the whole way back, and felt a great deal of my nausea recede. Back home, we ate some naan and hummus and watched Seinfeld. I immediately passed out. And awoke at 1 AM by my friends, trying to get me off the couch, since it was where they were going to sleep that night. I stumbled to bed and fell asleep. I would not take this chemical again. There was a very clear toxicity to the whole experience. The effects seemed comparable to alcohol, but not as good. While alcohol seems to facilitate conversation, GBL left me struggling to piece sentences together. The sleepiness, nausea and lack of motor skills induced by GBL were all unpleasant. Two thumbs down.

布托尔法诺 (斯塔多尔) 不是娱乐性的 我朋友的猫被杀了, 他们给他液体醇供应以口服. 我的朋友从来没有用过猫的东西, 因为他忘记了. 我碰巧有一天在他的房子里发现了它. 这样是什么样子? 它有最可怕的味道, 任何我曾经让触摸我的芽. 这味道像在混合器中液化腐烂的死动物. 这很粘. 类似于糖蜜. 吸入鼻腔, 烧伤时间大约2分钟, 但可以忍受, 试图追逐B-龙会产生更快的效果, 稍微粗的味道残留物, 对于我来说,我做了这两件事. 我认为使用纸方法更好. 我用纸来计算的数量要小得多. 影响? 缓解疼痛, 轻微但可见的性障碍. 可能有点镇静, 但它是抵消的失调. 对于我来说, 失眠症就像没有足够的睡眠醒来, 只是一个一般的"心情不好". 值得吗? 没有. 布托尔醇 + 其他药物: 托拉马多尔和布托尔醇一起增加了阿片的效果. 这种似乎比单独使用特拉马多尔更严重. 我不会因为巴托尔而发. 这两种药物一起会产生一种非常好的兴奋, 我认为甲醇可能会减轻一点点的刺激. 尽管我抽一支烟, 抽完之后通常会让我更紧张. 它只能持续2分钟. 这只是我今晚碰巧做出的组合. 总结: 除非与另一种类药物结合, 否则醇在所有娱乐用途中都是99%无价的, 一种类似于贝纳德利的增强剂. 希望这有所帮助. 时间表: 57979 发布时间:2018年7月26日 药品 - 丁醇 (307):组合 (3),回顾 / 摘要 (11),未知背景 (20)

Jul 26, 2018. erowid.org/exp/57979 DOSE: vaporized Pharms - Butorphanol (liquid) oral Pharms - Tramadol Inhalants - Nitrites Tobacco - Cigarettes BODY WEIGHT: 125 lb Butorphanol (Stadol) Is Not Recreational My friend's cat was neutered, and they gave him a supply of liquid butorphanol to be taken by mouth. My friend never used the stuff on the cat because he forgot. I happened to find it at his house one day. What's it like? It has the most god-awful taste of anything I've ever let touch my buds. It tastes like a rotted dead animal liquefied in a blender. It's pretty gooey. Similar to molasses. Taken intra-nasally, it burns for about 2 minutes, but is bearable, and not as bad as many other things taken intra-nasally. Trying to chase the B-Dragon results in a much quicker effect, slightly gross tasting residue, and for some reason, swollen glands, and soreness in the throat. For me, I did both a number of times. I find it much better to use the aluminum foil method. I think I had quite a few bad experiences because I had no way to figure out my dose, and was taking to much IN (intra-nasally). I take a much smaller and easier to calculate amount with aluminum foil. The effects? Pain-killing, and slight but noticeable dysphoria. Possibly a bit of sedation, but it is counteracted by the dysphoria. The dysphoria for me is like waking up without enough sleep, and being more easily annoyed by things. Just a general 'being in a bad mood.' Worth it? No. Butorphanol + other drugs: Tramadol and butorphanol together have increased opiate effects. The itching seemed slightly more profound than with Tramadol alone. I do not get itching from Butorphanol. Together they create a pretty damn good buzz (euphoria), but it's 95% the tramadol, I think. With the tramadol comes some stimulation, and I think the butorphanol may take a LITTLE bit of the edge off. I don't smoke anymore, but I had one cigarette and that seemed to help alot for some reason, though when I smoke a cigarette by itself, it usually makes me more nervous after I'm done with it. The Isobutyl Nitrite really does nothing to add to the effects, as it only lasts about 2 minutes. This is just the combination I happened to do tonight. SUMMARY: Butorphanol is 99% worthless for all recreational purposes, unless combined with another opiate, in which case it becomes slightly less worthless (maybe 90%), a potentiator along the lines of benadryl.

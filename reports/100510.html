首先讲一下我,每天吸大麻近20年, 拥有大量的经验, 没有一个糟糕的旅行或"回忆"只是偶尔的失败之旅. 我吸了一点麻醉,只是为了娱乐. 我试过其他各种药物, 但我来告诉你我经历过的最奇怪和最可怕的经历, 我试过一些香料和k2在一个朋友, 但从来没有感觉到任何其他比他被骗了! 后来有一天,为了娱乐,我在当地烟店买了一些叫做"Slappa"的东西. 我回家打了个烟斗, 打得很厉害, 期待着浪费钱的失望, 五到十分钟之内, 我就感觉了, 持续了大约45分钟. 忽略了对自己说话的部分,然后开始工作. 我回家喝茶时告诉女朋友, 她也认为假草是废话. 所以我给她留下一个管子, 告诉她以后再来看看, 我从来没有收到短信,我担心,当我从工作回家,她说这是疯狂的东西! 她以为自己心脏病发作, 我们都觉得很有趣. 我没有准备好接下来发生的事情! 她睡觉了,我开始看"辛普森一家", 喝了一口,,在10到15秒内, 我起床,走进厨房,随机触摸东西, 我再次意识到我在大声地对自己说话! 我问自己今天是什么日子? 我不知道! 什么日期? 我不知道! 我在的球! 然后在5~6分钟后它消失了,我只是了! 这很奇怪! 这种东西最终控制了我的生活超过3个月的时间, 直到警察搜查所有卖家和供应商, 我对这类东西有很多经验, 虽然它是我吃过的最可怕的毒品, 我们称之为恐惧, 因为一旦你碰到它, 在最初的5分钟里, 就像死亡一样, 我给我的两个最好的朋友发了一些, 他们同意, 经过我们使用的酸和其他药物, 总是让好故事这些天的朋友从来没有尝试它! 不能相信它是合法出售, 诚实地说, 不知道是什么CRA化合物是, 谢谢您的阅读! 其他: 发布时间:2023年10月25日 (26) 单独使用 (16)

Oct 25, 2023. erowid.org/exp/100510 DOSE: 1 bowl smoked Products - Spice-Like Smoking Blends BODY WEIGHT: 110 kg Start with a bit about me, daily pot smoker for near on 20 years with extensive experience with alot, a real lot of acid, being my favourite drug of choice! Never a bad trip or 'flashback' just the odd dud trip. I smoke meth a bit, just recreationally. Enjoy mdma, 2cb, cocaine and tried various other drugs, but I'm here to tell you about the strangest and probably scariest experience I ever experienced, with a simple fake weed. I had tried some spice and k2 at a mates but never felt anything other than he got ripped off! Then one day out of amusement I bought some of this stuff called slappa that was at my local cig shop. I got home and packed a pipe, hit it hard expecting disappointment on wasting money when I realised I was walking around my bar ( I have a bar under my house) talking out loud to myself! Within 5-10 minutes I just felt stoned which last roughly 45 minutes. Brushed off the talking to myself bit and went to work. I go home for my tea break and tell my girl about this slappa stuff, she too thought fake weed is bullshit. So I left her a pipe packed and said hit this later and text me what you think. Never get a text and I am worried, when I get home from work she says thats crazy stuff! She thought she was having a heart attack and was also wandering around the house talking to herself. We both found it kind of amusing. I am not prepared for what happened next! She goes to bed, I start watching the simpsons and pack a pipe of this slappa, one hit and oh fuck, within 10-15 seconds I am feeling totally foreign to my surroundings, but recognise it is my lounge, coffee table, etc.. I get up go into the kitchen and just start randomly touching stuff tey to feel normal and ignore this sensation! I realise again I'm talking to myself out loud! I ask myself what day is it? Fuck knows! The date? Dont know! I'm trippin balls! Then it wears off after 5-6 minutes and I'm just stoned! Wow weird! This stuff eventually controlled my life over a 3 month period until the cops raided all the sellers and suppliers, which I was glad for! There are so many more experiences I had on this slappa stuff but my report would go on too long. It was by far the scariest of drugs I have consumed, but very unique! We named it the dread because as soon as you hit it, you felt dread as to why would I do this again!! It was like death for those first 5 minutes, scary as hell and total fear! I sent some to my 2 best mates interstate and they agreed, after all the acid and other drugs we have done, this still stands out as the weirdest! Always makes for good stories these days to the friends that never got to try it! Cannot believe it was sold legally to be honest, dont know what the CRA compound was that was in it, but truely scary stuff! Thanks for reading!

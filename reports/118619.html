First time consuming 3-Me-PCPy. Potential tolerance: 3 Days prior I had taken a moderate amount of Ketamine. hh:mm t+ 00:00 5 mg insufflated. Very strong caustic burn. Smell reminiscent of MXiPr t+ 00:04 Pain in my nose starts feeling unhealthy. Rinse my nose with tap water. Pain subsides. t+ 00:09 slight physical euphoria as well as slight dissociation and slight motor control loss. t+ 00:21 stronger base dissociation, combined with a few short dissociative peaks of ~ 10 seconds. During those peaks a distinct confusion as well as heightened dissociation is present. Feels a bit like the substance 'kicks', then goes back to base dissociation level. Dissociation on the level of a low - common dose of ketamine. Warm physical euphoria. Short tingling sensation originating in my chest and spreading throughout the body t+ 00:30 my mouth feels numb, comparable to Lidocaine t+ 00:50 mouth not numb anymore t+ 01:04 all effects begin to subside t+ 02:04 back to baseline ------------------------------- second time consuming 3-Me-PCPy. Potential tolerance: 5mg 3-Me-PCPy insufflated 7 days prior. t+ 00:00 5mg insufflated. Terrible burn t+ 00:04 talkative and stimulated t+ 00:17 Burn is bad, wash my nose. Burn subsides. Slight dissociation t+ 00:23 insufflate another 5 mg t + 00:31 energised dissociation. Mentally rather clear, however proportionally strong impairment of motor control. Mouth feels numb. t + 01:09 at this point the experience feels like a common dose of Ketamine t+ 01:24 effects start to subside t + 02:30 back to baseline -------------------------------- Overall doses in the range of 5mg to 10mg insufflated produced a short lasting stimulating-dissociative experience. To me personally the effects felt like the upper bound of a low dose.

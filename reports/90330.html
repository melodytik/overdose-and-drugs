星期六早上, 我和朋友肖恩,杰森计划吃一些. 我们在下午1点左右醒来, 吃了一些食物在当地的五家伙, 我们走回我们的宿舍开始程序. 晚上3点 我和朋友们都吃了自己的. 我朋友肖恩和杰森每人吃了大约1.7克, 吃了大约半个小时的后, 我开始感到极度头, 我走了出去,立刻感觉好一点. 身体非常强壮, 像我抽了一堆香烟一样. 我在校园的路上吸了一根大麻, 但在路上, 事情开始发生变化, 形象开始形成. 我住的城市看起来像一个庞大的,的,拥挤的人群,奇怪的车辆和令人敬畏的建筑. 我每天都会穿过这座城市去上课, 但在这一刻穿过这座城市, 建筑物是美丽的,天空是惊人的. 而汽车看起来就像来自太空的未来车辆. 我在这个新世界里迷失了几次, 4:30 我和朋友们到达目的地, 走上一座巨大的山, 走向公园的顶部. 整个时间, 我们都在听我手机上的音乐, 这使得情况变得非常愉快. 我们到达了山顶, 从那里我们可以看到整个城市, 我看着我的朋友们, 他们几乎都说着另一种语言. 我们不得不不断地告诉自己我们在哪里,是什么日子,是谁, 这座城市不再是城市了. 我们处在一个美丽,广的环境中, 一切都是新的. 我们坐在这座山上很长一段时间, 我感到欣喜, 看到一个没有吸食的人, 然而,情况并非如此. 经过一个小时的公园活动, 我不得不跟随我的朋友整个旅程, 因为我不知道什么是什么了, 周围的建筑物, 我们决定进入一正在举办艺术展览的建筑. 这就是我的偏执开始的地方. 我不禁觉得有人在看着我们, 我不知道如何在普通社会中表现, 我不知道自己能做什么,不能做什么, 我和朋友们讨论了好几个小时, 我会随机发现我的朋友走到另一个地方或离开, 我感到恐惧, 轻微的偏执, 认为我的朋友在阴谋, 我觉得他们走在一起, 但我会独自一人, 我感到孤独. 我们开始步行回宿舍. 我走在后面, 看着周围的美丽环境. 我离开了他们, 走进校园里的一大楼, 这是一座大的建筑, 这就是我真正遇到地狱的地方. 进入大楼后, 我开始在大厅里漫步, 我确信有人试图让我进入这座大楼, 我记得我走进建筑物的浴室, 坐下来试图抓住东西. 我听到有人在浴室里说:"还有谁在里面?" 你的朋友在哪里? 我知道他在这里. 我听到一个扎, 听起来像有人被窒息. 我完全害怕坐在这个浴室摊位,并呼吁,但听到没有回报. 我打开门走出浴室, 我走下楼梯, 开始看到同样的东西, 一遍又一遍. 我看到了关于日本的标志, 在建筑物周围看到日本人. 我甚至发现自己是日本人, 我还记得有几次看到一个全身黑衣的男人拿着一本书对我笑. 他会以威胁的方式走向我, 但在最后一刻, 我对现实完全失去了视觉和视觉. 我记得我多次试图离开这座大楼, 我记得当时我看到有三个家庭, 我记得父母们在保护他们的孩子, 不让他们看我, 我一直在看自己, 我感到非常困惑. 开始是家庭,但后来我开始注意到, 我试图乘坐公共汽车回到宿舍, 我记得我乘坐一辆非常拥挤的巴士, 在我的右边是一个男性与24包 酒, 这种东西在校车上不常见. 他身后有两个女孩谈论自己有了荒谬的注射量,大概25次左右. 我觉得每个人都在着我, 我记得我看着旁边的女孩, 看到她的短信, 经过这一切, 等待公交车移动大约十分钟后, 我下了公交车, 回到了我以前的建筑. 我记得在进入大楼后, 我环顾四周, 看到完全不自然的东西. 工作和学习的人以极快的速度移动, 脚跳得非常快, 人们着我,看着我很奇怪, 他们有很多,令人难以置信的丑的人, 我开始听到建筑物中各种声音, 水的所, 管道的声, 声音都在说一些东西, 我完全确信我已经进入了另一个领域, 我已经进入了疯狂, 我记得我走在楼里, 现在完全没有它. 周围的每一个人都在低声指着我. 我完全认为我已经疯了, 我完全相信自己被困在这个建筑里, 我记得我坐在楼梯间, 感到很可怕, 他们会说:"你没事吗?" Mar 3, 2025. erowid.org/exp/90330 DOSE: T+ 0:00 3.5 g oral Mushrooms T+ 0:45 joints/cigs smoked Cannabis (flowers) BODY WEIGHT: 180 lb It was a Saturday morning, and my friends Sean, Jason and I planned on ingesting some mushrooms. We woke up around 1 that afternoon to get some food in our stomachs before we had any of the substance. After eating some food at a local Five Guys, we walked back up to our dorm to start the procedures. 3:00 PM – My friends and I each eat our share of mushrooms. My friends Sean and Jason ate about 1.7g each to themselves while I made a peanut butter sandwich containing an eighth of mushrooms. After about half an hour on mushrooms, I began to get extremely lightheaded sitting inside and told my friends I needed to get some fresh air while they rolled the joints. I went outside and immediately felt a little better. However, the feeling I was experiencing was extreme light-headedness and a very strong body high as if I had smoked a bunch of cigarettes. 3:45 – My friends meet me outside and smoke a joint of marijuana on the way down campus to get to a local park; our goal for the day. The walk to the field took about forty-five minutes but on the way things began to change, and images began to form. The city I lived in took the appearance of a vast, swelling, multitude of people, strange looking vehicles, and awe-striking buildings. Now, I walk through this city every day to get to class, but at this point walking through the city, I felt as if I were walking through it for the first time ever. The buildings were beautiful and the sky was amazing. Tree branches and such swirled into extravagant works of the earth’s art, and cars looked like futuristic vehicles from space. I became lost several times in this new world that I had just been birthed into. 4:30 – My friends and I reached our destination, walking up a gigantic hill toward the top of the park. The entire time we are listening to music on my phone, which generated a very relaxed feeling. And made the situation very enjoyable. We reached the top of the hill where we could see the entire city in a sprawling, beautiful, mess of metropolitan skyscrapers that now looked like floating castles or paintings upon the portrait of the sky. I looked at my friends who were all speaking in almost another language. We kept having to tell ourselves where we were, what day it was and who we were because the environment was so rapidly changing. The city was not the city anymore. We were in a beautiful, sprawling environment that had never existed before today. Everything was new. We sat up on this hill for quite a long time blabbering about nothing but just enjoying this incredible feeling of freedom when my friend Eugene came up the hill. I felt ecstatic seeing someone that was not on shrooms and could successfully keep us grounded on Earth. However, this would not be the case. 5:45 – After about an hour of being up in the park, we decided to move about the city and look at other things. I had to follow my friends the entire journey for I had no idea what was what anymore, the buildings all around me that I had come to know so well in my first year of college had changed, but looked utterly marvelous. We decided to enter a building that was having an art exhibit. This is where my paranoia began. Going into this building with the art exhibit, I could not help the feeling that someone was watching us, or we were doing something wrong. I did not know how to act in regular society anymore, I wasn’t entirely sure what I could and could not do or how loud I was talking or anything. My friends and I discussed for hours what we wanted to do but we never knew what we wanted to do. I would randomly catch my friends walking around to another place or leaving and became severely lost more than once at the exhibit. It was around this time that I felt afraid, a slight paranoia that my friends were plotting against me, that they weren’t really my friends. I felt as if they were walking together but I would be alone, looking around, lost in transition. I felt alone. 6:30 – It was around this point that we began to walk back to our dorm. The three of them walked far ahead of me as I waded along in the back looking at the wondrous environment all around me. As we were walking back, I departed from the group and began walking into one of the buildings on my campus without looking nor even caring whether my friends saw. It’s a sprawling building of about 28 stories, and a myriad of hallways, classrooms, and staircases. This is where I would literally encounter hell. 6:45 – Upon entering the building, I began wandering its halls and attempting to grasp reality but it was around this time that things began to change the most. I was convinced people were trying to get me in this building, and I was literally beginning a spiral into insanity. I remember walking into the bathroom at the building and sitting down to try and grasp things. It was at this point I heard someone else in the bathroom talking, and saying, “Who else is in here? Where are your friends? I know he’s here”. It was at this point I heard a struggle, and what sounded like someone being suffocated. I was utterly terrified sitting in this bathroom stall, and called out but heard no return. I saw signs upon signs about Japan, and I was seeing Japanese people everywhere around the building. I even had a revelation that I was Japanese and all my friends were somehow of Japanese descent. I also remember seeing a man several times dressed all in black with a book and snickering at me. I thought he was there to harm or something for at least twice, he would walk threateningly toward me but at the last minute walk by with a manic smile on his face. It was also around this time my visualizations and view of reality was completely lost. 7:30 to 10:00 (Blur) – I recall I attempted several times to leave this building but I couldn’t. Things started getting weird I remember when I saw three families of people with very small children. I remember the parents were shielding their children from looking at me, and I looked at myself all these times trying to find what was making me look so strange. I was so confused. It started with the families but then I began to notice there were many, many people looking, pointing, and whispering to each other about me. It was at this point I left the building and attempted to get on a bus back to my dorm but the bus was filled with people talking nonsense. I had planned to jump to the chandelier in the middle of the room but something held me back I remember. At one point, I even went on a running start to jump to but I stopped. I don't know why. A man came below me and began to talk to me while I was sitting on the ledge. I was utterly positive that whatever I thought, he would say, that I was controlling what he would speak. I was so sure all the things I saw and heard that day could never happen in real life. I was so disturbed from everything that day that I completely believed it had all been a dream. It hadn’t been a dream though. Now, this all happened yesterday, so I’m still waiting to hear of the consequences but no doubt, it was the most extreme, haunting, and terrifying experience of my entire life.

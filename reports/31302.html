我收到的粉末是纯白色的 (不是白色,棕色或那种黑色的东西). 我之前曾服用过Ethacetin HCl (剂量大约10毫克,鼻腔吸入) 并且没有发生太多事情. 眼睛也开始流. 在视觉上或精神上没有发生太多. 我当时正从旅行中回来, 这可能与此有关. 我的10毫克乙酸液体经验是舒尔金尺度上的+1. 我只是躺在床上,几个小时后开始看到从移动的物体和一些图案形成色只是在空气中. 这就是它. 现在,关于我们的报告... 在2004年初 (一月), 我用一杯水口服了30毫克的乙酸. 味道非常苦. 这将是我第二次使用这种特殊物质的"旅行". 需要大约30分钟才能起作用. 我注意到胃部有点不舒服, 但并没有发展成恶心或其他情况. 我开始注意到意识的变化, 约一个小时后, 我身体的不同部位在不同的时间都有轻微但可观察到的震 (相当类似于我在DPT HCl中发生的事情) - - ,等等. 我开始在天花板和地板上看到令人惊叹的图像. 图案,碎形等在移动. 在天花板上形成了旋转的线圈. 这是一个令人愉快的视觉. 我有点内省, 让我担心我生活的当前情况, 我们正在观看的节目是关于某个高层政府机构追逐我只能得出结论是毒贩, 我一直认为我们已经看过我们正在看的场景. 我可能会觉得我们一遍又一遍地看着同一个节目的预告片, 我认真地认为我的朋友们在玩笑我,因为我在,他们试图'我.'我很生气,但他们坚持说他们没有玩笑我. 我平静下来,因为,好吧,我,也许这一切都在我脑海里哈哈. 我在旅行中经历了多次6+的 deja vu. 在副作用方面, 抽/震/震是非常不舒服的. 另外,我不能保持恒定的温度. 现在我很热,下一刻又很冷. 我在里面穿着克, 汗流了一点. 我从经历中得到的回报, 整个体验大约持续了6个小时. ...一个小时来完全和3个小时 (也许有点少) 在峰值. 我肯定会再来一次. 但是,我可能下次尝试25毫克. 其他: 2007年1月8日发布 4-AcO-DET (188):小组 (2-9) (17),一般 (1)

Deja Vu 4-Acetoxy-DET by Da Jah Citation:   Da Jah. "Deja Vu: An Experience with 4-Acetoxy-DET (exp31302)". Erowid.org . Jan 8, 2007. erowid.org/exp/31302 DOSE: 30 mg oral 4-AcO-DET (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 120 lb The powder I recieved was pure white (not off-white, brown or that oozy black shit). I took Ethacetin HCl once before (dose ~10 mg, nasally insufflated) and not a lot happened. It burned my nasal passages really badly and made my eye tear. Not a lot happened visually or mentally. At the time I was coming down off of a mushroom trip, so that might have had something to do with it. My 10 mg Ethacetin HCl experience was a +1 on the Shulgin scale. I could not sleep although I was fairly tired going into the 'trip.' I just lay in bed and after many hours started to see minor tracers from moving objects and some patterns forming in maroon color just in the air. That was it. Now, to the report at hand... Early in 2004 (Jan), I took 30 mg Ethacetin HCl orally in a cup of water. The taste was pretty bitter. This was to be my second 'trip' with this particular substance. It took about 30 minutes to take effect. I noticed a little bit of stomach discomfort for a short while, but it didn't develope into nausea or anything. I started noticing a change in conciousness as friends and I watched TV. About an hour later I was peaking. There were mild but observable tremors all throughout different parts of my body at different times (quite reminiscent of what happens to me on DPT HCl) - shaking, etc. I began to see amazing imagery on the ceiling and floor. Patterns, fractals, etc moving about. Swirly circles of lines formed all across the ceiling. It was visually pleasing. I had a bit of introspection that had me fretting the current circumstances of my life, but I found this introspection not particularly meaningful (although it could maybe have been directed into something meaningful). The show we were watching was about some high up government agency chacing what I could only conclude were drug dealers, although I wasn't quite clear. I kept thinking we had already seen the scene we were watching. I could have swarn we were watching the same preview for the show over and over again, but when I would think back, other stuff had happened, and all seemed right. I seriously thought my friends were playing a joke on me because I was tripping, and they were trying to 'trip me out.' I got pretty irritated, but they insisted they weren't playing any joke on me. I calmed down becuase, well I was tripping, and maybe it was all in my head haha. I had multiple (6+) deja vu's during the trip. As far as side effects, the twitching/shaking/tremors were quite uncomfortable. Also, I couldn't stay at a constant temperature. One minute I'd be hot; the next minute I'd be cold. I was wearing a jacket inside, and I was sweating a bit. I came away from the experience rather stinky. The entire experience lasted about 6 hours. ...An hour to come fully up and 3 hours (maybe a tad less) at peak. It was quite enjoyable (probably even more in nature in the summer), I'll certainly be doing it again. However, I may try 25 mg next time.

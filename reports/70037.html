我一直在寻找一种有效的摄入方法, 尽管今天身体有所负荷, 遗憾的是,目前还没有任何视觉效果. 第一次尝试: 采用冷水提取方法提取大约700个晨光 (飞和珍珠门混合),种子被磨成细粉末,并放置在金属茶具上,冷水. 诚实地说,这是我第一次, 我认为提取是最不成功. 我从来没有摇或挤压材料, 这是一个完全的新手错误, 很好,但非常温和,持续了很长时间,大约8小时左右. 第二次尝试: 浸泡500个早晨光 (这一次是天上的) 在油中30分钟,用咖啡过器过油,等待近3天,直到它蒸发. 然后将材料放入瓶子, 倒入大约4司. 在黑暗中留下,直到下午, 时不时地摇. 然后添加了70毫克的葡萄酸,,离开了2天,每次我在附近. 我觉得我喝了太多的酒, 我觉得我喝得太多了, 我用咖啡过器过, 把整个东西和水混合起来, 这是一种令人厌恶的味道,你不知道,类的味道,汁,种子. 我以为我会吐, 但显然没有. 经过20分钟左右的时间, 实际上是非常严重的. 持续了一个多小时左右. 我希望我已经吐,但它从来没有. 我感觉很糟糕,大约8个小时,然后睡着了,第二天我有了我一生中最糟糕的腹. 我再也没有石油了. 我必须说,血管收缩这一次不那么糟糕. 或许胃痛更严重, 我没有注意到. 无论如何,非常... 我的意思是非常沉重的身体负荷. 没有油,没有酸与金酒混合. 疯狂的东西. 大的没有没有. 第三次尝试: 在水中放入500个Heavenly, 留下24小时. 接下来的一天我开始去除. 只有400种种子膨胀到足以去除外, 这花了大约3个小时. 我把剩余的材料尽可能多, 直接放入2司的金酒中. 添加,让它浸泡3天. 到了周末, 我加入了大约3司的东西. 酒,混合整个东西,过通过一个旧的衫和喝了该死的东西. 现在,你不知道它的味道是什么... 我开始感觉到一个立即的声,真正的硬和令人毛骨然,然后恶心来了... 我感觉到一种你无法想象的解脱, 我开始感到很紧张, 我感到很头, 但另一方面, 这就像是五次一次, 我认为我已经吐出了所有的酒精 (至少), 这是一种类似于LSD的体验, 非常令人困惑和快速. 很快就来了 但它只持续了大约30分钟, 然后实际上回到基线, 我继续我的路,画了一件很漂亮的艺术品, 我注意到我仍在酒的影响下, 因为我能融化在化的湿漆中. 坏东西不仅存在于外中, 也存在于内心的种子中. 再说一次:少喝酒! 第四次尝试 (今天): 发现了大约98%的酒精 (酒). 我住的地方这违法, 很惊, 但看来我父亲储存了一些瓶子, 磨碎500个晨光,把它们放在2个茶袋里,浸泡在酒精中,直到覆盖. 然后加上半个. 在接下来的2天内和挤压,然后取出茶袋. 我让酒精蒸发,直到只有一小部分酒精仍然存在... 所有的黄色和棕色 (顺便说一下) 还在那里. 我加入了1司的雪利酒,然后在黑暗中待了3天. 晚上7点, 吃了大约25个里维亚. 我把它们浸泡在水和汁 (2茶匙), 等待一个小时, 然后喝下来, 着种子. 感觉到非常非常轻微的效应持续了2到3小时, 我为什么等了这么久? 没有想法). 我讨厌雪利酒的味道, 所以我用可口可乐混合, 我等待看看效果,大约5分钟后它就开始了. 我开始感到不舒服. 我感觉到突然的冲动来了20分钟后的摄入. 血管收缩很严重, 但另一方面, 我已经在过去的四个周末这样做了, 尽管我开始使用LSD, 我必须承认,起初有些吓人. 我决定抛弃另一半的玻璃杯, 我认为效应会持续更长时间, 我希望我可以跨越门. 另一方面, 我对LSD不太敏感, 很有趣的是,我对草化合物不敏感, 如果我吸了太多麦芽, 摄入后一个小时左右, 感知变化开始发生, 但又停留了两个小时, 味道苦,但比我尝试过的任何东西都好. 我没有把它们浸泡或什么,只是它们. 很难说要使用多少,不是吗? 它们的功效真的很不同. 但考虑到我下午吃了25个, 没有发生太多事情, 我吃了这些药后20到30分钟就开始了. 这让我想起了我在使用MDMA时所获得的感知变化, 颜色变得更加鲜, 例如,如果我看出窗外, 非常高,但同时也非常有意识. 很有精神的行为. 这是一种隐藏的治疗方式,我可以告诉,但不能解释. 声音也变得非常高. 我看了一下我和妻子为我们的房子所画的一些画, 我似乎注意到视角 (视角) 就像你在3D程序上一样. 这真是太棒了. 我觉得我确实获得了完全的LSD剂量或类似的东西. 我从来没有得到视觉只有一个剂量LSD但我确实得到更多的狂喜感觉比这. 尽管这种感觉让我觉得我一直是这样, 而不是我通常是这样. 奇怪但真实. 我很高兴我终于获得了效果. 经过许多尝试和错误之后. 另一方面,你只能通过尝试来学习. 时间表: 土耳其 (116),晨光 (38):不适用 (38),制备/食谱 (30),组合 (3)

Healing in Hidden Ways Morning Glory, Alcohol & Turbina corymbosa (Ololiuqui/Rivea) by Swatchfinal Citation:   Swatchfinal. "Healing in Hidden Ways: An Experience with Morning Glory, Alcohol & Turbina corymbosa (Ololiuqui/Rivea) (exp70037)". Erowid.org . Dec 28, 2009. erowid.org/exp/70037 DOSE: repeated oral Morning Glory (extract) repeated oral Alcohol - Hard (liquid) repeated oral Turbina corymbosa (seeds) BODY WEIGHT: 55 kg For the last 4 weeks (weekends) I have been trying to find an ingestion method that effectively produces the effect without the body load. Even though today there’s some body load, I seem to have been able to achieve a full LSD like experience with morning glories. No visuals yet I regret to say. First Attempt: Extracted some 700 morning glories (flying saucers and pearly gates mixed) using the cold water extraction method, the seeds where ground to a fine powder and placed on a metallic tea holder on freezing water. To be honest it was my first time and I think the extraction was most unsuccessful. I never shaked or squeezed the material which was a complete rookie mistake, didn’t have any body load except mild vasoconstriction, but then again I just achieved a state of sedation, with mild dreamlike closed eye visuals while listening to music. Nice but very mild, lasted long, like 8 hours or so. Second Attempt: Soaked 500 morning glories (heavenly’s this time) in naphta for 30 minutes, filtered the naphta with a coffee filter and waited almost 3 days until it evaporated. Then placed the material in a bottle and poured roughly 4 oz. Gin (Sapphire Bombay) and left in the dark for until next afternoon, shook every now and then. Then added about 70 milligrams of tartaric acid, shook, left for a 2 more days and shook every time I was near it. It was a lot of gin, I think I overshot because when I took it, it felt like forever to chug it all down… and I got an alcohol buzz at first, bad choice, less alcohol is better I think. When Saturday came, I filtered with a coffee filter, blended the entire stuff with lemon soda and drank as fast as I could. What a disgusting taste it was, you have no idea, taste of alkaloids, OJ, seed matter. Thought I would vomit but clearly didn’t. 20 minutes passed or so and I started feeling cramps. Real bad cramps in fact. Lasted for over an hour or so. Wished I had puked, but it never came. I felt terrible for something like 8 hours, then fell asleep, next day I had the worst diarrhea I’ve ever had in my life. No naphta for me ever again. Body load was awful I must say, vasoconstriction not so bad this time. Or maybe the stomach ache was far worse and I never really noticed. Either way, very… and I mean VERY heavy body load. Lesson learned: No naphta for me, no mixing tartaric acid with gin and whatever not. Crazy stuff. Big No No. Third Attempt: Placed 500 Heavenly’s without grinding in water and left them for 24 hours. Next day I proceeded to remove the husks. Only 400 seeds bloated enough to remove the outer shell, so 400 inner seeds made it. This took about 3 hours. Mashed as much as I could of the remaining material and placed it directly in 2 oz. gin. Added lemon and left it to soak for 3 days. When the weekend came, I mixed with something like 3 oz. Sherry, blended the whole thing, filtered thru an old tshirt and drank the damn thing. Now this, you have no idea what it tasted like… I started feeling an immediate buzz, real hard and creepy, then the nausea came… like it had never come before… 25 minutes since I drank and I ran to vomit in a very intense and violent way. I felt a relief like you would never imagine, in fact I laughed uncontrollably with relief. I started feeling very jumpy and felt the effects coming real hard. I felt very dizzy, but then again, that was like a 5 shot in one, so the alcohol buzzed immediately. 10 minutes later the alcohol buzz settled, think I puked it all out (at least), then came down and I started feeling the effects real hard. A very LSD like experience, very disorienting and fast. Came real fast. But it only lasted for about 30 more minutes then practically came back to baseline, which was odd but then again I vomited everything. I went on my way and painted a nice piece of art, very trippy. I noticed I was still under the influence because I could melt with the melting wet paint. Lesson learned: the bad stuff is not only in the husks… it’s also in the inner seed. Again: Less alcohol! Fourth Attempt (Today): Found some 98% proof alcohol (rubbing alcohol). A great surprise since this is illegal where I live, but it seems my dad had saved some bottles, so I asked him for a bottle and he gave it to me. Ground 500 morning glories, placed them in 2 teabags and soaked with alcohol until it covered. Then added half a lemon. Shook and squeezed during the following 2 days, then removed the teabags. I left the alcohol to evaporate until only a small portion of alcohol was still there… with all the yellow and brown (separated stuff by the way) was still there. I added 1 oz. of sherry and left in the dark for 3 more days. At 7 pm took something like 25 Riveas. I soaked them in water and lemon juice (2 tbsp), waited an hour, then drank and chewed the seeds. Felt very very mild effects that lasted for 2 to 3 hours, then apparently back to baseline. At 11 pm I proceeded to take the liquid I had prepared (Why did I wait so long? No Idea). I hate the taste of sherry so I mixed with CocaCola to disguise the taste and chugged down half a glass. Waited to see the effects and about 5 minutes later it started. Restless, my body was shaking and I started to feel a bit uncomfortable. I felt the sudden rush come 20 minutes after ingestion. Vasoconstriction was heavy but then again, I’ve been doing this the last 4 weekends so obviously accumulates a bit. But even though the constrictive effects I started to enter LSD like experience. It was a bit frightening at first I must say. So I decided to discard the other half glass, wish I didn’t though. I thought the effects would last longer and possibly climb, but didn’t really. I wish I could have crossed threshold. Then again, I’m not very sensitive to LSD, so I guess I’m not particularly sensitive to LSA and ergot derivatives at all. Funny that I’m not sensitive to ergot compounds and then again I’m extremely sensitive to THC. I go literally crazy if I smoke too much MJ. An hour or so after ingestion, it settled and the perceptive change started to really take place, but stuck there for another 2 hours and decided I should try something else, so I chewed about 30 or 35 riveas. Bitter taste but much better than any other thing I tried. Didn’t soak them or nothing, just chewed them. It’s so hard to tell how many to use doesn’t it? Potency really varies. But taking in account I ate 25 in the afternoon and not much happened, I guess I’m compensating for the morning glories extract I threw out. It started about 20 to 30 minutes after I ingested them. Reminds me a bit of the perceptual change I achieve with MDMA, though no ecstatic feelings. Colors are incredibly brighter, like for example if I look outside the window I can see the way the city lights paint a reddish over the sky, something I would normally never in a lifetime notice. Very high, but at the same time extremely conscious. Very spiritual in deed. It is healing in hidden ways, I can tell but can’t explain. Sounds are incredibly heightened as well. I looked at some paintings me and my wife have done for our house, they are incredible, I totally perceived them in a unique manner. I seem to notice perspective (viewpoint) very much like you do on a 3D program. Amazing. I think I definitely achieved a full LSD dose or something resembling it. I never get visuals with just one dose of LSD but I do get more ecstatic feelings than this. Even though the feeling I get makes me think I’ve always been this way and never how I normally are. Strange but true. I’m happy I finally achieved the effects. After many tries filled with errors. Then again, you can only learn trying.

常见的尿液测试 (免疫检测) 是一个非常简单的测试, 可以被击败, 但这不是合法的. 只有饮用水和维生素补充剂才是合法的. 这份报告是关于我具体的运动饮食如何导致我摄入THC的负面结果. 医生建议每天饮用8-12杯水, 这相当于8-12份8液司的水. 作为一名骑自行车的人, 我每天喝的水量几乎是这个数量的三倍. 这将使许多人的 (和我的) 尿液在免疫测试中过于稀释, 我们必须补充免疫检测所寻找的化学物质以证明样品是人体尿液. 根据饮食, 肌酸可能是缺少的成分. 如果您想通过炼来建立健康的肌肉, 肌可以在肌肉建设粉末或市场上的一些新"能量饮料"中找到,例如"SOBE No Fear" (No Fear Inc.和"南海饮料公司 Inc".的注册商标). 我每天两次服用25毫克克reatine. 为了确保克reatine的适当水平, 这相当于每天两次使用1份指定能量饮料或配合粉状肌酸补充剂,这通常是每天两次使用1份,大约为1. 每 8 司液体杯水中 1 . 现在一些免疫测试站点检查您的尿液颜色, 但很多人会因为尿液清而失败. 补充维生素B组合是最好的补充剂, 但再一次, 指定"能量饮料" (出于"某种原因") 含有适当量的B6和B12来染色尿液. 我已经在18次连续的免疫检测中得出了负结果, 我不认为这样做会帮助我"胜过"测试, 我不知道为什么很多能量饮料都是这样制成的, 无论如何,作为一名骑自行车的人, 我还听说稀释的尿液会让你不合格. 我很高兴地惊,结果是没有! 但是为什么? 这就是为什么我调查并找出事实. 这是一个与运动相结合的饮食. 我更快乐,更健康, 因为我这样做, 你的结果可能会 (而且可能会) 不同. 假冒免疫检测可能是与雇主签订的合同违反, 可能会导致纪律处分或解雇. 这种饮食不违法, 并且是一种健康的饮食, 附註: 我是一名在宾夕法尼亚州的素食主义者和骑自行车活动家. 我是NORML的会员, 我不支持在骑自行车或在工作时使用任何非法药物. 已使用的证券

Exercise, Health, and Urine Testing Cannabis, Vitamins/Supplements & Drug Testing by Barabbas Citation:   Barabbas. Dec 18, 2008. erowid.org/exp/45377 DOSE: repeated Cannabis - Hash Vitamins / Supplements (daily) BODY WEIGHT: 178 lb The common urine test (immunoassay) is a very simple test and can be beaten, but this is NOT legal. What IS legal is the ingestion of water and vitamin supplements. This report is about how my specific exercising diet has turned up negative results for my ingestion of THC. Doctors reccomend drinking between 8-12 glasses of water a day, that is 8-12 servings of 8 fluid ounces of water. As a cyclist, (25 miles / 40.2 km a day) I drink almost three times this amount (2 gallons a day which is 32 servings of 8 fluid ounces). Alone this will make many people's (and my) urine too dilute for the immunoassay and you will fail for adulteration due to suspected dilution. To prevent a failure for adulteration we must replenish the chemicals that the immunoassay searches for to prove that the sample is human urine. Depending on diet Creatine may be an ingredient lacking. Creatine aids in muscle toning and building of lean muscle which is a good thing to take if you're trying to build healthy muscles from exercise. Creatine can be found in muscle building powders or in some of the new 'power drinks' on the market such as 'SOBE No Fear' (regitered trademark of 'No Fear Inc.' and 'South Beach Beverage Company Inc.'). To ensure the proper levels of Creatinine, the chemical that the immunoassay searches for to prove the sample is human urine and is what is excreted when creatine is metabolized, I take 25mg of Creatine twice daily. This equates to 1 serving of the specified energy drink twice daily or with a powdered creatine supplement, this is usually 1 serving twice daily which is approx. 1 'scoop' per 8 fluid ounce glass of water. Now some immunoassay testing sites check the color of your urine to back up the test. This is rediculous as color is subjective, but a lot of people will fail because their urine is totally clear like tap water. To add color to urine a B-Complex vitamin supplement is the best, but again, the specified 'energy drink' (for 'some reason') contains the proper amounts of B6 and B12 to color urine. With this diet of water, creatine, and B vitamins I have tested negative on 18 consecutive immunoassay tests where THC metabolites were being tested for. I do not think that doing this will help me 'beat' the test but rather that it ensures I do not 'fail for adulteration' by having dilute urine. I'm not sure WHY many energy drinks are formulated in this way except that they 'pump you up' and give you energy. But either way, as a cyclist, I was drinking this much water ANYWAY, and using creatine and vitamins ANYWAY. When I went for my first random screening I was sweating because I had smoked the night before and I had also heard that dilute urine can make you fail the test. I was happily surprised that I came up negative! But why? This is why I investigated and found out the facts. This is a diet that I have followed in conjunction with exercise. I am happier and healthier because I do it, and I can enjoy my herb of choice without fear of being fired for an invasion of privacy. DISCLAIMER: I am only relating PERSONAL experience. Your results may (and probably WILL) vary. The adulteration of the immunoassay is probably a breech of contract with your employer and may result in disciplinary action or termination. However, this diet is not illegal in any way, shape, or form...and is a healthy diet to promote stamina for exercise. P.S. I am a Vegan and Cycling activist in PA. I am a member of NORML and I do not endorse the use of ANY illegal drug while cycling OR while in the workplace.

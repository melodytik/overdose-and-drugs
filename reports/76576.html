让我先说我是一个正在康复的君子. 具有刺意味的是, 我在康复中被介绍这种药物作为麻醉止痛药的替代品. 我知道它是第五条目中的物质, 但我想, 洛莫蒂尔也是如此 (而且它是由一个治疗成的医生处方的), 我已经停用了一个半星期的suboxone (仍然有轻微的戒断症状), 孩子,我说的对了! 医生在下午给我开始服用275毫克的药. 我当时没有服用其他药物. 服用后大约一个半小时后, 我注意到自己变得非常聊和快乐. 这种感觉与神经激素类似, 但更好,更强. 我在下午3点服用,第二天早上感觉到了持续的效果! 我不敢相信这让我有多好的感觉. 我已经尝试过很多种物质. 我认为'这就是门票'. 我继续按照处方服用药物, 直到我完成了康复计划 (好像我有选择) 注意到我服用的时间越长, 上述效应就越减弱. 但这很快就会改变. 我开始尝试更高的剂量 (150-900毫克之间), 当然,我写的剧本比我应该写的多, 我当时并没有多考虑, 直到第二天我开始注意到我感到极度生病. ,没有! 我对自己说: 我只是用一种药物换取另一种药物! 提款是非常不舒服! 几乎和阿片类药物或类药物一样糟糕. 我无法睡觉, 整身都疼痛, 并且非常丧. 我决定去找另一位医生, 让他给我再写一本歌词. 这次我买了300毫克的囊. 六个月的价值. 我用了两个月的时间! 经过我用完这些, 我用了神经激素来排毒. 这似乎有所帮助. 我只是想让每个人都知道, 至少对我来说,这种药物是很容易成的, 我预计这种药物在未来将会受到更严格的控制, 这一切使我重新走上阿片的道路, 谨慎使用这个. 我觉得这很有趣,但提款是疯狂的. 在你的冒险中祝你好运! 报告的剂量:75~2200毫克 其他: 药物 - 普雷加巴林 (418): 和习惯性 (10), 医院 (36)

Feb 3, 2009. erowid.org/exp/76576 DOSE: oral Pharms - Pregabalin (daily) BODY WEIGHT: 200 lb Let me start off by saying I am a recovering opiate addict. Ironically I was introduced to this medicine in rehab as a replacement for narcotic painkillers. I knew it was a schedule 5 substance, but I thought to myself so is lomotil (and it was being perscribed by a doctor who treats addicts) so I figured what the hell?! At the time I was experiencing a fair amount of back pain and I was a week and a half off of suboxone (still experiencing mild withdrawls) so it sounded like something that could help with both. BOY WAS I RIGHT!! The doctor started me at a dose of 2 75mg pills in the afternoon. I was taking no other medications at the time. About an hour and a half after dosing I noticed that I was feeling very talkative and happy. It felt similar to neurontin, but better and stronger. I took at 3 pm and felt lingering effects the next morning! I couldn't believe how good it made me feel. It really isn't like anything else I've tried, and I've used a lot of substances. 'This is the ticket' I thought. I continued to take the medicine as perscribed throughout the remaining month until I completed the rehab program (as if I had a choice) noticing that the aforementioned effects diminished the longer I took it. But that was soon to change. As soon as I got out of rehab I began experimenting with higher doses (in between 150-900mg) feeling those wonderful effects as I did before. Of course taking more of my script than I was supposed to caused me to run out early. I really didn't think that much about it at the time until the next day when I started noticing I was feeling extremely sick. Oh shit! I thought to myself. I just traded one drug for another! The withdrawls are very uncomfortable! Almost as bad as opiate or benzo withdrawl. Upon withdrawing from lyrica my skin and eyes dry out, I can't sleep, I ache all over and I get very depressed. After feeling that way for a little over a week, I decided to go to another doc and have him perscribe me more lyrica. This time I got the 300mg capsules. 6 months worth. I went through that amount in 2 months! After I ran out of those I used neurontin to detox. It seemed to help quite a bit. I just thought I'd let everyone out there know this drug at least for me was very habit forming, and the withdrawls are extremely uncomfortable. I foresee this drug becoming more tightly controlled in the future due to how good it feels when I'm on it, and how shitty I feel when I'm not. All of this caused me to start back on the path of the opiate again and landed me back in rehab exactly a year later. Be careful with this one. I found it to be crazy fun, but the withdrawls are just crazy.

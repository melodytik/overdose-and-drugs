我在2月3日晚上, 想尝试一下我记得的东西, 在"马哈尼尔瓦纳"中,神希瓦说,当男性和女性的"本质"结合在一起时, "它们的混合物就像是子和合产生的药液". 为了把这放在历史背景中, 当一个男人和一个女人有良好的性生活时, 你会在情感上和体液方面出现很多滑落和滑落. 在传统的安排婚姻中, 显然你必须期待一定程度的干燥透和浅浅的性爱. 这句话究竟意味着什么? 摩拉对子有什么作用,人们是否喜欢呢? 我认为这一定会造成一些令人惊叹的破坏, 我买了一些全果果, 为了找出原因, 我通常在房子里了树脂, 在我实验的那天晚上, 我可能喝了两杯酒和两杯威士忌. 这似乎是一个好主意:一个的决定. 结果完全让我大吃一惊, 我用马尔科姆·X的配方, 一子火柴盒, 入一杯冷水中, 现在回想起来, 我还了大约半茶匙的. 两小时等待没有效果, 接下来的第二天早上大约9点钟, 我醒来时, 峰值在四小时左右, 漫长而持久的下降在++到晚上. 旅行中的一些关键点: 醒来后的第一印象, 仿佛被一个凶猛的, 侵略性的神所占据:深沉的, 怪异的声音, 穿着仪式装甲的眼睛凸起的白皮肤男子的OEV和CEV (Chintamani); 也是马哈卡拉的三眼面孔放大和缩小 从"现实世界"中脱离,转回头脑; "现实世界"被视为一个遥远而扭曲的窗口. 我可以看到它,但不能达到它影响它. 这一窗口也产生了精神上的火球或超新星. 这颗火球的身份是上面提到的凶猛的神. 诵读咒语的声音非常大, 声音有巨大的力量和回响. 我的声音似乎穿透墙壁和天空的回声 在熟悉的咒语中打开新的门/大道, 揭示新的知识, 隐藏意义的新视角 与日常现实脱离的同时, 一种矛盾的感觉, 成为noumena (精神事件) 的原因,而不是仅仅对它们做出反应 通过参与日常生活中我通常无法忍受承认的部分而实现的共享无意识的统治 (现在重读这篇文章, 我认为当时我有一个全身心思的过程, 这次旅行使我无用, 除了自我考察和近十个小时的声乐工作. 我做了所有我知道的/咒, 醒来后,我意识到, '不,不,不,我在,,' 我立即去做一些祈祷工作, 由于我多年来几乎每天都在做这件事, 我也听到了由吉姆禅修合唱团演唱的"大卡拉"和"桑格瓦杜巴"曲. 我在网上看着拉里·卡尔森的录像. 我现在已经 非常的 对于米拉/子制品的尊重. 它表现出比我预期的要多的力量和活力. 这次暴发的持续时间和严重程度超出了我的预期. 想想所有宿醉的原因, 持续了两天, 果仁实验也扰乱了我的日常生活, 我有某种价值来比较它. 我不知道是否有任何宗派或邪教组织使用子和作为仪式饮料和香. 其他: 子 (41), (591):第一次 (2), 组合 (3), 宿醉 / 几天后 (46), 多日经验 (13), 独自一人 (16)

Jan 3, 2013. erowid.org/exp/95112 DOSE: 2 shots oral Alcohol - Hard (liquid) 2 glasses oral Alcohol - Beer/Wine (liquid) 1.5 Tbsp oral Nutmeg (fresh) 0.5 Tbsp oral Myrrh (tar / resin) BODY WEIGHT: 160 lb On the night of February 3, I had a wild urge to try something I remembered from the tantras I'd read for an on-line course. In the Mahanirvana Tantra, Lord Shiva says that when the male and female 'essences' combine, 'their mixture is like the Elixir arising from the combination of nutmeg and myrrh.' To put this in historical context, when a man and a woman have good sex, you get alot of slipping and sliding both emotionally and in terms of body fluids. In traditional arranged marriages, evidently you have to expect a certain amount of dry penetration and cursory boffing. Well, what does that quote mean, exactly? What effect does nutmeg have with myrrh, and does one enjoy it or not? I thought it must wreak some kind of astonishing havoc to make it into religious writ. I had purchased a few whole nutmegs in order to find out and I usually have sifted myrrh resin around the house anyway so I didn't need anything else. On the night of my experiment I'd had probably two beers and two shots of whiskey. It seemed like a good idea at the time: a hasty decision. The result completely blindsided me, exceeding all expectations. Using Malcolm X's recipe of 'a penny matchbox full of nutmeg stirred into a glass of cold water' I grated mine fresh (having read that the volatile constituents evaporate quickly) and consumed it around midnight. Thinking back now, it seems to me that I used about a tablespoon and a half of nutmeg. I also fumed about 1/2 teaspoon of myrrh. Having waited two hours without effect, I decided to go to bed and sleep. At about nine o'clock the following morning, I awoke into a +++ experience which rapidly progressed into a ++++ experience. Peak at about the four hour mark, with a lengthy and lingering comedown hovering around ++ into late evening. Certain key points from the journey: Initial impression after awakening as-if possessed by a fierce and aggressive deity: deep, monstrous voice boiling out of me unbidden etc. OEVs and CEVs of a bulging-eyed, white-skinned man in ceremonial armor (Chintamani); also the three-eyed face of Mahakala zooming in and out Separation from 'the real world' by turning back into the mind; 'the real world' perceived as a far-away and distorted window. I could see it, but not reach it to affect it. This window also emanated and originated a mental fireball or supernova This fireball had as its identity the fierce deity mentioned above. Reciting mantra at top volume, with tremendous power and reverberation in the voice. My voice seemed to penetrate the walls and echo off of the sky New doors/avenues opened up inside familiar mantra, new knowledge disclosed, new perspectives on hidden meanings Simultaneously with the remove from everyday reality, a contradictory sense of absolute presence in the here-and-now Becoming the cause of noumena (mental events) instead of merely reacting to them Dominion in the shared un/subconscious achieved by participating in the parts of everyday life I usually can't tolerate acknowledging (Re-reading this now, I don't seem to remember what it means. I think that at the time I had an all-absorbing mental process that kind of mapped out tying up loose ends conceptually) The trip rendered me useless except for introspection and vocal work for nearly ten hours. I did all the tantric/sanskrit mantra I know, repeatedly. Just after waking up and realizing, you know, 'Oh no no no, I'm tripping tripping tripping' I immediately went and did some prayer work and sang mantra which I'd written myself. Since I have done this almost daily for years, it grounded me and gave me maneuverability within the experience. At a certain point I also listened to the Mahakala and Sangwa Dupa Tantras sung by the Gyume Tantric Choir. When I felt too sedentary to sing any more, I sat and watched Larry Carlson clips on-line. I now have tremendous respect for the myrrh/nutmeg preparation. It displayed much more force and activity than I had expected. The comedown exceeded my expectations for duration and severity. Think of the mother of all hangovers, lasting for two days, combined with lingering blur, slur and impaired judgment. The nutmeg experiment also disrupted my daily routines for weeks afterward, which in turn threw off my actions and reactions in public. Now the line from the Mahanirvana Tantra makes sense: I have a certain value with which to compare it. I wonder if any sect or cult ever performs rites where they use nutmeg and myrrh as the ceremonial drink and incense.

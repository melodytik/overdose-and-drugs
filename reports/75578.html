美国 由于担心成本, 我手动包装自己的皮拉和阿尼拉囊. 我通常每天三次服用一种安尼拉和四次 Piracetam, 总共约1600毫克安尼拉和12克Piracetam. 皮拉剂量已经高于推剂量, 我忘记了正确标记它们 (我把它们放在旧的葡萄糖胺瓶中) 并不小心服用了两轮 piracetam 剂量的 aniracetam. 我估计在12小时内服用了7-9克的安尼拉. 这大约是每日推的3000毫克的最大剂量的三倍, 开始大约一个小时后, 我感到强烈的恶心和肠道不适, 在接下来的两个小时里, 正常的心理效应开始出现, 在第一剂后的T+4:00左右, 严重的头痛开始伴随着口干. 布洛芬 (2x1000mg) 没有效果,但乙氨基 (1x700mg) 可能有更大的效果. 在我意识到自己的错误之前, 大约30分钟后,口腔干燥显著增加,头痛的强度增加. 恶心是持续的,但可以忍受. 我的脑袋感觉不连接, 模糊, 类似于感冒药的'药物头'. 由于手, 写字和打字变得比平时更困难, 似乎在这种水平上,安尼拉可能会产生一些类似刺激剂的效果,而低剂量则没有这种效果. 症状在T+18:00开始减轻,但头痛持续了大约24小时. 我会更仔细地观察我的剂量. 我从来没有在24小时内服用过5克以上的安尼拉 (3剂隔8小时), 在这种高剂量下, 保持在每天3克的范围内会很好. 其他: 艾尼拉塞坦 (286):多种 (28),艰难的经历 (5)

Mar 8, 2009. erowid.org/exp/75578 DOSE: T+ 0:00 4.0 g oral Aniracetam (capsule) T+ 12:00 4.0 g oral Aniracetam (capsule) BODY WEIGHT: 190 lb Due to cost concerns, I hand pack my own piracetam and aniracetam capsules. I normally take one aniracetam three times a day and four piracetam three times a day, for a total of about 1600mg aniracetam and 12 grams piracetam. The piracetam is already a higher-than-recommended dose, due to perceived ineffectiveness. After packing a new set of capsules and taking them to work, I had forgotten to label them properly (I put them in old glucosamine bottles) and accidentally took aniracetam at piracetam dosages for two rounds. I estimate to have taken 7-9 grams of aniracetam in two dosages over twelve hours. This is about three times the maximum recommended daily dosage of 3000mg and 5 times what I usually take in a day. Starting about an hour after the first dose (~4g), I felt intense nausea and intestinal discomfort, attributed to food at first. Over the next two hours the regular mental effects kicked in, with some side effects of racing thoughts, inability to concentrate as highly as usual, and general cloudiness, normally racetams produce mental clarity, this effect was reduced and eventually reversed. At about T+4:00 after the first dose, a massive headache began to set in along with dry mouth. Ibuprofen (2x 1000mg) was ineffective, but acetaminophen (1x 700mg) may have had more of an effect. Another ~4g of aniracetam was taken at T+12:00 before I realized my error. Approximately 30 minutes later, dry mouth increased substantially and the headache grew in intensity. The nausea was persistent but bearable. My head felt disconnected and cloudy, similar to 'medicine head' from cold medications. Writing and typing became more difficult than usual due to shaky hands, and work in general was very hard to accomplish. It appears as if aniracetam at this level might take on some stimulant-like effects, which are absent at lower dosages. Symptoms began to subside at T+18:00, but the headache persisted for approximately 24 hours. In general, the experience was a bad one and I will more closely watch my dosages. I had never before taken more than 5g of aniracetam over a 24 hour period (3 dosages 8 hours apart), which is beyond where the positive effects plateau. All of the positive effects of aniracetam were cancelled out and then some at this high of a dosage. It is not recommended at all- staying within the 3g/day range will do just fine.

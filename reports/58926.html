通过我的许多搜索, 加利福尼亚浮现了. 似乎可以替代我的睡眠辅助药物, 我先用红色的花来做茶. 我用了大约2茶匙的水. 我把它放了30分钟, 这导致一个非常深的红色液体, 这种液体的味道并不愉快. 事实上,这是相当难以忍受的看到我如何只能完成它后保持我的鼻子关闭和喝所有的液体一次. 药物开始起作用, 我开始感到镇静效应. 效果在1小时左右达到顶峰, 直到1.5到2小时, 我可以在任何时候在这个事件中睡着了, 但我想先知道药物的全部限制. 结论是它有很大的潜力作为一种睡眠辅助剂. 如果您在工作中进行毒品检查, 也可能有助于清除您的体内某些物质, 我真的看不到任何成性质, 影响 积极的 肌肉放松 梦想如经验 镇静作用 中立的 瞳孔扩大 睡眠问题 时间扭曲 没有 经常使用所 失去意识 时间表:58926 2009年7月3日发布 - 加利福尼亚 (279):一般 (1),第一次 (2),单独 (16)

Jul 3, 2009. erowid.org/exp/58926 DOSE: 2 tsp oral Poppies - California (tea) BODY WEIGHT: 130 lb Herbs had been my hot topic for a little while now and through my many searches the california poppy popped up. It looked like a drug that could potentially replace my sleep aids and with that in mind, I set off to gain a supply. Upon receiving it from an online vendor I preceded to make a tea out of the red flowers. I used about 2 teaspoons in a coffee mug of water. I let that sit for about 30 mins along with a tea bag of “apple cider” flavoring. This resulted in a very dark red liquid that stained anything that it touched. The taste of this liquid was not enjoyable. In fact it was rather unbearable seeing on how I could only finish it after holding my nose shut and drinking all of the liquid at once. It took about 30 mins for the stuff to kick in and I started to feel the sedating effects. The effects seamed to max out around the 1 hour mark and it stayed there until the 1.5 to 2 hour mark when I started to feel the effects subside. I could of at any point during this event fallen asleep but I wanted to 1st know the full limits of the drug. In conclusion it has great potential for a sleep aid. It could also maybe help flush some stuff out of your system if your were in going in for a drug test at work since it makes you go to the bathroom frequently. I really don’t see any addictive qualities that I could find since it would be a worthless recreational drug.

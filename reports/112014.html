美国 德托米丁 (Dormosedan 凝) 马类镇静剂 我使用的是口腔粘膜凝而不是静脉注射. 该凝适用于舌下使用,每毫升大约含有7. 6毫克的脱胺. 在纯的形式,它是一种白色粉末,可溶于水中. 一匹重达330到439磅的马应该得到1.00毫升,所以我用这个来转换给我的体重的剂量. 我对这种药物进行了三次不同的实验. 我在晚上9点半左右给了1/4毫升 (大约1. 9毫克) 的德托米丁. 我把这个下面的语言,肯定不是吞下任何. 需要30分钟才能感觉到效果. 我感到疲劳,头,眼睛稍微胀 (血管破裂),腹. 此外,最明显的是脉下降和呼吸浅. 经过大约40分钟的时间, 到了凌晨一点, 我突然醒来. 我感到充满活力, 很难入睡. 然而,在整个旅程中没有"高兴"的效果. 这次我在晚上10点服用了1⁄2毫升 (3.8毫克). 同样的症状 (增加了一点), 仍然没有令人愉快的效果. 然而, 我被安慰的速度稍快一些 (30分钟). 这次我醒来稍晚,凌晨3点, 第三次旅行:这次旅行我并没有打算服用Dormosedan, 我服用了大约200毫克的特拉马多尔, 我决定在特拉马多尔 (3. 8毫克) 后6小时, 在11点下午, 用直肠注射 1/2毫升. 至少在 对于我来说,去胺在直肠中效果更强. 我只需要15分钟才能感觉到效果, 我不记得我被镇静了, 这次我在凌晨5点醒来, 无法入睡. 这一次我感到,疲. 我的鼻腔有点排水, 眼睛也很. 结论:我已经确定 (至少根据我的经验) 德托米丁没有愉快的效果 (即使是阿片类药物),如果我有药物引起的失眠,也许只能用来使自己入睡. 如果您确实决定使用德托米丁,请特别注意剂量,因为有呼吸抑制和心跳动的风险. 根据我的经验和这些原因, 发布时间:2018年10月1日 德托米丁 (869):单独使用 (16),回顾/总结 (11),一般性 (1)

Oct 1, 2018. erowid.org/exp/112014 DOSE: sublingual Detomidine (liquid) rectal Detomidine (liquid) BODY WEIGHT: 173 lb Detomidine (Dormosedan Gel) Equine Sedative I used the oromucosal gel and not the IV formulation. The gel is indicated for sublingual use and has approximately 7.6mg of detomidine hydrochloride per mL. In its pure form, it is a white powder that is water soluble. A 330-439lb horse should be given 1.00mL, so I used this to convert the dosage for my weight. I had three different experiments with this drug. FIRST TRIP: I dosed 1/4mL (about 1.9mg Detomidine) around 9:30PM. I took this sublingually, being sure not the swallow any. It took about 30min to feel any effects. I experienced tiredness, dizziness, slightly swollen eyes (burst a blood vessel), and diarrhea. In addition, the most noticeable was a decreased pulse and shallow breathing. In about 40min I went to my bed and gradually fell asleep without noticing. At 1:00AM I suddenly woke up. I felt energetic and could not fall asleep very easily. However, there were no ‘euphoric’ effects throughout the trip. SECOND TRIP: This time I took 1/2mL (3.8mg) at 10:00PM. Same symptoms (increased a little), still no enjoyable effects. However, I was sedated a little quicker (30min). I woke up a little later this time, 3:00AM but could not return to sleep. THIRD TRIP: This trip I did not mean to take Dormosedan but ended up administering if for its sedative affects. I took approximately 200mg Tramadol and had trouble falling asleep. I decided to dose 1/2mL rectally 6 hours after the Tramadol (3.8mg) at 11:00. At least in me, detomidine was much more potent rectally. It only took 15min to feel the effects and it was all I could muster to go from the shower to the bed. I do not remember going under (sedation), it must happen suddenly because the lights were on and the towel was still wrapped in my hair. This time I woke up at 5:00AM and could not fall asleep. However, this time I felt significantly ‘groggy’ and tired. I had a little drainage from my sinuses and my eyes were puffy. IN CONCLUSION: I have determined (at least for my experience) that detomidine has no pleasurable effects (not even with opiates) and could maybe only be used to put myself to sleep if I were to have drug-induced insomnia. If you do decide to use detomidine, please pay extra attention to the dosages because of the risk for respiratory depression and tachycardia. From what I experienced and for these reasons, I would not recommend dormosedan gel for recreational use.

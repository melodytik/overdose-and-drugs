It came in a 100 gram jar and cost less than $30. A great bargain to say the least. I dose Phenibut in roughly half gram to a gram portions. The first day I took it I expected to have a high tolerance as a result of being on two GABA agonists (Ativan and Neurontin). So, I ended up starting with a two gram dose. I've also consumed Kava Kava. I mention these three particular drugs because they all modulate GABA enzymes in the brain. Phenibut is in the same classification of drugs.

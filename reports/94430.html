美国 这就是我的账户与diphenhydramine和我的剂量聪明的化学品加上加一些温和的镇静剂. 过去:40多种合法和非合法物质被消耗. 我有时会因为饮食,睡眠和吸食物质而变得双极. 现在:除了诺托普和温和镇静剂, T-0:服用了1000毫克的维生素C,1600毫克的皮拉塞坦,400毫克的草根与激情花和黄蜂,100毫克的5-HTP,351毫克的dmae. 我感觉触觉有点增加. 声音似乎更像是进入我的大脑深处, 随着音乐的强度增加, 可能是因为二胺药没有起作用, 我感觉T-0剂量开始. 真的让我想要跳舞 =D...btw我不跳舞... 我觉得我的思想非常开放, 我完全满足于写这份报告, 只是为了使用所, 我注意到我看到的每一个东西都在膨胀或微妙地重新排列, 就像普通的低剂量LSD幻觉. 我肯定觉得二胺已经起作用, 因为我有明显的感觉, 很难说出任何话, 当我这样做时, 我开始觉得好像有个舒适的云围绕着我的身体. 但这种感觉很好, 就像一条光线链按下快乐的按, 我决定尝试冥想. 现在我的内心声音非常强大. 放松的浪潮降临在我身上, 我听到的音乐让我感觉像在花落在地上跳舞一样. 像幻灯片一样在我的脑海中播放着一部关于我的生活和虚构事件的电影. 没有什么似乎很重要, 世界上一切都好, 我觉得自己很轻,很漂浮. 音乐在我的内心燃烧. 听力也好像有所提高, 我感受到强烈的身体浮动感. 我过去的二胺体验中明显的口感似乎很常见. 这种尾酒的空间性并不那么明显. 我认为所有智能药物都能抵消药物所造成的不良"阻碍性"效应. 我开始想到耶稣,我看到他的轮在我的房间没有完全吹了,但它好像我可以控制我的幻觉. T-2:+:影响更明显但更不愉快. 感觉比125毫克的胺更强烈. 这种混合物一定会增强效应. 我很难入睡. 可能是因为绿茶. 我醒来时感到和疲劳. 六个小时后仍然高. 工作的方向感觉有点和浮动. 目前还没有确定. 我只是想睡觉. 在我吃了一包急救药后, 概述: 积极 - 浮动感,,增加意识,轻微的高兴感. 一致性. 中性 - 中部浮动感,空间. 负面 - 太空化,疲,浮动不再有趣; 总的来说,我认为把这些药物结合起来是个坏主意, 经验的成本效益我会评价好4,坏7. 对于所有无聊而想的人... 找到一个很棒的爱好,爱你的邻居,学习学习. 让自己被冲动性控制, 给我和我的家人带来了许多可避免的痛苦. Past: 40+ substances legal & non-legal consumed. I am sometimes bipolar depending on diet, sleep, and substances consumed. Present: clean other than nootropics and mild sedatives such as Valerian root that I use for stress and sleep. T-0: Took 1000mg of Vitamin C, 1600mg Piracetam, 400mg Valerian Root with Passion flower and Hops, 100mg 5-htp, 351mg dmae. T-0:15: downed 5x 25mg diphenhydramine with glass of Arizona green tea with honey and ginseng totaling 125mg. T-0:30: My heart is beating faster and I feel tactile sensation increased slightly. Just turned on some psychedelic trance music, sound seems to penetrate more like its driving deep into my brain lobbying for room oddly energizing me. I feel sensations go down my spine as the musical intensity increases. Could be that the diphenhydramine hasn't kicked in and I'm feeling the onset of the T-0 dose. Honestly makes me wanna dance =D...btw I don't dance... T-0:45: A little less stimulated my mind feels very open and I am completely content writing this report still listening to music. Just got up to use the bathroom and I noticed everything that I looked at was either inflating or rearranging itself subtly like a common low dose LSD hallucination. T-1:00: I definitely think the diphenhydramine has kicked in because I have the distinct feeling that my voice box has been removed or never existed. It is very hard to say anything at all and when I do it feels like im doing a partially uncomfortable stretching exercise. I am starting to feel sleepy like a comfortable cloud has wrapped around parts of my body. Ooo spinal chill but the chill feels very good almost like a chain of light pressing euphoria buttons as it travels to its destination. Decided to attempt to meditate. T-1:15: My internal voice is so powerful right now. Waves of relaxation come over me and my cats fur feels sooo soft. The music im listening to gives me the feeling of dancing on rose petals as they fall to the ground. A movie of my life and fictional events play across my mind like a slide-show. Nothing seems important all is well in the world even though euphoria is very subtle. T-1:30: I feel very lite and floaty. Creativity is peaking & music burns within me. I also feel like my hearing quality has been increased...possibly just hallucinating. ;) Breathing is pretty shallow. I feel strong physical floating sensations. T-1:45: Distinct mouth sensations appear common of my past diphenhydramine experiences. The spaciness aspect is not so pronounced with this cocktail. I think all the smart drugs counteract some of the bad “retard” effects of the drug. I started thinking of Jesus and I saw his outline in my room not full blown but there its as if I can control my hallucinations. T-2:+: Effects are more pronounced yet less enjoyable. Feels stronger than just 125mg of diphenhydramine. The mix must have potentiated the effects. I struggled getting to sleep. Could be from the green tea. I awoke pretty itchy and tired. Still high 6hrs later. Headed to work feeling slightly itchy and floaty. Not positive at the moment. I just want to sleep. Feeling broke 5hrs into the day after I ingested a Emergen-C packet. Overview: Positive - Coming up floaty feeling, trippy, increased awareness, slight euphoria on come up. Oneness. Neutral - Mid Floaty feeling, spacey. Negative - Too spacey, tired, floaty not fun anymore ;(, itchy, grumpy. All in all I think combining these drugs was a bad idea because it lasted much longer than just the diphenhydramine alone and interfered with my day life. Cost-benefit of the experience I would rate good 4, bad 7. To all the people who are bored and just wanna get high... Find an awesome hobby, love your neighbors, and learn learn learn. Don't do what I did letting myself be controlled by an impulsive nature causing me and my family much avoidable pain.

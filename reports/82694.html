这是我第一次见到一个白人. 对于那些不熟悉"白色"这个词的人来说, 我已经经常吸草了大约5个月了. 通常每周4次,有时更多,有时更少. 我从来没有过八天以上没有吸过大麻. 现在,我将解释这个故事. 现在是星期四晚上, 我已经到达了学校的后派对, 我和一些人见面, 我们去房子的后面建造一个. 我们每个人都有2个,然后离开房子,以免被抓住. 经过15分钟左右, 我们走到后院, 现在我已经吃了4个了, 另外, 在吸毒之前, 我喝了1酒,1吉姆·比姆和可乐, 于是我回到房子里,走到外面, 我点了另一个香烟, 开始和人们交谈. 在这个时候,这将是大约5分钟以来我有草. 十分钟后, 我开始感到头, 我认为我的皮肤开始变白, 因为一个人停在我前面说:"该死的男人,你扔一个白. 这让我担心,我转到一把椅子约1米远离我. 我吸了大麻已经15到20分钟了. 这种感觉与我平时的高潮完全不同. 我很冷,非常非常冷. 类似于我在服用特拉马多尔并入睡后出现的冷汗. 我通常不会因为大麻而感到头痛, 我的朋友给了我他的衫,我穿上了,把我的头放在我的手里. 我感到恶心和疲,而且仍然很冷. 在大麻后30分钟. 坐在同一个椅子上, 只是空白地着地面. 人们开始来问我是否没事. 我尽我所能告诉他们我没事, 我只是需要坐下来, 人们开始拉起椅子坐在我前面. 没有任何乐趣, 因为他们一直在移动我的头看着我的眼睛, 人们不断地着我,说'让我们把他的手放进水里,让他尿到自己身上'或'让我们在他的脸上画一个阴茎.'这让我很丧,因为那时我几乎不能动或打开嘴. 我只想坐在椅子上闭上眼睛. 50分钟后, 观众仍然很多. 不同的人来来去去. 他们中的大多数都告诉我有白人是多么糟糕以及我可以期待发生的一切. 我开始觉得好一点, 打开眼睛看看周围. 然后恶心又回来,我又闭上眼睛. 两位女生来检查我的脉, 但他们压力太大, 这让我更加恶心, 我把头指向椅子的一边. 然后,有人抓住我的肩膀和摇动我. 这就是让我吐的原因. 但这很好,我现在感觉好多了. 不是很好,但更好. 我得到了一杯水和一些纸巾擦我的嘴. 现在我已经抽了大麻一个小时十分钟了. 我可以感觉到下降正在发生. 我的嘴里开始有唾液, 但仍然很慢. 我从椅子上站起来,走到大火旁,坐下. 这使我的体温回升, 我开始感觉好. 现在是凌晨1点半左右. 其他人都来和我坐火边, 我可以和他们交流一点. 我在聚会的剩余时间大部分时间都坐在火前. 我告诉他们我们将在3点被接到. 我现在又觉得正常了. 没有疾病,我可以说话. 我几乎想再打碎一些或再喝一些酒, 现在是3点,我们要离开. 我们回到我家, 大约在三点半睡觉. 我记得有一天早上我醒来时, 左腿非常严重, 持续了5分钟. 这让我想起我吃了夏威夷木种子后的, 我醒来的时候是12点左右, 虽然这次经历很糟糕,但我认为这很好 (如果不是在聚会上, 未来我只会在喝完酒后吸草, 完全不用担心吸烟, 时间表: 82694 酒精 (61),大麻 (1),烟草 - 香烟 (266):组合 (3),火车事故和旅行灾难 (7),宿醉 / 几天后 (46),大组 (10+) (19)

Nov 21, 2013. erowid.org/exp/82694 DOSE: T+ 0:00 2 glasses oral Alcohol T+ 0:00 3 joints/cigs smoked Tobacco - Cigarettes T+ 0:00 2 bowls smoked Cannabis T+ 0:15 2 bowls smoked Cannabis T+ 0:20 1 cig. smoked Tobacco BODY WEIGHT: 65 kg This is the first time I have ever had a whitey. For those of you unfamiliar with the term 'whitey' it basically means you have a bad reaction to weed (or any other drug) and become very cold, drowsy, nauseous and feel distant from reality. I have been smoking weed regularly for about 5 months now. Usually 4 times a week, sometimes more, sometimes less. Since I have started I have not been without weed for more than 8 days. Now, I will explain the story. It's a Thursday night, and I have arrived at the after party of the school formal ready to get baked. I meet up with some guys and we go to the back of the house to construct a bong. We each have 2 cones then move away from the house as not to get caught. After 15 minutes or so we walk down to the back of the yard behind a large tree and each pack more cones. At this point I have had 4 cones and decide to stop. Also, prior to the weed I have consumed 1 beer and 1 can of Jim Beam and Coke and about 3 cigarettes. So, I head back up to the house and make my way outside to where the majority of people are. I light another cigarette and start talking to people. At this time, it would be about 5 minutes since I've had the weed. 10 minutes after I've finished with the weed I start to feel a bit light headed so I sit down on a ledge. I guess my skin was starting to go pale, because one guy stopped in front of me and said 'Fuck man, you're chucking a whitey. You're gonna pass out and start spewing soon.' This gets me worried and I move to a chair about 1 metre away from me. It's now 15, maybe 20 minutes since I smoked the weed. This feels completely different to my normal highs. I am cold, very, very cold. Similar to the cold sweats I get after taking tramadol and falling asleep. I have a huge headspin which I do not usually get from weed, but I guess the cigarettes added to this. My friend gives me his jumper and I put it on and rest my head in my hands. I'm feeling sick and tired and still very cold. 30 minutes after the weed. Sitting on the same chair, just staring blankly at the ground. People are starting to come over and ask if I'm alright. I do my best to tell them I'm ok and I just need to sit down and be on my own. Now, people have started pulling up chairs and sitting in front of me. This is not fun at all because they keep moving my head to look at my eyes which apparently were very red and bloodshot, more so than usual after I've smoked weed. People keep constantly poking at me and saying things like 'Let's put his hand in water so he pissed himself' or 'Let's draw a penis on his face.' This was frustrating because at this time I could hardly move or open my mouth. All I wanted to do was sit on the chair and close my eyes. 50 minutes into it and the crowd is still big. Different people coming and going. Most of them are telling me how bad it is to have a whitey and all the things I can expect to happen. For a minute I start to feel better and open my eyes to look around. Then the nausea comes back and I close my eyes again. Shortly after this two girls come over to me and check my pulse, but they push a bit too hard and I have trouble breathing. This makes me even more nauseous and I aim my head over the side of the chair. Then, someone grabs me by the shoulders and shakes me. This is what caused me to vomit. But this is good, I feel much better now. Not great, but better. I am given a glass of water and some paper towel to wipe my mouth. Now it is maybe an hour and ten minutes since I have smoked the weed. I can feel the come down happening. My mouth is starting to get saliva in it, but still very slowly. I get up from the chair and walk over to the big fire and sit down. This brings my temperature back up and I start to feel good. At this time it is about 1:30 in the morning. Other people come and sit by the fire with me and I am able to communicate a little bit with them. I spend the most of the remaining time at the party sitting in front of the fire. At about 2:40 I get up and meet with my friends and tell them that we are getting picked up at 3. I am feeling normal again now. No sickness and I am able to talk. I almost feel like smashing a few more cones or having some more drinks but decide against it. It's 3:00 and we leave. We arrive back at my house and go to bed at about 3:30. I remember sometime in the morning waking up with an incredibly bad cramp in my left leg which lasts about 5 minutes. It reminds me of the cramps I get after I've taken Hawaiian baby woodrose seeds, except a lot more painful. I awake about 12:00 and notice my left leg is still very sore and tight. Although this experience was bad, I think it is good that it happened (although it would have been better if it was not at a party) because now I know what to expect and do in the future if this happens - just sit down away from people, keep my eyes still or closed and just wait to vomit. This has not put me off weed, alcohol or cigarettes, but in future I will only smoke weed once I have finished drinking and not worry about cigarettes at all seeing as they give me huge headspins and lower blood pressure.

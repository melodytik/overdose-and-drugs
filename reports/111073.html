让我们从头开始. 我31岁,来自德国 (请原谅错误). 毒品有时会对我的身体造成不良影响. 我对所有常见药物和一些RC有经验. 首先我一直是迷幻药的拥护者 (尤其是胺和LSD). 经过过多次使用MDMA,MDA和其他类型的药物, 我从来没有去看医生. 几乎没有人知道. 我在黑暗和安静的时候发作. 起初感觉就像一个电击. 我总是试图与这种感觉作斗争, 我从来没有这样过. 症状 (失眠 - - 因为"震惊", 睡觉时最真实的梦, 迷失方向, 幻觉, "光学", 头痛, 极度抑郁 - - 这是我人生中第一次真正讨厌自己和世界) 经过6周左右就好了. 我不得不停止吸食大麻, 两年后"冲击"大多停止了. 但是... 我找到了终极的药物:片. 我很快就成了. 在4个月内, 我从大约10mg的氧化到160mg的氧化 (每'服务' - 每日剂量更高). 第一次戒断发生在8个月后. 我认为这并没有像我经历过6个星期的地狱一样糟糕. 我大大降低了每天服用的剂量. 尽管如此,我还是说这是我经历过的最强烈的戒断之一. 我有很多这样的经历. 在德国,你可以进入美沙计划. 但我从来不想这样做, 因为我从来不想被认为是个徒. 我只碰了一针, 却找不到静脉. 我很高兴我没有. 黑市的海洛因和美沙也很快随之而来. 两年后, 我尝试了所有可用的阿片类药物和类药物. 我试过80种不同的药物, 我不是一个愚蠢的疯子. 我一直是学校最好的学生之一. 几年后,我找到了一份很好的工作. 摆脱了成. 但每隔2-3个月我都会订购Tramadol. 我真的可以忍受这样的使用. 遇到了我的女朋友. 她从来没有吸毒. 这两年后改变了. 我遇到了一个老朋友. 他和我有着相似的'生活方式'. 我们握手了. 在短短几周内, 很快我们就开始使用LSD,胺,MDMA,速度,N2O,大麻每一个**的周末. 工作的情况变得更糟 (不与毒品有关), 没有得到足够的睡眠和多余时间. 这段友因争而破裂. 我已经无法忍受了. 在上班的路上, 我停下来哭了两个小时, 完全失去了我的轨道. 失去了工作,失去了友. 他 (朋友) 告诉我我会在5年内死亡, 因为我身体有严重问题 (每天喝一瓶葡萄酒 - 或更多) 并没有去看医生. 那个朋友在5年后自杀了...几乎是他对我说的那一天...不能忘记这一点. 现在... 仍然是一个吸毒者. 我已经服用海洛因4年了 (每天服用2年). 没有针头! 但我确保自己的身体不会让我失望. 开始进行力量训练, 在8个月内减掉了大约75磅 (我之前是250磅). 我喜欢炼! 我的女朋友和我一样上. 我们有这么多的提款, 它们总是很糟糕, 我们每次戒药都想戒掉海洛因. 从来没有打开. 然后我们找到了Lyrica (普雷加巴林150-2100毫克). 我们的初始剂量为600毫克. 这让我们都惊呆了! 我以前经常被提议参加歌曲, 这几乎是我能想到的最愉快的毒品! 我们都几乎有相同的症状: - 极度愉快的身体负荷 (性或炼是惊人的) - 舒适的麻木 (粉红花朵帮助如果你找不到正确的词;) - 激活 - 非常好的睡眠 - 闭眼视觉以及开眼视觉 - - 非常强烈的内在视野 (对我来说像一个共剂 -> MDMA - - 我们都想到和谈论一个非常令人丧的时间段和哭泣...但它是治愈) - 强烈的高兴感 对于我来说, 这是一种直接的阿片类药物, 我们喜欢它, 发现它对戒烟有很好的帮助后, 但仍然... 毒更强烈. 我写这篇文章时服用了大约1500毫克的普雷加巴林, 普雷加巴林的问题是: 对于我来说,有时就是一种冲动. 我们总是寻求这种愉快的东西. 耐受性很快发展. 即使没有服用Pregabalin2周或更长时间后,我们也无法从最初的600mg (对我来说非常强烈) 中获得感觉. 这会让人上吗? 是的 但并非因为阿片类药物. 我看到了更容易成的可能性, 对于我来说, 现在,我知道. 两月前我有两天的完全昏迷 (只有一次在半幻药和频繁剂量大约1.5mlGBL,1-2Flunitrazepams, 我女朋友告诉我我服用了18毫克的布罗马泽,Lyrica和酒精, 我全身有很多伤. 在第三天我恢复记忆和跌倒严重 (或) 进入一个盒子,现在有真正的问题. 我很震惊, 当我的女朋友告诉我我做了什么, 因为他们都是我从不做的事情. 由于她当时在工作, 我感到非常愧, 回到普雷加巴林: 这对我来说是一种疗法. 我用它来解决一些问题. 我可以在戒断期间完全正常运作. 几乎消除所有负面症状, 只留下轻微滴水的鼻子. 我把普雷加巴林和一些药物结合起来, 大麻:增强身体负荷和CEV/OEV - - 好组合. 酒精:我不能安全地推它 - - 它增强了酒精的两面以及消极面 - - 我喝了一杯酒, 首先它增强了海洛因, 可卡因:我不认为它是安全的, 没有药物的戒断: 另一种治愈戒烟的方法是冷淋浴. 我知道这与其他一切完全相反. 我已经做了几个月了,我很喜欢. 两年来,我只能赤脚走路. 不管是冬天还是下雪. 这也帮助我的身体抗击感冒. 对我来说这是最糟糕的... 寒冷. 退款对我来说是一个永恒的冬天. 谢谢您的阅读. 其他: 药物 - 普雷加巴林 (418):组合 (3),回顾 / 摘要 (11),抑郁症 (15),成和习惯性 (10),各种 (28)

Oct 12, 2017. erowid.org/exp/111073 DOSE: oral Pharms - Pregabalin Heroin (daily) Various BODY WEIGHT: 183 lb Let´s start at the beginning. I´m 31 years old and from Germany (please excuse the mistakes). Drugs are a sometimes nasty thing for my body. I have experience with all common drugs and some RCs. At first I have been an advocant of psychedelics (especially Ketamine and LSD). But after some too frequent uses of MDMA, MDA and other kinds I had developed a psychosis. Never went to a doctor with this. Kept it from nearly everyone. I got seizures when everything was dark and quiet. It felt at first like a electric shock. I tried always to fight this feeling except one time which led me completely paralyzed for around 10 sec. where I had hallucinations and heard a extreme loud white-noise-ish sound with frequent voices and...pain). Never had it again this extreme. The symptoms (insomnia - because of the 'shocks', the most realistic nightmares ever when sleeping, disorientation, hallucinations, 'optics', headaches, extreme depression - the first in my life where I really hated myself and the world) went better after around 6 weeks. I had to stop my Cannabis-habit because it made it worse. The 'shocks' stopped for the most part around 2 years later. But... I found my ultimate medicine: Opiates. I went quickly into total addiction. In 4 months I went from around 10mg Oxycodone to 160mg Oxycodone (per 'serving' - daily dose was higher). First Withdrawal was after 8 months. I thought that it wasn´t as bad as the hell I went through the 6 weeks after the MDMA-overdose. I greatly decreased my daily dose for the withdrawal. Still I would say it was one of the strongest withdrawals I had. Well and I had many of that. In Germany you can go into the Methadone-Program. But I never wanted that because I never wanted to be associated as a junky. I only once touched a needle but wasn´t able to find a vein. I´m happy I didn´t. Heroin and Methadone (black market) followed soon. Around two years later I had tried every Opiate and Benzodiazepine which was available. I tried around 80 different drugs in a time period of around 4-6 years only. I´m not a dumb nut (wordplay from Germany ;) ). Always was one of the best in school. Had a really good job years later. Kicked off the addiction. But every 2-3 months I ordered Tramadol. I really could live with that type of usage. Met my girlfriend. She never used drugs. That changed two years later. I met an old (drug-related) friend. He had a similar 'lifestyle' I had. Also recovered from drug-usage...we shaked hands. It went from good life to drugged life in a matter of weeks. Quickly we did LSD, Ketamine, MDMA, Speed, N2O, Cannabis every fu**ng weekend. Things at my jobs went worse (not drug-related) and I was completely stressed out. Didn´t get enough sleep and had many extra hours. The friendship went apart in a dispute. My mind couldn´t stand it anymore. While on the way to the job I stopped and cried for 2 hours and went back home. Lost my track completely. Lost the job...lost the friendship. He (the friend) said to me that I would be dead in 5 years because I had severe problems with my body (drank a bottle of wine everyday - or more) and didn´t go to the doctor. That friend killed himself at around 5 years later...nearly exactly the day he said that to me...can´t forget this. Now.... Still a drug-addict. Since around 4 years I´m on Heroin (since 2 years daily). No Needles! But I made sure that my body wouldn´t let me down. Started strength-training which I still do to this day. Lost around 75 pounds in 8 months (I was 250 pounds before). Love working out! My girlfriend is exactly as addicted as I am. We had so many withdrawals because we did not have enough money anymore. They were always bad and we wanted to quit the heroin at each withdrawal. Never went off. Then we found lyrica (Pregabalin, 150 - 2100mg). Initial dosage was 600mg for both of us. It blew us both completely away! I had been offered lyrica so often before and always declined. It was nearly the most enjoyable drug I could think of! We both had nearly the same symptoms: - extreme pleasurable bodyload (sex or working out is amazing) - comfortable numbness (Pink Floyd helps if you don´t find the right words ;) ) - activation - very good sleep - closed eye visuals and also open eye visuals - very strong inner sights (to me like an empathogen -> MDMA - we both thought and talked about a very depressing time period and cried both...but it was healing) - strong euphoria To categorize the feeling: It is for me a direct crossover of Opiates, Benzos and a bit of Psychedelics/Empathogens. We loved it and loved it more after we found out that it helped really good with withdrawals. But still...the heroin-addiction is stronger. I write this while on around 1500mg Pregabalin and having a heroin-withdrawal. The problem with Pregabalin: The 'Rush' (it sometimes IS a rush for me. Love to dance on this....love to do everything on it - kills my depressions completely) is so pleasurable that we always seek this. The tolerance develops quickly. Even after 2 weeks or longer without Pregabalin we don´t get the feeling anymore from the initial 600mg (which for me was really strong!). Is it addicting? Yes! But not in way of Opiates. I see the addicting potential more in way of a Benzodiazepine which to me never were a problem. Well... 2 months before this I had a 2 day total blackout (only had it once for around 8 hours after a psychedelic and frequent dosages of around 1,5ml GBL, 1-2 Flunitrazepams, 200mg tramadol before all that, alcohol and cannabis). My girlfriend told me that I took 18 x 6mg Bromazepam, Lyrica and Alcohol and was really aggressive and not 'myself'. Had so many bruises all over my body. On the third day I regained memory and fell badly (or dumb) into a box and had real problems to get now. I was shocked when my girlfriend told me what I did because they were all things I never do. Since she was working in that time, I have a real shame because I hope I didn´t knock on someone's door or whatever. Back to Pregabalin: It is a cure for me. I was able to get inside some of my problems with it. I am able to function completely while on a withdrawal. It kills nearly all negative symptoms and only a slightly dripping nose stays. I combined Pregabalin with some drugs and had little to no problems. Cannabis: enhances the bodyload and CEVs/OEVs - good combination. Alcohol: I can´t recommend it safely -- it enhances both and also the negative sides of the alcohol - I went out after some beer and my girlfriend couldn´t wake me for half an hour. Heroin: At first it enhanced the heroin but my tolerance for heroin went though the roof. Cocaine: I don´t think it is safely but....pure fun! Withdrawals without medication: Another good cure for withdrawals are cold showers. I know this is exactly the contrary from everything else. I do it since some months and love it. Since two years I walk only barefoot. Doesn´t matter if it is winter and snowing. This also helps and strengthens my body to fight against cold. For me this is the worst...the cold. Withdrawals are a eternal winter for me. Thanks for reading.

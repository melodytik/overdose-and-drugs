虽然我发现MiPT是一个相对平凡的体, 最近的信息使物质更有趣在我的眼睛... 我的一个朋友 (我没有心情和拒绝加入他) 混合和同时吞下20毫克的MiPT与50毫克的DiPT. 他之前服用过70毫克和100毫克的DIPT. 他发现百毫克的DiPT对他来说是正确的. 他从来没有尝试过MiPT. 他发现MiPT显著增强了DiPT,远远超过50毫克的正常效果,但他说MiPT本身几乎没有"颜色"或"味道",尽管他确实注意到这次旅行比正常情况更加视觉. 根据包括我自己的个人经验在内的大多数报告, 我觉得MiPT将是一个很棒的材料来增强另一个胺的减弱作用, 我打算有一天自己找出答案. 我会在的峰期结束之前服用20毫克MiPT, 试着看看是否会延长的作用, 我认为MiPT的另一个好处是加强和激发智力团体讨论. 我觉得小剂量 (比如十毫克) 肯定会让六个人或多人聚焦, 我经历的MiPT并不是最震撼人心的化剂, 这位私人,完全没有执照的研究人员将继续进行进一步的实验. 其他: 专家组 (110),MIPT (251):二手报告 (42),组合 (3),小组 (2-9) (17)

Benificial as a Booster DiPT & MiPT by Morninggloryseed Citation:   Morninggloryseed. "Benificial as a Booster: An Experience with DiPT & MiPT (exp26385)". Erowid.org . Aug 25, 2003. erowid.org/exp/26385 DOSE: 50 mg oral DiPT 20 mg oral MIPT BODY WEIGHT: 130 lb While I found MiPT to be a relatively mundane entheogen, recent information has made the substance much more interesting in my eye... A friend of mine (I wasn't in the mood and declined to join him) mixed and swallowed at the same time twenty milligrams of MiPT with fifty milligrams of DiPT. He has taken DiPT before at seventy milligrams, and one-hundred milligrams. He found one-hundred milligrams of DiPT to be just right for him. Never has he tried MiPT. He found that the MiPT significantly enhanced the DiPT well beyond what fifty milligrams would normally do, but said the MiPT added little to no 'coloring' or 'flavor' of its own, though he did notice that the trip was a bit more visual than normal. Interesting considering MiPT does not cause much visual on its own according to most reports including my own personal experience. I have a feeling MiPT will be a fantastic material to boost the waning action of another tryptamine, or to intensify one if it is taken at the same time. I intend to find out one day myself. I will take twenty milligrams of MiPT just before the end of a psilocybin mushroom trip peak to see if it will extend the action of the mushrooms without making it feel like a different trip. Another thing I think MiPT will be beneficial for is to heighten and stimulate intellectual group discussion. There is a definite 'smart drug' aspect to MiPT and I feel sub-entheogenic dosages (like maybe ten milligrams) would definitely make for a lively and deep focus group of maybe six people or so, without causing everyone trip out and get so 'loopy' as to result in the event being a disaster. MiPT did not prove to be the most earth-shaking entheogen in my experience, but I have a feeling it will prove quite useful in other aspects. Further experimentation is warranted and will be pursued by this private and completely unlicensed researcher.

Mar 30, 2025. erowid.org/exp/64010 DOSE: 1/16 g oral Mushrooms Dimenhydrinate 2 bowls smoked Cannabis 30 smoked Tobacco - Cigarettes BODY WEIGHT: 135 lb I had never taken shrooms or any other hallucinogen, and had been enthusiastic about trying it for awhile. I have a great deal of experience with marijuana and various painkillers, so I know a thing or two about getting high. Tripping was something else, though, and I did a bunch of research before trying it. I split a quarter oz of shrooms with my friends A, J and L, all of whom I have known for a couple of years and with whom I am quite comfortable. There were also a couple of people there who weren't tripping, but who I am also comfortable with. A and J were trying it for the first time as well, while L is a very experienced tripper. L likes to almost narrate drug experiences, so he inadvertently served as a guide to help us sort out what was going on. We got burgers and orange juice to wash down the shrooms. L advised that shrooms can make you nauseous, and as one with a fairly weak stomach, I decided to take some Dramamine rather than risk a trip-ruining vomit session. We came back to my place and camped out in the living room, and ended up spending the whole trip just conversing and goofing off. The first thing I noticed was a, kind of, nervous tension take hold of my body. It felt like a less intense sort of adrenaline rush, with electric energy prickling up onto my skin all over. My palms grew sweaty. I then noticed a difference in the way things looked. I appreciated as if for the first time the particular angles at which light slanted in from the windows. Each casual, everyday image of my house looked fascinating, intriguing, a work of art. The television grew blurrier and blurrier until I no longer cared to look at it at all. Us three newbies were reacting much more visibly to the shrooms than L, who was still enjoying himself and commenting often on the come up. J got up and started pacing, in disbelief that we were all managing to sit still on the couch with no problem. He then started throwing things around the house, light things, very playfully. He said he just felt the need to manipulate objects in his hands. A and I decided to walk around the house, and each room we went to seemed like a new world of possibilities and strangeness, and each room emitted a strong and discernible feeling of its own. By the third room we were giggly as hell. We came back to the living room and we all started just laughing our asses off. Every point of conversation was hilarious. At one point we were laughing about the very concept of laughing. This seemed to go on for a very long time, with me laughing longer and harder than A or L. Sorry I can't give any reliable chronology of all this, since all sense of time completely disappeared. It would feel like an hour had passed and I would look at the clock to see only a minute, maybe two, gone. Every time I finished a cigarette it would feel like a long time since my last one, and I'd light up again. I smoked about 30 cigarettes over the course of four hours. While the laugh fest was in full force, the visuals began to kick in. The visuals inhabit the same plane of vision as an afterimage of a bright light or marijuana induced hallucinations, and as such are never confused for reality, at least at the dosage I took. It's not like I'm seeing dinosaurs and shit the whole time. That being said, they were certainly an enjoyable diversion, and I was surprised at the extent to which the images took clear shape. Whenever I looked at the ceiling, which served as a clean white canvas for visuals I would otherwise not notice as I looked around the room, I would see any number of things - smoke, triangles, wheels with spokes, a pirate. They were in constant shifting motion as well. At this point I peaked, and the plateau that followed was dominated not by euphoria or eye candy, but by thought itself. L described the effect of tripping as one's mental floodgates being opened fully. Rather than perceiving things one at a time with my mind filtering what I experience into something that makes sense and is useful, while tripping, I perceived everything at once. A visual example: we went outside for a cigarette, and though I had seen the street lights in my neighborhood a million times before, when stepping out into the street this time I noticed them all simultaneously. Each one shone like a brilliant, twinkling star. This same phenomenon happened with my thoughts. Each idea that entered my mind immediately branched off into a dozen more thoughts, every word has limitless unforeseen connections and connotations, every concept, multiple levels of meaning and potential for understanding. Each idea that entered my mind immediately branched off into a dozen more thoughts, every word has limitless unforeseen connections and connotations, every concept, multiple levels of meaning and potential for understanding. I felt tempted with every single item that crossed my mind to delve deep into it, to explore the connections I was seeing for the first time, to lose myself in contemplation. But, because we were in such a social atmosphere, I made the conscious decision to forget about these things and to continue making conversation. This was what I loved about shrooms. The way it affects my thinking. The power I felt over my own priorities, what I decided to grant importance, and the potential to find every single thing in the world deeply fascinating. Four or five hours after taking them and we had come down, we went to a party and I had a blast. I realized that I didn't need to care about anything that was going on there than that which I was interested in, and I was interested in plenty. I should mention that J's reaction was not so good. After the initial 'high' phase, he withdrew from conversation and laid listlessly on the couch for the rest of the time. Later, he didn't describe it as a bad trip per se, but said he just felt weird and then it passed. I feel that shrooms are just not for everyone. I was pretty wiped the next day, and haven't had occasion to try them again yet, though I plan to soon.

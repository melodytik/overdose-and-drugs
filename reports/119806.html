Across a Wide Dosage Range 4C-D by Starwind Citation:   Starwind. "Across a Wide Dosage Range: An Experience with 4C-D (exp119806)". Erowid.org . Jun 10, 2026. erowid.org/exp/119806 DOSE: 8 - 15+ mg oral 4C-D (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 185 lb Mixed Dosage Trials With 4C-D (Ariadne) Multiple Trials Report I conducted five trials with 4C-D (Ariadne) across a wide dosage range. At 8 mg, the effects were very subtle. Approximately one hour after ingestion I noticed a mild bodily alertness accompanied by slight visual crisping and minor sensory enhancement. There were no overt visuals or stimulation, just a generalized off-baseline feeling that persisted for roughly four to six hours. At 12–15 mg, the effects became significantly more pronounced. The same alert onset occurred around one hour post-ingestion, but with a much stronger sense of being off-baseline. Cognitively, I experienced clear stimulation and enhancement. My speech was noticeably sped up, word association became faster and richer, and I felt hyper-engaged with my surroundings, almost like a stimulating LSD microdose. Visually, the experience reached roughly the level of 80 µg LSD, with trails, color enhancement, and mild patterning on textures. Sleep was impossible for approximately 12 hours after these doses. No nausea or body load was present. The fifth trial was dramatically different. Believing the compound to be largely inactive based on Shulgin’s writings in PiHKAL, I eyeballed an amount of very clean, fluffy crystals. Because of the light, airy consistency, the actual dose was much higher than intended. I estimate roughly 80 mg. [Erowid Note: Two samples of powder (even of the same chemical) with equivalent volumes won't necessarily weigh the same. For this reason, eyeballing is an inaccurate and potentially dangerous method of measuring, particularly for substances that are active in very small amounts. See this article on The Importance of Measured Doses .] Onset was strong by the one-hour mark. The experience felt distinctly mescaline-like but without any nausea. Colors became extremely bright and rich with an expanded palette. Pronounced geometric patterning (Aztec and Persian snowflake styles) covered textures, strong trails were present, and closed-eye visuals were vivid. The visual intensity climbed steadily and peaked around the three-to-four-hour mark. The entire experience was very long-lived, with significant effects lasting approximately ten hours before beginning to fade. Very clear unaltered headspace. The overall character was strongly psychedelic with a classic phenethylamine/mescaline color palette, comparable in visual strength to roughly 150 µg of LSD.

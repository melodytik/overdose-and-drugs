由于对某个像dmt/5meo-dmt这样的免费实验报告感兴趣, 我寻找了更多关于这个问题的信息. 我在研究中得到的结果是: 一个人服用了25毫克的入HCL. 他们通过浸泡大量氨 (从杂货店购买的纯物质, 材料结晶成两个大块, 他们试图过它. 两个主要的水晶被恢复但斑点是徒劳的. 也许这相当于2mg (无论如何,在这个过程中损失了2mg,所以他们下降了4mg到20mg的自由基). 然后将入H20浸泡足够长的时间,使大部分蒸发. 有些粘在玻璃的两侧, 无法恢复. 结果是16-18毫克 (大约没有权重) 的入自由基. 这种材料被放置在用于crack,meth,dmt等的玻璃管中. 管道中含有大约4-5年的5meo-dmt和微量dmt. 所以整个报告都以这一事实为序. 用户报告说, 很难看到管道的入, 它有着熟悉的胺味道, 发病速度很快,但比5meo慢. 所有的材料都在一击中吸烟. 这首歌由Coil的专辑"时间机器"中的4个音调组成 (DOET是这首歌). 其他使用者也表示, 墙上的声变得更加强烈, 视觉效果是清晰的,像液体一样, 不像DMT或5meo-DMT的清晰视觉效果. 分析,一半的剂量得到了. 这份报告在质量和行动方面足够独特, 用户肯定会有现实溶解的体验. 时间表: 24882 第10章:未知背景 (20),准备/食谱 (30),第一次 (2)

Half Way There DiPT (freebase) by philosophicus Citation:   philosophicus. "Half Way There: An Experience with DiPT (freebase) (exp24882)". Erowid.org . Jun 30, 2003. erowid.org/exp/24882 DOSE: 12 mg smoked DiPT (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 120 lb Interested in the one strange report (located on erowid) of a dmt/5meo-dmt like experience freebasing dipt, I sought out more info on this topic. Here is what was recounted to me in my research. A person took 25mg of dipt hcl. They freebased the material by soaking in a bunch of ammonia (pure stuff from the grocery, be careful wear eye and respiratory protection). The material crystallized into two large chunks, and some speckles. It sat for 10-15 minutes and then they tried to filter it. The two major crystals were recovered but the speckles were futile. Perhaps that equated to 2mgs (there is ~2mg lost in the process anyway so they were down like 4mg to 20mg freebase). The dipt was then soaked in H20 for long enough to evaporate most of it. Some stuck to the sides of the glass and couldn't be recovered. What ended was like 16-18mg (approx. not weighed) of dipt freebase. This material was placed in a glass pipe of the sort used for crack, meth, dmt, etc. The pipe had ~4-5 years worth of 5meo-dmt and trace amounts of dmt in it. SO all the report is prefaced by that fact. The user reported difficulty in being able to see the dipt in the pipe which brings the estimate down to probably 12mg or something that was actually burned. It had a familiar tryptamine taste, but definately distinct from 5meo-dmt. The onset was swift but slower than 5meo. All the material was smoked in one hit. Coil's composition of 4 tones from the album timemachines (DOET was the song) was put on. The harmonics vibrating off the wall became intensified and everything had the muffled or distorted sound reported by other users. There was a clear rush and liquid like visuals, unlike the sharp visuals of dmt or 5meo-dmt. Analysis, half the dose was achieved. The report was sufficiently distinct in quality and action to rule out 5meo more or less. With double the amount, the user was certain a reality dissolving experience would be had.

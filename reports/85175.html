我是一名18岁的男性, 在过去的几年里, 我从小学初年开始吸食大麻, 之后我尝试了可卡因, 狂喜药, 氧化, 化, DXM, 氧化, LSD, , 沙维亚, 阿尔普拉佐拉姆, 迪亚泽帕姆, 费内泽帕姆, 阿德拉尔, 里塔林, 我最喜欢的研究药物:梅. 我已经尝试了很多药物, 并且发现我喜欢兴奋剂, 我没有太多的自信, 我讨厌失去控制的感觉, 尤其是因为我所在地区的卷子质量太低, 我必须服用四个左右才能得到良好的效果. 我很高兴, 因为价格低,质量高, 这就是我的经历的开始. 我喜欢毒品,也许有点太多, 我喜欢研究他们一样多, 由于缺乏人际关系和生活在家中, 我不仅有了自己的邮箱, 而且因为认识了很多新朋友, 我主要是用可卡因,X和Oxy, 但我对所有这些都开始高我的银行账户开始低, 我开始使用2c-I和2c-e, 接下来我下令使用JWH-018和. 这种感觉完全像是大麻一样. 由于健康因素和肺部中化学物质的不适, 我认为如果我吸入剩余的40毫克, 并且在接下来的三天内昏, 我试图用甲基隆和美德龙保持乐观. 我把这些留给最后, 因为效果应该像我最喜欢的两个, 我先吃了三百毫克的甲基隆, 感觉很好, 与我读到的报道一致, 然后我尝试了梅. 包裹里面有2克的白粉, 现在我真的喜欢它了. 我做了任何毒品爱好者都会做的事, 我测量出一个50毫克的低剂量, 我发现很难做出线条作为物质倾向于粘在一起, 但最终我得到一个体面的线条, 这会让人伤心. 尤其是第一次, 感觉好像是眼睛下面, 眼立刻流出来. 能量水平翻了一番, 想要四处走动和交谈的冲动也出现了. 我把150毫克分成两行75毫克, 通过左鼻和右鼻吸入. 这种事情很难. 我在房间里着电子音乐,每分钟都在说话. 这种感觉与Mdma相似, 你的身体感觉流动, 我对这种药物有着广泛的了解. 在我谈到这个问题之前, 我必须谈谈我最不喜欢的部分, 但随着这种冲动, 峰值效应也会以相当快的速度收回. 虽然我仍然有剩余的刺激, 我却有空虚感, 不满足于做任何事情, 抑郁的想法也通常伴随着下降, 我开始思考如何应对这种情况. 首先我试了一下Xanax, 找到了一行1毫克的Xanax, 这不好, 因为大部分时间我都想在聚会上使用它. 我很快就把它和酒精结合起来了, 饮酒和无人机排队是不好的选择, 因为这几乎总是会导致短期的昏迷, 我发现最有效的方法是喝酒,直到我有点兴奋 (对我来说像4个快速的镜头或6个酒,我有很高的耐受性),然后做一个很好的大小的线, 我在聚会上和任何人谈论任何事情, 这是我最喜欢的药物效应之一, 我有说话的冲动, 结合酒精的最好之处在于,即使梅德龙的高峰效应已经消失, 我最喜欢的配合是我只做过一次的配合, 我曾经在一个聚会上这样做, 了好几个小时, 甲基混合得很好, 形成了与x相同但更好的物质. 那天晚上我也做了一些氧化当我下来, 这是我最好的氧化高我有过除了酸和氧化. 我也只服用梅和氧化, 觉得很好玩, 除了在聚会上做爱, 我最喜欢的场所是俱乐部或音乐会. 舞蹈是惊人的在这种药物和结合增强的声音感知和强烈的视觉效果由于我从mephedrone的眼睛动 (眼睛动通常开始出现在125mg和惊人的当我接近175mg) 在一个黑暗的环境中, 的音乐和闪的灯光是我会有任何药物最好的时间之一, 有时超过x. 我在俱乐部时通常会带来500毫克, 每小时去洗一次, 每次服用125毫克. 我经常不能去洗手间, 因为可能会丢掉我的位置, 我通常在计划吞下药物前三小时不吃东西, 通常用纸巾包裹400毫克, 这会让人轻轻的感觉到高潮, 但会延长效果, 在做这件事的时候, 我还喜欢把100-125毫克放在一个小袋子里, 这让我感觉到美能给我带来最好的高潮, 如果我能定时, 经过6个月的大量使用, 我已经完成了. 虽然我比任何其他药物都更喜欢它,但它太好了,我担心它未来可能带来的负面影响, 我已经开始注意到我短期记忆力有所提高 (或更确切地说有所恢复), 对于价格和质量 (只要是合法的, 它刚刚在英国被禁止) 它真的无法战胜. 其他: 4-甲基甲丁 (458):各种 (28),旅行后的问题 (8),成和习惯性 (10),健康问题 (27),回顾 / 摘要 (11),一般 (1)

My Bubbly Love Affair 4-Methylmethcathinone (Mephedrone) by MeMeC From Erowid Extracts Collection Citation:   MeMeC. "My Bubbly Love Affair: An Experience with 4-Methylmethcathinone (Mephedrone) (exp85175)". Erowid.org . May 14, 2010. erowid.org/exp/85175 DOSE: repeated oral 4-Methylmethcathinone (powder / crystals) repeated insufflated 4-Methylmethcathinone (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 200 lb I am an 18 year old male and have had a long journey in the past couple of years with many different mind altering substances. Starting with marijuana in the beginning of my junior year of high school, I have since tried Cocaine, Ecstasy, Oxycodone, Hydrocodone, DXM, Nitrous Oxide, LSD, Shrooms, Salvia, Alprazolam, Diazepam, Phenezepam, Adderal, Ritalin, and upon running out of most of the well known drugs I began looking towards research drugs and since then have tried 2c-I, 2c-e, JWH-018, MDPV, Methylone and my favorite of the research drugs: Mephedrone. I have tried a lot of drugs and have learned that I love stimulants, I don’t have a lot of self confidence so when I take any one of my top favorite stims (X, Drone, Coke) I love the way it makes me feel while I still am in control. I hate the feeling of losing control like with hallucinogens, though I didn’t mind LSD. Unforntunately these are some of the costliest drugs especially because the quality of the rolls in my area are sow low I have to take around 4 to get a good effect. So naturally when I discovered Mephedrone back in the fall of 2009 I was thrilled because price was low and quality was high, and thus begins my experience. I love drugs, perhaps a little too much, and I love researching them just as much, understanding them and their effects they grant their users, I have read so many experience reports I figured the least I can do is give back. Doing all this research yielded a lot of drugs that I wanted to try and due to a lack of connections and living at home I was constricted in the amount I could actually do. That all changed when I went to college, not only did I now have my own mailbox but due to meeting a lot of new people the availability of the drugs skyrocketed. I stuck mainly with coke, x and oxy but me getting high on all of this was starting to cause my bank account to get low so I resorted to research chemicals and began ordering all the drugs that I had been reading about. I started with 2c-I and 2c-e and after a not so great experience with both I was a little disappointed with the research drug market. However, I continued to experiment ordering JWH-018 and phenezepam next. The JWH was great vaporizing only a few milligrams and getting extremely high with a feeling that felt exactly like weed. I quit that though due to the health factor and an uneasiness with chemicals being in my lungs. Phenezepam was a horrible idea, I was expecting something similar to xanax, so after feeling nothing after dropping what I thought was around 10 mg I decided I might feel something if I snorted the remaining 40 and blacked out for the next three days. Having only three research chemicals left that I wanted to try, I tried to hold some optimism with methylone and mephedrone. I was saving these for last because the effects were supposed to be like two of my favorites, coke and x. I got the methylone first and swallowed 300 mg and got a nice buzz that coincided with the reports that I read where the effects were felt most when conversing with other people that are close to you. And then I tried mephedrone. As soon as it came in the mail I ripped open the package inside was 2 grams of a fluffy white powder, which most closely resembled powdered snow or powdered sugar. The smell was not as bad as I thought it would be (from reading reports) and now I actually like it. Then I did what any drug enthusiast would: I lined some up. I measured out a low dose of 50 mg and lined it up. I found it very hard to make lines with as the substance tends to stick together, but eventually I got a decent line and after rolling up a 20, up the nose it went. It burns a lot. Especially that first time, it felt like it went right under my eye and immediately caused tears to stream out. Within minutes I was feeling better, light seemed brighter, energy levels doubled and the urge to move around and talk was present. Satisfied with the feeling and having the peak effects wear down a little after about 20 minutes I weighed out 150 mg and separated it into two 75 mg lines and snorted each up the left and right nostril. This shit hits hard. I was flying high feeling better then a large line of good coke or 3 or 4 pills of x, I was moving around the room blasting electronic music and talking a mile a minute. The feeling is similar to that of mdma where your body feels fluid and you feel like you are floating when walking, I was feeling great and I was in love. After that experience I have done a lot of drone since and have a vast understanding of the drug. Before I get into that I must address my least favorite part about it, which is the comedown. The initial effects hit you the hardest but with that rush is a retraction of the peak effect at a fairly quick rate as well. The best feelings leave so quickly that, even though I still have the residual stimulation, I get a sense of emptiness and am not content with doing pretty much anything, depressing thoughts also normally accompany the comedown and I pretty much convince myself that I will never do any drug again nearly every singe time. Loving the high so much but hating the comedown I began to think about ways to combat it. First I tried xanax and found a line of 1 mg of xanax to do the trick but that normally makes me just sit there having a numb brain. This is not good because the majority of the time I want to use it at parties. So it was really only a matter of time until I combined it with alcohol, and when that finally did happen I pretty much did not do it without. Heavy drinking and a line of drone is not the way to go as it will almost always lead to a short-term blackout where I act like I just dropped 6 rolls, and that is not the best situation when I don’t want to earn a reputation as a complete jackass. What I found works best is drinking until I have a small buzz (for me like 4 quick shots or 6 beers, I have a high tolerance), and then doing a nice sized line like 125-150mg, this yields me one of the best highs I can get from mephedrone with no comedown at all. I float around parties talking to anyone about anything, that is one of my favorite effects of this drug, I have an urge to talk and the conversation flows so easy allowing me to talk effortlessly to anyone, although I have said some dumb things on high doses, most of the time it works beautifully. The best thing about combining it with alcohol is that even when the peak effects of mephedrone fade away, that urge to talk and keep conversation seems to stick around for hours, so a line right in the beginning of the night is perfect for any party. By far my favorite combination with mephedrone, is one that I have only done once which is mephedrone, alcohol, methylone and weed. I did this once at a party and was on cloud nine for hours, it is by far the best chemical high that I have ever gotten. The methylone mixes so well and creates something that is identical to x but better. That night I also did some nitrous oxide when I was coming down and that was one of my best nitrous oxide highs I have ever had besides acid and nitrous oxide. I have also done just mephedrone and nitrous oxide and found that enjoyable but I don’t like it because I feel like the vasoconstriction and the lack of oxygen is a bad combination for the brain. Besides doing it at parties my other favorite place is to do it at clubs or concerts. Dancing is amazing on this drug and combined with the enhanced sound perception and the intense visual effects due to the eye wiggles I get from the mephedrone (eye wiggles normally start appearing at 125mg and are amazing when I approach 175mg) being in a dark environment with pounding music and flashing lights is one of the best times I will have on any drug and at times it exceeds x. When I am at clubs I will normally bring like 500 mg and visit the bathroom once every hour doing about 125 mg each time. However, when I am at a show, I often can not go to the bathroom because I might lose my spot or it is in the middle of a set, so I have to resort to swallowing the drug. When I do this I normally don’t eat for three hours before I plan on swallowing it and normally wrap up 400 mg in a paper towel and swallow that around 20 minutes before I want the effects to kick in. This creates a great gentle high that lacks the rush but lengthens the effects and mellows them out a little. When doing this I also like to keep 100-125mg in a dime bag and snort in right out of the bag about an hour after I parachute the initial dose. This throws me into one of the best highs I can get from mephedrone alone and is perfect if I can time it up so it hits right when a favorite song is going to come on, and eye wiggles are insane at this point. So after 6 months of heavy mephedrone use, I am done. Although I love it more then any other drug, it is too good and I am worried about the negative effects that it could have in the future, as I already noticed some negative effects in the peak of my use relating mostly to short term memory loss at a noticeable level, and scrambling of words when speaking and word recall seems to be hit especially. After one month of no use though, I am already starting notice an increase (or recovery rather) in my short-term memory and I don’t scramble my words as much, and word recall is slightly increasing though not that much. I am glad I am done but will miss it, I have had some of the greatest times on this drug and for the price and quality (as long as its legal, it has just been banned in the uk) it really cannot be beat.

我们之前曾经试验过标准的, 我们一天和一天早上都禁食, 我们只需切碎,和吞, 就能摄取泰国. 味道和你在超市里买的一样. 我们注意到四肢有的感觉. 我们只是坐下来等待视觉效果开始. 视觉效果与我们以前所经历的相似, 边缘视觉中的一般运动, 如果我们放松注意力, 这种感觉在几个小时后消失了, 店里的女士告诉我们他们有三种类型的:第一种是我们之前经历过的泰国,第二种是夏威夷,她解释说它更坚固,因此更贵,最后他们有松露,她说它很有思想性,有点. 我们的首选是夏威夷人, 我们三个人最终都买了30g的松露. 我们去了J的房子, 我们邀请了三位朋友来, 我们坐着等待朋友们到来, 这增加了我们的期待. 我们开始吃松露. 根据我们所读到的10-15g是标准剂量, 味道很糟糕, 在舌头两侧留下令人难以忍受的酸/苦味. 我们和朋友们等待效果开始. 我们感觉到熟悉的舒适的感觉在我们的四肢和一个轻微的头脑. 我站在花园里, 石头似乎形成了一个形状, 开始在我周围延伸, 那些原本打算观看的人不得不离开. 我们决定带这两个女孩回家. 旅程涉及一座长山, 我们离开了那些与我们一起走在山的底部, 开始走上. 我们的头脑就不再正常运作了. 我们无法停止笑, 谈话变成了一系列不连贯的爆发. 一个共同的视觉是山的部分类似于电影的背景. 我们继续慢慢地摇摇欲地爬上山, 我们终于到达杰米的家, 我们继续笑, 偶尔爆发不连贯的对话. 我们做出了一个非常愚蠢的决定:我们决定出去. 我们打算前往M的家, 在正常情况下, 这一旅程总共需要15分钟. 我们像疯子一样摇摇欲地喊着笑,在旅途中我们几乎被两辆车撞了. 每个人都着我们,我们不只是偏执,他们. 虽然我确信我们需要一段时间才能到达我们想要去的地方, 到了那里,我们被M的女儿迎接. 我们实际上设法保持在一起直到J说'牛'我开始笑自己. 我女儿怀疑地看着我们, 后来我们被邀请进来, 但L和我感到不安, 返回的旅程和最初的旅程差不多相同, 在旅程之前, 我们急剧地回到地球. 我们感到身体和情感都很疲. 旅行本身持续了6个小时, 我们退休到一个卧室和我们的单独的床, 开始看电视, 我也开始经历明显疼痛的胃, 我们家里食物很少, 两小时后, 胃变得无法忍受, 尽管没有食物可以吐, 我不完全记得发生了什么. 我似乎回忆起经历了一次头, 然而,随着头的到来, 我认为我会死,我的整个视力变成红色. 我开始昏迷, 开始扎, 肢体摇, 撞到墙壁, 我躺在地上恢复了意识. 杰伊和L来检查我是否好. 我告诉他们我没事,我只是需要水. 我离开了浴室,跑下楼来喝水. 我在水里喝了水, 感觉又有了同样的恐怖, 我醒来时玻璃碎了,L和J站在我身边. 他们告诉我不要动,因为玻璃碎了,但我觉得非常需要新鲜空气. 我半半跑到花园之前再次崩到地板上, 终于我的胃减缓, 疲劳增加到我觉得可以睡觉. 我觉得自己已经没有任何力量了. 我是我们三个人中唯一一个经历了这些负面影响的人, 我再也不会吃松露了, 其他: 2007年6月21日发布 - P. tampanensis (133):小组 (2-9) (17),一般 (1)

Cow Mushrooms - P. tampanensis by John Diaz Citation:   John Diaz. "Cow: An Experience with Mushrooms - P. tampanensis (exp33251)". Erowid.org . Jun 21, 2007. erowid.org/exp/33251 DOSE: 30 g oral Mushrooms - P. tampanensis (fresh) BODY WEIGHT: 154 lb L, J and I purchased 30g of what were described as fresh Thai cubensis from a local alternative shop the day prior to the experience. We had experimented with standard cubensis mushrooms in moderate dosages previously and were interested in having a more profound experience. We fasted the day and morning before we ingested the mushrooms in a hope to reduce nausea and reduce the period of onset. We ingested the Thai mushrooms simply by cutting them up and chewing and swallowing them. The taste was mushroomy, not unlike the mushrooms you would buy at a supermarket. Shortly after this ingestion we noted a buzzing feeling in the limbs. This was a pleasurable feeling and we simply sat down and waited for the visuals to start. The visuals were much like we had experienced before with general movement in peripheral visions and more profound movements if we relaxed our focus. This wore off after a couple of hours and left us feeling clear-headed. We then decided to go back to the shop and purchase some higher dosages. The woman in the shop explained to us that they had three types of mushrooms: The first were the Thais which we had experienced earlier, the second were Hawaiians which she explained to be stronger and thus more expensive and finally they had truffles which she described to be thinky and somewhat poular. Our first choice would have been Hawaiians but we were not carrying enough money. In the end all three of us bought 30g of truffles each. We went to J's house which we knew would be empty for the whole day. We had invited three friends round who were supposed to be getting drunk. We sat and waited for the friends to arrive which served to heighten our anticipation. Eventually the friends arrived and we began eating the truffles. From what we had read 10-15g was a standard dose, we decided to go with the whole 30g. The taste was foul, it left an unbearable sour/bitter after taste on the sides of the tongue. Eventually we managed to stomach the truffles and waited with our friends for the effects to kick in. Initial effects took about twenty minutes, we felt the familiar comfortable feeling in our limbs and a slight clouding of the mind. Soon after this I had my only strong hallucination of the whole experience: As I stood in the garden the stones seemed to form a pattern which began to extend around me, eventually I was left standing with this pattern stretching infinitely in every direction. Those who initially intended to observe then had to leave. We decided to walk the two girls who had initially been with us back home. The journey involved a long hill which it took us a very long time to travel down. We left those we were walking with at the bottom of the hill and began walking up. It was at this point that our minds simply ceased to function in the normal way. We could not stop laughing and conversation became a series of incoherent outbursts. A shared visual was that part of the hill resembled a film backdrop. We continued to slowly stagger up the hill for what seemed like, and probably was hours. Eventually we reached Jamie’s house at which point we sat then lay on the floor. We continued to laugh with intermittent outbursts of random incoherent conversation. And then we made a monumentally stupid decision; we decided to go outside. We intended to travel to M's house, a journey which under normal circumstances would take us a grand total of 15 minutes. We staggered about like madmen shouting and laughing, during the journey we were almost hit by two cars. Everybody was staring at us, we weren't just paranoid, they were. At this point time no longer had meaning and although I'm sure it took us a while to get where we wanted to go, I couldn't say how long. When we got there we were greeted by M's daughter. We actually managed to keep it together until J said 'cow' and I started laughing to myself. M's daughter looked at us suspiciously but continued to talk to us. Eventually we were offered to come in but L and I felt apprehensive and wanted to return to the sanctuary of J's house. The return journey was much the same as the initial one and left another set of bewildered passers by. Once we reached J's things continued in a similar fashion for a while before the trip dropped us sharply back to earth. We felt drained in every sense, physically and emotionally. The trip itself had lasted 6 hours although to us it could have been half an hour or a number of days. We retired to a bedroom and our separate beds and began watching T.V. we were still experiencing residual effects including a clouding of the thoughts. At this point I also started to experience decidedly painful stomach cramps this could be put down to the fact that all we had eaten in the past two days were magic mushrooms. We had little food in the house and so set to devouring a multipack of crisps (chips in the US). After about two hours my stomach cramps became unbearable and I took a paracetamol. Soon I was vomiting violently despite a lack of food to vomit. As I stood up something happened which I do not entirely remember. I seem to recall experiencing a headrush, however as the head rush came on so did a feeling of dread. I thought I was going to die and my entire vision turned red. I started to black out and started to struggle, my limbs flailing and hitting the walls before I collapsed to the floor with a pronounced thud. I regained consciousness with my face on the floor. J and L had come to check if I was alright. I told them I was alright and that I just needed water. I left the bathroom and ran downstairs to get a drink of water. Once I had the water the same feeling of morbid dread came over me and I blacked out again. I came to with my glass smashed beside me and with L and J standing over me. They told me not to move because of the broken glass but I felt an incredible need for fresh air. I half staggered half ran to the garden before collapsing once again to the floor, but as the fresh air entered my lungs I felt my mind clear. Eventually my stomach cramps receded and fatigue rose to a point at which I felt able to sleep. I awoke the next day with a headache and a 'washed out' feeling. I was the only one of the three of us to experience these negative effects and I'm not entirely sure what caused them. I will never try truffles again because of a mixture of the bad taste and lack of visuals.

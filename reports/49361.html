我觉得有必要发布这份报告, 并且没有一个确实表明这种药物有多有益, 我决定去看心理医生, 了解ADD的治疗方法. 我希望能回到社区学院, 我是一名19岁的男性,体重约130磅. 经过我们两人之间有趣的谈话, 然后他和我母亲 (她坚持要参与这件事), 他建议使用阿托莫克西丁 (Strattera) 作为治疗方法. 我开始每天服用10毫克, 第二天晚上我醒来时大约是凌晨3点, 像我有什么可怕的错误, 但所有的逻辑告诉我我没事. 这种奇怪的感觉, 我只有一次用这种药物. 在第3周, 每天服用25毫克, 我有轻微的自杀意图, 这种症状在7天后消失了, 开始我不睡觉, 违背他的建议, 大约一周后,这种效果停止了. 我注意到一些典型的SSRI/SNRI副作用:心率升高,性欲减弱,高潮后疼痛,犹,小便,轻微便秘,早起. 我绝对不会称之为负面的副作用. 所有这些似乎随着时间的推移而减少,幸运的是,除了早起的交易; 这仍然是一个不错的触摸. 在学校我感觉更加专注, 而不是在课堂上做白日梦, 不关心, 据医生说, 作为一种抗抑郁药, 我相信我在最初增加的自杀意图等方面看到了其中的一些, 我不再觉得我必须纠正每一个人当他们做错了什么, 整体而言, 斯特拉特拉可以很少产生副作用, 我喜欢放弃它只是为了提高性功能, 以及恢复我的心率到一个更正常的水平. (而不是提高20BPM) 我可以在服用阿托莫克西丁时喝酒, 我每天服用的阿托莫克西丁剂量与90至190毫克的DXM相结合. 其他: 药物 - 亚托莫克西 (316):一般 (1),组合 (3),回顾/总结 (11),健康益处 (32),医疗用途 (47),不适用 (38)

Jul 27, 2007. erowid.org/exp/49361 DOSE: 25 mg oral Pharms - Atomoxetine (daily) BODY WEIGHT: 130 lb I felt the need to post this report after finding so few reports already listed. As well as none of them really showing how beneficial this drug can be, assuming it's prescribed and used correctly. About 4 months ago upon self diagnoses, I decided to see a psychiatrist regarding the treatment of ADD. My hopes were to return to Community College, this time with the will to succeed. I'm a 19 year old male weighing around 130 LB. After a few interesting talks between the two of us, then between him and my mother(who insisting in having a part in this), he recommended atomoxetine (Strattera) as a treatment. I was to start on 10 mg daily for 2 weeks, then increase to 25. Upon starting treatment, the 2nd night I woke up around 3AM (going to sleep at 11) with a horrible feeling of just, not being well. Like there was something horribly wrong with me, but all logic told me I was fine. A strange feeling, that I've only had once with this drug. During the 3rd week, now on 25 MG daily, I felt slight suicidal intentions, just thinking of what it would be like to kill myself, which wasn't entirely normal for me. These passed nearly 7 days after, approaching my 4th week on the drug. At first it kept me from sleeping, contrary to his advice of it helping my mild insomnia. After about a week this effect stopped. I noticed a few of the typical SSRI/SNRI side effects overall: increased heart rate, decreased sex drive plus pain after orgasm, hesitation urnitating, slight constipation, and early awakening. Which actually regulated my schedule more, and I definitely wouldn't call it a negative side effect. All of these seemed to decrease as time went on, fortunately, except the early awakening deal; which was still a nice touch. At school I felt much more focused, and for once actually felt like I wanted to complete my work. Instead of just drift off and daydream in class, not caring, I actually cared. As told by my doctor, it had slight off label effects as an anti-depressant. I believe I saw some of these in the initial increased suicidal intentions etc. and then in a slightly changed outlook on others. I no longer felt like I had to correct everyone when they did something wrong, or that I was being verbally attacked by people who used sarcasm. Overall Strattera does it's job with few side effects, and is definitely a requirement for putting me through school. I love going off of it just for increased sexual functioning, as well as returning my heart rate to a much more normal level. (instead of raising it by 20BPM) I can drink alcohol perfectly fine while on atomoxetine, and have combined my daily dose of Atomoxetine with between 90 and 190 MG of DXM. (only after doing large amounts of research with combining DXM + SNRI) If anything, the atomoxetine potentiates the effects of the DXM.

药物/ROA/剂量:诺佩普特,鼻内,6毫克. 我19岁,男性,体重约165磅,身高6英尺2英尺,全职上大学. 我对许多常见和未知的精神活性物质有经验. 我已经尝试了你能说的几乎每一种娱乐性物质 (还有一些你可能无法说的), 我已经尝试过一些更受欢迎的无otropics (modafanil,aniracetam,phenibut,尼古丁在口香糖/vape/香烟的形式) 和兴奋剂 (甲基,安非他命,和好的旧咖啡因). 我很高兴能尝试noopept, 背景:我的公寓大楼. 我在那天早些时候服用了1克Phenibut, 我在连续两三天大量吸食大麻后感到有点模糊. 我的室友正在做他的日常生意. 我在下午四点左右服用了两次 (3毫克) 喷射. 两分钟后,我什么都没有感觉到. 我和我的室友聊了一会儿. 他似乎很忙,所以我着窗外几分钟. 然后我意识到. 绝对的,完整的,压倒性的,宁静的清晰度. 我从窗外看着城市的天际线, 感到完全放松. 这确实是皮的感觉, 这更清洁,更有创造性,更有灵感. 我不知道如何描述, 除了说我觉得我脑海中的一切是清晰的, 我觉得我脑海中的一切都清晰,完美和地连接在一起. 我感觉到激光的焦点. 我再服用两次喷射 (3毫克). 在五分钟之内, 我完全参与了周围的一切. 感觉非常兴奋, 我去了公寓楼体育馆. 在电梯下楼, 我和一个很可爱的女孩有了一个有趣的对话. 我感觉到平常的自信和魅力, 我说的是普通的马克·吐温的东西. 我对这两分钟的谈话感到非常激动. 我从来没有感觉到这样的phenibut, 在任何剂量. 我做了一次有成效的炼 (在跑步机上跑了10分钟, 兴奋是甲基胺给我的, 推动, 冲动去做我通常不会做的事情. 炼并不容易, 激励是一个更好的词. 我也在身体上表现得更好, 我只是在很深的层面上理解了为什么我在炼, 为什么我必须尽我所能努力. 这是一个专注的. 我应该注意的是,我在炼的时候, 脑子在跑步 (或更确切地说, 快速走路 - - 我能够保留我大部分的想法). 我对我目前的关系有了小启示, 我在玩弄一些学术概念, 这很好. 很不幸的是,我的不寻常行为导致我背部肌肉受伤. 虽然很痛苦,但我还是完成了炼, 我从来没有见过他的父亲. 虽然通常我会发现自己没有什么可说的,但我也能找到有趣的谈话话题. 很快他们就去吃晚饭了, 我会说在3到4个小时内就消失了. 我在晚上9点左右再服用了一些, 或是我达到了一个高原, 我认为这将是一个极其有趣的nootropic. 我听说过口服途径的不同意见,但我听说过的几乎所有关于鼻内注射noopept的东西都是积极的. 这已被证明是一个非常好的"激励器", 我有一个伟大的时间. 所以请小心, 不要将其与任何强烈的兴奋剂或镇静剂混合. 其他: 发布时间:2016年8月25日 诺佩普特 (579):组合 (3),提高性能 (50),发光体验 (4),单独使用 (16)

SET: I’m 19, male, about 165 lbs, 6’2”, attending college full time. I am experienced with many psychoactive substances, common and obscure. Having tried almost every recreational substance you can name (and some that you probably can’t), I’m looking toward drugs that can benefit my life, career, and personality - as a result, I’ve been exploring nootropics. I have tried some of the more popular nootropics (modafanil, aniracetam, phenibut, nicotine in gum/vape/cigarette forms) and stimulants (methylphenidate, amphetamine, and good old caffeine). I am excited to try noopept and am in a reasonably calm and stable frame of mind. SETTING: My apartment building. I had taken 1 gram of Phenibut earlier in the day and a cup of tea prior to the noopept. I was feeling a little hazy after 2 or 3 days of consecutive, heavy marijuana consumption. My roommate was going about his daily business. REPORT: I took two squirts (3 mg) at around 4:00 PM. After two minutes, I felt nothing. I talk with my roommate for a moment. He seems busy, so I stare out the window for a couple more minutes. Then it hits me. Absolute, complete, overwhelming, serene clarity. I looked out my window at the city skyline and felt completely at ease. It was, indeed, the feeling of phenibut, but with a dimension all its own. It was so much cleaner, so much more creative, so inspired. I don’t quite know how to describe except by saying I felt like everything in my mind was crystal clear and perfectly, harmoniously connected. I felt like everything in my mind was crystal clear and perfectly, harmoniously connected. I felt laser focused. I take another two squirts (3 mg). Within 5 minutes I am completely engaged in everything around me. Feeling very excited, I went down to the apartment building gym. On the elevator down, a very cute girl and I had an engaging conversation. I felt the regular phenibut confidence and charm, but I also felt unbelievably witty. I’m talking regular Mark Twain stuff. I was on fire for this two minute conversation. I've never felt like this from phenibut, at any dosage. I had a productive workout (with an unplanned 10 minute run on the treadmill, just because I felt like it.) My workout was excellent, but perhaps “excited” isn’t the right word. Excitement is what methylphenidate gives me, the push, the urge to do things that I wouldn’t normally do. The workout wasn’t easier, and I would have probably done it anyway. Motivation is a better word. I also performed better physically, but not in the way a stimulant like ephedrine or methylphenidate would make me feel. I just understood, on a very deep level, the reasons I was working out, and why I had to give it every ounce of effort I could. It was focused. I should note that my mind was racing (or rather, speedwalking - I was able to retain most of the ideas I had) while I was working out. I had a small epiphany about my current relationship, I was toying with some academic concepts, and thinking very deeply about everything. It was nice. Unfortunately, my unusual resulted in me hurting a back muscle. It was painful, but I managed to finish the workout and go back to my apartment. There was my roommate - and his dad - who I had never met before. While typically I would find myself running out of things to say, I had no shortage of interesting conversational topics. They soon left to go to dinner, and I would say it wore off within 3 or 4 hours. I took some more around 9 PM, but it didn't seem to do much. Either I hit a plateau or there is a ceiling of how much this can effect you (or at least me). SUMMARY: I think this will be an extremely interesting nootropic. I have heard mixed things about the oral route of administration, but almost everything I’ve heard about intranasal noopept has been positive. This has proved to be an extremely good “motivator” and helps a lot with focus. I had a great time. It does seem to have some mild stimulant effects, so folks, please be careful and don’t mix it with any strong stimulants or sedatives.

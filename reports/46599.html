我有烟草,酒精和大麻滥用的历史. 我尝试了许多源和草药. 我在一年前买了一司的干燥的阿特罗帕杏仁根. 我唯一的尝试是吸了一小部分 (0.1克) . 我感觉到一些效果,非常轻,也不是真的舒适. 我忘记了,因为我不太可能暴露自己在Solanaceae的毒性. 我觉得自己被根号召了. 我觉得自己被吸引了,被迫使用它. 晚上早些时候, 我喝了一些卡图阿巴茶, 我碎了一小部分杏仁根, 大约1/2克, 我添加了一点点Sceletium tortuosum (0.1克或更少). 我点了火,开始吸烟. 很快,我感觉到一种效果, 我停下来看看发生了什么. 感觉好像有什么东西在压在我身上, ",只有3次吹气,有多强大!" 十分钟后, 我再次点燃了这支烟, 味道很强烈,有点奇怪, 但在接下来的四分之一小时内, 肌肉松和缓解疼痛的效果逐渐显现. 我感觉有点昏迷. 世界似乎有点威胁, 这可能是由于曼德拉哥拉旅行报告我在网上阅读! 吸烟,不吃,不喝, 这种效应与Amanita muscaria的效应有共同之处, 我有这样的想法:"如果曼德拉哥拉和阿曼尼塔同时被捕获, 这次我没有服用阿曼尼塔, 我不知道是什么让我这么想. 我终于决定, 杏仁是一种"邪恶的双胞胎"阿曼尼塔. 由于使用了合理的数量, 我没有视觉或奇怪的梦想. 我感觉到一种中等强度的麻醉作用. 奇怪的是现实似乎有点不同于正常, 我有时会有偏执的想法, 感觉超自然的世界与现实并存, 很容易记住我在某种药物或电影或书籍的影响下, 没有 另一种感觉是,我几乎唤醒了超自然的东西. 我不知道这些想法是否受到我在网上读到的神秘报告的影响. 我睡得很好. 我醒来时有点头. 这不是问题. 没有其他后果, 除了轻而不舒服的"超自然"感觉. 我认为这种植物对我来说并不像其他植物那样善良. 也许它喜欢一些人,但我不知道它是否可以被视为盟友. 这不是一个有趣的药物, 但有趣. 我可能会再试一次,但要更加小心. 其他:

The Evil Twin Mandrake, Cannabis, & Sceletium tortuosum by User Citation:   User. "The Evil Twin: An Experience with Mandrake, Cannabis, & Sceletium tortuosum (exp46599)". Erowid.org . Jun 14, 2006. erowid.org/exp/46599 DOSE: oral Catuaba (tea) 2 joints/cigs smoked Cannabis (plant material) 0.5 g smoked Mandrake (ground / crushed) 0.1 g smoked Sceletium tortuosum (roots) BODY WEIGHT: 70 kg I have an history of tobacco, alcohol and Cannabis abuse. I tried many entheogens and herbal highs. I had bought an ounce of Atropa mandragora dried root one year ago. The only try I gave to it was to smoke a very little amount (0.1 g ?) with some pot. I felt some effect, very light and nor really comfortable. I forgot about it, as I was not likely to expose myself to the toxicity of Solanaceae. But some days ago, while watching TV around 11pm, I felt 'called by the root'. It was all in my head, but I felt attracted, pushed to use it. Earlier that day, I had drunk some catuaba tea and smoked 2 little MJ sticks in the beginning of the evening. I crushed a little amount of mandragora root, something like 1/2 gram and mixed it with good quality pot. I added a little pinch of Sceletium tortuosum (0.1 gram or less). I lit my joint and started smoking. Very soon, I felt an effect which was not from the other herbs. Having smoked 1/4 of the joint only, I stopped just to see what was setting on. It was like something was weighting on me, with some kind of vertigo. 'Whao, with just 3 puffs ? how powerful !'. 10 minutes later , I lit the joint again, and managed to smoke it slowly, over the course of 10 or 15 minutes. The flavor was strong, a bit weird with an 'animal' quality. The effects were not really comfortable, but evolved over the next 1/4 of hour toward a mix of muscular relaxation and a bit of analgesia. I felt a bit stuporous. The world seemed a bit threatening, which was probably caused by the mandragora trip reports I red online ! My expectations were influenced by those online accounts, and so was my decision to smoke it and not to eat/drink it, and to use a reasonable amount. The effects had something common with those of Amanita muscaria, but different at the same time, like inverted. I had that thought : 'if mandragora and Amanita are taken at the same time, it forces the Amanita to act as male in the couple, while it is normally female as well as male and not highly sexually involved.' I didnt take any Amanita this time, and I don't know what led me to think about it. I finally decided that mandragora was some kind of an 'evil twin' of Amanita muscaria. Due to the reasonable amount used, I had no visuals or weird dreams. I felt a medium strong narcotic effect. What was weird is that reality seemed to be a bit different from normal, in a lightly threatening way. When I'm stoned, I have sometimes paranoid thoughts, feelings of a supernatural world coexisting with reality, and things like that. It is very easy to remember that I'm under the influence of a drug or of a movie or a book, with mandragora,it was not : I had no way to think that it was not true. W Worse: this feeling stayed with me the next day, just like if I had nearly awaken something supernatural. I don't know if those thoughts we influenced by those mystical reports I've read online. I slept very well. I woke up lightly hung over. It was not a problem. No other after effect, except the light but uncomfortable 'supernatural' feeling. I think that this plant is not as benevolent as some other entheogens can be to me. Maybe it likes some poeple, but I dont know if it can be considered as an ally. Not a fun drug, but interesting. I'll probably try it again, but even more carefully.

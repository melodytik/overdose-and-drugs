I have done at least 50 or so hits of acid which is my drug of choice, and on top of that, I have tried mushrooms, ketamine, DXM, hydrocodone, suboxone, oxycodone, codeine, amphetamine, methamphetamine, methylphenidate, klonopin, xanax, salvia, K2, 2-cb, MDMA, MDA, DOb, Datura, Benadryl, and probably a few other things I forgot before this experience. I was simply dosing a small sample of local shrooms, and having gone to ski for the weekend, I was excited to get away. School had been fucked, as somebody's mother reported me for selling an e-cigarette when I quit smoking, and I had told the kid before not to try hard drugs, but his mom still thought I was the asshole and reported me. Luckily I had been cautious not to sell the kid anything else so I'm probably not in trouble, but Thursday, when I had been told the news by the kid, and Friday, were ridiculously anxiety ridden. Planning for the trip Friday I was really worried that I wouldn't have the greatest of times. What follows was a cool experience nonetheless. 0:00 I ate the mushrooms dry at 3:15, while I had just smoked a bowl and a cig before coming in. I had really bad dry mouth and almost had to heave the shrooms out, but I wasn't about to waste my happy herb, and finished with a cup of orange juice which I so badly needed. I listened to some peaceful cloud rap and this psychedelic orchestral indie-rock. It always adds to my persistent dreamy experience of any good hallucinogen, which I really look forward to. It's not in any regards an addictive lust, but one of respect of the chemicals, now that I have tried plenty and still come back to my good old friends acid and mushrooms. 0:20 My friend picks me up for the weekend and I smoke before I leave. I end up forgetting my charger, but I think I should blame the over-excited kid that I was, looking forward to a clean and safe trip, and some skiing for the end of the year. Beginning the trip I have a cig, and start to feel a bit of a body high settling in, but no visuals whatsoever. We listen to some good music, some which I still have stuck in my head, and since I was vibing out during the beginning of the road trip, I was too distracted to ask for song names. Bummer. 0:45 Had a few more cigs as I waited for the mushrooms to kick in completely. I had one small illusion of my vision, in which I saw a car dealship sign that seemed to melt to the ground in particular spots, making clear attributes of a face. I laughed, told my friend, and we moved on after he kinda roasted me for being stupid. 1:00 I start feeling pretty nice, and we go to a gas station. I chill in the car as he runs inside, and I felt like people saw how relaxed and happy I was, laying upwards across my seat with a half-grin. I got a little scared when I saw a cop, but I quickly transitioned to not caring. We arrive at a friends house. 1:15 After smoking 2 bowls, we left to head to the mountain resort, where we met up with a security and smoked. This guy was super chill and the mountain was closed. Snow started to fall, and we had roughly 2 inches so far. We smoked a lot, packing like 5-7 bowls between 3 people. My friend who drove me up drank lean, and sat next to me in the back of the truck. We kept laughing and saying stupid, incoherent sentences to get the others and ourselves laughing. 1:30 I reach into a utility basket and grab a traffic light, that strobes when you click the button twice.

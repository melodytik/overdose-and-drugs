我最近完成了一系列TFMPP/BZP组合的实验, 虽然我从来没有使用过MDMA,我自己也无法准确地估计它与MDMA有多么相似或不同;然而,在与我一位曾使用过MDMA的亲密朋友的讨论中,他表示我描述的感受和情绪与那种受人喜爱的药物非常相似. 如果想尝试这种组合, 1) 减少剂量是一个错误. 时间. 这是一个很大的诱惑, 我已经犯了几次重复剂量的错误, 我被迫在所前腰度过那美好的一天的剩余时间. 第二次,TFMPP令人高兴的效果完全消失了, 通常是有趣的, 但不是我当时想要的. 2) 在肚子充满的时候服用药物. 我是真的. 这将大大减少体质的痛苦, 你会想跳舞,跳跃或散步, 能继续前进的能量会减轻第二天的宿醉. 我试过BZP和TFMPP的剂量, 100毫克BZP与50毫克TFMPP (保持2:1的比例, 你们中的一些人可能还记得从文献中) 是我最喜欢的, 我的建议是:不要让总重量超过您的最大可容忍BZP剂量. 例如,如果200毫克BZP对你来说是不舒服的,不要试图混合100毫克BZP与100毫克TFMPP. 确定BZP的剂量要容易得多, 所以找到单独使用BZP的上限是一个很好的第一步. 也不要让你的TFMPP超过你的BZP. 我没有办法解释, 但每次我这样做, 没有BZP的TFMPP没有什么值得注意. 不要浪费你的药物. 我有一种直觉, 皮佩拉的剂量至少有点依赖体重. 我朋友喜欢每次服用200毫克. 我几乎不能处理这样的剂量, 远远更喜欢100毫克. 不要依靠朋友勃或互联网的经验报告来确定剂量. 像其他药物一样, 开始低,然后慢慢上升. 4) 如果您使用的是纯粉末,最有可能是这样,请正确包装您的囊. 这意味着:拿一个毫克的尺度. 眼球是不可接受的. (有便宜的尺寸可以达到2毫克,这足够接近这些目的. 提前准备囊是个好主意. 粉末的味道可能会加剧你可能感受到的身体痛苦. 5) TFMPP/BZP的副作用包括体力困扰,食欲丧失,口腔干燥,尿频繁,出汗增加,体温波动,脉增加. 对于大多数皮佩拉使用者来说,口腔干燥是最大的危险. 随着皮佩拉辛的使用经历的延续,您的身体会尝试清除所有多余的液体以弥补口腔干燥. 如果这种情况继续下去, 可能会导致低血症, 解决这个问题的办法是通过饮用同位素饮料 (如Gatorade) 或仅仅用食盐和水来保持血中的度在可接受的水平. 6) 与MDMA一样,使用5-HTP是个好主意. 这将防止TFMPP的效果过早消失. 此外,在旅行期间定期服用单个5-HTP囊将有助于增强上述效果. 最后,在睡前服用两种药物会帮助你入睡, 七) 是的,你会有一些宿醉. 但服用过高的剂量,再服用,喝过多的水,或没有食物几天, 肯定会让你无法爬出床几天. 我从经验中知道. 其他: TFMPP (100), BZP (101), - 5-HTP (196): 追溯 / 摘要 (11), 不适用 (38)

Series of TFMPP & BZP Experiments TFMPP, BZP & 5-HTP by zed ericksson Citation:   zed ericksson. "Series of TFMPP & BZP Experiments: An Experience with TFMPP, BZP & 5-HTP (exp12698)". Erowid.org . Jun 19, 2002. erowid.org/exp/12698 DOSE: oral BZP oral TFMPP BODY WEIGHT: 125 lb I've recently completed a series of experiments with the TFMPP/BZP combination, which has gained notoriety in certain Internet drug communities lately as a cheap and legal alternative to MDMA. Having never done MDMA, I cannot myself accurately gauge how like or unlike to MDMA it truly is; however, in discussions with a close friend of mine who -has- done MDMA, he indicated that the feelings and emotions I described were much like that well-loved drug. Over the course of these experiments, I discovered several things one should take stock of if planning to try this combination. 1) Redosing is a mistake. Period. It is a great temptation as one is coming down and pining for the euphoria of hours past, but it simply does not work. I've made the mistake of redosing a handful of times, and my experiences were sufficient to dissuade me from trying again. The first time rendered me violently ill, and I was left to spend the remainder of the otherwise-gorgeous day crouched before the toilet. The second time, the euphoric effects of TFMPP fled me entirely, and I was left languishing alone in the speedy realm of BZP for hours at a time. Normally fun, but not what I was looking for at the time. 2) Take the drugs on a full stomach. I mean it. It will drastically reduce the somatic distress that will surely result from 100-200mg (or more) of raw chemical hitting your stomach. In addition, your will and ability to consume food are reduced by these psychoactive piperazines, and you will want to dance or jump or go for a walk while you're on them. Having energy to go on will diminish the next day's hangover. 3) I tried dosages of BZP and TFMPP ranging from 50-100mg each. (At dosages lower than 50mg, I conjecture the side effects will be stronger than the desired effects.) 100mg BZP to 50mg TFMPP (keeping the 2:1 ratio some of you might remember from the literature) was my favorite, but 50mg BZP to 50mg TFMPP provided a delightful light roll. My advice is this: Don't let the total combined weight exceed your maximum tolerable BZP dose. For example, if 200mg of BZP is uncomfortable for you, don't try to mix 100mg BZP with 100mg TFMPP. BZP doses are much easier to determine, so finding your upper limit on BZP alone is a good first step. Also, do not let your TFMPP exceed your BZP. I can propose no mechanism to explain it, but every time I did so, I became ill. TFMPP without BZP does nothing of note. Don't waste your drug. I have a hunch that piperazine doses are at least somewhat weight-dependant. When doing BZP alone, my friend prefers 200mg each time. I can barely handle such a dose, far preferring 100mg. Don't rely on your friend Bob or Internet experience reports for dosages. As with other drugs, start low and work your way up. 4) If you're working from pure powder, as most probably will be, pack your capsules properly. This means: Get a milligram scale. Eyeballing is not acceptable. (There -are- cheap scales available that are good to 2mg, which is close enough for these purposes.) It's a good idea to prepare caps in advance of the planned dose. Weighing out powder and carefully pouring it into caps is a stressful experience, and the smell of the powder is likely to add to any somatic distress you might feel. 5) Side effects of TFMPP/BZP include somatic distress, loss of appetite, dry mouth, frequent urination, increased sweating, body temperature fluctuations, and increased pulse. The dry mouth poses the biggest danger to the majority of piperazine users. As the piperazine experience wears on, frequent urination will set in as your body tries to dispose of all the excess liquid you dump in to offset the dry mouth. If this situation continues unabated, it can lead to hyponatremia, the condition that has already been noted to have claimed the lives of several MDMA users. The solution to this problem is to keep the sodium concentration of the blood at an acceptable level by drinking isotonic beverages (like Gatorade) or merely by consuming table salt with your water. 6) As with MDMA, preloading with 5-HTP is a good idea. It will prevent the effects of the TFMPP from tapering off prematurely. In addition, popping a single 5-HTP capsule regularly during the trip will help to boost said effects. Finally, taking two before bed will help you get to sleep, and take some edge of the dreaded hangover. 7) Yes, you -will- have some hangover. If you do everything right, it will be nothing more than a slight headache; but dosing too high, redosing, drinking too much water, or going for days without food is guaranteed to leave you unable to crawl out of bed for a couple days. I know from experience.

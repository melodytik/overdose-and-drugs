我去看医生,因为胸部有些不舒服, 他们给我很多药物送回家. 我没有提到我使用作为本学期学校的小'提升'... 因为我知道不利的反应. 我确实想到混合DXM和Albuterol的效果可能会很糟糕, 这就是发生了什么: 在服用药物20分钟后, 一切变得非常明亮. 在接下来的一个小时内, 我不得不去参加创业课, 这次旅行很有趣, 但我不推它为一个高级大学班级. 也许是为了克服无聊, 但这是一个我需要专注的课. 整个路程, 我觉得我在飞. 我的整个身体感觉轻松. 我一到上课, 就完全无法集中注意力. 我一直跳动我的腿和玩东西. 我知道我的同学们已经很烦了. 问题在于我不想起床离开. 我觉得完全放松, 但紧张. 我觉得我的身体是麻木的, 但我的思想需要保持一切在运动. 腿跳动的振动让我更加放松. 我知道我应该离开,但没有. 两个小时的课程中, 我下来了. 我很饿,但我没有吃饭的精力. 课后我回到宿舍, 昏倒了. 这就是它. 我很可能会在没有上课的时候, 再试一次. 这是一种很棒的感觉. 其他: DXM (22),药物 - 阿尔布特罗尔 (241),埃弗德林 (23):组合 (3),大组 (10+) (19)

Feb 18, 2008. erowid.org/exp/56089 DOSE: 10 mg oral DXM 50 mg oral Ephedrine 2 hits inhaled Pharms - Albuterol BODY WEIGHT: 135 lb I went to the doctor for some chest discomfort and they sent me home with many drugs. I happened to leave out that I've been using ephedrine as a little 'pick-me-up' for school this semester...because I know the adverse response. It did occur to me that it could be bad to mix the ephedrine with DXM and Albuterol, seeing how the effects are somewhat bipolar. Here's what happened: About twenty minutes after taking it, everything got very bright. The effects intensified over the next hour or so. I had to go to my Entrepreneurship class...bad idea. The trip was fun, but I wouldn't recommend it for an upper-division college class. Maybe a G.E. to get through the boredom, but this is a class I need to focus in. The whole way there, I felt like I was flying. My entire body felt light. Once I got to class, I couldn't focus at all. I kept bouncing my leg and playing with things. I could tell my fellow classmates were getting annoyed. The problem was that I didn't want to get up and leave. I felt completely relaxed, yet jittery. It was like my body was numb, but my my mind needed to keep everything moving. The vibrations from my leg bouncing relaxed me even more. I knew I should've left, but didn't. Half way through the two-hour class, I came down. I had a fairly bad headache and was STARVING but I didn't have the energy to eat. After class I went back to my dorm and passed out. That's pretty much it. I will most likely try this again for recreation when I don't have classes to go to. It was a great feeling.

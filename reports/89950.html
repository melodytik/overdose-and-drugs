I feel more energetic than usual with pure Molly, and I haven’t had a redbull which is unusual for me. He tells me to take 2 more RollON. I do. T-2.5 Hours: I remember why they call this drug ecstasy. It is as if the reverberations of the music are drawing out some sacred primal spirit inside of me... My body feels electric and I am dancing harder than I EVER remember. I have a supernova engine boiler-room of energy in my stomach, and the feeling keeps coming in waves. I still have some good serotonin vibes going, but the anxiety is gone. I’m not particularly sleepy, and it doesn’t bother me that we are STILL lingering around downtown trying to decide what to do. T-Next Morning: I sleep for a good 7 hours and I wake up like a f*ing CHAMPION! Never before have I felt good enough to get out of bed the next day, let alone go for a RUN! I finally understand what my buddy was getting at.

下面是四年前发生的两起连续审判的笔记. 我给了40毫克, 分成以下剂量. 我是一位经验丰富的类真菌探险家, 1925年:摄入了15毫克的4-HO-DET 1945 年: 略微偏离基线 2005年:定义 +1. 目前还没有视觉, 但有特殊的视觉和音频感知. 轻微的恶心,不值得担心 (在20分钟内过去了). 2045年:与之前的录音相比,有所进一步发展. 没有视觉. 认知能力仍然很好. 有点摇摇欲. 2115:大麻吸烟大约10分钟前,微弱的CEV,微妙但移动缓慢的OEV (这两种不同于psilocybin). 一些轻微的听觉幻觉, 定义一般感知中的变化. +2 2130:从上述记录没有太大的进展,着一个特定的模式. 9140:音乐发生了重大变化. 似乎可以控制目前的情绪. 听起来比平时更好. (姆小矮人!) 2210:吃了,味道似乎没有改变. 绝对没有厌食症. 2225:视觉效果稍微不太突出,不确定是大麻或4-HO-DET (可能是两者兼而有之) 造成的. 2300:感觉接近基线. 还有一些小的OEV (地毯爬行,文本扭曲等) 和色的CEV. 2335:视觉已经消失了,现在只是一个温和的"石头". 0005:与之前的录音相比,没有太多的变化,更少的石头. 0100:基本上是基线. +/-整体上是一个很好的夜晚,期待尝试25毫克. 试验二 摄入25毫克的4-HO-DET 1855年:绝对偏离基线,有点. 1905年:快速到来 感觉很奇怪,非常轻微的CEVs. 1925年:有更多的发展. 轻微的"地毯爬行"和轻微的CEV. 头部感觉比使用psiloc更清晰. 1950年:轻微的视觉扭曲. 除非专注于它,否则不会引起注意. 虽然它没有像psiloc (毒药) 一样具有视觉,有趣,改变思想或内省性, 2005年: 感觉到一个美好的宁静. 不是高兴, 而是一种积极的内在温暖. 可能在高峰期. 视觉上类似于psilocybin, 但几乎更慢, 较少占优势. 这种不太强烈的感觉可能是个很好的派对药物, 像我说的, 远远没有像psiloc ((yb) in那样自我审视. 吸食大麻2小时前,视觉和其他心理效应略有加剧 (积极). 可能开始下降. 2300: 下降, 视觉接近不明显. 仍然可以感受到精神影响. 00:25 几乎是基线. 没有视觉. 只有一个温和的"石头"留下来. 0200:基本上是低音线, 虽然经验不如我所希望. 如果我能再试一次我会去35-40毫克, 看看它是如何坐着. 其他: 4-HO-DET (365):一般性 (1),未知背景 (20)

Nov 12, 2010. erowid.org/exp/80904 DOSE: 25 mg oral 4-HO-DET (powder / crystals) Below are the notes from two successive trials that occurred somewhere around four years ago. The material was a fluffy brown powder, I was given 40mg and split it into the following dosages. I am an experienced explorer of psilocybian fungi, and so there are numerous comparisons to this throughout the report. 1925: ~15mg 4-HO-DET ingested 1945: Very slightly off baseline 2005: Definate +1. No visuals yet, but peculiar perception of vision and audio. Slight nausea, not worth any concern. (passed within 20 minutes). 2045: A little further developed from previous recording. No visuals. Cognitive abilites still clear. A little shakey. 2115: Cannabis smoked some 10 minutes earlier, faint CEVs, subtle but slow moving OEVs (both unlike Psilocybin). Some slight auditory hallucination, repetitive psychedelic sounds with a quiet ring. Definate change in general perception. +2 2130: Not much progression from above recording, 'tranced out' staring at a particular pattern. 9140: Music is significantly altered. Seems to have some control over current emotion. Sounds better than usual. (Yay Ohm Gnome!). 2210: Ate, taste seemed unaltered. Definately no anorexic effects. 2225: Visuals slightly less prominent, not sure whether it is more the Cannabis wearing off or the 4-HO-DET (probably both). 2300: Feeling close to baseline. Still some minor OEVs (carpet crawling, text warping etc) and fading CEVs. 2335: Visuals gone, just a mild 'stone' now. 0005: Little development from previous recording, a little less 'stone'. 0100: Pretty much baseline. +/- Has been a nice night overall, look forward to trying at 25mg. TRIAL 2 1830: 25mg 4-HO-DET ingested 1855: Definately off baseline, a little shakey. 1905: Coming up fast. Feeling quite weird, very mild CEVs. 1925: A little more development. Slight 'carpet crawling' and mild CEVs. Head feels alot clearer than with psiloc(yb)in. +2 1950: Mild visual distortion. Not noticeable unless concentrated on. Not as visual, interesting, mind-altering or introspective as psiloc(yb)in - yet there is a similar feel. 2005: Feeling of nice stillness. Not euphoria, but some kind of positive inner warmth. Probably at peak. Visuals similar to psilocybin but almost slower and less predominant. Nice feeling, not too strong, may be a good party drug as it is much less demanding on the mind than psilocybin. As mentioned, nowhere near as introspective as psiloc(yb)in. 2225: Cannabis smoked two hours earlier, slightly intensified visuals and the other mental effects (positive). Possible starting to come down. 2300: Coming down, visuals close to non-apparent. Can still feel mental effects. 00:25: Almost baseline. No visuals. Only a mild 'stone' remains. 0200: Pretty much bassline, +\-. Nice experience, although again not as strong as I was hoping for. If I get to try it again I'd go 35-40mg and see how that sits.

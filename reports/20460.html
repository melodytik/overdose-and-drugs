卖给我们的人从朋友那里得到了, 我们想,为什么不尝试呢? 这种药物价格较低, 没有可找到的胺. 我记得它在瓶子里, 但里面没有液体, 只是一个大白色的 (或可能是黄色的) 石头在瓶子里, 我记得我抽了一点,当它第一次进来时, 感觉很好, 几乎像狂喜的开始, 在此之后,我什么都不记得... 我也在向朋友走近,用机器人声音告诉他我是机器人,我也每隔5分钟就重复地走近他,说',勃,我不知道你在这里,你什么时候来这里? 再一次,我不记得任何这. 第二次经验: 我决定要记住那段经历是什么样子的,所以我买了一些,几天后我在我的房间里尝试了. 我第二天醒来时完全没有回忆夜晚的... 我觉得它不再是值得说服的药物,所以我摆脱了它. 现在我们要做些什么? 在互联网上查看, 似乎人们使用这种药物. 我想知道为什么? 也许我做得太多了? 也许它是为了用在非常小的剂量. 根据朋友们对我说的, 这次经历似乎与天使尘埃的高有关, 我不认为我会再尝试Telazol. 保持安全,保持智慧 在使用药物之前, 首先要研究药物... 药品 - - 泰泰胺 (167):第一次 (2),小组 (2-9) (17)

Jun 8, 2006. erowid.org/exp/20460 DOSE: insufflated Pharms - Tiletamine (ground / crushed) BODY WEIGHT: 130 lb 1st experience: I came across this stuff which was described to me as 'Doggy K' The person selling it to us had gotten it from a friend who had worked in a animal hospital. We figured why not try it? It was being offered at a cheaper price and there was no Ketamine to be found. I remember it was in a bottle but there was no liquid inside just a large white (or could have been yellowish) rock inside the bottle..after breaking it up a little into a chunky powder it seemed that the more I cut it up the more it turned sticky and began to clump more, so we left it at that and put some small lines out to test. I remember snorting a little and when it first kicked in it felt good, almost like an onset of Ecstacy but nothing more, though it felt nice, my aim was to go into a K Hole so I put out another line and snorted it. After this I do not remeber ANYTHING...nothing at all After it was all over, My friends who had stayed sober were telling me that I was being very noisy, I was jumping off the couch thinking I was a bird, I was also going up to my friend and telling him in a robotic voice that I was a robot, I also repeatedly approached him every 5 minutes saying 'hey whats up Bob I didnt know you were here, when did you get here.?.... Again, I Do Not Remember any of this. 2nd experience: That fact that i had a Total Blackout of the night really bothered me. i was determined to remeber what the experience was like. so i got some more and a few nights later I tryed it in my room. I woke up the next day With absolutley no recolection of the night.. I didnt think it was a drug worth persuing anymore so i got rid of it. --------------------- Looking it up on the internet it seems people use this drug. I wonder why? Maybe i just did to much? Maybe it is meant to be used in very small doses.? From what my friends told me of the night..the experience seems to be more closely related to an Angel Dust high then it is to the Ketamine experience. I dont think i will ever try Telazol again. Be Safe people.Be Smart Research your drugs before you do them....

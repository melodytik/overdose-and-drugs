经历了相当艰难的一段时间后, 我开始注意到我的整体心理状态发生了一些变化. 我经历了轻微的偏执, 以及类似的焦虑. 在寻求"专业人士"的建议后, 我获得了一份Effexor XR处方, 起始剂量为37. 5毫克. 几天后,37.5毫克剂量似乎没有太大作用. 我在没有医生的批准的情况下增加了剂量. 我现在早上服用75毫克, 我在增加剂量的那一天, 我注意到我的精神状态有明显的变化. 我感受到一种令人惊叹的高兴感, 伴随着一种黑暗的底色, 我还注意到效应剂几乎完全扩展了我的瞳孔在正常到微弱的光线下. 我发现这很有趣. 在这个时刻, 我认为Effexor是对我来说的答案. 然而, 副作用开始显现. 我开始难以入睡. 晚上11点半睡觉, 但直到凌晨3点4分才睡觉, 我在服用药物的时间里一直这样. 我早上醒来时会昏昏欲睡, 想再睡5个小时, 我发现咖啡因和Effexor是相当协同作用的. 效应剂给我带来一种可控的梦幻般的快乐状态, 咖啡因增加了身体的刺激, 我很快发现自己在滥用效应剂. 我发现如果我打开囊, 把里面的小球直接扔到我的上, 我很快就从一个常规的剂量计划, 打开一个150毫克的囊在我的tongue每当我觉得. 医生对我的手稿进行了大量补充. 在心理上我已经崩了. 我除了对自己, 其他任何事都不感兴趣了. 我辞去了我的工作. 我不再上学了. 我常常开车去附近的咖啡店, 坐下喝咖啡, 抽烟. 我有时会写作,阅读或绘画. 我知道这听起来很正常, 在阅读一本书时, 我会在包含 (当时) 某种隐藏的意义或信息的单词或短语上划圈. 我会把它们连接起来, 并将它们与我目前的生活情况联系起来. 很快, 几乎所有我拥有的书都被画得如此糟糕, 我的绘画和写作也同样奇怪. 在这个时候,效果已经显著改变. 这很难解释. 我最好的表达方式是:高兴感和体力负荷似乎融合, 这会在我的身体中引起共, 我对这种感觉感到非常着迷. 我变得非常疏远. 由于某种原因, 我决定忽略这一事实, 我忽略了自己经常在自言自语. 我忽略了除了效果剂和咖啡之外几乎不再吃任何东西的事实. 但这将会发生变化. 最后,当权力发现我在1月份服用了3个月的Effezor时, 医生在电话中提到 (相当冷静地) 可能会出现戒断症状, 虽然我降低了我的剂量我觉得'正常'. 没有高兴感或心跳,没有,只是我认为正常的最低状态. 我认为我跑完之后, 没有戒断症状. 我感到震惊. 字面上的意思. 我在最后一两天服用药物后, 这次的偏执更严重 (如连续5天吸毒) 并伴随着负面的暴力情绪波动. 最重要的是偶尔的幻觉. 但这只是一个开始. 我开始受到这些'震惊'. 感觉好像我刚刚把手指塞进灯座里. 最糟糕的是,每分钟会发生几次. 另一个难以解释的感觉. 这种"电"源自我的脑后, 这种情绪通常是愤怒. 虽然听起来很愚蠢,但我实际上正经历着悲伤,愤怒,愤怒和偏执的"冲击". 在心理上这是一种折磨. 我发现自己一直在家里漫步. 我在随机时间随机地敲打东西, 每天晚上都有梦. 没有食欲. 汗水,紧张,疼痛. 我很难说话. 形成单词需要我不想付出的努力. 我相信你已经明白了. 有一件比这一切更糟糕的事情. 我当时的恐惧的消极自我审视状态. 我脑子里充满了我在生活中搞的每一件事情的幻象. 虽然这些记忆总是对我不利. 这似乎几乎是业余的. 这完全是Effexor给我的感觉的相反, 我绝对不希望任何人有这样的经历. 如果有读到这篇文章的人经历过我经历过的, 我知道有些人因为害怕副作用而害怕停车. 我只能希望那些想戒烟的人, 想起那些日子,让我肚子恶心. 我觉得自己很幸运, 否则...好吧...我甚至无法想象后果. 最后一个词: 不要使用Effexor. 至少不是以我的方式. 只有在药片表面的光泽消失之后, 你才会意识到底有什么梦. 其他: 药品 - 文拉法辛 (191):回顾 / 摘要 (11),健康问题 (27),过量服用 (29),宿醉 / 几天后 (46),旅行后的问题 (8),困难经历 (5),各种 (28)

Separation From Self Venlafaxine (Effexor XR) by Anonymous Citation:   Anonymous. "Separation From Self: An Experience with Venlafaxine (Effexor XR) (exp35190)". Erowid.org . Nov 16, 2004. erowid.org/exp/35190 DOSE: oral Pharms - Venlafaxine (daily) BODY WEIGHT: 180 lb I consider myself to be a relatively sane and rational person. At least now. However, after faring a rather difficult time in my life I started to notice a few changes in my overall mental state. I was experiencing slight to mild paranoia, along with anxiety of a similar degree. After seeking the advice of a 'professional', I aquired a prescription of Effexor XR with a starting dose of 37.5 mg. After a few days, the 37.5 mg dose didn't seem to do much. In an amazing feat of stupidity I took it upon myself to up my dose without a doctor's approval. I was now taking 75mg in the morning and 75mg in the evening. The day I upped my dose I noted a VERY distinct change in my mindstate. I had an overwhelming feeling of euphoria, coupled with a dark undertone that I wasn't able to put my finger on...yet. I also noticed that the effexor was almost fully dilating my pupils in regular to dim light. I found it to be quite amusing. At this point I thought that effexor was the answer for me. However, the side effects were starting to rear their ugly heads. I began having difficulty sleeping. Going to bed at 11:30pm but not falling asleep till 3-4am, then waking up at 7am. This continued for the duration of the time I took the drug. I would wake up in the morning groggy and wanting to sleep for another 5 hours, but after taking effexor and a strong cup of coffee I was good to go. I found that caffiene and effexor were quite synergistic. The effexor produced a very controllable state of dreamy euphoria for me, along with a body load not unlike a medium sized dose of MDA. The caffiene increased the body stimulation, and helped clear the 'foggy euphoria' a little. I quickly found myself abusing the effexor. I found that if i opened the capsules and dumped the little balls inside directly onto my tounge, the effects became MUCH more pronounced. I very quickly went from a regular dosing schedule to Just opening a 150mg capsule on my tounge whenever I felt like. My doctor was quite generous with the refills on my script. Psychologicly I was breaking down. I lost all intrest in anything but myself. I quit my job. I quit going to school. Most days I would drive to a nearby coffeeshop and just sit drinking coffee and smoking profusely. Sometimes I would write, read, or draw. I know that sounds pretty normal, but the manner in which I went about these activities was not. While reading a book I would circle words or phrases that held (at the time) some sort of hidden meaning or message. Throughout the book I would connect them and relate them to my current situation in life. After a short while, pretty much all of the books I owned were drawn on so badly that they are hardly worth reading now. My drawing and writing were equally as bizarre. At this point the effect had changed notably. It's really hard to explain. The best way I can put it is: The euphoria and body load seemed to MERGE and increase tenfold. It would resonate throughout my body to the very core of my bone structure. I was absolutely enthralled with this feeling. I became very disassociative. For some reason I decided to ignore the fact that my day-to-day heartrate had increased exponentially. I ignored the fact that I was frequently mumbling to myself. I ignored the fact that I had almost stopped eating anything except effexor and coffee. But that was about to change. Finally, the day came when the the powers that be found out I took 3 months worth of effezor in 1, and my supply was cut off. Over the phone, the doc mentioned (rather nonchalantly) that there *may* be some withdrawl symptoms and to step down my dose gradually with what I had left. While I stepped down my dose I felt 'normal'. No euphoria or heartbeat, no mumbling, just what I thought to be a normal mindstate. I assumed that after I ran out that there would be no withdrawl symptoms. I was in for a shock. Literally. A day or two after my last pill I quickly lapsed back into a paranoid mindstate. This time, the paranoia was MUCH MUCH worse (like smoking meth for 5 days on end) and it was coupled with VIOLENT mood swings of a negative nature. Top that off with the occassional delusion. But, of course, that was just the beginning. I started getting these 'shocks'. It felt like I had just stuck my finger in a light socket. At it's worst, it would happen a couple times a minute. Yet another difficult to explain feeling. The 'electricty' originated from the back of my head...often times accompanied with an extreme emotion. Usually this emotion was rage. Silly as it may sound, i was experiencing literal 'shocks' of sadness, rage, anger, and paranoia. Psychologically it was torture. I found myself pacing the house at all times. I was punching random things at random times, and felt on the verge of exploding 24/7. Nightmares every night. No appetite. Sweaty, nervous, achy. It was difficult to talk. Forming words took effort that I didnt want to exert. I'm sure you get the point. There was one thing that was worse than all of this combined. The terrifyingly negative introspective mindstate I was in. My mind was filled with visions of every little thing I had messed up in my life. It was mostly focused on certain past relationships that were long gone....yet somehow those memories turned against me. It seemed almost karmic. It was the exact reverse of how effexor made me feel when I took it.....plus interest. I would never wish this experience upon anyone. If anybody who is reading this is/has gone through what I have, you have my respect. I know a few people who are afraid to stop for fear of the side effects. I can only hope that those who want to stop will, with help from those who care, never have to pick up another pill again. Thinking back on those days sickens my stomach. I feel fortunate enough to have been forced to stop. Otherwise....well...I can't even imagine the consequences. Final word: DO NOT take effexor. At least not in the way I did. Once the lustre on the surface of the pill has worn away, only then will you finally realize the nightmare that is underneath.

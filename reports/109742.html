我和一个朋友一起服用了4-Aco-DMT与4-Aco-MET的组合, 两名男性,每人约170磅, 两人都在过去10年中进行了广泛的精神活性研究. 上一次旅行是在一周前服用40毫克的4-HO-EPT. 在空腹. 在家里, 乐器已经准备好了, 画画站也准备好了. 剂量 (用适量毫克尺度测量,含有+/- 1mg):每人30mg的4-Aco-DMT和20mg的4-Aco-MET,分别装入囊. 经验: 我们同时服用两种囊. 开始冥想. 非常轻松的警报,闭着眼睛冥想, 我放上大自然的声音 - - 海洋,雨等高质量的录音 - - 听起来很棒, 我感觉有些镇静, 这对我来说是4-Aco-DMT的典型. 我不能说我是否热或冷. 身体感到. 没有太多的混乱,没有可怕的感觉. 我往往会闭上眼睛. 我决定在工作台上播放一些音乐 - - 感觉很强大 - - 复杂的迷幻曲子很容易出来. 我朋友正在绘制一个很酷的抽象图, 他似乎在关注内在视觉的出现. 我开始觉得我的思想在漂移,我的心目充满了复杂的多色视觉, 感觉有点冷却但舒适. 我转向了我的声乐吉他, 我的朋友接管了键盘工作站. 吸了一些大麻, 这加剧了视觉和整体旅行的强度. - 感觉我们即将达到峰值! 这次旅程主要以4-Aco-DMT的严度为主,但以4-Aco-MET的愉快方式进行了修改 - 视觉效果有更多的多样性和某种梦幻感,OEV只包括一些颜色放大和影子运动,但CEV - OH MY! 颜色主要是灰色,深红色,深蓝色,紫色,黑色的复杂色调,金色发光的颜色! 视觉效果不像我在4-Aco-MET或4-Aco-DMT上看到的, 听起来就像吉米·亨德里克斯亲自进入了我的身体, 让我做出那些疯狂的独奏, 我从来没有这样演奏过! 我朋友在工作站的合成器上一起演奏, 我只是在几秒钟内就消失了 - - 那时我根本无法打开眼睛做任何事情, 眼睛闭着,我也能看到我的手指, 它们看起来像是多色烂的棒子! 我感觉像一个陌生女人, 尖叫, 低声, 发出奇怪的声音, "不可能"的心理状态,超越理解,与疯狂的边缘... 我感觉非常高兴, 时间被大大扩展, 我的自我感觉完好无损 (也许是由于4-Aco-MET的添加), - 电脑上找到了一些全音录音, 连接了几个耳机 - 听这些录音在影响下是一个非常强大的体验! 尤其是虚拟的剪发--我发誓我看到塑料袋在我的头上, 情绪不稳定, 意识到我对爱我的人是多么残忍, 我正在创作一首非常感人的歌曲, 伴奏吉他, 问上帝为什么我们如此无知, 不幸的是,我不记得歌词和和弦的序列. 我朋友的狗在附近睡觉, 我决定躺在那个黑白斑的尸体旁边. 简单地承认另一个有生命的存在, 也是一个巨大的存在, 我们决定抽大麻放松, 抽很多大麻 - - 感觉很好! 在此阶段会出现轻微的持续性头痛. 绝对是个迷幻的状态, 没有食欲. 我有一个朋友开始告诉我他需要去 DMV 照顾他的失去了标题很快, 这听起来像它应该是严重的东西, 那时听到某个汽车标题真是太好笑了 - - 听起来绝对荒谬,荒谬,愚蠢,很有趣 - - 所以我们都在20分钟之内歇斯底里地笑了. 我在吃时也会发生类似的事情. 我做了一些大麻卷,一些烟草卷. 在寒冷的夜晚散步是非常愉快的. 我感到非常平静, 我喜欢这种感觉! 我喜欢这种感觉! 时间是吃饭和旅行后喝酒! 食物的味道很正常,酒的味道真的很好! 看"里克和莫蒂"的同时, 仍然感觉很高... 发起了令人讨厌的头痛, 非常类似于我从大量的2C-P中得到的头痛. 我服用了400毫克的布洛芬,200毫克的5HTP和一些活性黄提取物来帮助. 在一个小时左右的时间里, 醒来时会感到清醒. 结论:这次我选择了更高的剂量, 添加20毫克的4-Aco-MET 缓解了4-Aco-DMT的"判断"效果, 添加20毫克的4-Aco-MET 缓解了4-Aco-DMT的"判断"效果, 但也可能引起头痛. 闭着眼睛弹吉他是史诗般的, 在一个舒适温暖的地方. 我觉得这个分子有着"火"的精神, 总的来说是一个很棒的组合, 下次我会尝试40毫克的4-Aco-DMT与15毫克的4-Aco-MET! 感谢您的阅读, 时间表: 109742 发布时间:2017年1月3日 4-AcO-DMT (387),4-AcO-MET (711):烂的体验 (4),音乐讨论 (22),组合 (3),小组 (2-9) (17)

Subjects: two males, about 170Lbs each, both with extensive psychoactives research over the past 10 years. Previous trip was on 40mg 4-HO-EPT about a week ago. Empty stomach. Set & setting: With a close friend, in the evening, at home, musical instruments are ready to use, so is painting/drawing station. Dose (measured on a decent milligram scales with +/- 1mg): 30mg of 4-Aco-DMT and 20mg of 4-Aco-MET for each person, put in separate capsules. Experience: T=00:00 - We take both capsules, each of us, at the same time. Say our prayers, start meditating. T+00:30 - Very light alerts, meditating with eyes closed, I put on sounds of nature - collection of high quality recordings of oceans, rain and so on - sounds great and very atmospheric! T+01:00 - Feeling some sedation, typical for 4-Aco-DMT for me. I cant say if I'm hot or cold. Body feels trembling. Not too much confusion, no scary feelings at all. I tend to close my eyes for the most time. Deciding to play some music on my workstation - feels powerful - complex psychedelic pieces coming out easily. My friend drawing a very cool abstraction meanwhile, he seems preoccupied with the internal vision emerging. T+01:30 - Starting to feel that my mind is drifting away, my mind's eye is getting filled with complex multi-colored visuals, mostly consisting of heavily distorted and modified bright mental images that were just flooding out my inner vision. Feeling a bit cold but comfortable. Switching to my acoustic guitar and my friend takes over the keyboard workstation. Smoked some cannabis and that intensified the visuals and the overall trip intensity. T+02:00 - Feels like we are about to peak! The trip is mostly dominated by seriousness of 4-Aco-DMT but modified in a pleasant way by 4-Aco-MET - the visuals have more variety and some sort of dreaminess to them, OEVs only consisted of some color amplification and shadow movements but CEVs - OH MY!.. Colors were predominantly greys, dark reds, dark blues, purples, complex shades of black, golden glowing colors! Visuals are not like I've seen on 4-Aco-MET or 4-Aco-DMT by themselves, nothing similar to mushrooms as well, they are quite unique and definitely have hyperspace quality to them. T+02:30 - I'm on a floor laying on my back with eyes closed, I'm hugging my guitar that is hooked up through some filters to the sound system - sounds like Jimi Hendrix himself got into me and is making me do those insanely crazy solos, I've never played like that! My friend is on the workstation synthesizer playing along and both of us sound so amazing and in tune! A brief thought of starting the monitoring recording while we play entered my mind but was swept away in a matter of seconds - I was simply unable to open my eyes at that moment to do anything and I was constantly being swept by the overflow of images and emotions going through me per second... Even with eyes closed I see my fingers and they look like fat multi-colored glowing sticks! My acoustic Breedlove guitar feels like an alien woman to me screaming and whispering, making all that weird sounds it feels like I'm making love to her - such an exctatic feeling! 'Impossible' states of mind, beyond comprehension, bordering with insanity... I feel incredibly high, time is heavily dilated, my ego feels intact (maybe due to 4-Aco-MET addition), although being 'observed' and 'judged' by some higher authority within myself. T+03:30?? - Found some holophonics recordings on the computer and hooked up a couple of headphones - listening to those recordings under the influence is quite a powerful experience! Especially the virtual haircut - I swear I saw the plastic bag getting over my head with just sounds triggering it. T+04:00 - Emotional lability, bursting into tears with realization of how cruel I am to the people who love me... Very deep understanding, I am making up quite an emotional song - playing on a guitar along, asking God why are we so ignorant, asking to give us strength and wisdom to be better and cleaner in our perceptions. Cant remember both the lyrics and the chords progression I was using unfortunately... My friend's dog (huge good looking great dane) is sleeping nearby and I'm deciding to lay next to that black and white spotted body. Simple acknowledgement of another living being, a huge one too, different than myself but wanting similar things in life, making me burst into tears again. T+04:40 - CEVs are pretty much muted at this point so we decide to smoke cannabis and chill out, lots of cannabis - feels very nice! Light lingering headache is spotted at this point. Definite psychedelic state with a nice afterglow setting in. No appetite. A friend of mine is starting to tell me that he would need to go to the DMV and take care of his lost title soon and it sounds like it's suppose to be something serious but I cant control myself and burst into uncontrollable laughing! It was just so hilarious to hear about some car title at that moment - it seems absolutely absurd, ridiculous and stupid, and funny - so we both laugh hysterically at this for a good 20 minutes. Similar things usually happen to me when on mushrooms. T+05:30 - Going outside now. Made some rolls with cannabis and some with tobacco. Walking at night in a wintery settings feels very nice. I feel a great calm and feeling of serenity and acceptance. My friend makes a remark that smoking tobacco when tripping is like opening your body and mind to great spirits for evaluation - I like that! T+06:00 - Time for some food and aftertrip beer! Food tastes normal, beer feels really good! Watching Rick and Morty and still feeling quite high... T+07:00 - A really nasty headache had developed, very similar to the one I get from a hefty dosage of 2C-P. So I take 400mg Ibuprofen, 200mg 5HTP and some activated turmeric extract to help with that. Sleep was achieved within an hour or so. Vivid dreams and feeling refreshed upon awakening. Conclusion: This time I went for a higher dosage, the previous ones being 15 and 22mg. Addition of 20mg of 4-Aco-MET smoothed the 'judging' effect of 4-Aco-DMT as I was hoping for, Addition of 20mg of 4-Aco-MET smoothed the 'judging' effect of 4-Aco-DMT as I was hoping for, added to euphoria and gave another depth to visuals but could have caused a headache as well. Playing guitar with eyes closed was EPIC, better in these terms than most of my LSD trips. 4-Aco-DMT imo is best enjoyed with eyes closed, obviously being somewhere comfortable and warm. I feel that the molecule has a 'fire' spirit, especially combined with 4-Aco-MET which I think has an 'air' spirit. Overall a great combination, next time I'll be trying 40mg 4-Aco-DMT with 15mg 4-Aco-MET! Thanks for reading and be safe!

医生已经完成了整个"睡眠卫生"程序, 我拿了一张绿色的处方单去药店, 拿着一张14粒小白色药片的纸带回来. 我不知道该怎么做; 在我被处方这种药物之前, 我已经抽了大麻很长一段时间, 我从事心理健康工作, 我很小心服用安眠药. 我总是很难控制我的思维, 尤其是在晚上. 我在特殊教育中花了很大一部分时间, 不是因为我的大脑不起作用, 无论如何, 当我处于我们称之为阿斯伯格症患者的"滚动"或"崩"状态时, 我喝了一杯牛奶, 喝了一粒药. 那是在晚上9点左右. 十五分钟后, 我得到了第一次使用佐皮克隆的迹象, 我又花了十分钟才真正感到昏昏欲睡, 到那时, 苦已经加剧, 我感觉有点软弱, 稍微醉了, 舒适,温暖和放松 - - 尽管我试图, 我终于找到了有效的药, 我不记得其他的事情 - - 我躺在那里几分钟, 感觉着同样的奇怪,舒适,温暖的冷漠, 我不相信我找到了一种奇迹般的治疗方法. 实际上, 关于我不幸的脑部状况, 我一直认为大麻是奇迹, 我从一开始就知道佐皮克隆可能是危险的 - - 我查了一下, 发现它有部分效果与类药物 (瓦利,卡纳克斯等) 一样, 我很担心, 如果我每三天晚上只服用一次安眠药, 他解释说, 虽然类药物和非类药物/Z类药物 (如佐皮克隆,佐尔皮德姆) 正确地被妖魔化, 但它们作为缓解焦虑和帮助睡眠的有效性是不可争议的, 所以,我继续这样做. 每隔三到五天晚上, 我会用一杯温暖的饮料吞下我的小圆平白色药片, 半小时以后, 我便在床上躺着, 脏的金属在喉上升起, 只有在第三或第四次服用之后, 我才开始注意到这种药物干扰梦想的方式. 我总是有奇怪的,几乎神秘的梦. 但 zopiclone 给这些梦想增加了新的,多维的视角; 它让它们看起来更真实, 我在诺曼底海与Yossarian进行了精神讨论. 我看到牛在切斯特和雷克萨姆之间与朋友一起散步, 我在科尼岛的海上亲吻了我梦想中的男人, 我发现几乎所有的末日梦都带有刺般的浪漫色彩. 我不会一分钟质疑佐皮克隆的有效性. 作为一种睡眠辅助, 我不能责怪它. 似乎对我来说几乎没有娱乐潜力 (尽管我的朋友,他滥用了比我高得多的水平,声称它是"有趣的");它不会引起"高" - - 更像是全身条 - - 和保持清醒,即"战斗"效果的"声",似乎不与 zopiclone;如果我不睡觉,我只是感觉有点头,不协调 没有快乐的感觉. 在我看来,作为一种娱乐药物, 这很无聊,而且可能会成, 然而, 作为一种纯粹的治疗手段来缓解神经失眠, 虽然它可能会有令人烦的副作用, 但它在缓解失眠的有效性是不可争议的. 保持强大,玩安全. 在药物方面, 和平,同志们. 其他: 梦想 (85),药物 - 佐皮克隆 (272):单独 (16),治疗意图或结果 (49),医疗用途 (47),组合 (3)

Jul 27, 2010. erowid.org/exp/83220 DOSE: 3.75 mg oral Pharms - Zopiclone (pill / tablet) smoked Cannabis I was first prescribed Zopiclone after the doctor was through with the whole 'sleep hygiene' routine, that they do in the UK to try and avoid giving out sedative-hypnotics. I was given a green prescription slip to take to the chemist (drugstore), and came away with a foil strip of 14 small, white pills. I was unsure of what to expect; I had smoked pot for quite some time before I was prescribed this drug, and I knew that whilst pot isn't particularly dangerous OR physically addicting, this is not the case with prescription sedatives. My mum works with mental health, and has spoken of people in protracted benzodiazepine withdrawal. So naturally, I was very wary of taking my sleeping pills. I have always had trouble controlling my racing torrent of thought, especially at night for some reason. I spent a large chunk of my education in special ed, not because my brain doesn't work, but rather because it works TOO efficiently, in my case. But anyway, at the time I was on what we Asperger victims call a 'roll' or a 'meltdown' - I just COULD NOT settle down and stop thinking. So, I got myself a glass of milk, and washed down a pill. It was around 9PM. Fifteen minutes later, I got my first indication of having taken zopiclone, the last thing I was expecting - a strange, thin, sour and metallic tang rising up the back of my throat and hovering around my tonsils. It took me another ten minutes to feel properly drowsy, by which time that bitterness had intensified, and now masked anything that I ate - not that I felt like eating. I felt sort of floppy and slightly drunk, but without the nausea. Comfortable, warm and relaxed - try as I did, I could not get any thoughts to work at all. Relieved that I had at last found a pill that worked, I decided that there was no point in staying awake, and went straight to bed. I don't remember much else - just lying there for a few minutes, feeling that same strange, cosy warm indifference eclipse me, and then I woke up the next day with a slightly fuzzy head, and a mouth that tasted of metal for much of the next day. I wasn't convinced that I had found a 'miracle cure', or anything. In truth, where my unfortunate brain condition is concerned, I did and still do consider cannabis to be if not a miracle, then the next best thing to one, where combating rogue brainwaves is concerned. And I knew from the beginning that zopiclone is potentially dangerous - I looked it up, and found out that it partly works like benzodiazepines (Valium, Xanax etc.) but also has some similarities with barbiturates, a group of sedatives notorious for producing uncontrollable addiction, erratic behaviour, and mucho overdosage. So naturally I was worried, and spoke to my shrink. He said that if I took my sleeping pills no more than one out of every three nights, then I shouldn't develop a problem. He explained that whilst benzos and nonbenzodiazepines/Z-drugs (things like zopiclone, zolpidem) ARE justly demonised, their effectiveness as anxiety-relievers and sleep-aids can't be disputed, and that if I was cautious, these things could just be a life-saver. So, I continued in this way. Every three to five nights, I would swallow my small, round flat white tablet with a warm drink, and smoke a joint whilst I waited for it to work. Thirty to sixty minutes later, I would be out for the count, flat on my back, that nasty metallic tang rising up my throat, and then abruptly descend into deep sleep. It was after around the third or fourth dosing, that I started to notice the way this stuff interferes with dreams. I have always had strange, almost cryptic dreams. But zopiclone added a new, multi-dimensional perspective to these dreams; it made them seem all the more real, as though I had stepped out of one life and into another. I got to engage Yossarian (Catch-22) in spiritual discussion on the beaches of Normandy. I got to witness cows eating one another alive, as I walked with a friend between Chester and Wrexham. I got to kiss the guy of my dreams on the beaches of Coney Island, even as NYC was destroyed all around me in an air-raid. I was disturbed to discover that almost all of my apocalyptic, zopiclone-induced dreams carried an ironic romantic overtone. I am not disputing the efficacity of zopiclone for one minute. As a sleep aid, I can't fault it. It seems to have little to no recreational potential for me (although my friend, who has abused it in much higher levels than myself, claims that it is 'fun'); it doesn't induce a 'high' - more of a full-body slump - and staying awake i.e. 'fighting' the effect for the 'buzz', doesn't seem to work with zopiclone; if I don't go to sleep, I just feel slightly light-headed, uncoordinated, sort of drunk without the happy feeling. As a recreational candidate, zopiclone is pretty useless, in my opinion. It's boring, and the addiction potential just isn't worth it. As a purely therapeutic means of ameliorating nervous insomnia, however, in my opinion Zopiclone is a chemically-engineered godsend. It may have its annoying side-effects, but its effectiveness at temporarily relieving insomnia can't really be disputed. Stay strong, play it safe. Always look before you leap, at least where drugs are concerned. Peace out, comrades.

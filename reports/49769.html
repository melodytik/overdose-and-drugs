我第一次听说这次LSD聚会是在2005年5月, 我预计这次研讨会会有点无聊,而且比我负担得起的要贵一些;然而,马克不理解"不"这个词的含义, 我还非常担心过度饮酒,非法药物使用和幼稚的恶作剧会毁掉这次活动. 我发现与会者相当清醒; 2000多名与会者, 据美国标准,在会议中心内或周围唯一可购买的非法物质是青, 似乎除非你带上自己选择的非法药物,否则你能"得分"的可能性几乎为零. 如果您正在考虑参加下一届大会, 我请您把"习惯"留在家里, 因为试图偷运毒品是愚蠢的, 如果我的行李里或身上的东西是我不该带来的, 我收到了来自夏威夷和澳大利亚的新朋友的名片. 假如人们能像我在这次会议上那样感受, 我只能为每一个见到的人分享我的快乐. 我每天都会遇到世界上最美丽的人 - - 不是受媒体狭的定义而美丽, 会议并没有建议每个人都应该"转向"使用LSD,简单的信息是,对于一些人来说,LSD (和其他迷幻药) 可以成为减少伤害的有效工具,并可以防止成性药物使用和自我破坏行为; 60年代的毒品文化已经成熟, 花儿们已经开花了! 这些美丽而充满爱心的人每天都会表达出让我和许多人,包括Dr. 霍夫曼承认他找不到言语来表达他对LSD的深感, 他继续说他现在相信他的"问题孩子" (LSD) 已经成为"神童", 我没有去寻找LSD会议参加, 它来寻找我; 我感到非常幸运能够参与这一历史性和关键的世界事件. 其他: 会议 (361):一般 (1), 节日 / Lg. 人群 (24)

Jan 24, 2006. erowid.org/exp/49769 I first heard about this LSD gathering back in May of 2005 from Mark McCloud (the convention didn’t even have a web site up at the time). I expected the symposium would be a bit boring and more expensive than I could afford; however Mark doesn’t understand the meaning of the word “no” and my finances improved. I also had severe concerns that excessive drinking; illicit drug use, and childish antics would ruin the event. I actually found the attendees to be quite sober; 2,000+ attendees with none of them appearing impaired; which is remarkable when you consider that the symposium was a celebration of one of the most powerful mind altering substances known. The only illicit substance (by American standards) available for purchase in or around the convention center was absinthe, which surprised me as I assumed the event would be a magnet for drug dealers. It seemed that unless you brought your own illicit drug of choice that your odds of being able to 'score' anything were virtually zero. If you are considering going to the next convention I beg you to leave your 'habit' at home, as it’s stupid to try to smuggle a stash, and there will be no drug dealers to greet you at the convention. My carry on luggage was searched four times and I was frisked once, if there was anything in my luggage or on my person that I should not have had I am sure they would have found it. This symposium was a major draw for intellectuals of all ages, and walks of life, I was handed business cards from new acquaintances that came from as far away as Hawaii and Australia. If people normally felt the way that I felt at this convention I’m sure recreational drug use would vanish. The one beer I had only served to dampen the joy I was sharing with everyone I met. Day after day I met some of the most beautiful people in the world- beautiful not by the narrow media driven definition, but in the sense of being free of the ego problems and biases that are pervasive in other fields (Google William Rafti). In no way did the convention suggest that everyone should 'Turn on' to LSD, the simple message is that for some people LSD (and other psychedelics) can be an effective tool for harm reduction and may prevent addictive drug use and self destructive behavior; the drug culture of the 60’s has matured and shed the juvenile behavior that is to be expected in any youthful pursuit. The flower children have bloomed! Day after day these beautiful loving people expressed thoughts that brought tears of joy to my eyes, as well as many others including Dr. Hoffman who confessed that he could not find words to express how deeply moved he was, he went onto say he now believes that his 'Problem child' (LSD) has become a 'Child Prodigy' and implored us all to spread the message of LSD to the world. I didn’t go looking for an LSD conference to attend, it came looking for me; and it turned out to be nothing less than a religious experience. I feel very blessed to have been a part of this historic and pivotal world event.

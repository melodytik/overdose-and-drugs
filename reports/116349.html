中文译文 兴奋剂

背景信息: 在 (遥远) 过去使用过Ritalin (甲基化物), 没有ADHD. 这两种剂量都没有做准备. 在服用药物时,在前一天晚上 (T-13:00左右) 除大麻外,没有使用过其他药物. 设置:一般感觉有点累,相当中立 (既不快乐也不快乐),轻松. 场景:在家里和各种小公园/城市散步. 第一个剂量为5mg4fmph 对于我来说,这些效应并没有显著的意义. 我确实经历了轻微的注意力增加, 我也似乎喜欢我做杂货比平常更多. 我不清楚其中有多少是由于4fmph和多少是基于我对Ritalin (mph) 和因此安慰剂相似的预期. 第二剂10mg4fmph (直接晚餐后) 这是我在学习时服用的瑞塔林的剂量. 想看看高剂量是否会产生更明显的效果. 由于第一剂的效果, 我不想再服用, 至少我相信如此. 主要是因为我已经认真考虑了这样的情况. 我也没有任何 (有意义的) 想要在20毫克以下的剂量下重新服用Ritalin的欲望. 如果不是安慰剂,那么5毫克的效果似乎也很小. 我和另外两个人一起走路, 他们也不知道我已经采取了4fmph,我宁愿他们没有发现. 欧里亚开始出现. 我发现自己笑得更多, 没有任何特别的原因. 这对我来说不同于Ritalin, 这会让我感觉良好, 我发现很难描述差异, 但这感觉更快乐, 里塔林感觉更中立的好感觉. 我感到有点焦虑的效果会变得如此强烈在峰值可能会被注意到我在一个兴奋剂, 简单地说,这只是我几次想到的想法. 虽然我仍然感到高兴, 现在我已经不再担心, 已经可以注意到我服用了兴奋剂. 我感觉很好. 走路时, 我会做一些小小的绕道, 我肯定会更快地决定我只是想去做. 没有人担心这种冲动性会引起怀疑. 我也没有收到任何关于表现奇怪的评论, 在散步后回家. 我认为我表现得比平时更活跃/快乐, 现在我感到既疲 (可能是因为走路), 我决定写下我有史以来的第一份经验报告, 我一直想做这件事, 但从来没有真正做过, 这与我过去服用Ritalin时的作业方式非常相似. 写完这篇文章, 决定看一些YouTube. 在此之前, 观看YouTube并没有与正常观看有什么不同, 我确实认为我可能会继续观看视频/比平时更耐心. 我和别人一起去刷牙,因为他们要睡觉了. 完成刷牙,决定玩一些火箭联盟, 显然新游戏模式已经发布. 我还想知道电子运动人士关于提高注意力有好处的说法是否有任何价值. (记住我读过他们使用阿德拉尔, 而不是利他林或4fmph) 有趣的一点: 仍然感到疲倦, 在这个阶段, 疲劳也是精神上的, 在解决磁盘安装问题,然后更新游戏 (新模式,新更新,我猜...) 之后, 我开始听音乐,因为我喜欢在演奏时听音乐. 几乎立即我注意到对音乐的欣赏/沉浸感略有增加. 在T+3:40 我最快失去对新模式的兴趣. 虽然我听说过其他人喜欢它我真的真的不想学习如何玩现在. 对于我来说这是意想不到的,因为我预计会更感兴趣的新奇的东西在一般,在散步似乎是这样的情况. 然而,在此案中情况并非如此. 尽管如此,他们还是决定玩正常模式. 没有注意到实际游戏性能,反应时间等有意义的增强. 我确实看起来更加专注, 并且更加坚定地专注, 音乐仍然很好听. 我感受到一种令人难以置信的幸福感, 一个关节的8个口 (指标) 我不想只听音乐,也不想在听音乐的时候做任何事情. 决定抽一点大麻, 继续看YouTube, 直到足够疲/不再太刺激睡觉. 我觉得有点, 不少于没有4fmph, 但有点醒了. 这比正常情况快得多, 这很有趣. 我也不再感到兴奋, 然而我仍然有一种奇怪的疲劳和清醒的结合. 我也不再以同样的方式感觉"好", 仍然很好, 虽然现在还有一点清醒, 但在这个阶段, 疲劳肯定会压倒它. 我睡得很快,比起起起初每小时4分钟的时间早半个小时. 最后的一些注意事项: 总的来说,我觉得4fmph对我来说与Ritalin没有什么不同. 在高峰开始时, 我也许会带它去上课或学习, 看看它在这些环境中是否具有相同的特质. 值得注意的是我没有报告任何身体影响. 因为我没有真正的经历. 我可能在高峰期比平时更, 但当时我没有注意到这有什么意义. 对于组合, 我没有吸烟很多 (为了我的耐受性) 我没有注意到太多的相互作用. 没有4fmph我可能会感觉更但我没有注意到它, 值得注意的是, 大麻的效果似乎没有像我一般那样持续, 时间表: 116349 发布时间:2022年5月11日 甲基化物 (728),大麻 (1):组合 (3),一般 (1),各种 (28)

May 11, 2022. erowid.org/exp/116349 DOSE: T+ 0:00 5 mg 4-Fluoromethylphenidate T+ 5:30 10 mg 4-Fluoromethylphenidate T+ 9:40 8 hits smoked Cannabis BODY WEIGHT: 50 kg Background info: Used Ritalin (methylphenidate) in the (distant) past, do not have ADHD. No preparation was done for either dose. On on medication, no prior drug use except cannabis the night before (around T-13:00). Set: Generally felt a little tired, pretty neutral (not happy nor unhappy), at ease. Setting: At home and in various small parks/the city for the walk. Sex: male T-5:30 first dose of 5mg 4fmph For the entirety the effects are not meaningfully noticeable to me. I did experience a slight increase in focus, and talkativeness. I also seemed to enjoy the groceries I did a little more than usual. However it was unclear to me how much of that was due to 4fmph and how much of it was based on my expectation of similarity to Ritalin (mph) and thus placebo. T+0:00 second dose of 10mg 4fmph (directly after eating dinner) This is the same dose I used to take of Ritalin when studying. I decided to go for a long walk through a bunch of parks, and wanted to see if a higher dose had more pronounced effects. I did not feel a desire to take another dose due to the effects of the first dose, or at least so I believed. Mostly because I had already been seriously considering taking such a dose for such a situation. As well as that I do not get any (meaningful) desire to re-dose on Ritalin at doses under 20mg. The effects of the 5mg also seemed very minor if not placebo. I am on this walk with two other people, neither under the influence of anything. They also do not know I've taken 4fmph, and I'd prefer they did not find out. T+0:30 Euphoria is starting to set in. I notice myself smiling more, without any particular reason. This feels different to me than Ritalin, which would make me feel good, but in a different way. I find it hard to describe the difference exactly, but this felt happier, where Ritalin felt like a more neutral good feeling. I feel a little anxious the effects will become so strong at the peak it might be noticeable that I'm on a stimulant, but this never happened with 10mg of Ritalin for me so I wasn't too worried. Simply a thought that crossed my mind a few times. T+1:00 Euphoria still there, but I am not smiling as much any more. My confidence is boosted and I am at this point no longer worried it's noticeable I'm on a stimulant. I feel great. On our walk I make a few small detours and get interested maybe a little quicker about things than I would normally be. I definitely decide quicker that I just want to go for it. (Leaving out specifics to remain anonymous, but small detours, looking at things on the route, nothing too special.) Worry that this increased impulsiveness would be suspicious was non-existent. I also did not receive any comments about acting weird, so it likely was not apparent I was on anything. T+2:20 Got back home from the walk. It was fun, and I think at most I acted a little more active/happy than I would normally have, but not outside of my normal range of activeness/happiness. At this point I feel both tired (likely from the walk) as well as rather awake, it's a strange feeling. I decide to write my first ever experience report (this one), deciding to give back to a community that has been valuable to me. I've wanted to do this for a long time, but never actually got around to it, this time however I decide to do it, and just start doing it. This is very similar to how I approached homework when I did Ritalin in the past. T+2:40 Finish writing this far, decide to watch some YouTube. T+3:05 Watching YouTube up to this point was not meaningfully different from normal, maybe a little more focused. I did think I might be more likely to continue watching a video/be more patient than I would normally be. Going to brush my teeth with others, as they are going to bed. T+3:10 Done brushing my teeth, decide to play some Rocket League, apparently a new game mode had released. I was also interested if claims by e-sport people about increased concentration being beneficial had any merit. (Keeping in mind I read they used Adderall, not Ritalin or 4fmph.) As an interesting side note: Still feeling tired, and stimulated at the same time, it's a little strange, but not unpleasant. The tiredness is also mental, not physical at this stage, so unlikely to be from the walk. T+3:30 Finally in game after fixing issues with disk mounting, and then updating the game (new mode, new update I guess...) The waiting was more annoying to me than normal. I started some music, as I like to listen to music while playing. Almost immediately I noticed a slightly increased appreciation/immersion for the music. T+3:40 Quickest I ever lost interest in a new mode. It's too different from normal, and though I've heard others like it I really really do not want to learn how to play right now. This was unexpected for me, as I was expecting to be more interested in novel things in general, which during the walk did seem to be the case. Here however it was not the case. Decide to play normal modes though. T+4:00 Did not notice meaningful enhancements in actual game performance, reaction times etc. I did seem slightly more focused, and more consistently focused, but I'm not confident that feeling was anything more than a feeling. The music still playing is very nice. I got a little bit of the feeling of incredible happiness/bliss I feel when enjoying music on psychedelics. T+4:10 8 puffs of a joint (indica) I don't want to just listen to music, and don't feel like doing anything while listening to music. Decide to smoke a little bit of weed and continue watching YouTube until tired enough/no longer too stimulated to sleep. The interaction of compounds is uninteresting, I feel slightly stoned, no less than without the 4fmph, but slightly more awake. T+4:55 Stoned feeling completely gone now, this is noticeably quicker than normal, which is interesting. No longer feel stimulated either, however I do still have a weird combination of tiredness and wakefulness at the same time. Also no longer feel "good" in the same way, still doing just fine, but my mood no longer feels lifted. T+5:30 Wakefulness is mostly gone by this point, there might still be some there but the tiredness is definitely overpowering it at this stage. At this point I decided to just smoke some more weed and go to bed, which turned out to be feasible, I fell asleep in a normal amount of time, which was about half an hour sooner than I expected to be able to when originally taking the 4fmph. Some final notes: Overall I felt 4fmph was not meaningfully different to Ritalin for me. At most it might be a little more euphoric during the come up and beginning of the peak. I might take it going to class and or studying to see if it has the same qualities in those contexts too. Also worth noting I did not report on any physical effects. Because I did not really experience any. I was maybe a little more clammy during the peak than I would normally be, but at the time I did not notice this as significant. As for the combination, I did not smoke a lot (for my tolerance) and I did not notice much of an interaction. Admittedly I was already coming down from the 4fmph at that point, and I might have been feeling more stoned without the 4fmph but simply didn't notice it as I wasn't all that stoned to begin with. It was noteworthy that the effect of the weed did not seem to last as long as it normally does for me, which might be an indication the 4fmph did blunt the original high.

我是一名二十多岁的男性, 在过去两年半的时间里, 我一直在接受米尔塔扎 (Remeron) 的处方, 我绝对不推使用街头毒品, 尤其是处方药, 首先,我想说米尔塔扎 (Remeron) 对我非常有效. 我有一个中等级的双极性障碍 (我通常是低,然而), 混合极度焦虑和失眠. 这种药物对每一个"问题"都起到了作用, 但副作用很小 (根据你对"小副作用"的看法, 无论如何,我会把你真正想知道的事情. 我没有开始尝试更强烈的药物, 我在高中时对毒品一点兴趣都没有, 直到我成熟一点, 我开始服用药物时, 由于医生的劝告, 我停止了两个月的饮酒 (对于这个疲的灵魂来说, 这是一个很长的时间). 医生告诉我这对身体和精神来说不是最好的事情, 他对了,吸是什么,好吧,吸应该是什么样子. 我和医生发现了适合我的"完美"剂量, 我发现我可以减少饮酒的量. 随着时间的推移, 我的耐受性有所增加, 很简单的例子是, 在服用药物之前, 我能够喝完一杯威士忌, 在药物治疗中, 两三杯酒足以让我变得相当醉. 我现在很少喝烈酒,主要是酒和葡萄酒. 六个酒让我很醉,在正确的夜晚. 我记得在服用这种药物时遇到的唯一问题是, 在睡前服用药物后 (我通常在睡前一小时左右服用药物, 百分之九十九的效果是神奇的), 我发现只有当我完全被抹去, 已经感觉有点, 我们可以说, . 我只是不再被完全摧毁; 这是我找到的最简单的方式来处理它. 我开始渴望更多的东西. 我总是说我永远不会做任何"比更难"的事情, 但我一直有,好吧,某种启示, 我觉得我需要尝试一些新事物, 而这些新事物是毒品... 诚实地说,除了大麻之外, 我不认为自己是任何药物的成者. 无论如何,我有一个朋友,他有一堆ES,所以我和几个其他朋友从他那里买了一些,在那天晚上爆发. 这次的第一次并不是我所想的. 我不认为我会再次这样做, 但谁知道. 另一个朋友也想试一下, 所以我们又买了一些, 我曾经吃过很多神奇的, 但与此相比, 长短的故事, 狂喜和我成为了非常好的朋友近一年. 我一直非常小心使用毒品. 我不是那种会因为某事而越来越多地爆发的人. 我使用毒品是为了享受自己, 我最多一次做的是两天半的时间, 我相信我有5到6个双. 他们也很好. 我也在那天晚上第一次抽了些可乐, 我不能说我是个粉丝, 我在周六下午2点左右开始吐 (我们在周五晚上开始). 我仍然相信这只是太多的酒精,E,和一些可卡因的组合. 吃麦当劳的早餐, 我认为你也会这样. 除了那个晚上,我对E唯一的另一个问题是,在做了一段时间后, 我的朋友仍然在飞行, 而我则在机着陆. 我知道这不是一个孤立的事件, 即使现在,我也许每年只会做一次. 现在我已经发现了LSD,我看不出有什么意义. 我总是被完全混乱,自由思考和幻觉的想法激起. 尽管我反对毒品, 我想要尝试LSD,而不是任何我听说过的毒品. 然而,考虑到我住的地方, 没有一个我认识的人拥有或能找到它, 坚持是有回报的. 我问她是否可以给我买一些. 她做到了. 所以我的女朋友和几个朋友尝试了. 我开始担心药物,因为这是我第一次服用e,但我认为如果我可以服用e (像我的药物一样会影响你的血清素水平), 而它并没有. 并且直到今天还没有. 我已经服用了足够多的酸, 好吧, 让我们说, 如果政府给我心理测试, 但它确实改变了我对这个注定要灭亡的世界的看法. 对于米尔塔扎的并发症, 我唯一能想到的是, 我可以很好地处理我的LSD,哈哈. 我在一个晚上吃的最多是五个, 我完全被搞了. 我从未有过传统意义上的幻觉, 我从来没有见过紫色的怪物从无处出现, 但每个人都会有痕迹, 我有时会吸很多大麻, 睡觉时间是什么时候呢? 其他人都常常要几天, 但我有我的"拐杖"来帮我. 对于LSD, 我最能说的就是, 自从我第一次使用它以来, 我相信这是件好事, 但有时我不太确定. 唯一的其他毒品, 我确实做了不止几次是. 我吃了很多神奇的. 我从未有过与药物结合的问题. 只是不要吃太多. 我认为两,两半克的好是足够的. 我发现这些比我记得的要多一点, 我通常只吃LSD和, 我在服用药物时服用了几次MDMA, 没有任何不良反应, 我发现这两次对我都没有太大影响, 我曾经在音乐会上睡着了, 我两次都感觉有点奇怪,事后. 我不知道为什么. 但与此同时,每当我服用除了LSD以外的任何药物, 我也在同一周末吸, 我喜欢片, 没有任何副作用, 它的味道甚至比哈希香. 我相信唯一一个还需要掩护的就是萨尔维亚. 我再也不会再喝沙尔维亚. 至少在我服用药物的时候. 我从来没有感觉到过这样的不舒服感. 我觉得自己正被吸进一个黑洞, 我以为我已经死了. 这真是太糟糕了. 幸运的是,它只持续了几分钟 (如果是那样的话). 我读过一些服用米尔塔扎的人对萨尔维亚有类似的反应. 别这样做. 这就是我的建议. 基本上, 我与街头毒品混合米尔塔扎的经历, 我绝对不建议你这样做, 我不建议你这样做, 但如果你这样做了, 但请记住, 每个人都不同,对化学物质的反应也不同 没有. 总而言之,我可能会有很糟糕的下降, 我永远不会做可卡因, 我对可卡因的体验并不像其他东西那样深入, 我从来没有用过LSD, 虽然我没有极端的幻觉 (尽管我相信大多数说他们看到紫牛等的人, 我从来没有用,片或吸大麻. 如果您在服用这种药物时要喝酒,请记住您的耐受性要低得多. 远远更低的地方. 我觉得我还应该补充说,每当我使用任何药物, 一旦我习惯了高,我会去冒险. 我也非常负责我投入的资金. 我建议你开始吃少量, 如果需要更多, 如果你想让你的胃充, 你总是可以服用更多的药物. 有趣的乐趣. 无论如何, 我希望这篇漫长的漫步文章能回答您的一些问题, 只是记住孩子们,安全. 应该有责任心. 永远记住你是高,世界其他地方不是. 这可能看起来不真实, 但它是. 祝你好运. 时间表: 69888 药物 - 米尔塔萨平 (311), 其他 (136): 组合 (3), 后视 / 摘要 (11), 未知背景 (20)

Jan 11, 2013. erowid.org/exp/69888 DOSE: 35 mg oral Pharms - Mirtazapine (daily) BODY WEIGHT: 215 lb I am a male in my late twenties, and I have been on a prescription for mirtazapine (Remeron) for the last two and a half years of my life and I have taken a wide variety of street drugs while on this medication, including LSD, MDMA, Ecstasy, cocaine, magic mushrooms, opium, Salvia, and, of course, marijuana and alcohol. I’d also like to state that by no means do I recommend taking street drugs, especially while on prescribed medication, but everyone has their vices, right? First off, I’d like to say that mirtazapine (Remeron) has been very effective for me. I have a mid-level bipolar disorder (I’m usually down, however), mixed with extreme anxiety and insomnia. This medication has helped every single one of these ‘problems’, while only small side effects (depending on your view of ‘small side effects, for me, they are worth the price of admission). Anyways, I’ll get on to what you really want to know about. I didn’t start experimenting with harder drugs until I was on medication. Drugs didn’t interest me at all in high school, it wasn’t until I was a little more mature that they become alluring. When I first started taking the medication, I stopped drinking for two months (a long stretch for this weary soul) at the plea of my doctor. I continued smoking a crap load of pot, which the doctor told me isn’t the best thing for the body and mind, but it wouldn’t hurt me any more or less. He was right on that one, smoking pot was what, well, what smoking pot should be like. After a few months had passed and my doctor and I discovered the ‘perfect’ dosage for me, I started drinking lightly again. As with most other medications, I found that I could drink considerably less. Over the years, my tolerance has increased, but not to what it once was. An easy example is that prior to the medication, I was able to finish a 26er of whiskey, and be alright with it. On medication, two or three drinks are enough to get me pretty drunk. I rarely drink hard liquor anymore, mostly beer and wine. Six beers get me pretty drunk, on the right night. The only problems I can recall having while drinking on this medication is that, after I pop my pills at bedtime (I usually pop an hour or so before I want to sleep, works like a charm 99 times out of 100) I would sometimes get very ill. I found this only to be if I was completely obliterated, and already feeling somewhat, shall we say, dizzy. I just don’t get completely smashed anymore; it’s the easiest way I found to deal with it. After being on the medication for about 8-10 months, I started to get the desire for something more. I had always said that I would never do anything ‘harder’ than mushrooms, but I kept having, well, epiphanies of sorts, and eventually I decided that I didn’t want to be 40-something with three kids and a wife, but no experience in life. I felt I needed to try some new things, and those new things were drugs…duh duh duh. And in all honesty, I don’t consider myself an addict whatsoever to any drug I take, with the exception of pot. Anyways, I had a friend who had a bunch of e’s , so me and a couple other friends bought some off him and popped that night. That first time wasn’t everything I had thought it would be. I didn’t think I would do it again, but who knew. Well another friend of mine had wanted to try it as well, so we got some more, and well I guess those ones I had the first time were complete shit, because I had never been this high in my life. I had done magic mushrooms a lot, but that was nothing compared to this. Long story short, ecstasy and I became very good friends for close to a year. I have always been very careful of my drug use. I’m not one of those people who are going to pop more and more just because. I take drugs to enjoy myself, to access parts of the mind that normally wouldn’t be accessed, not to run away from problems, get sick, or die. The most I ever did on one occasion was over a two and a half day period, where I believe I had 5 or 6 double stacks. They were really good too. I also did some coke for the first time that night, not something I can say I was a fan of, and I’ve only done it three lines in a year and a half since then. I ended up puking at about 2 in the afternoon on the Saturday (we started Friday evening). I still believe that this was just a combination of way too much booze, e, and some coke. Couple that with a McDonald’s breakfast, and I think you will too. Other than that one night, the only other problem I had with e was that after doing it for awhile, I started to crash really fast and hard. My friends would still be flying and I would be crash landing. After I realized that wasn’t going to be an isolated incident, I pretty much stopped taking ecstasy. Even now, I will only do it maybe, maybe once a year. Now that I have found LSD, I don’t see the point. I had always been turned on by the thought of complete confusion, free thinking, and hallucinations. Even when I was against drugs I thought it was pretty neat. More than any other drug I had ever heard of, I wanted to try LSD. However, given where I live, no one I knew had it or could find it, and believe me, I tried. Well, perseverance pays off. Eventually, a casual acquaintance told me they were getting some, and I asked to her to get me some. And she did. So my girlfriend and a couple friends of ours tried it. Going in I was kind of worried about the meds, as I was the first time I did e, but I figured that if I can take e (something that fucks with your serotonin levels, as my meds do), acid wouldn’t be a problem. And it wasn’t. And it hasn’t been to this day. That first time was a little over a year from now, and I have taken enough acid to, well, lets just say, if the government was to give me psychological testing, I would be thrown in a room. I don’t feel as though it has affected my everyday activity, but it has definitely changed my outlook on this doomed world we lived in. As for complications with the mirtazapine, the only thing I can think of is that I don’t get the same hallucinogenic effects as my other people. I can handle my LSD very well, haha. The most I have taken in one night is five, and I was completely fucked. I have never hallucinated in the traditional sense, but no one really does. No one I have done acid with has ever seen purple monsters appear out of no where, but everyone gets trails and things will morph. I get trails on occasion when I smoke a lot of weed, but not to the extent my friends do. However, since I take my meds before I go to sleep, I never have any trouble when I decide it is time to sleep. Everyone else is usually up for days, but I have my ‘crutch’ to help me out there. The best thing I can say about LSD is that I have no desire to do any other drug since my first with it. I believe it to be a good thing, but sometimes I’m not so sure. Since I started taking LSD, the only other drug I have really done more than a few times has been mushrooms. And I have eaten a lot of magic mushrooms. I have never had a problem combining these with medication. Just don’t eat a lot. I find that two, two and a half grams of good mush is more than enough. I have found the mushrooms to be a little more intense than I remember but I may just have had really good ones recently. For the most part, I stick to LSD and mushrooms, but occasionally I will venture outside the box. I have done MDMA a few times while on the meds, and I didn’t have any adverse reactions to it, however, I didn’t do very much, maybe three/four lines, and a half pill on another occasion. I found it didn’t do too much to me on both occasions, not nearly as much as it fucked up some friends. I actually fell asleep at a concert on one occasion, while my friend was wired. I felt a little odd both times, after the fact. I don’t know why. But at the same time I get a feeling of regret anytime I do any drug other than LSD. I also smoked opium on the same weekend as one of the mdma experiences. I like opium a lot, no adverse effects here, pretty much the same as hash, except you burn it at a higher temperature. And it smells even better than hash. Well, I believe the only one left to cover is Salvia. I will never, ever do salvia again. At least while I’m on meds. It was the most uncomfortable feeling I have ever had. I felt as though I was being sucked into a black hole, as though I would never see anyone I loved again. I thought I was dying. It was terrible. Thank god it only lasts a few minutes (if that). I have read some people taking mirtazapine having similar reactions to Salvia. Don’t do it. That’s my advice. So basically, my experiences mixing mirtazapine (Remeron) with street drugs, to this point (knock on wood) have not been disastrous. By no means am I telling you it's OK to do any of these, I don’t recommend it what so ever, but if you are, at least you know one other person has done it and been fine. But remember, everyone is different and will react to chemicals in different ways . In summary, with e, I may have a really bad come down and I will never do coke ever while doing e. My experiences with coke were not as, well in depth as everything else, but no real bad effects. I’ve never had an issue with LSD, although I don’t get extreme hallucinations (although I believe most people who say they see purple cows etc, are lying to make themselves and the drug cooler). I’ve never had an issue doing mushrooms, opium, or smoking pot. If you are going to drink while on this medication, remember your tolerance is a lot lower. A lot lower. I feel I should also add, that whenever I do any drug, I make sure I come up in a safe place. Once I’m used to the high, I’ll go adventuring. I’m also very responsible about how much I put in. I recommend that you start with a low number of hits, and if you need more, eat one more. You can always take more if they aren’t working, but you can’t take them out of you system once they are they, unless you desire your stomach to be pumped. Fun fun. Anyways, I hope this big long ramble may have answered some of your questions, seeing as I couldn’t find any information anywhere on mixing mirtazapine (Remeron) with street drugs. Just remember kids, be safe. Be responsible. And always remember you are high, the rest of the world isn't. It may not seem real, but it is. Good luck.

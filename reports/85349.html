Oct 30, 2022. erowid.org/exp/85349 DOSE: T+ 0:00 2 g oral Piracetam T+ 0:00 250 g oral Vitamins - Choline T+ 0:00 2 hits oral LSD (blotter / tab) T+ 5:00 inhaled Nitrous Oxide (gas) BODY WEIGHT: 140 lb Dropped all three at the same time. It was my first time testing this batch of Eggshell LSD, so I don't have a strong previous handle on what the head trip was like. I'm normally a very on-task, mission oriented person when I trip, as well. I definitely did not feel the sinister push that I often feel when an acid trip is beginning. It was a little slower in starting than usual, and it felt very comfortable and natural. My visuals were very good, but I wouldn't say they were particularly enhanced. The mental effects were HIGHLY noticeable though. I was able to carry on really intelligent conversations, reply quickly, bring up the exact words I was looking for, and respond far more quickly than I usually can when I trip. Mental imagery was definitely enhanced, that hilarious mental exercise that takes place when I trip was not only good by itself, but was augmented by the wide vocabulary I retained while tripping. Did I trip harder? Possibly not. I didn't feel near as fucked up as I usually do when I trip, but the visuals, head trip, and the feelings of being well woven into reality were all very strong. Unfortunately, about 5 hours into the trip, I did several whippets which completely took the mental edge off. I'll definitely be using piracetam from now on though, my biggest complaint with LSD as a social drug is how difficult it is to enunciate what I'm experiencing and my point of view. I feel like piracetam repairs a lot of this effect.

茶叶的时候, 我服用了3个1毫克的黑激素, 由于我讨厌浪费药物, 我大约在11点半睡觉. 我很渴望抽烟,但我决定抽一根大便, 我吃了3个50毫克的B-6药,然后就上床睡觉. 我从一个短暂的,但非常激烈和有意义的梦中醒来. 整个晚上我有各种各样的梦, 包括一个可能被认为是梦 (我很久没有记得梦). 我有一次变得清醒, 我注意到我比平时有更多的控制权 (通常我在清醒后不久就醒来). 虽然我经常醒来,但很容易再次入睡. 我意识到在梦境中改变姿势并没有帮助. 我醒来了,然后翻了过来. 我最后一个梦是非常奇怪的, 我和我哥哥在一起, 我把控制器给他, 让他玩. 我在6点45分醒来, 完全休息了. 尽管如此, 我还是按下了休息按, 现实似乎不如我的梦想. 其他: 甲素 (94), 维生素B-6 (160), (48), 梦想 (85):组合 (3), 单独 (16)

Aug 13, 2005. erowid.org/exp/17252 DOSE: T+ 0:00 150 mg oral Vitamin B-6 (pill / tablet) T+ 0:00 3.0 mg sublingual Melatonin (pill / tablet) T+ 0:00 2.0 g oral Valerian (tea) T+ 0:30 0.5 g smoked Catnip (dried) T+ 0:30 0.5 g smoked Valerian (dried) T+ 0:30 150 mg oral Vitamin B-6 (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 138 lb I had read that melatonin, B-6, and valerian can potentiate intense dreams so I headed down to the the store and bought some of each. at about 11 I popped 3 50mg B-6 and then I proceeded to make some valerian tea. While the tea was brewing I took 3 1mg sublingual melatonin pill and disolved them under my tounge. Since I hate wasting drugs I consumed the contents of my 2g valerian root tea bag and washed it down with the foul brew. I settle down for bed at around 11:30. I was craving a smoke but instead of tobacco I decide to roll a fat joint, half valerian and half catnip (provides a mild buzz). I then eat 3 more 50mg B-6 pills and go to bed. At 3:30 I awaken from a short, yet very intense and meaningfull dream. Throughout the night I have a variety of dreams, Including one that could have been considered a nightmare (I havent remembered a nightmare in a LONG time). I became lucid once and I noticed I had much more control than I usually do (normally I awaken shortly after achieving lucidity). Though I awoke quite often it was easy to get back to sleep. One time my back was hurting in the dream, realizing that changing my posture in the dream world didn't help. I awoke and rolled over. The last dream I had was very strange, I was with my brother and I was showing him the joys of flying in a lucid dream. Somehow my 'dream' turned into a video game and I passed him the controller allowing him to play. I awoke to my alarm at 6:45 and was fully rested. Still I hit the snooze button and tried to re-enter dream world. Somehow reality seemed less real than my dream.

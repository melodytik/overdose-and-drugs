我注意到喉开始干燥 (幸好不是很糟糕), 视力也开始模糊. 我也感觉有点恶心, 但这只持续了几个小时, 几个小时后, (尤其是在黑暗中), 我能看到墙上有点的线条, 我的手指/手似乎被厚厚的粘性东西所覆盖, 如此多, 我会觉得需要每20分钟左右洗手 (甚至洗脸)! 尽管如此,我现在感觉自己很好, 我开始谈话, 几秒钟后就忘记了我打算说什么. 我也会发现 (主要是因为别人告诉我) 我说的是胡说八道. 人们还必须重复他们对我说的话, 因为我常常会忘记他们所说的话! 我有时会看镜子, 现在,它们已经大开了, 曲的线条会让我的脸看起来有不同的表情 (甚至完全变成了另一个人!) 这种感觉持续了两天 (我的注意力和记忆也一样). 在这个时期,我几乎炸毁了公寓,在放进一个果派,打开气体,并立即忘记点燃烤箱! 幸运的是,我的男朋友在半个小时后注意到了, 终于我恢复正常. 眼睛的瞳孔恢复到正常大小, 再花了两天才能正常阅读. 这种经历几乎与我对塞特拉林的实验相同 (只是没有四天的便秘或睡眠困难). 我打算再做一次... 只要我从医生那里得到更多的药片. 我会建议任何想跟随我的脚步的人, 药品 - 普罗西克利丁 (233):一般 (1),小组 (2-9) (17)

Mad Memory - But Fun! Procyclidine by Rebecca Citation:   Rebecca. "Mad Memory - But Fun!: An Experience with Procyclidine (exp5515)". Erowid.org . Jan 18, 2002. erowid.org/exp/5515 BODY WEIGHT: 60 kg These are an anti-cholinergic drug so after around half an hour, I noticed that I was getting a dry throat (fortunately not a particularly bad one) and my eyesight had started to become blurry. I also felt slightly sick, but this only lasted for a couple of hours and never actually made me throw up. Around an couple of hours later, (especially as it was dark), I was able to see little squiggly lines on the wall and my fingers/hands appeared to be covered in thick sticky stuff – so much so, that I would feel the need to wash my hands (and even my face) every 20 minutes or so! Despite this, I now felt pretty good within myself and was having a great time. My memory then became fractured, leaving me beginning a conversation and then forgetting what I had intended to say, just seconds afterward. I would also find (mainly as a result of people telling me) that I was speaking gibberish. People would also have to repeat what they were saying to me, as I would often forget what even they had said! When in this state, I will occasionally look in a mirror, just to check out my pupils. By now, they were well dilated and the wiggly lines would make my face appear to pull different expressions (or even change into someone else altogether!) I still felt pretty good, and this feeling lasted for another two days (as did my problem with concentration/memory). During this period, I almost blew up the flat, after putting in an apple pie, turning on the gas, and promptly forgetting to light the oven! Fortunately, my boyfriend noticed after about half an hour or so and opened a few windows. Eventually I returned to normal. It took me an extra two days before my pupils returned to their normal size and another before I could read properly again. This experience was almost the same as my experiments with sertraline (just without the 4 days of constipation, or the trouble sleeping). I enjoyed it immensely (despite nearly blowing myself up) and intend to do it again some time… just as soon as I have got some more tablets off the doctor. I would, however, advise anyone wishing to follow in my footsteps to make sure that they have someone around for the whole period of their intoxication.

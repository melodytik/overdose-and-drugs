下面是我对25H-NBOMe新RC的经验总结. 它是目前强大且流行的NBOMe系列 (25i,25c,25b) 的非计划替代品. 关于我:以前使用的迷幻药:25I-Nbome,25B-Nbome,LSD,,LSA. 在经验报告之前最近使用过迷幻药物:3个月. 在测试前3周内不服用处方药或任何药物. 从来没有处方Benzo或SSRI. 一个25B的三分之一或一半的标签是足以给我一个激烈的旅行8小时. 没有一个非常舒适和相当好控制的环境, 无论如何, 我收到了大量的25H标签, 我决定自己试一试. 我有3张纸,所以我从每张纸上剪下一个标签 (以测试只有一张纸有缺陷的可能性) 并找到一个合适和舒适的时间和环境来. 下午10:30 (T+0) 取出第一个标签,放在下唇和牙之间. 一个极其薄荷,苦,金属,灰的味道是立即注意到. 这种味道比我以前与其他Nbomes的经历"强烈"得多, 舌头,牙和下唇都非常麻木. 在此时没有视觉效应,眼睛扩大. T+45:另一个标签被取,前一个标签被丢弃. 我耳朵, 任何类型的毒品 (包括大麻和酒精) 的使用都会加剧这种情况. T+75:没有明显的视觉效应. 我和一些陪伴我的朋友们分享了一段无聊. 我开始感觉晚上有点好些. T+95:另一个标签被取,前一个标签被丢弃. T+120:没有明显的视觉效果,我吃了一些食物,因为我没有吃晚餐,喝了一些瓶装水. 我能够保持社交和说话, 没有其他NBOMs的便秘/恶心, 我还注意到他还有一些血管收缩, 但不像其他25B那么严重. 现在我的两个朋友分了一大块巧克力, 上楼. 我决定躺下来短暂发短信, 不久之后就入睡了. 我闭上眼睛, 看到一些轻微的视觉效果, 时间+210 (大约凌晨2点):此时我很容易入睡,与其他Nbomes不同, 结束 另一位朋友服用了9片 (9x2000mcg), 我无法想象摄入这么多新型甲胺是健康的 (当然也没有任何成本效益), 如果用另一种方式进行, 也许会更有效, 但这是一个我不觉得有必要探索的途径 (我不是吸气或注射的粉丝), 其他: 25H-NBOMe (819):小组 (2-9) (17),第一次 (2),一般 (1)

Nov 22, 2017. erowid.org/exp/102545 DOSE: T+ 0:00 1 hit buccal 25H-NBOMe (blotter / tab) T+ 0:45 1 hit buccal 25H-NBOMe (blotter / tab) T+ 1:20 smoked Cannabis T+ 1:35 1 hit buccal 25H-NBOMe (blotter / tab) BODY WEIGHT: 220 lb Below is a summary of my experiences with the new RC known as 25H-NBOMe. It is a currently unscheduled alternative to the powerful and popular primary NBOMe series (25i, 25c, 25b). A little about me: Previous psychedelics used: 25I-Nbome, 25B-Nbome, LSD, shrooms, LSA. Most recent use of psychedelics prior to experience report: 3 months. No prescription drugs or any medications in system up to 3 weeks prior to testing. Never prescribed a Benzo or SSRI. A third or a half of a tab of 25B is more than enough to give me an intense trip for 8 hours. A full tab is almost too 'face-meltingly' intense for me without a very comfortable and fairly well controlled environment. Anyways, I received a large quantity of 25H tabs to test amongst myself and friends. So after a large number of reports that the tabs were nearly or wholly ineffective, I decided to try them myself. I had 3 sheets, so I cut one tab from each sheet (to test against the possibility of just one sheet being defective) and found a suitable and comfortable time and environment to trip. 10:30PM (T+0) First tab is taken, and placed between lower lip and gum. An extremely minty, bitter, metallic, chalky taste is immediately noticed. It tasted much more 'intense' than my numerous previous experiences with other Nbomes, all of which just tasted metallic/bitter T+5: Tongue, gums, and lower lip are all very numb. T+30: No visual effects at this point, eyes are dilated. T+45: Another tab is taken, and the previous tab is discarded. T+55: My ears are ringing, which is always exacerbated by any kind of drug use (even cannabis or alcohol). T+75: No apparent visual effects felt. T+80: Got bored and shared a blunt with some friends that were accompanying me. T+90: Smoking helped, and I began to feel a little better about the evening. T+95: Another tab is taken, and the previous tab is discarded. T+120: No apparent visual effects, I ate some food, as I hadn't had any dinner that night, and drank some bottled water. I was able to remain social and talkative, didn't have the constipation/nausea of other NBOMes, and would have been perfectly fine to drive and/or operate heavy machinery. I also noticed that there was still some vasoconstriction, but not quite as bad as other 25B. T+180: Still no effects felt, and at this point my two friends split a large shroom chocolate between them, and went upstairs. After a very long week I was quite tired, so I decided to lay down and text for a while before falling asleep shortly thereafter. When I closed my eyes, there were mild visual effects, but nothing to write home about and easily surpassed by a good bong rip or some DXM. T+210 (around 2AM): By this point I fell asleep easily, unlike other Nbomes which would have caused 8ish hours of energy and distracting visuals on as little as a third of a tab. END Another friend of mine took 9 tabs (9x 2000mcg) and had a light and mild trip. I can't imagine that ingesting so much of a new Phenylethamine is healthy at all (and certainly not cost-effective by any means), and one would probably be considerably better off using a more documented NBOMe or other drugs. The high provided by this substance is nothing special and can easily be replicated or surpassed by a number of drugs that I can get at the pharmacy or my friendly neighborhood dealer. Perhaps it would be more effective if administered in a different manner, but that is an avenue I don't feel any need to explore (I'm not a fan of insufflation or injection), especially when other NBOMes are readily and cheaply available.

我决定如果有机会, 我会试试. 几年过去了 (我猜可能接近8年), 我曾经尝试过的其他迷幻药是 (我无法数得出),LSD (4次),2C-B (两次),以及DXM (无数次). 现在几个月前我对DMT有了新的兴趣. 我告诉了我的女朋友, 我们都对它的效果做了大量的研究 (她决定她也想尝试), 并且发现很容易获得全谱提取的东西 (在网上被称为吉姆果) 所以快速前进到采样时间. 我们都有点紧张, 但主要是非常兴奋 (这毕竟是迷幻的迷幻药) 我们不想第一次彻底突破, 我们每个人都把油性红色残留物装入一个小碗大麻中, 现在我只能描述我的经历, 开始我只能感觉到大麻 (事实上我发现大麻使吸烟变得更难), 我确实有一种与其他迷幻药物相关的焦虑感, 似乎一切都想爬行, 只是在几分钟内, 这就是那个碗. 所以几分钟后, 我们决定再试一次, 这次是用水器, 而不是用碗. 我的女朋友为我加热它, 我把一个体面的大小的滴. 我还能把它救下来, 继续我的打法, 一个漫长而缓慢的打法, 只是很奇怪) 然后我坐在椅子上... 这一次我感觉到极大的快乐从我脊椎的底部散发到我的身体的其他部分.... 我觉得自己太棒了, 视觉上我没有注意到太多的东西, 我无法形容这是一种多么令人惊叹的感觉. 呼吸也很不规律, 但这并不令人担忧, 因为我感到非常高兴. 我女朋友也在经历同样的经历. 现在我们已经"弄湿了脚"并"了解"药物是什么感觉, 这次我会服用两次 (比上一次小) 滴. 我们也回到了基线 (虽然只是很少) 我开始吸气, 慢慢地吸入, 然后她拿着来再次加热指甲, 我感觉到第二次击中时我头发轻了, 我尽可能地保持第二次击中. 我在呼气的时候发呆了. 我坐着闭上眼睛. 这种情绪不会持续很长时间, 感觉好像我身体的每一个分子,甚至是每一个原子都在如此强烈地振动, 以至于我会爆炸,或者分解成空气 (我的女朋友后来告诉我我一直抓住我的手臂和肚子, 我无法描述任何清晰的视觉效果, 因为我的身体高潮是如此压倒性的, 强烈的振动,强烈的温暖,我的胃感觉像一片暴风雨的海 (我有点担心吐, 这真是太糟糕了! 我能再次说话,一旦我下降一点,不觉得我即将脱离存在,但我仍然很难表达我的感觉 (尤其是没有试图让它听起来可怕) 整个旅程, 实际上所有较小的旅程, 没有时间扭曲, 总的来说, 没有一次旅行持续超过10分钟. 除了第一碗装满大麻之外, 我们轮流给药, 我会说我的两次击球比一次击球强三倍或四倍. 这种东西是激烈的,没有笑话. 她回来时, 眼满面, 看到的美丽, 尽管我们都没有突破, 我们确实打算再做一次, 突破, 这种化学物质值得所有应有的尊重, 其他: 发布时间:2021年5月3日

May 3, 2021. erowid.org/exp/108455 DOSE: T+ 0:00 1 bowl smoked Cannabis T+ 0:00 smoked Mimosa tenuiflora (extract) T+ 0:10 1 hit vaporized Mimosa tenuiflora (extract) T+ 0:20 2 hits vaporized Mimosa tenuiflora (extract) BODY WEIGHT: 230 lb Several years ago I heard about DMT from a friend and decided if I ever got the opportunity I would try it. The years passed (probably close to 8 years is my guess) and the opportunity never arose. Other psychedelics I have had the experience of trying are mushrooms (more times than I can count), LSD (4 times), 2C-B (twice), and DXM (countless times). Now a few months ago I got a new interest in DMT. I told my girlfriend about it, and we both did plenty of research on its effects (she decided she wanted to try it as well), and discovered how easy to acquire the things for a full spectrum extraction (jim jam as dubbed by the online masses) with nothing but food safe ingredients available all over the place, so thats what we did. So flash forward to sampling time. Both of us are a little nervous, but mostly very excited (this is the psychedelic of psychedelics after all) and we don't want to go for a full on breakthrough for our first time, we just want to dip our toes in. We each load an amount of the oily red residue on a small bowl of weed and commence to smoking..... From this point on I will only describe my experience as I did not experience my girlfriends experience. I feel nothing but the weed at first (in fact I found the weed to make it harder to smoke) but half way through the bowl it starts getting hard to smoke. I definitely have the anxious come up feeling associated with other psychedelics, especially mushrooms. Everything appears to want to crawl and things feel slightly trippy for just a few minutes.... Thats about it for that bowl. So a few minute later we decide to try again, this time using a dab rig instead of putting it on a bowl. My girlfriend heats it up for me and I take a respectable sized dab of it. The nail was a little too hot so it caught on fire at first, but I managed to save it and continue my hit, a long slow hit that tasted the way tire shops smell (which is kind of neutral in its enjoy-ability.... It's not bad, just weird) and I sit back in the recliner.... This time I felt immense euphoria radiating from the bottom of my spine through out the rest of my body.... I feel so amazing I really don't notice too much going on visually, but I definitely feel like I'm trippin. I cannot put into words how amazing this felt. Also, it was extremely hard to get up until it wears off a little bit (no more than five minutes), and my breathing felt irregular, but this was not concerning as I felt so wonderfully euphoric. It finally wears off and my girlfriend takes her turn, more or less having the same experience . Now that we had 'gotten our toes wet' and 'understood' what the drug felt like we decided we wanted to experience at least a little bit of the visuals people talk about... This time I'm taking two (smaller than the last one) dabs of it. We are back to baseline (though just barely) for this one as well. So my girlfriend heats the nail up for me again (this time I wait a second for it to cool before dabbing) and I start inhaling, just as long and slow, then she takes the bong to heat the nail up again and I hold the hit in until she hands it back to me and again I inhale long slow and deep. I feel light headed by the time I even start the second hit this time, and I hold the second hit in as long as I can. I'm tripping balls on the exhale. I sit back with my eyes shut. This does not last long, the euphoria is so intense it's a little scary. It feels as if every molecule, or indeed every atom in my body is vibrating so intensely that I'm just going to explode, or disintegrate into the air ( my girlfriend tells me later I kept grabbing at my arms and my stomach like I was trying to hold myself together) To my eyes, I am frying really hard, I can't describe any distinct visuals as my body high was so overwhelming I couldn't focus on anything else. Intense vibrations, intense warmth, my stomach feels like a stormy sea (I was concerned a little about throwing up, but I didn't) The only words I can utter when it hits me is the classic line you'll see on the live experiences on youtube.... Holy Fuckin Shit!!! I am able to speak again once I come down a little bit and don't feel like I'm about to disitegrate from existence, but I still have a hard time expressing how it felt (especially without trying to make it sound frightening) This entire trip, indeed all of the smaller ones as well, had no distortion of time, but was shockingly intense nonetheless. All in all, none of the trips lasted longer than ten minutes. Other than the first bowl loaded on top of the weed we took turns dosing, and we still did all three of these experiences within one hour. I would describe my two hits as easily three times or four times as strong as the one hit. This stuff is intense, no joke. My girlfriends did get to experience visuals on her two hits, and she came back in tears from the beauty of what she saw, mind you neither of us broke through, and we are both utterly astonished by what we experienced. We do intend to do this again and break through, but I am very grateful for our caution. This chemical deserves all due respect, as even a threshold dose is indescribably powerful.

我第一次购买PIHKAL时, 这部分的标题是"第二本:化学故事". 我看了许多迷幻类胺, 想到我永远不会有幸品尝其中的几种. 在阅读2C-T-2和2C-T-7时, 关于2C-T-21的"定性评论"让它听起来像一个令人愉快的化学物质. 虽然它听起来不像2C-T-2或2C-T-7那么美妙, 我认为没有人会去制造这种药物. 后来有一天,我很幸运地有了一些. 在PIHKAL之外,几乎没有关于该药物的信息. 这让我感到担忧,也让我感到兴奋. 我担心其中的风险. 我很高兴能成为第一个尝试这种药物的人. 虽然我 (和其他人) 发现舒尔金对他的剂量非常谨慎, 我选择了10毫克, 这是他给出的平均剂量. 我小心地衡量了我的剂量, 放进一个空囊里. 接着我去了工作, 坐在那里数了几分钟, 我将利用这段时间来指出两件事. 首先,我刚刚 (再次) 戒烟, 我感觉身体不太好 (这有点矛盾). 我在晚上10点15分离开工作. 我打算和一些朋友一起度过这个晚上. 我们决定在当地肯塔基炸店见面, 决定要去哪里. 首先R和我回到我的房子,所以我可以改变我的工作衣服. 我打开冰箱里的一瓶水. 我拿起我的小药,深呼吸,吞下了它. 时间是晚上10点半. 我们走出去,进入里斯的车. 对于十月的一个晚上,天气特别温暖. 我们开车去肯塔基炸店, 我们有八个人,包括R和我. 我们去了餐厅订购食物. 我一整天都禁食, 所以和油脂的味道让我口水. 我知道许多2C-T-X的产品恶心, 添加炸食物可能不会帮助. 我决定只要有恶心, 我下了个订单,很快就来了, 食物非常好,并没有造成任何胃部问题. 除了R,我和我的朋友M, 其他人都在上大学. 所以我们在餐厅里花了时间分享最近发生的事情. 我真的很想每天和他们一起出去玩. 这让我更加珍惜我们在一起的时光. 现在我注意到我的思维有点改变了. 我看着我的手表,现在是11:30. 我看着餐厅的地板, 看到微微的闪. 甚至可能会出现非常微小的模式. 我目前在一个+1. 我们在午夜前离开肯塔基炸. 这种药物确实有效, 但它的效果仍然相当不明确. 我所感受到的绝对是迷幻的. 如果我试着,我可以迷失在思想中. 我还没有找到任何推动力. 每个人都决定去M的家. 我回到R的车,我们走了. 我闭上眼睛看看有没有发生什么. 我眼背后有生动的几何图案. 经过一段时间, 图案会改变, 形成一个道. 我沿着R的驾驶沿着道移动. 当她转身时,我也是. 这真是令人愉快. 在M家,我们有些人出去抽烟. 我当时的体温是+2,感觉还不错. 我还没有注意到任何负面的副作用. 我每次轮到的时候, 都会深吸大麻. 我一口气,我的一个朋友笑了笑,说:'我为你感到骄傲,这是一个大成功. 在里面,更多的故事出现了. 这一次,它们更有趣,更激烈. 虽然我现在已经达到+3级, 我不知道是因为大麻还是2C-T-21, 最后一个让我发出笑声的精神药物是青素. 无论哪种因素对我的行为起了更大的作用, 人们开始走出去. 我说再见,R和我离开了. 旅行回家的路程和旅行到那里的路程非常相似. 我一直在玩道游戏. 我们到达我的房子,进去,然后下楼. 现在的视觉效果更强烈了. 在房间周围有明显的模式和摇摆. 虽然它们的视觉效果并不像2C-T-7那样强烈, 我和R看了一段时间的电视. 虽然电视通常会让我无聊, 我笑得很大声, R一直提醒我安静下来, 在节目结束后,我和R做爱了. 2C-T-21在性环境中表现出色. 亲密感的水平高出了天花板, 触觉的感觉也大大增强了. 整个表演是如此美丽, 我几乎无法处理. 2C-T-21绝对是我服用的最好的药物之一, 我在晚上带R走到她的车里, 我拥抱她时注意到月亮. 这是一个满月, 穿过云层, 投射光线带到处. 一个似乎是照着我们. 这似乎照亮了我们在一起的一切. 我吻了她晚安,然后回到楼下. 我打开电脑,进入IRC. 时间凌晨2点过去了, 似乎没有人上线. 在AOL即时通讯上也没有人醒着. 在这个时候发生了一件很少发生在我身上的幻觉药物. 我开始感到无聊. 如果我愿意,我可以停下来更深入地思考, 但它似乎不吸引我. 我把Radiohead的"OK Computer"放上了, 音乐似乎让事情变得更加精彩, 听完这张CD,我把披头士乐队的"阿比路"放上. 由于我在高中缺乏努力, 我也想起了我所有的教授, 我也想到了我的成绩. 我目前的最低成绩是A-这对我来说是绝对的例外. 我为自己感到骄傲. 我第一次有动力去努力实现自己的目标. 这让我很高兴. 想到这些之后, 我看了看钟. 这是在凌晨4点之前. 视觉效果仍然存在, 鉴于PIHKAL列出的时间, 我预计我现在正在下降. 我服用了150毫克的二胺和6毫克的黑激素, 旅行结束时, 我往往倾向于听音乐或只是思考. 但我又一次感到无聊. 我打开我的游戏机, 玩"超级马里奥阳光". 我曾经尝试过玩电子游戏, 这一次我很喜欢. 我觉得自己更了解游戏, 我意识到在过去的两个小时里, 我一直玩到凌晨5点半左右, 我在下午3点醒来,洗,然后去上班. 现在,我们可以把这件事情说出来. 这将是一个很难写的结论. 我担心我会写的东西可能会让人们用它来快速钱. 我希望人们能更负责任地使用这份报告来教育自己. 尽管如此, 2C-T-21 是我使用过的最好的社交药物之一. 让人们想说话的方面与MDMA相当. 整体而言,这次旅行绝对没有负面的副作用. 完全没有恶心. 没有令人烦的身体负荷, 视觉方面是有趣的, 在我看来,它与2C-C视觉相似. 这种药物唯一真正的问题是精神方面. 对于我来说,2C-T-21提供了很少的精神刺激. 我感觉更像是吸了一支烟或者使用了麻醉剂. 我并没有觉得自己很愚蠢, 但玩电子游戏或看电视比思考更有价值. 我最喜欢的迷幻药是那些让我深入思考的药物. 2C-T-21对我来说是没有的. 我可以自我审视, 但没有这样的欲望. 我当时并没有真正理解, 也没有关心, 任何让我坐下来感到无聊的迷幻药都不是我最喜欢的. 我会再次使用这个吗? 我肯定会这样. 这绝对有很好的感觉. 它实际上让我想起了很多2C-C. 视觉和身体感受与我非常相似. 唯一的区别在于2C-C让你有了深入思考的选择. 2C-T-21几乎试图让我避免这样的想法. 也许另一个试验可能会产生不同的结果. 我想在音乐会或聚会上尝试这种药物. 这种药物似乎更像是派对玩具. 虽然2C-T-21不是我最喜欢的药物之一, 其他: 2C-T-21 (290):一般性 (1),烂的经验 Oct 13, 2003. erowid.org/exp/27542 DOSE: 10 mg oral 2C-T-21 (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 165 lb October 11th, 2003 When I first bought PIHKAL one of the things that caught my attention was the long list of chemicals in the back of the book. The section was entitled 'Book II: The Chemical Story'. I looked at many of the psychedelic phenethylamines and thought to myself about how many of them I would never have the privilege of tasting. When reading up on 2C-T-2 and 2C-T-7, I followed the list of 2C-T-X compounds until I reached 2C-T-21. The 'Qualitative Comments' on 2C-T-21 made it sound like a pleasant chemical. While it didnï¿½t sound as wonderful as 2C-T-2 or 2C-T-7 sounded, it still seemed like something I would like to try. I assumed though that no one would ever bother making this drug. Then one day, I had the good fortune on having some. There was little to no information about this drug which existed outside of PIHKAL. This both worried me, and excited me. I worried about the risks involved. But I was excited thinking that I would be among the first people to try this drug. While I (and others) have found Shulgin to be very conservative with his doses I decided it would be best to follow his guidelines, until more about this drug was known. I opted to go with 10mg, which was the average dose of his given spectrum. I carefully weighed out my dose and put it inside of an empty capsule. Then I headed off to work where I sat counting the minutes until I could leave and start my adventure. I'll take this time to note two things. First, I have just quit smoking cigarettes (again), so my body was feeling a bit healthier. But in contrast to that, I have been suffering from a cold, so I do not feel physically great (somewhat of a contradiction). I got out of work at 10:15 PM. I was planning on spending the evening with some friends. I talked to my friends on the phone telephone and we decided to meet at the local Kentucky Fried Chicken, and decide where we would go. First R and I went back to my house, so I could change out of my work clothes. When I finished changing, I opened a bottle of water from my refrigerator. I got my little pill, took a deep breath, and swallowed it. The time was 10:30 PM. We went outside and got into Rï¿½s car. It was exceptionally warm for a night in October. We drove over to Kentucky Fried Chicken, where we found our friends waiting. There were eight of us, including R and myself. We went into the restaurant to order food. I had fasted all day, so the smell of chicken and grease made my mouth water. I knew many of the 2C-T-X's produce nausea and that adding fried food probably wouldnï¿½t help. But I was so hungry I decided that I would just deal with the nausea if it came. I placed my order, which came quickly, and I sat down to eat. The food was very good, and didn't create any stomach problems. With the exception of R, myself, and my friend M, all of the others are away at college. They had all returned home for the weekend though, so our time in the restaurant was spent sharing stories of recent things that had happened. I really do miss hanging out with all of them on a daily basis. It makes me cherish the time we have together even more. I notice at this moment that my thinking is a bit altered. I look at my watch and it is 11:30. I look over at the floor panels of the restaurant, and see slight shimmering. There may even be very slight patterning occurring. I'm at a +1 at the moment. We leave Kentucky Fried Chicken just before midnight. The drug is definitely working, but its effects feel quite undefined still. What I feel is definitely psychedelic. There are definitely some visuals, and I can lose myself in thought if I try to. I don't find any bit of a push yet though. Everyone decides to go to M's house. I get back into R's car, and we head off. I close my eyes to see if anything is going on. Vivid geometric patterns dance behind my eyelids. After awhile the patterns shift and create a tunnel. I move down the tunnel along with R's driving. When she makes a turn, so do I. It was really pleasant. At M's house, some of us went outside to smoke a joint. I was at a +2 at this point, and feeling pretty good. I had yet to note any negative side effects. Whenever it is my turn I take deep lungfulls of marijuana smoke. When I exhaled, one of my friends laughed and said 'I'm proud of you son, that was a big hit.' This made me laugh uncontrollably. Back inside, more stories came out. This time though, they were funnier, and more intense. I was now at a +3, but still very much in control. I donï¿½t know if it was the marijuana or the 2C-T-21, but I was very giggly. The last psychedelic that made me go off into fits of laughter was psilocybin. Regardless of which was playing more of a role in my behavior, I was really enjoying myself. A little after 1 AM people started to head out. I said goodbye and R and I left. The ride home was much like the ride there. I played the tunnel game the entire ride. We reached my house, went inside, and headed down to my basement. The visuals were stronger now. There was noticeable patterning and wiggling all around the room. At no point did they become as intense as 2C-T-7 visuals, but they were nice to look at nonetheless. R and I watched TV for awhile. 'Crank Yankers' was on Comedy Central, and while TV usually bores me when I'm tripping, I found it absolutely hilarious. I was laughing so loud that R kept reminding me to quiet down, or else wake my parents. After the show ended R and I made love. 2C-T-21 worked excellent in a sexual setting. The intimacy levels went through the roof, and the tactile sensations were greatly enhanced. The whole act was so beautiful that I almost couldn't handle it. 2C-T-21 is definitely one of the best drugs I've taken as far as enhancing sex. I walked R out to her car in the night, which was still warm. While I hugged her I noticed the moon. It was a full moon, which pierced through the clouds, casting bands of light everywhere. One seemed to be shining right down on us. It seemed to illuminate everything we have together. I kissed her goodnight, and I came back downstairs. I went to my computer and went on IRC. It was after 2 AM and no one seemed to be on. On AOL Instant Messenger no one was awake either. At this point something happened that rarely happens to me on a psychedelic. I became bored. I could stop and think more introspectively if I chose to, but it didnï¿½t seem to appeal to me. I put on Radiohead's 'OK Computer', which has a beautiful sound to it. The music seemed to enhance things a bit, but I was still bored. When the CD ended, I put on the Beatles 'Abbey Road.' At this point I began to think about my life. Currently I am enrolled in a community college, due to my lack of effort in high school. I thought about the professors I have and how fortunate it is that I get along with all of them. I also thought about my grades. My lowest grade at the moment is an A-, which for me is absolutely exceptional. I was very proud of myself. For the first time in my life, I was motivated to actually work to achieve my goals. This made me happy. After I finished thinking about all that, I looked at the clock. It was just before 4 AM. Visuals were still there, but they seemed slightly milder. Given the duration PIHKAL lists, I expected I was now on the decline. I took 150mg of diphenhydramine and 6mg of melatonin, and headed into my room to lie down. Usually when I go into my room as the trip winds down, I tend to listen to music, or just think. But again, I was bored. I turned on my Gamecube (video game console) and played 'Super Mario Sunshine'. I have tried playing video games tripping before, and I got very bored with them. This time however, I loved it. I felt more in tune with the game, and I seemed to be able to anticipate enemy moves. I realized that for the past two hours, I've felt more like I was stoned that tripping. I played my game until around 5:30 AM, where I drifted off into sleep. I woke up at 3 PM, where I showered, and headed off to work. ------------------------------------------------------- This is going to be a tough conclusion to write. I fear what I will write may make this the next chemical people use to make a quick buck. I just hope people will be more responsible than that, and use this report to educate themselves. That being saidï¿½ 2C-T-21 was one of the best socializing drugs I have ever used. It is up there with MDMA in the aspect of making people want to talk. The overall trip had absolutely no negative side effects. There was no nausea at all. There was no annoying body load, which is often attributed to 2C-T-X drugs. The visual aspect was entertaining, but at no point overwhelming. It was similar to 2C-C visual in my opinion. The only real problem with this drug is the mental aspect. For me 2C-T-21 offered little mental stimulation. I felt more as if I had smoked a joint or used a narcotic. I didn't feel stupid, but doing things such as playing video games of watching TV was more rewarding than thinking. My favorite psychedelics have been ones which offered me good introspection. 2C-T-21 lacked that for me. I could be introspective, but the desire to be wasn't there. I didn't really understand, nor care at the time, but in retrospect, that part let me down. Any psychedelic that allows me to sit and be bored doesn't make the top of my list. Will I use this again? I'm sure I will. It definitely had a nice feeling to it. It actually reminded me a lot of 2C-C. The visuals and body feeling were very similar to me. The only difference was that 2C-C gives you the option of thinking deeply. 2C-T-21 almost tried to make me avoid such thinking. Perhaps another trial might yield different results. I think I would like to try this drug at a concert or party. At the current time, this drug seems more like a party toy than anything else. 2C-T-21 is not one of my favorites, but it was a friendly drug nonetheless, and it was a good experience.

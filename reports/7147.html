美国快递公司的员工刚刚给我带来了一种商业产品, 这是一种"合法高"的草药组合,声称含有LSA作为活性成分 (LSA是晨光种子中的活性成分). 晚上大约10点半, 我服用了六个囊, 半个小时后我开始感到臭名昭著的"晨光"恶心. 我开始感觉有点困惑, 感觉有所变化, 如果我躺在床上,看着天花板, 我也开始觉得有点醉了, 性欲激起 (我认为这是产品中的其他草药造成的, 而不是LSA). 我在电脑上安装了"迷幻屏幕保护程序" (我有三重显示器, 现在大约是凌晨1点半, 我用了四次深度的沙尔维亚并没有真正感觉到任何效果,所以我开始打包我的吸烟器. 我看到许多长线的幻灯光 (眼睛开). 我闭上眼睛, 忽然变成一块石头, 墙上画着一个小人, 我是子, 整个时间我是, 就好像我脱离了我的身体, 在房间的墙上旋转. 房间里还有一个小男孩, 我不知道我坐了多长时间看着墙, 但它可能不到五分钟. 在我倒在床上,再次闭上眼睛之前. 这次是通往太空的道. 终于站起来, 写下发生了什么事的简短描述 (虽然我还在). 我睡觉了 (虽然我一段时间没睡觉, 这可能是我一生中最奇怪的旅行. 我之前也曾单独和大麻一起服用过沙维亚神, 我真的不记得发生了什么事, 现在是第二天下午2点15分, 其他: 圣 (44),LSA (65):单独 (16),组合 (3)

Jul 2, 2002. erowid.org/exp/7147 DOSE: T+ 0:00 6 capsls oral LSA T+ 3:00 3 hits smoked Salvia divinorum (plant material) BODY WEIGHT: 180 lb It was a Tuesday night and the UPS man had just brought me a commercial product claiming to contain LSA that I ordered. It is a 'legal high' compilation of herbs that claims to contain LSA as the active ingredient (LSA is the active ingredient in Morning Glory seeds). At approximately 10:30 PM I took six capsules and retired to the bedroom. About a half hour later I started to feel the infamous Morning Glory nausea. An hour went by and I started to feel a bit trippy with various perception changes, light trails, and a little macroscopia. If I laid on the bed and looked at the ceiling it would seem to bow up like the room was curved. It was now about 12:30 AM and I had also started to feel kind of drunk and sexually arroused (I think this was caused by the other herbs in the product and not by the LSA). I then started to listen to some Velvet Underground and put The Psychedelic Screensaver on my computer(I have a triple monitor setup and the screensaver was spanned across all three in a completely dark room; it did wonders for my trip). It was now approximately 1:30 AM and for some reason I decided to smoke some Salvia Divinorum. I took four deep hits of Salvia and didn't really feel any effect so I started to pack up my smoking apparatus. It was then I looked up and saw many long lines of psychadelic light (with open eyes). I closed my eyes and I suddenly became a brick in a large multicolored wall. Painted on the wall was a blocky little man and I was a brick going in his foot area. The entire time I was the brick it was as if I was disconnected from my body and was circling around the room with the wall. Also, there was a small boy in the room which seem to be commanding me where to go. I have no idea how long I sat watching the wall but it was probably less then five minutes. At this time I got up and managed to put my smoking apparatus away before I collapsed on the bed and closed my eyes again. As soon as I closed my eyes I was in another multicolored room and this time there was a tunnel which seemed to go into space. I sat with my eyes closed looking thru the room for some time and finally got up and managed to write a brief description of what had happened(though I was still tripping pretty hard). Somehow it had gotten to be about 3:15 AM so I went to bed (though I didn't get to sleep for a while and managed to have a few more experiences that I can't remember while in bed). This was probably the strangest trip of my life. I had done Salvia Divinorum alone and with marijuana before but it was nowhere near as intense as it was while on LSA. I really can't remember much about what happened or how I felt after I smoked the Salvia, I just remember the visions I saw.

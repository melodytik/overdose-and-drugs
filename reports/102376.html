美国 自从我看到这颗小黄药... 芬迪米特拉具有经过个人验证的娱乐价值,寿命中等至高 (大约3-6小时). 效果似乎像阿德拉尔和可卡因之间的混合物, 不太关注牙磨牙和微妙的动脉缩, 而更多关注健康 - 身体急于高兴. 一个黄色的35毫克爆发,另一半 (15-25毫克) 吸入. 坐在我的房间里看电视. 在接下来的10分钟内, 效果开始显现. 一开始看上去是幻觉般的视力增强, 很快就会升级为阳光中充满热情的能量感 (在身体上类似于吞下一滴高品质可卡因, 我似乎已经深入到一种高的幸福和幸福的清醒 (疲劳的缓解, 微妙而完美的警觉, 没有浪费大量的上腺素储备的感觉) 和明亮的HD视觉. 我走到我的表妹家里吸草. 我觉得太阳的温暖抚摸着我的身体,脸上带着平静的微笑,说'我很高兴活着,你今天的早晨是什么样子?' 我没有注意到我在抽烟,直到我在11月7日停下来购买香烟, 但是在一种类似片的幸福状态下, 有能力运行高储量的能量. 我到达我的表妹的房子和吸一块 (1克两个头) 这就像没有魔法的MDMA, 没有降落的可卡因, 伴随着我保持清醒的状态, 走在街上,跳跃,笑得很开心. 我在一个纯粹的舒适的持续刺激状态中. 我开始调整... 有很多... 我感觉到我的眼睛和耳朵的压力升高了. 我可以听到血液在我的子. 我只是在玩. 接下来的30分钟与表妹的电视节目包括:起我的腿,把它们放下,坐起来,躺下,站起来,去厨房, 触摸我的脸,触摸我的肚子,再次触摸我的脸,站起来,看沙发,坐下来,等等... 后来我发现是因为我的高剂量以及大麻证明自己是强化剂. 我认为既然我已经玩了他们一点我可以提高我的剂量,但没有意识到这些小药可以有多强烈... 他们不会发展耐受性很快我猜 .. 我只剩下麻醉后的后果... 太阳交叉体中一种温暖的感觉与呼吸,吞以及任何对我温暖面部刺激的反应产生共. 我有平静的能量储备, 后效应保持到第二天, 只有清醒和温暖的平静. 详细评论: 任何对安非他命的耐药性都不会影响芬迪米特. 不需要服用超过35-50毫克才能感受到效果,与任何药物一样,你应该自己决定你的剂量应该是多少,你应该安全地开始 (35毫克是减肥的处方剂量). 吸烟后我感受到的令人难以置信的刺激, 已使用的证券

Nov 25, 2015. erowid.org/exp/102376 DOSE: T+ 0:00 35 mg oral Phendimetrazine (pill / tablet) T+ 0:00 17 mg insufflated Phendimetrazine (pill / tablet) T+ 2:00 smoked Cannabis BODY WEIGHT: 173 lb Since the second I set sight on this little yellow pill... Phendimetrazine has a personally proven recreational value, with a moderate lived high (roughly 3-6 hours). The effects seemed like a cross between adderall and cocaine, with less focus on teeth grinding and subtle ataxia and more focus on a wellness - body rushing euphoria. T:0:00 One yellow 35mg popped, and half of another (15-25MG) insufflated. Sitting in my room watching television. T:+0:30 Effects beginning to come apparent over the course of the next ten minutes. What starts with a seemingly psychedelic brightening of vision soon escalates into a euphoric rushing sense of energy in the solar plexus (similar physically to swallowing a drip of high quality cocaine, or the initial come-up from a strong dose of amphetamine.) The rushing effect lasts anywhere from 20 minutes to an hour. T:+1:30 I seemed to have delved into a plateau of euphoric well being and being blissfully awake (Relief of fatigue, subtle yet perfect amount of alertness without the feeling of wasting massive reserves of norepinephrine) and brightly painted HD vision. T:+2:00 I took a walk over to my cousin's house to smoke some weed. On this trip I felt the warmth of the sun caress my body with a peaceful smile on my face that just says 'I'm glad to be alive, how's your morning?' I didn't notice I was tweaking until I stopped at 7/11 to buy cigarettes and realized I was somewhat tweaked, my voice was slightly higher and I was slightly more anxious, but was in an opiate-like blissful state with the ability to operate with high reserves of energy. I arrive at my cousin's house and smoke a blunt (1 Gram on two heads) T:+2:45 It was like MDMA without the magic, Cocaine without the comedown, and a calming euphoria of Heroin without the Hypnotism.. A caressing warmness and a happy bliss in combination with the urge to run down the street and jump around laughing at the top of my lungs, paired with the lucidity to maintain myself. The rushing sensation began again and I was in a pure state of comfortable constant stimulation. T:+3:00 I began to tweak... A lot... I felt like I could actually perceive the pressure in my eyes and ears elevate. I could hear the blood pumping in my neck. I was fucking tweaking. The next thirty minutes of TV-Time with my cousin for me consisted of: Kick up my legs, put them back down, sit up, lay back down, stand up, go to the kitchen and... just go back to the couch. Touch my face, touch my stomach, touch my face again, get up, look at couch, sit back down, etc.... At first I thought it was just because of the marijuana, but later found out it was because of my high dose as well as marijuana proving itself as a potentiator. I figured that since I've been playing with them for a little I could up my dose, but wasn't aware of how intense these little pills could be... They don't develop tolerance that quickly I guess.. T:+4:00 The tweaking has now subsided, and I am left with just the after-effects with the marijuana high... A warmness feeling in the solar plexus resonates with breath, swallowing, and any response to stimulation of my warm face. I have calming reserves of energy and the after-effects kept until the next day, after effects being very reminiscent to low dose of adderall. Just alertness and a warm calm. Extensive Commentary : Any drug tolerance to Amphetamines does not affect Phendimetrazine. There's no need to take more than 35-50 Mg to feel the effects, as with any drug you should decide yourself what your dosage should be, and you should start safely (35Mg is prescribed dose for weight loss). The incredible tweaking sensation I experienced after smoking was simply because I had taken a large amount more than I would if I were going to smoke. I wouldn't smoke again to potentiate 70+ mg from the tweaky experience I witnessed, plus I find it recreational at low dose, with or without marijuana.

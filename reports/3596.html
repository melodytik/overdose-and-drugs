Nov 2, 2000. erowid.org/exp/3596 DOSE: 1 tablet oral Bad/Suspect Ecstasy BODY WEIGHT: 180 lb Whelp, A few weeks ago I was at a large outdoor rave in the Austin area, and I bought a roll with a mitsubishi stamp on it since I had a good experience with a similar pill a while ago. I am relatively sure now that it was PMA. It was, in short, pretty boring. There was no MDMA euphoria, just a really strong body rush that lasted about an hour, and a REALLY fast heartbeat. I can't say that I had a bad time, it was just a waste of time and money. I didn't feel that I was in any danger, but if I had taken two, I'm pretty sure I would have been. My advice is to just stay away from the mitsubishis altogether, its not worth it.

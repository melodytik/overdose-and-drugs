我从晚上11点左右开始,我的表哥在这个城市度过周末. 虽然我们玩的是任天堂64游戏, 他昏倒在楼下的沙发上, 但我还没有觉得好好睡觉, 我上楼去我的房间, 称重大约5毫克, 我决定从5毫克开始, 因为这是我在一些药物讨论论坛上摸索后, 我无法将事件按时间顺序排列, 因为这是几周前的事, 我还觉得有点梦幻, 但我的思维模式已经恢复正常. 幻觉... 是轻微的. 我感觉到一种强烈的感觉和正念的感觉, 视觉上没有太多, 没有任何真正的扭曲, 但绝对没有什么戏剧性的. 我第一次注意到这种现象是在外面抽烟时. 我听到房子的声音和类似的声音, 我记得我做了一些事情,比如爬楼梯或翻开门把手, 身体有些麻木, 有时有点. 我试图做一些按摩, 想看看我是否有超人力量, 我注意到有时我做的事情有点太强烈而没有注意到, 幸运的是,我没有在不知不觉中掉我的脸, 我得到的头脑空间很奇怪, 最接近我可以总结的是DXM+LSD与邪恶的疯狂的暗示. 有点混乱, 思想不稳定. 我仍然可以在脸书上和朋友进行连贯的对话, 我只是记得在山顶上排便后, 我看起来像一个不同的人, 但仍然是相同的, 我开始微笑, 没有什么特别的原因, 十秒钟后,这种冲动消失了, 但当我着自己的时候, 我认为这可能是我完全失去我的时刻所以我走了. 音乐对我来说听起来几乎和一半剂量DXM听起来一样. 能够听到歌曲中的微妙细节, 并立即单独跟随不同旋律. 每一点声音都被注意到并受到赞赏. 偶尔我会开始感到自己变得疯狂. 让我们做大量的按摩, 因为我不知道我真的是最伟大的. 尽管如此,我并没有服用那么强的剂量,当我开始乘坐那辆火车时, 我不想做任何鲁的事情. 我感受到的最奇怪的效果是对一切的熟悉感和怀旧感. 我看着像电池这样的东西, 然后想到自己', 自从我第一次看到电池以来, 我从来没有这样看过它, 但总体而言,你已经明白了. 接下来的几天, 除了下一天早上感到头之外, 只是为了纪录,几天后我了一次, 味道有点像PCP. 总体来说是相当温和的, 我得到了坚定的掌握什么药物是. 我很可能会再试10-15毫克, 肯定是在一个知道自己在做什么的旅行保姆的监督下. 对于我和我的强迫症倾向的品味来说, 其他: 3-MeO-PCP (558):音乐讨论 (22),第一次 (2),小组 (2-9) (17)

Apr 17, 2016. erowid.org/exp/106521 DOSE: oral Alcohol - Beer/Wine 5 mg oral 3-MeO-PCP (capsule) BODY WEIGHT: 150 lb I started around 11pm, my cousin was in town for the weekend. He was pretty wasted while we were playing some nintendo 64 games. He passed out on the couch downstairs but I didn't feel quite like hitting the sack yet, I had only had a couple beers. I went upstairs to my room and weighed out roughly 5mg, dumped it in an empty gelatin capsule and down the hatch. I decided to start with 5mg because that's what I gathered would be about half of a common dose after fumbling around a couple drug discussion forums. I am unable to put the events together in chronological order because it was a couple weeks ago but from what I remember I started feeling the threshold effects around 20 minutes in, about an hour is when it started to peak and about 2 1/2 - 3 hours in was when I noticed it was starting to fade out. 4 hours in, still felt a bit dreamy but my thought patterns had (mostly) returned to normal. Hallucinations... were mild. I felt the sort of heightened senses and feelings of mindfulness I get on acid or other psyches. Visually not much, no real distortions of any kind, but there was a slight 80's vibe to everything color/texture wise. But definitely nothing dramatic. Audio hallucinations were present, I first noticed when I went outside for a smoke. I heard sounds of the house creaking and similar noises that normally don't happen so frequently if at all. I remember doing things like stepping on stairs or turning a door knob and the sounds they made were a bit different than how I normally hear them. My body felt pretty numb, a bit tingly at times. I attempted to do some pushups to see if I had superhuman strength but only ended up doing a bit more than normal. I noticed sometimes I was doing things a bit too strongly without noticing, like pressing buttons on my phone kinda hard or cracking my knuckles too far. Fortunately I didn't chew the shit out of my cheek without realizing it like I always do on molly. The head-space I got was strange, the closest I could come to summing it up is DXM + LSD with a hint of evil madness. Somewhat disorganized and racing thoughts. I could still have a coherent conversation with a couple friends on facebook and do pretty much anything I felt like while looking sober. Elaborating on the 'evil madness': I just remember after taking a dump on the peak, walking over to the mirror and staring at myself for a bit. I looked like different person but still the same, can't describe it really. Then I started smiling for no reason in particular, and felt a small urge to punch the mirror as hard as possible. The urge subsided about 10 seconds later, but as I was staring at myself the left side of my mouth and eye did kind of a twitch which was extremely unsettling. I thought for a second that this might be the moment I completely lose my shit so I walked away. For most of the trip I ended up listening to music, and music sounded almost exactly to me as it does on a 1st/2nd plat dose of DXM. More alive, able to hear all the subtle details that are worked into songs and follow all the different melodies separately at once. Every little bit of sound is noticed and appreciated. Occasionally I could start to feel myself becoming manic. It was that wild slightly-too-high-amphetamine-dose rollercoaster feeling of 'let's do a shit ton of pushups because.. i don't know god damn I really just am the greatest'. Nevertheless I wasn't on that strong of a dose, when I started on that train I was able to quickly remind myself to slow down, I'm not superman I'm just high on PCP. I don't want to do anything reckless. The most peculiar effect I felt was a sense of familiarity and nostalgia with everything. I'd look at something like a battery and then think to myself 'wow I've never looked at that battery this way since the first time I saw it, it almost feels foreign or alien, and as if all my preconceived notions and ideas about what that battery was were just reset'. Nonsensical stoner babble perhaps, but you get the overall idea. The following days I didn't really notice any afterglow effects besides feeling hungover the next morning. But for the record I burped once a couple days after and it kinda tasted like PCP. Overall it was pretty mild, I got a solid grip on what the drug was like. Most likely I will try it again at 10-15mg, definitely under the supervision of a trip sitter who knows what he/she's doing. After the next trip, meh, it's an interesting experience and all but much to unpredictable for me and my OCD tendencies' tastes.

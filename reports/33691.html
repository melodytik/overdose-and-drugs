我16岁, 在这次经历之前, 我已经尝试过很多药物, 我曾经服用过一些"更强烈"的药物, 首先,我和一个朋友在他的家里做着平常的事情. 我们很无聊,没有草,所以我拿了一个吸入器. 我从来没有试过吸入吸入器, 汽油给了我可能是最惊人的旅行我有过. 我看了一下,发现药物叫阿尔布特罗尔. 我们开始从吸入器中获取. 一次抽三口, 保持呼吸和呼气. 几乎像其他吸入剂一样, 我立刻感到一种高兴感, 经过大约20次吸气, 我们停了下来. 我心跳很快,我也在. 我的朋友告诉我他感觉同样, 我肩, 十五分钟后,我们发现一支在一周前抽过的烟草. 我们抽了,大约是一碗,但足够好的草让我们高. 我很快就感觉到了草开始起作用. 我们听了一些音乐,放松了一会儿. 我和朋友着一些可视化图片很长一段时间. 我发现我对音乐有很大的欣赏. 我们决定再次使用吸入器. 我感觉很好, 唯一不愉快的感觉是我的手臂和手的. 我觉得我的血糖有点低,所以我吃了一些东西, 我们继续做着相同的抽习惯, 我感到高兴,有一些道视觉. 我周围的视力非常模糊, 艾布特罗尔使我能够轻松地专注于某些声音, 开始时缓慢, 速度逐渐加快, 如果我继续专注于声音小白点出现和飞行四处快像火虫或什么. 它们很快就充满了我的视野, 在这一点上,我要么醒来,要么进入另一个维度. 我停下来抽一支烟. 我心脏开始跳得很快, 就像我的心脏在我的身体上产生反弹效应. 我的朋友也感觉同样. 我们开玩笑了一会儿, 希望它会过去. 十分钟过去了,我的心跳会变得更快, 我和朋友用耳机听着对方的心跳, 我们等待,等待,等待,效果没有通过. 我知道这是吸入器中的药物,我几乎肯定是阿尔布特罗尔的副作用, 这很像是严重发烧的经历. 我听到的每一个噪音都让我感到愤怒. 几乎不可能看电视. 我睡着了. 很难解释我在睡觉时的感受. 我通常会偶尔做梦或根本不做梦, 我记得这件事是因为我必须专注于每一次呼吸, 这是一个最糟糕的感觉我有过. 经历了这场痛苦之后, 我跑去所, 开始猛烈吐. 我感觉好了,心跳几乎恢复正常, 我发现自己有点疲, 副作用正如我所预料. 我那天晚上所感受到的一切都是由于阿尔布特罗尔药物. 只有心脏停止和发作没有影响, 第二天早上我的朋友也没事只是有点. 他说他睡着了大约20分钟后进入床上,感觉很好. 这绝不是一个有趣的经历. 时间表: 33691

My Heart Will Go On Albuterol by Anonymous Citation:   Anonymous. "My Heart Will Go On: An Experience with Albuterol (exp33691)". Erowid.org . Jun 16, 2007. erowid.org/exp/33691 DOSE: 50 hits inhaled Pharms - Albuterol smoked Cannabis (plant material) BODY WEIGHT: 125 lb I am 16 and have experimented with lots of drugs before this experience,(coke, weed, pharms, inhalants, shroomeys, etc.) All of these I do rarely except for marijuana I smoke that probably everyday. I’ve done some 'harder' drugs but only once for the experience. First of all, a friend and I were hanging out at his house doing the usual. We were bored and had no weed so I picked up an inhaler. I had never tried to get high on an inhaler before so I was curious of the effects. I’ve done other inhalants, gasoline gave me probably the most amazing trip I’ve ever had. I looked it over and saw that the drug was called Albuterol. We proceeded to take hits off the inhaler. 3 puffs at a time holding our breath and exhaling. Almost immediately like other inhalants I felt some euphoria and blurred choppy vision, some light headedness due to lack of oxygen but the ordinary aerosol high. After about 20 puffs we stopped. My heart was racing and pounding violently, I was also shaking. My friend told me he felt the same way and I shrugged it off since inhalant highs go away in no time. About 15 minutes later we found some weed leftover from a joint we had smoked earlier in the week. We smoked it, it was approximately a bowl but good enough weed get us high. Soon after I felt the weed kicking in. We listened to some music and relaxed for a while. My friend and I stared at some visualizations for a good amount of time. I’ve found that I have a large appreciation for music in this state. Approximately 30 minutes had passed and we decided to hit the inhaler again. I was feeling good, the only unpleasant feeling was the shaking of my arms and hands. It felt kind of like I had a low blood sugar so I ate something and took some puffs. We continued the same routine of puff pass and once again I was high. I felt euphoria, had some tunnel vision. My peripheral vision was very blurry and I felt a little confused. Albuterol make it easy for me to concentrate on certain sounds like an EET EET EET EET. It starts out slow and increases in speed until its a constant BEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEP similar to someone’ heart monitor who has just flatlined. If I continue to focus on the sound little white dots appear and fly around fast like fireflies or something. They quickly fill my vision and it seems like I’m sitting right in front of a TV watching the black and white static. At this point I either snap out of it or trip into a different dimension is what it seems like. This all happened after about 30 more puffs and I stopped and had a cigarette. By this time my heart was pounding so hard I was twitching as if my heart had some sort of recoil effect on my body. My friend also felt the same way. We joked around for a while hoping it would pass. 10 minutes go by and my heart beat had only gotten faster if it had changed at all. My friend and I listened to each other’s hearts with a stethoscope stunned by how fast they were beating. We waited and waited and waited the effects didn’t pass. I knew it was the medicine in the inhaler, I was almost sure it was the side effects of the Albuterol but was unable to look it up since by this time I was delusional. It was much like the experience of a really bad fever. Every noise I heard made me aggravated same with visuals. It was almost impossible to watch TV. I fell asleep. It’s hard to explain what I felt while I was sleeping. Usually I will have the occasional dream or just not dream at all feeling as if I had fallen asleep seconds ago when I wake up in the morning. By this time my breathing was very irregular, I remember this because I had to concentrate on each breathe I took to breathe, I had a lot of trouble breathing and eventually passed out. It was the worst feeling I ever had. After this painful experience I ran to the bathroom and started vomiting violently. After this I felt much better, my heart rate decreased to almost normal and I was sweating very badly. The next morning I felt very drained and looked up the drug. The side-effects were exactly as I thought. Everything that I had felt that night was due to the drug Albuterol. The only effects I didn’t experience were Cardiac Arrest and seizure, fortunately I stopped when I did. The next morning my friend was also fine just a little shaky. He said he had fallen asleep maybe 20 minutes after getting in bed and felt fine. Not a fun experience at all.

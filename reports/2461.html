找到大量的甜蜜旗后, 我开始看看它会做什么. 第一次尝试: 吃了大约6英寸的干根. 似乎什么也没发生,所以我和我的表弟去加载冬季的木材. 我们正在装木头, 聊天, 当我抬起头看山顶时, 它是黑暗的和无限的,并没有看起来很高兴我们在它的山. 同时,这些图像似乎只是树木. 然后幻象消失了, 我又回去装木头 想知道这是一个过度活跃的想象力, 第二次尝试: 在一个聚会上,大约有6到7人吃了不同量的. 只有一个报告感觉"快速". 似乎没有人认为它有任何作用. 第三次尝试: 由于它是"快速的"并且似乎没有其他影响. 我吃了大约3英寸的新鲜之前不得不开车整晚. 这不好. 你知道那些晚上开车时从眼角看到的小事, 它们形成了形状. 在我前灯的边缘, 我一直在自言自语地说这是根,等待撞到一个. 我从来没有. 发誓永远不会吃它,晚上开车,太奇怪了. 也许只是疲和喝了太多咖啡. 第四次尝试: 吃了一些 (新鲜的) 和去过一个朋友的房子谁住在森林湖边. 他玩,想去镇上. 他坐在沙发上等待着, 望着一扇开放的门, 风开始吹过树, 过过树的光线开始形成小仙女. 树上充满了小小的声,他们跳舞在一个美丽的芭蕾舞. 妖精们消失了,我坐在一个黑暗的客厅里,我的朋友把手放在我的肩上. 这就像从睡眠中醒来一样, 第五次尝试: 吃了8英寸的干燥,什么也没发生. 第六次尝试: 带着一些朋友开车上山, 带着一些根, 一支长笛, 一块面包和一些烟. 我们吃了根,吸烟,吃面包, 和的音乐充满了森林. 这是一个美好的晚上. 根似乎没有什么奇怪的效果. 只是看着大自然. 接下来我们开车沿着一个风景优美的路线离开, 司机大声喊道:"看看! 布已经改变了自己. 布下面的岩石是一座巨大的复活节岛像雕像, 它着我们. 像是同时看着两个世界. 我可以正常运作和表现, 但另一种现实叠加在我现在的现实上. 我可以看到它,和存在可以看到我,知道我可以看到它. 我们了一会儿,然后回家,心情很丧. 第七次和第八次尝试: 两次尝试都是在森林里, 似乎在自然环境中发挥了最好的作用, 时间表: 2461 (106):一般 (1),回顾 / 摘要 (11),各种 (28)

Jul 15, 2000. erowid.org/exp/2461 DOSE: 8 in oral Calamus (roots) After locating a good supply of Sweet Flag I set out to see what it would do. First attempt: Ate about 6 inches of the dried root. Nothing seemed to happen, so I went off with my cousin to load wood for the winter. We were loading wood and talking, when I looked up on a mountain top and there was this giant figure looking down from the mountain top. It was dark and omnious and didn't look happy that we were on it's mountain. At the same time the figure appeared to be just trees. Then the apparition faded, and I went back to loading wood wondering if it was an overactive imagination, or the root. Second attempt: Passed it out at a party, about 6 or 7 people ate varying amounts. Only one reported feeling 'speedy'. No one else seemed to think it did anything. Third attempt: Well since it was 'speedy' and didn't seem to have much other effects. I ate about 3 inches of it fresh before having to drive all night. Bad move. You know all those little things you see out of the corner of your eye late at night when driving, but quickly dismiss. They took form. There were little gremlin like creatures scampering about just at the edge of my headlights. I kept driving telling myself it was the root and waited to hit one. Never did. Swore never to eat it and drive at night, way too weird. Then again maybe it was just exhaustion and too much coffee. Fourth attempt: Ate some (fresh) and went over to a friends house who lived in the woods on a lake. He was messing around, and wanted to go into town. Waited on his couch looking out an open door into a nice little field with a big oak tree. The wind started blowing through the tree and the light filtering through the tree started forming little fairies. The tree was full of little tinkerbells and they were dancing about in a beautiful ballet. Then I felt a pressure on my shoulder and a voice said 'It's time to go.' The faeries vanished and I was sitting in a darking living room with my friends hand on my shoulder. It was like waking up from sleep, but I hadn't been asleep, just a kind of trace-like state. Fifth attempt: Ate 8 inches dried, nothing happened. Sixth attempt: Drove up into the mountains with some friends, some calamus root, a flute, a loaf of bread, and some smoke. We ate the root, smoked the smoke, ate the bread, and the laid about with flute music filling the forest. It was a wonderful evening. No weird effect from the root it seemed. Just watching nature. Then we were leaving in the car along a scenic route past about three waterfalls. At about the second one the car comes to halt and the driver yells 'LOOK!'. The water fall had transformed itself. The rock under the falls was a giant easter island like statue who was drinking up all of the water. It was staring at us. At the same time I could see the normal waterful, it was like looking at two worlds at the same time. Both existed, I could function and act normal, but there was this other reality superimposed over the one I was in. I could see into it, and the being there could see me and knew that I could see it. We oooed and aaah for some time and then went home in a somber mood. Seventh and Eight attempts: Nothing happened, both attempts were in the woods--seems to have it's best effect in a natural setting but it just didn't seem to work these times.

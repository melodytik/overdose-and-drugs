我很惊我以前没有听说过这种药物 (或整个类药物, racetams). 我曾在两所顶尖大学学习过"核心"科学学科, 我对神经药物也很感兴趣, 无论如何,几天前在工作中感到无聊, 我在网上阅读, 经过一些研究, 我决定尝试另一种新型的拉塞坦, 我读到它对神经病痛,焦虑,记忆和认知有好处, 我注意到自己觉得很清醒, 但没有紧张. 工作时,我通常在同事面前感到焦虑, 结果似乎是矛盾的, 我应该同时具有抗焦虑和刺激作用. 感觉更像我睡得很好, 喝了一大杯强烈的绿茶, 我在下午稍后,在阅读一些科学文献时, 发现我的注意力比以往任何时候都更集中. 我没有感到无聊,分心或困惑. 如果我不理解一个概念, 我觉得我可以推进直到我做. 我很难入睡, 但可能只需要一个多小时才能入睡. 我也和男朋友发生性关系, 没有发现任何性副作用, (不管好坏). 第二天, 我服用了标准的2X750毫克剂量, 我在工作中更有成效,更专注, 我读到安尼拉通过激活质受体, 类似于一些典型的阿尔茨海默氏症药物,因此,安尼拉应该通过其副交感效应增加大脑功能. 无论如何, 我不能证明这一理论, 祝你一个好日子! 布兰多 时间表: 55576 尼拉塞坦 (286):一般 (1),第一次使用 (2),各种 (28)

Instant Brain Boost Aniracetam by brando c Citation:   brando c. "Instant Brain Boost: An Experience with Aniracetam (exp55576)". Erowid.org . Jan 17, 2007. erowid.org/exp/55576 DOSE: 3000 mg oral Aniracetam (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 180 lb Aniracetram: I am amazed that I'd not heard of this drug (or this entire class of drugs, the racetams) earlier. I've been a student in a 'hardcore' discipline of science at two top universities, so I could always use any help focusing on my work. I was also seriously interested in neuropharm., so how this one escaped my relentless web-browsing about chemicals is a mystery. Anyways, a few days ago while bored at work, I was reading online and came across the racetams, a class of drugs whose prototypical member is the water-soluble and relatively impotent piracetam. After some research, I decided that I would like to taste-test another newer racetam called aniracetam. I'd read that it has utility in neuropathic pain, anxiety, memory, and cognition, and I could seriously use help in these areas! The first day I took 3000mg spaced throughout the day, and noticed that I felt very alert, but not jittery. At work, where I am usually anxious around my coworkers, I felt social and confident. The effects seemed contradictory, that I should have an anxiolytic and stimulant effect at the same time. It was NOT a euphoric effect...it felt more like I had had a great night's sleep and a big cup of strong green tea - I was just on my game in every way. I noted later in the afternoon, while I was reviewing some scientific literature, that I had more focus than I'd had in a long time. I was not becoming bored, distracted, or confused. If I didn't understand a concept, I felt like I could push through until I did. That night I had some difficulty sleeping, but it probably only took an extra hour to fall asleep. I also had sex with my boyfriend and noticed no sexual side-effects, (good or bad). On day two, I took the standard 2X750mg dose, and largely noticed the same effects. I was more productive at work, more focused, and again, more social. I've read that aniracetam works by somehow acitivating glutamatergic receptors which are closely coupled to cholinergic receptors. Similar to some typical Alzheimers drugs, then, aniracetam should increase brain function through its parasympathomimetic effects. Anyways, I can't prove that theory, but I do believe that its cognition-enhancing effects ARE sunstantial, and I hope there are not serious side-effects or withdrawal.

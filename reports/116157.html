Apr 26, 2025. erowid.org/exp/116157 DOSE: 18 g oral Nutmeg (tea) 80 mg Pharms - Atomoxetine (daily) BODY WEIGHT: 230 lb The first time I tried nutmeg it was kind of an impulsive decision. True, I had done some research on the effects of nutmeg and the possible toxicity of its most important active component, myristicin. But I wanted to have a trip, and LSD or shrooms weren’t an option. So, I decided: It’s a legal high, it can’t be that bad. That’s when I decided to purchase some whole nutmegs (I’ll call them nuts from now on). At the time of my experience, I was taking 80 milligrams of Atomoxetine daily as a prescription for my ADHD. Quite possibly this affected my experience with nutmeg and (without having experienced with higher doses) may have changed its properties. I decided to follow a recipe calling for 30 grams of nutmeg, but I decided to dose it myself following the instructions of another user who recommended using between 0.75 – 1.25 grams per 10 pounds of body weight. In the end, I just ended up crushing 4 nuts (which ended up being 19 grams overall) with a mortar and pestle. When the nuts were crushed into a coarse powder, I added them into a simmering pot of water (750 milliliters) for a whole hour. To mask the taste, I added some peppermint herb and a little bit of black pepper during the “cooking process”. While my “nutmeg tea” was simmering on the pot I did feel a little something, but I think that had more to do with placebo effect than anything else. It did clear out my sinuses quite nicely though. When the hour was over I drained it with a straining mesh and added some cinnamon, orange juice, and honey to the mixture. I drank it hot. I took the “nutmeg tea” and I was surprised just how neutral the taste felt. I was expecting a horrible flavour, but it went down quite nicely excepting some mild burning sensation in my throat (probably due to all the things I added to the mix). I was immediately filled with a pleasant feeling of warmth as I drank my concoction, I tend to get the same feeling when drinking herbal and caffeinated tea too so I didn’t think too much of it. 45 minutes in I felt relaxed and mellow, I did also notice my concentration improving a lot (not due to my ADHD meds as I had taken those in the morning and my experience took place in the evening). Just a state of relaxation and hyperfocality. An hour and a half into my experience I started to feel what felt like some kind of onset of drunkenness? I couldn’t exactly pinpoint how that made me feel. It came with some very mild feelings of dizziness which I tend to enjoy. Unfortunately, this didn’t do much else for me. I will also skip taking my meds for that day to see if anything changes. I will also add another entry into the archive as an update when that happens.

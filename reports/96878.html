我对多西胺 (Unisom) 的体验始于我被派往圣何塞工作. 由于我总是觉得大多数药物的剂量太低, 我在午夜左右做了这件事. 半小时后我开始感到奇怪, 我觉得有什么东西影响着我的身体, 我觉得有强烈的欲望躺下, 经过大约45分钟的时间, 我开始感觉到它的全部效果, 我的想法很模糊,不清楚, 我记得我看了一部关于法国在阿尔及利亚的战败/投降的纪录片. 我觉得这很有趣, 当我清醒的时候, 我几次换了频道, 发现所有的频道都同样令人烦. 我关掉电视,试着睡觉. 我还躺在床上, 完全清醒. 但我的身体感觉很糟糕, 我的想法似乎超出了我的控制. 我开始过度出汗, 呼吸很重. 我没有睡了大约4个小时, 奇怪的一种副作用是它增加了我的性欲, 我对自己感到不舒服和恶心. 我终于在凌晨四点左右睡着了. 我觉得自己有点像酒精一样. 我相信这种物质被广泛接受, 显然,使用更舒服的化学物质制成的睡眠辅助剂更容易被滥用. 这就是为什么Quaaludes被禁用,而Ambien只有处方. 我不想再试图滥用这种药物. 我还剩下15粒药, 我只用了建议用量 (100毫克). 我感觉到一个轻微的不适, 但很快就沉睡了. 我已经使用了几次, 因为它有助于推剂量. 但这种药物是你只想服用绝对必要的东西. 不管用了多少, 我醒来时还是头. 其他: 药物 - 胺 (343): 单独 (16), 宿醉 / 几天后 (46), 艰难的经历 (5), 一般 (1)

Jan 10, 2015. erowid.org/exp/96878 DOSE: 750 mg oral Pharms - Doxylamine (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 150 lb My experiences with doxylamine (Unisom) started when I was sent to San Jose for work. I get somewhat restless when I travel so I decided to buy this over the counter sleep-aid at the store. Because I always feel like the dosages in most medications are far too low, I took about 15 pills, each containing 50 mg's of doxylamine. I did this around midnight. 30 minutes in I start feeling strange, I can't identify it as good or bad. It just felt like something was affecting my body, I felt a strong urge to lay down, but more no desire to actually go to sleep. After roughly 45 minutes I began to feel the full effects, it could best be described as an extremely dirty high. My thoughts felt very vague and unclear, as well as depressing/negative. I remember I was watching a documentary about the French defeat/surrender in Algeria. I found it very interesting while I was sober, and suddenly I considered it stupid and irritating. I switched channels a few times and found all of the channels to be equally irritating. I switched my television off and tried to sleep. One hour and thirty minutes in I was still lying in bed, still completely awake. But my body felt terrible and my thoughts seemed beyond my control. I was sweating excessively, and breathing quite heavily. I didn't fall asleep for about 4 hours when the effects seemed to reside and I began to feel more comfortable. One strange side effect was that it increased my libido, my thoughts were very sexual after about an hour. However, this was overpowered by how uncomfortable and gross my body felt. I eventually fell asleep around 4 am. Upon waking up at 7:30 the following morning I experienced a terrible hangover, very similar to the one produced by alcohol. I believe this substance is widely accepted and carried in stores because of the unpleasant feelings it comes with. Sleep-aids made with more pleasant chemicals are obviously more likely to be abused. This is why Quaaludes were banned, and Ambien is prescription only. I had no desire to try to abuse this drug again. I still had another 15 pills left in the box, and I used them only at the recommended dosage (100 mg). I felt a mild sense of discomfort but quickly drifted off to sleep. I have used it a few more times since, because it's helpful at recommended doses. But this medication is the sort of thing you only want to take if absolutely necessary. Regardless of dose, I am hungover when I wake up.

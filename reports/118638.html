The idea of taking dissos crosses my mind, as I feel relaxed and stress free. Although I’ve never been fond of NYE fireworks, the plan here is simple: make the most of the night enjoying my favorite class of drugs + smoking a joint when midnight strikes. Background: I have extensive experience with this substance, ranging from both small and high doses. I have extensive experience with this substance, ranging from both small and high doses. The familiarity gives me confidence in eyeballing, particularly when it’s in HCl salt form, which is supposed to be more stable for ‘accurate’ dosing. Typically, I enjoy dissos in more natural settings, such as lying in a hammock in the middle of a forest, walking around or drawing during summer. My primary motivation behind purchasing this substance was its availability domestically, avoiding borders (EU). My all-time favorite dissociative is 3-MeO-PCE, from which it differs in some ways: shorter duration, more pronounced dissociation and less euphoric character (my experience). The changes are more of a downside for me, but that doesn’t make this analog any less interesting. The other class of drugs I cherish are psychedelics; I use LSD analogues at moderate to higher doses (~300 ug), mostly just in between semesters. I also smoke weed but managed to limit my use to few times a week after sweding for few years. As with any drug, it’s important to not get reckless and redose during comeup. Eyeballing is an unreliable form of dosing and should be avoided. It’s not that big of an issue here tho, because of the short duration (quick recovery), so there is no need to panic. If I do feel overwhelmed, I keep moving (walking in circle is just fine) with controlled deep breathing, or go take a hot shower. It’s definitely more suitable for movement so a walk or at least slow dancing is highly recommended (watching movie/ytb was kind of a waste for me). Regarding dosage, I stay within range of 10 – 15 mg, anything higher and the confusion/disorientation gets overwhelming during peak and is not enjoyable by any means. On the other hand, lover doses (5 – 10 mg) are hardly noticeable and boring. I take longer breaks in between uses, as, apart from other concerns, Ketamine Bladder Syndrome is very likely relevant to this analog as well.

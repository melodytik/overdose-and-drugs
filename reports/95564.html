我是一名19岁的大学生, 幸运的是我没有成任何药物, 我目前正在学习遗传学和细胞生物学. 我对诺特罗普的使用经验有限,即金科比洛巴,戈图科拉,布拉米,酸胆/赛林/伊诺西托尔/乙醇胺,阿尔法GPC,美拉托宁,5-HTP和DMAE双酸盐. 这个拉塞坦需要记录. 这就是我的账户. 背景:我的大学卧室,星期二晚上 心态:有点累,很饿,一天早些时候从健身房里来,有点疲. 我的包裹是从加利福尼亚运来的, 氧化 规格:99% 截止日期:2015年3月 经验 [在过去24小时内没有服用过药物] 0:00 摄入750mg的Alpha GPC与2500mg的Oxiracetam (使用数字秤计量) 以及一些黑汁. 我已经吃了四个小时了. 0:37 我发现了一些东西. 颜色的色调似乎大大增强. 路灯,汽车,我自己的衫,就像摄像机的曝光变化. 这是一个非常好的改变在一个潮湿的昏暗的晚上:) 1:08 我在听我的iPod时注意到. 音乐似乎更令人愉快和惊人的. 我对每一首歌都有一种情景. 我正在听阿瓦沙伊·科恩的"记忆". 钢琴的音符似乎跳出了完美的曲调和对低音似乎更深,更丰富的质量. 我相信氧化有一些微妙但明显的刺激性质. 我能确切地指出的是... 我的情绪大大改善,我变得不那么烦躁,我以前的头脑雾缩成愿意从事精神活动. 我与同居者交谈, 并没有像往常那样被困在他们的谈话中. 我吃了一些食物, 因为我需要营养来恢复健身房. 每当我的邻居要求我提供信息时, 我似乎会记得更好, 完成了我在线的化学掌握作业. 发现这是一个愉快的经历. 这是一个积极的挑战. 没有任何额外的效应, 总结 我相信氧化是一种非常有用的诺托普. 只有精神效应, 几乎没有身体效应. 我的感官似乎主要是视觉和听觉. 视觉记忆也大大提高了口头记忆. 他们的度范围是巨大的. 我想要强调的是, 经历中所产生的一切感觉都很自然, 很有前途...让我们来参加夏季考试吧. 对于我来说oxiracetam获得五星, 在我看来比Ginko,DMAE,Alpha GPC,Gotu Kola和Brahmi都更好! 其他: 维生素 - 胆 (467),智能 - 氧化 (565):组合 (3),烂的体验 (4),提高表现 (50),一般 (1),单独 (16)

I have substantial experience with drugs ranging from A-Z , Thankfully not addicted to any, and I am currently studying Genetics and Cell Biology. I have limited Experience with Nootropics; Namely Ginko biloba, Gotu Kola, Brahmi, Phosphatidyl Choline/Serine/Inositol/ethanolamine, Alpha GPC, Melatonin, 5-HTP & DMAE Bitartrate. This Racetam needs documenting. So here's my account. Setting: My College Bedroom, Tuesday evening Mindset: Little Tired, Quite Hungry, Moderately fatigued from a gym session earlier in the day. My Parcel shipped from California and arrived earlier in the day Labelled: Oxiracetam Specification: 99% Expiration Date: 03/2015 Weight: 100g Sliver foil packet, tiny crystals similar to crushed up snow. **Experience** Time: 7:30 pm [No Drugs previously taken in the last 24 hours] 0:00 Ingested 750mg Alpha GPC with 2500mg Oxiracetam (Weighed out using digital scales.) with some blackcurrant juice. I had not eaten in around 4 hours. 0:10 Not noticing anything obvious, I get my laundry together and gather some money, deciding to pick up dinner from the shop. The shop is located 10 minutes down the road and I decided to bring my Ipod to keep me company 0:37 Something strikes me. The Hue of colours seems greatly enhanced. Street lights, Cars, my own jumper[sweater], Like the exposure changing on a camera. This was a very nice change on a wet dim evening :) 1:08 I noticed while listening to my Ipod. Music seems more pleasant and striking. Images of scenes appear that I associate with every song. I was listening to Avashai Cohen - Remembering. The piano notes seemed to jump out of the song perfectly pitched and The Double bass seemed deeper and richer In quality. 2:10 I believe Oxiracetam has some subtle but evident Stimulant Properties. The ones I could pinpoint were... Decreased hunger (the food I bought from the shop did not seem nearly as necessary as before I dosed) My mood greatly improved, I became less irritable and the mind Fog I previously had condensed into a willingness to engage in mental activity. 3:00 I talked with house mates and was not stuck for input into their conversation as I usually am. I ate some food as I needed nutrients to recover from the Gym. Whenever pressed for information by my neighbours, I seemed to recollect better and without hesitating. 3:35 Finished my online assignment on Mastering Chemistry. Found it a pleasant experience. Positively challenging. No additional effects precipitated so I decided to stop documenting. Summary I believe Oxiracetam is a very useful Nootropic. It had solely mental effects giving little if any hint of bodily effect when I ingested it. My senses seemed Sharpened mainly Visual and auditory. Visual memory greatly increased as well of Verbal. Concentration span was enormous. The take home point I want to stress is that everything arising from this experience felt very natural as if it was already there. Very Promising...bring on the Summer Exams. :) For me oxiracetam gets five stars and is in my opinion better than Ginko, DMAE, Alpha GPC, Gotu Kola and Brahmi all stacked!

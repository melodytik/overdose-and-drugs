我一直在寻找一些可以让我平静下来的东西. 我有一份Celexa (抗抑郁药) 的处方, 我最近给自己开了一份治疗焦虑症的Lyrica处方!! 这是一个好地方! 我第一次尝试这类药物是从朋友那里得到的, 我应该像他说的那样服用十个50毫克的囊. 但是在网上阅读之后, 我决定服用4个50毫克的囊. 没有什么极端的, 但我可以肯定地说, 这一天是干净的, 我也觉得睡得很好. 隔天,我没有宿醉, 只是想吃同样多一点的东西. 我也经历过类似的经历. 我没有更多的东西, 我也没有要求更多. 但也许一年后, 在一个可怕的片戒断成之后, 我不想解释,因为我只想告诉你关于Lyrica的消息. 紧急精神病医生真的很合作. 我告诉他我想要戒毒, 想要一个可靠的处方, 他给我推了很多药物, 他最后问我有什么可以帮助我,所以我告诉他我已经试过从一个朋友的Lyrica, 他决定给我一个Lyrica的处方!!! 现在就好了! 一天三次50毫克! 我每月服用90粒药. 但是上帝啊... 一日 -------- 3个药丸 第二天 ------ 6 药片 第三天 15 药片 其他. 药剂师告诉我他不能再给我Lyrica, 因为我滥用药物, 我总是说谎,失去整个瓶子,打开我的车门,感觉在一群水,它被打开了... 任何胡说八道,有更多的药. 我不得不停止冷火和没有任何关于撤回的想法... 的 两天后,我又开始服用阿片类药物, 这是一个可怕的焦虑和自杀的意图. 回到急诊室,精神科医生把我的剂量提高到250毫克 (每天5次50毫克) 我每天只吃4个,每天储存1个, 我总是等待我的iPhone发出信号, 我总是在寻找药, 试图储存药, 所以对我来说, 莱里卡是完美的药物. 我睡得很好, 体重增加 (对我来说很好), 不再有焦虑, 最后一句话, 如果您有处方的Lyrica, 我建议只在需要时服用, 但如果它能帮助你在你的生活... 就像我一样. 我可能永远不会抽药, 因为我永远不会停止服用! 美国: 90877 发布时间:2021年4月30日 药品 - 普雷加巴林 (418):不适用 (38),成和习惯性 (10),健康益处 (32)

I Will Never Stop Taking It Pregabalin (Lyrica) by ShaunKnowThemAll Citation:   ShaunKnowThemAll. "I Will Never Stop Taking It: An Experience with Pregabalin (Lyrica) (exp90877)". Erowid.org . Apr 30, 2021. erowid.org/exp/90877 DOSE: 150-750 mg Pharms - Pregabalin BODY WEIGHT: 125 lb At the age of 21, I had already been really experienced with weed, mushrooms, DXM, LSA, PCP, strong opiates, benzo, MDMA and a lot of pills. I suffer anxiety and I am always looking for something to calm me down. I have a prescription for Celexa (antidepressant) which works for me but ain't big at all. I recently have a Lyrica prescription for anxiety!! Hooray! I first try this stuff from my friend who had it along with his opiate prescription. I was supposed to take ten 50mg caps, like he said. But after reading on internet, I decide to take 4x 50mg caps. It was nothing extreme, but I could definitely tell the day was clean, nice, easy to achieve and I was talkative. I felt asleep really good too. The day after, I had no hangover, but a urge to take the same amount plus a little more than I did yesterday. I had pretty much the same experience. Finally, it was done with Lyrica, I had no more and I did not asked for more. But maybe 1 year later, after a horrible opiate withdrawal addiction, I find myself in emergency because I try to hurt myself... I won't explain because here, I want to inform about Lyrica, and nothing else. The emergency psychiatrist was really cooperative. I tell him I want to quit drugs and want a reliable prescription to have no more anxiety in my life. He suggested me ALOT of meds, but I knew them all and had no positive experience with any of those. He ended up asking me what could help me, so I tell him that I already did try taking Lyrica from a friend, and it was good for my anxiety. Finally after maybe 2 hours doing research for Lyrica and doing phones and such, he decide he gives me a prescription for Lyrica!!! HOORAY! 50MG 3X a day!! I had 90 pills per month. But oh god... DAY 1 -------- 3 PILLS DAY 2 ------ 6 PILLS DAY 3 ---- 15 PILLS Etc. After 1 month, the pharmacist tell me he cannot give me Lyrica anymore because I abused and took 4 month of prescription in 1 month. I always lied about losing the whole bottle, opening my car door and it felt in a flock of water and it was opened... Any bullshit to have more pills. I had to stop cold turkey and had no idea about withdrawal... Aww... After 2 days, I was back on opiate and still feeling like a crap. It was horrible anxiety and suicidal intentions. Back to emergency, psychiatrist upped my dose to 250mg (5x 50mg per day) I only take 4 per day, and stock 1 per day for bad rushing days. I have a tendency to abuse them, I snort em, I always wait for my iphone to beep me so I can take my next dose. I am always looking for my pills and trying to stock em so I can trip someday. So Lyrica for me is PERFECT drug. I sleep well, I gain weight (good for me), I have no more anxiety, I take wise choice and have a lot of comprehension, it helps me the way a medication is supposed to help!! SO, last word, If you have a prescription for Lyrica, I suggest to only take it if you need it, otherwise, it will not be worth the withdrawals. But if it helps you in your life... Like me... I will probably never have withdrawals because I will never stop taking it!

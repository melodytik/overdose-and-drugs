中文译文 抑制剂

现在是8点49分, 四分钟前我喝了5克蓝茶. 十分钟前喝茶我吸了一碗干的花. 花的效果在吸后很快就会出现. 这种轻微的快乐和镇静类似于片, 但更轻微的存在. 只是因为惰, 没有动力. 与片不同的是, 强烈的感官增强, 然而,与MDMA不同的是,在中毒期间没有同情的感觉. 烟花的效果比茶更快,更强烈, 但茶的效果持续时间更长. 尽管我不得不强迫自己这样做. 我终于打破了惯性, 放上了"死者可以跳舞"的CD. 音乐听起来很棒. 声音不同,但难以描述, 我认为现在是喝一杯葡萄酒的好时机 酒精似乎与蓝色百合有着特殊的协同作用. 酒精和莉莉的组合似乎比单独服用的更有同情效应. 喝酒后, 效果会从像片一样的中毒转变为像MDMA一样的安静感. 酒精产生的醉酒也增加了莉莉. 这种组合的总体效应是麻醉同情体验. 我之前的经历表明, 单独服用莉莉对冥想有明显的有益影响. 利利和酒精的效果更有利于谈话和讨论想法而不是冥想. 这使得花在魔法仪式中成为一种非常多功能植物. 如果一个人独自执行仪式, 那么他们可以把莲花不加任何东西, 如果正在进行团体仪式, 我很难让这份报告的段落流动和顺利. 如果我试图集中注意力, 虽然,我发现很容易使我的思想沉默使用简单的冥想技巧. 我会起身去喝一杯葡萄酒. 今晚葡萄酒的味道比清醒时更好. 食物的味道也增加了, 但我完全失去了食欲. 利利甚至在吸大麻后也减轻了我的食欲. 但除此之外,大麻和蓝色枝的协同作用相对较少. 尽管我通常更喜欢大麻, 而在利利酒精远远优越. 我能感觉到我的思想减慢作为酒的影响与莉莉的醉酒混合. 我的大脑正在漂流. 其他与利利结合的物质包括4-甲基-GHB和瓦, 我曾经经历过与4-甲基-GHB一起服用莉莉的闭眼模式, 也可以在或LSD之旅中有益, 如果同时服用,它几乎没有额外的益处. 我曾考虑在吸毒后吸蓝, 想看它是否能恢复这种效果, 如果您已经处于Lilly的影响下, 就更容易从气领域带回见解. 我对时间的感觉是高度扭曲的, 我几乎没有意识到一个小时已经过去了. 似乎只有几分钟. 利利和酒精的中毒现在非常类似于MDMA. 我曾经在这种组合中与我的一个朋友建立了深厚的联系. 他几乎无法区分这种混合物所带来的醉酒与单纯的酒精所带来的醉酒. 他从来没有使用过MDMA, 他最后给了我一枚戒指 (它有一个小乐队在中心旋转,所以你可以玩它如果你焦虑或担心), 戒指和我和朋友共度的夜晚对我来说非常特别. 现在是十点钟,我已经厌倦了写作了,我想睡觉了. 尽管莉莉的毒药还会持续几个小时, 希望大家都喜欢这份经验报告, 其他: 氧化物 (40),GHV (213),酒精 - 酒/葡萄酒 (199),莲花/百合 - 尼姆菲亚诺卡利瓦卡鲁莱亚 (105):小组 (2-9) (17),组合 (3)

Blue Lotus & Some Combinations Blue Lotus, Alcohol, GHV by Renwick Citation:   Renwick. "Blue Lotus & Some Combinations: An Experience with Blue Lotus, Alcohol, GHV (exp8370)". Erowid.org . Oct 28, 2001. erowid.org/exp/8370 DOSE: 5.0 g oral Lotus/Lily - Nymphaea nouchali var caerulea (tea) BODY WEIGHT: 150 lb It is currently 8:49; four minutes ago I drank a tea made out of 5 grams of Blue Lilly. Ten minutes prior to drinking the tea I had smoked a bowl of the dried Lilly flowers. The effects of the Lilly come on fast after smoking the flowers. A mild euphoria and sedation similar to opium but much more mild are present. However, I am not sleepy just feeling lazy and without motivation to move. Unlike opium there is a strong degree of sensory enhancement similar to MDMA. Unlike MDMA however there are no empathic feeling present during the intoxication. Smoking the flowers makes the effects come on quicker and stronger than the tea however the effects of the tea last longer. I would like to put some music on however I have to force myself to do so. I finally manage to beat the inertia and put on a CD by Dead can Dance. The music sounds wonderful. The sound is different but hard to describe, more 'whole' sounding. I think now would be a good time for a glass of wine. Alcohol seems to have a special synergy with the Blue Lilly. Alcohol and Lilly in combination seem to have more empathogenic effects than either taken alone. After drinking alcohol the effects change from an opiate like intoxication to a sedated MDMA-like euphoria. The drunkenness produced by alcohol is also increased by the Lilly. The over-all effect of this combination is a narcotic empathogenic experience. Taking the Lilly by itself has a definite, beneficial effect on meditation from my previous experiences. The effects of the Lilly in conjunction with alcohol however are much more conductive to talking and discussing ideas than meditation. This makes the Lilly an extremely versatile plant for use during magical rites. If one is doing a ritual alone then they could take the Lotus without mixing it with anything, increasing their focus on the ritual at hand. If a group ritual is being performed taking Lilly and alcohol simultaneously could increase the synergy of the groups members. It is hard for me to keep the paragraphs of this report flowing and on track. My mind keeps jumping around if I try to focus too much. Although, I find it easy to silence my mind by using simple meditation techniques. I am going to get up to get a glass of wine. The wine tastes much better tonight than it does while sober. The flavor of food is also increased but I have lost my appetite completely. Lilly decreases my appetite even after smoking marijuana. Marijuana and Blue Lilly have relatively little synergy other than that however. Even though I normally prefer marijuana to alcohol, while on the Lilly alcohol is far superior. I can feel my thoughts slowing as the effects of the wine mingle with the Lilly's intoxication. My brain is drifting. Other substances that combine well with the Lilly include 4-methyl-GHB and Valium, which provide a similar intoxication to the alcohol - Lilly combination. I have experienced closed eye patterning after taking the Lilly with 4-methyl-GHB, but otherwise Lilly does not cause visions when taken alone. Lilly can also be beneficial during a mushroom or LSD trip to ease anxiety. MDMA covers most of the effects of the Lilly and has little extra benefit if taken at the same time. I have considered smoking Blue Lilly at the tail end of an MDMA experience to see if it would bring back the effects of the MDMA, I have not done this yet though. Nitrous Oxide is definitely potentiated by the Lilly and it is much easier to bring back insights from the nitrous realm if you are already under the effects of the Lilly. My sense of time is highly distorted, it seems as though time is moving at about 1/4 speed. I barely realized that an hour had passed. It seemed like just a few minutes. The intoxication provided by the Lilly and alcohol in now very MDMA-like. I have experienced a deep bonding to a friend of mine on this combination before. He could barely tell the difference between the intoxication provided by the combination and the intoxication provided by alcohol alone. However he had never done MDMA, which does itself bare a striking similarity to alcohol intoxication. That night he ended up giving me a ring (which has a small band which spins around the center so you can play with it if you are anxious or worried), which an ex-girlfriend of his had given him because I was going through a bunch of stuff at that time, and he said I needed it more than he did. The ring and the night I shared with my friend have become very special to me. It is now ten o'clock and I am getting bored with writing and am thinking about heading off to sleep. Even though the Lilly intoxication will still be going strong for another couple of hours, I have had a long day and need my sleep. I hope you have all enjoyed this experience report and found it informative.

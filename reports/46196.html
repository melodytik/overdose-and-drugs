我最近在澳大利亚网上购买了Leonotis nepataefolia (野). 我在大学和家里一直在吸烟, 我发现它的强度大约是地面大麻的一半. 我已经有了滚子,但我买了一个管子, 虽然喉有点痛, 但还是可以吸的. 我确实有点失去了我的声音,从吸烟. 这样可以让我的喉脱下膜, 一个烟斗可能会更容易但我还没有试过这样. 我有一个朋友在抽烟时经常咳. 吸烟让我很放松. 我最近有过一次分手, 发现它能让我平静下来, 我在吸烟时立即感到头和高潮, 持续了几个小时. 我在早上抽烟,下午去上大学, 这闻起来很像大麻. 我不能在公共场合吸烟, 因为人们会着我. 毒品可能是因为它比大麻更弱. 我买了10克, 价格是5澳元, 这里的每克大麻价格是20澳元. 我已经抽了两个或两个卷,这对我来说很好,足够了. 我认为炼也会引起这种感觉, 我没有幻觉或任何东西, 但我笑得相当多, 如果我在上课前抽烟, 这是一种抑郁药. 我在吸烟后一直在喝酒, 我觉得有点恶心, 但同时喝醉还是不错的. 一个放松,轻松的高. 其他: 莱昂诺蒂斯·内皮福利亚 (371):回顾 / 摘要 (11),各种 (28)

Apr 11, 2006. erowid.org/exp/46196 DOSE: 2 bowls smoked Leonotis nepetifolia (dried) BODY WEIGHT: 75 kg I recently tried Leonotis nepataefolia (wild dagga) which I bought off the internet in Australia. I've been smoking it at uni and at home, by myself or with friends. I find it approximately half as strong as ground grown marijuana. I have been having rollies but I bought a pipe to use which is less time-consuming. It's reasonably harsh on the throat, but certainly still smokable. I did loose my voice a bit, from smoking it. It kind of strips the lining off my throat and I always have water handy. A bong would probably be easier but I haven't tried it like that yet. A friend of mine coughs a lot when he smokes it. I get pretty relaxed when I smoke it. I recently had a break up and have found it excellent in soothing my nerves, helps me cope. I feel giddy and a bit high immeadiately while smoking it and it lasts a couple of hours. I smoked in the morning and went to uni in the afternoon and I was giggling uncontrollably. It smells a fair bit like pot. I couldn't really smoke it publicly because people sniff and stare. It doens't make me paranoid like pot does, possibly because it is weaker. It is also dirt cheap, I got 10 grams for $5 Au dollars, and pot is $20 Au dollars a gram here. I've been smoking 2 cones or 2 rollies and that is nice and enough for me. I think exercise brings it on as well because going for a walk seems to reinvigorate it. I don't hallucinate or anything on it, but I giggle a fair bit and come across as pretty stoned. It's hard to concentrate properly at uni if I smoke before class. It's a depressant. I have been drinking after smoking, which is much the same as drinking after smoking pot. I feel kinda nauseous but drunk/high at the same time is still good. A relaxing, easy-going high.

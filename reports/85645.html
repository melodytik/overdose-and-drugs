中文译文 致幻剂

我正准备和一些朋友一起出去玩. 我当时的情绪和思维方式都很正常. 另一个是红色背景上的白色闪电. 下午2点 我在5点之前没有什么事可做, 知道它在那时才会起作用, 五岁左右, 我和和妹妹一起吃晚饭. 我开始觉得自己很高兴, 像一个气球在我的胸部和和 clothespins 在我的脸,所以我不能停止微笑. 我坐下,天花板上的光照在银器上, 直接射入我的瞳孔. 灯光有紫色,绿色和黄色的色调, 这是整个旅行的主题. 我知道我和妹妹知道,但我已经过了关心的时刻. 我上了我的房间, 邀请了一些人来. 晚上开始了吸烟和挂在我的屋顶. 我抽了两三碗重力,然后我在陆地上. 我坐在屋顶上,天空是紫色和绿色和街道是奇怪的颜色和移动. 我回到房间里,坐在床上. 我回答说"神圣的狗狗"或根本无法回答. 后来我基本上昏了. 我看到自己坐在床上, 一切都在变大. 我当时正在画画,但是在星空中观察自己. 在某个时刻, 我目睹了两颗恒星在不同星系的碰撞. 这让我害怕了,我想这就是事情开始下坡的时候. 我的朋友开始离开,当我有点恢复了意识我跑出门,因为我真的想和他们一起去. 我不知道接下来发生了什么, 接下来我知道我站在我的房间前, 她看着我的奇怪. 我不记得过去的五分钟或更多. 姐姐喊我,因为我把咖啡倒在了门上. 于是我下了地下室, 试图逃避所有疯狂. 我打开电脑,甚至连登录都没办法, 我周围视野中呈现出令人惊叹的黄色,绿色和紫色, 这有点像是毒中的酸. 不管怎样,我真的不记得接下来发生了什么,但这件事让我像野兽一样出汗,所以我最终裸体... 虽然裸体我想我跑下楼. 现在我和妹妹知道发生了什么, 我在胸前打我的妹妹和接我的母亲和咬出她的腿,我不知道发生了什么事. 我打电话给警察... 这就是事情变得粗的地方. 接下来我知道我躺在地上 (仍然裸体) 我的手在我身后,我不能移动他们. 我开始大喊大叫,后来我被告知我试图从阳台跳下来自杀 (这是愚蠢的,因为它只有1层,但) 和7个该死的猪在我身上. 现在让我们看一下. 七名成年男性和女性警察, 一个十五岁的小伙子, 我认为我对抗他们一点, 但我被告知他们打算给我一个电击, 故事的下一个部分是救护车和医院, 基本上我在救护车里说着一些疯狂的语 (这些语被录制, 医院里他们把我绑在椅子上, 我还在坚持下去, 认为自己是个游戏节目, 赢了, 我错了. 我不会详细说明, 但他们最终给了我镇静剂, 整整脸,背部,手臂和腿都有伤. 我有胆子带着我咬她的照片来, 把它擦在我的脸上, 后来我意识到时钟已经融化了, 呈现绿色,黄色和紫色. 接下来的三天我都在精神病房里度过. 总而言之,这是一次疯狂的旅行. 我的第一个错误是下午2点左右. 我应该在5点左右完成. 第二个错误就是在家里做. 这是一个很糟糕的想法. 已做的事已经做了,不能再做. 我不得不说我怀疑我会再次做DOC. 这是一种强烈的药物, 其他: DOC (357),警察/海关 (60):小组 (2-9) (17),火车毁和旅行灾难 (7),糟糕的旅行 (6),困难的经历 (5),第一次 (2)

7 Cops and One Bad Trip DOC & Cannabis by Seti Citation:   Seti. "7 Cops and One Bad Trip: An Experience with DOC & Cannabis (exp85645)". Erowid.org . Aug 19, 2011. erowid.org/exp/85645 DOSE: repeated smoked Cannabis (plant material) 2 hits oral DOC (blotter / tab) BODY WEIGHT: 130 lb It was a friday afternoon at my house and I was getting ready to go hang out with some friends. My mood and mindset were fairly normal at the time. I took 2 hits of the DOC (one was a playboy bunny, the other a white lightning bolt on a red background) at approx. 2pm. I had nothing to do until 5, knowing it wouldn't kick in until around then so I sat around on the computer. At about 5 I had to eat dinner with my Mom and sister. I was starting to feel really up, like a balloon was in my chest and and clothespins on my cheeks so I couldn't stop smiling. As I sat down, the light from the ceiling was reflecting on the silverware and right into my dilated pupils. This was fantastic, the light had shades of purples, greens and yellows which was a theme throughout the trip. I made it through dinner ok, I knew my mom and sis knew but I was past the point of caring. I went up to my room and had some people over. The evening began with some smoking and hanging out on my roof. I smoked 2 or 3 gravity bong bowls and I was in the land. As I was sitting on my roof, the sky was purple and green and the street was oddly colored and moving. I went back inside and sat on my bed. My buddies asked my throughout, 'How you feelin' man?' to which i replied 'holy shit dude' or couldn't answer at all. A little while later I basically blacked out. I went out of my body and saw myself sitting on my bed and everything kept zooming out. I was drawing at the time but watching myself from up in the stars. At one point I witnessed 2 stars collide in a different galaxy of some sort. This scared the crap out of me and I think thats when things started going downhill. My friends started to leave and when I sort of regained consciousness i ran out the door because i really wanted to go with them. I'm not sure what happened next but i ended up back at my house. Next thing I know I'm standing in front of my mom's room and she's looking at my weird. I couldn't remember the past 5 minutes or more. My sister was yelling at me because apparently I had spilled instant coffee all over the doormat. So I went down in the basement trying to escape all the madness. I went on my computer and couldn't even log on I was tripping so hard. The whole time there were magnificent yellows, greens, and purples in my peripheral vision and things were very acid like. Its kind of like acid on crack. Anyway, I honestly don't remember what happens next but this shit made me sweat like a beast, so i ended up naked…. While naked I guess I ran downstairs. By this point my mom and sis knew what was up so I guess they tried to restrain me. I hit my sister in the chest and tackled my mom and bit the shit out of her leg, I had no idea what was going on. My mom called the cops…. This is where shit gets raw. So next thing I know I'm on the ground (still naked) my hands are behind me and i cant move them. I start yelling and screaming and I was told later that I tried to jump off the balcony to kill myself (which is stupid because its only 1 story but oh well) and 7 fucking pigs are on top of me. Now take this in for a second. 7 full grown male and female police officers taking on a 130 lb. 15 year old kid tripping out of his mind. I assume I fought them off for a little bit but I was told they were about to tazer me and I gave up. The next part of the story is the ambulance and hospital which I'm going to go over very briefly. Basically i was rambling crazy ramblings in the ambulance (and they were recorded and read back to me in family court) and freaking out. So in the hospital they strapped me to a chair and this lady nurse was about to give me a catheter. I was still tripping really hard and thought I was a game show, had won, and was about to have sex with this sexy nurse. Boy was I wrong. I won't go into details but the ended up giving me a sedative and throwing me in the PICU. The next day I woke up with 2 broken wrists, bruises all over my face, back, arms and legs. My mom has the nerve to show up with a picture of the bite i gave her and was rubbing it in my face as if I didn't feel bad enough. I was still tripping in the PICU the next day, I couldn't tell right away but later I realized the clock was kind of melting and was green yellow and purple. I spent the next 3 days in the psych ward. To sum it all up, this was a crazy fucking trip. My first mistake was taking it at around 2pm. I should have done it about 5. Second mistake was doing it at home. BAAAAAD IDEA. Well whats done is done and cant be undone. I must say I doubt I will ever do DOC again. This is an intense drug and should be dealt with carefully.

经过脑瘤切除术后, 神经外科医生给我开了迪兰丁药, 我没有严重的记录副作用, 但我在药物不到6个月. 我要求我的医生让我停止使用迪兰丁, 因为它会影响我的情绪. 我根本没有感觉到什么. 我无法感到快乐,也无法感到悲伤. 没有焦虑,没有快乐,没有恐惧,没有满足,没有什么. 性生活甚至没有感觉. 我没有感到不好. 我只是没有感觉到. 我在戒药后一周左右就恢复正常. 感觉不好,悲伤,焦虑等等比没有感觉更好. 时间表: 21679 药品 - 素 (261):健康问题 (27),不适用 (38)

I Just Didn't Feel Phenytoin by S. Johnson Citation:   S. Johnson. "I Just Didn't Feel: An Experience with Phenytoin (exp21679)". Erowid.org . Mar 7, 2003. erowid.org/exp/21679 DOSE: 100 mg oral Pharms - Phenytoin (daily) BODY WEIGHT: 125 lb After a craniotomy for a brain tumor, my neurosurgeon prescribed dilantin to prevent seizures. I had no serious documented side-effects, but I was on the medication for less than 6 mo. I asked my doctor to wean me off of dilantin because of the effects on my emotions (or lack of). I essentially felt nothing. I couldn't feel happy, I couldn't feel sad. There was no anxiety, joy, fear, contentment, nothing. Sex didn't even feel. I didn't feel bad. I just didn't feel. A week or so after being off the drug I was back to normal. Feeling bad, sad, anxious, etc. is better than feeling nothing.

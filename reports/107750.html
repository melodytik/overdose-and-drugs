背景: 我对迷幻药没有太多经验. 自2015年7月以来, 吃过6次/松露. 两周前还有一次使用了LSD. 一些2cb的经验和一个2cp的经验总结了这一切. 我知道我对和胺的了解. 我几乎总是保持在中等剂量范围内, 因为我是一个完全的初学者, 避免了"毒品" (对我来说除了酒精和MJ之外的一切 - 但我的观点已经改变) 我第一次尝试使用MDMA. 我喜欢喝酒的程度也减少了很多. 我还在与朋友圈对毒品的愚蠢看法作斗争. 我从来没有使用过类药物, 也从来没有使用过片, 我不喜欢闻东西,时间. 我不是一个很快就会焦虑的人, 那些非常紧张的人. 毕竟,这些确实是我'改变人生'的故事. 因此我不加入 "好"和"坏"的旅行术语. 听到关于25I-NBOH的一些不好的报道, 这种化学物质的医疗史. 但几周后,当我有点平静的时候, 设置和设置: 我和我的女朋友在我们的公寓的客厅. 没有什么特别准备的晚上. 我知道她很快就会睡觉,我希望能和她一起体验, 事实上,我一直在睡觉,我觉得她身边很安全. 由于前一周的压力, 我在那天晚上就完成了我的待办事项清单, 我感到很满意, 一个好的开始. 我的女朋友知道我会试试,所以如果我用疯狂的表情唤醒她,她就不会感到惊. 由于这是一个非常新的化合物, 我想看看低剂量对我有什么影响. 由于这是一个非常新的化合物, 我想看看低剂量对我有什么影响. - 晚上十一点左右 我把25I-NBOH (1600微克) 切成4块, 把最大的一块放在我的舌头下 (大约500ug). 我立刻注意到苦味,我的舌头触摸了抹纸, 我对LSD没有任何这样的第一印象 (没有味道,没有麻木). - - 时间是0:30) 我正在看电视,但它让我无聊. 所以我把'地球上最后一个人'系列放上了. 我的脉感觉上升了. 我可能会抽一点大麻. 我了一根,但又等了20分钟,抽了大约5支. 我不觉得关节的很多, 这让我相信有什么东西来. - 时间1点 它正在到来. 我没有预期这么多的剂量. 但是我猜大麻可以增强它. 我感觉到 感觉有所提升,有点高兴. 但有一些想法很容易逃脱. - - 一点半 我对这部剧非常高兴, 只是很高兴活着和呼吸. 我完全没有身体上的不适. 开始感觉像是轻量级的2C-B, 但没有任何身体的下降. - - 一点四十五分 我不再喜欢看这个系列了. 我在想太多其他事情. 我不想想太多, 因为这仍然是基于语言的思维, 我关掉了灯,放了一些音乐. 我只是专注于呼吸和心跳. 我失去了 这种感觉让人感到惊, 我还在呼吸, 但这是一个奇怪的感知转变. 我知道我一直在呼吸 但大部分时间我都没有意识到这一点. 但那一刻是不同的. 我知道我会像一个 (物理) 身体一样运作, 思想开始涌入. 我不得不跟随一些有趣的想法. 这绝对是经历的高峰. 这对于聚会等社交场合来说也非常适合, 在我的视角旁边有点变形. 我闭上眼睛时, 几乎没有CEV, 但当我在冥想, 感受到放手的感觉时, 我对此非常满意, 实际上我很惊这种物质有多好地帮助我冥想. - - 时间2点 我不得不停止冥想, 因为我有太多的想法, 音乐还在播放, 我正在抽烟, 我在电视上欣赏着专辑封面的艺术作品. 完全的黑暗正在帮助我与我的CEVs和我关闭电视. 我无法再享受音乐了, 因为我不得不再次闭上眼睛, 我打开眼睛, 看到我的Wii发出红色LED灯, 这真是太棒了. 我感觉到它触摸了我的眼睛. 其他一切都变得模糊, 我知道我可以清醒地看到这一点, 这意味着,我的行为没有原因. 我只是为了体验而经历, 这感觉真的很好. 我不喜欢每天都过分思考实现目标的习惯, 我很高兴放弃这样的感觉, - 现在是3点 过去40分钟我又看了"地球上的最后一个人", 我知道它即将结束, 我不想延长经验在任何方式, - - 时间是4:30. 我和我的女朋友上了床. 我仍然有很多疯狂的想法, 闭眼视觉来了比以前更多,因为我真的想睡觉现在可以管理 在30分钟内完成. - - 12:30 时间 我醒来时感觉很正常. 没有太累或任何东西. 我没有去看牙医, 我确实需要睡眠,但我认为这是因为紧张的一周 在之前. 结论: 25i-NBOH是一种非常有趣的物质, 没有恶心,没有疼痛或任何坏事. 我建议你先尝试一下很低的剂量, 你永远不知道你的身体会如何反应. 冥想更容易, 让我感觉更美好. 我会加倍剂量, 谢谢您的阅读! 并且对我的语法表示歉意, 其他: 25I-NBOH (615):第一次 (2),一个人 (16)

Jan 22, 2016. erowid.org/exp/107750 DOSE: T+ 0:00 0.25 hits sublingual 25I-NBOH (blotter / tab) T+ 0:50 5 hits smoked Cannabis T+ 2:00 smoked Cannabis BODY WEIGHT: 82 kg Background: I have little experience with psychedelics. Took shrooms/truffles 6 times since july 2015. And once LSD about 2 weeks ago. A few experiences with 2cb and one with 2cp sum this all up. So I know my deal with phens and tryptamines. I almost always stayed in a medium dosage range, since I am a total beginner and have been avoiding 'drugs' (for me everything else than alcohol and MJ - but my viewpoint has changed) until I was 27 and tried MDMA the first time. That changed the view on drugs and since then I have been drinking a lot less, which I like. Still fighting the stupid opinions about drugs in my circle of friends. I never used benzos and I never used opiates but I tried coke twice and I don't like it (I know coke is not a opiate...). I don't like to sniff stuff, period. I'm not a person who gets anxious so fast and I would not call any of my trips a 'bad' trip, but there where really stressful ones. In the end of the day, those were really my 'life-changing' ones. So I don't join the 'good' and 'bad' trip terminology. Since I heard a bunch of bad reports about 25I-NBOH, I thought I might get a little too uncomfortable thinking about no medical history of this chemical. It might kill me, as opposed to LSD or shrooms, but I gave it a try a few weeks later, when I got a little calm. Set and Setting: Me and my girlfriend in the living room of our apartment. Nothing was especially prepared for the evening. I knew she would sleep soon and I would like to experience it with somebody around, even though she was in fact sleeping the whole time, I felt safe with her around. My mindset was pretty good, since I had a very stressful previous week. I finished my to-do list right on that evening and was still a little happy about my performance in the university (I played guitar and harmonica). So I felt not very joyful, but satisfied. A good start. My girlfriend knew I would be trying it out, so she wouldn't be surprised if I woke her with a crazy look on my face. Since this is a very new compound, I wanted to see how low dose affects me. Since this is a very new compound, I wanted to see how low dose affects me. -About 11pm (0:00) I cut a blotter of 25I-NBOH (1600 micrograms) into 4 pieces and put the biggest one under my tongue (about 500ug). I instantly noticed the bitter taste and as the tip of my tongue was touching the blotter I noticed that it was becoming a little numb. I did not have any of these first impressions with LSD (no taste, no numbness). -(0:30) I was watching TV, but it bored me. So I put on the 'Last man on earth' series. My pulse feels raised. I might smoke a little weed. I rolled a joint, but waited another 20 mins to smoke about 5 drags. I don't feel much of the joint and that was making me confident that there was something coming. -(1:00) It's coming. I did not expect that much for that dose. But I guess the weed enhanced it a little. I'm feeling mood-lifted and a bit euphoric. But having a few thought loops that were easy to get away from. -(1:30) I'm very happy about the series and have been laughing a lot. It's just nice to be alive and breathe. I feel no body discomfort at all. It starts to feel like a mild dose of 2C-B but without any body turn downs. -(1:45) I don't like to watch the series any more. I was thinking too much other stuff. I did not want to think too much, since it was still very language-based thinking and I wanted to see what happens when I'm meditating a bit. I turned the lights off and put on some music. I was just focusing on my breath and my heart beat. I lost the sense of feeling my breath for a short time and it was frightening and interesting at the same time. I was still breathing of course, but it was a strange shift of perception. I know I am breathing all the time but I'm not aware of it most of the time. But that moment was different. My awareness was pointed at my breathing and I knew I would function as a (physical) body if I want to or not, so I just went with the flow. Thoughts were coming in. Interesting thoughts I had to follow. This was definitely the peak of the experience. This would be a very good dose for social situations like parties as well, as I did only have very mild OEV. Brighter colors and a little morphing next to my point of view. When I closed my eyes there was very little CEV, but as I was meditating and feeling the sensation of 'letting-go' I noticed a real bright come-up, which was fading away the moment I was thinking about letting go and wanting (!) to let go. I was very fine with that and did not expect much more of this dose. In fact I was surprised how pleasantly this substance helped me to meditate. -(2:00) I had to stop meditating since I got too many thoughts and couldn't focus any more. Music was still playing and I was smoking the joint I rolled earlier, which gave me a boost into the experience. Music was wonderful and I was enjoying the artwork of the album cover on my TV. Full darkness was helping me with my CEVs and I turned the TV off. I couldn't enjoy the music much longer since I had to close my eyes again and feel what's coming. When I opened my eyes I noticed a red LED from my Wii which was shining right into my face. It was marvelous. I felt how it touched my eye. Everything else within my sight got blurred and had a grainy texture. I knew I could see this sober, but I was much more open and a lot less 'goal' oriented. That means, my actions had no cause. I was just experiencing for the experience, not for anything else. That felt really good. I don't like this day-to-day routine of thinking too much of achieving goals and I'm happy about letting go of the sensation that I have (!) to do something, in order to justify my being. -(3:00) For the last 40 mins I was watching Last man on earth again, which was entertaining. I knew it was coming to an end and I did not want to extend the experience in any way, since I had an appointment for the dentist in the morning. -(4:30) I went to bed with my girlfriend. Still having a lot of crazy thoughts which were all very interesting. Closed Eye visuals were coming a lot more than before, since I really wanted to sleep now and could manage to do so within 30mins. -(12:30) I got up and felt really normal. Not too tired or anything. I did not go to the dentist, and I didn't have to, since it was a regular checkup. I needed sleep indeed, but I think that was because of the stressful week before. Conclusion: 25i-NBOH is a very interesting substance since it hat no bad body load whatsoever. No nausea, no pain or anything bad. I recommend to try a very low dose at first, you never know how your body reacts. It was fun (euphoria and laughter) and introspective (meditating was a lot easier). When I repeat this experiment I will double the dose to have a blotter. Thanks for reading! And sorry for my grammar since it is not my native language.

我以前曾尝试过这种化合物,但用较低的剂量 (200ug) 和在农场工作一整天后, 这是一个重复的实验, 希望从中得到更多的信息. 我和我的室友J终于决定在晚上9点左右服用. 他女朋友A (我的另一位室友) 决定不去. 剂量是非常准确地预计和溶解在已知数量的水和我服用300ug量量在一些果汁. 没有明显的味道. 到了T+0:15,我收到了第一次警报. 我们听的是各种各样的音乐,有些摇摆,一些蓝调等. "A"一直忙着等待其他朋友的来电. 她的活动也让我有点烦. 我开始在半小时内爬上去, 似乎一直在试图让自己感到舒服, 不知道自己有没有感冒, 似乎是我的思想试图通过身体来解决的小矛盾. 我终于从舒适的椅子上摆脱出来, 站起来换上长和更舒适的衫. 走路肯定提醒了我这种效应的存在. 我继续试图适应这种影响. 我觉得当我站起来尿尿的时候 (虽然我们确实吃了), 我没有注意到任何开眼睛的视觉扭曲, 但我的头脑清晰而平静, 我专注于事实上已经有几周了, 我重新审视了自己的现状, 我做出了一些决心 (我经常做出决心, 现在已经是2点左右了. 在这个时候与我们的其他朋友取得了联系,并离开酒吧. 我和J继续躺在沙发上和坐在舒适的椅子上. 我们都清醒,能够回答A和对方的问题, 我认为他和我一样想着类似的事情 (CD的选择对我们的心理状态并不太有利). 我拿起我的CD盒, 翻了一遍, 很不满的是, 低音管的放大器 (一个非常大的低音管) 在电视房间里, 我计划在晚些时候看电影, 我们似乎都很,所以我决定把克鲁德和多尔夫迈斯特的"K&D会话"录入, 听完第一首歌,第二首歌的中途, 我已经忍不住了, 音乐没有深度,身体振荡的低音就不一样了. 一旦连接和运行 (在这种状态下连接立体设备时,特别是在盲目地试图将放大器插入墙上插座时,有一些小问题),音乐景观得到了极大的改善. 后来在专辑中, 我很高兴能把它连接起来, 因为它里面有一些惊人的低音效果. 我也觉得现在是吸大麻的时候了, 我觉得我的高峰可能正在减少 (~T+2:30). 几口吸了一些好的大麻和J和我安顿下来,听音乐闭着眼睛. 我有一些非常好的闭眼视觉和缓慢的触角负载的精神旅行. 我意识到自己正处于一种类似阿片类的催眠状态. 我似乎处于睡眠和清醒之间, 这也感觉相当好. 听起来这张专辑似乎很快就完毕了, 我翻了翻我的CD盒, 寻找其他有趣的东西来听. 我发现了一些我一直打算样本J与在这种性质的东西. 我播放了伦敦未来声音的ISDN. 听着这首歌, 我觉得在几个小时前, 这是我第一次意识到我在听K&D时所处的平原正在下坡 (~T+3:30). 让你不得不想起那些有时会有旋律的绿洲. 我继续思考, 接下来我们就会听到一个声音. 我现在大部分时间都在下 (~T+4:00) 最终J把一个非常好的环境专辑,自由的梦:勃·马利的环境翻译成Dub. 他继续躺在沙发上, 我听了一半的专辑, 我决定是时候开始减慢我的大脑速度了 (T+5:00). 我把一杯大麻混在了一果汁中. 在聊天中像普通杯子果汁一样喝. 没有任何效果. 我混了第二个,A回家了,我们聊了半个小时. 他们决定去睡觉, 我回到我的电脑. 我喝完了第二杯酒, 我决定在4:30 (T+7:30) 睡觉. 我在睡觉时,G使我的思想保持安静. 睡了大约6个小时, 唯一的身体影响是偶尔过敏的气味和持续的尿液效应. 晚上结束时, 我一定要试着补充我消耗的一些水分. 我也有一些张力/口腔不适,但我认为这是一个心理关注我对口腔持久的痴迷 (我有大量的牙科和正牙工作,多年来没有去过牙医,最近一直在思考它很多). 这是在经济上升期间, 这种烦似乎消失了. 总的来说,我发现这个复合体非常愉快. 它与LSD有很多类似的效果,但不那么强烈和苛刻 (尽管可能会在更高剂量时如此). 虽然它仍然有很长的尾巴, 但它有点刺激, 我从来没有看过它. 我注意到了一些痕迹, 想知道我的大脑中是什么地方造成的. 如果我专心的话,我可以画出一些扭曲. 闭眼的视觉效果主要是基于音乐输入和我的思维过程. 吸食大麻后的催眠快乐是一两个小时是非常愉快的. 我认为这种药物可以很好地与其他药物结合. 一种温和的兴奋剂很好地对抗惰 (如果它再次发生). 一种片或镇静剂可能会使一个人陷入这种催眠症. 在鱼上, 杯子 其他: 拉米德 (256):小组 (2-9) (17),第一次 (2)

Now Will It Do Something? Lamid by Beaker Citation:   Beaker. "Now Will It Do Something?: An Experience with Lamid (exp15304)". Erowid.org . May 21, 2002. erowid.org/exp/15304 DOSE: T+ 0:00 300 ug oral Lamid T+ 2:30 smoked Cannabis T+ 5:00 2.0 g oral GHB T+ 5:30 2.0 g oral GHB BODY WEIGHT: 170 lb I had tried this compound before, however at a lower dose (200 ug) and after a full days work on a farm, so the dozen or so of us who tried it were physically exhausted, and no one really felt anything (even people who took relatively high doses of 2CT7 instead). So this was a repeat experiment, in the hopes of getting a little more out of it. My roomate J and I finally decided to dose at about 9:00 PM. His girlfriend A (my other roomate) decided not to. The dose was preweighed very accurately and dissolved in a known amount of water and I took 300ug dosed by volume in some juice. No noticeable taste. By T+0:15, I had my first alert. We were listening to a pretty varied mix of music, some swing, some blues, etc. A was keeping herself busy, waiting for some other friends to call. I was starting to feel more and more lethargic as the effects increased, and her activity was slightly annoying. By about T+0:30, I started about half an hour of coming up where I seemingly kept trying to get comfortable, couldn't tell if I was cold or not, and itching various minor itches on my body. Seemed to be a little dissonance that my mind was trying to resolve physically. I finally nudged myself out of the comfy chair I was in and got up to change clothes into some long pants and a more comfortable shirt. Walking definitely alerted me to the presence of the growing effects. I sat back down and continued to try and settle in to the effects. I think when I got up and urinated (quite a bit, though we did have soup for dinner), it helped to break some of the spell and when I sat back down again I was pretty much settled. I didn't notice any open-eyed visual distortions, but my mind was clear and calm and there were some good closed eye visuals beginning. I focused on the fact that it had been several weeks since I had tripped on anything and decided to do some mental work. I went over my current situation and challenged myself on some of my faults. I was rational yet firm with myself and made some resolutions (which I often make and rarely keep while on psychedelics). This consumed a good hour, and by now it was about T+2:00. A had in this time gotten in contact with our other friends and was off to a bar. J and I continued to lay on the couch and sit in the comfy chair respectively. We were both lucid and could answer A and each others' questions, but were content not to speak. After A left, J commented that I was free to put in any music I wanted, as the CDs were starting to run out and I think he had been thinking similar things as me (that the CD selection was not very condusive to our mental state). I picked up my CD case and flipped through it, conflicted by the fact that the amp for the bass canon (a very large subwoofer tube) was in the TV room and hooked up to the other sound system. I had planned on watching movies later and did not really want to move the amp. We both seemed pretty lethargic, so I decided to put in Kruder and Dorfmeister's The K & D Sessions, Disc 2. After the first song and about half-way through the second, I just couldnt take it any more, and got up to get the amp. The music just wasn't the same without deep, body-oscillating bass. Once connected and operational (a slight concern about hooking up stereo equipment while in this state, especially while blindly trying to plug the amp in to the wall socket), the musical landscape was vastly improved. Later in the album, I was very glad to have hooked it up, for there are some amazing bass effects in it. At this time, I also felt it was time to finally smoke some cannabis, which I had declined from A earlier. I felt as if my peak might be diminishing (~T+2:30). A few puffs of some fine cannabis and both J and I settled in and listened to the music with eyes closed. I had some really good closed eye visuals and a slow tangent-laden mental trip. At one point I realized I was in an almost opiate like state of hypnogogic bliss. The lethargy previously felt was enhanced by the cannabis, and I seemed to be in a state between sleep and awake, that also felt quite good. The album seemed to finish quickly, and I flipped through my CD case looking for something else interesting to listen to. I spotted something I had been meaning to sample J out with while on something of this nature. I put on the Future Sound of London's ISDN. While listening to it, I felt it couldve been appreciated a lot more a couple of hours earlier, when our peaks were just beginning. This was my first realization that the plateau I had been on while listening to K&D was sloping down (~T+3:30). ISDN was still quite enjoyable (in that disturbing, unsettling manner that forces you to think about things with an occasional oasis of melody). After ISDN, we listened to silence for a while, and I continued to think. I was mostly down by now (~T+4:00) and eventually J put in a very good ambient album, Dreams of Freedom: Ambient Translations of Bob Marley into Dub. He continued to lay on the couch, and I listened to about half the album and then decided to get online and chat. When the CD ended and it switched back to the K&D, I decided it was time to start slowing my brain down (~T+5:00). I mixed up a glass of ~2g GHB in 8oz of juice. Drank it like a normal glass of juice while chatting. It had no effect. As I mixed up my second, A came home and we all talked for about half an hour. They decided to go to bed, and I returned to my computer. I finished my second glass, which now was at least noticable, but I still felt no urge to sleep. Nothing was happening online, and I definitely did not want to see dawn, so by 4:30 (T+7:30) I decided to slip into bed. The G helped keep my mind quiet as I slipped into sleep. Slept about 6 hours with only a little groginess in the morning. The only physical effects I felt were occasional hypersensitivity to smells and a persistent diuretic effect. I pissed out way more water than I took in, and was sure towards the end of the evening to try and replenish some of the water I eliminated. I also had some jaw tensions/mouth discomfort, but I think this was a psychological focusing of a lingering obsession with my mouth (Ive had tons of dental and orthodontic work and havnt been to the dentist in years and have recently been thinking about it a lot). This was during the upswing, when I was uncomfortable on a number of fronts. Once everything settled in, and I addressed the issue, this annoyance seemed to dissapate. All in all, I found the compound very enjoyable. It had many similar effects to LSD, but not so intense and demanding (though possibly could be so at higher doses). The shortness of the trip was nice, though it still had a long tail that was slightly stimulatory, at least in the sense that I felt I could not go to sleep without pharmaceutical assistance. I never had much open eyed visuals on it. I noticed some tracers for a while, and was interested in figuring out where in my brain was causing this. I could draw out some distortions if I concentrated. The closed eyed visuals were mostly based on the musical input, and my mental thought process. The hour or two of hypnogogic bliss after smoking cannabis was quite nice. I think this drug could combine with other drugs quite well. A mild stimulant would be nice to fight the lethargy (if it were to occur again).

T+3:15 - 第二次涌入 感觉就像一个召唤调整到这个频率再次, 我重新调整频率比第一次快得多. 这次我觉得我没有找到它; 它在等着我. 刚刚我达到完美的位置, 动这个词不够. 仿佛我大脑中的每一个神经元都在一次同步的爆炸中发射. 生命的意义和我所拥有的一切, 都被压缩成一个瞬间. 噪音感觉像数据, 传输我没有建立理解, 它是以一种我无法解码的方式. 快速而激烈,同时又无限复杂, 但让它变得疯狂的是,那毫秒感觉是永恒的. 在激增过程中,时间被打破了. 没有过去,没有未来, 只有那一瞬间的爆炸, 这种感觉永远不会结束. 像我一直在震撼中,永远会是. 这就是为什么离开我这个凡人的想法是有道理的. 因为在那一波浪潮中, 我并不是在一个身体里. 我不是在一个房间里. 我不是一个人. 我在那个时刻的内部. 我记得我认为这就是它. 这就是十字路口. 这就是一切结束的地方. 然后一切都消失在白色之中, T+5:00 - 复制 我在几个小时后醒来. 没有记忆到此时发生了什么, 我的记忆停止形成, 我现在认为我的身体关闭试图保护我, 我已经筋疲力尽了. 很冷的 但绝大多数都处于和平状态. 我觉得我的心灵已经向我展示了一些古老而重要的东西, 关于控制,恐惧,童年, 实体已经消失了. 频率已经消失. 海已经消失了. 我觉得那个地方在接下来的几个小时内都无法到达, 视觉开始显著下降. 经历结束了, 接下来我将深入融入, 时间表: 119372

Every twitch felt connected to something emotional, like my body was reacting before my mind could understand. And I could almost sense a sort of wire from my gut to my brain. My sense of me started peeling away right here. I could feel myself split into layers, the observer, the narrator, the part of me that panics, and the deeper, calmer part. Breathing felt mechanical and forced, and no attempt to control it was enough, thoughts looped. I kept forgetting how to exist in my body. Any time I opened my eyes or tried to interact with the room, it felt wrong, like the external world was frying my nervous system. I was pacing around endlessly, I tried to put on headphones and just listen to some music how I usually do, but I quickly realized that it was too much, I had to turn off all the lights in the room, take off my clothes, and was compelled to reduce all external stimulation, so I put on my eye mask. This is when I realized that if I stayed awake and outward, I’d get trapped in endless loops, and I did, I consistently kept getting up from my laying position to sit up, take off the mask and lay back down. I knew I had to do something at this point, and I know that cannabis could intensify the experience but at this point I genuinely felt as if my life was on the line, I knew it would calm me down, it would give me something to hold on to during whatever I was going through, so I got up, and vaporized some indica heavy cannabis. I then go back to my laying position and I started to discover remarkable things the moment I put the eye mask back on. Every time I drifted or my body twitched wrong, I’d lose it. Every time I concentrated, I’d get closer. Time stopped meaning anything. It felt like I was waiting in an endless sea, some sort of void. I don’t know how long this lasted. Could’ve been minutes, could’ve been years. And yet I wasn’t panicking, I was just stunned, like witnessing my own end calmly. It felt like if I followed it fully, I wouldn’t come back. And the entity seemed okay with that, just letting me look at the possibility. That neutrality is what made it so intense. The entity was gone. The frequency was gone. The sea was gone.

我最近和继子发生了一场完全意想不到的争, 由于我们是非常亲密的朋友, 我认为禁食可以提供良好的时间, 因为我们生活中的良好的时间都致力于所有欲望, 计划和细节的食物. 这让我有很长的时间去思考, 究竟是什么改变导致了我在朋友身上看到的怪物. 我一直在增加体重, 我是在2004年新的一年开始. 我最近的体检显示我的胆固醇量为297和三糖量超过600. 每天: 1 多种维生素和水 (不含食物) 这种经历使我发生了一些我没想到的变化. 在前两天我主要经历了对饥饿的适应, 第二天我也有一些恶心, 但它过去, 我的胃做出了奇怪的声音. 我觉得有点充满活力, 在棒尿液检查中显示出更粉红的紫色. 我知道如果我不运动, 我知道这意味着我的肝脏的糖原供应必须耗尽, 尤其是因为大脑是主要的器官使用我们身体所获得的能量, 在我的情况下从我的脂肪储备, 想知道我是否真的不再需要使用旧路线的额外葡萄糖. 似乎我们必须从头开始改变. 我觉得自己正好像在1万英尺高处行走一样 (我住在阿肯色州的高度约为700英尺). 一旦我回家感觉像一个意大利面串一切都改变了. 我在一小时之内就把房间清理好了, 我甚至帮助清理衣柜在我的和爸爸. 我觉得自己对学习和好奇心更加开放. 我只需要坐下喝一杯茶或水, 在日落或云彩中获得极为明显的快乐... 然后我会回到清洁和整理工作, 我认为这是禁食的好处之一. 节省食物费用,更多的空时间, 在11天的时间里减掉17磅. 我从那时起再做了几次, 但我认为更长时间的动力来自于情感上的乱, 我开始意识到我一直把友视为一种力量, 我意识到这已经是干扰的原因. 我已经认出了这一点. 我觉得自己已经过于依赖, 我的生活已经在改变, 很遗憾他也没有, 他的工作也失败了, 因为他的药物滥用问题已经变得不分青红白, 我不得不说我没有问题偶尔使用毒品的正确原因, 通常原因不仅仅是"高"和派对, 他被捕,现在在监狱里. 我应该在事情变得如此糟糕之前伸出援手. 但我觉得他必须独自去寻找自己的恶魔, 我认为如果他需要我, 他会打电话给我. 他从来没有和我不认为他可以从他或呼叫者ID已经除草,因为我从来没有回答奇怪或800像前#的. 但快速带来了我理解这不是我做了什么;除了我已经拉开了,所以他扔了一个适合我猜,就像任何孩子会. 因为我是真正的爸爸他疯了,但不能告诉我为什么. 这不是我们的错. 我可以说禁食是一种非常健康,清醒的体验, 我还能把胆固醇降到一个更可接受的196和三糖降到88和我的其他数字包括红细胞数和肝脏面板是正常的. 甚至我的电解质, 对于那些只喝水 (而不是矿泉水和至少每天摄取维生素) 的人来说, 有时患有高血压的人可以在这里找到真正的好处以及一个挑战, 因为适应适应可能就像习惯新血压药一样困难. (当然,在一些西藏僧侣中, 禁食的时间仍未确定). 无论如何,我在11天内从185升至168. 两年多前, 五个小时的夜晚, 似乎事情应该是双向的, 也许他确实需要我比我需要他. 这种行为需要一段时间才能表现出来, 我希望这是一个很好的例子. 在某些方面, 对于那些没有做好准备或意识到风险的人来说, 没有像螺丝刀这样的工具应该被扔出去玩, 已使用的证券 禁食 (178):关系 (44),回顾 / 总结 (11),各种 (28)

Mental and Physical Notes on 11-day Fast None by Stephen Citation:   Stephen. "Mental and Physical Notes on 11-day Fast: An Experience with None (exp50687)". Erowid.org . Jul 25, 2006. erowid.org/exp/50687 DOSE: 1 Vitamins / Supplements (daily) BODY WEIGHT: 185 lb I had recently been a victim in a fight with my stepson that had been totally unexpected and out of the blue, since we had been very close friends, although he was now 25 and married, for 14 years. My mind was troubled and so I decided that it would be a good time for me to try out some time of meditation and thought that a fast can provide since a good time of our lives are devoted to all of the desires, planning and details of eating food. This would give me long stretches of time for me to think on what had changed to precipitate this monster that I had observed in my friend. My body I expected would be cleaner and leaner, I had been putting on the pounds. I was starting at the beginning of a new year 2004. I was 20 lbs over my desired body weight according to the charts and my cholesterol had measured 297 and triglycerides over 600 in a recent physical exam. daily: 1 multivitamin and water (no food) The experience brought about some changes in myself I had not expected. On the first 2 days I was mainly experiencing the adjustment to hunger and especially a little weakness on the second and especially the third day. On day two I also had some nausea but it passed and my stomach made funny noises. By the third day toward the end I expected I had hit the ketosis point as I felt a tad more energized and I was showing a more pinkish purple tone on the ketone stick urinalysis. I knew that from that point on my body would have to run exclusively on fats and protein if I did not exercise. I knew that this meant my liver's supply of glycogen must be spent and no more glucose could be provided by the usual mechanisms. Ketosis (or the conversion of fats into usful glucose) for mental processes especially, since the brain is the primary organ using the energy our bodies have obtained, in my case from my fat reserves, was being activated,... but not quite wholly. On the fourth day I decided to do some fast walking to see if I was really out of the need for extra glucose via the old route. It seems one must kick start this change over. That fourth day seemed to be the kick start by exercise that left me winded as if I were walking at a 10,000 foot altitude ( I live in Arkansas at about 700 ft.). Once I came home feeling like I was a spagetti string it all changed. Within an hour I was cleaning my room, going though drawers and cleaning them out. I even helped clean the closets at my Mom and Dads. My mind was clearer and I felt much more open to learning and curiosity. At times I still would get tired but it only would take sitting down and a glass of tea or water and I would take exceptionally noticable pleasure in a sunset or clouds overhead... then I would be back to either cleaning and organizing and once done with all that, I would read and think. This was what I consider one of the many benefits of fasting. Along with saving money on food, more free time, and a loss of 17 lbs over the entire 11 days. I have since done it a few more times but I think the impetus to do it longer came from the emotional disturbance that forced me to reflect on my being and the psychology of others after the lose of a friendship that seemed like a fortress impenetrable from any disruption. I began to realize that I had been considering my friendship as a form of strength from which I had gained much of my self confidence and that I needed to be self confident without the friendship before the friendship could work. Yet I realized that had been the cause of the disruption. I was already recognizing that. I felt I had been too dependent and my life was already changing, I just hadn't been ready for that much of a change. Sadly neither was he and his job fell apart as his substance abuse problems has become indiscriminant and unhealthy. I must state I have no problem with occasional drug use for the proper reasons and usually the reason is not just to get 'high' and party but for self help and mental adjustment. He was arrested and is now in jail. I should have reached out to help before it got so bad. But I felt he would have to search through his demons alone to know why he was so upset. Because of how violent he became and without even apologizing I felt he would call me if he needed me. He never has and I don't think he can from where he is at or caller ID has weeded it out, because I never answer strange or 800 like prefixed #'s. In any case I feel no guilt in the result and plan to talk with him as soon a possible but the fast brought me to the point of understanding that it was nothing I did; except that I was already pulling away so he threw a fit I guess, much as any child would. Since I was really Dad he went so crazy but couldn't tell me why. It was neither of our faults. I can say that fasting is a very very healthy and mind clearing experience at least for me. I was able to work out personal problems as well as bring my cholesterol down to a more acceptable 196 and my triglycerides to 88 and the rest of my numbers including red blood cell count and hepatic panel were normal. Even my eletrolytes which can be an issue with persons drinking only water (instead of mineral water and at least a daily vitamin) were all well in range. Sometimes persons with high blood pressure can find real benefit here as well as a challenge, because weathering the adjustment can be as difficult as it is getting used to new blood pressure medication. And of course one cannot fast forever. (of course in some tibetan monks this stands unresolved how long one can fast). Any way I ended up from 185 to 168 in 11 days. some report even faster weight loss. It has been 2 years or more now since that night of 5 hours of screaming threats with a hammer but I think I will try to get in touch with him. Seems like things should work both ways maybe he did need me around more than I needed him. It just took a while for the behavior to play itself out..... much longer than it did for my fast to help me figure things out!! Well I hope this is a good example. It is certainly no more or less healthy in some ways than mind expansion with drugs. Rather I should say that it is risky for those who are not prepared or aware of the risks just as it is for drugs. No tool just like a screwdriver should be thrown around for fun you could hurt yourself if you don't realize the screw driver is for screwing and unscrewing screws; the ball is for catch...!!

When taking higher doses 10mg IR, there is just too much stimulation and not enough focus, there is also more body load from feeling anxious/jittery just general restlessness. I find ritalin makes me sleepy. I find low doses of adderall do the same to me, I have great power naps on adderall. IPPH should be revisited at a higher dose, It is now a potential runner up for 3-FMA for me.

Do not use this as a dose guide or as fact. Everyone is different and will experience this differently. Enjoy. Onset is always around 10-20 mins and peaks after 25-30 mins. I stayed here for 15-20 mins before a gentle comedown brings me back to normalcy. This is true regardless of the dose I took. Dose: 500mg Very slight effects.

我会尽量让这简短而甜美. 我是一个酒者. 我已经用过所有药物. 不管怎样... 我发现他们有30/500片可代因. 我开始对它们进行实验. 尝试不同的组合, 包括喝酒. 这不是一个好主意. 在一天的时间里, 我服用了大约250毫克可代因. 我试图提取,但我不能保证提取了多少代代因. 我在下午3点左右开始, 感觉很棒. 我慢慢地喝了一口, 到了午夜我感觉很好, 我在下午1点左右吃完最后一片, 开始担心我可能吃了太多. 我坐了一会儿, 没有出现任何阿片类药物过量症状, 我开始感到恐慌, 没有睡觉. 我觉得自己是个可怜的人, 我决定去诊所. 在诊所, 我被录取为严重脱水和可能过量服用. 估计我体内可能有5500毫克的青 (我可能至少提取了一些青降低了潜在量), 足以说, 如果我在发达国家, 我没有访问任何这些. 现在,自从我第一次经历疼痛72小时后, 我还活着, 我今天早上下院, 指示我喝大量的水和果汁. 故事的道德是不要麻醉剂如果我是一个酒者. 真的,它造成了一些严重的问题, 我仍然不确定我在清理. 另外,我不应该在发展中国家以外的国家干涉这样的药物. 我真的开始意识到我的生命没有任何价值当我躺在诊所看着孩子死于霍乱. 其他: 乙氨基 (337),可代因 (14):各种 (28),健康问题 (27),组合 (3)

Jul 7, 2009. erowid.org/exp/75620 DOSE: repeated oral Codeine repeated oral Acetaminophen BODY WEIGHT: 180 lb I'll try to keep this short and sweet. I'm an alcoholic. And I've done just about every drug in the book. Anyway... I was in a foreign country (name omitted to save myself, but suffice to say it's developing) and I discovered that they had 30/500 codeine effervescent tabs. Over the course of a week I began experimenting with them. Trying different combinations including drinking on them. Bad idea. Over the course of one day, I took roughly 250mg worth of codeine. I had attempted an extraction, but I can't really guarantee how much codeine was extracted. I started at about 3PM and felt great. I slowly sipped the concoction over the course of the evening and by midnight I was feeling good but a little disaffected, kinda weird. I finished the last of it at about 1PM and started kinda getting worried that I might have taken too much. I sat out for a while and didn't exhibit any of the signs of opiate overdose but about 3AM I started having a pain in my side, about where my liver was. I started freaking out and didn't get much sleep. I woke up the next day and felt miserable, and my side still hurt. I made the decision to go to the clinic. At the clinic, I was admitted with severe dehydration and possible paracetamol overdose. The estimated possible amount of paracetamol I could have had in my system was 5500mg (I probably extracted at least some of the paracetamol decreasing the potential amount), well below the threshold for a normal person, however I am an alcoholic which greatly decreases the toxicity level. Suffice to say, had I been in a developed country, I would have had access to better health care, blood tests and the antidote for paracetamol poisoning. I had access to none of these. Now it's just going on 72 hours since I first experienced pains and I'm still alive, but kinda nervous. I was discharged this morning with the instructions to drink plenty of water and juice. The moral of the story is not to mess with paracetemol if I am an alcoholic. Seriously, it causes some serious problems and I'm still not sure I'm in the clear. Additionally, I ought not mess with drugs like that outside the developing world. I really begin to realize that my life isn't worth anything when I am lying in a clinic watching children die from cholera.

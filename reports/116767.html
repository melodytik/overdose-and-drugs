Nov 7, 2022. erowid.org/exp/116767 DOSE: T+ 0:00 oral Vitamins / Supplements (edible / food) T+ 0:00 5 mg oral Pharms - Tadalafil (pill / tablet) T+ 4:00 70 mg oral 5-MAPB (capsule) T+ 4:00 5 mg oral 5-MeO-MIPT (liquid) T+ 5:30 120 mg oral MDMA (capsule) T+ 10:00 1.5 mg oral Etizolam BODY WEIGHT: 150 lb 5-MAPB – 5-MeO-MIPT – MDMA After Purchasing my products from a reputable vendor, and all going through a full 7-panel reagent testing regimen. It was time to try these chemicals. A couple of notes: I am a healthy male who is physically active, and religious about following the “3-month safety rule”. My partner is a healthy female who follows the same 3-month safety rule as I do. I am 150 lbs, she is 115 lbs. The 5-MAPB is fumarate, so my dosage process was as follows: 5-MAPB effects are roughly 2x dosage strength of MDMA. 120mg is my normal MDMA dose. Therefore, wanting to dose around 60mg equivalent of 5-MAPB fumarate. This would normally be 60mg x 1.34 = ~80mg. However, for the sake of caution decided to dose both my partner and I at 70mg 5-MAPB Fumarate. 5mg 5-MeO-MIPT would be added to the dose (male only) 120mg MDMA would be added only if 5-MAPB was underwhelming, or not providing the experience we sought after. 120mg MDMA would be added only if 5-MAPB was underwhelming, or not providing the experience we sought after. All ROA’s were Oral, 5-MAPB and MDMA were measured into separate gel capsules, 5-MeO-MIPT was dosed Orally via Volumetric dosing (5mg/ml). T – 4:00 = Lunch was an antioxidant rich smoothie with protein powder, antioxidant berry blend, oats, and greek yogurt. This was to act as a good “base” of antioxidants to attempt to mitigate any neurotoxicity. Magnesium was added, I took 5mg of Tadalafil (Cialis). T + 0:00 = Took 70 mg of 5-MAPB in a gel capsule. Took 5-MeO-MIPT in a glass of apple juice (male only). Both my partner and I went to take a shower and went on a walk around the neighborhood. T + 0:45 = First alerts on the 5-MeO-MIPT, a gradual body-load coming and tryptamine-like headspace. T + 1:00 = Slight feeling of nausea I took as a sign the 5-MAPB was beginning to kick in. Tactile enhancement. Completed our walk and went into our home to turn on some fun lights and some enjoyable EDM House type music. Began dancing and just appreciating our home and each other. Come up was extremely subtle/gradual. Came in waves of euphoria and energy, and a desire to cuddle and lay on the couch. Heart rate was elevated to 105 bpm when normal resting is around 65-70. T + 1:30 = Partner and I were fully enjoying the come-up. However, it was not as stimulating and euphoric as we were hoping for, so we decided to drop the 120mg MDMA. T + 1:45 = 5-MAPB began hitting harder, quite a bit of nystagmus and jaw clenching. Partner started using a bite stick she has for when the clenching is too much. Feeling the full loving and connectedness feelings, and just enjoying being touched and cuddling. Not as much tactile enhancement as just MDMA alone, however the openness and talking was extremely enjoyable. T + 2:15 = MDMA was beginning to kick in, and add a small amount of energy and sexual energy to the experience. Sex was off the table for this particular experience because we wanted to talk some things out and see if we could strengthen our feelings and create a healthy relationship together with sex and drugs. However, sex at this point would have been phenomenal, and the Tadalafil was allowing me to still get an erection. T + 3:00 = MDMA and 5-MAPB had fully kicked in. Lots of great conversation and connectedness. Tactile sensations were amazing and welcome. Music enhancement, light enhancement was excellent. A desire to move around a bit more, but remained cuddling and couch-locked somewhat. Nystagmus and jaw clenching was definitely peaking at this point, but was manageable. T + 5:00 = Beginning to come down from 5-MAPB and MDMA, wished we had redosed with MDMA at half the first dose to keep the experience going. Come-down was gentle but clear. T + 6:00 = Almost completely come down, small amount of jaw clenching remained but still slightly stimulated/awake. Decided to take some Etizolam to sleep. Afterthoughts: Full duration was around 6 hours. The experience was excellent and the come-down the following day was almost non-existent. We did take a full panel of antioxidants and vitamins to help mitigate any neurotoxicity/come-down issues. The following day we slept for about 10 hours (because of 1.5mg of etizolam) and woke up feeling primarily lazy and hungry. But felt nothing akin to psychosis or the “Come down blues”. After some good food (antioxidant smoothie again) and a 5-HTP supplement taken 14 hours after the last of the experience we were feeling as normal as ever. As far as overall effects: The slightly longer duration of the 5-MAPB was a welcome addition, I do not know if I would take MDMA without it. However, I will most likely try a re-dose of the MDMA at 60mg for my next experience to extend overall duration. I may also add some 2-FMA to the combination to add a little stimulation (similar to the “Borax combo”). The smooth come-up and come-down was very nice, and there was definitely a nice afterglow compared to some experiences with just MDMA.

我是一个24岁的男性, 我看过一次或两次, 它看起来像巧克力混合. 我从来没有服用过这种药物, 我决定尝试一下. 这一批看起来与第一批非常不同,它是棕色的阴影,并迅速变成红色使用马奎斯试剂 (MDMA风格,非常快速的反应,颜色甚至相似但红色不是紫色),和棕色的麦克. 我注意到互联网上关于这种物质的信息严重缺乏, 存在的信息可能是非常矛盾的 (因为它与主观报告有关, 对于所谓的纯MDAI,从红色到没有反应). 无论如何,从我收集的我认为这将是一个与夫人松的夜晚. 我认为它会有MDMA的同情效应和所有的血清激素活动,但没有速度,我希望它是温和的 (值得注意的是,一个非神经毒性版本的MDMA/MDA是我首先感兴趣的原因). 尤其是在计划的120毫克剂量, 据说在小端. 另外,我的剂量中大约有20毫克掉落在纸上, 我的同事L也服用了同样的120毫克. 30分钟 躺在床上,谈论过去的半小时,我想知道如果我真的要上来... 或是我服用的剂量是否过于轻微. ,好吧,我想. 我注意到我的动作有点不同, 在床上感觉到L更好. 我感觉自己的手感觉更多. 但这并没有什么激烈的. 在大约10分钟内, 在某个时候, 像MDMA一样, 它在1秒内从0到60英寸. 一秒钟,什么都没有;第二秒:一切! 这感觉有点像梅斯卡林, 然后我意识到... 一个小时 这实际上感觉不像MDMA! 但这感觉非常像MDA! 事实上,这种感觉比MDA更强烈, 对于这次体验有多么迷幻, 她说这对她来说有点像是三胺 (她对MDA不熟悉). 很难想出要说的句子. 这应该是一个浪漫的夜晚在一起变成了有点分离,安静的夜晚. 不是说我们没有联系. 时间为2.5至3小时 我们都觉得这次旅程更有幻觉效果 (就像大多数幻觉药和大麻一样). 这绝对是一个旅行而不是一个"滚球". 虽然有些同理心, 但很有限, 任何地方都有强大的追踪器. 我们再次躺下,在某个时候,也许在3点左右,强烈的放松打击我们两个. 我们彼此拥抱, 沉入一个有趣的睡眠. 在路上, 很多CEV, 它们在旋转. 但这是我第一次真正闭上眼睛, 这几乎感觉像一个迷幻片在这一点上,这是如此放松,我们都睡着了 6小时 虽然感觉已经开始减弱, 我们又抽了一口, 感觉好像在大麻上了一点. 这是一个意想不到的夜晚的非常有趣的结局. 虽然第二天我们确实感到有点乱. 我也很容易吸食胺和胺. 这并没有造成太大的问题, 而这样的东西甚至bk-mdma给我一个相当明显的崩. 我还想明确一点, 这是一种你应该知道来源的物质, 并 *有检测套件*, 尽管许多其他"RC"被消耗在没有这些措施的情况下, MDAI对不同的批量进行不同的检测, 缺乏信息也没有帮助. 其他:

Mar 1, 2012. erowid.org/exp/94689 DOSE: T+ 0:00 100 mg oral MDAI (powder / crystals) T+ 2:30 smoked Cannabis T+ 6:00 smoked Cannabis BODY WEIGHT: 150 lb I’m a 24 year old male, and for a long time I was interested in the chemical MDAI. I saw it once or twice, and it looked like chocolate mix. So I never took that stuff, and it ended up being less than 50% MDAI anyway, so I’m glad I abstained. However, a year later or so after seeing that it was about to be banned federally, I decided to try some. This batch looked very different from the first, it was a tan shade, and quickly turned red using the marquis reagent (MDMA style, very fast reaction, the color was even similar but red not purple), and brown with mecke. One thing I noticed is a serious lack of information about this substance on the internet, and the information that does exist can be very contradictory (because it has to do with subjective reports, and MDAI is known to sometimes be very impure, leading many people to consume other substances, sold as MDAI on “reputable” sources… the correct Marquis reagent color is not even empirically known, and varies from red to no reaction for supposedly pure MDAI). Regardless, from what I gathered I thought it’d be “a relaxing night with the missus”. I thought it would have the empathogenic effects of MDMA and all the serotonin activity but no speediness, and I expected it to be mild (it’s worth noting that a non-neurotoxic version of MDMA/MDA was the reason I was interested in the first place). Especially at the planned dose of 120mg, said to be on the small end. On top of that, about 20mg of my dose fell off the paper, so I ended up taking 100mg. My partner, L, took the full 120mg of the same batch. 30 mins Laying in bed, talking for the past half hour, I wonder if I’m ever really even going to the come up… I have heard of that happening. Or whether the dose I took was too mild. Oh well, I figure. After getting up to go to the bathroom, I noticed that movement was a little different, and it was nice to feel my own skin. Even nicer to feel L in bed. My hands felt like they “sensed” more. But it wasn’t anything intense. Within about 10 mins, at some point, I felt a sharp, unexpected MDxx-like comeup, with startling intensity. Like MDMA, it went from “0-60″ in 1 second. One second, nothing; the next second: EVERYTHING! This is feeling a little bit like mescaline, which surprises me. And then I realize… 1hr This actually feels nothing like MDMA! But it feels SO MUCH like MDA! In fact, it feels much trippier than MDA, and the MDA body feeling is very noticeable. L, who really likes tryptamines, was also very surprised by how psychedelic the experience was at this point. She remarked on how to her it kind of felt like a tryptamine (she is unfamiliar with MDA). Things were getting very dreamy, and it was very difficult to think of sentences to say. What was supposed to be a romantic night together was becoming a somewhat dissociated, silent night. Not that we didn’t connect. 2.5-3hr L and I had some cannabis, and both of us found the trip even more psychedelic (like with most psychedelics and cannabis). It was definitely more of a trip than a “roll”. There was some empathy, but it was limited, but the psychedelic effects were very strong. There were strong tracers everywhere. We laid down again, and at some point, maybe around 3hr, a strong relaxation hit both of us. We held each other and drifted into an interesting sleep. On the way, there were many CEV’s, like shapes turning into other things. Objects spinning around. The whole trip was filled with CEV’s but this was the first time I actually closed my eyes for a decent amount of time. It almost felt like a psychedelic opiate at this point, it was so relaxing that we both fell asleep 6hr We woke up at the 6 hour mark or so, definitely still feeling it, although it was waning. We smoked again to kick it back up, and it felt like a nice glow over the cannabis high. A very interesting end to an unexpected evening. There was very little in the way of stimulation, and there was no “crash”, though we did feel a bit scatter-brained the next day. NOTE: I would like to add that I happen to be very susceptible to MDMA and amphetamine. This didnt cause much of a problem, whereas something like that even bk-mdma gives me a pretty noticeable crash. I would like to make it clear, also, that this is a substance where you should know your source, and *have a testing kit*, even though many other “RC’s” are consumed without these measures, MDAI tests differently with different batches and the lack of information doesn’t help.

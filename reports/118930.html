Took about 13 seeds and toasted them, covered, on a warm pan until most popped. There was a roasty popcorn smell and a bit of smoke when I took off the lid. The seeds were now puffy and brittle. Then very gently peeled the outer husk off each seed. Put the seed meat into a coffee bean grinder and pulsed until a fine powder. Eyeballed 10:1 seed to calcium hydroxide. So about 1/4 teaspoon of calcium hydroxide powder for 13 seeds (some were small some were big so if say this works for anywhere from 10-15 seeds but haven't tried a lower or higher ratio). Added the CaOH to the coffee bean grinder with the seeds and pulsed again until mixed well. Poured the powder into a bowl and added enough water to make the mix pasty. 1/2-1 teaspoon. Mixed well with a silicone spatula. Spread the mix thin on the warm pan and turned the heat on and off to keep it warm and accelerate drying. Left it on the pan until it was a brittle flaky texture and the color had turned light and nothing felt soft. Smells just like peanut butter or roasted peanuts. Scraped everything up into a little foil dish and let sit for 48 hours, kneading the chunky powder now and then, peanut smell was almost gone in that time. Put the chunky powder back into the coffee bean grinder and pulsed until a fine powder accumulated on the cover. Dusted it off. Packed tepi with 1/2 teaspoon of snuff for each nostril.

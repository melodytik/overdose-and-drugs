Not sure if it has any bearing but the following all occurred while I had an empty stomach. I also am not on any medications, etc. I was nervous at first so he cut up a "baby line" for me with a razorblade (25mg). I began to feel the effects approximately 5 minutes after insufflation. I felt surprisingly sober, I expected to be incapacitated and not have a good time at all. The main effects were as follows: vision became notably worse (blurred, slightly unfocused), anxiety was quelled, train of thought slowed somewhat, and I tripped over my words while talking but not terribly. This lasted for approximately an hour and a half. By this point I had felt confident in trying a larger dose and insufflated 75mg. I did not feel as profound of an effect as I anticipated but this was likely due to mindset (no longer nervously testing the waters and trying hard to notice any small effects). I felt as previously described albeit more pronounced but was also slightly quieter and would become lost in thought for a minute or two at a time. I was rather uncoordinated and clumsy in my movements. I felt much less concerned with the material world around me, my anxieties regarding self consciousness and the like were nonexistent. I also felt much more tired, I kept putting my head in my hands and my eyelids felt heavy but not exactly in a way that made me want to go to sleep. It was overall very pleasant. I did end up going to sleep, approximately 2 hours later. I had strange nightmares but I get these regularly due to PTSD so I can't attribute that to this substance.

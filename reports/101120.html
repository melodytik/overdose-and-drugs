LSZ & Alcohol - Erowid Exp - 'The Misguided Psychedelic'

中文译文 致幻剂

我用两次LSZ (lysergic acid 2,4-dimethylazetidide) 剂量, 每次150μg. 我在过去有过相当多的迷幻体验, 我在晚上7点左右把两张纸落在舌头下面. +0.5小时; 经过前30分钟, 我感觉到头部有点变化. 我的朋友和我正在玩Xbox浪费一些时间. 因为我比我的朋友重, 他通常比我更快地得到药物的效果. 我们会叫他约翰. 由于他的新陈代谢很快, +1小时; 我们还在玩Xbox. 约翰和我回忆起我们高中时代, 谈论社会对大多数事情是多么无知. 我们开始体验到一个巨大的身体高,比LSD更积极. 我得到了一些不错的OEV,但几乎没有CEV. 我们一开始, 就关掉Xbox, 开一些音乐. 我通常在使用LSD时, 只有很少的音乐. 包括杰斐逊飞机,亨德里克斯和披头士. 我们把披头士乐队的"白色专辑"播放出来, +1.5小时; 半个小时过去了, 我们在全速, OEV的似乎比CEV对我有任何形式的闪的光. 这种闪持续了大部分的旅程. 这种药物与LSD相比, 尽管如此,它并没有缺乏视觉效果. +2小时; 我闭上眼睛, 发现车辆的车辆在那里, 却无路可走. 不管音乐有多深, CEV 们都在做自己的事, 这并不意味着视觉效果不强烈. 需要更多地探索LSZ CEV的复杂性, 我可以看到星星周围的光环. +4.5小时; 约翰建议我们走出去欣赏大自然. 我们一到外面, 感觉很棒, 身体的高潮在这个时候仍然很强烈. 我们都觉得和平的世界在我们的手. 我坐下来开始冥想, 不同于LSD, 我可以让视觉效果随心所欲. 我开眼睛, 在外面的整个时间, 这特别奇怪, 因为只有一定部分的天空在闪, 我可以告诉天空几乎看起来像宇宙的结构将撕裂如果我有更多的这种药物. 我们将继续在外面待上一个半小时. 我注意到这种药物对每个人来说都很不同, 灯光闪与OEV的过度供电. 我们在外面从我的车里拿出一些酒精, 准备一下晚上的剩余时间. +6小时; 我注意到视觉效果已经有所减弱. 尽管我仍然对OEV和CEV有些复杂. 约翰和我开始喝伏特加来放松我们的肌肉, 准备在几个小时内睡觉, 我们知道这次旅行的最佳部分已经结束. 我个人喜欢复杂的图案, 而不是我的整个视觉看起来像电影卷播放旧电影. +8小时; 我闭上眼睛, 发现这就像其他日子一样, 我闭上我的眼睛,我注意到它像任何其他日子与我的非常糟糕的HPPD但更强烈 因为药物仍在我的体内浮动. 音乐的欣赏仍然在上升, +10小时; 这不一定是旅程, 更像是后光. 我已经注射了好几个小时, 现在我觉得睡觉很舒服. 我躺下,意识到我的旅行结束了. 很难入睡,即使有酒精在你的系统当你仍然看到分数. +11小时; 我终于睡着了, 这就是我结束这次旅行的地方. 总结; 但缺乏LSD的精神和思维. 约翰和我都同意LSZ比其他lysergamides更像LSD. 我们都了8到10个小时, 它们的出现是逐渐的, 我注意到肌肉的紧张程度比其他很多迷幻药要小. 药物是深的,但真的没有去任何地方. 我觉得,我不仅看到整个颜色谱的幻象, 不仅是深层碎片, 我看到了一系列蓝色和深紫色的CEV和'泡'从我的视力中心辐射,向外与我的OEV. 身体上的高是比LSD更积极的, 在我看来, 我肯定会再次服用这种药物, 我会再添加150μg,使其达到450μg. 保持安全,并负责. 谢谢您的阅读, 希望这份报告能帮助您找到您想要的东西. 真诚地说, 没有. 其他: 长期视觉 (40),一般 (1),小组 (2-9) (17)

The Misguided Psychedelic LSZ & Alcohol by AE Citation:   AE. "The Misguided Psychedelic: An Experience with LSZ & Alcohol (exp101120)". Erowid.org . Apr 15, 2016. erowid.org/exp/101120 DOSE: T+ 0:00 300 ug LSZ (blotter / tab) T+ 6:00 repeated oral Alcohol - Hard BODY WEIGHT: 200 lb I dosed two blotter hits of LSZ (Lysergic acid 2,4-dimethylazetidide) at 150µg a blotter. I've had my fair share of psychedelic experiences in the past with lysergamides such as 600-750µg of LSD and 450µg of AL-LAD. I dropped the two blotters under my tongue around 7PM. +0.5hrs; After the first 30 minutes, I feel a slight head change. My friend and I are playing Xbox to waste some time. Since I weigh more than my friend, he usually gets the effects of the drug faster than I do. We'll call him John. He was experiencing a head change in less than fifteen minutes because of his fast metabolism. +1hrs; An hour has passed and we are still playing Xbox. John and I reminisce our high school days and talking about how society is clueless to most things. We start experiencing a huge body high, more positive than that of LSD. I'm getting some nice OEV's but barely any CEV's. As soon as we start to trip, we turn off the Xbox and put on some music. Usually on LSD, I keep my playlist to very few. Including Jefferson Airplane, Hendrix , and The Beatles. This trip with LSZ we played the White Album from The Beatles as a new spin on things since this isn't our first choice most of the time. +1.5hrs; A half hour passes and we're in full gear, the OEV's seem to be more than the CEV's for me with a flickering of any form of light. This flickering continues to go on most of the trip. Putting on Hendrix, we realize that this drug is not nearly as spiritual as LSD. It does not lack visuals however. +2hrs; Closing my eyes, I notice that the CEV's are there but don't lead anywhere. No matter how deep the music was, the CEV's were doing their own thing in no correlation to the music. This doesn't mean the visuals weren't intense. The intricacy of the LSZ CEV's need to be explored more, but I was cascaded with the vision of being in space and seeing the stars close up. I can make out auras around the stars. +4.5hrs; After a few hours of listening to some very psychedelic music, John suggests we go outside and appreciate nature. As soon as we get outside, it felt great, the body high was still intense at this time. We both feel at peace with the world at our hands. Sitting down, I start to meditate and get very into my CEV's. They still don't go any particular way, unlike LSD where I can make visuals happen however I want them to be. I open my eyes, and the whole time I'm outside I get profound flickering of the night sky with OEV's. It was particularly odd because only a certain portion of the sky was flickering with layers and layers of fractal spirals in between. I can tell the sky almost looked like the fabric of the universe would be ripping apart if I ever take more of this drug. We continue to stay outside for another hour and a half. I notice this drug is very different for every person since my friend was getting the opposite of me. Light flickering with the OEV's over powering it. We grab some alcohol out of my car while we're outside and prepare for the rest of the night. +6hrs; After inside, I notice the visuals have subsided a lot. Even though I'm still getting some intricacy with both OEV's and CEV's. John and I start taking shots of vodka to loosen our muscles up and prepare for bed in a few hours and turn the music back on. This goes on for another couple hours as we know the best of the trip is over. The flickering has calmed down a bit, which is a relief since I personally like intricate patterns instead of my whole vision looking like a film reel playing an old movie. +8hrs; Pretty down from the drug, I close my eyes and I notice it's like any other day with my VERY bad HPPD but more intense I close my eyes and I notice it's like any other day with my VERY bad HPPD but more intense since the drug is floating in my system still. Music appreciation is still up, and the body high is still there. +10hrs; It's not necessarily a trip anymore, more like an afterglow. I've been taking shots for hours now and feel pretty comfortable to fall asleep. I lay down and realize my trip is over. It's hard to fall asleep even with alcohol in your system when you are still seeing fractals. +11hrs; I finally fall asleep and this is where I'm going to end the trip. Summary; The drug showed its intricacy but lacked the spirituality and mindset of LSD. John and I both agreed that LSZ is more similar to LSD than other lysergamides. We both tripped for a good solid 8-10 hours with a few hours of the afterglow. The come up was pretty gradual and not as quick as LSD's. It was very smooth and I noticed less tightening of muscles than a lot of other psychedelics. The drug is deep, but really went nowhere. My opinion was instead of visions of the whole color spectrum going down deep fractals and went wherever my scene took me, it was more like I was in the middle of space with no planets, just the stars. I saw an array of blues and deep purples with CEV's and had 'bubbles' radiating from the center of my vision, outwards with my OEV's. The body high was a lot more positive than LSD in my opinion as it hits my 5-HT2A receptor more. I would definitely take this drug again, at a bigger dose though. I'd add another 150µg making it 450µg. Stay safe, and be responsible. Thank you for reading and I hope this report helped what you were looking for. Sincerely, A.E.

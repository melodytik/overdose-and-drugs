我从一个美国知名的供应商那里订购了一些普拉米拉囊, 订单本身花费大约30美元, 在网上阅读, 似乎300-600毫克是推的剂量, 而其他网站声称服用高达1200毫克的剂量没有任何副作用. 我订购的包装建议一个250毫克的囊. 在吃了一小袋水果零食后,我在中午服用了2个囊 (共500毫克). 没有真正明显的效果. 虽然我读过一些关于周围视力变得更清晰或颜色变得更生动的报道, 我似乎有更多的道视觉... 就像在一整天着电脑屏幕的感觉. 对于我来说没有什么不寻常. 虽然有点道视力, 但对焦能力没有明显的提高. 虽然有点道视力, 但没有明显提高集中能力. 我像往常一样容易被周围的东西分散注意力. 第二天早上吃完一顿饭后,我也服用了同样的剂量 (最近发现Pram应该与食物一起服用,因为它是脂肪溶性的,而不是水溶性的). 同样没有效果. 吃完大餐5小时后再服用500毫克, 结果仍然没有任何结果. 虽然我知道物质对不同的人有不同的影响, 我读过一些关于这种药物的有趣报道... 人们能够更容易地回忆事实和记忆, 我不得不说,我没有注意到这些服用两倍的推剂量. 最后,应该指出的是,我每晚都在抽大麻,所以也许我已经杀了Pram应该刺激的大脑的任何部分. 已使用的 普拉米拉 (599):一般 (1),不适用 (38)

The order itself cost about $30 incl. shipping for 60 250mg capsules. Reading online it seemed that 300 – 600mg was a recommended does while other sites claimed doses of up to 1200mg were taken without any negative effects. The packaging on my order recommended one 250mg capsule. I took 2 capsules (500mg total) mid morning after eating only a small bag of fruity snacks. No real noticeable effect. While I’ve read reports of peripheral vision becoming sharper or colors more vivid, I had a slight effect in the opposite direction. I seemed to get a bit more tunnel vision… like the feeling of looking up after staring at a computer screen in a tiny cubicle all day. Nothing unusual for me. And while there was a bit of tunnel vision, there was no noticeable increase in ability to focus. while there was a bit of tunnel vision, there was no noticeable increase in ability to focus. I was just as easily distracted by other things around me as I usually am. The next day I took the same dose in the early morning after a full meal (recently learning that Pram should be taken with food since it is fat soluble, not water soluble). Same lack of effect. Took another 500mg dose 5 hours after a large lunch and still nothing. While I understand substances have different effects on different people I’ve read some quite interesting reports on this drug… people being able to recall facts and memories much easier, being more eloquent in speech, having greater energy, etc. Have to say, I have noticed none of these while taking twice the recommended dosage.

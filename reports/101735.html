中文译文 致幻剂

我最近和我的朋友Ben一起尝试AL-LAD, 曾经使用过LSD, 发现AL-LAD类似, 我们订购了一些,并在周末进行了评估. 我服用225毫克,而本服用300毫克, 保持在嘴里15分钟,然后吞下. 没有任何味道. 4:30 感觉到第一个暗示来了. 轻微的肌肉紧张和神经能量持续2.5小时. 音乐慢慢变得更加强大,对话流动轻松, 我们有很多笑声. 6:30 现在已经很好地开始了, 颜色很鲜, 它们会触发CEV的古典化图像, 非常令人愉快. 我发现自己必须提醒自己放松. 7:00 在几分钟之内, 肌肉紧张显著放松, 布地毯有波浪穿过它, 布地毯中的图案正在创建一个3D纹理. 它们有轻微的能量波浪, 我在椅子上休息,然后跳起来,在房间里散步, 现在说话有点困难, 九点我很惊在这个点. 旅行已经持续了2个小时, 班是一个非常高能量的家伙,一直在说话. 我妻子到达并加入了谈话. 我很高兴,因为我已经厌倦了说话了. 我们都同意我们在7点时的水平完全相同. 这远远超过了我们上个月服用的AL-LAD剂量. 便说他无法区分这种药物和LSD, 十二点 我的妻子已经睡觉了. 我们还没有醒过来. 这里没有任何东西. 我们可以一起出去喝酒或跳舞. 我一点也不累. 但对他没有影响. 一点仍没有变化. 这就像是房客不想回家. 我已经清醒了,但我只是厌倦了谈话. 下次我这样做的时候, 便决定步行回家, 我喝了5毫克的瓦利安, 能量波正在逐渐减弱, 但颜色仍然和. 我上床睡觉,大约在3点左右就睡着了. 我在第二天早上10点半醒来, 感觉有点玩, 我真的很享受这次旅行. 我们都没有经历任何胃部问题. 我觉得再增加150克就能让视觉效果更好, 我的妻子说我们肯定会在这个问题上比阿尔-拉德更难受,这很明显. 我仍然觉得我可以在公共场所发挥作用. 旅程的长度是我能想到的唯一负面因素. 其他: 小组 (2-9) (17), 宿醉 / 几天后 (46), 第一次 (2), 一般 (1)

The Guest Who Wouldnt Leave LSZ by Horizbiz Citation:   Horizbiz. "The Guest Who Wouldnt Leave: An Experience with LSZ (exp101735)". Erowid.org . Jan 19, 2014. erowid.org/exp/101735 DOSE: T+ 0:00 225 ug oral LSZ (blotter / tab) T+ 2:30 repeated inhaled Nitric Oxide (gas) BODY WEIGHT: 235 lb I recently tried AL-LAD with my friend Ben and while it was a good time I was looking for a more LSD like experience (even though I have never done LSD). Ben had done LSD in the past and found the AL-LAD to be similar but certainly milder and a much less heady trip. Reading that LSZ was much more like LSD from the trip reports, we ordered some and assessed it over the weekend. 4:00PM I take 225ug and Ben takes 300ug, held in mouth for 15 minutes and swallowed. No taste at all. 4:30 Feel first hints of the come up. Mild muscle tension and nervous energy continue for 2.5 hours. Music slowly becomes enhanced, conversation flows easily and we have many laughs. 6:30 It has kicked in pretty good now, colors are bright and we have hit a few nitrous balloons. These trigger CEV kaleidoscope imagery and are very pleasant. The Muscle tension has built from earlier and I find myself having to remind myself to relax. 7:00 In the matter of a few minutes the muscle tension has relaxed significantly and the trip smoothed out very nicely. The furniture has paisley patterns that are mildly flowing, the carpeting has waves going across it and the pattern in the carpet is creating a 3d texture to it. There are mild waves of energy that ebb and flow. I am alternately relaxing in a chair and jumping up and walking around the room as we joke. Talking is a little more difficult now but still manageable. 9:00 I am very surprised at this point. The trip has continued at the same level for 2 hours now with no increase or decrease in intensity. Ben is a very high energy guy and has been talking the entire time. My wife arrives and joins the conversation. I am quite happy about this because I am getting tired of talking. 11:00 We both agree that we are at exactly the same level we were at 7:00. This has well exceeded the intensity of the same doses of AL-LAD we took last month. Ben says that he can’t tell the difference between this and LSD and is having a blast. 12:00 My wife has gone to bed. We are still wade awake. My experiences with psychedelics have generally been with mushrooms and the 4-tryptamines which have a sedating affect as the trip progresses. There is none of this here. We could both go out drinking or dancing right now. I am not the least bit tired. Ben has had the better part of a case of beer but it has had no effect on him. 1:00 Still no change in intensity. This is like the house guest who just won’t go home. I am wide awake but I am just tired of talking. Next time I do this I will plan more things to do while tripping. Ben decides to walk home and I pop 5mg of Valium and watch some TV while it kicks in. 2:00 the energy waves are tapering off but colors are still saturated. I go to bed and fall asleep around 3:00. I Wake at 10:30 the next morning feeling a little played, but in a very positive mood. I really enjoyed this trip. Neither of us experienced any stomach issues. There was not a lot of introspection going on with this dose but I felt upping it another 150ug would really bring on the visuals and give it more mental depth. My wife said we were definitely tripping harder on this then the Al-Lad and it was pretty obvious. I still feel that I could have functioned in a public place though. The length of the trip was the only negative I can come up with here.

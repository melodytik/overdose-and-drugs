中文译文 致幻剂

好吧,我用艰难的方式学会了这一点! 我和一些朋友口服了25毫克的2C-T-7 (我用眼睛测量了剂量,所以并不完全准确). 这一剂量需要大约2.5个小时才能产生作用, 虽然视觉效果很好, 但我觉得我可以轻松地服用30毫克. 我在旅行中没有精神问题, 因为我的头脑没有像酸一样被炸毁. 大多数Erowid的旅行报告都涉及这种物质的口服剂量. 只有一个人报告吸入了它. 这个人说他吸入了15毫克, 并且有"疯狂的视觉旅行". 这次旅程非常快, 我决定尝试这条路线, 因为它似乎有明显的优势: 我认为15毫克是个不错的量,因为我认为我可以口服30毫克 (我跟随口服-4吸入-2吸烟-1的比例). 于是我独自一人在我的卧室上午1点半, 我准备吸入2C-T-7. 我之前看过25毫克的剂量, 接下来我吸了三堆. 我也吸过一些痛苦的东西, 包括5-MeO-DMT,DPT和Crank. 这种东西彻底烧伤了我的鼻子和喉. 然后滴水的味道是绝对可怕的! 这种粉末比其他任何粉末都糟糕. 五分钟后, 鼻子开始发出强烈的声音. 尽管我疯狂地鼻水, 十五分钟后, 恶心的味道和可怕的烧伤再次涌入我的脑海, 某人说高剂量2C-T-7会导致感官过度负荷; 他/她绝对是对的! 我曾经有过热感和寒感. 我预计会有疯狂的画面, 虽然视觉效果比任何其他迷幻药都好, 每次我开始专注于他们, 我的心脏就会开始跳得非常快. 实际上,我的心在整个旅行中都很痛, 我真的担心我可能会心脏病发作, 这次旅行和我口头经历的另一个不同之处在于它对我的思想的影响. 我的思想完全消失, 我经历了复杂的幻觉. 我认为我已经跳出我的窗户,然后我告诉自己'等待! 这是一个幻觉. 我站在房子外面,然后我想'等待! 这是一个幻觉. 我终于意识到我还在床上, 我疯狂地祝自己没有真正跳出窗外. 我在旅行中经常经历这种奇怪的幻觉效应. 尽管如此,有时我完全清醒, 能够清晰地思考 (这可能使我避免了彻底的疯狂). 这些时刻并没有持续很长时间, 另一个令人惊的方面是听觉幻觉. 我听力比平时敏感十倍. 外面的活动听起来好像是在房间里和我在一起. 我一直拒绝听音乐, 我试图听音乐的音量是我在旅途中设置的. 我几乎听不到音乐, 在我的旅行中音乐也非常扭曲. 唱歌的人听起来非常不同,节奏比正常的要快得多. 试了几种不同的音乐, 终于听得太多了, 但即使是安静的房间也很! 我开始听到耳边的声变成了不同的音乐和声音. 我睡着了,大约在早上6点左右. 虽然我没有那么强烈, 连同一个关于毒品的头版报道, 一个穿着严峻的警官, 拿着毒品袋, 以及约翰·特拉沃尔塔在"战地地球"中扮演外星人的照片, 我在下午四点半左右醒来时, 视力仍有轻微影响. 总而言之, 2C-T-7 吸入是一种完全不同的体验, 我再也不会吸2C-T-7. 烧伤,不愉快的味道, 和粗的身体效应是太痛苦了. 如果我描述的野蛮视觉效果听起来有趣, 我可以很容易地看到一些孩子玩弄这个或不知不觉地服用,就像2C-T-7将是新的3PCP. 坚持使用可喜的口服剂量,但仍然要非常小心. 我想要尝试30毫克的口服剂量, 和平与安全. 时间表: 1687 2C-T-7 (54):一个人 (16),健康问题 (27),艰难经历 (5)

Ouch!! 2C-T-7 by HeWhoLives Citation:   HeWhoLives. "Ouch!!: An Experience with 2C-T-7 (exp1687)". Erowid.org . Oct 3, 2000. erowid.org/exp/1687 DOSE: 15 mg insufflated 2C-T-7 (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 160 lb Ok, I learned this the hard way! In the past I did 25 mg. of 2C-T-7 orally with some friends (I measured out the dosage by eye, so it wasn't entirely accurate). This dose took about 2.5 hours to come on and resulted in a very pleasant psychedelic afternoon. While the visuals were nice, I felt that I could have easily done 30 mg. I had no problem functioning mentally during this trip, as my mind was not blown apart like with acid,. Most of the trip reports on Erowid deal with oral dosages of this substance. Only one person reported snorting it. This person said that he snorted 15 mg. and had 'an insanely visual trip.' He said that the burn wasn't bad. The trip came on very quickly but was a little overwhelming. Well, I decided to try this route, as it seemed to have definite advantages: a more intense trip from less material (and therefore less money) with a faster launch period. I thought that 15 mg would be a good amount as I believed that I could handle 30 mg. orally (I was following the oral-4 insufflated-2 smoked-1 ratio). So alone in my bedroom at 1:30 am, I prepared to snort 2C-T-7. I had previously eyed out a 25 mg. dose, so I took this and divided it into five piles. I then snorted three of the piles. The burn was THE most intense pain I have ever experienced from snorting powders and I have snorted some painful stuff, including 5-MeO-DMT, DPT, and Crank. This stuff thoroughly burnt my nose and my throat. Then the taste from the drip was absolutely awful! Again, it was worse than any other powders. Within five minutes of snorting, it came on hard and fast. I was getting visuals even as I frantically dripped water in my nose. Fifteen minutes later the nasty taste and the terrible burn surged through my head again as I VIOLENTLY puked through both my mouth and my NOSE. Someone in the Shroomery said that high doses of 2C-T-7 resulted in a sensory overload; he/she was ABSOLUTELY right! I was alternately hot and cold. I expected the mad visuals but I did not expect the strong vibrations, which rattled up through my legs. While the visuals were better than any other psychedelic, I could not enjoy them. Every time I started to concentrate on them my heart would started pounding really hard. In fact, my heart hurt all throughout the trip and was sore for 24 hours afterwards. I was seriously worried that I might have a heart attack and I believe that I would have one at higher doses. Another difference between this trip and my oral experience was the effects on my mind. My mind was totally dissolved and I experienced complex hallucinations. For example, I thought that I had jumped out my window, then I told myself 'Wait! That's a hallucination. I'm standing outside the house,' then I thought 'Wait! That's a hallucination. I'm looking out my window,' finally I realized that I was still in bed and I frantically congratulated myself for not REALLY jumping out my window. I experienced this strange hallucination within hallucination within halluncination effect quite a bit throughout the trip. Even so, there were moments when I was completely lucid and could think clearly (this probably saved me from compeltely freaking out). These moments didn't last long and my mind would soon dissolve again. Besides the vibrations, mind dissolution, and heart problems, the other suprising aspect was the auditory hallucinations. My hearing was ten times more sensitive then usual. Activity outside sounded like it was right in the room with me. I kept turning down my music because it was overwhelmingly loud. The next day I turned on my stereo and tried to listen to music at the volume that I had set during my trip. I could barely hear the music; I had it turned almost all the way down!!!! Music was also VERY distorted during my trip. Singers sounded very different and the tempo was a lot faster than normal. After trying different types of music it was finally too much and I turned off my stereo. Yet even the quiet room was noisy! I began to hear a ringing in my ears which morphed into different music and voices. Around 6 am I fell asleep. I woke up at 8:30 am and read the newspaper, still tripping, although less intensely. The combination of a front page story about Meth, complete with a grim crew-cut wearing cop holding bags of Methamphetamines, and a picture of John Travolta as an alien in Battlefield Earth, was too much; I went back to bed. My vision was still slightly affected around 4:30 pm, when I woke up again. In summary, 2C-T-7 insufflated is a COMPLETLY different experience than doing it orally. I will never snort 2C-T-7 again. The burn, nasty taste, and rough somatic effects were too painful. If my account of the wild visuals sounds fun, don't be fooled; it was really not worth it. I could easily see some kids mess around with this or get unwittingly dosed, and just like that 2C-T-7 would be the new³ PCP. Stick to the enjoyable oral doses but still be very careful. I want to try the 30 mg. oral dose but I imagine that high oral doses could be just as intimidating. Peace and Safety.

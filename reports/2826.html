(植物材料) 这是我一生中最开明的经历. 我试过蓝莲花的前一天. 这让我意识到我的身体和朋友的光环. 我们都能感觉到我们之间流动的能量. 我觉得自己很新鲜和自然. 我的身体充满了柔软的能量. 我们可以感觉到太阳的能量在我们身上流动. 我们闻到了我朋友花园里所有的花. 我觉得我从美丽的角度来看美丽. 第二天是不同的. 我很久以前就想吃莲花和. 我确信这两种罕见而古老的圣礼, 一种来自新世界 (墨西哥), 一种来自旧世界 (埃及) 的圣礼, 在古埃及的木乃伊中发现了可卡因的痕迹. 可卡因来自南美洲. 在古代, 一个跨大西洋的文明, 其他证据,比如在日本海岸发现的巨型城市;在波利尼西亚发现的半沉城市;在也门和马尔代夫的古建筑上发现的古老符号;以及在美洲印第安人身上发现的欧洲线粒体DNA,表明在冰河时代之前,也许在此后的某段时间里, 他们交换了什么? 这种经历与其他任何事情都不同. 时间和空间似乎被分解成它们的组成部分. 我的意识是无时无地的. 有时我觉得你变成了一个无生命的物体. 在古希腊史诗"奥德赛"中, 奥德赛斯和他的同伴前往一个拥有莲花的岛屿, 莲花让人们忘记家乡. 乌迪修斯必须强迫他们回到船上. 在我拿走莲花之前, 我以为这意味着莲花会让你忘记. 现在我知道这意味着奥德赛斯和他的家人不是一个很好的地方. 我吸了足够多的,让我有点浮动,然后我有了一些莲花提取物. 我吸了,直到我开始轻微. 我能感觉到能量在我周围和我身边流动. 然后我对我的好朋友萨尔维亚说道: 我没有太多的, 足以让时间停止. 但当我站起来在花园里散步时, 效果令人难以置信. 我没有感觉我在任何药物. 我的头没有感觉从的阴云和没有让我困惑. 莲花使它们稳定. 感觉比以往任何时候都更自然,更清醒,更少感到困惑. 我没有任何感知扭曲,也没有幻觉. 一切都是如此清晰. 我可以看到所有东西的模式. 每种植物,昆虫和花朵,我都能看到它们是如何排列的. 我可以看到每一棵树上的每一片叶子, 我看着天空, 不仅看起来蓝色, 我可以看到一个非常大的空间, 园子似乎迷人. 我感觉到一个神奇的力量. 我能感觉到现实中固有的秩序, 我意识到没有上帝,没有大自然,没有男人和女人. 只有一个问题. 它是一切和无,它是无限大和无限小. 它是理性的和非理性的,混乱和有序的,确定和不确定. 这是一个完美的,但它不能以任何其他方式. 即使是不完美的, 它仍然是完美的. 下次我会尝试服用更大的和. 我觉得这两种植物已经让我对现实和古代文明有了深刻的了解. 我建议如果你想尝试西兰花或莲花你同时采取其他. 它们是密码的两个部分. 萨尔维亚把世界拆开,莲花把它重新组合起来. 如果你相信古老的神圣植物能教导我们关于我们自己和世界的事情, 它是最有效的理解工具, 时间表: 2826 莲花/百合花 - - 桃花 (Nymphaea nouchali var caerulea) (105), 神圣 (44), 大麻 (1): 单独 (16), 神秘经验 (9), 组合 (3)

Lotus and Salvia Unlock Reality Blue Lotus (extract), Salvia divinorum, & Cannabis by Ray Aten Citation:   Ray Aten. "Lotus and Salvia Unlock Reality: An Experience with Blue Lotus (extract), Salvia divinorum, & Cannabis (exp2826)". Erowid.org . Sep 8, 2000. erowid.org/exp/2826 DOSE: smoked Cannabis (plant material) smoked Lotus/Lily - Nymphaea nouchali var caerulea (extract) smoked Salvia divinorum (plant material) This was the most enlightening experience of my life. I had tried blue lotus on it's own the day before. It made me aware of my body's aura and of my friend's auras. We could all feel chi energy flowing between us. I felt fresh and natural. My body was buzzing with a gently fluffy energy. When we held our hands up to the sun we could feel it's energy flowing through us. We smelt all the flowers in my friend's garden. I felt that I was apprieciating beauty from beauty's point of view. The next day was different. I had wanted to take lotus with salvia for a long time. I was sure that the combination of the two rare and ancient sacraments, one from the New World (Mexico), one from the Old World (Egypt), would teach me something very important. Traces of cocaine have been found in ancient Egyptian mummies. Cocaine comes from South America. There must have been a global sacrament using civilization in ancient times which traded across the Atlantic. Other evidence, such as the giant city found submerged off the coast of Japan; semi-submerged cities in Polynesia; ancient symbols found both on ancient buildings in Yemen and the Maldives; and a European line of Mitochondrial DNA found in Native American Indians, suggest that before the ice age and maybe for some time after there were intercontinental trade links. What did they trade? -The plants of the Gods. The experience is unlike anything else. Time and space seem to break down into their constituent parts. My consciousness is timeless and spaceless. And sometimes it makes me think you've turned into an inanimate object. In the ancient Greek epic poem 'the Odyssey', Odysseus and his men go to an island of Lotus eaters who are kind to them and give them lotus. The Lotus makes the men forget about home. Odysseus has to force them back onto the ship. Before I took the lotus I thought that this meant that lotus makes you forgetful. Now I know that it means home for Odysseus and his men wasn't a very nice place. I smoked enough pot to make me a bit floaty, then I had some lotus extract. I smoked it until I started buzzing slightly. I could feel the energy flowing around me and through me. Then I said hellow to my good friend salvia, the divine diviner's sage. I didn't have very much salvia, it wasn't enough to make time stop. But when I stood up and walked around the garden the effect was incredible. I didn't feel like I was on any drugs. My head didn't feel cloudy from the pot and the salvia didn't make me confused. The lotus had stabilised them. I felt more natural, more aware, less confused than I have ever felt before. I wasn't having any distortions in my perception, no hallucinations. Everything was so crystal clear. I could see the patterns in everything. Every plant, insect and flower I could see how it was ordered. When I looked at some trees I could see every leaf on every tree at once, and I could see the pattern that they made. When I looked into the sky it didn't just look blue anymore, I could see an incredibly large space, like I was staring into infinity. The garden seemed enchanted. I could feel a magical force. I could feel the order inherent in reality, and the energy that drives it. I realised that there is no God, no nature, no man and no woman. There is only one thing. It is everything and nothing, it is infinitely big and infinitely small. It is rational and irrational, chaotic and ordered, certain and uncertain. It is perfection, but it couldn't be any other way. Even when it is imperfect, it is still perfect. I'm going to try taking a larger dose of both lotus and salvia next time. I feel that these two plants have already given me incredible insight into reality, and ancient civilisation. I suggest that if you want to try either salvia or lotus you take the other at the same time. They are two halves of a code. Salvia takes the world apart, lotus puts it back together again. If you believe that ancient sacred plants have something to teach us about ourselves and the world, take this sacrament. It is the most effective tool for understanding, and it's part of your heritage as a human being.

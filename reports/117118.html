A bit headier than the full body flush of a morphine or heroin, but it’s barely scratching the surface. I’ll give it 15 more minutes and see. T+1:15 Not much is happening. I feel a little spacey. Like a more euphoric Benadryl. I read online that each capsule is roughly 50mg. After further investigation it seems 30mg is equal to 5mg of morphine. I’m going to need a lot more. I determine that I should double my current dose; I take six capsules. T+1:35 I start feeling it come on. It hits slowly but then once it starts building it gets there pretty quickly. It feels like taking a slightly less euphoric oxycodone, mixed with a Zoloft or a similar antidepressant. This drug is definitely an anxiolytic and is pretty relaxing. It has some pep to it though as well. T+1:45 I decide it would be nice to enjoy some time in the hot tub. As I won’t be able to re-dose once I’m there I ought to take two more, so I do. T+1:55 I’m walking to the hot tub. Usually I love to look at the stars/planets and find the prominent ones. Venus was spectacularly bright a few nights ago so I’m looking for it. I don’t seem to care enough to think about where it should be. I misidentified a star as Venus which is unlike me to do. I’m more chatty than usual and I’m feeling pretty good about myself. I can definitely tell, without having looked in the mirror, that my eyes are constricted. That familiar squinty eyed, pinned feeling of opiates is in full force. T+2:00 I decide to walk to a different hot tub in the community next door. I stop back home first to take the remaining 4 capsules and get some water. I’ve got a little bit of dry mouth. By the time I go to take the caps I’m feeling a strong opioid buzz. This is definitely a full Mu opioid receptor agonist. It has a little bit of the limited feeling that comes with recreational buprenorphine. Basically it feels like even if I were to take a fistful of this drug it still wouldn’t feel quite as euphoric as the equal amount of oxy or heroin.

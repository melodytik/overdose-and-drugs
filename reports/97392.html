心态:准备好,愿意,快乐,但有点焦虑 场景: 在夏天的温暖晚上,我们和朋友们在海露营 过去的经验: 2CB,2CI,4-HO-MIPT,4-HO-MET,MDMA,MDA,,LSD 让我先说一下, 旅行对我来说往往是一种社交体验, 我觉得我已经感到快乐, 理智, 像我真的理解自己, 但有时它们会让我感到惊, 让我在意想不到的时候对生活有更深入的见解. 今晚将是这样的经历之一. 我们在一个偏的海上搭建营地, 波浪轻轻地,空气温和但清新,天空清, 流星雨意味着我们知道我们将会遇到一些星. 这感觉像一个完美的晚上. 20:00 - 我吞下了4-HO-MET. 20:15 - 我们把我们的空气床搬到悬崖边的悬崖下面,离水有点远 (我们不知道潮水会有多高,所以玩得安全). 我们看到海湾和夜空的美景. 21点 - - 首次效果. 我感觉到一股轻微的高兴浪潮冲过我, 21:30 - - 很黑暗,所以我们点燃一个小火. 色的光轻轻地照亮了我们的周围. 我抬头看着我们头顶的悬崖. 岩石似乎扭曲成身体和面孔, 几乎是阿兹特克人的性质, 我惊地发现了这些复杂的东西. 我着嘴笑着看着它的美丽. 我开始看到温柔的,细致的,小的线框碎片, 许多颜色, 他们抓住对象的边缘, 它们非常漂亮, 虽然很微妙, 波浪的声音变得更加数字化和闪闪发光, 22:15 - 我可以轻松地交谈,虽然说出我的想法比平时更难. 笑起来很容易, 看到事情的有趣面. 一块小石头从悬崖上掉下来. 我考虑搬到沙,远离悬崖边缘. 在我决定胜利的可能性非常小之前, 我会冒险. 很容易把负面/偏执的想法从我的脑海里赶走, 我决定我需要尿尿. 我站起来,离开我们的野火. 随着我深入黑暗, 我谨慎地踩着脚, 这几乎是黑洞. 在这个时候,我抬头看. 我大声说:',我的上帝' - - 夜间的天空看起来令人难以置信. 我很惊. 我住在城市里, 所以在黑暗的地方看到晴朗的夜空总是很愉快. 但这却是另一回事. 我说不出话了. 这几乎是痛苦的美丽. 我着星星长时间, 星星是通过这种"能量线"连接在一起的, 让人惊和敬畏. 我被宇宙的美丽所震撼. 我感觉到自己很高兴. 我觉得自己与宇宙有着完全的联系, 这是一次深刻的经历. 00:00 - 我重新加入我的朋友. 我感觉到它的效果已经减弱了, 谈话是轻松而有趣的. 一点钟 - - 几乎回到基线. 我有一些酒. 2点 - 睡觉 9点 - - 清晨醒来时感觉很好. 摘要:4-HO-MET有时被认为是一种"轻松"或"轻度"的迷幻药. 这是不公平的. 确实对身体和心灵来说都很轻松, 但它可以产生最美丽的视觉效果, 令人难以置信的高兴感, 巨大的笑声, 如果您不想整夜睡觉, 效果短暂是个好处. 我已经使用了4-HO-MET超过20次, 每次都很棒. 其他: 4-HO-MET (436), 酒精 - 酒/葡萄酒 (199):一般 (1), 烂的体验 (4), 自然/户外 (23), 宿醉/几天后 (46), 小组 (2-9) (17)

An Immersion of Colourful Euphoria 4-HO-MET & Alcohol by J Betts Citation:   J Betts. "An Immersion of Colourful Euphoria: An Experience with 4-HO-MET & Alcohol (exp97392)". Erowid.org . Aug 25, 2012. erowid.org/exp/97392 DOSE: T+ 0:00 20 mg oral 4-HO-MET (powder / crystals) T+ 5:00 repeated oral Alcohol - Beer/Wine (liquid) BODY WEIGHT: 66 kg MINDSET: Ready, willing, happy, but with a little touch of anxiety SETTING: Camping with friends on a beach on a warm summer evening DOSE: 20mg weighed on 0.001g scales PAST EXPERIENCE: 2CB, 2CI, 4-HO-MIPT, 4-HO-MET, MDMA, MDA, Shrooms, LSD Let me start by saying that tripping for me is often a social experience, rather than a spiritual journey. I feel already feel happy, sane, and like I really understand myself, so I use psychedelics mainly for fun. However, sometimes they take me by surprise, and give me a deeper insight into life when I'm not expecting it. This night would be one such experience. We set up camp in a secluded beach on a warm summer evening. Waves gently lapped, the air was warm yet fresh, the sky was clear, and a meteor shower meant we knew we were in for some shooting stars. It felt like a perfect evening. 20:00 - I swallowed the 4-HO-MET. 20:15 - We moved our air beds underneath an overhang on the cliffside, a little way back from the water (we didn't know how high the tide would get, so played safe). We had a great view of the bay and night sky. 21:00 - First effects. I felt a gentle wave of euphoria rush over me and feel the familiar energy that says 'you're about to have an interesting time'. 21:30 - It's very dark, so We light a small fire. The orange glow gently lights our surroundings. I look up at the cliff ledge above our heads. The rocks appear to twist and contort into bodies and faces, almost aztec in nature, the entire thing looks sculpted and is ever changing. I am astonished by the intricacy. I lay open mouthed and laugh at the beauty of it. 22:00 - I start to see gentle, detailed, small wireframe fractals, of many colours, they cling to the edges of objects, dancing around them playfully. They are incredibly pretty, and although subtle are easy to see and a joy to watch. The sound of the waves becomes somehow more digital and sparkling, rushing through me in a most pleasurable way. 22:15 - I can converse easily, although it's harder to vocalise my thoughts than usual. It's very easy to laugh, and see the funny side of things. 22:30 - A hint of paranoia. A small rock falls from the cliff. I think about the weight above us and consider moving out into the beach away from the cliff edge. This preoccupies me for perhaps 5-10 minutes, before I decide that the chances of a landslide are very small. I'll take my chances. It's very easy to push negative/paranoid thoughts out of my head, even though I'm a solid +++ by now. 23:00- I decide I need a piss. I get up and move away from our campfire. As I move further into the darkness I am careful where I tread, and urinate around the corner out of sight of the fire. It's almost pitch black. At this point, I look up. And when I do, I am astonished - my jaw drops and I say out loud: 'oh my GOD' - the night sky looks incredible. I am blown away. I live in the city, so seeing a clear night sky in a dark location is always a treat. But this was something else. I was speechless. It was almost painfully beautiful. I stared up at the stars for a long time, as they shimmered and glowed. The stars were joined up by these kind of 'energy lines', it seemed like a huge cosmic grid. Astonishing and awe inspiring. I was knocked back by the beauty of the universe. Waves of euphoria rushed through me. I felt utterly connected with the cosmos, in my own small way. It was one of the most profound experiences of my life. 00:00 - I rejoin my friends. I can feel it's wearing off now, but I still enjoy the aztec patterns in the rocks. Conversation is easy and fun. 01:00 - Almost back to baseline. I have a few beers. 02:00 - Sleep 09:00 - Wake up in the morning feeling great. Summary: 4-HO-MET is sometimes knocked for being an 'easy' or 'light' psychedelic. This is unfair. It's true that it's easy on the body and mind, but it can produce the most stunningly beautiful visuals, incredible euphoria, immense laughter, but also offer real insight and incredible appreciation of the world. It's also reasonably easy to socialise with on low to moderate doses, and the short duration of action is a bonus if you don't want to be up all night. I have used 4-HO-MET upward of 20 times, and every time has been wonderful.

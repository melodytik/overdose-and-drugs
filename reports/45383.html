我最近收到了粉末的维罗拉·泰多拉树脂. 大约1/8小匙的烟草是通过混合2部分树脂和1部分烤 (Yopo风格) 和砂和子来制备的. 这种自吸装置可以将粉末吹入鼻子, 而不是吸入鼻子. 烟草是被摄入的. 一个非常强大的烧伤和疼痛进入鼻和压力和皮肤颜色的变化在脸上到浅紫色 (像达到布丁但更轻). 一个非常温和的A. 感觉到了高素 (可能是布丁). 非常温和. 不知道是因为鼻腔和鼻腔中的吸烟, 还是因为化学成分. 疼痛变得强烈, 导致我连续25分钟吹鼻子和打喷. 这是在45分钟前, 疼痛还在减轻. 没有幻觉. 没有后光. 确实是个头脑的变化, 但也许与我鼻子里吸入开恩胡没有什么不同. 病毒种类 (319) :第一次 (2),一个人 (16)

Oct 28, 2006. erowid.org/exp/45383 DOSE: 0.125 tsp insufflated Virola spp. (tar / resin) BODY WEIGHT: 80 kg I recently received powdered Virola Theidora resin. About 1/8 tsp. of a snuff was prepared by mixing 2 parts resin with 1 part baking soda (Yopo style) with mortar and pestle. A self-snuffing device was fashioned so as to be able to blow the powder into the nose rather than snort it into the nose. The snuff was ingested. In about 30 seconds an extremely powerful burn and ache up into the sinuses came on along with a pressure and change of skin color in the face to light purple (like that attained with bufotenine but lighter). A very mild A. Colubrina (probably bufotenine) high was felt. Very mild. Not sure if it was just from the pain and histamine reaction to the snuff in the nasal way and sinuses or to the chemical contents. The pain became intense enough to drive me to blow my nose and sneeze continuously for 25 minutes. That was about 45 minutes ago and pain is still subsiding. No hallucinations. No afterglow. A definite head change but perhaps no different than had I snorted Cayenne pepper up my nose.

没有其他报道. 睡得很好. 第二天 下午4:30 摄入了750 五点四十五分, 警报已清晰. 我感觉到经典的身体信号, 我感觉到当我服用幻觉药, 没有任何进展. 没有视觉或思维障碍. 第二天 下午5点26分 3毫克注入. 像预期的那样, 这很痛苦. 鼻腔持续疼痛15分钟, 尽管我忍不住打喷或吹鼻子. 晚上七点半,第一时间. 晚上8:30没有更大的报道. 想知道前几天的效果是否是想象出来的. 定居下来看一部电影. 晚上九点 我吸了一小部分大麻 (四分之一碗). 感觉非常非常. 很难表达自己的想法. 意识到我在看人脸时会有微妙的视觉幻觉. 奇怪的是,没有CEV. 晚上十点半. 我仍然有微妙的幻觉, 这绝对是一个轻微的迷幻体验. 晚上11点服用3.5毫克的佐皮克隆来入睡. 我醒来第二天感觉好. 一个星期后. 下午2点17分服用5毫克与S (一个110磅的女性). 假设它需要几个小时才能像以前那样起作用. 下午2点30分,第一次警报. 这不会花几个小时才能起作用. 晚上2点45分, 经典的身体感觉开始起作用. 继续进行半小时的登. 没有视觉报告, 然而我有很大的困难在简洁地表达自己. 但她不想说什么, 因为我说"需要几个小时才会有效果". 我服用了5毫克的剂量, 假设它需要几个小时才能起作用, 我坐下来看"神奇的狐先生". 目前还没有我称之为经典的迷幻效果, 但像电视屏幕这样的效果似乎远离我, 下午3点, 完全迷幻状态. 大程度的视觉幻觉,碎形图案,明显的细节移动等等. --- 没有 上午3点, 过去12小时的++/+++状态持续. 我没有焦虑,抑郁或恶心. 报告也是一样. 她可能有较高程度的视觉幻觉. 很少出现的副作用是手. 她说她将无法入睡. 我不确定我是否能够做到. 我们都摄入了大约10毫克的佐皮克隆来入睡. --- 没有 第二天下午1点. 仍然有微妙的视觉效果, 我们都疲不堪. 我发作了相当严重的头痛. 没有. 我认为头痛是由于前几天的聚会导致的脱水. 头痛已经消失了,我感觉很好. 结论 TCB-2似乎不是超强的幻觉剂, 它似乎比DOI的效力稍微小一些. 已使用的证券 TCB-2 (581): 宿醉 / 几天后 (46), 回顾 / 总结 (11), 第一次 (2), 一般 (1), 小组 (2-9) (17)

Sep 30, 2012. erowid.org/exp/97779 DOSE: TCB-2 (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 80 kg Experiments with TCB-2 (hydrobromide salt) 4:35pm 250ug ingested. 6:30pm possible alert. Unsure. Nothing else to report. Slept well. Following day 4:30pm 750 ug ingested 5:45 clear alert. I feel the classical body signals I feel when I take hallucingens, an uncomfortability in my neck. 6:45, there has been no progression. No visual or thought disturbances. 7:45, body signals dissipate. Following day 5:26pm 3mg insuflatted. As expected, this is quite painful. The pain continues in my sinus for 15 minutes while I resist sneezing or blowing my nose. 7:30pm first alert. 8:30pm nothing greater to report. Wondering if the previous days effect was imagined. Settle in to watch a movie. 9:00pm. I smoke a small amount of marijuana (quarter of a bowl). Feel very very stoned. Having difficulty expressing myself. Realize I am having subtle visual hallucinations when looking at faces. Oddly, no CEVs. 10:30pm. Still having subtle hallucinations and still having difficulty expressing my thoughts when speaking. This is definitely a mild psychedelic experience. 11:00pm take 3.5mg of Zopiclone to sleep. I wake the next day feeling fine. One week later. 2:17pm ingest 5mg with S (a 110lb female). Being to make a stew, assuming it would take several hours to kick in as it did previously. 2:30pm, first alert. This is not going to take hours to kick in. 2:45pm, the classic body sensations I get are kicking in. The continue to mount for half an hour. No visuals to report, however I am having significant difficulties in expressing myself succinctly. S reports that she felt it kick in within 15 minutes, but didn't want to say anything after I had claimed 'it will take several hours to have an effect'. I took the 5mg dose assuming it would take several hours to kick in, and so I began to make dinner, however, I am already significantly disorientated as I make the bean stew, thankfully it just get it done before I start to experience co-ordination difficulties. I sit down to watch The Fantastic Mister Fox. 2:50pm Open eye Visuals are beginning to start (interestingly, no CEVs) Not currently what I would call classical psychedelic effects, but effects like that TV screen seems further from me than I know it is, or other size and scale distortions. 3:00pm, Full psychedelic state. ++/+++. Large degree of visual hallucinations, fractals patterns, apparent movement of details, etc etc etc. --- 3:00am, The ++/+++ state continued for the last 12 hours. I was without anxiety, depression or nausea. S reports similarly. She has had potentially a slightly higher degree of visual hallucinations. Insignificant side effects are very shaky hands. S is very stimulated and declares she will not be able to sleep. I am unsure if I will be able to. We both ingest approximately 10mg of zopiclone to sleep. --- 1:00pm the next day. There are still subtle visual effects, like visual snow. We are both tired and groggy. I develop a rather bad headache. S does not. I attribute the headache to dehydration due to general partying over the preceding days. The next day the headache is gone, and I feel fine. Conclusions. TCB-2 does not appear to be the ultra potent hallucinogen some of it's pharmacological properties suggest it should be. It appears to be somewhat less potent than DOI.

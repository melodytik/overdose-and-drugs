第一次使用4-HO-MCPT 我昨晚和一个朋友一起服用了27毫克的4-ho-mcpt. 这是一种有趣的化学物质, 我写了一些笔记, 因为关于这种化学物质的信息很少, 我和我的朋友可能已经了近一克的其他草我们vaped旁边. 这是一个相当棒的体验, 但更接近于更好的版本的被成比. 我们试图在水中溶解60毫克的4-ho-mcpt, 我们都喝了一半. 味道相当恶心, 但没有比我尝过的其他胺药更恶心. 我还没有吃任何东西, 除了果. 我开始意识到自己在. 没有真正的图像. 我觉得自己有点醉了, 但与大麻不同. 几乎没有比上一次检查更强. 现在的情况还不如最初的情况. 我在过去的几个小时里消耗了大麻, 现在我已经有了更多的机会, 现在的情况比以前更严重了. 4-HO-MCPT与大麻相结合. 我有一些轻微的CEV但我有更多从草结合轻微的HPPD. 我仍然在,一切看起来更光滑,更像玻璃. 虽然我已经不再,但我已经了. 我在接下来的几个小时里又吸了一些大麻, 并服用了一些催眠药来入睡, 时间表: 115163 发布时间:2021年2月11日 4-HO-MCPT (803),大麻 (1):第一次 (2),组合 (3),小组 (2-9) (17)

It's an interesting chemical but not super trippy. I took some notes because information about this chemical is lacking but they might not be the best notes because I also consumed a lot of weed during the experience. Me and my friend might have dabbed almost a gram next to the other weed we vaped. It was a pretty dope experience but closer to a better version of getting stoned than tripping. 20:38 T +0. We tried to dissolve 60 mg of 4-ho-mcpt in water but it ended up being 54. We both drank half of it. Tastes pretty gross but not really grosser than most other tryptamines I've tasted. We were both already stoned before this point and I hadn't eaten anything yet outside of some mango less than an hour before. T +0:05 I start to notice I'm tripping. T +0:39 still no real visuals. Colours feel a bit more saturated, my head space feels different and I feel a bit stoned but in a different way from weed. Barely stronger than last checkup. T +1:04 Still barely stronger than onset. I consumed weed during the last couple hours but I'll start to consume more now. T +2:20 Definitely tripping harder than before but that might be all the weed I did. T +2:28 Dabs are hitting me even stronger than before. 4-HO-MCPT combines well with weed. T +2:55 I have some mild cev but I've had more from weed combined mild hppd. T +3:27 I'm still tripping and everything looks a bit more polished and glassy. 1:16 AM T +4:38 I'm pretty much no longer tripping but I am very stoned. I then took some more weed over the course of the next couple hours and did some oxazepam to fall asleep because I usually sleep somewhere between 5 and 7 am.

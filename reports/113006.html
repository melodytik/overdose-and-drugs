Weighted Psychedelic Blanket 5-MeO-MALT by RCmelkor From Reddit Collection Citation:   RCmelkor. "Weighted Psychedelic Blanket: An Experience with 5-MeO-MALT (exp113006)". Erowid.org . Apr 23, 2019. erowid.org/exp/113006 DOSE: 8 mg vaporized 5-MeO-MALT (powder / crystals) repeated vaporized 5-MeO-MALT (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 180 lb Managed to procure a quarter gram of this, and haven't been able to find many reports (some of which had conflicting info regarding duration and onset) so I figured an experience report might do some good! Set + Setting: Had the mattress set up in the living room with some nice ambient music and a ocean documentary (on mute). Had no lights on, which helped the soft colours of the ocean and coral and really made this trip a thing of beauty. Did a little light breathing to slow my heart rate which proved to be beneficial. Did not take any medications or smoke cannabis prior to experiment. Trip: I weigh out 8 mg to start, and weigh another 20 on some aluminum foil in case it wanted to continue. Carefully vape/smoke the 8 mg in my pipe, held it in and lay down. The come up was very soft and smooth, mostly body sensations (I'll get to those later) and sharpening of colours. Over the course of about 1-2 minutes I gradually peak. The body feels incredible, this trip is much more mental and physical with light visual components but I can really see total disintegration at higher doses. The next 10 minutes were spent with an almost opioid like body high, I cant even begin to describe it. It's like a weighted psychedelic blanket, my mind and my body were so connected by this sensation of weight. There is a definite stimulant type feeling bud goddamn is it overpowered by the weight. I was becoming a puddle in the most beautiful way. The peak felt like it lasted longer than DMT, but in reality it was about the same (maybe just a smidge longer) but the comedown was so smooth and gradual and I was enjoying myself for an hour instead of the quick snap back to reality DMT gives me. It was difficult to remember any thoughts or insights I may have had. Almost like just a serene dissolution into nothingness that is truly indescribable and beautiful. As DMT is a acceleration of thought process and occasionally there is too much information to process, this was inverse. Everything seemed so simple. Instead of trying to comprehend existence, I was comprehending simply existing. I continued to make some trials over the eve, with some higher doses (~10-15 mg) producing body effects with greater intensity but also providing some visual entertainment. Everything looked like a fresh oil painting, and I was able to smear and blend object which was a nice effect. Noticed some mild HR increase during the come up of higher doses - but nothing alarming. Another interesting thing to note is that similar to DMT, tolerance was non existent. I could stack, repeat doses all with consistent effects. For me vaped with fb, duration was total 1 hour (1-2 min come up; 10-15 min peak; 30-45 minute gradual return to baseline). Doses were threshold around 1.5 mg; light at 4-7 mg; and becoming fairly strong around the 10 mg mark with 15 producing a extremely hypnotic (almost LSA-like in nature) and sedated state. All in all this was an extremely beautiful compound and I can't wait to give er a go again. 10/10 Anywho sorry for the novel, this was just such a beautiful and relaxed state. I could easily see this being a breakthrough compound but instead of flying up in a rocket ship you are slowly sinking into the earth dissolving into your foundation. Gah, so nice.

每天服用巴克洛芬治疗我的酒精. 下面是我在服用这种化合物时经历的"旅行报告": 时间=0:00:80毫克. 巴克洛芬是口服的 肌肉放松开始,虽然没有很明显. 时间1点:现在肯定更加平静, 肌肉放松现在已经在充分发挥作用, 没有观察到镇静作用; 存在轻微的嗜睡. 这些症状持续约3小时. T=4:00: 昏昏欲睡的感觉减弱,并被令人不舒服的头所取代,几乎与连续喝了几杯酒后几个小时感到头相同 ('房间正在旋转'). 轻微的恶心. 胃口受到抑制. 旅行结束报告. 注意:我从经验中知道这种化合物对轻度/中度戒酒有效. 它将治疗伴随EtOH戒断的失眠, 促进睡眠. 但它对早晨的焦虑没有很大的效果, 也没有提高的门. 其他: 发布时间:2018年9月18日 药物 - 巴克洛芬 (514):治疗意图或结果 (49),一般 (1),未知背景 (20)

Sep 18, 2018. erowid.org/exp/97469 DOSE: 80 mg oral Pharms - Baclofen BODY WEIGHT: 145 lb I take 80 Mg. Baclofen/day to treat my alcoholism. Below is a 'trip report' of what I experience when taking this compound: T=0:00: 80 Mg. Baclofen taken orally. T=0:45: Muscle relaxation sets in, albeit not very noticeable. T=1:00: Definately calmer now, with a greater desire to daydream. t=2:00: Muscle relaxation is now in full force, not unlike a small dose of Soma. No noted sedation; mild somnolescence present. These symptoms persist for about three hours. T=4:00: Somnolescence subsides and is replaced by an uncomfortable dizziness, almost identical to the dizziness felt hours after drinking several alcoholic drinks in succession ('the room is spinning.'). Mild nausea present. Appetite is suppressed. T=6:00: Dizziness subsides; end of trip report. Note: I know from experience that this compound is effective against mild/moderate alcohol withdrawal. It will treat the insomnia that accompanies EtOH withdrawal, facilitating sleep. It is not very effective against the morning jitters, however, nor does it raise the seizure threshold.

这一切都发生在昨晚的梦中. 我终于得到了一些4-ACO-DIPT. 我在TIHKAL上读到这个故事后, 没有太多关于这种化合物的旅行报告, 所以我决定从低端开始. 我和我的未婚夫每人摄入12毫克的4-ACE. 我们吃了一顿简单的饭, 我们只是在房子里玩音乐, 我在30分钟后开始收到一些警报. 起初只是一些和轻微的肌肉紧张. 这种感觉不是好还是坏,更是中立的. 我的未婚夫,我将称之为K, 报告了同样的事情. 在接下来的30分钟里, 寒和轻微的紧张开始加剧. 这种情绪变得更加紧张. 我感觉到我的肌肉在整个特别是腿部的震. 腿部感觉像果一样. 她的牙似乎要掉下来 (奇怪). 她还报告了类似的震感. 我的手是粘的像我通常得到大多数迷幻药. 这种感觉在接下来的半小时到45分钟内有所增加, 我们都没有受到任何心理影响. 思想过程是完全清晰的. 这一切都是关于尸体的. 我们两个都在2个半小时后开始下降, 基本上是3个半小时的基线. 我不得不说这是一次中立的经历. 我不介意,但没有真正印象深刻. 凯认为这是一个负面的旅程. 她不喜欢这种能量, 我认为如果我们的环境更好,例如在户外参加聚会, 这将是我们两人的更好的体验. 我不能动,因为我的腿被了. 这也可能会产生负面的感觉. 在户外环境下, 我仍然认为它有潜力,但,小心那些果腿! 其他: 4-AcO-DiPT (55):小组 (2-9) (17),第一次 (2)

Tension and Tremors 4-Acetoxy-DiPT by Sweatyb Citation:   Sweatyb. "Tension and Tremors: An Experience with 4-Acetoxy-DiPT (exp6831)". Erowid.org . May 14, 2001. erowid.org/exp/6831 DOSE: 12 mg oral 4-AcO-DiPT (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 190 lb This all occurred in a dream last night. I finally got my hands on some 4-ACO-DIPT. After reading about it in TIHKAL my curiosity was peaked. There aren't that many trip reports out there regarding this compound so I decided to start a the low end of the dosage range. My fiance and I each ingested 12 mgs of the 4-ACE in gelcaps. We had eaten a light meal about three hours earlier. We were just hanging around the house playing some music with the TV on in the background. I started to get some alerts in about 30 minutes. At first it was just some chills and light muscle tension. The feeling wasn't good or bad more neutral. My fiance, who I'll refer to as K, reported the same thing. The chills and slight tension started to intensify over the next 30 minutes. It turned into a nervous energy with stronger tension. My muscles felt tremors throughout especially the legs. The legs felt jello like. K said her teeth felt like they were going to fall out (weird). She also reported a similiar tremor sensation. My hands were clammy like I normally get on most psychedelics. These feelings increased a little bit over the next half hour to 45 minutes and then evened out. Mentally neither of us were affected at all. Thought process was totally clear. This buzz was all about the body. Both of us started to come down at the 2 1/2 hr. point and were pretty much baseline by 3 1/2 hrs. I would have to say this experience was a neutral one. I didn't mind it, but, was not really impressed. K thought it was more of a negative trip. She didn't like the energy tremor that is very prevalent. I think that if our setting was better, for example being outdoors at a party, this would have been a better experience for us both. I couldn't move around because my leg is in a cast. This could have cast a negative feel as well. I would try this material again at 15mgs. in an outdoor environment. I still think it has potential, but, watch out for those jelly legs!

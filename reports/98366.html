I use to think that burning breathless sensation with inhalation came from just that, yet I found I had that same sensation again. I sat around looking at my living room. Within two hours unable to feel any effects. I decide to half the dose and take one pill and see how that goes for round two. Again about an hour later I have numbness of my lips and cheeks, minor vision changes. Very little active high, same as achieved off a one or two hit high. Munchies come faster while high lasts shorter period of time. Again food goes down and none of the horrible side effects I’ve had for three years. In my first week, I have had vast improvement in quality of life. I have slowly been able to eat more in a sitting, no cramping; no heart arrhythmia changes after eating and most important no vomiting in a week. This is incredible as I have been vomiting up to several times a day for the last three years. Thanksgiving was a little trying as there was so much interaction I felt like a kid trying to talk to dad without him catching on I was buzzed. I will definitely keep taking my Marinol, in the safety of my own home or only with a designated driver. I have noticed my body is coping well and quickly adjusting as the Dr. said I would. Pros outweigh the cons. Would be big disappointment for anyone expecting to experience a high equal to inhalation of marijuana. On the other hand Teetotalers might find the first few weeks of “high” disquieting, but I urge them to hold on and see if their body adjusts, while staying open with the prescribing Doctor.

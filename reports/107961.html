首先我想说, 我对迷幻药物缺乏经验, 今晚的计划是去我朋友A的家, 试验一剂AL-LAD, 我和A在一起很舒服, A的房子是个很好的旅行环境,安静和平静. T+ 3:30 10毫克 西巴姆 我有相当糟糕的旅行焦虑由于我去年有一个糟糕的旅行, 为了对抗这我服用10毫克西大约半小时前口服管理1标签150ugAL-LAD. 我在晚上8点把它放进嘴里, 旋转它, 它大约20分钟, 我在一小时半前吃了两个塔科, 坐下来和A一起拍摄风,浏览 Tumblr,听一些音乐,洗. 一个小时变成两个小时, 变成三个小时, 在T+3 除了焦虑和胃部不舒服, 我走进门,问候我的,爬上床. 经历了我现在所认识的塞罗托尼哈欠, 我闭上眼睛,把脸压在枕头上, 我意识到这不可能发生, 我完全没有预料到的复杂的CEV攻击我. 在T+3.15 我坐了起来, 打开笔记本电脑, 想, 我经历了大约五分钟的第一集, 才意识到我已经开始倒了. 我起床打开灯光,坐在床上, 坐在笔记本电脑对面, 看着墙上的地图开始漂移, 我的一把枕头开始深呼吸, 我被一波强烈的焦虑袭击, 我打电话给A来接我, 他带着一辆破旧的土星来, 递给我神赐的礼物, 催化剂, 这是一个愉快的两个街区的车回家. 我们直接回到他的房间, 问候他的女朋友B, 和友好地聊我怎么做. 房间里的一切都在摇摆. 在T+4 我开始真正享受自己. 艾拉拿出一些LED杂球, 我没有得到任何标记或任何东西, 但拿着明亮的红色杂球, 仔细观察它, 经过几分钟的迷之后, 我起身跟着A走进浴室, 我对我们所处的空间的感知大大扩展, 就好像我站在体育场. 星星向后奔跑,旋转,然后回归原位置, 在T+5 我坐下来试着写一篇关于研究化学物质的报告, 我听了Coyote Kisses的Changing Guard, 观看他们EP的专辑艺术, 随着音乐的移动和放 (A+会推). 在T+6 虽然情况似乎有所缓和, 我决定我应该回家, 所以我又收拾我的东西, 我在浴室里停下最后一站,然后无事无事地回家. 抢走我的笔记本电脑, 打一场联赛, 在T+8 虽然我写这份报告时, 仍有一些漂流和勃发展, 总的来说是一个积极的经历. 尽管我很焦虑, 很有视觉效果,四处都是碎形和图案. 整个晚上我保持完全清醒,如果有点分心, 头部空间是不存在的. 我愿意将这归咎于,虽然,YMMV. 时间表: 107961 发布时间:2016年3月2日 拉德 (603):小组 (2-9) (17),第一次 (2)

Better Late Than Never AL-LAD & Oxazepam by LyudmilaStechkin Citation:   LyudmilaStechkin. "Better Late Than Never: An Experience with AL-LAD & Oxazepam (exp107961)". Erowid.org . Mar 2, 2016. erowid.org/exp/107961 DOSE: T+ 0:00 10 mg oral Oxazepam T+ 0:20 150 ug buccal AL-LAD (blotter / tab) T+ 4:00 10 mg oral Oxazepam BODY WEIGHT: 150 lb I'd like to start the report off by saying that I'm fairly inexperienced with psychedelics, having only used them a handful of times. The plan tonight was to go to my friend A's house, experiment with one dose of AL-LAD, decide whether further tests were warranted. A and I are very comfortable together and A's house is a good tripping environment, quiet and calm. T- 0:30 10mg Oxazepam T+ 0:00 150ug AL-LAD T+ 3:30 10mg Oxazepam I have rather bad trip anxiety due to a bad trip I had last year, and to combat this I took 10mg oxazepam about a half hour before buccally administering 1 tab 150ug AL-LAD. I placed the tab in my mouth at 8PM, swirled it around and gummed it for about twenty minutes, and then swallowed it. I'd eaten two tacos about an hour and a half earlier so I'd have some sustenance for the trip but nothing too heavy. Sat and shot the breeze with A, browsed through tumblr, listened to some music, took a shower. As one hour grew into two grew into three we decided the experiment was likely a bust, so I packed up my things and A drove me home. T+3 Up until this point I felt completely baseline, nothing out of the ordinary except trepidation and stomach unease that I always get pre-trip. I walked through the door, greeted my ma, and climbed into bed. Experiencing what I now recognize as serotonin yawns, I though perhaps it was time to hit the hay. The instant that I closed my eyes and pressed my face against the pillow I realized that this wasn't going to happen because I was assailed by complex CEVs that I was absolutely not expecting. I was assailed by complex CEVs that I was absolutely not expecting. T+3.15 I sat back up and turned my laptop on thinking, what the hell, I'll just watch some anime until this passes since those cevs are probably the only effects I'm gonna get off that tab. I make it through about five minutes of the first episode of Gate before I realize that I'm actually starting to trip pretty hard. I get up and turn the lights on and sit in my bed across from my laptop, and watch the maps on my wall begin to drift and sway as if floating on water. One of my pillows begins breathing deeply and regularly. I'm hit with an intense wave of anxiety and I call A to come pick me up and take me back to his place. T+3.30 A shows up in his beat up old saturn, hands me God's Gift, oxazepam, which I promptly swallow. It's a pleasant two block drive back to his house. We head straight back to his room, greet his girlfriend B, and chat amicably about how I'm doing. The walls are crawling with patterns by this point and everything in the room is swaying dizzyingly. T+4 The sweet, sweet oxazepam has kicked in by this point and I am beginning to really enjoy myself. A pulls out some LED juggling balls to see how I'll like them. I don't get any tracers or anything but taking the bright red juggling ball and peering closely into it I can see beautiful fractal patterns extending infinitely toward its center. After a couple minutes being dazzled by this I get up and follow A into his bathroom the far wall of which is covered with glow in the dark stars. After he shuts the door behind us we stand quietly there and my perception of the space that we're in expands dramatically, as though I'm standing in a stadium. The stars race backwards and spin before zooming back to their original positions and swaying to the same rhythm as the room outside. T+5 I sit down to try and write the original form of this report on r/researchchemicals, but keep getting distracted so I have A finish it off for me. Afterward I finally think I'm ready to try some music so I listen to Changing Guard by Coyote Kisses and watch the album art from their thundercolor EP shift and bloom in time with the music (A+ would recommend). T+6 Things seem to be winding down a little bit but are by no means stopping. I decide that I probably ought to get going home, so I pack my stuff up yet again and ask A to drive me back to my place. I take one last stop in the bathroom star Colosseum and then an uneventful drive home. Snag my ma's laptop and play a game of league as things wind down. T+8 As I write this report, there's still a little bit of drifting and blooming but nothing to write home about. Overall a positive experience. AL-LAD is a really forgiving substance despite my anxiety and one that I greatly enjoyed. Very visual, fractals and patterns everywhere. Throughout the night I remained entirely lucid, if somewhat distracted, the headspace was non-existent. I'm willing to blame that on the oxazepam, though, YMMV.

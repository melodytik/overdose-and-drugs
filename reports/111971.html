Aug 8, 2018. erowid.org/exp/111971 DOSE: 1 hit oral Unknown (blotter / tab) 1 drop oral Unknown (liquid) oral Alcohol BODY WEIGHT: 125 lb I've had many experiences with NBOMe's (25i and such) prior to this. The maximum amount I'd done was about 5 tabs. I was at a local desert rave with some friends. Take note I was going through a rough time and had a bad night before going out. Was not in a right mindset or setting really to be taking anything. We also drank a bit of alcohol before heading out there. My friend gave me a tab, I was walking around not feeling much yet. Then another friend of mine who I also came with gave me a liquid drop. He literally saw the large-ish drop stay on my tongue before it dissolved. I really started to feel it once I had to go piss out in the desert, I couldn't go alone. I asked my friend if she could come with me, I kept telling her I couldn't go because I was afraid a snake would bite my ass. And thats where it all began. Everything became so fuzzy and I was in a euphoric dream-like state. Last thing I remember was my friends and I sitting in the car smoking a bowl. I think they were trying to calm me down. I literally was not in control of myself and felt I was dreaming. I apparently, was running around the rave like a complete schizo. I literally was not in control of myself and felt I was dreaming. I apparently, was running around the rave like a complete schizo. I recollect running through the desert (the same bushes and rocks I refused to pop a squat by) like was seriously bolting. I was also told that I was talking about making kitten children in a river with my friend who had recently passed away about two years before I had this trip. My heart rate had tripled, I remember people trying to pour water on me to calm me down. My friends had no idea what to do with me, so they got in the car and drove me to the nearest hospital. On the way there, I was playing with my friends sisters long braids. Admiring them and being a total creep, which I also slightly remember. I took off my knee high socks and threw off my shoes in the back of her car. I was also trying to jump out on the freeway so they had to child-lock me in. Once they pulled up to the hospital, right as they unlocked their car. Before they could even look back at me I jumped out, climbed onto the hood of their car and did a back-flip off it. With no experience of doing a backflip before... barefoot in a hospital parking lot. Which I also remember landing this. I thought I was going to the hospital to give birth for some odd reason. It took 4 big cops to get me into the hospital. Woke up tied to the hospital bed with none but slight recollection of what had happened, till weeks later when I talked to my friends about it in person. Don't let this experience frighten you! I was in a horrible state of mind and going through rough times, not taking care of myself. I believe taking psychedelics can be beneficial done responsibly, in the right setting & mindset.

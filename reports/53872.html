为了合法治疗社交焦虑, 我绝望地寻找某种药物或化学物质, 我和医生讨论了试用克洛诺的可能性, 他告诉我克洛纳泽可能会让人上, 医生给我写了一份0.5毫克的药物处方, 条件是我留下药物, 只有在预计会出现严重的社交焦虑时才给我服用药物 (如上课,聚会等). 这种东西奏效了. 我对自己的焦虑有着"该死的"的态度. 我不再关心我的言行对别人的影响. 我觉得不再需要住我的舌头了. 我相信其他没有社会恐惧症的孩子也会有这种感觉. 如果我想说话,我说话. 如果我不想说话, 那么我会自满地不说话, 不会过分担心我的同龄人会因为不说话而把我看成一个失败者. 简而言之,对于患有严重社会焦虑的人来说, 但不要误解, 克洛诺也有可疑的副作用. 开始的时候我感觉很舒服. 我最能把它和我从瓦中得到的感觉联系起来, 另一种类, 醉酒,无忧状态. 我有点笑, 没有任何抑制. 现在我明白了为什么克洛纳泽帕姆会让人上, 在最初的处方书写后大约一年, 我被处方给了0.5毫克克洛纳泽每天早上服用. 我每天服用克洛诺的五六个月, 我对恐惧症保持"该死"的态度, 但在结束时, 我开始有记忆问题. 我并不是在昏迷, 完全忘记某些场合, 随着时间的推移, 这种情况变得越来越常见. 我几乎忘记了最近想到的一切. 进入我的房间时, 我完全忘记了我最初想要的东西. 我完全听从老师的指示进行实验, 几分钟后, 当我进行实验时, 我不知道该怎么做, 我只知道我在做实验. 记忆问题成为一个严重的问题. 他们让我很烦,我的成绩也开始下降. 我很快就放弃了这种药物, 我从那时起就不再服用克洛诺, 也没有觉得需要. 它做了自己的工作,而且很好. 但对于我来说, 我不知道每个人都会经历同样的经历, 其他: 药品 - 克洛纳泽 (125):回顾 / 摘要 (11),成和习惯性 (10),医疗用途 (47),不适用 (38)

The Good and the Bad Clonazepam by Sike Citation:   Sike. "The Good and the Bad: An Experience with Clonazepam (exp53872)". Erowid.org . Nov 7, 2008. erowid.org/exp/53872 DOSE: repeated oral Pharms - Clonazepam BODY WEIGHT: 160 lb After searching desperately for some sort of drug or chemical to legally treat my social anxiety, I came across a drug called clonazepam, or klonopin. When I discussed the possibility of trying klonopin with my doctor, he was hesitant to write the prescription. He informed me that clonazepam is potentially addictive, and my history of drug-abuse made the situation that much more questionable. But seeing that I was already taking paxil to treat depression and anxiety, and was still experiencing some severe anxiety in social situations, the doc wrote a prescription for 0.5 mg pills, under the condition that my mom hold onto the medicine and administer a pill only when severe social anxiety is anticipated (class presentations, parties, etc.). The shit worked. When I was on the drug, I sort of had a “fuck it” attitude towards my anxiety. I no longer cared what my words and actions may have meant to others. I felt no need to hold my tongue anymore. The feeling was what I believed to be the feeling that other kids, not suffering from much social phobia, felt. If I wanted to talk, I talked. And if I didn’t want to, well then I was complacent with not talking, and didn’t worry excessively about my peers seeing me as a loser for not talking. To make a long story short, it was a dream-come-true for a sufferer or severe social anxiety. But don’t get me wrong, klonopin had its share of questionable side effects. The first few times I got a pretty sweet buzz. I can best relate it to the feeling I get from valium, another benzodiazepine,a drunken, care-free state. I am a little giggly, and am not inhibited whatsoever. I could see now why clonazepam is habit-forming, but the relief it provided outweighed the possibility of addiction. About a year after the initial prescription was written, I was prescribed 0.5 mg of clonazepam to take every morning. During the five or six months I spent taking klonopin on a daily basis, I maintained that “fuck it” attitude towards my phobia, only without the buzz (tolerance kicked in quickly). But towards the end, I began to experience problems with my memory. I wasn’t blacking out and completely forgetting certain occasions, like one would after a night of heavy drinking. Instead I would forget small things here and there, and the occurrence became more and more common as time went on. Eventually, I was forgetting almost everything that had just recently come to mind. For example, I often thought of something I needed to get out of my room, and upon entering my room, I had completely forgotten what it was that I set out for to begin with. The same applied for the classroom, in that I would pay complete attention to the teacher’s instructions for an experiment that was to be done, and when I was to conduct the experiment a few minutes later, I had no idea what to do, I only knew that I was doing an experiment. The memory issues became a serious problem. They annoyed the shit out of me and my grades began to suffer. I promptly took myself off the medicine as soon as school ended for the summer. I have not taken klonopin since then, nor have I felt the need to. Clonazepam was a great drug. It did its job, and it did it well. But for me, it became too much of a problem to continue. I don’t know that everyone would experience the same thing I did, but I do know that benzodiazepines are known for their negative effects on the memory.

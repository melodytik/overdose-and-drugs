旅行前的事件 旅游活动 旅行后的想法 我和另外两个人 (D&E) 一天早上出发前往城镇, 经过几个小时的无效演出, 让我说我之前做过两次达图拉, 每次都取得了很好的结果, D知道一些公寓后面生长的达图拉. 我们拿出D的袋子, 拿出两个植物, 我们又回到我家, 我们决定等待几个小时,然后用Spots和joints来填补时间. 我抓住了两棵植物, 一个接一个地拔起了叶子, 大约40个叶子和3个倒在水里, 煮了半小时后, 酒已经准备好被消耗, 填满了三杯绿色液体, 这就是我们的监视员回忆. (时间:晚上7点半) 我们都坐在楼下各个椅子上. 他开始在楼下游荡, 寻找随机的物体. 于是我把三个人留在楼下,然后上楼去清理. 我发现E和D在沙发上闭着眼睛,而J坐在椅子上看着太空. 我说他闻起来像吐, 他继续告诉我他不能忍受口腔干燥, 他还告诉我他吐后窒息, 我告诉他我会把他的床做好准备,然后回来接他, (时间:晚上8点) 半小时后他抬起眼睛,说'已经?'就好像两分钟了. 很快就开始发和出汗. 他也喘不过气, 他的话语变得混乱, 我躺在他旁边的床上,告诉他他只是疯了,告诉他深吸一口气,用他的肚子推我的手, 我有一个饮料瓶, 每隔五分钟给他喝水. (下午八点半) 我下楼去看看D和E, 两人都在沙发上很舒服. 我发现J在所里, 我回到了他的卧室, 他通知我说他无法解开他的拉链, 他回答说没有,然后再去'睡'了,没有穿过子. (下午8点50分) 我把电视放在J旁边, 让我可以观察他的脸部和呼吸模式. 他坐起来,看着我,做一些奇怪的手动, 我让他躺下,给他更多的水. 这就是为什么他开始在口中喝水,因为他觉得水味很恶心 (后来他认为这瓶是个大瓶糖果, 于是我给他穿了一件很容易脱掉的拳击. 我跟着他走, 因为我确信他很疯狂, 我感到困惑,因为他走过所后门, 接着他走出去向我大喊'G! 我会告诉你他们对草做了什么. 我跟着他,他开始尿在草地上,之后似乎正常. 他走进厨房,他的阿站在那里. 她看着他,声称他看起来"不合适",他回答说:"我知道,我们昨晚谈到了这件事. 他拍了拍她的肩膀,说谢谢,然后回到床上. (时间晚上9点20分) 这时,我开始了非常糟糕的感觉. 他转过身来问我:'谁要去? 然后躺下,回到他的闭眼幻觉. 偶尔他会坐起来,摇摇头, 发出疯狂的笑声, 他着我,称我为D, 认为每个人都躺在他的床上, 经过多次保证我是G,我们在他的床上,D和E在楼下,他然后躺下,似乎放松. 接着他拥抱我,然后吻我,然后又闭上眼睛. (时间晚上9:45) 我跟着他走了. 他开始在花园里小便这一次,似乎相当有趣的整个过程. 他还在和一些不在场的人交谈, 我相信他想象着在他们身上尿尿. 我带他回家,告诉他要回到床上, 我把他推向他的房间, 但他决定他想走到休息室, 继续走. 我抓住他的手臂, 但他把我推到墙上, 像一个小女孩笑了笑, 他再次看着我,尖叫然后跑得更快的休息室. 他问:"C阿, 你还在用网吗?" 另一位阿L回应说他们已经完成了这件事. 她问我们要去哪里,当J在柜里时, 他回答说"镇上". (下午9点50分) 我终于让J回到床上, 他似乎连贯直到他躺下开始敲打周围的物体, 在此期间,他也以为我是E, 害怕从闭眼幻觉中醒来, 发现我们在拥抱. (下午10点) 于是我又跟着他走了, 但当他走进浴室时, 他拿起我的头发刷, 我说:"你不需要去所吗? 他看着我,仿佛我是一个完全愚蠢的人, 说:'不! 我只是为了把头发刷. 我问他是否想让我刷他的头发,他说是的,所以我们回到床上,我刷他的头发. 他拿起毯子,宣称它是用蛇皮做的. (下午10点20分) 睡了20分钟左右之后, 我醒来时发现J在床和我的衣服. 我问他是做什么, 他宣称这一切都是用草做的. 他挥手问我打开灯,我站起来,点开开关,几分钟后他睡着了. 我检查了他的呼吸情况, 在J的记载中, 我在G旁边醒来, 完全不记得发生了什么. 我下楼检查E和D,他们都醒了. 他们告诉我他们没有睡觉,D整晚都在看电视,E说她生病了. 他们都被接走了,回家了. 两天后我和D说话, 他告诉我, 总结一下 现在我对发生的事情也感兴趣. 我相信Datura能让我们了解人类大脑中存在的未开发的能量, 我仍然非常尊重达图拉, 相信我们还没有找到正确使用它的方法. 但目前我计划吃以获得灵性洞察力. 感谢您的阅读 没有 附录 我还告诉我的们, 其他: 导游/保姆 (39),艰难的经历 (5),小组 (2-9) (17)

Mar 13, 2010. erowid.org/exp/73530 DOSE: 1 glass oral Datura (tea) smoked Cannabis (plant material) BODY WEIGHT: 95 kg Pre-trip Events By J Trip Events by G Post-trip thoughts by J It all started on a Saturday me and two others (D And E) all left in the morning to town with the idea of scraping together some money for Dextromethorphan. After several hours to no avail we hit the stage where ANYTHING would have done. Let me state that I have done datura twice before with very heavy results each time vowing that I would never touch it again. D had knowledge of the whereabouts of some datura growing right behind some apartments. Once we reached the spot we pulled out D's Bag and pulled two plants out and put them in. We then made it back to my house where I had my two auntys housesitting. Sneaking downstairs we found a portable stove and a pot we then decided to wait a few hours before cooking and filled time with Spots and joints. It then came the time for chef J to boil what we called shamin juice I grabbed the two plants and one by one pulled leaves of it, about 40 leaves and 3 pods poured in water and put to boil. After boiling for about half an hour the brew was ready to be consumed filling three cups of the green liquid we then proceeded to drink the contents................ after that memory and time flew out the window. This is the recollection from our tripsitter. (T: 7:30pm) At this stage, we were all sitting in the various chairs downstairs. J had begun to have some trouble walking as he began to roam around downstairs looking for random objects. E had begun to get the giggles and D was starting to get what I call his 'trip face' so I left the three downstairs and went to clean up upstairs. After about twenty minutes, I went back down to check on them and found E and D on the couch with their eyes shut while J was sitting on a chair looking into space. I remarked that he smelt like vomit and he proceeded to tell me that he couldn't stand the dry mouth and had vomited in a dishwashing bowl in the corner of the room. He also told me that he choked after vomiting and was rather scared. I told him that I would get his bed ready and come back down to get him after I'd gotten him a clean shirt to wear instead of the vomit covered one. (T:8:00pm) I could tell that his awareness of time was shot as when I went to get him after half an hour he looked up with dialated pupils and said 'Already?' as if it had been two minutes. Shortly after he was tucked in bed, the shakes and the sweating began. He was also gasping for breath and his words became jumbled as he told me he couldn't breathe. I lay next to him in bed and told him he was just freaking out and told him to take deep breaths and push against my hand with his stomach to slow him down and give him something to focus on. I had a drink bottle that I gave him water with about every five minutes. (T8:30pm) I went downstairs to check on D and E and both seemed very comfortable on the couch. As I came back upstairs, I noticed that J was in the toilet and carried on to get a cigarette. When I came back to his bedroom, he notified me that he couldn't get his zip undone, well at least that's what I thought he said, and that he had managed to piss himself. Helping him into bed, I pulled off his jeans and boxers and asked him of he wanted some clean ones to which he replied no and went back to 'sleep' with no pants on. (T8:50pm) I had the tv on next to J so I could keep an eye on his facials and breathing patterns. The first sign of any real tripping was when he sat up, looked at me and did some weird hand movements while making a song tune with kazoo noises. I made him lie down and gave him lots more water. This was the start of the battle of getting fluids into him as 'the water tasted yuck at the end of his mouthful' (this later came to be that he thought the drinkbottle was a massive bottle of candy squeezee and the end of it was watery). J then announced he needed to go to the toilet again so I put some boxers on him that were easy to get undone haha. I followed him as I was now positive he was tripping balls and running into his aunties in that state was not a good idea. I became confused as he walked past the toilet to the back door and unlocked it. He then went outside and yelled at me 'G! I'll show you what they've done to the grass'. I followed him and he began pissing on the grass and after that seemed normal. He then came back inside and walked into the kitchen where his aunty was standing. She looked at him and claimed he looked 'out of it' to which he replied 'I know, we were talking about it last night.' Just for the record, it was 'last night' still at the time. He then patted her on the shoulder, said thanks and returned to bed. (T9:20pm) This was when the really bad tripping began. After being 'asleep' for about 3 minutes, he turned to me and said 'So who's going? You are' and then laid back down and returned to his closed eye hallucinations. Occasionally he would sit up, shake his head and do a psychotic laugh that actually made me scared. He then stared calling me D and thought that everyone was lying in his bed which was now 15m wide. After repeatedly reassuring that I was G and we were in his bed and D and E were downstairs, he then lay back down and seemed to relax. He then cuddled me and gave me a kiss before closing his eyes again. (T9:45pm) J then decided to go to the toilet yet again and I followed him outside. He began pissing in the garden this time, and seemed rather amused by the whole process. He was also talking to some people that weren't there and I believe he imagined pissing on them. After talking to them for a while, I lead him back inside and told him he needed to get back into bed before he talked to his aunties again. I pushed him in the direction of his room but he decided he wanted to go out to the lounge and kept walking. I grabbed his arm but he then pushed me against the wall, giggled like a little girl and ran away like we were playing tiggy. He looked at me again, screamed then ran faster into the lounge. He then said 'Aunty C are you still using the internet?'. Of course, she wasn't there but his other aunty, L, replied by saying they had finished with it. She then asked where we were going and as J was rummaging through the cupboards, he replied 'town'. (T9:50pm) I finally got J back into bed and he seemed coherent til he lied down and started knocking on the surrounding objects like a chair and my head. Sometime during this time period he also thought I was E and got scared to wake up from a closed eye hallucination to find us cuddling. (T10:00pm) J notified me that he needed to use the 'shitter' so yet again I followed him but got paranoid when he went into the bathroom that he was going to piss in the shower. Instead he picked up my hair brush to which I said 'Didn't you need to go to the toilet babe?'. He looked at me like I was completely stupid and said 'No! I only came in to get the hairbrush'. I asked him if he wanted me to brush his hair and he said yes so we went back to bed and I brushed his hair. He picked up the blanket and proclaimed it was made out of snake skin. (T10:20pm) After 'sleeping' for 20 or so minutes I woke to see J scratching the bed and my clothing. I asked him what he was doing he then proclaimed that it was all made out of weed. He then waved his hand and asked me to turn the light on I then got up and flicked the switch several minutes afterwards he was sound asleep. I checked his breathing and followed suit. They then told me they hadn't slept and that D was 'watching the tv all night' (the unplugged Tv that was facing the oppisite direction to where they were) and E told me that she felt sick. They both got picked up and went home. Three days later I talked to D, he then told me that for two days after E was still tripping but currently is ok. In Summary It is something I will never try again (Thank god says G) but too this day I have a strong interest in what occurs. I believe that Datura provides an insight into the untapped energy that exists in the human brain and is something that one could possibly acheive by means unknown at this present time. I still have ALOT of respect for datura and still believe that we haven't quite yet found a way to use it correctly. But for the time being I plan to take mushrooms for spirtual insight.

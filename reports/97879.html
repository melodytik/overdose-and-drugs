这是我的第一份报告. 之前的经验:MDMA,MDA,LSD,25i-NBOMe,,Bk-MDMA,可卡因,大麻 我每隔几天抽一次大麻, 几周前, 一个知名的消息来源收到了一批新的6-APB. 他免费给我试用, 解释说这是一种新的MDMA替代品. 我很高兴能尝试这种新物质. 我第一个错误:没有进一步研究这种药物 (我通常总是这样做). 我不知道6-APB比普通的MDMA具有更强烈的精神药物特性, 背景信息:我有两个来自家乡的朋友 (M和G;男) 周末来我大学探访. 我们三个人和我的大学朋友 (A,女性) 决定乘坐6号公交车前往市中心的舞会. 我们都21岁,身体健康, 我以前使用过的MDMA类药物 (超过20次) 都是在音乐会或狂欢节上. 设置:我心情很好, 我们已经计划了几周的夜晚,所以我在精神上做好了准备. 晚上11点半,A和我在市中心的一家酒吧里每人摄入100毫克的6-APB粉末. 已经摄入了200毫克的美. 我们每天都在入6-APB粉末并口服, 我估计在晚上结束时我已经服用了250-300毫克. 晚上11:45开始感觉到轻微的刺痛感, 感觉很好,准备走到俱乐部. 的感觉越来越强烈, 但还没有紧像我通常在滚动时. 灯光看起来很棒, 音乐也很好听. 我们需要更多的6-APB. 午间12:30到凌晨2:00 - - 我正在翻脸 我感到高兴和缺乏抑制. 虽然我不喜欢与其他人一起跳舞, 我很难与人交往, 因为音乐听起来很棒 (这对我来说很正常). 我在此刻非常努力地着我的下巴, 实际上,我的身体感觉相对正常, 我对每一个人的同情心和同情心都很高. 音乐是我最喜欢的, 我的朋友是我最喜欢的, 一切都很棒. 值得注意的是, 我会感到极端的强度为10-15分钟一次, 然后大约10分钟的增量感觉像完整的清醒. 我会考虑再洗, 但几分钟后我会再次感到极度热, 开始跳舞太难记住. 凌晨2点,M和G想离开俱乐部. 我还没有准备好, 我们决定步行回家而不是坐出租车 (40个街区). 我首先注意到的是我感到自己失去了平衡. 我有一种很奇怪的感觉, 我的左眼比右眼大得多, 我觉得这非常奇怪,很难在直线行走, 我们走路聊天. 我们在俱乐部讨论了各自的经历, 我立刻意识到A,M和G都很温和, 这让我很惊, 因为A和我服用了相同的剂量, 她比我小. 我接受我比他们更糟糕, 并且继续享受我们的谈话. 我在这段时间里头脑非常清晰, 凌晨三点半,我们回家了. 我们决定去游泳, 吸烟后, 我立即意识到这可能不是最好的想法. 我突然觉得比我整个晚上更糟糕了. 虽然我仍然感觉到6-APB的身体不安. 我仍然非常努力地磨粉, 没有什么是太深刻的, 像在倒, 但一切看起来真的很奇怪比它通常是什么和颜色真的很强烈. 每个人都需要5HTP来缓解下降, 我们都准备睡觉. 我的瞳孔仍然非常扩大, 虽然我觉得自己已经睡不着了. 我开始体验毒品的极度迷幻方面. 我在壁纸上看到面孔, 房间里闪闪发光的虹灯, 粉红色的灯光从风扇中投射出来. 几乎就像一切都看起来像是通过玻璃屏幕移动. 我试着闭上眼睛, 看到移动的碎形和奇怪的面孔. 我从来没有注意到过这样的视觉效果, 尽管房间里没有声音, 我试着享受它, 但在这一点上我只想睡觉. 虽然我脑子里还很清醒, 但这些视觉效果让我不小心. 尤其令人不舒服, 因为我仍然感到不安, 随着这些奇怪的浪潮不断涌起, 我不知道自己该怎么办. 我洗,看电视,然后躺在沙发上. 没有什么能让人平静或舒适, 我眼睛的瞳孔终于不那么扩张了, 我只是躺在床上就更舒服了. 终于在上午九点左右, 上午12点 - - 醒来,感觉有点恶心, 结论: 我不知道是草造成了效果的变化, 我不认为我比其他人多服用大量的6-APB. 我认为我会更享受这种药物如果我知道有明显的迷幻特质与它相关. 我也希望我能在俱乐部度过更多的时间, 我朋友没有经历过这样的经历, 尽管如此, 我没有像往常那样感到"星期二忧郁", 我真的很害怕这只发生在我身上, 一个重要提醒:总是研究你的药物, 已使用的证券 6-APB (516):艰难的经历 (5),第一次 (2),俱乐部/酒吧 (25)

Fun but Confusing 6-APB by whoknows Citation:   whoknows. "Fun but Confusing: An Experience with 6-APB (exp97879)". Erowid.org . Oct 23, 2012. erowid.org/exp/97879 DOSE: T+ 0:00 100 mg oral 6-APB (capsule) T+ 0:30 oral 6-APB (powder / crystals) T+ 1:00 repeated oral 6-APB (powder / crystals) T+ 4:00 smoked Cannabis T+ 4:30 oral Tryptophan (capsule) BODY WEIGHT: 125 lb This is my first report. I feel like others can benefit from hearing my experience, as there is not much information yet regarding 6-APB. Previous experiences: MDMA, MDA, LSD, 25i-NBOMe, mushrooms, Bk-MDMA, cocaine, Cannabis. I smoke weed every few days; I roll and/or trip about once per month. A few weeks ago, a reputable source received a new batch of 6-APB. He gave me some for free to try out, explaining it was a new strain of MDMA replacement. MDMA is my drug of choice, so I was excited to try this new substance. My first mistake: not researching this drug any further (something I usually always do). I had no idea that 6-APB has much more psychedelic qualities than regular MDMA, and this came back to haunt me later. Background info: I had two friends from my hometown (M and G; male) visiting me at college for the weekend. The three of us, and my college friend (A; female), decided to use the 6-APB and go to a dance club downtown. We are all 21, in good health, and have previous experiences with psychoactive drugs. All of my previous experiences with MDMA type drugs (20+ times) have been at concerts or raves. Set: I was in a very good mood, although a little tired from a late night the previous night. We had planned for a few weeks to roll that night, so I was mentally prepared. 11:30pm- A and I each ingest 100mg of the 6-APB powder in capsules at a bar downtown. M & G had already ingested 200mg of Mephedrone. Throughout the night from this point all of us repeatedly dipped into the 6-APB powder and ingested orally, although I do not have time stamps for these. I estimate that I took 250-300mg by the end of the night. 11:45pm- starting to feel slight tingling sensation, much more alert. 12:00am- feeling very talkative and good, ready to walk to the club. Tingling sensation is growing stronger, but no jaw clenching yet like I usually get when rolling. When we get to the dance club (electronic music only – built like a rave), the lights look amazing, and the music feels great. We take more of the 6-APB. 12:30-2:00am- I am rolling face. I experience euphoria and lack of inhibitions. Dancing feels amazing, although I don’t like dancing with other people. I have a hard time socializing because the music sounds so amazing (this is normal for me). I am grinding my jaw extremely hard at this point, but I notice the lack of body high that I typically experience when rolling. In fact, my body feels relatively normal and I am quite aware of it. I feel elevated levels of compassion and empathy towards everyone. The DJ is my favorite, the music is my favorite, my friends are my favorite, and everything is amazing. It is important to note that the effects hit me in very distinct waves. I would be feeling extreme intensity for 10-15 minutes at a time, then about 10 minute increments of what felt like complete sobriety. I would consider taking another dip, but within minutes I would begin feeling extremely hot again and start dancing too hard to remember. 2:00am- M and G want to leave the club. I am not ready yet but we decide to go anyway. We decide to walk home instead of taking a cab (40 blocks). When we step outside away from the lights and music, the first thing I notice is how off-balance I feel. I have a very strange sensation that my left eye is much bigger than my right eye, and the entire world seems to be leaning and expanding toward the left. I found this extremely odd and it was difficult to walk in a straight line, although I didn’t worry too much about it. We spend the walk talking. We discuss our separate experiences within the club, and it is instantly clear to me that A, M, and G all had very mild rolls, with some feelings of energy and euphoria but not very pronounced. This is surprising to me since A and I took the same amount, and she is smaller than me. I accept that I am way, way more fucked up than them and continue enjoying our conversations. I am very clear-headed throughout this time and not thinking about much other than being happy and walking straight. 3:30am- we arrive home. We decide to go for a swim and smoke a bowl. Immediately after smoking, I realize it probably was not the best idea. I suddenly feel more fucked up than I have all night. I get extremely introverted, although still feel the physical restlessness of the 6-APB. I am still grinding really hard, and having strange visuals. Nothing is too profound at this point like when tripping, but everything looks really weird than how it normally does and colors are really intense. 4:00am- Everyone takes some 5-HTP to ease the comedown, and we all get ready for bed. My pupils are EXTREMELY dilated still- there is practically no iris. All three of my friends fall asleep instantly, yet I realize I am far away from sleep. 4:30-8:00am- While laying in my pitch-dark and room, I start experiencing extremely psychedelic aspects of the drug. Open eye visuals of fractals, faces in my tapestry, neon colors flying around the room, pink lights being projected off of the fan. It very strongly resembles LSD visuals but with a much more electric look- almost like everything is looking like and moving through a glass screen. I try to close my eyes, and see moving fractals and weird faces. I have not noticed any visuals like this until now, and am taken extremely off guard. It sounds like dubstep is blasting in my head, even though the room is silent. I try to enjoy it, but at this point I just want to go to sleep. I do not feel mentally like I am tripping, as I am still very clear-headed, but the visuals take me off guard. It is especially uncomfortable since I still feel so restless, I am unable to lay there and relax to take it all in. I become more and more confused and upset as these strange waves continue. I do not know what to do with myself. I take a shower, I watch TV, I move to the couch. Nothing is calming or comfortable, and it doesn’t seem to be getting any tamer. 8:00-9:00am- My pupils are finally a little less dilated and I am not grinding my teeth as badly. I become more comfortable simply laying in bed. Eventually around 9am I doze into an interrupted and restless sleep. 12:00am- wake up, feel slightly nauseas but otherwise ok- just tired. Conclusions: I do not know if it was the weed that caused the change in effects, or the change in set/setting. I do not think I took a substantial amount more of the 6-APB than anyone else. I think I would have enjoyed this drug much more if I knew that there were distinct psychedelic qualities associated with it. I also wish that I spent more time at the club, in order to exert more of my energy. The fact that none of my friends had an experience like this also made the comedown extremely difficult. However, I felt no “Tuesday blues” like I normally do, and other than the exhaustion I felt fine by the next night. Even the trippy part was not bad, I was just really freaked out that it was only happening to me, and for so long. A big reminder to myself: always research your drugs, and remember that they affect everyone differently.

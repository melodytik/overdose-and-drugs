Seems a Bit Lackluster DALT by ThePoobaman Citation:   ThePoobaman. "Seems a Bit Lackluster: An Experience with DALT (exp109584)". Erowid.org . Jan 11, 2017. erowid.org/exp/109584 DOSE: DALT (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 165 lb A Few Trials With DALT 12 mg, smoked Produces pleasant, almost overwhelming body high. Lasts approximately 15 minutes, with an additional 45 minute afterglow. Visuals are very light, about the level of MDMA (tracers, very slight morphing, etc). Onset is sudden. Flavor of smoke is unpleasant. 25 mg, intrarectal I took it at a concert. It took about 10 minutes to come up. My girlfriend, who took the same dose, had a very bad time (nausea, disorientation, anxiety and sensitivity to light and sound) and we had to hang out outside the venue for a while. Our friend, who invited us, almost left because he thought we were tripping too hard. Eventually we made it back to the concert and had a great time dancing. 18 mg, smoked Entered a trance-like state for about 5 minutes. I was lost in a state of pure, rich imagination and fantasy. Very few visuals. Time dilation was remarkable. 3-5 mg, insufflated I started with a tiny line, and had about 20 mg layed out ready to go up the nose. The first line burned too much to continue. It felt like somebody had shoved a needle up my nostril. I will not repeat this. RETROSPECT: Shulgin says in TiHKAL that for a dose of 42 mg of DALT, orally, “there was nothing that caught my fancy.” Indeed, the drug seems a bit lackluster. It is extremely obscure, and when it is sold, it’s often sold cheaply, as though those selling it are just trying to get it off their hands? But to me, it’s a very interesting material. I’ve never experienced anything like it. It’s like all the tryptamine body buzz and none of the visuals, with a totally unique headspace. I’d be fascinated to see the receptor affinity and to see it studied in detail, as well as to read other people’s experiences.

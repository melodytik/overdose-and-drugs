经验 - - 更多的物质比我愿意提到的, 但没有很多胺, 我没有服用任何药物. 我在家,我很开心,我喝了几杯酒. 让我们尝试一下... 信心 - - 我很确定这不仅仅是为了更多钱而在另一包中放入的5-MeO-DALT, 对于这一点,aMT和5-MeO-DALT的马奎斯结果是不可分辨的,其他测试也没有. 在T减去2周和48小时后, ~1mg的过敏测试. 没有反应. T+0.0 一点点醉了. 不是很多,但有些. 试试一些4-AcO-DALT. 我可以查找哪些参考, 决定40毫克是合适的. 测量出20毫克和电子烟. 时间+0.5分钟 测量并蒸发另一个20mg. 这是一个... 时间+45分钟 - - 外面的雨听起来很好. 目前为止与5-MeO-DALT没有显著差异, 虫子不能抓住我,因为它们在窗上. 时间+55分钟 快乐 闪亮的 伸出我的舌头是非常有趣的. 我没有做过这么长时间. 世界是美丽的,即使是我周围的小区域被床边灯照亮. 嘴里没有理由有味. 这是一个令人高兴的时刻. 时间+65分钟 - 这就像5-MeO-DALT,它比眼睛更多地击中大脑,但它看起来很漂亮...... (侧记,轻微的恶心可能是由于早些时候的酒精消费) 时间+75分钟 - - 着窗外. 在夜空. 在另一边很酷. T+90分钟 - - CEV 实际上没有明显的OEV. 比5-MeO-DALT更令人高兴. 化和变形,但当眼睛打开时会消失. 时间+100分钟 - - 情依然存在. 观看"酸性曲线" (老式的非反应性可视化类型节目) 和听电工业音乐. 需要更多的消化不良药. 这很好,但现在肯定要回到基地. 玩电脑游戏,然后昏倒. 脚注 - 我不确定在0到45分钟之间发生了什么. 随着使用, 效果几乎立即出现. 如果我记得不错, 它们会在15分钟内积聚, 第二天没有任何不良影响. 总结 - 有趣的小胺,轻微的迷幻头部空间, 快速上升,在几个小时内返回基地. OEV仅限于轻微的融化/扭曲/发光 IIRC. 恶心是最小的. (*这些虫是我窗上的纹理) 已使用的证券 4-AcO-DALT (444):一般的 (1),单独的 (16)

Oct 25, 2015. erowid.org/exp/101690 DOSE: T+ 0:00 repeated oral Alcohol T+ 0:00 20 mg vaporized 5-MeO-DALT T+ 0:05 20 mg vaporized 5-MeO-DALT BODY WEIGHT: 200 lb Experience - more substances than I care to mention but not many tryptamines, only 5-MeO-DALT and whatever's in mushrooms before this. I am not on any meds. Set and setting - I'm home, I'm happy, I've had a couple of beers. Let's give this a try... Confidence - I'm pretty sure this wasn't just 5-MeO-DALT in a different packet for more money, but you never know these days. Marquis results for this, aMT and 5-MeO-DALT were indistinguishable and other tests were not available. T minus 2 weeks and 48 hours, ~1mg allergy test. No reaction. T+0.0 A little drunk. Not a lot but some. Decide as 5-MeO-DALT was benign let's try some 4-AcO-DALT. Look up what references I can and decide ~40mg is appropriate. Measure out 20mg and vape. T+0.5 minutes Measure and vape another 20mg. ... T+45 minutes - The rain outside sounds wonderful. Not significantly different from 5-MeO-DALT so far but some visual distortions. The worms can't get me because they're on the curtains*. T+55 minutes Happy. Shiny. Sticking my tongue out is much fun. I haven't done that in ages. The world is beautiful, even just the small area around me lit by the bedside light. Mouth tastes like salvia for no reason. Euphoria. T+65 minutes - This is like 5-MeO-DALT in that it hits the brain more than the eyes but it does look pretty out of the window..... (sidenote, mild nausea due probably to earlier alcohol consumption) T+75 minutes - stared out of window. Night sky. It's cool on the other side. T+90 minutes - CEV. OEV not really apparent. So much more euphoric than 5-MeO-DALT. CEV melting and morphing but goes away when eyes are opened T+100 minutes - euphoria still present. Watching 'acid warp' (old-skool non-reactive visualisation type program) and listening to electro-industrial music. Need more indigestion tablets. It's good but definitely coming back to base now. Going to play computer games and then pass out. Footnote - I'm not sure exactly what happened between 0 and 45 minutes. As it was vaped, effects came on almost immediately. If I recall correctly they built up for about 15 minutes before hitting the main plateau, which lasted somewhere over an hour. No bad effects noticed the next day. Summary - fun little tryptamine, mild psychedelic headspace, minor visuals but not overpowering for me at this dose. Up fast and back to base in a couple of hours when vaped. OEV restricted to minor melting/warping/glowing IIRC. Nausea was minimal.

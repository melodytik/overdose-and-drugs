(Since I was young I have always had an interest in reseaching the effects and characteristics of different drugs.) So one night after having it in my room for about a week I desided to try it. I was alone and since I had nothing to do and it was late I figured I would give it a try. First I put the peices of mandrake root into a tea bag with some lobilia which is another herb, and some sugar. After heating it up I drank the whole glass then quickly made another one with the same amount of substance. 0.00: Nothing yet just have a weird taste in my mouth 0.15: I feel slightly different but nothing that is really noticeable. 0:30: Went to get something to eat from the local store. When I went outside I noticed that the street lights and sky seemed a little (very little) different. 0.45: After eating I watched tv. Felt very little. Almost nothing After an hour of taking the first two doses I desided to try just putting the mandrake root in a glass with no tea bag or sugar or lobelia. I mix a very potent glass of hot water and mandrake and let it brew. Second dose: 0.00: Feel hot from the drink 0:15: Starting to feel mild euphoria so I lay down. 0:30: I am watching my light in my room which seems to be getting brighter and darker as I look at it. Its sort of going in repetitions. 0:35: I put on some Doors and listen to the music, I start to feel energetic and euphoric. A couple of minutes later I stand up and start moving to the music. 0.45: I am dancing to the music and I am having a great time. It is 2.00 o-clock in the morning yet I have lots of energy. I do all the movements and lyrics and really feel like I am singing in a concert. My eyes have been closed for more then 20 min now and I can picture the theater. 1:30 I continue to sing until my friends come home and knock on my door, opening my eyes and bringing me back to my room. I watch this for about two min and go to bed. The next morning I woke up with a bad taste in my mouth. I look at the clock and see it 1.00 in the afternoon. 30-50 grams Mandrake put me in a delusional state of happiness and illusions, and then put me in a deep deep sleep. Overall it was good and I would definatly try it again.

May 11, 2019. erowid.org/exp/100053 DOSE: vaporized Cannabis - High THC BODY WEIGHT: 190 lb Northern Lights and Light and Sound Machine I've not smoked any weed for well over a year, but recently I've been hooked up with some really potent northern lights. Due to lack of tolerance, it's pretty much been blowing me away after a few tokes (vaporizer). After getting moderately stoned earlier, I decided to use my laxman on a relaxation setting (music rather than binaural beats but with the strobing lights) The results were unexpected. Right from the beginning, the usually hazy geometric patterns I experience when using the machine sober were replaced with complex 'eye' structures the usually hazy geometric patterns I experience when using the machine sober were replaced with complex 'eye' structures . Thousands of eyes building up high curved columns, completing a huge complicated structure. I was flying through this structure as it rotated around me, sometime at insane speeds. Interspersing this structure were very realistic scenes which mostly matched the music. At one point there was a rain sound and the repeating visuals gave way to what looked like puddles reflecting a busy scene in an Indian city. Other visuals were more abstract, sort of like geometric frog men with rotating crystal eyes (I burst out laughing at this point as the eyes were sort of flickering and rotating too fast - makes no sense why I'd laugh at this now that I'm baseline (!)), or strobing scenes where people moved at angles but in a juddering fashion. I chickened out after about 20 minutes as my body felt like it turned to liquid, joined with the bed and I felt like I was moving downwards at a increasingly rapid pace. When I opened my eyes, my high felt like it was gone - but I think this was more due to the relative intensity between the closed eye experience and the opened eye high. It was crazy how the laxman pretty much made me feel like I was tripping. I'd like to try this whilst tripping, but am a little apprehensive about the results if it can intensify a moderate weed high to such levels.

那是圣诞节假期,我的地区有暴风雪. 许多主要的道路都关闭了,如果你想去某个地方, 我已经没有了大麻,但由于暴风雪,我找不到更多. 我开始无聊, 在家里寻找有趣的非处方药. 我正准备服用DXM, 发现药柜里藏着一个药瓶. 这是一种胺 (我忘记了确切的名字, 它们是我的减肥药. 我在互联网上搜索了关于这种药物的信息, 我吃了5粒药,我的记忆有点模糊,但我90%肯定是50毫克的药,下午2点左右. 我在看电影的开始时吃了药. 我没有注意到任何直接的影响,我想因为我坐在我的股. 但当电影结束后, 我去看镜子, 我的瞳孔是巨大的. 我没有感到过度精力充, 我也觉得自己很口, 晚上7点半左右, 我意识到为什么有时使用速度来治疗ADD. 我想试着把它变成一个立方体 (同一个图使用的立方体). 我在立方体上工作了2个小时, 让它变得完美. 在直线上稍微曲是不可接受的, 我整晚没有睡觉. 如果我强迫自己睡觉,我可以睡觉,但没有睡眠的冲动. 诚实地说,我大部分时间都在电脑上看色情片. 我感到非常偏执, 可能是心理反应. 我看到了类似于另一个经历的视觉变化. 影子似乎改变了方向. 第二天也是一样. 这次我吃了七粒药. 我没有感觉到与5的差异太大. 我再次花了一晚看色情片. 但是,我确实睡了15分钟. 我坐在一个昏暗的房间里, 但我立刻回来了. 在体验和影响方面,第三天与第一天完全相同. 第四天,我没有服用任何药物. 我在晚上9点左右睡觉. 我从来没有睡过那么深的睡眠. 我从来没有穿上床,也从来没有脱衣服. 我只是睡在床上. 但令人惊的是,我从醒来后一分钟都没有动. 我通常在睡觉中和滚动. 我通常在早上起床时床上一团糟. 但我昏迷了,就像睡着了. 我睡着了,手里拿着遥控器,醒来时手里还有遥控器. 我一点也不动,你甚至不能说有人睡在我的床上. 时间表:5104

Many major roads were closed and if you wanted to go somewhere you had to walk in really deep snow. I had ran out of weed, but I couldn't get any more because of the blizzard. I became bored and looked around my house for any interesting OTC/Prescription drugs. I was about to take some DXM when I noticed a hidden pill bottle in the medicine cabinet. It was -----phetamine (I forget the exact name, but the market name was Didrex). They were some old weight-loss pills my mom had. I searched up info on the drug on the internet, but much help because it's not a common drug, but I did learn that 4mg of Didrex was equal to about 1mg of 'street' speed. I took 5 pills, my memory is a little shady but I'm 90% shure they were 50mg pills, around 2:00pm. I took the pills at the beginning of watching a movie. I didn't notice any immediate effects, I guess 'cause I was sitting on my ass. But when the movie was over and I went to look in the mirror, my pupils were huge. I didn't feel overly energized, but I wasn't tired. I felt very talkative too, bringing up old memories and stuff. At about 7:30pm I realized why speed is sometimes used for ADD. I had a paper clip that I was playing with and I wanted to try to form it into a cube (the same cube that Silicon Graphics uses). I worked on the cube for 2 hours, making it perfect. A slight bend in a straight line was unacceptable, it needed to be perfect. I stayed awake all night. I could have gone to sleep if I forced myself, but there wasn't an urge for sleep. To be honest, I spent most of the night jerking off to porn on my computer. At about 2:00am I felt very paranoid, possibly a psychosomatic response. At about 4:00 I saw visual change very similar to those in another experiance listed here. Shadows seemed to change direction. Day 2 was very much the same. I took 7 pills this time. I didn't feel much of a differance from the 5. I once again spent the night looking at porn. But, I did take a 15 minute nap. I was sitting in my unlit room and drifted off. But I came right back with energy. The 3rd day was identical to the 1st day as far as experiances and effects. On the 4th day, I didn't take any pills. I went to bed at around 9:00pm. It was the deepest sleep I've ever had. I never got into my comforter, and I never got undressed. I just fell asleep on top of the bed. But the amazing thing was that I didn't move an inch from the time I woke up. I normally kick and roll around in my sleep. My bed is normally a mess when I get up in the morning. But I had a coma like sleep. I fell asleep with the remote in my hand, it was still in my hand when I woke up. I had not moved at all, you couldn't even tell that somebody had slept in my bed.

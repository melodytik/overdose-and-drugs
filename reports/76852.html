于是我来到了阿姆斯特丹, 我在前一天晚上抽了两支烟, 完全没有睡觉, 我也患有强迫症,所以我服用佐洛夫特和克洛扎平. 在我国, 我在服用这些药物时一直在喝伏特加, 仿佛我在一分钟内经历了其他人在两年内所经历的. 但回顾一下我的经验. 我到达阿姆斯特丹已经很糟糕了, 当我的朋友遇见我, 他们告诉我这是一份礼物. 然后他们在我之前进入酒店. 我自己抽了,没想到会发生什么. 然后我爬上楼梯到418号房间. 然后开始了可怕的感觉. 我心跳很快, 身体也很虚弱. 我甚至在几秒钟内失去了意识, 走廊似乎无穷无尽, 我跑上来跑下去试图找到房间, 我终于找到了418号, 冲进房间, 喊叫朋友叫救护车. 他们开始笑, 告诉我救护车在阿姆斯特丹需要300欧元, 我知道我不应该在过去24小时内吸了这么多烟, 另外,抗精神病药本身是相当危险的, 它们会导致心脏和呼吸停止, 我意识到我的朋友们不会打电话给救护车, 虽然我的眼睛滚滚,我几乎站不起来,我去所把头放在冷水里. 然后我开始失去面部感觉. 我开始自己,再次感觉到它, 我向我的朋友们大喊大叫, 后来他们意识到这很严重, 其中一个打了我几次. 这是一场悲剧喜剧,我喊道:打我,M,更厉害,我感觉不到! 邻居们已经在墙上敲打, 我很快就起身了, 因为我害怕会失去意识. 我在走廊里跌倒了, 没有动力. 我知道有些事非常不对, 我也感觉不到自己的脚, 两小时后,我终于开始有所好转. 他们一直告诉我这只是一个糟糕的旅程, 我试着尽可能保持清醒. 离开阿姆斯特丹两天后, 我仍然很震惊, 我继续服用佐洛夫特,克洛扎平,红牛或咖啡, 两周后, 虽然我发誓不再抽烟, 我再次遭受了可怕的恐慌, 现在我回到了我的国家, 我有一种奇怪的感觉,我无法呼吸,每个人都说这是我脑海里的, 我在晚上醒来,呼吸困难. 我总是感到恐慌, 头部和眼睛都很紧. 我甚至回忆起发生的事情, 妄想,呼吸困难和打电话给另一个朋友, 我真的想做脑部CT, 因为从互联网上读到的我可能有过渡性缺血性发作, 每个人都认为这一切都在我的脑海里. 我甚至更换了药片, 我现在正在服用黄胺而不是黄, 现在我不再喝酒,不再喝咖啡,甚至可口可乐和巧克力也让我的心跳快. 我甚至不得不远离茶. 所有医生都说我现在都好了, 但我还是想看看我的大脑是否还好. 我知道我会努力坚持我的治疗, 这让我很高兴, 其他: 大麻 - 大麻 (93), 克洛沙平 (562), 药品 - 塞特拉林 (88):各种 (28), 旅行后的问题 (8), 糟糕的旅行 (6), 组合 (3)

Jan 27, 2012. erowid.org/exp/76852 DOSE: oral Pharms - Sertraline (daily) oral Clozapine (daily) 300 ml oral Alcohol - Hard (daily) oral Caffeine (liquid) 1 cig. smoked Cannabis - Hash BODY WEIGHT: 140 lb So I was in Amsterdam and decided to make the most of it. I had smoked twice the night before and I hadnt slept at all, I drank several shots of vodka with red bull. I also have OCD so I was on zoloft and clozapine. In my country I have been drinking vodka while on these medicines for the past 4 years and I felt fucking great. It was like I was living in 1 minute what others were living in 2 years. But back to my experience. I arrived in Amsterdam pretty fucked up already, when my friends met me and and gave me one joint of hash. They told me it was a present for me. Then they went into the hotel before me. I smoked it by myself and didnt dream of what would happen. Then I climbed the stairs to get to room 418. Then the horrible feelings started. My heart was racing and I felt a terrible weakness in my body. I think I even lost consciousness for a few seconds, but I pushed myself to find the room. The hallway seemed endless and I ran up and down on it trying to find the room, but the numbers were going down in my head instead of going up. When I finally found fucking 418 I crashed into the room, yelling at my friends to call the ambulance. They started laughing and told me the ambulance was 300 euros to get in Amsterdam and told me to go to sleep :O then I really panicked. I knew that I shouldn’t have smoked so much – 3 times in the last 24 hours and the last joint of hash I had smoked by myself. Plus, the antipsychotics alone are quite dangerous, they can cause cardiac and respiratory arrest, not to mention mixing them with vodka, red bull and a lot of weed. I realized my friends were not going to get the ambulance, as they thought I was just having a bad trip so I tried to keep myself ok. Although my eyes were rolling and I could hardly stand up, I went to the bathroom to put my head under cold water. Then I started to lose feeling of my face. I started to slap myself, to feel it again and I screamed at my friends to slap me too. They laughed and didn’t want to at the beginning, but then they realized it was serious and one of them slapped me several times. It was quite a tragedy-comedy as I was yelling: slap me, M, harder, I cant feel it! The neighbours were already banging on the walls as we were making quite a hub-bub. Then for like 15 minutes I didn’t understand anything they were talking about, I kept falling on the bed but I soon got up, as I was afraid I would lose consciousness. M tried to get me outside, but I fell in the hallway, not having the power to move. I knew something was very wrong and I was afraid I was going to die. I couldn’t feel my feet either so I started slapping them. Finally, after 2 hours I started to feel better. What was the scariest for me is that they kept telling me it was just a bad trip and that I should go to sleep. But that wasn’t sleep that was pulling me in bed, so I tried to keep myself awake as much as I could. After 2 days I left Amsterdam I was still pretty shaken but I was ok. I continued with the Zoloft and Clozapine and Red Bull or espresso that I had been drinking for the past 4 months daily because of my job. Then after 2 weeks, although I had swore I wasn’t gonna smoke again I bumped into some people and I smoked. I had another horrible panic attack, but this time it was apparently mostly psychological. Now I’m back in my country and am not feeling well at all. I have these weird feelings that I can't breathe, everyone says its in my head but I think because of the big amount of weed that I took my body doesn’t synthesize oxygen properly. I wake up at night and have trouble breathing. I am in a constant state of panic and have pressure on my head and eyes. And I even had flashbacks of what happened, with paranoia, trouble breathing and calling another friend of mine, like I did in Amsterdam. I really want to get a CT of the head, because from what I read on the internet I might have had a transient ischemic attack, which is a minor stroke and might announce a stroke in the future. Everyone thinks its all in my head. I even changed my pills I am taking fluvoxamine now instead of Zoloft because I was too agitated and I'm not taking clozapine anymore because I'm afraid I might die in my sleep. Now I'm not drinking alcohol anymore, no more coffee and even Coca-Cola and chocolate make my heart speed. I even have to stay away from tea. All doctors say I'm fine now but I still wanna check if my brain is ok. Keep it safe guys I know I'm gonna try to stick to my treatment only, which was making me happy enough, but I just had to get as high as I could and now it really fucked me up.

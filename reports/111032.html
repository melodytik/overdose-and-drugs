之前的经验: 30 年前开始的 100 多次迷幻体验, 检测:马奎斯试剂显示黄色光色. 含量总重量 (不包括囊) 为140mg. 内容颜色强烈绿色. 耐受性: 4 天前试过6 mg. 背景:白天独自在家里. 设置:有点脆弱,想要物质帮助一些情绪释放 (它做到了). 我用了大约45分钟才能在空腹上感觉到. 爬了另一个小时或两个. 停滞了4至5个小时. 慢慢下来几个小时. 在10-12小时后的基线. 身体上的影响:轻微和张. 很少出现水病. 感觉血压可能有点上升. 有些增加了汗水. 一开始食欲不佳, 但5小时后食物变得美味. 经过6小时的轻度腹. 没有恶心. 很少有刺激. 与其他2C相比, 心理效果:在视觉上, 只是一些温暖的颜色, 也许更多的3D感觉到空间和形状. 如果着某个东西,可能会有轻微的动作. 音乐的欣赏得到了强化, 但不像其他化学物质那样. 有某种醉酒的感觉, 难以描述. 精神散乱,容易分心, 虽然它比其他精神散乱药物清晰, 触觉刺激以类似于MDMA的方式增强, 感觉温暖和心脏开放与明确的心理治疗潜力没有深度或潜在的压倒性的许多其他物质. 有一些情感释放, 感觉非常好. 绝对会再次使用. 其他: 2C-T-21 (290):一般 (1),单独使用 (16)

Sep 26, 2017. erowid.org/exp/111032 DOSE: 10 mg oral 2C-T-21 (capsule) BODY WEIGHT: 70 kg Previous experience: +100 psychedelic experiences starting 30 years ago with +10 different compounds (tryptamines, ergolines, phenetylamines & MDMA). Testing: Marquis reagent showed yellow-light orange. Dosage: 10 mg, came capsuled from very reputable vendor. Total weight of content – excluding capsule – was 140 mg. Content colored strongly green. Tolerance: Tried 6 mg 4 days ago. Setting: At home alone, daytime. Set: A bit vulnerable, wanted the substance to help with some emotional release (which it did). Took about 45 min to feel on an empty stomach. Climbed for another hour or two. Plateaued for 4-5 hours. Slow come down for a couple of hours. Baseline after 10-12 h. Physical effects: Slight chills & jaw tension. Very little mydriasis. Felt like BP might have increased a bit. Some increased perspiration. Initially suppressed appetite, but food delicious at about 5 hours. After 6 h light diarrhea. No nausea. Very little stimulation. Felt physically benign compared to some other 2C’s. Mental effects: Visually just some warmth to colors and maybe a bit more 3D feel to space and shapes. Maybe some slight movement if staring at something. Music appreciation amplified – but not to the same extent as with some other chemicals. Had some sort of drunken feeling to it, hard to describe. A bit absent minded and easily distracted – in a different way than many other psychedelics which have more clarity – though also makes me easily distracted. Tactile stimulation increased in an MDMA-like way – great sex-enhancer also at lower doses like 5-7 mg. Felt warm and heart opening with definite psychotherapeutic potential – without being as deep or potentially overwhelming as many other substances. Had some emotional release that felt very good. Will definitely take again.

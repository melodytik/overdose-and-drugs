我家人让我一个人呆在家里几个小时. 我在药柜里拿了一些我的威尔布特林. 她是因为抑郁症而被处方的. 我打开瓶子,拿出两个,然后把它们放在我的口袋里. 我认为这个特定的品牌是Zyban, 剂量相当高, 每个450毫克. 但因为我是个瓜, 我没有读到瓶子上说他们时间释放. 我吃了一杯,等待两杯, 我没有感觉到任何东西,所以我继续和另一个. 我没有得到太多的效果. 我在头脑里听到这样的声音, 就像电视没有频道, 现在它很微弱, 但当它变得安静我真的能听到它. 这是我最讨厌的一种副作用. 终于我回家躺在床上, 听着我脑海中的奇怪声音. 我睡着了. (我做了一个强烈的梦,我不知道这是因为wellbutrin,但我相信它没有帮助) 我突然在凌晨4点醒来, 紧张的能量, 声音在我的脑海里真的很大. 我口中非常干燥, 味道一般不好. 我觉得我真的很脱水,所以我走进厨房拿水. 我只能想到那个非常不愉快的味道. 我回去躺在床上一会儿, 身体很疲, 这就是事情开始变得奇怪的时候. 我听着音乐, 只是着墙壁. 我觉得很恶心,我担心我可能会在床上吐. 我在浴室里站了一会儿. 只是想到食物让我发. 我不能刷牙,因为牙膏的味道让我吐. 现在我只能听到我脑海中的噪音. 我坐在浴室的地板上. 我心里有一种悲伤的感觉. 偏执开始在我身上,我跳在最小的事情. 我很容易被吓到, 这对我来说不是正常的. 我狗从浴室门的另一边吠叫, 声音听起来很奇怪, 我一直在检查是否有人在门口. 我到处都看到小斑点. 影子会自行移动, 这并没有帮助偏执. 影子会变得非常可怕. 我一直看到有人从淋浴窗里着他们的头. 威尔布特林不是很有趣的. 我基本上只是给了我一个暂时的偏执精神分裂症. 时间表: 109517

Apr 29, 2018. erowid.org/exp/109517 DOSE: 900 mg oral Pharms - Bupropion (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 150 lb My family leaves me home alone for a couple of hours. I went through the medicine cabinet and took some of my moms wellbutrin. She is prescribed it for her depression. I open the bottle, and take two, then putting them in my pocket. I think this specific brand was Zyban, they had a football shape with some black numbers in the middle. The dose was pretty high with 450mg each. But because I am an idiot, I failed to read on the bottle that they are time released. I take one, and wait a couple for it to kick in. I wasnt feeling anything so I went ahead and took the other one. I didnt get much effect at this time. When I take wellbutrin I get this sound in my head, its almost like a tv when I dont have channel on it, or when I get hit on the head and the moment right after it. Its pretty faint at this point, but when it gets quiet I can really hear it. This is one side effect that I really hate. Eventually I just go home and lay in my bed, listening to the weird sound in my head. I fall asleep. (I had a intense dream, I don't know if it was because of the wellbutrin, but I'm sure it didn't help) All of a sudden I wake up at 4am with a instant nervous energy, the sound in my head was really loud. My mouth was really dry and had a generally bad taste. I felt really dehydrated, so I walk into my kitchen to get some water. No amount of water was helping my dry mouth, and the really unpleasant taste was all I could think about. I go back and lay in my bed for awhile, my body felt exhausted but there was no way I was going to be able to go back to sleep. This is when things start to get weird. I'm listening to some music and literally just staring at the walls. I felt really nauseous, I was afraid I might throw up in my bed. I stand in the bathroom for awhile. Just thinking about food was making me gag. I couldnt brush my teeth because the toothpaste smell was making me gag. The noise in my head was all I could hear at this point. I sit on my bathroom floor. I got this sad feeling in the pit of my stomach. Paranoia started creeping up on me, I was jumping at the smallest things. I was so easily frightened, which is not normal for me. My dog barked from the other side of the bathroom door, it sounded strangely distorted, but the sudden bark scared me so badly that I physically screamed. I was hearing knocking, and footsteps, the sounds were so vivid that I kept checking if someone was at the door. I was seeing little specks everywhere. The shadows would move around on their own, this didn't help the paranoia. The shadows would move very creepily. I kept seeing someone peek their head out from the shower curtain. Wellbutrin isn’t very fun. I basically just gave me temporary paranoid schizophrenia.

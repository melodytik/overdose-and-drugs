So I looked around for poppies for a while but I only found the wrong ones. Now an easy way to tell if you have the real poppy is for one thing, their foliage that grows at the base resembles more of lettuce looking than the other poppies. Second, the pods are usually much larger than the others, although some of the other kinds may have larger pods, but rarely. Third, the somniferum stems grow much more taller than others. It takes some time to really know how to differentiate between them but practice looking as much as you can for them. I just found i was tired of looking, at searched online for a supplier of dried pods. Success! I have and did find a source that was reliable. But warning, you should not heat it too hot. To be honest, i kept the heat at a low setting most of the time. I kept a very very close eye on a thermometer that i stuck in the pot too. The ones for cooking. I made sure that at all time i kept the temperature lower than 150 F otherwise I knew that going higher could possible destroy the chemical structure of codeine and morphine -- the two chemicals in opium that I want most of all.

我一直在使用Skelaxin以缓解慢性腿部和肌肉疼痛. 我以前使用的Skelaxin通常是无事件的,没有治疗效果,即使与少量酒精一起使用 (这似乎完全放松了疼痛的肌肉收缩). 我决定服用四片800毫克的药片. 之前的剂量是两到三种药, 经过几个小时的扭动,我终于睡着了. 斯凯拉克辛似乎只能在那天晚上醒着的两个小时内产生其通常的微妙镇静剂. 我觉得自己很, 缺水. 我很热,很不舒服,即使我回到床上. 由于胃不舒服和缺乏活力, 我认为大麻会帮助我恢复舒适的镇静水平. 而是斯克拉辛的焦虑和易怒性显著增加. 身体上的不适有所缓解, 但困惑和妄想开始. 我很难阅读简单的文字 - - 无法集中注意力. 我决定一个温暖的浴可能会有所帮助, 虽然我的肌肉变得非常松, 我头部感到温暖, 呼吸也有些不稳定, 我在浴里, 感到非常怒和焦虑. 我觉得自己被困在一块巨大的石头下, 麻醉通常在我的放松状态下产生的轻微的OEV变得非常夸张 - - 类似于在psilocybin的影响下看到的模式; 除了严重的视角扭曲. 经过一段时间的休息和伸展, 我可以肯定地说不愉快的效应持续到晚上9点左右, 这使我大约在T+20hr标记. 这让我想起了我在服用阿米特里普塔林后的可怕经历 - - 我必须说,大麻的效果从来没有如此疯狂地表达没有干扰其他,更强烈的迷幻药. 我很高兴现在我没有崩的感觉! 绝对不是一个推的体验. 其他: 大麻 (1), 药品 - 甲素 (244): 医疗用途 (47), 糟糕的旅行 (6), 组合 (3), 单独 (16)

Confused and Heavy Metaxalone (Skelaxin) & Cannabis by Neuroactive Citation:   Neuroactive. "Confused and Heavy: An Experience with Metaxalone (Skelaxin) & Cannabis (exp34960)". Erowid.org . Jan 7, 2007. erowid.org/exp/34960 DOSE: 3.2 g oral Pharms - Metaxalone (pill / tablet) 1 bowl smoked Cannabis (plant material) 1 bowl smoked Cannabis (plant material) BODY WEIGHT: 175 lb I've been using Skelaxin sparingly to help with chronic leg and muscle pains. My previous use of Skelaxin had been generally uneventful and untherapeutic, even when used in conjunction with a small amount of alcohol (which seems to totally relax the painful muscle contractions). Under extreme discomfort and multiple regions of pain, (a very sore arm and the reoccurring leg pains) I decided to take four 800mg pills. Dosages previously had been between two or three of the pills with very limited effects. After squirming around for a couple hours, I finally got to sleep. The Skelaxin only seemed to produce its usual subtle sedation for the two hours I remained awake that night. The next morning I awoke feeling extremely hungover and dehydrated. I was very hot and uncomfortable, even when I returned to bed. My apetite was destroyed by the uneasy stomach and lack of energy I had. I thought a bit of Cannabis would help me get back to a comfortable level of sedation. Instead, the anxiety and irritability of the Skelaxin increased dramatically. Some of the bodily discomfort was relieved, but confusion and delirium set in. I had an extremely hard time reading simple text - I couldn't keep my attention on anything. I decided that a warm bath might help, so I smoked the rest of the Cannabis and slipped into the bath. My muscles became wildly relaxed, but I was still in a great deal of discomfort. My head felt warm and my breathing was somewhat erratic, fading in rhythm and returning in anxious waves. While in the bathtub, I was overcome with a great deal of irritability and anxiety. I felt like I was stuck under a great rock, unable to move without great effort and discomfort. The mild OEVs that marijuana usually produces in my relaxed state became greatly exaggerated - resembling patterns seen while under the effects of psilocybin; minus the heavy distortion of perspective. After some rest and some stretching, I began to regain my strength over the day. I can definitely say that the unpleasant effects lasted until around 9 P.M. the night after I took the dose. This put me at approximately the T+ 20hr mark. In some ways, this reminded me of my awful experiences with amitryptaline - feeling like a zombie all the next day after dosing. I must say, the effects of Cannabis have never been so wildly pronounced without interference from other, stronger psychedelics. I'm glad to be at a point now where I don't feel like collapsing! Not a recommended experience by any means.

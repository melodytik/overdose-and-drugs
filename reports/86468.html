中文译文 致幻剂

我是一个20多岁的男性. 我已经服用了很多胺和胺. 我开始用一个包装118毫克的NBOMe-mescaline HCl. 在美国是七月四日. 我在晚上6点45分吸了大约50毫克, 几分钟后, 视力变得模糊, 颜色变动, 15分钟后发, 我走了25分钟,然后我平静下来了, 几乎没有光线可见, 在那之后,我真的觉得正常了. 在基线清醒. 在等待公共汽车, 感到无聊, 决定开车. 驾驶,感觉很好,没有搞. [Erowid的注意事项: 驾驶时喝醉,倒,或极度缺乏睡眠是危险和不负责任的, 因为它危害了其他人. 不要这样做! 在晚上8点停止. 这种东西很恶心, 但不像吸入一些2C. 我把最后一点放在舌头下. 接下来是光线跟踪器,移动的东西,非常奇怪的感觉. 任何时候都可能发生更多的, 但这从来没有发生过. 走过警察,各种各样的人, 感觉好像我在楼上倒, 很轻,非常轻的标记物. 音乐听起来很奇怪. 这种东西肯定有听觉成分. 这会影响我对声音的感知. 烟花也没有什么动作. 没有太多的闭眼. 到了晚上10点, 这种经验似乎真的很少. 我买了酒,开始喝了一些 (只有2杯酒),吸了一些大麻. 在检查我的酒后驾驶合法限制检查器拨号后开车回家. 我感觉很好. 上午四点. 我只是躺在床上试图睡觉,转动无线电拨号40分钟. 没有睡觉. 闭上眼睛,看到形状和光线, 移动,扭曲,变成其他形状和颜色的进出形状. 不是明亮的东西, 但有点多彩 - 虽然有很多黑暗. 如果只能放大... 或许在初始剂量后再给药对这种物质没有太大影响. 这已经是第二次我得到了淡淡的效果, 实际上没有增加效果, 一旦我重新注射在1小时或更多的初始剂量. 其他: 2011年6月9日发布 梅斯卡林-NBOMe (543): 单独 (16),一般 (1)

Jun 9, 2011. erowid.org/exp/86468 DOSE: T+ 0:00 50 mg insufflated Mescaline-NBOMe (powder / crystals) T+ 1:15 68 mg insufflated Mescaline-NBOMe (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 120 lb I'm a mid-20's male. I've done many tryptamines and phenethylamines. I'm starting with a baggie of 118mg of NBOMe-mescaline HCl. It's the 4th of July in America. I snorted about 50mg at 6:45 PM and put a little under tongue after that. Within minutes, I have blotchy vision with colors moving, chills at 15 minutes, with a trippy/anxious come up leading me on, making me think I'm about to trip hard. I went outside at about 25 minutes and then I calmed down a lot, virtually no visuals in the light, but much more in the dark of a house with no lights on but with the sun still up. After that, I'm really feeling normal again. Sober / at baseline. Waiting for the bus, got bored, decided to drive. Driving, feel fine, not messed up. [Erowid Note: Driving while intoxicated, tripping, or extremely sleep deprived is dangerous and irresponsible because it endangers other people. Don't do it! ] Stopped and at 8:00 PM. Snort the rest (~68mg). This stuff is nasty but doesn't hurt like snorting some 2C. I put the last bit under my tongue. Then there are light tracers, moving stuff, very strange feeling. Like things could burst into much more trippyness at any moment, but it never quite happened. Walked past cops, all kinds of people, feeling like I'm tripping upstairs, but the visuals weren't really there. Light, very light tracers. Music sounds odd. There is definitely an auditory component to this stuff. It affects my perception of sounds. Not much action with the fireworks. :( I talk to people just fine, buy a sweet tea and drink it. Not much with eyes closed. By 10:00 PM it seems over. This experience seems really lacking. I buy beer and start drinking a bit (only 2 beers) and smoke some pot. Drive home after checking my drunk driving legal limit checker dial thing. I feel OK. 4:00 AM. I just laid in bed trying to sleep, turning the radio dial for 40 minutes. Can't sleep. Closed eyes, see shapes and lights, moving, twisting, turning into other shapes and colors coming in and out of the shapes. Not bright stuff, but a little colorful - with a lot of darkness though. If only this could be amplified... Afterthoughts: perhaps redosing after initial dosing doesn't do much with this substance. This is the second time I've gotten lackluster effects and virtually no increase in effects once I've redosed at 1 hour or more from initial dosing.

我一直对过去的药品迷恋. 这让我在大学学习药理学. 我一直听说过"圣杯"镇静剂/催眠药的故事, 经过一些研究,我发现几乎所有这些药物都已停止使用,除了Seconal和Chloral Hydrate,这两种药物都极少被医生处方,而取而代之的是类药物 (triazolam,flurazepam,temazepam等),甚至最近出现的新型"Z药物"如佐尔皮德姆 (Ambien),索皮克隆 (Sonata),和埃斯索皮克隆 (Lunesta). 因为这些药物具有很大的治疗窗口, 以及更低的滥用潜力 (据说). 我很幸运 (或许很不幸) 遇到了一位尊重我的观点, 经过多种治疗失眠的第一线治疗, 我提到了酸盐, 我告诉他为什么我相信这是一种有效的治疗方法, 益处超过风险, 我需要两杯500毫克的5毫升茶匙. 所以总共1000毫克或1克. 在找到市内唯一一家有药店的药店后, 我服用了第一剂. 我感觉到一个一般的麻木不像3-4个酒精饮料的影响, 有轻微的高兴性质. 现在的效应已经达到顶峰. 我感觉有一种轻微的醉酒, 我很平静,我喜欢坐下来放松. 根据它的指示,我非常沉醉. 睡眠可以很容易. 我躺在床上, 慢慢地陷入深眠, 完全没有任何梦想. 我觉得自己没有休息. 第二天晚上几乎是相同的. 我在第三个晚上感到非常失望. 我服用了相同的剂量与相同的胃内含量, 我没有得到任何麻木或高兴, 我发现自己甚至在与失眠作斗争. 这就好像我在两晚内培养了耐受性! 我知道人们很快就会变得宽容, 但这是令人难以置信. 我知道为什么这种药物不再是第一线治疗. 我认为这种药物有趣但不可预测. 这也是必须尊重的. 我读到身体上依赖会在7-10天内发展, 包括妄和发作. 并且具有致癌性. 这种物质可能最适合于过去几代人的传奇故事. 其他: 化 (602):一般性 (1),第一次使用 (2),医疗用途 (47),治疗意图或结果 (49),不适用 (38)

May 21, 2013. erowid.org/exp/100216 DOSE: 1000 mg oral Chloral Hydrate (liquid) BODY WEIGHT: 180 lb I have always been enamored with the pharmaceuticals of the past. Leading me to study pharmacology in college.  I had always heard stories of the 'holy grail' sedative/hypnotics such as Qualuudes, Placidyl, Seconal, Chloral Hydrate, etc. After some research I discovered that almost all of these medications had been discontinued except for Seconal and Chloral Hydrate, both of which are extremely rarely prescribed by doctors in favor of benzodiazepines (triazolam, flurazepam, temazepam, etc.) or even more recently the new 'Z Drugs' such as zolpidem (Ambien), zopiclone (Sonata), and Eszopiclone (Lunesta), because these drugs have a large therapeutic window and much more manageable dependence and overdose. As well as a lower abuse potential (supposedly). I fortunately (maybe unfortunately) came across a psychiatrist that respected my opinions and was open to exploring the treatments that I thought would be effective. So after going through various first line insomnia treatments I mentioned chloral hydrate, which he informed me was very addictive. I informed him why I believed that this would be an effective treatment and that the benefits would outweigh the risks, so I was prescribed Chloral Hydrate syrup. I was to take  two 5ml teaspoons of 500mg. each. So 1,000mg or 1g total. +0:00        After finding the only pharmacy in town who stocked it, I took my first dose. +0:30.      I feel a general numbness not unlike the effects of 3-4 alcoholic beverages, including the increased sociability. With minor euphoric properties. +1:00.       The effects have now peaked. I feel a slight drunkenness which is mixed with euphoria. I am very calm and I enjoy just sitting and relaxing. True to its indication I am very sedated. Sleep could come very easily. +2:00.  I lay in bed and slowly drift off into a deep sleep, completely void of any dreams. The next morning I was extremely groggy and I felt un-rested. The second night was much of the same. My third  night I was very disappointed. I took the exact same dose with the same amount of stomach contents, and I didn't get any of the numbness or euphoria, only a light relief from anxiety. I found myself battling insomnia even. It was as though I had developed a tolerance over 2 nights! I knew that tolerance built quickly but this was unbelievable. I know why this drug isn't a first line treatment anymore. All in all this drug is interesting but unpredictable in my opinion. This is a substance to be respected as well. I have read that physical dependence develops in as little as 7-10 days with a withdrawal profile similar to alcohol. Including delirium tremens and seizures. As well as carcinogenic properties. This substance may be best suited for the glorified stories of generations past.

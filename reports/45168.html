我对Haldol的体验是我一生中最糟糕的药物体验. 我有一个朋友 (乔) 病了几天, 共同的朋友说哈尔多尔是一种阿片类止痛药 [Erowid注:哈尔多尔是一种抗精神病药物,不是阿片类药物. 所以我的朋友是快乐的药片爆发他被两个第一天. 他说他觉得很放松,然后了. 接下来当他的身体疼痛回来, 他吃了3个, 并回去睡觉. 他抱怨身体疼痛, 要求我从他的办公桌上递给他一些哈尔多尔. 我很高兴地喝了酒, 问他是否介意我喝几杯. 他答应了,所以我带了四个. 我之前的药物使用经验让我相信我需要几种药物才能感受到任何重大影响. 接下来的一个半小时, 我开始感到昏昏欲睡, 我甚至开始, 让我相信这真的是阿片. 我在两小时后昏倒, 十二小时后醒来. 感觉有点头和一点震动, 我去了乔的房间吸烟一些大麻和看看他是怎么做的. 他是可怕的疼痛和被扣在他的床上胎儿的位置. 我和他一起抽了些大麻,然后坐下来看电视. 我感觉好了,又饿了,所以我吃了他的冰箱里的巧克力蛋糕. 这就是我的整个生活发生了变化. 我开始用牙来, 仿佛我的嘴巴有坏的棉花. 我会把它拉回来, 然后它会慢慢向前边, 我觉得有点奇怪, 这大约是我服用哈尔多尔后的24小时. 由于我的部不自主地移动, 我无法入睡. 我喝了一些水,试图放松, 但情况越来越糟. 现在我的牙已经越过了我的牙, 我不得不强行把它拉回来, 后来我开始有点害怕. 我决定试着完全放松, 也许我可以睡觉我的伸出来. 我的继续向外推, 我开始惊慌失措, 紧紧着我的下巴大约10分钟后, 我的子开始慢慢向右曲. 像我的一样, 我无法阻止它的运动或保持我的头直立超过几秒. 现在我独自一人在我的房间里, 我的子被挤到外面, 我的头被压在我的肩膀上, 我哭了, 这持续了四个小时. 我脑子里发生了最可怕的想法. 我认为我会永远被困在这里, 担心我的室友会带着他的女朋友回家, 最后我睡着了. 我觉得自己很好. 我跳了起来,可能在房间里做了小小的动作. 我生命中最快乐的时刻是没有. 八小时后, 48小时后, 就像以前一样糟糕. 我失去了它. 如果我要永远这样被困, 我想死. 我要么要睡觉,要么在尝试中死去. 他回来后, 我消耗了五分之一的酒和12个安眠药. 没有什么. 仍然紧紧地在我的床上我决定去深端. 我吃了剩下的12粒安眠药和2粒X药, 一个小时后, 我终于醒来了. 我过去的4个月都害怕他们会回来. 乔的经历是10倍糟糕的. 他服用了三天的哈尔多尔, 他的整个身体被锁定了两天. 查看Haldol的网站上说"会引起帕金森症的症状". 这不是谎言. 哈尔多尔是邪恶的, 并不是为了滥用. 请不要尝试. 已使用的证券 药品 - (308):小组 (2-9) (17),不好的旅行 (6),第一次 (2)

Mar 8, 2007. erowid.org/exp/45168 DOSE: 40 mg oral Pharms - Haloperidol (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 200 lb My experience with haldol was the worst drug experience of my life. A friend of mine (Joe) had been sick for a few days, and was give several tabs of haldol by a mutual friend. The mutual friend said that haldol was an opiate pain killer [Erowid Note: Haldol is an anti-pyschotic medication and is not an opiate.] , so my friend being the happy pill popper he was took two the first day. He said he felt relaxed and passed out. The next day when his body aches returned, he took 3, and went back to sleep. On the third day when I found him, he was complaining of massive body aches, and asked me to hand him some of the haldol from his desk. I was pleasantly intoxicated on alchol and asked if he minded if I popped a few. He said yes, so I took four. My previous experiences with pill popping had lead me to beleive that I needed quite a few to feel any major effects. Over the next hour and a half I began to feel drowsy and had aquired a decent body high which could be compared to percocet. I even began to itch which led me to believe it really was an opiate. I passed out after about 2 hours, and woke up about 12 hours later. Feeling a bit hung over and a little shakey, I went down to Joes room to smoke some bud and see how he was doing. He was in horrible pain and was clinched up on his bed in a the fetal position. I smoked some pot with him and then sat down to watch tv. I was feeling better and got hungry so I ate some choclate cake that was in his fridge. THIS IS WHEN MY WHOLE LIFE CHANGED. About three bites into the chocalate cake my tounge began pushing against the back of my teeth, almost like I had bad cotton mouth. I would pull it back, and it would slowly edge forward until it was pressing against my teeth. Feeling a bit strange, I went to my room to lay down. This is approximately 24 hours since I had taken the haldol. Upon arriving in my room, I was unable to sleep because of the involuntary movements of my tounge. I drank some water and tried to relax, but it was getting worse. Now my tounge was pushing past my teeth and protruding from my mouth. I would have to forceably pull it back in and lock my jaw down. Then I began to get a little bit scared. I decide to try to completely relax and maybe I could go to sleep with my tounge stuck out. My tounge continued to push out until it strecthed to the point it was painful. I began to panic and forced it back in and clenched my jaw shut. After clenching my jaw for about 10 mins., my neck began to slowly bend to the right. Much like my tounge, I was unable to stop its movement or hold my head up straight any longer than a few seconds. Now I'm in my room by myself, my tounge is stuck all the way out, my head is crushed into my shoulder, and I am crying because it feels like I am about to break my own neck from the force of the muscles clenching. This lasted for four hours. I had the most horryfying thoughts going through my head. Thoughts of being stuck like this forever, and paranoia of my roomate coming home with his gf, and having to explain. Finally I fell asleep. Upon awaking the next morning, I felt fine. I jumped up and probably did a little jig around the room. The happiest moment of my life was to be convulsion free. 8 hours later, 48 hours from the initial dose, it came back. Just as bad as before. I lost it. For the first time in my life, I wanted to die if this was how I was going to be stuck forever. I sent my buddy to the store for liquor and sleeping pills. I was either going to go to sleep, or I was going to die trying. Upon his return, I consumed the fifth of liqour and 12 sleeping pills. Nothing. Still clenched up on my bed I decided to go off the deep end. I took the remaining 12 sleeping pills and 2 pills of X I had purchased for the weekend (I was willing to try anything now). An hour later, I finally drifted off sleep. I've spent the last 4 months of my life scared that 'they' will come back. Joe's experience was 10 times worse. He had been on haldol for three days, and his whole body locked up for two days. Looking at the haldol website says 'will cause parkinsons like symptoms'. That was no lie. Haldol is evil, and is not meant to be abused. Please do not try it.

很难说我什么时候开始使用阿片. 我只知道几年前, 当我大概15岁的时候, 我在高中, 我在继母的抽里找到了一些, 然后我们去购物. 我只记得坐在餐桌上吃洋, 风吹过我的头发, 让我感到最平静,最令人惊叹的身体感觉, 我从来不喜欢大麻, 因为它通常会让我变得偏执, 我现在最多每月抽一次. 除了酒精,大麻和处方之外, 我唯一尝试过的药物是DXM和可卡因 (如果可乐不那么昂贵, 我想尝试,但我认为我还没有准备好. 无论如何,我17岁时搬到了我家, 我们住在祖父母附近. 我开始尝试我祖父的药, 无论如何,我记得在网上查看他的药物. 他有10-15种不同的处方, 但经过一些研究, 我爱上了这些处方, 终于我得到了自己的 xanax 处方, 因为我有严重的社会和一般焦虑, 但这是另一个故事. 这是关于我使用片. 后来,我祖父开始注意到他的药丸消失了. 他会数它们,如果比预期的少, 他会把它们藏起来. 我总是找到它们,如果它们在一个锁盒里, 我找到了破解密码的方式. 我开始成为一个真正的君子. 我知道他需要它们,所以我试着一次只拿几支. 我无法阻止自己. 我甚至觉得也很好. 这只是为课程的标准. 我记得有一次,我从社区大学回家,服用了25毫克的维科丁, 我不知道如何描述它但我不能想象一个更好的感觉. 维科丁对我的精神产生了麻木作用. 如果我害羞, 服用10或20毫克, 如果我感到无聊,事情就变得有趣. 如果我有宿醉, 突然我有能量, 很高兴生活. 我和我的朋友Irene也会从朋友或亲戚那里买药, 虽然她现在在监狱里. 我告诉我,当我服用阿片类药物时, 工作人员称赞我的快速,友好的表现 (我在塔科贝尔工作). 这使得时间更快. 我可以努力工作, 忍受不舒服的情绪, 身体和情绪, 这通常是我无法忍受. 我做这一切都是微笑. 缺点是,当我几天没有任何,我的皮肤感觉像它在火,我成为一个狂暴的白痴和一个完全易怒的,我得到这个的拉感觉在我的胃, 我写这篇文章的原因在于, 几周前, 我总是为自己所做的事情感到不好, 但最后我告诉他我已经服用了它们, 我的家人知道我是个酒者和一个上者. 这给他们带来了很多麻烦, 我觉得自己是个负担,也是个失败者. 所以当祖父发现时, 他非常生气. 我能听到他在半夜吟和吟, 称我为子, 说他永远不会原谅我. 我想死, 感觉自己是魔鬼. 我想要停止想要这种药物, 医生给他开了一份氧化的处方, 我现在正在服用10毫克. 片让我感觉到我想象的正常感觉是对其他人. 如果我能在清醒的状态下拥有这种和平与满足的感觉. 我也是一个酒者. 我在19岁生日上参加了第一次阿联会议, 如果您有个性上, 请不要尝试阿片类药物或任何类型的麻醉品, 它会成为你的上帝, 你会偷窃,骗你的亲人, 它们确实能帮助一些真正受苦的人, 我希望我有应对技巧永远不会喝酒或服用任何药物. 未来我唯一计划服用的药物是诸如和皮约特之类的原体. 他们是缓慢的死亡. 这就是我的经验. 我只有19岁. 我希望我能在年轻的时候掉这件事, 但如果你是片或其他任何东西的成, 保持强大, 其他: 水 (111):各种 (28),家庭 (41),成和习惯性 (10)

"Coming Down Is the Hardest Thing: An Experience with Hydrocodone (exp86470)". Erowid.org . Nov 25, 2015. erowid.org/exp/86470 DOSE: repeated Hydrocodone (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 115 lb It’s hard to say when I began using opiates. All I know is that a few years back, when I was maybe 15-ish, I was in high school, living with my dad, and I realized that certain prescription pills would get me high. I found some hydrocodone (vicodin) in my stepmom’s drawer and took a couple. Then we went shopping. All I remember is sitting at a table eating onion rings, the wind blowing through my hair, and having the most peaceful, amazing body feeling, saying to myself, “This has to be what heaven is like.” At the time I also drank heavily and smoked pot occasionally, but I never really liked pot because it usually makes me paranoid and makes me think too much about negative stuff. I smoke it maybe once a month at most now. The only other drugs I have tried besides alcohol, pot and prescriptions, are DXM and cocaine (I could easily see myself becoming addicted to coke if it weren't so damned expensive). I want to try mushrooms but I don't think I'm ready yet. Ok anyway, I moved in with my mom when I was 17 and we lived near my grandparents. I started trying my grandpa’s pills to see what they would do to me. Anyway, I remember looking through all his pills and looking them all up online. He had 10-15 different prescriptions, but after some research, I realized that the only ones with any recreational value were his Xanax, Klonopin, and Vicodin. I fell in love with these prescriptions and began taking them on occasion to get looped. Eventually I got my own prescription for xanax because I have severe social and general anxiety, so I didn’t really take his xanax or klonopin anymore. But that’s another story. This is about my opiate use. At some point, my grandpa began noticing that his pills were going missing. He would count them and when there were less than he expected, he would hide them. But I always found them, and if they were in a lockbox, I found a way to crack the code. I was becoming a true addict. I knew he needed them so I tried to only take a few at a time. But the feeling was like nothing I had ever felt and I couldn’t stop myself. Even the itch felt good to me. It was just par for the course. One time, I remember coming home from my community college, taking maybe 25 mg of Vicodin, and lying in the grass in the park feeling like... I don't know how to describe it but I can't imagine a better feeling. Vicodin became a numbing agent for my psyche. If I felt shy and took 10 or 20 mgs, that went away. If I was bored, things became interesting. If I was hungover, suddenly I had energy and was excited about life. Me and my friend “Irene” would also find pills from friends or relatives, mostly other opiates, like darvocet and Percocet, and take them together. She is in jail now, though. My mom has told me it seems like I’m on speed when I’m on opiates. People at work compliment me on my quick, friendly performance (I work at Taco Bell). It makes time go by faster. I can do hard work and endure uncomfortable feelings, both physical and emotional, that I normally could not stand. And I do it all with a smile. The downside is that when I go a few days without any, my skin feels like it’s on fire, I become a raging idiot and a total irritable b*tch, I get this nagging tugging sensation in my stomach that just won’t go away unless I take more, and I don't sleep. What prompted me to write this was the fact that a couple weeks ago my grandpa began complaining that he didn’t have enough hydrocodone (he doesn't know that they are the same thing as Vicodin or Lortabs) to get him through the month. I have always felt bad about what I have been doing but finally I just told him that I took them, I have a problem, I wish to stop and it won’t happen again. My family knows I am an alcoholic and an addict. It has caused them many problems and it is very embarrassing to me. I feel like a burden on them and a failure. So when grandpa found out, he was very angry. I could hear him moaning and groaning in the middle of the night, calling me a b**ch and saying how he would never forgive me. I wanted to die and felt like the devil incarnate. I wanted to stop wanting this drug, but couldn’t imagine ever stopping. The doctor just gave him a prescription for oxycodone to help him get through the month, but he says they don’t help him, so I feel slightly less bad about taking them but still feel guilty. I am on 10mg of that right now. Opiates make me feel what I imagine normal feels like to other people. If only I could have this sense of peace and contentment in a sober state. I am also an alcoholic. I went to my first AA meeting on my 19th birthday but am scared to go back. If you have an addictive personality, please never try opiate drugs or any kind of narcotic because more than likely you will get addicted and your life will become a hell. It will become your God, the only thing you truly care about. You will steal, lie to your loved ones, and do things you never thought you were capable of doing. They really help some people who are truly in pain but it is a slippery slope. I wish I had the coping skills to never drink or take any drugs again. The only drugs that I plan to take in my future are entheogens like mushrooms and peyote. Opiates have no spiritual value. They are a slow death. Well that is my experience. I am only 19 years old. Hopefully I will be able to kick this while I’m still young, but right now I need these to keep me going. But if you are addicted to opiates or anything else, stay strong, I know the sh*t you are going through.

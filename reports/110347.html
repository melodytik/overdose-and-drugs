I turned on some music because I felt electrically charged (yes I know it sounds weird) and wanted to dance! Normally I listen to Drum and Bass but I felt like listening to softer music. T: 04:00: I think I am on my peak! I see a lot of faces on the wall and they scare me a little bit but its no problem to ignore them. The walls seem to wobble and move. Anything I look at seems so vivid but also not. My legs feel really heavy and I feel like I got pins and needles in them. Both 2C-B and DOB seem a lot easier to control than Mushrooms for example. T: 05:00 - 08:00: Boy was I wrong! I thought I was peaking when I was 4 hours into the experience but the trip got crazier and crazier! The 2C-B in combination with the DOB turned my living room into a really weird place! I felt quite uncomfortable on my couch, because somehow it felt like I was never sitting right! I changed my position like 10 times but it got worse and worse so I decided to stand up and walk around a little bit.. I went to my window to take a few deep breaths to calm myself down. I looked outside very cautiously (because I was scared to fall out) and saw so many weird patterns on the ground . They were very angular shaped but got spheric when I took a closer look. I then closed the window and grabbed a pillow. I started giggling like a little schoolgirl when I stroked the fluffy pillow and somehow I felt at ease when looking at it. I then turned the music louder and made myself a nice smoothie ! I wasn't hungry or thirsty at all but the fruits looked amazing! I had so much saliva in my mouth it was unbelievable ! I had so much saliva in my mouth it was unbelievable ! I had so much saliva in my mouth that at one point I started to crack up because of it! I had some tobacco on my table and I looked at it closely. The tobacco seemed like a lot of tiny, cute worms that were wiggling around. T: 08:00: About 8 hours into the experience I started to feel overwhelmed by the effects and started to freak out a little bit, because I noticed that I wasn't even half way through the trip yet. The constant restless feeling of the DOB made it even worse because I couldn't calm myself down... T: 08:30: Anyways I tried to focus and relax as much as possible and it finally worked. I put on some chill music and looked at my phone for a couple of minutes. I couldn't understand any of the messages I got that day but it was a nice feeling looking at them lol. Then I called up two friends that live nearby me and asked them to hang out. A few minutes later they dropped by and we smoked some weed together which intensified the trip even more.. T: 09:00 - 13:00: Not much noteworthy happened in that time. We smoked weed, watched TV and talked to each other. I had a lot of really hard but random conniptions. The visuals were still VERY impressive even 13 hours into the trip. I wasn't hungry but I came to the conclusion that I had to eat something! I ordered a salami-pizza but only ate 2 slices of it... T: 13:00-15:00: I noticed a little bit of an offset ... I mean I was still tripping but everything seemed easier to control. The colors were still very bright and there were a lot of funny shaped figures and patterns on the floor and walls. T: 15:00-18:00: The psychedelic effects of the DOB decreased to the point where they were no longer visible and the colors returned to normal. The only thing that really didn't decrease were the stimulating effects. My mind felt extremely exhausted but my body was still very pumped and full of energy.. T: 18:00-22:00: The stimulating qualities of DOB are very intense! So intense that I had the feeling that it would never stop... So I decided to take 2.5mg (half pill) of Valium to come down... T: 23:30: I waited 1,5 hours but that didn't help so I took the other half as well. T: 24:00 - 25:00: Somewhere between 24 and 25 hours I was able to sleep... Next Day: I slept for 13 hours and woke up with no hangover. I felt very drained though. I never have a hangover on 2C-B and maybe that's the reason why I don't have one now but still, I am very surprised because the DOB was very exhausting and tiring! NOTE: Handle DOB with caution ! I took 1mg and tripped for 18 hours and had a unpleasant and awkward 6 hour comedown... I know I combined it with 2C-B, but 2C-B never lasts longer than 6 hours!

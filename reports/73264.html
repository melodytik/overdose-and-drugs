美国 为了减轻疼痛和压力, 我服用了三种37.5毫克的特拉马多尔 (Ultracet) 325毫克的乙甲, 总共有112.5毫克的特拉马多尔和975毫克的乙甲. 我很少用超过1000毫克的乙甲来观察我的肝脏. 现在我心情有点好, 因为今天是工作周结束, 我先洗手,然后用消毒剂. 我用最大强度的疼痛配方Neosporin (油类型) 麻醉了手臂的静脉区域. 我在餐桌上用新洗的盘子, 快速释放的吗啡硫酸盐药片 (总量60毫克) 放入过的自来水 (小于一大匙) 的一个大无钢匙中. 这些药丸是小白色的圆形药丸, 溶解很快. 我使用过器来过水. 用针盖将溶液稍微一下. 我使用双火焰打火机来煮沸溶液. 我稍微煮沸了大约10秒. 我再次用洗手液消毒自己的手. 然后我把一小块紧紧卷起来的棉花球 (从一个大棉花球中拉出来) 放入消毒液中. 这将过出任何不需要的粘合剂从药丸当我抽出溶液进入注射器. 我用棉衣带将我的手臂绑在双腿上. 然后我取下针头的盖子, 轻轻地把针头插进满的小棉球里, 我曾经学到针尖上最轻微的打伤会很快使针头变得淡, 我可以把干的棉球放在针头上, 我慢慢地把溶液抽到1毫升的注射器里. 针与子处于一个角度, 就像我注射时的角度一样. 我不会冒着死去的风险. 然后我把注射器放在明亮的光线上, 用手指敲几次, 如果看到气泡,我再敲一下注射器,然后再把水栓推上去,把气体排出. 接下来我用抗菌药物利多卡因溶液 (带品牌) 冲进手臂的静脉注射区域, 我我的拳头几次得到一个好的丰满的静脉. 我是右撇子,我的右臂总是很好. 静脉变得如此硬,以至于我误认为是肌. 我用我的手指把它推进来, 放松压力后, 然后我把针头浸入酒精中, 这次用酒精很好地滑动手臂, 准确地找到准确的位置, 这是一个舒适的尺寸. 我下次使用较小的28或29尺. 它们甚至更舒适. 我还使用半英寸的针头. 我还会把注射器放到2毫升的清晰注射器里,这样我可以更好地看到气泡. 我把它放在我的手臂在25-30度的角度, 并且指向我的身体. 我把四分之一英寸的插进去,然后用左手拉出子,直到我看到血--那我就知道我有静脉. 我总是第一次被击中,因为我的静脉很大,很明显. 幸运的是我. 我慢慢地注入一半, 感觉到热浪般的感觉, 我总是先伤到腿, 腿和手臂也会变弱. 我经常发现,由于手臂的软弱, 我必须小心观察, 因为我通常会因为扎和注射针而伤自己. 我认为我无意识地过度补偿, 我必须非常小心. 一旦我恢复了一些控制,其余的注射. 我慢慢地拔出针头, 我通常会有伤,所以我用内部和局部. 我用了一大份缓解疼痛的新素. 然后几个小时后, 我将大量的外部直接注射到注射部位. 我会立即取消内部的. 我很好. 我不建议60毫克的静脉吗啡轻重的人和/或片不耐受的人. 这是一个相当高的剂量. 我有时会对片产生很强的耐药性, 体重为195磅, 我已经喝了茶, 足以杀死一些人. 这种药物可能含有大量的吗啡和其他阿片类药物, 药剂的强度也会有很大差异, 我每月服用一次或两次, 取决于我的疼痛程度, 有时我可以在没有严重的止痛药的情况下忍受两个到三个月, 只需依靠拜尔阿司匹林,泰伦醇, 或每4到6小时服用一粒37.5毫克的特拉马多尔/APAP. 我非常小心地观察吗啡的剂量, 两三粒Pharma Kava囊也很好地缓解了我的疼痛和压力. 在1毫升H2O中注射60毫克的吗啡和112毫克的特拉马多尔都会给人一种强烈的愉快快乐感, 我背部的疼痛完全消失了. 我感觉到一个适度无痛的快乐大约6-8小时和轻微的效果第二天大约6小时. 这种感觉真是太好了, 副作用是口腔干燥, 便秘, 但我用过的H2O补充水分, 太多的梅汁会引起一些严重的爆发, 清除你, 以及家里的每个人! 片也让我在3到5个小时内变得有点过度, 约2-3天后, 服用一剂药后, 我有点流水, 所以我服用了一些洛普拉米德化物- 伊莫迪亚 AD. 其他: 吗啡 (211), 药品 - 特拉马多尔 (149): 单独 (16), 治疗意图或结果 (49), 医疗用途 (47), 健康益处 (32), 和习惯性 (10), 准备/食谱 (30), 一般 (1)

Jan 3, 2012. erowid.org/exp/73264 DOSE: 112.5 mg oral Pharms - Tramadol (pill / tablet) 975 mg oral Acetaminophen (pill / tablet) 60 mg IV Morphine (pill / tablet) repeated oral Pharms - Tramadol (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 195 lb On a friday night after dinner around 5:00 PM, for pain and stress management, I took three 37.5 mg. tramadol (Ultracet) 325 mg. acetametaphen with plenty of water. So in total: 112.5 mg. of tramadol and 975 mg. of acetametaphen. I rarely go over 1000 mg. of acetametaphen to watch my liver. My mood is somewhat good because it is the end of the work week, but I am tired and suffering from lower back pain. I wash my hands and then use hand sanitizer. I numb the IV area of my arm with maximum strength pain formula Neosporin (petroleum jelly type). At the dining room table, I work on a freshly machine washed plate and put two 30 mg. (60 mg. total) rapid release morphine sulphate pills in a large sterilized stainless steel spoon of filtered tap water (a little less than a tablespoon). The pills are little white round ones that dissolve quickly. I use a water filter for the water. I stir the solution a little with the needle cover. I use a dual flame torch lighter to boil the solution. I boil it slightly for about 10 seconds. I sterilize my hands with hand sanitizer again. I then drop a little tightly rolled up ball of cotton (pulled from a large cotton ball) into the sterilized solution. This will filter out any unwanted binders from the pills when I draw the solution into the syringe. I then tie my arm at the bicep with my cotton robe belt. I then take the needle cover off and ever so gently stick the needle tip into the little saturated ball of cotton making sure the needle tip does not touch the metal spoon. I once learned that the slightest ding on the tip of the needle dulls it quickly and will cause a rough injection and a nasty bruise later on. I could put a dry ball of cotton onto the tip of the needle, but risk poking myself and dulling the tip. I pull up the solution slowly into the 1 ml. syringe. The needle is at an angle with the spoon like the angle that I put it when I inject myself. I don’t risk poking the spoon dead on. I then hold the syringe up to a strong light and I tap it a few times with my finger to push the air bubbles up to the top and then push in the plunger to get the air out. I then pull up some more solution and tap the syringe again if I see air bubbles and then push the plunger up again to expel the air. I then swipe the IV area of my arm with an antiseptic lidocaine solution (Band-aid brand), numbing it further (I hate needles). I pump my fist a few times to get a good plump vein. I’m right handed and my right arm always works well. The vein gets so hard that I mistaken it for a tendon. But then I push it in with my finger and it gives and comes back when I relieve the pressure. I then dip the needle tip into some alcohol, swipe the arm really good with some alcohol this time, precisely find the exact spot, and slowly inject the plump, firm vein, with the 27 gauge needle. It’s a comfortable size. I’m going to use the smaller 28 or 29 gauges next time. They are even more comfortable. I’m also using a half-inch needle. I will also go up to a 2 ml. clearer syringe so I can see the air bubbles better. I put it in my arm at a 25-30 degree angle and have it pointed towards my body. I put about a quarter inch in and then pull out the plunger a little with my left hand until I see blood- then I know I got the vein. I always get a hit the first time because my veins are big and visible. Lucky me. I slowly inject half of it and get the rush- a warm wave feeling all over and instant relaxation. It always hits my legs first and they go weak as well as my arms. I often find at this point, due to the weakness in my arms, that I push the needle all the way in unintentionally. I then have to watch carefully because I usually bruise myself from jostling and having the needle in all the way. My theory is that I unconsciously overcompensate trying not to pull it out prematurely or have it fall out by mistake. The rush can be overwhelming sometimes and so I must be very careful at this point. Once I gain back some control, the rest is injected. I slowly pull out the needle and quickly dress a cotton ball dipped in a little alcohol and secure it with a band-aid. I usually bruise regardless, so I take internal arnica and apply topical arnica as well. Arnica can be purchased at a health food store and is specifically for bruising and muscle soreness. When I take the cotton ball off my arm about 30 minutes later, I apply a liberal amount of Neosporin with pain relief. Then a couple hours later I apply a liberal amount of external arnica directly on the area of injection. The internal arnica I take right away. I’m all good. I wouldn’t recommend 60 mg. of IV morphine to light-weight people and/or people opiate intolerant. That is a rather high dose. I can be very opiate tolerant sometimes and I weigh 195 lbs. and suffer from severe chronic low back pain. I’ve taken poppy seed tea, and enough to kill some people. That stuff can have a lot of morphine and other opiates in it as well, and the batches can vary widely in strength, which makes it dangerous if not monitored in preparation, and consumed correctly with great scrutiny. I do the tramadol/morphine routine about once or two times per month, depending on my pain level and I always watch the dosage with great scrutiny. Sometimes I can go for two to three months without serious pain meds and just rely on Bayer aspirin, Tylenol, or one pill of the 37.5 mg. tramadol/APAP every 4-6 hours. I watch the morphine dose very carefully if I’m off opiates for a while, as I become less tolerant to them. Two or three Pharma Kava capsules from Herb Pharm works well too for managing my pain and stress. The 60 mg. of IV morphine in 1 ml. of H2O and the 112 mg. of tramadol both give a strong, pleasant euphoric feeling and virtually all pain gone for about 2 hours. The first moderately intense rush lasts about 10-12 minutes and my back pain dissolves completely. I feel a moderate pain-free bliss for about 6-8 hours and slight effects the next day for about 6 hours. The feeling is really great and a welcomed relief due to my ongoing nagging, and sometimes intense, lower back pain. The side effects for me are a dry mouth, and some constipation, but I hydrate a lot with filtered H2O and drink some apricot juice and/or a little prune juice. Too much prune juice can cause some serious blow-outs and clear you out, as well as everybody in the house! Opiates also make me a little hyper about 3-5 hours into it, and of course, drowsy and very sleepy later on. About 2-3 days later after taking the described dose only once, I get a little runny, so I take some lopramide hydrochloride- Imodium AD.

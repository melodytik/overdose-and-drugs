很久没有做过这样的愚蠢事了. 在那个时候, 西达胺品牌Tantum可以在没有处方的情况下获得. 我们在互联网上看到一些旅行报道, 想试试这种迷幻魔鬼药. 我们年轻,愚蠢,充满活力和一个盒子的愤怒就像一美元. 于是有一天我买了一些酒, 我买了三盒药, 每盒含有20片, 每片50毫克. 我们在一个小时内喝完酒, 我们之前做过这件事, 但用更低的剂量, 气最常见的效果是看到移动物体后面的痕迹. 经过30分钟左右的时间, 经过45分钟,1小时后, 路径发生每一个移动的物体看起来像从矩阵的场景, 发生了一些轻微的幻觉, 半年后,幻觉变得越来越严重, 但引起的幻觉不像酸或. 每一个幻觉都在我的眼角, 当我把注意力转移到幻觉上时, 幻觉就消失了. 像我说的我们是愚蠢的什么是服用幻觉剂的意义,如果你不能看他们. 这些幻觉主要是影子或一些小动物, 更多的对象 我们的客厅里充满了垃圾. 两小时后, 效果依然不变, 我们都知道,但仍在做. 这不像对药物产生焦虑, 狂妄症是超越焦虑的东西. 我记得我们三个人要去所时背对着对方, 因为我们很害怕, 我们不知道我们害怕的是什么, 但强烈的恐惧在那里, 我开始失去自我意识. 我决定背靠在房间的角落, 把腿在胸前, 我突然感觉到背上有一只手, 子里流着冷汗. 我被光线的线索所逗乐, 影子在房子周围移动, 也感觉到混乱的未知恐惧. 我躺在沙发上,我的朋友坐在我的右边,他右边有窗户的阳台门. 我着阳台,看到有人坐在椅子上背靠着我. 我在想"谁在阳台上坐着"一会儿, 阳台上的人向我转过来, 我意识到这只是我朋友的反映. 5小时 - 我们不得不喝很多液体, 因为棉花的嘴. 我不知自己在喝什么,还是清醒. 我决定和我的朋友聊天. 我不记得它是什么但我记得关键部分. 我跟他说话时,他说:"你为什么停下来,继续发生什么事?" 我说:"你在说什么? 我没有说什么. "我说"你会怎么做?" 他回答说"我会怎么做? 我只是说"我忘了我在说什么". 他又回答说:"你已经半小时没说话了, 我开始内心恐慌, 我在扬声器上听音乐, 随机的速度是5倍快,5倍慢. 我无法跟随音乐. 我不知道已经有多久了. 我们只是感到焦虑,丧, 凝视着墙壁. 我们不停地环顾四周, 我们感觉很糟糕, 疲不堪. 我想睡觉,但我不能. 我闭上眼睛, 看到的是华斯卡. 看到面孔或一些旋转的几何螺旋. 我没有经历任何幻觉, 但视觉障碍仍然存在. 我一直在看到移动的物体和它们周围的影子. 我没有睡过36个小时. 我吃不下去. 我的小子是5倍小. 我不能忍受一个. 我眼睛是血染的, 脚下有小棕色的斑点. 几年后我做了一些愚蠢的药物/meds. 最近主要是酒,但我不会,即使你付钱给我. 这是一种幻觉, 但它根本不是娱乐. 如果它不会引起偏执和失眠, 这将是很棒的, 但它是危险的, 现在它是处方药, 完全不受欢迎. 我们三个人多次服用了这种药物, 从长远来看, 我从那时起就一直有着焦虑. 很难将这种情况与愤怒联系起来, 但它可能会触发我内心的隐藏. 这是一场梦,不值得. 时间表: 115176 发布时间:2021年3月24日 班齐达胺 (730):回顾/总结 (11),旅行后的问题 (8),困难经历 (5),小组 (2-9) (17)

Paranoid and Confused Benzydamine & Beer by Semo Citation:   Semo. "Paranoid and Confused: An Experience with Benzydamine & Beer (exp115176)". Erowid.org . Mar 24, 2021. erowid.org/exp/115176 DOSE: 3 glasses oral Alcohol - Beer/Wine 1 g oral Benzydamine (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 62 kg Oh boy it's been a long time since I did this stupid thing. Benzydamine brand name tantum was available without prescription back then. We have seen some trip reports on the internet and want to try this psychedelic demon pills. We were young, dumb and full of energy and a box of tantum was like a dollar. So one day I got some beers and decided to get tantum from a local pharmacy. We were 3 and I bought 3 boxes of tantum each containing 20 pills as 50 mg each. We chug the beers in an hour or so and decided to swallow 20 pills each at once. We have done this before but on much lower doses we could barely call it a trip. Most common effect of tantum is seeing trails behind moving objects. After 30 minutes or so we were just waving our hands to see some trails to be sure of that if it was kicking in. After 45 min. - 1 hr later, trails were happening every moving object looked like that scene from matrix where neo dodging bullets by leaning back. And some mild hallucinations of shadows were happening but not that much noticeable. After 1.5 or so hallucinations were getting harder but hallucinations caused by tantum is not like acid or shrooms. It was happening outside of my focus so every hallucination was on the corner of my eye and when I move my focus to hallucination it gets disappeared. As I said we were dumb what is the point of taking hallucinogens if you cant watch them. And those hallucinations were mostly shadows or some small animals like rats or snakes. More objects in the room more hallucinations happening and our living room was full of trash. 2 hrs later effects stays the same and paranoia kicks in for everyone else and it is the worst part of this substance. We all know it but still doing it. It's not like having an anxiety over a drug, tantum paranoia is something beyond anxiety. I remember that 3 of us put our backs to each other to go to the toilet because we were full of fear since we can't see our back. We don't know what we are scared of but a strong fear was there and it will haunt us for the next 12 hours. 4 hr later effects starts to peak and I was loosing my sense of self. Because of the paranoia I decided to put my back on the corner of room and fold my legs to my chest and hug them. Suddenly I feel a hand on my back, cold sweat rushing through my neck. I was amused by the light trails, and shadows moving around the house also feeling a chaotic unknown fear. I was on a couch and my friend was on my right side and he had windowed balcony door on his right. I was gazing into the balcony and seeing someone sitting on chair with his back against me. I was like " who the hell is this sitting in balcony" for awhile and person in balcony turned his face to me I was sweating cold again. And I realized it was just reflection of my friend. 5 hr - we had to drink a lot of fluids because of cotton mouth. I was shifting in and out of consciousness and I wasn't able to tell if I was on something or I was sober. I decided to chat with my friend. I don't really remember what it was about but I remember the key parts. I was talking to him and he said " Why did you stop, go on what happened after? " I said " what are you talking about? I didn't say anything." and I said " What would you do?" He replied "What would I do? What are you asking about ?" I just said " I forgot what I was talking about". He replied again " You were not talking for the last half an hour dude". I started to panic from inside, thinking that I lost my mind and couldn't relax. I was listening to music on the speakers and it was going 5x faster and 5x slower randomly. I wasn't able to follow the music. I wasn't able to tell how long it has been. We were just feeling anxious, depressed and gazing on the walls. We were constantly checking around, thinking there is something behind us all the time. 6-8 hr - effects starts to diminish and we were feeling like shit and exhausted. I wanted to sleep but I couldn't. When I closed my eyes I was having huasca visuals. Seeing faces or some spinning geometric spirals. 12-36 hr - I didn't experience any hallucinations but visual disturbance was still there. I kept seeing trails of moving objects and shadowy reflections around them. Until it has been 36 hr I didn't sleep at all. I couldn't eat. My dick was like 5x smaller. I couldn't take a shit. My eyes were bloodshot and had small brown spots under my feet. After years later I did some stupid drugs/meds. Mostly binge benzo use recently but I won't do tantum even if you pay me. It is hallucinogenic but it's not recreational at all. If it wasn't causing paranoia and sleeplessness it would be awesome but it is dangerous and a dumb way to get high. Now it's a prescription medicine and not popular at all. 3 of us did that drug several times and in long term we didn't suffer and physical disturbances and 2 of my friends are healthy both physically and mentally. But I suffer from constant anxiety since then. It is hard to relate this situation alone with tantum but it might trigger what was hidden in me. It's nightmarish and not worth it.

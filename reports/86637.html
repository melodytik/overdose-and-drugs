我买了JWH-250而不是JWH-018因为018现在在阿拉巴马州是非法的. 虽然JWH-250的强度与018相同, 但持续时间更短. 如果我没抽过一段时间, 大麻会持续2到3个小时, 尽管如此, 问题不在于它更温和, 而在于它不会持续很长时间. 这可能是方便一些人, 午餐时间很短! 我得到的粉末价格约为每克每小时40美元, 我没有试图吸入它, 因为JWH-018在吸入时不活跃. 我不知道它是否能与018混合, 但在没有018的两周后, 我将这种化学物质比作吸烟最好的, 尽管如此,再次值得. Feb 20, 2011. erowid.org/exp/86637 DOSE: smoked JWH-250 (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 150 lb I bought JWH-250 instead of JWH-018 because 018 is now illegal in Alabama. JWH-250 is just like 018 in intensity, but it lasts an even shorter time. Marijuana lasts about 2-3 hours (if I haven’t smoked in a while) and JWH-018 lasts an hour to 1.5 hours, but the intensity of 250 lasts only 30 minutes. It is still worth buying, though. I can get freaky high on this stuff, the problem is not that it’s milder, but that it doesn’t last as long. This might be convenient for some people, though, who have a short lunch hour! The powder I got was about $40 a gram s/h included and was white to light pink, and melted on tinfoil at the touch of a lighter to a pink liquid which vaporized. I did not try snorting it, since JWH-018 was not active when snorted. I do not know if it is cross-tolerant with 018 but the intensity of smoking it after 2 weeks without 018 did not leave me wanting more. I’d compare this chemical to smoking the kindest of the kind bud, but the buzz leaves me very quickly. Worth it, though, again.

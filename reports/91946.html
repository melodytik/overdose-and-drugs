我同事为当地绿色企业举办了一场聚会, 其中一家在聚会上亮相的企业是巧克力贸易公司. 结果,整个房子都被巧克力,可可豆,可可豆,各种生巧克力包围了. 我们还有一个巧克力喷泉, 我们喜欢每当我们有借口, 我吃了大约4到5杯 (大) 巧克力, 几只手里有豆子和豆子, 也许还有一两条. 一位客人把可可豆扔进巧克力喷泉, 喷泉塞住了, 我决定唯一不浪费时间的清理方法就是用手指擦拭和它们. 我可能会以这种方式消耗另一半磅巧克力 (而我才开始消耗巧克力大约2-3小时前). 我开始觉得周围的聚会太多了. 我也感觉有点紧张. 我退休到厨房的角落里, 我坐下来, 缩, 迅速摇摆来回. 我没有想到要检查我的脉或体温是否升高. 很快之后 (我忘记了多久, 因为我在写这篇报道的时间已经过去了几年), 接下来的一天早上, 我感到昏昏欲睡, 头疼. 我也非常不愿意让头痛变得更严重,所以一天中持续了很长时间, 截止日期:91946年 巧克力 (182): 宿醉 / 几天后 (46), 一般 (1), 大组 (10+) (19)

An Overdose No Really Chocolate by sakithin Citation:   sakithin. "An Overdose No Really: An Experience with Chocolate (exp91946)". Erowid.org . Jul 25, 2011. erowid.org/exp/91946 DOSE: oral Chocolate BODY WEIGHT: 135 lb My partner was hosting a party for local green businesses, and one of the businesses being featured at this party was a chocolate trading company. As a result, the whole house was beset with chocolatl, cacao beans, cacao nibs, and various raw chocolate bars. We also have a chocolate fountain, which we like to fire up whenever we have an excuse, so that was loaded dark chocolate. Leading up to this experience, I'd had probably about 4 or 5 (large) cups of (strong) chocolatl, several hands full of beans and nibs, and maybe a bar or two. One of the guests had dropped a cacao bean into the chocolate fountain, and the fountain had jammed until the back pressure from the chocolate being pumped up was enough to force it through the top of the riser. This naturally led all this compressed chocolate to explode all over the room, and being the chocoholic I am, I decided that the only non-wasteful way to clean it up was to wipe it off with my fingers and lick them. I probably consumed another half-pound of chocolate or so in this manner (and I'd only started consuming chocolate about 2-3 hours prior). Partway through this consumption, I started to feel like the party around me was just too much. I also felt a little twitchy. I retired to a corner of the kitchen where I sat down, curled up, and quickly rocked back and forth. I didn't think to check if my pulse or body temperature were elevated. A short while later (I forget how long, since I'm writing this report a few years after the fact), I felt a little more normal and returned to the party, and eventually helped clean up. The following morning I was lethargic and headachey. I was also deeply reluctant to give my headache a little hair of the dog, so it persisted through much of the day, but I was back to normal the following day (including my appetite for chocolate).

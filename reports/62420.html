我只是想添加我的观察关于吃提取强度. 我目前最热的是[删除]但我希望在某个时候得到更热的. 据说可以释放内啡因, 我也同意. 这就是汁的好处: 我已经有了相当糟糕的一天在我的公寓留下了部分作业,它必须在当天交给所以我不得不走到那里回来收集缺失的纸张并把它交给. 这是一个巨大的努力. 我决定在回程中去健身房 (这也是一个很好的自然内啡高), 我把最热的汁加了四滴 (实际上是很多). 这种非常好吃,而且有很愉快的燃烧感, 我说完之后,嘴里有一段时间都着. 之后我只能坐下来检查电子邮件等. 没有什么特别的. 我告诉他一天真的很好, 然而当我想到它我意识到它是平的. 奇怪的是,我感到非常快乐. 我会把这种感觉描述为一种比MDMA的疯狂快乐更清洁,更轻微的感觉. 我在床上喝了一杯酒,读一本书, 所以你就知道了. 作为一种健康,自然和合法的高, 更不用说一个该死的美味的成分. 其他: 素 (421):一般的 (1),单独的 (16)

May 23, 2007. erowid.org/exp/62420 DOSE: 4 drops oral Capsaicin (liquid) BODY WEIGHT: 75 kg I just thought I'd add my observations about eating extract strength sauce. The hottest sauce I have at the moment is [deleted] but I am looking to get a hotter one at some point. Chili allegedly releases endorphins and I'm inclined to agree. So, here's an example of the pleasant effects of chili sauce: I'd been having a pretty poor day having left part of an assignment in my flat, it had to be handed in that day so I had to walk there and back to collect the missing sheet and hand it in. Major effort. I decided to go to the gym on the way back though (also a good natural endorphin high) and afterwards was pretty damn hungry. When I got back to my flat I cooked up some pasta with bolognaise sauce and added in about 4 drops of my hottest chili sauce (which is quite a lot really). It was very tasty and had a very pleasant burn to it which didn’t mask the nice garlic and tomato flavour of the sauce. My mouth felt nice and tingly for a while after I'd finished. So after that all I did was sit and check my email etc. Nothing special. When my flatmate came in and asked how my day had been I told him in all honesty that it had been really good, however when I thought about it I realized it had been mediocre at best. Strange I thought as I was feeling incredibly happy. I'd describe the feeling as a much cleaner and less intense feeling than the crazy happiness of MDMA. I finished my day by having a nice beer and reading a book in bed which was very enjoyable. So there you have it. Chili as a healthy, natural and legal high, not to mention a damn tasty ingredient.

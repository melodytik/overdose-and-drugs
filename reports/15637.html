几个月前, 我梦见我和另外三个人一起喝阿雅瓦斯卡. 在梦中,茶叶是用葡萄酒做成的, 结果是很弱的Chackruna (来自秘鲁供应商). 主持人用烟雾祝福了这个地区, 如果有人觉得需要做音乐, 乐器就摆出来, 如果需要的话, 每个人都有碗. 无论如何,在喝了几小时后, 房间里的气氛非常积极. 艾亚的力量很弱 (也许这是件好事,因为有2个初学者出席). 这不是因为没有经验,而是因为工厂的功率. 在沉默沉思了一会儿之后, 那些曾经服用过它的人同意他们真的能感受到葡萄树的力量, 演唱会的主持人开始唱出美丽而独特的"伊卡洛", 这逐渐成为一个宽松的混音节目 (因为缺乏更好的描述), 我们的声音似乎和, 但同时听起来完全随机. 我觉得在发电厂下, 哨声是最美丽,最温柔的声音. 音乐制作了大约30分钟后, 我自愿接受一些治疗. 我也想体验一下. 我在椅子上放松,闭上眼睛. 几分钟后, 他就像疯狂的蜂鸟一样, 着我的头, 我左手的指开始独立于我的大脑. 我注意到了这一点, 继续观看它, 我开始感觉到胸部有一种热感, 我看着他们, 我开始. 我震惊,就像你整个身体都冷了一样, 实际上,我确实想停止它, 因为震动变得越来越强烈. 我对主持人说'你对我做了什么?!' 我从座位上跳出来告诉他停下来. 我从来没有感觉到身体的感觉抓住我这么突然. 我没有冷,也没有热 (除了我的胸部),但我像一片叶子一样! 没有任何治愈工作. 在接下来的几分钟里, 我继续思考我经历过的事情, 我开始意识到我的呼吸比多年来好得多. 由于葡萄藤和医生, 几天前,真正的考验发生了. 我有一个朋友刚搬家,正在撕破旧地毯. 如果有一件事会让我患上喘, 我可以看到阳光中的尘埃, 其他在场的人正在打喷, 我希望我永远没有喘. 而且,在仪式上没有人知道我有喘... 植物知道你需要什么!!! 我只是想感谢那天晚上所有人以及主持人/治疗师 (当然还有植物) 让我更健康,更快乐, 爱与光! 没有 时间表: 亚亚瓦斯卡 (8),巴尼斯特里奥普西斯·卡阿皮 (169),心理 (170):小组 (2-9) (17),音乐讨论 (22),健康益处 (32)

Healed by Aya! Goodbye Asthma Ayahuasca (B. caapi & P. viridis) by shoebag Citation:   shoebag. "Healed by Aya! Goodbye Asthma: An Experience with Ayahuasca (B. caapi & P. viridis) (exp15637)". Erowid.org . Jun 27, 2002. erowid.org/exp/15637 DOSE: oral Ayahuasca (tea) I have had a fair amount of previous experience with Ayahuasca but the report below has had lasting positive effects. A few months ago I dreamt of drinking Ayahuasca with 3 other people. In the dream, the tea was made from vine & what turned out to be very weak Chackruna (from a Peruvian Supplier.) The host had gone to great lengths to bless the area with tobacco smoke & instruments were laid out if anyone felt the need to make music as well as everyone having bowls if needed. Anyway, a few hours after drinking, the atmpshphere in the room felt extremely positive. The strength of the Aya was weak (maybe this was a good thing as there were 2 first timers present.). This was not due to inexperience of making aya but IMO due to the plant potency. After a while of silent contemplation, those that had taken it before agreed they could really feel the power of the vine but without the light of the Chackruna. The host began to sing beautiful & unique Icaros which were a pleasure to listen to. This gradually became a loose jamming session (for want of a better description) as one by one, those present began to pick up instruments including a bongo & a rattle. Together with the icaros this was a magical moment & the sound we were making seemed to be in harmony with one another but at the same time sounding completely random. Some of us began to whistle and personally I find whistling the most beaufiful & tender sound when under the power plants. After about 30 minutes of making music, I volunteered to have some healing work performed on me. The host has a special gift for healing & I wanted to experience this. For some reason my legs were aching like they have never before and I relaxed in a chair, & closed my eyes. After a few minutes of shaking his hands over my head (like some kind of manic hummingbird!) he moved down to my chest & that's when something strange & wonderful happened. My left thumb began to rise independently of my brain. I noticed this and continued to watch it with some amusement until it stopped rising & looked like I was ready to hitch a lift. Just as I was commenting on this, I began feeling a hot sensation in my chest that can best be described as similar to when you rub Vics vapour on your chest. The hosts hands had stopped over my chest and as I watched them flutter I began to shake. I shook like you would if your whole body was freezing cold - it was completely beyond my control - I couldn't have stopped it if I had wanted to. Actually, I did want to stop it as the shaking was becoming more violent. I said to the host 'what are you doing to me?!' It suddenly got too much! I jumped out of my seat & told him to stop. I have never felt a physical sensation grip me so suddenly. I was neither cold nor hot (apart from my chest) but I was shaking like a leaf! This continued while I was standing with no healing work being done. It gradually subsided over the next few minutes. As the night progressed and the plants left us I continued thinking about what I had experienced as it had blown me away somewhat. The next day, I began to notice my breathing was much better than it had been in years. Over the next week I became convinced my Asthma had disappeared thanks to the vine & the healer. The real test came a few days ago. A friend had just moved house and was ripping up old carpets. If there is one thing that will cause me to have an asthma attack, it is dust. I could see the dust in the sunlight & other people present were sneezing & I was absolutely fine - not even a sneeze or cough! I hope I stay free of asthma for good. Also, no one at the ceremony knew that I had Asthma... The plants know what you need!!! I just wanted to thank everyone there that night as well as the host/healer (& the plants of course) for making me healthier & happier & giving me something I will never forget!

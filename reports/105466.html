瓦勒里安根 一个Unisom片 两杯酒 (共24司) 2杯葡萄酒 (总量约12司) 我同时给了4-AcO-DMT和5-MAPB. 4-AcO-DMT是以粉末形式吞下,并用水冲洗,而5-MAPB则是用凝罩,也用水冲洗. 我记得,时间是下午1:41. 我决定像我许多4-AcO-DMT体验一样, 我更喜欢在外面享受大自然. 我收集了一些东西,并准备好了狗去散步. 我们在下午1点47分左右出发. 我早上7点半才吃了一顿非常轻松的早餐, 我认为我的胃是空的. 我在10分钟内感觉到了4-AcO-DMT. 经过大约20-25次 (对我来说剂量范围只有5毫克,高达50毫克) 使用多个不同的批次, 1) 摄入后大约10-15分钟的初始高兴感 2) 随着高兴的浪潮袭击, 一个人的步伐和一般运动的轻松感也随之而来 3) 放松发生在剂量1mg-15mg,15mg后,我会说放松被警觉所取代,因为来了有更高的响应曲线在更高的剂量 4) 视力敏,有色彩的辨别能力,对象的外观差异化能力 我试过5MAPB几次, 并且总是以相同的50毫克剂量, 结果如下: 1) 大约30分钟后感觉到第一波 2) 快乐和轻微的非个人化. 这种感觉比MDMA更有精神作用, 3) 对颜色的感觉增强 4) 视觉波和扭曲 5) 瞳孔扩大 我将其描述为自我保护, 而不是自我丧失. 我在10分钟内感觉到了4-AcO-DMT. 尽管外面的世界总体色调是灰色的, 我觉得我注意到所有灰色的阴影, 在最初走出我的房子, 我和我的狗走了几个街区. 我们经过一个小公寓楼, 房间里面的家具遍布街上. 在此时刻,我注意到家具之前我注意到两个人在院子里看着我. 我意识到我处理所看到的东西的能力因为缺乏更好的术语而失控. 随着我们走路的结束, 仍然只有4-AcO-DMT的痕迹. 我还想指出, 我真的很喜欢在4-AcO-DMT的出现中发生的转变. 这就像从外向转向内向的开关. 视觉效果开始的同时发生, 到了我们回家的时候, 我决定在YouTube上观看一场音乐会. 我把我最喜欢的恐慌节目. 在75分钟左右. 我感觉到一个巨大的感知转变. 这是我第一次将5-MAPB与任何物质结合起来. 我不得不说,它真的把4-AcO-DMT推向了不同的方向, 这也使得我的体验比我预期的要好得多. 我想要一个非常轻松的体验, 我立即注意到我眼睛的视觉位置非常突出. 常见的情况是我在电视屏幕上看到一个惊慌失措的成员, 当我向右或向左看电视时, 我还注意到电视机周围的颜色会随着音乐的情绪而变化. 这是一个非常好的治疗. 音效也得到了很大的提升. 在精神上,我会说我的头脑跑得很快. 我有很多想法. 主要是关于我的生活, 总的来说是好的想法. 我有一些工作相关的压力. 但这次会议并没有真正解决问题. 我会说5-MAPB为4-AcO-DMT出现的开放因素提供了"盾牌". 我觉得这是一种非常无情的迷幻药, 我比平时更有信心. 由于我的神经感到"有线", 在第一集结束后, 我停止了恐慌表演. 时间是下午3点33分. 我不得不开始准备晚餐. 我曾承诺给未婚妻做拉萨尼亚, 因为她在一个相对艰难的周末后需要舒适的食物. 这很有趣. 我已经做了很多次. 但从来没有在这种情况下. 这次是我第一次做拉萨尼亚, 我要遵循一条食谱, 阅读是一项挑战. 我iPhone上的指令闪,移动,字母变大. 我很难阅读. 我发现自己不得不多次验证步骤和程序. 听起来比平时更难. 现在是时候等待它一点. 我拿了第二个酒. 我把"恐惧与厌恶"看上去, 在"恐惧与厌恶"的30分钟内, 时间是4点半. 我决定检查学生, 这并不奇怪. 我的眼睛非常大. 我还在经历的高峰期. 我也经历了比最初报道的更大的自我损失. 我觉得自己很分化. 我对旅行感到满意. 完全沉浸等同于沉醉的严重程度 (DPT和MXE出现在我的脑海中, 除了幻觉之外, 我开始有点清醒了. 我也服用了1克草根. 到了下午5点, 我开始着陆, 我的未婚夫在下午5:30左右回家. 我们聊了一会儿. 她去见一个朋友进行严的讨论, 我发现沟通是很容易的, 我们晚上6点左右吃饭. 味道得到了增强. 我对这顿饭感到满意. 我们享受了几杯葡萄酒,并谈了一些更多. 晚上的其余时间相当平静. 晚上9点左右, 我又吃了一克草根. 晚上9点15分左右, 我给他注射了一片Unisom作为着陆的最后阶段. 我会把我的睡眠归类为有些不安. 我感觉很好. 今天早上我的大脑有点"乱". 但是没有化学宿醉的存在. 我早上6点起床. 我喝了两杯咖啡, 完成了我平常的早晨活动. 我会重复这个组合. 或许可以增加4-AcO-DMT的数量5mg-6mg. 我认为这可能是一个很好的组合在这个范围内. 我还想将5-MAPB与lysergamide混合在一起. 其他: 4-AcO-DMT (387),5-MAPB (624):组合 (3),单独使用 (16)

My other reports detail my background. 30 year old male. weight 245lbs, age 30, height six feet two inches This journey took place after a previous journey about 30 days ago on 4-AcO-DMT. I use etizolam semi-regularly. However, its been about two weeks since I last consumed it. Previous night I had two drinks out before going home and going to bed around 11pm. For the experience, I was well rested, and overall in a good place mentally. Consumption Details: 9.15mg 4-AcO-DMT 50mg 5-MAPB 2.2 grams Valerian Root 1 tablet Unisom 2 beers (24 total ounces) 2 glasses wine (~12 total ounces) I administered the 4-AcO-DMT and 5-MAPB at the same time. The 4-AcO-DMT was swallowed in powder form, and washed down with water, and the 5-MAPB was in a gelcap, also washed down with water. I made note, it was 1:41pm. I decided that like with many of my 4-AcO-DMT experiences, I prefer to come up outside and enjoy nature. I gathered some things, and got the dog ready to go for a walk. We departed around 1:47pm. I had only eaten a very light breakfast of granola around 7:30am, and then a small sandwich around 11:30am. So I would categorize my stomach as being more on the empty side. I felt the 4-AcO-DMT within 10 minutes. Which after about 20-25 trips (Dose range for me being as little as 5mg, and as high as 50mg) on it utilizing several different batches, I would characterize the come up as almost always occurring in the same manner: 1) initial euphoria hitting about 10-15 minutes after ingestion 2) as the wave of euphoria hits, so does a feeling of lightness in one's step and general movements 3) relaxation occurs in doses 1mg-15mg, after 15mg, I would say the relaxation is replaced by alertness, as the come up has a much higher response curve in higher doses 4) visual acuity and a discerning presence of color and differentiation in appearance of objects I have tried 5-MAPB several times, and always at the same dose of 50mg, as I find that to be the sweet spot for me. The come up happens as follows: 1) first wave felt after about 30 minutes 2) euphoria and slight depersonalization set in somewhat quickly. It's much more psychedelic feeling than MDMA, which I prefer it over MDMA these days 3) heightened sense of colors 4) visuals waves and distortions 5) pupil dilation 6) feelings of empathy and connection, and what I would characterize as ego shielding, rather than ego loss So I felt the 4-AcO-DMT within 10 minutes. The weather was overcast, but I could distinguish a very noticeable difference in colors despite the overall tone of the outside world being grey. I felt like I was noticing all the shades of grey that upon initially walking out of my house I didn't notice. My dog and I walked several blocks around our neighborhood. We passed a small apartment complex, in which it looked like a tenant was evicted at some point. There was furniture all over the street. At this juncture, I noticed the furniture way before I noticed two guys on the patio watching me. It was at this time, I realized my ability to process the things I was seeing was out of focus for lack of a better term. The come up continued as we finished our walk. Still only signs of the 4-AcO-DMT. I would also like to note, I really enjoy the shift that occurs during the come up of 4-AcO-DMT. It's like a switch is flipped from being extroverted to introverted. And it happens the same time the visuals start, which is usually around 30-40 minutes after ingestion. By the time we had returned to our house, I was at +42 minutes since ingestion. I decided to watch a concert on youtube. I put on a favorite panic show of mine. at around 75 minutes. I felt an immense shift of perception. This was my first time combining 5-MAPB with any substance. I have to say, it really kicked the 4-AcO-DMT into a different direction in a very good way. It also amplified the experience way more than I expected. My goal was to have a very light experience, with an estimated duration of 4 hours. Immediately I noticed that I was having very prominent open eyed visuals. A common one would be I would see a member of panic on my TV screen and upon looking to the right or left of my television they would appear in a multicolored holographic form, and this would linger until I blinked. I also noticed that the area around the TV was subject to color shifts that fit the mood of the music. This was a very nice treat. Sound was very much enhanced too. Mentally, I would say my mind raced a good bit. I was having a lot of thoughts. Mostly about my life, and where I am right now. Overall good thoughts. I have some work related stress. But this session didn't really work through it. I will say the 5-MAPB provided a 'shield' to the open factor that occurs with 4-AcO-DMT. Which I feel can be a very unforgiving psychedelic if you get to a dark place in your mind. I felt more confident than I usually do. Around 3pm, I cracked a beer, as my nerves felt 'wired,' which is a normal feeling for me on Tryptamines. I cut the panic show off after the first set ended. It was 3:33pm. I had to begin to prepare dinner. I had promised to make my fiance lasagna since she was in need of comfort food after a relatively rough weekend. This was interesting. I've cooked tripping many times. But never in this situation. This was my first time making lasagna, and I was going to follow a recipe, versus my usual cooking style which is very much improvised, but that also comes from having made a lot of the regular meals I prepare, often. Reading was a challenge. The instructions on my iphone, glistened, shifted, letters changing sizes. I had a pretty tough time reading. I found myself having to verify steps and procedures multiple times. Comprehension was more difficult than usual. I managed to get the meat sauce prepared, and it was now time to wait for it to simmer for a bit. I grabbed a second beer. I put on Fear and Loathing just to have something on, as the silence to the house became kinda eerie for me. About 30 minutes into Fear and Loathing, and it was 4:30. I decided to do a pupil check, and not surprising. My eyes were extremely dilated. I was also still heavy into the peak of the experience. I had also experienced more ego loss than initially reported. I felt very segmented. I was content with the trip. Unlike other experiences, where full immersion equates to heavy levels of inebriation too (DPT and MXE comes to mind, or 4-AcO-DMT and MXE), I was fully functioning. Other than hallucinations, I began to reach a somewhat clear headspace. Around this time, I also took 1 gram of Valerian Root. By 5pm, I felt the initial effects of it as I was starting my process to land. My fiance arrived home around 5:30pm. We talked for a bit. She had gone to meet a friend to have a serious discussion, and it went well. I found communication to be very easy, with the slight exception that I did find a few times my mind got ahead of my mouth in terms of speaking. We ate around 6pm. Taste was enhanced. I was pleased with the meal. We enjoyed a few glasses of wine and talked some more. The rest of the evening was rather uneventful. Around 9pm, I took another gram of valerian root. Around 9:15pm, I administered a tablet of unisom as the final phase of the landing gear. I would categorize my sleep as somewhat restless. I feel good though. My brain is slightly 'scrambled' this morning. But there isn't a chemical hangover present. I was up at 6am. I had two cups of coffee, and got all of my usual morning routine done. I would repeat the combination. Perhaps upping the 4-AcO-DMT quantity by 5mg-6mg. I think that could be a very good combination in that range. I'm also interested to mix 5-MAPB with a lysergamide.

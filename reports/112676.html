There was a nice reset/step back from day to day worries that was partially brought on from the oxycodone but I know was more significant as a result of combining the substance with the floatation experience. I likely will repeat this composite of setting and substance in the future but I admit this substance is just as fun on the couch. It did not feel significantly more therapeutic or enjoyable in a tank than it is for me outside of one.

准备的药丸, 他们在哪里切成两半, 然后涂层被手工切开. 使用拉机是因为药丸不会很容易使用其他方法. 需要额外的粉碎才能把它变成粉末. 在所有情况下都引起了鼻子强烈的灼伤, 烧伤变成了一种麻木的感觉, 尽管有着燃烧的感觉, 我没有注意到有什么影响, 直到我的电话响了, 我开始和我的朋友聊天. 我手机的响声让我的耳朵有点疼痛. 走到外面,听到外面的孩子们和朋友在打电话, 这种感觉更像是无法保持静止, 我发现自己对他们想和我谈论的一切很感兴趣, 但却无法专注于他们的声音, 我转过身,继续穿过路, 到朋友家. 这位朋友从现在起将被称为A, 我在她家见过她, 我们立即回到外面去找另一个朋友, 我们在外面等待时抽了一支烟, 虽然服用药丸已经改变了它的味道, 在S的下落. 我们忙进去. 两种药物均含有150毫克. 我们都开始比平常多聊天了. 在第一剂后45分钟, 我们又回去注入每人150毫克. 这时我感觉到了高潮, 但它也像第一次一样燃烧. 这种高潮就像类似于白色的可恶线. 基本效果是说话,心率和血压升高. 眼睛一直流水, 我开始经常打喷. 整整第二次注射后的时间, 我一直在打喷, 服用药物后30分钟, 鼻子开始流水. 我吹了鼻子, 我们决定再去服用第三剂, 服用150毫克后, 我感觉到了类似可乐的高兴感, 我开始对眼角所看到的东西有着色彩的遮蔽. 这一剂量后, 混消失了, 事情再次变得清晰. 这种效应类似于可卡因第一次给我的觉醒, 停留并慢慢消失, 这不是一个好旅程,但它不是坏的. 眼睛关闭让我看到鲜明的视觉效果. 我仍然很不安, 不相信我会很快睡觉. 其他: 药品 - 布 (87):一般 (1), 其他 (28)

Sep 10, 2008. erowid.org/exp/43733 DOSE: T+ 0:00 150 mg insufflated Pharms - Bupropion (ground / crushed) T+ 0:45 150 mg insufflated Pharms - Bupropion (ground / crushed) T+ 1:15 150 mg insufflated Pharms - Bupropion (ground / crushed) BODY WEIGHT: 160 lb Preparing the pills they where cut in half then the coating was chipped away by hand. Pull crusher was used because pills would not crush easily using other methods. Additional crushing was needed to get it into a powder. During insufflating in all instances it caused a strong burning in my nose, similar to meth. That burning turned into a numbing sensation with burning mostly in the back of my throat. At this point the effects started mostly of euphoria despite the burning. I did not notice much of an effect until my phone rang, and I started talking to my friend. The ringing of my cell phone pierced my ears slightly painful. Walking outside the sound of the kids outside and my friend on the phone it became harder to concentrate and the anxiety kicked in. This is more of a feeling of just not being able to stand still more than anything, fidgeting with everything. At this point I made my way over to my friend's place, running into the neighborhood kids, I found my self interested in everything they wanted to talk to me about but it couldn’t concentrate on one of their voices long enough to respond. So I turned and continued across the way to my friend's place. This friend from here on will be called A, I met at her house and we immediately went back outside to get another friend she will be referred to as S. Waiting outside we smoked a cigarette, Although taking the pill had changed the taste of this to a more bland flavor but not an unwanted flavor. S was dropped off. And we hurried inside. A & S both had 150mg. We all began to talk a lot more than usual. 45 min after my first dose we went back and insufflated 150mg each. This is when the feeling of a high came but it also burnt as with the first time. The high was like similar to crappy line of white. After the basic effects of this plus the talking and slightly faster and raised heart rate and blood pressure. I had managed to get hold of the high and concentrate on a video game for about 20 min., during this time my eyes continued to water and I began to sneeze a lot. For the entire time after the second dose I was sneezing and my eyes where watering. As my nose became runny about 30 min after dosing. I blew my nose and we all decided to go back and take a third dose as the effects seem to have changed every time we took more. 150mg later I felt euphoria similar to coke but with a strange twist. I had begun to have a color drape over what I saw in the corner of my eyes. The confusion subsided after this dose and things became clear again. The effects similar to the awakening that coke gives me when it first hits, stay and slowly goes away keeping me awake and alert. It wasn’t a good trip but it wasn’t bad. At this point the visuals of colors have moved into some of my normal visual range enhanced by the darkness, closing my eyes give me vivid visuals. I still am very fidgety and don’t believe I will be going to sleep anytime soon.

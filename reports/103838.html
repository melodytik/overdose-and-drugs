Nov 5, 2015. erowid.org/exp/103838 DOSE: T+ 0:00 1 hit oral LSZ (blotter / tab) T+ 0:47 .5 joints/cigs smoked Cannabis BODY WEIGHT: 130 lb This was my second experiment with LSZ , my first being a week before this one. LSZ is an interesting compound with one isomer of it being more potent then LSD. It has other isomers that are active unlike LSD. 9:00am I drop 1 blotter on my tongue and wait. I observe the blotter tastes like ink slightly but mainly like paper. 9:05am I have held it on my tongue for 5 minutes now, with no numbness present which would indicate the substance on the blotter is not a NBOME. 9:15am I decide to swallow the blotter. 9:30am It hasn’t hit me yet but last time I took LSZ it didn’t take action till about an hour after taking it. 9:45am I decide to roll a joint feeling something from the LSZ but it’s very subtle. 9:47am Woah, after having a few puffs my HPPD suddenly increased, odd because it doesn’t increase from just cannabis alone anymore. 9:50am I put the joint out so I can save the rest for later feeling like I am coming up now. euphoria is noted and is steadily increasing. Small changes in visual field has started and sound distortion is occurring. 10:04am I looked at my pupils in the mirror no dilation as of yet. 10:19am Definitely starting to feel the serotonin activity but still no pupil dilation. 10:24am I decide to put on music while coming up the visual phenomena is starting to increase slowly. 10:31am I notice my hands are shaking from anxiety. This exactly like the last time I did LSZ as I got a lot of similar effects to compounds that mainly increase dopamine. 10:45am Mucus and saliva production has increased. I look at my floor, the carpet is moving around in patterns. Headache is noted. 11:00am I start peaking the anxiety is very strong, I might end up taking ativan 1mg to calm myself down. 11:55am after laying down and getting thru the hellish come-up I start feeling “OK” again. Stomach discomfort is noted and is getting annoying. 12:47pm I looked at my pupils and they were slightly dilated not as dilated as LSD dilates them. It was similar to how much methylphenidate dilates my pupils which isn’t a lot but noticeable. Visuals are apparent and are similar to a 100 mic dose of LSD. They were weaker then my last experiment. Unknown time: I spent the next few hours laying on my bed thinking, relaxing and enjoying the psychedelia. 3:15pm I feel like I am starting to come down. 5:31pm I am sober now. I would rate this experience as being a ++ on Shulgin’s rating scale. My last experiment was a +++ at 300 micrograms. This was a good experience worth repeating but the come-up of LSZ has been hellishly anxious for me and slow. The stomach discomfort was more apparent this experiment. I have given friends this substance and they reported no anxiety at all during their experiences so that is an individual reaction. I find LSZ is something to repeat and is enjoyable but I don’t think it will carry on much like how LSD did.

Jun 7, 2026. erowid.org/exp/101774 DOSE: 1 hit smoked Cannabis - High THC BODY WEIGHT: 120 lb Up until recently, I had not had very much experience with weed, but as a first year in college I figured it was my time to try on a few new hats and have some fun while I was at it. All was going well, until one night of smoking triggered one of the worst experiences I've ever had. I am a somewhat anxious person, but typically have no problem keeping my shit together even when I smoke. I have smoked somewhere between 15-20 times, and only gotten a panic attack once during the beginning. After that first panic attack I took it easy and never got stoned out of my mind again, and all was good. I knew some of the issues weed brings on after consistent use, but I never expected to wake up one morning and see something manifested so quickly. It all started on a Monday during a break, when I naturally had very little interest in doing anything productive. So me and a bud decided to go out to a nice field (around noon) and smoke some of the weed I bought off of a friend a while ago. After about half a bowl between the two of us, which got us both comfortably high, we went to watch a movie, and all was fine. A few hours later, we were both back to normal. Later in the evening, I went with some friends to a campfire looking for a good time, and decided to see if I could find someone to mooch off of. Luckily (or perhaps not), a different friend came up and offered to share his stuff with me. We were some distance from the fire, so I couldn’t see all that well, but the weed appeared to look normal as I helped him pack it. The bowl then began its trip around the circle, eventually reaching my lips. I took my hit and held it in for a long while – I remember exhaling and not seeing any smoke. After I exhaled I could instantly feel the sheer power of what I had just inhaled. My lungs already felt weird and unpleasant, which was unusual for just one hit. Coughing, it felt like I had a really deep queasy feeling in my lungs. The bowl had made its way back to me by this point but I passed, thank god, wondering why the effects were so apparent already. I asked my friend what exactly how powerful this pot was, and he said it was 45% potency. Already pretty far off, I forgot to exclaim my utter astonishment. With the knowledge that I was probably in for more than I wanted, I walked over to a log near the fire and hunkered in. My memory of the rest of the rest of that night isn’t all that clear. I remember coming very near to a panic attack, but being able to avoid it. Things all seemed very trippy; this one hit had likely gotten me more stoned than I had ever gotten before. I came down some by the time I left the campfire, and somewhat remember having a decent time the rest of the night, eventually watching half a movie and going to bed. The next day (Tuesday) I woke up sort of weed-over, which I tend to have sometimes after getting stoned. This feeling persisted the whole day. The friend I had smoked my own pot with in the afternoon was fine and functioning. I bummed around for the rest of the day, finished a paper, and went to bed. Wednesday, I woke up still feeling a little off, but not horrible. My day was pretty busy as classes has just begun again, but I got through it alright. I felt distinctly different all day. I asked my campfire friend about the weed again, and he replied that it was not laced and had 45% potency, again. I remember feeling light headed and dizzy at times during that day, but it didn’t bother me as much as what came next. Thursday I woke up and felt like shit. Things certainly didn’t seem right anymore, and it wasn’t so easy to bear. I remember wondering if I was dreaming. I felt detached and unreal. Its a hard feeling to describe, but it was one of the worst, hellish feelings of my life. My mind felt numb, and I just didn’t feel like I was actually there; things didn’t look right, it was almost like I was in a really emotionless movie. I had MAJOR anxiety, and had semi-frequent panic attacks through the experience. I couldn’t concentrate in class so I left because I wasn’t getting anything done, and instead went to make a doctors appointment. I think the doctor had heard of this before, but didn’t really know anything that could be done but to wait and see if I felt better. The feeling of derealization persisted until Sunday night. I spent the next two or so weeks feeling overly anxious and unstable, but generally felt on the mend. I did some research on the matter and found that I may have triggered a disorder called depersonalization / derealization. I'm a pretty lucky person: my episode only lasted about 6 days in its worst my episode only lasted about 6 days in its worst , but I've read stuff about this lasting years for some people. As one who didn’t know how to deal with it at the time, I can tell you its hell. I'm writing this about a month and a half after, and I still have some weird issues – I have not had any pot since the incident either. My anxiety is way more noticeable than it used to be, although I don’t get panic attacks anymore. I'm very sensitive to moods and mind altering substances such as alcohol. I also feel very strange right before falling asleep. Weed probably isn’t for me, which may or may not be due to my anxiety. This had never happened before all the other times I had smoked, and it sure isn’t going to happen again. I've found one of the signs that I'm better and normal is the desire to smoke weed again, and actually contemplating doing it sometime. Luckily, I have recently decided to read a bit more on depersonalization / derealization, and see a movie named Numb which deals with this issue, and realized that I have no wish to go through this experience again.

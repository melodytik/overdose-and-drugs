中文译文 致幻剂

在我吃了5-MeO-AMT之前, 这不是我自己选择的, 而是由于周围缺乏幻觉剂. 我已经吃了LSD2或3次一周稳定一段时间, 当我听到我的朋友J发现了5-MeO-AMT的消息时, 我为自己和我的室友S和D (每个1个) 买了3个剂量. 过去一个月我遇到了一些困难, 至少我更喜欢的现实. 我和S吃了5MeO-AMT的凝盖,然后给他们放上一些Floyd. 我们还把"爱丽丝在奇迹国"放进去, 并开始喝S买的12包酒. 由于某种原因, 看这部电影总是让我变得更容易. 我想是因为这太荒谬了. 他还要去看戏, 想回来后吃掉帽子. 现在我已经感觉到了. 子里有一种奇怪的感觉, 我试着站起来走路, 却发现我的腿几乎太重了. 只是我在脸前挥手时, 看到一些轻微的痕迹. 爱丽丝在奇迹国对我来说并没有太大影响, 这5MeO-AMT打我像一的. 我看到的是完整的视觉效果, 这时我决定要气. 我从来没有过到这个地步, 虽然我确实得很强, 我知道驾驶时跌倒是个坏主意, 但对我来说这几乎就像是用我的头脑来画画线. [Erowid的注意事项: 驾驶时喝醉,倒,或极度缺乏睡眠是危险和不负责任的, 因为它危害了其他人. 不要这样做! 这种东西感觉很像酸除了来起来和更强的身体声. 我认为这次旅程在和酸之间, 就身体和视觉而言. 唯一缺少的就是我在其他物质上倒时, 这就是我最喜欢的酸性, 因为它让一切变得如此荒谬和搞笑, 我还没有任何内省的感觉或个人启示. 实际上,我可以说我的思想完全一致. 没有奇怪的思维模式或任何类似的, 谈话很容易. 几乎比大麻更容易处理, 除了你强烈的幻觉. 我们把弗洛伊德的"动物"放上去 (顺便说一下,这是一张很棒的专辑), 这就是我的视觉效果变得荒谬的地方. 我坐在厨房的桌子上看着我们洗手间的窗, 窗和墙壁呈现出一种奇怪的紫色和红色. 我知道这是因为弗洛伊德, 因为随着音符的变化, 完全的麻醉. 现在D来了. 他吃了他的剂量, 但这时我们已经用尽了气. 我问他是否想去买更多, 他做了所以我们堆在他的车里, 现在我已经很难开车了. 我已经达到高峰很长一段时间似乎,但它真的已经在一个小时. 我们又发现了两个案例. 我们回到了家里, 开始用气. 我吃了一棵葡萄,很好吃. 接下来的几个小时, 没有什么不寻常的事情发生了除了我看到的东西. 我们几乎杀了所有的气. 我已经决定,气是光荣的当,当我从现在开始,我会确保至少有一个案例便捷. 它比清醒时能留在你的脑海里更长时间, 我现在已经下来了,但我还在. 记得我们还剩下10倍的香草, 我从来没有吃过, 说实话, 沙维亚本身就足够强烈, 和结合它与似乎是一个可怕的想法. 经过一点同行压力S和D说服我去打. 我坐在沙发上打了一次. 迪建议我保持沉默, 但我认为这是一个坏主意. 现在回想起来,我希望我有. 在受伤后,事情失控了. 似乎我从20英尺远的房间里看到. 房间的焦点又回到了焦点. 我不知道自己在哪里,我是谁,甚至不知道自己生活在什么宇宙里. 我以为我已经进入了另一个维度. 我站起来 (很糟糕的想法), 感觉整个房间扭转了45度. 我开始,试图理解这一切. 我以为他是在指导我如何回到我本来的房间. 他的笑声成为我视觉的一部分. 这真的无法解释. 终于,其中一个叫我坐在沙发上, 这真是太棒了. 之前我说过我希望自己在沉默中抽了香草. 我觉得音乐是扭曲了房间的原因. 我觉得自己融入了音乐. 我失去了自我意识, 陷入幻觉之中. 我觉得如果我在沉默中吸, 没有任何外部刺激, 迪说他把自己的灵魂投射到整个宇宙. 我想尝试这个. 我肯定会这样做, 也许甚至在我高峰的时候. 也使我在某个时候达到高峰, 并留在我身边一段时间, 这真是太棒了. 我开始喝酒,因为我认为自己很快就会昏倒. 德克萨斯的步行者刚刚出现在电视上, 我看了一会儿, 尽管我几乎完全昏, 我仍然很难理解这一集, 总的来说,我会说5-MeO-Amt绝对是一个值得的物质. 虽然它在视觉上与酸相似, 但它有很好的身体声, 它也非常易于处理. 这太容易了,我会给一个婴儿吃. 我只是在开玩笑. - - 萨卡朱维达 其他: 2008年3月12日发布

Out of Control 5-MeO-AMT, Nitrous Oxide & Salvia divinorum by Sackajuweeda Citation:   Sackajuweeda. "Out of Control: An Experience with 5-MeO-AMT, Nitrous Oxide & Salvia divinorum (exp51805)". Erowid.org . Mar 12, 2008. erowid.org/exp/51805 DOSE: 1 hit oral 5-MeO-AMT 1 repeated inhaled Nitrous Oxide (gas) 1 repeated oral Alcohol - Beer/Wine 1 hit smoked Salvia divinorum (extract - 10x) BODY WEIGHT: 160 lb Until I ate the 5-MeO-AMT it had been 3 months since my last trip. This wasn't by choice, rather, it was due to the lack of hallucinogens around. I had been eating LSD 2 or 3 times a week for a steady while, and was greatly disappointed when I lost the hookup. When I heard the news that my friend J had found some 5-MeO-AMT, I felt the excitement that only comes to me when I know I'm going to be tripping nuts shortly. I picked up 3 doses for myself and my roommates S and D(1 each). In the past month I've had some difficulties stemming from a situation that has made me stop smoking weed, so I felt the need to get in touch with 'reality'. The reality that I prefer at least. T: 0:00 S and I eat the gel cap with the 5-MeO-AMT and put on some Floyd. We also put in Alice in Wonderland and began drinking on the 12 pack S had bought. For some reason watching that movie always makes my come-up a little easier. I guess because it's just so ridiculous. D had a play to go to a little bit later so he was going to eat the cap when he got back. T: 0:30 J had informed me that the comeup was pretty intense, and by now I'm feeling it. I was getting pretty bad leg cramps and an odd sensation in my neck similar to acid but not quite the same. I tried getting up and walking but I find my legs are almost to heavy to move. T: 1:00 Still no visuals yet, other than some slight tracers when I wave my hand in front of my face. Alice in Wonderland isn't doing much for me, but the music had become noticeably more intense. T: 1:20 The 5-MeO-AMT hit me like a ton of bricks. I was having full fledged visuals i.e breathing walls, liquid ceilings, visual distortions and so on... It was then that I decided I wanted some nitrous. I had never had nitrous while tripping up to this point and while I was definitly tripping hard, I wanted it to be more intense. T: 1:40 S and I arrive at the local head shop without incident and I scoop up a case of nitrous. I know driving while tripping is a bad idea, but for me it's almost like coloring inside the lines just using my mind if that makes any sense. [Erowid Note: Driving while intoxicated, tripping, or extremely sleep deprived is dangerous and irresponsible because it endangers other people. Don't do it! ] T: 2:00 This stuff feels alot like acid except for the come-up and the stronger body buzz. I'd place the trip in between mushrooms and acid in terms of the body buzz and visuals. The only thing it's missing is the looped out feeling I get in my mind when I trip on other substances. That is one of my favorite things about acid, as it just makes everything so ridiculous and hilarious that it's impossible to have a bad time. I still have yet to have any sort of introspective feelings or personal revelations. In fact, I would say I was completly coherent in my mind. No wierd thought patterns or anything like that and conversation was easy. It's almost easier to handle than weed, other than the fact that your hallucinating intensly. S put on Floyd's Animals (which is a glorious album by the way) and we dig into the nitrous. This is where my visuals became ridiculous. I was sitting at our kitchen table looking at the curtains above our sink as I took my first balloon. It was a double and after inhaling the balloon I was sent into outer space. I saw a circle of heiroglyphics spiraling around my entire visual field and the curtains and wall had taken on a wierd purple and red color. I knew it was because of the floyd because as the notes changed so did the colors. Full-blown synaesthesia. T: 3:00 D arrives. He eats his dose, but by this time we had run out of nitrous. I asked him if he wanted to go get some more, which he did so we piled in his car and drove back to the head shop. By now I was definatly tripping to hard to be driving. I had been peaking for a long time it seemed like, but it had really on been an hour. We picked up 2 more cases. T: 3:30 We arrive back at the house, and get started on the nitrous. I ate a grapefruit and it was delicious. T: 7:00 For the next few hours, nothing out of the ordinary happened other than the fact that I was seeing shit. By this time we had pretty much killed all the nitrous. I've decided that nitrous is glorious while tripping and when I trip from now I'll make sure to at least have one case handy. It stays with you alot longer than taking it sober, and it definitly made my visuals more intense. T: 8:00 I've come down quite a bit by now, but I'm still tripping. S remembers that we had a little 10x salvia left and pulls it to out to smoke. I had never had salvia while tripping before, and to be honest I was afraid. Salvia is intense enough by itself, and combining it with tripping seemed like a terrible idea. After a little peer pressure S and D convinced me to take a hit. S packed up a bong hit for me and I sat down on my couch and hit it. D recommended I do it in silence, but I thought this was a bad idea. In retrospect I wish I had. After taking the hit things went out of control. I faded back into the couch and it seemed as if I was viewing the room from 20 feet away. But just as quickly as that had happened the room was back in focus. I had no idea where I was, who I was, or even what universe I was in. I thought I had ended up in a different dimension. D had turned the lights back on and as he did that I stood up (bad idea) and got the feeling that the whole room had twisted 45 degrees. I began stumbling around trying to make sense of it all. At this point D began to say alot of crazy things like 'your forgetting the sauce for the doorknob' but I thought he was giving me instructions on how to get back to the real room where I belonged. S couldn't stop laughing and his laugh became part of my visuals. It's impossible to explain really. Eventually, one of them told me to sit back on the couch and as soon I did everything felt right again. It was glorious. Earlier, I said that I wished I had smoked the salvia in silence. This was because I felt the music had been what distorted the room. I felt like I had dissolved into the music. I lost sense of my self and had become enveloped in the hallucination. I felt that if I had smoked the salvia in silence without any external stimuli there would be only one place to go... deeper into my own mind. D said he's projected his soul across the universe doing this. I would like to try this. The next time I trip I'm definitly going to try it that way, perhaps even while I'm peaking hard. The salvia also brought me back to a peak for a while and stayed with me a while, similar to the nitrous. It was glorious. T: 9:00-11:00 During this time I began drinking heavily in anticipation of passing out soon. Walker Texas Ranger had just come on T.V so I watched that for a while and then passed out. I could still hardly make sense of the episode even though I had almost come down completly, but I have difficulty making sense of that show even when sober. Overall, I would say 5-MeO-Amt is definitly a worthwhile substance. It's similar to acid in terms of visuals, but with a good body buzz though that acid seems to lack. It's also extremely easy to handle. It's so easy I'd feed it to a baby. I kid. -Sackajuweeda Exp Year: 2006 ExpID: 51805 Gender: Male Age at time of experience: Not Given Published: Mar 12, 2008 Views: Not Supported [ View PDF (to print) ] [ View LaTeX (for geeks) ] [ Swap Dark/Light ] 5-MeO-AMT (104), Nitrous Oxide (40), Salvia divinorum (44) : Combinations (3), Glowing Experiences (4), Small Group (2-9) (17) COPYRIGHTS: All reports copyright Erowid.

我不时地服用沙尔维亚, 我试图阅读尽可能多的东西. 我是美国原住民, 我认为S. 是"神圣的草药", 我只在仪式上使用, 通常是单独使用, 我通常会抽烟. 我开始向六个方向祈祷, 祈求保护和指导, 我也使用或其他草药抹时祈祷四风, 我还在吸烟前向S. 叶提供烟草, 我在准备时使用, 但一旦我开始吸烟, 虽然我的经历不同, 但总的来说有些相似之处. 有时伴随着转化为植物或其他物体的感觉. 对于视觉, 在开始时, 我经常得到螺旋... 通常绿色的品种与红色的突出或内部设计. 接下来发生的事情是不同的. 有时我看到和遇到不同的实体, 有时我体验自己作为各种事物或实体. 有时我会在不同的地方和环境中完成'旅程'. 有时我觉得我们所知道的宇宙只是一种幻觉, 也许你可以把这种感觉/感知称为末日. 有时我会收到非常明确的指令, 我常常觉得我注定要认识这个植物, 我经常会看到我称之为'别人的生活'的幻象... 我只在夜晚和黑暗中和她一起走. 我注意到之后, 通常我的视力会改善, 我发现烟草让我感觉很好, 让我有时间思考我看到/经历过的一些事情. 其他: 仪式 (129), 神圣的 (44): 独自一人 (16), 神秘的经验 (9), 准备/食谱 (30), 回顾/总结 (11)

Jul 13, 2001. erowid.org/exp/8070 DOSE: smoked Salvia divinorum (leaves) BODY WEIGHT: 180 lb I sporadically do Salvia...and have been doing so for about a year. Prior to using, I tried to read as much as I could. I'm native american, and consider S. to be a 'sacred herb' and treat her as such. I only use in a ceremonial context...generally alone, though on occasion with others. Usually, I smoke the leaf. First, I do a prayer to the six directions and ask both for protection and instruction, while singing and playing a gourd rattle. I also use a copal or other herbal smudge while praying the four winds, and while doing the plant. I also offer tobacco to the S. leaves before smoking, and blow smoke on them. I use a candle while getting ready, but once I start to smoke, I blow the candle out...and generally I have my eyes closed while sitting on the ground or floor for the 'walk.' While my experiences differ, there are generally some similarities. I often experience a twisting feeling, and sometimes this is accompanied by feelings of transformation into a plant, or other object. As for visuals, at the onset, I often get spirals...usually of a green variety with red highlights or interior designs. What happens next is what varies. Sometimes I see and meet different entities, and sometimes I experience myself as various things or entities. Sometimes I have full on 'journies' in which I am in various different places and settings. Sometimes I get the feeling that the universe as we generally know it is just an illusion and the end of this world is here... Maybe you could call this feeling/perception kind of apocalyptic. Sometimes I get very clear instructions about things...teachings. Often I feel like I have been destined to get to know this plant...and that circumstances have brought me to her... I not infrequently get visions of what I'd call 'other peoples lives'... I have only walked with her at night and in the dark. I notice that afterwards, usually my vision improves and stays quite sharp for a while longer than the primary effects last. I usually smoke tobacco and pray when finished, and I find the tobacco grounds me pretty good and gives me time to think about some of the things I've seen/experienced.

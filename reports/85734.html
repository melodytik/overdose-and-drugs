萨萨弗拉斯树是相当庄严的, 可以得到相当大的, 但直到最近我不知道一些生长在几英里内我的前门. 根被认为是萨萨弗拉斯茶的传统和首选部分. 我总是喜欢它的香气, 喜欢从大自然得到我的草药, 而不是互联网, 所以我获得了相当数量的粉红色的根在美国东南部生长, 医用植物在此壮成长. 这也是从一个成熟的,本地树,已经收获了它的 (土地的) 业主. 根的木材相当硬,纤维密度很高,如果使用根,可以多次重复使用一大块. 我决定做一个特别强烈的灌注, 沸水大量 (也许一升或更多), 从火中取出, 并放下一些根块. 我等待它出现粉红色红色. (它也可以在水中煮沸,但这应该做得更简短). 在那个点,注入液中显然有油或. 倒出大约16司,添加一些糖,和喝它后,它稍微冷却. 效果是很快的:一种柔和的效果,清醒但绝对放松, 我打电话给某人, 告诉他们这种美妙的桑草, 我已经忘了它是这样一个美好的植物, 影响持续了几个小时. 应该再次说明这是红色的根, 而不是树皮, 空气部分或叶子. 使用空气部件 (树干或树枝木) 的其他尝试令人失望,部分原因是这些部件的药用价值较低,而且这些部件已经氧化并且较老,绝对不是红色和纤维,也不是芳香. 其他: 萨萨弗拉斯 (390):准备/食谱 (30),一般 (1),未知背景 (20)

Jan 5, 2017. erowid.org/exp/85734 DOSE: oral Sassafras (tea) BODY WEIGHT: 135 lb Sassafras trees are rather stately and can get quite large, but until recently I had no idea some grew within a few miles of my front door. My experiences were with root, considered the traditional and preferred part for sassafras 'tea'. There are commercially-available extracts that are the real thing, but with the safrole removed (and the aroma, apparently), and I always loved its aroma and prefer to get my herbs from Nature, not the Internet, so I obtained a decent quantity of pinkish-red colored root that grew in the US Southeast, far from my own region and an area (the SE, that is) where medicinal plants flourish amazingly well. This was also from a mature, native tree that had been harvested by its (the land area's) owner. The wood of the root is quite hard and densely fibrous, and if using root, a large piece may be reused a number of times for infusions. One one occasion, I decided to make a particularly strong infusion, boiling water in a large quantity (perhaps a quart or more), removing it from the heat, and dropped in some chunks of root. It takes quite a long while until visible color change is evident in the water, so I waited until it appeared pinkish-orange-red. (It may also be boiled in the water, but that should be done more briefly). There is an obvious oil or terpene in the infusion at that point. Poured out about 16 fl. oz., added some sugar, and drank it after it had cooled slightly. Effects were pretty rapid: a mellowing effect, clearheaded but definitely relaxed, and felt really affable and happy. My vision changed nicely, too, to the point where everything had a golden hue, and I called someone on the phone and told them of this wonderful sassafras and how I had forgotten it was such a fine plant; now I had a very obvious reminder why I liked it as a kid, in addition to that aroma and taste. Effects lasted a couple of hours. It should be specified again that this was the red root, not the bark, aerial parts, or leaves. Other attempts using aerial parts (tree trunk or branch wood) have been disappointing, in part due to the lower medicinal value of those parts and because those pieces had been oxidized and were older, definitely not red and fibrous, nor as aromatic.

Apr 10, 2020. erowid.org/exp/111992 DOSE: Pharms - Citalopram 0.8 g oral Mushrooms - P. semilanceata oral Ginger (tea) BODY WEIGHT: 135 lb Weening off Citalopram for Shrooms Keywords: citalopram, ssri, ssri withdrawal, psilocybe semilanceata, liberty caps, first time, ginger Preface: I've been on the SSRI Citalopram for quite a while as a result of a traumatic breakup. It's about 10 months after and I feel less depressed but also kind of directionless and stagnant. I'm looking to shrooms for answers. I attempted to slightly ween off the SSRIs before consuming shrooms. I found the consensus online is that mixing shrooms and SSRIs results in lessened effects or effects that seem more like drunkenness than a thoughtful eye-opening trip. Doses: Citalopram: [20mg/daily] x 10 months, [10mg/daily] x 3 days, [5mg] x 1 day, [0mg] x 1 day. Liberty Caps: 0.8g over 1.5 hours. To explain the above, I took 20mg of citalopram (a low/moderate dose) for 10 months, then prior to shroom day I took 10mg for a day, 10mg for a day, 10mg for a day, 5mg for a day, zero for a day, and then I shroomed the following day. Prior to shroom day I experience mild SSRI withdrawal symptoms: dizziness, discomfort and disorientation upon sudden changes in momentum, infrequent brain-zaps. The experience: T+0:00 - I was nervous so I only took 0.8g grinded up into a hot chocolate with some sober friends. I strained some ginger into the water to attempt to bypass any stomach pains (seemed to work). I tasted a small dry piece of shroom and was surprised by mundane it tasted. I started sipping. T+0:15 - I'm not even close to finishing the hot chocolate but I feel slight more goofy. It's possible it was placebo at this stage. T+1:00 - Still not done but I feel slightly intoxicated. Not tripping, no hallucinations, but I do feel an array of positive-vibes and a cannabis-like brain fog. Nothing that feels too unique from a cannabis or alcohol but welcome nonetheless. T+1:30 - Looking back I must have reached the 'peak' before this point. Surprising to have that done so soon but I was on a low dose in combination with an SSRI. I finish the drink because I'm not scared of getting the full experience. T+3:00 - Things have remained pretty mundane. I begin to feel tired but extremely comfy in my friends' futon with my YouTube playlist running (mostly Tame Impala and the Tragically Hip). I sleep. T+4:00 - I wake up. Not long after this point I develop an annoying headache. I decide to take 5mg of citalopram to avoid further SSRI withdrawals. T+7:00 - I drive home and write this report. Headache is pretty annoying. Foreword: I would not call this a 'trip' and I think any cannabis user would feel at home with this experience. I'd like to take a higher dose (maybe 2g or 2.5g) when the SSRI is out of my system. That said, one shouldn't do psychs really often so I'll try to give it some time.

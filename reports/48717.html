我没有经常头痛,然而;今晚我做了. 由于只是轻微的头痛, 甚至不值得任何舒缓的缓解, 我不是为了减轻疼痛, 而是为了获得相对较强的止痛剂. 我服用了五粒每片200毫克的布洛芬. 我服用了两片Excedrin偏头痛药, 每片含有250毫克乙胺,250毫克阿司匹林和65毫克咖啡因, 我现在知道咖啡因也能缓解疼痛. 我服用了一种强烈的止痛药, 每片含有500毫克的乙氨基和25毫克的帕马布朗, 我还服用了两种记忆和注意力平衡药, 含有各种维生素和矿物质, 我一直对止痛药的有效性持怀疑态度 我从来没有感觉到它们的效果形成. 现在我可以说我已经感受到了止痛药的作用. 吃完大约10分钟后, 我感觉到一个令人满意的 (完全不刺激) 刺的感觉在我的头上. 有一个紧张的刺包裹从门到门下下线. 这只是暂时的,然后什么都没有. 我不推这种方法来缓解一般的疼痛, 药物恢复了完全的效果. 我发现我的整个身体放松了. 这不是那种过度敏感,压倒性的放松, 实际上,这是一种意识到的放松. 我感觉好像肌肉松了,放松了 (这可能是真的), 眼睛开着或闭着, 坐着或倒下, 我很舒服. 无需说,这是一个弱点. 也许这些剂量并不像我最初想象的那样多余. 另一方面 (正如我之前所说的) 乙氨基高剂量会导致肝脏损伤. 坦白说,它还没有足够强大的药物来开始让任何类型的健康妥协. 我利用作为一个试验在止痛强度. 我会在任何一天抽一碗烟 (或每天抽一碗烟). 时间表: 48717 维生素/补充剂 (231),药物 - 伊布罗芬 (249),乙胺基 (337),金科比洛巴 (195),咖啡因 (11):未知背景 (20),健康益处 (32),组合 (3)

Jul 7, 2007. erowid.org/exp/48717 DOSE: 1.0 g oral Pharms - Ibuprofen (pill / tablet) 1.0 g oral Acetaminophen (pill / tablet) 65 mg oral Caffeine (pill / tablet) 200 mg oral Ginkgo biloba (extract) oral Vitamins / Supplements (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 220 lb I don't have headaches often, however; tonight I did. Given it was only a slight headache and not even worthy of any soothing relief, I took relatively strong doses of pain relievers. I did not do this to really ease the pain, but rather to experience relatively strong doses of pain relievers. I took 5 Ibuprofen pills at 200mg a piece. I took 2 Excedrin Migraine pills each containing 250mg Acetaminophen, 250mg Aspirin, and 65mg Caffeine, which is odd because headaches are an effect of caffeine. I know now that caffeine is also an aid for analgesics. I took a stronger analgesic containing 500mg Acetaminophen and 25mg Pamabrom, which is just a diuretic, per pill. I also took 2 memory and focus pills containing various vitamins and minerals including 100mg of Gingko Biloba 24% extract per pill. I have always been skeptical of the actual efficiency of an analgesic. Because painkillers are so gradual and faint, I have never felt their effects take shape. If nothing else, I can now say that I have felt the effects of analgesics. About 10 minutes after consumption I could feel a satisfying (not irritating at all) tingling sensation capping my head. There was a tense tingling wrapping from temple to temple under the jaw line. This lasted only temporarily and then nothing. That alone rid of the trivial headache without a doubt, although; I do not recommend this recipe for any general pain relief for the simple fact that Acetaminophen causes liver damage at high doses. 30 or so minutes after that, the pharmaceuticals returned in full effect. I found my entire body relaxed. It was not the oversensitive, overwhelming kind of relaxation that I find while high on any kind of psychoactive. Instead, it was a realized relaxation. It was as though my muscles loosened and relaxed (which they probably did) and I was just comfortable. Eyes open or closed, rather sitting up or collapsed, I was comfortable. Needless to say, it was weak. Maybe the doses where not as superfluous as I had originally thought. Then again (as I had said before) Acetaminophen at high doses causes liver damage. Honestly, it is not near strong enough of a drug to begin making any type of health compromise what so ever. I exploited as a trial in analgesic intensity. I would smoke a bowl any day (or every day for that matter) instead.

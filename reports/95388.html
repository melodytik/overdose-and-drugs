在过去的几个月里, 总的来说,它们对我的精神健康,精力和专注提供了轻微但显著的提升, 我很高兴我能尝试使用它们. 我开始每天服用1克皮拉和1克胆酸盐. 我选择了这些剂量, 因为它们每一个都带有1克的子. 经过一周的"我不确定"和"也许我感觉到一些", 我感觉头脑更清晰, 能更好地集中注意力,心情也好一些. 在某种程度上, 然后我添加了每天2.5毫克的deprenyl (selegiline). 这立刻产生了效果:我的心情稳定了,变得更加阳光明,颜色变得更加丰富和吸引人. 我试过每天服用5毫克, 但这并没有给我带来超过2.5毫克的好处, 这与皮拉和胆相结合, 感觉就像一种心理保湿剂:它缓解了我心灵的粗和痛点, 我变得更加有弹性:它让我能够摆脱那些可能会让我陷入困境的东西. 我在同一时间添加了300毫克/天的硫胺和100毫克/天的皮卡米隆. 我对这些药物的效果不太清楚, 他们似乎强化了其他,使总体效应更强大,更持久. 我一周前用完硫胺, 没有它也没有什么大不同, 皮卡米隆确实有轻微但显著的抗焦虑作用, 终于我开始间歇服用莫达菲尼尔, 每天服用50mg至200mg. 我更有动力去完成无聊或烦人的任务; 我即使在努力工作, 也能长时间保持清醒; 奇怪的是, 如果我感到焦虑或压力, 它可能会"混乱":就像咖啡因一样, 它可以增加这些感觉, 而不是帮助我处理它们. 但总的来说, 这对我在学习和写作时有很大的帮助, 我之前连续服用三天, 然后恢复正常的生活方式, 我有时会在使用它后的第二天发现我的动力下降, 这比其他补充剂更贵, 但每天200毫克的价格与双杯咖啡相等, YMMV, 作为补充剂的总是. 这些帮助了我 没有. 我饮食健康, 经常运动, 不经常喝酒或吸毒, 但幸运的是,它们足够便宜,我可以试用它们而不会破产. 没有. 时间表: 95388 维生素 - - 胆 (467),皮拉塞坦 (95),药物 - - 塞莱格林 (228):回顾性 / 摘要 (11),增强性能 (50),组合 (3),不适用 (38)

Like Moisturiser for the Mind Piracetam, Choline, Selegiline, Sulbutiamine, Picamilon & Modafinil by the kiwi Citation:   the kiwi. "Like Moisturiser for the Mind: An Experience with Piracetam, Choline, Selegiline, Sulbutiamine, Picamilon & Modafinil (exp95388)". Erowid.org . Apr 28, 2017. erowid.org/exp/95388 DOSE: 1 g oral Piracetam 1 g oral Vitamins - Choline 2.5 mg oral Pharms - Selegeline 100 mg oral Picamilon Modafinil BODY WEIGHT: 185 lb I've been experimenting with some nootropics over the last few months. In general, they have provided a mild but significant boost to my mental health, energy, and focus, with no side effects and at a very low price. They're now a part of my daily routine and I'm happy that I gave them a try. I started with a combination of 1g piracetam and 1g choline citrate a day. I chose those doses just because they each came with a 1g scoop. After a week of 'I'm not sure' and 'Maybe I feel something', I eventually started to get a consistent boost every time I took them. It was subtle, to be sure: my mind felt clearer, I was more able to concentrate on things, and I was in a slightly better mood. In a way it was like the feeling of recovering from a hangover. Then I added 2.5mg/daily of deprenyl (selegiline). This took an effect straight away: my mood stabilised and got sunnier, and colours became richer and more engaging. I tried taking 5mg/day but that didn't offer much of a benefit over 2.5, and I don't want to get dependent on it. This, combined with the piracetam and choline, felt like a psychological moisturiser: it soothes the rough patches and sore spots in my psyche, and makes things feel smoother and more fluid. I got noticeably more resilient: it made me able to shrug off things that would have gotten under my skin without it. From there I added, at the same time, 300mg/daily of sulbutiamine and 100mg/daily of picamilon. I'm less clear about the effect that these had, just because their effects mixed in with all the other nootropics. They seemed to potentiate the others, making the general effect stronger and longer-lasting. I ran out of sulbutiamine a week ago, and haven't found much of a difference without it; but perhaps I'll notice it in the weeks to come. Picamilon does have a mild but significant anti-anxiety effect, provided I stick to low doses. Finally, I started taking modafinil intermittently, in doses from 50mg to 200mg in a day. It's pretty strong: I'm much more motivated to get boring or annoying tasks done; I'm awake for long hours even if I'm working hard; and, strangely, it suppresses my appetite too. It can be 'messy' if I'm feeling anxious or stressed: like caffeine, it can add to those feelings rather than helping me to deal with them. But on the whole it's been a great help when I've been studying and writing essays, and since it has no real impact on my mood I'm not worried about dependency or addiction here. I've taken it three days in a row before and then gone back to my normal lifestyle without noticing any problems. I sometimes notice a drop-off in my motivation the day after using it, but that's very slight, and could just be from all the work that I've done. It's more expensive than the other supplements here, but 200mg/day is the same price as a double espresso, so I can justify it to myself (and I'm a broke-ass student). YMMV, as always with supplements. These have helped me out . That said, I have a pretty healthy diet, exercise a lot, and don't drink or do drugs too often; their effect would probably be less noticeable on a person with a crazier lifestyle. But thankfully they're cheap enough that I can try them out without going bankrupt .

Dec 30, 2024. erowid.org/exp/117460 DOSE: T+ 0:00 90 mg 2-Fluorodeschloroketamine T+ 14:00 35 mg insufflated DMXE T+ 14:55 smoked Tobacco - Cigarettes BODY WEIGHT: 55 kg Dosed 90mg of 2-FDCK approx. 14 hours ago, all effects faded and I had a good 7 hours of sleep in that time, but I would presume it had an effect on my dissociative tolerance. I found the experience very underwhelming, so I busted open my stash of RCs, starting with DMXE I have previously used DMXE, but never had the time, or setting, to give it an honest try. I have an expanse of experience with psychoactive substances of numerous classes, including Dissociatives. (Ketamine, DCK, 2-FDCK, 3-HO-PCP, 3-Me-PCPy, O-PCE, MXiPr, DMXE, 3-MeO-PCE) T+ 0:00 35mg Insufflated, very slight burn, strange plastic-y taste and smell. It kind of smells like burnt plastic, but I'd describe it as pleasant and interesting. Listening to various genres of DnB, mostly liquid, one of my cats laying in my lap. Good start to any experience if you ask me. T+ 0:05 Slight feeling of numbness, or rather a faint pressure in the bridge of my nose. My vision is slightly sharper, music sounds a bit more pronounced. My vision is slightly sharper, music sounds a bit more pronounced. The auditory effect it has on me reminds me a little of 4-HO-MET, in the sense that the volume of my hearing sometimes changes subtly from louder to softer, as if someone was slowly walking back and forth (away from me and back) with my speaker in their hand. Though it is much less prominent than 4-HO-MET's is to me. T+ 0:10 The roof of my mouth is numbed considerably, along with the nostril I snorted the DMXE in. It is not as numbing as Cocaine, but it is more noticeable than other Dissociatives I have experience with. I feel very stimulated and alert, it is a comfortable sort of stimulation. I don't feel like moving my legs, or clenching my jaw. My vision has slight distortions every now and again, but nothing that cannot be disregarded. The distortions are akin to being slightly tipsy from Alcohol alone I'd say. T+ 0:20 Starting to feel a bit more dissociation now, faint ringing in my ears, and my vision is getting a bit more blurry and glow-y. The numbness has reached a point very similar to Cocaine now, which I find surprising, as I don't recall noticing this with any other Dissociative I have tried. The bass of my speakers has been enhanced considerably, I can almost feel the dull hum of the music in my brain. My throat is a little numb as well from the residual drip, which tastes not at all unpleasant. I like that a lot. Overall I'd still describe this as being on Alcohol + Cocaine without the physical side effects of either substance. I would rate it higher than the aforementioned combo. My cognition is slowing considerably, I find it harder to find the words to describe this experience as it goes along. Some parts of the music are highlighted, which I have never noticed before. Similar to how THC can make background instruments in songs more prominent. T+ 0:30 Cats love hanging out with me when I do Ketamine (and its analogues), I don't know if they can smell it or something, or if trace amounts of the substance somehow affect them, even though I practice extreme caution in not exposing them to any of the substances that I do. But they seem extremely chill and drawn to me. As I pet my cat, I definitely would describe the physical effect on my sense of touch as Tactile Supression rather than Enhancement. My head feels a little heavier, numbness is ever so slowly fading. So far, I could probably see this as a decent socializing recreational dose, perhaps 20mg would be appropriate for open settings with other people. T+ 0:40 Standing up to stretch my legs and get something to drink. I can feel a slight decrease in my ability to walk, but nothing problematic. I can hold conversations with my family with no issue for short periods, I do not appear to be inebriated to them. T+ 0:55 Switched to headphones to better appreciate the auditory effects. I find that DMXE is strikingly similar to Ketamine, with a lot less immobilizing effects, and less auditory distortion. Music sounds a lot better on DMXE than Ketamine. Gonna smoke a cigarette while I listen to more tunes. T+ 1:00 Cigarettes are definitely better on DMXE, though I don't usually smoke cigarettes anymore, just substituting weed. Feels smoother is the way I'd describe it. Getting a bit more of the Dissociation now, manifesting in a soft buzzing and slow shifting of my body. T+ 2:00 Effects mostly subsided after 1, and completely after about 2 hours.

我已经了好几年了, 我终于决定寻求帮助. 我使用了suboxone去排毒后, 给我服用了5毫克的纳尔特克松. 十分钟后, 我开始有无法控制的. 由于我不确定会发生什么, 我于凌晨1点去急诊室. 我躺在急症室的床上, 我向后腰, 五分钟后, 看起来像五个小时, 护士跑进来, 我很高兴我睡了一会儿. 护士们没有什么可做,所以我在凌晨2点左右回家. 到了我家后, 我服用了一种睡眠, 这一切持续了四个小时, 我没有吃饭,也没有和任何人说话. 我甚至没有意识到我的身体正在崩. 我花了一整周的时间才恢复正常. 现在我再也不会碰片了. 其他: 纳尔特雷 (338):艰难的经历 (5),医疗使用 (47),健康问题 (27),医院 (36)

Jul 21, 2010. erowid.org/exp/76222 DOSE: 10 mg Naltrexone Pharms - Lorazepam Pharms - Quetiapine BODY WEIGHT: 150 lb OK...I've been an opiate addict for a number of years and I finally decided to get some help. After using suboxone to detox, I was put on 5mg of naltrexone. 10 minutes after taking it I go into a uncontrollable siezure-like spasm. Since I wasn't really certain of what might happen I end up goin to the ER at 1am. While laying on the bed in ICU, I am bending backward so far that my feet are touching the back of my head. 5 min later, which seemed like 5 hours, the nurse runs in and gives me a shot of ativan which knocks me out cold. While asleep I was told that I was still convulsing, but I was happy I was asleep for a little of it. The nurses couldn't realy do anything else about it so I went home around 2am. After arriving at my house, I took a serequil so I could sleep, which I soon did. All of this lasted about 4 hours which seemed like would never end. The next day I didn't get out of bed, didn't eat, and didn't talk to anyone. Since I worked out muscles I didn't even know I had my body felt like it was falling apart. It took me an entire week to become normal again. Now I will never touch another opiate.

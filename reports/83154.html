这一天似乎结束了所有的日子. 我没有为此做好准备 我买了这个便宜的Flatliner, 认为媒体对它的杀人潜力的作只是作. 我错了. 我吃了药 我正在等待药物的作用. 由于我肚子空了, 我觉得自己被欺骗了. 这仍然不起作用,我不能相信这个** 摄入后1小时 - 发生了;我的世界变成了另一个星球. 我是两个人彼此交谈. 我是一个卡通人物. 我是在另一个星球. 我在脑海里尖叫,但不能说出自己的话. 我绝望地跑来跑去, 试图让这段经历结束, 我知道我已经用了太多的药物. 我已经用过安非他命和迷幻药, 甚至我能处理胺, 世界变成了一个充满好人的地方, 我是一个外星人伪装成人. 如果同胞们知道呢? 如果他们不知道他们肯定会发现. 我听到一些声音告诉我我将要死, 我像猪一样出汗. 我觉得我无法呼吸, 其他人正在呼吸空气. 但空气对我来说不合适,我需要另一个气体. 我感到迷失了. 上帝告诉我我会这样做. 然后恶心,我生病了这个药物. 不断吐和干燥的动. 我觉得我的身体已经毁了. 我无法给出时间表, 就我而言, 时间早已融化. 我在巴士上回家. 当地球号召我开始感觉到轻微的安非他林效应. 快速和宽松的整个经验结束了. 我仍然认为我很幸运没有死于这种病. 我认为这不是一个可玩的药物. 它的负面影响似乎超过任何积极的属性. 但是,如果你想失去理智,那就继续吧. 我非常怀疑实验室是否还能生产这种药物. "Flatlined Beyond Comprehension: An Experience with 4-MTA (exp83154)". Erowid.org . Apr 30, 2010. erowid.org/exp/83154 DOSE: 1 tablet oral 4-MTA BODY WEIGHT: 160 lb The day to end all days it seemed. An experience I was NOT prepared for. Having little money at the time I bought this cheap Flatliner thinking that the media hype surrounding its potential to kill was just well, hype. I WAS WRONG. I take the pill 10+ I am waiting for the pill to kick in. As I have an empty stomach I expect that to be anytime soon. 30+ Still not happening for me, I feel agitated and cheated. 45+ This is still not working, I can't believe this s*** 1 Hour after ingestion- BOOM It happened; my world became another planet. I was 2 people talking to each other. I was a cartoon character. I was on another planet. I was screaming inside my head but couldn't talk properly. I ran desperately around to try and get the experience to end, my mind was ruined. I knew I had gone too far taking this drug. I was seasoned with amphetamines and psychedelics even ketamine I can handle, but this was beyond recognition, beyond comprehension. The world became a place that was filled with good people, but I was most certainly not one of them. I was an alien in a human disguise. What if the fellow people knew? If they didn't know they would most certainly find out. I could hear voices telling me I was going to die and that I should probably end it all. I was sweating like a PIG. I felt like I couldn't breathe, everyone else was breathing air. But air wasn't right for me, i needed another gas. God I felt lost. God was telling me I was. Then the nausea, I was sick off this drug. Constant vomiting and dry heaving. My body felt ruined. I can't put a timeframe on this, time had melted away a long time ago as far as I was concerned. But slowly I remembered who I was and left for home on the bus. When Planet Earth beckoned I started to feel a mild amphetamine effect. Speedy and relieved that the whole experience was over. I still count my blessings to this day I didn't die from this. In my opinion this is not a drug to be messed with. Its negative effects seem to outweigh any positive attributes. However if you like to lose you sanity then go ahead. I doubt very much that labs produce this drug anymore.

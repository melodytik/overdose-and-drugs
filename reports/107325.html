由于互联网上并没有太多关于它的信息, 我决定写一篇关于我的经历的旅行报告. 我从英国的供应商那里买了两颗50毫克颗粒. 我读到50毫克是很弱的剂量. 吸入会增加效果,所以我压碎了颗粒, 首先吸入20毫克, 现场报道: 10:07 (T+00:00) 时间 吸入了半颗粒的三分之四, 大约是18-20毫克. 据我所知,这应该相当于50毫克口服. 我打算在一个小时内吸入另外5毫克, 或更多, 我不打算做一个完整的旅行, 但我也不想因为效果薄弱而失望 (我和bk-2C-B一样). 它的燃烧程度与2C-B相同, 但与剂量相比较较轻. 呼吸的时候最疼. 目前还没有效果. 我鼻子的灼伤已经倒退了. 滴水的味道是可以的. 所以我决定吸入剩余的药丸, 如果这种药物 (或我的颗粒) 如此薄弱, 烧了一点点. 我也开始有点鼻子堵了. 时间是12:00 (T+01:53) 几乎没有任何效果, 除了感觉和身体感觉的轻微变化. 外面阳光明,看起来比平常更漂亮. 我也会吸我的另一颗颗粒. 另一个50毫克. 很遗憾它很昂贵,但我宁愿有一个完整的经验比两个半个. 我的供应商卖给我坏东西还是这药真的那么弱? 现在非常痛苦. 我正在考虑在外面骑自行车或玩一些Poi,当疼痛减弱. 12:15 (T+02:08) 时间 我在我的PSVITA上玩游戏, 但我不能静坐, 现在我看到一些奇怪的东西在我的外视的下部, 像一个盲点在云的形式, 当我闭上眼睛是黄色的光. 我有点担心这个盲点, 但它已经消失了. 现在大部分疼痛都消失了, 喉的后部也感到麻木. 我之前读过, 这种药物确实有麻木作用. 12:30 (T+02:23) 时间 我觉得自己有一部分是清醒的. 我认为我的头脑不是在,而我的其他部分是. 我很满意这一点. 我现在正在吃饭,这是我忘记吃早餐以来的第一顿饭. 这真的很好. 我有点. 两天前我也服用了一剂2C-B, 但这次情况更糟. 感觉有点像, 但不够说它很像它. 我现在感到失望. 除非真正的效果还要发挥作用, 我认为我被我的供应商操了, 只是精神状态略有改变, 有些效应在我的大脑里有点有毒. 的感觉正在消失,也许主要是因为忘记了我的早餐. 13:00 (T+02:53) 时间 我认为如果我忘记了我有多大的期望, 我看到一些动作,更多的色彩. 尽管外面天气很阳光明, 我甚至想看电影, 我从来不想在任何迷幻的, 我会强迫自己骑自行车, 我认为我不会因此失望. 13:10 (T+03:03) 时间 似乎我不能强迫自己出去. 我感到有点兴奋,但同时也有一点昏迷. 我真的不知道该感觉什么. 我不知道该怎么做,我太激动了,不能坐着,但动作不足. 视觉效果确实存在, 东西在四处移动, 这似乎对我不感兴趣. 盲点还没有完全消失,我闭上眼睛时仍然能看到它. 我不知道是我的眼睛还是我的大脑在做这件事. 13:15 (T+03:08) 时间 ,我忘了关于性的欲望. 让我们试试看. 13:30 (T+03:23) 时间 ,我的上帝,自慰是惊人的! 我会吃一些人参, 也许这会激励我足够去外面. 我觉得有点内, 在秋天的阳光下待在室内. 一个小的DMT关节也听起来很好,让我们带来一点爱. 现在想想, 这种5-MeO-DiBF化学物质缺乏爱. 也许这就是为什么我似乎对什么都不感兴趣, 我很惊一个迷幻药 (如果你甚至可以这样称呼它) 可以如此无趣. 另外,音乐天赋有所减少, 而迷幻药通常会增加. 我用我的琴进行了测试. 我只需要一个琴就能演奏几个小时. 德米特确实让事情变得更加有趣. 即使是一小剂量完全压倒了这次旅行. 这就像DIBF效应变成了DMT, 我渴望胺, 我认为它会很好地与5-MeO-DiBF, 我订购了一些Ephenidine (一种解离药), 我预计今天或明天, 这太糟糕了. 我几乎被诱惑去服用醇来让事情变得更好, 我鼻子时发现里面还麻木. 和色的 14:15 (T+04:08) 时间 感觉有点丧 不是很多,但它在那里. 我正在努力阻止自己服用一些让我感觉更好而最终让我感觉更糟的东西. 我在公园里埋藏了一些1,4-butanediol (因为我不能把手放下). 1,4-二醇与GHB非常相似, 我很想去买一些, 但这些东西很容易搞我. 我认为我只需要处理不断的想法"我可以得到一些有趣的事情. 即使这只是酒精. 我已经戒酒了. 14:30 (T+04:23) 时间 现在已经接近基线了. 消极的情绪也在消失, 人参没有做任何事情. 我对人参有很大的免疫力, 即使是高剂量也没有影响我. 或许我只是没有意识到. 现在我可以坐着,所以我会继续玩我的生命. 我会享受一天的剩余时间没有药物. 15:15 (T+05:08) 时间 大约95%回到基线. 我觉得清醒了,但当我着某个东西, 几秒钟后它开始移动. 很高兴我没有选择1,4-butanediol. 我还在考虑使用醇, 我还需要测试一些美醇. 现在我才注意到人出来了,终于一部好电影. 很遗憾我没有在旅途中看到它, 它可能会对我的情绪产生很大的影响. 我已经达到了下载限制. 这真是糟糕的一天. 我还是服用了美西,做了另一个实验. 我很好奇这会怎样. 第二天: 感觉又好了,没有后果. 睡得很好,做了很多美好的梦. 梅提索拉姆的效果很弱,一会儿让我更加伤心, 总的来说, 我有一些视觉效果和强烈的色彩, 在精神上, 完全没有幻觉. 我感觉自己完全清醒了. 也有轻微的刺激, 没有动机, 这有点烦人. 总是想做点什么,却从来没有做过什么. 只是听一些音乐. 这听起来比平常好一点. 我在背景感受到了微小的快乐, 我可能会喜欢一部电影, 但我看了好电影几次, 操确实让我感觉很好, 但如果我不寻找性欲, 为了弥补失望, 我一开始心情很好,所以我把负面部分归咎于药物. 我不认为我会喜欢效果如果他们更强. 但是我不确定. 我认为我从我的供应商那里得到了一些坏的颗粒. 然而, 鼻子上的烧伤,滴水和麻木, 显然表明有很多化学物质. 也许我在3天前和5天前做的一些2C-B给了我一些交叉容忍. 从我读到的,100毫克吸入应该像200毫克口服,这应该是很多所以肯定有什么不对. 如果将来有人向我提供5-MeO-DiBF, 我读过好坏的旅行报道, 其他: 2015年11月6日发布 5-MeO-DiBF (701):单独 (16),第一次 (2),一般 (1)

I bought two 50 mg pellets from a UK vendor. I have read that 50mg is a weak dosage. Insufflation increases the effects, so I crushed the pellets and first snorted 20mg, 50 min later 30mg and than an hour later 50mg. Live report: 10:07 (T+00:00) Snorted 3/4 of half a pellet, which is about 18-20 mg. From what I've read, this should be about equal to 50 mg oral. I'm planning on snorting the other 5mg withing an hour, or more, depending on the strength of the trip. I'm not planning on having a full blown trip, but I don't want to be disappointed with weak effects either (which I was with bk-2C-B). Divided between two noseholes, it took a few minutes to start burning, it was the same kind of burn as 2C-B, however less intense compared to dosage. Hurts most when breathing out. 10:21 (T+00:14) No effects yet. The burn has moved backwards in my nose. Taste of the drip is okay. 10:58 (T+00:51) No effects at all yet, So I decided to snort the rest of the pellet, another 30mg. If this drug (or my pellets) are that weak, maybe I'm gonna have to use my second pellet too. 11:00 (T+00:53) Burns quite a bit. Also my nose starts clogging a little. 12:00 (T+01:53) Almost no effect, except for a slight change in perception and body feel. It's sunny outside, looks a little more beautiful than normal. I'm going to snort my other pellet too. Another 50 mg. Too bad it's expensive, but I'd rather have one full experience than two half ones. Did my vendor sell me bad stuff or is this drug really that weak? 12:06 (T+01:59) Very painful now. I'm thinking of going outside to ride the bike or play some poi, when the pain subsides. 12:15 (T+02:08) I'm playing a game on my psvita but I can't sit still, there's definitely some stimulation. Also just now I see something weird in the lower part of my peripheral vision, like a blind spot in the form of a cloud and when I close my eyes it's yellow light. A little worried about the blind spot, but it's fading already. The pain is mostly gone now, and the back of my throat feels numb. As I read before, this drug has definitely a numbing effect. 12:30 (T+02:23) It's like part of me is tripping a little and part of me is sober. I think it's my mind that is not tripping and the rest of me is. I'm quite ok with that. I'm eating a meal now, first meal of the day since I forgot breakfast. And it's really good. I'm shaking a bit. I had it too with a small dose of 2C-B two days ago, but this time its worse. Feeling a little hint of mushroom, but not enough to say it resembles it. 12:50 (T+02:43) I'm disappointed now. Unless the real effects still have to kick in, I think I am fucked by my vendor, or this is some shitty drug. Only slightly altered state of mind, and some effects feel like a little toxic in my brain. The shakiness is going away, maybe it was mostly from forgetting my breakfast. 13:00 (T+02:53) I guess it's okay if I forget how much I was expecting, and how much money I've wasted. I see a little movement here and there, and more color. But I am not motivated to do anything, even though it's very sunny outside. I even want to watch a movie, which I never want on any psychedelic because I'm too busy enjoying other things. I'm gonna force myself to take a bike ride, I think I won't be disappointed by that. 13:10 (T+03:03) It seems I can't force myself going outside. I feel a little stimulated, but a little lethargic at the same time. I don't really know what to feel. And I don't know what to do, I'm too stimulated for sitting still activities, but not motivated enough to move around. The visuals are definitely there, things moving around and stuff, though not very obvious. And it doesn't seem to interest me. The blind spot isn't completely gone, I still see it when I close my eyes. I don't know if it's my eyes or my brain doing this. 13:15 (T+03:08) Oh, I forgot about sex drive. Well, let's test it out... 13:30 (T+03:23) Oh my god, masturbation was amazing! Gonna take some ginseng, maybe that will motivate me enough to go outside. I feel a little guilty to stay inside with all that sun in autumn. A small DMT joint sounds nice too, let's bring a little love into it. Now that I think about it, this 5-MeO-DiBF chemical lacks love. Maybe that's why nothing seems to interest me even though everything looks beautiful and doing stuff doesn't take much effort. I'm surprised that a psychedelic (if you can even call it that) can be so uninteresting. Also, musical talent is reduced a bit, while psychedelics usually increase that. I tested it with my jaw harp. When I take 2C-B I can play for hours with just a jaw harp. 14:00 (T+03:53) DMT definitely made things more interesting. Even a small dose completely overpowered the trip. It's like the DIBF effects changed into DMT and are now coming back. I'm craving ketamine, I think it would go along nicely with 5-MeO-DiBF, mostly because of the added happiness. I ordered some ephenidine (a dissociative) which I'm expecting today or tomorrow, just looked into the mailbox but no luck. Too bad. I'm almost tempted to take a benzo to make things a little nicer, but I have to be careful with benzos these days. When I pick my nose I notice the inside is still numb. And orange. 14:15 (T+04:08) Feeling a little depressed. Not much, but it's there. I'm struggling to keep myself from taking something to make me feel better that eventually makes me feel worse. I have some 1,4-butanediol buried in a park (because I couldn't keep me hands off of it). 1,4-butanediol is much like GHB but very bad for the liver. I'm very tempted to go get some, but this stuff screws me up very easily. I guess I'm just gonna have to deal with the constant thought of 'I could get something fun to do. Even if it's just alcohol'. And I quit alcohol. 14:30 (T+04:23) The 5-MeO-DiBF effects are going away, almost at baseline now. Negativity is also going away, but still it left me feeling disappointed. Ginseng didn't do anything. I'm pretty much immune to ginseng, but I keep taking it hoping for some effect to happen. Even high doses don't affect me at all. Or maybe I'm just not conscious of it. Now I can sit still again so I'm gonna continue playing on my vita. I'm gonna enjoy the rest of the day without drugs. 15:15 (T+05:08) About 95% back to baseline. I feel sober, but when I stare at something it starts to move around after a few seconds. Glad I didn't go for the 1,4-butanediol. But I'm still thinking of benzos, I have some metizolam to test. Now I just noticed Ant-man is out, finally a good movie. Too bad I didn't see it earlier in the trip, it could've made a big difference in my mood. I reached my download limit. What a sucky day. I took the metizolam anyway, another experiment. Curious how that will work out. Day after: Feeling good again, no after effects. Had a good sleep with good dreams. Metizolam was weak, it made me even more sad for a moment, but then suddenly I felt good and its effects went away. Overall, I was very disappointed by the underwhelming experience. I had some visuals and heightened colors, but I lacked any interest in that. Mentally, nothing psychedelic at all. That part of me felt completely sober. Also slight stimulation without motivation, which was a little annoying. Always on the verge of going to do something but never did anything. Just listened to some music. It sounded a little better than usual. And I had a tiny bit of euphoria in the background, but somehow I couldn't really enjoy it. I could've enjoyed a movie, but I watched all the good movies already a few times and I didn't notice a good new movie was out. Masturbation did feel very good, but sex drive didn't come if I didn't look for it. In the end I felt the need to take something else to make up for the disappointment. I was in a good mood to begin with, so I blame the drug for the negative part. I don't think I would've enjoyed the effects if they were much stronger. But I'm not sure. I think I have gotten some bad pellets from my vendor. However, the burn in my nose, drip and numbing clearly indicated a lot of chemical stuff. Maybe the little 2C-B I did 3 and 5 days ago gave me some cross tolerance. From what I've read, 100mg insufflated should be like around 200mg orally, which should be a lot so there was definitely something wrong. If anyone ever offers me 5-MeO-DiBF in the future, I wouldn't take it, even if it was a low price. I have read good and bad trip reports, so I guess it's something that some people like and others don't.

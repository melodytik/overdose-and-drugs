这是一个我一年多前的经历. 我在早上11点左右开始服用35毫克的TMA-2囊, 我没有服用药物/补充剂, 我们走到外面, 发现我和我的朋友都不会生病. 我感觉到温暖,我的眼睛在阳光下流. 身体的兴奋感很像MDA, 我们坐在一个公园里, 他没有吃到足够的皮约特, 我一直觉得自己正处于视觉效果的边缘, 我慢慢放弃这种经历会发展成更实质性的东西的想法. 尽管如此,这时相对很愉快,后来我们乘车上去的时候,我有了短暂的'融入音乐'的机会. 在精神上, 也没有太多发生, 没有特别同情或迷幻的精神状态的发展. 我觉得,在这个方面,有点,我可能是非常好的草,但这就是它. 经历 (或至少身体部分) 拖延了很长时间, 随着时间的流逝, 晚上8点或9点左右, 这种药物甚至没有减轻头痛. 这是一种无聊的,脉动的痛苦, 尽管其他药物的效果已经减弱, 我发现自己处于想睡觉的状态, 大约在午夜左右, 事实上,它持续到第二天, 直到下午才消散, 我有另一个TMA-2的经历, 这一次,它与LSD和MDA结合在一起, 这一次, 我当然有了明显的迷幻体验, 但这两次旅行的共同点是, 这次头痛的消散时间较短, 简而言之:我完全不喜欢这种物质. 我觉得它很弱--与MDA相似,但却比它劣, 我不知道头痛是否可能是某种损伤的迹象 (舒尔金在PIHKAL中谈到头痛是一个特别糟糕的迹象), 乔希 其他: TMA-2 (112):健康问题 (27),第一次 (2),小组 (2-9) (17)

Jul 25, 2001. erowid.org/exp/8325 DOSE: 35 mg oral TMA-2 (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 170 lb This is an experience which I had a bit over a year ago. I started at about 11 in the morning, taking a capsule of 35 mgs TMA-2, while a friend injested some peyote buttons. I was on no medications/supplements, and I did not eat that morning before dosing. After determining that neither I nor my friend was going to be violently ill, we went outside. I felt warm, and my eyes teared in the sunlight. The body high felt a lot like MDA, but not as consistantly present, nor as strong. We sat in the afternoon sun, in a park, and watched people go by. He had not eaten enough peyote buttons to trip and I, well... I kept having the feeling that I was on the verge of having visuals, like maybe a building would change color momentarily and then return. It was actually rather frustrating, as I had slowly to give up the notion that the experience was going to develop into anything more substantial. Still, it was relatively enjoyable at this point, and I had a brief moment of 'merging with the music' as we rode in a car later. Mentally, there was also not much going on, with the development of neither particularly empathogenic nor psychedelic mind states. I felt, in this respect, somewhat stoned, as I might be on very good weed, but that was it. HOWEVER... the experience (or at least the physical parts thereof)dragged on and on, with the drugged body high decreasing ever so slowly as the hours ticked by. At about 8 or 9 PM, I began to develop the worst headache that I can remember ever having. Codeine asprin (the over-the-counter kind from Isreal, so not particularly effective stuff) did not even put a dent in the headache. It was a dull, pulsing pain, and it took over most of my consciousness. Even as the other drug effects wore off, and I found myself in a condition to think about sleep, at about midnight or so, the headache persisted. It persisted, in fact, well into the next day, not dissipating until the afternoon, over 24 hours after the ingestion of the chemical. I have had one more experience with TMA-2, which occured maybe four months after the initial one. This time, it was combined with LSD and MDA, both substances that have (more or less) treated me fine in the past. This time, I of course had a significantly psychedelic experience, but the thing that the two trips had in common was that again, about eight or nine hours after the ingestion of the TMA-2, I develped an *awful* headache. This time, the headache took a bit less time to dissipate, but it was still pretty terrible while it lasted. In brief: I don't at all like this substance. It seems pretty weak to me -- rather akin, but inferior, to MDA, if perhaps longer lasting -- and the physical price it exacts is pretty high.

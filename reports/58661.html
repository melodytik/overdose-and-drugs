美国 这是一个我记得最好的记述, 以及我周围的人在我为期两天的旅程中对我有史以来最奇怪的药物体验的观察. 让我先说一下,我不会推这个药物给任何人, 这一切都始于一个无聊的夜晚,就像往常一样. 我走了平常的便宜路线, 搜索药柜, 希望能看到除了布洛芬和抗酸药外的东西. 阅读了一些关于滥用药物的文章后, 我得出结论, 服用20粒375毫克的药丸会有所帮助. 我吃了这些药,等了一两个小时, 现在已经很晚了, 我很快就睡了. 在梦中, 我梦见了一整天的记录, 于是我吃了剩下的12粒药. 现在我知道我已经睡了. 我接着去了我的女朋友的家. 这是在上午10点左右. 到了女朋友的家, 开始发生一些非常奇怪的事情. 首先,我昨晚梦见的朋友现实出现了. 她没有被烧伤, 但我确信我的梦不是梦, 我决定坐在后面的门廊上抽一支烟. 这才是最糟糕的时刻. 烟草的消失事件 烟草消失事件是迄今为止最奇怪的事情发生在这个经历. 我会点燃一支香烟,抽几分钟, 然后看着它在我的手指尖之间消失. 我会到处寻找,但永远找不到. 这让人很困惑, 我问我的女朋友是否注意到我的香烟有什么奇怪. 她说我会一再模仿点燃和吸烟没有香烟, 然后惊和困惑当它消失了. 我实际上是在幻觉中吸烟. 没有出现的党派 我决定回到客厅坐下. 我试图用我腿上的遥控器关闭不存在的无线电. 据当时的护理人员说, 我对自己的"腿遥控器"不起作用感到极度不安. 我注意到几位年轻人,男女, 走进前门, 坐在客厅里. 我曾与这些人交谈过, 记得有个女孩好像从椅子上长出来. 总的来说,我见过和交谈过的人大约有10个人. 后来我发现他们都不存在. 我决定使用所. 一位年长的黑人女性似乎是一名银行家冲进了浴室, 她在浴旁坐下, 说她会等我说完. 我冲了下水,我还没开始洗手,她就坐在所上排尿. 我开始随便地和她聊天, 显然这位完全陌生的人在我面前使用所, 我离开所后, 有一个朋友坐在门外, 问我在所里和谁说话. 我向她解释了黑人妇女的情况, 我还见到15个不存在的人, 并且与他们交谈. 有些在我的车里,有些在我的房间里, 这些人对我来说非常真实, 我也经常受到蜘蛛的攻击, 不停地问为什么外面还没有黑暗. 这绝对是我有史以来最生动,最真实的幻觉体验. 其他任何时候我有幻觉,我只是看到不同的事情,即-色彩的,明亮的光,影像,等等. 总的来说,我见过和交谈过30个不真实的人. 我记得这些人是如此真实, 第二天我完全惊地听到除了我的两个朋友, 一个难忘的一天,是的,但回想起来,我现在确切地知道完全疯狂是什么感觉. 时间表:58661 小组 (2-9) (17),实体/生物 (37),困难经历 (5),一般 (1)

The Party That Wasn't There Hyoscyamine by richard Citation:   richard. "The Party That Wasn't There: An Experience with Hyoscyamine (exp58661)". Erowid.org . Jan 9, 2007. erowid.org/exp/58661 DOSE: T+ 0:00 3.0 mg oral Hyoscyamine (pill / tablet) T+ 12:00 4.5 mg oral Hyoscyamine (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 190 lb This is an account to the best of my memory as well as the observations of those around me during my 2 day journey into the strangest drug experience I have ever had. Let me start by saying that I would recommend this drug to noone, as it was probably the most stupid and irresponsible thing I have ever done. It all started one boring night, like it always does. At the time of the experience, I was 18, out of highschool, without a job, and thus the funds for 'real' drugs were not present. i took the usual cheap route of searching through the medicine cabinets, hoping to catch a glimpse of something other than ibuprofen and antacid tablets. Not expecting to find anything, since I had taken everything else, I was surprised to find an old prescription for hyoscyamine. after reading a few articles on the abuse of the drug, I came to the conclusion that taking 20 of the .375 mg pills I had would do the trick. I popped these pills, waited an hour or two, and finally started to feel them coming on. At this point, it was pretty late, and I quickly fell asleep. Amidst my sleep, I managed to have a dream documenting an entire day, and during this 'dream day', a good friend of mine was badly burned. The next morning, I woke up, and even though I was still high, I figured I had slept most of the effects off, so I took the remaining 12 pills I had. I then proceeded to go to my girlfriends house. This was around 10 am. Upon arriving at my girlfriend's house, the really wierd things started to happen. First, the friend who had been in my dream the last night showed up, in reality. She was not burned, but I was convinced that my dream was not a dream, and I could not figure out how she healed so fast. I decided to sit on the back porch and smoke a cigarette. This is where shit really hit the fan. The Dissapearing Cigarette Incident The dissapearing cigarette incident was by far the strangest thing to happen during this experience. I would light up a cigarette, smoke it for a few minutes, and then watch it dissolve into nothing between my fingertips. I would look everywhere, but would never find it. This was quite confusing, and happened many times. The next day, after coming down, I asked my girlfriend if she noticed anything wierd about my cigarettes. She said that repeatedly i would mimic lighting up and smoking a cigarette that was not there, and then be surprised and confused when it disappeared. I was actually hallucinating smoking cigarettes. The Party That Wasn't There After giving up on trying to smoke a cigarette, I decided to go back inside and sit in the living room. After watching a blank tv screen for half an hour (according to the people that were there), I attempted to turn off the radio that didn't exist with a remote that was apparently in my leg. According to the people 'taking care' of me at this point, I got extremely distressed at the fact that my 'leg remote' did not work. At this point, I noticed several young people, both male and female, walk in through the front door and sit down in the living room. I had conversations with most of these people and remember one girl very vividly who seemed as though she was sprouting from a chair. Overall there were about 10 different people that i saw and talked to. I learned later that none of them existed. After having my talks with the group of people in the living room, I decided I would use the restroom. After walking in and getting ready to pee, an older black woman who appeared to be a banker barged into the bathroom, without realizing I was there. Once she realized she had intruded on my urination, she sat down on the side of the bathtub and said she would wait for me to finish. I flushed, and before I could even start to wash my hands, she had sat down on the toilet to pee. I began to talk to her casually, apparently not bothered at all by the fact that this complete stranger was using the toilet in front of me. After leaving the bathroom, one of my friends was sitting outside the door and asked me who I was talking to in the bathroom. I explained to her the situation of the black woman, and my friend just looked at me like I was crazy. Along with the cigarettes, the people in the living room, the dream I thought was reality, and the woman in the bathroom, I also saw and talked to about 15 other people that did not exist. Some were in my car, others were in my room, and so forth. These people were very real to me at the point in which i saw them. I also was consistently attacked by spiders, and kept asking why it was not dark yet outside. This was, by far, the MOST vivid and realistic hallucination experience i have ever had. Any other time i have hallucinated, i have only seen things differently, ie- swirls of color, bright swirls of light, shadowfigures, etc. Over all, i saw and talked to about 30 people that were not real. None of them had i ever seen before in my life, but i remember these people being so real that the next day i was completely surprised to hear that no one but my two friends had talked to me on the day of my experience. An unforgettable day, yes, but in retrospect i now know exactly what it is like to be completey insane.

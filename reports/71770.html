中文译文 致幻剂

只有135磅. 不幸的是,我不能回忆起我与5-MeO-MiPT的大部分经验的细节,因为我在不太长的时间内 (大约10次) 摄入了它. 两到三个月). 总结某些剂量对我的主要影响应该很容易. 所有剂量: 在15分钟内就能感觉到第一种效应. 即使使用12毫克, 身体负荷也很小. 2 - 3毫克 (6或7次经验): 在这种剂量范围内, 每种音乐都更令人愉快, 基本上是清醒的, 很容易出现在公共场合, 总的来说, 焦虑也会减轻, 情绪也会稍微好转. 身体上也有一种轻微的兴奋, 让我想要深呼吸. 但感觉比大麻清洁得多. 视觉效果几乎不存在. 在5小时后睡觉是很困难的, 但有可能. 4~6mg (经验是2~3次): 所有从前范围的影响都被放大. 这种积极的情绪转变仍然很普遍. 让熟悉的歌曲获得全新的意义, 仿佛重新被发现. 我常常会感受到抽象的印象 (比如听一首歌的时候, 我可以清楚地想象一个平面, 放松变得更像是心态而不是身体, 感觉有很多能量在流动. 我偶尔会因为快乐而发, 脸上还会有巨大的微笑. 深呼吸往往会导致这种情况. 孤独的性活动会带来非常令人满意的体验, 高潮会非常强烈. 没有对生活的洞察力, 虽然有时可以感受到洞察力和与人的联系. 尤其是在这个层面, 艺术的灵感似乎出现了, 虽然我通常使用黑白, 虽然公众游览仍然不那么不舒服. 没有增加与自然的联系或欣赏经验, 摄入后最多7小时难以获得睡眠. 尽管在5毫克的旅行中有一个显著的例外. 我坐着,从我的眼角我看到一块绳子在地板上移动. 我直接看着它,它是完美的静止. 我看了看另一方面, 马上线在地板上扭曲 - 一个绝对明确的OEV. 我再次直接看着它 - 完美静止. 这一举动成功地重复, 直到我失去了兴趣. 12mg (经验一次) 像以前一样, 但身体的影响和石头变得足够强大, 视觉效果变得明显,表面有轻微的波纹和轻微的颜色强化. 虽然可能有点活跃,但CEV并不比4-6mg范围明显多. 积极的情绪转变会导致脸上永久的微笑. 沟通并不难. 睡眠和之前的剂量范围一样困难. 这次经历有几个值得注意的特点. 极度减轻焦虑, 以至于通常会让我感到非常不舒服的事情, 我走过一棵巨大的树, 我非常敬畏它 - - 它看起来既不友好,也不威胁,只是极其强大. 我只能一路看到它, 因为我必须跟上小组的步伐. 结论 虽然我不认为我会在不久的将来再次获得任何. 虽然我的朋友们都喜欢它. 我最喜欢的剂量范围是2-3毫克, 实际上,我会说这是体验中最好的方面, 也可能是我购买更多音乐的唯一原因 (尽管我可能会被与另一个人进行性活动说服). 虽然有趣,也让人放松, 我喜欢寻找后者. 其他: 5-MeO-MIPT (287):音乐讨论 (22),回顾 / 摘要 (11),各种 (28)

A Summary of Experiences 5-MeO-MiPT by Thaumaturgist Citation:   Thaumaturgist. "A Summary of Experiences: An Experience with 5-MeO-MiPT (exp71770)". Erowid.org . Jul 29, 2008. erowid.org/exp/71770 DOSE: 4.0 mg oral 5-MeO-MIPT (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 135 lb Age at time of experiences: 21 Weight: approx. 135 lbs. Gender: Male Unfortunately, I cannot recall the details of most of my experiences with 5-MeO-MiPT offhand, as I have ingested it somewhere around 10 times in not too long of a time period (approx. 2 or 3 months). It should be easy for me to summarize the primary effects that certain doses have on me, though. All doses: First effects are felt in about 15 minutes. Very little body load, even at 12mg. 2-3mg (experienced 6 or 7 times): At this dose range, there is a definite relaxing effect. Music of all sorts is more enjoyable, and an ease of fantasy is present. Mostly clearheaded and definitely easy to be out in public on - interactions with other people is not an issue. General relief from anxieties and a minor positive mood push is also present. A minor body high is also present and very pleasurable. It also opens up my airways and makes me want to breathe much deeper than normal. A general 'stoned' feeling is also notable, but feels much cleaner than cannabis. Visual effects are practically non-existent. Sleep after 5 hours is difficult, but possible. 4-6mg (experienced 2 or 3 times): All effects from the previous range are amplified. The positive mood shift is still prevalent. Music and other sounds open up, causing familiar songs to take on an entirely new meaning as if being rediscovered. Visualization to music is readily achieved with eyes closed, and I often feel abstract impressions (such as when listening to one song, I could imagine clearly a flat surface that would occasionally push upwards in some spots as if a needle was poking from below every time a piano note sounded). The relaxation turns into more of a mind-set than a body thing at this point, where it feels like a lot of energy is present and flowing. I'll occasionally shudder from the pleasure and have a huge smile plastered on my face. Taking deep breaths often leads to this. Solitary sexual activities result in a very satisfying experience, with orgasm being markedly intense. No insight into life is gained, though insight and connections with people is sometimes felt. Particularly at this level, inspiration for art seems to occur, and while I normally work with black and white, this substance demands use of color. The stoned feeling from the previous level becomes much more intense, though public excursions still aren't uncomfortable. No increased connection with or appreciation of nature is experienced as I feel on most other psychedelics. Sleep is difficult to obtain for up to 7 hours post-ingestion. As far as visual effects go, they are still almost non-existent, though there was one notable exception during a 5mg trip. I was sitting down and out of the corner of my eye I saw a piece of string on the floor move. When I looked directly at it and it was perfectly still. I looked back the other direction and immediately the string was writhing on the floor - an absolutely clear OEV. I looked directly at it again - perfectly still. This was repeated with success until I lost interest. 12mg (experienced once) As before, but body effects and stoning become strong enough that walking is a bit odd (though not difficult). Visual effects become apparent, with mild rippling on surfaces and mild color intensification. CEVs are not much more apparent than at the 4-6mg range, though are perhaps a bit more active. Positive mood-shift results in a permanent smile plastered on the face. Communication is not difficult. Sleep was just as difficult as previous dose range. Specific to this experience, there were a couple of things of note. Extreme reduction of anxiety, to the point that things that would normally make me feel very uncomfortable were smile-worthy (such as a car slowing down to watch me and my group of friends walking down the street late at night). At one point, I walked past a very large oak tree that seemed to be radiating strength and power. I was very much in awe of it - it did not appear either friendly nor menacing, just extremely powerful. Unfortunately, I only saw it in passing as I had to keep up with the rest of the group. Conclusion 5-MeO-MiPT has been an enjoyable substance, though I do not think I will be acquiring any in the near future again. It doesn't have much depth to it (though most of my friends love it). My favorite dose range has been 2-3mg, where the experience is more of a coloring of the day than a commanding experience. It has proven very useful when I wish to really enjoy music - as a matter of fact, I would say that is the best aspect of the experience, and probably the only reason I would purchase any more (though I experiencing sexual activities with another person could possibly persuade me). It's fun, it's relaxing, but it's not enlightening or particularly exceptional. I like to look for the latter.

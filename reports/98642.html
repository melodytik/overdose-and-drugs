于是我开始倒出一点多的小指长度. 我在浴室里吸了这句话, 却没有立刻感觉到. 我问他是否知道这件事, 经过30分钟,我开始感觉像LSD一样. 他带我去火车站,带我回办公室, 我们走下楼梯, 一切都变成了水. 我们继续步行到他的车里, 一切都非常明亮和多彩, 随着我们进入他的车, 整个仪表板开始变形和融化, 我开始听到一个的声音, 像你曾经去除灰尘, 这也持续了旅程的其余部分. 我没有提及我还没有告诉他我做了, 因为我不想让他发疯. 他停在当地一家酒店, 进去给我买了一些香烟, 他回来时,我已经出汗了. 我无法真正理解发生了什么事. 我周围的一切继续变得越来越糟糕. 我说:"我通常不喜欢用迷幻药, 因为我爱我自己, 他问我做了多少, 我告诉他"我的小指的长度超过一点" 他问你确定不是DMT吗? 我说是的,我知道DMT是什么. 他说好了,你可以过夜,你会好起来的. 他没有告诉我我大概有80到200次. 他说"别担心,我们可以开车到处去,你可以有一个愉快的旅行"我说"不,我不认为你明白这很糟糕,一切都在融化,我听到很大的声音,让我们回到你的房子好. 所以我们开车回家. 我试图保持冷静,但情况变得越来越疯狂. 我开始看到一个沙漠, 我看到这只狮子, 他着我, 他开始跑得越来越近, 随着它撞到地面, 它就像水泡玉米一样, 成粉红色的糖, 我开始把它拉出来问我表哥是什么样子他也看到这个他说什么我说的是从你的嘴里出来. 他笑了笑,继续开车,我们进入他的车库,并告诉我在那里等待. 他要去买一些牛奶和糖和枕头. 我以为他还在车里, 周围的一切都是白色的背景. 警方跑到我们身边, 他一直试图告诉我把它藏起来 (此时他已经离开了, 他们走到我们身边,问你们有没有吸毒. 他们的身体和我的身体都变成了金属, 我看着我的表哥, 在这个白色的房间里, 我在里面写着"世界末日". 我知道发生了什么不好的事情. 我表哥告诉我他只离开了大约两分钟, 他回来了, 试图让我喝牛奶和糖, 我不能喝他给了我一支雪茄, 他试过所有这些东西大约五分钟后, 我开始发作, 他用一只手抱着我,用另一只手开车, 在最后一个十字路口我整个身体了我没有脉. 他到医院说我的表兄弟死了我的表兄弟死了. 安全人员问他发生了什么事,他说过量. 警卫告诉他保持冷静, 拿出我口袋里的毒品. 我身上有大约八分之一的大麻. 我很确定这次旅行还在继续, 我不记得昏迷期间的任何旅行, 除了最后一部分, 某种存在走近它, 可能是我理解的更高的力量, 它摇了摇头, 我到底是我的表哥. 他从嘴里说出一些反复的字, 围绕着我, 我意识到它说的是"DMT"而不是简称. 他还没有得到它之前, 他说:"这就是为什么他们叫它DMT", 我从昏迷中醒来, 看到我几个月没见过的家人. 我很高兴我有这样的经历, 不是因为它可能会杀人, 其他: 25C-NBOMe (540):小组 (2-9) (17),火车残骸和旅行灾害 (7)

Jan 28, 2013. erowid.org/exp/98642 DOSE: insufflated 25C-NBOMe (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 130 lb So my cousin came across a this substance called 2cc NBOMe. I had no idea what this substance was when he told me about it nor did I know how much to take. So I proceeded to pour out a little more then a pinky length. I snorted this line in the bathroom and it didnt hit me right away I had asked him if he knew anything about it and he said he didnt just that it was some crazy psychedelic he had came across and had yet to try it. About 30 minutes went by and I got this LSD type stomach feeling. It was time for him to take me to the train station back to the OC so we walked down his stairs and everything turned into a water type substance I don’t think I could really say there was even a come up it just kinda hit me really hard all at once. We continued to walk to his car and everything was very bright and colorful kind of like a pink blue type color I like to use the term blurple from taking DMT a couple months before. As we got in his car the whole dashboard started to morph and melt along with everything else inside and outside the car. Once we got on the freeway I started hearing a wimping type noise like if you’ve ever done dust off it sounded a lot like that. This continued through the rest of the trip too. So we get close to the train station and I failed to mention that I hadn’t told him I did it yet cause I didn’t want him to flip out. He stopped at a local liquor store and went in to get me some cigs and left me in the car. When he came back I was in a full sweat and pretty much freaking out. I couldn’t really understand what was going on everything around me continued to get worse and worse I looked at him and then I told him. I said “I'm not usually one to bitch out on psychedelics cause I love me but I'm bitching out now I think I took too much of that 2cc stuff' and he started to panic. He asked how much I had did and I told him 'a little over the length of my pinky” immediately I could tell he was worried. He said are you sure it wasn’t DMT I said yeah I know wat DMT is like theres no way its been going on for about 45 minutes. He said ok u can just stay the night you will be fine. He had failed to tell me I probably took about 80-200 hits of it. He said “don’t worry we can just drive around and u can have a good trip” I said “no I don’t think you understand this is bad everything is melting and I'm hearing loud noises, lets just go back to your house ok”. So we proceeded to drive back home. On the drive back I tried to just stay calm and it just kept getting crazier I started to see a desert with wild animals running in the horizon it was quite beautiful to be honest. I saw this one lion in particular and he was staring at me he started running closer and closer as it got about 2 feet it morphed into a Subaru and started to race us as we got off the freeway to make a left turn it flipped over off the overpass. As it hit the ground it popped into pink cotton candy like a kettle corn of popcorn along with everything else around me and continued to grow out of my body like hair. I started to pull it out asking my cousin wat it was like he was seeing this too he said whats what I said its coming out of your mouth. He laughed and continued to keep driving we got into his garage and told me to wait there. He was going to go get some milk with sugar and a pillow. As he left I thought he was still in the car and everything around us was a white background. As I was sitting there I thought he threw some cocaine on me and these cops ran up to us and he kept trying to tell me to hide it (he was gone at this point to remind you). They walked up to us and said are you guys doing any drugs. At at this point their bodies and my body's turned into metal and I looked at my cousin who wasn’t even there and from the outside in his body was peeling like sheets of metal and the noises turned into loud screeching noises. Once his body was done peeling then it was my turn and before I started peeling in big letters in this white room I was in it said END OF THE WORLD. As I started to peel I knew something bad was happening to me. My cousin told me he was only gone for about two minutes and he came back and was trying to make me drink the milk with sugar and I couldn’t drink he gave me a cig and I couldn’t even smoke it and he said I kept saying “ I don’t know what I'm doing” over and over and over. About five minutes after he was trying all these things I was going into a full seizure so he got in the car and drove as fast as he could to the hospital about five minute away. He was holding me with one arm and driving with the other hand saying “Please don’t die please don’t die”. Around the last intersection my whole body went limp I had no pulse. He got to the hospital saying my cousins dead my cousins dead. The security asked what was going on and he said an overdose. The security guard told him to stay calm and take any drugs out of my of my pocket. I had about an eighth of marijuana on me. They induced me into a coma and I'm pretty sure the trip was still going on for the four days I was in a coma. I don’t remember any of my trip during the coma except the last part where I had walked up a cloud of stairs guessing it was the famous “stairway to heaven” and I got to the top and waited at this gate. Some kind of being had walked up to it probably the higher power of my understanding and it shook its head and pointed back down. When I got to the bottom there was my cousin. Some words came out of his mouth backwards wrapped around me from the point of me to him was what had seemed to be about 100 yards. Once the word wrapped around me I realized it said DMT not abbreviated. Before it got to him he said 'and that’s why they call it DMT' and snapped. The words went into his snap and I woke up out of the coma seeing all of my family there which I hadn’t seen in months. This was an experience I’m glad I had in a way not the fact that it could have killed but that now I know don’t sniff things we don’t know about kids.

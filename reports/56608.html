经过几次试用高达50毫克的剂量和相对温和的经验, 我把100毫克放进一个蔬菜里, 我刚刚下班, 有一些家务要做, 知道我在之前的试验中经历过的刺激性质, 时间+30分钟 - 开始感觉到能量涌现,能量循环,开始感到寒. 时间+60分钟 - 感觉上升有点落后,也许我在下降之前吃得太快. 尽管如此,效果仍在增加, 时间+75分钟 - 好了,现在我做得更好了. 在头皮和四肢上明显的, 似乎随着活动而增加. 我偶尔需要休息一下, 我肯定不想碰到一个同事或我的. 是很好的和相当稳定的在这一点上, 提醒我很多的乙像声, 但在之前的经验中, 时间+120分钟 - 没有明显的变化,这是高峰时间,和寒丰富,虽然水很好,但根本不需要食物. 我为我的女儿做了一些麦克和奶酪, 品尝它, 它的味道是最平淡的麦克和奶酪... 没有任何迹象表明我在较低剂量试验中经历过短暂的下降, 也许有一个门需要被越过, 这次我做到了. 身体上的感觉也许有所减弱, 折叠衣服让我大汗淋. 尽管如此, 至少令人愉快的效果在过去两小时内或多或少减弱了. 我仍然清醒,仍在流汗, 仍然有点, 我女朋友说除了我太出汗之外, 她看不出, 经过几个小时的愉快, 效果已经降级为厌食症, 失眠, 和轻微的动荡. 我只想睡觉, 却在床上醒了好几个小时. 因此,最初的短期效应仅仅是低剂量. 我可以说100毫克对我来说可能已经足够了. 我可能会再尝试一下, 但我担心自己无法冷静下来, 如果不是因为俱乐部和狂欢节的极端炎热, 我可能还建议不要与其他兴奋剂一起使用, 无论如何,这就是为2-AI现在,我们将把它放回魔术盒, 其他: 2-氨基 (291):一般 (1),小组 (2-9) (17)

Oct 13, 2006. erowid.org/exp/56608 DOSE: 100 mg oral 2-Aminoindan (capsule) BODY WEIGHT: 200 lb So after a several attempts with dosages up to 50mgs and relatively mild experiences I decided that not only was this substance seemingly lacking in the reportedly potentially 'dangerous' potency levels but that I wanted to really take it for a solid test drive. I measured out 100mgs into a veg-cap and down the hatch. I had just gotten off work and had some housework to do and knowing the stimulant properties that I had experienced in earlier trials, I thought it an appropriate setting to take it for a spin. T + 30min - Starting to feel the come up, energy circulating and a few little chills beginning. T + 60min - The come-up feels like its lagging a bit, perhaps I ate too soon before dropping. Still, effect increasing, but slowly. T + 75min - Ok, now I'm going a little better. Defined tingles on scalp and in extremities, seems to increase with activity. I occasionally have to take breaks from cleaning to just breathe and be with the feeling. T + 90min - Hot...Sweaty...a little scattered, definately would not want to run into a co-worker or my Mom. Tingles are nice and pretty consistent at this point, reminding me a lot of the ephedrine like buzz but as noted in previous experiences, a little cleaner feeling, no palpitations, nausea, etc...feeling pretty good. T + 120min - No marked changes, this is peak time, tingles and chills abound, no want of food at all though water is good. I make some Mac and Cheese for my girl and taste it and it tastes like the most bland mac and cheese ever...thought she reports that 'it is good'. T + 180min - Still zooming along, no sign of the short come down experienced with my lower dosage trials, perhaps there is a threshhold to be crossed in there and this time I did. The physical sensations are trailing off a little perhaps, or at least becoming less frequent. Folding laundry is making me sweat profusely. T + 300min - Still so hot...but the effects, at least the pleasant one, have more or less subsided over the last two hours. I am still alert, still sweating, and still geeked a little bit, but I could probably deal with my Mom or a co-worker at this point. My Girlfriend says she can't tell except that I am so sweaty, but that otherwise I am acting normal, if not just a little too awake for this time of day for what is typical with me. I began this experiment at 4:30 with first effect noted at 5:00ish...after several hours of pleasant, the effects have degraded to anorexia, sleeplessness, and mild agitation. I just want to sleep but I stay awake in bed for hours...I got up to pee and get water at about 1:00am and fell to sleep shortly after returning to bed. So that puts the short term effects initially experienced as just low dosage. I can say that 100mgs was probably enough for me. I might try more at some point but I am concerned about my inability to cool off, even with all the windows open on a cool night with my shirt off, simply folding laundry was like a work-out at the gym...lots of sweat and heat. My previous assumption that it might be fun for dancing would be true if not for the extreme heat at most clubs and raves, so I would recommend test driving outside of such a heat prone environment before taking it out to the clubs. I would probably also recommend not using it with other stimulants unless perhaps as a booster for MDMA or something, as the body feelings might be quite pleasant in combination. Anyway, that's it for 2-AI for now, we'll stow it back in the magic box until a time when it might be useful comes along.

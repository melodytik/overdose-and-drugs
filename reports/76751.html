我之前曾两次尝试过这种化合物, 8毫克和10毫克, 希望这个剂量 (15mg) 能更好. 我在一小时前吃了一顿小饭, 现在我正在做一杯咖啡, 加快开始, 卷起一根烟, 半小时内抽完, + - 0.15 - 发现一些胃部不适. 没有什么严重的, 只是一种类似的感觉, 我也开始感到高兴和轻微的高兴. 开始感觉很有沟通力, 我写这篇文章时, 我现在要抽一支烟, + - 0.45 - 视觉的第一个迹象正在出现. 它们似乎很轻微地移动和波动. 我对时间和距离的感觉也开始受到影响. 时间似乎过得很慢, 像走路和打字这样的简单任务变得越来越困难. 现在是我最喜欢的一部电影的时间了, 这部电影的声音和音乐都被降低了. 我开始感觉到一些刺激,我发现自己一直在拍拍我的脚. 视觉和头部空间也开始变得更加激烈. 电视上的云层似乎正在膨胀, 思考变得越来越困难. 我也注意到乐队录制的音乐对我来说听起来比电子音乐更好, 在音乐中表达的情感似乎更强烈. 另外,我开始感到胸部有点紧张, 发, + - 1.15 - 我在周围视力中不断发现虹色的闪光,颜色比平时更亮, 电子音乐似乎和现场音乐一样好 (甚至比现场音乐好). 我可以看出这是一个伟大的俱乐部药物在这个剂量. 黑暗DnB带来了强烈的跳舞的冲动, 总体而言,效果比8-10毫克强得多, 我可能会允许一周左右来防止任何耐受性问题然后尝试20-25毫克推动更强烈的迷幻效应. + - 2.00 - 这种化学物质绝对是最好的享受与可跳舞的音乐. 它似乎比任何其他东西更适合作为一种兴奋剂. 我觉得自从那个小时以来, 我肯定已经在Plataeu了, 我刚刚打字时, 画面上出现了物体的移动, 我也注意到, 任何事物要花几秒钟以上的时间都变得更加困难. 刺激效果似乎已经减弱, 视觉效果变得更加强烈, 物体变形,发光,颜色轻微变化. 也有更多的痕迹. 我刚刚在电话里和一个朋友说话, 这引起了相当大的混乱, 2C-B-FLY开始让我想起它的表妹 Bromo-DragonFLY 在低剂量. 现在是时候抛弃可跳舞的音乐, 视觉效果和头部空间现在都处于相当强烈但可控的水平. 我认为在这个阶段, 对于那些经验较少的旅行者来说, + - 4.00 - 视觉和头部空间仍然很强,但开始略有减少. 看着它们在我的视觉中会产生鲜的斑点, 这些斑点不断移动和变形. 如果我想继续这样做, 我会考虑在这个时候再给5-10毫克. + - 5.00 - 除了短暂的混乱时刻外,头部空间已经显著放宽. 我仍然留下了微妙的视觉效果和一个漂亮的身体. 我觉得自己已经恢复到原来的状态,我认为自己可以开车, 结论: 这剂量比8-10毫克更具幻觉效果, 我认为我可能会在不久的将来尝试18-20毫克, 任何更多的我认为头部空间将开始变得无法管理, 但我们会看到. 我还剩下大约85毫克, 足以进行进一步实验. 其他: 2C-B-Fly (350):音乐讨论 (22),一般 (1),一个人 (16)

Feb 10, 2009. erowid.org/exp/76751 DOSE: T+ 0:00 15 mg oral 2C-B-Fly (powder / crystals) T+ 0:00 1 cup oral Coffee T+ 0:30 1 cig. smoked Cannabis BODY WEIGHT: 150 lb I have tried this compound twice before at 8mg and 10mg and found both to be good overall, but lacking visually. Hopefully this dose (15mg) will be better. I ate a small meal about an hour ago, so my stomach should now be empty enough to not hinder the performance of this compound too much. + - 0.00 - Ingested 15mg of 2C-B-FLY in a 'bomb' made from half a rolling paper and I'm now making a cup of coffee to speed up the onset and rolling a joint to smoke in half an hour as I find this always enhances other drugs. + - 0.15 - Some stomach discomfort noted. Nothing serious, just a similar feeling I get after ingesting MDMA. Also, starting to feel the head high and slight euphoria coming on. + - 0.30 - Beginning to feel very communicative, words flow very easily as I write this. Going to smoke the joint now to help bring on the effects. + - 0.45 - The first signs of visuals are appearing. Objects appear to move and ripple very slightly. My sense of time and distance are also beginning to be affected. Time seems to be passing very slowly and simple tasks such as walking and typing are becomming more difficult. It's time for an old favourite of mine, a movie with the sound turned down and music. * Music sounds much 'bigger' than normal and feels as if it surrounds me. + - 1.00 - I'm starting to feel a bit of stimulation now, I keep finding myself tapping my foot. The visuals and head space are also starting to become slightly more intense. Clouds on the TV appear to be bulging and thinking clearly is becoming difficult. Also noted that music recorded by bands sounds much better to me than electronic music, which is unusual for me on psychedelics. Emotions conveyed in music seem much stronger somehow too. Also, beginning to feel a slight tightness in my chest and shivers reminiscent of piperazines. + - 1.15 - I keep noticing flashes of neon colour in my peripheral vision and colours appear brighter than usual although visuals are still only slight. I gave electronic music another try and it appears to be just as good as (if not, better than) live music. I could see this being a great club drug at this dose. + - 1.30 - Dark DnB is bringing on a strong urge to dance which is worrying as I'm a firm believer that dancing on your own is very, very sad. Overall the effects can be felt much more strongly than with 8-10mg, but this compound still only has a slight psychedelic edge. I will probably allow a week or so to prevent any tolerance issues then try 20-25mg to push for stronger psychedelic effects. + - 2.00 - This chemical is definitely best enjoyed with danceable music. It seems better suited for use as a stimulant than anything else. I feel like I've definately been at a plataeu since the hour point, haven't noticed anything worth mentioning since then. ...Just as I typed that objects started moving around the screen and I'm starting to notice definate tracers. Also noticing that concentrating on anything for more than a few seconds has definately become more difficult. + - 2.30 - The stimulant effects seem to have subsided and visuals are becoming more intense, this is more like what I was hoping for. Objects are warping, glowing and changing colour slightly. Also, more tracers are appearing. I just spoke to a friend on the phone and it caused quite a lot of confusion, I think I was coherent but the head space has definately intensified. 2C-B-FLY is starting to remind me of its cousin Bromo-DragonFLY at low doses. It's time to ditch danceable music in favour of Shpongle. + - 3.00 - Visuals and head space are both at a nice fairly intense but managable level now. As much as this felt like a great club drug earlier I think it would be easy for things to become overwhelming in that enviroment at this stage, especially for less experienced trippers. + - 4.00 - Both visuals and head space are both still strongly present but starting to dwindle slightly. Lights appear to be emitting neon glows and looking at them is causing brightly coloured spots in my vision, these spots keep moving around and morphing. If I wanted to keep this going I would consider an 5-10mg redose at this point. + - 5.00 - The head space has let up significantly except for brief moments of confusion. I'm still left with subtle visuals and a nice body high. + - 6.00 - I feel almost back to baseline now, I would consider myself fit to drive and feel I will be able to sleep tonight without much trouble. Conclusion: This dose was much more psychedelic than 8-10mg, although it could still have been more visual. I think I will probably try 18-20mg in the near future, any more and I think the head space would begin to become unmanageable, but we'll see. I still have roughly 85mg left so there's still enough for further experimentation.

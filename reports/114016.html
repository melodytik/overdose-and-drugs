关于GABA药物的回顾 首先,一些背景. 我曾经尝试过各种各样的药物, 我发现自己在吸食LSD与Tramadol和Codeine, 由于工作中脊髓受伤, 他通过所有主要的止痛药, 似乎没有什么能帮助他, 所以他完全可以用剩余的药物帮助我缓解轻微的疼痛. 2016年左右, 禁毒局开始严厉打击阿片类药物, 令我父亲的疼痛管理诊所吓坏, 这并没有真正影响我父亲, 因为药物一开始就没有起作用. 经过一两个星期, 他找到了另一家专门从事脊椎损伤的诊所, 由于阿片类药物危机仍在肆虐, 开始每天四次服用150毫克, 这些塑料感的马药很快成为我藏品中的支柱, GABA活性药物与典型的阿片类止痛药有很大的不同. 而不是一个强烈的,高兴的节点, 加巴丁更像是MDMA. 服用药物后一个小时, 我感觉到一波稳定的能量从我的胸部散发出来, 这种状态会持续6个小时, 实际的身体疼痛的好处很小, 我每次服用3克的零星剂量, 感觉就变成了一种半幻体验. 格巴丁是一种真正的高兴性麻醉剂, 然而,我父亲的痛苦仍在继续. 医生将他的加巴替换成普雷加巴林, 也被称为Lyrica. 随着他的转变,我也转变了. 我开始在与 gabapentin相同的水平上使用pregabalin, 在150毫克的剂量下, 能量会轻微增加, 经过大约450毫克的药物中毒, 让我头脑清醒了8个小时. 我感觉上帝亲自用神圣的宁静触摸了我. 从外人的角度来看, 事情是不同的. 妻子多次表示我开始语, 随机失去平衡感. 普雷加巴林似乎对运动技能和一般认知有着集中作用,并提供了粉红色的世界观. 我开始服用越来越多的药, 我从来没有感觉到同样的高兴的满足感. 耐受性的增长速度很快, 我开始追逐白色囊用BuPap (布塔尔比塔尔,乙氨基和咖啡因) 和酒精, 服用阿片类药物和吸食大麻与药丸几乎似乎减轻了这些GABA药物典型的高兴"波". 吸烟的经验是完全与之前的经验相仿, 总的来说,我对这些物质有很多乐趣. 让我足够快乐, 成为我妻子需要的男人, 宽容是我迅速建立起来的障碍, 我不知道我是否能再次体验这些药物给我的真正幸福, 如果有足够的时间, 龙也许会回来, 和我再一次跳舞. 其他: 药品 - 普雷加巴林 (418),药品 - gabapentin (183):不适用 (38),成和习惯性 (10),回顾 / 总结 (11)

Mar 25, 2020. erowid.org/exp/114016 DOSE: 600 - 2400 mg oral Pharms - Gabapentin 150 - 1200 mg oral Pharms - Pregabalin BODY WEIGHT: 220 lb A Retrospective Glance of the GABA Drugs First, some background. I’ve dabbled in a vast expanse of pharmaceuticals, ranging from stimulants, to benzos, and everything in between. I’ve found myself ingesting LSD with Tramadol and Codeine, so I can definitely lay claim to experience. A few years ago, my father was disabled due to a spinal injury sustained on the job. He worked his way through all of the major analgesics, starting with codeine and ending with fentanyl patches. Nothing seemed to help him, so he was entirely fine with helping me out with minor aches and pains with his surplus medication. Around 2016, the DEA began seriously cracking down on opioids, which scared my father’s pain management clinic into discontinuing most prescriptions. This didn’t really affect my father, as the medications weren’t working to begin with. After a week or two, he found another clinic that specialized in spinal injuries, and began to attend a monthly checkup, followed by a visit to a nurse practitioner for a prescription. With the opioid crisis still raging, this particular clinic began writing scripts for Gabapentin and Pregabalin for pain management. Starting with 150mgs four times daily, they worked him up to 2400mgs daily via 800mg pressed tablets. These plastic-feeling horse pills quickly became a mainstay in my stash, and proved to be invaluable. GABA-ergic drugs are DRASTICALLY different from the typical mu-opioid analgesics. Rather than a strong, euphoric “nod”, Gabapentin was more relatable to MDMA. An hour after dosing, I felt a steady wave of energy radiate from my chest, and noticed music and lights felt clearer. This state would continue for up to 6 hours, constantly surging in and out like a synthetic tide of joy. The benefits for actual physical pain were minor, but I felt so damn ecstatic it didn’t even matter. I worked up to sporadic doses of nearly 3 grams at a time, at which point the feeling plateaued into a quasi-psychedelic experience. Gabapentin was a genuinely euphoric intoxicant, in a unique way. However, my father’s pain continued. After a while, the doctor swapped his Gabapentin for Pregabalin, also known as Lyrica. As he switched over, so did I. I began experimenting with Pregabalin on the same level as Gabapentin, and found it to be a much different experience. At 150mgs, it’s a slight boost of energy, followed by a solid sense of well-being for around 4-6 hours. Around 450mgs, the intoxicating effects went into overdrive, and cleared my head for around 8 hours. Thoughts became fluid, waves of euphoria washed over me at random intervals, and I felt like god himself had touched me with divine serenity. From the outsiders perspective, things were different. My wife stated numerous times that I began slurring my words, and that I would randomly lose all sense of balance. Pregabalin seemed to have a concentrated effect on motor skills and general cognizance, and offered a rose tinted view of the world. I began to take more and more, working my way up to 1200mgs sporadically. After the first dose over a gram, I never again felt the same euphoric contentedness. The tolerance buildup was fast, and harsh. I began chasing the white capsules with BuPap (butalbital, acetaminophen, and caffeine) and alcohol, in a feeble attempt to reconnect with the once lauded effects I had experienced prior. Taking opiates and smoking weed with the pills almost seemed to dampen its euphoric “waves” that seemed typical for these GABA drugs. Smoking cigarettes while peaking tended to push the experience to the absolute closest facsimile of its prior strength, but failed to truly aid in my chasing the dragon. All in all, I have plenty of fun with these substances. They help curb the edge off a rough day in retail, and keep me happy enough to be the man my wife requires when the kids throw fits. Tolerance is a barrier I built quickly, and it takes astounding patience to whittle it back down to size. I don’t know if I’ll ever get to experience the true bliss these drugs provided me in the beginning again, but I keep faith. With enough time, maybe the dragon will return, and dance with me once more.

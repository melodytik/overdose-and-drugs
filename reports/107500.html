时间+0 (5:45pm) 下降了100毫克. 感觉有点昏昏欲睡,可能只是实际的疲劳,而不是醉酒. T+1:55 确定的效果,相当轻微. 头部仍然完全清晰, 但感觉明显变化. 我觉得自己也在做梦. 这部电影是一系列在梦中进行的哲学对话. 我很好地理解这些复杂的对话的逻辑. 绝对比MXE更清醒. 强烈的身体高与显著的放松和平静的感觉. 经典的分离性情绪, 虽然在身体上不如MXE那么高兴. 给了我更多的能量, 但也给旅程带来了很多混乱. 试图和一些朋友出去, 但对正在发生的事情相当混乱, 外面的天气很冷 (虽然我一点都不介意) 所以大家都跑回去, 我觉得大部分的高是从2C-B的凸起而来. 我认为现在埃芬尼丁已经完全消失了. 我只是在刺激2C-B旅行的结束. 我看了几个X档案与我的SO. 吃了一黑色素, 很快就入睡了, 睡了好10个小时. 总的来说看起来很不错, 我不认为我会再订购它, 我还剩下一点点, 我可能会把它和一个好的旅行混合在一起, 不像有些人说的那样, 感觉不那么刺激, 其他: 2C-B (52), 乙 (689):小组 (2-9) (17),组合 (3),第一次 (2),一般 (1)

Dec 7, 2015. erowid.org/exp/107500 DOSE: T+ 0:00 100 mg oral Ephenidine (powder / crystals) T+ 4:00 7 mg insufflated 2C-B (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 150 lb T+0 (5:45pm) Dropped 100mg. T+0:45 Feel slightly droopy, could just be actual tiredness though, not intoxicated. T+1:55 Definite effects, fairly mild. still completely clear headed but changes notable in sensation. T+3:20 Watching waking life, pretty weird because I feel like I'm dreaming too. The movie was a series of philosophical conversations in a dream. I was able to follow the logic of these somewhat complicated conversations fine. Definitely a lot more clearheaded than MXE. Strong body high with significant relaxation and a feeling of calmness. Classic dissociative feelings, less physically euphoric than MXE though. T+4:00 Snorted ~7mg of 2C-B, gave me some more energy but also added a lot of confusion to the trip. Tried going out with some friend but was fairly confused about what was going on and didn't have a ton of energy. The place we went ended up being at capacity and it was freezing outside (though it didn't bother me at all) so everyone ran back and I followed. T+5:30 Definitely less dissociated, think most of my high is from the bump of 2C-B now. T+6:45 I think the ephenidine is completely gone now. I am just in the stimulation that is the end of a 2C-B trip. Watched a couple episodes of the X Files with my SO. T+8:45 Took a ton of melatonin and fell asleep pretty quickly, slept a good 10 hours. Thoughts: Overall seems like a pretty good drug, don't think I'll order it again as dissociatives aren't my fave, but was worth a try. I have a bit left and will probably mix it with a good trip, maybe 20mg of 2C-B or 4-AcO-DMT. Didn't feel super stimulating as some have said but definitely less of a completely fucked in the mind feeling than MXE.

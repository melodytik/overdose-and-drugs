美国 在过去的十年里, 我曾轻微尝试过各种各样的精神活性物质 (如可卡因,阿片类药物,类药物,美) 但自从八年前我第一次接触这种药物"家族"以来, 我所说的远远的经历, 涉及到半个早晨的光种子, 经历了36个小时的惊幻觉, 脱离, 和其他可怕的效应, 我对LSD和类似的研究化学物质产生了兴趣, 我从朋友那里买了25I-NBOM, 尽管我做了大量的研究和与那些曾经或正在服用酸药的朋友进行了无数的谈话, - 0:20:我口服20mg的Adderall IR (对于我来说这是正常的剂量,因为我每天服用大约60mg). + 0:00:我把一块25i-NBOMe的纸张放在我的舌头上. 慢慢地溶解, 随着轻微的麻木感和微弱的苦味在我的嘴里散去. 我避免吞下我的唾液大约十五分钟, 现在,我吞下了唾液和剩下的纤维. 旅行的第一种迹象是轻微的焦虑和脑右半球轻微的头痛. 我吸入两次强烈大麻,然后躺在沙瓦萨那, 这是一种姿势/阿萨那, 也被称为"尸体姿势". 房间里唯一的光源来自闪光飞盘的变色LED. 莫扎特的交响曲在背景中轻声演奏. 我觉得平静和沉思, 我发现自己有点迷失方向, 这种感觉就像在长时间的睡眠后醒来,发现自己在一个意想不到的地方. 我很快就消失了,因为我专注于我上面的天花板. 我开始体验OEV与碎形形成和瓦跨越天花板. 这让我想起了我在书中看到的"魔眼"视觉效果, 例如,碎形在改变形状时似乎会以波形形式辐射. 在天花板上形成的色彩是流动的, 不同的, 真正迷人. 这是一个特别美丽的星座, 呈现为一个六叶的花朵, 似乎发出能量与闪闪发光的"zing". 这是我第一次接触到共感, 我觉得它很迷人. 我开始体验颜色, 而不是仅仅观察它们. 我又闭上眼睛, 看到深深的紫色, 我开始感到胃痛. 这种轻微的疼痛让人想起酸性反流,但更像是"紧张"的感觉. 接下来的几个小时内, 这些疼痛的严重程度会变化. 它们从来没有变得难以忍受, 但偶尔不愉快到打乱我的幻想之旅. 每隔几个小时服用一些可抗酸药似乎可以减轻不适. + 1:15:接下来的两个小时里, 我的旅程不时地升级, 我专注于地毯上的波浪图案, 逐渐相信地毯包含了我认为美丽的一切. 这种令人愉快的视觉扭曲让我思考什么是艺术. 我开始意识到我们认为艺术的表面性, 并开始思考艺术与非艺术的基本特质. 我的思维速度加快了,我的理性不再能够跟上我脑海所揭示的明显启示. 我在旅行的第一阶段所呈现的复杂的视觉扭曲, 我闭上眼睛时, 熟悉的面孔和形状的图像开始从中心分裂成四个对称的部分. 它们从中心向外移动, 感觉就像我穿过一条道, 每一张照片都是独一无二的, 很快,我的脑海开始同时穿过两个这样的道, 这座道的结构是双螺旋式. 道的截面通常是不一致的, 尖的边缘似乎不属于相同形状的顺序. 整个双螺旋变成一个更大,更复杂的道的内部面板, 每一个看似不完美的东西, 就好像是一个更大,更完美的系统的一部分, 每一个"缩小"都增加了一层复杂性和美丽的新维度. 我开始将道墙壁的巨大复杂性解释为我未来的潜力. 每次我试图推断出一个逻辑的真相, 我发现自己无法做到这一点, 我开始在脑海中演奏音乐, 我终于明白了为什么科技和酸这么好地结合在一起, 因为节奏似乎指导和调节我的旅程通过双螺旋道. 我认为我从来没有考虑过我创作音乐的潜力, 我在技术节奏和声音效果的顶部, 添加了一层摇滚音乐 (乐器和歌声), 这听起来很棒. 我加入了古典小提琴和长笛, 所有的音乐都令人惊叹. 我觉得这只是一个展示我可以用我的头脑实现的奇妙潜力. 我觉得自己正在使用自从婴儿时期和幼儿时期以来一直处于休眠状态的脑部部分. 我从来没有经历过这么多有意识的精神活动. 我脑子从来没有处理过这么复杂的声音,光,音乐和反射的交响曲. 我经常想到的是, 这尤其适用于文字, 它们似乎提供了对事物之间的关系的模糊和错误理解. 我看到人们经常因为群体的身份而互相仇恨, 标签只能说明一个人的故事的很小一部分, 这种标签很方便,可以使人们有错误或不完整的理解感,但当它们使标签者对标签的全部盲目时,它们可能会有害. 我不能再把别人视为自由主义者,保守主义者或独立者, 我是一个比我更大的东西的一部分, 它包含了一切, 偶尔,随着时间的推移,我的注意力会越来越分散, 我有时会在脑子不断变化的思维过程中发现欢乐, 其他时候,我会被这种经历的强大程度所震惊,惊地笑着说:'为什么我等了这么久才尝试酸呢?'这次旅行的最深层次是最激动人心的,所以我尽可能多地试图回到那种心态. 时间似乎完全不相关. 我认为这是疲劳造成的, 因为我的脑力耗费了大量的能量. 我喝了一瓶"运动前燃料" (含糖量高), 它工作了. 我开始觉得我的整个生物体即将开花. 似乎是大自然中常见的, 形象是我的能量和眼中散发的CEV. 伸出指尖的手指向空中, 然后用眼睛开, 拥抱宇宙中所有天空的滋养. 我感觉到一个巨大的冲击超过我的身体和明亮的黄色光从上面淋浴. 我觉得我已经真正实现了精神上的重生. 我是一个世俗的无神论者, 我认为这是一种传奇般的情感, 这次旅行已经比我预期的要激烈得多, 我吸了一碗大麻, 道旅行更简单,更轻松, 我很快就服用了0.5毫克的克洛纳泽 (口服) 并抽了一支香烟. 我很少吸烟或服用. 香烟的味道令人惊叹, 焦虑迅速消退, 精神疲劳也越来越大. 我又抽了一碗, 沉思着我发现的奇迹. 我决定必须把这段经历告诉别人. 我希望找到一个无家可归的老妇人, 给她一点魔力, 我希望所有朋友都能欣赏到这美丽的深, + 6:00:我上床着天花板. 红色的点在石膏的天空中愉快地振动. 我地笑了笑, 想着自己从来没想过能从某种物质得到更多的东西, 我的生活永远变得更好. 其他:

Sep 15, 2013. erowid.org/exp/99572 DOSE: T+ 0:00 20 mg oral Amphetamines (daily) T+ 0:00 1 hit oral 25I-NBOMe (blotter / tab) T+ 0:20 2 hits smoked Cannabis T+ 4:15 2 bowls smoked Cannabis T+ 4:15 0.5 mg oral Pharms - Clonazepam T+ 4:15 1 cig. smoked Tobacco - Cigarettes BODY WEIGHT: 172 lb Over the past decade, I've dabbled lightly into a wide variety of psychoactive substances (e.g. cocaine, opioids, benzodiazepines, mephedrone) but have entirely avoided psychedelics (excluding cannabis) since my first experience with that drug 'family' eight years ago. The now distant experience to which I refer entailed consuming half of a morning glory seed pod and undergoing a terrifying thirty-six hours of hallucinations, disassociation, and other frightening effects that turned me off to psychedelics for the subsequent seven years. Over the past year, however, I've cultivated an interest in LSD and similar research chemicals and performed a reasonable amount of online research on the subject. After acquiring 25I-NBOMe from a friend, I finally felt prepared for my first trip. Despite my research and countless conversations with friends who had tripped or were currently tripping on acid, I had no clue what was in store. - 0:20: I consume 20mg Adderall IR orally (this is a normal dose for me, as I take about 60mg daily). + 0:00: I place one square of 25i-NBOMe blotter on my tongue. It slowly dissolves with a mild numbing sensation and a faint bitterness dissipating throughout my mouth. I avoid swallowing my saliva for about fifteen minutes, upon which the paper is almost entirely dissolved. At this point, I swallow the saliva and the remaining fibers of the dose. + 0:20: The first signs of the trip begin with a mild anxiety and the slight trace of a headache in the right hemisphere of my brain. I inhale two hits of potent cannabis and lie down in Shavasana, a yoga position/asana also known as the 'corpse pose.' I remain in this pose for about twenty minutes with my eyes closed, focusing on the rise and fall of my chest during each deep breath. The only light in my room emits from the color-changing LED of a light-up Frisbee. Mozart's symphonies are playing softly in the background. I feel calm and meditative as I slow my thoughts along with my breathing. + 0:40: Upon opening my eyes, I find myself a bit disoriented, somewhat unfamiliar with my immediate surroundings. The feeling is similar to waking up after a long sleep and finding oneself in an unexpected place. This subtle confusion soon fades as I focus on the texture of the ceiling above me. I begin experiencing OEV with fractals forming and tessellating across the ceiling. This initially reminds me of 'Magic Eye' visuals I used to see in books, but with several added layers of complexity. For instance, the fractals seem to radiate in wave formations as they change shape. Furthermore, colors within the formations on the ceiling are fluid, heterogeneous, and truly captivating. A particularly beautiful constellation appears as a tessellating six petal flower that seems to radiate energy with a glistening 'zing.' This my first encounter with synesthesia and I find it enthralling. I'm beginning to experience colors rather than merely observe them. Upon closing my eyes again, a deep, rich purple nearly overwhelms me with its sublime beauty. + 0:50: My first stomach pains begin. The mild pains are reminiscent of acid reflux, but incur more of a 'tightening' sensation. These pains come and go with varying levels of severity for the next few hours. They never grow unbearable, but are occasionally unpleasant to the point of disrupting my phantasmagoric trip. Taking a few chewable antacids every couple hours seems to mitigate the discomfort. + 1:15: For the next two hours or so my trip escalates intermittently, oscillating between successively deeper trains of thought to shallower, semi-euphoric states of amazement, often dominated by breathtaking visuals. I focus on the undulating patterns in a rug for some time, growing convinced that the rug contains everything I've ever considered beautiful. The delightful visual distortions lead me to ponder what constitutes art. Given the endless beauty within the rug, I begin to realize the superficiality of most of that which we consider art and begin thinking about the basic qualities that distinguish art from non-art. Starting from this quasi-logical approach, my thought-process speeds up and my rationality is no longer able to keep up with the apparent revelations my mind divulges. A remarkable change in my thinking emerges seamlessly from the magnificently complex visual distortions that characterized the first stage of the trip. As I close my eyes, images of familiar faces and shapes begin to split apart from the center in four symmetrical pieces. As they move from the center outward, it begins to feel as if I'm traveling through a tunnel with vivid images on the walls. Each image is unique, though similar, even as I pick up speed and begin racing through the tunnel. Before long, my mind begins to wander through two such tunnels simultaneously, one for each closed eye. Together the tunnels follow a double helix pattern. A cross-section of the tunnel will frequently be incongruent, with a sharp edge that doesn't seem to be belong to the otherwise smooth sequence of identical shapes. Every time this happens, I manage to 'zoom out' from the situation and the entire double helix becomes a mere panel on the inside of a larger, more complex tunnel encompassing endless panels. It's as if each apparent imperfection is part of a larger, more perfect system that nothing can disrupt. Each 'zoom out' adds a layer of complexity and a new dimension of beauty. My mind begins to interpret the vast intricacies of the tunnel walls as potentials for my future. Thoughts are racing and seemingly revelatory, but each time I attempt to deduce a logical truth, I find myself unable to do so, as if the n-bomb were telling me to stop trying to understand with my preconfigured prejudices and to simply experience what lay before me. I begin to play music in my head, starting with a hard beat and looping chords, similar to techno rave music. I finally understand why techno and acid go so well together, as the beats seem to guide and regulate my journey through the double helix tunnels. I think of how I've never considered my potential to create music, and I decide to explore it. On top of the techno beats and sound effects, I add a layer of rock music (instrumentals and vocals) and it sounds amazing. I add a classical violin and a flute section and everything about the music is breathtaking. Some 'inner voice' seems to tell me that this is merely a demonstration of the wondrous potentials I could achieve with my mind. I felt like I was using parts of my mind that had remained dormant since infancy and early childhood. Never before have I experienced so much conscious mental activity. My sober brain could never process such complex symphonies of sound, light, music, and reflection. A recurring theme in my thoughts is that of removing prejudices from the way I interpret things. This applied especially to words, which seemed to offer such an obfuscated and false understanding of the relationships between things. I saw that individuals often hate one another merely because of group identities, which are epitomized in labels. A label can tell only a tiny part of a person's story, and can thus serve to position individuals against one another arbitrarily. Such labels are convenient for achieving an often false or incomplete sense of understanding, but are potentially harmful when they blind the labeler to the totality of the labeled. For instance, I can no longer regard others as liberal, conservative, or independent, as I am all of these without contradiction. I am a part of something greater than me that contains everything, and everything is as it must be. Occasionally, with decreasing frequency over time, I would get distracted or otherwise start to 'come back' from the deep trip immersing my consciousness. I would sometimes find hilarity in the ever-changing thought processes endured by my brain and laugh full-heartedly. Other times I would be blown away by how powerful this experience was and laugh with astonishment, thinking 'why the hell did I wait so long to try acid!?' The deepest phases of the trip were the most thrilling, so I tried to return to that mindset as often as possible. Time seemed entirely irrelevant. When my thoughts started to slow down, I attributed this to fatigue, given the tremendous amount of energy my mind was using. I drank a bottle of 'pre-workout fuel' (with a high sugar content) to revitalize my mind. It worked. From a closed-eye, slumped posture on the couch, akin to a fetal position, I began to feel as if my whole organism were about to blossom. The slender S-curve of a drooping flower petal, which seemed so prevalent in nature, seemed to characterize my energy and all of the CEVs radiating through my eyelids. It was then that I felt an indescribable urge to arch my back, tilt my head back, and reach my pointed fingers into the air before opening them wide with my eyes, embracing the nourishment of all the skies in the universe to which I belonged. A huge rush overcame my body and bright yellow light showered from above. It felt as if I'd truly achieved a spiritual rebirth. I am a secular atheist, but I have no better words describe this event. When the rush subsided, I felt calmer periods of what I concluded to be that legendary emotion known as psychedelic bliss. + 4:15: The trip had already proven far more intense than I'd anticipated and a creeping anxiety began to surface. I smoke a bowl of weed and am able to restore some of the more pleasant effects of the trip. The tunnel-travel is simpler and much less intense, but the hilarity of certain wild thoughts and perceptions continues. I soon consume 0.5 mg of Clonazepam (orally) and smoke a cigarette. I rarely smoke tobacco or take benzos. The cigarette tastes amazing and restores a trivial degree of normalcy to my mindset. My anxiety rapidly subsides while my mental fatigue increases. I smoke another bowl and think deeply about the wonderment I'd discovered. I decide that I must share this experience with others. I want to find an old homeless woman and give her a hit of this magic, as I know she'll be blown away with a euphoric state of broadened understanding. I want all of my friends to appreciate the unfathomably deep beauty that nearly drowned me in tears of joyous gratitude. + 6:00: I go to bed and gaze at the ceiling. RGB dots vibrate pleasantly across the plaster heavens. I grin idiotically and think to myself 'I could never ask for anything more from a substance,' before drifting peacefully into a long slumber. My life is forever changed for the better.

Generally, my reactions to psychoactives are idiosyncratic, so I don't post them. There's not much use if you can't extrapolate from them. For instance, I react badly to cannabis and MDMA, but enjoy bufotenine and salvia. My experience this time seemed representative from the other reports I read, though, so I'm passing it on. Been weeks since any psychedelics were used, so no tolerance likely. Background: Have used the following once or twice Positive or neutral experiences with: BZP, BZP+TFMPP, salvia, mescaline, 5-MeO-DMT, DMT, NMT, ketamine, DXM, kratom, adrafanil, psilocybin, amphetamine, amanita muscaria, lsd, bufotenine, ayahuasca, nitrous oxide, hawaiian baby woodrose extract, ephedrine, mesembrine, sulbutiamine, Negative experiences with: cannabis, 2c-c, hawaiian baby woodrose whole, MDMA, phenibut Friend and I had a heavy breakfast at 10AM. Friend had only just recovered from multi-day stomach flu (enterovirus) and was still pretty worn down from holiday work. It had been two weeks since a low dose tryptamine experience (Moxy), months since he had had any psychedelics. He generally is sensitive to lyergoloids, but not more than usual to PEAs. I haven't enjoyed any PEAs other than mescaline. I had a tension headache that is not responsive to ibuprofen. Took 2 at 9am, two at 12:45. Method: 12:45 Parachuted 10mg, +/- 2mg weighed out. 12:50 Notice mood lift and lightheadedness. Way too early for alerts. Must be placebo. 12:56 Mood lift and lightheadedness more pronounced. Dancing and goofy. Must be placebo. 1:00 Decided to potentiate ibuprofen with 100mg of caffeine. Heard it potentiates mescaline, as well. Jaw tension already. 1:45 Not feeling much of anything. Weigh and take 10mg. Felling a little cold and hands feeling very shaky, making this difficult. 2:45 Beginning to feel something. Can't decide whether or not to take more. Decide to wait. 2:45 Beginning to feel something. Can't decide whether or not to take more. Decide to wait. Effects slowly climb over the next half hour. For me: cold, tremors, weakness, shivers, and general nervous attenuation are striking and pronounced. Jaw tension remains minor. Somatic effects feel a little dirty, toxic, and miasmic--a little like the come down from LSD. The somatic sensation is one of mild illness and weakness for me, not for my friend, who seemed to enjoy it well enough. This is not particularly unpleasant, just distinct from the vibrant healing warmth that accompanies the weakness of mescaline's onset. The MDMA/mescaline glow is not present. Conversation is facile, but I become easily distracted. 3:30 The intensity is good. We like where we're at, but after some consideration, we decide we'd like to bump it up a tiny bit. 5mg more ingested, with great difficulty as my hands were extremely shaky. 4:00 Once settled into the space, the slightly noxious feel of it disappears entirely. It feels quite nice, pleasant now. I had to stay conscientiously upbeat during the comeup, which did not lead me to believe I was going to enjoy the experience. My friend took to the space much more positively. We enjoy the tactile and erotic components, ahem, a lot. Great tactile enhancement, orgasms were mindblowing. For several hours. I snacked on crackers, cheese, hot chocolate, and mandarin orange slices without too much difficulty. My friend had a few slices of orange, and that was it. Eating seems like it may have exacerbated the gas cramps I later experienced, but I don't know. Surprisingly clear headed space for how intense it was. Visuals were varied and striking; I, but not my friend, experienced prototypical mescaline rainbows with eyes both open and closed. We both experienced some 2C cartoonishness, breathing surfaces, and persian carpet patterns. Conversation was easy and open for the most part. The erotic was interrupted by noticing that the cat, utterly mortified by the state of us, had turned away from us and buried his head into the wall in an effort to block out what was going on. Eruptions of uncontrollable mirth ensued. 5:30 - Peaked hard. Lost in thought for a while. Friend also peaking, and was glimpsing cosmic infinity and humanity's essential oneness; profound empathy. My experience not so cosmic or grand, but a solid +++ nonetheless. Crying comes easily. Orgasm impossible to achieve. 5:50 - Precipitous drop from peak. Like sobering up from vaporized DMT precipitously. Back at ++. 5:50 - Precipitous drop from peak. Like sobering up from vaporized DMT precipitously. Back at ++. Must have been the little bump of 5mg? Could easily fall asleep. 6:00 - Give my friend a massage. He falls asleep for a while. Barely ++ for me for the next 3 hours. Took some magnesium and some peppermints, which helped with the gas cramps tremendously. Sex was excellent. Tremors, shaking, and cold surges weaken, but are still present for the next there and a half hours. My friend enjoys some lemon sorbet. 9:30pm. Gas cramps still present, too. Still some rainbow visuals. Friend has homemade veggie soup for dinner. I have a high tolerance to spice, but it and my typical spicy snacks taste almost unbearably piquant. He saw no rainbow visuals at all throughout, just typical crawling patterns, breathing surfaces, and such. 10:00 - Tail of come down a little like acid, but not nearly as difficult. 10:30 - Now at + except for my legs, which are still tripping. My legs tripped way harder than the rest of me the whole time, what with the cold, the tremors, the rushes, the shakes, the weakness, etc. My friend agreed. 11:13 - I'm functionally at baseline, but my pot smoking friend is still buzzing a little bit. Following day, 12:00pm - We are both experiencing a pleasant afterglow. I nearly hit two cars, however, while driving when I fail to see them in my blindspot while changing lanes. I was driving my friend's car, which has quite poor visibility compared to my own, but I suspect my attentiveness and visual acuity was still compromised. Scenery still looked sharp and high resolution. My mood was high, food tasted great (still spicier), empathy for people was still strong, and the world felt fresh and new. My friend felt similar, but was felt exhausted, just totally depleted, probably due to having no reserves of energy due to the stomach flu. He experienced one residual visual distortion where a statuary bust cocked its head at him. He complained of intermittent nausea throughout the day. The following day, we were truly at baseline, although he still experienced some intermittent nausea--not sure why. He is still complaining of intermittent nausea four days later. Summary: overall positive experience. The lack of GI side effects was not unusual for me--I don't even get nausea from ayahuasca. Persistent nausea for my friend was troubling. His experience was much more emotionally warm and positive than mine. Trip seemed pretty linear, not particularly multiphasic like tryptamines frequently are. Time dilation was minimal. Tough to imagine how different the experience would have been solo.

Nov 7, 2015. erowid.org/exp/104752 DOSE: T+ 0:00 20 mg sublingual Noopept T+ 2:30 150 mg oral 4-Fluoroamphetamine (capsule) T+ 7:00 50 mg oral 4-Fluoroamphetamine (capsule) BODY WEIGHT: 74 kg T+ 0:00 - I took 20mg noopept before heading out sublingual T+ 2:30 - Took 150mg 4-fa T+ 3:00 - Massive side effects, cramping, head aches, tearing unable to move T+ 4:00 - Side effects subside and regular stimulation sets in T+ 7:00 - Dropped 50mg 4-fa booster with no unexpected side effects I want to keep this brief in case anyone is doing research on combining fluoroamphetamine and noopept/racetams: Party night and heading about to head out. Took 20mg noopept sublingual as it helps with alcohol-induced stupidity and has proven itself to be a good in these situations. I also prepared 150mg of 4-Fa + a 50mg booster - both capped in a gelatin pill. Dropping the 4-fa about 2.5 hours later was followed with the usual come-up after 30 min. Then things went south: A stinging pain in my brain/head followed by a feeling like my brain is cramping. In short I was in pain feeling like my brain is getting overloaded. My eyes started to tear, random yawning and an incredibly tense bodyload felt in every muscle followed. Luckily I could sit/lie in a chair trying to hold it together and not draw too much attention to myself. I had some earplugs with me that helped me concentrate and endure the situation I have found myself in. After about an hour I felt reasonably fine again and 3 hours later dropping the 50mg booster was causing no issues either. I had dropped 4-fa multiple times before, same dosage and a friend who was accompanying me had no issues himself so I am sure the noopept played a factor here. Some months later I noticed the same potentiation effect with noopept+2-FA however due to the much lower dosage with no ill side effects. I caution everyone to reduce their dosage accordingly and do a test run if planning to combine those substances. In my experience the potentiation is definitely there and can be quite overwhelming. I assume similiar issues could happen when combining MDMA with noopept, confirmed by some experience reports I have read, talking about piracetam potentiating MDMA.

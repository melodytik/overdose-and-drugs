我第一次喝阿卜辛提大约一年半前,我相信. 我尝试过许多进口品牌, 虽然这是一次非常昂贵的冒险 - - 如果你看过互联网供应商的价格, 我已经找到一种非常有效的缩混合物, 我每天喝不少于3杯酒 (目前每周喝1升). 我正在打造一天中的第四杯酒, 任何酒精都有其独特的味道, 而酒是我最喜欢的"味道". 开始时, 酒的感觉与服用止痛药类似, 我总是保持清晰的思想, 这实际上激发了你通常不会花时间思考的事情. 它会给人带来愉快的情绪, 现在, 效果不如我开始时那么强烈, 因为我的身体已经习惯了Thujone, 我从来没有真正喜欢常规酒精的效果 - - 虽然我在很多场合都在醉酒,但我从来没有觉得醉酒太吸引人. 青是一种完全不同的物质, 在这个时候我不太确定我是否能够, 更不用说愿意放弃. 尽管这是我没有后悔的习惯之一, 也不想戒掉. 总之,阿卜辛是一种奇妙的饮料. 让绿色仙女带领你走上路, 但要知道, ~祝你在你的冒险中好运,享受! ,我知道你会喜欢的. 其他: 2007年4月25日发布 阿卜辛 (4):回顾 / 摘要 (11), 和习惯性 (10), 不适用 (38)

Apr 25, 2007. erowid.org/exp/34589 DOSE: 3 glasses oral Absinthe (daily) BODY WEIGHT: 135 lb Ahh Absinthe.. I first drank Absinthe about a year and a half ago I believe. I've tried many imported brands though this was a very expensive adventure - if you've looked at the prices from internet suppliers you know what I mean. I have since found a concentrated mixure that works excellently and tastes very similar to an expensive brand at a very nice price (about 26$ a liter for the finished product). Since I've found this way of doing things I've been drinking no less than 3 glasses a day (going rate a liter a week currently). *I'm working on my 4th glass of the day as I type this* Absinthe is like no other alcohol - every alcohol has its own feel to it and Absinthe is my favorite 'feel'. The initial feelings of Absinthe are similar to taking pain killers and not at all like being flat drunk. I always keep a clear mind about things and it actually inspires thought on things you normally wouldn't give the time to. It gives a very pleasant mood but unlike some say, most certainly - not even in high doses, will cause hallucinations. Nowadays, The effects aren't as strong as they were when I started since my body is well used to the Thujone but I still love it. I never quite gained a serious liking for the effects of regular alcohol - being in a drunken stupor never seemed too appealing though I've been there on many occasions. Absinthe is quite the different substance and it grew on me almost instantly. At this point I'm not so sure if I'm able, let alone willing to give it up. Absinthism proves to be real and separate from alcoholism, though this is one of the habits I've got into that I don't regret nor wish to quit. In conclusion - Absinthe is a wonderous drink..

Jan 22, 2016. erowid.org/exp/104730 DOSE: Testosterone Anabolic Steroids BODY WEIGHT: 190 lb I had been training for about 9 months before I started on an anabolic steroid regimen. The recommended dosage for the initial cycle is 250mg of Test-E E3d. Because I don't really have any problems with normal dosage (it usually takes about 1.5x of recommended dosages for me to feel effects) and my source only had Test-E in 300mg/ml dosages I decided to bump up the dosage. Also, my source had some Dianabol on hand, so I decided to pick that up as sort of a kickstart. I was also taking Aromasin to combat the estrogen rise at 12.5 mg as needed when my nipples started to fell itchy, or when I started to enjoy Adele songs. My cycle looked like this. Week 1-12 Test-E 300mg E3d Week 1-4 Dianabol 50 ED Immediately after beginning, I started focusing more on the gym. Instead of going 3 days a week, I was going four and five days a week. Dianabol is a really cool compound. I don't actually feel a difference, like you do on other drugs, such as marijuana, MDMA, or LSD. However, what makes it so special is when I'm lifting, I feel like I could maybe do 7 or 8 reps on a certain weight, and then wind up doing 10 or 12. Week 5 is when I really started noticing the effects. I was getting compliments, and people that didn't see me everyday were asking if I was lifting weights and bulking up. By no means did I look like a professional bodybuilder, I just looked like I was serious and dedicated to lifting weights. By the end of the cycle, I had gone from weighing 174 pounds at 16% body fat, to 190 pounds at 12% body fat. Now, I'm sure you're wondering about the negative side effects. We'll cut to the chase and talk about the issue known as 'Roid Rage' It doesn't exist. It's part of propaganda. It's similar to Reefer Madness. I did gain a lot of confidence, and I started feeling more inclined to call people out when they were knowingly doing something wrong, but I was never mean, aggressive, or offensive. My skin got oily, and my hair got a little thinner, but not overwhelmingly noticeable. Two weeks after I pinned my last injection, I started my post cycle therapy. I used Nolvadex at 40 mg for the 1st two weeks, then 20 mg for the second two weeks. Not awful, but not great, I kept a lot of my size during my cycle.

1克托利西克利丁HCl (3-甲基罗利西克利丁,3-Me-PCPy) 找到自己的方式. 我已经戒药了 (2019年最后一剂), 我可能会用剩余的东西来更准确地确定水溶性. 标签上写着3-Me-PCPy并且显示了这种结构,但IUPAC给出的名称是4-甲基乙基 (3-Me-PCE). 然而,正如这里所报道的那样,最终的标识是3-甲基罗利西基丁. 这是一种淡淡的白色粉末, 有点, 颜色和度有点像咖啡奶油, 答案是苦的,正如预期的那样. 我将其进一步稀释为5毫升, 其中1毫升=5毫克将通过直肠注射, 在一个小时内,5毫克的直肠迅速出现,在3分钟内警觉,在10分钟内显著发展, 开始对我来说很焦虑,但3-MeO-PCP也是如此. 很快它就变成了一个充满梦想的状态, 解离和刺激的高兴感很好地结合在一起. 梦幻般的分离比刺激成分更重要. 我在身体上"不得不做点什么", 但我也很有梦想, 低剂量可能会让人感觉性感, 它的强度大概和一般的PCP类药物一样强大, 我已经足够醉了,5毫克直肠对我来说是一个强大的++尽管我有相当大的分离剂耐受性2年前. 这些水域比3-MeO-PCP的宁静还要荒凉. 这些水域比3-MeO-PCP的宁静还要荒凉. 这就像一种带有MDMA味道的乱, 欣喜感是反应性的, 尽管我感觉很好, 毒品不再是我选择的道路. 我倒掉了剩下的4毫升=20毫克. 通常使用PCP作为自由基. 我想知道这种托利西基林酸盐是否适合使用. 不是说我吸烟, 但人们会想这样做. 我用茶匙,葡萄酒杯和注射器把它溶解在3毫升的甘醇中. 你能说"为了科学"吗? 当你在5毫克的药物中享受美好时光, 为了科学家们! 我预计糖/糖醇 (另一种蒸汽载体) 将会溶解更多的, 让我们去找出. 不,事情并没有那样发展. 100mg的托利西克利丁液不能充分溶解到10ml的甘油中,因此在实际使用中很少使用. 然而,500毫克完全溶解在2.5毫升水中,温度为26.5摄氏度 (这里是热的),使其具有显著的水性. 剩下的药物被丢弃了, 除了第二个5毫克, 这次用2毫升水, 用于直肠, 在这个实验中使用的3Me-PCPy的99%都被丢弃了. 工作结束后,奖励! 这里是5毫克更多! 我必须首先说, 两剂药物都引起肠壁局部刺激, 轻微的, 品尝溶液没有刺激作用,但这可能会影响打算使用电子烟或注射静脉注射的人. 由于托利西基丁是一种三级氨基,因此,比HCl更少的基本性是酸性,也许它的某种酸性盐有助于这种效果. 第二剂没有任何焦虑感, 眼睛闭着的梦幻的浪潮吞没了我, 我平静地漂浮, 随着剂量增加,梦幻/麻醉效应会超过警觉/刺激效应. 如果一个人采取更大胆的剂量和更多的重复, 我相信今晚我会像一样睡觉, 除非刺激会在尾部占据主导地位. 在我试过的非基因环胺中, 这种感觉比3-MeO-PCP,3-MeO-PCE,4-MeO-PCP和3-MeO-PCMo更好. 托利西基丁具有一种令人惊叹的麻醉效果, 对于我来说,这无疑是最容易暴饮暴食的. 它的 *和平*,然而 我已经准备好了. 这意味着越走越远 我走了,越诱人和丰富的梦幻般的状态成为. 我还能完全敏捷, 但绝对不会参与交通, 如果我必须对其进行校准, 我会把5毫克托利西克利丁放在30毫克甲基胺和7.5毫克3-MeO-PCP的水平上. 首先要消散焦虑,然后再深入, 避免刺激的效果将这些焦虑变成不愉快的东西. 像MXE一样, 我书中的优点在于重复增量, 我写了一封电子邮件给供应商, 告诉他们产品的标签错误, 缩写和结构是3-Me-PCPy, 这种感觉与N-单基有所不同, 感觉就像一种循环氨基. 我对兴奋剂不耐受, 一杯咖啡太多了, 这是一种迪索的刺激, 而不是一种胺/胺/咖啡因. 连我都觉得很好. 头 + 刺激打字的冲动 药物已经离开我的房子, 进入我的身体. 我喜欢这种感觉,但我不喜欢"被",所以这很棒, 但我不会重复. 我不会重复,因为如果我这样做了, 我不会重复,因为如果我这样做了, 黑洞是为了把你推到光速, 而不是旋转进去. 尊重实质:我可以完全感觉到这将是坏如果 一个剂量太高或太频繁. 这是一件好事. 我觉得我正在愉快地下降, 但这可能是一个相当愉快的长时间下降. 这种激发性离散性至今还没有出现. 我曾经有过甲胺问题, 正是因为这新的一种感觉很好, 我不想再服用, 让我更加渴望不再使用分离剂. 我再次感受到旋风中心的诱惑力. 认为这是一个社会服务的前迪索奉献者. 现在是第二剂后六小时. 它大多结束了,但如果它停留愉快 我想要屈服于它,如果令人讨厌 我想做其他事情. 让我们说它是1/5的,但我不会感到惊如果第五延迟到午夜, 其他: 发布时间:2021年9月8日 3-Me-PCPy (951):一般情况 (1),制备/食谱 (30),第一次使用 (2),单独使用 (16)

Sep 8, 2021. erowid.org/exp/115504 DOSE: T+ 0:00 5 mg rectal 3-Me-PCPy (liquid) T+ 2:15 5 mg rectal 3-Me-PCPy (liquid) BODY WEIGHT: 380 lb 1 gram of Tolicyclidine HCl (3-methylrolicyclidine, 3-Me-PCPy) found its way my way. I've quit drugs (last dose 2019) so the plan is, one session, discard the rest. I may use the leftovers to more precisely establish water solubility. Whats immediately apparent is that the label says 3-Me-PCPy and shows this structure, but the IUPAC name given is that of 4-methyl-eticyclidine (3-Me-PCE). The definitive identity however, is 3-methylrolicyclidine, as reported here. Its an off white powder with a very slight odor, somewhat phenolic, color and consistency kinda like coffee creamer, 25mg dissolved readily, completely and clearly into 1ml lukewarm water. Solution is bitter, as expected. I diluted it further to 5ml, of this, 1ml=5mg will be rectally administered, the way I was used to take my dissociatives. One hour in, the 5mg rectal came up fast, alert within 3 minutes, significantly developed in 10,min, mostly there at 30 min after the plunge. It started out anxious for me, but so does 3-MeO-PCP. It soon gave way to a dreamy state with a stimmy, slightly restless undertone. The euphoria of the dissociation and stimulation go well together. The dreamy dissociation outweighs the stimulant component. I physically "had to do something" but I'm also way dreamy so, whoa, not too much :) My palms are warm and sweaty, ,especially in the beginning. A low dose of this could potentially be sexy - this might be just the ticket for sensual exploration. It's about as potent, I think, as typical PCP type drugs, so probably 3-15!mg, like PCP and 3-MeO-PCP. I am sufficiently intoxicated, 5mg rectal is a strong ++ for me, despite that I had a considerable dissociatives tolerance 2 years back. Dreamy yet alert, disinhibited but euphoric, a tad restless, these are wilder waters than the 3-MeO-PCP lotus pond of tranquility. Dreamy yet alert, disinhibited but euphoric, a tad restless, these are wilder waters than the 3-MeO-PCP lotus pond of tranquility. Its like a disso with a hint of MDMA, the euphoria is reactive and genuine. I'm confident, good as I feel, drugs are no longer The Way for me. I poured the remaining 4ml = 20mg away. PCP is usually smoked as the freebase. Smoking is sooo 20th century, so I was wondering whether this Tolicyclidine HCl as such was suitable for vaping. Not that I vape, but, people will want to do that. 1 weighed out 100mg 3-Me-PCPy and with teaspoon, wineglass and graduated syringe I managed toi dissolve it into 3ml of propylene glycol. Can you credibly say "For Science" when you have a great time on 5mg and pour 100mg down the sink? For science guys! I anticipate that glycerin/glycerol (another vaping carrier) will dissolve more of it, being more polar. Let's go find that out. ..And nope, it did not work out that way. 100mg Tolicyclidine HCl did not satisfactorily dissolve into 10ml glycerin, so it is of little practical vaping use. 500mg did however dissolve completely in 2.5ml water at 26.5'C (its' hot here) making it remarkably aq sol. The leftovers were discarded, except for a second 5mg, this time in 2ml water, for rectal use, now 2:15 hour after the first dose when I'm still comfortably on the plateau. 99% of the 3-Me-PCPy used in this experiment went down the drain. After the work, the reward! Here's to 5mg more! 2h after the second dose and about 4:30h after the first dose, I must say first of all that both doses caused a bit of localized irritation of the bowel wall, a slight itch, and this at 0.25-0.5% concentration. Tasting the solution did not have irritant effects, but this may affect people intending to vape or inject IM. Since Tolicyclidine is a tertiary amine so, less basic than HCl is acidic, maybe the fact that its a somewhat acidic salt contributed to that effect. The second dose had NOTHING of the anxiety, it was this wave of eyes-closed dreaminess engulfing me, I peacefully floated, a lotus upon the tranquil pond, an effect shared with 8mg 3-MeO-PCP but, more pronounced. As the dose increases, the dreamy/anesthetic effects overcome the alert/stimulant ones. I take back my previous assumption, if one were to take bolder doses and more repetitions one could probably hole out fantastically on this one. At 5+5mg, I do believe I'll sleep like a rose tonight unless stimulation takes over on the tail end. In terms of non-keto arylcyclohexylamines I tried, this one takes the cake. It feels better to me than 3-MeO-PCP, 3-MeO-PCE, 4-MeO-PCP and 3-MeO-PCMo. Tolicyclidine has that wonderful -anesthetic- feeling I appreciate so much in the Keto drugs like Methoxetamine. Of these, for me this one would no doubt be the most binge-able. Its *peaceful*, and yet I am alert. This promises that the further down the path I go, the more seductive and rich the dreamlike state becomes. At 5+5mg I an still fully agile, but definitely wouldn't take part in traffic, not even on a bicycle. If I had to calibrate it, I would place 5mg Tolicyclidine on the level of 30mg Methoxetamine and 7.5mg 3-MeO-PCP. One would probably do good taking a small dose first to dissippate anxieties before going deeper, to prevent perhaps the stimulant effect whipping those up to something unpleasant. Like MXE, the virtue in my book is in repeat increments, where I equate 5mg Tolicyclidine with 30mg Methoxetamine. I'd be surprised if this turned out to be the Eticyclidine analog, it has a different feel from the N-monoalkyls, it really feels like a cyclic amine.

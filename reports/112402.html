我第一次接触泰马兹帕姆是在2009年. 一位友善的医生给我写了一本关于应对时差的剧本. 我从来没有使用过它, 但发现它非常有用, 现代城市人似乎有相当混乱的睡眠时间表, 定期不得不很早起床或在很短的时间内夜, 我认为这会影响睡眠质量. 我会在睡觉之前, 随时服用10毫克的特马泽. 一瓶25片药用了我2年,平均每月只有一片. 每次我都会得到一个非常体面的不间断的夜间睡眠. 我会觉醒时完全清爽, 完全能够跑步一个月. 然而,最后一粒药是不同的. 我有一天晚上喝了一些社交饮料, 于是第二天早上我很早起, 我想试着吃最后一粒药, 在沙发上小睡一下, 我在中午左右醒来, 感到非常平静, 我从来没有试图重新写下剧本. 快速推进9年 我找到了一个很好的药品品质的Temazepam来源, 为了避免可怕的毒成, 我仍然能从一个10毫克的药片中得到一个很好的夜晚的睡眠, (我曾经嘲笑当地一个夜总会的口号,说我们过的晚上睡得很好,并不是我们永远记得的夜晚) 我发现它在晚上喝酒后特别有用,如果我有点头,感到焦虑,晚上难以入睡. 我每月只做两次以上. 一周内大约4到5天, 包括将剂量提高到20mg (前一天晚上没有大量饮酒). 因为我会开始有过于生动的梦想, 这感觉完全不像是好睡觉. 我认为我的大脑会在睡眠中继续运行而不是因为某种原因而平静下来. 我也试过在早起后服用10毫克的特马泽帕姆, 我认为我们对精神活性药物的一些经验特别有价值, 我们应该更好地利用时间和身体资源, 时间表: 112402 发布时间:2018年12月11日 药物 - 泰马泽帕姆 (159):回顾 / 摘要 (11),医疗用途 (47),单独使用 (16)

Dec 11, 2018. erowid.org/exp/112402 DOSE: 10-20 mg oral Pharms - Temazepam (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 140 lb My first encounter with Temazepam was back in 2009. A friendly doc wrote me a script for me to deal with jetlag. I never used it for that but found it very useful to get my sleep/wake cycle back on track once in a while. Modern urban people seem to have a pretty chaotic sleep schedule, periodically having to get up very early or stay up until late on short notice just because things come their way. I think it messes up sleep quality in the long run. So I would take one 10mg Temazepam pill when I felt it was time, shortly before going to bed. A 25-pill bottle lasted me 2 years, averaging to a pill per month. Each time I would get a very decent uninterrupted night sleep. My sleep would be ‘denser’ and I would wake up completely refreshed and perfectly able to run for another month. However, the last pill was different. I had a bit of social drinking one night and had an important errand to run early next morning. So I had a very early start next morning and was back home by 9am. Having nothing else going on for the day, I figured I could try taking the last pill and have a bit of daytime nap on the sofa to get refreshed. That worked wonderfully and I woke up at around noon feeling very calm and at complete peace with the world. (Not implying I have much conflict with it normally.) That glowing state really stuck in my mind but I never tried to renew the script. Fast forward 9 years. I found a nice source of pharma-grade Temazepam and decided to see if it still worked for me the same way. Of course, with more supply at hand, I did my best to go easy in order to avoid the dreaded benzo addiction. I am still able to get a pretty nice night sleep from one 10mg pill, if not as nice and memorable as 9 years before. (I used to laugh at a local night club’s slogan saying that the nights we spent having a sound sleep aren’t the ones we remember forever.) I find it particularly useful the next night after a night of heavier social drinking, if I get a bit of hangover and feel anxious and unable to fall asleep easily in the evening. I don’t do this more than a couple of times per month. However, I have had a stretch or two of more frequent before-sleep use, roughly 4 or 5 nights in a week including upping the dose to 20mg (no heavy drinking the night before was involved). Each time I just stopped on my own because I would start getting excessively vivid dreams connected to events of my prior life. That didn’t feel like good sleep at all. I guess my brain would keep running in high gear in sleep instead of calming down for some reason. I have also tried taking 10mg Temazepam once or twice after an early start when I had nothing else left for the day but, alas, I have never got that glowing at-peace-with-the-world state again and stopped chasing it for good. Now I think some of our experiences with psychoactives are particularly valuable because they are unique and irreproducible. Carefully and responsibly looking for new ones should be a better use of our time and body’s resources.

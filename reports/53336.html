最近医学杂志上有报道称片成者滥用克洛尼丁来获得类似片的高. 我还找到了一个政府网站, 列出克洛尼丁是新兴的滥用药物, 医学杂志上的一项新发现也表明, 片抗剂纳洛可以逆转克隆的过量服用. 这表明mu片受体参与克隆的过量使用症状. 更多的医学文本列举克隆的过量服用与麻醉药物过量服用有类似的症状. 这种证据让我认为克洛尼丁的过量服用与毒品的过量服用相似. 我在药物论坛或任何药物网站上都找不到人们使用克隆的证据, 我唯一的指导是一个海洛因成者服用2毫克克隆尼丁, 我选择慢慢地从0. 600ug (微克) 增加剂量到1.2mg. 这种方法比一次服用所有药物更安全. 另外,考虑到克洛尼丁的半衰期很长, 我很可能不会因等待而失去任何效果. 需要大约两个小时才能达到我的总剂量 (≥1.2ug). 在600ug我感到轻微镇静. 没有真正的片, 没有满足感, 只是镇静. 在800ug镇静剂增加了, 但再次没有阿片的满足感. 我感觉很麻醉和头. 我很难集中注意力, 我决定睡觉, 睡得很好几个小时. 我经常觉醒时肢体麻木, 我也感到口腔非常干燥. 我决定吃一些东西来对抗口腔的干燥. 我几乎昏倒了. 我回楼下测血压. 这是一个90分60 (脉90). 据推测在站起来时,它下降了更多. 这让我很困扰,但并非意外. 这种东西是为了降低血压. 我决定留在床上,因为如果我试图做任何事情, 这些昏迷是我经历过的最糟糕的一次. 听力变得空洞, 我听到一种我从未听过的声. 我眼睛的视力变成了道模式, 只是为了明确这些效应是直接由低血压引起的,它们不是克洛尼丁的主要效应,尽管克洛尼丁引起了低血压. 我决定睡觉, 确保我的血压不会下降. 我喝了很多水, 用一些口香糖来治疗喉干燥. 我不认为这会有那么多乐趣. 没有任何精神变化. 不像放松我的瓦利, 这只是镇静, 经历已经十个半小时了, 尽管我喝了近一咖啡, 现在我的血压是120/90我的脉已经下降到60 站起来时我仍然感到头. 总的来说,我认为这次经历并不是很有趣. 我不喜欢不能站起来. 我不喜欢没有兴奋的镇静剂. 我不喜欢一个不断的口干. 其他: 药品 - 克隆 (344):一般 (1), 后视 / 总结 (11), 健康问题 (27), 单独 (16)

May 18, 2009. erowid.org/exp/53336 DOSE: 1.2 mg oral Pharms - Clonidine BODY WEIGHT: 205 lb Recently reports have been emerging in medical journals of opiate addicts abusing clonidine to get a opiate like high. I also found a government website that lists clonidine as a emerging drug of abuse and found reports that clonidine is being sold on the streets of new york. Also a new finding in a medical journal showed that the opiate antagonist naloxone could reverse clonidine ODs. This suggested mu opiate receptor involvement in clonidine OD symptoms. Further more medical texts list clonidine OD as presenting similar symptoms to narcotic overdoses. This evidence suggested to me that clonidine ODs would be similar to narcotic ODs. I could find no evidence in drug forums or on any drug website that people were using clonidine and thus I had no dosage guidelines to go by for experimentation. My one guideline was of a heroin addict that took 2mg of clonidine and said it gave him a nod better then heroin. I chose to slowly titrate my dose from .600ug (micrograms) to a total of 1.2mg. This had the advantage of being slightly safer then taking all my pills at once. Also considering clonidine has a long half life I would most likely not lose any of the effect by waiting. It took approximately two hours to reach my total dose(1.2ug). At 600ug I felt slightly sedated. There was no real opiate buzz present, no feelings of contentment, just sedation. At 800ug the sedation was increased but again feelings of opiate contentment were not present. Once I reached my total dose of 1.2mg I felt very sedated and dizzy. Concentration was hard but I didn’t feel all that messed up. I decided to go to sleep and I slept very nicely for a few hours. However I woke up frequently with numb limbs presumably because there was insufficient blood flow to the area. Also my mouth was very dry. After waking up again I decided to get something to eat to combat the dryness in my mouth. On the way up the stairs to the kitchen I nearly passed out. I went back down stairs and took my blood pressure. It was 90 over 60 (pulse 90). Presumably upon standing it dropped even further. This bothered me, but it was not unexpected. This stuff is made to lower blood pressure after all. At this point (4 hours into the trip) I decided to stay confined to my bed since if I tried to do anything I would pass out from insufficient blood to the brain. These blackouts were the worst I have ever experienced. My hearing became hollow and I heard a rushing sound I have never heard before. My vision went into tunnel mode and was very annoying. Just so that it is clear these effects were directly caused by low blood pressure, they were not primary effects of clonidine, although clonidine caused the low blood pressure. I decided to just sleep it off and make sure my blood pressure didn’t go any lower. I drank a lot of water and I treated my dry throat with some chewing gum. The sedation that was present was similar to zopiclone sedation or even valium, but I would not rate it as being as fun. It was sedation without any mental change. Unlike valium which relaxes me this was just sedation nothing more. It is now ten and half hours since the experience and I am still fairly sedated even though I have drunk nearly a pot of coffee in an attempt to reverse some of the side effects. Currently my blood pressure is 120 over 90 and my pulse has decreased to 60. I still feel dizzy upon standing up. Overall I think this experience was not fun. I don’t like not being able to stand up. I don’t like sedation when there is no exhilaration. I don’t like a constant dry mouth.

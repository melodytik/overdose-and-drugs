我最近经历了一段短暂但强烈的恋爱, 人类的可用性受到限制,其使用通常仅限于动物安乐死. 我完全相信, 过量服用巴比他能导致死亡的方法是很糟糕的. , 刺之处并没有消失在我身上! 我知道我走在一条不稳定的紧绳上, 完全意识到风险和危险, 我和我的恶魔们亲切地创造了Pento,Pentorhythm,Nembsy,Nemo等药物, 我发现的效果与其他类似巴比酸盐的药物,如卡里索普罗多尔,甚至高剂量的酒精, 其他人认为更像是芬太尼,而不是卡里索普罗多尔, 是我喝过的最恶毒,最苦味的毒品, 可能甚至超过了GHB, 我最初使用的是每天口服150毫克, 我说半休, 因为虽然我会在睡觉前严格地服用它, 看电视,上网,阅读等等. 由于我长期失眠,迫切需要学习, 至少在学业结束之前, 我对学习的成没有什么顾虑. 我发现150毫克完全足以在半小时内引起睡眠. 虽然它只持续4小时左右, 产生轻微的宿醉效应, 虽然类药物可能会让人更容易入睡, 但在服用这种药物时, 对于酒来说, 没有这样的东西:我像闪光一样昏迷, 经过大约一个月的每日口服, 我转为肌肉内注射, 我以前曾在内服过胺, 很少厌恶这样做, 有趣的是,内服注射的起作用时间与口服药物相比,大约20分钟. 尽管我一直向自己保证永远不会用静脉注射, 但生活因素的结合使我为这样做辩护. 另一个故事,另一个时间. 我猜测经过多年试用各种精神活性药物, 与针相关的耻辱已经消失, 在给我第一剂之前, 我用盐水练习了大约一周. 静脉注射是最优秀的药物, 但我想任何一个徒都会告诉你, 虽然我从来没有尝试过, 口服后留下的恶心口味被绕过, 并且在15秒内让pento起到作用时, 口味变得愉快, 虽然稍微苦. 我总是怀疑有人说你真的能品尝到静脉注射的味道 (例如DMT的金属味道), 我来品尝这种味道...就像我从静脉中拔出针头,让自己感到舒适一样, 我总是尝试酒, 很快,我便会陷入, 保持平静和放松, 我通常不超过175毫克, 发现这会产生中等程度的中毒. 我从半娱乐性转变为纯娱乐性, 虽然他们拒绝参与注射, 确实,我周围的人口服药剂量要高得多, 一个观察是,在高剂量下, 服用pento的人倾向于表现出不规律和奇怪的行为. 想象一个猎人. 普森在"恐惧与厌恶在拉斯维加斯"中的酒狂欢...心灵在恐惧中退缩,无法与脊柱沟通,这很有趣,因为你实际上可以看到自己以这种可怕的方式表现,但你无法控制它. 经过650毫克口服后, 我自己也变得极度无节制, 只有那些目睹我的行为的人, 能记住这一事件, 但如果他们的说法是准确的, 关于静脉护理的一点:由于我不知道的原因, 尽管我没有吹过枪, 需要几天时间才能软化, 我怀疑这可能是由于中存在的醇, 尽管这显然不足以让我完全停止使用. 虽然我不会说我上了,但我很难减少使用. 我去看我的精神病医生寻求帮助. 我解释说我已经开始注射pento, 但他很不同情, 事实上,他更感兴趣的是它是如何获取它而不是帮助我. 这是一个警钟, 不久之后, 情况是双边的; 虽然我已经彻底享受了它的效果, 变得非常清楚, 我的使用既不是长期可持续的, 也不是有意义的幸福. 让我感到宽慰和失望的是, 我从那时起开始注射海洛因 (,你必须准星星!), 没有达到预期. 实际上,我发现海洛因的成性远低于. 我觉得在某种程度上, 我可以使用大麻, 现在我已经戒掉了和海洛因, 虽然我没有上任何药物, 我肯定是朝着那个方向. 我经常想注射,即使只是盐水. 注射本身的仪式:准备发作;抽取抽样;用带绑住;注射,所有这些都能成为强烈的上. 我打算让我的血液检测型肝炎, 禁果味道总是甜的两倍, 经过一段时间的回归, 我想我会回来做任何有自尊的精神探险家最擅长的事情: 其他: 巴比酸盐 (210): 不适用 (38), 追溯 / 总结 (11)

Sep 5, 2008. erowid.org/exp/68249 DOSE: repeated oral Barbiturates repeated IM Barbiturates repeated IV Barbiturates BODY WEIGHT: 80 kg I was recently able to experience a brief but intense love affair with pentobarbital (Nembutal), a short-acting barbiturate which was present in a thick, bitter and profoundly distasteful solution. Human availability of pentobarbital is restricted and its use is generally limited to euthanasia of animals. More recently, it’s been used in human euthanasia, and I’m thoroughly convinced that death via pentobarbital overdose would be a hell of a way to go. Indulging recreationally with a drug used to kill animals and humans – no, the irony had not been lost upon me! I knew that I was walking a precarious tight rope and was fully cognizant of the risks and dangers, but I felt so profoundly miserable, that death, if it did eventually come, wouldn’t be too bad a fate anyway. Pentobarbital, which my fellow fiends and myself affectionately coined Pento, Pentorhythm, Nembsy, Nemo, amongst others, came into abundance for several months. I found that the effects of pento were comparable to other barbiturate-like substances such as carisoprodol, or perhaps even high doses of alcohol, although I initially believed carisoprodol to be the superior drug. Others found pento more akin to opiates such as fentanyl than carisoprodol, although I have no grounds to personally qualify this comparison. Regardless, pento is the foulest and most bitter tasting drug I’ve ever drunk, probably even surpassing the likes of GHB, and the best way to drink it is to quickly chase it with a lemon-flavoured soft drink. My initial use of pento commenced with daily oral administration of ~150 mg, which I used semi-recreationally as a sleep aid. I say semi-recreationally because although I’d strictly take it before bed, I would enjoy staying up for an hour or so before nodding off to relish its intoxicating effects. Watching TV, surfing the net, reading, etc. Having become a chronic insomniac and desperately needing to buckle down with study, I had few qualms with developing dependence to it, at least until I had finished with school. In terms of sleep, I found 150 mg wholly adequate to induce sleep within half an hour. And although it only lasts around 4 hours and produces a moderate hangover effect, it was a reliable ally for dealing with insomnia. Whereas benzos such as diazepam might make it easier to fall asleep, it’s still entirely possible to toss and turn for hours while on the stuff. With the pento, no such thing: I'm out like a flash, and on the odd occasion that I absolutely-need-to-get-to-sleep, it’s a gift from the heavens. After a month or so of daily oral dosing, I progressed to intramuscular, and then intravenous injection. I had previously shot up ketamine IM, and had little aversion in doing so, although I must note that IM pento is quite painful in comparison to k. Interestingly, the onset of action of IM injections is comparable to oral administration, about 20 minutes. Although I had always promised myself that I would never go IV, a confluence of life factors lead me to justify doing so. Another story, for another time. I guess after x many years trying a wide spectrum of psychoactives, the stigma associated with needles erodes away to leave IV injecting as just another drug experience to cross off the list. I practised for about a week with saline before administering my first dose. Intravenous pento is by far the superior route of administration, but I guess that’s what any junkie will tell you, regardless of the drug. Granted, I’ve heard wonderful things about rectal pento, though I’ve never indulged. The rancorous taste in the mouth left by oral administration is bypassed, and in its place comes a pleasant albeit slightly bitter taste to the mouth as the pento kicks in within 15 seconds. I always questioned with scepticism reports that you can actually taste an IV injection (e.g. the metallic taste of DMT), but I can attest that this is the case with pento. I came to relish this taste… Just as I’d pull the needle out of my veins and get myself comfortable, a wave of peace and warmth would overcome me with increasing intensity. I would always taste the pento before it hit me. Within a matter of moments, I’d be in my pento stupor, calm and relaxed while still retaining most of my mental faculties. I generally took no more than ~175 mg IV, and found this to produce a moderate level of intoxication. Once I’d finished with university, my use of pento shifted from being semi-recreational to just plain recreational, and I had no shortage of people willing to indulge, although they declined to partake in injecting. To be sure, the people around me were taking much higher oral doses, and this was characterised by ataxia and embarrassingly high levels of disinhibition. One observation was that at higher doses, people on pento have a tendency to engage in erratic and bizarre behaviour. Think Hunter S. Thompson’s ether binge in Fear and Loathing in Las Vegas – “…the mind recoils in horror, unable to communicate with the spinal column, which is interesting because you can actually watch yourself behaving in this terrible way, but you can’t control it.” For instance, a usually placid friend of mine kicked off the side-view mirror of a taxi without any hesitation after a customer left the cab and told him “This driver is crazy.” After consuming ~650 mg orally I myself became extremely disinhibited, but for the purposes of anonymity will refrain from further detail. I have no memory of the incident except the word of those who witnessed my behaviour, but provided that their account is accurate, I’m quite shocked by the depths of my depravity. As an aside, a note on vein care: For reasons unknown to me, pento has a tendency to temporarily block my veins. The next day, a small, hard bump or “knot” tends to form in them, despite my not having blown the shot. It takes a few days for the knots to soften, and several weeks to disappear entirely. I suspect it may be due to the sorbitol present in the pento which gives it a viscous consistency, and have noticed that this effect is less prominent when I dilute it with saline. This was a cause of concern, though apparently not enough for me to stop using entirely. Over the coming weeks, my pento use became somewhat habitual, and although I wouldn’t say that I was addicted, I found it difficult to curtail my use. My depression was increasing in severity and I visited my psychiatrist for help. I explained that I’d taken to injecting pento, but he was quite unsympathetic and told me that it’d eventually kill me. In fact, he was more interested in how it’d gotten access to it than helping me. This was a wake-up call, and shortly thereafter my supply of pento dried up. The situation was double-edged; although I’d thoroughly enjoyed its effects, it was becoming abundantly clear that my usage was neither sustainable in the long term, nor conducive to any happiness in the meaningful sense of the word. So it came as just as much a relief as it did a disappointment that I could no longer source the stuff. I’ve since proceeded to shooting up heroin (hey, you’ve gotta aim for the stars!), but have found it that it hasn’t lived up to its reputation as the destroyer of young brains, minds and foetuses. It’s fallen woefully short of expectations. In fact, I’ve found heroin to be far less addictive in comparison to pento. I guess in a way I was able to use smack to wean myself off the pento, a realisation which is an endless source of amusement for me. Fast forward a couple weeks, and I’m currently off pento and heroin and intending to stay that way. Although I wasn’t addicted to either drug, I was definitely headed in that direction. I often think about shooting up, even if it’s just saline. The ritual of injecting itself: preparing the fits; swabbing; tying up with a tourniquet; and, injecting, can all become powerfully addictive in their own right. I intend to get my blood tested for Hepatitis C, and provided that I have a clean bill of health, I can’t see myself having any regrets about this brief chapter in my life. After all, the forbidden fruit always tastes twice as sweet, and after a bit of time gathering my senses, I guess I’ll be back doing what any self-respecting psychonaut does best: looking for the next drug to cross off my list.

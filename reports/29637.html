我最近因表现出"妄想"而被家人送进当地医院的精神病房. 医院认为我可能是精神分裂症患者, 但决定他们不能知道我年纪大了, 因为我17岁. 医院里每天给我两片Abilify, 一个是睡前, 一个是早上. 我不确定实际的剂量. 医院出院后的第二天, 我和一些朋友一起抽了点可乐, 我觉得可卡因是原因, 因为我没有吸过太多大麻. 只是因为我吸了更多的烟, 我觉得我只是因为我们整晚开车而感到恶心. 几天后,我和一些朋友一起吸了一根大麻. 让我感到丧的是,这让我再次感到非常生病. 我认为大麻是原因. 然而,我一直在吸大麻. 在过去的几年里, 我决定使用大麻和Abilify来治疗自己, 因为Abilify的副作用仍然很轻微 (例如视力模糊). 虽然可卡因仍然有效,但我不能吸大麻, 其他: 药品 - 阿里皮普拉 (422),大麻 (1):各种 (28),医疗用途 (47),回顾 / 摘要 (11),组合 (3)

Jun 16, 2007. erowid.org/exp/29637 DOSE: repeated oral Pharms - Aripiprazole (pill / tablet) repeated smoked Cannabis (plant material) BODY WEIGHT: 185 lb I was recently placed in the mental ward of my local hospital by my family for exhibiting 'delusions.' The hospital deemed me to be potentially schizophrenic but decided they cannot know until I am older, due to me being 17. While in the hospital, I was given two Abilify each day; one before bed and one in the morning. I am unsure of the actual dosage. The day after being released from the hospital I did some coke with some friends and took a few hits from a pipe going around. Within a few minutes I felt incredibly nauseated, but I reckoned the coke did it because I had not smoked very much pot at all. A few days later I smoked a joint with my friend's family and got the same sickness, only worse because I had smoked more, but I figured I was just car sick because we had been driving around all night. Another few days later, I smoked a joint with some friends. To my dismay, this made me very sick once more. Being the sleuthful debunker of mysteries that I am, I have concluded that the marijuana did it. However, I've always smoked pot. I've probably smoked my weight in pot over the last few years. Then I determined that I cross medicated myself with marijuana and Abilify because I was still getting mild side effects of Abilify (Blurred vision, for one). Cocaine still works, though, but I can't smoke pot, for a good while, I imagine.

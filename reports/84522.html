首先, 我认为自己是一个相当熟练的阿片类药物使用者, 但从来没有真诚的习惯或成持续超过一个月或两个. 我曾经用过口服,静脉注射,肌肉内注射,皮下注射和吸入的阿片类药物, 如氧化,化,编解素,二化,吗啡,甲,普洛西芬,特拉马多尔, 以及街头和医疗级别的二甲酸盐 (海洛因). 这份报告是关于迪劳迪德的. 我第一次听说迪劳迪德是在十六岁时从阅读威廉·伯罗斯的书中. 我还记得它在"药店牛仔"中被提及, 我几乎认为这种强烈的阿片类药物很少被处方, 我朋友曾经有过一种美沙问题, 提到他偶尔会从邻居那里得到2毫克的迪劳片, 我立刻要求向他介绍. 我们走到隔壁, 遇到了一个叫埃文的家伙. 埃文也有一点的美沙成, 但因为他没有静脉注射药物, 他并不真正关心dilaudid因为他的高耐受性. 另外,当口服或吸入时需要的水合的量要大得多, 埃文同意第二天下午2点左右给我药物, 所以第二天我在他的房子1点50分, 我给他25美元买5个2毫克的药片, 总共10毫克. 他坐上了车,开车到经销商的家, 20分钟后又回来, 拿着几粒非常小的白色药片, 我真诚地感谢他, 开车到当地药店去, 在那里我买了十包1cc的胰岛素注射器. 我尽快回家了. 医生说:"我知道, 我准备了下面的家用物品来准备注射: 2个无菌的酒精抹布 1小匙大小的金属匙 纸 一个打火机 我忠实的麻皮带/带 (我选择不使用棉花, 因为我听过朋友们关于棉花热的不好故事, 我知道我可能会冒更大的风险不过我的药物, 然而, 由于dilaudid是医疗等级我不太担心未知的固体粒子. 一般来说,过街头品质药物总是个好主意. 棉花热不会杀死你, 血栓塞可以. 在我开始在我的夜桌上煮之前, 我先放上"破碎的社交场景"唱片来设置情绪. 我用手机将它们粉碎成粉末. 接下来,我打开1厘米注射器的包装, 抽取大约55单位未蒸的自来水进入桶, 然后重新分配水到子. 我把粉末倒入水中, 看着它慢慢溶解. 根据我在网上读到的, 建议使用热来加快过程, 我用打火机慢慢加热液体, 看着子底部剩余的粉末消散成一种美丽的微微乳的溶液. 底部还有一点粉末, 我可以推断大部分是药片被切割的填充物, 我小心地把针子浸入溶液中, 谨慎地不要让针头触及底部的粉状沉积物, 因为这可能会导致针塞住. 我非常小心地将45个单位的天堂液体注入了注射器中. 我用酒精抹布准备了我的手臂, 紧紧地围着我的手臂, 这是真相的时刻. 我有点紧张和拙, 因为我的脉升高, 我深呼吸,然后顺地将针头插入静脉, 这针是如此细, 我几乎没有感觉到穿透. 一旦它在我轻轻地拉回和一个小羽毛的深红色血类似的孔雀羽毛混合在注射器内的溶液. 我知道我已经注册了, 我慢慢地按下, 我抽出了针头,放开了我的呼吸. 在我手臂上,脊椎上, 最后充满了我的大脑 我喘不过气,倒在柔软的床上, 感觉无比高兴, 我开始沉重地点头, 完全满意我的环境和周围的一切. 我觉得"高潮"不是描述阿片类药物热潮的正确词. 我觉得"低"是一个更好的形容词. 我觉得自己沉浸在现实之下, 沉浸在一种冷漠的平静和宁静中, 我在晚上八点左右开始下床, 但当我晚上十一点左右睡觉时, 我也喜欢我只能描述为阿尔法阶段的睡眠, 我经常在服用阿片类药物后睡觉时遇到这些"片梦", 总的来说, 迪拉化物已成为我最喜欢的输液阿片类药物, 这种冲动也同样, 如果不是更高, 我在接下来的一周内用同样的方式做了剩余的迪劳迪德. 让我有点害怕, 我有这样一个稳定的来源这些天堂的小平板. 我可能会再次使用, 但我知道我必须谨慎地使用, 不会造成问题. 片是我最喜欢的药物, 但由于我过去曾因片而失去朋友, 让人们难以享受生活的非物质快乐. 其他: 2015年11月13日发布 水合摩 (300) :回顾 / 摘要 (11),一般 (1),单独 (16)

Nov 13, 2015. erowid.org/exp/84522 DOSE: 4 mg IV Hydromorphone (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 130 lb To start off, I’d consider myself a rather well seasoned opiate user but have never had a sincere habit or addiction that lasted more than a month or two. I have used orally, intravenously, intramuscularly, subcutaneously and insufflated opiates such as oxycodone, hydrocodone, codeine, dihydrocodeine, morphine, methadone, propoxyphene, tramadol, as well as street and medical grade diacetylmorphine (heroin). This report is about dilaudid. I first heard about dilaudid when I was around sixteen from reading William Burroughs. I also remembered it being mentioned in Drugstore Cowboy and that it gave a better rush than heroin when IV’d. I pretty much figured that an opiate that intense was rarely prescribed and that the chances of me ever getting a hold of any were slim to none; I was wrong. One night a friend of mine who was going through a bit of a methadone problem mentioned that he occasionally got 2mg dilaudid tablets from his neighbor for around $5 a pop. My jaw dropped and I immediately asked to be introduced to this guy. We walked next door and we met the guy, whom we’ll call Evan. Evan was also a bit of a methadone addict as well but because he didn’t IV drugs, he didn’t really care much for dilaudid because of his high tolerance. Also, the amount of hydromorphone needed to get high is substantially more when taken orally or insufflated as opposed to taken intravenously. Evan agreed to get me the drugs the next day around 2pm, so the next day I was over at his house at 1:50pm on the dot as if I wanted to make a good impression at an interview. I gave him $25 for five 2mg tablets, a total of 10mgs in all. He got in his car, drove over to his dealer’s house, and came back twenty minutes later with a few very tiny white pills that had an “M” printed on one side and the number “2” printed on the other. After we exchanged numbers for future transactions I thanked him sincerely and drove off the local drug store where I was able to procure a ten pack of 1cc insulin syringes. After giving the pharmacist the money and the earnest smile of an opiate connoisseur who knows they’re about to get blitzed on deliciously rare pharmaceuticals, I was off to my house as quickly as possible. When I got home I prepared the following household items needed to prepare my shot: 2 sterile alcohol swabs 1 teaspoon-sized metal spoon Wax paper 1 lighter My trusty hemp belt/tourniquet (A normally important item that I chose not to use is cotton, as I have heard nasty stories from friends about cotton fever and choose not to filter with cotton when possible. I know that I am probably taking an even bigger risk by not filtering my drugs, however, with the dilaudid being medical grade I wasn’t very concerned about unknown solid particles. In general it’s always a good idea to filter street quality drugs. Cotton fever will not kill you, an embolism can.) Before I commenced cooking the dilaudid on my nightstand, I put on a Broken Social Scene record to set the mood. I then placed two of the small pills in between the folded piece of wax paper and crushed them into a fine powder with my cell phone. Next, I opened up the packet of 1cc syringes and drew around 55 units of undistilled tap water into the barrel, then redistributed the water into the spoon. I then poured the powder into the water and watched it slowly dissolve. From what I’ve read online, applying heat was recommended to expedite the process and help separate the dilaudid from the filler, which apparently is not easily soluble in water. I slowly heated the liquid with a lighter and watched the remaining powder at the bottom of the spoon dissipate into a beautiful slightly milky and slightly viscous solution. There was a bit of powder still at the bottom which I can deduce was mostly the filler that the pills are cut with to keep them together. I carefully dipped the needle into the solution, cautious not to let the tip of the needle touch the powdery sediment at the bottom, as it would likely cause the needle to clog. Ever so gently I drew up about 45 units of the heavenly liquid into the barrel of the syringe. I then prepped my arm with the alcohol swabs and tightened the belt around my upper arm, a few inches above that magic spot in the bend of the elbow. It was the moment of truth. Not having IV’d drugs in over a year, I was a little nervous and clumsy because of my elevated pulse and general feeling of giddiness mixed with anticipation for this new chemical that I was welcoming onto my body. I took a deep breath and smoothly inserted the needle into my vein as flat as possible, around a 30 degree angle. The 31 gauge needle was so fine I barely even felt the penetration. Once it was in I gently pulled back on the plunger and a small plume of dark crimson blood resembling a peacock feather mixed with the solution inside the syringe. I knew I had registered and I slowly pressed down the plunger until there was all but a unit of liquid left, which was mostly blood anyway. I pulled the needle out and released my breath. There were about three or four tension-filled seconds before a tidal wave of warmth and comfort came rushing up my arm, up my spine, and finally filled my brain with euphoric opiate bliss, like and old friend. I gasped and fell back onto my soft bed and was overcome with indescribable elation, just looking up at the ceiling whilst wrapped up so gently in dilaudid’s buoyant embrace. As the rush faded I was left in a fetal position and began to nod heavily, completely satisfied with my environment and everything around me. I don’t feel like “high” is the right word to describe the opiate buzz. I feel that “low” is a much better adjective. Whenever I’m in a heavy nod I feel like I’ve sunk below reality into a blanket of apathetic calmness and serenity, almost exactly how it’s portrayed in Ewan McGregor’s overdose scene in Trainspotting. The effects persisted for the next five hours and I finally started coming down around 8pm, but was still extremely comfortably buzzed when I went to sleep around 11pm. I also enjoyed what I can only describe as alpha stage sleep with vivid dreamlike sequences similar to what I would imagine lucid dreaming would be like. I often experience these ‘opium dreams’ while going to sleep/nodding off after having taken opiates and it’s part of the experience that I’ve grown quite fond of. Overall, dilaudid has become my favorite opiate to IV; even more so than heroin because it’s much safer to judge the amount to use and it’s not cut with all kinds of shit from the street. The rush is also equally, if not more, euphoric than smack. I ended up doing the rest of the dilaudid that I had in the same manner over the course of the following week. It kind of scares me that I have such a steady source of these heavenly little tablets. I will probably do it again eventually but I know that I must be careful in spacing out my use as to not create a problem. Recreationally, opiates are and always will be my favorite drugs in the whole world, but having lost friends to them and had problems with them in my past, I also respect the power they can wield on your life if you aren’t careful. The artificial joy they produce makes it hard to enjoy the non-material pleasures of life.

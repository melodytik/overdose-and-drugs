Very strong and clear. Strong to the point of the semi-common and often unsettling strong trip occurrence of a brief moment of persistent deja vu for a few minutes or so at maybe an hour or so in. Enough to shake my equilibrium briefly without impacting the overall stability of the trip. Strong enough to likely be scary if entering unprepared, stressed, etc. although that comes with the territory. Lasted as before, about 3-4 hours. This is a beautiful substance, in my limited experience. I personally would prefer this over the natural form, 2nd only to maybe a half and half mix. This particular substance, based solely on the past 12 hours or so, reminds me of a blend of psilocybin and MDMA, though more clearheaded, and not overly entactogenic. Will definitely be dipping back into this in a week or two after I've let this experience percolate. I will close with saying that I am seemingly predisposed both to weak trips, and to less visual trips even when strong. With that in mind, the less visual nature of this (to me) may not be fundamental to the substance. It is definitely potent, though, with 0.010-0.020 being likely a safe territory, adjusted according to level of preparation.

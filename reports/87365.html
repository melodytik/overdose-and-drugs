美国 我第一次听说racetam家族的药物时, 我对一种无otropic药物非常感兴趣, 这就像一种药物完成了人类大脑. 尤其引起了我的兴趣, 经过几个月的研究之后, 我决定使用无otropic 子洞, 另外,它还有一个号"draganon", 第一次: 我第一次服用的是750毫克的安尼拉和2克莱西. 诚实地说, 根据我从别人那里听到的报道, 我并不期望会发生很多事情. 但在击落飞船20分钟后, 我被一种非常奇怪的清醒感知状态所击中. 它的效果是温和的, 但不是任何方式微妙. 像电视广告这样的小外围噪音会跳出来,让我感到惊. 这种情况已经不再发生在我身上, 但这很可能是因为我已经习惯了安尼拉的头部空间. 那天晚上,我和一些朋友一起参加了大火. 我记得我的一个朋友在放火前打电话给我, 根据电话号码, 我预计我的朋友J的声音. 我脑子无法处理那个信息, 只有A在和我说话, 而不是J, 朋友们经常打电话给对方, 但我无法拼出连续的句子, 我可以在社交环境中正常运作. 晚上是放松和结束没有太多不同于常态. 现在我知道750毫克的阿尼拉对我来说是个巨大的过量. 如果我服用超过半个囊 (可能大约400毫克是我的上限), 我发现安尼拉的效果持续4至6小时 (峰值在2至3小时左右),这取决于活动水平,我的胃里有什么,以及我最近服用了它. 如果我先吃一顿饭 (甚至吃一些含脂肪的小零食,比如布朗尼或饼干), 我觉得最好每隔一天或几天服用一次, 作用和有效性: 抑制性欲:虽然我不认为阿尼拉是厌食症,但它似乎使对任何东西的欲望 (尤其是食物和性) 消失在后台. 我不是没有身体的欲望, 而是有更好的事情可以做, 当药物效应减弱时, 我感到非常饥饿, 因为我10小时没有吃东西, 增加视力敏度:阿尼拉的视力效果与我从大麻中获得的效果相似, 我将视觉效果描述为我的大脑正在处理比以前更多的信息; 我看不清楚 (我近视和色盲),但我可以一次看到更多的图片. 几乎就像我视野的边缘视野更少, 而主要视野更多. 在大自然中看起来更有趣,更美. 增加听觉感知:我发现我对音乐的欣赏不仅增加了, 我常常会发现自己的脸上着微笑, 头部动, 脚跳动. 音乐中的微小细节如背景吉他或其他模糊的声音效果都被提出来并放大. 这几乎就像你能想象音乐从扬声器中滚出来. 每种乐器都有自己的舞台, 每种音乐元素都可以分别被感知, 虽然比大麻的听觉效果更微妙, 用作学习/工作辅助:通常我很难长时间保持任何思维. 我可能有注意力缺陷障碍, 但当它与咖啡因相结合时, 我可以连续工作数小时. 我曾经用5-10毫克的甲基酸盐 (Ritalin) 结合安尼拉, 测试这种药物增强认知效果的方法之一是对自己进行测试, 我很失望地发现, 平均而言, 酸并没有减少完成题的平均时间. 虽然我没有发现我在苏多库的表现在安尼拉上有所提高,我发现我的思考和说话能力有了显著的改善. 德语 (我的第二语言) 像是我的母语一样流动, 思想和想法只是在没有任何努力的情况下自由流动. 咖啡因通常对我有很小的影响, 但当100毫克咖啡因与300毫克的阿尼拉相结合时, 我发现自己专注,充满活力,警觉. 皮拉的强化效应已被广泛记录, 皮拉经常被MDMA使用者用来恢复"". 我从来没有尝试过MDMA与aniracetam一起使用,但我已经将aniracetam与咖啡因,甲基,LSA,JWH-018,JWH-250,JWH-081和JWH-019结合起来,可以毫不怀疑地说,每一种药物的效果都显著增加 (我会说大约是1.5倍). 我不会说每种物质的效果都变得更明显或变化,而是需要更小的剂量才能达到相同的效果. 我发现阿尼拉的奇特之处之一是它使我成为乒乓球的天主. 虽然我通常是平均水平的球员, 并且与我的朋友赢得了偶数的比赛, 我已经赢得了21比7的阿尼拉塞坦, 这在清醒时是闻所未闻的. 我不知道我的反应时间是否减慢了, 或是我的手眼协调是否有所改善, 我还没有在其他运动中测试过这种方法, 是一种有趣的物质. 我发现它娱乐更多的任何东西. 我只能坐着着天空看着云层滚过来, 没有崩 (尽管我在咖啡因或甲基酸等刺激物下降时比平常更难崩), 对于发明了这种东西的人来说, 其他: 复合剂 (3), 后视 / 摘要 (11), 不适用 (38)

"Id Suppression at Its Finest: An Experience with Aniracetam (exp87365)". Erowid.org . Oct 20, 2010. erowid.org/exp/87365 DOSE: repeated oral Aniracetam (capsule) BODY WEIGHT: 155 lb When I first heard of the –racetam family of drugs I was extremely intrigued by a nootropic that proported to have no crash, no addictive potential, and relatively few side effects. It was like a drug that completed the human brain. It especially sparked my interest because it could be purchased cheaply without a prescription. After researching the racetams for several months I decided to take the plunge down the nootropic rabbit hole with aniracetam, a fat-soluble piracetam derivative that also acts as an ampakine. Plus it has the nickname draganon which, let’s be honest, is a pretty bad-ass name for a “dietary supplement”. First Time: For my first time I took a 750mg capsule of aniracetam along with 2 grams of lecithin. To be honest, based on reports I had heard from others I did not expect much to happen. But about 20 minutes after downing the capsule I got hit with a very strange mind clearing perceptive state. Its effects were mild but not in any way subtle. Small peripheral noises like television commercials would jump out at me and startle me a bit. This doesn’t happen to me anymore but that is most likely because I have become accustomed to the aniracetam headspace. I went to a bonfire with some friends that night. I remember one of my friends calling me before the bonfire looking for a ride. I expected my friend J’s voice based on the phone number. Instead my other friend A said hello and my mind simply couldn’t process the information that A was talking to me not J, especially since I had a thing for A at the time. This situation really wasn’t out of the ordinary since my friends call from each others’ phones all the time, but I could not put together a string of coherent sentences for the life of me piecing together a series of uhhhs, yeahs, and okays instead. Once I stopped paying attention to the effects of the drug, I could function normally in a social setting. The night was relaxing and finished without much different from the norm. Dosage: I know now that 750mg of aniracetam is a massive overdose for me. I get serious brain fog if I take over a half a capsule (maybe about 400mg is my upper limit) though about 250-300mg seems to be ideal for me. I found that the effects from aniracetam last anywhere from 4 to 6 hours (with a peak around 2-3 hours in) depending on level of activity, what’s in my stomach, and how recently I have taken it last. I found that if I down the aniracetam on an empty stomach that I get minimal effects and that if I eat a meal (or even a small snack with a high fat content like a brownie or a cookie) beforehand then the effects are increased by an order of magnitude. There is perhaps some tolerance build up for me and I find it best taken every other day or every few days for the best results. Effects and Effectiveness: Id suppression: Though I would not consider aniracetam to be anorectic in any way, it seems to make desires for anything (food and sex especially) fade into the background. It’s not that I don’t have physical desires, it’s more like I have better things to do than eat or look at porn. On several occasions while on aniracetam I found myself ignoring the desire for food and when the drug’s effects wore off I got intensely hungry and felt burnt out because I had not eaten in 10 hours. Increased visual acuity: the visual effects from aniracetam are similar to the effects that I get from weed, though with a much clearer head, though it is a bit different. I would describe the visual effects like my brain is processing more incoming information than before; I can’t see better (I’m near-sighted and colour-blind), but I can see more of the picture at one time. It is almost like less of my field of view is in my peripheral vision and more is in my main field of view. Things look more interesting and more beautiful, in nature especially. Increased auditory perception: I found that my appreciation for music is not only increased, but greatly increased on aniracetam. If I hear a song that I like, quite often I will catch myself with a smile plastered onto my face with my head bobbing and my feet tapping. Minute details in music such as background guitars or other obscure sound effects are brought out and magnified. It’s almost as if you can visualize the music rolling out of the speakers. Each and every instrument has its own stage and every musical element can be singled out and separately perceived, or the music as a whole can be experienced. It is similar to the auditory effects of weed though much more subtle. Use as a study/work aid: Normally I have trouble holding any train of thought for an extended period of time. I probably have attention deficit disorder but I never went to a doc to get diagnosed. Aniracetam by itself does not significantly increase by attention span, but when it is coupled with caffeine I can work for hours on end. I combined aniracetam once with about 5-10mg of methylphenidate (Ritalin) and turned into a goddamn machine for about 6 hours doing about 30 hours worth of work in a fraction of the time. One way I devised to test the effectiveness of the cognitive enhancing effects of the substance was a test against myself comparing Sudoku times on aniracetam and sober. I found to my disappointment that, on average, aniracetam did not decrease the average time to complete a sodoku puzzle. While I have not found my performance at Sudoku to be increased on aniracetam, I have found that my ability to articulate thoughts and speak are improved significantly. German (my second language) flows as if it were my native language and there isn’t a search for vocabulary that I normally have when trying to speak or thing in German. Thoughts and ideas just flow freely without much effort at all. Substance potentiation: caffeine usually has a minimal effect on me but when 100mg of caffeine is combined with about 300mg aniracetam. I find myself focused, energetic, and alert. The potentiation effects of the racetams have been documented extensively with piracetam which is often used by MDMA users to get their “roll” back. I have never tried MDMA on aniracetam myself but I have combined aniracetam with caffeine, methylphenidate, LSA, JWH-018, JWH-250, JWH-081, and JWH-019 and can say without a doubt that the effects of each is increased significantly (I’d say multiplied by about 1.5x). I wouldn’t say that any of the effects of each substance is clearer or changed but rather a smaller dose is needed to achieve the same effects. One of the quirkier parts of aniracetam I found is that it makes me a God at ping pong. Normally I play about average, winning about an even number of matches with my friends, but on aniracetam I destroy them consistently. I’ve won 21 to 7 on aniracetam and this is unheard of when playing sober. I don’t know if my reaction times are decreased or if my hand eye coordination is improved but for some reason my ping pong ability on aniracetam is improved significantly. I haven’t tested this with any other sports but I would suspect the results to be similar. Aniracetam is an interesting substance. I find it recreational more than anything. When on aniracetam I am content just sitting and staring at the sky watching clouds roll by where it would be difficult for me to sit still for an extended period of time without seeking some kind of mental or physical stimulation. There is no crash (though I crash much harder than normal when coming down from stumulants like caffeine or methylphenidate) and a somewhat potent mood lift. Mad props to the guy who invented this stuff.

我和朋友们决定买我们家伙所拥有的东西, 三种我从未听说过的药物. 我通常讨厌止痛药和其他让我感到困惑的药物, 他说它们是7.5毫克,所以我和朋友买了三个, 药片的一侧印有十字架. 幸运的是,第三个朋友也说他也想尝试. 否则我会更糟糕的感觉, 感觉自己慢慢地死. 我决定吸一半, 等待平常的时间, 一个半小时后, 感觉就像是两杯. 我觉得这半部分是我能得到的最高,所以我吞下了其余部分. 我其他两个朋友一下子就嗅到了这一切. 我们决定下楼坐沙发观看"奇怪的爱情博士". 在电影中期, 我们都开始点头, 我不得不沉, 因为最轻微的动作都会让我发出强烈的鼻. 电影结束后,我的朋友决定要去睡觉, 我觉得'不如现在就这么做, 我试着慢慢走, 让我的胃赶上我的其他部分, 但吐的冲动又回来了. 每个人都开始生病,当他们认为他们已经完成, 他们回来更多. 我和朋友走回家, 清凉的空气非常清爽, 我们进去后, 两人又感到恶心, 吸烟也让我很生病. 我发现这种东西就像止痛药中的LSD, 效果低于一毫克. 我们知道这将是一个漫长的旅程. 我们一夜之间都睡不着半个多小时, 偶尔起床吐. 我们终于在日出后有了几个小时, 下午3点起床. 我们仍然在疯狂的那些药. 我的朋友无法忍受任何食物, 我认为这是摆脱这种可怕药物的唯一方法. 我们只是躺在床上,痛苦,像狗一样生病,看电影,睡在这里和那里. 六岁时,我的鼻子已经减弱到可以忍受的程度, 我们买了一些披萨, 吞了它, 到晚上10点, 我朋友们在第二天仍然病了. 我得出的结论是,一个人不应该在想要上升时服用降压剂! 尤其是那些旨在治疗硬核海洛因成的人. 我会说这会是很好的给孩子们吓到他们, 让他们从药物终身. 其他: 药品 - 布普伦诺芬 (265):第一次 (2),困难经历 (5),健康问题 (27),宿醉/几天后 (46),多天经历 (13),不好的旅行 (6),大群 (10+) (19)

Oct 10, 2010. erowid.org/exp/51900 DOSE: T+ 0:00 3.75 mg insufflated Pharms - Buprenorphine (ground / crushed) T+ 1:30 3.75 mg insufflated Pharms - Buprenorphine (ground / crushed) BODY WEIGHT: 145 lb After trying to aquire some stimulants with no sucess, my friends and I decided to get what our guy had, which were three pain pills of some substance I'd never heard of. Usually, I detest painkillers and other pharms that just space me out, also I like to research what I'm putting into my body, to know what to expect. He said they were 7.5mg, so my friend and I bought all three, thinking that if they were like perc we'd need more than that for a good buzz. The pills were imprinted with a crussifix on one side. Luckilly a third friend said he wanted to try it too. Otherwise I'd have been more fucked and all, feeling like I was slowly dying by puking myself to death. Seeing as I had no idea what this shit would do to me, I decided to snort half and wait the usual time it takes for pain pills to peak for me, about an hour and a half. At an hour and a half, it felt like a couple percocet. I felt like I was as high as I was gonna get from that half, so I swallowed the rest. My other two friends just sniffed the whole thing at once. There was a party going on and that was getting to be more and more overwhelming by the minute, so we decided to retire downstairs on the couches and watch Doctor Strange Love. By the middle of the movie, we'd all begun nodding, but I felt strangely very awake, not neccisarilly alert. By the middle, I had to slump down, because the slightest movements gave me intense nasea. When it movie was finished, my friend decided he was going to go to bed and we all agreed it was probably time to sleep off this horrible drug. The urge to vomit became stronger and stronger, and I figured 'might as well do this now instead of later'. After vomiting, I drank some water, and tried to walk slowly so as to let my stomach catch up with the rest of me, but the urge to puke came back again. Everyone started getting sick and just when they thought they were done, they came back for more. My friend and I walked home and the cool air was very refreshing and I hardly noticed how cold it was. When we got inside, we both felt sick again, and went to throw up more. Smoking cigarrettes also made me very ill. When I got home, I hopped online and discovered that this shit is like the LSD of painkillers, being effective at doses less than a milligram. It also lasted 24-72hrs, so we knew it was gonna be a long ride. We spent the rest of the night unable to sleep for more than a half hour at a time, intermitantly getting out of bed to throw up. We eventually got a few solid hours after the sun rose, and got out of bed at 3pm. We were still insanely fucked up on those pills. My friend couldn’t handle eating any food, but I forced myself to. I figured it was the only way to get off this horrible drug. We just layed in bed, miserable, sick as dogs, watching movies and dozing here and there. By six, my nasea had disapaited to a tolerable level, and I could walk around fairly normally. We got some pizza and devoured the fuck out of it and by 10pm the effects were finished for me. My friends still remained sick for the next day. My conclusion is that one should never take a downer when they want uppers! Especially not something that is designed to treat hardcore heroin addiction. I’d say that it'd be good to give to kids to freak them the fuck out and turn them off from drugs for the rest of their lives.

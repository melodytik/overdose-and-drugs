Aug 31, 2018. erowid.org/exp/112304 DOSE: 30 mg vaporized MET (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 155 lb Background: I have extensive experience with various psychedelics, and pretty much the full range of drugs available as well as a few more exotic ones. I have an intimate knowledge of DMT, and I should mention quite a nasty heroin habit for the last few years than I have just recently left behind, I always smoked the heroin, which I only mention for reasons that will become clear later in the report. After a previous night experimenting with some MET I had got my hands on, with varying doses and effects, each of which I found it hard to distinguish from my previous experiences with DMT, I decided I would try again tonight, and venture a little further. The little further became a LOT further. I vaped the substance in the same way that one would ‘chase the dragon’. Placing it on a flat sheet of tinfoil, with a rolled up ‘tooter’ (a post it note) in my mouth and heating the foil from below with a lighter, and chasing the beetle with the tooter. I wouldn’t recommend this ROA to anyone that didn’t have a lot of experience chasing the dragon, but for me I have found it to be the most effective way to perfectly vape DMT in the past, having used a variety of other methods, and if used correctly it can be a way to achieve a breakthrough with a relatively smaller dose than would be needed with other methods. I loaded up the foil with a large pinch around 60mg, knowing that with this ROA I am only likely to vape around 50% in the first three breaths, which is usually enough to start the breakthrough. I began vaping, getting quite a few laps of the foil in my first two breaths. I squeezed in one extra up and down as things started to deteriorate. The tin foil become pixelated and my hand got shaky. I managed to reach over to turn off the light and lie back in my bed. What followed was one the most intense experiences of my entire life. Dwarfing all of 100+ previous DMT trips. Obviously these trips are completely indescribable. All I can attest to was the level of intensity, detail, realism and colour was higher than anything I had ever had before. In a vain attempt to actually describe what was happening I would say I was staring into the face of a multi headed, multi bodied, almost robotic like creature with millions of eyes, perpetually changing shape and form yet remaining the same, that took up my entire field of Vision. It was realer and more detailed than anything in real life, and I was completely overcome by it. I was no longer aware of my body or the noise of the rain outside. I must have said ‘What the fuck!!!?’ Over and over again 100 times throughout the entire experience as what I was seeing was completely unfathomable to me. The intense part probably only lasted around 3 minutes, with a still relatively strong after effect lasting another 10 or 15 minutes, which I spent curled up repeating phrases like ‘what the fuck?’ ‘Jesus Christ’ etc etc. I have never ever ever experienced anything like it. The entire experience was completely overwhelming yet positive. I felt no fear, only awe. Not much has been written about MET, most of what I had read said it was very similar to DMT, and at regular doses it is difficult to tell any difference between the two, but I do honestly believe that is has the potential to be more powerful than DMT if administered correctly at the mid to upper levels of dosage. God luck and god speed to anyone lucky enough to get their hands on this amazing substance, that I will be forever grateful for.

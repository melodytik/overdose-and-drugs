之前使用过毒品:8个月前开始使用毒品. 大麻,酒精,Kratom,,,DMT,萨尔维亚,维科丁,酸 (可能不是LSD),4-Aco-DMT,胺. 我昨晚抽了大麻. 最后一次服用的胺是三周前的4-Aco-DMT. 我得到了大约50毫克的4-HO-MPT样品, 我以前曾经和他们一起过, 我没有为这次旅行设定任何具体的目的, 这种材料有点苦, 但没有粗, 样品被称重,然后分成三条相同大小的线,并将其放入囊中,然后再称重. 我和A拿了15毫克和16毫克,而M拿了18毫克. 但我发现我的比例相当不错. 在此之前, 我已经吃了大约3个小时. 到了六点半, 我们在我的家里接受了凝治疗. 我姐姐来和我们聊了一会儿, 20分钟后我们出去散步. 虽然很冷,但还不错. 我预计现在会有点感觉到, 因为通常我很快受到胺的影响. 在五分钟内, 我开始感觉到它. 缺乏我从和4-Aco-DMT中得到的冷静感. 没有紧张或类似的东西, 没有坐下来等待它来袭. 药物慢慢地进入我的体内, 在服药40分钟后, 我们继续走路,到达海, 我得很厉害, 比18毫克的4-Aco-DMT还要厉害, 一切都在移动,但没有呼吸. 地上的木片变成了叶子, 树木也转移了. 一切看起来有点模糊, 很难说话. 我们都注意到我们笑着笑着. 我感觉到身体有些, 我感到非常困惑; 矛盾的是我既很冷又奇怪地温暖. 我有点高兴, 但经常想到悲伤的事情. 思想是有意义的,但没有. 我在一个类似于和4-Aco-DMT的思维空间中, 虽然我们并不关心, 我们着墙壁一会儿, 墙壁没有旋转或呼吸, 但A和我并没有. 走过人们是有点奇怪的, 因为我们知道如果我们看着他们, 药物注射后大约一个半小时, 视觉效果显著减弱, 我开始思考自己有多幸运, 一段时间我专注于这件事. 我们走进了我的房子, 聊了一会儿,M还在, 我对此感到惊, 两小时后我的视觉都消失了, 总的来说,这是一个愉快的经历, 但没有特别有意义. 虽然它与和4-Aco-DMT相似, 但没有任何有意义的想法, 我可以记住我的想法, 他们觉得他们有一些价值, 我不记得自己在想什么, 我知道这些想法是毫无价值的. 视觉效果比我从4-Aco-DMT中体验到的要好, 这种感觉比之前提到的其他物质要少得多. 降落让我感到有点空虚. 我也很惊, 但他确实比A或I多了一点点. 另一个问题是,我认为3是一个很难被误解的数字. 它失去与两个人建立的亲密联系, 也没有四个人或更多人群的团队思维. 最后, 像往常一样, 服用药物7小时后,它仍然存在. 我忘了补充说, 服用后大约两小时我检查了我的CEV, 在服用药物7小时后, 很难让它起立. 美国 4-HO-MPT (530):第一次 (2),小组 (2-9) (17)

Mar 2, 2011. erowid.org/exp/89739 DOSE: 16 mg oral 4-HO-MPT (capsule) BODY WEIGHT: 130 lb T+0.00: 16 mg 4-HO-MPT Freebase Age: 18 Weight: 128 lbs Gender: Male Prior substance use: Started doing drugs 8 months ago. Marijuana, Alcohol, Kratom, Nitrous, Shrooms, DMT, Salvia, Vicodin, Acid (likely not LSD), 4-Aco-DMT, Ketamine. I had smoked marijuana last night. Last tryptamine taken was 4-Aco-DMT three weeks ago. I had gotten a sample of approximately 50 mg 4-HO-MPT which I split between my two close friends M and A. I had tripped with them before but not with the two of them together. I hadn’t set any specific purpose for this trip as I like to do, but that wasn’t much of an issue. The material tasted a bit bitter but not harsh, appeared white and fluffy, and was received one week ago. The sample was weighed out and then split into three equal sized lines which were placed into capsules and then weighed later. A and I got the 15 and 16 mg ones, while M got the 18 mg one. This all could be off by one or two mg in either direction, but I’ve found my scale to be pretty good. I hadn’t eaten for about 3 hours before this. At 6.30 we took our gel caps in my house. My sis came and talked to us for a little bit and 20 minutes later we took a walk outside. It was cold but not bad. I expected to be feeling it a bit by now, since usually I’m quickly affected by tryptamines. Within 5 minutes I was starting to feel it. There was a lack of the chilled feeling I get from shrooms and 4-Aco-DMT. It was a bit more spacey, chill, and euphoric, no nervousness or anything like that, no sitting back and waiting for it to hit. It just slowly crept up to me and within 40 minutes of dosing I was pretty close to peaking. We kept on walking and made it to the beach, where we sat around for a while. I was tripping hard, much harder than 18 mg 4-Aco-DMT, I was at a most definite +++. Everything was moving, but not breathing. The woodchips on the ground morphed into leaves and the trees shifted about. Everything looked a bit fuzzy, not at all crisp or 3D like (something I get from shrooms). Talking was very hard. We all noticed we were laughing and smiling tons. I was feeling rather tingly and removed from my body. I felt very confused; paradoxically I was both quite cold and yet oddly warm. I was a bit happy but often times thinking about sad things. Thoughts made sense but didn’t. I was in a similar mind space to shrooms and 4-Aco-DMT but my thoughts had much less meaning. After a little while we started walking back, we knew we were cold even though we didn’t really care. We stared at a wall for a little while and it didn’t swirl or breathe, the texture just became shapes, specifically little gnome like creatures. M mentioned he was seeing textures he felt, but A and I didn’t have that. Walking past people was a bit weird, for we knew if we looked at them we would stare and search their thoughts and minds. About an hour and half after dosing my visuals were significantly less pronounced, still noticeable but not all that strong. I started thinking about how fortunate I am, and was focused on this for a while. We went into my house and talked for a little while, M was still tripping pretty hard but A and I were definitely coming down. I was surprised by this but wasn’t going to complain. My visuals were all gone after two hours and the trippy thoughts tapered to an end after another two hours. Overall it was an enjoyable experience but not particularly meaningful. While it had a similar head space to shrooms and 4-Aco-DMT nothing meaningful came of the thoughts I was having, it was lacking in depth. After taking those substances I can later remember my thoughts and they feel like they have some merit even while sober. With 4-HO-MPT I don’t really remember much of what I was thinking and know that the thoughts were pretty meritless. The visuals were greater than what I’ve experienced from 4-Aco-DMT and different from shrooms in the way they didn’t breathe, they moved in other ways. There was significantly less euphoria from this than the other previously mentioned substances. Coming down made me feel a bit empty. I was also very surprised by the short duration, only tripping hard for one and a half hours. M tripped a little while longer than A or I, but he did take a teeny bit more. On another note, I think that 3 is a hard number to trip with. It loses the intimate connection one makes with 2 and doesn’t have the group mentality of 4 or more. Lastly, like usual there is a tingly effect that lasts significantly after the trip. Seven hours after dosing it’s still there. Note from the next day: I forgot to add that around two hours after dosing I explored my CEV, they were very immersive, calming, and fractal like. And seven hours after dosing it was very hard to get it up.

我从来没有尝试过NBOMe, 我常常吐, 身体也会受到很重的负荷. 此外, NBOMe 的声誉有点危险. 这就是我担心的原因, 经验前几天, 我用试剂测试了一小部分涂料. 我觉得我应该分享我的. 马基斯的颜色慢慢变为黄色,然后变成橄黑. 梅克开始棕色,并变成了红棕黑. 最后,西蒙变成了蓝色. 最后一个在区分LSD和25x-NBOMe擦拭器时可能非常有用. 经历我的那一天, 我禁食, 禁食咖啡因. 在此之前的5天里, 我也禁酒了. 我在一个美丽,温暖,阳光明的星期五下午, 这张账单有苦味. 为了感觉到未来, 我决定徒步到草地. 在T+15分钟, 我开始感觉到牙上的刺麻木, 在大约T+20分钟, 我开始感觉到第一个出现的迹象. 我眼睛和身体开始感觉像是微妙地震动. 我刚刚在T+30分钟的时间到达一个令人愉快的草地. 声音有奇怪的回声, 我下面的草似乎安排自己对称的图案, 我遵守一个突然的冲动躺在温暖的下午阳光. 我闭上眼睛, 遇到了漂亮的 CEV. 有各种各样的色彩,旋转,情感动画面孔, 旋转在彼此之间, 速度不同, 像时钟轮. 我感觉到幸福和爱从这些视觉转移到我自己的存在中. 身体和情感上的快乐随着我升到一个的++++, 我周围的每一个声音都变得像笑声, 显然这将成为一个比我预期的更愉快的旅行. 我打开眼睛, 决定走去见一些朋友. 愉悦和爱的振动继续在整个经历中保持愉快的基调. 开放眼睛的视觉效果最初是古典的, 颜色鲜, 我在去找朋友的路上停下来与野生动物交谈, 观看日落的光线在风景中以彩色的三角形舞蹈. 我终于与其他人见面了, 他们的面孔异常扭曲, 他们的嘴巴继续绕着他们的头和眼睛. 我很难在空间上定位自己. 另外,我似乎无法讨论简单的话题, 因为思想变得太复杂了, 在T+4 1/2小时, 通过言语表达自己的困难减轻, 然而, 迷失方向持续了整个体验. 幸运的是,我的朋友们非常有帮助, 我意识到我一直在经历强烈的触觉快乐, 我也发现自己有足够的灵感来开始绘制我正在经历的一些视觉. 我的朋友提供披萨和酒. 虽然我觉得口感和舌头上的食物和饮料是令人愉快的, 偶尔在T+3小时到T+7小时之间我会发现很难调节我的体温. 我惊地发现我还能很好地. 眼睛的视觉效果持续不减. 我决定继续睡觉, 享受这次车程. 我那天晚上做了生动的梦, 即使是第二天早上, 我仍然觉得自己处于经验的门. 实际上第二天身体几乎没有任何负荷, 总的来说,我非常喜欢这次的经历. 虽然我预计身体负荷会很高, 这种东西似乎会持续一段时间, 所以不要计划在同一天做任何重大承诺! 时间表: 101767 25B-NBOMe (564):一般 (1),第一次 (2),自然/户外 (23),宿醉/几天后 (46),公共空间 (博物馆,公园等) (53)

Curling Faces Countless Colors 25B-NBOMe by Ohio Jones Citation:   Ohio Jones. "Curling Faces Countless Colors: An Experience with 25B-NBOMe (exp101767)". Erowid.org . May 21, 2014. erowid.org/exp/101767 DOSE: 1 hit buccal 25B-NBOH (blotter / tab) BODY WEIGHT: 155 lb Never having tried NBOMe's before, I entered this experience slightly nervous. On earlier experiences with the 2Cx series, I always vomited and experienced heavy body loads. Additionally, the NBOMe's have a reputation of being somewhat dangerous. This was the source of my concern, which turned out to be unnecessary. A few days prior to the experience, I tested a small amount of blotter with reagents. As there are few reliable reports on the reactions out there, I feel I should share mine. The Marquis slowly changed yellow then transitioned to olive black. The Mecke began brown, and turned a reddish-brownish-black. Finally, the Simon changed blue. This last one may be very useful in distinguishing between LSD and 25x-NBOMe blotters. The day of my experience, I fasted and abstained from caffeine. For 5 days prior, I also abstained from alcohol. I dosed a single tab purported to be '1 1/2 hits strong' on a beautiful, warm, sunny Friday afternoon immediately after leaving a work meeting. The tab tasted bitter. To feel out the come-up, I decided to hike to a meadow. At T +15min, I began to feel a tingling numbness on my gums where the tab was placed, which gradually spread through my mouth. At roughly T+20min, I began to feel the first signs of a come-up. My eyes and body began to feel as if they were subtly vibrating. I reached a pleasant meadow just as I came into a distinct +1 at roughly T+30min. Voices had a strange echo, and the grass beneath me seemed to arranged themselves symmetrical patterns, somewhat like Mescaline visuals. I abided by a sudden urge to lay down in the warmth of the afternoon sunlight. As I closed my eyes, I met gorgeous CEVs. There were colorful, spinning, emotive cartoon faces of various sizes, rotating around each other at varied speeds like clockwork gears. I felt happiness and love transfer from these visuals into my own being. The physical and emotional pleasure built as I blasted up to a brilliant ++++, flirting with breaking through to the next level. Every sound around me shifted tone to sound like laughter, every nerve in my body seared with the sensation of love. Clearly this was turning into a much more pleasurable trip than I anticipated. At roughly T+2hrs, I opened my eyes and decided to walk to meet some friends. My mind wandered through musings of social structures, and the vibrations of pleasure and love continued to set a pleasant tone throughout the rest of the experience. Open eye visuals were initially kaleidoscopic and brightly coloured, and I would categorize my experience as still a ++++. I stopped to talk to wildlife en route to my friends, and watched the rays of the sunset dance in colorful triangles on the landscape. At T +3hrs, I finally met up with other people, and was taken aback by what I saw. Their faces were bizarrely distorted, with their mouths continuing to wrap around their heads and encircle their eyes. It became extremely difficult to orient myself spatially. Additionally, I could not seem to discuss more than simple topics, because thoughts became too complicated of objects to transmit orally. By T+4 1/2 hours the difficulty expressing myself through speech subsided, however the disorientation persisted throughout the rest of the experience. Thankfully, my friends were more than helpful in safely guiding me/transporting me where I needed to go. At T+5hrs, now down to about ++ or +++, I realized that I had been experiencing a strong degree of tactile pleasure, yet could not pinpoint when it began. I also found myself collected enough to begin to draw some of the visuals I was experiencing. My friends provided pizza and beer. Although I found the sensation of taste and the touch of food and drink on my tongue pleasurable, I decided I did not care to actually eat or drink. Occasionally between T+3 hrs and T+7hrs I would find it difficult to regulate my body temperature. At T+9hrs, I was surprised that I was still tripping pretty good. Open and closed eye visuals continued unabated. Having thought the experience would have ended by this point, I decided to go to bed still tripping and enjoy the CEV's. I experienced vivid dreams that night, presumably because I was still tripping. Even the following morning, I felt I was still on a threshold experience. There was essentially no body load the following day, and I actually felt energized to hike about. Overall I greatly enjoyed the experience. Although I anticipated a high body load, I did not experience that. This stuff does seem to last a while, so don't plan on making any major engagements the same day!

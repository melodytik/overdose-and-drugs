我吃了大约10毫克的4-Methylaminorex粉末在周日早上10点左右. 没有天平. 作为一种学习辅助, 克服写作障碍 (或其他创作障碍) 的好物, 我本意是试试这种材料的效果. 我打算留在工作中; 这是一个不寻常的日子, 这种温和的刺激效应加上营养素使我能够保持警觉和投入 (而不是无聊和丧). 经过45分钟的时间, 我意识到效果已经开始了, 因为我突然发现自己对同事们特别的关注和善待. 我很容易对别人失耐心, 在压力的时候, 我常常不太善良. 这种关心和善良的质量几乎与我在使用MDMA时所经历的质量相同, 我问问题,认真地听答案, 这种经历就像能量一样. 我在凌晨2点 (14小时后) 仍然感觉到效果. 从基线到峰值的发展非常顺利, 峰值实际上是一个非常长的高原. 我从来没有觉得身体过度刺激. 我觉得特别注意整天和晚上. 我从我的乏味的工作没有疲倦. 这一天只是无事无事地过去了. 我没有觉得我驾驶回家的能力受到了影响. 我打电话给一个情人, 试图让她来. 我认为性生活会很好. 我从来没有尝试过甲基胺. 我有兴趣比较两者. 我希望尝试使用这种材料, 用它来写或做我的税务. 其他: 4-甲基亚胺 (56):小组 (2-9) (17),第一次 (2),一般 (1)

Erowid.org . Feb 17, 2002. erowid.org/exp/12684 DOSE: 10 mg oral 4-Methylaminorex (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 124 lb I ate about 10 mg of 4-Methylaminorex powder at around 10 AM on a weekday. Did not have a scale. The drug was introduced to me as a study aid, a material good for overcoming writer's block (or other creative blocks), something useful for getting lots of work done, especially intellectual work. My intention was to get an introductory taste of the material's effects. I was planning on staying at work; it was an unusual day, we were doing a boring yet essential quarterly task. The mild stimulant effect coupled with the entheogenic quality made me able to stay alert and engaged (rather than bored and frustrated). After about 45 minutes, I realized the effects had come on because I suddenly noticed myself being especially attentive and nice to the other people with whom I was working. At that time in my life, I got easily impatient with people, and I was often not very nice during times of stress. The quality of the attentiveness and niceness was almost identical to that which I experience while on MDMA, if maybe a little attenuated. I was asking questions, and earnestly listening to answers, with all my senses present and alert. This experience was like the Energizer Bunny in duration. I was still feeling effects at 2 AM (14 hours later). It was very smooth in its development from baseline to peak, with the peak really being an extremely long plateau. At no time did my body feel overwhelmed or hyperstimulated. But I did feel a pleasant stimulant effect, and felt especially attentive all day long and into the night. I did not tire from my tedious work. The day just went by uneventfully in this alert state of mind. I did not perceive my ability to drive home as being affected. Around midnight I called a lover and tried to get her to come over. I think sex would have been nice. I've never tried methamphetamine. I'd be interested in comparing the two. I'd like to try this material, and use it for writing or doing my taxes.

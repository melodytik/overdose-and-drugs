中文译文 抑制剂

精神病医生给我开了这份药, 因为我有令人虚弱的焦虑症,抑郁症,恐慌发作, 虽然我每天服用的Xanax (1.5毫克) 确实有助于对抗恐慌发作, 医生给我开了50毫克的Seroquel XR, 我昨天晚上第一次服用. 我在晚上10点45分服用了桃色药, 吃完30分钟后, 我觉得有点昏昏欲睡, 我不得不把书放在一旁. 我看了一些电视,但我的眼睛变得太沉重,所以在午夜,我去睡觉. 我睡得很好, 不过我并没有像你在其他报道中看到的那样失去意识. 我早上8点醒来去所, 感到有些昏昏欲睡, 我睡了一小时,然后在9点起床, 我注意到焦虑,竞赛,消极的想法已经远离了我. 在上午10点30分. 我穿上衣服,出去买东西, 只是为了测试我的警觉. 我没有被车撞倒,我没有惰地移动,我安全地回到了家, 我发现如果我抗拒并保持活跃, 我连续演奏了30分钟, 很高兴Seroquel也没有影响我的演奏能力. 现在, 摄入后19小时, 我感觉有点模糊, 我希望它能让我摆脱可怕的焦虑和消极的想法. 然而,我看不到任何娱乐价值. 这是一种强大的东西, 只有那些有严重精神疾病的人才能使用. 已使用的文件: 药品 - 奎平 (273):第一次使用 (2),医疗用途 (47),单独使用 (16)

Dec 24, 2017. erowid.org/exp/82192 DOSE: T+ 0:00 50 mg oral Pharms - Quetiapine (pill / tablet) T+ 10:15 1.5 mg oral Pharms - Alprazolam (pill / tablet) T+ 10:15 oral Coffee T+ 10:15 smoked Tobacco - Cigarettes BODY WEIGHT: 203 lb I got this prescribed by my psychiatrist because of debilitating GAD, depression, panic attacks, racing thoughts, occasional rage and dissociative symptoms. SSRI's didn't do it for me and although my daily dose of Xanax (1.5mg) does help to counter the panic attacks, it does next to nothing for my other issues. So, along with my Xanax, the doc prescribed me 50mg of Seroquel XR which I took yesterday evening for the first time. I took the peach colored pill at 10.45 pm and continued to work on my pc for another 20 minutes, then I went to bed and grabbed a book. 30 minutes after ingestion I noticed a slight drowsiness but I was still able to concentrate on my book. One hour after ingestion the Seroquel really kicked in and I had to put away the book. I watched some tv for a while but my eyes became too heavy so at midnight I went to sleep. I had a good night sleep but I was far from being 'unconscious' in the way you read in some other reports. I woke up at 8 am to go to the bathroom and I felt somewhat groggy but luckily no signs of vertigo, slurred speech or rubber legs. I went to sleep for another hour and got up at 9 to take my Xanax, a smoke and some coffee. At this point I actually felt pretty relaxed; kinda stoned and I noticed that the anxiety and the racing, negative thoughts were way in the background. At 10.30 a.m. I got dressed and went outside to buy something just to test my alertness. Well, I didn't get ran over by a car, I didn't move sluggish and I made it back home safely and refreshed. The rest of the day I kept feeling kinda sleepy but in a positive way and I found out that if I fought it and stayed active, I was rewarded with a mild euphoria. I played guitar for 30 minutes straight and to my great relief, Seroquel also hadn't affected my playing abilities. Now, 19 hours after ingesting I feel somewhat foggy but I feel good and I am still able to think clearly and to type this. So my first experience with Seroquel is positive and I have good hope that it will liberate me from my dreadful anxiety and negative thoughts. However, I don't see any recreational value in it. This is powerful stuff and only meant to be used by people who have a serious mental illness.

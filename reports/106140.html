Oct 22, 2020. erowid.org/exp/106140 DOSE: 5 - 10 mg oral Phenethylamine 5 - 10 mg oral Hordenine oral Cacao smoked Phenethylamine BODY WEIGHT: 100 lb Phenylethylamine Experiments and Experiences 1st Time: 5mg PEA, 5mg Hordenine 1min - slight rush 2min - feeling the buzz 3min - buzzy euphoria 5min - buzzing keeps going 10min - feeling on top of the world; peaking. 15min - coming down 20min - just a slight buzz 40min - mostly gone 2nd Time: 10mg PEA, 10mg Hordenine, 50mg caffeine (in coffee) Exactly the same as previous. Except with less caffeine jitters. I sipped throughout the afternoon in a boring lecture room - that made for a more entertaining afternoon. Definitely made me listen to the teacher heaps. This PEA has been predictable in all tests. I like that it's fucking predictable. Totally unlike pot. Or DXM. However I once did PEA eight hours after I had a moderate dose of Curcumin earlier in the day. The PEA rush came on as normal, but didn't really die out till like 2 hours later. That was without Hordenine. Interesting. Cue a month later. I did this test: 5mg PEA, one Cacao nib, 5mg Hordenine. 1min - feeling great (ooh this is different) 2min - the familiar buzz 3min - everything is warm 5min - smiling because I feel fucking great 10min - still smiling and on top of the world 20min - still smiling, coming down a bit 30min - feeling great 50min - coming down. No longer smiling but feeling at ease. Did it again. This combo definitely rocks. Consistent. Almost as great as say morphine + cacao. I never did that combo again - it was like I had found the motherlode of effective opiate potentiation. DXM? Nah pass. Codeine is my go to if I ever get struck with opiate WDs out of nowhere and that's rare nowadays. Kicked that habit knowing how many people have died from heroin ODs. Just recently I tried smoking PEA despite what everyone said about this ROA. Feels like getting hit harder by 3x the amount compared to oral. And with more buzzing euphoria and no need for hordenine. I can imagine it would be too good with a small sample of cacao nibs. 1min: The excitement rush 2min: More rushing 3min: Oh god getting tingles 5min: Smiling at everything 10min: Dancing crazy this music sounds great 20min: More dancing yay feeling on top of the world 40min: Coming down. Feeling at ease with myself 60min: Back to normal. Smoked whatever was left around. Just felt my heart jack up to moderate-high bp with no euphoria. Weird. Smoking seems to be a non effective method of staying high for longer. *TL:DR; - Oral seems to be a more reliable method of repeat dosing. - Smoking great for the rushy feel goods and dancing.

上午3点47分 刚刚服用了大约25毫克的内脏抗胰岛素. 上午4点12分 得到相当好的头部高兴感. 不是真正的快速,但高兴. 鼻子 感觉有些刺痛. 音乐的高兴. 我不知道它会如何影响我的DXM旅行, 似乎增加了对话的流动, 但我没有注意到任何实际的情绪升高习惯于GHB. 上午4点23分 鼻子的刺痛已经消失了, 现在只是头部的高兴, 音乐的高兴, 没有任何视觉扭曲. 我感觉很好. 这确实有点像MDMA. 上午4点58分 这种情绪会波而来,头部,感觉好. 像一个低剂量的 下次使用50毫克. 我觉得自己很开放和说话. 上午5点21分 消耗一些酒精. 我觉得自己很开放,很有同情心. 感觉真的很好. 不是激烈的, 但非常好的积极的态度和能量. 不是像使用GHB那样'让我们出去做点什么'的能量, 而是快乐,满意的生活, 酒精肯定会增加这种情绪. 上午6点29分 兴奋,失禁 (可能是由于添加的酒精 - - 非常协同作用). 我只能想象GHB和IAP会一起做什么. 这种情绪令人惊叹, 我觉得自己更开放,更口. 如果这只能作为一种抗抑郁药而不是帕克西尔和普罗扎克, 世界将是一个更快乐的地方. 上午6点50分 刚刚用AK-47毒品了一下. 我不能告诉你IAP与大麻/酒精有多么协同 - - 感觉就像一个很好的清洁的MDMA, 我发现了第二个最好的东西, 如果耐受性不是问题, 我看到它如何在更高的剂量. 这种药物与THC具有惊人的协同作用. 我现在有全身声 没有. 这两者是相辅相成的. 我认为GHB+IAP+THC会带来全身高潮, 睡眠是很困难的. 我吃完12个小时后才睡觉 这就是它. 酒精和大麻可能会延长效果, 睡眠,当它来了,是正常的. 今天我感觉很好, 我注意到没有与DXM的互动. 胃口正常,其他一切正常. 既然它持续了这么久, 我可能不会在一周内多做一次. 其他: 大麻 (1), 酒精 (61), IAP (280): 单独 (16), 组合 (3), 一般 (1)

Amazing but Long IAP, Alcohol & Cannabis by gravol Citation:   gravol. "Amazing but Long: An Experience with IAP, Alcohol & Cannabis (exp25477)". Erowid.org . Jul 21, 2003. erowid.org/exp/25477 DOSE: 25 mg oral IAP (powder / crystals) oral Alcohol smoked Cannabis 3:47 am Just took approximately 25 mg of IAP. 4:12 am Getting pretty nice head euphoria. Not really speedy, but euphoric. Nose feels tingly. Music euphoria. It is a possible SSRI so I do not know how it is going to affect my DXM trips, but I like what I am seeing thus far. Seems to increase the flow of conversation, but I do not notice any actual mood elevation that comes accustomed to GHB. 4:23 am Nose tingling has gone away, now it is just head euphoria, music euphoria, and an opening of senses without any visual distortion. I feel good. It does feel a bit like MDMA. 4:58 am Euphoria comes in waves, head is tingly and feels good. Like a low dose of MDMA - will experiment with 50 mg next time. I feel open and talkative. 5:21 am Consumed some alcohol. I feel very open and empathetic. Feeling really good. Not intense but very nice positive attitude and energy. Not 'let's go out and do something' energy like with GHB but just happy-go-lucky, content with life, talking a bit more than normal, easy to converse, etc. Alcohol definitely adds to the euphoria. 6:29 am Euphoria, disinhibition (probably from the alcohol added - very synergistic). I could only imagine what GHB and IAP would do together. The euphoria is amazing - it encompasses my entire head. And I feel more open and talkative. If only this could be marketed as an antidepressant instead of Paxil and Prozac, the world would be a MUCH happier place. 6:50 am Just got stoned with AK-47 strain. I can not tell you HOW synergistic IAP is with weed/alcohol - it feels like a good clean MDMA with waves of euphoria all over my head, without much body buzz or intense effects by itself - increases the flow of conversation, it is truly a remarkable substance. I have found the second best thing to GHB. Perhaps even better, if tolerance is not an issue and I see how it goes at higher doses. THC provides an amazing synergy with this drug. I now have a full-body buzz going. The two go hand in hand. I imagine that GHB+IAP+THC would bring about a full-body orgasm, much like MDMA+N2O. Sleep was difficult. I was not able to sleep until 12 hours after ingesting it. It is a lot longer acting than I thought - the alcohol and marijuana may have prolonged the effects, but I doubt it. Sleep, when it came, was normal. Today I felt fine, and I notice there is no interaction with DXM. Appetite is normal, everything else fine. Since it lasts so long, I probably wouldn't do this more than once a week.

因为他们从来没有长期感觉良好, 对于我来说就是这样. 我没有抽大麻一天也过不去. 我感觉很好, 仅仅是几次, 所有的问题都消失了. 问题在于,我知道长期来看,大麻会让我更加丧, 很快我需要在朋友过来之前吸毒, 这种生活感觉不好. 我不是说不要吸大麻, 无论如何,我没有写这关于大麻,我写它关于5-HTP. 作为一个贫穷的十九岁青少年, 两个月前我在全食店买了一道沙拉, 终于从药架上偷了5-HTP. 我以前读过关于5-HTP的文章, 我只是知道它是一种抗抑郁药. 我甚至没有计划拿走它, 那时偷窃似乎是最理想的方法, 因为我很破产. 我17岁时, 一位免费青少年医疗诊所的心理学家诊断出我患有双极性情绪. 两个月前, 我一直在向父母抱怨:"我不想再来这里了", 我花了大部分的空时间, 我觉得这是最好的选择, 因为我没有理由活着. 虽然我很自私, 但我并没有自杀, 我真的不想让他们陷入这样的境地. 我的父母安排我去看一个昂贵的精神病医生, 我很不愿意去, 因为如果我的父母把325美元放进我的口袋, 但他们最终付出了代价. 精神病医生是一个很酷的人. 我告诉他我的感受大约40分钟, 我不愿服用药物, 只是让你知道,我对诗歌很痴迷. 我把所有的孤独时间都花在了自由设计,节奏拳击,和写诗. 我相信我要么找出如何钱, 要么自杀. 我认为这种药物会破坏我的自由设计能力, 这都是一堆胡说八道. 他诊断出我患有双极四, 我也无法接受他的诊断, 因为他只认识我一个小时. 他不知道整个情况. 简而言之,当时我正在为我叔叔的面包生意配送面包. 我每周五天送货, 每天早上3点半起床. 作为典型的青少年, 我会在凌晨1点半睡觉, 凌晨3点半醒来做生日, 情绪最糟糕, 终于完成生日, 回家, 吸大麻, 睡几个小时, 醒来, 再吸一碗, 和朋友出去玩, 再喝醉, 凌晨1点半睡觉, 凌晨3点半醒来做生日. 这就是我的睡眠时间表. 显然,从我吸了多少大麻和我睡觉的方式来看, 我生活在一片雾中. 工作周结束时, 睡眠不足会让我感到困惑. 我觉得自己是精神分裂症患者, 吸大麻和每天平均睡五个小时 (每天睡两三个小时) 让我产生了幻觉. 我的精神病医生一半都不知道. 我不再去看心理医生了. 我想要改变工作和睡眠时间, 改变毒品习惯和成, 我想知道如果我像正常人一样睡觉, 我在叔叔的包包店提前两周的通知. 它们就在我的办公桌上:5-HTP. 我甚至没有想到他们做了什么, 我决定每天服用两粒药, 一粒在睡前, 一粒在早上. 我假装它们是糖丸, 只要我服用它们, 我还在一天中吸大约四到五次. 我肯定感觉更好,因为我睡着了. 连续七个小时的睡眠是多么美妙. 睡眠已经成为我的新好朋友. 让我们回到5-HTP. 让我感到困惑的是人们在使用狂喜药之前服用500毫克的5-HTP, 因为我甚至没有真正感受到5-HTP直到我定期服用两周. 我在网上看了一些东西, 这大概就是感觉到效果的时间. 我在十天后开始感觉很好, 我读过它们含有黑色素, 帮助调节睡眠周期, 我每天晚上10点到12点睡觉, 尽管我深夜在电脑上刺激自己, 当我醒来时, 我觉得自己重生了, 就像每天早上, 在服用5-HTP之前, 我常常躺在床上, 我害怕从床上起床, 我只能继续睡觉, 直到身体无法再睡觉. Feb 3, 2012. erowid.org/exp/72094 DOSE: 100 mg oral Tryptophan - 5-HTP (pill / tablet) smoked Cannabis BODY WEIGHT: 135 lb My names Dylan, I’m a nineteen-year-old male, and live in Albuquerque, New Mexico. I weigh 135lbs, am 5’8, and my hobby’s are: going to the gym a couple times a week, hanging out with friends, beat-boxing, writing, free-styling, and writing poetry. I used to believe depression was a part of my character. I mean it ran all over my mother and father’s side of the family — mostly depression and anxiety. I’m no scientist, but when someone is depressed he or she will have less serotonin in their brain. Having less serotonin means they don’t feel as content. Naturally, if a person’s serotonin baseline is lower they will begin to fixate (or obsess) over whatever they can do to make themselves feel better in the moment, like: sex or drugs. Their life could be run by impulsive behaviors because they never feel good in the long-term or when they’re not under the influence of substances, or in the act of sex. This was the case for me. I could not go a day without smoking pot. I felt so good after just a couple hits and all my problems went up in smoke. The problem is I know that in the long-term weed insidiously makes me more depressed, without me necessarily even knowing it. It’s not long before I need to get high before a friend comes over just so I’m not too irritable around them. It doesn’t feel good living your life this way. I’m not saying don’t smoke pot, but I definitely feel better about doing substances when I use them in moderation. Anyways, I didn’t write this about pot, I wrote it about 5-HTP. Being a poor nineteen year old teenager, I don’t have much cash. Two months ago I was buying a salad over at Whole Foods and ended up stealing 5-HTP off the medicine shelf. I had read about 5-HTP before, but didn’t know too much about it. I just knew that it was an antidepressant. I wasn’t even planning on taking it, but I figured it might be useful to have. It was somewhere in the ballpark of $25, and at the time stealing seemed like the most ideal way to get it, because of how broke I was. I’m not saying if you’re depressed go to the Health store and steal 5-HTP, but on second thought, if you’re good at slipping pills in your pocket, more power to you. When I turned seventeen, a psychologist at a free adolescent healthcare clinic diagnosed me as Bipolar II. About two months ago I was constantly complaining to my parents that, “I didn’t want to be here anymore”, because I didn’t. I spent the majority of my spare time, which was when I was not working, lying on my bed fantasizing about hanging myself. It seemed like the ideal thing to do, because I had no reason to live. Even though I am selfish, I was not selfish enough to kill myself, because I couldn’t imagine my parents coming up with an explanation for my friends and family. I didn’t really want to put them in that kind of situation. My parents set me up for an appointment with an expensive psychiatrist--$325 for the initial session. I was reluctant to go, because if my parents put $325 in my pocket I figured I’d be cured from my depression, because I’d actually have money to do shit. But, they ended up paying for it. The psychiatrist was a pretty cool guy. I told him about how I was feeling for about forty minutes, and that I was reluctant to taking pharmaceutical drugs because they might zap my creativity abilities. Just to let you know, I am obsessed with poetry. I spend all of my alone time free-styling, beat-boxing, and writing poetry. Also, since in that time period I was contemplating suicide daily, I was convinced that I would either figure out how to make money rhyming or kill myself. I thought that mood stabilizer medication would destroy my free-styling abilities and make me unsuccessful as a creative artist. It was all a bunch of bullshit. He diagnosed me as Bipolar four, or atypical Bipolar Disorder. I couldn’t really accept his diagnosis either, because the fucking guy only knew me for an hour. He didn’t know the full picture. Long story short, I was delivering bagels for my uncle’s bagel business at the time. Delivering them five days a week and I had to wake up at 3:30AM in the morning. Being the stereotypical teenager, I would go to bed at 1:30AM, wake up at 3:30AM for my deliveries, be in the shittiest mood ever, eventually get through the deliveries, go home, smoke pot, sleep for another couple hours, wake up, then smoke another bowl, hang out with friends, get stoned again and go to sleep at 1:30AM, and then wake up at 3:30AM for deliveries the following morning. That was my sleep schedule. Obviously, from how much pot I was smoking and how I was sleeping, I figured that alone was enough to cause bipolar symptoms. I was living in a fog. Towards the end of my work week the sleep deprivation would catch up to me. I felt like I was schizophrenic, because I could no longer control my thoughts. The combination of smoking weed and averaging five hours of sleep per day (between two or three naps) had made me delusional. My psychiatrist didn’t know the half of it. I stopped seeing my psychiatrist. I wanted to change my work and sleep schedule, modify my drug habits and addictions, and then see how I felt after that. I wanted to see if I would still fit the atypical bipolar diagnosis after sleeping like a normal person. I put in my two weeks notice at my uncle’s bagel business. There they were sitting on my desk: The 5-HTPs. I didn’t even think they did anything and at the time I didn’t want to get addicted to another substance. But, for some reason, I decided that I would start taking two pills a day; one before bed and one in the morning. I pretended they were a sugar pill and as long as I was taking them I would have to cut down on my cigarette and weed smoking. Initially, I wasn’t really sure what the pills were doing, because I was still smoking weed about four to five times a day. I was definitely feeling better because I was sleeping though. I could not believe how great it was to sleep seven hours in a row interrupted. Sleep had become my new best friend. Back to the 5-HTP. I get confused when people take 500mgs of 5-HTP before rolling on ecstasy, because I didn’t even really feel the 5-HTP until I was taking it regularly for two weeks. I looked online somewhere and that’s about how long it’s supposed to take to feel the full effects. I started feeling them pretty good after about ten days, but the sleep benefits came on almost immediately. I read somewhere that they have melatonin in them, which helps regulate the sleep cycle, and I believe it. I’m able to go to bed between 10 and 12pm every night, which is early for me. In spite the fact that I’m stimulating myself late at night on the computer, I eventually start yawning, pass out, and sleep like a rock. And when I wake up I feel reborn — like every single morning I’m leaving momma’s vagina. Before taking 5-HTP I used to just lie in bed and contemplate hanging myself when I woke up in the morning. I would dread getting out of bed, and usually wouldn’t even get out of bed. Instead, I would just keep sleeping until my body was incapable of sleeping anymore. After that I would smoke pot. In other words, I was escaping from reality as much as I could. My thoughts obsessed over suicide, my ex-girlfriend (who dumped me about seventy days ago), cigarettes, or smoking weed. I only lived for being high or having sex. Nothing else seemed worth living for. I did not live for socializing with my guy friends, because I didn’t get much pleasure out of talking to other sausages for four hours. It just didn’t seem gratifying to me. All the conversations and jokes we had were inevitably the same, so I saw no point in hanging out with friends because our hangout sessions were so predictable and boring. After about eleven days of taking 5-HTP there was a difference in my life. I woke up and wanted to do shit. It didn’t seem like a big deal cleaning my room. The biggest thing I noticed is I didn’t contemplate killing myself anymore. I had a reason to live, and it was more than just smoking pot or having sex with women. I enjoyed going in my backyard and sitting outside with my dog and enjoying the beauty of nature, and I wanted nothing more than that. I no longer obsessed over the next time I am going to get high. I believe this is because 5-HTP gave me more serotonin. I simply didn’t have the desire to constantly engage myself in adrenaline-pumping pleasures all day long. I didn’t even have to consciously make a decision to cut-down on smoking weed and cigarettes; I just did it. Don’t get me wrong, I still get high once a day, or every other day, but it’s more of a balance. I don’t feel guilty about doing it because I don’t abuse it, and I get even higher, because I am doing it less and there is not as much of a tolerance. 5-HTP changed my thoughts, but did not change who I am. I am no longer Dylan who impulsively lives for the next high. I’m Dylan who feels comfortable in my own skin, enjoys socializing with the sausages, and can actually wait to have sex. 5-HTP shaved off a considerable amount of my sex-drive, which makes me wonder if having sexual cravings is a symptom of low serotonin. It probably is. Sex is still important to be, but it’s not what I live for, and that’s the difference. 5-HTP probably did not save me from suicide, but it made my life much more desirable. After reading so much about SSRI’s and other antidepressants, those drugs were not very appetizing for me. I like 5-HTP because instead of blocking reuptake of serotonin, it aids the brain in producing more serotonin. Lately I have been taking 100mg’s before I go to bed every night, instead of 50mg’s before sleeping and after sleeping, and that seems to be working better for me. I still have only been taking them for about seventeen days and feel like a completely different person. I might try tapering off them in a month and see what happens from there, but in the meantime, I am going to continue taking them. For the first time in my life I feel content enough to not want to kill myself. In other words, I can sit down for five minutes without a single suicidal thought in my mind—what a concept. 5-HTP changed my life. After struggle with addiction and not feeling good in my own skin, I gave 5-HTP a shot. It worked for me.

美国 这实际上是72小时间隔的同一种物质的两次体验. 它们是密切相关的, 所以我想把它们都放在一份报告中. 我是个痛药的粉丝, 我除了海洛因, 还有其他可想象的片. 我曾经抽过片, 这是一次很棒的体验, 现在我还可以坐在这里. 我还没有尝试过的唯一止痛药. 我买了两粒120毫克的药, 药片中含有120毫克的硫酸吗啡, 我曾经有相当高的耐受性;我可以服用160毫克的氧气, [Erowid的注意事项: 驾驶时喝醉,倒,或极度缺乏睡眠是危险和不负责任的, 因为它危害了其他人. 不要这样做! 晚上7点 我把120毫克的药丸拆开,把所有的小球放在一起. 我花了15分钟将球粉碎成粉末. 最终粉末变得足够光滑, 看起来像是可卡因. 我不确定自己能忍受多少, 我把粉末分成八行大致相同的尺寸 我清理我的鼻子和鼻子的前两行. 经过5分钟, 我开始感觉到我非常喜欢的古老的片. 虽然感觉不那么强烈, 决定再做两行. 感觉有点强烈, 滴水现在变得很恶心, 我应该把120毫克的药物分成两行. 决定等待,看看它是否会"起作用", 八点钟 再做两行,现在烧得很厉害,不太好玩. 我有一个可怕的头痛吗啡没有 countering. 坦率地说,30毫克的水过比这更好,这并不是说太多. 身体很重, 但头疼, 感觉有些高兴. 8:30 时间 完成最后两行. 没有什么真正的变化, 鼻子让我死, 眼睛是注血,头痛, 时间9点 我变得非常热和出汗, 像我的头发是浸泡在汗水. 我知道接下来会发生什么. 在接下来的两个小时里, 我吐了6次. 试着去睡觉. 无法入睡,终于在凌晨3点我沉睡. 第二天早上8点 我去上班,跑进浴室,吐了两次,喝了一些Pepto感觉更好. 一整天都觉得疲. 我有史以来最糟糕的片"旅行", 我恢复了几天, 显然鼻腔途径没有起作用, 我不想一次使用那么多粉末, 因为我的鼻子会变得不好, 我又吃了另一个药丸, 再把它磨碎, 这次我花了更多的时间, 我将它磨成非常细的粉末. 然后把所有的粉末到一个空杯子里. 我把一些水运到它稍微热,不太热,但非常温暖. 我把这个倒进粉末中. 我把它旋转到没有粉末了, 我在空腹时迅速地把它下来. 味道相当可怕,但我有更糟糕的. 5:30 时间 我觉得它像上次一样. 现在我也会服用120毫克的药物. 身体变得非常沉重, 五点四十分 有更多的高兴. 仍然不是我从这样一个高做什么的期望. 我更喜欢其他任何我试过的止痛药. 六点钟 现在已经非常镇静了,几乎和甲一样,但不完全. 试图闭上眼睛,放下我的头. 在黑暗中听音乐. 这才是我对片的真实考验. 我在黑暗中听我最喜欢的音乐, 这就是我的仪式. 我觉得自己很好,比起清醒时更容易迷失在声音中, 时间9点 过去几个小时的情况非常相似, 轻度的高兴, 沉重的镇静, 我慢慢下来, 睡觉有点困难, 但不像前一晚那么糟糕. 结论 吗啡是一个很大的失望. 我认为液体形式的固定是伟大的, 但硫酸盐留下了很多可取. 我对这次体验抱有很高的希望, 因为所有在线转换图表都让我相信这是一种强烈的片, 我不推这种药物, 即使你能找到便宜的, 即使是普通的过水是一种更强大的和愉快的体验比吗啡硫酸盐. 其他: 2007年11月3日发布 吗啡 (211):一般 (1),第一次 (2),回顾 / 总结 (11),音乐讨论 (22),宿醉 / 几天后 (46),困难的经历 (5),独自一人 (16)

Nov 3, 2007. erowid.org/exp/44932 DOSE: T+ 0:00 30 mg insufflated Morphine (ground / crushed) T+ 0:15 30 mg insufflated Morphine (ground / crushed) T+ 0:40 30 mg insufflated Morphine (ground / crushed) T+ 1:10 30 mg insufflated Morphine (ground / crushed) T+ 69:40 120 mg oral Morphine (liquid) BODY WEIGHT: 190 lb This is actually two experiences with the same substance 72 hours apart. They are closely related so I figured I would put both in one report. I am a huge fan of pain pills; it is the only drugs I take anymore. I have had every opiate imaginable besides heroin. I smoked opium once and it was a great experience, and I have taken every pill I can get my hands on and compared the experiences of them all looking for the 'best high'. Hydromorphone still holds this place for me. Morphine sulfate was about the only pain pill I had not tried. I got two 120 mg pills and decided to experiment. The pills were 120 mg of morphine sulfate, instead of powder inside the caps it comes in the form of dozens of little balls. I used to have a fairly high tolerance; I could do 160 mgs of Oxy and still be good enough have a fluid conversation or drive. [Erowid Note: Driving while intoxicated, tripping, or extremely sleep deprived is dangerous and irresponsible because it endangers other people. Don't do it! ] T 7:00 PM I take apart the 120 mg pill and get all of the little balls together. I then spend about 15 minutes crushing the balls into a fine powder. Eventually the powder is smooth enough to the point it looks like cocaine. Since it had been a few months since I’d had opiates and I wasn’t sure of my tolerance I decided to do it a bit at a time. I divided up the powder into 8 lines of roughly equal size 7:20 I clear out my nose and snort the first two lines. It burns but I have had far worse so I deal, after about 5 minutes I start to feel the good old opiate come up I love so much. However it isn’t very intense, I feel kind of warm but nothing else really. 7:35 Decide to do two more lines. Feel it a little more intense, the drip is getting pretty wicked now, nothing too great to show for it. Starting to be let down by the whole experience, maybe I should have done all 120 mg in 2 huge lines. Decide to wait and see if it will “kick in”, 8:00 Do two more lines, burns like hell now, not very enjoyable. I have an awful headache that the morphine isn’t countering. Honestly 30 mgs of Hydrocodone filtered is a better high than this and that isn’t saying much. My body feels very heavy but my head hurts and I am having little Euphoria. 8:30 Do the last two lines. Nothing really changes, nose is killing me, eyes are bloodshot, head hurts, and have a pseudo-opiate buzz. 9:00 I get really hot and sweaty, like my hair is drenched in sweat. I know what is coming next. Soon enough I got horrible nausea and rush to the bathroom, vomit 6 times over the next two hours. Attempt to go to bed. Cannot fall asleep, finally at 3 in the morning I drift off to sleep. T Next Morning 8 am I get to work, run into the bathroom, throw up twice, have some Pepto and feel a lot better. Feel lethargic all day. Worst opiate “trip” I have ever had, all the negative of an opiate trip and very little positive. I recover for a few days and decide to try something else when I am at baseline. Obviously the nasal route didn’t work, I wouldn’t want to do that much powder at once as my nose would be in bad shape, so I decide a quick oral route may be best, I have tried this with other opiates with good results. T: 5 pm I take the other pill and grind it up again, this time I spend more time getting it as fine as possible. I grind it up to very fine powder. I then scrape all the powder into an empty cup. I run some water till it is slightly hot, not too hot but very warm. I pour this into the powder. I swirl it around till no powder is left, and it is completely cloudy. I then quickly shoot this down on an empty stomach. Tastes pretty horrible but I have had worse. 5:30 I feel it coming on a lot like the previous time. More substance too it now as I am taking the full 120 mg at once. Body becomes very heavy and I feel very out of it. 5:45 Have a little more euphoria. Still not what I expected from such a high does. I prefer just about every other pain pill I have tried to this. 6:00 Became very sedated now, almost as much as Methadone but not quite. Temped to close my eyes and lay my head back. Decide to listen to music in the dark. This is my true test of how good an opiate is. How good I feel listening to my favorite music in the dark, it is my ritual. I feel pretty good, easier to lose myself in the sound compared to when I am sober but this is a far cry from the best opiate experiences I have had. 9:00 The last few hours have been very similar, mild Euphoria, heavy body sedation, nothing too exciting. I come down slowly, and have a little difficulty falling asleep but not as bad as the previous night. Conclusion Morphine was a very big letdown. I imagine the liquid form for fixing is great, but the sulfate leaves a lot to be desired. I had very high hopes for this experience as all the conversion charts online led me to believe this was a strong opiate at this dosage, and obviously it was given how sick I was the first time, but all of the best parts of opiates, the complete lack of anxiety, and the overwhelming euphoria were nearly absent. I wouldn’t recommend this even if you could find it for cheap, even common Hydrocodone filtered is a far more powerful and pleasurable experience than Morphine Sulfate.

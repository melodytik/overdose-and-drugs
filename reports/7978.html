美国 我经历了一段艰难的时光, 我妻子离开了我, 我决定再做一次, 但需要一些帮助. 我有一个大树的Brugsmansia sanguinea我从切割生长. 我做了一杯茶, 不知道正确的剂量, 猜到2片叶子和两个红色的花. 我去睡觉,试着打一些鼓. 经过一段时间, 我感觉到药物开始, 相当强烈, 我睡着了. 我做了可怕的梦, 梦中有一种恶魔追着我, 我从疼痛中醒来, 冲进厨房的柜台, 冲到我身后, 摔在硬地板上, 我很惊,我很痛苦,我又昏了. 这就是我所记得的. 我觉得很奇怪. 床单上有血,我的腿被切断,我的额头上有一些不好看的伤口. 我儿子站在床的脚下,着我. 他奇怪地看着我说:'爸爸,我想你需要进客厅来.'我跟着他,惊恐地着这场屠杀. 一个大玻璃窗,大约4英尺×8英尺, 已经被打破 - - 它不是安全玻璃, 有不祥的玻璃. 似乎我已经通过它, 幸运的是膝盖先, 我把后门的链撕下来, 衣干燥机也是如此. 我问他:发生了什么事? 他说我在夜间疯了,他们不知道为什么. 我在房子里跑来跑去, 冲着全速撞上墙壁 (显然是我梦见被魔鬼追逐的同时). 我大喊大叫,说一些奇怪的话. 我站在淋浴室里, 穿着衣服, 哭了. 我似乎打算摧毁自己,做了上述损害的窗户和门. 我去看镜子. 我看起来像火星人, 蓝绿色的眼睛完全黑, 我被伤得非常严重. 我可能会被杀, 这真的是我一生中最可怕的经历. 时间表: 7978 布鲁格曼西亚 (84):过量服用 (29),实体/生物 (37),火车毁和旅行灾难 (7),不好的旅行 (6),第一次 (2),独自 (16)

The Terror Brugsmansia sanguinea by John Citation:   John. "The Terror: An Experience with Brugsmansia sanguinea (exp7978)". Erowid.org . Jul 10, 2001. erowid.org/exp/7978 DOSE: 2 leaves oral Brugmansia (tea) 2 flowers oral Brugmansia (tea) BODY WEIGHT: 190 lb I was going through a bad time, my wife had left me, I was stuck with the kids, no money, no food, no transportation. I had used shamanic drumming before to discover certain things, and I decided to do it again, but with a 'little help'. I had a large shrub of Brugsmansia sanguinea I had grown from a cutting. I made a tea, and not knowing a proper dosage, guessed at 2 leaves and two reddish orange flowers. I went to bed, and tried to do some drumming. After a while, I felt the drug coming on, quite strong, but for some reason, I fought it. I fell asleep. I had terrifying dreams of being chased by a demon who kept smashing my face into the projectors in a movie-theatre projection booth where I worked. I woke up at one point, from pain, as I ran into the kitchen counter at full speed, which smacked me down, and I crashed on my back on the hard floor, no hands to cushion me whatever. I gasped from the shock and pain and 'passed out' again. That's all I remember. When I awoke the next morning, I felt very strange. There was blood on the sheets, my legs were cut up, some nasty cuts on my forehead. My son stood at the foot of the bed, staring at me. He looked at me strangely and said, 'Dad, I think you need to come into the living room.' I followed him and stared aghast at the carnage. A large plate glass window, about 4 feet by 8 feet, had been smashed - it was not safety glass, there were ominous daggers of glass. It seems I had gone through it, fortunately knees first, or I would have been decapitated. I had torn the back door off its hinges, same thing with the clothes dryer. 'What happened?' I asked him. He said I went crazy during the night, they didn't know why. The three kids cowered in their room as I ran back and forth through the house, smashing full speed into walls (evidently at the same time as my dreams of being chased by the demon). I was yelling and saying strange things. I stood in the shower with my clothes on and wept. I seemed intent on destroying myself, and did the aforementioned damage to the window and door. I went to check myself in the mirror. I looked like a Martian - my blue-green eyes were completely black, like those guys infected by the alien virus in the 'X-Files'. And I was cut up very badly. DO NOT TRY THIS STUFF!!! maybe under controlled circumstances with an experienced shaman it would be a different experience, but I could have been killed, and it was truly the most frightening experience of my life.

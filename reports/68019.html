有关物质:2-ME-DMT 经验:第一次 背景:在家里,一个人 使用: 鼻腔,口腔 我用鼻子吸了一根小线, 想看这东西是否能用鼻子, 没有很严重的烧伤,但它是感觉到. 我在MSN上聊天, 基本上心情很好. 滴水刚刚开始. 现在,我已经知道了. 这种东西有可怕的燃烧滴滴, 就像DPT. 我喝了一些水,抽了一支香烟. 已经有了效果. 清醒和药物之间的区别是它现在的影响. 现在效果正在逐渐显现, 我等待看到它会变得多么强大, 这种感觉是非常轻微的,所以根本不打扰我. 这是一种迷幻的感觉. 可能感觉更温暖. 瞳孔已经扩大. 时间23点02分 决定服用剩余的100毫克凝囊. 这种感觉与低剂量的任何其他胺类药物相似. 不能从其他胺中区分出来. 需要达到更强的效果才能更好地解释这种物质的独特之处. 我在电视上看了一些电影, 没有. 我不喜欢恐怖电影. 一种轻微的迷幻感觉, 听一些音乐. 思考人类的未来. 我很担心. 毒品使我比吸毒更关心自然灾害. 这首美丽的音乐与它完美地配合. 我想要帮助. 我不知道如何. 这就是我现在的思维模式. 开始变得更加严. 现在又在MSN上聊天了. 我们是否得到了更强烈的效果? 我感觉到一个触觉感觉通过我的头. 感觉有些. 我在思考概念时, 感觉到我的思维模式有了更多的洞察力. 可能更抽象. 我还在脑子里着. 积极的一点是我还没有感觉到任何感觉. 这种经验当然是积极的. 虽然很愉快, 但却不强大. 不是一个旅行,因为我还在这里. 我开始感觉到身体的其他部位的触觉. 主要是通过我的头. 观察到颜色的不同. 第一个视觉活动. 现在越来越强. 情绪有所改善. 随着清醒的来临. 我一直在微笑. 我感觉到和寒冷. 我把热量增加了. 我在MSN上和一个清醒的人聊天, 通过心灵感知. 其他我谈过的人都吸过其他东西, 所以没有办法确定. 我主要体验的是触觉. 我感觉到整个身体都了. 我现在比我服用这种物质之前更快乐. 旅行本身并不足以让我忙碌. 我仍然能够做我通常做的所有其他事情. 我喜欢笑,也喜欢玩. 但也许需要做一些事情. 这种药物可能需要更高的剂量来达到充分的效果. 我不知道这种药物对大脑的受体有什么影响, 时间2点 没有增加效果. 或需要更高的剂量,或这种化合物是无聊的. 没有任何变化,所以我不再写任何东西. 这就是这种物质的目的. 虽然它还没有磨损,但我没有什么可以补充的. 我只是在MSN上聊天,听音乐. 这是一个很好的情绪提升. 像几乎所有其他迷幻药一样. 没有视觉. 但几乎. 我认为一切物质都是精神. 这种物质是所有东西的真正组成. 但是是的. 每个迷幻药都会这样. 仍然可以. 我注意到已经消失了. 不知道他们离开了多久. 我没有错过它. 打哈欠 我吃了一些水果. 药物帮助我让我的思想更加清晰. 这是一次积极的经历, 我越来越累了. 六点钟 效果仍然存在,但我会试着睡觉. 我认为这种药物可能对那些想要进一步探索心灵灵感觉的人有用, 但如果你想要真正的旅行, 晚安 其他: 2-Me-DMT (453):单独使用 (16),第一次使用 (2),一般 (1)

Jan 28, 2008. erowid.org/exp/68019 DOSE: 1 line insufflated 2-Me-DMT (powder / crystals) 1 capsl oral 2-Me-DMT (capsule) BODY WEIGHT: 60 kg Substance: 2-ME-DMT Experience: 1st Time Setting: At home, Alone Administration: Nasal, Oral Gender: Male Age: 19 22:34 Snorted a small line to see if this stuff works nasally and perhaps works better this way. Doesn't burn badly but it is felt. I'm talking on msn and I'm basically in a good mood. Drip just started. Ewww Burns! This stuff has a horrible burning drip much like DPT. I drink some water and have a ciggarette. 22:47 Effects are noticed. Distinguishing between sobriety and the drug it's effects now. The effects are coming on gradually now and I am waiting to see how much stronger it's going to get before I take more. Come up is felt but very light and so not bothering me at all. Psychedelic feeling. Possibly feeling warmer. Pupils are dilated. 23:02 Decided to ingest the rest of the 100mg in a gelcapsule. Snorting this stuff isn't exactly pleasant. The sensation is pretty much like every other tryptamine at a low dose. Undistiguishable from other tryptamines. Stronger effects need to be reached to be able to explain better what is unique about this substance. Watched some movie on tv for a while to see if I liked it. Nope. I don't like scary movies. A light psychedelic feeling with no character of it's own yet is felt. Listening to some music. Thinking about the future and humanity on earth. I am worried. The substance is making me care more about the situation of natural disasters than my drug use. The beautiful music goes along with it perfectly. I want to help. I don't know how. That's where my thought patterns lay now. A more serious mood developed. Talking on msn again now. 23:55 Are we getting stronger effects yet? I feel a tactile sensation through my head. Chills. As I am thinking about concepts I perceive more insight in my thought patterns. Possibly more abstract. Chills continue through my head. A positive thing is that I haven't felt any come up feelings yet. And the experience of course is positive. Enjoyable but not strong. Not a trip because I'm still here. 00:13 Starting to feel the tactile sensations through the rest of my body. Mainly through my head. Colour difference is observed. First visual activity. Coming on stronger now. Mood improving. As lucidity comes. 0:36 I keep smiling the whole time. I feel chills and cold. I turn up the heat. One person that was sober that I was talking to on msn noticed effects I believe. Through telepathy. All the other people who I talked to were high on other things so no way to be absolutely sure. Tactile sensations is what I experience for the most part. I feel chills through my whole body. I am happier now than I was before I took this substance. 1:18 The trip itself isn't strong enough to keep me busy. I'm still capable of doing all the other things I usually do. I laugh and have fun. But perhaps need something to do. This drug might require a higher dosage to achieve full effects. I don't know what brain receptors this drug effect but something in my brain that regulates my temperature may be affected. 2:00 No increase of effects. Either a higher dosage is needed or this compound is boring. 2:47 Nothing is changing so I don't write anything anymore. That's about it for this substance. It's not worn off or anything yet but I just don't have anything to add. I'm just talking on msn and listening to music. It's a good mood enhancer though. Like almost every other psychedelic. NO visuals. But almost. I perceive everything that is of matter as spirit. The substance that everything is really made of. But yeah. Every psychedelic does that. 3:41 Still going. I notice the chills are gone now. Don't know for how long they've been gone. I don't miss it. 5:26 Yawn. I ate some fruit. The drug helped me by making my mind more lucid. It was a positive experience but not a real trip. I'm getting more tired. 6:00 Effects are still present but I'm going to try and get some sleep. I think this drug might be useful for those who want to explore telepathy further but if you're looking for a real trip I recommend a different substance.

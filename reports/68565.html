美国 大家好, 我是16岁的狂喜药使用者. 我已经服用了四次狂喜药, 每次都带来了难以形容的结果. 一切都是纯粹的幸福... 直到后果开始. 每次我喝了Ecstasy后, 我都会感觉到严重的恶心和冲动. 这些症状在初始剂量后的几个小时到几天之间. 我感觉自己不合适, 至少一个星期, 我意识到如果想继续吸食狂喜药, 我需要找到一种方法来对抗这些感觉. 我看到一瓶合法止痛药, 每片含有500毫克的乙胺/12.5毫克的二胺. 我从瓶子里拿出两粒药, 20分钟后... ! 我记得有一种清晰的感觉, 我注意到严重的恶心和空虚感已经消失了, 完全正常,实际上是我几个月来感觉最正常的. 我可以在社会 (学校) 中专注, 履行自己的角色, 乙氨基和氨基停止使用后, 狂喜的后果会再次出现, 开始我相信这一切都是我脑海里想出来的,但在服用了第二剂 (1片药) 后,我立刻恢复了完全正常的感觉. 它会带来痛苦,头,情感感觉, 实际上让它消失! 在四到五个小时! 非常有用, 现在我不会使用狂喜除非我有乙氨基与二胺. 现在我知道如何对抗MDMA的负面影响. 其他: MDMA (3), 双胺 (109), 乙氨基 (337): 宿醉 / 几天后 (46), 健康益处 (32), 不适用 (38)

Jan 2, 2010. erowid.org/exp/68565 DOSE: repeated MDMA repeated oral Acetaminophen (pill / tablet) repeated oral Diphenhydramine (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 187 lb Hello everyone, I am 16 and an avid Ecstasy user. I have ingested Ecstasy four times, each with results beyond definition. Everything is pure bliss... until the aftereffects took course. Each time after ingesting Ecstasy, I would notice severe nausea and a rush type feeling. These symptoms would range from hours to days after initial dosage. Mix these two feelings together and I simply felt out of place, and extremely spaced out for at least a week. I realized that I needed to find a way to combat these feelings if I wanted to continue to take Ecstasy. One day in my kitchen, browsing the medicine shelf for possible solutions, I saw a bottle of a legal painkiller which contained 500 mg Acetaminophen/12.5 mg Diphenhydramine per pill. Not really thinking, I took two pills out of the bottle and popped them both, not thinking much of it. 20 minutes later…BAM! I remember a feeling of clarity, and I noticed that the severe nausea and spaceyness had vanished and I felt, well, normal. Completely normal, actually the sanest I’ve felt in months. I could focus and perform my role in society (school) without the least bit of nausea and never once felt out of place. After the Acetaminophen with Diphenhydramine would where off, the aftereffects of Ecstasy would return, but it definitely was not as overwhelming as before. At first I believed that this was all in my head, but after I took a second dosage (1 pill) I returned right back to feeling completely normal. It takes that painful, dizzy, emotional feeling and literally makes it vanish! For 4-5 hours! Very useful, and now I will not use Ecstasy unless I have Acetaminophen with Diphenhydramine. To me this was an amazing discovery, and now I know how to combat the negative effects of MDMA.

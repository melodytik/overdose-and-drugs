菲奥里塞特已经是我选择的药物多年了. 我在十几岁的时候就被处方为慢性偏头痛. 我很快发现我得到了最惊人的高通过服用略高于我的医生推的剂量. 菲奥里塞特 (或通称Esgic) 是一种非配方药片,只提供含有50mg布塔尔比塔尔/ 乙氨基325mg/ 咖啡因40mg的片. 品牌的颜色是蓝色,通用版本是白色. 我注意到仿制药与品牌药的吸收率有所不同, 品牌药的吸收速度更快, 开始的时候,我吃了3-4粒药. 我的耐受性很快就增强了. 这是一种非常快速的行为和快速的高, 我会打扫我的房间,跑一圈,但是在和平做. 我会发现自己有信心和生产力, 它将让我开始一个非常好的减肥计划在同一时间 (感谢咖啡因含在药理学). 我花了很多时间在药物上做任何事,工作,清洁,看电视. 这是一种相当安全的药物, 就我在早期的整体损伤而言. 菲奥里塞特的缺点在于, 当我撞车时, 我可能会变得贫血, 这是一种很难摆脱的药物, 我喜欢和讨厌这种药物大约10年, 我还在做梦. 我仍然喜欢它. 我应该慢慢地戒掉它 - - 每天服用更低的剂量 - - 但我认为我们都知道这很难当我喜欢高) 我经历夜间出汗,梦,肌肉,抑郁, 我会睡2天. 时间表: 不适用 (38), Hangover / 几天后 (46), 和习惯性 (10), 回顾性 / 总结 (11)

Nov 30, 2007. erowid.org/exp/39635 DOSE: repeated oral Butalbital (pill / tablet) repeated oral Caffeine (pill / tablet) repeated oral Acetaminophen (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 115 lb Fioricet has been my pharmaceutical of choice for many years now. I was originally prescribed the drug for chronic migraines when I was a teenager. I quickly discovered that I got the most fantastic high from it by taking slightly over the dosage my Dr recommended. Fioricet (or generically Esgic) is a non-formulary pharmaceutical tablet that only comes in a tablet containing 50mg of Butalbital/acetaminophen 325mg/caffeine 40mg. The brand name is blue in colour and the generic versions are white. I have noticed some differences in the absorption rates of the generic vs. the brand name, the brand name absorbs quicker in my opinion, but both yield the same results ultimately. In the beginning I take 3-4 pills. My tolerance built very quickly. It is a very fast acting and speedy high, but also has a nice smoothe happy feel to it. I will clean my room, run around the block, but be at peace doing it. I will find myself confident and productive and it will start me on a very nice weight loss program at the same time (thanks to the caffeine contained in the pharmacology). I have spent time doing anything and everything on this drug, working, cleaning, watching TV. It's a pretty safe drug as far as my general impairment in the first stages. The downside of Fioricet is that when I crash, and I WILL crash, I crash hard and will sleep for lengths of time that I did not think possible. I can become anemic and notice myself bruising at the slightest touch of someone. This is a very difficult drug to shake lose from and withdrawel is not a pretty picture. I loved and hated this drug for about 10 years and have only recently become free of it. I still dream about it. I still love it. When I stop cold turkey (which I should not, I should ween myself off it slowly-taking a lower dosage each day-but I think we all know that's VERY hard to do when I love a high) I experience night sweats, nightmares, muscle spasms, depression and I will sleep for about 2 days.

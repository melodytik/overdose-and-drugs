我第一次和唯一一次使用PMA的经历完全是偶然的. 我开始我的星期五晚上像其他, 去我最喜欢的夜总会得到一些x. 作为一个大家伙, 有相当的耐受性到MDMA从做很多我通常得到三个标签. 我到达俱乐部, 在我到达前几分钟, 发生了一起抢劫事件, 他告诉我他可以给我买几辆三车, 我以前曾经乘坐三车, 经历不错, 所以我让他们认为我很幸运能找到任何东西. 我会在十一点左右拿起药. 在半小时内我认为我开始了我生命中最好的滚动. 开始时就像X一样, 它像一一样击中了我. 但在15分钟内我开始发假发. 肌肉紧张, 不住, 感觉就像我在冰箱里. 我也无法正常呼吸. 朋友们非常担心这一切发生, 但在几分钟后, 我又开始感觉好了. 我觉得自己跳舞得很疯狂,一切都在变得如此快. 这持续了大约20分钟左右. 然后我摔倒了,我的肌肉又紧张了,我开始,一切都被住了,我的眼睛无法集中,我再次,但我很热. 然后一切都变黑了. 朋友们让我躺在俱乐部后院的长椅上. 他们说我变红了, 我仍然是高,感觉很好,但它不是一卷. 这持续了大约一个小时. 总的来说, 我说这些都不安全, 我感觉很好几次只相当于一个半小时, 我感觉到后果一个星期. 一个持续了五分钟的回忆. 其他: PMA (126):旅行后的问题 (8),健康问题 (27),俱乐部/酒吧 (25)

Shaking, Seizure, and Aftereffects Ecstasy (PMA?) by keredpdx Citation:   keredpdx. "Shaking, Seizure, and Aftereffects: An Experience with Ecstasy (PMA?) (exp4180)". Erowid.org . Dec 24, 2001. erowid.org/exp/4180 DOSE: 2 tablets oral MDMA BODY WEIGHT: 210 lb My first and only experience with PMA was completely accidental. I started out my friday evening like any other, going to my favorite night club to get some x. Being a big guy and having quite a tolerance to MDMA from doing it a lot I usually get three tabs. I get to the club and there was a bust a few minutes before I got there so the whole club is dry. I talked to a friend of mine who worked security and he told me that he could get me some Mitsubishis and only two. I had taken the Mitsubishis before and had a decent experience with them but not great, they were usually just weak x. So I went ahead and got them thinking that I was lucky to have found anything. I get my pills and drop them at around eleven. Within a half an hour I'm thinking I'm starting on the best roll of my life. The onset is like x and it hit me like a ton of bricks. But within fifteen minutes I'm starting to wig. All my muscles tensed up and I couldn't stop shaking, I felt like I was in a deep freezer. I couldn't breath right either. My friends were extremly worried when all of this happened because I can handle my drugs. But after a few minutes I was starting to feel good again. I felt like dancing like crazy and everything was moving so fast. This lasted maybe twenty minutes or so. Then I crashed, my muscles went tense again, and I started shaking, everything was muffled, my eyes couldn't focus, I was shaking again but I was hot. Then everything went black for me. When I came to my friends had me lying on a bench outside on the back patio of the club. They said that I turned beat red and had a seizure. I was still high and felt pretty good but it was not a roll at all. this lasted about an hour. All in all I'd say that these are not at all safe and the few times I did feel good only amounted to an hour and a half, and the times I was freaking out amounted to about three hours (the seizure lasted for fifteen minutes). I felt afteraffects for a week to. Uncontrollable shaking, chills, hot flashes, and one full blown flashback that lasted about five minutes.

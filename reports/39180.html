有50毫克的3CP. 我从来没有这样做过, 也不知道有谁这样做过, 然而我被告知它在50毫克范围内有效. 我非常谨慎, 不想做完整的50毫克, 所以我给了25毫克,告诉我的女朋友, 她开车到乡下, 我不得不回忆一下, 自从我这么做一年多以前, 不过不像2ct2那么好,感觉不如2ct2那么快乐. 经过3到4个小时的时间, 当我确信没有任何惊喜等待着我时, 这样做对我来说是个浪费, 在这种剂量下对我没有什么好处, 总体来说, 持续了大约八个小时. 我对它没有印象深刻, 但我不喜欢安非他命. 这感觉不像是安非他命, 这绝对是迷幻. 但不是一个伟大的. 我很高兴有机会尝试. 我觉得这让我有点烦躁 (但这可能是外来影响), 她偶尔会问我的实验如何,所以她不能告诉我采取任何东西. 如果需要的话, 可以与任何人互动. 这就是它. 我不认为我会再试一次, 我看到这里列出的剂量比我小得多. 实际上我通常只需要上述剂量的50%左右才能充分发挥作用. 我希望我的两分钱有所帮助, 但它仍然很少去. 时间表: 3C-P (320):小组 (2-9) (17),第一次 (2),自然/户外 (23),一般 (1)

"Not A Great One: An Experience with 3C-P (exp39180)". Erowid.org . Dec 23, 2004. erowid.org/exp/39180 DOSE: T+ 0:00 25 mg oral 3C-P T+ 4:00 25 mg oral 3C-P BODY WEIGHT: 200 lb Had 50 mg of 3CP. Have never done this before, or know anyone that has, not even the individual who gave it to me knew of anyone trying it. However I was told it was active in the 50 mg range. I was very cautious, and didn't want to do the full 50 mg, in case it was too much (other rc's it would be too much). So I did 25 mg, told my girlfriend, and she drove into the country so we could hike with our dogs. I'm having to recall upon memory as it was over a year ago since I did it, but with no reports available I felt I should throw in my two cents, sorry it's not in proper report format, too much time has elapsed. It came on ok, wasn't too strong, not exceptionally stimulating, sort of 'like' 2ct2, but not quite as good, didn't feel as happy as on 2ct2. After what had to be three or four hours, when I was sure there where no surprises waiting for me, I'd past peak, I took the other 25 mg. It seemed a waste not to, it wouldn't do much for me at this dose, so might as well piggy back on what I had going on. After a couple hours or so the effects went up a notch, and overall it lasted (taken this way) about eight hours. I wasn't that impressed with it, but I'm not much for amphetamines. It didn't feel like an amphetamine, it was definately psychedelic. But not a great one. I was grateful for the opportunity to try it. I felt it made me a little irritable (but that might have been external influences), but my partner never knew that or even suspected it, so it wasn't a heavy component, something I could easily cover up. She'd ask me occasionally how my experiment was going, so she couldn't tell I'd taken anything. Could definately interact with anyone if needed. Thats about it. I dont think I'd try it again, wasn't that great for me (didn't dislike it), but very valuable for experience sake. If you come across some, do start small (safety, safety, safety), I see that the listed dosages here are considerably smaller than I did. I'm not a hard head, in fact I usually require only about 50% of the listed dose for full effects. I hope that my two cents helps, but it's still very little to go by.

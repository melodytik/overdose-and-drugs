为了继续培养和扩大对这种被误解和滥用的药物的知识, 我提出了一个简单的观察, 在给药前25分钟, 我已经喝了半杯GF果汁, 效果的开始稍微延迟了 (30-35分钟),快速的高兴 (10分钟) 坚定地击中惊呆的天花板. 感觉像是重的羊毛毯子被放在一个人的智力和语言能力的头上. 我的头部充满了棉花, 我喜欢胺, 但这种状态令人丧, 也许是因为我进入了一个社会环境, 我的声音得像其他在场的人一样, 像一只鱼一样平坦而无声. 我有时在GBL体验开始时会有这种强烈的感觉, 但从来没有超过几分钟, 这一次持续了大约1 1/2 - 2小时. 没有高兴感, 却充满了自豪感, 这对我来说不是一个大优势. 在这个时候, 我已经使用大约1.7g - 2.5g的GBL大约3-5次一周2周. 我通常会使用1 (或2) .5g - 1.5g的强剂量, 在仔细计时药物和管理我的每日吗啡之前. 我必须说我的身体正在对GBL产生反应, 并且混这些观察的具体情况, 但效果持续时间的方面直接受到葡萄汁摄入的影响. 而不是跟随着一个温和,烂,说话状态,我可以集中和集中,也许,提高能力,我有问题进行对话和思考, 整个体验持续了大约4. 5个小时,没有增强剂 (不需要),比平时多了一个多小时. 虽然GHB/GBL不是一种"越多越好"的毒品,但如果高兴感和社会化效应与剂量增加直接和比例地增长,那么更高的剂量就会有某种意义,但事实并非如此. 只有混乱,恶心,无用和睡眠等待使用者更高的剂量. 这对我来说是药物太多, 由于缺乏清除, 总而言之,一个更长的经验不是我想要的如果我必须花所有的时间在这个无价值的雾. 我不会重复. 其他: 2007年9月14日发布 GBL (89):一般 (1),组合 (3),小组 (2-9) (17)

Grapefruit Juice: An Anzymatic Observation GBL by nutopia Citation:   nutopia. "Grapefruit Juice: An Anzymatic Observation: An Experience with GBL (exp50217)". Erowid.org . Sep 14, 2007. erowid.org/exp/50217 DOSE: 2.6 g oral GBL (liquid) Morphine (daily) BODY WEIGHT: 180 lb In an attempt to continue to cultivate and grow the body of knowledge on this greatly misunderstood and misused drug, I present a simple observation on the specification of the drug's effects brought on by the intestinal CYP 3A4-mediated metabolism of grapefruit juice. I had ingested a half cup of GF juice about 25 minutes prior to dosing with 2.6 g of GBL dropped into another half cup of juice. The onset of the effect was slightly delayed (30-35 min.) with a fast euphoric come-up (10min.) hitting firmly into a ceiling of dumbfoundedness. The peak was the feeling of a heavy woolen blanket being placed over the head of one's intellectuality and ability to wield cohesive language. My head was full of cotton and floating akwardly off its axis in a manner similar to that induced by ketamine. I love ketamine, yet this state was frustrating, perhaps because i had entered a social situation intending to call on the socializing effects of the GBL and only recieving a mind so full of pitfalls and dark rooms that I would lose track of the structuring of my sentences and the use of appropriate voice intonation. My voice grated on me as i'm sure it did to others present, it was flat and dead like a beached flounder. I have, on occasion, gotten this strong feeling at the beginning of a GBL experience, but never for more than a few minutes, 15 at most. This time it lasted for about 1 1/2 - 2 hours. It ran short on euphoria but long on fucked-upped-ness, not a huge plus for me. At this point in time, i had been using about 1.7g - 2.5g of GBL about 3-5 times a week for 2 weeks. I would usually use 1 (or 2) .5g - 1.5g booster doses as well, before carefully timing out the drug and administering my daily morphine. And so it must be said that my body was evolving a response to the GBL and confounding the specifics of these observations, but the aspect of duration of effect was directly effected by the ingestion of grapefruit juice. The peak plateau-d for over an hour as opposed to 10 - 15 minutes and instead of being followed by a mellow, glowing, talkative state where i can focus and concentrate with, perhaps, increased ability, I had trouble carrying conversation and thinking and, as a result, found myself board and frustrated. The entire experience lasted about 4.5 hours without a booster (not desired), more than an hour longer than usual. GHB/GBL is not a 'more-is-better' drug; if the euphoric and socializing effects were to grow directly and proportionately with an increase in dose, then higher doses would make some sort of sense, but this veryreallytruly is not the case. Only confusion, nausea, uselessness and sleep await the user of the higher doses. This was too much of the drug for me, due to the lack of clearance, it hit me like a punch that throbbed for 4.5 hours. All in all, a longer experience isn't what i want if i must spend the whole time in this valueless fog. I will not repeat.

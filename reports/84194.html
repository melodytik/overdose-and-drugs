中文译文 大麻素

我昨晚坐在学校里试图为宏观经济学考试做准备. 我服用了20毫克的快速释放阿德拉尔来帮助学习, 但阿德拉尔给我的"尖端"完全不愉快. 几个朋友来我的房间借用布我做了特定的目的吸烟这新香料的东西. 我们发现它大约一个月前, 从那以后我们都经常吸烟. 虽然我完全不喜欢这种东西, 但它的效果正是我在一天结束时需要放松的东西. 我去他们的房间抽烟, 这样可以减轻阿德拉的紧张. 我看了一下时钟, 才意识到现在只有晚上11点. 我还有时间学习, 准备考试, 我去找一些香料. 我和几个人聊了聊, 发现另一个小伙子在几层楼下我有一些史诗般的东西. 我进去看了那个包裹,它叫"太空". 几分钟后, 我开始感觉到药物的镇静作用, 这就是事情变得有点奇怪的地方. 让我先说我已经尝试了相当多的药物. 我曾经用过和酸, 但这与我之前的旅行完全不同. 这绝对是一次旅行. 以下是完全正确的 (至少我能记得的). 十五分钟后:我感觉非常奇怪. 眼睛需要几秒钟才能赶上眼睛的位置. 我全身都有一种的感觉, 我记得我还有一个考试,所以我起来锁上门. 我回到了床上, 发现走路或任何其他简单的运动都比应该更难. 我实际上是在床上摔倒. 20分钟:我唯一正确工作的感官是听觉. 我在电脑上播放音乐, 这就是旅程开始的地方. 在旅途中, 我不知道我是谁, 我真的觉得自己正在死去, 我的思想与身体完全分离, 我不确定它在哪里. 我记得我觉得自己被完全撕裂, 我记得发现呼吸很难. 我记得我认为我已经彻底搞了我的生活, 如果不是我自己, 这样就不会那么糟糕, 感觉好像我已经离开了它比我实际上有更长的时间. 我倒在椅子上,撞到它, 倒在桌旁的地板上. 我猜我的肩膀打了因为它现在真的伤了. 我不知道我是谁,我在哪里,或者我刚刚做了什么. 我觉得自己是从无处诞生的, 就在我的地板上. 我听到有人在敲门,我很害怕,所以我不打开门 (我怀疑如果我想打开门,我也不能打开门). 我开始重新找回我自己是谁,在哪里, 35-40分钟:我开始意识到这是一次旅行, 我现在不那么害怕, 我觉得我的神经被烧了,我几乎不能想到除了我有多糟糕. 我躺在那里大约10分钟, 因为旅行的激烈部分结束后, 在这十分钟之后,我睡着了. 我对大麻,和酸以及香料的作用非常熟悉. 我从来没有做过这样的事. 感觉好像我已经离开了好几天. 我很抱歉这份报告没有更好, 我有几个朋友昨晚也尝试过这个"空间", 这种东西真的吓坏了我, 已使用的证券 2010年4月3日发布 胺类药物 (6), 产品 - 类似香料的吸烟混合物 (472):单独 (16),组合物 (3)

Apr 3, 2010. erowid.org/exp/84194 DOSE: 20 mg oral Amphetamines (pill / tablet) 1 hit smoked Products - Spice-Like Smoking Blends (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 200 lb Last night I was sitting at school trying to study for a macroeconomics test. I took a 20 mg instant release Adderall to help study but the 'edginess' that Adderall gives me is not pleasant at all. A few of my buddies come into my room to borrow the waterfall I made for the specific purpose of smoking this new Spice stuff. We found out about it about a month ago, and we have all smoked it regularly since then. We are subject to random drug tests and, while I do not enjoy the stuff at all, the effects are just what I need to relax at the end of the day. I went to their room to smoke a bong and it did the job of cutting the jitters from the Adderall. I got done studying for one test, looked at the clock and realized it was only 11:00 PM. I still had time to study for my other test, but it was time for my reward for studying like I should have. I left to find some more spice. I talked to a few people and found out that another kid a few floors down from me had some epic shit. I went in and saw the package; it was called 'Space.' He packed a bong and I ripped it. A few minutes later I start feeling the calming aspect of the drug and returned to my room satisfied. Now this is where things get a little strange. Let me start off by saying I have tried a fair amount of drugs. I have done shrooms and acid as far as hallucinogens go, but this was completely different than any other trip I have had. And yes, this was definitely a trip. The following is completely true (at least as much as I can remember.) 15 minutes in: I'm feeling really strange. When I turn my head it takes a few seconds for my vision to catch up to where I am looking. I have a tingling sensation all over my body, which is actually kind of enjoyable. I remember that I still have a test to study for, so I get up and lock my door. I make it back to my bed, and I find that walking or any other kind of simple movement is exponentially harder than it should be. I literally fall on my bed. 20 minutes: The only sense that is working correctly is my sense of hearing. There is music playing from my computer, but I can't see, feel, smell ANYTHING. This is where the trip starts. For the duration of the trip I had no idea who or where I was. It honestly felt like I was dying and this was what death was like. My mind was completely separate from my body, and I'm not entirely sure where it was. I remember feeling like I was being completely pulled apart, and I remember finding that it was hard to breath. For some reason I remember thinking that I had completely fucked my life up, and it was going to be like this forever. Maybe if I wasn't by myself this would not have been so bad, but I really don't think I would have been responsive enough to understand what anyone was saying to me anyway. When I started coming out of the really intense part of the trip it felt like I had been out of it for much longer than I actually had. 30 minutes in (+/-): I come to and I am falling towards my chair, I hit it, and fall to the floor by my desk. I'm guessing my shoulder hit it because it is really bruised now. I have no idea who I am, where I am, or what I just did. It felt like I was born out of nothing, right there on my floor. I hear knocks at my door, which scares me so I don't answer it (I doubt I could have if I wanted to.) I had to literally crawl on the floor for a few minutes to regain any kind of balance and finally made it back to my bed. I am starting to regain my perspective of who and where I am, but this is a slow process. 35-40 minutes: I'm starting to figure out that this was a trip, but things are still really strange. I'm not as scared of it now, but I am still really freaked out. My nerves feel fried, I can barely think of anything aside from how fucked up I am. I lay there for about 10 more minutes because after the intense part of the trip was over it was much more enjoyable, more like a high than a trip. Sometime after this ten minutes I fell asleep. Now like I said, I am extremely familiar with the effects of marijuana, shrooms and acid, as well as spice. This was like nothing I had ever done before. It felt like I was gone for days. I'm sorry that this report isn't better, but I simply can't remember enough of what happened to give it a fair analysis. A few of my friends also tried this 'Space' last night, and all with similar effects. This stuff literally scared me, and the fact that it was bought over the counter makes it even scarier.

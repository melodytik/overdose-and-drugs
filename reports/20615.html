这只是发生在几个小时前, 我没有睡过30个多小时, 背景:我23岁,以前尝试过很多物质 (从未有机会尝试LSD或,我已经尝试过幻觉药物:5-Meo-Dipt,5-Meo-Dmt,2C-I...) 我认为自己是个有经验,平静,稳定的人. 这是我第二次尝试TMA-2, 上次我参加了一个大家都喝酒的聚会, 这让我观察了一些人喝醉时的愚蠢程度. 我第一次没有任何视觉效果, 只是感觉到身体充满了强烈的声, 思想工作确实很有趣, 我喜欢TMA-2的第一次旅行, 并且自动认为第二次用相同剂量会有相同的感觉, 我在旅行前一晚起床吸了一些高品质的香烟,听一些好音乐和绘画. 我有个聪明的想法, 摄入40毫克的TMA-2凝囊, 作为最后一次的第一小时只是等待物质工作, 我感觉到药物的第一效应, 开始认为这是TMA-2囊的摄入是一个错误:我已经睡不着一段时间了,我的公寓不是按照我习惯的顺序. 我认为我在服用这种药物时犹不决. 我开始享受这种提升情绪的感觉. 别再认为吃了那个囊是个错误, 我预计在旅途中会保持这种心态. 现在墙壁都活了起来:视觉效果很好, 一些使用者没有报告腹. 墙壁充满活力, 天花板看起来像沙拉沙漠. 迄今为止最好的视觉效果, 我在T+3:00和T+6:00看到的或发生的以下事情, 我根本无法追踪那个时间, - 墙壁开始融化, 包括天花板, - 我的电视试图转变为一个不同的对象, 虽然不能识别. - 我只是认为我可能有一个壁炉在我的客厅, 至少我可以看到火焰在我的眼角. - - 房间里一切都活着, 电视在跳起来跳下, - - 我不得不放下音乐,我太深入了旅程, 我觉得自己正敲打++++体验的大门, 我只是躺在沙发上, 穿越我的大脑和想象力. - 影子似乎出现在每一个地方,抬头看着灯,看到一个女人的脸,这吓坏了我很好. 现在是T+12:45,我又回到了原点, 仍然想知道我这次旅行的目的. 我似乎没有意识到刚才发生在我身上那些奇妙的事情. 我可能需要几天来处理这些想法... 整体而言, 这是一个非常神奇的旅程, 下次我在, 我应该确保我的地方是顺序, 我很高兴我的环境, 下次我尝试TMA-2 (相信我,我愿意这样做) 我甚至可能会再试一次, 但正如任何药物 (尤其是研究化学品) 可以用错误的方式. 最重要的是:记住要有乐趣!

Massive Visuals TMA-2 by Fatsuma Citation:   Fatsuma. "Massive Visuals: An Experience with TMA-2 (exp20615)". Erowid.org . Jan 22, 2003. erowid.org/exp/20615 DOSE: 40 mg oral TMA-2 BODY WEIGHT: 95 kg This just happened a couople of hours ago, and I haven't slept for 30+ hours so bear with me on this report. Backround: I'm 23 years old, have tried numerous of substances before (Never had the opportunity to try LSD or mushrooms, hallucinogenics I've tried: 5-Meo-Dipt, 5-Meo-Dmt, 2C-I...) I consider myself somewhat experienced and fairly calm and stable person. This is my second try on TMA-2, the last time I was in a party where everyone used alcohol, some of the people didn't even know that I was tripping. That let me to observe the level of stupidity in some people when drunk. The first time I didn't get any visuals whatsoever and felt just a strong buzz in my body, mindwork was definitely interesting and I seemed to find different patterns of thought during conversations with drunken people. I liked the first trip with TMA-2, and automatically thougt that the second time with a identical dose would pretty much feel the same, how wrong was I.... I stayed up the night before the trip just smoking some quality-buds, listening to some good music and drawing. At 7 am I had the bright idea of ingesting the 40mg TMA-2 gelcap that I had saved for future use. As the last time the first hour was just waiting for the substance to work, meanwhile I was getting very tired. T+ 1:15 - I felt the first effects of the drug, that familiar body buzz that I've had several times before with substances of the similar nature. T+ 1:45 - Starting to think that was the ingestion of the TMA-2 capsule was a mistake: I haven't slept for a while, my apartment was not in that order what I'm used to. I think that I hesitated in taking this drug. T+ 2:00 - Euphoric feeling, I'm starting to enjoy this lift of mood. Don't think anymore that ingesting that capsule was a mistake, just too busy feeling good. I expected this state of mind to remain during the trip. T+ 2:45 - A visit to the bathroom, the walls are all alive now: visuals are ok but not overwhelming. No diarrhea reported by some users. T+ 3:00 - Visuals are becoming pretty gorgeus, walls are alive, the ceiling looks like Sahara during a sandstorm. Best visuals ever so far, I'm fairly suprised and very high. The following things I saw or happened during T+ 3:00 and T+ 6:00, I simply couldn't keep track of the time when I was too busy watching my carpet transforming into a deep forest: - Walls are begining to melt including the ceiling, pretty cool looking stuff I might add :) - My TV is trying to transform into a different object, although not recognizable. - I just think that I might have a fireplace in my living room, atleast I can see the flames in the corner of my eye. - Everything is alive: the room is spinning, TV is jumping up and down, my sofa seems to have a mind of his own, my tv-table tries to walk and my remote controller tries to crawl to me. - Had to put the music down, I was getting too deep into the trip and honestly I was getting afraid what might happen next. I felt like I was knocking the gates of ++++ experience and realised that I'm not ready for such a trip yet. I would just lie on the sofa travelling around my brain and imagination. - Shadows seems to appear everywhere, looked up at the lamp and saw a face of a woman, that spooked me real good. Now it's T+ 12:45 and I'm back to baseline, still wondering my purpose on that trip. So many questions fill my brain and I don't seem to realise the fantastic things that happened to me just a while ago. I may need a few days to process these thoughts... Overall it was a very magical trip, what enhanced it more was the fact that I did not expect visuals of such magnitude... everything was so beautiful: deep colours look absolutely stunning. Next time I am tripping I should make sure that my place is in that order that I'm happy with my surroundings, and have some light food and a lot of cigarettes. I think that me being so tired at first brought out the visuals much better, the next time I try TMA-2 (Believe me, I'm willing to do that :) I might even try again staying up late and trip in the morning. This substance definitely needs further exploration to show it's true identity, but as with any drugs (especially research chemicals) you can use this also the wrong way. Just keep safe, be careful and the most important thing of all: remember to have fun!

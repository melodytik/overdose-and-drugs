我和我的男朋友决定尝试一种相对'新'的药物, 我们希望在跳进去之前, 确定它值得冒险. 我们检查了许多来源, 关于使用AMT时的化学相互作用的任何信息. 我使用作为抗抑郁药, 我们决定这将是尝试AMT的好时机. 我们被告知, 病发需要2个小时左右, 我相信我会没事的, 因为我的男朋友有很多经验. 我们都开始服用15毫克的AMT. 我开始看到一些微小的视觉效果, 我觉得有些可怕, 我决定再服用15毫克的囊, 因为我的"旅行"很小, 发病时间已经过去了. 这是我可能犯的最大错误, 我的男朋友也做了另一个, 经过大约一个半小时的时间, 我在浴室的地板上摇着, 感觉墙壁和地板在, 甚至空气, 开始我很喜欢, 彩虹的幻觉消失了, 邪恶的恶魔取而代之, 人们从各个方向抓住我, 尖叫在我的耳边, 我一直在尖叫,哭泣,抓着东西. 我无法站起来. 我男朋友把我带到一个卧室, 他的脸完全扭曲. 他的言语都是邪恶和威胁. 我知道我需要帮助, 这不仅仅是一个糟糕的旅程. 他一直告诉我这是一次糟糕的旅行. 他给我带了牛奶,我没有喝. 他试图给我一些让我平静下来,我了他. 我曾多次要求他打电话给警察, 为了摆脱这种疯狂, 我威胁要自杀. 我觉得如果继续这样, 那天晚上我经历了我最大的恐惧. 接着我有很多呼吸困难, 我认为他想推我,所以我与他作斗争. 他告诉我他要带我去医院, 我吓坏了, 因为我认为他正经历着同样的疯狂. 后来我发现他能控制一切, 不像我. 我试图打开门. 我不记得如何打开它. 我以为有帮助在另一边, 我们会死这样. 然后我看到我的手上有血, 我认为我在无意中伤害自己, 没有血,只是在我的脑海里. 这是我记得的一切. 他把我带到车上, 开车送我去医院, 他停在一个加油站打电话给911. 警察带着救护车来. 他不得不经历与警察的地狱, 但它不是我们想象的那么糟糕. 他确实打电话给他们. 医生带我去医院,检查我每种可想象的药物,连接到一个IV....取血塞我狗...我猜我在第一次与他们斗争,因为他们把我绑在束当我醒来时....我有一个导管在我的膀和氧管在我的鼻子 (它仍然没有感觉到足够的空气) 和更多的管在我的手臂...我醒来了,我和我的监护人 在那边有个警察. 警官很冷酷, 考虑到我经历过的事情, 他告诉我我差点死了,他根本不在乎,因为这只是一个尸体报告或过量使用报告. 我没有足够的力量或心智来回应. 医院的账单很大, 最重要的是, 我现在可能被永久性地铁或6英尺地下. 我对自己的经历不公平, 我不认为任何人会知道这有多可怕, 如果您决定服用这种药物,请不要过于宽松. 如果您必须这样做, 请不要再服用任何处方药或非处方药, 其他: 医疗药物 (7),药品 - (91):组合 (3),不好的旅行 (6),健康问题 (27),医院 (36)

Eternity in Hell AMT & Lithium by Amber Citation:   Amber. "Eternity in Hell: An Experience with AMT & Lithium (exp22462)". Erowid.org . Jan 27, 2006. erowid.org/exp/22462 DOSE: T+ 0:00 15 mg oral AMT (powder / crystals) T+ 2:00 15 mg oral AMT (powder / crystals) T+ 0:00 Pharms - Lithium (daily) BODY WEIGHT: 110 lb My boyfriend and I had decided to try this relatively 'new' drug, AMT. Before jumping into it we wanted to be certain that it would be worth the risk. We checked out numerous sources, for any information pertaining to chemical interactions while using AMT. I take Lithium as an antidepressant, and it was not listed as a negative interactant. We had the whole house to ourselves and we decided that this would be a good time to try AMT. We were told that the onset takes about 2 hours, and not to take any extra AMT and that it would hit us in time. I was an unexperienced tripper, so I really did not know what to expect, but I trusted that I would be ok, since my boyfriend had alot of experience. Well we both took a 15 mg cap of AMT to begin. After the first hour I started to see minor visuals, to me they seemed a bit scary, but nothing that I could not handle. Then we went upstairs and somehow about an hour later I decided to take another 15 mg cap, because my 'trip' was pretty minor, and the onset time had passed. This was the worst mistake I could have made, due to the fact that I was on Lithium. Well, my boyfriend took another one too, just to be on the same page as me. After about another hour and a half is where I lost track of time and reality. I was on the bathroom floor in the corner shaking and feeling the walls and floor rippling, even the air, it was sort of like a water bed effect. I was enjoying it at first, but then it became too intense. The rainbow vision was gone and to replace the good feelings were evil demons, thats the only way I can describe it. People grabbing at me from all directions screaming in my ears whispering things like, 'You are going to die.' I was screaming and crying and clawing at things. I could not stand up. My boyfriend carried me into a bedroom and set me down on the bed. At this point even he looked evil, his face completely distorted. His words all came out evil and menacing. Then there were three second bouts of sanity, where I actually knew what was going on and I knew that I needed help, this was more than a bad trip. He kept telling me that it was a bad trip. He brought me milk and I wouldn't drink it. He tried to give me something to calm me down and i kicked him. I told him repeatedly to 'call the cops' take me to the hospital...MAKE THEM STOP!!! Then after what felt like eternity, I threatened to kill myself just to get away from the insanity....I felt if I stayed this way much longer it would be like this forever, and I would lose it even more, if that was possible. I lived all my worst fears that night. Then I had alot of trouble breathing, I felt as if I was suffocating. Finally he took me down the stairs....I thought he was trying to push me, so I fought him. Then he told me he was going to drive me to the hospital and I freaked out, because I thought he was going through the same madness as me. I later found out that he had everything under control, not like me. Well once downstairs I was trying to open the door. I couldn't remember how to open it. I thought there was help on the other side and we would die like this. Then I saw blood on my hands, I thought I was hurting myself unintentionally, just like all the horror stories I hear... There was no blood, it was in my head. Thats all I remember from there. He had taken me to my car and was driving me to the hospital, when I turned blue, from not breathing. He pulled over at a gas station and called 911. The police came with an ambulance. He had to go through hell with the cops, but it was not as bad as we thought it would be. He did call them, after all. The EMT's took me to the hospital, tested me for every drug imagineable, hooked up an iv....took blood stuck me with shit...I guess I was fighting them at first because they had me tied down with restraints when I woke up....I had a catheter in my bladder and oxygen tubes in my nose (it still didnt feel like enough air) and more tubes in my arms...I woke up all doped up and with my guardian and a cop there. The cop was pretty callous, considering what I had been through, I was in no condition for a lecture. He told me that I almost died and that he didnt give a shit basically, because it was just a morgue report to him or an overdose report. I didn't have the strength or the mind power to respond. My car was impounded, because it was left at the gas station, the hospital bill was huge, and on top of it all I could be permafried or 6 feet under right now. I did not do justice to my experience, and I don't think anyone will know how terrible it was until they have experienced that, but I would never wish that on anyone. Just don't be too lax with this drug if you ever decide to take it. I would not recommend it at all, but if you must, then please refrain from taking any perscription or OTC drugs before doing this, and for perscription drugs wait about 2 weeks, just to be safe....it's your life, don't be stupid.

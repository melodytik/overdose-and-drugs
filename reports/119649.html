Compared to some other tryptamines, this one seems rather heavy in body load. Pleasantly so, however. I'd say the total duration for the main substance, including come-up and visual effects residue, was about 4 to 5 hours, with headspace effects being 3 to 4 hours. Cannabis probably only had a minor effect on this. This was also the least introspective trip on any tryptamine I've had thus far. An effect I did not note during the experience but distinctly remember: During the peak (so at about T+2:00), I often had moments where anything outside my field of view seemed to be very different if I looked back and forth. At one point, I even tested this. This might be related to the time dilation effect. I'd basically expect some folks to be somewhere in the room, I'd briefly look away, look back, and completely different folks would be right there, in completely different positions. Another one: At one point, already towards the come-off, the edges of faces/hair of folks and objects around would start to shift in a sort of double vision sort of way, shifting when I move, but would also shift "outwards" when stationary. After having had some sleep, I still see those added details in many things, including my face, and they are verifiably real, because I can point them out to others and they'll see them too. I just don't seem to have noticed them for a while. I'm sure this perceptual enhancement will fade again sometime soon, but it's interesting to see. I wonder if this is how some folks get really self-conscious about skin blemishes? At around T+23:50, I walked along a very dark path by a stream of water. There, the particularly dark parts of my environment were oddly highlighted, almost as if they were exuding darkness, similar to how a lamp would work, but for darkness instead of light. Fascinating. Just a bit later, a shadow cast on a house by a tree had a similar effect. Not sure what to make of this, certainly unexpected. I have to say, I did not notice any such effect earlier, but this is also the first time it has been nighttime since full sobriety set in. At T+24:01, this effect fades, but I am clearly having a hard time focusing on the dark when surrounded by other light sources. Granted, some of this is normal, but it's even harder than usual. At T+24:14, I checked my pupil reflexes in a mirror and dimmable light, and indeed, what used to be dinnerplates last night have turned into the opposite. My pupils refuse to widen when given less light. One day later, at 18:30, I checked my pupil reflexes in the mirror again, and they were back to normal. At precisely 20:00, I've been at the same spot where I noticed the darkness yesterday, it was back to normal, too. Tryptamines generally fuck with my light perception in a rather short-to-mid-term lasting way, so this might be one of those effects. Another time in the future, I shall avoid doing anything besides 4-PrO-DMT to see if it reoccurs on its own, or if it was caused by my THC intake. Another reason for doing so might be to determine the duration of after-effects. About ten days later, the detail enhancement in my vision seems to have been of merely perceptual nature; the details are filtered normally when I don't specifically seek them out, but when I do, they're still there. I think this trip just made me more aware of this fact again by preventing this filtering momentarily.

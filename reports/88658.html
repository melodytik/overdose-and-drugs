May 29, 2019. erowid.org/exp/88658 DOSE: 1600 mg oral Piracetam 1000 mg oral Vitamins - Choline Caffeine Vitamins / Supplements BODY WEIGHT: 180 lb I had tried Piracetam by itself for a semester of college and performed very well earning 3 A’s and a B. This was a good step up from my previous semesters where it was mostly B’s with scattered in A’s and C’s. The first week or so of Piracetam I noticed increased reaction speeds, awareness, and enjoyment of music, but those effects soon became negligible. However after reading some research I learned of a study on mice where aside from the control, a Piracetam group, a Choline group, and a Piracetam+Choline group were submitted into the study. While the Piracetam only group did well the Piracetam+Choline group scored exceptionally on their memory tests. With this knowledge I went to my local pharmaceutical specialty store to get some Choline. All I could find was a hair formula with 1000 mg of Choline Bitartrate. This fall semester I took 1600 mg of Piracetam with a 1000mg hair formula pill every morning M-F and sometimes on the weekends if I had a large project to work on. The results were staggering. I am a forestry major and our classes our pretty difficult to do well in. I only studied for about an hour the night before and the morning of all of my tests and didn’t score under a 100 on a single test. And these were senior level courses! Forest Economics, Forest Hydrology, Forest Insects and Diseases, and Landscape plant Materials. These classes were no joke, but with this smart pill combo I made them look that way. I knew the content so well just from attending class and a short studying session that my hand would cramp up from writing so fast and I couldn’t get my answers on the paper fast enough. I earned an A in all of my courses and made the Presidents list with minimal effort on my part, the pills did most of the work for me. Other inputs: Vitamins, Fish Oil, Caffeine, Daily Exercise Side Effects: NONE! I had one mood swing during the first week because a soda machine gave me a diet soda twice and I was rushing on an assignment because I procrastinated, I threw the second soda really hard into the trash can and felt immediately better.

From the first three I can be pretty certain its 5-APDB from what I can see on many different forums. Sadly I cannot confirm the colorimetric result to Simon’s reagent due to the lack of data found. Setting: Me and 7 friends in my living room, I was the only one experimenting 5-APDB. Smoking cannabis and hashish while talking will be our primary activity, alongside gaming. • T – 2:00 : Preloaded with 150mg magnesium citrate, 100mg of CoQ10, 70mg of Grape Seed Extract, 200mg of Alpha-Lipoic Acid and drank a lot of orange juice for Vitamin C, as I would for standard MDMA. Better safe than sorry. I put on sweat pants and a bandana, as I like to be comfy when I roll. Only ate some snacks today, so I would dose on an empty stomach. • T – 0:30 : Almost ready to roll. I smoked some hashish to get me started then I dosed my parachute: 120mg. 20mg more than the first time I tried it. I was pretty amazed how only 100mg got me rolling balls for 6 hours straight, totally couchlocked, smoking weed and hash all along. • T + 0:00 : I swallowed the 120mg bomb and sat in a pile of pillows, waiting for the high to kick in while reading other 5-APDB trip reports. • T + 1:20 : Strangely I’m not feeling anything at this stage yet, maybe I drank too much beforehand. 80 minutes have already passed, so I consider safe the idea of redosing at this point. I dropped 50mg to see if it would provide a good boost to the first bomb. • T + 1:30 : Finally I feel something, small waves of wellbeing electrifying my whole body. My sight seem a bit altered, colors were brighter, I can see some visual distortion if I look long enough at something. I feel stoned, not energetic at all. Tactile feelings are also enhanced, my skin feels soft, I’m sitting on this pile of pillows feeling as if I was on a cloud, letting the high submerge my whole body and mind of its vaporous shade. • T + 2:00 : Come up achieved. This feeling is definitely great. Almost no bruxism, probably related to the magnesium preloading. My hands are moist, but it wasn’t too hot with the windows open. The breeze of the outside blew through the windows to cool up this room filled with 7 stoned young adults. I took another 200mg of Alpha Lipoic Acid to ensure maximum antioxidant effect and avoid most of the possible neurotoxicity. I read on forums that there is probably no neurotoxicity through metabolisation of 5-APDB but I haven’t found any legitimate studies about it so I want to take my precautions. • T + 3:00 : Me and some friends went to the gas station to pick up some munchies. Although when I started eating I realized I had absolutely no appetite. I swallow my last bite of bread with difficulty. I went to the bathroom to find out about the vasoconstrictive effect, I couldn’t pee. I sat back in my pile of pillows and started smoking some hash, laidback in my living room with everyone playing videogames around me. I decided that some music would be great and I put on my Glitch Hop library. • T + 4:00 : I reached the plateau state and I just listened to my music, well relaxed and laidback in my pile of pillows, rolling a joint. This drug is relaxing as hell. Although it took me forever to roll that kingsize. I could barely stare at the joint or my vision would kind of bend. Small visuals, that was trippy. There is probably a link with the strong affinity of this drug to bind with 5-HT2A receptor. 2nd try, I rolled it well, with a lot of concentration, and so I went in my smoking lounge (a small room before the backyard porch) to smoke it. I sat in the sofa for 30min, as this kingsize seemed like it was forever burning. • T + 4:30 : I had a sudden energy burst and started cleaning my whole house because it was a mess after my friends left. I would only focus on this until I finished cleaning and picking up everything. • T + 5:00 : The rush faded out as I finally sat down after cleaning. Started watching That ‘70s show. I was still very much high and it lasted for another hour and a half until I started to come down. • T + 6:30 : I took a 10mg melatonin tablet for anti-oxidation and as an sleep aid, watched another episode before going to bed. I didn’t get much rest, it took me at least an hour to fall asleep. The morning after at around 7am, I ripped the bong before going to work, and this launched me back into the high for a while. I was feeling suddenly alert and my pupils were blown for like an hour after the bong hit.

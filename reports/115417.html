My Bad: Too Much ETH-LAD & 1cP-LSD by Zeb Jones Citation:   Zeb Jones. "My Bad: Too Much: An Experience with ETH-LAD & 1cP-LSD (exp115417)". Erowid.org . May 10, 2021. erowid.org/exp/115417 DOSE: 450 ug oral ETH-LAD (blotter / tab) 200 ug oral 1cP-LSD (blotter / tab) BODY WEIGHT: 300 lb Background: 5 years experience with psychedelics. 1P-LSD, 1cP-LSD, 1B-LSD, ALD-52 I've always been very careful with RCs. Slowly ramping up dosages, only changing one variable at a time (dose or supplements), never going alone, etc. However, I've always been attracted to new and novel experiences. So, once I find a good molecule, I'll test until I think I've maximized its potential. Occasionally, I'll try something new. (After a lot of research) I received 5x150ug ETH-LAD a few weeks ago. I was waiting for a good opportunity. Well, last Friday, after much humming and hawing, the wife and I decided to have a trip evening. The reason we were uncertain was because we had tripped the Friday before as well. "No problem," I thought, "the tolerance calculator has worked well for us before." (Spoiler: it worked perfectly for her. Me, not so much.) I was excited for ETH-LAD as I had read that there was the potential for more visuals and maybe a larger headspace. Yes, I knew there was the danger of throwing up, but I have a very good constitution and rarely, if ever, puke. My sweet spot for 1P-LSD is 500ug, which is what I took the week before. The tolerance calculator said I would need ~625ug to achieve the same level. Well, I only had 5 tabs of 150ug. I could've taken 4 (600ug) which would be close enough. But that would only leave me one. I decided to take three. (450ug) In the hours leading up to the drop (16:00), I started to question how much I'd actually feel with my current tolerance. I wondered what I could add. Since my experience with 1cP-LSD was that it was a good body load with little visuals or headspace, I decided to add 200ug of 1cP-LSD. Totals: 450ug ETH-LAD + 200ug 1cP-LSD. T+00:00 Dropped. Scheduled pizza delivery for T+04:30. T+01:00 Some OEVs and headspace. My body was tingling. Fairly pleasant. T+02:00 I feel underwhelmed with the visuals and headspace. I feel slightly nauseous. Body is starting to vibrate. T+03:00 I now feel very nauseous and my body is going crazy with vibrations. I do not feel well. I can't say how the visuals and headspace were at this point as I was concentrating on not being sick. (Note, I was regularly checking my pulse. It was a little higher than normal, but not much.) T+03:30 Nausea seems to have diminished somewhat. Still not feeling great though. T+04:00 Sudden diaphragm spasms. I was going to puke. I put my hand over my mouth and raced to the bathroom. Made it! (barely) I hadn't eaten since -04:00 (8 hours ago), so there was little solidity. Mostly liquid. Spasmed and puked three times. T+04:15 Rinsed mouth and washed hands and face. Actually felt a lot better. No longer nauseous. No longer feeling bad. Visuals and headspace still disappointing. T+04:30 Pizza arrived. Normally, I'm starving by this point. This time, I was just mildly hungry. I only ate half of what I normally would. T+11:00 Lied down, slept. Once I puked, the remainder of the trip was good, if underwhelming. I was really looking forward to the enhanced visuals and headspace. They did not materialize. OK, I know I screwed up. That was way too high of a dose for a first time with that molecule. My wife (together 11 years) mentioned that this was only the second time that she's ever seen me puke. (The other time was because of kidney stones.) I still have 300ug left of the ETH-LAD. I'm willing to give it another try. I'm just going to wait for a few months, not combine it with anything, and only take it when I have no tolerance.

十月 2007年12月 这是我第六次喝阿雅华斯卡的经历, 首先,我的身高6英尺,160磅,我19岁. 我是一位相当有经验的心理飞行员, 已经使用了大量的,LSD,2C-B,阿雅华斯卡,梅斯卡林,5-MEO-DMT,布福丁,氧化,MDMA,沙维亚,大麻, 我之前曾经多次尝试过阿雅华斯卡, 在这个特殊的场合, 我决定是时候终于在我的意识中进行一段旅程, 我开始在下午2点左右造一些阿雅华斯卡. 我已经学会了如何用一种简单的方法制作一些非凡的阿雅瓦斯卡. 我开始用我的数字秤量出20克的米莫萨霍斯蒂利斯根皮, 放入机中粉碎, 我把根树皮放进一个1~2公升的里,加上1000毫升的水和1大匙5%酸度的. 我把它煮沸,然后把它放到沸. 我让它煮沸,直到水量降至半杯左右, 我每隔10-15分钟一次. 我把使用的根皮放回里, 再做两次. 但是第二次沸时我使用750毫升,第三次沸时我使用500毫升. 我把水从中取出来的液体放回里, 让煮, 整个过程需要3到5个小时. 我认为这将是一次单人旅程, 接到一位朋友的电话, 我称之为J, 他问我是否想出去玩, 我对J说我今晚的计划. 他曾尝试过三次阿雅华斯卡, 但没有成功, 在感觉到效果之前清洗, 两人大约在八点半到达我家, 我们开始用3克的叙利亚种子. 种子很粗,所以我有一种特殊的小方法来减少味道. 首先,我把种子分成六堆, 每堆大约有半克. 然后, 我拿一个索引卡, 曲一个, 起一个堆. 我把一些饮料 (最好是根酒) 倒进嘴里,把它填满一半,把种子倒在饮料上,再喝一口,然后像药丸一样吞下. 尽管有时种子会漂浮到我喉的后面. 在接下来的半小时内, 于是我们拿起酒,我了一杯,然后把它分成三分之一. 我们三个人一整天都没吃什么,所以我想我们有更好的机会不排泄, 我和A都保持了自己的足够长的时间吸了几碗大麻来缓解我们的恶心. 吸烟后, 我开始感觉到DMT的轻微影响, 我很丧, 知道我已经做了大量的阿雅华斯卡, 我拥有一只Bufo Alvarius, 我受到了巨大的打击, 尽可能长时间地保持, 在我吹出烟雾之前, 这是一个人! 我必须说这是一次激烈的旅程. 视觉是真正的液体,流动. 我记得我看着地板, 看到各种各样的曼达拉轮, 虽然身体的兴奋是如此强烈, 这真是令人惊叹, 确实有那种接近死亡的感觉, 我体内有MAOI, 确实使5-MEO-DMT的效果变得更强大. 视觉效果更生动,持续时间更长. 虽然我确实注意到缺乏色彩和多色,不断变化的能量碎片我看到在阿雅华斯卡. 我走到厨房, 脑袋中一阵震动, 我觉得自己就像一个发送和接收信息的信标, 经过30分钟到一个小时的5-MEO-DMT, 我们吸了一口大麻. 我开始注意到阿雅瓦斯卡的效果慢慢增加, 另一小时过去了, DMT 开始消失. 现在我很失望, 但大麻仍然让我高兴, 我只能说吃饭一定启动了我的胃的消化过程, 并同时消化了我胃里的DMT, 在我意识到之前, 我倒了比我以前倒过的更强烈. 我眼前的一切都变成了我从未见过的现实操纵. 它变成了一系列不断变化的,多彩的能量, 我觉得我的眼睛被迫闭上, 随着我闭上眼睛, 我开始看到这个DNA螺旋旋转向我, 然后它爆发成无限量的烟花, 我被传送到DMT房间, 感觉就像我在一个立方体里, 房间里就像一个马戏团, 那些狂欢的游乐场和这些生物 (几乎像我在萨尔维亚上看到的生物), 他们非常开心, 更加热情地欢迎我, 欣喜地向我展示这个陌生而又熟悉的地方. 我看到所有这些"人"更像是人们的灵魂在欢笑和享受, 这种能量创造了我眼前的一切. 我在宇宙中流动, 看到各种各样的恒星,星球和星系. 我停下来,开始在这个无重空中浮动,然后这些生物向我展示了一块行星,上面有一棵巨大的树在太空中漂浮,然后它开始解体, 人类正在摧毁地球, 我们需要与地球和地合作, 然后我又一次流宇宙. 那些生物带我去这个蓝色的星球, 它看起来就像地球, 我们飞越了这个星球, 观察着它所居住的各种植物和生物. 这颗星球的设计与地球非常相似, 它更像是史前地球, 我参观了一个大沼泽区, 里面有巨大的, 它们的身体和头像, 眼睛像, 另一个出舌头, 吃了一只. 我惊地发现,与地球相比,这个星球上的东西是多么相似, 我在这个星球上看到的许多生物看起来比任何东西都更像爬行动物或两动物, 它们让我想起"永无止境的故事"中的阿特雷尤, 它们是白色的, 有毛的, 羽毛的翅膀和巨大的爪子与尖的爪子几乎像你可能想象一个, 我看到这些毛的虫般的生物, 黑色的眼睛和黑色的毛皮只覆盖他们的脚. 它们会像小狗一样, 一起玩. 巨大的,毛的,小柱! 我在地球上停留了一段时间后, 通过大气层回到太空, 如果我们所有人共同努力, 阻止我们使用地球而不是滥用它造成的破坏. 人们需要把注意力集中在一切的积极方面, 我们应该欣赏一切, 生活不是关于你拥有的东西,而是关于经验. 随着DMT的消耗, 宇宙就变成了一个球体, 我开眼睛, 感觉自己是个全新的人. 我真正体验过DMT, 这是我从听说阿雅华斯卡开始期待的体验. 意识的扩展,外在的身体,异世界,迷幻的旅程. 我在生活中经历过许多改变思想的物质, 我一定会在我的脑海中再次去那个地方. 他说他与上帝有了非常宗教性的经历, 终于明白了爱的真正意义, 他对无限的可能性开放了他的头脑, 并说他肯定会再次使用阿雅华斯卡. 其他: 青毒 (46), 华斯卡组合 (269), 叙利亚街 (45), 弥莫萨青 (74):小组 (2-9) (17), 烂的经验 (4), 一般 (1)

Oct 27, 2007. erowid.org/exp/65377 DOSE: T+ 0:00 3.0 g oral Syrian Rue (seeds) T+ 1:00 0.3333 cups oral Mimosa tenuiflora (extract) T+ 1:00 3 bowls smoked Cannabis (plant material) T+ 0:00 1 hit smoked Bufotenin T+ 0:00 1 cig. smoked Cannabis (plant material) BODY WEIGHT: 160 lb Aug. 12, 2007 This is an account of my 6th Ayahuasca experience, it was a breakthrough experience and definitely report worthy. To start I'm 6'1', 160lbs and I'm 19 years old. I'm a fairly experienced psychonaut, having used a nice amount of Mushrooms, LSD, 2C-B, Ayahuasca, Mescaline, 5-MEO-DMT, Bufotein, Nitrous Oxide, MDMA, Salvia, Cannabis, and different combinations of these and various other substances. I’ve experienced Ayahuasca several times before but I never felt I had got the full experience. On this particular occasion I decided it was time to finally take a journey through my consciousness, to voyage places foreign and far beyond anything recognizable in this realm of so called “reality”. I started by brewing up some Ayahuasca early on in the afternoon around 2. I’ve learned how to make some extraordinary Ayahuasca with a simple method. I start by weighing out 20 grams of the Mimosa Hostilis root bark on my digital scale and the put it in the blender to powder it so the DMT will be extracted more thoroughly. I put the root bark in a 1-2qt pot with 1000ml of water and 1 tablespoon of 5% acidity Vinegar. I bring it to a boil and then turn it down to a simmer. I let it simmer until the water is down to about a half cup to a cup and I stir it every 10-15 minutes. Then, once it has cooled I strain it through a cotton t-shirt. I put the used root bark back in the pot and repeat two more times. But on the second boil I use 750ml and on the third I use 500ml. After that, I put the liquid from the boils back in the pot, let simmer until the water reduces to about a half cup to a cup and let cool. This whole process takes anywhere from 3-5 hours. So I finished this around 6:30, thinking this was to be a solo journey, I get a call from a friend of mine, who I will refer to as J, asking if I wanted to hang out because another friend of ours, who I will refer to as A, was bringing some Cannabis. So of course I said yea and I told J my plans for the evening. He has attempted Ayahuasca on three other occasions to no prevail, purging before feeling the effects so he wanted to try again, and A had never tried it so I was happy he was coming over to finally experience DMT. They both arrive at my house around 8:30 and we begin by taking 3 grams of Syrian Rue seeds. The seeds are pretty gross so I have a special little method I do to reduce the taste. First, I separate the seeds into 6 piles more or less a half gram each. Then, I take an index card, bend a crease in it, and scoop up one of the piles. I pour some drink (preferably root beer) in my mouth filling it half way, pour the seeds on top of the drink, take another small sip, and swallow them like a pill. Although, the seeds do have a tendency to float to the back of my throat sometimes. We do this over the next half hour and then after taking the last of the seeds, wait about 30 minutes before taking the brew. So about 9:30 we take the brew, I had a cup made so I just split it in thirds. All three of us hadn’t eaten anything really all day so I figured we’d have a better chance of not purging but yet again J vomits before getting visuals, though this time he did get the body high from it. Both A and I held ours down long enough to smoke a couple bowls of Cannabis to calm our nausea. After smoking I started feeling some light effects of the DMT, seeing this layer over everything that slowly moved and morphed. I was kind of frustrated knowing I had made a good batch of Ayahuasca and I wasn’t feeling strong effects so I decided to boost it by smoking a bowl with some 5-MEO-DMT/Bufotein on top. I own a Bufo Alvarius toad which has blessed me with quit a few crazy moments. I took a huge hit, held it for as long as possible and was tripping before I blew out the smoke. WHOA MAN!!! I must say this was an intense ride. The visuals were real liquid, and flowing. I remember looking at the floor and seeing all these different mandala wheels and hieroglyphics and weird symbols. While the body high is sooo intense, it feels like my body is an empty physical shell for my spirit though void of its energy. Truly amazing and it really does have that near-death feel because the come up is so quick. Having the MAOI in my system really made the effects of the 5-MEO-DMT a lot stronger. The visuals were much more vivid and lasted much longer. Though I did notice the lack of colors and the multicolor, ever-changing, energy fractals I see on Ayahuasca. I walked to the kitchen and this shock went through the top of my head and ran down my spine sending a warm tingling sensation through my body. Then I felt as though I was a beacon sending and receiving information almost like a central point for energy to go to. After about 30 minutes to an hour of tripping on the 5-MEO-DMT, we smoke a blunt of Cannabis. I start to notice a slow increase in the effects of the Ayahuasca but still nothing mind blowing like I was expecting. Another hour goes by and it starts to feel as though the DMT is fading. I’m pretty disappointed at this point but I’m still high off the Cannabis and I start to get the munchies. So I eat like four croissant rolls and all I can say is eating must have started my stomachs digestive process and digested all the DMT that was sitting in my stomach at once because it hit me HARD. And before I knew it I was tripping harder than I’ve ever tripped before. Everything before my eyes became a manipulation of reality to another degree I had never witnessed before. It became an array of constantly changing, multicolor, energy in the form of shapes and designs in numerous patterns flowing into me as if I was absorbing the energy from everything that was around me. It became so intense to the point I feel my eyes being forced closed so I can witness the true potential of DMT. As my eyes are closing I begin to see this DNA helix spinning toward me, then it bursted into an infinite amount of fireworks and as the fireworks are erupting, a magician, decked out in the full attire top hat and magic wand included, appears above the fireworks, waves his wand and BAM!!! I’m teleported to the DMT room, which to me felt like I was in a cube where the walls had the same constantly changing, multicolor effect the energy I see with open-eyed visuals has. In the room it was like a circus with all these carnival rides and these beings (almost like the beings I’ve seen on Salvia), that were so excited that I was there with them. They were just so happy and were more than welcoming, ecstatic to be showing me around this strange yet familiar place. I zoom through all these rides seeing all these “people” more like spirits of people laughing and enjoying themselves, and I feel as if everything is energy, pure positive energy. That energy is what’s creating everything before me. When instantly, I’m flowing through the universe seeing all these different stars and planets and galaxies. I stop and begin to hover in this weightless void, then the beings show me this chunk of a planet with this enormous oak tree on it floating in space, then it begins to disintegrate almost as if on fire. The beings tell me with this symbolism that humans are destroying the planet and we need to work together in harmony with the planet for the better good of everything and to keep a peaceful balance. Then I’m off again flowing through the universe. The beings take me to this blue planet that looks just like earth only more like if earth had never been touched by man’s cruel hands. We went in for a closer look flying over the planet looking at all the different plants and creatures that inhabited it. The planet was so earth like in design, it was more like a prehistoric earth, full of natures beauty, no buildings or structures of any kind. I visited this large swamp area with these huge dragonfly things that were more dragon than fly which was inhabited by these big salamander-slug looking things. They had a salamander body and head and slug eyes but no back legs. As I was looking at one, another one shot it’s tongue out and ate one of the dragonfly things. I was awed in amazement at how similar things were on this planet compared with Earth yet utterly unfamiliar. A lot of the creatures I seen on the planet looked more reptilian or amphibious than anything, though there were some other creatures that were kind of similar to some of the creatures on earth but completely different, some just crazy looking. There were these weird fluffy, flying creatures that reminded me of Atreyu from “The Never Ending Story” but they looked more bird-like. They were white and had furry, feathery wings and gigantic paws with sharp claws almost like you might picture a gryphon but not quite. Then I seen these furry caterpillar-like creatures that were big enough to ride on. They had white and golden fur that parted in the middle, black beady eyes, and black fur covering just their feet. They would waddle around being real playful with one another, almost like puppies. Enormous, fluffy, puppypillars! After I had stayed on the planet awhile I was lifted back through the atmosphere into space, the whole time I felt like I had accomplished so much. While floating there the beings told me that Earth could be peaceful like that planet if we all worked together to stop the destruction we are causing by using the planet, and not abusing it. People need to focus their minds on the positive aspects of everything, we should appreciate everything down to a single breath of air we take because just the fact that we exist is a gift. Life isn’t about the things you possess, it’s about the experience. The DMT began to wear off and as it did the universe wrapped itself in a sphere and whirled away. I opened my eyes and felt like I was an entirely new person. I had truly experienced DMT, that was the kind of experience I had been looking forward to from the moment I heard of Ayahuasca. I mean that was definitely a WOW experience, a true mind blowing, consciousness expanding, outer body, otherworldly, psychedelic journey. I have experienced many mind altering substances in my life but nothing and I mean NOTHING even comes close in comparison to a full blow DMT trip. I will definitely be traveling to that place in my mind again. As for A’s trip, he experienced light visuals the entire night but said he had a very religious experience with God, finally understanding the true meaning of love and how love is pure positive energy and that energy is God. I’m really glad he finally got to trip on DMT, he has opened his mind to infinite possibilities and said he will definitely be doing Ayahuasca again.

我有一个漫长的工作周. 我已经计划了很长一段时间试用iprocin, 这里是我第一次用iprocin的经验的简要描述. 在囊中吞下5mg的4- ho- dipt. 去杂货店买了一些果汁,然后不久就去H的家. 在一个囊中吞下10mg. 第一个警报被注意到,一些提高的意识. 我的子很紧,否则很好. 时间17点30分, 起跑非常顺利, 没有太多的视觉效果,地板像厚厚的液体一样流动, 视觉感知有轻微的改变, 我很昏迷, 可能是因为前一晚没有睡觉. 伊早上3点起床, 我们直到5点才让她睡觉, 没有任何形式的身体推力. 气氛柔和轻松, 类似于之旅. 我试图引导讨论到一些哲学问题, 我主要躺在沙发上听音乐. 经过一段时间,又出现了一些朋友. 我注意到我比吸草的朋友多说话. 我倾向于倾听他们的谈话, 这可能会成为更有趣的体验. 另外,如果多吃几毫克也不会有什么问题. 19.35我前往公共汽车. 晴朗的天气让我有点醒来. 坐在公共汽车上, 我开始看到伊普罗辛的深处. 我到达城镇, 在那里走路时, 看着沿着H街的树木, 我无法用言语表达这些想法, 我意识到树木和城市环境形成了一个大生态系统, 人类是其中的一部分. 由于某种原因, 树木总是很美丽, 我觉得这可能是一个深层次的物质重量. 虽然在城市里一切看起来很正常, 我却觉得自己与其他流浪者不同, 这是否是社会中"独一无二"的迷幻版本? 没有答案. 想知道有多少路人见过这种迷幻灯光. 他们中有多少人是开明的? 我是不是有点高贵? 我认为那些没有见过这种迷幻"光"的人, 他们是吗? 这是我生命中不可思议的一件事. 我在想自己多年来经历的经历. 我在生活中经历了许多重要而又独特的事情, 吃饭,睡觉,听音乐,恋爱,生气,看到不同的文化和国家, 酒精也会有这种作用, 是的, 许多其他东西也会这样, 咖啡也能做到这一点. 这只是非常不同的东西. 如果我从未经历过迷幻精神状态, 我现在的感受. ,也许我最好回家. 半小时后, 我乘火车回家. 我几乎没有注意到任何东西. 伊普罗辛已经离开了大楼. 后果: 想到什么? 显然剂量很小,我远没有被'浪费',但仍然... 这显然是一个扩大大脑的物质, 现在我毫不怀疑, 伊普罗辛在更高的剂量下不会是一个深刻的体验. 其他: 4-HO-DiPT (281):小组 (2-9) (17),第一次 (2),一般 (1)

Soft Start 4-Ho-DiPT by Anjovis Citation:   Anjovis. "Soft Start: An Experience with 4-Ho-DiPT (exp28626)". Erowid.org . Jul 5, 2004. erowid.org/exp/28626 DOSE: T+ 0:00 5.0 mg oral 4-HO-DiPT (capsule) T+ 0:17 10 mg oral 4-HO-DiPT (capsule) BODY WEIGHT: 83 kg I had a long working week behind. I had planned to try iprocin for quite a while and this day I had the opportunity. Here is a short description of my first experience with iprocin. 16.28 5mg of 4-ho-dipt swallowed in a capsule. Went to grocery to buy some juice and headed to H's place soon after. 16.45 10mg more swallowed in a capsule. 17.03 The first alert is noted, some heightening of awareness. My neck is a big tight, otherwise fine. 17.30 The start was very smooth, nearly unnoticable. Not much visuals, the floor is flowing like a thick liquid and slight alteration of visual perception, but nothing to awe at. I am very lethargic, which might be due to lack of sleep previous night. E had woken up 3am and we couldn't get her back sleep until after 5am, then I woke up at 8am to go to work today. No physical push of any kind. Thoughts are in motion, atmosphere is soft and easy, similar to mellow mushroom trip. I try to lead the discussion to some philosophical questions, but I am too lethargic to keep up the discussion. I mainly lay on the couch listening to music. After some time a few more friends appear. I notice that I am a lot less talkative than my friends smoking weed. I tend to just listen to their talking and think about some miscellaneous things flowing around my head. Less lack of sleep and this would propably be a lot more interesting experience. Also a few more milligrams wouldn't be bad either. 19.35 I head to the bus. Brisk weather wakes me up a little bit. While sitting in the bus I begin to see the depth of Iprocin. I arrive to town and while walking there, looking at the trees lining the H-street, I get some 'big thoughts' that seemed to come out of nowhere. Thoughts that I cannot express in words, but I will try anyway. I realized the trees and the urban enviroment create a large ecological system where people are part of it. For some reason, trees have always been magnificent and very inspiring to look at when I am in an altered state of mind. I feel that this could be a profound substance on heavier dose. Everything seems visually normal in town yet I sense as if I was a different species of all other wanderers going by, yet sharing a lot of genes with many of them. Is this a psychedelic version of 'uniqueness' in society? Couldn't answer that. I wonder how many of those passers-by have seen this psychedelic light. How many of them are enlightened? Am I being a bit elitish here? Thinking those that have not seen this psychedelic 'light' are somehow less in my eyes. Are they? This is a remarkable thing in my life. I think about my experiences through years. There are many important and very distinct things I have experienced in my life, eating, sleeping, hearing music, falling in love, getting angry, seeing different cultures and countries, having good friends, laughing, crying, being a father, having sex and .. experiencing psychedelia, altered state of consciousness. Alcohol does that too, yes, but that is like getting your field of vision shrinked and motorical coordination whacked. Many other things does that too, when I am sleepy or when I am having strong emotions. Coffee does that too. This is just something very different. I wouldn't be the same man, I would be less, if I had never experienced psychedelic state of mind. This very thing I am feeling now. Uh, perhaps I had better go home. After half on hour walking in town, I take the train back home. 20.45 I can barely notice anything anymore. 21.30 Iprocin has definitely left the building. Aftermath: What to think? Clearly the dose was small, I was far from being 'wasted', but still ... deep thoughts and easily provoked to any direction by a little effort. This is clearly a mind expanding substance, by the very definition of the word. I have no doubt now that Iprocin wouldn't be a profound experience on higher amounts.

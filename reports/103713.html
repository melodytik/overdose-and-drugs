品尝乙烯酸盐 基于其分子与著名的甲基酸 (Ritalin) 的相似性, 在我的国家完全合法,我能够从一个信誉好的来源获得合理数量的500毫克 (我对之前订购的产品感到满意). 首先我往往对未知物质非常谨慎, 所以我决定吸入10毫克的小量, 小10毫克线注入. 其他报道所描述的情况远远不及. 燃烧是存在的,虽然可以控制. 虽然我的鼻孔有点灼热, 我必须注意到我的心率几乎立即上升. 我心跳已经平静下来, 我认为这可以归因于安慰剂效应 但最初的效应迹象出现了. 我觉得自己更清醒了, 然而我的视力变得更加清晰和明亮. 感觉到某种东西, 虽然不是我所期望的. 没有什么不满足的欲望, 我只需要走路. 没有紧或竞赛的想法. 我的视力非常清晰, 走路感觉很好. 喉很干燥. 虽然我不确定它是否只是安慰剂. 这不是很激烈. 头脑非常清醒, 身体仍感受到影响. 燃烧一支香烟似乎是一个好主意, 我没有感觉到任何身体效应, 这绝对是结局, 什么也没发生. 我觉得自己已经昏迷了, 缺乏能量. 没有什么严重的,几乎在基线. 我准备了一条40毫克的脂肪线, T:0 吹进了线路. 燃烧相当强烈但我可以通过它. 眼睛又开始流水了 这就是它. 感觉和昨天一样. 视力更敏,颜色更清晰,更明亮. 音乐听起来很好, 没有, 心跳再次稍微升高 (谢天谢地). 现在我没有任何疑问这个产品是工作. 虽然不是我所期望的. 身体的感觉很强烈 (肌肉紧张,头部有压力), 但我的头脑清晰, 它们仍在上升. 现在是峰会, 我感觉很好. 我只是喜欢坐在公共汽车里, 看着周围的人. 我不能专注于一件事, 虽然我可以保持很好的对话. 瞳孔没有扩大. 他们的手掌非常出汗,感觉很冷. 现在,我们已经完成了. 我喜欢乘坐公共汽车, 只是看着道路, 有时我会感到焦虑. 只是几秒钟, 但它肯定存在, 提醒我大麻的坏旅行. 但我经历了很多,我可以处理它. 我认为它现在正在消退. 我不觉得很舒服. 发生事故? 也许有点焦虑爬行,冷汗和最后一点刺激. 非常不舒服. 时间1点 效果已经消失了. 我感到疲倦,有点焦虑,明亮的灯光只会让事情变得更糟. 我在阴影下点燃一支雪茄, 一会儿就变得更好了. 我不得不放下耳机, 听到人们是否在谈论我. 现在感觉好得多, 经历过一个小时后就变得不愉快. 在T 2:30的基线. 我心跳平静而稳定, 汗水消失, 我和朋友聊天感到舒服. 谢天谢地,这已经过去了! 我的判断: 我会给这个物质10分之5的评分. 它有很多积极的方面, 虽然很不舒服, 但也能轻松处理. 我认为它可能会变得更糟糕的大量饮食后! 我认为我可以尝试较小的剂量 (大约20毫克) 与酒精, 这让我想起了不好的街头可乐. 优点: 刺激 改善视力 快乐 (轻度) 只有轻微的心跳变化 瞳孔大小没有变化 没有紧 缺点: 简短的行动 突如其来的崩 偏执和焦虑可能 不太适合活动 不确定关于聚会 时间表: 103713 乙基 (563):一般 (1),第一次 (2),未知背景 (20)

Its completely legal in my country and I was able to obtain a reasonable amount – 500 mg from a reputable source (I was satisfied with previously ordered products). At first I tend to be very careful with unknown substances so I decided to insufflate a small 10 mg amount which I hoped would give me a taste of threshold effects. T:0 Little 10 mg line insufflated. Not nearly as bad as was described in another reports. The burning was present although manageable. For a minute my left eye was watery and my nostril was burning but nothing horrendous. I must note that my heartrate rose up almost INSTANTLY. T : 0:05 My heartbeat calmed down to just above average. I think I can attribute this to placebo effect. But first signs of effects appeared. I felt more awake, not unlike a strong coffee or a good sleep. However my vision got sharper and more bright. T:0:10 Feeling something definitely although not what I had expected. There is no unsatiable urge to do something, I am fine with just walking. No jaw clenching or racing thoughts. T:0:15 Hit the plateau. My vision is very clear and broad and walking feels fine. Quite dry throat. Some feelings are present although I am not sure if its not just placebo. Its not very intense. T:0:30 Peaking definitely. Very clear headed and still feeling the bodily effects. Lighting a cig seems like a great idea and of course it is pleasurable. T: 1:00 All has come to a sudden end in course of ten minutes. I dont feel any body effects and stimulation is already gone. T:1:30 This is definitely the end, nothing happens. I feel quite out of it and lack of energy. Nothing serious, almost at baseline. Next day I had more courage and prepared a fat 40 mg line to see if I can get any good out of it. T:0 Insufflated the line. Burning quite intense but I can get through it. Watery eyes again T 0:03 There it is. The same feeling as yesterday. Vision more sharp, colours lucid and bright. T 0:05 Intense mood lift. Music sounds great – not trippy, just more enjoyable and edgy. Heartbeat again only slightly elevated (thank god). T 0:10 Now I dont have any doubts this product is working. Although not as I expected. The rush is getting quite intense, bodily feelings are present (tight muscles, pressure in head) but my mind is clear and I am not losing train of thoughts. Still rising higher. T 0:15 This is peak ladies and gentlemen. I feel great. The high is more chill than I expected i just enjoy sitting in a bus, watching people around. I cant focus on one thing although I can keep conversation just fine. Pupils are not dilated. Palms very sweaty and they feel cold. T 0:30 The euphoric side is over. Now body stimulation is very present, I like this journey in the bus and doing nothing but watching the road is pleasurable. Sometimes I feel on the edge of anxiety. Just for a few seconds but its definitely present and reminds me of marijuana bad trips. But I experienced them a lot and can manage it. T 0:45 I think its subsiding now. I dont feel very comfortable. Crash?? Maybe – slight anxiety crawling, cold sweat and just last bits of stimulation. Quite uncomfortable. T 1:00 The effects are gone. Hints of paranoia creeping in, had to put down my headphones to hear if people are not talking about me. T 1:30 Feeling much better now, although still not at baseline. This experience got dysphoric past 1 hour mark. T 2:30 Baseline. Heart beat calm and regular, the sweats gon away and I feel comfortable chatting with a friend. Thank god its over so quick! My judgement : I would give this substance five points out of ten. It has positive sides, but also many negatives. Onset is quick, as are main effects and crash is manageable although not comfortable. I think it might get a lot worse after binging! I think I might try smaller doses (around 20 mg) with booze, it might be quite enjoyable. Reminds me of bad quality street coke a bit.

大约一个月前我接受了100毫克的TMA-2. 这是一个浅棕色/棕色的颜色. 首先我很担心,但在看到舒尔根合成的tma-2产生相同颜色后, 我在空腹时服用了50毫克. 开始是缓慢的,光滑的,和微妙的. 我开始有这样的感觉, 这种感觉持续了大部分的旅程. 我躺在沙发上, 开始有我有史以来最令人放松的幻觉体验. 我通常把这种效果与2cb联系在一起. 表面上的一切都看起来像有头发从上面脱落. 我开始注意到另一件事:我的视觉几乎完全没有色彩. 我已经习惯于让我沉浸在色彩世界里的迷幻药, 所以开始用黑色,白色和不同灰色的视觉效果是很新鲜的. 这就像看黑白电影. 旅行越来越激烈, 我越来越放松. 我不像以前那样在脑海中奔跑, 而是以自己的节奏向现实走向. 我很容易在经历的高峰时入睡. 它们与无色的2cb非常相似, 但也有两维的几何图案, 虽然我没有像应该那样闭着眼睛花费太多时间, 在旅途中, 我室友在黑暗的衣柜里给我做了一场灯光表演. 我失去了尺寸和比例的感觉. 现在他似乎离我很远, 接下来他就像一个巨人. 总的来说我很喜欢这个物质. 整个旅程大约持续了9到10个小时. 这是一种新奇的药物,与其他幻觉药物完全不同. 其他:

Mellow Peace TMA-2 by Electric Shaman Citation:   Electric Shaman. "Mellow Peace: An Experience with TMA-2 (exp43164)". Erowid.org . Aug 16, 2006. erowid.org/exp/43164 DOSE: 50 mg oral TMA-2 (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 160 lb Around a month ago I received 100mg of tma-2. It was a light brown/tan color. This concerned me at first, but after seeing that the results of Shulgan's synthesis of tma-2 produced the same color, I was reassured. I took 50mg on an empty stomach. The come on was slow, smooth, and subtle. I started to get a feeling like I was asleep and dreaming even though I was wide awake. This feeling persisted for most of the trip. I lay on a couch and started to have the most incredibly relaxing hallucinogenic experience I have ever had. First things started to look furry, an effect I usually associate with 2cb. Everything surface looked like it had hair coming off of it. Around then is when I started to notice something else: my visuals were almost completely colorless. I'm used to psychedelics immersing me in a world of color, so it was quite novel to start to have visuals in black, white, and varying shades of grey. It was like watching a black and white movie. As the trip became more intense, I became more and more relaxed. My mind did not race along like I am used to, rather it ambled along sideways to reality at it's own pace. I could have easily fallen asleep at the peak of the experience. OEVs where very similar to colorless 2cb, but there were also two dimensional geometric patterns that appeared to be around 5 feet in front of my face. While I didn't spend as much time with my eyes closed as I should have, I noted that CEVs where detailed and easy to get immersed in. At one point during the trip, my roommate gave me a light show in a dark closet. I lost all sense of size and proportion. One second he seem miles away, the next second he was a giant towering over me. Overall I enjoyed this substance a great deal. The entire trip lasted around 9 or 10 hours. It was very novel, quite different from most other hallucinogens.

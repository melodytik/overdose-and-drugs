Today I awoke at about 1:30 from a night of watching the first 4 episodes of Breaking Bad. Through the night I remember having very weird dreams that seemed extremely inspired by Breaking Bad. I've never in my life had dreams about meth or anything, and when I entered my kitchen I found an envelope with my name on it. Looking into methiopropamine, I knew it was what I ordered. Would compare to low dosage of amphetamines, about a 10mg adderall. 2:55PM - Decided to take 25mg orally via parachute, personally suck at taking pills and eating nasty chemicals and because of rushing myself to avoid the taste, I almost choked and coughed up some water, but instantly began to chug more and got the chemical down. Began to take a shower. 3:10PM - After getting out of the shower, I smoked a cigarette. Very comparable to smoking cigarettes on adderall or when typically stimulated, much more enjoyable. Productive thoughts of FINALLY rearranging and cleaning certain parts of my room (which I've been procrastinating on for weeks to months) come to mind. 3:20PM - Decide first step in the organization process is cleaning the sheets on my bed, and then making it because an unmade bed can be very easily distracting. Please note I never make my bed, much less even think about washing the sheets. I am very lazy with laundry usually. 3:30PM - Decide to take a gravity bong hit of high quality weed, and once again very comparable to smoking on adderall. An almost trippy effect, music sounds much better, but still very alert. 3:35PM - Start taking stuff off a bookshelf that I've been planning on getting rid of, and eventually realized that I needed to be documenting this feeling. 4:05PM - Starting this documentation at 3:45PM, I have written all of this in 20 minutes. 4:20PM - Pulled another gravity bong of weed, gotten a lot done, organized this random pile of shit that I've been meaning to do for over a month, and got rid of the shelf that I've been trying to do for the same amount of time. My dresser has been out front in the garage-type area for almost 2 months now and I've been meaning to do this so I could bring the dresser in here but I have not been caring enough. This drug mixed with weed is especially underrated. I've read so many bad reviews on it but to me it feels like a twin of amphetamines. Even smoking on it feels like smoking on amphetamines. 4:45PM - By this point it feels better than amphetamines. Much more clear and clean, and I have an interest in everything. I've been doing the amount of laundry I would do in a week, and all my thoughts are productive such as wanting to learn music, wanting to go on a gravity board ride, but I know that before any of that I should get my room straightened out, but it feels so clean that I didn't even finish my cigarette so I could rush in and document this. The downside of amphetamines and weed or sometimes amphetamines alone is that I get overfocused and can't decide what to do. It can also be a lot more jittery and just drug-feeling. Surprisingly, this research chemical barely even feels like a drug. I suppose I could compare it to vyvanse more than adderall. 5:15PM - Been going through the process of moving my dresser into my room, organized some drawers and bagged up some old clothes and threw stuff away. Pulled another gravity bong of weed at this time. (1 gravity bong of weed is about .05g btw) 5:30PM - All the drawers of the dresser are organized and in my room, now just have to move the dresser. Eating a bowl of cereal even though I'm not very hungry, just to make sure I don't forget. 5:35PM - Sprinkled 25mg onto my spoonful of cereal, very chemical tasting for about 1 second but then chugged down my milk and cereal, which quickly got rid of the taste.

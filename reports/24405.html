我终于尝试了一年来一直在吃的伏坎加种子. 我真的没想到会发生什么. 我磨碎了4克种子,大约60粒, 经过45分钟的时间, 没有任何迹象表明有任何影响, 虽然现在是凌晨12点半, 我也感到疲. 我睡得很好, 没有奇怪的梦或改变睡眠模式. 第二天我感到疲倦,心情很好. 我有点想起了蛇毒药的作用, 让我整天都感觉沉睡. 我下午2点左右服用了维科登10毫克, 到了晚上6点, 整个腹部和胸部都开始疼痛. 我感觉胸部很紧, 肩膀之间也很痛. 我非常疲和虚弱. 我在回家后经历了一次非常痛苦的情节, 在这个特殊的时间我感到有点发烧,与奇怪的想法通过我的头. 我只能躺下接受这些症状, 随着夜晚的推移, 我写的是在消费后的第二天早上. 整整33个小时后, 在第二天晚上睡了4到5个小时之后, 我仍然感到恶心. 症状主要是背部/胸部上部胃部的奇怪疼痛. 也许我应该在种子上进行提取. 我对这些种子作为可能的原体的看法是有疑问的. 我对这些种子的看法可能令人愉快的体质酒的怀疑. 鉴于我能找到的关于这些种子作为酒/药物的应用的信息很少, 我仍然有兴趣听到有关这些种子消费的其他报道, 我不会推我采取或没有采取的措施. 服用维科登可能会与中枢神经系统发生相互作用, 没有食物就会引起胃痛. 随着这些种子的来源, 我想知道有多少人已经吸收了? 对于那些发现这些种子有所用的人, 其他: 非洲语音 (49):第一次 (2), 宿醉 / 几天后 (46), 健康问题 (27), 孤独 (16)

Jun 11, 2003. erowid.org/exp/24405 DOSE: T+ 0:00 4.0 g oral Voacanga africana (seeds) T+ 0:45 4.0 g oral Voacanga africana (seeds) T+ 15:15 10 mg oral Hydrocodone (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 170 lb I finally got around to trying voacanga seeds that ive had for almost a year. Reading what few reports there are on the internet, I didnt really expect much. I ground 4 grams of seeds, about 60 or so seeds, and swallowed them with water. 45 minutes later, after no real indication of any effects, i swallowed 4 more grams. An hour later I felt mildly stimulated, although being 12:30 in the morning, I felt tired too. I slept fine with no strange dreams or alteration of sleep patterns discernable. The next day I felt tired, and in a good mood. I was reminded somewhat of rauwolfia serpentina's effects as far as a feeling of underlying sedation that carried throughout the day. A day trip into the mountains was the plan, and at around 2 pm I took a vicoden 10 mg for what seemed to be an ache between my shoulder blades. By 6 pm this ache had manifested itself throughout my stomach and chest area. My chest felt tight, and I had a horrible ache between my shoulder blades. I was very tired and weak. On returning home I experienced a very painful episode of what I can only describe as muscle contractions in my chest/back/stomach areas. During this particular time I felt somewhat feverish, with strange thoughts running through my head. Laying down and accepting these symptoms was all I could do although a trip to the hospital was in the back of my head. The symptoms somewhat subsided in intensity as the night wore on. I am writing this the morning after the morning after consumption. A full 33 hours later, after being able to sleep only 4-5 hours (on the second night) I still feel sick. The symptoms mostly being strange soreness in the back/chest upper stomache area. Perhaps I should have done an extraction on the seeds. My opinion of these seeds as possible entheogen is doubtful. My opinion of these seeds as possible enjoyable somatic inebrient doubtful. Given the low amount of information I have been able to find on the applications that these seeds have as inebrient/medicine, besides their use as an intermediate in the production of other compounds, my conclusion is to stay the hell away from them. I am still interested in hearing other reports on the consumption of these seeds, more out of morbid curiosity now. I would not recomend the steps I took or didnt take. Taking a vicoden could have interacted with the central nervous system depressant effects these seeds have been purported to cause. Grinding the seeds and eating them without food could cause stomache distress alone. With all the sources for these seeds out there I wonder how many others have imbibed? Hats off to anyone that has found a use for these seeds.

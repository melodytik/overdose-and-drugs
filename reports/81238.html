我每天只服用一小剂量 (10毫克). 我还服用60毫克的西塔洛普拉姆化物,400毫克的特拉佐 (仅用于睡眠),偶尔服用达沃塞特. 我没有注意到吸食大麻时的恶心, 很好的本地和强大的, 发生在我身上的事情是,我每次抽都会发作. 我眼睛, 肌肉失控. 这真是一个很好的感觉. 虽然我似乎无法戒毒, 这使我对大麻不满意. 我吃了5克好吃的, 我的女朋友吃了7克, 每个人都了,除了我. 除了血红眼睛之外, 没有任何常见的副作用 (眼睛水,恶心等). 我每月也会抽一两次, 我有类似的头像像吸烟大麻, 但更强烈, 不包括肌肉控制的损失, 现在已经持续了一年多了, 似乎没有任何不良影响. 我唯一的抱怨是,我不能得到真正高在任何东西. 已使用的证券 药品 - 阿里皮普拉 (422):回顾 / 摘要 (11),医疗用途 (47),组合 (3),各种 (28)

I also take 60 mg of citalopram hydrobromide, 400mg trazodone (to sleep only), with the occasional darvocet. I’ve noticed no nausea when smoking pot, good homegrown and strong, as some have reported while taking abilify. What happens to me is that I get a major head rush with every toke. My eyes cloud over and I lose muscle control momentarily. It’s really a nice feeling. I can’t seem to get stoned, though, and that’s turning me off on pot. At a mushroom party recently, I ate 5 g of good boomers, my gf had 7, and the other 6 people had 4 g or less each. Everyone tripped balls but me. I laughed quite a bit, and had none of my usual side effects (eyes watering, nausea, etc.) except for blood-red eyes. I also do a small amount of speed about twice a month (only about 250mg) by smoking it. I get a similar head rush as when I smoke pot but its more intense, doesn’t include the loss of muscle control, and does make me nauseous. These issues with my prescription drugs and recreational drugs began when I added the abilify to my regimen and have continued for just over a year now with seemingly no ill effects. My only complaint is that I can’t get really high on anything anymore.

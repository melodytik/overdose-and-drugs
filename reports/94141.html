Dec 5, 2015. erowid.org/exp/94141 DOSE: 200 mg oral 5-MeO-DALT (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 140 lb It was 2:45AM Sunday, December the 18th when I made up my mind to take a high dose of 5-MeO-DALT. The previous night, my wife, brother-in-law, and I had taken a dose of about 25mg with little effect. I was determined to have a remarkable experience. What I wasn’t prepared for was the state of Samadhi. I had not eaten for at least 7 hours prior to the ingestion of the powder, all except a glass of water. Carefully, I measured out 200mg on my digital scale. Believe me, I was so nervous but confident that nothing would go wrong. Quickly, I poured the powder into my mouth. It was 2:55AM when it entered my body. For those who have not tasted it, it has an unpleasant metallic taste. Within the next 5 minutes, I made it back to my bed where my wife was already partially asleep. I kept waking her up getting in and out of bed. I say to her that I had just taken 200mg and that whatever happened within the next 3 hours or so to not call an ambulance or panic. Of course she was worried, but I assure her to calm down and just try to go back to sleep. It is about 3:10 when I start to feel a heaviness over my body and somewhat of a relaxation. I had also my headphones on listening to soft meditation music I found on a Pandora Radio station. No more than 10 minutes later I start to hear the music more pronounced and as if time were slowing down slightly. My legs start to tremble, followed by my hands, until it was throughout my whole body. I made a conscious effort to stop and it did momentarily. I looked at my hands as I lay in bed and notice a glow from my body. I remember my wedding ring shining as well. ( It is silver and very dull) “WOW,” I say to myself. At this point my wife wakes up from the shaking and asks if I am ok. I proceed to tell her I am ok and not to worry. I finally tell myself to just relax and let go. The shaking persist until I finally lose consciousness of my surroundings and enter a dreamlike state. It was no more than an hour since I had downed the powder. In my vision there is a moment of confusion that seemed to last about 30 minutes but very little panic or fear. I can remember in my mind continuing to say to myself, “just let go.” I see what appears to be the entire universe unfold in front of me and very quickly shrink until there was pure darkness. At that moment, I thought I was dead. I had no senses for these few seconds. Finally, I see an explosion of white light consuming me as well, followed by the sound of a chime. At the same time, this moment seemed to last for an eternity. I remember being overwhelmed by bliss and feeling as if I was with GOD himself. “Again, this moment seemed to have lasted a lifetime and as the dreamlike state faded, the bliss continued to increase. “OH MY GOD!!! OH MY GOD!!!” I must have shouted it over and over about 50 times or more but out of joy and NOT fear. My whole body was relieved from worry and stress and zero tension in my body. I was bawling my eyes out shouting to my wife, “WE MADE IT! ENLIGHTENMENT!” It was what first came to mind. I felt like a completely different person filled with joy and excitement. I later realized that my wife had no idea what I was shouting for and that I was the only one who went on the journey. She was actually scared out of her mind until I stopped shouting. What I remember the clearest is the state of bliss that I had reached and total relief and relaxation.

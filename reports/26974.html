这是一个关于人们变得非常扭曲的故事. 我已经在另一个报告中给出了我的背景,但我会再次过来:大麻,,酸,Mdma,Meth,2CB,Salvia,Nitrous,一些真正可怕的可乐. 报告中最有趣的部分可能是BZP, 在我来自新西兰的地方, 它是合法的, 但其中也含有BZP, 对严的实验者来说, 我认为很难找到比这更好的场景. 它们位于高的山中, 我和我的朋友们立刻感到欢迎. 这将是我第四次在E和我的第二个糖果翻转,但我很快发现我这次得到的装备远远优于那些我刚才提到的时间. 我心中充满了紧张的兴奋, 我们用大量的来消磨时间, 但我的一些朋友已经不耐烦了, 他们去买了一些, 我认为每片药物含有50毫克BZP或75毫克. 这使我怀疑它们是50. 我朋友G,P和D每人吃了四粒药. 我另一个朋友 (P也) 吃了两个. 一个半小时后, 我曾经尝试过"出埃及记",那些患有TMFPP的人, 我尽力警告他们我经历过的许多负面副作用 (极度关,肠道收缩和食欲持续下降), 这几乎和滚球一样好. 我们都坐在阳光下慢慢消逝, 在大夜前享受最后的几分钟的放松. 我有一个气球, 这证实它是一个很好的半沉, 一些非常有趣的视觉已经发生. 另外,因为Euphoria有四个包装, 我朋友P2有几个备用, 到了夜晚 (T 1:45) 我充满了钱, 预计E真的会做生意. 倒计时是个反高潮, 可能是因为我们还没有放下. 我们想尽可能地坚持下去, 但在凌晨1点, 我们再也忍不住了 (T3:30) 并放弃了. 我们走了一会儿,消磨时间,很快我感到非常奇怪. 我不知道它发生了什么时候, 但在某个阶段我突然意识到我不再在地球上. 我不是在比喻地说, 我认为它是火星或其他东西. 我觉得自己完全迷失了, 时间扭曲是令人难以置信的. 几秒钟看起来像几个小时. 我,P1和G终于到了部落帐, 试图跳舞一会儿, 我们做出了明智的决定, 回到了基地营, 我朋友P1有另一种药,叫做X档案, 他确信他看到外星人. 既然我在火星, 那么这对我来说是有道理的. 让所有硬的人都带着闪光棒跑来跑去, 我开始狂热地谈论进化, (我一直在听很多比尔·希克斯) 我们抬头看着星星, 我们坐了大约两个小时, 直到我们的卷变得有点可控. 我们走到主舞台看我们来找的是什么, 一个来自48 Sonic和Riddle的DnB集. 看到像我们一样醉的人, 站在一片野外的光中, 演出非常精彩, 他们演出了非常恰当的"晨光"由康科德黎明. 之后我们回到基地, 在我的帐里有一些No'我一直保存. 我有个气球, 这时我发现自己走在无限的走廊里, 我可以同时看到前面和后面! 这很奇怪. 其他人都在外面抽着一支含有两种大麻和大量大麻 (没有烟草, 但它还是像雪茄一样大). 我们正在参与其中时, 我经历了一件奇怪而神秘的经历. 一个小孩子 (四五岁) 走到我们身边. 我立刻对她产生了同情心. 她告诉我她是如何失去她的朋友, 虽然她看起来不生气或关注. 我看着大麻烟雾在她脸上的巨大的云层中漂浮, 我希望她不在那里,但我知道她有某种信息给我. 虽然我无法解释这个隐藏的信息. 这就像一个梦, 我知道当我醒来时我会忘记. 我现在下来,所以我寻求舒适的帐. 不幸的是,看起来有十几种或更多的白尾蜘蛛 (有毒). 这不是我的想象! 我决定睡在外面的地毯上, 我还能感觉到身体中的化学物质. 我们决定换个场景, 我和最亲密的朋友们躺在星空下, 我觉得在这个疯狂的星球上是个真正的幸运, 其他: 酒 (101),LSD (2),MDMA (3):组合 (3),烂的体验 (4),狂欢/舞蹈活动 (18)

Resolutions LSD, BZP, MDMA (Ecstasy), Nitrous Oxide & Cannabis by Metalhead Citation:   Metalhead. "Resolutions: An Experience with LSD, BZP, MDMA (Ecstasy), Nitrous Oxide & Cannabis (exp26974)". Aug 5, 2005. erowid.org/exp/26974 DOSE: T+ 0:00 smoked Cannabis (plant material) T+ 0:00 0.5 hits oral LSD (blotter / tab) T+ 0:45 inhaled Nitrous Oxide (gas) T+ 0:45 2 tablets oral BZP T+ 3:30 1 tablet oral MDMA BODY WEIGHT: 70 kg This is a yarn about people getting really, really twisted. I've given my background in another report but Ill skim through again: Pot, Shrooms, Acid, Mdma, Meth, 2CB, Salvia, Nitrous, some real shitty coke. The most interesting part of this report is probably going to be the BZP, which where I come from (New Zealand) is legal and is sold under the brand names Frenzy, Euphoria and Nemi. TMFPP, which is another form of Piperazine is sold as Exodus, but there is BZP in these as well, not very helpful for serious experimenters. The setting was at a New Years eve outdoor Dance party, and a better setting would be hard to find in my opinion. The various areas and campsites were nestled amongst tall hills, a beatiful and unspoiled natural environment. The vibes were amazing, the weather was sunny and my friends and I immediately felt welcome. This was to be my fourth time on E and my second candyflip, but I was soon to find out that the gear I'd got this time was far, far superior to those times I've just mentioned. I was filled with nervous exitement, but being among a large group of friends who I'd been tight with for ages meant that there was nothing approaching a negative thought in my mind. We broke out the pot in large amounts to pass the time but some of my friends were getting impatient and had heard there were some legal pills for sale that had an effect similar to speed. They went to obtain some of these while I continued to chill. The ones they got were Euphoria which I think had 50mg BZP per pill or maybe 75. Frenzy certainly have 75mg, and are stronger which makes me suspect they were 50. After being responsibly(!) informed by the supplier that they would go well with an E my friends G, P and D each took four of these pills. My other friend (P also) took two. An hour and a half later they were all visibly mashed. I had tried Exodus, the ones with TMFPP, before, and did my best to warn them about the many negative side effects I had experienced (extreme jaw clamping, contracted gut and extended loss of appetite) but everyone just saw me as a spoilsport and didn't pay any attention. P1 (one of the dudes on four) said that it was almost as good as a roll. At this point I dropped my half Acid blotter (T 0:00) and we all sat around in the slowly fading sun, enjoying the last few moments of relaxation before the big night ahead. As my acid kicked in (T 0:45) I had a balloon and this confirmed that it was a good half dip, some very interesting visuals were occuring already. Also, since Euphoria came in four packs my mate P2 had a couple spare, so I thought what the hey and joined the others in partaking of this strange chemical. By nightfall (T 1:45) I was well charged and anticipating the E to really do the business. The countdown was a bit of an anticlimax, probably because none of us had dropped our eccies yet. We wanted to hold out as long as possible, but at one am we could no longer resist (T3:30) and dropped. We walked around for a while, to pass the time and soon I was feeling extremely strange. I have no idea when it happened but at some stage I suddenly realised that I was no longer on Earth. I don't mean this metaphorically at all, I was literally on another planet. I think it was Mars or something. I felt like I was completely lost, but in a good way. The time distortion was just unbelievable. Seconds seemed like hours. Me, P1 and G ended up at the Tribal tent and tried to dance for a while but then we looked at each other and came to the realisation that we were seriously, seriously twisted. We made the wise decision to head back to base camp and have bizarre, in-depth discussions about nothing in particular. My mate P1 had a different pill to us called an X-files and he was convinced that he was seeing aliens. Since I was on Mars, this made sense to me at the time. It helped that all the hard-trancers were running around with glow sticks. I started raving on about evolution, (I had been listening to a lot of Bill Hicks) and we looked up at the stars which were bright in a way that you only get in the middle of the bush. We sat there for about two hours, until our rolls became a bit more manageable. As the first light appeared, we headed out to the main stage to see what we had come for, a DnB set from 48 Sonic and Riddle. It was kinda weird seeing a whole lot of people as wasted as us in the light in the middle of a field. The set was awesome, they spun the hugely appropriate 'Morning Light' by Concord Dawn. After this we went back to base, where in my tent were a few Nos' I had been saving. I had a balloon, and this was when I found myself in a corridor of infinity, that was marked by web-like electric blue patterns. I could see in front of me and behind me at the same time! Weird. Outside, the others were smoking a blunt that had two types of weed and generous amounts of hash in it (no tobacco, but it was still as big as a cigar). As we were partaking of this I had a bizarre and mystical experience. A small child (four or five yrs old) had wandered over to our group. I immediately felt empathy towards her. She was telling me something about how she had lost her friend although she didn't seem upset or concerned. I felt a queasy sense of guilt as I watched the hash smoke waft in huge clouds around her face. I wished to god that she wasn't there, but knew she had some kind of message for me. I couldn't interpret this hidden message though. It was just like a dream that I knew I'd forget when I woke up. I was coming down now, so I sought the comfort of my tent. Unfortunately, so had what seemed like a dozen or more white tailed spiders (poisonous). I wasn't imagining this! I decided to sleep outside on a rug in the sun instead. The next day I could still feel the chemicals in my system. We decided on a change of scene and went to stay in Hahei the next night. A few tokes of Pot brought me a sense of warm well-being and as I lay under the stars with my closest friends I felt truly blessed to be on this crazy planet, no matter how fleeting and strange my experience of it has been and will be.

12.8 个月 用于半片 (500ug) 25C-NBOH,口服. 23:30 (时间+20分钟) 目前还没有任何效果. 我还在上唇和牙之间. 演奏一些钢琴,即兴一些想法. 我认为这次旅行我会留下记录按的持续时间. 23:40 (时间+30分钟) 我开始感觉到身体上的影响. 我头部背部有很重的感觉, 虽然没有什么重要. 00:00 (T+50分钟) 仍然没有注意到任何迷幻效应或视觉效果. 我完成了录音,然后打开主灯. 事物确实看起来更清晰,更充满活力. 在部/肩部仍然有点不舒服. 00:10 (T+1小时) 吞下了这张纸, 现在注意到一些视觉障碍. 我会继续演奏钢琴, 看看它对听力有什么影响. 00:30 (T+1小时20分钟) 听觉效果比视觉效果更重要. 钢琴的速度减慢和加快对周围环境和音调有明显影响. 玩得更快似乎会让音调更清晰,反之亦然. 这是一个很有趣的玩. 01:10 (T+2小时) 我还觉得很恶心, 没有太多明显的视觉效果, 但有一些更广泛的酸性感觉, 我也注意到我走路时的运动协调有所不稳定. 这可能是由于""在周围在一个过于有意识的尝试安静 (家里每个人都睡着了). 我喜欢看猫玩, 但令人惊的是, 我不知道是不是因为手部的血管收缩 (与室外寒冷的温度相结合), 这只是纯粹的猜测, 但我听说这些化合物可能导致血管收缩. 02:10 (T+3小时) 我回到了我的房间, 听音乐, 我对CEV感到惊, 由于开眼和精神效应几乎没有被注意到, 现在恶心已经消失了. 我觉得自己完全清醒了. 没有明显的后果. 只是放松, 听音乐和在线聊天. 一个令人愉快但相当平静的剂量,所有说. 总结 总的来说,这种剂量太低, 无法产生显著的迷幻效应. 让我感觉很开放和社交 (这对我来说很罕见在迷幻化学品 - 我通常更喜欢坚持独自体验它们). 我可以想象500ug剂量可以很有趣的出去或与一些朋友. 但对于所期望的迷幻效果, 下一次 (一两周后避免耐受性) 我会服用750ug. 时间表: 101803 发布时间:2016年9月4日 25C-NBOH (763):音乐讨论 (22),第一次 (2),一个人 (16)

Sep 4, 2016. erowid.org/exp/101803 DOSE: T+ 2:00 smoked Tobacco T+ 0:00 500 ug buccal 25C-NBOH (blotter / tab) BODY WEIGHT: 12.8 st 23:10 Administered half-tab (500ug) 25C-NBOH, sub-lingual. 23:30 (T+20mins) Not feeling any effects yet. Still have tab between top lip and gum. Playing some piano, just improvising some ideas. I think for this trip I will leave the record button on for the duration. 23:40 (T+30mins) Starting to feel the physical effects a little. The back of my head feels heavy and there is a tiny amount of nausea. Nothing significant though. 00:00 (T+50mins) Still didn't notice any psychedelic effects or visuals. I finished recording what I was playing and turned the main lights on. Things do seem sharper and more vibrant. Still feeling a little bit uncomfortable in the neck/shoulders. 00:10 (T+1hr) Swallowed the blotter, now noticing some visual disturbances. Will continue playing piano to see what auditory effects it has. 00:30 (T+1hr20mins) Auditory effects are significant, compared to the visuals. Slowing down and speeding up on the piano has noticeable effects on the surroundings as well as on pitch. Playing faster seems to make the tone feel sharper, and same to the contrary. This is a lot of fun to play with. 01:10 (T+2hrs) Still feeling quite nauseous so went to get some tea and have a smoke (tobacco) outside. There aren't a whole lot of obvious visuals but there are some broader, acid-like feelings which I could imagine really getting absorbed with on higher doses. I'm also noticing some unsteadiness with motor coordination when I walk. This could be due to 'trip-toeing' around in an over-conscious attempt to be quiet (everyone in the house is asleep). I enjoyed watching my cats play but, surprisingly, didn't get much pleasure from stroking them. I'm wondering if it's because of some mild vasoconstriction in the hands (combined with the cold outdoor temperature), desensitising them physically. This is pure speculation, but I have heard that vasoconstriction is possible with these compounds. 02:10 (T+3hrs) After this point I went back to my room and listened to some music, which was nice to get lost in. I was surprised by the CEVs which were unexpectedly strong given that open eye and mental effects were almost unnoticeable. Nausea was pretty much gone by now. Within another hour or so I felt completely sober. No noticeable after-effects. Just chilled, listening to music and chatting to friends online. A pleasant but quite uneventful dose, all told. Summary All in all this dosage was too low to get any significant psychedelic effects. However it was pleasant, and did make me feel quite open and sociable (which is rare for me on psychedelic chems - I generally prefer to stick to experience them alone). I could imagine that a 500ug dose can be quite fun to go out with or take with some friends. But for desired psychedelic effects it really isn't enough. Next time (after a week or two to avoid tolerance) I will take 750ug.

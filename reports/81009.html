May 1, 2019. erowid.org/exp/81009 DOSE: 200 mg smoked Chamomile (dried) BODY WEIGHT: 200 lb Cozy Chamomile - For Quiet Moments While at Hemp Fest 2009 in Seattle Washington I saw a list of herbs to vaporize for various reasons, such as cough, relaxation, energy, and so on. Chamomile was on the list. I had forgotten all about it but the other day I remembered while looking in at my tea collection one night. I didn’t have any Chamomile tea so I went down to the super market and bought a box for $3 dollars. Before smoking this I had eaten. I drank nothing but water all that day and had consumed no alcohol. I tore open a tea bag and I loaded up my sweet potato pipe. I’m guessing I used about 2/10ths of a gram or so but had no way to measure it. I do not have a vaporizer but that would be my first choice, bong being my 2nd and fruits or veggie pipes being the third. I took a big hit from the sweet potato, I’m not much of a smoker but it was relatively smooth and had a pleasant taste. I took 2 or 3 more hits till the bowl was kicked and was very pleased with the flavor. Within a few moments I felt very relaxed and calm. It made my room smell nice also. I went to bed shortly after so I’m not sure how long the effect lasts but I do believe that I’ll be smoking this stuff every night now that I’ve tried it. I may even go out and get a bong for it. I’ll try it during the day sometime and time the effects. ---- Had one piece of toast with honey on whole wheat bread for breakfast and nothing else but water until the time of the experiment, 2:50 PM. Smoked about 2/10ths of a gram from a pipe that I made out of a sweet potato. Effects took place nearly immediately. I felt calm, chilled out, I’d almost say similar in some ways to 420. Smooth smoke with a good flavor and pleasant aftertaste. Felt high. I did some work on the computer after smoking so not a lot of movement or activity on my part. The “high” lasted for about 1 hr and 20 minutes. Peaked in under 5 minutes and effects started to wear off after about 1 hour. I plan to smoke it often. Tonight on a full stomach I just smoked 3 bowls from a pipe I made from a squash and I’m feeling really good right now.

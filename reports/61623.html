美国 我也曾经经历过一些紧张的时刻, 我常常处于恐惧的边缘, 今天我将揭露我与精神分裂症患者17岁的妹妹 (目前没有服用药物) 的遭遇. 开始像往常一样, 向我大喊大叫, 激起我内心的恶魔. 让我发出愤怒需要很多压力, 我几乎打了她一拳 (我的力量和重量,我很容易打破她的下巴...) 但是我还是控制住了自己. 我退到我的房间,并决定拥抱化学 这就是我的救世主, 称为布罗马泽. 我把6毫克放在我舌头下,决定用硬的声音来掩盖尖叫的尖叫声, 涌动的水. 随着我爬进的的避风港, 我感到一种平静的感觉扫除负能量 像在一块尘土的地毯上的吸尘器一样. 我不知道为什么我甚至费心关心这样的微不足道的事情 我怎么会考虑使用暴力来制止这种愚蠢的行为. 我用山羊肥 (敏感皮肤) 擦拭身体的每一英寸, 在宁静的水圣所 我觉得很累,这是一个相当长的一天,我进入我的第14个小时 没有任何的清醒. 在玩了20分钟的"魔兽世界3:冷王位"之后, 我觉得今晚要休息一下. 我决定步行5分钟到一个废弃的房子, 两个寒冷的加拿大冬天, 和我疯狂的精神分裂症妹妹. 总的来说,这是我最好的帮助. 它的价值往往被低估,因为我被护士们多次责备 如何在长期内不值得服用这些药物. 我真的不同意,因为在SSRI的我已经无法感受到任何愤怒, 没有理由. 似乎限制了我真正快乐的能力, 我对情感的清晰度, 更不用说我当时的药物 (Effexor XR) 比我现在的药物要贵得多. 我在停止服用时没有出现戒断症状 (lorazepam/ diazepam 往往会导致戒断症状). 让我生气,当我的剂量被削减). 其他: 发布时间:2018年6月20日

Jun 20, 2018. erowid.org/exp/61623 DOSE: 6 mg sublingual Bromazepam (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 195 lb I've had my share of tense moments, witnessing a murder (by my best friend, no less)..and the aftermath. So naturally I'm normally always on the edge of a pool of fear with clouds of frustration swirling overhead. For today I'll disclose an encounter with my schizophrenic 17 year old sister (not currently on medication). It started as it usually does, screaming accusations at me...stirring an immense demon within me. Something thats as dangerous as it is stubborn, it takes a LOT of stress to send me into a rage, I very nearly punched her (with my strength and weight, I'd quite easily have shattered her jaw...) but managed to contain myself. I withdrew to my room and made the decision to embrace the chemical agent that is my personal savior, that known as bromazepam. T:00:00 Put 6 mg under my tongue, decided to drown out the piercing screams with the sound of hard, rushing water. T:00:02 As I climbed into the grimy haven of rust, I felt a calm feeling sweep the negative energy from my mind like a vacuum on a dusty carpet. T:00:10 I'm wondering why I even bother to care about such trivial matters as the ravings of a complete lunatic, and how I could even consider resorting to violence to silence such idiocy. T:00:30 I finish wiping every inch of my body with goat soap (sensitive skin), and end my visit to the watery shrine of tranquility T:1:00 I'm feeling quite tired, its been somewhat of a long day and I'm entering my 14th hour of wakefullness. T:1:20 after 20 minutes of playing Warcraft 3:The Frozen Throne I feel like retiring for the night and decide to begin the 5 minute walk to a derelect house that I occasionally take refuge in from both the cold canadian winter, and my crazed schizo sister. All in all, this is the best aid I have. Its value is oft underestimated, for I've been chided more than once by nurses (who told me I'd be better off on anti-depressants) and how this medication isn't worth taking in the long term. I truly beg to differ as with SSRI's I've been unable to feel any anger, to the extent that I became unrealistic in my reasoning. And in addition there seemed to be a cap on my ability to be genuinely happy, like it had set barriers to my emotional clarity, and not to mention what I was on (Effexor XR) was FAR more expensive than what I'm currently on. Unlike some benzodiazepines I experience no withdrawal symptoms when I halt my dosage (lorazepam/diazepam tended to make me angry when my dose was cut).

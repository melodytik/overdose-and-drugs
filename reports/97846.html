a-PVP来自波兰,呈现为白色 (几乎棕色) 粉末. 我舌头上的一小部分化学物质证明是一种强烈的麻木剂, 两者都不愉快. 一个2mg线被注入. 十分钟后我的心跳开始加快, 我有更多的精力. 我很高兴看到这部电影, 并且很高兴看到这部电影. 但绝对不能平衡药物引起的焦虑和心率高. 我在30分钟的时间重新注射了. 另一个2-5毫克吸入. 感觉有点高兴,有点性冲动, 但远远不及MDPV, 心率也很快, 药物闻起来很像精子. 我非常怀疑一个人能辨别出它们是吸入精子还是粉和a-PVP的溶液. 像MDPV一样,a-PVP可以被标记为心率XR或焦虑XR. 没有价值我的时间当奇迹药物如4-胺存在. 时间表: 97846 阿尔法-PVP (585):一个人 (16),不好的旅行 (6)

Nov 19, 2012. erowid.org/exp/97846 DOSE: T+ 0:00 2 mg insufflated alpha-PVP (powder / crystals) T+ 0:30 3 mg insufflated alpha-PVP (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 170 lb a-PVP came from Poland as off-white (almost brownish) powder. An small amount on my tongue proved the chemical to be an intense numbing agent with a sharp sour taste. Neither was pleasant. A 2mg line was insufflated. Within 10 minutes my heart was beating a little faster and I had more energy. Within 15 minutes I wondered where the euphoria I know and love is but remembered this wasn't MDPV. 20 minutes in the euphoria was somewhat there but definitely not balancing the anxiety and tachycardia (high heart rate) the drug was causing. I redosed at the 30 minute mark. Another 2-5mg insufflated. A little more euphoria and slight sexual urges but nowhere near MDPV, and the rapid heart rate was so bad I couldn't enjoy the little positive effects this drug has. Insufflated, the drug smelled very much like sperm. I highly doubt one would be able to discern whether they are insufflating sperm or a solution of starch and a-PVP. Like MDPV, a-PVP can be branded as Tachycardia XR or Anxiety XR. Not worth my time when wonder drugs like 4-Fluoroamphetamine exist.

我已经爱上了安非他家族, 尝试了几种, 我开始了一天, 买了100毫克的阿德拉尔, 我和我的男朋友M每天早上11点左右服用100毫克, 我坐在家里直到晚上七点左右听音乐, 思考药物带来的体验和开启思想的特质. 晚上7点左右, 我朋友打电话给我说:', 我们有药, 今晚要去聚会. 我们 (我的亲密朋友:我,A,S,B) 坐在那里拍摄这些东西, 虽然我不知道这种组合, 我没有预期会有任何问题. 我开始感到奇怪的身体"峰值", 开始在我的腿部, 经过一个小时的不断增加的冲击, 我开始失去控制, 我和S决定我应该喝一些水,躺下. 开始变为全面的身体发作, 随着夜晚的推移, 发作与同时的高峰变得越来越强烈; 我变得越来越不连贯, 唯一能让我活在这个世界里的是船员们不断的爱和鼓励. 房间里充满了充满爱与宽慰的气氛. 我在上午10点左右才完全停止感觉到高峰. 唯一能让我度过这夜的, 是经验丰富的船员们的支持, 他们知道如何处理这种情况; 我看了几个网站, 没有看到任何其他类型的报告与这种化学品的组合. 我觉得我应该警告其他人这种危险. 我意识到我正在使用一个口腔吸入器来帮助我在每两个小时的时间间隔内定期吸入三到四次呼吸. 快乐的旅行,安全! 时间表: 21489 MDMA (3), 胺类药物 (6), 药物 - 醇 (241):小组 (2-9) (17), 健康问题 (27), 组合 (3)

Hours of Convulsions Amphetamine (Adderall), MDMA & Albuterol by Egor Citation:   Egor. "Hours of Convulsions: An Experience with Amphetamine (Adderall), MDMA & Albuterol (exp21489)". Erowid.org . Feb 21, 2003. erowid.org/exp/21489 DOSE: T+ 0:00 100 mg oral Amphetamines (pill / tablet) T+ 10:00 1 tablet oral MDMA T+ 0:00 repeated inhaled Pharms - Albuterol BODY WEIGHT: 200 lb At the time of this experiance I had fallen in love with with the amphetamine family and experimented with several, and this was my only problem The day started with me procuring 100 mg aderall under the name A-D; or 'after death'. Me and my guy M took 100mg each at around 11am and spent most of the day contemplating life, religin, and reality. At about about 4pm, M had to go run some erands and I sat at home until about 7pm listening to music and contemplating the experiance and the mind opening quality of the drug. Around 7, my close friend I called me ans said 'hey, we got some pills and we were going to party tonight.' I drank some gatorade, ate a little rice to pad me stomach and was on my way. We (my close group of friends: I,A,S, and B sat around shooting the shit and just catching up until around 9pm when we decided to dose. I was mostly down from the aderall when I dosed, so I did not expect any problems; though I did not know of the combo. Within 30 min I began to odd physical 'peaks', similar to cns surges, but with more of an electrical shock feel to them. The peaks started mostly in my legs, but quickly spread up my body while also increasing in intensity. After about an hour of the increasing surges, I began to lose control of them and my limbs began to shake and shudder. S and I decided I should drink some water and lay down. The peaks began to transform into full blown physical seizures, but at the time I remained conscious and talking. As the night went on, my seizures with concurrent peaks became more and more intense; and I became less coherent, though never fully losing consciousness. The only thing keeping my in this world was the constant words of love and encouragement from my crew. At around 5am I began to regain control of my body, and there was an intense air of love and relief in the room. I did not totally stop getting the peaks until almost 10am. The only thing that got me through the night was the support of an experianced crew who knew how to handle this type of situation; and I am sure that they are the only reason that I survived the night, and I am thankful to have been in their company that night. I looked on several sites and did not see any other reports of this type with this combination of chemicals. I felt I should warn others other of this danger. As an after thought, I realized I was using an albuteral inhaler to help deal with shortness of breath at regular intervals of three to four puffs about every two hours throughout the night. happy tripping, be safe!

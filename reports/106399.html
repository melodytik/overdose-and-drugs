这是我有史以来最疯狂的2C-P体验. 我已经用了40毫克的药物, 没有其他时间是如此疯狂. 我对乙胺非常有经验, 我会尽可能详细地描述, 所以让我们开始这次旅行报告吧! 我和我的朋友服用15毫克,我服用35毫克的黑色生酒, 我们都开始感觉到身体上的伤害, 背部的不舒服, 我开始看到一些轻微的视觉效果, 时间在慢慢慢慢, 我们在聊天, 唱着"A Tribe Called Quest"这首很棒的歌. 我真的推它用于. 晚上5点, 视频开始涌入, 我的朋友也开始, 但也少了. 晚上5点25分,我们的朋友来了. 我们无法与他们交谈, 因为我的视觉效果太强烈, 他们离开是因为我们无法与他们交谈, 视觉效果开始随着音乐流动, 我和朋友之间的对话也很顺利, 这一切都非常激烈, 但也令人惊叹的乐趣! 这时,事情开始变得太激烈了, 我失去了对视觉的全部视力, 我不断地忘记现实, 我以为我进入了一个平行宇宙, 忘记了一切, 晚上7点,我开始放弃了. 随着我从一个不同的现实进入另一个现实, 我失去了一切的感觉. 由于视觉效果太强, 我让我的朋友给我打包一碗 (烟草和大麻),当我吸烟时, 我回到现实生活中, 晚上7点25分 - - 实际上是25分钟, 感觉就像3个小时. 我已经完成了,我求帮助. 我吞下1.5毫克克洛纳佐拉姆等待旅行完成. 晚上7点50分-我不能再忍受了我求下来这次旅行. 这时我会因为酒而昏迷. 我朋友告诉我我直到12点左右,但我没有回忆. 总的来说,我不建议任何人使用这种药物, 因为这样一剂真正好的2C-P会让你的脑袋混乱, 你会失去视力. 我在旅途中有点疯狂, 因为我从来没有经历过这样的事情. 即使是40毫克的2C-P旅行. 玩得开心,小心小事! 其他: 2C-P (305), 克洛纳佐拉姆 (686):一般 (1), 艰难的经历 (5), 糟糕的旅行 (6), 小组 (2-9) (17)

A Trip Into Diffferent Realities 2C-P & Clonazolam by 2cpguys Citation:   2cpguys. "A Trip Into Diffferent Realities: An Experience with 2C-P & Clonazolam (exp106399)". Erowid.org . Jul 25, 2015. erowid.org/exp/106399 DOSE: T+ 0:00 35 mg oral 2C-P (powder / crystals) T+ 4:25 1.5 mg oral Clonazolam (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 125 lb This is single handedly the most insane 2C-P experience I have ever had. I've gone up to 40mg with this drug and no other time was as insane as this. I'm very experienced with phenethylamines and this blew me out of the water. I am going to try to go into as much detail as possible but some of the visuals I had are unexplainable with words. So let's get this trip report started! 3:00PM- Me and my friend dose, he takes 15mg and I take 35mg in some dark ginger ale, tastes a little off but nothing much. 3:25PM- We both start to feel in physically affecting our legs, and the uncomfortableness in our backs as we walk to go pick up some pot for the night. 4:00PM- I start to get some mild visuals, while my friend is not getting much of anything but the body load. 4:25PM- Time is slowing down as we are talking and jamming to some A Tribe Called Quest, very groovy stuff. I really recommend it for tripping. 5:00PM- I am starting to trip hard as the visuals start pouring in. My buddy starts to trip too but too a lesser extent. 5:25PM- Our friends come over J and R. We are unable to talk to them as my visuals were way too intense and my friend is laughing too much. 6:15PM- They leave because it is unbearable to be around us since we can't hold a conversation with them. Once they are gone I put A Tribe Called Quest back on and my visuals start literally flowing and bumping with the music, conversation between my friend and I flow very smoothly as well since we are both on the same “loop” as we called it. Everything was very intense but it was also amazingly fun! 6:45PM- This is when things started to get way too intense, I was losing all of my vision to visuals and everything just started to bump, swirl, flow, and groove. I kept on forgetting about reality and started to go into different ones. I thought I had entered a parallel universe and had forgotten about everything, where I was, who I was, everything. 7:00PM- I'm beginning to give up. As I go from a different reality to another, to another I lose sense of everything. I am unable to see as the visuals are too strong. I get my friend to pack me a batch bowl (Tobacco and weed), as I'm smoking it I get back into the real reality for a little bit, then the visuals come back even stronger while the flowing of the visuals continue, everything is just so jazzy and groovey, but too intense for me so I question taking some benzos to come down. 7:25PM- What was really 25 minutes felt like 3 hours. Im done and begging for help. I swallow 1.5mg clonazolam waiting for the trip to be done. 7:50PM- I can't take it anymore Im begging to come down from this trip. Around this time is when I black out from the benzos. My friend told me I was up till about 12PM, but I have no recollection of it. Overall I would not recommend this to anyone unless they have done DMT or a drug of similar sorts. Because a dose like this of real good 2C-P will mess with your head and you will lose your vision. I went a little crazy during the trip and because I had not experienced anything like this. Not even with the 40mg 2C-P trip. Have fun and be safe guys!

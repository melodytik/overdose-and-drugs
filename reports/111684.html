Based on my previous experiences with F-Phenibut, I felt I should have been feeling it by now. T+10:00  I began to experience absolutely horrible stomach pain right now, I could not stand up straight without feeling extreme pain. While other reports suggest the onset of F-Phenibut is fast, which I too have experienced, I do not believe its as predictable of a substance as I previously thought.

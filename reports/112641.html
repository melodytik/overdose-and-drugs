Dec 13, 2018. erowid.org/exp/112641 DOSE: oral 3-HO-PCP (powder / crystals) oral Alcohol - Beer/Wine BODY WEIGHT: 220 lb I recently acquired some 3-HO-PCP from a refutable vendor that I believe many people have ordered from in the past year or so. It wasn't advertised as such when I bought it but I somehow got some of the sandy grey looking batch that I had read has been going around. Being my first time that I have ever had 3-HO-PCP I cannot compare it to a purer product but compared to the O-PCE crystals I had from this vendor previously it was definitely of a lower quality synth. The first night that I indulged in it I tried the recommended dose of ~8 mg. My scale isn't as accurate as it could be but I'm confident I got close enough. The substance itself was dissolved into a beer. It is slightly caustic in nature so I did experience a little bit of a burning sensation on my tongue after drinking the beer but nothing too crazy. (I believe this is because the grey batch has a high amount of impurities in it compared to the white and crystalline batches) It was a warm feeling and was definitely active at that level. I in fact even remember a warm feeling of euphoria combined with a push to socialize. It was nice but not really what I was going for bc it lacked the distinct dissociation that other ACH's have given me without fail. I believe in part that this result is because 3-HO-PCP hits on opioid receptors on a higher level than the other chemicals in its class. It did very much so feel like I had taken some kratom or codeine with a stimulating edge and my pupils did shrink like they would with any other opiate. This experience was nice but left me wanting more. This experience was nice but left me wanting more. The next two attempts at dosing I increased the dose to 15 mg and 25 mg. These were experiences more on par of what I was looking for but they still lacked the warm and heavy dissociation that I had read this chemical could bring. They were definitely more of ACH feeling than an opiate at this dose but not really enough to push me over the edge and for the most part left me in a confused state of mind that was slightly disappointed. Fast forward to last night. I decided to say fuck it. Maybe I just need to take more, so I did. I poured 20 mg into a craft beer, chugged it down then proceeded to work on some music that I had been putting off for the past few weeks while sipping another beer that I had put roughly 15 mg into. About 30 minutes to an hr after taking the doses I started to feel the warm and familiar dissociation that O-PCE, 3-MEO-PCE, and MXE had given me in the past. It was numbing, powerful, and was definitely very close to the edge of a hole. Music sounded amazing and prob an hr or two after still trying to produce some I had to go lie down in my bed and just listen to jams and live in the moment. I smoked some weed and ended up in a cloud of my own thoughts that kept me smiling until the end of it all. I finally got the experience I was looking for with this chem and I could not have been happier. Took roughly 3-4 hrs to come back down to baseline from the come up and I passed out easily. Woke up feeling like a champ today. So all in all. I went from thinking I should throw the stuff away to believing I found myself another worthwhile chem. Definitely a keeper, hope I get some of the white batch next time bc if the grey is a bad synth, oh boy the white must be wonderful.

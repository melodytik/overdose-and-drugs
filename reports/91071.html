美国 虽然我第一次体验到这令人着迷的RC世界, 尽管我年轻时曾经经历过这种情况, 我发现了几份报告, 一份报告显示每份药物剂量为200毫克. 我小心缩小了自己的规模. 120 mg 4-FA来自可信来源. T+10 分 180 毫克甲基 T+20分钟非常强烈的心跳 T+30 急性头痛,强烈的心跳 (没有血压监测器可用) 轻微和愉快的安非他命像刺激. 在15分钟内出汗. 接下来的两个小时在愉快的刺激,高兴和恐惧之间交替, 心脏跳出我的胸部. 脉下降到每分钟44次 (通常是72次) 在2.5个小时内, 测量血压169/95 (对我来说是115/70) 买了一些低剂量阿司匹林, t=3. 5小时 60 mg 4-FA. 愉快的刺激,没有额外的心脏问题. t=5小时 80毫克甲基. 让血压再次升高,头痛又回来了, 但不是那么严重. 在今晚剩下的时间里远离甲基. t=8小时 60毫克4-FA. 那天晚上睡了一小时, 失方向, 结论:这种组合需要小心, 200+200报告可能适用于有耐受性的人. 其他: 4 - 氨 (276), 甲基隆 (255):第一次使用 (2), 健康问题 (27), 组合 (3), 单独 (16)

Aside from mushrooms last fall, I have been away from mind-altering chemicals for 10 years or more, though widely experienced in my youth. I thought this combination sounded interesting, found several reports, one positive stating a typical dosage of 200 mg each. I scaled mine back out of caution. T=0 120 mg 4-FA from trusted source. T+10 min 180 mg methylone T+20 minutes very strong heart beating T+30 acute headache, intense heart pounding (no bp monitor available) mild and pleasurable amphetamine like stimulation. Intense sweating for 15 minutes. Next two hours alternating between pleasant stimulation, euphoria and fear I would stroke-out. Heart pulsing out of my chest. Pulse rate drops to 44 bpm (normally 72) At 2.5 hours I had come down enough to make trip to nearby drug store. Measured bp 169/95 (normal for me 115/70). Picked up some low-dose aspirin and took a few just in case. t=3.5 hrs 60 mg 4-FA. Pleasant stimulation, no additional cardio issues. t=5 hrs 80 mg methylone. Brought the bp back up, headache returned but not so bad. Stay away from methylone rest of the night. t=8 hrs 60 mg 4-FA. Maybe 1 hr sleep that night, disoriented but no speed crash next day. Conclusion: Care needed with this combination, and the 200+200 report is probably for someone with tolerance.

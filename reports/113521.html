Aug 31, 2019. erowid.org/exp/113521 DOSE: T+ 0:00 oral 4-HO-MCPT (powder / crystals) T+ 2:00 smoked Cannabis BODY WEIGHT: 280 lb First 4 HO MCPT Experience After 25 years of no psychoactive chemicals, I had become bored and complacent. I discovered Research Chemicals from a young person smoking a joint while I walked the trails with my dogs. I declined a puff but added I was quite a psychonaut in my youth. He explained the online RC market. A few months later I said OK I'll do it!! And bought a number of novel substituted tryptamines. So far I've tried 4 aco MIPT, 4 ho MIPT, 4 ho MET, 4 aco DMT and yesterday I did 4 ho MCPT. I am writing this report because of the lack of reports on MCPT. All the other RC's I did between 20 and 40 mg's. All the experiences I had are relatively close to what I find for them in experience reports. In fact, they are surprisingly accurate. Each Tryptamine definitely has distinct characteristics roughly as reported by others. 0.00 I took roughly 40 mg's of MCPT . 0.15 I felt a little "coming up", very gentle. 0.40 I turned on music like "Echo and the Bunnymen" and the Cars. There was a definite increase in music enjoyment. The come up continued very slowly and gently. 1.00 I could see a faint bit of colors and the attempt for patterns to start. Each time I saw something it would begin then fade away. 1.30 Continuation of the same. A neighbor's dog got into my back yard and I leashed it and returned it. Something that would have been IMPOSSIBLE with any other of the other RC's I mentioned. 2.00 The faint colors and very faint CEV's reduced slowly. I never got much higher than than perhaps a few stiff drinks. I never got much higher than than perhaps a few stiff drinks. I tried a little weed and that enhanced "it" ... To a minor degree. 3.00 Starting to become pretty much sober. It was over after it barely even got going... 5.00 I went to sleep. No sleep aides, slept well. Woke up as if nothing happened at all. Conclusion: Not unpleasant, a mild tryptamine intoxication with little fanfare. I don't see the point in that and will probably not touch it again ... I wonder if 100 mg's would do something, but I again don't see the point.

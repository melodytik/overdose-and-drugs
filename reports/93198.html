最近我收购了一袋乙基. 我曾经在音乐会上尝试过这种药物, 我把我拿到的克分成两份, 画出一条长线的乙基, 然后把它分成等数部分, 他说他从来没有感觉过如此美好. 我曾经服用过这种药物, 我在服用乙卡丁前四小时抽了一支烟, 我把一半的袋子 (我有大约200毫克) 量出来, 喝了一杯水去洗掉味道. 我听说过很多关于乙基甲的报道, 我预计它会撞到我,但什么都没有发生 T+1:20 - 除了脉增强之外,还没有发生任何情况 T+1:40 - 与我同时服用相同剂量的朋友聊天 - 我们决定划出一条线 T+1:50 - 轻微刺激 T+2:00 - 脉率增加,音乐声音稍微好一些 T+2:38 - 绘制了另外两条每条约为30或40毫克的线. 虽然有点痛苦, 但是一种很好的痛苦. 没有更多的东西. 朋友告诉我他感觉到高兴 - - 我感觉不到什么. T+3:20 - 失望了,去睡觉. 我不确定这是否与吸毒或凌晨3点睡觉有关. 我朋友的体重约为60或70公斤, 身高约为1.75米. 我身高2米,重70公斤, 只是一些额外的信息. 另外,在服用这种药物之前, 我三周来第一次吃肉, 无论如何,我建议不要口服这种物质, 我有200毫克,但我担心服用太多, 但这只是从一次经历,所以不要把它作为规则. 和平! 2011年10月14日发布 乙基卡丁 (463):一般 (1),小组 (2-9) (17)

I had tried the substance before - at a concert and really liked it - the euphoria was amazing. I split the gram I had, by drawing a long line of Ethylcathinone and then separating it into even parts, so everyone got an even amount of it. I met with a friend who had just took it an hour ago, and he said he never felt so good in his life. I had taken the substance before, so I smiled and became a bit inpatient to go back home and dose. I had smoked a joint about four hours before taking the Ethylcathinone and I believe the effects had completely diminished. I weighted out the half of the bag I had (I had around 200 mg) and ate it taking a glass of water to wash away the taste. I heard many reports of Ethylcathinone being very nasty to insufflate, but in my opinion eating it is worse as its taste is very bad. T+0:40 - I'm expecting it to hit me already, but nothing is happening T+1:20 - Still nothing happening except for stronger pulse T+1:40 - Chatting with friend who dosed the same amount at the same time as me - we decide to draw a line T+1:50 - Slightly stimulated T+2:00 - Pulse rate increases, music sounds slightly better T+2:38 - Drew another two lines of about 30 or 40 mg each. My friend is about 60 or 70 kg in mass and is about 1,75 meters tall. I am two meters tall, also 70 kg, but very, very skinny. Just some additional information. Also, before taking the substance I ate meat for the first time in three weeks, so that might have affected my metabolism. Anyways, I would suggest not taking the substance orally, but drawing a few lines. I had 200 mg, but I was concerned about taking too much and started very slow. But that's just from a single experience, so don't take it as a rule. Peace!

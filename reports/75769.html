May 7, 2021. erowid.org/exp/75769 DOSE: 750 mg oral DMAE (daily) 1 cig. smoked Tobacco - Cigarettes BODY WEIGHT: 140 lb I began taking DMAE a little over a month ago. When I first started I was taking one 250mg capsule every morning and one more in the afternoon. I noticed a stimulation that felt slightly edgy/jittery and less euphoric than amphetamine but the body high was pretty much identical. After a few weeks of this two capsule routine I upped my dosage to just two in the morning and 1 in the evening. This 750mg dose is when I began experiencing the side effects. Headaches that would come and be gone within 10 minutes, and of course the dreaded insomnia. Its not that I had trouble falling asleep, but rather I would always wake up about 5 hours later and not be able to go back to bed for at least an hour. Other than those minor disturbances there was no big problems. This is also when I learned that taking two separate doses caused the most side effects, so instead of 2 in the AM and 1 in the PM I just took three every morning. 750mg DMAE bitrate every morning. And since 1/2 the weight of DMAE bitrate is the bitrate salt that leaves only 375mg actual DMAE. The health benefits of this amino alcohol are phenomenal . I use to get irritable at nothing. I mean like my mom would wear belly shirts and it would piss me off for no reason really. I knew I needed not to get upset but for some reason I would get irritated. Now, luckily, I finally got what I knew needed to happen, it doesn't irritate me. It absolutely helps balance emotions if they seem to be off balance for whatever reason. (After taking it faithfully for 3-4 weeks.) I also have an unfailing motivation to work out. See usually I would work out for like a month, but than I would stop for a few months, it's always been in cycles of up and down. However now I have an unfailing motivation to work out, I dont moan and groan at the thought of working my muscles. Also smoking Tobacco is a thing of the past! Not that I would be able to smoke anyway, one cigarette is enough to get me vomiting . I believe this has absolutely everything to do with now DMAE works in the body. Actually the thought of smoking is disgusting to me. Since the major health benefits of DMAE didn't become apparent til I hit 750mg DMAE bitrate (equivalent to 375 DMAE free base) I doubt this is too much, but am excited of what has happened to me because of taking this. And honestly I am not eager to reduce my dose. When my bottle drops down to about %25 I'll order another one.

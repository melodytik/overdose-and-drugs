我有一个毒品问题, 导致我入狱. 我在县监狱里待了7个月, 药物还没有用完, 无论如何,我们有一个孩子,他会从床上站起来,一寸向前曲, 然后他会后退几英寸滑动他的脚仍然曲,直到他到达他的床上,要么躺下,或者做几次然后躺下. 无论他走到哪里, 他都会像小龙卷风一样旋转. 但他仍然能够理解事物并像正常人一样与人交谈. 他只是在他的头上. 他告诉我这两种药是帮助我睡觉的. 我一直在服用贝纳德里尔, 我打破了一个囊,吃了它,然后吞下了另一个. 我没有感觉到什么,所以一小时后就睡觉了. 我醒来6到7个小时后感觉很奇怪. 我觉得自己被压了. 我只能看到眼睛的高度, 我走来走去, 告诉自己一切都会好起来的, 我吃了早餐,然后睡了一小时, 我一直在想每个人都有重要的事情要做, 于是我开始思考, 我睡着了,我的朋友们在睡觉时都在喊我, 他们在我的耳朵尖叫我,我只是走了. 我醒了,决定用我的时间, 等药效减去, 这让我很害怕, 于是我和一个叫斯坦的无家可归人一起玩断游戏, 我强迫自己玩几个小时, 我打电话给护士和看护人员, 然后我告诉他们,如果这种情况继续下去, 他们把我带到一个单独的牢房前面, 警官一直在通过小窗口跟我说话, 我把面包塞进通风口, 我不得不在一个金属床上死24小时. 这种药物适用于精神障碍的人, 永远不要用于娱乐用途, 其他: 药品 - 齐普拉西 (353):第一次 (2), 艰难的经历 (5), 宿醉/几天后 (46), 不好的旅行 (6), 各种 (28)

Sep 2, 2008. erowid.org/exp/68500 DOSE: 1 capsl oral Pharms - Ziprasidone (powder / crystals) 1 capsl oral Pharms - Ziprasidone (capsule) BODY WEIGHT: 170 lb Well I had a drug problem which landed me in jail. I spent 7 months in the county jail and was not done with drugs just yet so I would take whatever guys on prescription meds would get. Anyway we had this kid that would get up off his bunk and inch forward bent over about 5-10 feet here and there at odd times. Then he would inch backwards sliding his feet still bent over until he reached his bunk and either lay down or do it several times then lay down. He would spin in circles wherever he went like a little tornado lol. But he was still able to make sense of things and talk to people like he was normal. He was just f-ed up in the head. So I traded him a honey bun (pastry) for 2 pills which he told me were sleeping aids. I had been taking benadryl just to sleep for 12 hours to sleep the day away. I broke one capsule open and ate it then swallowed the other. I didn't feel anything at all so I went to bed after 1 hour. I woke up about 6-7 hours later feeling weird as hell. I felt boxed in my mind like I was being crushed. My vision was restricted to eye level meaning I couldn't see above myself very well unless I concentrated. I walked around telling myself it would be ok because I have been made mistakes like this before. So breakfast came and I ate then I went back to sleep for an hour after laying there for about 2 hours. The whole time I was thinking that everyone had important things to do and I had nothing to do. Then I was thinking about how I was going to be a vegetable for the rest of my life. I went to sleep and my friends I had made were yelling at me while I was sleeping trying to wake me up knowing I took this kid's pills. They were in my ear yelling at me and I was just gone. So I woke up and decided to just occupy my time until the pill wore off which freaked me out because it's about 11 am now and I took them around 8 pm the night before. So I played Monopoly with a homeless man named Stan who was quite nuts himself. I forced myself to play for an a couple hours while just walking around freaking out. Finally around 4 pm I called the nurses and guards and told them what I had done. Then I told them I was going to kill myself if it didn't wear off. So they took me up front to a single holding cell and stripped me naked putting me in a padded vest which smelled like it hadn't been washed in some time. The female sergeant guard kept talking to me throught the little window telling me to chill and it would be ok. I stuffed bread in the vent to keep the air out because it was at least 50 degrees in that room. Then around 5 pm it wore off but I had to stay in the cell for 24 hours freezing to death on a metal bed. This drug is for people with mental disorders do not ever take it for recreational use it's about the worst thing you can take.

美国 (囊) 大麻 (植物材料) 有关物质:大麻 大麻晚上3或4碗 设置:从2003年9月到2004年9月, 我回归是我与2c-e的第一次尝试. 很遗憾我没有得到我想要的旅行, 约一个月后, 在我最好的朋友的聚会上, 我有机会使用5-MeO-DMT, 我有一个相当好的旅程,但像往常一样,它跑得太快了. 由于我的3个月的指导方针已经过去了, 我有一些抑郁的阴影在我的精神边缘, 但总的来说我最近相当满意. 我现在每天服用多种维生素,B12补充剂 (我是素食主义者) 和Depakote (酸). 背景:我和妻子T在家. 我已经整理好了大杂乱, 我忘了把电话拔掉,但它只在我的旅行结束时响起. 最近天气相当凉爽,所以我在晚上大部分时间都开着暖气. 这是我首次来到这所房子, 我在8个小时前才吃了一块小健康饼干. 报告:今天早些时候我准备了自己的剂量. 尽管我不喜欢这种做法, 我已经准了50到75毫克. 在00蔬菜囊中,当轻轻地敲击时,粉末会进入圆形底部的顶部. [T+0:00] 我用果汁吃药. 像我每次服用药物时一样, 药物却在子中流入我的食道. 我把一些"辛普森"看在等待的妻子. 我曾经认为我应该等待吸烟大麻当我尝试一个新的化合物, 直到我很好地在我的路上, [T+0:31] 检测到第一个短暂的身体声. 这表现为"三胺",头部/部区域轻微紧张,以及与4-AcO- DiPT最相似的一般"",但比5-MeO- DiPT和5-MeO- DMT更弱. 在接下来的30-45分钟内, 身体的声继续增强和稳定, [T+1:01] 标志着时间的延长, 以及传统思维模式的松. 似乎已经过了两个小时. 我没有任何问题理解实际上已经过了多少时间. 实际上, 在这段经历中, 我也注意到一种显著的高兴感, 随着我的身体感觉的加剧, 情感变得越来越普遍和令人渴望. 由于我很熟悉披头士乐队的"白色专辑"和"修道院之路", 我喜欢热水, 浴室常常是出游的好地方. 我经常经历短暂的视觉变化进入房间, 即使清醒. 对于这段故事的应用是, 我在旅途中拍摄的大部分视频都是在这个房间里. 然而,其他因素也可能导致了这种情况. 首先是因为水很热, 也许因为我在水里呆了很久, 第二个,也许是最大的贡献, 视觉效果相对较小. 一些轻微的"呼吸"和二维的表面重新排列. 我对T表示,这种化合物缺乏声波扭曲, 胃部也表现得非常好. 我有报道说这种情况也发生在其他使用这种物质的人身上, 在"黑鸟"中, 我听到了我的第一个改变. 接下来的1小时45分钟, 基本上很容易区分基音和那些有精神变幻的部分,例如吉他音符, 它们听起来像是循环碎形. 其他人所说的声音变得更深. 我和我的妻子的声音我们都降低了,使她听起来像一个男人. 披头士的声音也被降低了,约翰在几个曲目中听起来像芝加哥的蓝调人. [T+4:18] 声音的变化已经消失了. 虽然我已经开始下降了, 轻微的头痛是可见的,但比许多其他药物少,并且不需要止痛药. 现在已经很晚了, 我出去看"杀死比尔:第一卷" 2自己因为她不想看到它无论如何. 这部电影吸引人, [T+7:19] 在吃了一点点之后,我去睡觉. 我发现自己很容易入睡. 听起来比平时更. 总结:今天是第二天, 根据我对大多数药物的个人耐受性和对其他药物的测量, 我估计我得到的药量在25到50毫克之间, 我觉得这是一个很好的介绍, 听觉效应绝对不同, 甚至与我对的其他听觉幻觉不同. 视觉效果比其他胺类药物更清晰. 在情感上和逻辑上, 我觉得自己处于水池的浅处, 我希望能在DIPT的深水中游泳. 美国 第一次 (2), 小组 (2-9) (17)

A Gentle but Intriguing Introduction DiPT & Cannabis by Way Citation:   Way. "A Gentle but Intriguing Introduction: An Experience with DiPT & Cannabis (exp39848)". Erowid.org . Feb 4, 2005. erowid.org/exp/39848 DOSE: 1500 mg oral Pharms - Divalproex (daily) oral Vitamins / Supplements (daily) oral DiPT (capsule) smoked Cannabis (plant material) BODY WEIGHT: 180 lb 18:11 Substance: DiPT, Cannabis Dose: DiPT - Unknown (filled the rounded bottom of a 00 veg-cap when gently tapped) Cannabis – 3 or 4 bowls over the course of the evening Set: From September 2003 until September 2004 I took a hiatus from psychedelics except cannabis. My return was my initial trial with 2c-e. Unfortunately I didn’t quite get the trip I was looking for and just slept through much of it. About a month later at a party at my best friend’s I had the opportunity to do some 5-MeO-DMT, which is one of my favorite chemicals. I had a fairly good trip, but as always it fled too quickly. As of late I’ve wanted another trip, and since my 3 month guideline had passed I decided to embark on yet another adventure and try out another novel substance. I’ve had some tinges of depression lingering at the edges of my psyche, but overall I’ve been fairly contented lately. At this time I am taking daily multi-vitamins, B-12 supplements (I’m vegan) and Depakote (valproic acid). Setting: I am at home with my wife T. I’ve tidied up the major messes, but have left the everyday mess as it is. I forgot to unplug the phone, but it only rang towards the end of my trip. It has been fairly chilly lately, so I run the heater most of the night. This is my inaugural trip at this house and I am looking forward to it. I’ve only eaten a small health cookie approximately 8 hours earlier. Report: Earlier in the day I prepared my dose. Unfortunately I don’t have access to a scale, so once again I was eyeballing despite my dislike for the practice. I’ve aimed for 50 – 75 milligrams. In the 00 vegetable capsule the powder comes just to the top of the rounded bottom when gently tapped. [T+0:00] I take my pill with some fruit juice. My throat half-heartedly attempts to prevent its passage, as it always does when I dose, but the pill glides down my esophagus in the torrent of apricot nectar. I put on some “Simpsons” to watch with the wifey while I wait. I used to think I should wait to smoke pot when I’m trying a new compound until I was well on my way up, but I’ve never had it not go well with a psychedelic and it helps me relax into the experience so nowadays I just dive in when the spirit hits me. [T+0:31] The first ephemeral body buzz is detected. This manifests as a “tryptamine buzz”, slight tension in the head/neck area and general “tingliness” most similar to that of 4-AcO-DiPT, being less intense than that of 5-MeO-DiPT and 5-MeO-DMT. The body buzz continues to build in strength and constancy for about the next 30-45 minutes at which point the bodily sensations plateau. [T+1:01] Marked time dilation is noted, as well as a loosening of conventional thought patterns. It seems like 2 hours have already passed. However, upon viewing the actual time I have no problem comprehending how much time has actually passed. In fact throughout this experience I remain markedly functional and grounded. I also note a significant euphoria, much more than marijuana by itself imparts. With my intensifying body sensations eroticism is becoming more prevalent and desired. At about this time I put on “The White Album” and “Abbey Road” by The Beatles since I am very familiar with them and wanted to listen for any alterations. Then I went to take a shower, as I adore hot water and often find bathrooms to be excellent trip spaces. Interestingly, my bathroom at this house has a strange energy for me and I often experience brief visual alterations upon going into the room even while sober. How that applies to this story is that the vast majority of my visuals during the trip were in this room. Other factors probably contributed to this however. The first was that the water was quite hot and probably raised my temperature somewhat as I was in there quite a while, about 1.5 – 2 hours. The second, and probably greatest contributor, was that it was during this period that I peaked. The visuals were relatively minor. Some light “breathing” and 2D surface rearrangement. I commented to T on the lack of sound distortions for which this compound is famed. My stomach also behaved exceptionally well. Often I get nausea and feel like I will have diarrhea, and I had reports of this happening to others with this substance, but I only felt the slightest hint of such discomforts and then only briefly with no fruition. [T+~2:05] During “Blackbird” I hear my first alterations. These intensify for about the next hour and 45 minutes before beginning to fade. For the most part it is easy to distinguish between the “base” sound and the parts which have psychedelicized – for instance guitar notes that sound like they are going in circular fractal pattern. The deepening of voices others have noted was observed. My own and my wife’s voices we both lowered, making her sound like a man. The Beatles voices were also lowered, with John sounding like a Chicago blues man on several tracks. [T+~3:00] I got out of the shower, had T braid my hair and then we hung out together on our bed, just listening to the music and conversing. Conversation was easy and enjoyable throughout the trip. T looked very beautiful, even more so than usual and we soon engaged in some very nice coitus. Lovemaking was found to be enhanced with additional energy, desire and staying power, although not being as good as 5-MeO-DiPT if sexual enhancement is the only criteria one values. [T+~3:45] The sound alteration begins to fade. [T+4:18] The sound alterations are essentially gone at this point. I am definitely starting to come down at this point, but am still above a simple cannabis high. A slight headache is noticed, but less than with many other substances and no analgesic is needed. T heads to bed as it is getting late. I go out to watch “Kill Bill: Vol. 2” by myself since she did not want to see it anyways. The euphoric mood remains and the movie is engaging and followed without difficulty. [T+7:19] I head to bed after some snacking. I find myself able to easily drift off to sleep. However, in the quiet of the late night I find my tinnitus to be louder than usual. Summary: It’s the next day and I have no after-effects to report on. I was pleased with my trip and from the amount of material I put in the cap, my personal tolerance for most drugs and gauging my experience against others' I would guess I got between 25 and 50 milligrams, but of course I’ll never know for sure. I found it to be a great introduction, showing off some its unique flavor for sure. The aural effects were definitely distinct, even from other auditory hallucinations I have had say on mushrooms for instance. The visuals had more of the “fuzzy” feel phenethylamine visuals have for me than the crisper visuals I’ve gotten with other tryptamines. Emotionally and logically I was pretty grounded the whole time. I felt like I was in the shallow end of the pool, but that the pool went further out. At some point I would like to go swim in DiPT’s deep water.

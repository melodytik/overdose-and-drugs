五年多以后, 我有机会第二次服用伊博加 (ibogaine). 我在墨西哥北部的一个新诊所做了这项工作. 这家诊所的主要目的是治疗药物滥用, 现在是我的个人报告. 这次第二次旅行的重点是我与'Mr. 他向我显现为一个穿着西装的黑人. 在我最初的幻象中, 他带我上楼梯, 到"布维蒂城堡". 在这个幻象中, 伊博加给我直接的电报答复我的问题. 我问伊博加是什么,得到的答案是:『人类的原始智慧教师!』我的大部分旅行都集中在我的个人缺点上,这些缺点被详细展示,我对自己因为无法纠正这些缺点而感到愤怒. 我得到的答案是'直截了当! 当我说这真的不重要时, 答案是:"一切都重要!" 我不知道最小的行为或手势有多重要. 通过找到正确的道路,然后拒绝偏离,不管发生什么,不被吓倒. 我问,我该怎么办? 我还意识到,我很幸运! 我感觉是'先生 像佛陀一样, 他选择了另一种形式, 作为植物灵而不是人类老师, 我问他是否愿意以人类的身份再现身, 答案基本上是"已经做了" 我还认为"维度"对于幻觉药物来说太过严格, 更准确的是说振动,间隔和共振. 我认为这可能是像拨号声, 标志着你接收的频率发生了变化. 是一个直接的热线"上帝" (银河系订单局)? 在某个时候, 我也穿过太阳系飞到太阳, 我直观的理解是, 每个星球和恒星都有自己的意识表现形式, 我们是地球意识的表现,它的感觉器官和自我反射能力, 我们正在迅速变化, 因为地球正处于加速转型的状态. 我想起我对猫和狗的过敏,并意识到 (或被告知) 导致过敏的原因是"对动物的恐惧".这种恐惧的条件是建立在一个人的前世. 我看到了我和另外两个大祭司的特殊形象, 我也没有认真对待这件事. 我想到了世界可怕的状况, 看到可能发生核战争的图像, 红色的火焰吞城市, 我得到的答案是: 我也看到了令人厌恶的"灰色外星人"降落在灾难后的地球上, 后来,当我再看启示录的图片时,信息是:'一切都在上帝手里是安全的!'这个信息,虽然听起来像是圣经的漫画,但实际上给了我很大的安慰. 我从这次旅行中得到的证据, 支持"伊博加"在圣经中所说的"善恶知识树"的理论. 它在赤道非洲的位置和严格的道德清晰性使它成为这个角色的最佳候选人. 它的关于善与恶的'原始智慧教导'不是抽象的,而是极为个性化和直接的. 虽然不是最令人愉快的经历, 诊所的医生说他们治疗成的成功率似乎在50%左右.

Jan 18, 2003. erowid.org/exp/20492 DOSE: 3 tablets oral Ibogaine BODY WEIGHT: 185 lb Last week, I had the opportunity to take iboga (ibogaine) for a second time – more than five years since my visit to Africa. I did this at a wonderful new clinic in northern Mexico. The clinic’s main purpose is to treat substance abuse but it is also available for those who want to try iboga for personal or spiritual reasons. Now for my personal report. The main aspect of this second trip was my long conversation with 'Mr. Iboga,' who appeared to me as a black man in a suit. In my initial vision, he led me up the stairs to the 'Castle of the Bwiti.' I was a 5-year-old girl in this vision, and he was holding my hand. Iboga gave me direct, telegraphed answers to my questions. I asked what iboga was, and the answer I got was: 'PRIMORDIAL WISDOM TEACHER OF HUMANITY!' Much of my trip focused on my personal faults, which were displayed in detail, and my anger at myself for being unable to correct them. The answer I got was 'GET STRAIGHT! DO WHAT IT TAKES TO STRAIGHTEN OUT THE SHIT!' When I argued that it really didn’t matter, the answer was: 'EVERYTHING MATTERS!' – that I have no idea how important the smallest act or gesture can be. I was given insight in how to become a 'spiritual warrior,' by finding the right path and then refusing to deviate, not being deterred, no matter what. I asked, What should I do? 'WORK HARDER!' I also realized, 'I am lucky!' My sense was that 'Mr. Iboga' was a form of enlightenment mind, like the Buddha, who had chosen a different form, as a plant spirit rather than human teacher, to work with humanity. I asked if he would consider incarnating as a human, and the answer I got was, basically, 'already did that' – as if, in some previous cycle, he had passed through the stages of evolution we were now navigating. I also had the thought that 'dimensions' was too rigid a term for psychedelics, as well as everything that preserves a hard 'subject-object' distinction. More accurate would be to talk about vibrations and intervals and resonances. Iboga causes a loud ringing in the ears, and I thought this might be something like a dial tone, signifying a change in the frequency you are picking up. Is iboga a direct hotline to 'GOD' (Galactic Ordering Directorate)? At one point, I also went flying through the solar system and into the sun, where angelic beings were shooting around the core at a tremendous rate. My intuitive understanding was that every planet and star has its own form of manifestation of consciousness depending on its level of evolution and vibration. We are the manifestation of Earth’s consciousness, its sensory organs and self-reflective capacities, at its present state of evolution. We are changing quickly because the Earth is in an accelerated state of transformation. Some information had to do with what might possibly be (?) 'previous incarnations.' I thought of my allergies to cats and dogs, and realized (or was told) that what caused allergies was 'fear of animality.' The conditions for this fear is set up in one’s previous lives. I saw particular images of myself with two others as High Priests in tall hawk’s head masks, perhaps Babylonian? I couldn’t really identify the historical context, nor do I take this that seriously. I thought about the terrible state of the world – saw images of possible nuclear war, sheets of red fire devouring cities – and the answer I received was: 'GOD IS JUST!' For a while, I also saw sickening images of 'Grey Aliens' landing on a post-cataclysm Earth, and herding hordes of suburban Americans into their UFOS (this narrative taken from David Jacobs’ book, The Threat). Later, when I went over apocalypse images again, the message was: 'EVERYTHING IS SAFE IN GOD’S HANDS!' This message, Biblically caricatured as it may sound, actually gave me considerable comfort. What I took from this trip was support for the theory that iboga was, in fact, 'The Tree of Knowledge of Good and Evil' in the Bible. Its placement in equatorial Africa and its stern moral clarity make it the best candidate for this role. Its 'primordial wisdom teachings' on good and evil are not abstract, but extremely personal and direct. While not the most delightful experience, the iboga trip is one that I would recommend for most people. The doctor at the clinic says that their success rate for treating addiction seems to be running at about 50%.

我的阿片类药物历史始于我拥有无限供应 (并由政府支付) 的氧康丁. 我完全不知道阿片类药物是什么, 也不知道氧康丁含有类似于吗啡的阿片类药物氧. 我使用它们是因为我有腰痛. 但很快,我开始使用更多,甚至吸,等等. 终于我停止了 (有大量的戒断), 但几个月后我想再次尝试阿片类药物. 遗憾的是,我住的地方已经没有Oxycontin了, 我不认识任何卖片的人,所以我花了一段时间才找到一些. 我很高兴听到她使用了这种药物! - - 特拉马多尔150毫克药片和可代因. 她给了我一大堆,当这些都完成了,我想要更多. 所以我搜索了互联网,发现了Codethyline. 这是一种止咳药, 虽然它只销售50x5mg的包装,但它非常便宜,比代因更强. 问题在于,我需要大量的东西才能感受到任何东西. 休用剂量至少为60mg. 这意味着我至少要服用12粒药. 它们很小 (像火柴头),所以我可以一次把它们都抓住. 一个小时前我服用了17粒药 (85毫克). 现在开始感觉像典型的片狂欢, 我感到有些头, 现在我认为我几乎达到了高原. 这种感觉和他们给我手术前用吗啡时的感觉一样 没有. 想想这些可以在柜台上买到,而且很便宜! 实验室级,100%纯乙吗啡. 我确实有点恶心, 总体来说是一次很好的体验, 其他: 2020年5月27日发布 药品 - 乙吗啡 (443):成和习惯性 (10),烂的经历 (4),第一次 (2),未知背景 (20)

May 27, 2020. erowid.org/exp/90674 DOSE: 85 mg oral Pharms - Ethylmorphine (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 90 kg I am fairly used to taking opiates. My opiate -history began when I had an unlimited supply (and paid for by the government) of oxycontin. I was totally oblivious to what opiates were and did not know that oxycontin contained oxycodone, an opiate similar to morphine. I used them because I had lower back pains. But soon enough I would use more and more, I even snorted them etc.. Eventually I stopped (had massive withdrawals) but after a couple of months I wanted to try opiates again. Sadly I can't get a hold of oxycontin's where I live anymore so I had to search for other opiates. I don't know anyone who sells opiates so it took me a while to get a hold of some. One day I went to a friend of mine who has fibromyalgia and I asked if she used certain painkillers for her condition. i was very happy to hear that she did! -- tramadol 150mg pills and codeine. She gave me a bunch of them all and when those were finished I wanted more. So I searched the internet and found Codethyline. This is a cough suppressant and it is freely available over the counter in my country. Although it is only sold in pack of 50x5mg it is very cheap and more potent than codeine. The thing is though that I have to take alot of them to feel anything. The recreational dose is a minimum of 60mg. This means I have to take at least 12 pills. They are very small though (like the head of a match) so I can take them all in once. I took 17 pills (85mg) an hour ago. It's now starting to feel like the typical opiate euphoria, together with the happy warm feeling in my stomach I get from opiates. I feel a bit dizzy and I have to correct tons of mistakes in my text. This took about 30 minutes to come up and now I think I nearly reached the plateau. This has the same feeling as the time they gave me morphine right before an operation . And to think this is available over the counter and dirt cheap! Laboratory grade, 100% pure ethylmorphine. I do feel a bit of nausea though, but certainly not as much as the nausea I got from oxycontin.

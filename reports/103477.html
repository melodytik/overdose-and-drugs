美国 我认为自己很熟悉三种药物,如LSD,psilocybin,梅斯卡林,DMT, 我也曾经使用过甲基胺,3- 甲基胺,胺和各种片. 我在前一天晚上用5毫克的艾莉做过过敏测试, 没有任何不良反应, 我在下午2点40分独自在家中服用了. 外面是个美丽的天气, 我今天没有任何承诺或计划, 我现在处于一个伟大的精神空间, 我利用这段时间为未来制定计划, 并从我在工作中的可怕抑郁状态转变为一个完全更新/复兴的自我, 无论如何,我收到了250毫克的阿利斯卡林样品, 这是一种浅色粉,具有非常闪亮的/晶的外观. 这让我想起了闪闪发光的哈马拉. 我还应该提到我在注射前几个小时吃了一顿饭, 我确实有很少的Suboxone在我的系统. (在之前的3天里,我每天服用一半的8mg片). 我研究中似乎没有任何禁忌, 因此按照舒尔金的建议服用27毫克. 我在YouTube上观看有关古代文明的纪录片, (所有化学药物/剂量均通过ORAL ROA) 我开始感觉到一种熟悉的温暖感觉, 没有什么重大,只是能量稍微增加. 我已经是个大腿跳, 我注意到我的腿跳得比平常还要多. 我经历过的任何兴奋剂的典型副作用. 1:30 小时 - - 没有太大的变化. 我决定再服用25毫克, 以达到我在几份报告中看到的剂量范围. 我读过一些关于逐步增加剂量的报告, 我现在感觉很好, 头脑也比较清晰, 我会抽一些这可爱的蓝奶酪, 在这个时候, 我肯定会感觉到更多. 温暖,石头,非常轻微的迷幻. 没有什么重大报道. 没有OEV/CEV. 我已经采取了蓝奶酪的击时不时. 两者之间没有明显的同步性, 但我可以肯定感觉到THC和艾利之间的差异, 之后没有什么事发生. 这是一种轻微刺激的化学物质, 如果这有任何意义的话, 我还应该说, 在整个经历中我没有感到任何恶心. 我决定这种体验不会自行进一步发展, 所以我服用了35毫克的米素. 这是一种漂亮的白色粉末. 没有什么特别的外表. 5小时 - - 一旦米素开始起作用, 在那个时候, 很可能是因为米普罗辛, 这种微小的能量从盟友补充了很好的平滑的素效果! 我觉得自己就像是着天堂的楼梯上跑步的洛基巴尔博阿! 准备迎接世界! 我开始有光线标志物和值水平的纹理变化. 星星看起来令人难以置信! 我在凌晨3点左右步行到CVS, 我住在医院附近, 在这个层面上看起来不太亮/明亮, 很容易沟通. 不幸的是, 她喘着走到柜台前, 实际上只能对我低声说话. 虽然我觉得这很奇怪, 我在回家时服用了一小剂量MXE (50毫克), 我从来没有真正感觉到它在米普罗辛上, 我很开心地笑了笑, 想到人类和我被困在这个奇怪的身体里. 我也开始有了MXE突破 (我称之为MXE突破), 突然间我能够看到我眼前很长一段时间的东西, 我终于找到了我过去15年创作和录制的歌曲, 听到它们直到凌晨6点左右, 在这次经历的最后几个小时里, 我像疯子一样在家里跳舞, 我对自己和我生命中所拥有的机遇感到非常高兴. 我确实在凌晨4点到6点之间发现了一些彩色的CEV. 没有任何图案或任何东西, 只有温暖的流动色彩的波与旋律同步振荡. 总体而言,这是一个很棒的经历. 我肯定会对艾莉进行更多的实验, 我不确定这是否会使体验更强烈/更迷幻, 她似乎有潜力! 其他: 4-HO-MiPT (342),阿利利斯卡林 (573):单独使用 (16),发光体验 (4),组合使用 (3)

Nov 3, 2015. erowid.org/exp/103477 DOSE: T+ 0:00 27 mg oral Allylescaline (powder / crystals) T+ 1:30 25 mg oral Allylescaline (powder / crystals) T+ 0:00 repeated smoked Cannabis T+ 4:00 35 mg oral 4-HO-MiPT (powder / crystals) T+ 0:00 50 mg oral Methoxetamine (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 190 lb I consider myself well acquainted with trypts and phens like LSD, Psilocybin, mescaline, DMT both orally and vaporized with and without the addition of harmalas, 2cp, 2cd, 25cnbome hcl, 4-ho-met, and 4-ho-mipt. I also have experienced methoxetamine, 3-meo-pcp, ketamine and various opiates. I did an allergy test with 5mgs of Ally the night prior and had no bad reactions so I was ready to give this chem a shot! I dosed at 2:40 pm while home alone. Its a beautiful day outside and looks to be a promising day as far as weather is concerned. I don't have any commitments or plans for the day and I'm in a great mental space right now as I'm on a bit of a vacation from corporate hell where I worked for 10 years. I've been using this time to make plans for the future and to transition from the horrible depressive mind state that I was in at my job to a completely renewed/rejuvenated self that is excited about what the future holds! Anyways, I received a 250mg sample of Allylescaline from my trusty supplier. It is a light tan powder with a very shiny/crystalline look to it. It reminded me of sparkly harmalas to an extent. I should also mention that I had eaten a meal a couple hours prior to dosing and I did have a very small amount of Suboxone in my system. (I had taken half of an 8mg strip each day for the prior 3 days). There did not seem to be any contraindications in my research and therefore took 27mgs as suggested by Shulgin. I had been watching youtube documentaries about ancient civilizations earlier in the day and thus continued during the come up phase of the experience. (All chems/dosages were taken via ORAL ROA) 1 hr - I'm starting to feel that familiar warm feeling that I've perceived with cacti. Nothing major but a slight energy increase. I'm already a major leg bouncer and I've noticed my legs bouncing even more than usual. Typical side effect of any stimulant use that I've experienced. 1:30 hr - Not much of a change. Decided at this point to take an additional 25mgs and get myself up to the dosage range that I've been seeing in the few reports available. I've read a few reports of gradually stepping up the dosage as with mescaline in order to reach the desired level. I'm feeling great and relatively clear-headed at this point but would like to reach a more psychedelic effect level. I am going to smoke some of this lovely blue cheese that I have at the moment and see how I feel. 2:30 hr - definitely feeling it more at this point. Warm, stoney, very slightly psychedelic. Nothing major to report. No OEV/CEV to speak of. I've been taking bong hits of blue cheese from time to time. No noticeable synchronicity between the two but I can definitely feel a difference between the THC and ally as I smoke more. Nothing more really happened after this. It was a slightly stimulating chemical that just made it over the threshold of being psychedelic at the 52mg dosage that I ended up taking. It was almost like a threshold dose of LSD with a slightly more 'mescaline' feeling to it if that makes any sense. I also feel I should mention that I DID NOT experience any nausea whatsoever during the entire experience. 4 hrs - I decided that the experience was not going to develop any further on its own so I took 35mgs of miprocin. (4-ho-mipt) It is a nice off-white colored powder. Nothing special about the appearance. 5 hrs - Once the miprocin started kicking in I was really starting to feel good. It seemed like quite the symbiosis at that point. Probably most likely due to the miprocin but I was on an incredible spiritual high! The little lift of energy from the ally complimented the nice smooth psilocin-like effects very well! I felt like rocky balboa jogging up the stairway to heaven! Ready to take on the world! I started having light tracers and threshold level textural changes. The stars looked incredible! I walked to CVS a couple blocks over for a pack of smokes somewhere around 3am and was amazed at how everything seemed to 'pop' with interest. I live right by a hospital and thus the area is well lighted in the evening. It did not seem overly bright/glaring at this level and was quite easy to communicate. Unfortunately the lady at CVS could not communicate herself.... She wheezed her way up to the counter and literally couldn't do anything but whisper to me in order to talk. While I found this highly bizarre in my wonkified state, I just kept it moving. I lost track of time after that but when I got home I took a small dose of MXE (50mgs) to top off what had been an incredible evening. I never really felt it kick in over the miprocin but then all of the sudden I found myself having a conversation with myself about how hilarious life is. I was having a great time just laughing at the entire thought of human beings and me being stuck inside of this weird body. I also started having an MXE breakthrough (as I call it), where all of the sudden I'm able to look at something that has been right in front of my face for a long time, but in a totally different light, which made me extremely happy at the time! I ended up pulling up my personal discography of songs that I've composed/recorded over the last 15 years and listened to them until around 6am before finally falling asleep. The last couple hours of this experience I was dancing around my house like a madman and having a wonderful time. I felt so good about myself as a person, and about the opportunities that are coming up in my life! I did notice some colorful CEV's between 4-6am. There was no patterning or anything, just swashes of warm flowy colors that oscillated in sync with the tunes. Overall it was a great experience. I will definitely be doing more experimentation with Ally at a slightly higher dose. I'm not sure if that will make the experience stronger/more psychedelic but I definitely am not ready to set Ms. Ally aside as she seems to have potential!

Sep 28, 2017. erowid.org/exp/99506 DOSE: repeated vaporized Cannabis - High THC (extract) 2 drops oral MIPT (liquid) BODY WEIGHT: 127 lb This is actually three reports of different reactions in three different people. Yesterday me and a buddy were smoking some dabs & he asked me if I wanted to try some MiPT. It was not in a freebase form, this stuff was liquid and tasted like asshole, it also burns. He told me the dosage range was in milliliters and all we had was a dropper. I had never heard of this stuff except for the entry in TIHKAL and wanted to be cautious and filled the dropper with as little I could get, literally just a drop. I took two of the little drops as did my buddy following my lead. (the 3rd person comes later) So after 30 minutes I can definitely feel like I have taken something and it's doing its work on my body. My friend notes he is having trouble because 'he is not used to this come-up' He said he felt a light body load but I had a slightly heavy body load as I feel with most Tryptamines. The psychedelia was totally there but the actual hallucinations just were not there. It was very short lived and we both felt our brains burning at the same time. I blurted it aloud and my buddy said he felt it too. We also had tightness in the neck and chest. To me the tightness in my neck made it hard for me to breathe properly and I was loosing air. I also started feeling pain deep in my arms and legs. Now later on after we came down my buddy's roommate pops up and took a whole dropper-full of the stuff and he kept calling it acid for some reason even though he knew it was MiPT. Within 20 minutes he was completely twacked, he was screaming for us to help him and that he never wanted to trip again. His skin was red and he was sweating profusely. His shirt was completely drenched and he said he felt like shit. Utter insanity, he wouldn't even smoke a Dab. So I felt like this was time for me to leave, went home 8 hours after dosing and felt normal aside the lingering psychedelia and slight paranoia. Felt great the next morning, Roommate was still tripping by the next day too so if anybody gets liquid of anything please please please just test it with DROPS as little as possible, don't think your mr. badass and take a whole dropperfull of whatever just cause. Geez.

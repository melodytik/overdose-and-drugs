中文译文 致幻剂

我昨晚做了一个梦,它是这样的: 这是我对2C-T-2的第一次实验. 过去一两天我每天补充50毫克的5-HTP. 预装50毫克的5-HTP应用程序. 在摄入2C-T-2前2-3小时. 这种5-HTP补充剂还含有5毫克尼雅,5毫克维生素B-6,25毫克和50毫克根. 还使用了500毫克的DL- 氨酸补充剂. 晚上七点半 摄入7毫克的2,5-二甲基-4-乙烯基胺 (用降落). 我在当天早些时候吃了饭, 但至少四个小时都没有. 晚上7点40分 我还在喝酒, 但我觉得我开始感觉到. 晚上7点45分 感觉到了. 在外面,和动物一起休息. 奇怪的是,有人刚刚开车在路上敲响喇像婚礼, 但这只是一辆车. 现在,他们只是回来回来沿着小径做同样的事情. 我看到我不是唯一一个在药物今天. 8) 没有 晚上8点10分 身体的冲动. 感觉像一个健康的剂量Sudafed. 呼吸速度非常缓慢,心率下降. 绝对是肌痛. 我刚刚用了另外一个6.48毫克的凝. 感觉真的很好. 我可以告诉你,我真的很糟糕. 我还不太确定自己该怎么办. 晚上九点 我决定把自己从事"星际迷航"的最后一季. 迄今为止的进步非常顺利和愉快. 我已经在地板上和一个巨大的笑容在我的脸上在过去的几分钟. 我觉得身体负荷很大, 身体很疲, 比我预期的要强得多. 晚上9点45分 让我告诉你,在过去的半小时里, 我躺在地板上观看航天器, 只是在自己内心翻转, 随着这种感觉的强度逐渐增加, 感觉就像我吸了一颗巨大的气球, 到了九点十五分, 我无法感觉到我的手臂, 我觉得我没有得到任何氧气, 只有我能想到的是,'不,我不会死,不是今天.' 我惊慌失措地跳起来跑到浴室. 我试图集中注意力, 我来回步行一会儿摇我的手臂试图得到感觉回来. 感觉如果我把头稍微转向一边, 慢慢地我得到了感觉回到我的怀抱. 视觉效果超越了尺度, 我认为视觉效果在此时已经超过了我服用过的任何一种LSD. 过去几分钟我一直想着我的家人, 如果我因为某种未知的化学物质而倒, 他们会怎么感觉. 我一直在思考生活中真正不可或缺的东西, 我还不想失去这个礼物,我还没有准备好. 晚上十点半 我洗,这本身就是一个冒险. 自从恐慌发作或过敏反应或任何我经历过, 我觉得我打开我的头或什么,我的头不疼,我只是感到震惊,像'2加2吗?'过去一个小时左右我一直经历一些肌痛,如果我不动,这是好的,但当我移动它就像我的肌肉不断锁定在任何位置他们最后一次. 只是不舒服, 就好像我的肌肉增加了质量, 另外,自从袭击发生以来, 我感觉极度寒冷, 淋浴是热的,现在我再次穿上衣服,但我仍然,我似乎无法得到温暖,无论如何. 事实上,除了恐慌发作之外, 到目前为止, 感觉几乎完全像一个沉重的DXM旅行, 我告诉熊,我今晚晚会去酒吧,但我绝对不想今晚更高. 我只想加入一些音乐, 试着享受剩下的体验. 下午十一点 我花了过去半小时左右完全分析我的生活. 我已经意识到我做的一些愚蠢的小事, 我不想在这里详细介绍, 但我必须说我们人类最需要做的事情, 我们作为个人和整体的首要任务, 我知道这听起来很陈词滥调, 爱情是最高的智力, 这是一个开始和结束, 取决于我们做它之间的. 晚上十一点半 我现在感觉绝对很棒, 实际上是重生! 享受着音乐,享受着热潮,享受着视觉. 这就是我对化学物质的期望. 我没想到会有45分钟到1小时的高峰, 这比2C-T-7更像是糖果翻转, 身体负荷现在并不不舒服, 皮肤感觉极其美妙, 上午1点半 我今晚要退休了,旅行已经结束了. 虽然我的认知功能已经恢复到原来的水平, 皮已经消失了,但我现在仍然没有感觉到应该的温暖. 总而言之,我感觉很舒服. 尽管在那个45分钟的时间里, 我真的很担心自己的生命. 我也应该指出,与许多人不同, 我可以想到一些关于高峰的严重性. 一个是错误计算剂量. 虽然我的体重大约是12-14毫克, 如果校准不对, 我可能实际上已经消耗了接近20毫克或更多, 首次服用6-7毫克剂量可能更适合我的心理/生理. 总的来说,这种化学物质比2C-T-7更安全, 但我经历了更多的症状, 我在使用LSD时经历了更严重的肌痛和非病理性结, 因此我认为这些不适的背后很可能有心理原因. 唯一让我感到困扰的是我经历的近乎低温的情况. 我以前也曾经因失血而感到震惊, a) 肢体冷, b) 身体, c) 保持意识困难, 需要对这些乙胺进行毒性研究. 尽管这次旅行有点恐惧, 我仍然觉得这种化学物质确实有更多的希望, 像我在上面说的, 我经历了一段令人惊叹的自我审视时期, 我相信这种化学物质可以帮助很多人. 低至中等剂量与经验丰富的治疗师在舒适的环境中相结合, 为了战胜死亡,你只需要死去. 其他: 2C-T-2 (53): 孤独 (16),健康益处 (32),令人愉快的经历 (4),健康问题 (27),困难的经历 (5),第一次 (2)

Jun 1, 2001. erowid.org/exp/7174 DOSE: T+ 0:00 7.0 mg oral 2C-T-2 (powder / crystals) T+ 1:00 7.0 mg oral 2C-T-2 (capsule) BODY WEIGHT: 125 lb I had a dream last night, it went something like this: This is my first experiment with 2C-T-2. The past day or two I've supplemented 50mg 5-HTP daily. Preloaded 50mg 5-HTP app. 2-3 hours prior to ingestion of 2C-T-2. The 5-HTP supplement also contains 5mg Niacin, 5mg Vitamin B-6, 25mg Magnesium, & 50mg Valerian Root. A 500mg supplement of DL-Phenylalanine was also consumed. 07:30 pm Ingested app 7 mg 2,5-dimethoxy-4-ethylthiophenthylamine (parachuted). I ate food earlier in the day, but not for at least four hours. 07:40 pm Still high from bong hits but I think I am starting to feel it. getting fuzzy at the edges. 07:45 pm Feeling the tingle. Outside, chilling with the animals. Weird, someone just drove down the Trail honking their horn like a wedding, but it was just one car. Now, they just came came back up the trail doing the same thing. I see I'm not the only one on drugs today. 8) 08:10 pm Body rushing. Feels like a healthy dose of sudafed. Breathing very slow with tachycardia. Definite mydriasis. I just took another 6.48 mg in a gelcap. Feel real nice. Went to burn a CD & I can tell that I'm really fucked up. Not quite sure what to do with myself yet. 09:00 pm I've decided to occupy myself with the season finale of Star Trek Voyager. The come up so far as been really smooth and pleasant. I've been on the floor with a huge grin on my face for the past few minutes. There seems to be quite a lot of body load, I feel completely washed out but I'm still having fun. Tripping much harder than I expected. 09:45 pm I just hit the peak & am on the descent now, and let me tell you I was very very concered for the past half hour or so. Here's what happened: I was lying on the floor watching Voyager, just rolling inside myself when I started to feel a tingling in my limbs. I got deja-vu of 'i know this feeling from somewhere' and as it slowly increased in intensity I made the connection; nitrous. It felt like I just sucked on a huge balloon and was going up up up up. At 9:15 it peaked, I couldn't feel anything in my arms, they were tingling with pin cushions as if they had fallen asleep. I felt like I wasn't getting any oxygen & was about to pass out. A bolt of fear shot through me as I was about to lose consciousness, and all I could think was, 'no, i'm not going to die, not today.' I jumped up in a panic & ran up to the bathroom. I looked in the mirror trying to focus my concentration and talk myself down a little. I paced back and forth for awhile shaking my arms trying to get the feeling back in them. The faintness hit me a few more times in strong waves, it felt like if I would turn my head just a little to either side I would faint. Slowly I got the feeling back in my arms. The visuals went off the scale, I think the visuals at this point surpassed any LSD I have ever taken. I wish I could enjoy them but the past few minutes I've just been thinking about my family, how they'd feel if I just keeled over from an adverse reaction to some unknown chemical. I've been thinking about the things that are really imortant in life, like the ability to taste a cup of coffee or feel the warmth of the sunshine. I don't want to lose this gift yet, I'm not ready. 10:30 pm I took a shower, that was an adventure in itself. Since the panic attack or allergic reaction or whatever I experienced, it has been next to impossible to concentrate. I feel like I cracked my head open or something, my head doesn't hurt, I just feel stunned, like '2 plus 2, huh?' The past hour or so I've been experiencing some myalgia, it's ok if I'm not moving at all, but when I move it's like my muscles constantly lock in whatever position they were last in. It doesn't really hurt, just more than uncomfortable, as if my muscles gained mass and lost mobility at the same time. Plus I've been EXTREMELY cold, since the attack I've had goose bumps all over and my finger tips feel like ice. The shower was hot and now I'm dressed again but I'm still freezing, I can't seem to get warm no matter what. Actually everything past the panic attack so far has felt almost exactly like a heavy DXM trip, and I absolutely abhore that junk. I told Silly Bear that I'd go to the bar later tonight but I have absolutely no desire to get any higher tonight. I'm just gonna throw in some music & try to enjoy what's left of the experience. 11:00 pm I've spent the past half hour or so completely analyzing my life. I've been made aware of some of the stupid little things I do that I can change to better myself. It's mostly personal so I won't go into it all here, but I must say the most important thing we humans need to do, our number one priority as individuals and as a whole, is love no matter what. I know it probably sounds cheesey & cliche, but it is really all that matters. Love is the higher intelligence, it is where we all come from & where we'll all go. It's the beginning and the end, it's up to us to make it the inbetween. 11:30 pm I feel absolutely FANTASTIC now, practically reborn! I've thrown in some trance and have just been enjoying the music, enjoying the rush, and enjoying the visuals. THIS is what I expected from the chemical going into the experience. That 45 min - 1 hour peak, that I wasn't expecting, but this is absolutely wonderful. This is more like a candy flip than 2C-T-7 was, the body load is not uncomfortable now, my skin feels supremely wonderful, and the visuals are stronger now than during that crazy peak, or at least more fluid. 01:30 am I'm about to retire for the evening, the trip is pretty much over now. I'm back to baseline as far as cognitive function goes but my body still feels washed out (but it's pleasant, I like some body load) & I am still getting some visual action. The goose bumps have gone but I still don't feel as warm as I should right now. All in all I feel cozy. The night was a good experience even though for 45 minutes or so I was really concerned for my life. I should note also that unlike a lot of people I did not experience any nausea with this chemical. AFTERTHOUGHTS: Well, there are a few things I can think of as to the severity of the peak. One is that the dose was miscalculated. I really had nothing to calibrate against, so although I weighed out roughly 12-14 mg, if the calibration was off I may have actually consumed close to 20 or more milligrams, I simply do not know at this point. The intial 6-7 mg dose is probably more suited to my psyche/physiology. The general consensus seems that this chemical is much safer than 2C-T-7 across the board, but I for one experienced more symptoms that I would relate directly to some sort of toxicity from this chemical than I ever have in the handful of times I've tried 2C-T-7. Having said that though, I have experienced myalgia & nonpathologic fasciculation to a much greater degree on LSD, and there is no toxicity associated with LSD. Therefore I'm suggesting there could very well be a psychosomatic reason behind these particular discomforts. The one thing that does bother me is the near hypothermia I experienced. I have been in shock before due to blood loss and a) the coldness in extremeties, b) the washed out body feeling, and c) the dificulty in maintaining consciousness, these symptoms were all experienced during my peak & were nearly identical to those experienced bleeding all over the place in the back of an ambulance. Toxicity studies NEED to be conducted on these phenethylamines. Even though this trip had it's moment of fear, I am left with the feeling that this chemical does indeed have much more promise, therapeutically speaking, than it's conterpart. As I said above, the period after my peak was like a rebirth. Once I got my shit back together I went through an amazing period of introspection unrivaled by most other entheogenic substances I've tried. I am convinced that this chemical could really help a lot of people. A low to moderate dose combined with an experienced therapist in a comfortable setting has the potential to open more people up to themselves than most conventional therapeutic means, in my opinion. 'To conquer death, you only have to die.'

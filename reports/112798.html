Jan 28, 2019. erowid.org/exp/112798 DOSE: T+ 0:00 120 mg oral Glaucine T+ 3:00 2.5 g oral Kratom BODY WEIGHT: 130 lb Setting: In my bedroom. T+0:00 Dosed 120mg of glaucine I did not really know what to expect from this substance other than that it supposedly mixes well with kratom. The experience it led to was very different than what I expected. I was in for the laziest day I would have in a while. T+0:45 I start to notice the first effects from the Glaucine. I started having neon green and blue patterns overlaying every part of my vision. The visuals were similar to visual snow except patterned. A heavy sedation washed over my body along with a strange dissociative headspace. T+2:00 I have not gotten up from my chair for the past hour or so. The sedation was so strong that I could barely open my eyes. I was talking to a few friends on discord and they were telling me that I sounded very quiet and very out of it. The body high was strange. My head felt like a balloon or something of that nature which was fairly bizarre. T+3:00 Around this time is when I decided to dose 2.5g of some Green Malay kratom. T+3:30 The kratom kicks in. This is where the substance really started to shine. The glaucine potentiated the kratom to an extreme degree. I was quickly put into this warm, dissociative space that immediately put me into a pseudo-sleep state. I got up to lay down in bed. T+7:00 I've been laying in my bed with my eyes closed for many hours now. During this period my mind was hopping back and forth between these strange, lucid-like dreams even though I was aware that I was not asleep. The mild dreamscape I had been floating through for the past several hours was very pleasant due to the potentiated effects of the kratom. I check my phone and realize how long I had been laying in my bed. This did not feel nearly as long as it was but it was a very calming, pleasant experience overall. T+9:00 I go to sleep. The next morning I experiences no hangover or afterglow. Conclusion: This was easily one of the most unique substances I have tried. While by itself I would not say it is very recreational or pleasant, it mixes very well with other substances such as kratom and weed. It almost reminded me a little bit of ambien except with dissociative properties. The potentiation between Glaucine and Kratom made for a toasty and peaceful experience.

I had 6yrs ago had my first Psilocybin, 3g. I don't have depression or anxiety, I take half of 27mg Methylphediate ER. Now the introductions are out of the way. I have a positive mental attitude, not the fake kind. I problem solve, to me a problem is a puzzle, I know people can get burned out and side effects of life happen. On the way home my day at work a little agitated was lied to from the boss. I was thinking about a program that helps get people motivated into recertifications for better paying jobs, so I was excited, millions of possibilities. I was taking something else for insomnia and ran out, had Trazodone which I don't like causes massive headaches and sleep is a suggestion and don't get a full 8. I had ran out of Trazodone, my partner had the generic of Lexapro. My partner told me that it was like Xanax it relaxes then easy to fall asleep. I had it a couple of times when younger, so I was thinking ok small dose no big deal. I read the label not in the entirety SSRI I know serotonin is a mood regulator, I didn't see anything that stood out, normal side effect claims including sleepiness and possible. I had a nearly full stomach when I took it, heavier foods, like pasta this was about 1am. I went to lay down and turn on the tv, I was watching King of the hill (yes I am that plain). It doesn't require any focus and the easy pace and familiarity makes it easy to sleep to. There was an onset of brighter colors, it was more vivid, I was thinking about the onset of the effects, my mood didn't change still tired, still watching. I was having a headache, I drink water quite alot especially when medication can dry me out. While I lay there I was feeling nauseated. I didn't want to get up and disturb my partner. Eventually I turned off the tv this was about 2:30ish am. My thoughts eazed away to sleep. I woke up some time after 5am a bit on the disturbed side, like waking up from a nightmare, I don't remember what I dreamt about, I fell asleep then I woke just before sunrise. This was Psilocybin all over again. I know what makes a good or bad "trip" I recalibrated my thoughts, for me it's thinking about the beauty of all GOD's creation of the earth to me GOD is there watching out for me, yours might be different. This was not negative thoughts or depression this was like being hoodwinked. I wasn't expecting this being prescribed without telling people what to expect. Then I remember reading about the trials on Psilocybin having a positive effect on people with depression. I was thinking did they synthesize it? How many people also have the same reaction and think it is something wrong with them? How many people take this, are on it and triggers happen and "move" their moods in the wrong direction? Was it out of Malfeasance or ignorance? I couldn't go back to sleep tossing and turning woke up my partner both little sleep. I lay there, I remember this website, started reading experiences I was like well this is very common. I think people should know before they get hoodwinked. As my final thought the residual is there as I type this, it is like coming out of a daze, like waking up from a nightmare and I easily forget what it was about.

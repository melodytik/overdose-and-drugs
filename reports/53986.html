我曾经在一家天然气公司工作, 要求我制造5%CO2,30%O2和65%N2O的麻醉校准混合物. 这种混合物令人愉快, 由于我们的气体混合集散器具有数字压力指示装置 (可变的部分压力70-100%),我试验了在纯氧平衡下找到最佳N2O度,从而产生最高水平的高兴感,而不会产生"昏"的感觉. 这被确定为约75%的N2O和25%的O2. 较高的N2O水平开始导致音响失明和幻觉感觉, 而75/25比例导致持续且荒谬的高兴和麻木感. 氧化物 (40),氧气 (187):组合 (3),工作场所 (51)

This mixture was a blast to inhale and provided constant euphoria. Since our gas mixing manifolds had digital pressure-indicating devices (variable partial pressures 70-100%), I experimented on finding the optimum N2O concentration with a pure O2 balance which produced the highest level of euphoria without the 'black-out' feeling progressing. This was determined to be ~75% N2O & 25%O2. Higher levels of N2O began to cause audio black-out & hallucinatory feelings wheras the 75/25 proportion resulted in a constant & ridiculous level of euphoria and numbness.

一个小小的决定会对一个人的一生产生多大的影响. 对于那些"坚强"的吸毒者来说, 这似乎不是什么大不了的事, 像许多青少年一样, 我也曾经使用过维科丁和瓦. 由于我以前曾经使用过瓦, 我没想到洛拉塞帕姆会有这么大的效果, 我在爸爸的衣柜里寻找一个领带. 我注意到色的药瓶, 想这可能是我父亲最近背部问题所留下的可代因. 而是一瓶洛拉塞帕姆. 我打开盖子,拿出6片1毫克药片,一侧印有"沃森". 我最近有过很多事情. 我的父母离婚了, 面临严重的经济问题. 我决定带一个或两个去舞会, 只是为了减轻压力, 我把另外四个用于其他可能有帮助的情况. 我把其余的药物放进我的钱包里, 然后在三点半前离开去女朋友家看电影, 还有三个人要来 (一个没有约会的女孩,另一个带着男朋友的女孩). 她的房子里一切都很好,除了一个不可抗拒的冲动去滚动在湿草. 我决定这不是一个好主意. 我们放弃了电影,只吃了一点,聊了聊. 我们决定在6点穿好衣服. 舞会在7点开始, 距离我女朋友家很远. 我有机会在6点10分使用所. 我决定再服用两种药, 因为一种似乎没有那么大的效果. 它们足够小,所以我不需要用任何液体来吞下它们. 我们不得不晚些时候离开, 因为其中一个女孩有问题与她的衣服. 这就是事情变得有点模糊的地方. 我不记得开车去参加舞会的会议中心. 我不是在开车. 我确实记得我停在那个地方, 我不记得交了罚单和身份证, 舞蹈开始了. 我记不起任何特定的东西, 即使我尝试了大约一个半小时. 我记得从钱包里拿出其他药, 任何我能记住的经历都与梦境无关. 我和女朋友在街上散步, 我见过一位老师, 想和她聊天, 我记得唯一的好处是,如果有一首歌我真的喜欢, 这是一种令人惊叹的感觉, 现在我已经不记得那种感觉了. 在晚上10点. 舞会结束了. 我很模糊地记得舞会结束后走到车上, 在车里,那个抽真的让我的女朋友笑了,所以我觉得这很有趣. 我记得我女朋友的告诉我吃一茶匙糖来消除抽. 我记得我到她家吃了糖. 显然这有所帮助, 因为我被告知我的抽停止后. 在那之后,我只能记得接下来发生的事情的5%. 我告诉你的每一件事, 都是我的女朋友 (从现在起, 我们只能叫她N) 告诉我的. 我很抱歉没有任何时间框架. 听N和她的朋友说话. 我只是想让她说话. 给我们做了炸. 她给我我的,她问我是否想要番茄. 他们的谈话如下: :你想要一些番茄吗? 我:没有 尼:你只是要吃它们? 我:是的 五分钟后 我:我想要一些番茄. (拿着番茄放在桌子上之后) 我:末. 你好吗? 为什么不躺在沙发上. 我:可以. 我躺在沙发上. 她和朋友开始谈论一个英语项目. 我还没有收到海报. 我: (我会坐起来) 张贴? 答:是的,英语项目. 我:可以. 我穿上了正常的衣服, 把衣服放进了沃尔玛的袋子里. 躺下一会儿后, 我站起来拥抱N, 听起来很好把我带回家,所以她告诉我把衣服放在车库里的车里. 我做到了,走在车后面. 我在车库的地板上吐. 我确实记得因为某种原因而吐. 打电话给我爸爸, 告诉他她会让我在她家睡觉, 接下来我记得是醒来在N的沙发上. 我环顾四周,发现她睡在地板上. 我又有了抽. 尼:你想我给你一些水吗? 我:没有. (大约30秒后) 我:,N? 是的? 我又睡着了,没有回答,吓得我一半死. 我不记得这次的谈话, 于是我开始下来, 于是第二天早上7点半, 我还记得接下来发生的事情. 我显然是足够清醒走上楼梯到我的床上我睡到大约12:00 我当时已经清醒了, 但仍然难以保持清醒, 眼睛的视力也会分裂, 我不停地在电脑和床上做我平时做的事情. 我最终睡着了直到下午四点左右. 在那里我认为自己大部分是清醒和功能. 我开始说话时, 完全不懂为什么N的没有带我去医院. 只有我知道我服用了镇静剂. 我父亲 (前毒品官) 很快就会发现, 如果她把我带到医院, 我肯定会因为药物滥用而被治疗, 我甚至无法描述我那天晚上的行为可能带来的后果. 很少剂量确实对我的意志力产生了很强的影响, 剂量相对较低, 如果我那天晚上没有轻微服用过量, 我的行为确实很糟糕, 我本可以做一些非常愚蠢的事情. 保持安全. 附註: 如果故事有漏洞,我很抱歉. 药物 - 洛拉泽帕姆 (79):第一次 (2),不好的旅行 (6),过量服用 (29),各种 (28)

Jul 17, 2013. erowid.org/exp/69361 DOSE: T+ 0:00 1 mg oral Pharms - Lorazepam (pill / tablet) T+ 2:55 2 mg oral Pharms - Lorazepam (pill / tablet) T+ 4:00 3 mg oral Pharms - Lorazepam (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 115 lb It's amazing how much a small decision could impact someone's life forever. It may not seem like such a big deal to the 'hard-core' drug users, but it changed my life forever. I have a couple experiences with over-the-counter highs (benadryl) like a lot teenagers. I've had one or two experiences with Vicodin and Valium also. Since I have had experiences with Valium before, I wasn't expecting lorazepam to have that much effect, but I was very wrong. It was the day of the Who's Who dance at my high school and I was looking for a tie in my dad's closet. I noticed the orange pill bottle and thought it might be some leftover codeine my dad had had from his recent back problems. Instead it was a bottle of lorazepam. I opened the cap and took out 6 of the 1 mg tablets with 'WATSON' imprinted on one side. I had been under a lot lately. My parents were splitting up and facing major financial problems. I decided I wanted to take one or two for the dance just to relieve some stress and I just wanted to have some fun with my girlfriend. I took the extra four for any other situations where it would help. At around 3:15, I took one of the pills with a banana and peanut butter sandwich and put the rest inside my wallet before leaving for my girlfriend's house at 3:30 to watch a movie and eat before the dance. 3 other people were coming (one girl without a date, and another girl with her boyfriend.) Everything at her house was fine except a had a irresistible urge to go roll around in the wet grass. I decided it was not a good idea. We ditched the movie and just ate a little and talked. At 6:00 we decided to get dressed. The dance started at 7:00 and the location was quite a awhile from my girlfriend's house. I got a chance to use the bathroom at 6:10. After I changed I decided to take two more of the pills because one didn't seem to have that much of an effect. They were small enough so I didn't need to down them with any liquid. We had to leave late because one of the girls had a problem with her dress. This is where things got a little fuzzy. I don't remember the car trip to the convention center where the dance was being held. I was not driving. I do, however, remember pulling up to the place and getting out of the car. I have no recollection of handing in the ticket and showing my ID, but I remember waiting for the girls to turn in their shoes. We then socialized a little while; the dance got started. I couldn't remember anything specific if I tried for about maybe an hour and a half. I then remember getting the other pills out of my wallet and somehow without making it obvious downed them all at once. Any experiences from then on that I can remember are indistinguishable from dreams. This includes seeing the dance club perform, my girlfriend and I walking around, my seeing a teacher and wanting to go talk to her but for some reason not being able to, etc. The one positive thing I remember was if there was I song I really liked, I would hold onto my girlfriend, close my eyes, and dance. It was the most amazing feeling and some very positive closed-eye visuals were present. I can't remember the sensation much now anymore as with anything else. At 10:00 P.M. The dance was over. I very vaguely remember walking to the car after the dance with the worst hiccups. In the car, the hiccups were really making my girlfriend laugh so I thought it was funny. When discussing what happened what happened with my girlfriend I remembered my girlfriend's mom telling me to eat a teaspoon of sugar to get rid of the hiccups. I remember getting to her house and eating the sugar. Apparently it helped because I was told my hiccups stopped after that. After that, I can only remember 5% of what happened next. Every thing I tell you is from what my girlfriend (let's just call her N from now on) has told me. I apologize that there aren't any time frames to go off. I went to sit at the table and talk, but rather instead a just kinda put my head down and listened to N and her friend talk. N's mom then made us some chicken fries. When she gave me mine, she asked if I wanted ketchup. The conversation followed like this: N's mom: Do you want some ketchup? Me: No N: You're just going to eat them plain? Me: Yeah. (5 minutes later) Me: I want some ketchup. (After getting the ketchup and putting it on the table) Me: Mustard. N's mom: Are you okay? Why don't you go lay down on the couch. Me: Okay. I went and layed on the couch. N and her friend started talking about an English project. N's friend: I haven't even gotten my poster. Me: (I would sit up) Poster? N: Yes, the English project. Me: Okay. Now sometime during all this I had changed back into my regular clothes and put my dress clothes in a Wal-Mart bag. After laying down for awhile I got up and hugged N who was still sitting at the table. N's mom sounded like it was a good idea to bring me home by that time so she told me to put my clothes in the car which was in the garage. I did that and walked behind the car. I then threw-up on the garage floor. I do remember throwing up for some reason. N's mom called my dad and told him that she was going to let me sleep at her house because she didn't think a car ride would be good for me. The next thing I remember is waking up on N's couch. I looked around and noticed she was sleeping on the floor. I had the hiccups again. N: Do you want me to get you some water? Me: No. (About 30 seconds later) Me: Hey N? N: Yes? I fell asleep again without replying, scaring N half to death. I do not remember this conversation and N has told me there were a couple of conversations similar to this through-out the night. I started to come down for the high, and at I'm guessing 7:30 the next morning, N's mom brought me home. I can remember bits and pieces of what happened next. I was apparently sober enough to walk up the stairs to my bed where I slept till about 12:00. I was pretty sober by then, but still had trouble staying awake and my ability to text N was difficult. Every now and then my vision would split up and I would see double of everything. I kept going back and forth to my computer to my bed trying to do what I normally do. I eventually fell asleep till about 4:00 p.m. Where I considered my self mostly sober and functional. It's an absolute mystery why N's mom didn't bring me to the hospital when I started slurring my speech and not making any sense. No one else but me knew I was high on sedatives. My dad (who's an ex-narcotic officer) would've spotted it in seconds just from the stories from N's mom, but she's said nothing except that I threw up. If she would've brought me to the hospital I would've definitely been treated for substance abuse and would've put me in all sorts of problems such as suicide watch, which would've totally overwhelmed my parents to the point where they would completely lose all hope for me. I can't even describe the possible consequences of my actions that night. The very small dose definitely had a very strong effect on my will power with a relatively low dose and I'm sure if I would've had more of the drug I would've taken it all. With my small body weight and empty stomach, I could have easily over-dosed if I hadn't already, mildly, that night. My actions were definitely screwed up and I could've done something really stupid. Be safe. P.S. Sorry if the story has holes in it.

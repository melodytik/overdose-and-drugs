It's been a while since I've I taken any psychoactive substances, the last time I took a psychedelic was in 2015. Now that I'm 33 yo, I wanted to re-discover some substances in a less festive context. I wanted to test a dissociative similar to Ketamine and I turned to 2F-DCK which is the closest I've read on the net, I also know that the product acts longer than Ketamine but is less potent. Nevertheless, my tolerance to dissociatives is at zero and the only real experiences I've had with dissociatives were with DXM and Methoxetamine, unfortunately I can't tell you the effects I felt any more, so it's as if everything was new to me. Here's a little trip report or rather notes I took during my experience: Dosage: - 12.5mg (Light) [According to PsychonautWiki] Method of administration: Oral Duration: 2h30 to 5h00. Ingestion time: 22:32 Estimated End time: 3:30 AM ~ Oral intake of a 50mg 2F-DCK tablet, cut in 4 parts. I take a piece of about 12.5mg. I took 1mg of Xanax around 5:00 pm and 80mg of caffeine around 8:30 pm. Took the substance at 22:32. Effects: + 00:15m: I'm starting to feel the effects when I play World of WarCraft. My fingers are a little sweaty, a little excited about the effect. Mental clarity: OK + 00:30m: I feel a bit “warmer”. I also feel a little lighter physically. It's not unpleasant or pleasant, it's fairly neutral. + 00:30m: I feel a bit “warmer”. I also feel a little lighter physically. It's not unpleasant or pleasant, it's fairly neutral. No visuals noted, it's easy to establish one's thoughts. I decide to change game. I find it hard to play Hearthstone, the music is nice to listen to. I'm hot and cold at times, and I'm sweating quite a bit (maybe stress?). In fact, why do I stress? There's no reason... Hot/cold sensation and still a little lightness in my body. My legs are warm, which is quite pleasant. My mental faculties don't seem totally impaired. I can still think in a normal way, but it takes me longer to find my words. I can also feel a bit physically "heavy". My ears feel warm, which is nice. 00:20 : Feeling like the effects are wearing off? I want to redrop but I fear that the comeup will be stressful again. 00:41 : I'm a flat spaghetti. Like a redrop craving but no that will be for another time with a friend. 00:57 : Time seems to pass slowly, then quickly. The music is incredible tho. I don't have any more shivers or boring sensations in my chest. 1:22: I was able to go down to the bathroom and get a bottle of water quietly. Apart from a physical effect and heavy eyes, I don't feel much anymore. At times it feels like I'm in some cotton. Calm, neutral state of mind - I was a little apprehensive at the start of the experiment. I've noticed that when I'm talking on Discord in a group of people, I use very long sentences to say very little or nothing. It's now 1:57 a.m. and I feel 80% sober, the music is still catchy but I no longer have any physical effects like heat, for example. In terms of physical effects, at first I felt a slight tingling in my fingers, and an impression of a change in body temperature (between hot and cold, but mainly hot). My heart was speeding up a little which made me nervous. A little pressure on the chest appeared, but this is most probably linked to stress and apprehension about the substance. This sensation subsided as the hours went by. Still no visuals with eyes closed or open. I'll try again at 12.5mg with a friend now that I know what to expect. On the whole it was OK even if I didn't particularly like the high, I also notice that the product acts in waves. There were moments when I felt sober and then a few minutes later, I felt in a second state. End of trip: 02:03 a.m. I think I might be sensitive to dissociatives. Peace out and take care!

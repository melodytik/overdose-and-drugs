美国 我被处方给了Fioricet [乙氨基 (325毫克),布塔尔比塔尔 (50毫克),咖啡因 (40毫克) ]的一般形式治疗我的偏头痛. 我通常会把它们从脑海里出来, 我今天凌晨四点左右醒来时发烧和头痛, 由于Imitrex通常是为了在偏头痛发作之前切断偏头痛, 我很担心,因为我已经被明确警告过它们会很容易上, 我在下午1点左右服用了第一粒药 (全剂量为两粒). 我开始在1:30左右感觉到一些东西, 但仍然很痛苦, 所以我等到2点 (当我开始更充分地感觉到药物的效果) 然后服用第二个. 我在工作时用Paxil的短暂时间里, 便会出现轻微的幻觉, 我子还有些硬, 疼痛, 但最严重的疼痛已经消失. 我的手感觉很重,有点拙, 几乎像醉酒一样, 我身体感到轻松和沉重, 我的情绪状态很平静,很冷静, 总之,我感觉很好. 我有很多东西在我的身体中被打破,紧张或损坏, 这不是一种壮观的药物, 但是在缓解疼痛和放松方面是有效的... 另外,它会很好地配合一些可爱的醉酒花. 其他:

Mar 19, 2016. erowid.org/exp/62518 DOSE: T+ 0:00 325 mg oral Acetaminophen (pill / tablet) T+ 0:00 50 mg oral Butalbital (pill / tablet) T+ 0:00 40 mg oral Caffeine (pill / tablet) T+ 1:00 325 mg oral Acetaminophen (pill / tablet) T+ 1:00 50 mg oral Butalbital (pill / tablet) T+ 1:00 40 mg oral Caffeine (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 155 lb I was prescribed Fioricet [acetaminophen (325 mg), butalbital (a barbiturate, 50 mg), and caffeine (40 mg)] in the generic form for my migraines. My Imitrex prescription usually kicks them out of my head, but occasionally I need the 'big guns' to get relief. I woke up with a fever and a pre-migraine headache at about 4 AM this morning, and had troubled fever dreams until about noon. I woke up with a full blown migraine and as Imitrex is usually intended to cut off a migraine before it goes crazy I decided to take the Fioricet. I was apprehensive as I had been warned in no uncertain terms that they were very addictive and she had battled with other patients because she refused to give them more than a monthly refill. So, in pain and anxious, I took the first pill (the full dosage is two pills) at around 1 PM. I began to feel a little something around 1:30, but was still in a great deal of pain, so I waited until two (when I began to more fully feel the effects of the drug) and took the second one. My family has a history of sensitivity to drugs (I hallucinate slightly on a low dose of Benadryl and my thankfully short stint on Paxil involved words sliding off the page of the computer at work) so I was worried about the potential side effects as the hallucinations I have experienced on pharmaceutical shave been less than pleasant. Around 3 my pain was almost entirely gone, my neck was still slightly stiff and sore but the worst of the pain had evaporated. My hands felt (and still feel, now around 4 PM) heavy and somewhat clumsy, an almost drunkish feeling but without a loss of mental clarity. My body feels relaxed and heavy, a loose feeling I have once or twice felt on higher doses of Valerian and melatonin. My emotional state is calm and very chill, not quite flat but not many bumps in it either. In short, I feel good. I have a number of things broken, strained or damaged in my body and I feel quite free from them at this moment. This is not a spectacular drug, but it is efficacious in terms of pain relief and relaxation... also, it would go wonderfully with some lovely heady bud.

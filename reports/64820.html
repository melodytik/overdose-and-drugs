我是一个重达158磅的男性, 作为一个草药学家, 类型的psychonaut我对新的经验是开放的. 这是我的故事. 在连续四天摄入10克卡瓦之前, 我不规则地使用了卡瓦卡瓦3次. 这种咖啡是从网上卖家那里购买的 (不愿透露姓名). 所有的粉末, 没有杂的根在砂和子. 在水中三分钟. 简单的 我一口就喝完了. 诚实地说,味道并没有那么糟糕, 接着是轻微麻木的嘴唇. 随之而来的放松让我感到温暖的,令人愉快,我知道它的作用. 我的头脑是清晰的,放松,感觉社交. 听得最好的是技术音乐,然后是乡村音乐,然后是说唱,最后是摇滚. 技术音乐充满了我 (清晰) 的头脑深沉的音调, 清晰度令人惊叹. 乡村是非常放松的, 我完全听歌词, 我通常不这样做. 享受歌手的声音. 拉普,在技术领域,节奏变得令人讨厌后重复. 岩石,我不能忍受,我只是不喜欢它在卡瓦. 让我来澄清一下旧石头和新石头. 旧的岩石 (亨德里克斯) 是比新的 (ETC...) 岩石更耐受. 这种快乐是温和的. 我没有问题. 在技术上,轻轻摇摇头是很有趣的. 时间慢慢流逝,晚上7点到9点似乎是永恒. 我在12点钟就上床睡觉了. 我还没有意识到, 两个小时后我会醒来, 在今晚之前, 我曾经有过轻微的失眠. 但没有像这样的. 这使得夜晚更加糟糕. 我几乎无法形容这种感觉是如何回来如此强烈. 随着呼吸短促, 伴随着呼吸短促的焦虑. 我立即开始查看是否是卡瓦的副作用. 确实是这样. ',好吧,现在是凌晨3点,我有这个狗. 我发现"重度使用者"比每天服用300毫克更容易出现这些症状. 所以... 我是一个沉重的使用者甚至没有一个完整的星期,我得到这个. 我真的很生气. 我不想认为这完全是卡瓦, 绿茶和前一天晚上两袋香茶. 绿茶会让人呼吸短促, 我几乎没有足够的时间来消除这种副作用. 所以没有了. 我从来没有用过香, 直到前一晚. 虽然我可能不同意, 所以没有了. 所以剩下的是卡瓦. 我直到5点半,睡在一个袋子的香. (早晨) ,对于报告说第二天你会清醒的头脑, 我觉得自己有点昏昏欲睡,又出现了时间扭曲. 我在9点半醒来回去睡觉,醒来10点半,认为是12点半. 我只能睡5个小时. 胃部不太好, 但可以忍受, 我开始用水冲洗自己. 我在沙发上放松一下, 去剃须/洗. 这就是我注意到它的地方, 我注意到它的光, 我变得更加情绪化 (而不是抑郁), 我能做些什么来改善我的生活,欣赏我拥有的东西等等. 我下楼发现自己四处, 想知道', 这可以改进'他们怎么能让它这样工作?' 在外面的颜色有点生动. 更新:我注意到我的鼠标手上有一个很小的泡. 这可能是皮肤干燥的副作用. 我把Aveeno涂在我的手和手臂上, 另一种:我认为我再也不会尝试这种方法, 这是一件好事. 适量使用. 其他: 卡瓦 (30):健康问题 (27),一般 (1),单独 (16)

Feb 4, 2016. erowid.org/exp/64820 DOSE: 15.5 g oral Kava (liquid) BODY WEIGHT: 158 lb Hello, I am a 158lb Male who has just started to use Kava Kava as a relaxant at the end of the day. Being an herbalist, pyschonaut of sorts I am open for new experiences. Here is my story. I have used Kava Kava 3 times irregularly, before taking in 10grams of Kava for four days in a row. The kava was purchased from an online vendor (who will remain nameless.) It was Papua New Guinea 'Premium Kava' -- not the strongest but by far not the weakest. All was powdered, no messing with roots in a mortar and pestle. Just straining, kneading in water for 3 minutes. Simple. I drank it in one gulp. To be truthful it didn't taste THAT bad but I was used to it. Instantly the tipper part of my tongue was numb followed by mildly numb lips. Relaxation ensued leaving me with a warm shiver, delightful, I know its working. My head is clear, relaxed, feeling sociable. Techno seemed to be the best to listen to, then country, then rap, last was rock. The techno filled my (clear) head with deep tones that I would not be able to get off a dank bowl. The clarity was amazing. Country was really relaxing, I was listening to the lyrics fully, which I usually do not do. Enjoying the singers voice. Rap, around the area of techno, beats got annoying after being repeated. Rock, I couldn't stand it, I just didn't like it on kava. Well let me clarify old rock and new rock. The old rock (Hendrix) was a lot more tolerable than the new (ETC...) rock out. The euphoria was mild. I had no problem with it. On techno it was pretty fun to sway your head lightly. Aside from the music the time went by pretty slow..7PM to 9PM felt like an eternity. I went to bed at exactly 12 o' clock. What I did not know was that I would wake up 2 hours later to find myself in the worst state of insomnia I've been in. Prior to this night I have had light cases of insomnia a couple beforehand. But nothing like this. Along with the insomnia came shortness of breath which made the night even worse. Being a prior asthmatic I cant nearly describe how this felt coming back so strong. With the shortness of breath, along came anxiety about the shortness of breath. So I start looking up immediately if this is a side effect of kava. Sure enough it was. 'Oh great its 3AM and I'm having this shit.' On further investigation I find out 'Heavy Users' are more likely to have these symptoms rather than someone taking 300mg a day. So... I'm a heavy user for not even a full week and I get this. Oh boy am I fucking pissed. Not wanting to think it's fully kava I look up what I've been drinking lately. Which is: Green Tea and the other night Two Bags of Chamomile Tea. Green Tea has shortness of breath..I hardly take enough to lift that side effect. So that's out. Chamomile I haven't used in a really long time until the night prior. It 'may' not agree with me but I severely doubt that. So that's out. So that leaves the Kava. I'm up till 5:30, go to bed on a bag of chamomile. (Morning) Wow, for reports saying the next day you'll be clear headed, that wasn't the case for me. I was drowsy, somewhat sluggish, and yet again time distortion occurs. I woke up at 9:30 going back to sleep, awakening at 10:30, thinking its 12:30. So I'm left with 5 hours of sleep. My stomach wasnt at its best but it was tolerable, my respiratory condition subsided which was a good thing. I proceded to flush myself out with water. I relaxed on the couch for a bit, went to shave/take a shower. This is where I noticed IT, I noticed the afterglow, it was exactly like the afterglow after mushrooms. I got more emotional (not depressed), was thinking about everything I could think of. What I could do to improve my life, appreciating what I have etc.. I went downstairs to find myself wandering around wondering 'hmm that could be improved'' how do they get that to work that way?' I was interested in everything my eyes laid on. Outside the colors were slightly vivid. Update: I notice a very small blister on my mouse hand. This could be a side effect of dry skin starting/started. I put Aveeno on my hands and arms to prevent any future skin side effects. Other: I think I will never try this again just because of the side effects that I got from it. It was nice while it lasted. Use in moderation.

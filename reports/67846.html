我一直是一个相当令人担忧的人只要我能记住. 我很容易因为一些小事而烦躁, 我认为这是正常的, 我开始经常有恐慌发作. 我很高兴看到你. 据他们告诉我, 我和父亲都患有抑郁症/焦虑症/恐慌症, 我认为这是完全合乎逻辑的, 这种东西可能有遗传倾向. 我开始寻求我大学医疗中心的精神病医生的帮助. 他诊断出我患有普遍焦虑症和恐慌症. 他给我写了一份处方, 给我服用0.5毫克克隆纳西巴姆 (一般的克隆诺, 每天服用两次) 和50毫克塞特拉林 (一般的佐洛夫特). 我几乎立即注意到它的好处. 开始服用这种药物后的1-2天内, 我感到的焦虑几乎完全消失. 我的恐慌发作消失了. 我觉得自己的生活更清晰,更无忧,更愉快. 美国 药品 - 克洛纳泽巴姆 (125),药品 - 塞特拉林 (88):组合 (3),烂的经验 (4),健康益处 (32),治疗意图或结果 (49),医疗用途 (47),不适用 (38)

Sep 27, 2009. erowid.org/exp/67846 DOSE: 1 mg oral Pharms - Clonazepam (daily) 50 mg oral Pharms - Sertraline (daily) BODY WEIGHT: 240 lb I've been a pretty worrisome person for as long as I can remember. I get worked up easily over little things, and I find it very easy for me to panic in situations that aren't even that bad. I had thought this was normal, though, to a point. About the time I left for college, I started getting panic attacks on a regular basis. When my parents found out about this, they told me to see help immediately. From what they told me, both my mother and my father suffered from some combination of depression/anxiety/panic disorder, which come to think of it, didn't really surprise me. I think it'd be perfectly logical that this sort of thing could have a hereditary predisposition. I started seeking the aid of the psychiatrist at my university's health center. After about a 1-hour session, he diagnosed me with generalized anxiety disorder and panic disorder. He wrote me a scrip for some .5mg clonazepam (generic Klonopin, taken twice daily) and 50mg sertraline (generic Zoloft). I noticed its benefits almost immediately. Within 1-2 days of starting this medication regimen, my anxiety that I felt was lifted almost completely. My panic attacks went away. My life seemed clearer, more carefree, and more enjoyable.

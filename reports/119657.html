Apr 4, 2026. erowid.org/exp/119657 DOSE: T+ 0:00 ~2.4 mg sublingual 25P-NBOH (liquid) T+ 1:00 50 mg Ketamine T+ 2:00 40 mg Pharms - Diazepam 25p-NBOH Nasal Spray (2.4mg) Substance: 25P-NBOH (Novice Chem) Dosage: 6 sprays (total~2.4 mg) Route: nasal sprays Setting: Alone at home, evening Experience Report: T+0:00 – I administered 6 sprays of 25p-NBOH sublingually, totaling approximately 2.4 mg. Within 10 minutes, I began to feel an unusual jitteriness in my limbs. My hands and legs started shaking and pulsing in a way that felt beyond simple nervous energy. It was almost like my body was preparing for something intense. T+0:15 – The physical tremors intensified. I noticed my heart rate increase slightly, and a mild sense of nausea accompanied the tremors. My thoughts began racing, and I realized I had underestimated the potency. The sensation was sharper and more insistent than anything I had experienced with classic psychedelics. T+0:30 – The onset became visually apparent. I threw up, something I never do on psychadelics. My vision was flooded with rapidly swirling pastel colors, almost blinding in their intensity. They twisted and danced across the walls, ceiling, and even my closed eyelids, though CEV less pronounced then mushrooms, OEV more intense then 500ug LSD. The visuals were accompanied by uncontrollable laughter as I watched an episode of Neighbors on HBOMAX (twice, something I never do, but I missed so much the first watch, I had to re watch, it was just as hilarious the second go). It was euphoric in a chaotic, almost overwhelming way. I felt like I was caught in a loop of sensory overload. T+1:00 – The peak was overwhelming as I was finishing my first round of the episode. Tremors persisted, but 50mg ketamine helped. My body pulsed with energy, and I could not sit still until the ketamine took effect, still, my body was tight and tense. My thoughts became disjointed, bouncing between hilarity and mild panic. Every surface seemed to breathe, swirl, or shimmer with an intensity that was more aggressive than any LSD experience I had before. The laughter continued in fits, uncontrollable and almost absurd. It was euphoric but bordering on exhausting. T+2:00 – I realized I had undoubtedly taken too much. While the visuals and euphoria were incredible, they were far too intense to maintain comfort. My body felt overstimulated, and the shaking made any attempt to relax difficult. I considered my options and decided to use 40 mg of Valium to help bring the intensity down. T+2:15 – The Valium started to work. My body gradually calmed, and the visual chaos began to subside. Tremors and pulsing slowed. My laughter reduced to occasional giggles as I settled into a more manageable state. The overall euphoria remained but became much more serene and comfortable. T+3:00 – Effects had largely subsided. I felt drained but euphoric, reflecting on the experience. I realized a lower dose (perhaps half) would likely have been far more enjoyable I realized a lower dose (perhaps half) would likely have been far more enjoyable —allowing the intense visuals and laughter without overwhelming the body and mind. Conclusion: 25P-NBOH is extremely potent and physically intense. Tremors and pulsing can make the experience physically challenging, and the visual and emotional intensity can be overwhelming for a novice. For those considering this substance, lower doses are strongly recommended to achieve a euphoric, manageable experience. Valium proved effective in terminating an overly intense trip. Rating: 8/10 for intensity, 6/10 for overall enjoyment at this dose.

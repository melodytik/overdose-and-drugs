到了早上8点半, 我偷偷地拿到了12个10毫克的Temazepam囊. 我在上学前拿了六个, 但因为我没有什么可以容纳水的东西, 于是我又开始服用其他6个 (使我摄入的总量达到10个). 这份报告是在事件发生后写的,所以我会记得我能记得的. 到了上午九点课结束时, 我几乎无法控制我的眼睛, 它们在四处走动, 我开始后悔服用10个这些当我从来没有做过他们之前, 我只知道没有什么重大事情发生, 我只是被麻醉了 (视力模糊,言语模糊,失方向). 我觉得我在睡觉和清醒, 这种药物如果一个人不睡觉, 我真的不知道为什么我吃了10个, 没有真正的理由, 我不知道你需要服用多少才能真正生病, 十个可能是坏的,也可能是无关紧要的,我不知道. 其他: 发布时间:2020年2月14日 药品 - 泰马泽帕姆 (159):第一次 (2),学校 (35)

Feb 14, 2020. erowid.org/exp/38351 DOSE: T+ 0:00 40 mg oral Pharms - Temazepam (pill / tablet) T+ 0:30 60 mg oral Pharms - Temazepam (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 58 kg Around 8:30am I managed to rustle up 12 10mg Temazepam caps. On heading to school I took 6 before class (9am) but had to take them dry as I had nothing to hold water in, which in turn made me bring up 2 of them. At 9:30 I felt almost completely normal so I proceeded to take the other 6 (bringing the ingested total to 10). This report is written well after the event so I'll remember what I can. Toward the end of the 9am class I started to feel quite light-headed and kinda dizzy. I could barely control my eyes which were going all over the place and I was starting to regret taking 10 of these when I'd never done them before and all I knew was that they were pretty much sleeping pills. From around 10am on everything is pretty much a blur, all I know is nothing major happened and I was just pretty much sedated (blurred vision, slurred speech, disorientated). It lasted all day and I just felt like I was asleep and awake at the same time, which I guess is pretty much a given with this drug if one does not sleep. I really don't know why I took 10 of these, there was no real reason and nowadays I probably wouldn't do it, either because I'm paranoid or because it actually is dangerous. I don't know how much you need to take to get really sick or even if one can get sick from them. 10 could be bad or 10 could be nothing, I don't know.

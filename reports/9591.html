我尝试过许多药物, 但从来没有经历过如此激烈和不寻常的旅行. 我猜大部分经验来自于5-meo-DMT, 我使用咖啡清器提取了代胺. 我决定尝试一种新的组合, 我很高兴尝试这项技术, 我已经准备好了一个非常高兴的晚上. 旅行30分钟后, 我开始感觉到身体的温暖. 这种感觉最多持续了一个小时. 经过了几个小时, 我只感觉到一点速度. 很奇怪, 因为我确信MDMA是好的 (测试), 我当时想起我一直在喝圣. 在过去的一周里每天早上都喝红茶. 由于它是一种轻微的SSRI, 它可以解释失去了MDMA效应吗? 可能,但茶是如此微弱,难以相信. 在旅程的第一部分, 我记得我还有50毫克的5-meo-DMT, 我读过一个报告, 某人吸入了9毫克, 所以我吃了整50毫克的药, 燃烧的感觉令人难以置信, 我记得地走到厨房,然后回到我的房间,躺在床上. 感觉很高兴, 但同时也非常丧. 我认为我过量服用或更糟糕的是, 这种感觉难以形容. 我的思想集中在一个感觉上,我无法思考. 感觉消失了,我起床,很高兴活着,去看电视. 我眼睛发, 有轻微的幻觉. 我发现我的整个身体都伤了. 我的脚受伤,我的背部,我的手指节;我的两个肩膀和我的两个部受伤和伤. 我回到我的房间里, 房间是一团糟. 电话线完全被开, 我的固定自行车 (重型) 被从通常的位置移动了大约一米. 浴室里也有一些瓶子被推倒了. 在那5分钟内发生了什么? 我不记得. 我还记得我觉得自己是个无辜的人. 我的自我已经消失了. 其他: 这种药物不仅仅仅是为了消毒,而且是为了消毒. 草 (142): 独自 (16), 列车毁和旅程灾难 (7), 健康问题 (27), 艰难的经历 (5), 组合 (3)

Dangerous & Intense Codeine, MDMA, 5-meo-DMT, & St.John's Wort (Tea) by EBS Citation:   EBS. "Dangerous & Intense: An Experience with Codeine, MDMA, 5-meo-DMT, & St.John's Wort (Tea) (exp9591)". Sep 23, 2001. erowid.org/exp/9591 DOSE: T+ 0:00 1 cup oral St. John's Wort (tea) T+ 5:30 125 mg oral MDMA (powder / crystals) T+ 5:45 120 mg oral Codeine (powder / crystals) T+ 9:45 50 mg insufflated 5-MeO-DMT (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 150 lb I have tried many substances but never had such an intense and unusual trip. I'm guessing that the bulk of the experience was from the 5-meo-DMT, but I want to try it alone just to be sure. I obtained 10 Tylenol 2's and extracted the codeine using a coffee filter. I decided to try a new combination and do the codeine along with a cap of pure MDMA. I was excited to try this because of the simultaneous dopamine and serotonin release. I was prepared for a very euphoric evening. About 30 mins into my trip I started feeling a warm body sensation. This feeling lasted for another hour at most. Another couple of hours passed but the MDMA never took full effect. All I felt was a bit of speed. This was very strange because I was sure that the MDMA was good (tested) and I haven't dosed in 3 months. I then remembered that I've been drinking St. John's Wort tea every morning for the past week. Since it's a mild SSRI, could it account for the lost MDMA effect? Possible, but tea is so weak, its hard to believe. Never during the first part of my trip did I feel even slight euphoria. I then remembered that I still had around 50mg of 5-meo-DMT, but smoking it was out of the question since I had no access to a DMT pipe. I've read a report of someone who insufflated 9mg and had a profound spiritual experience. So I took the whole 50mg and snorted it. The burning sensation was incredible, but it quickly went away and I started feeling very strange. I remember wobbling to the kitchen and then back to my room and lying down on the bed. It felt euphoric but at the same time EXTREMELY overwhelmed. I thought I was overdosing or worse, dying. The feeling is very hard to describe. My mind was concentrated on one feeling, I couldn't think. The feeling went away and I got up, glad to be alive and went to watch some T.V. My vision was shaking and I had mild hallucinations. I then noticed that my whole body was in bruises. My ankle was cut, my back, my knuckles; both my shoulders and both my hips were bruised and scratched. I got back to my room it was a mess. Things lying on the floor, telephone wire completely ripped out of the jack and my stationary bike (heavy) was moved about a meter from where it usually stood. The bathroom also had bottles knocked over. What happened during that 5(?) minutes? I don't remember. I recall feeling like I was nothing. My ego was gone.

Oct 2, 2015. erowid.org/exp/103039 DOSE: 200 mg insufflated S-Ketamine (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 190 lb Some background: Ketamine is commonly sold in racemic form, R+S isomers mixed. In a clinical study participants found that S-Isomer was almost always associated with positive feelings, while racemic was more mixed. The R-isomer inhibits the breakdown of the s-isomer leading to a longer, more drawn-out comedown. The R-isomer also contributes to visual distortions and delusions of grander that don't seem to be exist with s-isomer. Every sense I heard of s-isomer ketamine I knew I had to try it. I managed to find an extremely clean source of s-isomer k and obtained a gram. I insuflated about 100 mg on three occasions and then did 200mg to test the strength. I got very close to the hole on that trip. S-Isomer is interesting in that it is about 1.5 times as powerful as racemic ketamine (S and R isomer ketamine mixed together) but at the same time it is not a deliriant in any sense, which is very unlike racemic. Even at that high dose (my tolerance was very low) I could still see clearly and think completely clearly. Thoughts came and went and I reviewed them all in a totally positive light. Everything was good for a time. It was also highly pleasurable to feel my body melt away and be at peace with my mind. During this trip I had a musing to try meditation while under the influence of S-isomer K. That led me to this experience. I had a dry apple cider about an hour before taking the ketamine, and hadn't eaten for at least 5 hours. I laid out 150 mg in four lines, two for each nostril and swept the floor and laid a towel down to sit and meditate. I queued some 'meditation tones' from youtube, as I find these to be extremely beneficial to meditation. t= :00 insuflated two lines, one in each nostril laid back on the couch and let the drip slowly drip down my throat. t= :05 felt first effects, insuflated next two lines and again laid back until I felt the drip in my throat.

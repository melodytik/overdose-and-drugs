我尝试过几次LSD和, 但我从来没有像我结合高剂量的佐皮克隆和大麻那样幻觉. 我有Imovane的处方, 我通常在睡觉前服用2粒药, 那天晚上,我像往常一样吃了两粒 (15毫克) 药,但一个小时后我还在床上醒,所以又吃了一粒. 另一个小时过去了, 我仍然无法入睡, 我终于得到了一些睡眠,但只有3-4小时. 接下来的一天, 我感觉有点高, 虽然心情有些波动, 但总体上我感觉很好, 很平静, 我会为什么都哭,然后感觉好一分钟后. 我还有一些奇怪的小幻觉; 我会从眼角看到某人, 我没有多考虑, 在晚上,一个朋友来了,我们抽了一些大麻. 我没想到会发生这种情况. 我看着这支烟, 看到这支烟就像漫画中的彩虹螺旋, 我很惊,告诉我的朋友我看到什么, 他问我是否服用一些MDMA或迷幻药, 直到我意识到这是因为安眠药! 后来我有其他幻觉. 外面的雪开始形成动物. 我记得一只狮子和一只羊, 它们都相互影响. 我看着屋顶, 看到一些迷幻的形状, 有更多的,只是一个疯狂的. 我没有感觉到任何特别的除了平常的放松的心情吃草和平静的一天我一直感觉到. 我试着用同样的剂量和组合再次高, 但它从来没有工作. 我认为这与我没有睡过所有Imovane效应有关. 因为如果服用过多或与其他抑郁药物结合, 它们是危险的. 时间表: 112126 发布时间:2018年8月21日 药品 - - 佐皮克隆 (272):医疗用途 (47),抑郁症 (15),多天经验 (13),组合 (3),各种 (28)

Aug 21, 2018. erowid.org/exp/112126 DOSE: T+ 0:00 15 mg oral Pharms - Zopiclone (pill / tablet) T+ 1:00 7.5 mg oral Pharms - Zopiclone (pill / tablet) T+ 2:00 7.5 mg oral Pharms - Zopiclone (pill / tablet) T+ 24:00 smoked Cannabis BODY WEIGHT: 130 lb I have tried LSD and shrooms a few times, but I've never hallucinated like the day I combined a high dose of zopiclone and weed. I had a prescription of Imovane and I would usually take 2 pills before going to bed and I would fall asleep around 15 minutes later. That night, I took the two pills (15 mg) as usual, but an hour later I was still wide awake in my bed, so I took another one. Another hour had passed and I was still not able to sleep, so I took another one. I finally got some sleep, but only 3-4 hours. The day after, I was feeling a little high, like I was on a very low dose of psychedelics. I had some mood swings but I felt generally pretty good and peaceful, which is weird because I had depression. I would cry for nothing, then feel good the minute after. I also had some weird small hallucination; I would see someone from the corner of my eyes and when I turned my head around, there was no one. I didn't think much of it and went along with my day. During the evening, a friend came over and we smoked some weed. Holy hell did I not see that coming. It started when we were smoking it, I looked at the joint while taking a puff and I saw the joint as if it was a cartoon with a rainbow spiral on it, that was moving around it. I was very surprised and told my friend what I saw, he asked me if I took some MDMA or psychedelics, I said no and I didn't understand either. Until I realized that it was the sleeping pills! I've had various other hallucinations later. The snow outside began to form animals. I remember a lion and a sheep, there was other ones though. They were all interacting with each other. When I came back inside, I looked at the roof and I saw some psychedelic shapes, like a kaleidoscope. There was a lot more, just a crazy. I didn't feel anything particular other than the usual relax mood of taking weed and the peaceful one that I've been feeling all day. I've tried to get high again with the same dosage and combination, but it never worked. I believe that it has something do to with the fact that I didn't sleep through all the Imovane effects. Always be careful with sleeping pills because they are dangerous if too much are taken or combined with other depressants.

我不知道有没有人听说过可怕的消息... 我住在佛罗里达州, 在两个县 (我住在其中一个) 他们有新的药物检测方法. 没有更多的血液检测,没有更多的头发检测,没有更多的尿液检测. 新的测试是视网膜扫描. 很难相信, 但我向你发誓, 这被称为"通道点". 这台机器大约是老虎机的大小, 位于达德和马丁县. 这台机器据说花费2万5千美元, 测试的原理是这样的,首先你必须在机器中3次进行"基化". 测试只能用右眼进行. 你看着机器, 把前头放在一个平台上. 在短管的末端有一个绿色的点. 然后按一下绿色按开始测试. 机器会发出高音, 让你知道它找到了你的视网膜. 然后,绿灯向前向后从右向左移动6至8次 (这是为了跟踪眼睛的运动以及扫描视网膜). 然后点将返回中间8秒,让您有时间眼并重新滑眼睛 (您不能从声音发出时眼到此时眼,否则您将错误测试并不得不重新进行测试). 在8秒后,小点又在中心,四个大绿点闪在它周围. 大点是1/2秒的闪,它们又闪了6-8次 (这检查瞳孔膨胀). 机器会发出另一个高音声,告诉你你已经正确完成了测试 (并不意味着你已经通过了). 如果您在测试期间移动或眼,机器将发出错误声音,您将不得不重新测试. 在你通过测试后,你的结果会被打印出来. 结果要么说低风险 (您的眼睛测试足够接近您的基线,所以您的"低风险"是负面的,即您通过了) 或高风险 (您的眼睛没有足够接近您的基线). 如果您的风险低, 如果您是高风险患者,则必须等待5分钟并重新测试. 如果在第二次检查后, 您的风险较高, 视网膜扫描失败, 然后他们与实验室尿液测试进行交叉参考 (非常严格的尿液测试, 我每周都会做一次这样的测试, 我想知道有没有人知道如何打败这台机器, 但他们最好开始学习. 其他: 药物检测 (59), 其他 (136):不适用 (38), 一般 (1)

"The Horrible New Way: An Experience with Various (Drug Testing) (exp39873)". Erowid.org . Jan 18, 2005. erowid.org/exp/39873 DOSE: Various BODY WEIGHT: 140 lb I need help!!!! I dont know if anyone has heard about the horrible news... I live in Florida and in 2 counties (I live in one of them) they have a new way of drug testing. No more blood testing, no more hair testing, no more urine testing. The new test is a retinal scan. Yes, its hard to believe but I swear to you it is true (I'm stuck testing on it every week after going through Drug Court!). It as called 'pass-point'. The machine is about the size of a slot machine and is located in both dade and martin counties. This machine supposedly costs $25,000 and is impassable. The test works like this, first you must be 'baselined' into the machine 3 times. The test must be done with your right eye only. You look into the machine and rest your forhead on a platform. There is a green dot at the end of a short tube. You enter your # into the machine and line up your eye with the green dot, then you press a green button to begin the test. The machine will make a high-pitched sound letting you know it's found your retina. Then the green light moves back and forth from right to left 6-8 times (this is to track eye movement as well as scan the retina). The dot then returns to the middle for 8 seconds giving you time to blink and relubricate your eye (you cannot blink from the time of the sound to this time or you will fault the test and have to retake it). After the 8 seconds the small dot is in the center again and 4 large green dots flash around it. The large dots are 1/2 second flashes and they flash another 6-8 times (this checks for pupil dilation). If you did the test correctly the machine will make another high-pitched sound telling you that you took the test correctly (doesn't mean you passed....). If at any time you move or blink during the test the machine will make an error sound and you will have to retest. After you get through the test then your results are printed. The results either say low-risk (your eye tested close enough to your baseline so you are at 'low risk' of being negative aka you passed) or high-risk (your eye was not close enough to your baseline). If you low-risk, you have passed an you are done. If you are high-risk then you must wait 5 minutes and retest. If after you retest you get high risk for the 2nd time, you fail the retinal scan and then they crossreference it with a lab-urine test (very strict urine test the also tests for dilutes, additives, adulterants, ect.). I get this test once every week plus randoms!!! I want to know if anyone knows any ways to beat this machine so I can smoke a god damn blunt! Most people have never even heard of this since it is so new but they better start learning.

Nov 29, 2016. erowid.org/exp/109321 DOSE: IV Methoxmetamine IV alpha-PVP IV Amphetamines BODY WEIGHT: 140 lb Intravenous Mix I remember coming down off a month long binge doing big shots of MXM. I had just left a festival after taking a dose of acid . I'd also been drinking beer and smoking spice along with alpha PVP. I had also eaten amphetamine the first time I ever tried it. I felt it within thirty minutes and have never felt that way off anything I had ever taken before. Extreme energy and movements so fast I probably looked like a blur if you were recording me. So I get back home and I remember feeling ultra depressed and I just wanted to end it all. I loaded everything I had into one needle and did it. I experienced the most intense rush of my life. My eyeballs became ping pong balls slamming from side to side in my eye sockets. I started to hear the slamming and also the fluids rushing from my spine and coating my brain, my spine, neck and head were contracting making my head bend back. This lasted about two to five minutes. I remember going into shock and saying oh my god. After the eye slamming stopped I stood up and remember feeling really big like I grew two feet in all directions. My visuals were surprisingly normal and wasn't really feeling any body euphoria. I ended up walking outside and what I saw I will never forget. Transparent glowing human beings everywhere outside, especially in and around the trees. Some were dancing, some just standing still, others were pushing on tree branches that were swaying with the wind. It wasn't until I ended up saying out loud 'I see dead people' and that's when I started to hear very strange unexplainable noises. I became extremely paranoid and laid down next to my car on the pavement. I kept hearing trees cracking multiple times followed by a loud unsettling rumble as if it had fallen right next to me. I was looking up toward the trees that's when I noticed the huge swirling balls of energy or electricity. Counted about 8 of them all in a row right above my head just above the tree tops. I remember saying to my self what the f... I ended up walking around the neighborhood for a hour or so just watching the glowing transparent humans. I made it back to my room and that's when a crowd of them began walking into my room and taking seats all around.

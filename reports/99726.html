背景信息: 这是我第二次服用迷幻药. 我之前的药物使用经验是几个月前服用MDMA, 我和我的朋友最初打算去做, 也开始阅读4-HO-MiPT, 由于我们都同意DiPT似乎有短期效果,而MiPT持续时间更长,并且有显著不同的效果, 我知道我的耐受性已经比一般的"研究人员"稍高, 所以我决定和我的朋友一起, 这意味着将20毫克的4-HO-DiPT和20毫克的4-HO-MiPT放入一个单一的炸弹中, 我从前一天早上1点起就没吃过饭, 在旅行前后没有吸烟,吃东西或饮酒. 情况/药物详情: 坐在他住的朋友房间里, 但他的房东/亲属不在. 房间的另一端是他的电脑. 他大部分时间都躺在床上听着电脑上的音乐. 这两种物质可以被描述为毛的白色粉末. 结果/经验: 下午2点 服用40mg (总量) 炸弹与水 稍微恶心可能只是让我们知道它是口服活跃的. 时间感完全消失,直到晚上7点左右, 我和一位经验丰富的研究人员在一个安全的环境中, 我们采取了许多预防措施, 享受这个故事, 因为回想起来很有趣, 有时我们看着时钟,看起来像一个小时已经不到一分钟, 在窗上看到一些图案, 移动和轻微移动. 房子的窗户的稍微移动, 似乎呼吸. 我们听到一些民间/摇滚/歌手/作曲家的东西, 我认为这两种药物的高峰是同时发生的, 并且持续了一段时间, 对于OEV来说, 它们非常强大, 具有生动的图案, 门似乎越来越大,越来越小,越来越宽,越来越窄,越来越远. 电脑桌下面的房间的黑暗部分似乎是蓝色的. 不仅在上面出现,而且从上面流入房间周围的空间. 我失去了时间的感觉, 空间的感知也变得如此扭曲, 奇怪的是,偶尔在超级激烈的一小时后, 我猜大约1到1.5小时, 我觉得我应该是头,但我没事. 旅行的强度,视觉效果,这一切都很久以前, 我现在只能记住,因为我可以然后最重要的旅行的部分和有趣的故事的一天. 我记得在最初的几个小时内没有发生过什么事, 感觉很棒, 几乎是性感的, 电流的波浪会穿过我的身体, 不是从任何地方开始, 这绝对是奇怪的, 但值得. 这些都是最激烈的前几个小时, 有一个地方有点不对. 这是我第一次经历一次我不喜欢的旅程. 我看到模式在闭着眼睛改变. 随着不同的歌曲的播放, 我在倒时不太喜欢, 很难解释这些尸体是如何变形的, 但这就像是新的体从体中出来, 从无限的体中出来. 我开眼睛, 意识到我已经躺下很长一段时间了, 我走到电脑前, 换了一首歌, 感觉好一点, 没有什么可怕的,仍然有趣但有点令人不安. 旅行的另一个点... 住在朋友和他的叔叔和阿回来了, 试图进入, 我特别小心不要打破它, 就像上一扇门多年来被磨损, 我们只有半小时后才意识到, 他们几乎打电话给一个锁匠或警察看看是否有什么问题或门被打破, 为了我的朋友. 我下楼道歉, 门被锁住了, 这是我第一次离开房间, 周围的一切都在变形, 不像以前那样多彩, 他的阿跟我说话后说这是好的,我看了看这里,试图表现得正常,我们决定去试图找到外面的地方,享受旅行的剩余部分在和平... 我的朋友站在门口走廊下笑他的乳房. 他距离我大约只有一米,但看起来像一英里. 他的叔叔站在楼梯我的左边和他的在我面前. 我看着他和他的阿说话. 他的微笑告诉我他知道. 我的朋友显然被他笑的方式和他的和叔叔已经看到它之前所以不在乎但他们从来没有看到我做. 我变得如此偏执... 汗水从我身上流下来. 她问我生活是怎么回事, 如果我和女朋友还没问题. 在这个州,我无法提供这些细节. 朋友笑着说我很尬, 她告诉我闭嘴, 我以为我说了,但只想"她知道吗?"我们说了几秒钟. 她停下来问:"你有没有吃东西?" 但这完全是针对我的朋友, 她继续跟我说话, 问我一个问题, 我没有听到, 因为我凝视着她的微笑越来越大, 我注意到她已经停下来,等待我回复, 我开始道歉, 认为会有一些严重的后果, 她说没关系,因为我的朋友经常这样做, 我们赶紧走了出去... 这次旅行对我来说是非常糟糕的... 我们住在山上,有很多小公园,很多狗主人,很多孩子,附近有几所学校. 我还在沉醉. 我们冒险走向山顶的公园, 很快就决定要散步, 一个长个子的男人看起来像一个可怕的卡通尼科贝利克从GTAIV走出来走过我们没有注意到我们. 对于我来说,这次散步是非常陌生的, 因为对我来说很明显, 我们开始笑. 我们不得不保持安静. 我非常偏执. 听力敏感度的提高使得事情变得更加糟糕. 路上有一扇门打开, 感觉好像它就在我身后. 我不知道自己在哪里, 只是想着女朋友看到我在街上随机走来走去, 没有关系. 楼层房屋似乎向内倾斜, 虽然我在走下去, 但有时候我们爬上和下来的山似乎是垂直的. 有时我觉得自己会跌倒. 我们终于进去, 想知道为什么我的朋友坚持要我们出去, 旅行终于结束了,一旦我们进入了我只是希望它是一个平静的下车, 我只是躺下欣赏音乐的视觉效果. 结论:我可能会再做一次, 也许是我第一次独自旅行, 我觉得如果我自己去做的话, 我认为它是非常强烈的, 但从我服用的数量来看, 如果我服用了40毫克与MET, 我不喜欢外出或与人打交道, 副作用是微不足道的:通常的疲劳感,也许是饥饿和口渴,因为几个小时没有吃或喝 (除了一点水). 我希望你们喜欢我关于尬和错误计划的故事! 记住阅读所有关于安全性的指南, 尤其是在尝试组合时, 确保它们不会一起产生负面反应. 我们决定使用这种组合, 因为我们认为效果会很好地相互补充, 其他: 4-HO-DiPT (281),4-HO-MiPT My previous experience with drugs is taking MDMA a few times months ago and 4-HO-MET once, again months ago. My experience with MDMA didn't border on unusual and my experience with 4-HO-MET which I've reported on and was a +3 on the shulgin scale. I'd read up on 4-HO-DiPT which my friend and I were initially planning to do and also started reading up on 4-HO-MiPT since we also had acquired some of that. Because we both agreed the DiPT looked to have a short effect and the MiPT lasted considerably longer and had significantly different effects we decided to do both. From my experiences with MET I knew my tolerance was already slightly higher than that of the average “researcher” so I decided to join my friend, who is more experienced than me with psychedelics, to take 20mg of each substance orally in powder form as a bomb. Ie putting 20mg of 4-HO-DiPT and 20mg of 4-HO-MiPT into a single bomb and drinking with water. I had not eaten since about 1am the previous night after which I went to sleep and didn't wake until 11am so was fully awake and ready for a strong length experience. Didn't smoke anything, eat anything or consume alcohol at any point during before or after trip. Situation/Drug details: Sat in my friends room where he lives but his landlord/relatives were out. Small room with a massive double bed taking up most of it and his PC on one end of the room. Most time was spent lying on the bed with music blasting from his PC. Both substances could be described as fluffy off white powder. Outcomes/Experiences: 2pm Take 40mg (total) bomb with water 2.15pm Slight nausea probably just letting us know that it's orally active. 2.20pm onwards all sense of time is lost until about 7pm, what follows is an amazingly intense experience. I was with an experienced researcher in a safe environment and we had taken many precautions and things still could have gone quite wrong as you will see. Enjoy this story because in retrospect it was hilarious but at times I began to sketch out. As this story continues time is almost irrelevant, at times we looked at the clock and what seemed like an hour had been less than a minute and sometimes minutes were over half an hour. Started seeing some patterns in the curtains moving and shifting slightly. Looked outside and window ledges of opposite houses were moving slightly and seemed to breathe. Music was absolutely amazing, we were listening to some folky/rocky/singersongwriter stuff, some fleetwood mac and pink floyd. Pink floyd was the high point music wise because I think the peak of both drugs was simultaneous and lasted for some time as we listened to the whole of the 30 min song “shine on” which seemed like only a few minutes. As for OEVs, they were amazingly strong and vivid pattern wise but the amount of morphing and distortion was incredible. The door seemed to get bigger and smaller, wider and narrower, further away and closer. The darker part of the room under the PC desk seemed to glow blue. The ceiling was flowing and swirling with lots of colours and patterns not just appearing on it but flowing from it into the space around the room in all dimensions. I lost all sense of time and my perception of space was so distorted I couldn't imagine any proportion. Stranger than that, occasionally after the super intense hour which I would guess at about 1-1.5 hours in, lasting for about an hour, the distortion was still completely vivid but I could walk around and balance perfectly fine. I felt like I should have been dizzy but I was fine. The intensity of the trip, the visuals, all of it was so long ago from me starting to write this again 3 weeks later from this point. I can only remember now as I could then the most significant parts of the trip and the funny story of the day. One of the things I remember which didn't really happen after the first couple of hours or so was the physical aspect I hadn't experienced before. All over my body felt great, almost in an erotic way but not really sexual although it probably could have been if my friend wasn't with me. Every now and again waves of electricity would shoot through my body not really starting from anywhere but ending in my fingertips or toes and making them curl and it probably looked like I was having an orgasm... It was definitely weird but worth it. These were throughout the most intense first couple of hours which went by like time had stopped but later seemed to have gone fast. There was a part where it went a little off. This was my first time experiencing a trip take a turn I didn't like. I saw patterns changing with my eyes closed. Kaleidoscopic and changing shapes and as a different song came on, something I didn't really like while tripping, the shapes morphed into swastikas with people coming out of their own bodies... It's hard to explain how the bodies morphed but it was like new torsos coming out of torsos coming out of the new torsos ad infinitum. I opened my eyes, realised I'd been lying down for a long time with them shut because it was getting dark outside, lots of music notes multicoloured, streaming in through the window like I could see the wind and it was made of music. I went to the computer and changed the song and felt amazingly better but I stood for a while as I didn't feel ready to lie and close my eyes again yet in case the darker visuals came back. We only realise after half an hour and them nearly calling a locksmith or the police to see if anything was wrong or the door was broken and it was a funny situation... For my friend. Everything was morphing around me, not as colourful as before but a lot more weirdly proportioned things. His aunt was talking to me after saying it was ok, I looked at here and tried to act normally, we'd decided to try and go find somewhere outside to enjoy the rest of the trip in peace... We hurried outside... Here's where the trip went significantly worse for me... We live in a terraced suburb on a hill with lots of little parks, lots of dog owners, lots of children, near a couple of schools. It was still late afternoon and, I at least, was still tripping intensely. We ventured out towards the park at the top of the hill and quickly decided we'd have to go for a walk because we couldn't sit in the park with so many people around. As we walked we went past an alley and a tall guy who seemed to look like a scary cartoon niko bellic out of gtaiv popped out walking past us not noticing us. It was so alien for me this walk because it was so clear to me I was tripping while to everyone else I probably looked normal. Irrelevant. The terraced houses seemed to lean inwards above us, it felt claustrophobic not comfortable. The hills we walked up and down seemed to be vertical at times even though I was walking off. I felt like I could fall off the ground sometimes. It wasn't great being outside and we eventually went in with me wondering why my friend had insisted we went outside anyway considering his family would be fine with us staying in as long as we turned the music down. Eventually the trip came to an end, once we got in I just wanted it to be a calm ride down because it had been way more intense than I was expecting and we needed to be quiet. I just lied down enjoying the rest of the visuals with the music. Conclusion: I'll probably want to do this again, perhaps on my own as my first solo trip, to see how the difference in environment effects this trip. I feel like perhaps on my own it would have approached a +4 experience. Compared to my one other previous trip which was very different I think it was very stronger but judging by the amounts I took if I had took 40mg with MET it would have been stronger there however without the bodily effects. I didn't enjoy going outside or dealing with people while on this combination. After effects were negligible: usual feelings of tiredness and maybe hungry and thirsty from not eating or drinking (except a bit of water) for a few hours. It was very fun and memorable this time and I hope you enjoyed my story of awkwardness and misplanning! Remember to read all the guidelines about safety especially when trying combinations, be sure they will not react together negatively. I may try each substance separately in future, we had decided on this combination because we thought the effects would compliment each other nicely and the structure of the chemical did not seem like it would be reactive.

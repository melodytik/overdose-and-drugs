Dec 8, 2015. erowid.org/exp/107448 DOSE: repeated vaporized Pentedrone (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 200 lb I have never done a experience report for the various things I have tried throughout my journey through space and time, but I figured it was time to shed some light on this since it has very little on the webs about peoples dabbling. I have been on and off depending on the availability of the rc over the last two-ish years. The first time I had it was so long ago I actually cannot remember. However I do know that I was given it thinking it was amphet, that was almost cokie since it gives a bit of a numbing effect. I had insufflated it almost every time I had acquired until very recently. I must say off the bat that this is very, very addictive. I have many friends I see and are worried about of the prolonged use and the ready accessibility of it. When I first started getting it the texture of rc was a very fluffy white sticky powder and just recently I have had the chance to get some in crystal form, which has changed the desired way of dosing. I have recently got a glass vaporizer. So naturally I decide to give vaporizing a go. Around 9pm I take a few small crystals and place them in the chamber, just about 10mg or so. After applying a little heat the compound liquefies and starts to smoke very easily. The vapour is very easily inhaled and once the heat is removed the rc turns back into a solid state, ready to be toked again. After about 5- 10 seconds I get an instant head rush and euphoric feelings all throughout my body. I almost feel lighter on my feet and the feeling lasts for about 10 minutes. Almost immediately after I want to continue hitting the pipe in order to prolong the most enjoyable feelings I am getting. I just reapply heat to the bowl and it reliquefies and starts to steam up. I continue to hit it until there is very little left in the chamber. It last about 10 minutes of constant hitting and cooling. Once I see that it's gone I couldn't help but to put more in and continue adding to it to keep the whole experience going. I reload and repeat. At 9:45pm, after breaking the hold on me, I decide to put music on, start playing video games and doing research on the computer. I am feeling so stimulated I stop sitting down and decide to start cleaning. I couldn’t commit to doing any one thing for too long before my I change my mind and want to do something else. All this time I’m still feeling very happy and productive. After about 3 hours around 12am, I still feel very up and alert but I can feel the motivation to be doing things start to dwindle and all I can be bothered doing is playing games. I think about redosing and have to really try to convince myself that I should probably not. I think about redosing and have to really try to convince myself that I should probably not. I continue to play games for the next hour and then decide to try to get some sleep. Not sure if I was going to be able to, I lay my head down and can feel my heart still beating rather fast in my chest. After making myself yawn about 20 minutes later I start to doze off and I fell asleep rather easily. I wake up the next morning feeling fine with no notable withdrawals or side effects. Overall I say the pentedrone is one of my favourite Rc’s I've gotten to try and I believe it to be very enjoyable. It definitely has its addictive qualities and should be taken knowing this with care.

我还在学习一门名为"科学哲学"的课程. 我认为后者只是浪费时间, 在我最近的一篇论文中, 我提到了"自由意志与决定性论论" (见:主观性) 的徒劳性. 我最近还读了一些关于宇宙起源的理论. 我不需要能够找到一个符合我信仰和个性的有组织的宗教团体. 我可以找到自己的路. 于是我独自一人在我的房间里, 中间时间 我之前的尝试涉及大约12g和16g的干燥, 两次都没有发生什么. 我所有的研究都表明一定会发生一些事情, 我读过的一些报道称,5-15克比用户预期的多. 实际上, 这些的效果有很大差异, 后来,我又买了28克,但质量较低. 我愚蠢地在空腹中吞下了整整28克. 半小时后, 我开始感到腹部恶心. 我吃了几块火片, 因为我空腹吃了, 但保持静止似乎有所帮助. 我决定躺在床上. 我闭上眼睛,开始感到放松. 我开始做白日梦. 我呼吸有点低沉, 当我呼吸时, 我想象四个空气, 就像视频游戏右下角的图标一样, 接下来我记得的是... 旅行 我经历了宇宙的创建,直到它的热死亡, 我几乎陷入了迷幻和中毒状态. 很奇怪的是, 这一过程引起了短暂的疼痛. 如果我的脆弱,人类的心理无法处理无限的概念. 我认为我是在做梦, 但重复性让我意识到我在. 后来我觉得自己已经死了, 然后,我以为我是上帝. 但它并没有停止. 我认为,上帝,请让它停止!但我是上帝,它不会停止. 我以为我已经死了, 我开始感到恐慌. 我看不见,也没有意识到有嘴巴和声音. 我试图在宇宙的每次创造中, 我能感觉到口袋里的钥匙和钱包, 我能感觉到地板上的地毯, 现在我还不能告诉你我的眼睛是打开还是关闭. 我相信我终于习惯了之前的痛苦感觉, 我开始玩小游戏, 以免发疯. 我会告诉犹太人我是耶和华,基督徒是耶稣,穆斯林是阿拉,印度教徒是布拉汉,中国人是道,等等. 我不记得我还试过什么, 但每次一切都会发生就像以前发生的那样, 似乎我 (作为上帝) 总是比我领先一步. 每次宇宙被创造时, 一切都发生在完全相同的方式, 我确实在躺下2个小时后才下来. 虽然我从小就觉得自己是个好人, 我终于醒来了, 只是为了一次又一次的下降. 终于停止了, 我坐起来, 扔进垃圾桶里, 我心跳得很快, 喘不过气. 我觉得自己已经死了, 我甚至没有离开我的床整个时间. 每当我决定搬到另一个房间, 我的回忆就会消失, 我起身去所, 也拿出我的隐形眼镜. 我只是想回到床上睡觉, 我回去检查两只隐形眼镜的情况. 我眼睛的视力很模糊, 我在地板上寻找另一个联系人, 我反复回去寻找我的联系人, 我睡觉了,但是在睡觉之前, 我把公寓周围的灯都关了. 我会把一灯关掉,忘记关掉另一灯, 但当我回到房间时, 我觉得自己与周围的一切都有一种平静和联系. 我仍然有时能感觉到它, 其他: 2011年4月5日发布 阿曼尼塔斯 - - A. muscaria (70):一个人 (16),神秘的经历 (9),困难的经历 (5)

First I Met God, Then I Was God Amanita muscaria by Synthesis Citation:   Synthesis. "First I Met God, Then I Was God: An Experience with Amanita muscaria (exp66368)". Erowid.org . Apr 5, 2011. erowid.org/exp/66368 DOSE: 28 g oral Amanitas (dried) BODY WEIGHT: 180 lb Prelude I had taken a philosophy class called “comparative religions,” and I was also currently taking a class called, “philosophy of science.” I regard the latter as a bit of waste of time, as we largely talked about what can be “known,” perception and various other completely subjunctive things. Every week we have to write a single-spaced, once page paper, and in my most recent paper I had brought up the futility of the “free will versus determinism” debate (see: subjectivity). I also had recently done some reading about theories of the origin of the universe. The comparative religions class was useful in that I learned that I don’t need to be able to find an organized religious body that is in line with what I believe and what I am like personality-wise. I can find my own path. Unfortunately I didn’t have anyone I knew that could fill the role of sitter, so I was alone in my room, where I spent most of my time anyway. Intermezzo My previous attempts involved roughly 12 g and 16 g of dried, commercially available Amanita muscaria. Nothing happened both times. Of course, all my research suggested that something definitely should have happened, especially at those doses. Some accounts I had read cited 5-15 g as being more than the user had bargained for. In reality, the potency of these mushrooms vary substantially, making them risky for first-timers. Later on, I bought another 28 g, but of a lower grade. I thought about a 20-22 g dose, but I that would have left me with a “useless” 6-8 g leftover dose (based on previous experience). I foolishly ingested the whole 28 g on an empty stomach. About 30 minutes later I started to feel sick to my stomach. I ate a few tortilla chips since I had eaten the mushrooms on an empty stomach thinking that would help. It didn’t, but keeping still seemed to help. I decided to lie down on my bed. I closed my eyes and began to feel relaxed. I started into a state of daydreaming/hypnagogia. My breathing was slightly depressed, and as I was breathing, I imagined 4 tanks of air as if it were an icon in the lower right corner of a video game, and as I breathed out, I filled the 4 tanks. The next thing I recall was… The Trip I experienced the creation of the universe to its heat death in a single blink of the eye, over, and over, and over again. I pretty much had reached a delirious, poisoned state. The strange thing was that there was this brief pain sensation associated with this process. It was if my fragile, human psyche couldn’t handle the concept of infinity. At first, I thought I was dreaming, but the repetitiveness clued me into the fact that I was tripping. After a while I thought I had died and merged with God. Then, I thought I was God. But it wouldn’t stop. I thought, “Please God, make it stop!” But I was God, and it wouldn’t stop. I thought I had died and gone to Hell. I started freaking out. I couldn’t see and wasn’t aware of having a mouth and a voice. I tried to will myself out of the trip with each successive creation of the universe, but it didn’t work. I could feel the keys and wallet in my pockets, I could feel the carpet on the floor, but all I experienced was the Big Bang, over and over. To this day I couldn’t tell you if my eyes were open or closed. I believe I eventually got used to the painful sensation I felt before, or it went away by itself. I was experiencing infinity and began to play little games to keep from going crazy. Don’t ask me how I knew this, but what would happen is that I would tell the Jews I was Yahweh, the Christians I was Jesus, the Muslims I was Allah, the Hindus I was Brahman, the Chinese I was the Tao, and so on. I can’t remember what I else I tried, but every time everything would happen just the way it happened before, and I couldn’t change anything. It was like I (as God) was always one step ahead of me. Each time the universe was created, everything happened exactly the same, exactly the way it was supposed to happen. I did eventually come down about 2 hours after I had lain down. However, this repeating motif continued as I experienced coming down and seeing my TV (which I had left on), and so it seemed like I wasn’t really coming down per se. It was if I was finally coming to, only to have the feeling of coming down repeat over and over again. It did stop eventually, which involved me sitting up and throwing up in the wastebasket which I had propositioned just for this situation. My heart was pounding like crazy and I gasped for air. It was as if I had died and was reborn. I hadn’t even left my bed during the whole time. Whenever I decided to move to another room, my recollection would fade out, and then things would fade in and I would find myself in the room I wanted to be in. I got up and went to the bathroom, and also took out my contacts at this time. I wanted to go back to bed and just go to sleep because I felt exhausted. I went back to make sure I had taken out both contacts. My vision was fuzzy, and it looked like I had only taken out one contact. I looked for the other contact on the floor, but couldn’t find it. I repeatedly went back looking for my contact, only to eventually find that I had actually put both of them in the same side of the case. I went to bed then, but before I did, I turned off all the lights around the apartment. This was much more difficult that it sounds, as I would turn off one light and forget to turn off another, but I would realize this when I was already back in my room, as time would jump around on me. The next day after awakening, I felt a certain calm and connectedness to everything around me. I can still feel it sometimes, if I stop and relax and remember to breath.

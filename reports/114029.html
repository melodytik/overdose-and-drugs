我很焦虑, 当地的医生几乎无能为力, 我已经被处方了几周的利布里亚 (二氧化). 但英国卫生部门通常不会给人开几周以上的处方, 无论如何, 作为我第一次 (而且也是唯一一次), 我读过有人怀疑这种物质是否能穿过脑血屏障, 我是多么错误的 (我后来发现自己用其他物质/组合进行自我治疗时可能是非常危险的). 我没有为即将发生的事情做好准备. 七年后我写这篇文章, 我不记得花了多长时间才产生影响, 我几乎考虑做一些愚蠢的事情来摆脱我脑海中的可怕感觉. 我确实有时候想打电话叫救护车, 这持续了大约一个小时. 我记得我还有一些氧化物,所以我服用了10毫克. 我在接下来的几个小时里仍然很紧张, 几小时后, 我又服用了10毫克的氧化物, 我希望我有一个更大的供应的本佐. 我再也没有服用GABA粉了. 也许我不是最好的对象, 因为我发现很难放松和'放下头发'. 也许高 (如果这是什么) 是太多的我的快乐和恐慌. 我从来没有有过这样的经历, 其他: GABA (362):第一次 (2),困难的经历 (5),未知背景 (20)

Feb 7, 2020. erowid.org/exp/114029 DOSE: T+ 0:00 0.5 tsp oral GABA (powder / crystals) T+ 1:00 10 mg oral Pharms - Chlordiazepoxide T+ 3:00 10 mg oral Pharms - Chlordiazepoxide BODY WEIGHT: 240 lb I was suffering badly with anxiety and the local doctors were next to useless, so I started to scout around for alternatives. I had been prescribed Librium (chlordiazepoxide) for a few weeks. Benzos work, but the British health service won't usually prescribe for more than a few weeks at a time because "they're addictive" and they don't want to be sued. Anyway, for my first (and as it happened, only) time, I thought I would just take a half a teaspoon (about 1.5g?) of Gaba powder in case of any adverse side effects. I had read that people doubted whether this substance crossed the brain-blood barrier, so I wasn't expecting much, if anything. How wrong I was (and it can be very dangerous to be wrong when self-medicating as I found out subsequently with other substances/combinations). I was unprepared for what was to happen. I am writing this seven years later, so I don't recall how long it took to have an effect, but an effect it certainly had, and a very bad one. I became even more anxious to the point that I almost considered doing something stupid to get rid of the terrible feelings in my head. I certainly felt like calling for an ambulance at times, such was my panic that I was losing my mind; of being out of control. This lasted for approximately an hour. I remembered that I still had some chlordiazepoxide left, so I took 10mg. Not much, but it calmed me down somewhat, but I was still very edgy for the next few hours and paced up and down because I couldn't settle. I took another 10mg of chlordiazepoxide a few hours later and I was more or less back to 'normal'. I wish I had had a larger supply of benzos. I have never taken Gaba powder again. Perhaps I was not the best subject, as I find it very hard to relax and 'let my hair down'. Maybe the high (if that is what it was) was too much for my anhedonia and I panicked. Mind you, I have tried many other substances and never had this experience, at least with a single chemical in moderation.

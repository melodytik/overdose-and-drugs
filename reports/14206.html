我已经使用了达尔文很多次, 第一个是最好的, 晚上1点左右, 哥哥问我是否愿意带上一些达尔文. 我不知道它是什么,所以我对它有点谨慎. 他告诉我这是一个类似于维科丁的止痛药. 我是一个非常大的粉丝的 (维科丁), 没有什么可以喝的,所以我不得不积累唾液来把它. 我们决定在镇上散步一会儿, 聊起关于迷幻药的愉快谈话, 我们决定去商场买点食物. 我注意到达尔文还没有打我, 他告诉我再等一个小时,如果没有发生什么,他会给我另一个. 我同意了,我们去买披萨. 我离开排队去找一张桌子, 胃开始充满, 我告诉哥哥,他说这是正常的, 达尔文会带走我的食欲, 我起床拿了一些纸巾, 因为我早些时候忘了它们, 我突然觉得自己的身高有20英尺. 我无法感觉到自己的身体, 这是我在任何药物中最愉快的感觉之一. 我坐在我们的桌子上试着吃我的披萨. 我注意到我无法集中精力, 无论我多么想. 我想告诉我哥哥我感觉很好,但它就像我忘记如何说话,我不能动我的嘴. 吃完我一块披萨, 我们决定去商场散步, 我感觉非常好,这是不可思议的. 在飞行过程中, 绝对没有什么能打扰我. 我甚至开始在书店上下移动我的手臂, 因为每次都让我浮得更高, 一切都是如此美丽. 我与世界上一切都和, 无论如何,达尔文的效果很快消失了,大约2小时后, 我喜欢它. 但任何想开始娱乐性使用达尔文的人, 要小心; 我哥哥有一段时间就上了, 其他: 2007年6月23日发布

Jun 23, 2007. erowid.org/exp/14206 DOSE: 100 mg oral Pharms - Propoxyphene (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 130 lb I have used Darvon many times, the first being the best, so I'll share it with you. It was early afternoon, around 1:00, when my older brother asked me if I wanted to take some darvon with him. I didn't know what it was so I was sorta cautious about it. He told me it was a pain killer similar to Vicodin. I, being a very big fan of hydrocodone (vicodin), took his offer on the darvon. There was nothing to drink so I had to build up the saliva to get it down. We decided to go walking around town for a while having enjoyable conversations about psychedelics and where the best place in town to trip would be, so we did some exploring looking for such a place. About an hour and a half later we both got hungry and decided to go to the mall to get some food. I noticed that the darvon wasnt hitting me yet and asked him for more. He told me to wait another hour and if nothing happens he would give me another. I agreed to it and we went and bought some pizza. As I was leaving the line to go find a table my stomach started feeling like its was filling up, but I hadn't eaten anything. I told my brother about it and he said it was normal, and that the darvon would take away my appetite, so I quickly tried to get some food in my stomach. I got up to get some napkins, since I forgot them earlier, and right when I reached for them it hit me. All of a sudden it was like I was 20 feet tall. I couldn't feel my body anymore, and with every step I took I floated higher and higher. It was one of the most enjoyable feelings I've had on any drug. I sat down at our table and tried to eat my pizza. I noticed that I couldn't concentrate worth shit no matter how hard I wanted to. I wanted to tell my brother how good I was feeling but it was like I forgot how to talk, I couldn't move my mouth. After about an hour of eating my slice of pizza, we decided to go walk, or 'fly' around the mall to see what was new. I felt so good it was unbelievable. Absolutely nothing could've bothered me during my 'flight'. At one point I even started moving my arms up and down in a bookstore because it made me float higher each time, and I'm sure I looked like a complete dumbass to someone else, but It was so cool and felt so good that I couldn't resist. Everything was so beautiful. I was at peace with everything in the world, similar to a good acid trip. Anyways, the darvon wore off very quickly, about 2 hours after kicking in, and the comedown wasn't bad at all, no side effects, no after effects, just pure 'ecstacy'. I loved it. But to anyone who wants to start using darvon recreationally, be careful; my brother got hooked on them for a while and almost overdosed many many times, and now has an irregular heartbeat.

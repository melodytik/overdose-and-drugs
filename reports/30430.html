2C-T-21是一种白色粉末, 在大量动后, 味道完全不令人讨厌, 让我想起可卡因 (没有麻木). 我开始服用6毫克, 这是错误的! 但事实证明,这是一次非常愉快和有回报的经历. 我在出差. 我心态很放松,充满活力, 工作人员和顾客都非常友好. 在45分钟左右, 首次警报. 我最近吃了一顿轻食的晚餐 (一块小烤牛肉三明治和一杯酒), 开始的症状有点头 (我猜头) 和线性思维困难. 随着时间的推移, 没有任何恶心,也没有特别的能量. 峰值持续了两个小时. 我很想给别人钱,给他们买饮料. 虽然我很拙,也难以直线思考, 幸运的是, 我和一个深受东方神秘主义影响的女孩交谈, 虽然我的非线性有时会有问题, 我认为可能会有视觉效果潜伏在表面下. 火盒的金属外套看起来很迷人. 他们似乎采取一个温暖的光,像的光. 我感觉到腿部有点活力, 但没有太大. 绝对是紧,口腔也干燥, 可能至少有点脱水. 我真的需要用嘴做些事情. 我吃了很多冰,吸了太多的香烟. 轻微的触摸感觉真的很好, 尽管如此,它仍然是一种非常有感性的药物. 我敢打法国亲吻是为了死在这种药物. 我感到非常满意. 我一直在思考心脏空间, 能与某人进行情感交流是多么的酷. 因为任何特定的东西都不过是它本身, 不管我们给它什么名字, 我一直闻着烧焦的爆米花的味道. 我没有看到任何爆米花, 臭味似乎在房间的不同地方. 不清楚这是对真实的气味过度敏感, 还是完全幻觉. 在接种剂量后5个半小时, 后果包括轻微的冷却. 适度难以入睡 (我有一种不安腿综合征,所有乙胺使我在试图睡觉时都会发. 第二天没有宿醉. 总的来说,我很喜欢这个. 相比于12毫克的2C-I剂量, -21的视觉效果较少, 在2C-I, 我是一个友好,无意识,快乐的混蛋. 我不确定我能预见到麻烦的到来,所以我避免任何地方的人进入战斗. 对于我周围的氛围更敏感, 并且可能更好地避免冒犯任何人. 2C-T-21的刺激性远不及2C-I或MDMA. 事实上,在6毫克我会把它描述为放松. 能量水平较低, 我也无法进行连贯的对话. 虽然它不像MDMA那样有性感, 另一方面,第二天我也没有那么失望, 我最近的经历不太令人满意, 不管是因为耐受性还是黑市问题. 我很高兴-21能够促进情感亲密和身体性感. 如果有任何视觉效果出现在更高的剂量, 我可能更喜欢它比MDMA. 我不喜欢过度活跃, 也不喜欢三天头, 考虑到这一点, 我怀疑-21会成为一个很好的俱乐部药物. 不管是新朋友还是老朋友, 我迫不及待地想和一些亲密的朋友一起尝试, 我希望尝试更低的剂量以及更高的剂量. 我真的认为我可以通过一个晚宴没有被抓到可能是4-5毫克, 这可能是一个非常酷的经历. 我们也会尝试8毫克, 看看这些视觉效果是否能发挥作用. 2C-T-21 (290): 俱乐部/酒吧 (25),一般 (1), 烂的体验 (4)

Very Sensual 2C-T-21 by Dr. Demento Citation:   Dr. Demento. "Very Sensual: An Experience with 2C-T-21 (exp30430)". Erowid.org . Jan 28, 2004. erowid.org/exp/30430 DOSE: 6.0 mg oral 2C-T-21 (powder / crystals) repeated smoked Tobacco - Cigarettes BODY WEIGHT: 220 lb 2C-T-21 is a white powder that I was able to dissolve in water at a concentration of 1mg/ml after considerable shaking. The taste is not at all unpleasant, reminding me of cocaine (without the numbness). I started with a 6mg dose, thinking it would be a barely threshold dose. Wrong! But it turned out to be a most enjoyable and rewarding experience. I was out of town on a business trip. My mindset was relaxed, energetic, open to new experiences. The setting was a small pub around the corner from my hotel, where the staff and customers were pretty friendly. The come-up occurred over the course of one and a half hours, with first alerts coming at about the 45 minute mark. I had recently had a light dinner (a small roast beef sandwich and a beer) and perhaps on an empty stomach, the onset would have been quicker. The initial signs were a little bit of dizziness (head rush I suppose) and difficulty with linear thinking. As time went on, I began to notice euphoria. There was no nausea at all, and no particular rush of energy. The peak lasted for two hours. I was definitely loved up; I wanted to give people money, buy them drinks. I was clumsy and had problems with linear thinking; however, I was able to carry on conversations reasonably well. Fortunately I talked to a girl who is way into Eastern mysticism so my 'deep thoughts' did not freak her out too much. It was not too difficult to connect with other people emotionally although my non-linearity was problematic at times, found myself constantly having to get back to the point. I thought there might be some visual effects lurking below the surface. The metallic-looking cover of a matchbook seemed iridescent and fascinating. The bar was lit in part with marquee-lights, and they seemed to take on a warm glow, like candle-light. During the peak I felt a little surge of energy in the legs but not much. Definite jaw clenching, and a dry mouth too, probably at least some dehydration. Really needed something to do with the mouth. I ate a lot of ice and smoked way too many cigarettes. Light touches felt really good, in an MDMA like way, although not quite to that extent. Still a very sensual drug nonetheless. I'll bet french kissing is to die for on this drug. I had wonderful feelings of contentment. I kept thinking about heartspace and how cool it is to be able to communicate emotionally with someone. It struck me at one point that everything is perfect, because any particular thing is nothing more or less than exactly what it is, no matter what we name it, or what we try to make of it. I kept smelling something like burnt popcorn. I didn't see any popcorn in there, and the smell seemed to come up at various places in the room. Unclear whether this was exaggerated sensitivity to an actual smell, or an outright hallucination. Gentle comedown for an hour and a half, ending with being abruptly re-deposited in reality five and a half hours after the dose. After-effects included mild chilling. Moderate difficulty getting to sleep (I have a touch of restless leg syndrome, and all the phenylethylamines send me into a spin, literally, when I try to go to bed). No hangover the next day. Overall, I liked this a lot. Compared with a 12-mg dose of 2C-I, there were fewer visuals with -21 but much more empathy. On 2C-I, I am a gregarious, oblivious, happy jerk. Not sure I could see trouble coming and so I avoid any place where people get into fights. Much more sensitive to the vibe around me on -21 and probably much better able to avoid offending anyone. 2C-T-21 is not nearly as stimulating as 2C-I or MDMA. In fact, at 6mg I would describe it as relaxing. Compared with MDMA, the energy level was less, and I was less able to carry on a coherent conversation. It is not as sensual as MDMA, but then nothing is. By the same token, I did not feel nearly as washed out the next day with -21. This may seem heretical, but except for my first MDMA experience, I've found recent experiences somewhat less satisfactory, whether due to tolerance or black market issues or whatever. But I was very happy with with -21's ability to promote emotional closeness and physical sensuality. If any visuals come out at higher doses, I might like it more than MDMA. Granted, I'm at an age where I don't really like being spastically hyperactive, or hung over for three days, so the mellowness of -21 is an advantage to me. With that in mind I doubt -21 will prove to be a good club drug. Much better for lounging around with friends, whether new friends or old friends. I can't wait to try this with some close friends because I think the potential for bonding and intimacy is great. I would like to try lower doses as well as higher doses. I really think I could make it through a dinner party without getting busted on maybe 4-5 mg, and that would probably be a really cool experience. We will also try 8mg to see if any of those visual effects blossom.

这是我第一次接触AP238. 我是一个热衷于阅读经验报道的记者. 关于自己的一些背景. 28岁的男性身高172厘米,体重约75公斤. 列出我没有使用过的 (流行) 药物比列出我使用过的药物要快. 为了本报告的目的, 鉴于AP238是一种阿片类药物研究化学物质, 我每天服用特拉马多尔和克拉, 我每天服用特拉马多尔和克拉, 我也服用可,氧可,吗啡,布林诺芬, 基本上任何药品级片我可以得到我的手当我有机会. 我之前也使用过一克2-甲基AP-237 (在10月份). 我对阿片类药物有耐受性, 那些对阿片类药物无知的人应该开始服用比我在报告中列出的剂量更低的剂量. 星期二 下午四点 +0分钟:大约25毫克的剂量. 我觉得我的剂量是用珠宝秤. 我测出了0.2 (200毫克) 并将其分成四行, 每行大约50毫克. 我把其中一个分成两半, 再次测量, 结果是0.18, 我用果汁一起吞下了粉末. 味道并不糟糕, 但它确实让我的舌头麻木了大约2-3分钟. 我开始感觉到效果的开始. 我将其描述为一个温暖的开始冲洗我. 我在洗碗, 感觉好像自己在自动驾驶, +30分钟:还在洗碗. 喝了一杯咖啡, 打破了一杯咖啡. 我不知道是因为失去了运动协调,还是因为我拙. 我认为后者,因为我的协调似乎很好. 我认为效果已经达到顶峰. 我心中的一切都稍微慢了下来, 考虑使用其他25毫克. +60分钟:再次服用25mg,口服. 我打算放一些音乐,做一咖啡,一些三明治,和一些卡夫特麦当劳和奶酪我和我的妻子. +80分钟:第二剂开始起作用. 现在我已经达到我通常喜欢的阿片类药物的水平. 疼痛变得更加昏暗, 头部空间缓慢. 身体感觉有点重, 但不是压倒性的. 只是感觉"好" +100分钟:喝了一杯咖啡 +120分钟:感觉正在消失 总而言之,与2map-237相比,ap238的腐蚀性似乎较小. 高度类似,但不像237那样"温暖". 如果要比较它与更为众所周知的药物, 根据我的经验, 它的持续时间很短, 但这可能是由于我对片的耐受性. 我不打算一直使用这种药物, 因为它基本上是完全未经研究的药物. 但它确实有令人愉快的 (对我来说) 影响. 已使用的证券 AP-238 (929):成和习惯性 (10),医疗使用 (47),单独使用 (16)

I'm an avid experience report reader, first time reporter. Some background on myself. 28 y/o male 172cm tall approx 75kg weight. It would be quicker to list the (popular) drugs that I haven't used rather than the ones I have. For the propose of this report, seeing as AP238 is an opioid research chemical I'll just have you know that I am a constant user of Opioids for herniated disk at t6-t7. I take tramadol and kratom daily, but on the day of this report I used neither. I take tramadol and kratom daily, but on the day of this report I used neither. I also take hydrocodone, oxycodone, morphine, buprinorphine, basically any pharmaceutical grade opiate I can get my hands on when I get the chance. Also, I've used a gram of 2-methyl AP-237 prior to this (over the course of the month of october). So all that is to say I have an opiate tolerance and opiate naive people should start with lower dosages than what I am going to be listing in this report. Tuesday 4pm +0 min: Dosed approx 25 mg. The way I figured my dose was with jewelry scales. I measured out .2 (200 mg) and split it into four lines which should be approx 50mg each. I then split one of them in half and re-measured it coming to .18, but my scales always seem to weigh a little light so this is why I'm estimating it at 25mg. I swallowed the powder with a drink of apple juice. Taste wasn't terrible but it did make my tongue go numb for about 2-3 min. +20 min: I'm starting to feel the beginnings of the effects. I would describe it as a warm starting to wash over me. I'm doing the dishes and feel as if I'm on auto pilot, things seem less mundane and the constant sharp ache in my back is slightly dulled. +30 min: Still doing dishes. Had a whoopsie and broke a coffee mug. Can't tell if it's due to loss of motor coordination or if I'm just being clumsy. I'm thinking the latter, as my coordination seems fine otherwise. +40 min: finished dishes, I think the effects have reached their peak. All in all my headspace is slightly slowed, bit of a happy, content warm feeling. Contemplating dosing the other 25mg. +60 min: 25mg dosed again, orally. I'm going to put on some music, make a pot of coffee, some sandwiches, & some kraft macaroni and cheese for my wife and I. +80 min: second dose is kicking in. Now I've reached the level I typically like to be at with opiates. Pain much more dulled and feeling a slow headspace. Body feels a bit heavy but not overwhelmingly so. Just feel 'nice'. +100 min: drank a cup of coffee +120 min: feeling is fading now In conclusion, compared to 2map-237, ap238 seems to be less caustic. The high is similar, but not quite as 'warm' as 237. If I had to compare it to a more well known drug, I would liken it to hydrocodone in its body/headspace. It's duration is fairly short in my experience but that may be due to my opiate tolerance. Not something I plan on using all the time seeing as it's basically a completely unstudied drug. But it certainly does have pleasant (to me) effects.

我已经服用抗精神病药 olanzapine (Zyprexa) 几个月了, 我的精神病医生每月都在增加剂量, 首先从五毫克增加到十毫克, 后来从十毫克增加到二十毫克. 虽然我确实从增加剂量中受益, 减少精神病的行为和思维, 他给了我一个处方, 指示我可以随时服用多达三片5毫克药片. 我研究了药理学, 发现普罗西克利丁具有抗肌酸和抗尼古丁性质. 作为一种抗胆药, 我假设这种药物可能有潜在的娱乐价值. 经过十几次使用后, 我吃了七片5毫克的药片, 总共35毫克, 一个小时内就开始显现:皮肤干燥,眼睛干燥,口腔干燥,鼻孔干燥, 然后真正的声开始了. 感觉就像你刚从一激烈的过山车后的胃部感觉. 这既不愉快,也不令人不快,只是存在. 有一些短暂的间歇性恶心, 但不足以造成负面体验. 我不得不承认, 确实有一些严重的色彩增强和痕迹. 只是着白色的墙壁看起来很漂亮. 我觉得有点疲, 我觉得不断渴. 这绝对不是聚会或社交的药物. 事实上,我不确定这种药物是否适用于其他任何药物, 这比子更糟糕, 但另一方面, 我很奇怪, 眼睛干燥,嘴巴干燥等. 我在整个经历中, 吸和烟草混合, 每次我吸一口, 我都会有点恶心. 这让我想吸烟少了. 这是否是件好事, 是个人的决定. 我把药瓶放回柜里, 结论是我现在只能使用普罗西克林来减轻腿部的不舒服感. 已使用的证券 药品 - 普罗西克利丁 (233),药品 - 奥兰扎宾 (260):医疗用途 (47),组合 (3),单独使用 (16)

Apr 15, 2018. erowid.org/exp/94483 DOSE: 20 mg oral Pharms - Olanzapine (daily) 35 mg oral Pharms - Procyclidine (pill / tablet) repeated smoked Cannabis - Hash repeated smoked Tobacco BODY WEIGHT: 70 kg I have been on the antipsychotic olanzapine (Zyprexa) for a few months, and my psychiatrist has been upping the dose every month, first from five to ten milligrams and later from ten to twenty milligrams. While I have indeed benefit from the increased dose in terms of reduced psychotic behavior and thinking, there has also been a drawback in the form of akathisia. I explained this to my psychiatrist, and he gave me a script for procyclidine with the instructions that I can take up to three 5 mg tablets as the need arises. I researched the pharmacology and discovered that procyclidine had anti-muscarinic and anti-nicotinic properties. As an anticholinergic, I hypothesized that there might be potential for some recreational value of this drug. After having used it on a dozen occasions in doses of three 5 tablets, I felt comfortable exploring slightly higher doses. I took seven 5 mg tablets totalling 35 mg, swallowed with water after not eating for six hours. The effects came on within an hour: dry skin, dry eyes, dry mouth, dry nostrils, everything is dry. Then the actual buzz began. It feels like the feeling you have in your stomach just after coming off an intense roller coaster ride. It's neither pleasant nor unpleasant, but merely there. There has been some brief intermittent nausea, but not enough to make for a negative experience. I must admit that there is some serious color enhancement and tracers. It looks pretty just to stare into a white wall. I feel a bit fatigued, and I feel constantly thirsty. This is definitely not a drug for parties or even socializing. In fact, I am not sure this drug is for anything but taken as prescribed. It's a worse buzz than nutmeg, but then again, I am strange in the sense that I enjoy the nutmeg buzz. This, however, is nothing like it except for the dry eyes, mouth etc. I smoked hashish mixed with tobacco during the entire experience, and every time I took a hit, I felt slightly nauseous. It made me want to smoke less because of this. Whether that is a good thing is up the the individual to decide. I put my pill bottle back in the cabinet and concluded that I now will only use procyclidine to reduce the uncomfortable sensations in my legs.

我在兽医诊所工作, 珍视自己的未来, 只有当机会出现时, 之前我服用了10毫克的瓦和一些药 (我不知道这些药很糟糕), 无论如何,昨天我们正在麻醉动物进行手术, 他开始战斗所以兽医去得到镇静剂. 他告诉我这是一种叫做"强效镇静剂"的药物. 我注意到药物瓶放在柜台上, 这是一个50毫升的瓶子,所以我认为3毫升不会被注意到. 我抽出一个注射器, 放进口袋里 (这让我整天都很紧张)!!! 我回家的时候, 已经有一半的液体泄露出来, 剩下1.5 毫克. 所以我用蒸汽干燥,然后把白色的水晶物质起来. 我认为我没有干燥它足够--所以我卷起一个小球,吞下它. 经过几次干燥后, 它变得更加粉状, 我真的很遗憾吹了这个东西, 对于经历本身来说, 如果我服用更小的剂量, 可能会有趣, 因为当它开始时, 我有一种轻微的高兴和镇静的感觉, 然而似乎不安全剂量的门非常低). 虽然这是一件非常痛苦的事情, 穿上汗水 (我感觉很冷, 舒缓剂和镇静剂的常见效果) 躺在床上, 我希望这只是睡眠, 我每隔两个小时醒来时, 就会有你能想象到的最疯狂的嘴巴. 经过5个小时左右的时间, 我仍然能感受到药物的作用, 今天早上我醒来时, 感觉恢复正常, 没有宿醉. 无论如何, 我只是想分享我对这种药物的经验, 我强烈建议大家远离这种药物, 我真的很幸运, 今天我完全康复了, 如果我用了更大的剂量, 我毫无疑问我可能不得不去急诊室. 我读到这种药物会导致心率和呼吸率严重下降. 我一定要喝很多水, 我有一个朋友知道我喝了, 现在回想起来, 这可能是我有过的最愚蠢的药物体验 (我有过一些有趣的经历!!) 每个人都保持安全,并试图从别人的错误中学习. 吸毒不一定是危险的, 但我们都知道, 其他: 克西拉 (610):单独 (16),第一次 (2)

Stay away from Xylazine Xylazine by TokingTech Citation:   TokingTech. "Stay away from Xylazine: An Experience with Xylazine (exp93724)". Erowid.org . Sep 13, 2014. erowid.org/exp/93724 DOSE: T+ 0:00 oral Xylazine (powder / crystals) T+ 0:00 insufflated Xylazine (powder / crystals) T+ 5:00 smoked Cannabis BODY WEIGHT: 200 lb I work at a vet clinic and value my future but also am on the look out for free highs that I think carry no risk for me. I only take what will never be noticed and only then when the opportunity arises. I took 10 10mg valium and some phenobarbs (which I didn't know sucked) previously and that was a few months ago. Anyway, yesterday we were anesthetizing an animal for surgery and he started fighting so the vet went to get a sedative. As I am a pre-vet student I always ask a lot of questions and he told me it was a 'powerful tranquilizer' called Xylazine. I noticed the bottle of this drug sitting out on the counter while the only other employees were in the operating room. It was a 50ml bottle so I figured 3mL would not be noticed missing. I drew up a syringe and put it in my pocket (causing me to be very nervous the rest of the day)!!! When I got home half had leaked out and 1.5cc remained [reported as 150 mg]. I didn't want to shoot this drug (I actually might have but had no rigs) so I dried it out over steam and scraped up the white crystally substance. At first it was really sticky--I think I didn't dry it enough--so I rolled up a little ball and swallowed it. After a few more dryings it was a bit more powdery so I was able to do a few lines. I really regret blowing this stuff as much as doing the drug itself as it was very sticky and caused terrible congestion all through today (day after). As for the experience itself, it was almost entirely very unenjoyable. Maybe if I had done a smaller dose it would have been fun because as it was coming on I had a slightly euphoric and sedated feeling which then escalated to clumsiness and even profound closed eyed visuals (if this level could be maintained I feel there may be potential for recreational use of this drug. However it seems as though threshold for unsafe doses is very low). At this point I was enjoying the experience somewhat even though it had been a pain in the butt to administer. However as I kept coming up, I got really drowsy and ended up stumbling into my bedroom, putting on sweats (I was really cold, a common effect of sedatives and tranquilizers) and bundled into bed for what I hope was sleep but could maybe have been more of a borderline comatose state. I woke up every 2 hours or so with the craziest cotton-mouth you can imagine. After about 5 hours I was able to get up and have something to eat and smoke a bowl to calm my nerves. I could still feel the effects of the drug but I was definitely over the hump. When I woke up this morning I felt back to normal, no hangover. Anyway, I just wanted to share my experience with this drug for which there is almost no information available. I strongly recommend staying away from it or using a tiny dose to see the effects. I am honestly feeling lucky to feel totally recovered today as that crap lingered for a lonnnng time. Had I used a larger dose I have no doubts that I may have had to go to the ER. I have read that this drug can cause serious decreases in heart rate and breathing rate. I made sure to drink lots of water and had a friend who knew I had taken it so if I didn't call him back he would have sought medical attention. Looking back this is probably the dumbest drug experience I have ever had (and I have had some interesting experiences!!) and will never do a drug again without doing more than very basic research. Stay safe everyone and try to learn from others mistakes. Getting high doesn't have to be dangerous at all but as we all know it can be in many ways.

我是一个多发性硬化症患者, 很长一段时间以来一直对LDN疗法感兴趣, 我也是一名酒鬼, 几年前给我开了纳尔特雷克松药来抑制酒精渴望. 其他人的感觉 - - 解离,不良的身体高,恶心等等. 我只能用这个剂量维持一周. 我把瓶子放在柜子里,然后完全忘记了它. 快速前进到现在. 经历了一次令人恐惧的多发性硬化症恶化, 再次与我的医生谈论治疗, 我记得我还有一瓶纳尔特, 开始低剂量治疗. 我在开始这项工作后的一周内, 感觉有了更多的活力,清晰度和'明亮'的情绪. 我对酒精的渴望也减少了, 食物和香烟的渴望也稍微减少了. 这一切都伴随着 (据称) 免疫功能的提高, 对于我来说, 这就像是应该放入水系统的物质! 其他: 发布时间:2016年8月30日 纳尔特雷 (338):治疗意图或结果 (49),医疗用途 (47),不适用 (38)

Wonderful at Low Dose Naltrexone by Luxeffexor Citation:   Luxeffexor. "Wonderful at Low Dose: An Experience with Naltrexone (exp108953)". Erowid.org . Aug 30, 2016. erowid.org/exp/108953 DOSE: 5 mg oral Naltrexone (daily) BODY WEIGHT: 195 lb I am an MS patient and have been interested in LDN therapy for a long time but couldn't find a neurologist to prescribe it because it still lacks 'gold standard' clinical evidence. I am also an alcoholic and was prescribed naltrexone several years ago to curb alcohol cravings. My experience with it at the full strength (50 mg) was awful and similar to others' - a sense of dissociation, bad body high, nausea, etc. I only managed about a week of it at that dose. I put the bottle in the cabinet and forgot all about it. Fast forward to now. Experienced an MS exacerbation that was pretty frightening, talked to my Dr about treatment again but I still can't convince myself that the pharmaceutical MS treatments are for me, for a number of reasons. Remembered that I still had almost an entire bottle of Naltrexone and started myself on the low-dose therapy. I have been experiencing increased energy, clarity, and 'bright' mood in the week since I started. Full of love for the boyfriend and family, less prone to frustration, just slightly trippy-happy-high ;) My cravings for alcohol are also lessened, and also a small reduction in food and cigarette cravings too. All this comes with (purportedly) an increase in immune function, protection from cancer, and other anti-inflammation properties? To me, it feels like a substance that should be put in the water system!

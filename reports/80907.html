没有任何止痛药 (除了利多卡因). 医生直接注射到我脊椎的神经根. 这很痛苦,但有了Versed,它不会打扰我. 所以,关于真正的药物. 每个月我都会去注射, 他们会着着, 直到找到合适的静脉, 感受到了温暖和放松的浪潮, 但这种冲动与阿片类药物如迪劳迪德或海洛因的冲动有很大不同. 这是一个很好的冲动仍然,只是非常不同. 这种感觉比口服其他类药物更强烈. 这种药物比静脉注射的瓦利更好, 但我不确定它是否比其他静脉注射的子更好. 总的来说,这是令人难以置信的. 唯一的缺点是极短的半衰期 (大约2小时,效果会消失). 没有任何其他资产 2011年6月28日发布 药品 - 米达佐兰 (435):医院 (36),医疗用途 (47)

Jun 28, 2011. erowid.org/exp/80907 DOSE: 10 mg IV Pharms - Midazolam BODY WEIGHT: 180 lb I was given a dose to sedate me slightly for a short procedure with no pain killers (other than lidocaine). The procedure was injections directly into the nerve roots in my spine. This is VERY painful, but with versed, it wont bother me a bit. So, about the actual drug. Each month I go for an injection and they poke and prod until they find a suitable vein, and then the doctor comes in and gives me a bit of versed. When it hits me, a wave of warmth and relaxation crashes over my body, it feels incredible. It certainly is a rush, but the rush is FAR different from that of an opioid such as dilaudid or heroin. It is a good rush still, just very different. The feeling post rush is much more intense than other benzos taken orally. The drug is better than IV valium, but I'm not sure if its better than other IV'd benzo's. Overall, its incredible. The only downside is the extremely short half life (about 2 hours and it wears off).

Nov 3, 2025. erowid.org/exp/119321 DOSE: 24 g oral Mushrooms - P. atlantis (sclerotia) BODY WEIGHT: 55 kg I had my first two guided journeys three months ago, on a first day with magic truffles and on the second day I smoked changa. After three months my spiritual and intellectual curiosity led me to my fourth ever journey. In between I had had one milder journey, but I wanted to get a bit deeper. On the day of this journey, I ate light breakfast, meditated for a half an hour and prepared the truffles into a brew. I used the journey play list my guide had provided me. Slowly drifting into my experience, I felt my body being tucked, pushed, pulled and poked from all directions, the areas being poked at were changing swiftly. I experienced trouble breathing and had to focus on my breath. The experience told me that my body and breath were being brought into alignment. Slowly, the poking and pulling changed into an all-encompassing multidimensional embrace, similar to what I had experienced under changa. I was held firmly and lovingly from all angles, each cell of my body and being wrapped in love, peace and bliss. I didn’t experience the DMT-entities as visual entities, but I could sense the presence of a higher intelligence. The state was extremely blissful, just like with changa. I had lost the sense of place and time and self as who I am in my everyday life. Once the embrace was released I asked the experience if this was a simulation of my changa experience or a ”true” hyperspace experience, and I was answered that there is only one universal consciousness, where all experiences lie, we may just take different paths to reach those experiences. I wondered how, if there is one universal consciousness, can I still experience a self. The workings of the universe were exposed to me in a complex manner that my analytical mind could not comprehend. The workings of the universe were exposed to me in a complex manner that my analytical mind could not comprehend. I asked to be explained how it actually works and I received many metaphors to explain the relationship between the universal consciousness and individual selves. I was shown a tree with multiple branches, the leaves mirroring the selves. An ocean with waves, a disco ball sending small beams of light landing on the walls as tiny dots of light. Or how everything is like a big puzzle. But this diversity was also found within each person, each self. Each person constituting of multiple different ways how to be oneself and each one piece of the self deserving to be expressed. That we must nourish all that diversity within us for us to flourish, in order for us to become whole persons. No part should be pushed in to the shadows. Then, my persona, my earthly ”me” was pulled out of me - although it was already lost in my changa simulation, in a miraculous move it was still being pulled out of me, of my ”body”. I wasn’t fully aware of my body, so the ”me” the persona being pulled out was rather pulled out from the point of the perspective of my consciousness. I saw and experienced my persona twisting and twirling along with different complex, multidimensional shapes. My earthly persona was being reshaped. I wondered how I was able too see that, since persona / the self is not something visible - and later I wondered also who was the one witnessing all that happen? I was told I was made into a better person. In midst of the shaping, my ego fell out like a little black tiny dot. It was being tossed around, which I first found funny. But the tossing started to turn more furious, so I asked the universe to be a little more gentle with my ego. Making that request, my consciousness started to expand, all the way to the Milky Way. And from that perspective, the black dot that was my ego looked so miniscule. We laughed together with the universe at my ego, but gave it also some sarcastically comforting attention; ”Oh poor Ego”. The shaping of the persona continued as I was being made a better person. I was nowhere, there was just the witnessing of the persona twisting and twirling. Together with the universe we took stock on my waking consciousness and self - I was glad to receive excellent note: high empathy, kind, loving, creative and so on. Then the washing and purification of my persona began. My persona and soul was sucked deeper into the dancing shapes, dissolving into tiny pieces flowing together with the multidimensional structures. I watched, felt and experienced purification happening in my persona, inside out, outside in, from all angles, penetrating each facet. Water, sand, energy, vibrations and light flowing through all of my being, yet I was not there, but I still saw that and experienced it - both from an ”outside” perspective and as a direct experience. The washing seemed to last forever and it was a blissful state. Just like a blissful, long hot shower, but for my soul. Every hurt, every disappointment, every negative experience I had every experienced, all my pain was being gently washed and rinsed away. I was then returned back to my waking consciousness, my soul washed, purified of all negativity. My body and my soul had been brought into alignment with my soul’s purpose, which I had received three months ago in my changa experience. I was reminded to keep my spiritual connection, the connection to the universal consciousness, alive through dancing, music and meditation in my everyday life.

美国 我选择了MAOI (摩克洛贝米德) 与一种兴奋剂 (甲基隆) 的可怕组合. 我通常的甲基隆剂量是220毫克, 所以我认为对MAOI进行实验的起点是20-30毫克. 我只是想增加所需的剂量. 根据我从低剂量体验中得出的结论, 莫克洛贝米德确实会使甲基隆增强至少2倍, 经验的"传播"如下: 在T-1小时:消耗了75mg的Moclobemide. 在t+0小时, 我感觉到一种轻微的情绪提升和头部空间的改变从moclobemide. 我的手很冷. 我在15分钟内喝了28毫克溶于水中的甲基. 在t+20分钟,我感觉有点冷. 我也经历了常见的身体冲洗 - - 消化道的"冲洗" (清洁,欢迎腹). 我跳进浴, 感觉到一个的平常. 对于28毫克来说, 如果现在是全剂量, 我应该感觉到有趣的效果, 但我几乎没有任何情绪提升, 只有轻微的心率/血压升高, 嘴巴很潮湿 (通常是干燥的), 我的成员绝对没有缩小 (它通常缩小, 与大多数安非他命常见). 我只感觉到深呼吸的轻微感觉. 这种感觉最多是70-80毫克甲基 (没有MAOI). 奇怪的是, 我仍然觉得事情越来越激烈. 通常事情已经平稳了. 这感觉像是低剂量,这是好的. 我没有打算今天玩得很开心. 这种逐渐增加的效果可能是MAOI增加了甲醇胺, 现在我只是感到疲倦了. 我也感觉比平时更困惑/精神慢. 这种低剂量不适合. 我几乎睡着了 - - 我感到疲倦,温暖, 然而, 感觉好像我刚刚经历过150-200毫克的体验, 而不是28毫克的体验! 我认为这是一个很大的负面, 因为我没有得到高兴,心脏开放, 或洞察力更高剂量经验, 随着时间的推移, 然而,这种强度与通常的甲基隆时间表相重叠! 没有. 因此,看起来好像有趣的,初始效应 (T+0.5到T+2小时) 可能不会被MAOI强化, 现在我觉得自己很疲, 鉴于这一发现, 我相信, 较大剂量的甲基隆与moclobemide可能会产生大, 负面后果, 然而,我觉得最初的效果不会被同样强化. 换句话说, 这种组合看起来不值得, 特别是考虑到MAOI与兴奋剂混合的潜在危险! 我计划在本周晚些时候试用56毫克的甲基隆与75毫克的摩克洛贝米德, 我猜这将是MAOI+兴奋剂药物=增强的另一个数据点, 在科学的名义, 没有人 其他: 甲基隆 (255),莫克洛贝米德 (75):组合物 (3),单独使用 (16)

In the Name of Science; Definitely Not Worth It Moclobemide & Methylone by MMan Citation:   MMan. "In the Name of Science; Definitely Not Worth It: An Experience with Moclobemide & Methylone (exp38345)". Erowid.org . Nov 22, 2004. erowid.org/exp/38345 DOSE: T+ 0:00 75 mg oral Moclobemide T+ 1:00 28 mg oral Methylone (liquid) BODY WEIGHT: 172 lb In the name of science (and because methylone is now unavailable), I chose to explore the dreaded combo of an MAOI (moclobemide) with a stimulant (Methylone). My normal dose of methylone is 220mg, so I figured a good starting point for experimentation with an MAOI would be 20-30mg. I was looking to potentiate required amount only (dosage). I was hoping to turn my shrinking store of methylone into 3 or even 4 times the amount of experiences left. Quick conclusion: from what I gathered from this low-dose experience, moclobemide does appear to potentiate the methylone by a factor of at very least 2x, and likely more... but not necessarily in a linear or positive way. Here is the 'spread' of the experience: T-1 hour: Consumed 75mg Moclobemide. At t+0 hours, I was feeling a slight mood lift and alteration of headspace from the moclobemide. My hands were cold. I drank 28mg methylone dissolved in water over the course of 15 minutes. At t+20 minutes, I felt slightly chilly. Also around this time I experienced the usual body flush - a digestive track 'flush' (cleansing, welcome diarrhea). I hopped in the bath, feeling a twinge of the usual lethargy. Some nice tingles were present - pretty nice for 28mg. t+40 mins: I should be feeling fun effects if this were a full dose about now, but I feel barely any mood lift, only slight heart rate/BP elevation, mouth is plenty moist (it usually is DRY), and my member is decidedly NOT shrunk (it usually shrinks, common with most amphetamines). I experience only minor feelings of deep, satisfying breathing. This feels like perhaps 70-80mg methylone (sans MAOI), at most. T+1.5 hours: weird, I still feel like things are getting more intense. Usually things have plateau'd by now. This feels like a low dose, which is fine. This is in the name of science - I wasn't planning on having a full blown fun time today. Is this gradual increase in effects perhaps the MAOI increasing catecholamines, since they aren't being broken down as quickly? T+2.5 hours: Ahh, bummer, any sign of mood lift is gone, just feeling tired now. Perhaps the mood lift lasted a little longer than usual, but I'm also feeling more confused/slow (mentally) than usual. Not good for such a low dose. T+4 hours: I almost fell asleep - I feel tired, warm, in the usual methylone come-down kind of way. However, the come-down feels like I just went through a 150-200mg experience, not a 28mg one! I consider this a big negative, because I didn't get the euphoria, heart opening, or insight of a higher-dose experience, but the lethargy of a larger-dose experience is there... Well, it seems like the MAOI continued to make things stronger as time progressed. However, this strength was overlayed on the usual methylone timeline! Blah. So it appears as if the fun, initial effects (T+0.5 to T+2 hours) probably won't be greatly potentiated by the MAOI with higher doses of methylone, but the after-effects will continue to grow in intensity. I now feel wiped out and mentally slow. Given this finding, I believe that a larger dose of methylone with moclobemide would likely produce large, negative after-effects, including lethargy and loss of motivation. However, I have the sense that the initial effects wouldn't be similarly potentiated. In other words, this combination doesn't look like it's worth it, especially given the potential danger of mixing MAOIs with stimulants! I was planning on trying a 56mg dose of methylone with 75mg Moclobemide later this week, but after today's experiment, further experimentation will cease. I guess this will be another data point for MAOI+stimulant drug = potentiation, but not in a good way, and definitely not worth it.

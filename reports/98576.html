昨晚我对普罗米塔辛有了一个不愉快的经历. 我有习惯每天服用4种特拉马多尔. 是的,我沉迷于它,但这是我正在处理的问题. 4个特拉马多尔是我减肥策略的一部分. 无论如何,昨晚我从柜子里拿出错误的药瓶, 吃了4个25毫克的Phenergan. 它们的大小和形状几乎与特拉马多尔相同. 我没有意识到我服用了错误的药物, 我开始感到极度疲. 我无法坐起来. 我开始在外视中看到闪闪发光的东西. 我认为',好吧,今天是超声波给我发作的日子. 但是我没有发作. 我感觉像是中风或其他什么. 我是在幻觉. 我不能说话, 没有失去我的思维在句子中间. 每次我试图走路, 我都会开始倾斜. 我丈夫想带我去急诊室, 但我说服他让我躺下. 我睡了15个小时. 我试图弄清楚发生了什么事, 对于任何考虑以娱乐为目的使用这种药物的人, 没有任何令人愉快的感觉, 我的四肢还在. 其他: 药物 - 普罗米塔辛 (336):一般 (1), 困难经历 (5), 过量服用 (29), 宿醉/ 几天后 (46), 不适用 (38)

Dec 13, 2012. erowid.org/exp/98576 DOSE: 100 mg oral Pharms - Promethazine (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 150 lb Last night I had an unfortunate experience with promethazine. I have a habit of taking 4 tramadols every day. Yes, I'm addicted to it but that is an issue I'm dealing with. 4 tramadols is part of my tapering down strategy. Anyway, last night I grabbed the wrong pill bottle out of the cabinet and took 4 25mg Phenergan. They are pretty much the same size and shape of the tramadols. I didn't realize I'd taken the wrong stuff and I went about my day. About an hour after taking them I started feeling exceedingly tired. I could not sit up. I then started seeing shiny things in my peripheral vision. I thought 'oh great, today is the day that ultram gives me a seizure'. But I didn't have a seizure. I had what felt like a stroke or something. I was hallucinating. I couldn't speak without losing my train of thought in mid sentence. Every time I tried to walk, I'd just start leaning over. My husband wanted to take me to the ER but I convinced him to just let me lay down. I slept for 15 hours. I tried to figure out what happened and noticed that the promethazine bottle was in a different place than it was the day before. To any person out there who is considering taking this drug recreationally, let it be known that you are making a mistake. There was nothing pleasant to say about how it felt while this stuff worked its way out of my body. My limbs are still shaky.

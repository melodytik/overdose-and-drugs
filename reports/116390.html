首先是关于我的一些背景. 现在我不再像过去那样使用迷幻药来"旅行", 它们让我着迷. 我使用迷幻药作为一种工具, 最好是少量, 我每天都在冥想, 在冥想时, 我能感觉到精灵体内的能量流动. 我经常练习呼吸与视觉, 激活相应的脉动. 我对它们和它们的工作方式知之甚少. 我最近才发现. 我也对天体投射和清醒梦感兴趣. 我做了相应的练习 (到达精神清醒身体睡眠状态和睡眠,写一个梦日记,等等). 我在这些状态中几乎没有成功. 我在童年曾经有过睡眠麻, 最近我在睡眠中经历了自发的振动状态. 振动状态通常发生在星体投射之前. 我吸过DMT无数次. 问题在于这种影响是短暂的. 很自然,我正在考虑服用MAOI,然后再服用DMT. 传统的亚亚瓦斯卡中含有哈马拉类化合物,主要是哈马林,哈马林和四哈马林. 我读到在好的阿雅华斯卡中, 哈马林的含量是尽可能低的. 哈马林已知有恶心的作用, 因此,我使用了哈尔和四哈尔的混合物. 我在减少/哈马林混合物 (从叙利亚种子中提取) 与甲醇中的酸后获得了这种混合物. 纯度通过HPLC和1HNMR进行检查. 它非常纯净,HPLC纯度>99% (没有其他化合物). 很久以前我也用更复杂的化学合成制备了DMT. 它也是非常纯净的 (通过1H和13CNMR检查). 它在高真空下蒸,然后从六/二甲重新结晶以产生漂亮的白的晶体. 通常DMT是黄色的, 含有一些氧化产物, 这种香味很香,很舒服. 在经历之前我只吃了一块小包饼和一些普通奶酪. 我根本没有紧张. 我心情很愉快. 通常我吸DMT时会很焦虑. 我通常单独服用迷幻药, 我在毫克尺度上测量了148毫克的哈尔 (67毫克) 和四哈尔 (81毫克) 混合物. 我用几毫升的花茶将它悬浮起来. 由于茶很酸, 溶解了一些哈马拉化物, 我在9点吃了它. 随后在9点25分, 我测量出51毫克的DMT自由基, 这是一个错误,因为DMT的味道很糟糕... 下次我必须把它放在一个凝囊... 在9:45我开始感到恶心. 我坐在沙发上, 试着慢慢呼吸. 很快恶心就消失了, 我看到墙上的图案, 当我集中注意力时. 我用它来检查自己有多. 虽然我觉得非常. 身体感觉像DMT,但不同. 我猜哈马拉化物是体验的增强者, 我躺在沙发上,用毯子遮住自己. 眼睛一闭, 我感觉到身体在左边的轴线上有强烈的旋转. 我从来没有感觉到. 眼睛一闭, 我感觉到身体在左边的轴线上有强烈的旋转. 我从来没有感觉到. 然后,我的注意力从我平时的注意力中心 (眼睛后面) 移到身体左边. 感觉很奇怪,所以我慢慢地重新集中自己. 有时我在深度冥想时也会有类似的经历. 接下来的一个半小时我躺在沙发上. 在这段时间里, 我不断地将注意力转移到我所说的人类自我的视角和我灵魂的视角. 至少这就是我在经历中所想到的. 我觉得灵魂是某种外星人, 人类生活和肉体现在感觉异常, 灵魂是某种多维能量场 (某种类型的虫,章鱼或七足动物类生物体 (如电影"抵达"中). 我感觉很正常, 没有什么不寻常的. 这是一个非常强大的和神似的实体. 人类看起来像是某种笑话, 荒谬和原始的生物. 尤其是在人体及其生殖器官. 我认为这种性状态是一种强大的无条件的爱 (无法用言语表达) 对其他灵魂而言, 这感觉像是个很有趣的笑话. 我无法理解. 我笑了很多. 我从灵魂的角度来看我现在的生活. 我在这个生活中没有太多的朋友, 但我想象了两个亲密的朋友, 我把注意力集中在母亲身上, 她有精神病发作, 必须多次住院. 现在她每天都要服用抗精神病药物. 我没有觉得她与我有任何关系. 我对父母和家人也有这种疏远的感觉. 我似乎使用他们来化身在这里, 但他们不是发达的灵魂. 它们也与我不亲近, 在这种状态下, 我似乎能够感知到人类多层次的本质 (意识只是一个小层, 我无法理解我看到和得到的东西. 这是我第一次感知到它. 感觉就像是一些多维,层次的信息... 我无法用言语来形容. 我认为我的母亲是这样的, 首先我觉得她在很多方面真的很糟糕. 在这种思维过程中, 我反复把注意力转移到我的身体和人类角色/自我. 我也在冥想中这样做. 听到通常的声音和视觉图像, 我意识到如果我想学习天体投影, Jun 17, 2022. erowid.org/exp/116390 DOSE: T+ 0:00 67 mg oral Harmala Alkaloids (freebase) T+ 0:00 81 mg oral Tetrahydroharmine (freebase) T+ 0:25 51 mg oral DMT (freebase) BODY WEIGHT: 67 kg First some background about me. I now do not take psychedelics to "trip" as I used to do in the past in which I was mostly interested in visual hallucinations. They fascinated me. Now, I use psychedelics as a tool and preferably in small amounts so that I am still in control and can use the state to do self-exploration, combined with meditation. I do meditate daily for about a year now. During meditative sessions I can feel the energy flows in my subtle (etheric) body. Most of the time I do breathing exercises combined with visualization to activate corresponding chakras. Little do I know about them and how they work. It just opened to me recently. Also, I am interested in astral projection and lucid dreaming. I do corresponding exercises for this (reaching the mind awake body asleep state and sleep paralysis, writing a dream diary, etc.). I had little success in achieving those states so far. In my childhood I had sleep paralysis once and recently I experienced a spontaneous vibrational state during sleep. Vibrational state usually occurs before the astral projection. I smoked DMT countless times. The problem is that the effects are short lasting. Naturally I was thinking to take MAOIs and then DMT. In traditional ayahuasca there are harmala alkaloids, mostly harmine, harmaline and tetrahydroharmine. The content of those varies and I read that in good ayahuasca, the content of harmaline is as low as possible. Harmaline is known to have nauseating effects which I wanted to avoid. Therefore, I used a mixture of harmine and tetrahydroharmine. I obtained this mixture after reduction of harmine/harmaline mixture (extracted from Syrian rue seeds) with sodium borohydride in methanol. The purity was checked by HPLC and 1H NMR. It was very pure, HPLC purity >99% (there was no other compounds present). DMT was prepared by more complex chemical synthesis a long time ago by me as well. It is also very pure (checked by 1H and 13C NMR). It was distilled under high vacuum and then recrystallized from hexane/dichloromethane to produce nice off-white fluffy crystals. Usually, DMT is yellow and contains some oxidation products and smells really bad. This has nice soft and pleasant smell. Before the experience I had only a small baguette with some regular cheese. I was not nervous at all. I was in pleasant mood. Usually when I smoke DMT I get quite anxious. I usually take psychedelics alone and this was the case as well. I weighed out 148 mg of a mixture of harmine (67 mg) and tetrahydroharmine (81 mg) freebase on a milligram scale. I suspended it in few mL of rose hip tea. Because the tea is quite acidic, it dissolved some of the harmala alkaloids and the mixture was a bit bitter tasting but nothing unbearable. I ingested it at 9:00. Then, at 9:25, I weighed out 51 mg of DMT freebase and took it in a similar way. This was a mistake because DMT tastes awful... Next time I must put it into a gelatin capsule... At 9:45 I started to feel nausea. I sit into a meditation posture on my couch and try to breath slowly. The nausea subsided soon and at 10:00 I already started to feel the DMT effects. I saw the patterns pulsing on walls when I focused attention. I use this to check how much I am tripping. I felt really trippy though. Body feel was DMT-like but different. I guess the harmala alkaloids are really enhancers of the experience and bring their own flavor to it. I lay down on my couch and covered myself with a blanket. As soon as I closed my eyes, I experienced a strong spinning sensation to the left around the axis of my body. I never felt that before. As soon as I closed my eyes, I experienced a strong spinning sensation to the left around the axis of my body. I never felt that before. Then, my attention moved out of center of my usual attention point (behind my eyes) to the left from my body. It felt weird and so I slowly centered myself again. Sometimes I experience something similar in deep meditation. The next hour and half I was lying in my couch. During this time, I was constantly shifting attention to what I would call a human ego's perspective and my soul's perspective. At least that’s what I though during the experience. From my ego's perspective I felt the soul is some kind of alien, it felt really alien-like. On the other hand, I then switched to soul's perspective and the human life and the physical body felt now alien and something really odd. The soul was some kind of multidimensional energy field (some kind of amoeba, octopus-like or heptapod-like organism (like in the movie Arrivals). That's how I perceived it and it felt very normal, nothing unusual. It was a very powerful and god-like entity. The human seemed like some kind of joke, ridiculous and primitive organism. Especially the human body and its reproductive organs. I viewed sexuality in this state as a powerful unconditional love (inexpressible in words) for other souls and this powerful force was meant to be felt by these joke-looking reproductive organs? It felt like some really funny joke. I could not comprehend it. I laughed so much. I then looked at pieces of my current life from my soul's perspective. I don't have many friends in this life but I picture two close friends and felt this incredible powerful love for them and I understood we are similar souls. I then focused my attention on my mother who is unfortunately mentally ill, but fortunately stable now. She had psychotic episodes and had to be hospitalized many times. Now she takes antipsychotics every day. I did not perceived her to be close to me at all. I had this distant feeling to my parents and family as well. I seemed to use them to incarnate here but they are not developed souls. They are not close to me also in my ego's perspective. In this state I seemed to be able to perceive the multilayered nature of a human being (consciousness is only a small layer, then there are multiple layers in the subconscious). I could not comprehend what I was seeing and getting. It was the first time I was able to perceive it like this. It felt like some multidimensional, layered information... I cannot describe it is words. I perceived my mother this way and first thing I felt was that she is really fu*ked-up in many ways. During this mental reasoning I was repeatedly shifting attentions to my body and to my human role/ego. I was grounding myself as I do in meditation as well. The perception of the usual sounds and visual imagery grounds me in this reality. I realized that I cannot count on this if I want to learn astral projection. The only certainty are the energy centers (chakras) that persist after death of the physical body. I then thought about bad trips from the soul's perspective, and it was so clear to me and ridiculous as well. Bad trips only arise when the ego cannot comprehend and make sense of the transpersonal experiences... At the end, I realized I should take care more about my avatar (body) in this lifetime, I should run more and generally keep the body healthy. At 11:30 I woke up from the state and looked at my hands. They looked so strange. I counted my fingers and was thinking why the hell do I have 5 fingers, I could very well have less or more. I was generally surprised by this body I live in. It is interesting that I didn’t have closed eye visuals during the trip. Usually, I have them all the time when I smoke DMT. After that I went to toilet 2 times in a short amount of time. I guess the DMT and harmala alkaloids takes out a lot of water from the body. So, I drank some more water and went to sleep. Always after these trips I feel exhausted. I was in a dream-like state but don't remember anything specific. I woke up at 15:30. The effects were completely gone, and I felt relaxed, as nothing happened. I then finally made some lunch (vegetable soup) and then went for a walk. I was feeling an increased amount of energy. Overall, it was very interesting experience, and I will be processing it for some time.

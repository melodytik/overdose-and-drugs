中文译文 抑制剂

我的弟弟有社交焦虑和上帝只知道什么其他问题, 他可能甚至不吃,所以我偷了一些以后实验. 星期五的中午,我在数学课上, 在笔记本上涂, 我很无聊,然后我记得我有Paxils. 现在是个很好的时机, 让他们在一天的后半段度过高峰期 (这是我认为需要多久的时间, 我把它们直接放下喉, 没有触摸舌头, 这东西像货运列车一样撞击. 我突然有一种奇怪的感觉, 我周围没有什么是重要的, 我甚至不试图关注我的教练或我们正在讨论的问题, 我周围的任何东西都没有意义, 我内心的独白和周围的世界之间有越来越大的沟. 我觉得现在我可以随时起身离开, 因为这所学校对我来说毫无意义. 在此同时,我的眼睛也在不断地变焦, 在接下来的15分钟里, 很难看清楚任何东西, 现在是最困难的部分--我突然感到可怕的,灼热的胃痛, 现在,我不得不去洗手间, 因为我可能会吐, 我很难在浴室里和朋友聊天, 我坐在所上几分钟, 不是为了放松自己, 很快钟声就会响, 下一堂课不够重要, 服用药后大约20分钟, 所有的效果都消失了, 我感觉好了. 我觉得有点放松,但有点. 我的头几乎完全清晰, 除了我的眼睛定期漫游的焦点. 我在公共汽车上, 意识到我自己的意识是我生命中唯一不变的东西, 其他一切都不真实. 像我在一个矩阵里, 我是唯一一个人. 其他所有影响都消失了, 这种东西可以让我做一些很酷的事情, 我通常是个很害羞的家伙, 这真的是令人毛骨然的不合适的狗, 我没有在我的脑海中排练. 这只是流出. 我从来没有这样看过自己. 这是一种令人惊叹的感觉. 这一切都是在那个基本的,稍微混的THC上, 影响持续了大约13个小时左右, 直到第二天我醒来. 总的来说, 帕克西尔 (除了最初的胃痛) 是一个非常棒的, 振奋的情绪增强剂. 我只是在非法娱乐层面说话, 其他: 2011年3月4日发布 药品 - 帕洛 (148):学校 (35),一般 (1),第一次 (2)

I'm in the Matrix Paroxetine (Paxil) by Joeyramoney Citation:   Joeyramoney. "I'm in the Matrix: An Experience with Paroxetine (Paxil) (exp65970)". Erowid.org . Mar 4, 2011. erowid.org/exp/65970 DOSE: 3 tablets oral Pharms - Paroxetine (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 145 lb My brother has social anxiety and God only knows what other problems, and has a prescription to a Paxil everyday. He probably don’t even take ‘em, so I nicked a few to experiment with later on. My rule of thumb with pharms is three times the suggested dosage (yeah I live on the fucking edge), so I take three little pink pills and stick them in my wallet. Mid morning on a Friday, I’m in trig class, doodling in my notebook because I haven’t got a damned clue what the teacher is going on about and don’t really care. I'm just bored outta my skull, and then I remember I have Paxils. Now’s as good a time as any to take them and ride out the high for latter part of of the day (which is how long I figure it’ll take since I’m kinda full). I drop them straight down my throat without touching my tongue, trying to avoid the ubiquitous bitter aftertaste in the back of my throat where I always think that I don’t have taste buds. T-00:03- The stuff hits like a freight train. All the sudden, I get this bizarre feeling that nothing around me is of importance or even real, and that my consciousness is the only definite thing in the universe. I don’t even try to pay attention to my instructor or the problems we’re going over, and resume doodling in the margins of my notebook. Nothing around me has meaning, even the things I just drew. There is a widening chasm between my internal monologue and the world around me. I think at this point I could just get up at any point and leave, since this freaking school means nothing to me. At the same time, my eyes drift in and out of focus, and for the next 15 minutes or so, it’s pretty challenging to see anything straight, but I don’t really need to anymore since the only things of matter in this universe now exist inside my head. Now the hard part- I suddenly get a horrible, searing stomach ache, like an alien parasite fighting it’s way out of my belly. I excuse myself to the bathroom because most immediately I am possibly about to vomit and secondarily since I’m just loosing my shit in the middle of math class. I have the challenge of making small talk with a friend in the bathroom, but it’s hard to talk. I sit on the toilet for a few minutes, not really to relieve myself but just to breathe enough air into my stomach to convince myself I’m not gonna throw up. T-00:10- Pretty soon the bell’s gonna ring, and the next class is not important enough to bear through like this, so I just lie down on the couch in the senior lounge. About twenty minutes after I took the pills, all the effects fade out, and I feel better. I feel a bit relieved, but a bit ripped off. My head is near totally clear, aside from my eyes periodically wandering out of focus. T-02:30- I’m on a public bus, and I get the realization that my own consciousness is the only thing that is constant in my life and thus the only thing that I should consider. Nothing else is real. It’s like I’m in the matrix and I’m the only one that’s human. All the other effects have faded, but a lengthy, intense delusion of intense alienation has ensued. This shit can make me do pretty cool things when nothing else is real. I’m usually a pretty shy dude, but I was talking to every girl that came my way. This was really creepy inappropriate shit that I didn’t rehearse in my head at all. It just flowed right out. Nothing happened with them, but I’ve never seen myself that way before in my life. It’s a really amazing sensation. This was all on top of that elemental slightly confused-THC like high that effects my immediate perception. The effects lasted about thirteen more hours or so, until I woke up the next day. All in all, Paxil was (aside from the initial stomach pain) a really awesome and invigorating mood enhancer. Of course, I’m just talking at the illicit recreational level, and have no ezperience with what the reccomended dose would do.

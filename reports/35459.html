我已经吸烟了大约3年了, 起初它是秘密的, 隐藏的, 我曾经得到一个巨大的,但从来没有告诉任何人. 事实上,我和女朋友分手是因为她吸烟. 跳过两年时间, 到我大学的第二年. 我住在一个像宿舍的公寓里, 我在秋冬的消费量大大增加, 每天大约三分之一包. 我真的很喜欢这项工作, 一个香烟和咖啡开始一天. 我经常吃 (我仍然非常喜欢这种事). 我经常在我的房间里吸大麻, 最终被大学警察抓住, 我每天的吸烟量至少增加到半包, 我当时的压力很大. 我第一次意识到我完全依赖它们, 头痛, 在我的朋友生日 (跳到前几个月, 我搬出住房回家后), 我们去了海边在海上打鼓和吉他. 我真的无法闻到海洋的味道. 在那一天之后, 我没有抽烟两个月, 直到我开始法院命令的社区服务. 结果我有120个小时的时间来完成. 我不得不停在第一个加油站买一包汽油. 我会不时地戒烟, 说'这是我最后一包, 我发誓', 我总是给自己设定最后期限, 这让我们回到我第一次尝试酸的时刻. 我在那天吸了一整包香烟, 非常享受, 整天经常渴望吸. 这次经历并不是我周末的结局, 我在朋友S的家中清晨醒来, 我们开始吃一堆, 那一天早上改变了我的生活. 我失去了自己的情感,自然,和爱. 经历我最终会记录, 但它仍然是太早之后完全解释. 整天的剩余时间, 我几乎了9个小时. 后来,我去划独木舟,喝了几杯酒. 仍然在抽烟. 很简单地说, 我对它们完全没有任何感觉. 我不想吸烟,我根本不在乎. 我意识到我改变生活的经历在一夜之间沉没了, 我分享这个故事, 希望其他试图戒烟的人也能用类似的迷幻药来了解他们真正关心的东西, 改变了我的生活, 和平的 其他: 烟草 - 香烟 (266):小组 (2-9) (17),健康益处 (32),成和习惯性 (10)

Jan 16, 2007. erowid.org/exp/35459 DOSE: repeated smoked Tobacco - Cigarettes 4.0 g oral Mushrooms BODY WEIGHT: 160 lb I've been smoking cigs for about 3 years now, and it started out as something that was secret and hidden, something i enjoyed in a park. I used to get a huge buzz, but never told anyone about it. As a matter of fact, i basically broke up with my girlfriend because she smoked. Skip ahead 2 years to my sophomore year at college. I was living in an apartment like dorm, with a fire escape I could smoke on whenever I so desired. My consumption went way up in the fall and winter, up to around maybe a third of a pack a day. I really enjoyed it, and it became part of what I did. A cigarette and coffee to start the day. This same year I had been smoking a lot of pot and doing mushrooms not infrequently (something i still greatly enjoy). Well, due to my living situation, I smoked pot in my room often, eventually getting busted by the University Police, which ultimately resulted in my getting kicked out of housing. My smoking consumption shot up, to at the very least half a pack a day, if not more. The stress I was under was intense. It was around then that i first became aware of being completely physically dependent on them, getting headaches, and crankiness when I didn't have a cig. On my friends birthday (skipping ahead a few months, after I had moved out of housing and back home), we went up to the ocean to play drums and guitars on the beach. I literally couldn't smell the ocean. After that day, I didn't have a cigarette for 2 months, until I started court ordered community service. The university had pressed charges against me, and I wound up with 120 hours to complete. On the way to the first session, the intensity of what I was doing hit me, and I had to pull over into the first gas station to buy a pack. This then led to a 3 month stretch where I would quit intermittently, say 'This is my last pack, I swear', and then the next day buy one. I kept making deadlines for myself, then not keeping them. This gets us up to the point I was at when I tried acid for the first time. Going through nearly a whole pack of cigarettes that day I enjoyed intensely, craving them quite frequently throughout the day. This experience was not the end of my weekend however, for the next day would yield something quite different. I woke up at dawn at my friend S's house, and we proceeded to eat a whole pile of mushrooms, at least 4 grams each. That morning has changed my life forever. I lost myself in emotion, nature, and love. The experience I will eventually chronicle, but it is still too soon afterwards to fully explain. For the rest of the day I was tripping, almost a full 9 hours. Afterwards, I went canoeing and drank a few beers. Still smoking cigarettes. The next morning, I woke up, saw the pack in my pocket, and quite simply, had absolutely no feeling about them either way. I did not want to smoke them, I didn't really care about them at all. Throwing them out, I realized that my life changing experience had sunk in overnight, and that cigarettes were not something my life needed. I share this story in the hopes that other people trying to quit tobacco can similarly use psychedelics to realize what truly matters to them, and can jettison habits that have dogged them for a long time. Mushrooms have changed my life irrevocably over the years, always for the best. Paz.

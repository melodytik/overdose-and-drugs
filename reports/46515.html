Aug 7, 2018. erowid.org/exp/46515 DOSE: smoked Phalaris Grass (powder / crystals) smoked Cannabis BODY WEIGHT: 120 lb I was planning on growing the botanical alled Phalaris Arundincea by seed. I bought 1/4pound of the seeds from a plant vendor as well as studied the plant. When the seeds came, I sprinkled some on some soil/sand, and within a week they began to sprout right in my bedroom, kinda cool I thought. My friend wayne lives far away, and I don't get to visit too often, so I wanted to drop him a letter to let him know how I was doing, I decided to split my bag of grass seeds and send him some as a gift. I then poured out the entire bag of seeds onto a white sheet of paper, after folding the paper in half I slid the seeds down the crease into seperate baggies. Once all the seeds were away, and my letter was done I looked at the piece of paper I used as a seperation tool and to my amazement I saw trace quanities of white/clear crystal particles, I took a plastic card and scrapped them together I saw trace quanities of white/clear crystal particles, I took a plastic card and scrapped them together , and they had a distinct off white / tan color, and my mind had an idea of what it was. At the time I was sorta smoking some very potent buds, what some would call 'the funk', chronic, kind bud, if you smoke weed, you have an idea of what type of bud this was, one hit wonder stuff. I was hitting it out of this small glass cigarette (a one hitter), and out of sudden curiosity I pinched a piece of bud and pushed it on the crystals I scraped together on the piece of paper, the crystals stuck to it almost like a magnet, the bud was pretty sticky. I packed it in and took a rip, burning the material to pure carbon ash. I held it in like I sometimes find myself doing when I only have a little bit of weed, and wanna get really high, I let my lungs take the damage of absorbing the smoke as well as possible, yeah this isn't benefitial for my long term health, but for this short duration it is quite effective. Exhaling I began to be lost in my mind and was feeling voices again, the auditory noises sounded like they came from inside, though I heard them, somehow the voices were detached from my physical ears, like I was hearing it from another dimension. I heard a male and a female, madly arguing I heard a male and a female, madly arguing , screaming at one another. A petty argument with harsh abrupt statements, very rude. This mellowed out and faded away within 3 minutes. I feel the lingering feeling of a 5meo buzz at initial levels. The experience proved to be interesting .

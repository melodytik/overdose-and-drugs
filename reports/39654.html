中文译文 致幻剂

这一切始于三月左右, 我试过它, 认为这将是一次梦幻般的旅行, 我对毒品一无所知, 在我做这件事之前, 没有. 我的朋友应该和我一起尝试这个,但他的瓜睡着了! 他来访了,吃了2大匙,并保持一点它没有影响我们所以他只是崩,我试图告诉他给它一些时间,但他只是睡着了,这让我生气. 我开始玩PS2来消磨时间,我玩了一点MGS:2然后我感觉到这种能量涌入,我只是想'它即将开始!'我停下来玩了一会儿,只是坐下来一会儿得到了一些 kool-aid,当我重新开始玩游戏时,它听起来非常不同. 所有电子声, 声音减慢了速度, 我暂停了游戏,想知道有没有变音器坏了,然后我重置了它. 这听起来真的很可怕! 我把电话关掉了,父母叫我去睡觉, 我试图睡觉,但我无法入睡,我没有感到奇怪或高或任何东西. 我的想法对他们来说更有意义. 我躺在那里仍然清醒, 我开始恶心和真正的头. 我去所吐. 我吐了很多东西! 我告诉他我只是生病了, 他问我是否想把垃圾桶放在床边, 我说是的. 和他说话是非常安慰. 和任何人说话会让我感到安慰. 我告诉他留下来,说话更长时间我告诉他我有上帝可怕的梦,他留了几分钟. 我爸爸醒来问我是否还好. 我只是说我有点不舒服. 他只是告诉我喝点水,我喝了,我感觉好一点. 他们又睡了一会儿. 我只是躺在床上,闭上眼睛, 只是不经意间出现的想法. 我开始想起一个我从来不认可的女孩. 她的脸突然出现,然后她变成了像一些骨, 它发出一种可怕的低声叫 (是的,这是一个低声叫). 这吓坏了我! 我跑到浴室,看着镜子,我的脸是扭曲的,看起来不对. 我真的很害怕,开始向上帝祈祷. 这让我有点安慰, 但每次我看镜子我看起来很奇怪. 我仍然看起来一样,但我可以看到自己真的是. 似乎我的内在美丽 (实际上是内在的丑) 正在浮出水面. 我从来没有注意到我有多少粉刺,我的皮肤有多油, 我从来没有高看过自己,但当你认为你看起来更糟糕的时候! 我只是祈祷让这一切结束. 我试着玩一些游戏, 但游戏太激烈了, 刚刚关掉电话,我父亲问我能不能低声说话. 我说好吧,我的朋友醒了,我告诉他我得多厉害,他似乎没有被迷住,这对我来说很奇怪. 我们开始聊了一会儿,我求他不要再睡觉. 但他变得皮,威胁我,我告诉他放心. 他确实是,他只是因为醒来而发,他似乎没有意识到情况的严重性. 这并没有对他产生任何影响. 他给了我一些安眠药, 我害怕他们会把我搞得更糟,但他说他们不会,他不会给我任何会杀我的东西. 我相信他的话,相信他们. 他回到床上. 我躺下了好几个小时. 我脑海中流传着不同类型的新歌, 我不喜欢它. 我看着我的手在毯子下, 它看起来非常非常奇怪. 我很害怕,因为它开始流血,我立刻转眼,因为它看起来很真实. 我知道这是幻觉. 我开始告诉自己我多么愚蠢, 效果开始逐渐减弱,我累了,睡了. 我没有看到任何疯狂的几何图案或任何疯狂的刻板印象的废话, 只是我的好奇心付出了代价. 环境和思维方式都是错误的. 如果我能改变这次旅行, 我会在白天做, 在自然中, 并且降低了我的剂量. 我已经学会了不那么天真了. 我终于克服了发生在我身上的事情. 由于我对此事的了解增加了十亿倍, 其他: 皮约特 (42):小组 (2-9) (17),艰难的经历 (5),第一次 (2)

May 30, 2006. erowid.org/exp/39654 DOSE: oral Unknown (pill / tablet) 5 Tbsp oral Peyote (ground / crushed) BODY WEIGHT: 120 lb This all started around March or so that I obtained some peyote. I tryed it out thinking it would be some fantastic trip with just hallucinations and goofy hippy shit like that, but no, I was unaware of the power peyote can have on me. I had absolutly no knowledge about drugs, hell I haven't even smoked weed prior to the time I did this! No shit. My friend was supposed to try this with me but his dumbass went to sleep! He came over for a visit and took 2 tablespoons and stayed up for a lil bit it wasn't effecting us so he just crashed, I tryed to tell him give it some time but he just went to sleep which pissed me off. I started playing the PS2 to kill time for awhile I played a little of MGS:2 then I felt this rush of energy and I just thought 'It's coming on!' I quit playing for awhile and just sat down for awhile got some kool-aid and when I started playing the game again it sounded very different. All the electronic beeping sounds, sounding slowed down, kinda like down an octave like a flat note. I paused the game and wondered if the stereo was on the fritz then I reseted it. It sounded really really scary! I just turned it off and my parents told me to go to bed because I promised to go to church the next morning. I tried going to sleep but I just couldn't sleep, I didn't feel wierd or high or anything. My thoughts had more meaning to them. I was lying there still awake, and I started to get nauseated and real dizzy. I went to the bathroom to puke. I puked hard! My brother came out of his room and asked if I was ok, I told him I was just sick, and he asked me if I wanted the trashcan by my bed and I said yes. Talking to him was very comforting. Talking to anyone would've been comforting. I told him to stay and talk longer I told him I had god awful nightmares and he did stay for a few minutes. My dad woke up and asked if I was ok. I said I just felt a little ill. He just told me to drink some water and I did, I felt a world better. They went back to sleep for awhile. I just layed in my bed, I closed my eyes and I started to see thoughts take a visible image. Just thoughts that manifested themselves upon me involuntarily. I started to think about this one girl I never pay any recognition to. Her face popped up and then she turned into like some skeleton that resembled a bug but still retained her facial image. It was letting out some eery low screech (yes it was a low screech). This scared the living shit outa me! I ran to the bathroom and I looked at the mirror and my face was all contorted and didn't look right. I got real scared and started praying to god. This comforted me a little but everytime I looked into the mirror I just looked strange. I still looked the same but I could see myself as I really was. It was like my inner-beauty (actually innerugly) was surfacing. I never noticed how many pimples I had, how oily my skin was or how crappy I looked. I never thought highly of myself but man, just when you thought you couldn't look any worse! This all scared me deeply and I just prayed asking for it to end. I tried playing some more games but the games were just too intense and took on a whole new meaning that wasn't appealing to me. Just when I turned it off my dad asked if I could keep it down. I said ok and my friend woke up and I told him how hard I was tripping he really didn't seem to be fascinated which seemed really odd to me. We started talking for awhile and I begged him to not go back to sleep. But he was being all bitchy and threatened me and I told him to chill out. He did,he was just cranky from waking up he didn't seem to realize the seriousness of the situation. It wasn't affecting him any(the peyote that is). He gave me some sleeping pills and told me to chill out. I was afraid to take them for fear that they would fuck me up even more, but he said they wouldn't and he wouldn't give me anything that would kill me. I took his word for it and took them. He went back to bed. I layed down for what seemed like hours. New songs of different genres played in my head, they sounded out of this world. I didn't like it. I looked at my hand under the covers and It looked very very odd. It terrified me alot cause it started bleeding I looked away immediately cause it seemd so real. I knew it was a hallucination. I started telling myself of how stupid I was for even wanting to try this. The effects were starting to taper off and I got tired and slept. I didn't see any crazy geometric patterns or any of the crazy stereotypical bullshit I thought I would achieve if I took any psychadelic. It was more of a mind game that my curiosity had to pay the consequences for. The setting and mindset was all wrong. If I could change this trip I would've done it during the day, in nature, away from any electronics and tripping with someone else. And reduced my doseage. After this experience, I have come to respect drugs and learned to not be so damn naive. I finally got over what happened to me. If it happened again I could handle the situation alot better since I have become a billion times more educated on the matter.

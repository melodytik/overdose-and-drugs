中文译文 致幻剂

我先从我以前的迷幻体验开始, 我已经服用了大约50到60次,两次LSD,四次X,半打次机器炸,几次JimsonWeed和之前两次圣佩德罗体验. 两次圣佩德罗之旅都吃了大约30厘米的仙人掌, 这两次旅行都相对轻微 (强烈的身体高和闭眼视觉,但轻微的幻觉). 我和我的室友为这次旅行买了一棵两尺高的秘鲁火仙人掌以及一棵两尺高的圣佩德罗仙人掌. 我打算吃一英尺的秘鲁菜, 而我的室友则吃一英尺的圣佩德罗, 我们有额外的仙人掌作为备用, 这次旅行的思维方式不可能更好, 这是我冬季假期的第一天, 我们有自己的房子和大多数邻居已经回家休息. 为了准备仙人掌, 我们取下了棘和皮, 我把一英尺价值的绿色材料从秘鲁, 混合在一杯水... 坏主意. 这变成了我见过的最恶心的液,就像两杯粘液. 我必须在每口饮的结束时咬开仙人掌, 没有煮或干燥之前, 永远不要吃这些东西. 我开始喝了这棵仙人掌后45分钟, 我吐了一切, 两个小时后吃了仙人掌我的室友是,但我觉得什么,所以我混合和煮剩余的圣珀多的脚. 我煮了大约45分钟, 我坐下来喝了两杯圣佩德罗果汁, 虽然我还没有开始吸圣佩德罗, 我用了10分钟就喝完了这两杯, 尽管我已经吐出了所有的秘鲁火, 我仍然必须有足够的时间吸收所有的美斯卡林, 完成圣佩德罗之后我倒, 天花板和墙壁移动, 我们决定在荒凉的校园里散步. 视觉变得越来越大. 我在不同建筑的墙上看到人脸. 我们坐下来观看日落大约十五分钟. 我们的思想是清晰的, 我们能够有一个正常的对话, 我感觉到身体的高潮比我在使用 MDMA 时所经历的任何事情都强大. 我的身体完全麻木, 我记得看着我的脚, 我们走回家重新评估情况,决定去看电影院. 我的室友没有那么强烈, 但我即将达到顶峰. 我们在去看电影的时候, 吃了第一个仙人掌后大约5个小时, 但我不确定, 视觉效果比我在LSD或中看到的任何东西都好, 我看到疯狂的东西我甚至不能描述, 和强度是该死的惊人. 最好的部分是闭眼视觉... 充满幻觉的狗我从来没有想象过之前. 一切都是3D, 变化很快, 从邪恶的卡通转向我无法描述的东西. 这次旅行的强度是最惊人的. 我真的感觉到我的头顶两次因为我认为它已经破裂开放因为我有思想的流. 这是我六七年来吸毒中最棒的经历. 我甚至无法站起来走进电影院, 我们不得不回家. 我在这之后达到了一个漂亮的平面,持续了整晚. 我可以去购买第二天出国旅行所需的用品, 开眼视觉已经平静下来, 但身体高和闭眼视觉仍然在整个夜晚. 我睡着了,第二天醒来时感觉很好. 梅斯卡林是最好的药物, 其他药物现在似乎无所谓, 这是一个精神上的高不等于任何我曾经经历过, 它吹我的巴米兹瓦水. 这是一种需要更多人服用的药物. 我觉得我这么脚是因为我吃了这颗生, 但这很恶心, 其他: 仙人掌 - T. peruvianus (69),仙人掌 - T. pachanoi (64):小组 (2-9) (17),烂的经验 (4),组合 (3),第一次 (2)

The Greatest Drug Experience of My Life Cacti - T. pachanoi & Cacti - T. peruvianus by Mescalito Loco Citation:   Mescalito Loco. "The Greatest Drug Experience of My Life: An Experience with Cacti - T. pachanoi & Cacti - T. peruvianus (exp21756)". Erowid.org . Apr 23, 2003. erowid.org/exp/21756 DOSE: T+ 0:00 oral Cacti - T. peruvianus (tea) T+ 3:00 oral Cacti - T. pachanoi (tea) BODY WEIGHT: 160 lb I'll begin with my previous psychedelic experiences, which consist of a good variety of drugs and experiences. I have taken shrooms about 50-60 times, 2 LSD trips, 4 times on X, robofried about half a dozen times, Jimson Weed a few times and two previous San Pedro experiences. The two San Pedro trips involved eating about a foot of the cactus boiled down to two or three cups of nasty liquid. Both trips were relatively mild (Strong body high and closed eye visuals, but minor hallucinations). For this trip my roommate and I purchased a two foot Peruvian torch cactus as well as a two foot San Pedro. My plan was to eat a foot of Peruvian, while my roommate was to consume a foot of the San Pedro, his first experience with mescaline. We had the extra cactus as backup in case one of us didn't trip. The mindset of this trip couldn't have been better, it was my first day of winter break after an awesome 4.0 semester, so my spirits were high. We had the house to ourselves and most neighbors were already headed home for the break. To prepare the cactus we removed the spines and skin and then cut the 1/4 inch or so of green material right under the skin to use. I ground a foot worth of green material from the Peruvian, mixed with about a cup of water...bad idea. This turned into the nastiest slime I'd ever seen, it was like two cups of mucus. I had to bite the cactus apart at the end of each sip to get it down. Never eat this shit without boiling or drying it first. Forty five minutes after I began to drink the cactus, I threw up everything, and it all came up in one slimy chunk. Two hours after eating the cactus my roommate was tripping, but I felt nothing, so I blended and boiled the remaining foot of San Perdo. I boiled the cactus for about 45 minutes, so it had been about three hours since I began to consume the Peruvian. I sat down to drink the two cups of San Pedro juice, which I mixed with Cranberry juice (good idea, this makes it a lot easier to drink). We smoked a few bowls and I began to feel some effects, even though I hadn't started to consume the San Pedro. I finished the two cups in about ten minutes and I was already fucked up. Even though I had puked out all of the Peruvian torch, I still must have had enough time to absorb all the mescaline from it, because I tripped balls for the next 14 hours. After finishing the San Pedro I was tripping, the ceiling and walls were moving and I felt a great body high. We decided to walk around the deserted campus. The visuals became greater and greater. Leaves on trees appeared to be in intracate geometric patterns, and I saw faces in all the brick walls of the different buildings. We sat down to watch the sunset for about fifteen minutes. Our thoughts were clear and we were able to have normal conversation even though I was tripping my brains out. We got up to walk home and I had a body high that was stronger than anything I had ever experienced on MDMA. My body was totally numb and I remember looking at my feet and not being able to understand how the fuck I could even walk. We walked back to the house to reappraise the situation, deciding to go to a movie theater. My roommate wasn't tripping that hard, but I was about to peak. The peak came when we were in the car driving to the movies. This had to have been about 5 hours after consuming the first cactus, but I'm not sure because I had no sense of time at this point. The visuals were better than anything I have ever seen on LSD or shrooms, and I have taken 8 or 9 grams at a time previously. I was seeing crazy shit I can't even describe, and the intensity was fucking amazing. The best part was the closed eye visuals...full on hallucinations of shit I had never even imagined before. Everything was 3D and changed rapidly, moving from wicked cartoons to other things I really can't describe. The intensity of this trip was the most amazing thing. I honestly felt the top of my head twice because I thought it had cracked open because of the thoughtflow I was having. This was the greatest experience I have ever had in 6 or 7 years of drug use. I couldn't even get up to walk into the movie theater and we had to go home. I reached a nice plateu after this that lasted through the night. I was able to go shopping for supplies I needed for an overseas trip the next day and go to the bar and meet friends. The open eye visuals had calmed down, but the body high and closed eye visuals remained through the night. I fell asleep and woke up the next day feeling excellent. Mescaline is the way to go, every other drug seems like nothing now, and I have no interest in LSD or shrooms any more. This was a spiritual high unequal to anything I've ever experienced, it blew my Bar Mitzvah right out of the water. This is a drug that more people need to do. I think the fact that I tripped so hard had something to do with consuming the cactus raw, but this is disgusting, so I suggest drying it out in a food dehyrator or a dark place.

No psychedelic effects beyond confusion at this point, except the apartment feels very spacious for some reason, as if the outer walls were stretched out. A pretty nice feeling, truth to be told. Somehow I couldn't relax at first; K kept talking when I'd have preferred silence. I laid in the water for a moment, restless, and then decided to take control of the situation and rise up to admire the light show I set up; I happened to have a sheepskin drying up and a few plants waiting to be potted in my bathroom which looked pretty spectacular in the smoothly changing LED lighting. I started to feel, in a word, funky, and rose up to dry myself so I could dance and grab something to eat. We decided to call it a night and took a walk to the woods nearby to split a joint of marijuana, just a little something to relax to afterwards; since the whole thing was dying out and we felt in control of the situation we felt confident enough to leave the apartment. The forest was very nice and quiet, no sounds of traffic could be heard from the nearby town (it was past midnight on Sunday, after all). Even the mosquitoes were sleeping. The joint was lighted and the weed kind of kicked the 3-MeO-PCP experience back up a notch, I got the same spacious feeling in the woods as I did in my apartment earlier. A few words of pondering our existence in this universe were exchanged in the midst of beautiful summer nature. After we finished smoking and got up walking suddenly got somewhat difficult; it wasn't a problem in the end since I live right next door. After coming back I immediately crashed into bed, closed my eyes and started seeing small globes of light going around something. It was kind of like looking at a galaxy of sorts, with solar systems circling the center. While observing this phenomenon I felt quite a few weird sensations: mostly some dull pressure (not painful at all) in the upper part of my forehead and a tingling sensation of relaxation all over my body. This went on for about half an hour and the rest of the night was spent sleeping, which came easily. In the morning I felt very tired, yet hopped into my car and went to work, with a nice feeling of afterglow. Final verdict: 8 mg of 3-MeO-PCP is more of a stimulating, light experience than a full-on psychedelic blast. I'll be sure to try a bigger dose next time.

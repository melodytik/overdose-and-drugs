我已经尝试了3-MEO-PCP和3-MEO-PCE, 我觉得没有对前者进行比较就不公平地对后者进行报告. 这两种化学物质无疑是姐妹. 对于我来说, PCP 变种的整体是非常的, 3-MEO-PCE (或简称PCE, 为了流动性而在本报告中将被引用) 是一个迷人的分离剂. 我对它感到非常紧张, 身体的声很棒, 但总是有点像安非他命的焦虑和"吐"的感觉. 我很喜欢这种药物, 但是在第一次尝试时, 我总是坐在座位的边缘; 这种化学物质的PCE方面完全不同. 我曾在网上读到狂热和成是这种化学物质的主要原因, 但我的经验是相当平静的. +2小时 这两种化学物质彼此产生阴阳的反应. 虽然3-MEO-PCP是磨粉和机械的,但3-MEO-PCE是热和流动的. 如果我不得不逃避抽象的形容词, 身体的高度与其姐姐的高度相似, 但我的思想更高兴和平静. 如果我把我的思想比作水体, PCE 是一片轻轻地在水面上发出波的叶子, 两者都达到相同的效果,只是通过不同的机制. +4小时 现在正在越过山峰. 很多笑声和欢呼, 几乎没有动力. 现在我已经玩了一两个小时了, 我对音乐的欣赏也得到了增强, 如果我快速转动, 很可能是我自己脑海中创造的视觉效果, 而不是化学物质持续地做的事情, +7小时 现在我大部分都下来了. 我确实注意到有些头痛, 但是在喝了大量的水之后, 很可能是两者兼而有之. 为了享受乐趣而来. 刺激效果肯定还在,但我开始感到疲倦. 我注意到身体高是与大麻高度协同, 我可以带回一些身体高感觉之前, 总的来说是一次非常愉快的经历, 我希望尝试更高的剂量 (大约在25-30毫克范围内), 因为我还没有任何不良经历, 但这将不得不等待几天, 因为我觉得这种下降是我不想让我的大脑经常经历的事情. 有关经验:MXE,3-MEO-PCP,LSD,MDMA,M1,可卡因,海洛因,还有其他一些我忘记了 [报告的剂量为"15mg IF" 其他: 3-MEO-PCE (536): 未知背景 (20),回顾 / 摘要 (11),一般性 (1)

Feb 13, 2017. erowid.org/exp/109975 DOSE: 15 mg 3-MEO-PCE (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 140 lb I have tried both 3-MEO-PCP and 3-MEO-PCE and I feel it is unjust to give a report on the latter without it’s comparison to the former. The two are undoubtedly sister chemicals. The PCP variant as a whole came off as very jagged to me, and the understanding of this is what clarifies the experience of 3-MEO-PCE. 3-MEO-PCE (or simply PCE as it shall hereafter be referred to in this report for the sake of fluidity) is a fascinating dissociative. Its sister chemical was not my favorite thing that I had tried as I had felt very on edge with it. The body buzz was wonderful but there was always something reminiscent of the amphetamine anxiety and “tautness” as I feel it. I enjoyed the drug but was constantly on the edge of my seat the first time I tried it; ready to chime in with my next contribution to the conversation the instant someone else finished speaking. The PCE side of this chemical is not the same at all. I had read reports online of mania and addiction being the main concerns with this chemical, but I must say the points seem a bit over stressed in my eyes. By all means, all chemicals of this nature should be treated with respect, but my experiences have been quite calm. my experiences have been quite calm. +2 Hours The two chemicals come off as yin and yang to one another. Where 3-MEO-PCP is grinding and mechanical, 3-MEO-PCE is warm and fluid. If I had to escape from abstract adjectives, I’d call it euphoric. The body high remains so very similarly from its sister, but my thoughts are far more uplifted and calm. If I were to liken my mind to a body of water, the PCE is a leaf that gently sends ripples along the surface, while the 3-MEO-PCP is a rock. They both achieve the same effect, just through different mechanisms. +4 Hours Coming over the peak now. Lots of laughter and elation over all with minimally impaired motor skills. I have been playing video games for an hour or two now and performing as well as I normally would. My appreciation for music is enhanced as well and I have some stop-motion-animation style vision if I turn quickly. Most likely visuals created in my own mind rather than something the chemical is doing consistently, but noticeable none the less. +7 Hours I have mostly come down at this point. On the tail end I did notice some degree of headache but it was quelled by drinking an obscene amount of water so it’s possible it had more to do with dehydration than the compound. Most likely a little bit of both there. Come down is relatively mellow for the amount of fun had. Stimulant effects still present for sure but I’m beginning to get tired. I have noticed the body high is highly synergistic with cannabis and this effect remains on the come down (i.e. I can bring back some of the body high felt before even though it is mostly gone now). Overall a very pleasant experience, although this head ache is a bit of a bummer. I’d like to try a higher dose (around the 25-30mg range) seeing as how I haven’t had any bad experiences yet, but it will have to wait a few days as I don’t feel this come down is something I’d want to put my brain through too often.

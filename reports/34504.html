Nov 29, 2022. erowid.org/exp/34504 DOSE: 1/8 oz oral Mushrooms - Panaeolus cyanescens (dried) BODY WEIGHT: 118 lb It started as a normal Thursday, woke up, went to school, slept through first and second period. The usual. Until lunch. A friend of mine (L) informed me she had just come into a rather large amount of Magic Mushrooms. A little background real fast: I am 17 years old, I started smoking chronic in the summer of my sixth grade year, and since then I have smoked consistently. I had never done anything else (other than drink of course), and was eager to try out Mushrooms in particular. That said, I naturally told her (L) that I would be interested in buying some... Friday: Obtained 1/4 of Cyanesan (sp?) Mushrooms. I had already called my friend M to see if he would like to trip with me, as he was also wanting to try them out. We talked his mom into being our 'sober buddy' for the night (she's cool, since then she's sat in for us being our 'sober buddy' about 10 times) since she had done extensive Mushrooms and LSD as a teenager. Set and setting: M's house, game room, 58" plasma screen tv, surround sound speakers, couches and recliners, a few different movies to choose from (Alice in Wonderland, Matrix 3, Wizard of OZ and Fear and Loathing in Las Vegas), lots of OJ and of course the 'shrooms. Started off just munching down half an eighth each (T+00:00) with some OJ and popped in The Matrix 3. T+ 00:30: Being new to mushrooms, didn't quite know what to expect (thinking that they were somewhat similar to pot, as in a pretty quick coming up), we were slightly disappointed they weren't effective yet. T+ 00:50: Slight tracers now, movies a little distracting, can't quite focus on it. Decide to munch down the rest of our 'shrooms. T+ 01:10: Definitely feeling something, not quite sure, a little like being stoned. Laughing at things that aren't really funny. T+ 01:40: Laughing my ass off at nothing in particular, slight hallucinations, the 'Mechs in Matrix are really life like. Can't quite shake the feeling of being 'in' the Matrix. T+ 02:30: Haven't been watching the movie for awhile now, I have been conversing with the neatest little guy sitting on my arm, he reminds me of a little dwarf with a pipe in his mouth. Haven't been watching the movie for awhile now, I have been conversing with the neatest little guy sitting on my arm, he reminds me of a little dwarf with a pipe in his mouth. Definitely hallucinating now. T+ 03:00: Peaking, seeing some really cool tracers on the Mad Hatter in Alice in Wonderland (we changed movies somewhere in there, but hell if I know when), and colors are really distorted. Went to the bathroom, looked in mirror and couldn't see any white in my eyes, total pupil dilation. Little man walked off down a bizarre path in the middle of M's hallway. Started talking to cute girl standing next to me (turned out to be his lamp, very bizarre). T+ 03:30: Little man back on my arm, just whistling and swinging his feet, tune sounded very similar to Tool's song Opiate (which I had playing in my headphones at this time). T+ 04:00 Definitely coming down, no more hallucinations, some minimal tracers and slight pupil dilation (nothing as compared to before). T+ 05:00 Passed out, still slightly high, very odd dreams. All in all, it was a very good trip, all throughout I had a little inner monologue, talking to myself about anything and everything. I came to the realisation that there is a god and that he's very tolerant of what we do. I really enjoyed this trip and since then I have tripped a few more times, although I went outside for my second and really enjoyed nature, much more 'godlike' feelings through that trip. One other thing I noticed although didn't really pay a lot of attention to during the whole trip, geometrical figures, everywhere. They covered everything, my hands, the walls, the floor. I believe I saw something very similar to Psychohedron, it was right before I went to bed, and the CEV's were very intense for awhile there.

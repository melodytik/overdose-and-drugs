我们的故事始于阿姆斯特丹美丽的运河街道. 我已经在荷兰生活了一年多了, 这是一个特别旅行的故事, 这一天开始很好,从一个咖啡店到另一个咖啡店, 我决定自己吃一盒新鲜的. 一盒被认为是高剂量. 然后我又吃了2克. 这是迄今为止我吃过的最多的. 这次旅行开始很顺利. 在阿姆斯特丹的街道上走来走去, 3个小时后我发现自己在蓝鸟咖啡店抽着大在角落里. 我开始变得令人难以置信的高. 我开始感到紧张. 我开始感到胃痛, 而在咖啡馆里, 我站了起来,转过身,走向门口. 我走了两步,然后昏了. 这不仅仅是一个停电. 我完全昏倒了. 站在我的脚. 我刚刚在地板上倒在中间的地方. 我记得黑暗是令人愉快的. 接下来我看到的是我不认识的一堆脸. 然后我坐在地板上的角落里喝着运动饮料. 我不记得有没有收到这杯酒, 店主把它和一块巧克力一起送给我. 我无法说话半个小时. 我只是无法形成这些词. 他们只是不存在. 我终于站起来离开了. 我很好奇是否有其他人因吃过高剂量的而失去意识. 有什么评论? - - (P. cyanescens) (67), - - (P. cubensis) (66):各种 (28), 过量使用 (29), 健康问题 (27)

Oct 14, 2002. erowid.org/exp/18169 DOSE: 15 g oral Mushrooms - P. cubensis (fresh) 2.0 g oral Mushrooms - P. cyanescens BODY WEIGHT: 180 lb Our story begins in the beautiful canal laden streets of Amsterdam. I've been living in the netherlands for over a year now and this is the story of one particular trip where I pushed it a bit too far. The day began nicely enough going from coffeeshop to coffeeshop getting ripped out of my mind. The next stop was to a smartshop where various psilocybin mushrooms and other such psychoactive drugs can be purchased. I decided to up my personal and eat to boxes of fresh cubensis. 1 box is considered a high dose. Then I ate another 2 grams of cyanescens. This is by far the most mushrooms that I've ever eaten. The trip started out nicely enough. Walking around the streets of amsterdam tripping out of my mind and still breaking no laws. 3 hours later however I found myself in the Bluebird coffeeshop smoking a fat spliff in a corner. I was beginning to get incredibly high. I was on the second story of the shop and looking out over the street which appeared to be moving back and forth, closer and further. I started getting nervous at this point. I was beginning to get pain in my stomach and I thought I would puke. And inside of a coffeeshop was not the place to be doing that. I stood up turned around and headed for the door. I took about 2 steps and blacked out. It wasn't just a blackout though. I completely passed out. Standing on my feet. I just collapsed on the floor in the middle of the place. I remember that the blackness was pleasent. The next thing I knew I was looking up at a bunch of faces I didn't know. Then I was in a corner sitting on the floor and I was drinking a sports drink. I have no recollection of recieving the drink, but it was already half gone. The owner had given it to me along with a candy bar. I was unable to talk for half an hour. I just couldn't form the words. They just wern't there. I eventually got up and left. I am curious if anyone else has experienced a loss of conscienceness from an incrediably high dose of mushrooms. Comments?

作为一个长期患有集群头痛的患者, 我失去了两家企业,一个商业大楼, 我需要找出原因. 下面是关于素有限成功的简要报告. 订购了子和袋子, 收获和干燥. 在摄入前2周内清除所有药物. 开始用2克干药, 晚上有轻微的幻觉和轻微的攻击. 由于所有发作都严重且持续超过20分钟, 在第一剂后, 我没有H/A大约6天, 只有轻微的阴影. 根据我的阅读, 我决定再用2克进行后续治疗. 几乎相同的反应发生在旅行结束时轻微的攻击. 五天后我又发生了典型的严重发作. 我试图增加剂量,因为2克提供了一些缓解,也许3克会提供更多. 基本上, 我是想打破这个循环, 尽可能少地让人感到不舒服. 3克的试验持续了一周又一周,持续了大约4个连续的周末,结果与结束旅行的头痛和剂量之间的阴影相似. 我在六个星期前尝试了一种4克的治疗方法, 几乎每天都会有这样的攻击, 总而言之, 作为一个慢性患者, 我在治疗psilocybin时有不同的结果, 如果/当我发现头痛的表现有变化时, 我将重新接受这种治疗. 显然这种疗法对我们病情的神经生理学有直接影响, 其他: 2007年11月8日发布 - P. cubensis (66),偏头痛 (154):对健康的益处 (32),医疗用途 (47),不适用 (38)

Nov 8, 2007. erowid.org/exp/16882 DOSE: repeated oral Mushrooms - P. cubensis (dried) BODY WEIGHT: 140 lb As a chronic sufferer of cluster headaches for the past year I was desperate to find a way out of the random pain prison. I lost 2 businesses, a commercial building, and countless nights of sleep. Preventatives dealt with symptoms (miserably I might add) and I needed to get to the cause. The following is a brief report of limited success with psilocybin mushrooms. Ordered spores and bag for sterile growing. Harvested and dried. Detoxed on all medication for 2 weeks prior to ingestion. Began with 2 grams dried and got slight hallucinations with a mild attack at the end of the evening. This was unusual since all attacks for me are severe and last >20 minutes. After this first dose I was H/A free for about 6 days with minor shadows. From my reading I decided to do a follow up dose again with 2 grams. Almost identical response occurred with a mild attack at the end of the trip. I got a typical severe attack about 5 days later. Being chronic I attempted to raise the dose as 2 grams provided some relief perhaps 3 would provide a bit more. Basically, I was looking to break the cycle and find the least amount of discomfort (the ride) in amount of mushrooms as possible. The 3 gram trial went on week after week for about 4 consecutive weekends with similar patterns of end of trip Headache and shadows between dosing. Finally I tried a 4 gram treatment about six weeks ago and have only had a handful of attacks with the same characteristics as before the mushroom treatment. That would be an attack almost every day the very second my eyes opened in the morning. In summation I have had mixed results with psilocybin treatment as a chronic sufferer and will re-visit this therapy if/when I detect a change in the manifestation of the headaches. Clearly this therapy has a direct effect on the neurophysiology of our condition and I'm very grateful to those who have helped to amass knowledge on this subject.

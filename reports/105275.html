中文译文 兴奋剂

我以前曾经使用过一些其他迷幻药物和催化剂, 我很少使用这些 (每年最多4-5次) 所以没有容忍或熟悉倒或滚动. 我开始把一整天的时间放在一边, 因为听说在40-60毫克的范围内, 我吃了早餐,然后突然有意想不到的客人直到下午. 我决定吃一点, 我打算在家度过这一天与我的S.O.,G, 只是放松和享受我的休息日. 另外,如果有什么事情发生,她会支持我. 我通常在她面前感到安全和平静. 我在下午3点半服用了药, 我会直接解释所有事情, 时间 (00:00) 15:30 喝了一些茶来帮助对抗我读过的恶心. 由于外面太热, 无法散步或骑长板 (德克萨斯州典型的夏季天气). 时间 (+ 0:32) 16:02 我开始感觉到第一种效应, 这可能是安慰剂, 但它只是突然出现. 时间 (+ 0:54) 16:24 我刚刚和G谈论一件事, 她提到要去我们学校的足球场和她的朋友A一起足球. 我对这个想法感到兴奋, 我可以肯定地说这是我感受到的第一种效应. 很奇怪,因为它只是一种非常轻微的感觉. 这让我想起了像MDMA这样的毒药物带来的"振奋"感觉, 我同时也感觉到我的胃不舒服. 时间 (+ 1:09) 16:39 我注意到视力有轻微的变化. 外面很明亮, 房子里面的光线似乎比平时更亮. 虽然有时会有轻微的恶心, 我下楼从厨房里拿水, 我的S.O.把盘子扔在柜台上, 她笑了,因为她知道我已经开始. 她想把她的朋友带到学校, 让他们可以骑自行车到场上足球, 我们只有的车, 我觉得让她开车有点不舒服, 我戴了太阳镜, 遮住眼睛的瞳孔, (+ 3:01) 时间18:31 我觉得我们有时会过快 (这很愚蠢, 她驾驶完美). 尽管如此, 我平静下来看着外面的天空和周围环境. 午后太阳从后面照亮了整个云. 在云层中,明亮的黄色色调转变为深蓝色, 简而言之,它看起来完全令人惊叹. 我们到达艾斯的房子,她带着她的自行车出来. 我正准备把它抬起来放进后备箱里, 告诉他我已经设法把它进了小箱子里. 我觉得自己还在慢慢地上升. 我跟他们骑着自行车走了一段时间, 我回到家里,在后院躺下. 时间 (+ 4:13) 19:43 现在躺在草地上, 我觉得自己沉浸在我的旅程当我更加密切地关注我周围的. 我家靠近水, 远离后院的景色也很好看. 暴风雨已经向前推进, 太阳几乎没有伸出最后的光线, 云层在我的身上飞来飞去, 那里风很大, 能亲眼目睹大自然的动态和强大力量, 让我更加沉浸在整个时刻中. 这真是太美了. 时间 (+ 4:50) 20:20 我朋友K打电话说要我去朋友P的家. 我觉得旅行已经达到了一个高原, 我问A&G是否愿意与我们见面 (我们都是朋友). 我等待着看我能坚持多久, 他说他马上就知道了, 因为我整天脸上的微笑 (他甚至不知道我在微笑). 时间 (+ 5:00) 20:30? 我们到达P的房子, 想知道为什么有一个未识别的汽车在车道上. 我们正要走进去 (P告诉我们他已经回家了, 不打开门, 他通常会叫我们进来), 我们很快回到K的车知道两个陌生人, 一个有巨大的瞳孔和奇怪的行为, 无论如何,我们等待P实际上回家,告诉他故事,并嘲笑它. 我不得不严格使用所在这一点上 (我经常使用所自从我第一次用药) 并决定看镜子在我的出路. 像LSD一样, 我的脸开始变老, 随着时间的推移, 我一转眼睛, 眼睛就会恢复正常, 直到我再次开始专注于一个点, 我在所呆了15分钟, 我回来向他们保证我只是在着自己的脸玩得开心. 后来我们去了他的公园, 和他女朋友C一起抽几支J. 我不想吸烟,因为我只想体验纯粹的MAL. 暴风雨仍在远处, 让人清楚地看到每隔几秒就会发生的闪电. 月亮照在云后面, 照亮的光线与当天早些时候的太阳相似. 另一个很棒的巧合, 完全符合我的非清醒状态. 时间 (+7:30) 23:00 我很高兴见到G, 她几乎睡着了, 在她入睡之前, 我只是躺在她旁边. T (+ 10:10) 1:40 时间 视觉扭曲已经消失, 但光感仍然存在. 我可以睡觉,但我想要听一些音乐之前不久. 这对我来说是个很好的结论. T (+ 10:30 时间) 2:00 我决定去睡觉. 我感觉自己已经放松了. 我立即入睡,醒来时已经休息了. 没有宿醉或后果, 只是一种良好的感觉从昨天有这样一个美好的一天. 总的来说,我对马尔的印象非常好. 在刺激方面非常柔和, 没有像LSD那样强烈的"头脑"空间. 这也使得这段时间变得愉快, 我仍然觉得我的注意力集中在经验上, 因为我越关注周围环境, 我很可能会再试一次, 我认为这是一个伟大的物质, 特别是如果他们整天是空和自由的责任. 其他: 甲基莱斯卡林 (586):第一次 (2), 其他 (28)

A Day with MAL Methallylescaline by A-346 Citation:   A-346. "A Day with MAL: An Experience with Methallylescaline (exp105275)". Erowid.org . May 2, 2015. erowid.org/exp/105275 DOSE: 45 mg oral Methallylescaline (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 170 lb I first head of Methallylescaline, or MAL, and similar Mescaline analogues online and how relatively rare and unpopular they were. I further read a few experiences and was interested in giving the substance a try. I had prior experience with a few other psychedelics and entheogens such as LSD, MDMA, Psilocybin, and one research chemical, 2C-I. I used these very infrequently (about 4-5 times a year at most) so there was no tolerance or strong familiarity with tripping or rolling. I began with setting my whole day aside, as I heard the duration averages around 12-16 hours at the 40-60 mg range. I had breakfast and then had sudden unexpected company until the afternoon. At this point, I decided to have a soup, so that my stomach wouldn’t be entirely empty. I was planning on spending the day at home with my S.O. , G, just relaxing and enjoying my day off work. Plus, if something went downhill, she would be there for me. I usually feel safe and calm in her presence. I dosed at 3:30 pm and began a log of the events that took place, partially on my computer and on my phone. However, I’m going to just explain everything directly while including an occasional timestamp. T (- 0:00) 15:30 I took 45mg of MAL powder orally in a small clear capsule, drank some ginger lemon tea to help against nausea which I had read about. I was mainly just sitting inside my room with G as it was way too hot outside for a walk or ride on the longboard (typical Summer weather in Texas). T (+ 0:32) 16:02 I began to feel the first effects, beginning with a very slight stimulation. It may have been placebo, but it just came suddenly. T (+ 0:54) 16:24 I was just talking to G about something when she mentioned going to the soccer field at our school to play soccer with her friend A. I got excited about the idea and suddenly felt a body high sneaking along. This I can say for sure was the first effect I felt. It was strange, as it was just a very light feeling. It reminded me a little of the coming up “uplifting” feeling on an entactogen like MDMA, but still a bit distant as it didn’t come with the emotional change. I also felt an unease in my stomach at the same time. T (+ 1:09) 16:39 I noticed a slight change in vision. It’s very bright outside and the light shining inside my house seems to look a bit more intense than usual. Moments of slight nausea occur periodically, but they don’t bother me as I just feel great with the light body high that has settle in by now. T (+ 2:11) 17:41 I went downstairs to get water from the kitchen and my S.O. dropped a plate on the counter and I jumped back in shock, it seemed excessively loud to me, although she said it wasn’t particularly loud. She laughed as she knew I had begun tripping. At this point, she wanted to pick up her friend so they could ride their bikes to our school and play soccer on the field, we only had my mom’s car available and she never had driven it before. I felt a bit uneasy about letting her drive, but eventually agreed as she was a good driver. I got my sunglasses to cover my now significantly dilated pupils in case we would come across her friend’s parents. T (+ 3:01) 18:31 During the entire drive, I was making sure everything was going alright and that G was driving well, as I felt we were going to fast sometimes (which was silly, she was driving perfectly). It was nevertheless a very intense drive. As we got closer to her friend’s house, I calmed down and looked outside at the sky and the surroundings. A huge storm cell was developing in the distance and the afternoon sun illuminated the entire cloud from behind. Shades of bright yellow transitioned into the deep shades of blue within the clouds, with all the colors in between. Simply put, it looked just purely amazing. We got to A’s house and she came outside with her bike. I was about to come lift it up and put it into the trunk when her dad came outside and wanted to help. I became a bit nervous but just greeted him friendly and told him I already managed to somehow wedge it inside the small trunk. We got home, and I felt I was still coming up (slowly but surely). I rode my bike with them for a bit but eventually said I wanted to stay home for a bit as riding by the busy road on the way would make me nervous. I got home and laid down outside in my back yard. T (+ 4:13) 19:43 Now lying motionless outside in the grass, I felt myself sink into my trip as I paid more close attention to what was around me. My house is situated near the water and I can see decently far from the backyard. The storm cell had progressed forward and the sun was barely reaching out its final beams past the wake of the clouds. It was very windy up there as the clouds zoomed over me but it remained silent down where I was laying. I felt amazing, being able to witness this dynamic and powerful force of nature right in front of me. The stimulating body high I still felt seemed to make me even more engulfed in the whole moment. It was beautiful. T (+ 4:50) 20:20 My friend K called and wanted to pick me up to go to friend P’s house. I felt I had reached a plateau in my trip and decided to hang out with them. I asked A & G if they wanted to meet us there (we all were friends). Once K arrived, I waited to see how long I could last without him noticing I was tripping. It didn’t come up once in conversation until I asked him, to which he said he knew immediately because of the slight grin I had on my face the whole time (didn’t even know I was grinning). T (+ 5:00) 20:30? We arrived at P’s house and wondered why there was a single unidentified car in the driveway. We were about to walk inside (as P told us he was home and wasn’t answering the door, he usually asks us to come inside) until we realized they were selling their house and they currently had people touring the house. We quickly went back into K’s car knowing that two strangers, one with huge pupils and acting strangely, would have just walked in on potential buyers of our friend’s house. Anyways, we waited for P to actually get home and told him of the story and laughed it off. I had to severely use the restroom at this point (I had been using the restroom frequently since I first dosed) and decided to look in the mirror on my way out. Similar to LSD, my face began to age and change expressions. However, after a while, my face stopped changed and permanently had become a sort of different version of me. Once I shifted my eyes, it returned back to its normal appearance until I again started to focus only on one point, where it would again age and change expression. I spent a good 15 minutes in the restroom until K & P became worried that something bad happened. I came back out and assured them that I was just having fun staring at my face. We then went to his park to smoke a few Js with his lady friend C. I didn’t want to smoke as I just wanted to experience purely the MAL. The storm cell was still present in the distance, giving a clear view of all the lightning that struck every few seconds. The moon shone behind the clouds and gave a similar illumination as the sun had earlier that day. Another great coincidence that just matched perfectly with my state of non-sobriety. T (+ 7:30) 23:00 K gave me a ride home, I was happy to see G who was almost asleep from riding her bike to A’s house and back. I still felt comfortably stimulated and just laid next to her for a bit before she fell asleep. T (+ 10:10) 1:40 The visual distortions had subsided, but the light feeling still remained. I could have gone to sleep but I wanted to listen to some music shortly before I did. It fit well at the moment, as it was a sort of conclusion to my day. T (+ 10:30) 2:00 I decided to go to sleep. The stimulation was almost gone at this point and I just felt relaxed and calm. I fell asleep immediately and woke up well rested. No hangover or after effects, just a sort of good feeling from having such a nice day yesterday. Overall, I had a very good impression from MAL. It was very mellow in terms of stimulation and not nearly as intense of the “mindfuck” headspace that LSD gives. This made the duration enjoyable as well, as going through 12 hours of tripping would normally be very exhausting. However, I still felt that my attention was directed towards the experience as the more I focused on my surroundings, the more prominent the effects became. I will likely try it again at a higher dose and at a time where I can explore the outdoors more. I think it’s a great substance, especially if they whole day is unoccupied and free of responsibilities.

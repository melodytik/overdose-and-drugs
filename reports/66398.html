背景:我最近被诊断出患有注意力不集中类型的ADD, 在我被诊断出ADD后进行研究时, 艾美因诊所建议ADD患者补充 (智能药物) l-tyrosine. 而ADD患者的多巴胺缺乏. 现在我们可以看到, 我读到个体对l-tyrosine的反应可能有很大的差异, 幸运的是,我自己对l-tyrosine没有注意到任何类似的情况,因此我每天服用1500毫克l-tyrosine (药店将其与维生素一起出售) 3次, 我发现它有帮助. 有一天,我服用了l- tyrosine 500 mg药丸和Adderall XR 20 mg药丸 (完全是偶然的,在其他日子里,我至少在T+2: 00和之后服用了l- tyrosine,而不是Adderall). 孩子们,不要把它们放在一起,它们玩得不好. 在T+3:00到T+5:30之间, 心跳,心率增加 (120-140),眼睛后面的压力 (=血压升高),恶心/感觉有线的浪潮,特别是当盐的另一种水平从阿德拉尔中入时,感到昏迷/必须躺下,在亚当果周围非常不舒服,疼痛和令人担忧的喉紧张,使吞变得令人不安的困难和痛苦. 感觉就像达尔特·韦德把我拉到墙上, 轻微的恐惧/偏执/恐慌/发生了什么, 专注于任何症状会立即使它变得更糟. 尽管呼吸是可以的. 我写这篇报告是为了拯救别人! 其他: 素 (363), 胺类药物 (6):单独使用 (16),健康问题 (27),组合使用 (3)

"Do NOT Take Together: An Experience with Tyrosine (L-Tyrosine) & Amphetamine (Adderall) (exp66398)". Erowid.org . Oct 17, 2007. erowid.org/exp/66398 DOSE: 500 mg oral Tyrosine 20 mg oral Amphetamines (capsule) BODY WEIGHT: 165 lb Background: I was recently diagnosed (at 43 if you can believe it) with Inattentive Type ADD and was prescribed Adderall XR 20mg, which I take as prescribed orally once a day. In researching ADD after my diagnosis, I found that several sources, including Dr. Amen of ADHD Amen Clinic fame, advise ADDers to supplement with (smart drug) l-tyrosine. L-tyrosine is an L-DOPA precursor which is itself a dopamine precursor, and ADDers are pretty short on dopamine all around. I had read that individual reactions to l-tyrosine could vary quite a bit, and that some people have unpleasant side effects such as increased heart rate, elevated blood pressure and arrythmia. Fortunately I noticed nothing of the kind myself with l-tyrosine, and consequently was taking 1 500 mg l-tyrosine (drug stores sell with the vitamins) 3 times a day for a total daily intake of 1500mg. I found it helpful. One day I took the l-tyrosine 500 mg pill and an Adderall XR 20mg together (quite by chance, on all other days I had taken the l-tyrosine at least T+2:00 and later from the Adderall). Don't take these together, kidlets, they don't play well. T+3:00 to T+5:30 were not fun. Pounding heart, heart rate increase (120-140), pressure behind the eyes (=elevated BP), waves of nausea/wired-ness especially when another level of salt kicked in from the Adderall, feeling faint/must lie down, and a very uncomfortable, sore and worrying tight throat around the adam's apple, making swallowing disturbingly difficult and painful. It felt like Darth Vader had me against the wall off my feet and was squeezing, per 'Star Wars'. Slight fear/paranoia/panic/what is happening, concentrating on any symptom would immediately make it worse. Breathing was OK tho. While not emergency room calibre, this was a VERY unpleasant 2 1/2 hours and I am writing this report to save someone else from it!!!

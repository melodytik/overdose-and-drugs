100磅的 我偶尔会服用5毫克维科丁治疗月经抽筋, 我做了一些搜索, 试图弄清楚我应该服用什么剂量的甲醇. 我有它因为我的猫刚刚做了手术, 兽医给他开了处方, 其他娱乐性毒品都是大麻,LSD和胺. 兽医指示我给我的猫2.5毫克, 我读了别人的报告说服用15毫克太多, 现在回想起来, 我大约下午6点吞下了一粒5毫克的药丸. 我在Netflix上播放了一部自然纪录片. 到了下午6点55分, 我开始感觉有点脱节 - - 不是完全脱节, 我站起来走路时, 两只腿同时重, 我感觉很好,所以在晚上7点15分, 吸入另一个2.5毫克. 我开始感到非常紧张 (我有一般焦虑症),但我认为,如果我服用太多,我的心脏可能会减慢,而不是像疯子一样跳动. 我决定躺下. 我开始"休息"了45分钟左右, 在休息期间我会在脑海中创造出奇怪的随机场景, 这很奇怪,但有点愉快和快乐. 这种情况在45分钟的时间里不停地持续, 直到我的搭档回家. 我知道这会让我感到压力, 因为我不能告诉他我吃了猫的药, 我尽力表现得很正常, 我试图帮助他做晚餐, 但在某个时候, 食物的味道让我不得不跑去浴室坐下. 我没有生病,但我觉得我会. 我在几分钟内就好了, 我一直觉得很昏昏欲睡,直到我吃晚饭,之后我觉得更清醒. 我觉得睡觉的时候已经清醒了, 在那个时候我抽了一碗大麻 (大约是凌晨12点左右). 尽管如此, 我在凌晨3点左右醒来, 吐了一次, 我在下一天下午写这篇文章. 我没有感觉到任何进一步的影响. 其他: 药品 - 丁醇 (307):单独 (16),第一次 (2),一般 (1)

May 18, 2017. erowid.org/exp/110454 DOSE: T+ 0:00 5 mg oral Pharms - Butorphanol (pill / tablet) T+ 2:15 2.5 mg oral Pharms - Butorphanol (ground / crushed) T+ 7:00 1 bowl smoked Cannabis BODY WEIGHT: 100 lb I will start this report by saying that I have no experience with painkillers/opiods for recreational purposes. I occasionally take 5mg of Vicodin for menstrual cramps but only to alleviate the pain. I did a bit of searching to try to figure out what dosage of Butorphanol I should take. I have it because my cat just had surgery and was prescribed it by the vet, and there are some extras leftover. My other recreational drug experiences have all been with cannabis, LSD and ketamine. I was instructed by the vet to give my cat 2.5 mg, and I read someone else's report about taking 15 mg and it being too much, so I ended up deciding to take 7.5 mg. In retrospect it was too much perhaps for my bodyweight and tolerance. I swallowed a 5mg pill at approximately 6 pm. I put on a nature documentary on Netflix. By 6:55 pm I began to feel a slight disconnect- not quite dissociation but 'a haze between myself and what I [was] trying to say' (from my notes). I got up to walk and I felt both floaty and like my legs were heavy simultaneously. I was feeling good so, at around 7:15 pm, insufflated another 2.5 mg. In about 3-4 minutes I began to feel very nervous (I have general anxiety disorder), but reasoned with myself that, had I taken too much, my heart probably would be slowing, not pounding like crazy. I decided to lay down. I proceeded to “rest” for 45 minutes or so- during this resting period I would have strange and random scenarios created in my head that I felt engaged in, but then would open my eyes a little wider and would “wake up” and snap out of it and realize I had drifted off half asleep. It was weird but kind of pleasant and trippy. This continued on and off during this 45 minute period, until my partner came home. I knew this would stress me out because I couldn’t tell him I had taken the cat’s medicine to get high, he would be upset with me. I tried my best to act normal but I felt very “out of it” as I told him. I tried to help him cook dinner but at one point the smell of the food got to me and I had to run and sit down in the bathroom. I didn’t get sick but I felt like I was going to. I was fine in a few minutes and was able to go back out and sit down and sip some water while he cooked. I continued to feel sleepy and out of it until I ate dinner, after which I felt much more sober. I felt sobered up by bed time, at which time I smoked a bowl of weed (around 12 am). However I woke up at approximately 3 am to vomit once and then I was ok. I am writing this the following afternoon. I don’t feel any further effects.

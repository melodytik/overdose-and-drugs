中文译文 致幻剂

2ce比LSD更强大, 几乎不如LSD, 但它的效果更疯狂, 我听说我的朋友德鲁在网上订购了两次, 他声称自己失去了大约6个小时的头脑. 所以我们去德鲁买了一些. 他表现得愚蠢,无法理解我们在说什么,当我们试图从他身上得到2ce时,他一直说他已经死了,他无法释放它. 终于我们让他让我们看他的钱包 (他把2ce放在钱包里). 我们开始拿出2倍, 它是在一个小的马尼拉信封里, 我倒出了两条巨大的线 (估计25毫克), 我的朋友说'丹,不要那么多只做一半的那些线. 我回应说'我已经做了很多药物,这什么都不是. "一旦我吸了线我们离开了,大约2分钟后吸了它我要求回家,因为我觉得它开始,出于某种原因想回家. 我住在德鲁的街上,所以我回家5分钟后的线路已经被摄入. 我感到非常不安全, 我感觉到鼻子有点, 我大概已经了15次, 经过20分钟的恶心之后, 我在床上缩成一个球, 感觉很冷我的大脑无法理解任何事情. 我还能拿起一卷纸巾在午夜清理掉它. 我不记得太多,但我记得我只是想让这些感觉结束了6个小时,然后我喝了一点伏特加, 我为自己做了一个小小的带回家袋子, 我从来没有学会我的教训任何药物,所以从晚上恢复后在下午5点左右. 我打开了小袋子,在浴室里吸了它. 这次是正常的剂量, 但我的鼻子仍然感觉像我吸了玻璃. 我转过身来了3次,然后感觉很好. 我有一个非常顺利的旅行. 之后,每次服用2CE, 我都会觉得有人在我的脑海里加了一种使物体容易移动的物质, 我意识到世界上所有的奇迹, 喜欢活着. 看到天空,闻到空气让我感觉很好. 另外,我所有的想法都顺利地发生了. 几天后,我和朋友格伦有了两次接触,他也和我一样, 虽然我们告诉他不要太多, 五分钟后,他躺在他的背上我的朋友完成了地下室. 鼻子开始像水一样从他的脸上流下来. 我从来没有见过这么多的鼻子之前, 他的整个脸的底部是一个闪闪发光的层鼻子泄漏的脸的两侧. 他感到困惑,开始歇斯底里地笑. 然后他开始吐. 我数了他的吐20次,然后我离开房间,他继续吐. 他坐在沙发上,没有任何回忆. 他一直在问我们是谁,他在哪里. 我们试图和他说话,他只是不断重复什么? 你是谁? 我在哪里? 我离开之前他可以回现实, 因为我也在倒在它对我来说太多,所以我出去看了一些树, 在那次旅行之后, 格伦说他永远不会再这样做, 如果它再出现,我会再次跌倒. 2C-E (137):回顾 / 总结 (11), 艰难的经历 (5), 独自一人 (16)

Insanity 2C-E by 2ce Snort Citation:   2ce Snort. "Insanity: An Experience with 2C-E (exp61198)". Erowid.org . May 5, 2010. erowid.org/exp/61198 DOSE: insufflated 2C-E (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 160 lb Before I start I must tell everyone….2ce is more powerful than LSD, it is as no where NEAR as good (nothing really is) as LSD but its effects are much more insane, not in a good way. I had heard that my friend Drew had ordered 2ce online and it was some obscure hallucinogen. My friend Taylor had done something like 40mg one night (way over what your suppose to do) and he claimed he had lost his mind for like 6 hours. So wanting to experience it for myself we went to Drew's to buy some. When we arrived he opened the door, pale as a ghost and said nothing other than 'I just died.' He acted foolish and couldn't comprehend what we were saying when we were trying to get the 2ce from him he just kept saying that he was dead and that he could not release it. Finally we got him to let us 'see his wallet' (he was holding the 2ce in his wallet.) We proceeded to take the 2ce out it was in a little manila envelope with a little baggie inside which contained the powder. I poured out 2 huge lines (which were estimated at 25mg) without knowing anything about the drug not even how much this was or how much I should take. My friends said 'Dan, don’t do that much only do half one of those lines.' I responded 'I have done allot of drugs this is nothing.' As soon as I snorted the lines we left and about 2 minutes after snorting it I asked to go home because I felt it setting in and for some reason wanted to be home. I lived up the street from Drew so I got home like 5 minutes after the lines had been ingested. I felt very insecure and the threshold was HEAVY. I then felt extreme nausea and my nostrils burned as they always do when snorting 2ce. I must have puked about 15 times then just kept dry heaving. After about 20 minutes of being sick I felt okay. I curled up into a ball on my mattress and felt very cold my brain could not make sense of anything. When I was puking I puked out of my window and I still functioned enough to grab a role of paper towels and clean that up at like midnight. I don’t remember much but I remember just wanting the feelings I had to end for like 6 hours then I drank a little vodka when my trip was coming down and fell asleep. When we were at Drew’s I had made a little take home baggie of 2ce for myself that consisted of about 10 mg. I never learn my lesson with any drug so after recovering from the night before at about 5 p.m. I opened the little baggie and snorted it in my bathroom. This time it was a normal dosage but my nose still felt like I had snorted glass. I turned around and puked 3 times then felt great. I had a very smooth trip. Then and from that point on whenever I've taken 2ce it makes me feel like someone put a substance which makes objects easy to move in my thoughts, such as grease or Vaseline. I realized all the wonders of the world and just loved being alive. I went out and just looking at trees and seeing the sky and smelling the air made me feel wonderful. Plus all of my thoughts came and went so smoothly it was heavenly. A couple days after that we got my friend Glen some 2ce and just like me he thought he could handle it even though we told him not to do that much he snorted about 20 mg. 5 minutes after snorting he laid on his back of my friends finished basement. Snots began running down his face like water. I had never seen so much snot before the bottom of his whole face was a shiny layer of snot leaking off the sides of his face. He then became disoriented and laughing hysterically. He then began puking. I counted his puking up to 20 times then I left the room and he continued to puke. Once his sickness got over he sat on the couch and had no recollection of anything or anyone. He kept asking us who we were and where he was. We would try to talk to him and he would just keep repeating what? Who are you? And where am I? I left before he could snap back into reality because I was also tripping on it and that was too much for me so I went outside and looked at some trees then listened to some music and enjoyed another nice smooth trip. After that trip glen said he would never do it again and to this day he has not. I will trip on it again if it ever comes around.

一种有趣的模糊解离剂. 在加拿大,它是合法的,可以从研究化学品供应商订购. 虽然它比胺更贵,所以即使在喜欢它的人中也不是很受欢迎. 为了尝试所有可用的分离剂, 我尝试了这种化学物质, 在尝试了所有物质之后, 终于我剩下的就是提莱他. 如果您在尝试RC之前没有服用过ketamine, 那么您可能会更感兴趣, 唯一让我印象深刻的化学物质是3PCP, 2FDCK就像是胺酸, 但除了燃烧之外, 另一种化学物质是3 MeO的PCP和PCE. 但其余的似乎是meh,或我没有走得足够远或什么. 无论如何, 现在, 在漫长而自我放纵的疯狂享乐主义的尽头, 这种化学物质不太喜欢分离的头脑, 并且有着奇怪的声誉, 这些然而没有注意到我在我的旅行. 我第一次尝试吸烟, 这绝对是化学品的有效途径. 我把15毫克放进一个碗里, 用DMT的方法抽出来. 我成功地得到了一个好命中. 后来我的再剂量是60毫克鼻入. 这种感觉最初是奇怪的分离, 完全是独一无二的, 一个被激发和镇静. 当一个人想移动自己的手臂时, 却会被推迟, 软弱, 并且通常很难在身体上完成. 我觉得自己很好. 我的头部有点像在胺中感觉到的头部压力. 基他胺的魔力在这种化学物质中最为明显, 这是一种散的感觉, 墙壁漂移,轻微呼吸, 但这就是视觉的程度, 另外,我的视力有轻微的绿色色调, 这让我有模糊的矩阵振动, 我会嘲笑很多心理探险家, 这让我有模糊的矩阵振动, 我会嘲笑很多心理探险家, 我常常会做一些陈词滥调和宗教迷思, 只是为了体验我认为不可能的事情, 效果在1小时30分钟内达到顶峰. 由于我的耐受性, 我吹了它,感觉到了它. 不同于胺, 它不燃烧太严重, 并没有可怕的味道后明显的挥发性. 这次持续时间为3小时或更长, 虽然很难说3小时后的效果是下降还是特别强烈的后果. 我认为最多只能持续5到6小时, 让我几乎无法入睡甚至躺下舒服. 我仍然活跃, 在我的房间里移动. 继续吸食大麻 然而,化学物质很难洞,即使在我的高剂量,所以一个应该小心,因为与大多数分离剂一个将达到超狂热之前一个达到一个洞. 我一直处于这种状态, 直到第二天晚上10点左右, 现在第二天我有一些事情要处理,至少我认为我做了. 我最终忘记了我需要做什么才能滑板. 由于我的协调能力不佳, 我在滑板上滑了一段时间, 终于无意识地踩到我的药房. 买了几克大麻, 很多问题和一些奇怪的眼神由budtender 我回家滑冰时, 没有什么特别的事情发生, 现在我已经疲不堪, 我醒来时头痛很轻, 在我补水之后, 我怀疑脱水是大多数人宿醉的原因. Jan 7, 2022. erowid.org/exp/115933 DOSE: T+ 0:00 15 mg vaporized Pharms - Tiletamine T+ 1:40 60 mg insufflated Pharms - Tiletamine T+ 0:00 repeated smoked Cannabis BODY WEIGHT: 158 lb Tiletamine: a fun obscure dissociative. It is legal in Canada and is able to be ordered from Research chemical Vendors. Though it is more expensive than ketamine so its not terribly popular even among those who like it. In an effort to try every dissociative I had available to me led me to trying this chemical after trying everything from the diarryls Like methox, diphen and ephenidine, to the arrylcyclos like 3MeoPCP/PCE, 3 Chloro PCP, OPCE, DCK, 2FDCK, 3HOPCP/PCE, And classic ketamine and DXM. Finally all I had left was tiletamine. In my opinion, RC dissociatives don’t compare to ketamine, if you havent had ketamine before trying the RCs then one may be much more impressed but as a ket head, it took a lot to impress me. The only chemicals that impressed me were 3 Chloro PCP, which felt like PCP with a hint of Ketamine. 2FDCK was like ketamine lite but got the job done besides the burning. Another chemical is 3 MeO PCP and PCE. But the rest seemed to be meh, or I hadn't gone far enough or something. Either way I wasn’t really impressed and a bit disappointed. Now at the end of a long and self indulgent hole of manic hedonism I was staring at the last stepping stone: tiletamine. The chemical, is not very liked among dissociative heads and has a reputation for being strange, and causing headaches the next day plus a mentioned extreme hangover. These however were not noted to me during my trip. My first time I tried it smoked as well as insufflated later as a redose. Smoked the chemical hits strong and it is definitely an effective route for the chemical. I packed 15mg in a bowl and smoke it via the method one uses for DMT. I successfully get a good hit. Later my redose would be 60mg intranasal. The feeling starts out as a strange dissociation hitting the body. Completely unique and distinct from any other dissociative. One is paradoxically stimulated and sedated. While one feels like moving mentally, when one sends the thought to move one's arm, it is delayed, weak and generally hard to do physically. The euphoria however is extremely good, I feel great. There is an electric tingling feeling rushing through my body, and my head feels vaguely like the head pressure felt on ketamine. The magic of ketamine is the most pronounced with this chemical if one knows what I mean. It is that glowing dissociative feeling that’s not present with most other dissociatives like even DCK. I am so high that I simply sit in my bed, the walls drift and slightly breathe but this is the extent of the visuals beyond slight colour vividness increase as well as a slight brightness effect like one turned up the contrast or exposure of my vision. Additionally there is a slight green tint to my vision and the closed eye visuals are mainly green. This gives me vaguely matrix vibes, something I would make fun of many psychonauts for drawing reference to, yet here I was. This gives me vaguely matrix vibes, something I would make fun of many psychonauts for drawing reference to, yet here I was. Something that I often did with cliché and religious ramblings of most psychonauts; only to experience the thing I touted to be impossible and delusions of certain weak minded psychonauts. The effects peak about an hour and 30 minutes in. So I redose intranasally around 60mg due to my tolerance. I insufflated it and felt the come on. Unlike ketamine, it doesn’t burn too bad and does not have an awful after taste of noticeable volatility. This time the duration lasted 3 hours or more, though it is hard to tell if the effects passed 3 hours were the comedown or particularly strong after effects. So I'd say at most it lasts 5-6 hours with an additional 8-10 hours of after effects. The insomnia and stimulating effects made it near impossible to sleep and even to lay down comfortably. I remained active and moving about my room. Continuing to smoke cannabis. However the chemical is hard to hole on, even at my high dose so one should be cautious as with most dissociatives one will reach hypermania before one reaches a hole. I remained in this state until around 10pm the next day, taking the chemical originally smoked at 1am, with the redose at around 240ish. Now the next day I had some errands to take care of, or at least I thought I did. I ended up forgetting whatever I would've needed to do to go skateboard. A semi good but fairly bad idea due to my poor coordination. After failing to run onto my board and a few stumbles off my board as well as a few other wipeouts I skated for a while. Eventually absent mindedly pedaling to my dispensary. Buying a few grams of weed, with much issue and a few weird looks by the budtender. Nothing else notable happened as I skated back home and it eventually wore off. Now exhausted I went to sleep. I woke up with a slight headache that went away after I rehydrated my parched soul. I suspected dehydration is the source of most peoples hangovers. (Note from future edit: I have taken it a second time and stayed hydrated, while still present it was extremely minimalized. So it wasn’t too much of a deterrent to trying it again.)

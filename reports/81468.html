我只是想提供一份关于一种几乎没有任何信息的新化学物质的研究报告. 在25次平均6.5毫克/公斤的旅行之后, 我发现自己无法从旅行中获得任何东西. 我在二月份停止使用它. 09年,从那以后没有碰过它. 听说有更多的离散式RC正在上市, 我很高兴, 为了再次感受到这种美妙的感觉, 我把它混合在一杯水中, 大约8.3毫克/毫升. 没有问题. 立即解散. 我在8月9日空腹服用了150毫克. 这种化学物质应该持续12个小时左右, 大多数报告都是这样, 我做好了准备, 我在晚上9点喝了液体, 味道相当糟糕. 像是某种燃烧的化学物质 只是很恶心. 但它只用了两口. 半小时后, 我开始感到一种. 这几乎与DXM的出现完全相同. 我很兴奋. 一个小时后, 虽然DXM是一个缓慢的登, 这就像在一个快速移动的车辆. 虽然并非火箭发射, 但仍显然效应正在加快. 很快,大概在15分钟内, 我已经漂浮在墙上! 很像DXM的第三高原, 但有不同的感觉. 我发现同样的深度感知扭曲, 但是在另一个维度的某种类型. 很难解释,有许多相似之处, 身体是麻木的,和舒适的静止. 我感觉像在DXM上一样融化, 像是静电, 不像是皮球, 就像我从DXM中得到了阿片类麻木感. 这次旅行在T+90分钟时非常顺利. 我有过一些有趣的 CEV,酷的 OEV, 还有几个有趣的回忆, 这真是令人愉快. 然而在T+2hr左右,事情开始慢下来. 我几乎陷入了一种感官洞, 我开始进去,然后又浮回到我的床上. 由于无法回到那一点, 我认为我没有足够的剂量, 或许这种药物是波浪而来的. 然而我会被证明是错的. 影响继续逐渐下降. 最终,在T+4hr, 没有任何视觉. 身体仍然有点沉重. 但总的来说,我发现自己是正常思考的. 这不是我们期望的,也不是我们想要的. 未来的230人审判也会揭示同样的真相, 似乎我对DXM的耐受性并没有减弱, 我决定不继续使用4-meo-pcp. 我可能会避免使用任何解离词, 希望在未来能够再次使用它们. 我还会探讨其他项目. 希望这能帮助任何想要获得4-meo-pcp的人. 我非常喜欢我在这里所花的时间. 已使用的: 4-MeO-PCP (498):单独使用 (16),第一次使用 (2)

It Cross Tolerates 4-MeO-PCP by Delsymfan Citation:   Delsymfan. "It Cross Tolerates: An Experience with 4-MeO-PCP (exp81468)". Erowid.org . Oct 8, 2009. erowid.org/exp/81468 DOSE: 150 mg oral 4-MeO-PCP (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 200 lb I just figured I'd provide a report on a very new research chemical that has hardly any information on it. I was a frequent DXM user, and after about 25 trips of 6.5mg/kg average, I found myself unable to get much of anything from the trips. I stopped using it in Feb. 09, and havent touched it since. When I heard more dissociative RC's were hitting the market, I jumped for joy, as Dissociatives still always were my fancy. Hoping to once again feel that wonderful feeling, I sought out 4-meo-pcp. After procuring a gram of the stuff, I mixed it in a cup of water, about 8.3mg/ml. No problem. Dissolved right away. I dosed 150mg on an empty stomach in August 09. This chemical was supposed to last 12 hours or so, most reports go there, with an afterglow lasting well into the next day. I was prepared and had a clear schedule. I took the liquid at 9 PM, quite awful tasting. Like a burnt chemical of some sort. Just nasty. But it went down with two swigs. After 30 minutes, I started to feel a sort of tingle. It felt nearly identical to the come up of DXM. I was excited. At an hour, the effects started to tear off. Whereas DXM is a slow climb, this was like being in a faster moving vehicle. Not a rocket launch, but still it was clear the effects were gaining speed. Before long, probably in 15 minutes, I was floating through the walls! Much like on DXM 3rd plateau, but with a different feel. I found the same depth perception distortion, but it was in another dimension of some sort. Its hard to explain, there were many similarities, but still each one was a bit different. The body was numb, and comfortable holding still. I felt dissolved as I do on DXM, but less fuzzy. More like static electricity, less like a fuzzball like I get that Opiate-style numbness from DXM. The trip was going great at T+90 minutes. I was having interesting CEVs, cool OEVs, and had a couple interesting flashbacks to previous locations I know, and people I have been with. It was quite delightful. However at about T+2hr, things started to kind of, well, slow down. I was on the verge of falling into some sort of sensory hole, I could feel myself slipping. And I started going in, and then just floated back up into my bed. Unable to get back to that point, I was a little frustrated. I assumed I had not dosed enough, or perhaps this drug comes in waves. Yet I would be proven wrong. The effects continued to taper downward. Eventually, at T+4hr, there was nothing visual. There was still a difficulty walking and a bit of a heavy feel in the body. But overall, I found myself thinking normally. This was not expected, or desired. A future trial with 230 would reveal the same truth, that 4 hours would be the max I could get. It appears that my tolerance to DXM has not faded much at all, and it also affects other dissociative chemicals. I have decided not to continue with 4-meo-pcp. I probably will avoid all dissociatives for some time, hopefully getting the ability to use them again in the future. In the meantime, I will explore other items. Hopefully this helps anybody looking to get some 4-meo-pcp. I highly enjoyed what time I did have on it.

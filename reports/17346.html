菲奥里塞特是50毫克的布塔尔比塔尔,40毫克的咖啡因和325毫克的乙胺. 它们是针对疼痛的药物, 几年前我受伤时还留下了这些药, 所以它们可能已经失去一些效力. 我试过维科丁和德梅罗尔, 但没有其他药物. 虽然它们不是完全相同的药物, 菲奥里塞特不是阿片类药物. 我在和一个喝酒的朋友出去玩的时候, 吃了这些, 晚上10点 - - 我在空腹时服用了两粒药. 晚上10点半 - 开始感觉有点醉和沉重,没有高兴, 这就像Demerol, 但没有Euphoria, 现在开车是个坏主意. 我再服用0.5个药来增加效果, 谨慎服用乙胺. 我觉得自己有点醉了, 很高兴和朋友一起看电影. 没有什么壮观的. 只是很冷静. 上午12点 - - 服用另一个0.5片. 我不会再服用更多, 因为我不想超过1000毫克的乙胺. +10分钟 - 我开始得很厉害,我也很热,我肚子很烧,虽然我没有饿,强迫自己吃一些食物,喝3杯水. 这让我有点平静下来,我伸出手来看看我的震动有多糟糕,越来越好. 我也被推向了更深的醉酒状态, 凌晨1点 - - 不再, 我觉得有点不舒服, 但没有什么叫做高潮, 我可以正常工作, 结论:如果我想喝酒, 我真的不明白为什么有人会上这些, 除非他们长时间服用它们来缓解疼痛, 不是很糟糕,但也不是很好,一个""的药物. 时间表: 17346 巴比酸盐 (210):第一次 (2),小组 (2-9) (17)

They are perscribed for pain and are addictive. I had these pill left over from an injury years ago and forgot about them. So they may have lost some potency. I have tried Vicodin and Demerol but no other pharms for fun. So I will use these as a comparison even though they are not exactly the same kind of drug. Fioricet is not an Opiate. I took these while hanging out with a friend who was drinking, I didn't have any to drink because of the Acetaminophen. 10:00 pm - I take 2 of the pills on an empty stomach. 10:30 pm - Start to feel a little drunk and heavy, no euphoria, I was hoping these would be Euphoric but no go. Like Demerol but with no Euphoria, just a heavy 'off' feeling. It would be a really bad idea to drive now. I take another .5 a pill to increase the effect, warry of my Acetaminophen intake. 11:00 Pm - hits me a little stronger now, I feel kind of drunk and high, having fun hanging with my friend watching movies. Nothing spectacular. Just way chilled out. 12:00 am - Take the other .5 a pill. I am not going to take more after this because I don't want to go over 1000mg of Acetaminophen. +10 min - I start to shake pretty bad, I am also very hot and my stomach is burning even though I am not hungry, force myself to eat some food and drink 3 glasses of water. This calms me down some, I hold my hands out to see how bad my shakes are, getting better. I am also pushed one notch 'deeper' into my intoxicated state, pretty null and void to my surroundings, very mellow and at ease. 1:00 am - No more shaking, and my high is all but gone. 4:00 am - The time of this writing, all effects are pretty much gone, I feel slightly off but nothing I would call a high, I can function fully, going to bed. Conclusion - These are ok if I want to be intoxicated but not drink alcohol, they simply aren't worth $, they don't have euphoric effect. I honestly don't see how anyone could get addicted to these unless they are taking them for pain for too long, as far as recreation goes, there are much better Pharms out there. Not really bad, but not really good, an 'Eh' drug.

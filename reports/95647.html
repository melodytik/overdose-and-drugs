这是我第一次使用真正的迷幻药除了DXM, 我以前使用过其他药物, 其中包括大麻,德克斯特罗梅索芬, 无论如何,我设法得到五个含有1.5毫克的DOI每一个和打开两个标签总共3毫克. 接下来,我只需要等待... 我旅行的时间部分写在一张纸上, ~下午4点 服用药片, 在此之前经常吸食大麻. 感觉到可能是DOI, 也可能是大麻. 我最后一次抽是在抽前30分钟. 我在腹部有这种不寻常的刺痛感, 我现在有这种令人惊叹的头, 现在令人惊叹的头已经变成一种令人困惑的感觉. 我走了出去,喝了几次大麻, 现在我开始怀疑这些标签是否足够, 现在已经转变为良好的, 现在已经蔓延到我的身体其他部位. 我刚刚从散步回来, 发现 DOI 乞求接管. 现在我目睹了有史以来最壮观的视觉效果. 我还在外面休息的时候喝了一半碗大麻. 这种东西肯定会影响我. 我有这些令人烦的震动, 时间被关闭了. 我记下了这一切,但我确实记得之后发生的其他事情. 我和哥哥一起去他的吉普车里, 他对DOI表现出一点兴趣, 但不足以要求一些. 离开吉普车后, 我回到我的房间上楼, 在那儿玩Xbox一会儿, 我在床上躺下, 灯光照亮, 轻松地体验, 躺在那里看着木地板上的粒子曲和移动, 仿佛它还活着. 在经历的某个时候, 我睡着了. 我会说这种化学物质是我最喜欢的药物之一, 我还必须说大麻在我的旅行中帮助了我很多. 其他: DOI (259):烂的经历 (4),第一次 (2),孤独 (16)

Nov 29, 2012. erowid.org/exp/95647 DOSE: 2 hits oral DOI (blotter / tab) BODY WEIGHT: 175 lb This was my first time useing a true psychedelic besides DXM which is a dissocative and isn't a grand as DOI. I have used other drugs before of which include marijuana, dextromethorphan, codeine, hydrocodone, and clonazepam, all of which have been used within the last four months prior to taking DOI. Anyways, I managed to get five blotters containing 1.5mg of DOI each and popped two tabs for a total of 3mg. Next, all I had to do was wait... The timed part of my trip was written down on a piece of paper which I will transfer below. ~4:00PM Took tabs, before which were frequent hits of cannbis. T+0:15 Feeling what could be the DOI, but could also be the cannabis. Still feeling high and my last toke was about 30 minutes before taking the blotters. T+0:20 I have this unusual tingling feeling in my abdomen, which is triggering interesting spasiums. T+0:30 I now have this wonderful head tingle that reminds me of a codeine high. T+0:35 The wonderful head tingle has now changed into a some-what confusing feeling. T+0:40 I went outside and took a few hits of weed to help calm me down. T+1:00 It's hard to tell where the DOI is coming from, and now I'm starting to wonder if the blotters were enough, or were just fakes. T+1:25 The confusing feeling has now reverted back to a good tingle, which has now spread to the rest of my body. T+2:05 I just got back from a walk on which I noticed the DOI begging to take hold. Now I'm witnising the most spectacular visuals ever. I also had about half a bowl of weed while relaxing outside. T+2:30 This stuff is definitely effecting me. I have these annoying shakes, but I am seeing wonderful visuals. Time is being shuttered. That's all I wrote down, but I do remember other things that happened after that. I went out to my brother's jeep with him to hotbox it and while doing so, told him how it was going so far. He showed a little interest in the DOI, but not enough to ask for some. After we left the jeep, I went back up stairs to my room where I played my xbox for a little while, then watched a movie until the effects of the DOI were too overwelming to be able to preform simple tasks. I then went and layed down in my bed with minumal lighting on, eased into the experiance and watched the amazing visuals. I had this uncomfortable need to shift position while layed there and watched the grains in the wood flooring bend and move like it was alive. At some point during the experiance, I fell a sleep. I would say that this chemical was one of my favorite drugs and would gladly try DOI again in the future. I also do have to say that marijuana helped out big time during my trip.

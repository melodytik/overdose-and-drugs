毒品已经成为我生命的一部分, 两天前我经历的不可避免地永远改变了我. 生命是我们不能给予的,也不能从别人那里夺走的. 这就是我们内在的. 你不能从一个人的灵魂,或偷他们的心理. 每个人都是我们自己的想象的圣殿; 现实,思想,意识,存在. 现在谈论我对生活的感受是浪费时间的, 我已经使用AMT/Foxy几个月了. 我已经有五次 AMT 经验, 对于我来说,AMT是一个礼物. 任何一天的12个小时, 我的心情都会升高, 我只是坐在这里, 想到那句话:"你永远不知道你有什么, 我们几周前才开始上学, 我女朋友总是反对我吸毒, 我猜她现在已经放弃了. 我真心爱她, 真心相信我们有特别的东西, 我从来不知道我的愚蠢会摧毁我生命中最珍贵的东西. 我女朋友 (我们称她为K) 开始看着我做什么. 她开始对毒品和毒品对我的影响感兴趣. 她总是开放的思想和创造力, 她只是如此完美. 随着她的兴趣越来越大, 由于我们的家庭,或缺乏, 有他们的分歧, 我们只会在学校见面大部分时间. 虽然我第一次在学校尝试吸毒的后果, 我记得我对自己说'坏事只发生在别人身上'. 我只是希望我可以把时间倒退. 我在学校向亲密的朋友出售Foxy/AMT, 了不少钱, 但我还是继续这样做. 或许我只是个瓜. 我们为K第一次接触福克斯定了一个"日期", 那天我,K和F (亲密的朋友) 来到了学校, 我和F有15毫克,因为我们有经验,我给K12毫克. 总的来说,她喜欢它. 这可能是她很长一段时间以来发生的最好的事情之一. 我觉得自己能让她快乐, 我感到自豪, 尽管如此,一周后我们有了一个精彩的想法. 我打算让她尝试一些AMT, 这肯定会提高她的精神, 我们都非常兴奋. 计划是在早上5点给F和K一个饮料, 我在给他们注射药物的前一天晚上, 他处理化学品,我卖了他们. 我告诉他我需要什么,我去睡觉,他告诉我他会把它放在饮料和冷藏他们. 但他向我保证我可以睡觉, 他会把它们留给明天. 第二天早上我醒来了, 40毫克的AMT混合在汁中, 服用药物后一个小时, 我开始感觉到效果的开始, 到了离开的时候,我走出了房子, 我走进学校时, 身体的动作是流动的, 我知道发生了什么事. 我以前也曾服用过AMT, 我在早上见到F和K时, 问他们怎么样, 我问我的女朋友她喜欢它, 和她的回答与狐是'我的上帝,是的!'她只是说'它是好的,我想'这有点担心我, 这时我意识到有些事非常不对. 我告诉F和K这是"正常的",J可能给了我们几毫克多. 我和K一起走到她的课堂上. 第二课时她告诉我有什么不对. 我问她怎么样,如果她喜欢它,她说没有. 这让我非常丧. 我开始担心, 担心她会有不好的旅行. 她向我撒谎, 告诉我她很好, 没关系, 我认为AMT没有足够的力量来激发一个完整的坏旅程, 我认为她会像"滚出它", 接下来的一段时间是高峰期. 我知道我服用的药物不仅仅是40毫克. 我得到了令人难以置信的闭眼/开眼视觉效果, 我觉得世界上任何事情都可能出错, 我正在经历我一生中最美好的旅行. 在第五课时,K的午餐时间, 我在走廊上去所, 必须是上帝的旨意让我有冲动进入那个大厅, 我听到无线电:" (我的女朋友的全名) 需要被护送出楼" 我在走廊里停了下来, 最糟糕的恐惧在我的身体中奔跑. 起初我以为这是幻觉, 但我的智慧告诉我这是非常非常真实的. 我开始在空荡荡的走廊里走来走去, 寻找任何证据, 我开始想,也许发生了一些不涉及她,但我吹出我的脑海. 我从来没有如此惊慌失措. 我不知道该怎么做. 我开始认为这可能是幻觉, 我知道这是幻觉. 现在,我知道这是幻觉. 我非常害怕, 我以为我会有一个非常糟糕的旅行. 我告诉他我在, 他问我怎么样. 我告诉他我没事,但我认为卡特里娜有问题,她可能受伤,我不知道. 他告诉我,我很疯狂,要我冷静下来. 他把我当作一个婴儿, 像我说的每一句话都来自某个疯子. 我还见了一些朋友, 他们知道我在, 我和他们谈了,他们告诉我,他们听说K发生了什么事. 这使我放心了最大的恐惧. 许多人告诉我, 听说有人说"K的太厉害了, 其他人告诉我她刚刚发生了什么事. 我非常害怕. 我决定离开食堂,走到院子里,与我的朋友单独交谈, 他开着无线电话 (这在我们学校并不罕见). 我开始从无线电中听到一些话,然后... 最糟糕的是... 我听到他们说我的全名. 我完全吓坏了. 我的朋友让我平静下来, 说我可能刚刚听到, 我告诉他们发生了什么事, 问他们该怎么办. 他们帮助我很多 (我还在. 我搜查了我的书包,口袋和所有东西, 并扔掉了我知道的一切与毒品有关, 这使得我不用清理我的书包了. 我从来没有这么紧张. 我走到最后一个课堂, 路上我看到一群管理员在大厅里. 两名警察, 和地区负责人 (顺便说一下, 这里是高中). 他们立即告诉我走进办公室. 这就是我的结局. 我从来没有低过. 我试图保持冷静,但很难. 他们把我带到一个房间, 他们告诉我我陷入了很多麻烦. 当然,幸运的是我扮演了瓜. 他们开始问我今天是否给了K什么. 我拒绝了. 然后他们开始告诉我关于K的故事, 他们告诉我,在第五课时,她的午餐时间,她完全疯了,她不得不被带到医院和约束. 他们告诉我她处于非常糟糕的状态. 我再次否认他们指控我参与其中. 他们看着我的眼睛, 他们问我是否有任何影响, 他们试图让我承认, 于是他们打电话给我. 我和祖母住在一起,所以他们跟她说话. 他们告诉她我可能在某种影响下, 我给了我的女朋友某种药物, 我祖母急忙去上学, 既然我否认了一切, 当他们搜查我的书包时, 但我不得不去做毒品测试 (尿液/血液). 尾曲 现在是星期天, 虽然K已经出院了, 但她住的地方禁止我再见到她, 取决于药物检测结果, 看起来不太好, 我可能会被停职, 假设有搜查令,所以我哥哥会把化学药物扔掉. 我不确定K所居住的机构是否会起诉我, 我今天发现了为什么会发生这种情况. 我哥哥J给了我们极高的剂量. 这是一个错误,他累了,并没有测量正确. 我从他告诉我的,我和F在某处超过60毫克,和K是45毫克,她的第一次! 我只是希望我可以把时间倒退,恢复... 我的爱,失去了. 其他:学校 (35),人际关系 (44),二手报告 (42),火车事故和旅程灾难 (7),艰难经历 (5)

Apr 8, 2003. erowid.org/exp/17806 DOSE: oral AMT (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 260 lb Drugs have been a part of me for around two years now. What I experienced two days ago inevitably, changed me forever. Life is something we cannot give, or take from someone. It is built into us. You cannot tear the soul from someone, or steal their psyche. Every individual is a temple of our own imagination; reality, thought, consciousness, being. It would be a waste of time to babble on about my feelings about life at this point, so I'll save you the time and maybe teach you something. I have been using AMT/Foxy for a few months now. I have had around 5 experiences with AMT, and many more with Foxy. To me, AMT was a gift. For 12 hours on any certein day, my mood would be lifted, and I would appreciate everything around me. I just sit here and think about that saying: 'You never know what you had, until you lose it' We had just started school a few weeks ago, andour group of friends have already been coming to school under the influence of various drugs. My girlfriend was always against my drug habits, and would always pester me about them. I guess now, she just gave up or something. I loved her with all of my heart, and truely believed we had something special, something that would last an eternity. I never knew that my utter stupidity would destroy the one thing dearest to me in my life. My girlfriend (we'll call her K) started watching what I was doing. She began to get interested in drugs and how they affected me. She was always open-minded and creative, she was just so perfect. As she became more and more interested, the topic of trying drugs herself, came up. Since our families, or lack-of, had their differences, we would only see each other at school most of the time. Though I thought about the consequences of trying a substance at school for the first time, I remember saying to myself 'Bad stuff only happens to other people'. I just wish I could turn back time. Meanwhile, I was selling Foxy/AMT at school to close friends, and was pulling in a nice amount of cash, which however, didnt weigh out the risk. But, I kept doing it. I guess I found some type of thrill or exhiliration in my endevours- or maybe I was just stupid. We set a 'date' for K's first Fox experience, and that day me, K, and F (close friend) came to school and dosed on Foxy. Me and F had 15mg since we were experienced, and I gave K 12mg. All in all, she loved it. It was probobly one of the best things to happen to her in a long time. I guess I felt proud of myself for making her so happy, but on the other hand, her excitement worried me. Regardless, a week later we had a brilliant idea. I was going to let her try some AMT, which was sure to raise her spirits and give her what she needed. We were both very excited. The plan was to dose at 5 in the morning by giving F and K a drink to take home the night before, so they could dose the next morning before school. The night before I gave them the doses, I talked to my brother, J, who was in sense, the 'drug-lord'. He handled the chemcials, I sold them. I told him what I needed and I went to bed, he told me he would put it in the drinks and refridgerate them. It was no doubt he was tired after a day of work, but he assured me that I could get some sleep and he would leave them out for tomorrow. The next morning I woke up and dosed. The 40mg (or so I thought) of AMT was mixed in orange juice so it wasent that bad of a taste. An hour after I dosed I started feeling the beginning effects, so I started getting ready for school. When it was time to go, I walked outside of the house and immediatly new something was different. As I walked in the school my movements were fluid, like I was walking under-water. At that point I knew something was up. I had taken AMT before, and it was never anything like this. When I met F and K in the morning, I asked them how they were doing and they said they were alright. I asked my girlfriend is she liked it, and unlike her response with Foxy which was 'Oh my God, YES!' she just said 'Its okay, I guess' which kinda worried me, but I didnt think anything of it. Here is when I noticed something was very wrong. It was becoming increasingly intense around 2 hours into it, and I told F and K that this is 'normal' and J probobly gave us a few mgs extra. I walked with K to her classes for the first 2 periods. Second period she told me something was wrong. I asked her how she was doing, and if she liked it, and she said no. This got me very upset. I started really worrying, fearing she was going to have a bad trip. She lied to me obviously, and told me she was fine and It's okay, and even told me 'It's Fun!' after she knew I was getting real worried. After she left for her next class, I calmed down, and just thought that AMT wasent powerful enough to excite a full-blown bad trip, and I thought she would just kinda 'roll out of it', so I stopped worrying and destined myselfed to have a great time! The next period was the peak. At that point I knew I was on something much more than just 40mg. I was getting incredible closed/open eye visuals and a lot of tracers, which didnt bother me. I felt that anything in the world could go wrong, and I would still be as happy as I was at that point. I was having the best trip of my life. During 5th period, K's lunch period, I was walking down the hall to go to the bathroom, and I passed a school administrator that had one of those walkie-talkies on. It must have been God's will that gave me the urge to go into that hall and pass that administrator at that exact time. I heard over the walkie-talkie : ' (My girlfriend's full name) needs to be escorted out of the bullding' I stopped dead in the hallway and the worst fear ran through my body. At first I thought it was a hallucination, but my better wits told me it was very, very real. I was totally panicked, I started walking around the empty hallways searching for some sign that this was really happening, but I didnt find one. I started thinking that maybe something happenened that didnt involve her tripping, but I blew that out of my mind. I was never so panicked in my life. I had no idea what to do. I started thinking that maybe this was a hallucination, but the fear still remained. From that point on to 6th period I was getting visuals of police officers, which I knew was a hallucination. I was very scared, I thought I was going to have a very bad trip. During my lunch period I saw my friend who is drug-free, and I told him I was tripping and he asked how I was doing. I told him im fine but I think theres something wrong with Katrina, she might be hurt I dont know. He told me im tripping and to calm down. He treated me like a baby, like everything I was saying came from some insane person. I also saw a few of my other friends who know I was tripping, and know K very well. I talked to them and they told me they heard that something happened with K. This reassured my worst fear. Most of them told me they heard rumors about people telling them that 'K was rolling so hard that she was biting her lip so hard it was bleeding'. Others told me that something just happened to her. I was so scared. I decided to leave the cafeteria and go out to the Courtyard and talk to my friend alone, and see if he could reassure me or something. Well, it just so happened that there was also one of those officials out in the courtyard, and yes he had a walkie-talkie on (This wasent out of the ordinary at our school). I started hearing words from the walkie-talkie, and then... the worst part... I heard them say my full name. I totally freaked out. My friend calmed me down and said I might have just heard it, but then they said it again and he heard it. We went back to the cafeteria and our group of friends started talking and I told them what happened and asked them what to do and all. They helped me out a lot (I was still very much tripping). I went through my school bag, pocket and everything and threw out everything I knew that had to do with drugs' all the notes from K that talked about us tripping, and even a dose container. It saved me A LOT of trouble cleaning my school bag. I was never so nervous in my life. I walked to my last period class, but on the way there I saw a group of administrators in the hall. It was my grade-level administrator, the principal, two police officers, and the head of the district (this was High School, by the way). They immediatly told me to step into the office. This was the end of me. I was never lower. I tried to remain calm but it was hard. They brang me to a room and I sat down with them. They told me im in a lot of trouble. Of course, I played dumb thankfully. They started asking me if I gave anything to K today. Of course, I denied it. Then they started telling me about K, which blew my mind. They told me that during 5th period, her lunch period, she flipped out totally and she had to be takin to the hospital and restrained. They told me she was in very bad shape. Again, I denied all of their accusations that I was involved. They then looked into my eyes, which were ALL pupil. They asked me if I was under the influence of anything, and they tried to 'fool' me into cracking uder pressure. They messed with my mind, trying to get me to admit to it, but luckily my defenses were up. At that point they called my house. I live with my grandmom, so thats who they talked to. They told her that I was probobly under the influence of something, and that I gave some kind of drug to my girlfriend, and shes hospitalized. My grandmom rushed to school, and blah blah blah. Bottom Line: Since I denied everything, and when they searched my schoolbag they found nothing, I was set free. They ddint suspend me or anything (at least not yet), but I had to go for a drug test (urine/blood). -------- Epilouge ---------- It is now Sunday as im writing this. K is out of the hospital, but the establishment she lives in is prohibiting me from ever seeing or talking to her again and the school expelled her, Im never going to see her again. Depending on the drug test results, which arent looking to good, I might be suspended or worse. Im assuming that there is a search warrent for the house, so my brother is getting rid of the chems. Im not sure if the establishment K lives in will press charges and if I will have to go to court to defend myself. I found out today WHY this happened. My brother, J, gave us all EXTREMELY high doses. It was a mistake, he was tired and didnt measure right. Im guessing from what he told me, that me and F were on somewhere above 60mg, and K was on 45mg, HER FIRST TIME! I just wish I could turn back time, to recover..... my love, lost.

中文译文 致幻剂

2003年11月18日星期二 我从邮寄中拿起我的2C-I. 用图纸方法,我把500毫克放成一个5x5的方形. 然后把它切成大约20毫克的方块. (这并不难,因为2C-I非常块). 我把一个方块扔进200毫升的水中, 吃了一小口, 味道就奇怪了. 这不是一个坏的味道,但强烈. 这不是最准确的测量方法, 但从我读到的, 它应该为2C-I工作. 晚餐时间为30分钟, 只有碳水化合物. 在T-0:05抽了一小碗大麻. 我喝了100毫升的水 (10毫克). 在水中几乎没有味道. 现在是下午6点. 我个人不知道有谁曾经尝试过这种药物, 五次旅行 (其中一个非常激烈), 一次E和一些止痛药. 现在,我已经做了很多事情. 我在宿舍里, 过去10分钟写了这份报告. 也许有点东西,可能是安慰剂. 躺下时半恶心. 音乐变得更加强烈. 考虑再添加5或10毫克. [这将在整个体验中持续] 失去了我的笔,在我的口袋里. 我离开了房间,因为我的室友离开门稍微打开,当他出去抽烟. 警方敲了门, 门轻微打开. 我从后门走了出来, 不想和她打交道, 很少有视觉效果,地板波浪,阅读有些困难. 室友说,当他从抽烟回来时, 很容易与RA交谈. 图像确切,但并没有令人丧. 总体来说,感觉很好. 有轻微的紧张, 但比E更少. 考虑再增加5至10毫克. 在8点决定. 我感觉很好, 服用最后5毫克. 我现在已经摄入了大约22毫克. 它们的效果与完全不同. 我没有得到任何精神fuck. 我们一直在失去东西. 室友有严重的时间延伸. 击确实增加了视觉效果, 大多数视觉效果来自颜色; 大多数现实世界只是看起来有点不同. 心理的情况有所增加. 强大的时间扩展. 树粒可以产生有趣的图像. 时间扩展到地狱. 过去10分钟对我们两人来说都像几个小时. 接下来我几乎觉得自己正在下降, 然后某种东西会把我推回去. 现在这个循环每分钟发生一次. 我吸了另一个碗. 现在的视觉效果很强, 但仍不像那样令人震惊. 离开去朋友的房间, 雨在下, 黑暗, 我在整个旅程中有一点能量, 现在,我跑步,避开几乎无处不在的水池, 现在我回来了,疲不堪,我正在下楼,要去抽烟,洗睡觉, 我用电脑和人们交谈. 我再抽一碗烟,然后再试着睡觉. 我有间歇性的头痛. 我了20分钟左右, 总体上感觉很糟糕. 我终于睡着了. 2C-I是一种有趣的药物, 尽管如此, 虽然C没有像我那样困难, 但他也难以入睡. 大麻似乎没有帮助,也许有更强烈的镇静作用? 我从来没有对这种药物产生任何负面感觉, 尤其是如果能找到一种方法来减轻这种下降. 另外,这似乎是一个很好的狂欢药物, 2004年1月4日的太阳 自从我撰写第一份报告以来, 我所经历的三次旅程都不同, 我从那些我给药的人那里收到的报道, 大约20毫克的剂量, 从"我真的感觉不到任何东西"到"我觉得我爱上了2C-I". 我认为环境对体验有着巨大的影响. 在宿舍房间度过的旅行通常是平淡的. 我第二次经历是在朋友家 (他也吸过毒), 2C-我在这个环境中工作得很好;虽然这不是一个令人惊叹的旅行,但它只是一个好时光,少了我第一次旅行的能量和更多的冷静. 我第三次旅行是在新年派对上, 这次旅行让我想起了适量的, 我有更多的视觉效果, 整个晚上似乎真的很奇怪. 有趣,但很奇怪. 我不知道有多少是毒品,有多少是现实, 我对2C-I仍然感兴趣. 在我之后的旅行中, 降落并没有那么艰难, 这将有助于减少探索经验所涉及的变量. 我目前的一个假设是, 提前服用全剂量可以大大提高功效. 我最强烈的旅行是我在服用药物后一小时内全部服用的. 时间表: 29727 大麻 (1), 2C-I (172):小组 (2-9) (17),音乐讨论 (22),烂的经历 (4),回顾 / 摘要 (11),第一次 (2),一般 (1)

Jan 4, 2004. erowid.org/exp/29727 DOSE: T+ 0:00 1 bowl smoked Cannabis (plant material) T+ 0:05 12 mg oral 2C-I (powder / crystals) T+ 2:05 5.0 mg oral 2C-I (powder / crystals) T+ 2:25 5.0 mg oral 2C-I (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 150 lb Tues, Nov 18, 2003 T=6:00 T-2:00 I pick up my 2C-I from the mail. T-1:00 Using the graph paper method, I arrange my 500mgs into a 5x5 square. I separate one side, then cut it up into (roughly) 20mg squares. (This is not hard as the 2C-I was very clumpy). I toss one square into 200mL of water, and give another to my roommate, C. Taking a small dab of the substance, it tastes odd. It’s not a bad taste, but strong. This isn’t the most accurate way of measuring, but, from what I’ve read, it should work for 2C-I. T-0:30 Light dinner, all carbs. T-0:05 Smoked a small bowl of marijuana. T-0:00 I drink 100mL of the water (10mg). The taste is barely noticeable in water. It is presently 6pm. I know no one personally who has tried this drug before, but I have researched it online and am intrigued. My drug history is limited to lots of marijuana, 5 shroom trips (one of which was incredibly intense), E once and some painkillers. C has done weed and shrooms. T+0:30 Nothing yet; I’m in my dorm room, spent the last 10 minutes writing this report. T+0:40 Maybe a little something, maybe placebo. T+0:45 Something’s there, a little body load. Semi-nausea when lying down. T+1:00 A little body buzz and some fuzzy thinking T+1:10 Shpongle on, music is intensified. Contemplating adding another 5 or 10mg. The drug seems to come in waves. [This will continue throughout the entire experience] T+1:30 Lost my pencil, it’s in my pocket. I left the room because my roommate left the door slightly opened when he went out for a cig. The RA knocked on the door and it opened slightly. I went out the back door, not wanting to deal with her, especially if it smells like weed. Very slight visuals, wavy floors, some difficulty reading. Roommate remarks on how easy it was to talk with the RA when he was returning from his cigarette. T+1:50 Definite visuals, not overwhelming though. General good feeling. There is a slight jaw clench, but much less than on E. Contemplating another 5-10mgs. Will decide at 8. T+2:00 I take another 50mL (5mg) of water, roommate takes another 10mg T+2:20 I’m feeling pretty good, and take that last 5mg. T+2:40 I’ve now ingested roughly 22mg. The effects are entirely different from mushrooms. I’m not getting any mindfuck at all. We keep losing things though. T+2:50 I decide to take a bong hit T+3:10 Roommate has severe time dilation. Bong hit definitely increased the visuals, still not overwhelming though. Majority of the visuals come from colors; most of the real world just looks a little different. T+3:30 Psychedelic mindfuck is increasing a little bit. Strong time dilation. Wood grain produces interesting visuals. T+3:45 Time is dilated to all hell. The last 10 minutes have felt like hours to the both of us. [Right here, the wavy effect is in full force. One moment, I’m tripping like crazy, then I almost feel like I’m coming down, then something will thrust me back into it. At this point, this cycle is happening, if I had to guess, about once a minute] T+4:30 And then all of a sudden it’s half an hour later. I smoke another bowl. T+4:45 Strong visuals now, but still not overwhelming like shrooms. T+5:00 Leaving for friend’s room, it’s pouring rain and dark, looks sort of like a movie out there. I’ve had a bit of energy throughout the trip, but haven’t really expended it. Now, I run, avoiding the puddles of water which are almost everywhere is fun. T+7:30 Back, tired, well on my way down, going to smoke a bowl, take a shower and go to sleep, have to reflect on the experience tomorrow. T+9:00 That didn’t work, I can’t sleep at all. I get on my computer and talk to people. T+9:30 I smoke another bowl and try to sleep again. T+12:00 This is getting annoying, I want to sleep, but just can’t. I have an intermittent headache. T+14:00 I vomit for a good 20 minutes or so and generally feel like crap. T+14:30 I finally fall asleep. 2C-I is an interesting drug, it warrants further exploration. The comedown, however, was pretty rough. C didn’t have quite as hard a time with it as I, but he also had trouble sleeping. Marijuana didn’t seem to help, perhaps something with a stronger sedative effect? I never felt any negativity to the drug and my mind was often very clear. Thus, I concur with the sentiment that this could be a good intro to psychedelics, especially if a way could be found to reduce the comedown. Also, this seems like it would be a good rave drug, with its energy and affinity for music and colors. Sun, Jan 04, 2004 Since the writing of my first report, I have experimented twice more with this material as well as having shared it with almost 10 others. All three of my trips have been vastly different; as well, every report that I have gotten from others have been very different. The reports that I have received from those who I have given the drug to, all at a dose of roughly 20mg, have varied from “didn’t really feel anything” to “I think I’m in love with 2C-I.” One female who took 2C-I as her first psychedelic really enjoyed it. I think that setting has an enormous impact on the experience. Trips spent in dorm rooms have generally been subdued. My second experience was at a friend’s (who also dosed) house, with other friends coming and going, with a lot of weed smoking. 2C-I worked well in this setting; while not a mind-blowing trip, it was just a good time, less of the energy of my first trip and more chill. My third trip was at a new years party, where there was a lot of alcohol and a little marijuana, but I was the only person tripping. This trip actually reminded me of a moderate dose of mushrooms, I had far more visuals, and things were fairly confusing. The whole night seemed really weird. Fun, but weird. Of course, I don’t know how much of the weirdness was the drugs and how much was reality, as my friends who were just drunk and stoned said that it was really weird. 2C-I continues to be a drug which interests me. The comedown was not nearly as hard on my subsequent trips, nor on anyone else. I should shortly be receiving an accurate scale, which will help cut down on the variables involved in exploring the experience. One hypothesis I have at the moment is that taking the whole dose up front increases potency by a good margin. My strongest trip by far was the one where I took it all within an hour of dosing.

我第一次和最后一次的达图拉体验始于我和两个朋友坐在一房子里决定掉一个达图拉植物. 我们坐在朋友家里煮了半个小时, 喝了大约3杯. 现在我是一个非常有经验的吸毒者, 但接下来发生的事情我完全没有准备好... 我几乎站不起来. 我的朋友也没有. 这是一个大惊喜! 我在朋友的公寓里和同居者聊天, 我说'我现在想回家'. 他说你已经回家了. 我说'我想回我的家. 她说'我是你的'. 这是一个大惊喜! 我突然意识到我已经在家, 记忆回来时, 我记得我抽了很多隐形香烟, 基本上不知道在哪里,什么,如何,或为什么. 我后来发现我一直在地板上爬行, 当他的异性恋妹妹打电话给救护车时, 我从阳台上跳下来, 然后我被打电话 (我还不知道是谁打电话), 她带我回家, 一个朋友在林中无带, 子围在膝盖上, "听"音乐. 另一个在医院里, 他第二天被释放. 他一直在服用抗抑郁药, 我可以告诉你这是一个非常令人不安的经历, 吸一口烟, 发现你没有一个. 或是跟你哥哥谈论自己以为自己是别人... 或是让你的朋友来看你怎么样, 我记不清, 因为这只是三天前的事, 我确实记得试图吸烟我的皮带,扔我的猫在浴里, 清单还可以继续. 时间表: 10436 达图拉 (15):小组 (2-9) (17),旅行后的问题 (8),火车毁和旅行灾难 (7),健康问题 (27),不好的旅行 (6),第一次 (2)

Apr 7, 2002. erowid.org/exp/10436 DOSE: Datura BODY WEIGHT: 90 kg My first and last datura experience started with me and 2 friends sitting around at a house deciding to rip off a Datura plant. After finding one and tearing a few flowers off it we sat at mates house and boiled it all up for half an hour and drunk about 3 cups each. Now I'm a very experienced drug user but what happened next I was totally unprepared for... At first the only feeling I felt was extreme muscle tiredness and I was almost un-able to stand. Neither could my friends. BANG! I'm at my friend's flat chatting to his flatmate, I say 'I want to go home now'. He says 'you are home'. I say 'I want to go to home where my mum lives'. SHE says 'I am your mum'. BANG! I suddenly realize I am at home, its almost midday. As my memory comes back I remember smoking many invisible cigarettes, thinking my mum and brother were friends of mine, talking to invisible people. Basicly not knowing where, what, how, or why. I later found out I had been crawling on the floor, where we originally drunk the datura, eating 'bugs' while my friend convulsed and foamed at the mouth. When his straight sister called the ambulance, I lept off the balconey in pursuit of a friend who was actully on the other side of the house. Then my mum was called (by whom I still don't know) and she took me home where I spent hours rolling imaginary joints, snorting imaginary lines, talking to thin air about crap and basically scaring the shit out of my family. One friend wound up in some bushes with no belt, pants around his knees 'listening' to music. The other was in hospital in a morphine induced coma. He was released the next day. He had been on anti-depressants and that fucked him up. I can tell you its a very disturbing experience to take a drag on your ciggerate and find out you dont have one.. Or to talk to your brother about himself thinking he's someone else... or having your friends come round to see how you are, having a long chat with them only to realize they are not there. I cannot remember it all as it was only three days ago and I can't operate properly (its taken 2 hrs so far to write this because i cant see). I do remeber trying to smoke my belt, throwing my cat in the bathtub, thinking my mum was Spike Milligan. The list goes on.

在口服 莲花/百合花 - 尼姆菲亚·诺卡利·瓦尔·卡鲁莱亚 (干燥的) 三次射击 在口服 酒精 - - 强烈 (液体) 在口服 莲花/百合花 - 尼姆菲亚·诺卡利·瓦尔·卡鲁莱亚 (茶) 莲花/百合花 - 尼姆菲亚·诺卡利·瓦尔·卡鲁莱亚 大麻 我们对这次旅行的报道很感兴趣, 所以我们买了100克这种东西 (它真的很便宜,而且合法). 如果你曾经读过"奥德赛", 听说过"莲花食者之国", 霍默知道这些东西,所以他一定是个吸毒者? 所以我们决定尝试一下. 首先我们从烟斗里抽出一些大麻 (一个漂亮的水管) 用一个很容易容纳大约3克大麻的滑板, 时间是0:30 ,我们有很多的莲花,所以我们把滑板包装在6或7碗的莲花,莲花比大麻轻很多,所以你不能把那么多放在一个碗里,但我认为我们抽了大约3克, 烟味和气味很好,它有效地掩盖大麻的气味,但它对喉有点苛刻 (我的正常小组的一些成员在第二天抱怨喉有多疼, 尽管第二天早上我感觉很好, 时间1点 大约一个小时, 大麻的效果会减弱, 我觉得很放松,有点镇静;我觉得我可以,如果我愿意睡觉 (我是一个失眠者,所以我通常很快就会服用可能是潜在的睡眠辅助药物). 我希望我有更多的草. 好吧,现在我们要去哪儿了? 房间里有人说:"我觉得有点奇怪...但是在好的意义上!" 我感觉到一种平静和幸福的感觉, 我觉得好吗? 我的身体真的很放松吗? 我认为这实际上是两者兼而有之. 虽然我一直觉得自己完全清醒, 我对这次经历的记忆是清晰的, 我知道那个该死的莲花正在对我们的头脑做一些事情, 我肯定我们不是因为大麻而变得愚蠢, 因为我们五个人中有四个人在过去的三个月里每天都在吸大麻, 所以我们真的不再笑,甚至不想吃东西, 我开始注意到颜色和光线比以前更明亮,更生动. 每当我看某物或试图专注于某物时, 它会发出某种光. 很难描述,但当我闭上眼睛时,它肯定还在那里. ,好吧,如果你闭上眼睛,轻轻地擦你的眼皮,直到你开始看到灯,然后想象,不断覆盖任何我看,我猜你有一个很好的想法, 我通常在吸了很多大麻后会这样, 但我注意到蓝莲花让它变得更强大, 没有什么不愉快的, 但没有什么特别有趣的. 我不确定是否还有其他人有这种效果, 我也应该指出, 我个人很容易产生幻觉; 我往往会在任何我尝试过的幻觉剂中, 也许光线效应只是我脑部或其他脑部有曲的特征; 我不知道. 一点五十分 我开始厌倦,所以我试着再摄入两克,然后我下楼到我的房间, 伏特加下面很好, 似乎很高兴当它达到莲花在底部. 现在我喝了一些伏特加, 所以我要去做一些疯狂的旅行, 我们决定出去吃晚饭, 因为现在是晚上7点45分左右, 我注意到我的身体有一种惰的流动性. 散步是有趣的,我感到头和高兴. 灯光比以前更加突出. 虽然我的头脑似乎还清晰, 记忆也清晰, 我觉得我已经找到了某个东西, 三点钟 晚餐结束了,我们吃了披萨,一个中等的胡饼和一个中等的, 晚餐是放松和镇静;有很少的谈话,但又没有人觉得需要或愿望;我们都享受莲花的愉快效果. 虽然我们付了钱回来后, 所以我们决定再试一次, 这次我们决定茶! 我把精确的15克,三袋Camile (sp?) 茶从天空调味, 和一堆吸水器在一个充满水的热. 我让它在低温上,只有那个水平,你得到几个泡不是一个完整的吹沸和不断混合它与切棒. 三点半 味道非常有趣. 似乎甘茶和糖让它一起工作非常好 (尽管我认为添加一些蜂蜜也会改善味道). 我很快发现这茶实际上是相当令人愉快, 有一个非常复杂的味道 (我认为我可能会造未来的杯子只是因为我非常喜欢味道, 它真的很独特). 我们热切地吸取了这种药, 我们在等待时吸了两碗大麻和一些莲花 (大约2克). 在四点 我感觉像我以前吸烟时,我没有喝它,但我认为所有的莲花在我的身体让我更快. 时间4点20分 好,现在灯光回来了,轻微的高兴和心灵的平静. 这种流动性似乎只有在莲花与酒精结合时才会出现. 五点四十分 莲花已经或多或少地消失了, 我没有感觉到任何不良副作用, 也没有感到异常疲, 我只能在晚上读书,打扫房间,洗衣服. 我没有感觉有所不同,只是清醒. 现在回想起来,我认为蓝莲花是一种相当拙的药物. 有些报道将其与可代因,Mdma和片进行比较. 在我的时间里我有大量的可代因,MDMA,和片, 我觉得放松,可代因和片可以做到这一点, 吸一口泰伦诺3药, 45分钟后就难起床了; 吸一口莲花烟, 虽然添加一些酒精会让我感到头和高兴, 但仅仅因为它激发了幸福感, 事实上,没有人能感受到像MDMA那样强烈的快乐,幸福,温暖等等. 吸了一堆蓝莲花后, 我们花了大部分时间在阅读"高时代"杂志, 这种无所事事的渴望, 只是平静地享受周围的安静, 我个人认为, 莲花是我在睡前应该吸的, 我也认为它可能是有用的良好的下降药物与大麻结合, 也许在一个艰难的夜晚吸可乐,卷,等等? 然而,我相信这种药物可能提供3种可能的替代品,用于进一步的娱乐 (滥用). 其他报道指出, 将大量莲花浸入红酒似乎极具效果. 我打算把两瓶卡伯内酒 (或如果我有心情的话, 我认为我对酒精和莲花的耐受性很高, 随着饮酒的增加, 便会产生更多的流动性, 这非常有趣, 我敢打,如果我真的,真的喝醉了,然后尝试了一些莲花我可能会得到一些. 整个旅程中, 尤其是我喝了几次酒后, 我觉得自己正处于某种境界, 另一种方法是找到某种方法来集中蓝莲花的活性成分, 我没有在网上找到莲花提取物, 也不知道如何从植物中自然提取物质, 最后, 和蓝莲花被认为是很好的协同作用. 我个人不喜欢,所以我会让我的团队的其他成员尝试这种组合 (他们总是在上). 保持安全 更多 其他: 大麻 (1), 莲花/百合 - - 尼姆菲亚·诺卡利·瓦尔·卡鲁莱亚 (105):小组 (2-9) (17),组合 (3),一般 (1)

From Our Western Heritage Blue Lotus & Cannabis by Crackhead840 Citation:   Crackhead840. "From Our Western Heritage: An Experience with Blue Lotus & Cannabis (exp13255)". Erowid.org . Feb 18, 2005. erowid.org/exp/13255 DOSE: T+ 0:00 smoked Cannabis (plant material) T+ 0:30 smoked Lotus/Lily - Nymphaea nouchali var caerulea (dried) T+ 1:50 2.0 g oral Lotus/Lily - Nymphaea nouchali var caerulea (dried) T+ 0:00 3 shots oral Alcohol - Hard (liquid) T+ 3:00 oral Lotus/Lily - Nymphaea nouchali var caerulea (tea) T+ 3:30 smoked Lotus/Lily - Nymphaea nouchali var caerulea T+ 0:00 smoked Cannabis BODY WEIGHT: 170 lb Anyhow, so it happens that one of my intrepid pals discovers this stuff called Blue Lotus (Nymphaea caerulea, if you want to be technical) and we were naturally intrigued by the trip reports so we bought 100 grams of the shit (its really, really cheap and legal). Plus, if you've ever read the Odyssey and heard of the land of the Lotus-Eaters you just have to try something that's so connected to our Western Heritage (I'll admit, we're all really big dorks). I mean shit, Homer knew about this stuff so he must have been a druggie? So we decided to give it a shot. Time 0:00 First we all smoke some weed out of the bong (a nice water tube) with a slide that can easily hold about .3 grams of weed so I believe that between the five of us we finish off the weed pretty quickly. Time 0:30 Ok we're all stoned (and we all know what that's like) so lets try some lotus and hey, we have plenty of lotus so we pack the slide about 6 or 7 bowls of lotus, lotus is a lot lighter than weed so you can't pack as much into a bowl but I presumed we smoked about 3 grams after I reweighed our big bag 'o Lotus. The lotus doesn't do much at first but its a very pleasant smoking experience; the smoke tastes and smells wonderful and it masks cannabis odors effectively, but it is a little harsh on the throat (some members of my normal group were complaining of how much there throat hurt the next day, but that could be because we smoked a LOT that night, irregardless I felt fine the next morning i.e. when I'm writing this). Time 1:00 Its about one hour and the cannabis wears off and the lotus starts to fill in. I feel very relaxed and slightly sedated; I feel that I can, if I so desire go to sleep at will (I'm an insomniac, so i usually pick up on drugs that may be potential sleep aids very quickly). So far nothing great here; I kinda of wish I had more weed. Time 1:15-20 Ok, now were getting somewhere...but where? One person in the room said, 'I feel kinda of weird...but in a good way!' This is a pretty good description. I felt a general sense of peacefulness and well-being, mild but there. It was difficult to place the exact nature of the effect: do i feel good b/c my body is really relaxed or is this stuff actually effecting my mind? I think its actually a little bit of both. Although i felt fully cognizant the entire time and my memory of the experience is clear, I know that damn lotus was doing something to our heads b/c people were not making sense, conversations were breaking down a bit, ciggarettes were being lit at the filter, i.e. basic stupid stuff that indicates that despite whatever you're currently thinking your mind's definitely being affected. I'm positive that we didn't get the stupids from the weed 'cause 4 out of the 5 of us there had been smoking weed everyday for at least the last 3 months, some of us for years. So we really don't get giggly or even the munchies anymore, just high. Time 1:30 By this point I start to notice that colors and lights are much brighter and vivider than before. And i also noticed the lights; whenever I look at something or try to focus on something it emits a kind of light. Its hard to describe, but it was definitely still there when I closed my eyes. Hmmmm, well if you close your eyes and gently rub your eyelids till you start to see lights and then imagine that constantly overlaying anything I look at and I guess you have a pretty good idea as to what I saw. I usually get like that after I smoke a lot of cannabis but I noticed that the blue lotus made it come on a lot stronger and last longer. Not unpleasant but nothing particularly interesting. I'm not sure if anyone else had this effect, b/c I neglected to ask. I should also note that I am personally very prone towards hallucinations; I tend to trip harder and longer on less of most every hallucinogen I've ever tried. So maybe the lights effect was just unique to my brain or others whose brains have a trippy bent; I don't know. 1:50 I'm getting bored so I try ingesting another two grams and then I go downstairs to my room and pour a triple shot of vodka. The vodka goes down fine and seems to be quite pleased when it reaches the lotus at the bottom. So I'm thinking, ok, i've smoked a shit load, ate some, and now had some vodka so I'm gonna get one of those crazy trips that some of the other posts talk about, right? 2:10-20 We decide to go out for dinner since its about 7:45pm and everyone's really hungry. I notice my body has a certain languid fluidity to it. Walking is fun and enjoyable, I feel giddy and euphoric. The lights are much more prominent than before. However, my mind still seems unclouded and my memory is clear; I'm on to something, I think, but am I at the threshold and I just haven't stepped through yet? 3:00 By this time dinner's over and with all that food (we had pizza, a medium pepperoni and a medium mushroom, chicago style). Dinner was relaxed and sedate; there was little talking but then again no one felt the need or the desire; we were all enjoying the pleasant effects of the Lotus. However, by the time we paid and walked back we could feel the effects wearing off. So we decided to try again with this Lotus; this time we decided to brew some tea! So I put exactly 15 grams, three Camile (sp?) tea bags from Celestial Seasoning, and a bunch of suger into a hot pot filled with water. I let it simmer on low heat, just that level were you get a few bubbles not a full blown boil and constantly mixed it with a chop stick. 3:30 The taste was very interested. It was extremly bitter at first but there was a sweet after taste too it; it seems the camile tea and the sugar made it work very well together (although, I think adding some honey would have improved the taste as well). After several sips I soon found the tea to actually be rather pleasant and have a very sophisticated flavor (I think I'll probably brew up future cups simply b/c I love the taste so much, its really unique). We imbibed this elixer eagerly and eagerly awaited the results. Of course while we waited we smoked two more bowls of weed and some more Lotus (about another 2 grams). 4:00 I felt like I did before when I smoked it and didn't drink it but I think all this Lotus in my body's made me come up quicker. 4:20 Ok, now the lights are back and the mild euphoria and the peace of mind. The fluidity's not here though; that seems to be an effect that only shows up when lotus is combined with alcohol. 5:45 The lotus had more or less worn off and the group breaks up for sleep/work/etc. I don't feel any adverse side effects nor do I feel unusually tired as I usually do after a heavy weed smoking session. Instead, I stayed up the rest of the night reading, cleaning, doing laundry. I did't feel different, just sober. In retrospect, I think Blue Lotus is a pretty lame drug. Some of reports compare it to codeine, mdma, and opium. I've had plenty of codeine, mdma, and opium in my time and I can say that Blue Lotus doesn't hold a candle to these drugs. Sure I felt relaxed, codeine and opium will do that, but they do I much better job of it. Take a tylenol 3 and I'll have trouble getting up after 45 minutes or so; smoke Lotus until I'm blue in the face and I still can't come close to one pill. Sure add some alcohol and I'll get giddy and euphoric, but just because it inspires a sense of well being doesn't make it like MDMA. In fact, no one felt anything like the intense feelings of joy, happiness, warmth, etc. that MDMA inspires. It does resemble opium in the sense that after we smoked a bunch of this Blue Lotus we spent most of our time lounging around reading back issues of High Times, just chilling, instead of getting into an animated conversation, which is what we normally do when we get fucked up. That desire to lounge and do nothing and just be calm and enjoy your suroundings quietly reminded me greatly of opium, minus the obvious narcotic effect of opium and its ability to dilate time. Personally, it seems to me that lotus is something i should smoke as a night cap right before bed; its a terrific sleep aid. I also think it may be useful as a good come down drug in combination with cannabis, maybe after a hard night of snorting coke, rolls, etc.? However, I believe that the drug may offer 3 possible alternatives for further recreational (ab)use. Other reports have noted that steeping large amounts of lotus in red wine seems to be extremly effective. I plan on steeping two bottles of cabernet (or merlot if I'm in the mood) in 20 grams of Lotus, each. I figure I have a high tolerance for alcohol and lotus by now, so 20 grams steeped in wine should do something. The extra fluidity that arrives with just a little alcohol is very interesting; I definitely want to explore that some more. I bet if I got really, really drunk and then tried some lotus I might be getting somewhere. Throughout the trip, especially after I imbibed a few shots I felt I was on the cusp of something but i just couldn't put my finger on it nor could I push myself past the threshold. Another method would be finding someway to concentrate the active ingredients of Blue Lotus and then take it that way. I haven't found lotus extract online nor do I know of a way to extract stuff naturally out of plants so i'm just going to skip this option. Finally, salvia and Blue Lotus are suppose to have an excellent synergy together. Personally, I can't stand salvia so i'll let the rest of my group try that combo (they trip on salvia all the time).

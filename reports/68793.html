我在2000年至2001年因滥用LSD和狂喜药物而患有幻觉持续感知障碍. 现在我已经戒掉了所有毒品,酒精,尼古丁,甚至咖啡因, 我目前每天服用3毫克克洛诺和150毫克埃弗克索尔XR, 我已经服用克洛诺近4年了, 我知道我已经上了, 我服用Effexor以帮助稳定我的情绪, 它有点帮助焦虑, 我不得不说,它效果比我尝试过的任何东西都好. 我只在使用它一年, 但我无法想象没有它的生活. 我只是想举一个例子说明为什么它让我害怕. 没有钱补充我的处方, 两天没能服用150毫克的剂量. 我开始感到非常丧. 我服用了一些额外的克隆, 睡了一晚. 第二天 我在工作时才意识到. 我不仅感到生病,而且比以往任何时候都更加丧, 我知道我必须做什么:我必须自杀. 这完全有道理, 无论发生什么, 我都无法说服自己放弃! 我服用了更多的克洛诺来试图平静下来, 但没有帮助. 工作人员注意到我在打东西, 虽然我否认了, 我开始比平常更多的幻觉:幻觉,浮动,静态,影像,闪光等等. 是的,我必须自杀! 但等待! 我必须杀死其他人,也是! 这完全是有道理的! 我回家休息15分钟, 装上我的AR-15, 开车回去工作, 带来足够的弹药来完成工作. 装载所有的片到柄. 让警方派特种部队进来, 我们会发生枪战, 这真是太棒了! 我只需要跑到浴室吐,然后我可以把这一切. 我的计划没有实现, 因为我大部分时间都在吐, 直到工作结束的时候. 我回到家里, 装进我的38 revolver, 旋转手枪, 我把它放在我的头上, 我做了两次. 我觉得如果上帝真的存在, 他必须阻止这场梦, 电话响了 - - 我前妻打电话问我的情况. 我告诉她我没有Effexor了,我必须自杀. 但她很好, 付了钱, 拿了Effexor来给我. 我立即服用了300毫克. 我很高兴她来了. 我需要三天才能恢复正常. 但是这种Effexor让我害怕! 我从来没有想过这种想法之前服用这种药! 没有使用Effexor, 我会比服用Effexor之前更加丧, 我仍然每天服用150毫克. 我把所有的枪都交给了我父亲, 我已经服用了许多类型的药物, 在其中的一些, 遭受了撤回但我从来没有任何让我像Effexor撤回的坏状态! 时间表: 68793 药品 - 文拉法辛 (191), 药品 - 克洛纳泽巴姆 (125): 和习惯性 (10), 医疗使用 (47), 抑郁症 (15), 宿醉 / 几天后 (46), 旅行后的问题 (8), HPPD / 持久的视觉 (40), 多天的经验 (13), 糟糕的旅行 (6), 各种 (28)

Dec 29, 2008. erowid.org/exp/68793 DOSE: 150 mg oral Pharms - Venlafaxine (daily) 3.0 mg oral Pharms - Clonazepam (daily) BODY WEIGHT: 170 lb I have had Hallucinogen Persisting Perception Disorder for seven years from abusing LSD & Ecstasy back in 2000 - 2001. Since then I have quit all drugs, alcohol, nicotine, and even caffiene & still am aflicted with this terrible disorder! I am currently on 3mg Klonopin/day and 150mg Effexor XR/day just to get by. I've been on the Klonopin for almost 4 years and I know I'm addicted to it but it's the Effexor that worries me more than anything. I take Effexor to help stabilize my mood and it helps a tad with the anxiety but it's mostly for my depression. And I have to say it works like nothing else I have ever tried. I've been on it only a year but I cannot see life without it. I just wanted to give an example as to why it scares me. Ran out of money to refill my prescription and couldn't take my 150mg dose for 2 days. The first day I just felt slightly sick and I started to start feeling pretty depressed. I took some extra Klonopin and fell asleep for the night. Day 2. It didn't hit me until I was at work. Not only was I feeling sick but I was more depressed than ever and everything irritated me. I knew what I had to do: I had to kill myself. It made perfect sense and no matter what I could not talk myself out of it! I took more klonopin to try to mellow out but it didn't help. People at work noticed I was banging things around and something was wrong even though I denied it. I started hallucinating more than usual: Auras, Floaters, Static, Afterimages, Flashes of Light, etc. Yes, I must kill myself! But wait! I must kill everyone else, too! It made perfect sense! Go home for my 15 minute break, load up my AR-15, drive back to work and gun everyone down. Bring enough ammo to get the job done. Load all the clips to the hilt. Gun everyone down & get the police to send the SWAT team in, we'll have a shoot-out and then they will shoot me down & I'll finally be free!! It was perfect! I just needed to run to the bathroom to throw up & then I could get this all over wth. My plans weren't realized because I was puking most of the time until it was time to get off work. I got home and loaded a round into my .38 pistol and spun the revolver before slamming it shut. I put it to my head and pulled the trigger. I did this twice more. I figured if there was a God he would have to stop this nightmare or I would! The phone rang - my ex-wife calling to see how I was doing. I told her I ran out of my Effexor and I had to kill myself. But she was nice enough to pay & pick up the Effexor and bring it to me. I took 300mg immediately. I'm glad she came. It took about 3 days to get stabilized to where I was feeling 'normal' again. But this Effexor scares me! I never thought these kind of thoughts BEFORE taking this drug! I mean I get way way more depressed without Effexor than I did before I ever took it & never delusional! I'm still taking 150mg/day. I gave all but one of my guns to my dad for him to hold because I don't want to hurt myself or anyone. I've taken so many types of drugs, got hooked on some of them, suffered the withdrawals from them but I've never had anything that has put me in a bad state as Effexor withdrawal!

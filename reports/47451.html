在一个偏的地区, 远离城市, 两个性别的人群, 一位大师以当地印度方言 (巴西) 的古老颂歌开幕仪式, 席上的人喝了一杯植物性饮料 (80-100毫升), 然后向其他成员提供, 会议期间或之后不使用大麻, 一种苦苦的药液通常会引起吐, 尤其是如果你在治疗前几天喝过酒. 虽然我被遮住了, 我却感觉到极度寒冷, 不停, 能听到钟声的响声, 每次听到钟声的响声, 在一次会议中微笑很多,下一次无情地哭泣. 我以前不太相信灵性, 但这些会议帮助我更接近我的创造者, 改变了我个性的一些方面. 我发现自己从上面看着其他参与者, 漂浮,快乐,与地球母亲联系在一起, 如果我愿意, 第二天我觉得完全充满活力, 没有感觉像吸烟或喝酒了很多天如果不是整个星期. 整体而言,这是一个改变人生的经历. 其他: 雅华斯卡 (8):回顾 / 总结 (11), 团体仪式 (21)

Thoroughly Life Changing Ayahuasca by DT Citation:   DT. "Thoroughly Life Changing: An Experience with Ayahuasca (exp47451)". Erowid.org . Jan 28, 2006. erowid.org/exp/47451 DOSE: 90 ml oral Ayahuasca (tea) BODY WEIGHT: 75 kg Sessions were held fortnightly over 4 months in a secluded area, away from the city, a small community of people of both genders including children 13-15 years imbibing in vegetal. A master opens the ceremony with the chanting of ancient hyms a local indian dialect (brasil), after which those at the table take a drink of vegetal (80-100ml) which then proceeds to the other members, no marijuana is used during or after the session, just vegetal in varying doses, you are free to ask for another glass if the amount ingested is not sufficient, a bitter bitter potion usually inducing vomiting for the first couple of sessions especially if you have had alcohol in the days previous to the session. After some more sessions I never felt like vomiting, althought I did feel intensely cold even though I was covered up I kept shivering, I could hear the ringing of bells and every time I heard them my experience peaked to a new level, like waves crashing on the shore, although I am short sighted I could see every detail in crystal clear clarity. Smiling a lot in one session, crying uncontrollably the next. During the session we were encouraged to ask any question, for which inevitably there were answers, previously I was not a very strong believer in spirituality, but these sessions helped me to come closer to my maker and changed some facets of my personality for good. Some sessions I felt detached from my body, found myself looking at the other participants from above floating, happy, connected to mother earth, I could if I wished travel into the leaves and roots felt the energies and auras. After the sessions which lasted a couple of hours, they were then closed with further incantations and we were free to eat or talk, the next day I felt completely energised, did not feel like smoking a cigarette or drink alcohol for many days if not the whole week. On the whole a throughly life changing, experience.

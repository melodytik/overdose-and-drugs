May 4, 2018. erowid.org/exp/111221 DOSE: 2 oz oral St. John's Wort (liquid) BODY WEIGHT: 160 lb First off, I’ll tell my drug experience. I am very experienced with weed, vyvanse and alcohol. I have done lsd multiple times. Shrooms twice and mdma once. I have also done my fair share of vicodins and percocets. I had a whole bottle of st. John’s wort, at the time I was badly addicted to the drug vyvanse. It was one of the nights I couldn’t sleep so I figured I’d try the st. John’s wort. Ingestion time was around 9pm T+15:00- felling slight sedation, probably placebo. T+30:00- feeling heavy, still stimulated from vyvanse. T+1:00- feeling completely sedated, completely killed vyvanse high. All anxiety relieved. T+1:30- very thirsty, get up to get a drink. I feel like I’m buzzed on alcohol. T+1:45- very sleepy, go to bed. All in all, it’s a good herb that could be used for sleep/reducing anxiety. I got no euphoria.

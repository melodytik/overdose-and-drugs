之前的经验: 我已经用了15种不同的物质了40次, 我曾经在值剂量下进行过MET检测. 第一个试验: 我最初被告知这是HCl盐, 但在我第一次试验后, 我被告知这是自由基... 经过5到10分钟的痛苦烧伤, 很快就升级到类似DMT的状态. 这比我预期的要多得多. 我打算吸入一小部分, 和一些朋友一起出去玩, 这并没有发生. 我花了大约15分钟检查我的新公寓的墙壁, 因为我已经确信它们是蓝色的, 在所有平面上也有一些可见的波形运动. 尽管视觉效果并不令人印象深刻, 这与吸烟的10到20毫克DMT相似, 我平衡和运动能力很快就下降了, 我感到有些烦, 因为我感觉到效果如此强烈, 我终于放上了一些音乐, 这真的有帮助. 整体而言,高原持续了大约45分钟, 逐渐下降结束在3个半小时左右. 现在我知道这是一个免费的基地,我可能下次尝试吸烟. 我猜它更像是吸烟的DET, 因为它不能像DMT那样迅速分解. 第二个试验: 序言: 我终于有机会探索这种化合物的潜力. 我会在晚上八点左右在他的公寓见到我的一个好朋友. 他认为去那里会很有趣, 因为他刚搬进来, 买了一台新的液晶高清电视, 无论如何, 我们计划从普通的油管中抽出freebase. 我先把20毫克的MET放入自由基管中, 我们轮流服用小剂量. 第二次被击中时, 我吸入的速度太快了, 我很失望,因为每次2次击中后, 管道里还有一些MET. 肺部也感觉不太好, 所以我们决定这不会起作用. 另一位朋友在八点半时出现, 我们都吃了一粒囊中的粉末 (我90毫克, 但除了传统的和LSD之外, 他从未尝试过其他任何东西. 另一位没有这么多经验, 我们都在过去1个半小时内吃过晚餐, 这可能会使吸收速度有所减缓. 接下来我们花了一个小时抽,等待, 观看一段关于珊瑚礁的高清纪录片. 这灯让我觉得我在水下. 我不认为这是由于药物虽然. 我们都感觉到一个明显的身体声, 我觉得可以伸展和走动. 房间看起来有点明亮, 当我闭上眼睛, 除了一般的亮度增加和一些突出的红色和绿色之外, 没有令人印象深刻的OEV. 我建议我们试着再抽一次. 这一次我们要找出正确的技术. 你需要保持火焰至少1英寸从管的底部和吸气非常,非常慢. 我把大约50毫克放进管子里, 另一位朋友希望谨慎, 确保口服剂量不只是缓慢发展. 我可能吸20毫克,我的朋友吸30毫克. 我感觉到头部空间发生了变化, 我很快就把另外50毫克放进烟斗里, 吸了大约30毫克. 我现在处于MET空间, 类似于DMT空间, 我闭上眼睛时, 经历了一种类似于我通常在DMT上经历的现象. 立刻闭上眼皮, 三维空间就消失了. 我觉得自己就像一个二维平面上的一维点. 对于我来说,没有前方或后方的概念. 没有实体可以说, 但有视觉级联的信号从我的视网膜疯了. 我看到了许多有色彩的图案. 我感觉自己很棒. 我可以在甲板上散步,享受新鲜的空气. 但与DMT不同, 降低是非常渐进的. 我觉得在30分钟后我已经达到基本水平, 我们冒险进入我的朋友的卧室, 听一些勃·马利. 由于某种原因我没有真正感觉到, 这是奇怪的, 因为我通常喜欢勃·马利当我. 我终于让一个没有吸过烟的朋友来吸几次, 他一直在夸大自己的感受. 他不停地说他感觉有多么棒, 他是如何倒如此强烈, 但仅仅因为他说得如此清晰, 这有点烦人. 我相信他感觉很好, 因为身体的声非常高兴, 根据我的要求, 我立刻感觉到情况好了一百倍. 我还有一些视觉效果, 我给管道添加了一些MET, 总共大约50毫克, 我们分开了. 我可能吸了20-25毫克,一个朋友又吸了20-25毫克,另一个吸了5毫克. 我不再关心他, 试图让他以正确的方式做这件事有点有趣. 在接下来的45分钟里, 我漂浮在MET空间中, 其他两个人又回去看电视了, 我感觉自己完全平静了. 我终于和另外两个人一起看电视, 我们聊了一会. 由于第二次吸烟剂量较小, 我已经开始恢复正常. 我觉得我至少可以再享受一个小时的效果, 但通过将我的注意力转向对话和电视, 效果更快地消失了. 一个小时后我回家了. 我花了一些时间在BL因为我有一些残留刺激. 我重新阅读了我的第一份旅行报告, 我几乎可以把自己带回那个经历. 我在最后一次吸烟后大约3个半小时就睡着了. 摘要: 我很喜欢这种体验, 我怀疑我会再试着用口服剂量, 我认为IM和吸烟可能是最好的路线. 其他: 经营于市场经济的企业 (381):小组 (2-9) (17),一般 (1)

An Early Voyage into MET Space MET by Dondante Citation:   Dondante. "An Early Voyage into MET Space: An Experience with MET (exp54930)". Erowid.org . Aug 1, 2006. erowid.org/exp/54930 DOSE: T+ 0:00 15 mg insufflated MET (powder / crystals) T+ 0:00 90 mg oral MET (capsule) T+ 1:15 20 mg smoked MET T+ 1:15 30 mg smoked MET T+ 2:15 20 mg smoked MET BODY WEIGHT: 175 lb Previous experience: I’ve become fairly experienced with psychedelics in the past year. I have tripped about 40 times with 15 different substances and close to 30 of those are from this past year. I have sampled MET one other time at a threshold dose. Trial 1: Insufflated 15 mg MET freebase I was initially told that this was the HCl salt, but after my first trial, I was informed that it was the freebase ... which may have been why it burned like hell. After about 5-10 minutes of an agonizing burn, I felt a definite alert. It quickly escalated to a somewhat DMT-like state. It was much more than I expected from such a low dose. I was planning on insufflating a small amount and going to hang out with some friends and maybe have a nice buzz. Well, that didn't happen. I spent about 15 minutes examining the walls in my new apartment, as I had become convinced that they were blue ... they are completely white, but I couldn't convince myself otherwise. There was also some noticable wave-like motion on all flat surfaces. Other than that the visuals were not too impressive, but I felt like there was a lot of potential. It was similar to maybe 10-20 mg DMT smoked, except much more gradual in and out. My balance and motor skills went downhill pretty quickly, but it wasn't really uncomfortable ... kind of like DMT. I was somewhat annoyed that I was feeling the effects this strongly, when I wasn't expecting it. I finally got around to putting on some music, which really helped out. Overall, the plateau lasted for about 45 minutes, with a gradual decline ending at about 3 and a half hours. Now that I know it is a freebase, I may try smoking it next time. I'm guessing that it would be more like DET smoked since it doesn't get broken down as quickly as DMT. Trial 2: Prologue: Finally I get the chance to explore the potential of this compound. I meet up with one of my good buddies around 8:00 p.m. in his apartment. He thinks it would be fun to trip there since he'd just moved in and bought a new LCD HDTV, which, on his budget seems like the biggest waste of money I could think of, but I must admit it is pretty damn impressive. Anyway, we planned on getting started by smoking the freebase out of a common freebase oil pipe. I first placed 20 mg MET in the freebase pipe and we took turns taking small hits. My second hit, I inhaled too fast and burnt my throat pretty badly. I was discouraged as there was still some MET in the pipe after 2 hits each. I wasn't feeling anything other than a faint lightheadedness and my lungs weren't feeling too good so we decided that this wasn't going to work. T+0:00 At 8:30, another friend shows and we all ingest the powder in a capsule (90 mg for me, 75-80 for both of them). The one friend that just showed up is fairly experienced with psychedelics, but has never tried anything other than the traditional mushrooms and LSD. The other was not quite as experienced, but had tripped with me before on mushrooms and 4-AcO-DET. We had all eaten dinner within the last hour and a half so this may have slowed the absorption quite a bit. We spent the next hour smoking bud, waiting, and watching an amazing Discovery Channel documentary in HD about coral reefs. The Ambi-light makes me feel like I'm underwater. I don't think this is due to the drug though. T+1:15 We all have been feeling a distinct body buzz coming on, but it seems like it has started to level off. I feel like stretching and walking around. The room looks a little brighter, and when I close my eyes, there is some mild patterning. Other than a general increase in luminosity and some accentuated reds and greens, there are no impressive OEVs. I propose that we try smoking it again. This time we figure out the proper technique. You need to keep the flame at least an inch from the bottom of the pipe and inhale very, very slowly. I put about 50 mg in the pipe and split it with the friend that showed up later. The other friend wants to be cautious and make sure the oral dose was not just slow to develop. I probably smoke 20 mg and my friend smokes 30 mg (2 hits each). I feel an immediate change in headspace that is reminiscent of DMT. I quickly poured another 50 mg in the pipe and smoked an estimated 30 mg. I was now in MET space, kind of like DMT space except I'd been perhaps a little further out with DMT. When I closed my eyes, I experienced a similar phenomenon to what I usually experience on DMT. Immediately upon shutting my eyelids, 3-dimensional space vanished. I felt like I was a one dimensional point on a vast 2-dimensional plane. There was no concept of in front of or behind for me. There were no entities to speak of, but there were visual cascades of signals from my retina gone haywire. Colorful kaleidoscopic patterns dominated my visual field. I felt amazing. I was able to walk outside on the deck and enjoy the fresh air. The peak probably lasted about 15 minutes, but unlike DMT, the comedown was very gradual. With DMT, I feel like I'm pretty much at baseline after 30 minutes, but MET was much longer acting. We ventured into my friend’s bedroom and listened to some Bob Marley. For some reason I wasn't really feeling it, which is weird because I usually love Bob Marley when I'm tripping. T+2:00 I finally got my friend who hadn't smoked to take a few hits, but he really just couldn't get the technique down. After smoking what was maybe 10 or 15 mg in 3 hits, he kept completely exaggerating how he was feeling. He would not stop talking about how amazing he felt and how he was tripping so hard, but just by the fact that he was talking so clearly and so much, I could tell that he was barely tripping at all. This was slightly irksome. I'm sure he felt great, because the body buzz is very euphoric and somewhat reminiscent of MDMA, but it was obvious acting on his part. We put on some Flaming Lips per my request and I instantly felt 100x better about the situation. T+2:15 At this point I still had some lingering visual effects but I wanted a boost. I added some MET to the pipe to about 50 mg total and we split it. I probably smoked about 20-25 mg, the one friend smoked another 20-25 and again the other smoked maybe 5 mg. I didn't really care anymore, and it was kind of funny trying to get him to do it the right way. For the next 45 minutes I floated through MET space while the music created beautiful morphing tunnels and churning visual displays. The other two went back to watch TV, but I could not bring myself to get up. I felt so utterly peaceful. T+3:00 I finally joined the other two watching TV and we talked for a while. I was feeling a little spacey, but since the second smoked dose was smaller, I was already beginning to return to normality. I think I could have enjoyed the effects at least for another hour, but by redirecting my attention to conversation and the TV, the effects faded more quickly. An hour later I returned home. I spent some time on BL because I had some residual stimulation. I reread my first trip report which was interesting to do under the influence. I could almost transport myself back to that experience. Finally I fell asleep about 3 and a half hours after my last smoked dose at around 2:30. Summary: I enjoyed the experience, but felt that the oral dosing was insufficient, at least at 90 mg. I doubt I will ever try to dose orally again, and insufflation is out of the question. I think IMing and smoking are probably the best routes.

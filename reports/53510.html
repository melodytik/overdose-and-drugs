我第一次尝试的时候我已经被了,并没有注意到什么. 下一次是在一个安静的早晨,独自在家里. 吃完早餐,我了一大块加利福尼亚叶, 烟雾很, 像没有充分干燥的大麻叶. 我一直在吸烟, 到了我的咖啡喝完的时候, 我感觉到一个非常轻松,愉快的声,相当类似于一些哥伦比亚杂草的单击. 只是一种温暖,愉快的感觉, 同时也充满活力. 我和史密斯一起走到公园里, 经过半小时的时间, 我决定在回家后再抽一点. 我先在烤箱里再干了一点, 我抽了两根,但从来没有抽过. 由于困惑, 我放弃了, 接下来的一天我尝试了一些, 和得到同样的温暖,愉快的声半个小时左右. 虽然那天下午又一个对我没有什么好处. 几天后我又试了一口, 在聚会前和朋友一起吸. 他似乎认为这也很奇怪. 现在,我认为这是很奇怪. 很快就到达我们吸了一碗一些平大麻. 我们都惊地发现, 烟雾的高度超过正常, 这是一件好事. 其他: - 加利福尼亚 (279):一般 (1),组合 (3),失去魔力 (34),回顾 / 摘要 (11),不适用 (38)

Very Light, Pleasant Buzz Poppies - California by BrianB Citation:   BrianB. "Very Light, Pleasant Buzz: An Experience with Poppies - California (exp53510)". Erowid.org . Jan 21, 2010. erowid.org/exp/53510 DOSE: repeated smoked Poppies - California (leaves) BODY WEIGHT: 170 lb I first tried it when I was already stoned, and didn't notice anything. The next time was a quiet morning, home alone. After breakfast I rolled a fat joint of dried california poppy leaves and sat at the dining room with a cup of coffee. Lighting up, I first noticed that the smoke was very harsh, akin to marijuana leaves that haven't been sufficiently dried. I kept the smoke down, and by the time my coffee was done so was the joint. I felt a very light, pleasant buzz, quite similar to a single hit of some Columbian weed. Not enough to be disorienting, just a warm, pleasant feeling that was somehow energizing at the same time. I walked down to the park with Smith The Dog and watched him roll in the mud. After about half an hour I felt the buzz going away. I decided to smoke some more when we got back to the house. I dried it a bit more in the oven first, which got rid of most of the harshness. I smoked two more joints, but never got off again. Bewildered, I gave up and went to work. The next day I tried some more, and got the same warm, pleasant buzz for half an hour or so. Another joint that afternoon did nothing for me though. A few days later I tried another joint, sharing it with a friend before a party. I told him about it's odd effect of only working once a day, and he seemed to think it was strange as well. Shortly after arriving we smoked a bowl of some mediocre marijuana. Knowing this smoke from prior experience, we were both surprised to find that we got higher than normal, and that the buzz from the pot lasted much longer than we would normally expect. Since then I've verified the 'once per day' experience, as well as the augmentation of the effects of marijuana. It's a nice thing to have around.

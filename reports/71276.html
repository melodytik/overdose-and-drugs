我和朋友们在E的家聚在一起. 然后我的朋友和我出去, 拿起大约15剂DOC, 他们拿到了一剂DMT. 在DOC中,有5-7毫克溶于一块糖. 我们回去,我给了2个糖块. 这是第一个错误. 我回到家中, 异常快速的到来 (30分钟), 我把它摆脱了,退到另一个房间. 我不记得整个夜晚,所以从现在开始,我将给出关键方面. 我有一个糟糕的旅行. 我听到我的父母尖叫叫着我的名字, 我觉得我看到警察灯到处. 我躺在地上, 感觉好像我在空中的云上漂浮, 波克蒙在我周围跳舞, 我认为我的62200+磅的朋友是我的小狗. 我试着在车库里驾驶一辆停放的汽车, 没有关键, 强力给我酒棒并没有让我昏倒, 只是让我变老, 我第二天早上醒来听到我朋友爸爸讲座, 解释了那晚发生的事情, 其他: 发布时间:2018年8月27日 DOC (357) : 其他 (28), 家庭 (41), 艰难经历 (5), 一般 (1)

Aug 27, 2018. erowid.org/exp/71276 DOSE: 12 mg oral DOC BODY WEIGHT: 130 lb So my trip begins as any other night getting together with friends at “E’s” house. Then my friends and I go out and pick up about 15 doses of DOC and they got one dose of DMT. The DOC had 5-7 mg dissolved into a sugar cube. We go back and I dose on 2 sugar cubes. That was mistake number 1. I get back to the house and have an abnormally fast come up (30 minutes) which takes me completely off guard. I shake it off and retreat to another room. I cant remember the entire night so from here on I will give key aspects. I had a bad trip. I heard my mother and father screaming and crying my name and I thought I saw police lights everywhere. I layed down ad felt as if I was floating on a cloud in the air and pokemon were dancing around me which was pretty cool. I thought my 6’2” 200+ lbs friend was my puppy. I tried driving a parked car in a garage with no key in the ignitition while naked to go get pizza. The force fed me bars which didn’t knock me out just made me senile and tripping which I blacked out for the rest. I woke up the next morning to a lecture from my friends dad an explanation of the night and I went to temple for the first time in a long time still mildly tripping.

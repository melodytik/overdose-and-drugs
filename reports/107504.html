前言: 之前的经验:30多种迷幻药物和诱导药物 (RCs和经典药物), 我以前有几次使用过2C-C, 并且喜欢它独特的放松体验方面. 我发现80-100毫克对我来说是完美的, 我获得了一批新的2C-C (我当时不知道, 由于下面概述的经验,我发送了一个样本进行GC/MS测试,确认其99%+纯度25C-NBOMe). 经验: 我给自己取了80毫克,给我的朋友取了50毫克. 白色粉末被放置在一个凝囊中并口服. 我开始感觉到轻微的效果, 颜色变化, 她一直告诉我, 感觉这会变成比她想要的更激烈的东西. 虽然这种剂量比我预想的要强, 通过药物的镜头欣赏周围的世界. 我看到很强的颜色变化和一切是波浪的. 虽然我的平衡有点失衡, 我朋友的经历正在发展成非常不愉快的事情. 她报告说"在颜色之间看到颜色",身体紧张增加, 我试图让她平静下来, 我为她感到难过, 希望药物能对她有同样的效果. 这似乎是峰值. 我还在享受它, 我们出去坐在车道上, 因为我的朋友报告说, 唯一能避免的方法似乎是外面的新鲜空气. 我看着树叶和树枝跳舞,旋转,微笑, 我仍然感到轻松, 有点高兴. 我朋友的经历仍然非常负面. 她很害怕, 很努力地把它放在一起. 她告诉我她确实有过量服用, 我们在外面呆了好几个小时, 直到效果开始减弱, 偶尔进去喝点点饮料, 我还处于一个放松的状态, 但视觉效果并不像以前那么强烈. 我预计效果会完全消失, 但我的朋友和我仍然看到轻微的痕迹和视觉扭曲. 我们想睡觉, 但不能, 所以我们都服用20毫克的甘诱导睡眠, 第二天没有发现任何副作用. 结论: 由于我经历的异常长时间和强度, 我怀疑我们所遇到的东西是否真的是2C-C, 我怀疑它可能是25C-NBOMe, 如果这种物质是口服活性的, 我和我的朋友可能都会死, 我非常小心地衡量我的剂量并将其倒入囊中, 但我不能100%确定我们所经历的效果是由于囊外的残留物或25C-NBOMe在高剂量时实际上是口服活性. 无论如何, 我不打算再次口服任何大量的NBOMe. 我很高兴我们还活着. 其他: 发布时间:2016年7月5日 25C-NBOMe (540):火车残骸和旅行灾害 (7),小组 (2-9) (17)

Jul 5, 2016. erowid.org/exp/107504 DOSE: 80 mg oral 25C-NBOMe (capsule) BODY WEIGHT: 215 lb Preface: Previous Experience: 30+ psychedelics and entactogens (RCs and classics), you name it I’ve probably done it. I’ve had 2C-C a few times before, and enjoyed the relaxing experience aspect that is unique to it. I also learned that I seemed to require a higher dose than everyone else, and found 80-100mg to be perfect for me, where as most others had strong experiences with just 30-50mg of the same batch. I obtained a new batch of 2C-C (I did not know this at the time, but it was mislabeled, and was actually 25C-NBOMe. Due to the experience outlined below, I sent a sample to be GC/MS tested which confirmed it to be 99%+ pure 25C-NBOMe). Experience: I weighed out 80mg for myself and 50mg for my friend (250lb female, early 40s). The white powder was placed in a gelatin capsule and consumed orally. T+0:30 - I start to feel mild effects, color shifting, and some of the relaxation I was coming to expect. My partner however was very anxious, and kept telling me it felt like it was going to develop into something that was more intense than she wanted. T+1:00 - The psychedelic quality is stronger than I thought it would be at this dose, but I find it extremely enjoyable. I’m relaxed, my feel like my usual muscle tension is simply gone, and I’m enjoying the world around me through the lens of the drug. I’m seeing pretty strong color shifts and everything is wavy. My balance is slightly off, but I am able to walk without too much difficulty. My friend’s experience is developing in to something very unpleasant. She reports “seeing colors between the colors”, increased body tension and is starting to get fearful. I try to calm her, but can’t help smiling due to how much I’m enjoying the experience. I feel bad for her, and wish the drug was doing for her what it was doing for me. T+2:30 - This seems to be the peak. I’m still enjoying the hell out of it, we went outside and sat on the driveway because my friend reported the smells from the bathroom overwhelming her, no matter where in the house we went. The only way to avoid it seemed to be the fresh air outside. I watch the leaves and twigs dance and swirl and smile as I watch the ground shift colors and shape. I still feel relaxed and slightly euphoric. My friend’s experience remains very negative. She's scared and trying hard to hold it together. She tells me she has definitely OD’d, but that she didn’t feel it was life threatening. We remain outside for several hours until the effects start to come down, only going inside occasionally for refreshments and to use the bathroom, which she reports to be an overwhelmingly disgusting experience. T+6:00 - We finally decide to go back inside, the smells are no longer an issue for my friend, I’m still in a relaxed state but the visuals aren’t as intense as they were. T+8:00 - I expected the effects to be completely gone as this point, but my friend and I are still seeing mild trails and visual distortions. We would like to sleep, but can’t, so we both take 20mg of diazepam to induce sleep, which effectively knocks us both out until the next morning. No after effects were noted the next day. Conclusion: Due to the unusually long duration and intensity of my experience, I questioned if what we had was actually 2C-C, and decided to have it tested. I suspected it may have in fact been 25C-NBOMe due a previous experience with 25C; and it turns out I was right. If this substance was orally active, my friend and I would possibly both be dead, and I’m thankful the experience was as mild as it was (despite being pretty strong). I am very careful when weighing out my doses and pouring them in to capsules, but I cannot be 100% certain that the effects we experienced were due to residue outside of the capsule or that 25C-NBOMe is actually orally active at high doses. Either way, I do not intend to take any large quantity of any NBOMe orally ever again. Doing so is dangerous, and I’m thankful that we are both still alive.

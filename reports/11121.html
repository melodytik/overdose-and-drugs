中文译文 抑制剂

对于所有可能正在经历GHB成的人来说, 我在过去两年半的时间里使用了这款产品, 我开始增加剂量, 每3小时增加30毫升, 这对我来说是一个很长的等待时间. 我试过冷火, 但焦虑和恐慌发作和缺乏集中度是太难以忍受的. 最后我去了康复中心, 在那里我接受了子治疗 (librium 50 mg), 似乎是最好的, 每4到6小时, 第二天我的剂量减少了一半. 戒断的最大危险之一是高血压. 第二天我感觉好了很多. 在第三天,他变成了另一个人. 两年后,我被同一个蛇咬伤, GHB有很强的吸引力. 医生也给我开了利布里和克洛尼丁的处方 (我绝对不是在医生监督下这样做的建议!!!). 对你的医生说实话. 我知道我一直在拼命寻找有关它的信息. 像任何药物滥用一样, 当生活开始围绕着它旋转时, 我之前曾多次尝试过这种药物. 只要记住,如果你确实服用它,不要太频繁 (永远不要与酒精或其他药物混合) 并总是休息2或3天以达到多巴胺水平. 祝你在康复过程中一切顺利. 时间表: 11121 GHB (25),GBL (89):旅行后的问题 (8),成和习惯 (10),各种 (28)

Nov 9, 2005. erowid.org/exp/11121 DOSE: repeated oral GHB repeated oral GBL BODY WEIGHT: 235 lb For all who may be experiencing GHB addiction there is hope! I had used the product over the past 2.5 years with no problems. After a few changes at work and added stress I started to increase my dosage to about 30 ML every 3 hours and that was a long wait time for me. I tried cold turkey, but the anxiety and panic attacks and lack of concentration where too unbearable. Finally I went to a Rehab center where I was treated with benzo (librium 50 MG) which seems to be the best, every 4 to 6 hours and clonodine .2 (for high blood pressure) at the same interval. My dosage was reduced to half of that the second day. One of the biggest dangers of withdrawal is very high blood pressure. The second day I felt much better. The third day a was a different man. Well 2 years later I was bitten by the same snake, GHB has a strong attraction. This time I was smarter, I monitored my blood pressure and had my doctor prescribe the librium and clonidine (I'm by no means suggesting to do this without doctor supervision!!!). Be honest with your doctor. There is hope I know I was desperately seeking information on it. GHB has had both positive and negative impacts in my life, like any substance abuse when life begins to revolve around it THERE'S A PROBLEM. GHB definitely helped me stop drinking, something I tried many times before. Just remember if you do take it, don't do it too often (never mix alcohol or other drugs with it) and always take 2 or 3 days off to level of the dopamine level. Wish you all the best of luck with recovery.

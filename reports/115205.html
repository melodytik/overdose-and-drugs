I expected a feeling somewhat similar to GHB. Starting around 3pm I felt the effects of GHB creeping up on me. It left me with an unpleasant feeling which is indescribable and an entire day wasted the next day to a hangover that needed to be treated with heavy sedatives oxycodone and lorazepam (5mg). All in all, I read the experiences and no one had warned me of the hangover, I expected to be able to sleep it off. I still now am dealing with the side effects. This drug is useful for medical reasons but I do not think it has much recreational use. Just a warning from a very experienced pill user.

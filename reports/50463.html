咖啡因已经成为我最喜欢的药物. 我已经戒了尼古丁. 我完全避免使用可卡因, 我有时会服用阿德拉的形式的安非他胺, 但一次只服用5毫克或更少. 我对兴奋剂很敏感,但我喜欢它们的作用. 在我看来,咖啡因是最好的. 但其良好的效果与相对温和的性质相结合, 我也喜欢好咖啡的味道! 我很少一次喝超过一杯普通咖啡. 这对我来说肯定足够了. 音乐是我的生计, 我觉得音乐能刺激身体和创造力, 我喜欢在表演时喝一杯咖啡. 这有助于我的协调和专注, 我听到音乐更好. 我可以在咖啡因下更好地玩. 我可以更熟练地阅读音乐. 这种剂量不会有任何恶性衰退. 没有什么可恶的. 当主要效应结束时, 它就会消失. 我可以在舞台上喝一杯咖啡, 我可以喝它,同时我教课给孩子,没有人关心. 他们的父母通常会在看到我拿着咖啡时, 评论他们对咖啡因的欣赏. 我从来没有退出过. 我常常在没有喝咖啡因的情况下度过几个星期或几个月. 没有头痛或其他症状. 我发现咖啡因是我真正想要的刺激剂. 药物安非他命剂量很低有时是更好的选择 (长途驾驶,全夜学习,做非常乏味的工作), 其他: 咖啡因 (11),咖啡 (173):音乐讨论 (22),回顾 / 摘要 (11),不适用 (38)

Ode to Caffeine Caffeine (Coffee) by Merle Citation:   Merle. "Ode to Caffeine: An Experience with Caffeine (Coffee) (exp50463)". Erowid.org . Apr 30, 2008. erowid.org/exp/50463 DOSE: repeated oral Coffee (liquid) BODY WEIGHT: 130 lb Caffeine has become my favorite drug. I've had significant experience with other stimulants: nicotine, cocaine, various amphetamines, ephedrine, and free-form amino acids like acetyl l-carnitine, l-tyrosine, etc. I've quit nicotine. I avoid cocaine entirely and have for years. Sometimes I take amphetamines in the form of Adderall, but only 5 mg or less at once. I'm sensitive to stimulants yet I enjoy their effects. In my opinion, caffeine is really the best of the bunch. Its legality certainly plays into my qualification, but its good effect combined with its relatively benign nature are what really make it so nice. I enjoy the taste of a good latte, too! I rarely drink more than a regular cup of coffee at once. That's certainly enough for me. I play music for a living, and I find it to be stimulating both physically and creatively, unlike amphetamine which is only stimulating physically. I prefer to just sip on a coffee while I perform. It helps my coordination and concentration and I hear the music better. I can play by ear better on caffeine. I can read music more proficiently. And there's no nasty comedown at this kind of dose. There's really nothing nasty about it at all. It just fades away when the primary effect is over. I can drink a cup of coffee up on stage and nobody thinks twice. I can drink it while I teach lessons to children and nobody cares. Their parents usually comment on their own appreciation for caffeine when they see me with my coffee. I've never had withdrawal. I've often gone weeks or months without consuming caffeine. Never a headache or other symptom. All in all, after having tried the harder stuff, I find caffeine to be really all I want in the stimulant department. Pharmaceutical amphetamines in very low doses are occasionally a better bet (for long driving trips, all-night studying, doing really tedious work) but I rarely need all that stimulation.

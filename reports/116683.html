Sep 29, 2022. erowid.org/exp/116683 DOSE: oral Cannabis (extract) 20 mg oral 4-HO-PIPT BODY WEIGHT: 85 kg Piprocin, A Review One of my favourite rc suppliers recently started to carry piprocin and I always wanted to to try it. Seeing as there are no trip reports out there yet I decided to do a brief overview of the effects I felt in case anyone else wants to try this shit. The n propyl tryptamines are my favourite drugs. DPT, deprocin, meprocin are all just incredible drugs, they have a magical and mildly dissociative feeling to them that I love and absolutely beautiful visuals. The n isopropyl tryptamines also have a special place in my heart because of their bodyloads and auditory effects, miprocin in particular I have always absolutely loved. I'm not sure if that helps this story or review but my brain is fried from the other night when I finally got my piprocin and took 20mg. The onset was short, maybe 20 minutes before I noticed the first alerts, a couple of hours earlier I had taken 25mg THC and 25mg CBD orally so it was hard to tell during the come up if the mild visual effects were from the drug kicking in or the weed. The visuals were the first part I noticed but at this dose were very dulled during he entirety of the experience, rather than explicit geometry everything just looked much sharper, more detailed, but I had trouble with focussing on anything, I felt like it was easier to do things by touch than by sight and during some points of the experience I would close my eyes while doing things because it felt more natural, this is something I often find myself doing on deprocin but normally there are stronger visuals at that point. The bodyload was intense and brilliant, just a clear vibratory energy flowing through me, it was extremely numbing too. What made it really amazing though was how it interacted with the headspace, I'm not sure there was much euphoria in the headspace until I start touching things. I just want to touch, feel and stroke everything to feel the texture. If I hug something or someone I will melt, it's an insanely euphoric experience, everything's softer than anything I've felt sober and I love everyone so much. I think that was the best part of the experience, pure tactile euphoria. The headspace was, like the visuals, quite mild. I felt something but I'm not sure what, most of the euphoria came from the bodyload and while that was intense my thoughts and my way of thinking felt quite sober. I'm not sure if it was the weed or the piprocin but I was very tired the whole time and went to lay down in bed, I was surprised because I had expected the drug to be quite stimulating, even during the experience it was like with psilohuasca, I feel like I want to run around and be active but my body just wants to lay down. Regardless of the weak visuals and headspace at this dose it was a really great time. I think I started to come down about 5 hours in which I think is a good amount of time for a drug like that. Next time I'll try piprocin at 30mg and hopefully see some nice visuals.

中文译文 抑制剂

里斯佩里并不是为了试图吸毒或旅行. 这主要是自杀企图, 药物是我的处方的一部分 怀疑双极性障碍和偏执. 幸运的是/不幸的是, 我决定把我带来的药丸[20~30克]全部吞下来. 虽然我喝了酒, 但当时我的协调能力还很好, 事情开始变得糟糕. 首先我决定躺下,让药物抓住我, 坚定地认为我会失去意识并死亡. 实际上,我的心开始跳得很快, 我脑袋里发出惊人的感觉! 这种感觉难以形容. 这就像抑郁症, 这不是悲伤,而是完全消除了思想和情感. 就像灵魂被撕裂一样. 我开始失去意识控制 - - 由于某种原因, [记忆会对整个回家的旅程保持空白,这是大约1-2英里步行] 我的整个身体都在努力保持意识. 我一直在跌倒,在膝盖上崩. 如果从外面看, 我可能会看起来像我喝醉了, 我觉得我的身体正在被压碎和沉重. 这种无灵魂的感觉再次蔓延到我们身上. 我身体疼痛,我去所,好像想吐,但没有. 这几乎像是一种本能反应以某种方式感觉更好, 也许吐是方法. 我倒在地板上, 头撞在床旁的抽上. 有一个严重的伤口和血液, 但我后来发现没有骨折. 我爬上床,然后昏倒了. 我觉得自己比以往任何时候都更可怕. 我无法正义地说出这感觉有多糟糕. 曾经的空虚感现在变成了一种强烈而真实的抑郁, 房间似乎明亮但黑暗,我的身体疼痛. 这是一个很大的混乱. 我听到隔壁房间的同伴正在打包回家. 我试图站起来去寻求帮助, 我心跳和血压都很高, 喉,肺,心脏和头部的击是无法忍受的. 喉很干, 感觉好像要窒息, [这种经历持续了16个小时,症状几乎没有改变 - - 想昏倒,但由于"感觉"的强度而无法. ] 星期四下午三点 我终于能够从床上站起来, 但运动是非常困难, 然而, 我去所, 终于决定打电话给, 她打电话给紧急服务. 我不想让其他同居知道我试图这样做, 所以我爬下楼, 由于心率和血压高得很危险, 星期四 (医院) 在医院, 一种奇怪的症状开始出现, 整身, 完全把我锁定在医院的推车上, 医生似乎在治疗它之前花了很长时间, 因为我显然在痛苦和哭泣 - - 没有水, 因为我还干燥. 我背部好像快要破裂, 双臂也很糟糕, 我可能看起来像个脑部有严重疾病的人. 最终我得到了一种镇静剂, 医生给了我另外两次注射, 大约10分钟后, 后果: 这让我经历了地狱. 我绝不会向我最坏的敌人也许这件事. 经历给我的背部和肩膀留下了巨大的情感伤痕, 这种无灵的,黑暗的感觉是可怕的. 我从来没有经历过真正的死亡 - - 缺乏生命. 我觉得不好只是打字这个. 其他: 药品 - 利斯佩里 (253):旅行后的问题 (8),火车事故和旅行灾难 (7),医院 (36)

Attempted Suicide Risperidone & Alcohol by Ashiro Citation:   Ashiro. "Attempted Suicide: An Experience with Risperidone & Alcohol (exp42901)". Erowid.org . Sep 28, 2006. erowid.org/exp/42901 DOSE: 25 g oral Pharms - Risperidone (pill / tablet) 300 ml oral Alcohol - Hard (liquid) BODY WEIGHT: 164 lb The Risperidone wasn't taken as an attempt to get high or trip. It was primarily taken as a suicide attempt and the medication was part of my prescription for suspected bi-polar disorder and paranoia. Fortunately/unfortunately it failed and the experience was horrific. T:00:00 Wednesday After the vodka had been drunk over about 2hrs previously and sat outside under a tree I decided to swallow all of the pills I'd brought with me [20~30g]. At that time - although having drunk - I had a reasonable level of coordination and my memory of up to that point is fine. T:00:25 Wednesday (Approx) [Beginning] Things begin to go wrong. At first I'd decided to lie down and let the drugs take hold with the firm idea that I'd simply slip out of conciousness and die. However, my heart began beating so hard and palapatating it actually knocked some sense into me. My head was pounding and the feeling!! The feeling is difficult to describe. It was like depression but different in some way. It wasn't a sadness but a complete removal of thought and emotion. Like the soul had been torn out. I began losing conscious control - and for some reason had got up and was walking towards home... [Memory goes blank for the entire journey home which is about 1-2miles on foot] T:01:30 Wednesday (Guess) [Felt like Death] My whole body is struggling to stay conscious. I keep falling over and collapsing at the knees. It feels like I'm literally fighting an anasthetic or sedative not an antipsychotic - if seen from the outside I probably would have looked like I was drunk but in pain and 'compressed' from the outside. It felt as if my body was being crushed and weighed down. Again there's the dark soul-less feeling all over. My body ached and I went to the toilet as if I wanted to vomit but didnt. It was almost like an instinctive reaction to feel better in some way and maybe vomiting was the way. When I went back to my room I passed out and collapsed to the floor banging my head badly on the drawers next to my bed. There was a bad cut and blood but I was later to find no fracture. I crawl onto my bed and pass out. T:08:30 Wednesday (Approx) [Hell] I wake feeling more horrendous than I have ever felt in all my life. I can't put do justice to how bad it felt. What was an empty feeling before now became an intense and very real depression, dark, evil compressed. The room seemed bright yet dark, my body ached. There was a lot of confusion. Voices phased in and out as I heard my house mate in the next room packing to go home. I was trying to get up to go for help but couldn't move. My heart rate and blood pressure were so high it seemed to paralyse me. The hammering in my throat, lungs heart and head was unbearable. My throat was so dry it felt as if I was going to suffocate but I couldn't move to get the water from my side. [A further 16 hours of this experience with little change in the symptoms - wishing to pass out but unable due to the intensity of the 'feelings'] T:00:00 Thursday [Paramedic called] I was finally able to get out of bed but movement was immensely difficult, however the mind had returned to a state of deadness. I went to the toilet and finally decided to call my mum and tell her what happened. She called the emergency services. I didn't want the remaining housemates to know I'd attempted this so I crawled downstairs and lay outside on the front path. I was taken to the hospital immediately because my heart rate and BP were dangerously high. T:00:45 Thursday [Hospital] In the hospital a strange symptom began to occur - especially considering this was 24hrs after the dose. An all over body spasm that completely locked me up into a horribly contorted shape on the hospital trolley. The doctor seemed to take an age before treating it as I was obviously in intense pain and was crying heavily - without tears as I was still dry. My back feels as if its about to snap and my arms are screwed up and I was completely unable to move. I probably looked like someone with a serious brain disorder. Eventually I am administered a sedative which doesn't work. They then give me another 2 shots and after about 10 mins I begin to relax. Aftermath: It put me through hell and back. I would never EVER wish this even on my worst enemy. The experience has left a significant emotional scar and also a physical shock feeling in the nerves through my back and shoulders. The soul-less, dark feeling was horrendous. It was the closest thing to REAL death I have ever experienced - a lack of life. I feel bad just typing this.

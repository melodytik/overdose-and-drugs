平均每隔两周末一次, 有时会四五天的狂欢. 我从来不是一个没有经验的用户, 我知道要寻找什么,我知道它是什么感觉, 上个周末,星期五我和几个朋友去了朋友家. 我们打算休息一晚,喝酒. 我们都接受了, 因为我们都不想睡觉. 我经销商来了,他的头和带着他20个药丸,他和他 - 粉红色的女士,我有他们之前,速度炸弹, 我们都吃了第一杯, 我开始感到非常性欲, 这是不寻常的, 因为我最后一次感觉到这样的药是,好吧,大约一年半前. 然后它慢慢地出现,然后突然我完全被粉碎了, 我决定这还不够,所以我又打了个. 再一次,它慢慢地出现了,然后突然你被打碎了. 这就像是完美的药丸; 我同时感到快速,充满爱和快乐. 感觉就像一个糖果翻转与速度的一条线在侧面. 我们都去了休息室跳舞. 我记得我说过一些'这些药丸会很适合Q舞', 如果我继续跳舞, 我可能会早点陷入麻烦. 我停止了,因为我感到恶心. 我们都回到外面, 现在我吃了3个. 我感觉很好, 只是我开始注意到我的心跳, 我忽略了它. 我们下去了游泳池房子,我坐在阳光下大约2秒钟之前我开始过热非常严重. 我注意到我的同伴躺在上, 他说如果他移动他会通过,所以我离开他, 我的心脏仍然以百万英里/小时的速度,然而我很平静,所以有什么不对. 我开始太了. 我朋友在我旁边说话, 我几乎听不到他. 一切开始变得非常安静,好像没有什么是真实的. 我没有想到任何事,我的脑海完全空白,没有一个想法会让我头跳动. 我很快就醒来了,因为躺在地上的朋友说他无法呼吸. 我们没有冒险,所以我们自己去医院. 另一个朋友从车里跳出来跑到医生那里, 说他也无法呼吸. 两名同事被接纳并被监视了大约10个小时. 在此期间,至少有8个小时他们的心率超过120次. 我坐在外面,并没有检查,担心我的父亲会发现. 我在医院,所以如果有严重问题, 我一直有恐慌发作. 我让救护人员检查我, 她说我应该冷静下来, 如果你曾经有过, 你会知道它有多可怕. 你觉得自己在,手,头疯狂, 至少在我的情况下, 就像有人在我的眼睛前面放着闪光灯, 很有趣的是,我从来没有过恐慌发作,我不是一个焦虑的人, 最小的东西会引发一个巨大的恐慌. 我一直都知道,但很高兴知道. 我们很幸运. 我们每人吃了3次PMA, 这可能会更糟. 如果我用这些去Q-Dance, 我很可能会死. 这是我一生中最可怕的经历. 我再也不会服用狂喜药了. 这太危险了,你不知道你得到什么. 其他: PMA (126):艰难的经历 (5),健康问题 (27),不好的旅行 (6),小组 (2-9) (17)

First and Last Experience with PMA PMA (4-methoxy-amphetamine) by Daniel Citation:   Daniel. "First and Last Experience with PMA: An Experience with PMA (4-methoxy-amphetamine) (exp69903)". Jul 1, 2009. erowid.org/exp/69903 DOSE: 3 tablets oral PMA (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 75 kg I had been taking ecstasy for over a year and a half on a regular basis, on average every second weekend with the occasional 4-5 day binges. I was by no means an inexperienced user, I liked to get on it and when I did I went hard. Id had both MDMA and speed countless times, I knew what to look for and I knew how it felt, this experience however was like nothing I'd had ever before. Last weekend, on Friday I went round to my mates place with a couple of friends. We were planning to have a night off and just drink. Around 4am one of my mates suggested we get pills and we all went with it as none of us really felt like sleeping. My dealer came round, off his head and bringing with him 20 of the pills he was on, with him - Pink Ladies, Id had them before, speed bombs, but considering he was completely wasted we decided to take the chance. We all took our first one and not much happened for a while. I started to feel REALLY horny, which was unusual because the last time I felt like that off a pill was, well, about a year and a half ago. It then came up slowly, then all of a sudden I was completely smashed, like out of no where. I decided that wasnt enough so I poped a second one. Again, it came up slowly then all of a sudden you're smashed. This was like the perfect pill; I felt speedy, lovey and trippy all at the same time. It felt like Id candy flipped with a line of speed on the side. We all went into the lounge room to dance. I remember saying something like 'These pills would be perfect for Q-Dance', how wrong was I. PMA pills, had I kept dancing I might have been in trouble a lot sooner. I stopped tho because I felt sick. We all went back outside where we took 1 more pill each. Now Id munched 3. I felt alright except I started to notice my heart was racing, I ignored it. We went down to the pool house, I was sitting in the sun for about 2 seconds before I started to overheat really badly. I noticed my mate lieing down on the tiles he said if he moved he would through up, so I left him but still kept an eye on him as I started to notice some weird things happening to me. My heart was still going a million miles an hour, how ever I was calm, so something wasnt right. I started to trip balls way too hard. My mate was next to me talking and I could hardly hear him. Everything started to go really quiet, like nothing was real. I couldnt think of anything, my mind was completely blank, not a thought going threw my head. I snapped out of it quickly when my mate lieing on the ground said he couldnt breath. We werent taking any chances so we got ourselfs to the hospital. We got there, another one of my mates jumped out of the car and ran to the paramedic, saying he also couldnt breath. 2 of my mates got admitted in and monitored for around 10 hrs. During that time, atleast 8 hrs their heart rates were above 120 bpm. I sat outside, and didnt check in, fearing my dad would find out. I mean, I was at the hospital anyway so if anything went seriously wrong Id be fine. The whole time I was having panic attacks. I got the paramedic to check me out and she said to just calm down, and it would be fine. If you've ever had one you'll know how scary it can be. You feel like you're chocking, your hands go numb, you get insanely dizzy and, in my case at least it was like someone had a strobe light in front of my eyes, couldnt see anything. The funny thing is, Ive never had a panic attack, Im not an anxious person, at all, the opposite accually, yet the PMA made me like that. The slightest thing would set off a massive panic attack. When we got out we tested the pills, they came up as PMA, which I knew all along but it was nice to know. We got lucky. We took 3 PMA smacks each and came out kickin. It could have been a lot worse. If I went to Q-Dance on these I most likely would be dead. It was the scariest experience of my life. Im never taking ecstasy again. Its too risky, you dont know what you're getting.

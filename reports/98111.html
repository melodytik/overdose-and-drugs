It's been about 6 months since I've used stimulants regularly, so my tolerance is at baseline. In terms of other drugs I've done, I am very experienced with opiates, and have done other drugs like benzodiazepines, DXM, nitrous oxide, mushrooms, and a billion others I can't seem to remember right now. The point is this: I am pretty experienced with mind altering chemicals and psychoactives, and consider myself a 'psychonaut.' On a side note, I recently quit 3 years of suboxone. This was a week and a half ago. So my body may be more sensitive to the effects. The Experience: (note: I took 100-1500mg of L-Tyrosine thoughout) Prop trial 1 I picked up a single benzedrex inhaler around 4pm on a Sunday afternoon. I have read quite a bit about it, so I was familiar with what to do; I broke open the container and cut up the cotton rod into 5 or 6 pieces--then, I placed them into gel caps. I consumed them all on an empty stomach. 00:45 I started feeling a noticeable increase in heart rate, and I feel myself becoming more mentally 'clear.' It reminds me of coming up on other stimulants, where I start to feel an increased clarity in my mental ability. I have a strong desire to keep playing video games even though I know I should get to working on my music. This is a typical effect of stimulants for me--I tend to get drawn into stuff I'd otherwise find boring after an extended amount of time. 1:00 Stimulation in hear rate is less pronounced. My hands are pretty cold and clammy. Was hoping for stimulation at this point, but perhaps its still coming on. I believe I have zero tolerance for stimulants right now, so it's strange I'm not feeling more of it than I am. On top of that, I recently quit opiates, so my sensitivity to uppers should be heightened. 1:40 I decided to join my Dad for a walk. I've been at home for a majority of the fall doing an internship while I take a semester off from college. When he said he was going on a walk I felt a desire to join him (plus the exercise would be good), so I joined him. 1:45 while walking I feel an increased desire to talk, and we converse about some interesting topics. We talked about everything and anything that's been going on. We talked about my work, which is nice because he has a background in it and was able to give me suggestions about projects I have coming up. 2:30 Back home now. I've been home for about 20 minutes now. There was no increase in stimulation after the initial come up. The buzz, albeit mild, is reminiscent of the amphetamines found in the medications I used to take. It feels less like Ritalin and more like Adderall, which isn't a surprise since propylhexedrine is chemically related to methamphetamine (according to the web). 2:35 Decided to take 1000mg of L-Tyrosine to see if I can increase the effects. I've decided that it would probably be best for me to take 2 benzedrex rods next time. My overall impression from both experiences is this: Propylhexedrine seems to be a decent stimulant compared to other traditional stims (at least for me). Positives are the mood boost, increased chattiness, motivation increase, and the energizing effect. I think the biggest plus is that it's OTC in the USA. However, for $7 a rod, I didn't find it all that worth it, which I blame on the fact that I'm experienced with stims in high doses. Yet even with the price, I will not hesitate to use it again in the future for something like a concert that I can't find other stims for.

Good to Very good physical condition (Biking 1h every day, 2-3 hours a week of hard physical exercise), and sometimes Yoga. Vegan. Previous experiences include MDMA, MDAI, 4FA, Pentedrone, Buphedrone, Methoxetamine, N02, LSA (Morning glory & Hawaian woodrose), LSD, cocaine, speed, oral cannabis, Ayahuasca, poppers, salvia. Very experimented in Mushrooms (probably took almost 100 doses in my life). Never smoke. And of course, alcohol, although I never drink more than a few glasses anymore, and less than once a week. Never bad-tripped in my life. My mostly-healthy lifestyle because It helps with the sensitivity so I can enjoy drugs like a beginner. Before anything, apologies for my bad English, which is not my native language. This is my first report, and I am excited to share my experiences for promoting safe drug use. I was supposed to receive some new psychedelic in my courier this morning, but since I didn’t get any, I decided I was going to try some 4P granules. I have nothing to do all afternoon, except maybe a feminist conference later, if I feel like it. I can eventually go out, since I only drive a bike and I know how to be careful. Dose: 207 mg 4P (Cristallized Pentedrone/4-MEC). Proportions are 50% 4-MEC, 20% Pentedrone, 30% sugar/coloring. Diluted in a glass of water (weighted precisely). The little blue balls completely disappear in the water after one minute of dilution. It is worth noticing that I took absolutely nothing since 10 days. Dosage seems to be 100-200 mg oral. I decide to go over the top a bit for I am disappointed I would not get my psychedelic today. Taste bad, as always with research chemicals. 11h35 Am = T0. Before that, ate a piece of pie at 10 Am and drank like 6-8 coffees stevia-flavored (no sugar). T+10 min: First signs of physical change (I “feel my body” more. For me, that’s how it always starts with almost every drugs). T+15 : The need for music feel pressing. I load a good playlist. Starts to feel good. T+25 : Jaws starts to grind a little. But very easy to control. T+30 : Start to feel the empathogenic effects. T+30 : Start to feel the empathogenic effects. T+45 : Start to feel very energetic. Which is not an easy thing since I had an intense workout the day before. Feeling good and optimistic. T+50 : Start to feel the need to pee, as usual with stimulants. Also, when I drink so much coffee. Starts to feel a “clear” state of mind. At this point, feeling very good. Listening to some dubstep. T+1h : Slight anxiety feeling. Changed music. Stayed for a min, then left. T+2h : Have been feeling very good until now. Flatmates are now violently arguing. I try to calm them down a bit, but quickly give up. After this, I went to discuss with both of them and apparently helped them both a lot. It is worth noticing that I would have never wanted to do this if I hadn’t been uninhibited by the drug. T+2h 30: Time is around 14h. Although the weather is very bad, I miraculously find the motivation to go to the conference/debate. 20 min biking. Feeling (not surprisingly) very dry, but I had prepared a full bottle of water. T+3h to T+5: It seems that the drug is helping me to get involved in the conference. Although I stay tense, I’m able to participate. T+5h : Following a friend to his home for some tea. Starting to feel the comedown. The tension in my back hurts a little. As soon as I arrive I do some stretching and it’s better. I eat some Chinese bread so I can accelerate the comedown. T+6h : Feeling now very tired, and a bit anxious. Decide to go back home on bike (Don’t want to bike, but I wanted to get home) T+6h30 Arrived at home. The comedown is ending, feeling better, still a little anxious. Conclusions: Even though I started a day without motivation, the drug helped me to feel good and do stuff. The empathogenic effects helped me to help my flatmates. The drug didn’t alter my physical abilities and gave me energy. Regular spikes of anxiety, but I get this a lot on mood enhancers, since I’m often afraid of the comedown. The comedown isn’t very good but could be worse. All in one, this drug helped me get the best out of my day without impairing anything. It is worth noticing that I stayed tense during the 2-3h comedown. The need for redose was very strong. It is probably a very good drug in a party or social context. The interest as a recreational is very limited.

中文译文 致幻剂

我很想试试. 现在,我已经知道, 我是一名20多岁的男性, 曾经使用过多种类型的迷幻药, 我遇到了人生的十字路口, 具体来说是从第七个,冠状轮. 我有一个朋友,我认识他十多年了,他也经历过迷幻药作为我的"生命线到现实" 将10毫克的4HO- DiPT与10毫克的5- Meo- DiPT混合到凝盖中. 注意:下列时间是近似的,大约是+/-10分钟. 我没有吃任何食物或饮料 (除了水) 8 小时. 我躺在沙发上,听着一些非常好的科技音乐. T+1:30 - 开始感觉时间变得扭曲和慢,变得难以移动,因为我的感知严重扭曲 (当我移动时). 我可以"看到"尘埃在空气中漂浮; 我认为这是因为此时的时间真的很慢. 对于我来说,这似乎是1分钟的"真实时间". 我几年前读了一本名为"用户幻觉"的书, 作者是Tor Nrretranders, 这种药物组合似乎解开了带宽问题, 让我看到那些在物理上存在但被我的大脑丢弃的东西. 我可以处理这些信息而不会被压倒. 几乎达到顶峰,难以看到任何类似于现实的东西 - - OEV是强烈的. 我看着我的手表, 秒针似乎移动非常非常缓慢. 在我的脑海中,1秒等于10秒. 现在我终于明白了现实, 我喝了10毫克的迪亚兹帕姆, 我完全脱离现实 (++++在舒尔金的尺度上), OEV真的很惊人, 我已经失去了深度感知, 我现在看到的似乎是一个四维立方体, 螺旋式旋转, 似乎扩展到我的整个视野, 我似乎能够在潜意识中控制这种行为一段时间... 后来,我开始听到一个声音... 我自己的声音, 它告诉我一切都是一个,而一个是一切 (哈哈) 在这个时候, 不管出于什么原因, 我意识到我可能已经死了, 这种感觉消失, 完全取代了快乐, 我突然跟随着扩张和收缩的模式... 颜色变化很大, 我认为...但Tesseract总是存在,几乎作为我的指南. 现在我正处于一种高兴和快乐的浪潮中, 这几乎就像我骑着一波纯粹的能量. 整个峰值持续了大约一个半小时. 尽管在经验的高峰期, 人类对时间的概念完全消失了. 我能够听到和看到我的朋友, 我能够坐直立. 我可以和她说话, 但时间仍有轻微的扭曲, T+5:40 - 主要的动几乎消失了,语音扭曲和时间扭曲也消失了,尽管仍有轻微的空间扭曲. 我已经饿了,因为我没有吃东西14个小时了. 我走过街道去7-11, 买一些食物... 现在一切都恢复正常, 我准备睡觉了. 我没有睡过两个小时, 因为我无法停止思考这次经历和我所看到的... 我觉得自己有新鲜感,充满活力. 我醒来时感觉清醒, 并且对别人真诚善意. 我必须抓住每一天, 真正生活和与地球母亲 (盖亚) 保持联系. 这次旅行的奇特之处在于音乐似乎是不变的, 如果我对时间的感知被扭曲, 音乐也会被扭曲. 我根本无法解释. 其他: 5-MeO-DiPT (57),4-HO-DiPT (281):单独使用 (16),组合使用 (3)

The Tesseract 4-Ho-DiPT, 5-MeO-DiPT & Diazepam by trancemaster Citation:   trancemaster. "The Tesseract: An Experience with 4-Ho-DiPT, 5-MeO-DiPT & Diazepam (exp38709)". Erowid.org . Feb 2, 2005. erowid.org/exp/38709 DOSE: T+ 0:59 10 mg oral 4-HO-DiPT (capsule) T+ 0:59 10 mg oral 5-MeO-DiPT (capsule) T+ 2:00 10 mg oral Pharms - Diazepam (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 147 lb Having read Shulgin's THiKAL notes on 4HO-DiPT and 5-Meo-DiPT potential interactions, I was curious to try it. I'm a male in my mid-20s and I've had experience with many types of psychedelics and was looking forward to reaching ++++. I had hit a crossroads in my life and was seeking guidance from within. Specifically from the Seventh, Crown Chakra. I had a friend, whom I've known for over ten years, who also has experience with psychedelics act as my 'life line to reality' 10mg of 4HO-DiPT was mixed with 10mg of 5-Meo-DiPT into a gel cap. Note: the following times are approximate, +/-10 minutes or so. T+0:00 - down the hatch w/ a glass of Cranberry juice. I had not ingested any food or drink (except water) for 8 hours. T+0:50 - starting feel space becoming distorted. (seemed to take longer to feel the effects), No real feeling of nausea, I am lying on the couch, listening to some very good techno. T+1:30 - starting to feel time becoming distorted and slower, becoming difficult to move around as my perception is seriously distorted (when I move). T+1:45 - my sensory perception is magnified by several hundred times: I am able to 'see' dust floating around in the air; I attribute it to the fact that at this point time is really slow. What seems to be 1 minute of 'real time' feels like 5 minutes to me. I read a book a few years ago entitled 'The User Illusion' by Tor Nrretranders, which basically said that the human brain receives lots of sensory input (specifically visually) and discards a lot of the information (since our brain is locked into a 'narrow bandwidth' mode). This drug combination seems to unlock that bandwidth problem, allowing me to see things that are physically there but are discarded by my brain. Also given that it seems to slow down the perception of time, I am able to process that information without becoming overwhelmed. T+2:00 - nearly peaking, difficulty seeing anything resembling reality - OEVs are intense. I look at my clock and the second hand seems to move very very slowly. 1 second = 10 seconds in my mind. At this point with my last grasp on reality, I down 10mg of Diazepam with a swig of water. T+2:10 - I am completely disconnected from reality (++++ on Shulgin's scale), the OEVs are truly amazing and mesmerizing and I find it difficult to close my eyes. At this point I have lost all sense of depth perception and my 3D reality has disappeared. At this point I am seeing what looks like to me to be a Tesseract (4-Dimensional cube, which is spiraling in a never-ending tail, which seems to expand to fill my entire vision, or collapse into nothing ness). I seem to be able to subconsciously control this behavior for a while... After a time, though, I start to hear a voice... my own, which is explaining to me the secret to existence and the purpose of being. It told me that everything is one, and one is everything (huh?) At this point, for whatever reason, I realize that I might be dead, though that feeling lasted only briefly. The feeling passes and is replaced with complete happiness and suddenly I am following the expanding and contracting patterns... the color shifts dramatically and almost becomes a bright light at one point (I surely must be dead! I thought)... but the Tesseract is ever present, almost acting as my guide. Now I am riding a wave of euphoria and pleasure which made me orgasm continuously for sometime. It was almost like I was riding a wave of pure energy. The entire peak lasted approximately an hour and a half. Though, during the peak experience all human notions of time disappeared completely. T+3:45 - reality slow creeps back in, and I am able to hear and see my friend, and I am able to sit upright. I am able to talk to her but there is still a slight time distortion and it is unnerving when I hear my own voice being distorted. T+5:40 - Major trippiness is almost gone and voice distortion and time distortion are gone as well, though there is slight space distortion still. I'm hungry as hell from not eaten for almost 14 hours. I walk across the street to the 7-11 to get some food... T+6:30 - Everything is back to normal, and I'm ready for bed. I couldn't sleep for another two hours though, as I just couldn't stop thinking about the experience and what I saw... The next day I woke up after sleeping for only 6 hours, but I felt so refreshed and energized. I awoke with a new sense of clarity, and with a genuine sense of good will towards other people. More than that, with a feeling that I have to seize every day and really live and be in touch with Mother Earth (Gaia). One thing that was odd about this trip was that the music seemed to be constant and never got distorted. Though, if my perception of time did become distored, one would think that the music would become distored as well... I can't explain it at all.

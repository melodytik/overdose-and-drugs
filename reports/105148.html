我正在使用它来治疗双极性障碍. 我通常服用的药物Trileptal不再起作用. 我每天都想自杀. 我是个极度自杀的人. 我对无生命的物体非常生气. 在被列入新药类之前, 医生轻率地笑了笑, 说如果我愿意的话, 我认为我相信这个家伙为什么不采取4毫克. 首先,我想要自杀,我愿意尝试任何事情. 其次,我想用一把刀杀死我丈夫. 我愿意尝试任何事情. 最后,我的皮肤在爬,我不能坐不动我不能停止对任何人和一切大喊大叫. 我感到偏执. 我一整天都在快速骑自行车. 所以我吃了4个药,睡了好几个小时. 我有两个孩子要照顾,这不会起作用. 我醒来后感觉非常好. 我打电话给我的医生, 就像他要求我一样, 我从来没有跟他说话,也从来没有听他的话. 我通常会把电视机打碎, 然后指责我丈夫, 我不敢相信我说了这个. 我觉得有人占据了我, 因为我多年来没有说过这么平静的话. 第二天我只吃了一个, 仍然睡了几个小时. 医生建议我服用1/4毫克. 他知道这会发生,然后当它发生时表现出惊. 我每天三次服用四分之一毫克, 到目前为止效果很好. 我应该每天服用两次, 但我能感觉到它消失了, 我希望能增加剂量, 让我一整天都能感觉很棒, 其他: 发布时间:2021年2月25日 药物 - 利 (308):不适用 (38),治疗意图或结果 (49),抑郁症 (15),医疗用途 (47),健康益处 (32),回顾 / 摘要 (11),第一次使用 (2)

Feb 25, 2021. erowid.org/exp/105148 DOSE: 4 mg oral Pharms - Haloperidol (pill / tablet) 0,75 mg oral Pharms - Haloperidol (daily) BODY WEIGHT: 250 lb I am using this for bipolar disorder. My normal medication Trileptal was no longer working. I just wanted to kill myself everyday. I was extremely suicidal. I was very angry at inanimate objects. I tried several other mood stabilizers before being put into a new classification of medication. My doctor kind of laughed casually and said that I could take 4 mg to start if I felt like it. I thought sure I trust this guy why not take 4 mg. First off, I wanted to off my self, I was willing to try anything. Secondly, I wanted to off my husband with a knife. I was willing to try anything. Lastly, my skin was crawling and I couldn't sit still I couldn't stop yelling at anyone and everything. I felt paranoid. I was cycling rapidly throughout the day. So I take all 4 pills and slept for hours. I have 2 kids to take care of and this was not going to work. I felt AMAZING after I woke up. I actually called my doctor, like he asked me to, and told him that it felt like drugs and I felt great. I never talk to him or listen to anything he has to say. Some minor thing happened such as losing the remote and my normal response would be to break the TV and then blame my husband for it while holding a knife to his throat, but instead I said 'It's not a big deal.' I couldn't believe that I had said that. I felt like someone had possessed me, because I had not said something so calm in years. The next day I only took one and still slept for hours. I met with my doctor who told me to take a 1/4 of 1 mg. He knew this would happen and then acted surprised when it did. I have been taking 1/4 of a milligram 3 times a day and it has been working great so far. I am supposed to take it twice a day, but I can feel it wearing off and I slip back into the hulk. I am hoping to go higher on the dosage so I can feel amazing all day long like I did when I took 4 mgs.

我刚刚得到了我的新莱克索坦尼尔6毫克 (布罗马泽) 片. 这些都让我非常惊,主要是因为所有报告都说5-6毫克相当于2毫克阿提万或1毫克克隆诺. 这些药比克洛纳泽,泽甚至是阿尔普拉佐更好. 我这么说的原因是因为我服用6毫克的药丸, 没有想到会有什么感觉 (因为我通常会服用3-4毫克的克纳克斯或6-8毫克的克洛诺, 我当时又服用了6毫克, 很快就感觉到了. 血水平达到峰值后, 需要60分钟至4小时的时间, 几乎没有催眠作用,即使是18毫克. 这是一个非常好的本佐, 这比阿提万,瓦,克纳克斯,克隆,尼特拉塞帕姆更好, 治疗焦虑,压力, 虽然对于睡眠问题, 我会用克萨克斯或克洛诺. 它们具有更强烈的催眠作用. 对不起这篇长篇报告. 其他: 发布时间:2018年6月20日 布罗马泽 (654):一般 (1),第一次使用 (2),回顾 / 总结 (11),烂的经历 (4),治疗意图或结果 (49),未知背景 (20)

Jun 20, 2018. erowid.org/exp/45709 DOSE: T+ 0:00 6 mg oral Bromazepam (pill / tablet) T+ 1:00 6 mg oral Bromazepam (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 150 lb I just got my new Lexotanil 6mg tabs (Bromazepam). These surprised the hell out of me, mostly because of all the reports saying that 5-6 mg is equiv. to maybe 2 mg of ativan, or 1mg of klonopin. IMHO, these are far better then clonazepam, diazepam, and even alprazolam. The reason I say this is because I took a 6mg pill the day I got them, not expecting to feel anything (since i would normally take 3-4 mg of xanax or 6-8 mg of klonopin with little effect other than decrease in ability to control ones self, emotions, temper, and the infamous non existant memory) After an hour, I felt suprisingly great! I took another 6 mg at that time, and felt it pretty quickly. These can take anywhere from 60mins to 4 hours for peak plasma levels, so at 4 hours, I was doing awsome. All anxiolytic, almost no hypnotic action (sleepy), even at 18 mg. This is a very good Benzo in my opinion, that is overlooked by almost everyone! This is way better then ativan, valium, xanax, klonopin, nitrazepam for anxiety, stress, or just wanting to chill out, ALOT! For sleep problems though, I'd stick w/ xanax or klonopin. They possess much more hypnotic action. Sorry for the long report.

我在网上购买了一司的维罗拉西多拉树皮, 我吸了一些,抽了一些,做了一杯非常温和的茶,然后忘了它. 我记得最近我有一些我粉末为了尝试提取酒精. 我不知道剩下多少,可能15克. 我用剩下的茶做了茶. 我在一个小时内喝了这三杯. 喝完第一杯酒后, 我在想全球阴谋,媒体操纵等等. 很容易理解,一切都是合乎逻辑. 我也感觉自己变得更强大,更自信了. 我吸了大麻, 这增加了经验. 我重新煮沸了粉末, 得到了更轻的红茶. 我喝了三个杯子, 这似乎提高了一些事情. 只是不那么高兴了, 我不再笑了, 我饿了,所以我吃了一些奶酪. 吃饭让我比以往任何时候都高了20分钟 我记得DMT (Epena的主要化学物质) 没有口服活性. 由于它确实产生了相当强烈的效果,我当时认为树皮要么不是Epena,要么含有MAOI,这可能会导致奶酪的问题. 我肚子有点不舒服,但这就是全部. 我在网上搜索了有关维罗拉树皮中MAOI存在的信息. 我发现的文件不清楚, 除了可以忍受的轻微的胃痛, 我没有感觉到任何不良影响. 后果是非常持久的. 我觉得自己是个好人, 我已经丧了一周了, 感觉虚弱和偏执狂但Epena治愈了这一切. 虽然白天的宿醉消退了, 这件事发生在两天前, 我仍然感觉很好. 具有相当强烈的高和持久的抗抑郁作用是意想不到的. 我订购了一大批粗皮和缩的提取物, 我希望能有个令人愉快的体验. 其他: 2009年3月6日发布 病毒种类 (319) :一般 (1), 单独 (16)

Mar 6, 2009. erowid.org/exp/73099 DOSE: oral Virola spp. (tea) BODY WEIGHT: 70 kg I've bought an ounce of Virola theidora shredded bark online three years ago. I've snorted some, smoked some and made a tea with very mild effects, then forgot about it. Recently I remember that I had some that I powdered in order to try an alcohol extraction. I dont know how much was left, mabe 15 grams (?). I made a tea with all that was left. I drank the resulting three cups over the course of one hour. Soon after having drunk the first one, I felt somehow drunk and happy and couldnt stop laughing. My mind was sped up and I was thinking about global conspiracies, media manipulation and so on. Things relate very easily and all was logical. I felt also stronger and self confident. I smoked pot, and it added to the experience. I reboiled the powder and it produced a lighter red tea. I drank the three cups and it seemed to boost things a little. After a while, I was still happy and inebriated, just less high and I didnt laugh anymore, but still was feeling fine. I was hungry, so I ate some cheese. Eating made me higher than ever before for 20 minutes. After that, I remembered that DMT (a main chemical of Epena) is not orally active. As it did produce a rather strong effect, I thought at that time that the bark either was not Epena or contained an MAOI, which could lead to problems with cheese. My stomach was a bit upset, but that was all. I searched the web for info regarding the presence of a MAOI in Virola bark. The documents I found were unclear and I dont know if they were regarding Virola theidora or other species. Except the bearable light stomach pain, I didnt feel ill effects. the peak lasted between one and two hours. After effects were very long lasting. The next day, I woke up hungover and very very happy, balanced and in a positive mood. I was depressed since a week, feeling weak and paranoid but the Epena cured it all. The hangover cleared during the day, but the well-being feeling remained. It took place two days ago, and I'm still feeling fine. Having a rather strong high and persistant antidepressant effects was unexpected. I ordered a large batch of crude bark and concentrated extract to research further with this great ally. I hope to have an entheogenic experience.

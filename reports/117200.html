A Cold Desert Night Peyote by eight ace Citation:   eight ace. "A Cold Desert Night: An Experience with Peyote (exp117200)". Erowid.org . Aug 19, 2025. erowid.org/exp/117200 DOSE: 5 buttons oral Peyote (fresh) BODY WEIGHT: 85 kg This happened in late 2021, when I was traveling through Mexico. I had previously tried shrooms, ayajuasca, LSD, 5-MeO-DMT, MDMA, and other substances, so naturally, it seemed like a great opportunity to experience peyote for the first time in its natural habitat. I was traveling with a lady-friend, and after some convincing we were fast on our way deep into the Mexican desert. I will skip the details of our long journey there, but we ended up staying at a remote village which is somewhat famous for peyote. We found a very modest accommodation in a spare room in a local family's yard. It had a terribly uncomfortable mattress-less bed with blankets, and was full of dirt. The day after we arrived, we went into the desert and found several beautiful buttons of peyote. I cut them as cleanly as possible with a machete right above the root-line (it's important for the survival of the plant). After returning to the house, I washed the peyote, cleaned them, and got ready to eat them. I fasted for two days before the trip. My friend was not interested in trying the peyote, as she only had one previous psychedelic experience, and it wasn't a pleasant one. About an hour and a half before sundown I sat down to eat the peyote - 5 medium to large sized buttons. Eating them was the most difficult part of the experience. It was a collection of adolescent cacti and intricate spirally grandfather peyotes, and each had a slightly different taste and texture. Some were a bitter, chewy torture to eat and some were more palatable and similar to the cacti leaves eaten casually in Mexican cuisine. It took me about 30 minutes to finish swallowing all of the cacti while taking breaks to recover and rest. We had some time before the effects were to begin, so we went to a local mini-market to stock up on water and snacks. The locals that we met on the way were nice and supportive when told about my eating a plate-full of peyote; that put me in a positive and expectant mood, perfect for the trip. On the way back to the house, the sunset was in full bloom, and I was beginning to feel some effects. I stopped to stand and stare at the mesmerizing desert sunset, and after a while of standing there I puked all of the peyote up - a chunky green mess in the sand. For the main part of the trip I went into a bushy area protected from the desert by a gate. I didn't have a watch to time the experience. I started out by standing alone among the bushes for a long while. I walked around a bit, I was feeling euphoria and was not able to navigate around the bushes in the moonlit night. The bushes seemed extremely beautiful, each illuminated with a multitude of small dots of white light, like thousands of fluorescent flowers. The bushes seemed extremely beautiful, each illuminated with a multitude of small dots of white light, like thousands of fluorescent flowers. I had a music player with me and listened to some of my favorite psychedelic rock songs with closed eyes. I had some closed-eye visuals and most notably, a strong feeling of my mind/ consciousness bending or flipping in three or four dimensions during parts of certain songs. It's hard to describe. After an unknown period of being alone in the desert, my friend and the señor of the house came to find me. I was a bit confused, but lucid enough to recognize them and the situation. The heat of the day was slowly fading away and was being replaced by the freezing air of the desert night. The señor lit a campfire to warm us up and to scare off curious coyotes. Me and my friend were left alone to enjoy the fire. My friend was distant, standing and watching me sit and roll in the sand around the fire. The upward cast shadows and judgmental expression did not paint my friend in a good light, and I perceived her as ugly and demonic, so I directed my attention elsewhere. Luckily, the family's friendly farm dog came and started cuddling with me. I scratched the dog, and enjoyed it immensely. My friend was less enthusiastic; even the dog's family were not willing to touch the flea-ridden mutt. I listened to music laying in the sand, while the dog was laying in my lap for a long while. Eventually, my friend softened and we had a pleasant experience cuddling and listening to music, getting warmed by the campfire. The effects I felt at this point were a strong enjoyment of music and of touch, and some closed eye visuals. It was getting very cold, the fire was slowly dying out. Every tiny desert sound made my friend jump up with fear of a coyote attack. We decided to head back into our room. It took us some time, but we eventually managed to find it, and as the sun was getting ready to rise, we jumped into the bed and tried to cover ourselves with the blankets. The bed and blankets were perhaps the worst I ever experienced, and we weren't able to sleep or to completely hide from the cold. I was covered with sand and imaginary (or not) fleas. Closing my eyes, I was surprised to see that I still had closed-eye visuals. I thought I had returned to normal. After lying in the cold rigid bed for several hours, we made the long journey back to the city. We got a room at a normal hotel. The shower and the soft hotel bed felt absolutely heavenly. I immensely enjoyed my first experience with peyote. Sure, it wasn't the most comfortable experience, but I am no stranger to rolling in the dirt while tripping. There were plenty of similarities to my mushroom and LSD trips, but peyote felt more euphoric and the puking added a cleansing element. I will try to have another mescaline experience when I get the chance.

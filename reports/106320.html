I brought the 3-FPM with me and did three small bumps throughout the two hour adventure and it was nothing short of spectacular. The effects of the MXP were very present throughout the adventure and the bumps of the 3-FPM kept my energy going along with a stop at a local corner store for some Gatorade. All in all, I had a truly fantastic experience. The 3-FPM by itself at first was very unenjoyable but the MXP made the night an experience to remember.

我和我的朋友想试试乘坐美丽的卫星. 一个早晨. 我们前往城镇寻找种子. 有几个品种的荣耀可以选择. 我们选择了天堂蓝色. 每个3个包. 现在我还不知道品牌是什么, 但它们是黄色包装, 店里的女士看着我们买了8包, 虽然当时不是种植的季节, 我们把它们带回家, 用砂和磨种子. 每个包装约有50种种子. 我们把三包粉碎, 把粉碎的材料放进一杯水中. 为了我的朋友的剂量重复这个程序. 我们让材料浸泡半个小时,偶尔. 大部分的碎片和种子碎片分离并沉入玻璃的底部. 混合物是一个黄色的阴暗液体. 这不是一个不愉快的气味. 我们喝了一口,避免在底部的泥. 味道根本不是苦或不愉快的. 实际上它是有点像地球的. 几乎像一个非常弱无糖的茶. 我注意到我的舌尖上有轻微的刺痛, 这些碎片被丢弃了. 我们很快就离开了, 接了一个清醒的朋友, 我们在下午2点左右采集了种子混合物. 我们在四点左右到达海. 这种症状大约在45-60分钟内出现,开始时胃部会感到不舒服 (但并非难以忍受的),并伴随着状的昏昏欲睡. 我们都有一点焦虑和轻微的不适大约半个小时. (注:后来经验表明, 在波浪中出现和达到峰值. 这种梦幻般的品质逐渐变成一种奇怪的麻醉状态. 我们都感到愉快. 这种情绪状态有点不那么强烈, 关于LSA的真正奇怪之处在于, 我们的判断力似乎没有受到损害, 我们的思维过程大部分是相当正常的, 有一个沉重的身体负载,梦幻般的麻醉剂. 增强了颜色和边缘, 没有运动或足迹. 这就像是幻觉剂的全身效应, 包括分离现实, 在接下来的四个小时里, 我们回家,决定再吃点东西. 这次我们又吃了两包, 与水溶液的效果相同,但更强烈,胃部更不舒服. 在晚上结束时大麻的效果显著增加. 我们都睡得很好,第二天感觉很好. 我头痛,可能是因为前一天没有喝足够的水. 总而言之,这是一个愉快的经历. 我曾经和其他人交谈过, 值得注意的是,我每天服用20毫克的帕克西尔来控制情绪. 这种药物对LSD和有影响,但LSA似乎没有受到影响. 时间表: 3856 LSA (65):准备/食谱 (30),一般 (1),小组 (2-9) (17)

Morning Glory at the Beach LSA by jman Citation:   jman. "Morning Glory at the Beach: An Experience with LSA (exp3856)". Erowid.org . Dec 26, 2001. erowid.org/exp/3856 DOSE: T+ 0:00 150 seeds oral Morning Glory (extract) T+ 4:00 100 seeds oral Morning Glory (ground / crushed) BODY WEIGHT: 175 lb My friend and I wanted to try tripping on a Beautiful Sat. Morning. We launched off into town in search of the seeds. We had to go to a couple of stores before we found packets that were specifically marked “untreated” on the labels. There were several varieties of the glories to choose from. Having read the FAQ we decided on the heavenly blues. 3 packets each. The brand escapes me at this point but they came in a yellow packet and were from Holland. The lady at the shop gave us a strange look as we bought 8 packs. It was the off season for planting, but there was no problem buying them. We took them home and used a mortar and pistil to grind up the seeds. Each pack contained about 50 seeds. We took three packs crushed them and put the crushed material into a glass of water. Repeated this procedure for my buddy’s dose. We let the material soak for about ½ and hour stirring occasionally. The majority of the gunk and seed debris separated and sunk to the bottom of the glass. The mixture was an off yellow cloudy liquid. It didn’t smell unpleasant. We sipped at the stuff avoiding the sludge at the bottom. The taste was not at all bitter or unpleasant. It was actually kind of earthy. Almost like a very weak unsweetened tea. I noticed a slight tingle on the tip of my tongue shortly after drinking the stuff. The debris was discarded. We left shortly after and picked up a sober friend and headed out to the beach. We took the seed mixture around 2 PM. We hit the beach around 4 or so. Onset occurred in about 45-60 min. and started with an uncomfortable (but not unbearable) feeling in the stomach accompanied by a mushroomy drowsiness. We both had a bit of anxiety and mild discomfort for about 1/2 an hour. (note: later experiences have revealed that a small 10mg dosage of diphenhydramine negated the negative effects of onset). Coming up and peak happened in waves. The dreamy quality fading into a strangely narcotic sort of state. We both felt pleasant. The emotional state was somewhat less intense but similar to mushrooms. The really strange thing about LSA is that neither of us had the really strange reeling thoughts that usually come with pot, acid and shrooms. Our judgment didn’t seem impaired and our thought processes were for the most part fairly normal, and our emotions were fairly normal. There was a heavy body load, dreamy narcotic. Enhanced colors and edges, no movement or trails to speak of. It was like having the full body effect of a hallucinogen including reality separation with out loosing your noodle at all. It came in increasing waves for about the next 4 hours. We went home and decided to eat more. We crushed up and ate 2 more packs with out any water this time. The same effects occurred as with the water solution but came on stronger with more stomach discomfort. Pot increased the effects dramatically at the end of the night. We both slept well and felt OK the next day. I had a headache, which could have been from not drinking enough water the previous day. All in all it was a pleasant experience. I have talked to other folks who have gotten the treated seeds and have had terrible effect form them so I am careful. It is also worth noting that I take 20 Mg. of Paxil a day for mood control. LSD and Mushrooms are dulled by this drug, however LSA didn’t seem to be affected.

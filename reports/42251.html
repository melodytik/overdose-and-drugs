我刚刚和一个朋友搬进了我的第一套公寓. 我是一名19岁的大麻贩子, 帮助我实现这一目标的其中一个人是我一位为兽医工作的顾客. 她总是会带来一些新的和令人兴奋的交易大麻. 她以前带来的一切都是我以前听说过的, 我朋友带来了四种药物, 她详细解释了前三种药物是什么以及预期的情况. 她解开了药物附带的信息, 并指出药物在高剂量时会导致幻觉的副作用. 这很酷. 几天后,我卖掉了最后一袋药, 我在晚上8点和室友一起看电视, 我室友一直在喝酒, 所以他只好看. 我在一两个小时前抽了大麻, 我计划在沙发上放松,享受旅程, 我开始感到一种平静和镇静. 到了8点15分, 没有幻觉. 我唯一能看到的就是我脑海中的床. 我觉得身体很沉重,没有能量. 我花了20秒钟才从沙发上站起来. 我告诉我的室友我很累,要去睡觉. 我设法保持清醒的旅行到我的卧室. 在我的头撞到枕头之前我已经睡着了. 时间是周一8点15分. 我醒来后有一段时间在汗水池. 我在房间里的温度大约是90度, 我花了大约30秒才知道我在哪里. 我看了看时钟,现在是下午二点半. 我感到疲倦,就像我躺下的时候一样. 我唯一起床的原因是要尿尿喝点东西. 我从床上爬出来,在所后面的墙上靠着我的头, 接下来,我在水龙头的上站立着, 我喝下了水,然后立即把脸重新插进我的枕头. 我醒来时感觉不那么昏昏欲睡. 我看了看时钟,时间又是二点半. 我开始意识到我已经昏迷了多久. 我尿了,喝了两杯水. 我走到厨房,开始做三明治, 我和一个同房的人走进来,问我去过哪里. 我告诉他我的故事,并问这是什么日子. 他笑了起来,说今天是星期四. 我无法相信我已经睡了近四天. 我仍然不确定是星期二还是星期三醒来. 我躺在沙发上,睡到八点. 我立即打电话给一个朋友, 我检查了我的呼叫器, 它有30多个新页面. 我打了几通电话告诉人们我还活着, 我吸了大约200毫克, 终于感觉正常了. 接下来的几天我逐渐恢复正常的睡眠模式. 我认为直到星期六, 药物才离开我的体内. 经历了这种经历之后, 我不打算在不久的将来再服用这种药物. 几个月后我又试了一次, 发现30毫克是个很好的剂量, 无论我服用多少, 甲醇的效果都持续了12个小时, 我从此以后再也没有服用药物, 其他: 巴巴丁 (208):小组 (2-9) (17),宿醉/几天后 (46),第一次使用 (2)

Aug 21, 2006. erowid.org/exp/42251 DOSE: 225 mg oral Phenobarbital (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 140 lb It was summer in Los Angeles and I had just moved in to my first apartment with a friend of mine. I was a 19 year old pot dealer on a mission to experience every drug known to man. One person helping me accomplish this goal was a customer of mine that worked for a Veterinarian. She would always bring something new and exciting to trade for pot. Everything she had brought previously was something that I had heard of before and generally knew what to expect. When my friend arrived she had brought 4 different drugs for me to try. She explained in detail what the first three drugs were and what to expect. For the bottle with the big purple pills she unraveled the info that comes with the drug and pointed out where it listed hallucinations as a side effect of the drug in higher doses. Cool. A few days later after selling my last sack I decided to try the Phenobarbital. I was watching TV with my roommate at 8pm when I swallowed three 75mg coated tablets. My roommate had been drinking beer which was a big no-no according to the patient information. So he was content to watch. I was more or less sober besides some marijuana smoked an hour or two earlier. My plans were to relax and enjoy the trip from my couch in front of the TV. At 8:10 I started to feel a sense of calm and sedation. At 8:15 I felt extremely sedated and drowsy. No hallucinations. My only visual was my bed in my mind. My body felt heavy and out of energy. It took me a good 20 seconds to get up from the couch. I told my roommate I was tired and going to bed. I managed to stay awake for the trip to my bedroom. I was asleep before my head hit the pillow. It was 8:15 on Monday. I awoke some time later in a pool of sweat. It was about 90 in my room and the mid day sun was beating down on me. It took about 30 seconds to figure out where I was. I looked at the clock and it was 2:30pm. I felt just as tired as I did when I laid down. The only reason I got out of bed was to pee and drink something. I dragged myself out of bed and took a long piss while resting my head against the wall behind the toilet. Then I held myself upright on the faucet knob while filling a glass with water. I downed the water and then immediately face planted again into my pillow. I awoke the next time feeling slightly less groggy. I looked at the clock and again it was 2:30. I started to realize how long I had been out. I peed and drank 2 glasses of water. I went to the kitchen and started making a sandwich, still feeling like I could use a nap. My roommate walked in and asked where I had been. I told him my story and asked what day it was. He started to laugh and said it was Thursday. I couldn't believe I had been asleep for almost four days. I'm still not sure if I woke up on Tuesday or Wednesday. I lied down on the couch and slept until 8. I immediately called a friend and asked him to bring me speed. I checked my pager and it had over 30 new pages. I made a few calls to inform people I was alive when my friend arrived with the speed. I snorted about 200mg and finally felt like normal. Over the next few days I gradually returned to a normal sleep pattern. I'd say it wasn't until Saturday that the drug had left my system. After that experience, I had no plans of taking that drug again anytime soon. I tried the drug again a few months later and found 30mg to be a good dosage to put me out after a long night. No matter how little I took, Phenobarbital’s effects lasted 12 hours before it left my system. I never took a drug after that without researching it thoroughly first.

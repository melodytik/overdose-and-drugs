我决定向医生询问关于睡眠辅助的药物. 我先吃了安比恩, 效果很好, 但因为有个奇怪的保险, 他给了我两片Lunestra的样品. 睡觉的时候, 我打开两片2毫克的药片, 一个小时没有. 眼皮也许有点沉重, 但我仍然有足够的精力去做任何事情. 基本上,没有足够的疲劳来入睡. +2小时 仍然没有任何变化, 感觉完全没有改变. 我决定躺在床上等待我的身体去睡觉. 我需要30分钟以上才能入睡. 第二天晚上,可怕的便. 现在,Olestra已经注意到,随着时间的持续消费 (这是一个低脂肪的油芯片烤或类似的), 我不会说发生了什么, 但它肯定不是一个标准的肠道运动. 我在使用所后的另一个小时内, 结论:如果我在服用正常剂量后有这种感觉, 有没有人有爆炸性腹? 记住,这种情况可能会发生, 但用更高的剂量可能不会变得更糟. 所以,行程可能会有所不同. 其他: 药品 - 埃斯索皮克隆 (373):第一次 (2),单独 (16)

Oct 23, 2006. erowid.org/exp/53039 DOSE: 4.0 mg oral Pharms - Eszopiclone (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 190 lb Being on Antidepressants has given me some insomnia (I blame Paxil, which my body is now addicted to, yay withdrawals), so I decided to ask the Doc about sleep-aid. I had Ambien first, which worked very well, but being on a weird insurance setup I had to pay full price. $100/bottle is a bit much per month. So going back in later he gave me a 2 tab sample of Lunestra. Coming up to bed time, I pop both 2mg tabs and sit in front of the computer waiting for it to kick in..... +1hr Nothing. Maybe a wee bit of heavy eyelids, but I still had enough energy to do whatever. Basically, not tired enough to fall asleep. +2hr Still nothing, no change in feeling at all. +4hr Decided to just lay in bed and wait for my body to just go to sleep. Took about my normal 30+mins to fall asleep. Next day evening, horrible loose stools. Now, Olestra has been noted to, over time of continual consumption (it's an low fat oil chips are baked in or something of the sort), can give you anal leakage. Well I wouldn't say that happened, but it sure as hell wasn't a standard bowel movement. For about another hour after using the bathroom, my stomach still wasn't feeling kosher, but I didn't have to 'go' again. Conclusion: If I felt like this after just taking a NORMAL dose, I'd hate to experience a recreational use! Explosive diarrhea anyone? Just keep in mind that could happen, but may not be any worse with a higher dosage. So, milage may vary.

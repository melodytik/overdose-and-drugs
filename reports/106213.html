我在美国购买了250毫克的5-APB. 星期四到达, 昨晚我第一次使用它. 这是一个回顾. 精确地说是9点, 我降落85毫克 (给或取5). 即使在报纸上,这些东西也非常痛苦,但你能做什么? 现在我只是在等待游戏. 我看了一些YouTube影片, 和我一小群朋友一起休息, 到了我走路的时候,已经是10点15分了. 我开始感到有点紧张, 因为我有了不合时宜的产品 (即使知道这种化学物质的使用时间很长), 我在十点半做一个. 它工作了. ,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,, 我立即被推入熟悉的气状态, 我站着,但我根本感觉不到我的脚在地板上. 现在我知道我没有买坏的产品. 我决定坐在地板上, 我几乎开始出汗,有点. 我现在肯定感觉到了这些东西. 触摸我的皮肤是一种崇高的感觉. 我经常用手着头发, 因为每次都会感受到强烈的快乐. 没有什么性感,只是好. 虽然触摸感觉很棒, 但实际上我感觉不到像MDMA那样高的身体. 我觉得身体完全正常, 这对我来说是积极的. 我发现虽然MDMA的高兴感令人惊叹, 有时候我不想和任何人说话, 这对我来说是一把双刃剑. 随着5-APB, 没有这种分心. 我的皮肤感觉很好,音乐听起来比正常好, 我也没有动作和/或跳舞的欲望. 这并不是什么大惊喜, 因为MDMA对我来说也很麻烦, 如果一个人想,他们可以很容易地去睡觉. 实际上我睡了几次, 但如果我会浪费一个卷! 现在只有我和四个朋友. 这就是我能感受到同情心的地方. 我对他们的爱情并没有像你在使用MDMA或甲基隆时所感受到的那样. 我开始感受到那些深深植根于爱情和友的感情. 我给一个朋友发短信说我的想法和感受 (我不想大声说出来,让大家感到尬), 他发回的短信让我过了一夜. 只是知道某人有同样的感受, 无论如何,随着夜晚的发展, 我变得越来越软弱, 晚上剩下的时间都没有什么特别的事件发生 (尽管我的一个朋友最终独自吃了3袋爆米花, 我们只是聊了聊,放松了一会儿, 这是我最喜欢做的事情. 我终于在凌晨3点左右睡着了. 现在是第二天早上, 我绝对没有宿醉. 没有后光. 我感觉自己100%正常, 我也很高兴地说,这是一个非常令人满意的经历. 我不是一直在寻找另一个机会, 这是我第三次有这样的感觉 (第一次是在我第一次用LSD后, 这让我有机会休息一下,欣赏这段经历. 快速总结 (有数字!): 触觉:十分之一. 触摸到的一切都令人高潮. 穿过头发是天堂般的, 皮肤是光荣的. 我从来没有做过性爱, 但在这种东西的影响下, 欧里亚:四分之一 我说的是身体的高具体在这里. 整个晚上有几波快乐, 但没有任何一致或令人震惊的. 我确实得到了暂时的阴影, 这总是太棒了. 我喜欢我的眼睛振动,感觉很有趣. 同理心: 10/10. 强度与MDMA完全相同, 如果不是更多的话. 我发现我需要一个小小的团体. 我认为任何超过6人的人都太多了. 无论如何 在沟通时绝对没有障碍,我是100%的自己. 身体负荷: 2/10. 我在起床时有点出汗, 但在一个小时后就消失了. 没有其他负面情绪. 一些比较: 对于MDMA:几乎相同, 同样的情感,但更加清醒. 有时在使用MDMA时, 我的思想会变得混乱, 没有5-APB. 如果我没有入睡, 我认为它会继续下去. 也让我觉得更... 我知道这不是一个词, 但这是我能描述的最好的方式. 对于梅西龙:好得多. 我喜欢甲基 (可能比普通人更喜欢), 没有真正的间歇. 5APB的升高 (长时间),峰值,高原5或6小时 (假设你不上床),然后在一个或两个小时内缓慢下降. 虽然在第二天, 甲基使我感觉150%, 总的来说,这个药对我来说是9/10. 我认为这将把它放在10/10的区域. 我不认为我能找到比MDMA更好的东西, 但这可能是它. 没有恶作剧,而且是合法的. 如果您喜欢MDMA,我百分之百推. 谢谢你花时间阅读! 其他:

Oct 16, 2015. erowid.org/exp/106213 DOSE: T+ 0:00 85 mg oral 5-APB (powder / crystals) T+ 1:30 1 cart. inhaled Nitrous Oxide (gas) BODY WEIGHT: 150 lb Last Saturday I purchased 250mg of 5-APB from an RC site in the US. It arrived this Thursday, and I used it for the first time last night. Here's a recap. At exactly 9:00 I parachute ~85mg (give or take 5). Even through the paper that shit was super bitter, but what are you gonna do? Now I'm simply playing the waiting game. I watch some YouTube, chill with the small group of friends that I'm with, and eventually go for a walk around the apartment complex. By the time I go on the walk, it’s 10:15. I'm starting to get a little nervous that I got bunk product (even knowing about the long come up times this class of chemicals has), so I decide to try and kick the drug in myself with the help of some N2O. I do a whippet at 10:30. It worked. Boy oh boy did it fucking work. I was immediately catapulted into the familiar nitrous state of wompy wompy bliss. I'm standing, but I can't feel my feet on the floor at all. I'm floating, with the stupidest fucking grin on my face, and I know for sure now that I didn't get bad product. I decide to take a seat on the floor for fear of falling over, and wait for the whippet to finish. I’m on the verge of sweating, kind of clammy. I'm definitely feeling this stuff now. Touching my skin feels sublime. I run my hands through my hair constantly, as every time I get a very strong wave of pleasure. Nothing erotic, just good. While touch feels fantastic, I don't actually feel any type of body high as with MDMA. I physically feel completely normal, which is actually a positive for me. I find that while the MDMA euphoria is fucking amazing, it can be a little overbearing and distracting at points. There have been times where I didn't want to talk to anyone because I was simply so focused on myself feeling good. It's a double-edged sword in my eyes. With the 5-APB, that distraction isn't there. My skin feels nice, and music sounds better than normal, but I'm completely lucid. I also have no desire to move and/or dance. This isn’t much of a shock since MDMA is also very stoning for me, but this shit is downright sedating. If one wanted to, they could easily just go to bed. I actually dozed off a couple of times, but I’ll be damned if I’m going to waste a roll! A couple people leave, so now it’s just myself and 4 friends. This is where I can feel the empathy. Earlier there had been quite a few people in the apartment, and I didn’t really get the love for them all that you do on MDMA or methylone. Once some of them left however, the empathy shows up and I’m starting to feel those deep seeded feelings of love and friendship. I text one of my friends my thoughts and feelings (don’t want to say it aloud and make everyone feel awkward), and he reciprocates. The texts he sent back made my night. Just knowing someone feels the same way as you is always fulfilling, y’know? Anyways, as the night progresses I get more and more mushy, until the high reaches its peak around 11:45. Nothing super eventful happens for the rest of the night (although one of my friends ended up eating like 3 bags of popcorn all by himself, and another ate around a dozen cookies within an hour). We just talked and chilled for a while, my favorite thing to do on entactogens. I end up falling asleep around 3 AM. It is currently the next morning, and I have absolutely no hangover. No afterglow either. I feel 100% normal, which is always welcome. I am also glad to say that this was one of those rare experiences that was just completely satisfying. Not that I’m always looking for another chance to get high, but I feel no urge to do any drugs for a while. This is only the third time I’ve felt this way (first being after my first LSD trip and the second after my first roll), and I like it. It gives me a chance to take a break from everything and just appreciate the experience. Quick summary (with numbers!): Tactile sensations: 10/10. Everything feels orgasmic to touch. Running my hands through my hair is heavenly, and skin is glorious. Never had sex, but it would without a doubt be enthralling while under the influence of this stuff. Euphoria: 4/10. I’m talking about the body high specifically here. There were a couple waves of pleasure throughout the night, but nothing consistent or mind-blowing. I did get the temporary nystagmus though, which is always awesome. I love when my eyes vibrate, it feels so funny. Empathy: 10/10. Intensity wise exactly like MDMA, if not moreso. I found that I needed the group to be kind of small though. I think anything over 6 or so people would be too much. Anyways. Absolutely no barriers when communicating, I’m 100% me. Bodyload: 2/10. I got kind of sweaty on the come-up, but it went away after about an hour. No other negative feelings. A few comparisons: To MDMA: Almost identical, just without the euphoria from the body high. Same lovey dovey feelings but more lucid. Sometimes with MDMA my thoughts get kind of disorganized and it gets hard to string sentences together without getting distracted. None of that with 5-APB. 5-APB also gave me a longer roll, and I think it would have kept going had I not fell asleep. It also feel more…..mungsty I guess. I’m aware that isn't a word, but that’s the best way I can describe it. To Methylone: Much better. I enjoy methylone (probably more than the average person), but it is a very fleeting high I find. It’s come-up, peak, come down, with no real breaks in between. The 5-APB was come up (long too), peak, plateau for 5 or 6 hours (assuming you don’t go to bed) and then a gentle come down over an hour or two. Methylone has me feeling 150% the next day though, so I guess it’s better in that regard. Overall, this drug is a 9/10 for me. Next time I’m going to jump to 100mg, and I think that will put it into 10/10 territory. I didn’t think I would be able to find something better than MDMA, but this is possibly it. Smoother, less distracting, no hangover, and it’s fucking legal. I 100% recommend it if you like MDMA. Thanks for taking the time to read if you did!

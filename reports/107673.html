Nice buzz. Nothing to write home about. 11.00pm - redosed another 75mg 3-FPM. Quite wired and having a good time, but still nothing too special. 1.00am - we decided to leave the party and have two 100ug blotters of 1P (this is a low dose for us, and our usual 1P experiments consist of 4-500ug) on the way home. The walk was beautiful, especially with the scenery where I live. This was all well and good. 2.00am - got back to my house and we were both at a nice level where we would usually put on a film and have a laugh, so that's what we tried to do. 2.30am - things started to become a little intense. VERY strong visuals, now I'm not just talking geometry and fractals, I am talking cartoons climbing out of books and dancing. I am talking watching my friend turn into a giant slug-like alien creature with tentacles for a beard. This trip took visuals to a place I have not experienced before, and my friend was on the same level. The air was made up for complex geometry. A painting that is usually grey and white scenery was a purple and green forest with a river flowing through it and down the wall. The walls seemed infinite and any details on the walls were floating around in the middle of the room like planets. This level of visuals is not for the faint hearted, and was achieved from only 2 blotters of 100ug. We are both very experienced and it got even a bit overwhelming for us, but Benzos did not seem to work to bring it down! Both of us had to ride out the awesomeness in its full. The point of this report is just to warn people that may have the opportunity to combine the two - 3-FPM + 1P-LSD = absolute insanity 3-FPM + 1P-LSD = absolute insanity , and I would advise anyone that is a bit inexperienced to steer clear of this combination!

This stuff really does smell like Pokémon cards. T- 0:15. Out of all the dissos I've tried so far, the onset feels closest to "FXE" (presumably 2-Fluoro-2-oxo-PCE), but warmer. That said, it is present to a lesser degree throughout my body. T- 0:25. The warmth intensifies. I am experiencing a bout of tinnitus, but it's light. I notice mild color saturation; on top of that, there's a vague "tunnel vision" effect. A profound relaxation begins to set in, like getting into a warm bath. I hit my THC pen, which deepens my profound content. T- 0:40. Sharp tingles are running all up and down my body. This stuff is quite potent. I feel sedated and at peace, but also mentally stimulated. I text some friends as the warm body high increases. T- 0:45. I experience a feeling describable only as bliss. I put on Sufjan Steven's "Futile Devices" - the instrumentals are beautiful sober, and even more so while on MXiPR. I could almost feel the music in my body, with each note producing tingles in my very soul. T- 0:55. I feel somewhat numb, but the anelgesic effects are not yet quite as pronounced as 2-FDCK or ketamine. This disso has an interesting warmth to it - possibly indicating more serotonergic action? I am soon hit with another wave of euphoria and sedation, which encourages me to lie down and check for CEVs. They are present, though somewhat indistinct, possessing a vague rainbow hue. MXiPR exhibits an interesting blend of sedation and stimulation. Mentally, I find it stimulating at all doses. Physically, however, it is relatively stimulating at lower doses and sedating at higher doses. Even though my thoughts are often clear and sharp, articulating them can be difficult. My heart rate increases throughout the duration of the experience, as well. Motor control skills decline as dosage increases, replaced by numbness. These effects are not quite as intense as 2-FDCK, 2F-OPCE, and ketamine. The unpleasant plastic taste lends itself to a bit of nausea, but I have never needed to throw up, and the feeling typically passes quickly. MXiPR produces a unique body high, which is best characterized as pleasurable buzzing in the extremities, including arms, legs, feet, hands, and the head. This tingling alternates between cool and warm, and it comes in distinct waves. My body also feels somewhat light, as if I am floating and weightless. MXiPR sports noteworthy visual effects. During the onset, my visual acuity is enhanced, but my vision blurs soon after. At higher doses, it can be difficult to focus on a single point due to double vision. Shutting one eye is the only way to type effectively. OEVs are limited to blurring and mild color saturation. The CEVs are colorful, but indistinct. There are jagged lines behind my eyelids, but it's hard to put my finger on it. The auditory effects of MXiPR are noteworthy, too. The tinnitus isn't all that different from what I expect from 2-FDCK, but there's an occasional whooping sound that I haven't encountered before. Music sounds noticeably better, and any media is more immersive. In terms of cognitive effects, there's an element of "magic" - a warm serotonergic blanket - unlike any disassociative I've ever tried. The feeling somewhat resembles what I've heard about MXE, though I never had the pleasure of trying it.

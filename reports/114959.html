这次经历是我从未打算发生过的... 并且肯定改变了我对这些化合物的治疗方式的看法. 我没有尊重,我被在我的股. 我几乎每隔一周就会服用这种药物. 我会服用10或15毫克的囊,然后白天和晚上重新注入各种注入剂量. 我从当地的健身俱乐部工作回家时, 现在是晚上十点半左右, 我想喝醉, 玩Xbox来放松心情. [T- 30 分钟] 我喝了6杯吉尼酒, 感觉到酒精的温暖和醉酒覆盖了我. 我不想再强迫手洗手液的感觉下来. [T+0] 时间 我冲动地测量出21毫克的3-meo-PCP, 我觉得自己变得分离了, 失去了比已提供的酒更多的协调. 我很快收拾了我的秤和研究化学品, 我花了大部分的时间看着坏的破裂, 但这是徒劳的. 我无法理解这些人物在说什么或在做什么. 我觉得我的灵魂被迫离开我的身体. 仿佛我距离这里有千里之遥, 仍然能移动,听到,说话,看到... 我女朋友随机打电话给我, 接电话后她意识到我非常糟糕. 她问我是否没事, 我只能做一个简短的回应, 我向她解释了我服用了什么和多少, 我把Xbox关掉,把手机放下, 完全忘记她在另一条线上. [T+ 15 分钟] 这时我有一个启示,这是一个我不熟悉的. 我的生命即将结束... 在一个小时内. 我已经无法站起来做任何事情了. 我开始失明, 无法辨认眼睛在说什么, 我听到我女朋友在另一条线, 非常微妙的红色和绿色几何图案, 我抓住我的床和周围的一切, 试图抓住一些东西让我留在地球上, [T+ 30 分钟] 我正进入一个我从未经历过的地方. 尽管如此, 我确信自己即将死去, 我不知道我拿了什么,我在哪里,或者发生了什么. 但我知道发生了一些可怕的事情. 我的人生开始在我眼前闪过. 我看到童年时代的回忆, 一生陪伴我的朋友和家人, 似乎我有一个快速回顾我的生活之前灯会关闭. 我开始被压缩到一个单一的点, 但同时又处于一个空虚的两维形状, 我觉得这些形状正在解开我的灵魂. 我心中的所有轮都被拆开了, 随着它迅速扩展到无人居住的空间里. 我所知道和爱的一切都被慢慢拆开了, 我看到巨大的机器,令人难以置信的复杂的灰色装置, 我女朋友回忆起这一点, 告诉我我没有反应大约10-15分钟, 我记得我突然重新学会了像婴儿一样移动,说话等等. 然而这一切都发生在20分钟之内. 一旦我重新学会了如何操作和重新学会了自己的身份, 我听到我的女朋友说"你带走了3meo... 一切都会好起来的... 很长一段时间我一直在问她发生了什么事, 我一直在问她我是否在一个思想循环和如果有人发现. 尽管如此, 没有人来救我, [时间+80分钟] 我慢慢地收集自己,并开始哭泣比我有任何化合物. 我很困惑,我不知道发生了什么事, 我相信我已经死了,游戏结束了,然而突然我回来了. 我慢慢地恢复了自己, 解释我爱我的女朋友是多么的, 我对自己给女朋友带来的恐惧感到内, 也为自己做出如此粗心的决定感到内. 我仍然不知道为什么我不留在任何我去的地方,但... 我没有抱怨. 我只知道我已经有足够的化学物质知道它将是一个很长的股时间之前我决定再次触摸它. 在撰写这份报告时,我并不知道我会在接下来的几周继续使用3meo,在工作中服用,或者大多数晚上睡前服用. 这样令人震惊的现实经历并不足以让我远离3meo所能提供的冷酷清洁的头脑空间. 用这个故事作为一个警告. 如果您和我一样, 可能需要不仅仅是一次可怕的经历, 时间表: 114959 酒精 - 硬 (198),3-MeO-PCP (558):一般 (1),组合 (3),困难经历 (5),不好的旅行 (6),独自 (16)

Accidental Slip Into the Void Alcohol - Hard & 3-MeO-PCP by BitBitt Citation:   BitBitt. "Accidental Slip Into the Void: An Experience with Alcohol - Hard & 3-MeO-PCP (exp114959)". Erowid.org . Nov 16, 2020. erowid.org/exp/114959 DOSE: T+ 0:00 6 shots oral Alcohol - Hard (liquid) T+ 0:30 21 mg insufflated 3-MeO-PCP (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 125 lb This experience is one I never intended on happening... and definitely one that changed my outlook on the way I was treating these compounds. I didn’t respect it, and I got put on my ass. I was currently binging this drug almost every other week. I would go about taking 10 or 15mg capsules, then redose with various insufflated doses day and night. This experience all started when I got home from a shift at a local fitness club. It was around 10:30 PM and I wanted to get drunk and play some Xbox to unwind. (please note, I was already on the comedown of a 15mg oral dose from earlier in the day) [T- 30 minutes] I took 6 shots of gin, and felt the warm intoxicating blanket of alcohol fall over me. My craving for intoxication still was not satisfied, and I did not want to force the hand sanitizer feeling of gin down my throat any longer. [T+ 0] I impulsively weigh out 21mg of 3-meo-PCP and destroy the line of bitter white powder in one rail off my scale. IMMEDIATELY I feel myself getting dissociated and lose more coordination than the gin already offered. I quickly packed away my scale and stash of research chemicals and made it to my bed. I spent most the come up watching breaking bad, but it was fruitless. I couldn’t understand what the characters were saying or doing. It felt like my soul was being forced out of my body. Like I was miles away yet still able to move and hear and talk and see… My girlfriend called me randomly, upon picking up the phone she realized I was extremely fucked up. She asked if I was okay and all I could do was a short response until I said “I made a terrible mistake”. I explained to her what I took and how much before my short term memory gave out. I turned off the Xbox and set my phone down, completely forgetting she was on the other line. [T+ 15 Minutes] At this moment I had a revelation, and it was one which I was unfamiliar with. My life was coming to an end... within the hour. Yet once I realized this I was already too incapacitated to get up and do something. My vision began to fail me, I could not make out what my eyes were telling me, until they disconnected altogether. I could hear my girlfriend on the other line but I was not listening, or even aware that she was on the line once her voice stopped. Very subtle red and green geometric patterns resembling different symbols and objects developed on my walls. I was grabbing my bed and everything around it trying to grasp for something to keep me on earth, but before I knew it my sense of touch faded. [T+ 30 Minutes] I was entering the hole, a place I’ve never experienced before. Yet I was convinced I was dying and that this was my one of last moments. I didn’t know what I took, where I was, or what was happening. But I knew something horrible was happening to me. My life began to flash before my eyes. I saw memories from my early childhood, friends and family who were with me throughout my life were all flooding my brain all at once. It was as if I had a quick recap of my life before the lights would go out. I began being compressed to a single point yet at the same time being in a cast open void of two dimensional shapes all rotating the same way and in the same manner. It felt like these shapes were unwinding my soul. All of the gears of my mind were being taken apart as it was rapidly extended miles upon miles out into desolate space. everything I knew and loved was being slowly taken apart and freed from my mind into the void, until I was nothing but an empty vessel, floating through nothingness yet still moving at the speed of light in all directions. I observed glimpses of immense machine scapes, incredibly complex greyish contraptions moving in ways which defied physics. My girlfriend recalls this point, and tells me I was non responsive for about 10-15 minutes before beginning to mumble again. I remember suddenly relearning how to move,talk, etc like a baby. Yet this was all within 20 minutes. Once I relearned how to operate and relearned my own identity, I began to feel my bed and everything around it. I heard my girlfriend saying “you took 3meo... everything is going to be okay...” over and over and over. I was apparently asking her what was happening for an extended period of time, even when I wasn’t aware I was talking. I kept asking her if I was in a thought loop and if anyone found out. Yet despite all of this no one was at my door to save me, just my girlfriend on the call. [T+ 80 minutes] I slowly gathered myself, and began to sob more than I have from any compound. I was so confused, I didn’t know what had happened and I didn’t know how I was still here. I was convinced I was dead and game over, yet all the sudden I’m back. Albeit VERY fucked up from the 3meo, I slowly regained myself before explaining how much I love my girlfriend for going through that and not knowing if I was going to wake back up. I was flooded with guilt for the fear I inflicted in my girlfriend, and guilt for allowing myself to make such a careless decision. I still don’t know why I didn’t stay wherever I went, but... I have no complaints. All I know is that I’ve had enough from this chemical to know it’ll be a long ass time before I decide to touch it again. At the time of writing this report, I was not aware that I would further my 3meo use the following weeks, dosing at work, or most nights before bed. A reality shattering experience such as this one was not enough to keep me away from the cold and clean headspace 3meo has to offer. Use this as a story of caution. If you are like me, it may take more than a terrifying experience to get you off of a compound.

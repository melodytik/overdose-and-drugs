I remember crouching underneath a seat window knocking on her basement window, wondering why I couldn't stand correctly. Of course, there was a ceiling above me, but I couldn't understand why a ceiling above me would obstruct my ability to stand up completely. Once inside, I remember playing piano with our hostess and crying, (she was crying too as we convinced her to eat 12 pills), thinking that I was playing Beethoven's missing masterpiece. It was beautiful and emotional, although not altogether true. At some point we decided that we 'weren't feeling it yet' - even though we'd been tripping hard for almost four hours - keep in mind this drug makes you forget that you even took it - and we decided to take another 1/2 box. After this, being the rational young man that I am, I decided to call my parents to let them know I was ok. Suffice to say, it being almost 4 o'clock or so they wanted to know where the hell I was. Somehow my mother got the address from me and lo and behold my parents were picking me up from this girl's house. 15 minutes later I was at the hospital with my parents and the doctors trying to figure out what drugs I was on. While the tests were being done we were in the cafeteria. For the life of me I thought we were at the airport. While sitting at the table, I noticed small movements of the hair on my legs. Obviously, I looked, and noticed small bugs moving individual follicles in a sort of rhythmic motion. I started picking them off, and when my mom asked what I was doing, I told her that I was picking the 'microscopic' bugs off my legs. My poor mom. I went home, my parents found my car, found the Merazine packets, and I went to sleep. I slept for almost 15 hours. My friend, at my parents' insistence, picked me up to play raquetball soon after I awoke. We were playing at the YMCA, and I kept on noticing my gym teachers eyes through the small slit of the door. I mentioned it to my buddy; each time he checked there was no coach. The third time I actually noticed our coach sitting in the corner of the court. Needless to say, he was not there. Fucked up drug, scary, best trip in the world. Never do it. **** In college, I told the above story to other friends, they decided to do it. They took a pack each, I was their sober chaperone, and they tripped balls. They spend our walk having conversations with shadows and lampposts. When we finally got back to my dorm room, one was watching pure static on the tv while the other was lounging on my couch. The one on the couch kept on cringing almost as something were about to fall down on him. Upon asking, he said that there was a soda can on a shelf that was about to spill. The other told me that there were scary bat-like things coming out of the tv that he had to avoid. They did this for almost 2 hours. Scary drug, never do it.

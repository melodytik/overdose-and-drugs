让我先说一下, 我曾经接触过相当多的毒品 (大麻,安非他林,LSD,,吗啡,甲基,,DXM,片和乙), 有一天在学校, 一个朋友给了我一瓶阿德拉尔 (大约120粒20毫克药). 我今年早些时候服用了阿德拉尔XR, 经过两周的耐药性, 我对单独服用这种药物失去了兴趣, 我当时唯一的其他药物是埃弗德林和. 对于我来说, 乙几乎完全像服用阿德拉尔, 无论如何,我选择将阿德拉尔与. 我已经服用了Hydrocodone很长一段时间,但我的耐受性仅为27.5毫克的片. 我开始服用6种阿德罗尔药,一小时后没有效果, 我决定服用两种水. 服用这些药物后, 我感觉与正常的高速药不同. 45分钟后, 我又服用了一剂水. 吃了这些药后, 我感到非常专注. 甚至比阿德拉尔单独能做的更多. 这是一种能够做任何事的最高感觉. 时间飞逝, 似乎是2分钟, 却是20分钟. 我在晚上11点左右服用了所有的药物. 我打算整晚醒. 我花了大约5个小时从那里建造一个盒子类型的东西来容纳我的音频线和插头. 我不知道为什么我投入这么多, 但对我来说, 我完成了盒子的时候是凌晨4点12分. 这就是梦的开始. 我开始感到极度恶心. 我在服用7种无耐药的水时会感到恶心. 我开始让自己吐, 因为在我的个人经验中, 我做了这件事,没有任何用处. 我感觉越来越恶心. 我决定躺下,试着睡觉. 我开始非常害怕我的心脏停止跳动. 我心跳非常不规律, 有时甚至会停顿5秒钟. 另一种对我的心率的影响是呼吸时心率会加快到极端. 我尽力保持冷静,保持稳定的呼吸速度. 然后我睡着了. 我真的觉得睡着了之后我进入了昏迷状态. 我一直在听音乐, 我把它放在一个播放列表上, 晚上是非常混乱的. 我躺在那里听每首歌至少四次, 我脑子还在运作,还在工作, 我似乎获得了我从未有过的能力,比如躺在那里睡觉, 想'我不喜欢这首歌'. 我知道这听起来很荒谬, 但这是我唯一能描述的感觉. 第二天早上我醒来,必须去上学. 在我去之前, 我吐了3次, 我睡了我的第一课, 有相同的效果我前一晚. 我感觉自己是我一生中最糟糕的感觉, 我非常反对将任何类似安非他命的药物与任何阿片类药物混合在一起. 我决定再也不要吃药了. 如果我只看看抽里的水, 对于阿德拉尔药也是一样. 我担心那天晚上我会死去. 我有时觉得我永远不会醒来,或者我已经死了, 这非常可怕. 胺类药物 (6), 可 (111), 片类药物 (207): 恶性旅行 (6), 宿醉 / 几天后 (46), 组合 (3), 单独 (16)

Oct 17, 2007. erowid.org/exp/27193 DOSE: T+ 0:00 120 mg oral Amphetamines (pill / tablet) T+ 1:00 50 mg oral Amphetamines (pill / tablet) T+ 2:30 15 mg oral Hydrocodone (pill / tablet) T+ 3:15 7.5 mg oral Hydrocodone (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 139 lb Let me start by saying that I have had a decent amount of exposure to drugs (weed, amphetamines, LSD, hydrocodone, morphine, methylphenidate, nitrous, DXM, opium, and ephedrine) and this is by far the worst experience I have ever had with drugs. I have experimented with Hydrocodone, Amphetamines, and Marijuana the most out of these drugs, so I can say I've been greatly experienced in the usage of the drugs in this report. A friend gave me a full bottle of Adderall (about 120 20mg pills) at school one day. I had taken Adderall XR earlier this year but I lost all tolerance. After 2 weeks of building up a tolerance to 160 MG (8 pills) I lost interest in the drug alone and decided to combine it with another drug. At the time the only other drugs I had was Ephedrine and Hydrocodone. Ephedrine for me is almost exactly like taking Adderall but with more hallucinogenic properties. Anyway I chose to combine Adderall with Hydrocodone. I have taken Hydrocodone for a long time but in increments to where my tolerance stayed around only 2 7.5 MG tablets. I started by taking 6 Adderall and after an hour of no effect I took 3 more and then I began to feel the effects. After getting into the speed high considerably (about 1 and a half hour after taking the extra 3) I decided to take 2 hydrocodones. After taking these I felt very slightly different from the regular speed high. 45 minutes later I took 1 more hydrocodone. Now after taking all these I felt extremely focused. Even more than the Adderall could do alone. It was the upmost feeling of being able to do anything. Time flew by as what seemed to be 2 minutes would be 20 minutes. I took all of the drugs at around 11:00 at night. I planned on staying up all night. I spent about 5 hours from there constructing a box type of thing to hold my audio wires and plugs. I had no idea why I put so much into this but to me it had to be perfect and I had no problem with it being perfect since I had the feeling of being able to achieve absolute perfection in everything. It was 4:12 AM when I finished the box. This is when the nightmare started. I began to feel extremely nauseated. The type of nausea I get when I take 7 hydrocodones with no tolerance. I began to make myself throw up because in my personal experience of taking hydrocodone it helps to throw up if the high turns bad. I did this and had no avail. I felt more and more sick with every minute going by. I decided to lay down and attempt to sleep. While laying there I felt my heart rate and began to become extremely terrified of my heart stopping. The heart beat I had was extremely irregular and it would at times seem to stop for as long as 5 seconds. Another effect this had on my heart rate was it would speed up to an extreme rate while breathing in. I tried my best to keep calm and keep a steady breathing rate. Then I fell asleep. I honestly think that after falling asleep I entered a coma like state. I had been listening to music and I had it on a playlist where it would play all night when I went to sleep. The night was extremely confusing. I layed there and heard every song at least 4 times and felt half way awake even though I felt deep in sleep. My brain was still functioning, still working, and still able to ponder thoughts on an awake level while being asleep. I seemed to gain abilities I never had such as laying there asleep and thinking 'I don't like this song.' then I would wake myself using my thoughts and change the song. I know it sounds rediculous but it is the only description I can give of the feeling. The next morning I woke up and had to go to school. Before I went I threw up 3 times and then 2 more times when I arrived at school. I slept through my first class and had the same effect I had the night before. For the rest of the day I felt the worst I had ever felt in my entire life, and that is saying a lot. I extremely disapprove of mixing any amphetamine-like drugs with any opioids. My experience was so bad that I have decided to never take any pills again. If I so much as look at the hydrocodones in my drawer I began to gag. The same goes for the Adderall pills. I was afraid I would die that night. I felt at times that I would never wake up or that I had died and I was still thinking in a spirit like state. It was extremely frightening.

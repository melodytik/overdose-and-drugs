过去一年里, 我吸了大约600毫克的5-MeO-DMT. 我真的很喜欢这种物质 (与大多数人的感受完全相反), 现在我没有在MAOI下口服, 但我相信我有一些东西要补充. 我最喜欢的摄入方法是服用8到10克粉碎的叙利亚 (当然是为了射击毛伊, 我确实在5分钟内完全溶解 (小心至少吞下), 我一直在晚上11点左右做这件事. 第2个小时完全不同于5-MeO-DMT. 我认为我会说我在网上看到的评论是正确的, 两小时后, 旅行发生了巨大的变化. 更多的像是5-MeO-DMT吸烟, 身体已经平静下来, 我觉得它开始以熟悉的方式冲过我. 我把电脑屏幕和房间的灯光关掉了 (因为任何类型的光都会让我在5-MeO-DMT上感到烦), 接下来的四个小时, 我看到了我一生中最惊人的视觉体验. 眼睛不管是打开还是闭上, 太阳在山上升起 (或落下) 的形象. 我猜到大约在凌晨五点, 便开始吐 (令我的地毯感到丧, 这是我第一次吐除了酒精之外的东西), 所以我下来了,对吗? 哈哈,对了 我感觉到一种完美的平静感, 很多大的标记, 我环顾四周, 我希望使用互联网来找出如何去除地毯上的吐污点. 只有我无法操作一个简单的搜索引擎, 我最终确信互联网不工作, 我在浴室里打开收音机, 这真的是很奇怪. 我听到一个不一致的声, 听起来就像我们的手机, 我听说过这些事情, 但从来没有亲身经历过. 我一刻也没有想到我听到的不是真实的. 我坐在那里,直到我的室友敲门问我是否好,我告诉他是的,好只是. 我跳出,看看时钟,我从上午九点到下午一点在里面. 我问为什么没有人接电话. "什么电话"? 这时我才意识到发生了什么. 我感觉到我只能描述为完全平静和快乐 (比我在滚动中所经历的任何经历都好, 这种情况持续到下午3点, 没有人知道. 我不认为5-MeO-DMT主要影响了这次旅行. 我认为这肯定是因为它背后的力量, 但味道绝对是叙利亚. 虽然视觉效果比较强烈, 但与我使用高剂量的叙利亚与相结合时的效果非常相似. 其次, 由于后续实验 (我的一个朋友也很喜欢吸烟的5-MeO-DMT), 我相信持续时间也主要受到叙利亚的影响. 他以同样的方式摄入了相同的剂量,同一个大小的人. 半小时后, 他开始吐. 他花了一个小时在浴室的地板上吐, 他很快就恢复了自己的状态. 虽然我毫不怀疑, 5-MeO-DMT 是一种口服/下语言活性的MAOI, 我觉得它真正能提供的是两者的混合. 5-MeO-DMT (58), 叙利亚路 (45), 药水 (822): 单独 (16), 组合 (3), 一般 (1)

Total Calm and Bliss Pharmahuasca (Syrian Rue & 5-MeO-DMT) by Highwayman Citation:   Highwayman. "Total Calm and Bliss: An Experience with Pharmahuasca (Syrian Rue & 5-MeO-DMT) (exp20861)". Jan 29, 2003. erowid.org/exp/20861 DOSE: 9.0 g oral Syrian Rue (ground / crushed) 30 mg sublingual 5-MeO-DMT (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 150 lb Well, over the past year I have gone through about 600mg of 5-MeO-DMT by way of smoking. I have come to really love this substance (quite contrary to most people’s feelings), and see it as probably the best (bar none) drug transaction I have ever made. Now I haven't taken it orally w/o a MAOI, but I do believe I have some things to add here. My favorite method of ingestion is to take 8 to 10 grams of crushed Syrian rue (certainly over shooting the target for maoi, more on this later), then 30mgs of 5-MeO-DMT sublingually about 15 minutes after. It indeed does dissolve completely in less than 5 minutes (being careful to swallow a minimum), and I did feel effects within 5 minutes of that. I did this all around 11:00pm. The first 2 hours was totally uncharacteristic of 5-MeO-DMT. I was tripping lightly, slight visual disturbances, and I believe I would say that on-line comments I have seen are correct in the raise of blood pressure (though conversely I wouldn't say I felt it was dangerously high). At about the two-hour mark, the trip changed drastically. Much more like 5-MeO-DMT smoked, and almost instantly. My body had calmed, and I felt it start to wash over me in a familiar way. I turned off my computer monitor and the lights in my room (as light of any type annoys me on 5-MeO-DMT) and sat back on my bed. Over the course of the next 4 hours I saw the most amazing visuals I have ever experienced in my life. Eyes opened or closed, colors were swirling everywhere. For one brief instant, they even formed into what looked like a stained glass window depiction of the sun rising (or falling?) over a hill. This went on until I'm guessing about 5:00am, when I began to vomit (much to my carpet's dismay, first time puking from anything other than alcohol btw), after which I curled up and passed out for I have no idea how long, but I'm guessing about another 3 hours as I was flooded by daylight the moment I opened my eyes. So I'm coming down, right? Heh, right. I feel this perfect sense of calm, lots of big tracers, I look around and notice the vomit after playing with tracers crossed my ceiling for what was probably an hour. I hop up to use the Internet to find how to remove vomit stains from carpet. Only I in no way could operate a simple search engine, I end up convinced the Internet is not working and get up to take a bath. While in the bath I turned on the radio and kicked back. This is honestly where it got weird. While in the bath whenever I ran the water I heard a inconsistent ringing that sounded exactly like our phone, when the water was off I hear whispering voices which I could faintly make out. I had heard of these things before but had never experienced them myself. My mind not wired strait, I didn't think for a moment that what I was hearing wasn't real. I sat in there until my room mate knocks on the door asking if I'm ok, I tell him yes, fine just tripping. I hop out and check the clock and I had been in there from 9:00am to 1:00pm. I went out to discuss it with my roommate, I asked why nobody had gotten the phone the whole time. 'What phone'? That’s when I realized what had been going on. For those last few hours I felt what I could only describe as total calm and bliss (way better than any experience I've had rolling, only none of the neat touchy physical effects, and this was a more relaxed state). This lasted until 3:00pm when it gradually tapered of through the day. --------- Alright, I don't believe the 5-MeO-DMT primarily influenced this trip. I think it was certainly the knock me on my ass power behind it, but the flavor was definitely the Syrian rue. The visuals, though much more intense, were very similar to those I have on high Syrian rue dosages combined with mushrooms. Second, due to a follow-up experiment (with a friend of mine that has also quite the taste for smoked 5-MeO-DMT), I believe the duration was also mainly influenced by the Syrian rue. He ingested the exact dosages in the same manner, same size guy. 30 minutes into it, he began to throw up. He spent an hour on the ground in the bathroom, puking occasionally, after which he spent about 3 hours tripping strong, but not in any way the intensity I had experienced. He came quickly to baseline after that. I guess what I'm getting at is, while I have no doubt at all that 5-MeO-DMT is orally/sublingually active w/o an MAOI, or more specifically the harmala alkaloids. I feel that what it really has to offer comes from mixing the two.

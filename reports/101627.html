我觉得现在是时候再试一次了. 互联网上有一些建议, 因为我最近的实验需要几天的时间来增加剂量, 从与一年前相同的批次中 (在4°C温度下保存) 测量出5. 4毫克TCB- 2 (化盐). 对于S (110磅重的女性) 服用4. 4毫克的药物进行了权衡. 下午12点 每一剂药物都在一个小杯子中"溶解"并被吞 (药物不会迅速溶解,但动力浮在水中). 时间12点05分. 很快就有微妙的迹象表明药物正在起作用. 头,身体 etc... 晚上12点12分 我们要看"". 在接下来的两个小时里, 迷幻状态会增加. 一开始看不太清楚, 更多的困惑, 迷失方向, 难以清晰地思考, 倾向于'深入'的想法. 天气很糟糕. 我们看另一部电影. 在下午2点10分. 视觉幻觉对我来说变得非常强烈. 我不能看一个人脸, 每个脸都有自己的扭曲:有些人额头凸, 另一方面,S报告的视觉幻觉不那么明显,只有极少的闭眼视觉 (CEV). 然而,她有一个强烈的迷幻精神状态. 下午四点 雨停了,我们在公园里散步. 太阳是美丽的, 草和树木也是. 移动很容易 (尽管从沙发上站起来很困难). 我们继续在看电影和散步之间交替. 周六下午进入周六晚上, 虽然眼睛的视觉效果已经减弱, 但我仍然看到极其详细的CEV, 整个晚上S的手都很震动, 这已经发展成大,全身抽. 我的和子非常硬. 开始遭受非常痛苦的胃,以及头痛. 我患有极度的头痛. 我们都服用7.5毫克的佐皮克隆. 我和S的头痛和胃痛还在继续. 显然她正在遭受相当严重的痛苦. 奇怪的是,我的CEV的对称性就破裂了. 在某个时候,S和我睡了. 报道说他们很少. 她已经没有胃痛了, 但我的头痛更严重. 我回去睡觉了. 晚上3点我又醒来, 仍然有严重的头痛. 我喝了两杯水. 我有1000毫克或胺/乙胺,300毫克阿司匹林,100毫克咖啡因和16毫克的可代因. 头痛在接下来的一个小时内消失. 总体而言,这是一次很好的体验, 我发现我的尿非常棕色. 即使经过大量的补水和大量的尿液产生, 这让我感到非常担心. 虽然我不一定会认为这是TCB-2的错, 这,结合S的不适, 幻觉的强度和我的头痛 已使用的证券 TCB-2 (581):一般的 (1), 宿醉 / 几天后 (46), 小组 (2-9) (17)

Oct 22, 2013. erowid.org/exp/101627 DOSE: T+ 0:00 5.4 mg oral TCB-2 (powder / crystals) T+ 15:00 1000 mg oral Acetaminophen T+ 15:00 100 mg oral Caffeine T+ 15:00 16 mg oral Codeine BODY WEIGHT: 75 kg A year following my previous TCB-2 I felt it was time to attempt it again. There were some suggestions on the internet that as my last experiments involved several days of building up the dose, that I may have been suffering under some degree of tolerance. 5.4 mg of TCB-2 (hydrobromide salt) was weighed out from the same batch as a year ago (which had been kept at 4 degrees C). 4.4 mg was weighed out for S (110 lb Female) to take. 12:00pm (noon). Each dose of drug was 'dissolved' in a small shot glass and swallowed (drug did not rapidly dissolve, but the powered floated in the water). 12:05pm. As with last time, rapidly there were subtle hints that the drug was taking effect. Light headedness, body tingles etc... 12:10pm. We being to watch 'Rubber'. Over the next two hours, the psychedelic state builds. Initially not very visual, more confusion, disorientation, and difficulty in thinking clearly and a tendency towards 'profound' thoughts. The weather is terrible. We watch another film. 2:10pm. Visual hallucinations are becoming very strong for me. I can not look at a face without it being heavily distorted. Each face has its own particular distortion: some people have hugely bulging foreheads, other people have vast double chins. S, on the other hand, reports less significant visual hallucinations, and only minimal closed eye visuals (CEVs). However, she has a strong psychedelic mind state. 4:00pm. The rain stops, and we walk in the park. The sun is beautiful, as is the grass and trees. Movement is easy (though getting up off the couch is very difficult). We continue to alternate between watching movies and walking. However, as Saturday afternoon moves into Saturday night, we find drunk people on the street intimidating, and retire to the house. 12:00am The open eye visuals have subsided, but I am still having highly detailed CEVs, the most interesting I have ever seen (for instance, instead of typical fractal, psychedelic patterns, I see fields of belt buckles, or complex patterns made from the edges of childrens' books). The whole night S has had very shaky hands, and this has progressed into large, whole body twitches. My jaw and neck are very stiff. S begins to suffer extremely painful stomach cramps, as well as a headache. I am afflicted with an extreme headache. We both take 7.5 mg of Zopiclone. 3:00am My headache and S's stomach pain and headache continue. She is obviously suffering quite significantly. As soon as I took the zopiclone, curiously, the symmetry of my CEVs broke up. 10:00am At some point, S and I have had some sleep. S reports getting very little. She no longer has stomach pain, but my headache is worse. I go back to sleep. 3:00pm I wake again, still with significant headache. I drink two pints of water. I have 1000 mg or paracetamol/acetaminophen, 300 mg of aspirin, 100 mg of caffeine and 16 mg of codeine. The headache clears over the next hour. The experience was overall a good one thought it was heavily colored by S's discomfort. However, over the following day, I notice my urine is extremely brown. It continues to be for several days, even after significant rehydration and prodigious urine production. This causes me significant concern. While I will not necessarily conclude this was TCB-2s fault, this, combined with S's discomfort, the strength of the hallucinations and my headache means that I do not believe I will push beyond 5.5 mg of TCB-2 again.

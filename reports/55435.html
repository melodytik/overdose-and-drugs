背景信息 在这次经历之前, 我有很多与快乐和速度的经验. 我也曾尝试过酸,,可乐,可代因,大麻 (我住在英属哥伦比亚省):P 心态和环境 这是我第一次娱乐性地服用德拉胺. 两周前我接受了胃部手术. 我的男朋友不在城里,我独自一人在家里和无聊. 我当时的情绪并不舒适, 应该指出我有慢性抑郁症的历史与双极倾向. 我被诊断为患有普遍焦虑症和极端恐慌发作 ((我心率超过180BPM超过3小时并不罕见). 我没有服用抗抑郁药, 因为副作用, 我偶尔服用洛拉塞帕姆 (2毫克) 当我有恐慌. 我还患有贫血 (缺乏红细胞),因此心脏不断跳动 呼吸短促. 剂量和制备方法 我吃了8片德拉胺和180毫克的代因, 几天前我在药店买了Gravol, 为了缓解手术后的恶心. 在加拿大, 我很幸运能从家庭成员那里得到 (相对不限) 的Tylenol 3s, 我当时没有服用任何药物,也许是避孕药, 我每天服用Keflex (铁补充剂) 以及维生素B补充剂治疗贫血. 旅行 我有一个亲密的朋友告诉我, 我在网上读了一点关于它, 尽管90%的休服用者不喜欢它的效果, 我抽了几碗优质的BC,等待它它. 我从来没有像别人说的那样感到疲倦. 我第一次意识到自己已经超出了基线, 我无目的地在家里漫步, 打扫房间,玩电脑,和猫玩, 现在回想起来,我很紧张. 我通常在服用药物时非常小心, 尤其是新药. 我从来没有 (除了这一次) 一个人倒. 尽管我知道这不是一个好主意, 药物注射后大约一个半小时, 我坐在电脑前,转过身来, 看到一颗奇怪的白色果漂浮在我身后的猫上方. 我坐在她旁边,把我的手放在果. 我发现我可以用我的手来移动它. 另一个猫来问候, 她也带着白色的果. 我注意到它也在塔楼上. 现在房间周围看起来有点模糊了... 我之前曾经经历过这种情况. 我觉得我在旅行, 尽管我认为我已经采取了可敬的剂量. 我试图保持冷静, 通过MSN与朋友聊天. 果现在无处不在, 我意识到, 这是由热量引起的. 它覆盖了任何温暖的地方, 我接下来的视觉照顾也来了计算机的塔,小碎形图案,发光的黄色,开始出现. 它们会变形,移动,变成百合花, 我越看它们, 它们似乎越长越. 我不得不去洗手间. 我在镜子里看着自己, 我低头看着我的双臂, 它们变成了线条而不是点, 再变成血的伤口. 我告诉自己不要看他们. 现在,我已经不想再吸毒了. 我很害怕, 并且正在抵抗一个邪恶的恐慌发作. 我过去曾经有过自残问题... 毫无疑问,这种视觉是从压抑的记忆中诞生的. 在电脑室里,地毯上的图案和桌子上的木质变得越来越明显. 它们变形, 相互流动, 我决定躺下一会儿, 希望我能平静下来, 因为我还在震动. 关掉灯,我躺在那里,我的眼睛打开... 由于某种原因, 每当我倒, 我都不记得闭上眼睛. 房间里的阴影, 投射在我的窗户的路灯显然是活的. 它们像蝙蝠,黑暗灵魂或其他东西一样飞过我的头上. 在卧室的角落似乎有一个绿色的阴影. 我看着这雾的绿色云, 我看到它变成了一匹摇马, 一匹微型的绿色摇马... 它爬上了一个夜桌,摇来回,然后冲进墙上... 这很有趣. 我把注意力集中在卧室的地板上, 我住的房子有两层, 卧室在底层. 我看着星星跳舞了一会儿, 这是旅程中唯一愉快的部分. 现在我意识到我的内心对话没有改变, 没有每秒几千个想法, 一次性地冲击着我, 让我感到压倒, 让我处于一种持续的混乱状态. 我猜这就是为什么它被称之为妄想药. 这些是我那天晚上唯一值得注意的OEV. 我觉得这次旅行并不愉快. 我是个天真和不负责任的, 单独使用格拉沃尔作为一种娱乐性药物, 我为此付出了代价, 通过抵制偏执和恐慌 幸运的是,我有很多经验生活在极端的恐慌边缘. 试图向急诊室的医生解释我服用抗恶心药是为了"高", 我不会重复这样的经历, 我也不会推给任何人, 快乐的旅行,和安全:D 其他: 2009年3月5日发布 双酸盐 (17), 代代因 (14):第一次 (2), 组合 (3), 糟糕的旅行 (6), 单独 (16)

Frightened and Confused Dimenhydrinate & Codeine by Niki Citation:   Niki. "Frightened and Confused: An Experience with Dimenhydrinate & Codeine (exp55435)". Erowid.org . Mar 5, 2009. erowid.org/exp/55435 DOSE: 180 mg oral Codeine (pill / tablet) 1800 mg oral Acetaminophen (pill / tablet) 200 mg oral Dimenhydrinate (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 115 lb Background Information Before this experience, I had much experience with extacy and speed. I had also experimented with acid, shrooms, coke(crack and precook), codeine(my drug of choice), marijuana (I live in BC :P You're jealous, I know :D), and crystal meth. Mindset and Setting This was my first time taking dramamine recreationally. I'd undergone stomach surgery 2 weeks earlier. My boyfriend was out of town, I was alone at home and bored. I was not in the most pleasant space of mind, simply looking to 'get high'. It should be noted I have a history of chronic depression with bi polar tendancies. I have been diagnosed with generalized anxiety disorder and extreme panic attacks(heart rate above 180BPM for over 3 hours is not uncommon for me). I do not take antidepressants because of the side effects, I occasionally take Lorazepam (2mg) when I have a panic attack. I am also anemic (lack of red blood cells), so I suffer from constant heart palpitations and shortness of breath. Dosage and Preparation I took 8 tablets of dramamine(Gravol) and 180mg of codeine(Tylenol 3) orally, on an empty stomach. Gravol was obtained through the pharmacy, days earlier, to ease my nausea(from the surgery)... It is not an OTC here in Canada. I am lucky to have a (relatively unlimited) supply of Tylenol 3s from family members who recieve monthly prescriptions(for arthritis, I believe) and are happy to share. I was not taking any medications at the time, perhaps birth control pills, though I can not remember specifically. I take Keflex(iron supplement) daily as well as a vitamin B supplement for the anemia. Trip I was told by a close friend that taking Gravol could 'make me trip out'. I read a little bit about it online, and despite the fact that 90% of people who have taken it recreationally did not enjoy the effects, I went ahead and dosed, out of curiosity. I smoked a few bowls of prime BC bud, waiting for it to kick it. I did not, at anytime, become tired as others have reported. When I first realized I was coming off baseline, I was actually feeling quite hyper. I wandered around my house aimlessly, cleaning up, playing on the computer, playing with the cats, trying to find anything to keep my mind at ease. Looking back, I was rather nervous. I am usually very careful when taking drugs, especially, new ones. I have never(other than this one time) tripped alone. Somehow, I was able to justify taking these drugs despite knowing if was not a good idea, and knowing I would not have a good experience. My first visual came, roughly an hour and a half after dosing. I was sitting at the computer, and turning around, I noticed a strange white jelly floating above my cat who was sitting behind me. I sat down next to her, and placed my hand into the jelly. It wiggled around, and I found I could move it as I desired using my hand. The other cat came over to say hi, and she had the white jelly above her as well. Getting back to the computer, I noticed it(the jelly) was also hovering above the tower. It was somewhat foggy looking, all around the room by now... I've experienced this before with acid. By this point I was feeling a little unsure, as, perhaps I had underestimated the strength of this drug, and, I could feel that I was in for a trip, even though I thought I had taken a respectable dose. I tried to keep distracted and calm, by talking on MSN messenger to some friends. The jelly was everywhere now, and I realized, that it was caused by heat. It covered anywhere which was warm, that being my computer, cats, and myself. My next visual also came care of the computers' tower, little fractal patterns, glowing yellow, began appearing. They would morph and move about, they turned into daffodils which seemed to be growing and shrinking the more I stared at them. At this point, I had to go to the washroom. Looking at myself in the mirror, I noticed the yellow lights accross my body. I looked down at my arms which they covered. They morphed into lines instead of dots, and morphed again, into bloody cuts. They were dripping shiney red blood, this unnerved me, and I told myself not to look at them. By this point, I did not want to be high anymore. I was frightened, and fighting off a wicked panic attack. I have had problems with self mutilation in the past... No doubt this visual was born from surpressed memories. Returning to the computer room, patterns in the carpet and woodgrain of the desk were becoming more obvious. They morphed, and slithered amongst themselves, similar to my LSD experiences. At this point, I went into my bedroom and decided to lay down for awhile, hoping to calm myself, as I was still shaken. Turning out the lights, I layed there with my eyes open... For some reason, whenever I trip, I can never remember to close my eyes. The shadows in the room, cast by street lights by my window were obviously alive. The flew around, like bats, or dark spirits or something, above my head. There also seemed to be a green tint to a corner of the bedroom. I watched this foggy green cloud thing for awhile... I saw it turn into a rocking horse, a miniature green rocking horse... It climbed a nightstand and rocked back and forth, before gallopping off into the wall... Interesting. Concentrating on the floor of the bedroom, I could see out the stars and moon out of the corner of my eyes. It looked like the roof of my house was missing, which is strange because the house I live in is 2 floors, my bedroom being on the bottom floor. I watched the stars dance about for awhile, this was the only enjoyable part of the trip. Throughout the trip, I realize now that my internal dialogue had not changed much as it usually does while under other hallucinogens. Rather than having thousands of thoughts per second, all hitting me at once, and feeling quite overwhelmed, I was instead an empty shell. Gravol stunted my ability to think and comprehend, and left me in a constant state of confusion. I guess that's why it's called a deliriant. These were the only noteable OEVs which I had that night. I did not find this trip enjoyable. I was naive and irresponsible in taking gravol as a recreational drug, alone, and in the mindset I was. I payed for this, by fending off paranoia and panic for the rest of the night... It's a good thing I have alot of experience with living on the edge of hardcore wicked intense panic. It wouldn't have been fun, trying to explain to the doctors in the ER that I had taken antinauseants to 'get high'. I will not repeat this experience, and I don't recommend it to anyone, though if you must try it, at least have a sitter, and be confident & happy in your decision. I hope that in sharing my experience with you, an understanding of the effects, and intensity of this drug could be helpful to someone.

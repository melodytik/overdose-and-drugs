Oct 25, 2006. erowid.org/exp/13617 DOSE: 180 mg oral Pseudoephedrine (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 169 lb Why the fuck did I even do this drug again? I popped 3 60mg pills of Actifed around 9PM. I figured I wouldn’t be going out, so it was ok. I usually like to experiment with drugs when I’m alone. It gives me the chance to find out what drugs do, while I don’t act like an idiot in front of other people. For the next hour, I would feel a constant increase in drowsiness coming on. After that, it only took 30 min To knock me out. I saw a friend online, and I wanted to hang out; unfortunately, it wasn’t going to happen. If I hung out, I’d smoke weed. This would make me feel 10x worse. Instead, I laid down on the futon and tried to relax. I wouldn’t get up from that point on. I noticed myself getting dizzy, and that did it. I was gone, and it was only 10:30. I woke up at 530AM to a head that felt like it was cooked on the inside, so I went outside to smoke a cig. I could feel the hangover. It does feel bad. It feels like I’m at the verge of a headache, but not there yet. Standing there, I couldn’t really pay attention to everything around me. I’d either have to pay attention to the sound of the wind blowing, or getting some eye visuals. Cigarette smoking took a back seat to this. Simple things would amaze me as well. A plane flew over, and I found myself staring at it until it disappeared into the clouds. The hangover the following day isn’t pleasant. I’ve had much better drugs. Acid doesn’t even have this bad of a hangover. Fuck it.

我已经醒了大约两天, 呼吸也很困难. 这并不是因为我长时间起床的结果, 我很难呼吸. 我去看医生, 医生给我开了20毫克的普雷尼松. 我回家后立即服用了一次. 我认为重要的是要指出, 由于我的疾病, 经过一个小时, 我开始感到疲劳. 这种疲劳非常不正常. 感觉强迫和人为, 后来我昏了. 我清楚地记得自己曾经经经过某种虫洞. 转化了我身体现实的黑暗, 变成了非洲沙漠的色彩. 我遇到了类似人类的实体. 它们的沟通方式比我们自己的沟通方式更加奇怪, 仿佛它们用几何和象征来讲故事, 绝对的现实, 但如果我把沙漠里的非洲部落和周六早上的卡通画混在一起, 我认为这是一种正常的沟通方式. 毕竟,这是我自己的脑海里讲述的故事. 这些人正在讲述宗教故事. 他们会跳舞,用自己的声音创造祭品. 她们的舞蹈似乎没有任何规则. 任何动作,任何颜色,任何东西. 它都说出了自己的目的. 仿佛他们正在做的事情正在产生纯粹的情感. 我明白这一切是如何运作的, 听起来很奇怪. 我明白了,现在是时候看看背后是什么. 我看到了一个象征像基督教的十字架, 除了有深度它在这个世界上看不到任何东西. 这位神的真实性透过这个符号闪耀. 这是一个纯粹的情感和理解. 然后,我醒来了. 两个小时过去了. 我撕下了我的厚重的毯子. 我决定停止服用普雷尼松药物, 试图理解这种极其深刻和完全意想不到的经历背后的原因. 我不是不喜欢探索完全陌生和奇怪的, 只是我不想每次在一天结束时寻找一些安慰. 其他: 药品 - 普雷尼松 (226),睡眠剥夺 (140):医疗用途 (47),单独使用 (16)

Fatigue Tripping Prednisone by Paradoxical Citation:   Paradoxical. "Fatigue Tripping: An Experience with Prednisone (exp26426)". Erowid.org . May 24, 2006. erowid.org/exp/26426 DOSE: 20 mg oral Pharms - Prednisone (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 135 lb I had been up for about two days, and been having trouble breathing. This was not a consequence of my being up for so long, I stay up long periods of time almost weekly. However, I was sick, and could hardly breathe. I went into the doctor, and was perscribed 20 milligrams of prednisone. I took one immediately after I got home. I think it is important to note here, that due to my sickness, the normal sleep deprivation experience took a strange turn. About an hour later, I started to get tired. The tiredness was very abnormal. It felt forced and artificial, as if my body has been turned into a feather pillow. I then passed out. I distincly remember falling through a wormhole of some sort. One which transformed the blackness of my physical reality into one which took on the colors of a psychedelic african desert. I met humanlike entities. However, the method in which they communicated was infinitely more bizarre than our own communication-- It was as if they would tell stories with geometry and symbolism, through almost a storybook medium. Absolute reality, but the kind I would find if I mixed african tribes in the desert with the saturday morning cartoons and a color scheme pulled straight from the brightest colors in a crayola box. However, this mode of communication seemed normal to me. It was, after all, my own mind telling the story. These people were telling a story of religion. One in which they would dance and create offerings with their voice. When they danced, no rules of anything seemed to apply. Any motion, any color, anything. It all spoke it's own purpose. As if the things they were doing were creating pure emotion. I understood how this all worked, as bizarre as it sounds. I understood, now it was time to see what was behind it. I saw a symbol like the christian cross, except there was a depth to it unseen in anything of this world. The reality of this god shined through the symbol. One of pure emotion and understanding. Then, I awoke. Two hours had passed. I ripped the heavy coating of blankets from me. Confused and sweating, trying to make sense of the reasons behind that extremely profoud and completely unexpected experience, I decided that I'd better stop taking prednisone. It's not that I don't enjoy exploring the totally alien and bizarre, it's just that I'd rather not have to do it every time I'm looking for some solace at the end of the day.

我第一次尝试用Dilaudid (水合) 几乎是一年前, 只有这样我才能负担得起使用它们, 因为这里每毫克的价格在5到15美元之间. 我唯一使用的药物是静脉注射. 根据我读到的和听到其他用户的说法, 迪劳迪德必须通过注射 (IV,IM,SC) 给药才能充分发挥作用. 除非服用过多 (12-20毫克) 否则,吸入或吞药丸似乎会产生非常轻微的效果. 如果您要支付15-35美元的4毫克药丸, 或是很快无法获得药物, 我反对注射任何未成液体的药物, 但Dilaudid具有独特的成分. 它们在水中分解而无需煮熟. 注意:这仅适用于名牌 (Knoll;一侧有"K",另一侧有"1"",2"",3"或"4"). 水会稍微变色, 但Dilaudid的药理学允许在被粉碎和溶解后注射. 我知道目前唯一可以安全注射的药是这种药, 现在我已经提供了关于这种药物的信息, 我昨天晚上12点左右收到了4毫克的Dilaudid. 药丸本身非常小,圆和黄色. 我找到了一支未使用的1cc注射器和一根草, 我把药丸放进了草里, 折叠起来, 用打火机粉碎了它. 我从注射器上取下子, 将粉末倒入桶中. 接下来的最后一步是抽取大约30个热水单位,然后将注射器动大约30秒 (直到水中没有固体颗粒). 0:00 - - 我记录深红色,并推下子. 只有15秒之后, 我能感觉到我的胸部开始有什么东西. 开始你不确定感觉到底是什么, 但你肯定知道某种东西即将到来. 我唯一能形容的感觉是你的肌肉紧紧紧紧, 这种感觉从胸部开始, 这场热潮持续了大约一分钟, 这种药物的滥用潜力主要来自于它的冲动几乎与海洛因相同. 0:10 - 现在冲动已经消失了,但我身上的温暖感和高兴感是波而来的,特别是在闭上和打开眼睛 (点头) 时. 我认为, 催化剂更令人高兴. 0:45 - 仍然感觉很好, 虽然我已经习惯了这种感觉, 有时忘记我还在高, 1:30 - - 情已经稍微消退, 但仍然非常明显. 2:00-4:00 - 感觉没有疼痛, 但快乐快速消退. 我认为迪劳迪德是一种很好的药物, 这种危险是自言自语的, 但对我来说这是我的小小天堂. 水合摩 (300) 单独 (16), 和习惯性 (10), 回顾 / 摘要 (11), 一般 (1)

A Tiny Yellow Piece Of Heaven Hydromorphone (Dilaudid) by kane Citation:   kane. "A Tiny Yellow Piece Of Heaven: An Experience with Hydromorphone (Dilaudid) (exp21421)". Erowid.org . May 6, 2004. erowid.org/exp/21421 DOSE: 4.0 mg IV Hydromorphone (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 170 lb I first tried Dilaudid (hydromorphone) nearly a year ago, but didn't start using it regularly up until a few months ago. One of my family members gets a prescription for them, which is the only way I could ever afford to use them because around here they go for between $5-15 per milligram. The only method I ever use to ingest the drug is intravenously. From what I have read and heard from other users, Dilaudid has to be given by injection (I.V., I.M., S.C.) for its full effects to be shown. Snorting or swallowing the pills seems have very mild effects unless taken in excess (12-20mg). So, for the most part, if you're going to pay $15-35 for a 4mg pill or will not be able to acquire the drug anytime soon, injection is the only way to go. For the most part, I am against injecting any drug that isn't already in liquid form, but Dilaudid has a unique make-up. It breaks down in water without having to be cooked down. NOTE: this is ONLY true for the name brand (Knoll; has 'K' on one side and '1', '2', '3', or '4' on the other). The water will be slightly discolored, but something about the pharmacology of Dilaudid allows it to be injected after just being crushed and dissolved. This is currently the only pill that I know can be safely injected without being cooked down (strained through cotton, heated, etc.). Now that I've given a little information about the drug, I'll share an experience I recorded yesterday. I received a 4mg Dilaudid yesterday at around 12pm. The pill itself is extremely small, round and yellow. I retrieved an unused 1cc syringe and a straw and head to the bathroom. I inserted the pill into the straw, folded it over, and crushed it with a lighter. After that, I removed the plunger from the syringe and poured the powder down into the barrel. The final step after this was to draw up around 30 units of hot water and shake the syringe for about 30 seconds (until there are no solid particles left in the water). 0:00 - I register dark red and push down the plunger. Just 15 seconds afterwards, I can feel something starting in my chest. At first you're not sure exactly what the feeling is, but you damn sure know SOMETHING is coming. The only way I can describe the feeling is that all your muscles tighten quickly and then immediately become completely relaxed. The feeling starts in your chest and moves throughout your whole body in a matter of seconds. The rush lasts about a minute and can only be described as heaven on earth. Hydromorphone's abuse potential comes primarily from the fact that it's rush is nearly identical to that of heroin. 0:10 - The rush is gone now, but I am left with a warm feeling throughout my body and a euphoria that comes in waves, especially when closing and opening the eyes (nodding). The high is similar to oxycodone, but is not nearly as sedating and hydromorphone is more euphoric in my opinion. 0:45 - Still feeling great, although I've become slightly used to the feeling and sometimes forget that I'm still high, but as soon as I think about it, I feel it again. 1:30 - The euphoria has dimished slightly, but is still very apparent. 2:00-4:00 - Feeling no pain, but the euphoria is subsiding rapidly. In my opinion, Dilaudid is a great drug, but like all opiates is extremely psychologically and physically addictive. It's dangers speak for themselves, but for me it's my little piece of heaven.

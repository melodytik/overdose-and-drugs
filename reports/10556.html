我最近意识到, -瓦莱洛 (GVL) 正在作为一种替代GHB的药物销售. 几年前我读过罗关于-酸 (GHV或4-甲基-GHB) 的报告, 我使用了大约两年的GHB, 但在两年前就停止了, 这让我更糟糕的方式比我喜欢的感觉. 描述GVL表明它是一个比GHB温和的药物. 我很好奇,决定买一瓶来试试. 约一个星期后, 我收到了我的4司瓶. 瓶子中含有3份GVL和2份水的混合物, 标签上写着一个剂量是2.5到5毫升的溶液. 我测量了4毫升并把它放进一个小杯子里. 这种液体闻起来很恶臭, 让我想起了超弹性泡塑料 (你们中的一些人可能还记得这个1970年代的东西, 我加入了一些果汁试图掩盖味道,并喝了. 不幸的是,它的味道和气味一样... 果汁只会让我喝得更多. 我很高兴,因为它触及我的舌头. 这很恶心! 十分钟后, 我开始有点放松, 也许有点头. 这种情绪持续了20到30分钟. 没有高兴感,也没有像GHB一样的中毒感. 肌肉松,头轻微, 这几乎是全部. 两小时后, 我决定再试试. 这一次, 我决定尝试与NaOH反应, 我测量出3毫升的溶液,并添加了一些额外的水. 我加入了一些NaOH (我没有对其进行测量) 它产生了轻微的外热反应. 我得到了正确的pH值,然后添加了一些果汁,喝了它. 味道有所改善,但没有太多. 虽然口感不那么强, 但还是像喝超弹性泡塑料. 在10分钟内, 这也是非常温和. 我睡了两三小时后, 仍然感觉到一些效果, 我决定再次尝试GVL. 这一次我决定服用更大的剂量. 我测量了7毫升并放进杯子里. 没有试图用果汁掩盖它, 我只添加了一些水, 一次性地了下来... 效果在10分钟内就会显现出来, 这也是非常温和. 事实上,我没有发现7毫升和4毫升之间有什么区别... 这与我读到的内容一致. 显然大约5毫升以上的剂量不会产生任何更强的效果. 知道这种东西在大脑中会做什么是很有趣的. 经过一个小时左右,我上床睡着了. 没有GVL, 睡觉也没有那么容易. 总而言之,GVL和GHV完全不同于GHB! 这更像是草根. 这可能会让肌肉放松, 也可能有助于缓解焦虑. 如果您在清醒时服用它, 它不会让您入睡. 这里没有约会强奸的机会! 它可能在化学基础上相似, 它可能与GHB受体结合, 没有任何娱乐潜力. 这有什么用处吗? 我不知道. 首先必须记住,这必须被视为一种实验药物. 我不知道有没有正式的人类研究, 我不相信有动物研究已经进行了 美国食品和药物管理局认为GVL是一种安全的食品添加剂, 没有办法知道它是否安全或可能存在的风险. 如果我不得不猜,我会说它可能是可以偶尔使用... 但这只是一个猜测. 如果它是安全的, 那么它可能有某些用途. 一个可能的用途可能是作为GHB成者解毒计划的一部分. 我在互联网上看到了一些成功使用GVL的人. 也许可以给GHB成者逐渐降低GVL或GHV的剂量, 另一种用途可能是作为一种温和的,非中毒的治疗急性焦虑发作. 不过,这不是我首选的焦虑缓解药. 我怀疑它对严重甚至中度焦虑发作有任何用处. 如果它具有与GHB相同的生长激素释放特性, 也许它可以像GHB一样用作健美或抗衰老药. 如果它与GHB有相同的特性, 缺乏显著的精神活性可能是个真正的优点, 试试这很有趣, 我不后悔买它, 但我怀疑瓶子会持续我很长很长一段时间, 这不是坏东西,只是我有更好的事情要做. 其他: GVL (214), GHV (213):一个人 (16),回顾 / 摘要 (11)

Super Elastic Bubble Plastic GVL & GHV by Murple Citation:   Murple. "Super Elastic Bubble Plastic: An Experience with GVL & GHV (exp10556)". Erowid.org . Nov 13, 2001. erowid.org/exp/10556 DOSE: GVL (liquid) GHV (liquid) BODY WEIGHT: 170 lb I recently became aware that gamma-valerolactone (GVL) was being marketed as a GHB replacement. I had read Rhodium's report on gamma-hydroxyvalerate (GHV or 4-methyl-GHB) several years before, which had given me a passing interest in trying it. I used GHB for a period of about 2 years, but stopped about 2 years before because I came to look at it as sort of a waste of time. It gets me more fucked up in an out-of-it way than I like to feel. Descriptions of GVL indicated that it was a much milder drug than GHB. Curiosity overcame me and I decided to buy a bottle to try. About a week later, I received my 4 ounce bottle. The bottle contained a mixture of 3 parts GVL to 2 parts water, with a little potassium sorbate added as a preservative. The label said that a dose was 2.5 to 5 milliliters of the solution. I measured out 4ml and put it into a small glass. The liquid smelled nasty... it reminded me very much of Super Elastic Bubble Plastic (some of you may remember this stuff from the 1970s, you'd squeeze a little ball of plasticky gel from a tube, put a wad on the end of a straw, blow into the straw, and it'd expand into big multicolored bubbles). I added some juice to try to cover the taste, and drank it. Unfortunately, it tasted like it smelled... all that the juice did was give me more volume to drink. I cringed as soon as it touched my tongue. Nasty! Within 10 minutes I began to feel somewhat relaxed and maybe a little light-headed. This built in intensity for another 20 or 30 minutes. There was no euphoria, no GHB-like intoxication. My muscles were relaxed, I felt very mildly light-headed, and that was pretty much all. About 2 hours later I decided to try taking some more. This time, I decided to try reacting it with NaOH to make GHV. I measured out 3ml of the solution and added a little extra water. I put in a little NaOH (I didn't weigh it) and it created a mild exothermic reaction. I got the pH right and then added some juice and drank it. The taste was improved somewhat, but not much. It had less bite but was still like drinking Super Elastic Bubble Plastic. Again, the effects came on within about 10 minutes. Again, it was very mild. I went to bed after about 2 or 3 hours still feeling some of the effects, so I can't say how long the effects lasted. Four days later, I decided to try GVL again. This time I decided to take a larger dose. I measured out 7ml and put it in a glass. Instead of trying to mask it with juice I just added a little water and chugged it down in one shot... if you can't cover the taste, might as well just get it over as quick as possible. The effects were felt in about 10 minutes and built up for the next 20 minutes, as before. Again, it was very mild. In fact, I could detect no difference at all between 7ml and 4ml... this is in line with what I've read. Apparently doses above about 5ml do not give any stronger effects. It would be interesting to know what this stuff does in the brain. After about an hour I got in bed and fell asleep. It wasn't really any easier to sleep than it would've been without GVL. In summary, GVL and GHV are not at all like GHB! It's more comparable to something like valerian root. It might make a good muscle relaxer, and it might help for mild anxiety. It might possibly even help someone who was slightly tired fall asleep, but if you took it when wide awake it would not put you to sleep. Not a chance of date rape here! It may be similar on a chemical basis, and it may bind to GHB receptors, but the subjective experience is nothing like GHB. It has no recreational potential at all. Is it of any use? I don't know. First of all it must be kept in mind that this must be considered an experimental drug. I know of no formal human research on it, and I don't believe any animal studies have been conducted to find out what the risks of long-term use as a drug might be. GVL is considered a safe food additive by the FDA, but when used in this way it is in doses far far lower than its doses as a drug. There's no way to know if it is safe or what the risks may be. If I had to guess, I would say it probably is OK to use occasionally... but this is just a guess. If it is safe, then it may have some uses. One possible use could be as part of a detox program for GHB addicts. I have seen reports on the internet from people who have used GVL successfully for this purpose. Perhaps gradually decreasing doses of GVL or GHV could be given to GHB addicts to lessen withdrawals, while keeping them essentially sober. Another use may be as a mild, non-intoxicating remedy for acute anxiety attacks. It wouldn't be my first choice for an anxiolytic though. I doubt it would be of any use for severe or even moderate anxiety attacks. Finally, if it shares GHB's growth hormone releasing properties, perhaps it could be used as a bodybuilding or anti-aging drug like GHB. If it shares this property of GHB, its lack of significant psychoactivity would likely be a real plus, as you could take it before a work-out and not have to worry about passing out in the gym. It was interesting to try it, and not at all what I expected. I don't regret buying it, but I suspect the bottle will last me for a long long long time, and I doubt I'll buy more. Its not bad stuff, its just that I've got better things to do.

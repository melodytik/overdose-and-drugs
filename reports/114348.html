Apr 28, 2020. erowid.org/exp/114348 DOSE: 50 - 150 mg oral 3-Methylmethcathinone (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 18 st Prior experience: 4-MMC, MDMA, MDA, Meth, Amphetamine, Dexedrine, cocaine etc. This stuff was vile. The vendor has always sold what's advertised in the past however. Sourced early 2020. Prior to this no drugs for over a year except 2.5mg diazepam per day. Trial 1 150mg held in mouth then swallowed 8am, alert in 10 mins then about 60 minutes of jittery and unpleasant stimulation followed by digestive issues, cramping and intermittent nausea, a pretty strong headache and an unpleasant spaced out feeling leading into the next day. Slept that night pretty normally though. Trial 2 (2 days later) 50mg swallowed on an empty stomach, short period of mild pleasant stimulation 30 mins, followed by equally strong as before spaced out feeling, tremor, coming and going mild headache, digestive issues, temperature regulation issues (hot then cold etc). The rest went to landfill. Real bottom of the barrel drug, general toxic feeling from the start. Appearance was slightly off white crystals and powder, smell very similar to 4MMC.

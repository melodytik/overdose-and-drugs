Jul 8, 2026. erowid.org/exp/119856 DOSE: 3 mg sublingual Noopept (powder / crystals) 300 mg oral Vitamins - Choline (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 190 lb A Very Pleasant First Experience Previous Drug Experience: I wouldn’t call myself an avid drug user, but I’ve tried most of the readily-available, non-“hard” ones in my country: plenty of weed, some shrooms, a bit of LSD. I’ve also had one DMT trip and have taken prescription ADHD meds (ritalin, concerta, vyvanse) a few times recreationally, both for partying and for focus. This year I remembered a bad experience I’d had on my first-ever shroom trip, and connected it to some depression and a general sense of feeling lost in my life that developed right after it. It was like I’d hurt myself badly and only realized five years after the fact. Since that realization I’ve begun to slowly work through and get over it, and as such the neuro-generative and -protective qualities of noopept intrigued me. Mindset: I was at the end of a long stretch of time off from work, and was enjoying it. I was reading a lot and feeling content. I was in a generally good, unhurried and happy state of mind. It was a sunny day in late spring and I had it completely free. My brother got some noopept in the mail a few days before, and he’d tried it and liked it, so I thought I’d give it a whirl. Experience: I took 3mg sublingually and went to make breakfast. This was maybe not the best choice, since I was salivating a lot trying to keep the powder under my tongue. I was aiming to keep it there 20 minutes or so. I made my breakfast and just had to stare at it, but eventually swallowed and sat down to eat. I also had 300mg of choline in water along with my meal. After I’d finished, it occurred to me that I didn’t really know what this substance I’d put in my body was, and opened up the noopept wikipedia page. It kicked in just about then, 30 minutes or so after taking it, a subtle focus that arrived with a slight tickling sensation in my head. I read wikipedia for an hour and a half, getting really into the pharmacology of the drug. This isn’t something I normally do, but I felt fantastic learning and diving into the subject. The writing was more technical than I’m used to, but I was taking the time to understand each concept and turn things over in my brain until I felt I understood them. I eventually tore myself away from reading and did everything in my apartment I’d been putting off: all the dishes, my laundry, sweeping. There was a hint of the usual “I’ll-just-do-that-later” that usually comes with house chores, but I had a happy motivation that allowed me to ignore it and just get things done. My mind was looking for things to do, and I had some writing I’d been putting off, so I sat down to it. Normally there’d be a lot of things competing for my attention, especially as I was writing on my laptop. As soon as I hit a momentary stall, or the writing gets a little hard, I’ll usually click away, to youtube or instagram or whatever. But I was locked in, and wrote for almost two hours. My brother had described feeling some brain fog when he’d taken noopept for the first time, and while I could see what he was getting at it wasn’t the same unpleasant stupid feeling that I’ve had after some psychedelic trips. It was more that there was nothing occupying my mind except for what I was focusing on - no background thoughts, no nagging need to attend to any screen-based addiction. It was different from the study drugs I’ve taken in the past, too. The sense of uncertainty I’d felt on those, of manic focus, wasn’t there, replaced instead by a calm and natural-feeling attention. It felt like my brain was working as intended It felt like my brain was working as intended , that all the distractions and damage I’ve subordinated and subjected it to had fallen away. I went to a department store after this to run some errands, which, after the pleasant morning of solitude and focus I’d had, felt like a misuse of the drug. It was around this time, 5 or so hours after, that I stopped feeling its effects as strongly. After Effects: I had an incredibly pleasant rest of my day, and felt like I carried forward the sense of motivation and clarity that I’d had in the morning. I was able to remember most of the salient points from my wikipedia binge, so it appears like I was actually learning something, not just deluding myself. I had no headache or any adverse effects at all, although that might be due to the choline I took. For the next few days I felt like I could enunciate incredibly well, and that my speech was clearer than usual. Conclusion: A fantastic experience, and a drug that I will certainly try again. While I felt my brain was working better, it didn’t feel like my base intelligence had been raised. It gave me a boost in motivation to do things that I felt mattered to me, and the focus to get them done without distraction. Pairs well with wikipedia, writing, and I would expect any activity that requires sustained attention. As for the neurogenerative properties and healing from my shroom trip a half decade ago: It’ll definitely take more doses to know if it’s going to help me with that.

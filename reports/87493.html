我有幸在我的生活中享受各种各样的药物. 我更喜欢轻微充满活力和开放思想的东西. 最近我一直在研究组合, 我最好的朋友M在网上买了一些Olmifon. 他赞扬这些东西超过任何东西, 他玩的智能药物至少和我一样. 他建议我同时服用两种药, 才能获得最大的效果. 我还有一瓶皮拉粉, 上午8点45分 (T+0) 周六早上, 我醒来时, 脑子里有很多事情要做. 我早上醒来后就服用了两片奥米. 我是一个习惯的咖啡饮用者,所以我从床上滚出来, 我已经喝完第一杯咖啡, 我和M一起吃早餐. 我已经喝完了第二杯咖啡, 我开始感觉到头部有轻微的变化, 他说一个小时后就能感觉到完全的效果; 这似乎是对的. 我通常在工作日喝两杯咖啡, 我从来没有感觉到真正的动机! 我觉得我刚刚喝了三行非常好的可乐. 只有咖啡因和阿德拉菲尼尔, 身体的活力就变得荒谬了! 咖啡因本身我从来没有感觉到一个可感知的声, 但这是惊人的! 添加皮拉只会放大这种效果. 我和M分享了一碗'紫色果'休息. 我了一种含有5.02克 (百厘米级) 的可怕味道的皮拉塞坦. 我用第四杯咖啡喝下去 (时间+3点). 通常,大麻会让我的头脑从纯粹的赛车思想下降一级, 但阿德拉菲尼尔会让我保持清晰. 皮拉已经开始起作用. 我已经好几周没有服用它了, 因为它令人厌恶的塑性苦味 (比任何安非他命药更糟), 我在帮助M做家务时, 与他进行了一次非常好的理论物理对话. 我们比平时更快地进行对话. 像可卡因一样的兴奋感稍微消退, 只是被一种更愉快的四方混合物所取代. 时间+4点 通过脑力数学和小知识测试彼此的心理能力 (我们在一起享受任何毒品体验时都会这样做, 我们继续修复他的房子, 我觉得自己非常有成效和动力. 我能够非常快速准确地思考, 我觉得因为我体内的四种物质都是帮助我专注的, 我享受着超级专注的状态, 我通常是个很分散的脑袋, 有着ADD倾向, 知道自己有家务要做, 我回家打扫厨房和卧室. 我开始感受到巨大的内在紧张, 绝对是药物引起的, 我需要大约75分钟的时间来听我自己的声音告诉我该做什么. 这绝对是我一天中最愉快的75分钟, 我从来没有这么放松. 我开始相信这种紧张会持续到我的日常生活, 我把一个小碗给自己,然后洗. 我从那里走出来时感觉很新鲜. 我坐在电脑前玩几个小时的网络第一人称射击游戏. 我注意到我的反应时间不过是顶级的, 我开始做晚餐:希腊轮回三明治. 我最好的朋友们都聚集在M的家里吃饭. 我们在吃饭时,像往常一样,抽烟. 尽管我们吸了大量大麻, 时间+~15:15 (午夜) 我无法形容我对自己不的动机的惊. 我已经清理了M的整个厨房,下楼的两个房间, 我知道酒精只会降低我的兴奋,所以我避免了. 我们继续吸更多的草, 包括同样高品质的肥, 虽然清晰度仍然存在, 但它正在消失. 现在皮拉已经完全消失, 咖啡因大部分都消失了, 时间+~18:00 我在1:30 (T+16:45) 左右回家, 从那时起, 我一直在不断地添加我写的不完整歌曲. 我没有任何疲劳感, 也没有任何睡眠的欲望或理由, 时间+~21:00 我还能玩一些第一人称射击游戏. 我现在的表现不如以前, 太阳已经升起,我还完全清醒. 我也没有因此感到烦, 我可以告诉我的大脑有点疲从过度的清醒时间开始, 星期天上午10点 艾德拉菲尼尔的效果刚刚开始减弱, 我已经醒了, 洗了几件衣服, 时间+~27:15 艾德拉菲尼尔的效果大多已经消失, 被妄想和精神疲劳所超越. 我在过去27小时内所经历的压力表明我的更高的推理区域不想工作. 我觉得我的反应时间很慢. 我觉得自己已经很累了, 我睡得很深, 只有四个半小时, 周日剩下的时间, 我做了我能做的学业. 晚餐时间左右, 我的大脑正常运转, 我在周一凌晨1点睡觉, 结论:阿德拉菲尼尔不是一个笑话. 它具有强烈的高兴潜力, 这也是我服用过的最激励药物. 实际上, 超级动机的自然状态似乎被打开了. 我在那一天做了很多工作, 我很确定如果我再服用更多的阿德拉菲尼尔, 我读过人们可以连续5天在阿德拉菲尼尔上保持注意力或动机. 添加皮拉会增强所有药物的效果, 咖啡因的含量远远超过眼睛所能看到的. 它与阿德拉菲尼尔的相互作用令人惊且有趣. 我确实感觉到一种可卡因般的浮动感, 我推我今天使用的所有东西, 尤其是阿德拉菲尼尔. 这种药物真的是一种奇药. 其他: 阿德拉菲尼尔 (216),皮拉塞坦 (95):一般 (1),组合 (3),小组 (2-9) (17)

Dec 23, 2012. erowid.org/exp/87493 DOSE: T+ 0:00 600 mg oral Adrafinil (pill / tablet) T+ 0:25 1 cup oral Caffeine T+ 0:50 1 cup oral Caffeine T+ 1:30 1 cup oral Caffeine T+ 2:45 0.5 bowls smoked Cannabis T+ 2:45 5 g oral Piracetam (powder / crystals) T+ 3:00 1 cup oral Caffeine T+ 7:10 1 bowl smoked Cannabis BODY WEIGHT: 165 lb I've had the privilege to enjoy a wide variety of drugs in my life so far. My preference tends to the slightly energizing and mind-opening. My experience with nootropics is fairly extensive too and I've recently been working with combinations to find what I like. My best friend M had purchased some Olmifon from an online vendor. He praised the stuff more than anything and he's played with the smart drugs at least as much as I. So he gave me two pills to try, recommending I take them at the same time for maximum effect. I also had a container of piracetam powder that I figured I would add just for a kick. 8:45 am (T+ 0) On a Saturday morning, I wake up with several chores on my plate. I take both Olmifon pills just after waking. I'm a habitual coffee drinker, so I roll out of bed and make a tall pot of double brew. T+ 0:25 I've finished drinking my first cup of coffee while standing over the stove cooking a bacon and potato breakfast. T+ 0:50 I sit down to breakfast with M. I've finished my second cup of coffee and I'm beginning to feel a slight head change, most likely from the adrafinil. He said full effects would be noticed by an hour; this seemed to add up. T+ 1:30 After a third cup of coffee I'm thoroughly awake (I normally drink 2 on weekdays) and the adrafinil is creeping into my motivation center, working some serious magic. I've never felt this genuinely motivated! T+ 2:00 I feel like I just did 3 lines of some really good coke. The body buzz is ridiculous for just caffeine and adrafinil! With caffeine alone I never feel a perceptible buzz, but this is amazing! Adding piracetam would only amplify this effect. T+ 2:45 After moving around lots of furniture and cleaning half of the upstairs main room, M and I share a bowl of 'purple berry' for a break. I mix up a strongly flavored drink containing 5.02 g (centigram-scale-assured) of vile-tasting piracetam. I chug it with chagrin and wash it down with a fourth and final cup of coffee (at ~T+ 3:00). Normally weed pleasantly numbs my mind one level down from pure racing thoughts, but the adrafinil punches straight through with a persistent clarity. T+ 3:10 The piracetam is hitting already. I haven't taken it in weeks because of the disgusting plasticky bitterness (far worse than any amphetamine), and the razor sharp mental edge is slicing though every puff of mental fog. I'd been having a remarkably good conversation about theoretical physics with M while helping him with chores. We jump through the conversation faster than we normally would. The cocaine-like buzz wore off slightly only to be replaced by a more pleasurable 4-way mix of cognitively active drugs. T+ 4:00 M and I have been testing each other's mental abilities (something we do when we enjoy any drug experience together, LSD included) with mental math and trivia, and we were shocking ourselves and each other with our temporary mental nimbleness. We continue working on his house, now mostly finished. T+ 5:00 (1:45 pm) I feel astoundingly productive and motivated. I'm able to think extremely fast and accurately, or conversely focus all of my attention to performing a single task. I suppose that since all four of the substances in my body are focus aides to me on their own, I'm enjoying a hyper-focused state where I have complete control over my attention. I'm typically very scatter-brained and have ADD tendencies, but I feel completely on point. Knowing I have chores to do at my own apartment, I go home and clean the kitchen and my bedroom. T+ 5:35 I was beginning to feel an enormous amount of unrooted inner tension, definitely drug-induced, so I run through my extended yoga routine. This takes me about 75 minutes listening to a recorded track of my own voice telling me what to do. This is by far the most enjoyable 75 minutes of my day, and perhaps even my week. I have NEVER been this relaxed. I was beginning to believe the mounting tension would persist through my routine, but if anything this mix helped me relax further. T+ 7:10 I toke a small bowl to myself and then take a shower. I emerge feeling very refreshed. Happy with my productivity for the day, I sit down at the computer and play a few hours of online first person shooter. I notice my reaction time is nothing short of top notch, and my accuracy is up above my average. T+ 10:15 (7:00 pm) Still feeling as energized as I did 7 hours ago, I start cooking dinner: Greek gyro sandwiches. My whole group of best friends all gather at M's place to eat. We smoke repeated bowls, like always, as we eat. My head hasn't stopped feeling completely clear and still piracetam-sharp, despite the large amount of weed we smoke. T+ ~15:15 (midnight) I can't describe my amazement at my unrelenting motivation. I've cleaned M's entire kitchen, two rooms downstairs, and finished cleaning the main upstairs room while everyone else was downstairs drinking. I know that alcohol would only cut down my up-ness, so I refrain. We continue smoking even more weed, including a fat blunt of the same high quality, and by now I'm very, very high. The clarity is still there, though, but it is fading. The piracetam has worn completely off by now, and most of the caffeine from earlier is gone, but the adrafinil persists. T+ ~18:00 I went home around 1:30 (T+ 16:45) and since then I've been working non-stop on adding to incomplete songs I've written. I feel no inkling of tiredness and I have no desire or reason to sleep, so I do this for a while. T+ ~21:00 Still fully awake and alert, I play some more first person shooter. I'm not doing as well as earlier, but I'm still doing very well. T+ 22:15 The sun is up and I'm still fully awake. I'm not getting annoyed with it either, I just feel consistently motivated and alert. I can tell my brain is slightly tired from the beginning of excessive waking hours, but I only barely notice it. T+ 25:15 (10:00 am Sunday) The adrafinil is just now beginning to wear off, in combination with a slight delirium. I'm awake enough to do a couple loads of laundry, and I watch Fear and Loathing in Las Vegas on netflix. T+ ~27:15 The adrafinil effect is mostly gone, being overtaken by delirium and mental exhaustion. The amount of stress my mind was under for the last 27 hours shows in how much my higher reasoning regions don't want to work. Math and complex planning are both impossible, and my reaction time feels very slow. T+ 28:30 Tired of being awake and no longer able to focus, I go to bed. I sleep very deeply and restfully for only about 4 and a half hours, waking up at about 5 pm quite alert. For the rest of Sunday, I did what schoolwork I could. Around dinner time my brain was working at normal levels of speed and focus. I went to bed at 1 am Monday morning and woke up at 8:30 to get ready for classes like I always do. Conclusion: adrafinil is no joke. It has a strong euphoric potential that's opened up by caffeine. It's also the most motivating drug I've ever taken. It truthfully seemed like the natural state of being hyper motivated was simply switched on. I was very productive that day, and filled it with non-stop activities. I'm pretty sure that if I had redosed some more adrafinil I would have stayed awake much longer. I've read that people can stay up 5 days straight on adrafinil without losing focus or motivation. Adding piracetam to the mix potentiates all drugs, and weed is always great to add to any new drug experience. As for caffeine, it is more than meets the eye. The interaction it has with adrafinil is surprising and interesting. I really honestly did feel a pronounced cocaine-like floaty feeling and buzz from only those two in concert, not to mention being supremely motivated. I recommend all of the stuff I used on this day, especially adrafinil. This stuff is truly a wonder drug.

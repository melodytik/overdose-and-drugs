最近我发现自己面临着学习一些外星人的逻辑. 我当然指的是为SAT考试学习. 我在工作中度过了10年的时间, 我有四周的时间学习, 购买了官方的SAT学习指南和计算机学习工具. 我很惊地发现, 维诺可以在处方药店买到, 我相信维诺是非常有帮助的. 我不记得在90年代, 虽然我当时并没有经常服用, 旧的帽子是蓝色的,在纸包装. 新的盖子是透明的凝和瓶子. 两者都装有白色粉末. 我相信蓝色帽子中的粉末比平面凝帽更具有晶体性质, 我在试图通过舌下摄入时发现了这一点, 我每天至少学习5-8个小时, 阅读, 写作, 计算机化实践考试, 我最近完成了一项证书课程, 不过,SAT是一项奇怪的考试, 不是关于你在学术科目中的能力, 而是关于你学习考试制造者的逻辑和考试形式的怪癖的能力. 这部剧有9个节目,大部分时间为25分钟. 一个人必须忙,我通常不会在思考时这样做. 我在服用芬波西丁之前研究了大约一周. 我无法确定它在前两天是否有影响. 到了第三次, 我注意到精神力增加了. 我每天服用3次5毫克的维诺. 首先在早上喝咖啡或茶,其他的是在吃饭前15-20分钟. 积极的效果似乎在一周内增加, 到了第二周中旬, 我开始有睡眠困难, 我在练习中自然会更快地完成测试, 我不能说到什么程度. 我确实感觉到自己的能量水平有所提高. 我也更加乐观, 我觉得维诺会影响情绪. 在第3周, 我注意到我比平常更容易勃起. 网上没有提到这一点. 我感觉有点,有点疯狂. 必须说,我处于极度紧张的状态, 我确切地确定的是精神精力和专注度的增加. 我觉得这可能会提升情绪, 我感觉自己有点快了. 在测试周内,效果保持稳定. 考试的第一天,我感到自信. 我比任何练习测试都更容易通过了测试. 我坚持在休息时间吃70%以上的可可巧克力和干香. 我在那一天停止服用维诺. 我觉得它已经完成了它的工作, 因为它有着明确的身体效应, 我经常服用胆和维生素B, 我有第二次测试预定下周, 因为我觉得我做得很好, 我继续每天学习2-3个小时. 经过3天,我发现自己有点不舒服. 我一直认为我看到小孩子出我的眼角, 只是转身发现这是一个影子或脚. 这一天发生了好几次. 我心情也不是很好,但我很气. 我曾经经历过几次严重的脑震荡, 第二次考试让我感到疲倦,头. 我认为这是因为在一个状态的流动. 这是一个暂时的状态, 但肯定低于基线. 我还没有收到第二次测试的结果, 考试不仅让我觉得考试更加困难, 必须记住这些都是标准考试, 我已经完成了口头部分, 经过一周到10天的时间, 我所说的效应并非令人衰弱,只是奇怪的. 我会再次服用芬波西丁. 其他:

On the Scholastic Aptitude Test Vinpocetine by Agog Citation:   Agog. "On the Scholastic Aptitude Test: An Experience with Vinpocetine (exp49501)". Erowid.org . May 24, 2006. erowid.org/exp/49501 DOSE: oral Melatonin (daily) 15 mg oral Vinpocetine (daily) oral Codeine (liquid) BODY WEIGHT: 180 lb Recently I found my self faced with learning some alien logic. I am of course referring to studying for the SAT examination. I'm returning to school after a 10 year excursion in the work force. I had about 4 weeks to study, and purchased both the official SAT study guide and a computer study aid. In addition I purchased a bottle of 5mg caps of vinpocetine from my local health food store. I was surprised to see that vinpocetine was available over the counter as I had purchased some in the early 90s and had to acquire it from over seas. I believe vinpocetine was a great help. I don't recall getting much of an effect from the stuff back in the 90s. However I wasn't taking it regularly at that time as I was on a regiment of piracitam. The old caps were blue and in a foil pack. The new caps are clear gell and come in a bottle. Both were filled with a white powder. I believe the powder in the blue caps had more of a crystalline nature than the plane gell caps, which seem to have allot of inert material as filler. I discovered this while attempting sublingual ingestion, unsuccessfully. I was studying at least 5-8 hours a day, reading taking paper and computerized practice tests as well as doing research on the web. I had recently completed a course of study for a certificate program, so my skills were up to par. The SAT however is a bizarre test, not so much of your abilities in academic subjects as it is in your ability to learn the test makers logic and the quirks of its form. It is 9 timed sections most are 25min long. One must rush, something I don't usually do while thinking. I had studied for about a week before taking the vinpocetine. I was unable to determine if it had an effect for the first 2 days. By the third I noticed increased mental stamina. I was taking 5mg vinpocetine 3 times daily. The first with coffee or tea in the morning the others 15-20min before eating. The positive effects seemed to ramp up for about one week, then even out. By the middle of the second week I was having some trouble sleeping and would take melatonin at midnight which seemed to help. I naturally got faster at the tests by practicing, but I believe vinpocetine was of some benefit. To what degree I can not say. There was certainly an increase in my energy level. I was also more optimistic, I feel vinpocetine may effect mood. In week 3 I noticed I was getting allot more unbidden erections than normal. No mention of this on the web. I was feeling kind of jacked up, and a little manic. It must be said that I was in a high stress situation, and was intensely focused on my studies. All I determine with certainty is an increase in mental energy and focus. I feel it is likely to elevate mood and increase reaction time. I felt a little quicker on the uptake. The effects were stable up through test week. The morning of the SAT came and I felt confident. I riped through the test easier than any of the practice tests. Insistently I ate 70+% cacao chocolate and dried bananas during the break. I stopped taking vinpocetine that day. I felt it had done it's job, and as it had a definite physical effect may not be good to take continuously. I take choline and vitamin B regularly, but my body told me to stop with the vinpocetine. I had a second test scheduled the next week and as I felt confident I'd done well thought it might be interesting to take it sans vinpocetine. I continued to study 2-3 hours a day. After 3 days I noticed feeling a little out of sorts.Lack of the up feeling, not so much fatigue. I kept thinking I saw little children out of the corner of my eye, only to turn and find it was a shadow or a foot stool. This happened several times a day. My mood also was not so good, but I'm moody. I have had a couple of severe concussions and remember the feeling of things being not quite right which persisted for days or more after. The second test I took I felt tired, fogy headed. I believe it was from being in a state of flux. A temporary state but certainly below baseline. I have yet to receive the results of my second test, but I can say with certainty they will be lower than those of the first. Not only did the test feel more difficult, and it must be remembered these are standardized tests, but I was unable to complete several sections of the verbal sections in time. Even on my first practice test I finished the verbal sections with time to spare. After a week to 10 days I feel I was back to baseline. The effects i'm talking about here were not debilitating, just odd. I would take vinpocetine again.

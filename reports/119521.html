Feb 13, 2026. erowid.org/exp/119521 DOSE: T+ 0:00 300 ug oral 1D-LSD (blotter / tab) T+ 2:30 1.2 mg oral 25E-NBOH (blotter / tab) T+ 2:30 2 mg oral Pyrazolam T+ 0:00 repeated vaporized Nicotine BODY WEIGHT: 66 kg Soooo I made a really dumb mistake and ended up tripping on 300ug of 1D-LSD and 25E-NBOH. I haven't seen any trip reports on this specific combo, and despite the incredible stupidity involved in its occurrence, I figured I'd make a report in case anyone in future decides to take this combo (Don't). So, basically I've been planning a 300ug trip for awhile now, did all my usual pre trip rituals and what not, feeling pretty damn good. 00:00 I drop the two 150ug tabs of 1D-LSD, under my tongue as usual. 01:00 Nothing, not even a comeup, feeling a bit worried that these tabs might have been bunk or unevenly dosed. It happens, I've had bunk tabs before, so it wouldn't be a complete surprise if they were. I decided to have a couple beers to pass the time. 02:00 Still nothing, at this point I'm fairly convinced they are bunk, I've been really excited to trip tonight and feeling quite disappointed. This is where I fucked up, I broke the golden rule of LSD, **Do not take more if you think your tabs are bunk.** I don't know why, I should have know better, but not wanting to waste more perfectly good LSD and being very experienced with 25E-NBOH, I took a single 1.2mg tab not wanting to waste more perfectly good LSD and being very experienced with 25E-NBOH, I took a single 1.2mg tab . I guess I figured that I should at least have some sort of trip. 02:30 This is when I realized I fucked up. # The LSD was not bunk. It felt as if the 25E-NBOH triggered the LSD to finally start working. Whenever I take a very high dose of a psychedelic, the first sign that I'm in for a very intense trip is my vision starts "vibrating". At this point the 25E-NBOH is in full swing, normally this would be just a fairly standard trip, but quickly everything started vibrating, everything around me started shaking, like a tuning fork being hit with a hammer. I was not prepared for this and knew that it was time for a benzo, I took 2mg of pyrazolam which I'm fairly sure stopped me from having a much worse experience later on. 03:00 From dropping the 25E-NBOH to now, roughly 30 minutes later, the entire 300ug dose of LSD is peaking. Its as if the NBOH accelerated the LSDs comeup into a single simultaneous comeup that was more intense than any other psyche I've done. The comeup from 500ug of LSD I had done previously doesn't compare. 04:00 The visuals were bizarre, like a mixture of organic all encompassing geometry fighting complex geometric patterns, anything that resembled an ordinary object was morphing and twisting into unnatural shapes unrecognizable from their original form. My entire visual field was filled with visuals of varying types. Except people. I had a show on when I dropped the LSD which was still playing, if I looked at the people in the show they appeared seemingly normal, the background behind them filled with geometry and patterns that blended and spread beyond the bounds of the TV and onto the walls behind it. But if I stared too long, they too began to distort, they're eyes narrowing and turning black, the visuals surrounding them morphing into waves emanating from the person, vibrating intensely. 05:00 No matter in what direction I look, nothing is solid, everything is flowing like the sea under a gentle breeze causing waves to slowly travel across the surface. There are posters on a wall in my bedroom, mostly artwork, band posters, etc that I like to look at whilst tripping. Normally the colors just pop, shapes move, etc. Not this time, this time the artwork was extending outwards beyond the paper, the pink floyd poster for dark side of the moon in particular was breathtaking, the prism rotating and fluctuating in size, the light diffracting outwards and being projected onto surfaces around my room. On LSD colours are pretty, but on 25E-NBOH, colours learn more towards neon highlights. Combined? All I can say is that my normally dark room felt like it was lit up by beautiful neon lights on every surface. 06:00 - 08:00 The visuals are beginning to tame, this whole time I've been vaping every so often this whole time I've been vaping every so often , but now I've become infatuated by the vapor hanging in the air, watching it flow like a liquid through the air above me, like a sea of colourful waves, the vapor occasionally twisting and contorting into recognizable and abstract shapes. 09:00 It feels as if the influence of the 25E-NBOH has finally waned and the LSD has taken over resulting in more conventional and recognizable LSD visuals. Content with having experienced the more impressive/intense visuals from before, I settled into spending the rest of my trip doing my usual routine of watching something funny on my TV and enjoying some snacks. ??:?? Eventually I managed to fall asleep without much difficulty. Overall, it was an exceptionally intense and unique trip, despite not being mentally prepared for such an experience, I enjoyed it but I don't think I'd do it again. It wasn't a standard high dose LSD trip, it feels like LSD and 25E-NBOH are extremely synergistic in ways that I hadn't considered previously and that 25E-NBOH might possibly potentiate LSD or vice versa. Funnily enough, having now done some reading online, I've seen people ask about this combo and people warning about vasoconstriction, heart rate, anxiety and other physical side effects, I didn't experience any real negative physical side effects during this trip. Mentally and visually it was overwhelming at times, but at no point did I feel overwhelming anxiety or physically unwell like others had warned about.

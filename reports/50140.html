Sometimes extremely high doses reported are errors rather than actual doses used.] Best High off of a Benzo I first want to start off by saying that I have been doing prescription drugs for a while now and mostly opiates and benzodiazepines and the rare barbiturates that I can come across every so often. Oxycodone, hydrocodone, codeine and demerol were the opiates I used(and still do) most often. Dilaudids are great whenever they are available and morphine is even better. Valium, xanax, serax and ativan are the benzos I do and temazepam was one that I wasn't quite familiar with. It was in the middle of July of 2005 on a hot afternoon and I called my dealer to see what 'he had'. Told me he had nothing but some 30 mg temazepams. I asked him if they were any good as I had never tried them before. He told me that they were better then valium, but took longer to kick in. So I was decided to give them a try and so I went and bought all ten that he had left. So I got back home and decided to save them for later. Later came and I was thinking how many should I take? I normally take about 3 10mg valiums and so I thought I should try 5 of the temmazies (I thought they can't be that much stronger then valium). I took em and sat down to watch TV. In about 45 minutes I started to feel the typical 'benzo buzz', but very intensely. Much more intense then both valium and xanax. I felt very heavy, light-headed and just extremely relaxed and completely worry free. I then called my girlfriend and started to talk to her. As I was talking I realized that my mouth felt quite heavy and my speech was slurred. It was all so nice and I felt so good and content, words just cannot explain the feeling. After that I don't know what happened. I woke up on the same couch that I was laying in when I was talking to my girlfriend. I had apparently put the phone back in it's place and went back to the couch. The television was still on, perhaps I was watching tv and fell asleep. I just cannot remember the entire conversation and what happened after that. Anyways, when I woke up the next day I didn't really have a hangover or anything. It was definitely the best benzo I had ever experienced. The high was intense and pleasurable. Today, temazepam is the benzo that I seek. It's actually not that hard to come by and I've been able to get some ever since that day. I read up on it and it's definitely not the strongest benzo mg for mg, but in it's effects at the doses equilvalent to other benzos it is unmatched. It was beautiful.

我已经使用了Datura (很久以前, 没有价值), 大麻 (当然), , dxm (在我知道胺之前, 哈哈) 和许多其他东西. 昨晚是相当疯狂的. 我没有服用任何药物, 我现在像个混蛋一样, 我找到了我哥哥的吸入器. 1:10 - - 吸了7口气, 忍了一会儿. 我当时躺在床上. 我开始听到钢琴的节奏, 意识到节奏是我的心跳. 我的心跳很快, 在我的身体里发出节奏. 我可以感觉到节奏. 1:11 增加剂量到10次. 我感觉到了最初的冲动, 同样的基本情况,只是更强的头冲动. 在银行里有1:12-13的吸气. 我告诉自己这是最后一次, 这次我没有记住吸入了多少次. 可能25到30岁. 我忍受了很长时间. 这已经成为我有史以来最强烈的冲动. 巨大的紫色和绿色环覆盖着我的墙壁, 我能听到,感觉到,看到, 味道几乎像糖果一样, 很奶油. 我重复了几次, 但不是同样的强度, 我已经开始胃痛了. 听起来就像从一个古老的音箱里出来的声音. 这有点酷. 我从来没有经历过这样的头. 我只是为了听到自己笑而笑. 我的声音听起来很陌生. 优点:短暂,强烈,便宜,易于获得. 缺点:恶心,今天早上我醒来时像奥齐一样. 这太疯狂了. 我身体总是有动, 希望我没有造成任何永久的伤害. 现在是中午,我不那么. 我敢打这会消失. 时间表: 28074 药物 - 艾布特罗尔 (241):一个人 (16),宿醉 / 几天后 (46),音乐讨论 (22),第一次 (2)

Temporary Parkinson's Disease Albuterol by Skinny Puppy Citation:   Skinny Puppy. "Temporary Parkinson's Disease: An Experience with Albuterol (exp28074)". Erowid.org . Jun 16, 2007. erowid.org/exp/28074 DOSE: smoked Cannabis (daily) repeated inhaled Pharms - Albuterol BODY WEIGHT: 144 lb I've used Datura (long time ago, not worth it), weed (of course), shrooms, dxm (before I knew about ketamine, hehehe) and many other things. Last night was pretty crazy. I am not on any medication, but I rock the ganja every day. I'm shaking like a bastard now, but I think that will go away eventually. I found my brother's inhaler. 1:10-Took 7 puffs, held it in for a while. I was lying in my bed at the time. I started hearing a rhythm with a piano, and realized that the rhythm was my heartbeat. My heart was beating hard, making a rhythm in my body. I could feel the rhythm. 1:11-Up the dosage to 10 puffs. I feel the initial rush, and it is considerably stronger. Same basic thing, just a stronger head rush. 1:12-13 puffs in the bank. I tell myself that this is the last session, but it feels crazy good. 11:15-I didn't keep track of how many inhales I took this time. Must have been 25 or 30. I held it in for a good amount of time. This has become the strongest rush I have ever felt. My heart was beating out of my chest, giant purple and green rings were covering my walls, and I could hear, feel, see, and taste the rush flowing through my body. Tasted almost marshmallowy, very creamy. I repeated this a few more times, but not with the same intensity, so I called it quits. My stomach was starting to hurt anyway. I noticed it made music sound very strange, made it sound like it was coming out of a shitty old boom box. It was kinda cool. I never experienced such a head rush. I was laughing just for the sake of hearing myself laugh. My voice sounded foreign. Upsides: Short lived, intense, cheap and easy to get. Downsides: Nausea, and this morning I woke up shaking like Ozzy. This is crazy. My body is in constant spastic motion, especially my hands. Hope I haven't done any permanent damage. It is now noon, and I am shaking less. I bet this will go away.

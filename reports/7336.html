以下描述可能是我做过最愚蠢,最粗心的事情. 8:30 时间 摄入2个Soma. 我了它们,这是我见过的最糟糕的味道药丸. 没有感觉什么从前2个药丸,所以我吃一个. 让我感到丧,所以我吃了3个. 这无疑是一个错误. 五分钟后我感觉到一个强烈的声, 我知道它不可能是我刚刚服用的3个药丸, 在接下来的45分钟里, 坐在椅子上, 凝视着太空, 一个真正的死头的感觉. 一个完全空白的头脑. 完全没有思考. 坐下来,一个小时就飞过了, 我在椅子上摇摆, 这就是所谓的"Soma Shuffle". 站起来走一下,走路真是太有趣了. 只有这样才能享受这种乐趣. 但我坐下来继续进行索马混音. 我会在15分钟内昏倒. 三点钟 在急诊室醒来. 我感到非常困惑. 我记得从我昏倒到现在的时间, 我不得不喝木炭. 我完全没有意识到这一点. 两个人抓住我的手臂, 带我走了好300英尺, 我也经历了一些. 这些信息来自于看到我在上面的人. 希望有人能从我的错误中学习. 有些药物需要耐心. 有时很难保持耐心. 这是可以理解的. 如果您要服用这些或其他任何药物, 我建议您先服用一粒药,然后两粒, 对于大多数人来说, 这很明显, 但我相信还有其他人, 其他: 药品 - 卡里索普罗多尔 (186):火车毁和旅行灾难 (7),健康问题 (27),孤独 (16)

I chewed them up, it's the worst tasting pill I've ever encountered. 9:30 Not feeling anything from the first 2 pills so I eat one more. 9:40 Getting frustrated so I eat 3 more. This was a mistake no doubt. 5 minutes later I was feeling a STRONG buzz and I knew it coudln't be the 3 pills I just took so I realize I'm fucked, but I don't worry about it. 9:45 Spend the next 45 minutes sitting in a chair staring off into space and occsionally laughing. A real dead head feeling. A completely blank mind. Complete lack of thought. 10:30 Get up and walk around a bit, sit down and an hour flies bye with me rockin around in a mostly circular motion in my chair. This is called the 'Soma Shuffle.' 11:30 Get up and walk around a bit, walking is a lotta fun. It's about the only way to enjoy this sort of buzz. But I sit down and continue the Soma Shuffle. I pass out with 15 more minutes of this. 3:00 Wake up in the ER. Feeling very confused. I can remember maybe 3 flashes of mental images from the time I passed out to now. I had to drink charcoal. Some additional things that maybe should be noted is that I was COMPLETELY out of it. Two people took me by the arms and walked me a good 300 feet and I don't remember it and never woke up. I also went through some convulsions. This information came from people who saw me on it. Hopefully someone will learn from my mistakes. Some drugs can require patience. It's hard to be patient sometimes. This is understandable. If you're gonna take these or anything else I suggest starting with one pill and then two and so on but only after you're positive the last dose you took is completely out of your system, and stop increasing the dosage once you get a good effect. This is probaly obvious to most people though, but I'm sure there's someone else out there stupid enough that maybe they needed to be told this.

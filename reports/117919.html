Nov 17, 2025. erowid.org/exp/117919 DOSE: 70 mg oral Lisdexamfetamine (daily) IM Pharms - Buprenorphine BODY WEIGHT: 152 lb This is not a "trip report" per se with specific times, this is just a report of my overall opinion on this substance. I started taking prescribed 30mg Vyvanse on (USA) 1/31/24. At first it would just make me anxious and feel like I was out of it, but a few days later that would pass and I wouldn't see any benefits out of it. About a week later, my doctor upped the dose to 50mg and this seemed to increase my attention span and impulse control, but it still wasn't enough. So yesterday, 02/22/2024, my doctor ended up prescribing the maximum allowed 70mg. I can say that today I can see many more benefits. I am able to hold a conversation without wanting to immediately stop it and leave. I am able to focus on a single task without wanting to do 5 other things at once. I am able to hear music like it was the first time I was ever hearing it. I don't have that "out of it" feeling, and I feel motivated and sure of myself. It is a nice feeling for a change. I am worried about tolerance though, and am debating taking weekends off or even just a single day out of the week to make it build a bit slower. Vyvanse does take quite a long time to take effect, that is the one drawback that I have about this drug. I'm considering asking my doctor to prescribe an initial 5mg dextroamphetamine tablet to carry me until the Vyvanse starts to take effect. The nice thing about Vyvanse for me is that I don't really get a comedown, the stimulation just gently wears off and there is no dysphoria or "crash". I'm not sure if this is due to my Sublocade injections or if it's just a clean(er) feeling drug than other amphetamines like meth and adderall, which I have done in the past and I always get a crash with those; although I was never on sublocade at the times I used those drugs. Make sure to use all drugs responsibly and if possible, do not abuse them otherwise that can come back to bite you and trust me, it is not worth it.

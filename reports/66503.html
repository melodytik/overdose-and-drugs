我20多岁,身高超过6英尺. 2007 年我接受了双眼激光手术. 在手术前, 我所做的一切就是前一天晚上喝了几杯酒, 我在外科手术的第二天早上, 肚子空, 睡了4个小时, 没有多种维生素,没有乙L卡尼丁. 直接从床上,到汽车,到外科医生的办公室. 我填写了表格,看了护理说明,所有这些混乱. 接下来,一个杯子里有两粒药. 我问了一些关于他们的问题, 看护师/助理似乎对此不太了解. 我以为他们是瓦. 我解释说我对这种药物有点奇怪, 我不情愿地服用了其中一粒药. 另一位医生介入, 我解释了药物的情况, 她告诉我服用另一种药物, 我说不谢谢, 然后她告诉我带着那个的纸杯, 里面有药物, 带我去外科等待室. 我开始感觉到一个药正在着我. 我感到有点放松. 我不想在手术期间感觉自己需要尿尿. 在走到所,回来时, 情况有点空虚. 我觉得这应该足以做好手术. 医生从另一位护士那里听说我只服用了一半的药物. 他告诉我他真的希望我服用这两种药. 我解释了我的担忧, 他解释了他的, 告诉我它不是太强的剂量, 他明白我在哪里说"失去控制". 我放弃了,解开了纸杯, 并按照医生的指示. 其他1毫克的阿提万也下去了. 在5到10分钟之后, 我记得我在墙上. 时间有点错了,我不知道我着墙多久. 我清楚地记得有人叫我的名字去手术室, 我站起来, 确保我有我的支票簿, 我很高兴他们问我的名字. 我不记得他们问我的另外两个问题是什么, 答案是肯定的, 我笑了笑, 第一个眼睛很好,没有问题. 然而,当第二只眼睛的时间到来时, 幻觉开始了. 眼睛里有一些麻木剂, 医生告诉我不要再动我的眼睛. 我告诉他光线在移动. 他告诉我灯被螺栓到这个,这是螺栓到这个,和螺栓到地板,和重量XXXXX. 灯没有移动. 由于某种原因, 医生解释说不是, 我认为他还说如果发生地震, 他和他的助手们会感觉到它. 医生终于把吸管放在我的眼睛上, 说"我抓住了" (我认为...), 闻到我的眼球在燃烧, 一个助手帮助我站起来, 脸上带着微笑. 接下来我被带到一个躺椅上. 在那里一切都很好. 我很有趣地玩了遥控器, 想知道按对我的视力有什么影响 (这是一个按摩椅). 我一直试图把座椅靠向后方, 我一遍又一遍地尝试着, 我认为一个护士听到我, 带我到另一个躺椅. 她解释说, 另一个躺椅是不会回去的, 我得到了一些好东西. 一个装有巧克力糖果, 另一个装有零食. 我拒绝了巧克力,而选择了的,脆脆的东西. 我还记得看看配料是否含有乳制品 (乳制品有时会让我生病). 虽然黄油和类似的东西可以. 从那时起,事情就变得模糊了. 我完成了一个包裹. 那天早上我没有什么吃,我想要更多的零食. 在服用阿提凡时说话, 在那一刻,我服用了第一粒 (1mg) 药后大概1小时,第二粒 (1mg) 药后30-45分钟. 我环顾四周, 发现装有脆脆, 的食物的容器. 我从椅子上走出来, 通过它来看. 我拿着一些饼干,和票混合 (所有都是"零食大小"包裹. 我问房间里的另一个人, 他之前曾经接受过治疗, 吃了点心后, 我开始注意到我的嘴巴真的很干. 另外, 盐真的很突出. 我知道我是一个扭曲的. 我喝了一些水,这让一切变得更好. 我还记得吃了混合物, 当我正在时, 这花费了很多精力. 我认为,我不想丢掉袋子,所以我把它放在桌子上,睡着了... 现在我不得不依靠自己的记忆, 我会记住一些事, 想想, 我真的这么说了吗? 我是不是这么做? 没有. 我记得被带到一个房间, 让外科医生可以通过某种医疗设备来看我的眼睛, 我不记得有人带我回到椅子上. 后来我回忆起的是,依靠模糊的记忆. 如果我真的说了或做了这些事情, 我试着嘲笑它... 我记得我眼睛附近有一张纸巾, 有人告诉我不要碰眼睛. 我不知道我什么时候戴上了太阳镜, 我觉得我说过我在做爱方面遇到的问题. 我觉得我谈论我的阴茎 (如果我不谈论我的阴茎, 我使用过的药物,我认识的人,等等. 总的来说,我觉得我对几乎不认识的人说了很多私人事情. 我仍然不确定为什么我这么做了, 我也不知道有多少人着说话, 我被摧毁了. 我模糊地回忆起有人检查我的脉. 我认为医生后来问我,在我的半睡昏迷我有没有其他药物当天. 他一定问过几次,或者大声说,我记得. 我认为我说了一些"我吸了大麻,木种子,狂喜"..."不,你今天有没有吸过毒品?" 我认为医生说了更多之后. 我认为既然这些问题困扰着我的休息,我告诉他他可以取我的血液,或取我的尿液并测试它,我不在乎. 我模糊地回忆起我在服用第一粒药后2个半小时被接走. 我不记得走出大楼,我不记得走回市中心街道到停车场. 我确实记得停车罚单的验证机器. 我没有回忆回家的路程. 我记得我看着前门说:"今天没有包裹. 医生在晚上七点打电话检查我. 我当时完全不记得Lorazepam的那些愚蠢的效果. 尽管如此,我感到充满活力. 那天晚上,我在两个小时内喝了四杯酒, 最后一段,是第二天. 我在拉斯基眼镜检查后的第二天去看, 由于眼神和笑声, 我知道我可能在手术后表现得很. 晚上9点左右, 在家里, 我开始感到焦虑, 焦虑,接近恐慌. 我有一些应急药物, 用于这种情况, 我服用了 1⁄4毫克, 拉西克手术后的一周, 我有另一个检查愈合的约会. 我非常尬地站在那里. 我觉得,上周我本可以脱衣服在所有员工面前赤身裸体走来走去, 我注意到更多的微笑, 有些员工似乎试图住微笑, 我在访问期间解释说我不喜欢阿提万, 一点点可以走很长一段路. 我想要离开,迅速. 我几乎不认识的人,知道谁知道我,不确定我说的是什么,有多少是真的,夸大了,等等. 从现在开始, 毒剂对我来说只能是低剂量. 任何接近2毫克的阿提万, 我一定会有车来接我从手术尽快, 我也曾经因酒精而昏迷, 在那段时间里, 我也谈到了各种各样的东西. 我也曾经在认识的人面前拿出我的阴茎. 没有什么比阿提万的经验有太大的不同. 我一生中曾经有5到10次这样的昏迷. 虽然不多,但足以让我宁愿避开他们! 洛兹帕姆药也类似, 只不过它持续时间较短, 给我带来了一些轻微的幻觉, 根据我所读到的, 影响我有 (外向,幻觉) 是相当强大的剂量我有. 在老年人,儿童,中枢神经系统问题患者,体内还含有其他药物的患者,GABA通讯受损者等中更为常见. 我不确定我在哪里适合. 我从这次经历中学到了一些东西. 我从来没有在不知多少人面前说过这么多随机的话, 他们也许告诉了其他人, 据我所知, 虽然回去做检查有点尬, 写下这次旅行的经历有助于. 我不是一个隐秘的人, 然而我通常不会和任何人谈论某些事情. 有时我会嘲笑它,有时它会困扰我. 我绝对不会在陌生人面前重复这句话. 其他: 药房 - 洛拉泽 (79):第一次 (2),不好的旅行 (6),医院 (36)

Dec 10, 2007. erowid.org/exp/66503 DOSE: T+ 0:00 1.0 mg oral Pharms - Lorazepam (pill / tablet) T+ 0:22 1.0 mg oral Pharms - Lorazepam (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 140 lb I’m in my mid 20’s, over 6 foot tall. 2007 I underwent custom Lasik surgery on both my eyes. Prior to the surgery, all I had done drug wise was had a few beers the night before, and during the week I had been taking my usual multivitamin and 2mg of Acetyl L Carnitine I take daily. The morning of the surgery I went to the office on an empty stomach, and 4 hours of sleep. No multivitamin, no Acetyl L Carnitine. Straight from bed, to the car, to the surgeon's office. I filled out the forms, went over the care instructions, all that mess. Next, arrived a cup with 2 pills. I asked a bit about them, the nurse/assistant didn't seem to know much about them. I thought they were Valium. I explained I act a bit peculiar on that drug, and would rather take half. I reluctantly took one of the 2 pills. Another medical person stepped in, I explained what was going on with the pills, she told me to take the other, I said no thanks, she then told me to take the crumpled paper cup, with the pill inside, with me to the surgical waiting area. I was starting to feel the one pill creeping up on me. I felt a bit relaxed. Around that time I figured I should take a break for the toilet, as I don't want to feel like I need to piss during the surgery. During the walk to the toilet, and back, things were a bit spacey. My feeling was, “Good, this should be enough for the surgery”. The surgeon heard from another nurse that I only took half of the meds. He told me he would really rather I take both pills. I explained my concern, he explained his, told me it wasn’t too strong of a dose, only 2mg of Ativan, I should be fine. He understood where I was coming from about “losing control”. I gave in, uncrumpled the paper cup, and followed the doctor’s orders. Down went the other 1mg of Ativan. Things start to blur 5-10 minutes from there. I remember spacing out at the wall. Time was a bit off, I have no idea how long I was staring at the wall. I recall clearly hearing my name being called to the surgery area, standing up, and making sure I had my checkbook, and everything on my person so they did not grow legs and walk off. I was a bit giggly when they were asking questions about what my name was. I can’t recall what the other 2 questions were they asked me, though I did ask them, “You’re asking me these questions to make sure I’m coherent, right”. They said yes, and I giggled with a sense of accomplishment as to figuring out why the questions were asked. The first eye went fine, no problems there. However, when it came time for the second eye, the hallucinations started. After getting some numbing drops in that eye, the light I was to stare at started to move. The doctor told me to please stop moving my eye. I told him the light was moving. He told me the light was bolted to this, that is bolted to this, and bolted to the floor, and weighs XXXXX. The light isn’t moving. For some reason, my mind then went with, “Maybe it’s an earthquake”. This seemed to be the only explanation, I seriously thought, “Well, it must me an earthquake.” The doctor explained it wasn’t, I think he also said if there was an earthquake he and his assistants would be feeling it. From there, things get even more blurry, and all I recall clearly was being told to stop moving my eye, the doctor finally getting the suction thing on my eye and saying “Gotcha” (I think…), smelling my eyeball burning, and an assistant helping me up, with a smile on his face. Next I was led to a recliner. All was ok there. I had an interesting time playing with the remote, figuring out what the buttons did with my blurry vision (It was a massage recliner). I kept trying to lean the recliner back, but the back wouldn’t go. I tried over and over again, thinking maybe I could get it to go back THIS time. I think a nurse heard me, and led me to another recliner. There, she explained that the other recliner wasn’t going back because of the wall behind it…… I was offered treats. It appeared one container had chocolate candies, and the other salty snacks. I said “No” to the chocolate, and went for the salty, crispy stuff. I recall looking at the ingredients to see if they included dairy (Dairy makes me sick sometimes). Whey and such in dry foods doesn’t bother me much, though butterfat and similar can. From there, things blur a bit. I finish one package. I had nothing to eat that morning, and I wanted more snacks. It took a lot of energy to speak while on this Ativan. At the moment, it had probably been 1 hour after my first pill (1mg), and 30-45 min after my second (1mg). I looked around and spotted the container with the crispy, salty food packages. I make my way out of the recliner, and look through it. I grab some sort of cookie, and chex mix (all were “snack size” packages). I ask another person in the room, who was treated prior to me if he wanted anything from over here, he said no, and I returned to my recliner with more treats. After eating treats, I began to notice my mouth was really dry. Also, the salt REALLY stood out. I knew I was twisted. I drank some water I had, that made it all the better. I recall eating the chex mix, and as I was chewing, having trouble chewing. It took a lot of energy. I figured, I didn’t want to drop the bag, so I place it on a table, and nod out….. This is the moment when I have to rely on my memory an awful lot, as I can’t recall much of what I said or did after this. Things have been coming to me later, 24-48 hours after this I would remember bits and pieces and think, “Did I really say that? Did I do that? Oh no….” I recall being led to a room so the surgeon could look at my eyes through medical device of some sort, I’m guessing to make sure the flaps in my eye were seated properly. I don’t recall being led back to the recliner. What I recall after that, is relying on a blurred memory. I’m a bit embarrassed, it is going to take me a while to get over some of it if I actually said or did the things I did, hahaha. I try to laugh about it… I think I cried at some point, I vaguely recall a tissue near my eyes and someone telling me not to touch my eyes. I have no idea when I got the sunglasses placed on my eyes, but they were there when my ride picked me up. I THINK I talked about problems I’ve had with getting laid. I have a feeling I spoke about my penis (It’s never a genuinely fucked up trip if I don’t talk about my penis, or take it out for many to see!). Drugs I’ve done, people I’ve known, etc, etc. Overall, I have a feeling I spoke about A LOT of personal stuff, to people I barely know. I’m still not certain why I did this, where I thought I was, etc. I’m also not sure how much was mumbled, or spoken clearly. I was smashed. I vaguely recall someone checking my pulse. I think the doctor later asked me, in my half sleeping stupor had I done any other drugs that day. He must have asked this a few times, or said it loudly, as I recall it. I think I said something to the effect of, “I’ve smoked weed, Woodrose seeds, ecstasy…' “No, have you done any drugs today???” “No”. I think the dr. said more after that. I think since the questions were bothering my rest, I told him he can take my blood, or take my piss and test it, I don’t care. I vaguely recall being picked up about 2 – 2 1/2 hours after that first pill. I don’t recall walking out of the building, I don’t recall walking back through the streets of downtown to the parking area. I do recall the validation machine for the parking ticket. I recall nothing of the drive home. I recall looking at the front door and saying, “No packages today”. From there I slept on a couch for about 5 hours, when the doctor called at 7pm to check on me. At that time I recalled nothing of the silly Lorazepam effects. I felt energetic, however. That night around midnight I drank 4 beers in 2 hours, and took it easy. Last bit, was the day after. I went for my day after LASIK check-up, and I could tell due to looks I was getting, and laughter, that I probably did act a fool after surgery. Around 9pm, at home, I started feeling anxious, like I have from some psychedelic drugs. Anxiety, bordering on panic attack. I have some emergency Xanax for such situations, and I took ¼ mg to take the edge off, and ease that anxiety away. The week after Lasik, I had another appointment to check on healing. I was very embarrassed to be there. I feel, last week I could have stripped down and walked around naked in front of all the employees, and felt less embarrassed, than some of the stuff I may have spoke about during my stupor. During this appointment, I noticed more smiles, and some employees who seemed to be TRYING to hold back smiles, but with the smile just PUSHING through. I explained during that visit I did not like the Ativan, and I am sensitive to benzos. A little goes a long way. I wanted to leave, fast. People I barely know, knowing who knows what about me, not being sure exactly what I spoke of, how much was true, exaggerated, etc. Long story short, benzos, from now on, will only be low dose for me. Anything close to 2mg of Ativan, I will be certain to have a ride ready to pick me up from surgery ASAP, or it will be consumed around friends and such. This was similar to times I’ve blacked out from alcohol, and had fragments of the black-out come to me later on. During those times I’ve also spoken about all sorts of stuff. I’ve also once pulled out my penis in front of a few people I’d known for a while. Nothing too much different than this Ativan experience. This was very similar to those alcohol blackouts I’ve had, and I’ve had 5-10 of them during my life. Not many, but enough that I prefer to avoid them! Loazepam was similar, only it lasted a shorter amount of time, gave me a few mild hallucinations, and left me very anxious 36 hours later (popping 0.25mg of Xanax calmed me down). From what I’ve read, the effects I had (Extroversion, hallucinations) were pretty strong for the dose I had. More common in elderly, children, people with CNS problems, people who also have other drugs in their system, damaged GABA communication, etc. I’m not sure where I fit in. I learned a bit from this experience. I’ve never said so much random stuff in front of who knows how many people that I only knew for that day. They may have told others, and for all I know most of the practice’s employees probably heard something of me. It’s a bit embarrassing to go back for check-ups, however I try to laugh about it. Writing this trip experience helps. I’m not a secretive a person, however I generally don’t talk about certain things to anyone. Sometimes I laugh about it, other times it bothers me. I more than likely would not repeat this again around strangers.

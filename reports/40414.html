这是我第一次使用DON, 据PiHkAL的报道,这可能是DOB的更刺激,更少的迷幻版本. 我开始服用2.5mg (低于PiHKaL建议的3. 0mg初始剂量). 这里是: 这是在空腹摄影的. 在前一天晚上被一些GBL到脸上之后, 这可能是由于Lactone被报道的多巴胺抑制作用而产生或没有影响. 感觉完全正常. 一点钟:开始感到轻微的张和血压升高. 在这个时候,没有别的. 据说这次的上升时间大约为3到3个半小时. 也注意到一些不愉快的"胃效应". 胃部已经平静, 没有真正的副作用, 我注意到刺激增加, 增加了一点点混乱. 颜色看起来非常明亮, 但没有视觉. 类似于低剂量 (1.5毫克) 的DOB, 我不觉得有无可控制的冲动去做像安非他命那样的事情, 但也没有像一样超级冷静的快乐. 我几乎感觉到一种'内心的平静', 只是一个很好的温和高, 只是稍微被我的腿震动. 虽然在拉伸之后感觉不错. 我也在这里喝了一些茶, 这总是一个很好的提升. 我刚刚从淋浴中走出来, 感觉清新, 放松. 这让我想起了第四或第五天的麻醉, 当我真的不能得到旋转了, 更多的只是让我不给一个该死的和真的柔和;-) 也有点精神混乱与DOB这通常让我搞我做的一切. 这比我试过的其他类型的胺更令人高兴 (例外是2C-I和2C-B), 但它并没有像STP在高峰期那样让我过度刺激. 仍然得到"笑"有点像任何胺 (这只是一个愚蠢的笑很多人得到我认为是过度刺激的结果;我得到它与2C-B,DOB和STP). 现在是我今天的动作的时候了, 我看起来不太糟糕当我在这个和不像DOB, 这里允许我更好地思考和清晰, 任何类似于迷幻体验的东西都消失了, 剩下的只是半高兴的刺激, 我对此感到非常丧, 但并没有像 DOB 一样糟糕, 现在是T+12:00 刺激正在逐渐减弱. 我去当地药店买了克隆泽, 服用了半片1毫克. 这提供了我与DOB描述的协同效应. 克洛纳泽帕是一种非常深刻的情感体验, 然后我吸了很多大麻, 又服用了1毫克克洛纳泽帕姆, 无论如何,我们的结论是: 是安非他命的替代品, 比麻醉药更省钱, 我可以想象副作用可能会有点粗在4.5-5.0毫克但我几乎没有STP和DOB的副作用. 可以成为一个很棒的派对毒品. 和平,保持安全 时间表: 40414 2007年4月26日发布 唐 (375):一个人 (16),

First Time Low-Dose Trial DON by Smoker Citation:   Smoker. "First Time Low-Dose Trial: An Experience with DON (exp40414)". Erowid.org . Apr 26, 2007. erowid.org/exp/40414 DOSE: T+ 0:00 2.5 mg oral DON (liquid) T+ 12:00 1.0 mg oral Pharms - Clonazepam (pill / tablet) T+ 12:00 smoked Cannabis (plant material) T+ 12:00 1.0 mg oral Pharms - Clonazepam (pill / tablet) T+ 12:00 oral Alcohol - Beer/Wine (liquid) BODY WEIGHT: 160 lb This was my first time with DON and purely a research endeavor, as there was little to no information provided about this compound. The entry in PiHkAL suggested that this be the more stimulating and less psychedelic version of DOB. I started with a dose of 2.5mg (lower than the 3.0mg starter dose suggested in PiHKaL). Here goes: Dose: 2.5 mg orally dissolved in tea. This was taken on an almost empty stomach. Mindset: Woke up early in the morning after getting absolutely shittedfaced on some GBL the night before. This may or may not have an effect due to Lactone's reported dopamine-inhibitor action. Felt completely normal though. T+1:00: Started feeling a very slight jaw tension and a rise in blood pressure. Not much else at this point. Come up is said to be around 3-3.5 hrs with this one. Also noticed some unpleasant 'stomach effects'. T+1:30: The stomach had settled so no real side effects except for slight shaking of the limbs. Noticed an increase in stimulation and a little bit of added confusion. Colors seem really bright but no visuals. Similar to a come up on a low (1.5mg) dose of DOB except for there is a lot more stimulation and a lot less side effects. T+2:00-2:30: This is nearing the 'peak' I suppose. Not much of a psychedelic acpect here really, just a nice even at times calming stimulation which goes like this: I do not feel the uncontrollable urge to do stuff like with amphetamines, but no super-calm-as-a-tank euphoria as with meth either. I almost feel some kind of 'inner peace' without any schitzophrenic ideations as I get with DOB. Just a nice mellow high, spoiled only slightly by shaking in my legs. Feels fine after some stretching is done though. Also drank some tea here which is always a nice booster. This is T+3:00 and I just got out of the shower feeling refreshed and relaxed. I guess this reminds me of a fourth or fifth day up on meth when I can't really get spun anymore and doing more just makes I not give a damn and really mellow ;-) Also little bit of mental confusion as with DOB which usually makes me fuck up everything I do. So this is more euphoric than most other phenetylamines I've tried (exceptions would be 2C-I and 2C-B) but doesn't quite turn me out on overstimulation like STP does at the peak. Still get the 'laughs' a little bit as with any phenetylamine (this is just a silly laugh a lot of people get which I think is a result of overstimulation; I get it with 2C-B, DOB and STP). It is now time for me to make my moves for the day, so we'll hit the road and see how well this fares as a 'workable high'. I dont look too messed up when I'm on this and unlike DOB, this here allows me to think better and clearer, a very amphetamine-like quality. Anything even closely resembling a psychedelic experience was gone after the come up and the only thing left was the semi-euphoric stimulation that lasted another 15 hrs before subsiding. I felt quite cracked out on this but not anything as bad a DOB where you ask yourself 'Why did I do this to my brain?' ))). Now it is T+12:00 and the stimulation is gradually subsiding. Visited my local pharmacy for some clonazepam and took half a tablet equaling 1mg. This provided the synergy I described with DOB. Clonazepam was an unusually deep and emotional experience and felt like 'medicine for the soul' - a very nice way to end the 'trip' and get the nice calm 'afterglow'. Then I smoked a lot of Cannabis and took another 1mg Clonazepam then went to a bar with some girls where I had a beer. So anyways here's the conclusion: DON is a nice substitute for amphetamine, more economical than meth, which I wanna do more and then more and after that wait 2 minutes and do some more again, all at lower doses. I'd imagine the side effects could get a bit rough at 4.5-5.0mg but I had little to no side effects common to STP and DOB. Could be an awesome party drug for sure.

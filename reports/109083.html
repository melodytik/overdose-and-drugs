这种经历发生在上周四. 我刚刚收到了一克4H-MIPT, 到了早上, 我做了一次过敏测试, 等了两个小时. 我告诉我的一个朋友 (杰伊) 我得到了什么,他也感兴趣尝试它. 我从一个推的来源订购了一磅种子. 我和杰伊和另一个朋友 (戴夫) 分享茶三分. 经过40分钟左右的时间, 由于我没有秤, 很难说确切的剂量, 十分钟左右过去了, 我已经感觉到4小时的微弱影响. 这次旅行是微妙的, 但一些视觉扭曲是存在的. 我从来没有看过这样的节目. 我们决定每人吸入另一条线, 比上一条稍大一点 (20毫克). 视觉效果在最后一剂药后很快就开始显现, 然而我的头脑相当清醒, 强烈的视觉效果与之相匹配, 身体的高度与茶相结合, 轻微的高兴和非常放松, 另一位朋友 (我们称他为罗伊) 也和我们在一起, 我们四个人在下楼看"工作狂"并开始吸草. 身体高感觉很好, 草似乎不会引起任何偏执, 罗伊离开去上层使用洗手间,然后出去抽烟. 十分钟后, 杰伊建议我上楼去我的房间, 我上楼听到水流声,以为有人在打尿,门开着,我敲了墙,没有回应. 我走过来,发现水龙头已经开了,水冲过水槽,地板湿透了. 我们在浴室里有 lino, 但它是一个老房子, 它有一个巨大的洞穿过木头右下水槽. 我惊慌失措,打电话给杰伊上楼, 他拿起毛巾, 我下楼发现厨房灯的玻璃盖上充满了水. 我非常害怕和担心. 最终我们把所有的水都干掉了, 结果发现水从通风口流过来, 就在灯口附近, 幸运的是, 让我们回到沙发上看更多的"劳动狂"并吸一些大麻. 其他: 发布时间:2016年12月1日 - (43):火车残骸和旅行灾难 (7),组合 (3),小组 (2-9) (17)

Dec 1, 2016. erowid.org/exp/109083 DOSE: T+ 0:00 oral Poppies - Opium (tea) T+ 0:40 insufflated 4-HO-MiPT (powder / crystals) T+ 3:40 insufflated 4-HO-MiPT (powder / crystals) T+ 3:40 smoked Cannabis BODY WEIGHT: 140 lb This experience happened on last Thursday. I had just received a gram of 4-ho-mipt and was eager to test it out. In the morning I took an allergy test bump and waited for two hours. I told a friend of mine (Jay) what I had gotten and he was also interested in trying it. Before dosing we decided to make a batch of poppy seed tea from a pound of seeds I had ordered from a recommended source. Jay and I split the tea three ways with another friend (Dave). 40 minutes or so pass and we are all feeling the poppy seed tea pretty strongly. We both snort a small line of 4-ho-mipt, since I don't have a scale it is hard to say the exact dosage but I would guess 10 - 15mg. 10 minutes or so pass and I am already feeling some slight effects from the 4-ho-mipt. The trip is subtle but some visual distortion is present. Jay decides to put on Workaholics, a show I haven't really watched before. Three hours pass and we are fairly certain we are at or at least near the peak and decide to snort another line each, slightly bigger than the last one (20 ish mg). The visuals really kick into gear shortly after that last dose, however my mind is fairly sober, I have a little trouble thinking and staying focused but other than that the head space is very mild. The strong visuals keep up, the body high paired with the poppy seed tea is mildly euphoric and very relaxing, I am not stimulated at all, however I have a slight soreness in my lower back. Another friend (we'll call him Roy) was also with us, however he only took a small bump of the Mipt. All 4 of us are downstairs watching Workaholics and begin smoking weed. The body high feels nice, and the weed doesn't seem to cause any paranoia like it does for me with most psyches, the visuals increase as I get high. Roy leaves to go and use the washroom upstairs and then goes outside to have a smoke. After maybe 10 minutes Jay suggests I head upstairs to my room so I can grab some hash. When I get upstairs I can hear water running, thinking someone might be taking a leak with the door open I knock on the wall before walking by, no response. I walk past and notice the tap is running and has overflown the sink, the floor is soaked. We have lino in the bathroom but it is an old house and it has a huge hole through to the wood right by the sink. I panic and call Jay upstairs, he gets towels and starts ringing them out in the tub, I pace around grabbing towels unsure what to do. I go downstairs and notice the glass cover for the kitchen light is full of water. I am extremely panicked and worried. Eventually we get all the water dried up, and unscrew all the light bulbs in the kitchen. It turns out the water had a path by a vent that was right by the light socket and thankfully none of the wood was soaked. All worked up we resign back to the couch to watch some more Workaholics and smoke some weed about it.

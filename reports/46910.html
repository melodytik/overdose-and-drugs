中文译文 兴奋剂

我试图从720毫克的伪中制备甲乙酸 (我无法获得任何盐酸), 我和我的朋友L. 将粉末分成大约十行. 我们每个人都吸了一根线, 意识到添加了太多的酸. 我们将另一条线分别溶解在水中并消耗. 我们洗了一些衣服, 并暂时分开来做一些清洁. 每次我坐下,我的腿都会跳动. 据L.报道,她无法静静地坐着. 我感到有点抽, 需要排便 (但只能排出一点水). 我们决定再给药. 她试图吸入另一条线, 但烧伤太强烈, 我口服了一行. 服用药物后, 我们开始长途散步. 一切似乎利的我们两个. 我感觉到我的感官已经很好了. 我没有过度说话,也没有过度烦, 最强烈的影响似乎持续了大约两个小时. 对于我来说,速度是波而来的. 我从来不确定自己是停滞还是下降. 我在前一天晚上做了DXM, 我觉得身体有点疲 (我倾向于得到疲但仍然醒的效果在上部). 没有食欲, 无法入睡7个小时. 我在几个小时后睡着了, 但睡眠有点中断. (在散步后, 我认为我们消耗了一行每人更多的水溶液, 这似乎是对的. 其他: 甲基 (81):小组 (2-9) (17),一般 (1)

Mar 21, 2006. erowid.org/exp/46910 DOSE: repeated Methcathinone BODY WEIGHT: 140 lb I attempted to prepare methcathinone acetate (I couldn't obtain any hydrochloric acid) from 720mg of pseudoephedrine using the chilled permanganate method. My friend L. and I divided the powder into roughly ten lines. We each snorted a line, and realized that too much acetic acid had been added. We dissolved another line each into water and consumed that. We did some laundry and temporarily parted ways to do some cleaning. Whenever I sat down, my leg would bounce. L. reported that she could not sit still. I got some cramps and felt the need to defecate (but only managed to emit a bit of water). After about two hours of laundry and cleaning/organizing, we decided to dose again. L. tried to snort another line, but the burning was too intense, so she consumed the remainder of the line orally. I consumed one more line orally. After dosing, we set out on a long walk. Everything seemed sharp to the both of us. My senses felt fine-tuned. I wasn't over-talkative nor irritated, but I was pretty happy in general. The stronger effects seemed to last around two hours. For me, the speediness came in waves. I was never sure if I was plateauing or coming down. I had done DXM the previous night and the cat cleared up any last 'fuzziness' feeling remaining in my head from the DXM. I felt a bit physically worn out (I tend to get the tired-but-still-wide-awake effect on uppers). L. had no appetite, and couldn't get to sleep for around seven hours. I managed to sleep after a few hours, but the sleep was somewhat interrupted. (After the walk, I think we consumed a line more apiece in water solution, but my memory is fuzzy... I have about two lines left, so this seems right.)

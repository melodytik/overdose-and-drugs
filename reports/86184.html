我在春季初种植了加利福尼亚, 一旦开花, 四种开花植物 (关键词是开花,因为类化合物度达到峰值) 被干了2.5周,悬挂在一个干燥房间的盒子中的绳子上. 我把草收集起来, 留下茎来抽烟. 烟味非常不愉快. 干燥的草被磨碎, 然后将1.5克卷成一支烟. 由于草不是那么密集, 我定期喝水以对抗导致的尬味道和轻微口腔干燥. 在5-7分钟内意识的明显变化是可见的. 这时, 香烟的四分之一已经消失了. 我猜测大约0.4克的剂量是有效的达到确定的值剂量. 三十分钟后, 效应仍然存在. 我决定在药物上测试我的认知能力, 虽然我看不到思考速度的增加或减少, 我吸了两小时后就睡着了, 吸了八小时后醒来, 效果已经消失了. 我不会说这棵植物能帮助我睡觉, 这种温和的镇静作用是有根据的, 没有感到不安或昏昏欲睡. 实际上,我觉得自己有能力和愿意在心灵和身体上活跃. 我觉得这种植物非常可爱, 特别是因为没有负面副作用. 虽然这种体验本身绝对不是壮观的, 但我会说这是一种非常随意的物质, 因为这次经历并没有引起我的全部注意. 换句话说,日常生活所需的认知功能大多没有受到抑制. 时间表: 86184 2020年6月24日发布 - 加利福尼亚 (279):第一次 (2),未知背景 (20)

Jun 24, 2020. erowid.org/exp/86184 DOSE: 1/4 cig. smoked Poppies - California (dried) BODY WEIGHT: 150 lb A Warming Experience I had planted eschscholzia californica by seed in early spring and once flowered I decided to test the psychoactive properties. Four flowering plants (key word flowering because the alkaloid concentrations are at their peak) were dried for 2.5 weeks, hanging from a string suspended in a box in a dry room. Once dried I collected the herbage and left out the stems for smoking. Stems make the smoke taste very unpleasant. The dried herbage was grinded and 1.5 grams were rolled into a cigarette. Since the herbage isn’t that dense it ended up being quite the bomber. When smoked, the taste, surprisingly, is very mild and not harsh although, I periodically drank water to combat the awkward taste and mild dry mouth that resulted. Within 5-7 minutes a definite shift in consciousness was noticable. By this time a quarter of the cigarette was gone. So I would surmise a dose of about 0.4 grams is effective in achieving a definite threshold dose. Thirty minutes later the effects were still present. I decided to test my cognitive abilities on the drug so I started doing some logarithms on a chalk board. Although I could see no increase or decrease at the rate of thought processing, I noticed a heightened sense of focus, which can be said to indirectly influence the rate of thought processing. I fell asleep two hours after smoking the plant and upon waking up after a total of eight hours after smoking it, the effects were gone. I wouldn’t claim that this plant is a sleep aid because it was 11:47pm when I took the first hit and I don’t experience sleep problems. The claims of its mild sedative properties are well founded as a warm and relaxing feeling was evident throughout the experience. Restlessness or sleepiness was not experienced. In fact I felt able and willing to be active in mind and body. I find this plant very amiable, especially because of the absence of negative side effects. Though the experience itself is not spectacular by any means, I would say it is very casual substance analogous in usage to a cup of coffee or tobacco. This is because the experience did not demand all of my attention. In other words, cognitive functions that are needed to go about daily life are, for the most part, uninhibited.

我希望分享我对2C-I进行的前两次试验. 这是我服用的第一个乙胺. 我在第一次体验后记录了我的一些想法, 随访经验让我发现2C-I在剂量范围内的作用有所不同,但我更喜欢将第二个事件作为一个脚注而不是单独的报告. 我之前只服用过一种法乙烯胺, 我希望这能提供更轻松,更愉快的体验, 我以凝囊的形式服用2C-I. 我所采用的2C-I化粉样本呈现出经典的外观, 它形成了白色的球状颗粒, 它们最终会形成有趣的结构, - - 第一个试验 周日上午11点30分口服16毫克. 在至少45分钟的时间里, 时间大约在1点左右, 我开始感到轻微的刺激, 时间似乎在流逝, 时间的流动是加速还是减慢, 我感觉到这种物质像一个温暖,泡的斑点融化在我的胃. 这是一个善良的感觉. 在T+1:30左右,一个强烈的,沉浸式的身体感觉发展,以及颜色的亮化. 首先我感觉到这些物质的热能流向我的手指和脚. 现在它已经完全包围了我所有的特征. 从我的头到我的脚. 随着分界线的移动, 广的色谱部分被新的,更明亮的色调所取代. 在接下来的几个小时里, 尽管我看到鲜的颜色, 没有任何视觉运动. 墙壁没有呼吸或流动. 这使得整体视觉效果明显比我服用的其他迷幻药更微妙. 我开始经历"情感幻觉". 我在某个时候打开电视. 一位可怕的喜剧演员出现, 这是一个典型的糟糕的电视节目. 每一个平的笑话,每一个社会尬的暗示,每一个人的元素都非常突出. 我已经失去了平时能忽略烦人的言语和想法的能力. 我意识到我的情绪是开的, 我听着熟悉的音乐, 音乐本身听起来很正常, 没有听觉扭曲. 我对音乐的感受更加强烈. 对于音乐的兴奋,对音乐的放松,对那些让我了解某些歌曲的人的怀旧和回忆, 音乐本身相对较小. 在整个2C-I经历中, 我发现自己在自己内心的激烈对话中交替, 在2C-I的某个时刻, 想和朋友聊天或找人陪伴的欲望会变得压倒性的. 我可以在2C-I上很好地社交,并运作合理. 我似乎有点过度敏感, 然而, 由于我的同情心增加, 在高峰期内,效应变化非常缓慢,主要是从T+2小时到T+6小时. 开始时, 我感觉到药物在我的胃中沉入, 发出温暖的感. 我把它想象成我肚子里的一个小能量反应堆, 后来感觉从我的肚子上升到我的头上. 2C-I最终以旋转的光线在我的头骨后部显现, 触及它的纹进入我的思想和想法, 灯光消失后, 在我的脑海里留下轻微的头和疼痛. 药物的效果在8个半小时后消失. 后果很轻微, 第二天我恢复正常. -第二次审判- 约一个半月后, 一个22毫克的2C-I囊在星期六下午12点05分被服用. 在T+0:45, 检测到初始效应. 颜色变亮发生相当突然. 胸部出现轻微的紧张. 想到的东西都不一样, 能量从胃流向手指和脚. 在T+1:30时, 毒品的迷幻效应已经完全发展. 我在户外散步时, 发现了两种视觉效果, 首先,有一个轻微的痕迹效应, 红色或绿色的灯会在我看的任何东西上短暂地叠加. 在一秒钟之内它就会消失. 第二,我闭眼看到的影像, 绝对比我清醒时看到的更生动. 我记得当我闭上眼睛时, 看到许多闪光的白色斑点, 然而,仍然没有运动或曲线的直线与眼睛打开. 我从外面回来, 意识到一种不愉快的身体感觉开始了. 我感到头,出汗,紧张, 有点恶心. 我开始怀疑和质疑这种实质. 经历的其余部分被我自己的消极情绪所污染. 接下来的几个小时, 我觉得需要照顾我的身体, 遗憾的是,这削弱了2C-I的魔力, 随着时间的推移,我的身体和情绪都得到改善, 尤其是发生的出汗和温度不规则. 然而,这些副作用与2C- I相比,对2C- E的表现更加温和. 在T+5:00左右, 一种不寻常的效应被发现: 听觉幻觉. 在一个几乎无声的房间里, 这是一个微妙的, 但明显. 仿佛我用耳朵开始想象物质的动能量流. 我听到自己身体中的能量过剩. 我注意到另一个不寻常的情况:相当严重的瞳孔扩张. 这似乎很奇怪,因为该物质的视觉,情感和身体效应在T+2小时时达到最大强度,而当时的瞳孔扩张是轻微的. 似乎只有当该化合物的作用终于开始消退时, 才会发生完全的瞳孔扩张. 在第一次体验中,瞳孔膨胀一直是轻微的. 这很奇怪... 这一剂量的效果持续约10小时. 到了第二天, 我完全恢复正常. - 结论 - 我发现2C-I主要强调情感和身体效应, 在这两次试验中, 首先发现了身体效应和颜色变化, 我第一次服用2C-I时, 我读过一些用户看到生动的2C-B视觉效果. 这与我遇到的完全不同. 没有波斯地毯的覆盖层在四处,甚至没有移动的线条和摇摆的纹理. 经历在情感层面上很有趣, 这种物质确实显示出一些前景. 我发现它的微妙之处在于它的魅力. 较高的剂量似乎没有什么好处. 在较高剂量时,有点更全面的迷幻效应值得研究,并且它确实开始成为部分共感 (如低剂量的2C-E). 但这种经历的感觉却变得不那么愉快. 需要保持平衡很快就显而易见了,6毫克只是太多了. 这种物质对社会化有好处, 也可能有治疗价值. 尤其是16毫克的剂量时, 事实上, 低到中等剂量的这种物质甚至可以进行冥想. 2C-I将是一个相当好的选择, 引入陌生人进入乙胺的世界. 我没有经验使用MDMA或任何真正的同情药物, 我唯一能完全接受和喜爱的药物是迷幻药. 由于我自己的偏见, 我觉得2C-I有点无聊. 那些对同情药和迷幻药有真诚同等的欣赏者将会认为这是一个宝藏. 这不是世界上最具感官沉浸力的物质. 但在适当的剂量下, 它具有独特而值得的效果. 其他: 2007年11月27日发布 2C-I (172):单独的 (16),回顾 / 摘要 (11),一般的 (1)

Nov 27, 2007. erowid.org/exp/67025 DOSE: 16 mg oral 2C-I (capsule) 22 mg oral 2C-I (capsule) BODY WEIGHT: 160 lb I would like to share a little about my first two trials with 2C-I. This was the first halo-phenethylamine that I have taken. I recorded quite a few of my thoughts after the first experience, and I had a very good feeling for what the drug's properties are after only one try. The follow-up experience allowed me to discover how the effects of 2C-I differ across the dosage spectrum, however I preferred to include the second episode as a footnote rather than a separate report. I had taken only one phenethylamine before this, that being 2C-E. I hoped this would provide a lighter, more enjoyable experience, but I knew quite well the unpredictable power of the 2C family. I took my 2C-I in the form of gel capsules. My sample of 2C-I hydrochloride powder had a classic appearance to it: Puffy and fluffy in consistency. It formed into white, spherical pellets that stuck together. These ultimately build up into interestingly textured structures that somewhat resemble styrophome packing kernels. -First Trial- 16 Milligrams were taken orally at 11:30AM on a Sunday. Nothing much was felt for at least 45 minutes. Around T+1:00, I started to feel mildly stimulated, and time seemed to be drawing out, but in an uncertain way. I could not tell whether the flow of time was speeding up or slowing down. I felt the substance as a warm, bubbly blob melting down in my stomach. It was a benevolent-feeling thing. Around T+1:30, a strong, immersive bodily feeling developed, as well as a brightening of colors. First I felt the substances warm energy coursing out into my fingers and toes. Whereas at first the 2C-I just lurked in the center of my body, now it was fully enveloping all my features. Then it seemed as if an invisible ring passed down over me, hovering from my head down to my feet. As this dividing line moved by, broad sections of the color spectrum were replaced with new, brighter tones. The vivid new colors stayed for the next several hours. In spite of the brightened colors I saw, there was no visual movement whatsoever. Lines did not curve, textures did not crawl, walls did not breath or flow. This made the overall visual effect decidedly subtler than with the other psychedelics I have taken. Around T+2:00 I began experiencing 'emotional hallucinations'. I turned on my television at one point. An appalling hack comedian appeared, performing a tired puppet act for a somewhat baffled audience. A typical bad TV show. Except that every mediocre joke, every hint of social awkwardness, every irritating element stood out immensely. My ordinary ability to tune out annoying words and thoughts had been snatched away. I realized that my emotions were wide open and exposed to all stimuli. At T+2:30 I was listening to familiar music and keeping my mood in positive territory. The music itself sounded normal, with no auditory distortion. However, my feelings about the music were intensified. Excitement for music, relaxation from music, nostalgia and memories of the people who introduced me to certain songs, these emotions were tremendous. The music itself was small in relation. Throughout the 2C-I experience, I found myself alternating between intense internal dialogues within my own mind, balanced with the impulse to socialize with others. At some point on 2C-I, the urge to talk to a friend or just go find people to be with can become overwhelming. I could socialize just fine on 2C-I, and functioned rationally. I may have seemed a bit hyper-sensitive, however, as a result of my increased empathy, the openness of my thoughts and feelings was difficult to conceal. The effects changed very slowly over the course of the peak period, which was mainly from T+2 hours to T+6 hours. Initially, I felt the drug sinking into my stomach, giving off warm tingling sensations. I visualized it as a little energy reactor in my stomach, discharging miniature lightning bolts in random directions. Later the sensations shifted from my stomach up towards my head. The 2C-I eventually manifested as a swirling light in the back of my skull, touching its tendrils into my thoughts and ideas, amplifying any emotions it chose to. When the light receded, it left behind a slight dizzy, aching feeling in my head. The drug's effects ended after about 8.5 hours. The after-effects were mild and I was completely back to normal by the next day. -Second Trial- About a month and a half later, a 22 milligram capsule of 2C-I was taken at 12:05PM on a Saturday afternoon. At T+0:45, Initial effects were detected. Color brightening occurred fairly suddenly. A slight tightness in the chest was noted. There were racing thoughts and the urge to socialize. Energy crept out from the stomach towards the fingers and toes. At T+1:30 the psychedelic effects of the substance had fully developed. While I was walking outdoors, I noticed two visual effects that were not present during the first experiment. First, there was a slight tracer effect, most evident with bright lights. When I looked at a traffic light and then looked away, the red or green light would carry, superimposed briefly over whatever else I looked at. In a second or so it would fade away. Second, there was closed-eye imagery that was definitely more vivid than what I would see while sober. I remember seeing numerous glowing white spots when I closed my eyes, flashing like never-ending fireworks. However, there was still no movement or curvature of straight lines with eyes opened. Around T+2:15 I came back from outside and realized that a nasty physical feeling was setting in. I was dizzy, sweaty, jittery, and a little nauseous. Sadly, once the bodily feeling became uncomfortable, I began to doubt and question the substance. The rest of the experience became tainted with my own negativity. For the next couple hours, I felt the need to take care of my mildly ill body and I could not be out in public. Sadly, this undermined the magic of 2C-I, as I see its social element as being a large portion of its potential. My body and my mood improved over time, but there was a nagging discomfort that couldn't be entirely shaken. The symptoms were reminiscent of coming up on my first 2C-E experience, especially the sweating and temperature irregularity that occurred. However, these side effects manifested more mildly with 2C-I than they had with 2C-E. Around T+5:00 an unusual effect was noted: Auditory hallucinations. I detected a crackling, hissing undercurrent in a mostly-silent room. It was subtle, but noticeable. It was as if I had begun to visualize the substance's jittery energy current using my ears. I was hearing the excess energy within my own body. Around T+6:00 I noted something else unusual: Fairly extreme pupil dilation. This seemed odd given that the visual, emotional, and physical effects of the substance were all at full strength by T+2 hours, and pupil dilation at that time was mild. It seemed like, only when the compound's effects were finally beginning to recede, did full pupil dilation occur. During the first experience, pupil dilation was consistently mild. Strange... The effects at this dose lasted about 10 hours. Again, I was completely back to normal by the next day. -Conclusions- I found that 2C-I emphasizes emotional and physical effects primarily, with subtle sensorial alterations. During both trials, physical effects and altered colors were noticed first, and empathogenic qualities developed a bit later. The first time I took 2C-I I was a little disappointed by its subtlety. I had read about some users seeing vivid, 2C-B-like visual effects. This was nothing like what I encountered. There was no persian rug overlay flapping about, or even shifting lines and wiggling textures. The experience, however, was interesting on an emotional level and enjoyable on the whole. The substance certainly showed some promise. The second time I took 2C-I, I discovered that its subtlety is it's charm. Higher doses seem to have little to offer. The slightly fuller psychedelic effects at a higher dose were worth examining, and it does start to become partially synaesthetic (as with lower doses of 2C-E). But the overriding feeling of the experience becomes far less euphoric. The need for balance quickly became apparent, and 6 milligrams was just too much of an increase. This substance could be good for socialization, and probably has some therapeutic value as well. It was not quite as stimulating as expected, with little raise in pulse rate, especially at the 16 milligram dosage. In fact, it might even be possible to meditate on a low to moderate dose of this substance. 2C-I would be a reasonably good option for initiating the unfamiliar into the world of the phenethylamines. I am not experienced with MDMA or any true empathogen and I do not have any particular passion or obsession for the family. The only drugs I seem to be able to completely accept and love are the psychedelics. And due to my own prejudices, I find 2C-I a little bit dull. Those who have a true and equal appreciation for both empathogens and psychedelics will consider this to be a treasure. It is not the most sensorially immersive substance in the world. But it enjoyable at the right dose, with a unique and worthwhile effect.

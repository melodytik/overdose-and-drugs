I am familiar with several well-known psychedelics and a few less widespread ones. I am a bit of a hardhead with all the phenethylamines that I tried (and I tried many), and with most tryptamines. I use no prescription drugs on a regular basis, with the exception of a nootropic (20-30 mg Noopept daily). I avoid even OTC meds like aspirin unless I have real need. SET AND SETTING New Year's Eve at a friend of a friend's. I don't know the guy too well, but I'm told he's quite cool (and he is). I do know over 50% of the few guests there. I'm there because of a friend who extended the invitation to me so we could have a small band going for some live music. There are dozens of bottles of wine, both still and fizzy champagne or champagne-like. Our host is a sommelier and has lots of samples from several wineyards. So it's wine all the way and I do enjoy some, in moderation and with accompanying food. CHOICE OF SUBSTANCE AND DOSAGE The last time I did some allylescaline must have been like 10 years ago (after checking, I see it's actually over 11 years ). I remember that allylescaline is one of those molecules that really do not impair my playing. If anything, I think it enhances it. Time for a confirmation! As for the dosage, I didn't want to go overboard for several reasons, mainly that I didn't know how much alcohol and weed would be consumed. Turns out, quite a lot. So I picked something on the low side. I'd gone as far as 50 mg in the past, but this time I did just 14 mg, without expecting it to do much. I was wrong: it worked quite effectively. About dosage: I could have had some lingering cross-tolerance from a tryptamine I did less than a week ago. That's another reason not to be expecting much. I was wrong: did I mention that already?

我最近获得了大量高品质的DMT, 为了这个特殊的实验, 我服用了两只大多充满00的4x叙利亚提取物的凝帽, 40分钟后我又服用了DMT提取物的凝帽 (大约250mg). 这是我多年来第一次使用叙利亚. 由于我使用阿雅华斯卡时使用这种药物, 我现在有自然的倾向在与结合时清洗. 接下来又会出现干燥的动, 实际上从来没有动过 (这也使得这次旅行变得更加激烈). 我通常会感觉好些, 虽然可能会有一些过渡性恶心和很长一段时间的胃部不舒服, 我也发现叙利亚的和的结合非常有趣. 事实上,我见过的最高水平是这样的组合,我唯一一次遇到的实体是叙利亚和10克新鲜的夏威夷. 我也很好奇叙利亚的Rue如何与DMT混合. 我最后一次的"法拉瓦斯卡"旅行, 我认为这种组合可能会更强烈. 事实上,这是一个更温和的旅程. 服用囊后大约20分钟, 服用DMT后大约30分钟, 心理效应开始, 我现在认为caapi是更强大的MAOI, 尽管叙利亚的可能更强 (类似于DMT比更强大的LSD更强). 经过大约45分钟的清洗, 接下来还有一点恶心, 但它是不舒服的. 没有任何不舒服的感觉. 我感觉完全放松,温暖和满意, 在这个阶段,视觉是温柔的,并保持这样一段时间. 我决定从我新发现的玻璃管中抽出一些DMT提取物. 我发现吸食DMT是一项相当大的艺术. 有些尝试似乎完全失败了, 我发现很难吸足够多的烟来获得突破性体验 - - 管中的DMT越多, 结果,我只在几次接近突破. 今晚我没有乐观,在黑暗中抽了一点抽取物, 由于处在黑暗中, 管道的设计允许新鲜空气流入, 这使得肺部容易呼吸. 我开始注意到一种微妙的声, 一种奇怪的视觉效应, 我之前只有一次有过这种效果, 在一个极其强大的/ayahuasca之旅, 视觉效果突然变得超级大, 我似乎把头脑带到一个完全广的黑色空间里, 感觉就像我进入了太空. 这空间彻底吞了我, 让我感到惊的是那种完全的幸福感和狂喜. 我通常不会在清醒时吸食DMT时感到高兴, 但的MAOI效应似乎使这次旅行稍微减慢, 在整个经历中没有一个焦虑或困惑的时刻. 这次旅行的视觉效果令人难以置信. 我沉浸在极美而复杂的抽象几何幻觉中. 不同于其他迷幻药所带来的无意义的碎形混乱, 这种感觉的每一片都充满了强烈的意义, 经过10分钟左右, 很难说, 吸烟经历的效果已经消退, 我又回到了以前的温柔健康经历. 我觉得很幸运能经历如此深刻的经历, 然而,一个小时后, 出于某种原因, 我也很难睡觉, 因为充满了精力, 我很想在未来再次尝试这种组合. Final Breakthrough Experience Huasca Combo (Syrian Rue & M. tenuiflora) by Sam Citation:   Sam. "Final Breakthrough Experience: An Experience with Huasca Combo (Syrian Rue & M. tenuiflora) (exp68203)". Erowid.org . Apr 5, 2008. erowid.org/exp/68203 DOSE: T+ 0:00 2 capsls oral Syrian Rue (extract) T+ 2:00 300 mg smoked Mimosa tenuiflora (extract) BODY WEIGHT: 70 kg Having dabbled with a variety of synthetic and natural psychedelics, I recently have had access to large quantities of very good quality DMT extracted from Mimosa hostilis root bark. For this particular experiment, I was taking two mostly filled ‘00’ gel caps of 4x Syrian rue extract and 40 minutes later I took another gel cap of DMT extract (around 250mg). This was my first time using Syrian rue in quite a few years. Since my last time using it I had turned to using B. caapi as an MAOI, but because I use this when taking ayahuasca, I have now a natural tendency to purge when combining it with mushrooms. However, when I purge with this combination on mushrooms I end up feeling nauseous for a while, and then dry heaving, never actually purging (this also makes the trip a lot more intense quite suddenly). I usually feel better after this, although there maybe some transient nausea and uncomfortable stomach feelings for quite a while, which is annoying as I never used to get nausea on mushrooms and Syrian rue (apart from maybe some stomach awareness while coming up). I also find the Syrian rue and mushroom combination quite interesting. In fact, the highest I’ve ever been by an order of magnitude was on such a combination, and the only time I’ve encountered an entity was on a combination of Syrian rue and 10g of fresh Hawaiian Copelandia cyanascens mushrooms. I was also curious how Syrian Rue would mix with DMT. My last ‘pharahuasca’ trip combining 20g of B. caapi tea with 300mg of DMT started off in an extremely intense fashion, but was quite short lasting. Due to Syrian rue being a more potent MAOI, I thought this combination might be more intense if anything. In fact, it was a much gentler trip. I felt distinct MAOI effects come on from the rue extract around 20 minutes after taking the capsules, and around 30 minutes after taking the DMT extract, psychedelic effects began, along with a subtle but growing nausea, along with a bit of stomach gas. I’m now of the opinion that caapi is the more powerful MAOI, although Syrian rue may be more potent (in a similar way to DMT being more powerful than the much more potent LSD). Around 45 minutes later I purged, and as purges go this was remarkably easy. Following this there was still a little nausea, but it was uncomfortable. Unlike the start of my previous experience, there were no uncomfortable feelings at all. I felt completely at ease, warm and content, and in a very wholesome, earthy way. At this stage visuals were gentle, and remained so for a while. Around an hour and half later I decided to smoke some DMT extract from my newly discovered glass freebase pipe. I have found that there is a considerable art to smoking DMT. Some attempts seem to misfire completely, while others produce only a threshold experience. I find it hard to smoke enough to get to a breakthrough experience-the more DMT in the pipe, the harder it is to vaporize all at once and inhale quickly. As a result I have only been what I would call close to a breakthrough a few times. Tonight I wasn’t optimistic, and smoked a little extract in the dark, with a poor lighter, so the odds weren’t exactly stacked in my favour. After heating it up for a while and taking several long, deep tokes, I still wasn’t expecting much, due to the fact I couldn’t tell if I was inhaling much or not, due to being in the dark, and the design of the pipe allows for an inflow of fresh air which makes it easy on the lungs. I began to notice a subtle buzz, and a curious visual effect, where part of my visual field appears to ripple in a strange, liquid fashion. I have had this effect only once before, on an extremely powerful shroom/ayahuasca trip, so now I was thinking something might be about to happen. And then quite suddenly the visuals went off the scale, and I seemed to depart my head into a utterly vast black space-quite a different environment to that enlargement of the interior visual field I get on other psychedelics. It felt like I had entered space. This space completely engulfed me, and as it did so I was amazed at the feeling of complete bliss and ecstasy. I don’t usually feel much euphoria when smoking DMT while sober, but the MAOI effects of the rue seemed to slow this trip down slightly making it easier to integrate, and the euphoria was incredible. Throughout the experience there was not a single moment of anxiety or confusion. The visual aspect of the trip was quite incredible. I was being submerged and bombarded with extremely beautiful and complex abstract geometric hallucinations. Unlike the sometime meaningless chaos of fractals I can get a lot on other psychedelics, it felt every ounce of this was brimming with intense meaning – exactly what though I don’t know. After 10 or so minutes, it is hard to say, I gradually entered my head again, but the euphoria was still present. When the results of the smoked experience had worn off I was back to the gentle wholesome experience previous to this. I felt very lucky to have experienced something so profound, and thought it was time no for sleep. However, an hour later, for some reason, the trip quite suddenly kicked off, but a different order of magnitude than before. I also had trouble sleeping afterwards due to lots of energy and racing thoughts, I feel as a direct result of the smoked experience. This was an incredible experience, and I am keen on trying this combination again sometime in the future.

T +2:40: The physical and visual effects started to get stronger. It was a nice feeling. T +2:55: Now I decided that I wanted to try to combine this with Mxipr and this is were the experience got really strong. I took 70mg and then I started to watch a series. T +3:15: All of a sudden the Mxipr hit and the experience got really strange and heavy. I didn’t now anymore what reality was and it was as if I was sometimes inside the series, sometimes in my room, sometimes somewhere completely else. The room turned and sometimes it got bigger, sometimes smaller, sometimes it looked as if I didn’t know what was above me and what beneath me and what was to the left of me and what right of me. Sometimes it looked as if my mind was just floating, sometimes it appeared to me as if I had a totally different body and it looked as if I was an old woman to me and it was very confusing. I had the feeling of being outside reality and I didn’t know where I was or what was real. Sometimes I recognized something of my room and then I want a bit back, but then it started turning again. T + 4:15 I think this crazy state lasted this strong for about an hour. Eventually, I managed to get control of the situation and I managed to see that I was in my room with my body. When I looked at my watch, approximately 1h had passed. The room was still spinning heavily, but I was back in reality. I managed to go to the toilet and I put on some music. Now I felt calm and good and I noticed that when I closed my eyes, I saw geometrical patterns. In this state, I tried to play a game of chess online and it went well and to my surprise I even won. Playing chess with drugs is always funny. It was a very quick game but in my mind it was as if the game took at hours. T +8:15 I went asleep. The effects were still there but I was tired. T +10:15 I woke up. It was still spinning. T +13:15 I woke up again. There were no effects anymore.

中文译文 抑制剂

瓜,我已经半受挫折我的原始提取物的乳 (我们有各种各样的阶段的明显scariola-virosa杂交肆虐,在本地,所以的磅不是一个问题)...一个有效的沉醉剂在5-10% (估计) 的功效"黑色原料",但为了任何神有爱心在他们的心脏疯狂的炼金术士 我不仅要做得更好 - - 不仅涉及职业自豪感, 对于那些渴望知道细节的人, 我相信有相当多的细节关于酸提取... 看看. 我用酸盐,USP - - 植物已经处理了酸相 - - 并用过代替以太层, 因为我是一个可怜的炼金术士... 我把一些细粒度的泥 (白色的晶体 + 棕色的有机茶 = 色的泥) 入一个小冰淋样品子中, 我使用原油来修复破裂和脱的关节... 所以我把我的小块水晶装满了管子 (它们干了,变成了'裂石'), 因为我想', 这可能只是未溶解的和一些维生素和氨基酸,? 对于炼金术士来说,好奇心往往是一种致命的力量. ...所以我接受了打击. 冰淋样品的边缘可以很舒服地放置, 并且几乎立即蒸发. 没有! 通过时间我呼吸出来,我该死的几乎在下面;拿起刀,,我不会注意到. 这与碳素的"我处于一个完全不同的世界"的感觉有相似之处 (我从来没有有过 *与*氧气 - 空2ltr), ...和峰值下降,我发现几毫克集中冲到大脑是显著不同于几毫克稀释通过160磅...它仍然是相同的挫折,看世界感觉, 在我的总体经验中, 麻醉症 (或是麻醉症) 在最初的20-40分钟后就开始了... 我猜测,这种植物中的高兴剂是乳和/或乳酸. 根据"亚当·戈特利布的法律高兴剂" (一个名字翻译为"上帝的爱"), 显然, 乳素只是一种麻醉剂, 所以,对所有'你们说'菜是近bunk/bunk,'我只会嘲笑你...是的,未经培养的沟是相当'温和,'直到你异构油 - 或花30年在一个育种项目... 我不建议, 也不负责, 那些被毒的人把自己或别人切成碎片. 事实上,我对任何事情都不负责. 其他: 乳种类 (153): 单独使用 (16), 准备/食谱 (30), 一般 (1)

Testing the Homebrew Wild Lettuce (L. virosa extract) by Psi Locybe Citation:   Psi Locybe. "Testing the Homebrew: An Experience with Wild Lettuce (L. virosa extract) (exp8519)". Erowid.org . Mar 26, 2002. erowid.org/exp/8519 DOSE: 17.5 mg smoked Lactuca - L. virosa (extract) BODY WEIGHT: 160 lb Narr, I've been semi-frustrated by my crude extracts of the lactuca (we have various stages of apparent scariola-virosa hybridization rampant, locally, so scads of pounds is not an issue)... an effective inebriant at 5-10% (estimated) potency 'black crud,' but for the sake of whatever gods have a fondness in their heart for mad alchemists, I simply *must* do better - not only is professional pride involved, but I've a friend with a signifigantly broken ribcage... s/he deserves the right to laugh unfettered... Thus it was that I ended up with several grams lactucin freebase, and more on the way... (for those of you desperate to know the details, I believe there's a fair bit of detail on acid-base extractions out... look around. I use baking soda, USP - the plant already took care of the acid phase - and replace the ether layer with filtration, 'cause I'm a poor alchemist... thanks be to our tweak(TMX)-addicted culture that coffee filters are a half-cent a piece!) Spooning forth a little bit of the fine-grained mud (whitish crystals + brownish organic tea = beige mud) into a small ice-cream sample spoon reserved for alchemical purpouses, I kick the rest back into the wash (h2o)... an alchemist must have *some* pride. (the damn things came out looking the same... grr...)... run over to my friends house, and came back disappointed... ::sigh:: s/he can live with their pain; me, I use the crude (sap) to re-set broken and dislocated joints... So I load up the pipe with my small chunk 'o crystals (they dried to 'crack rock' consistency), 'cause I'm thinking 'hey, just how *do* I know I've got the right part? This *could* just be undissolved baking soda and a few vitamins and amino acids, eh?' Curiosity is often a fatally overpowering force, for alchemists. ;) ... and so I take the hit. It's small - this *is,* after all, the amount that will sit comfortably on the very edge of an ice-cream sample spoon - and vaporizes near-instantly. Pheow! By the time I breathe out, I'm damn near under; grab the knife, bubba, I won't notice. It has a similarity to the 'I am in a completely different world than the world I'm in' feeling of carbogen (which I've never had *with* the oxygen - empty 2ltr), but is, naturally, it's own thing... ... and as the peak subsides, I discover that a few milligrams concentratedly rushing to the brain is signifigantly different than a few milligrams diluted through 160 lbs... it's still the same set-back, watching the world sensation, but there's a *lot* less of it. 'course, it sticks around longer than the inhalation peak does, up to about 6 hours in my sum-total experience... narcosis (or was it narcolepsy) sets in after the first 20-40 minutes... Speaking of sum-total experience, based on such and 'Legal Highs by Adam Gottlieb' (how poetic, a treatise on the power ordained unto various plants by a person whose name translates 'the love of God.'), findable here, I surmise that it is lactosol and/or lactucic acid which is the euphoriant (that's *high-dose* euphoriant) in this plant. Lactucin is, apparently, just a narcotic analgesic whippet. ;) So, to all 'yall who say that 'opium lettuce is near-bunk/bunk,' I'm just going to have to laugh at you... yeah, uncultivated ditchweed is pretty 'mild,' 'till you isomerize the oil - or spend 30 years on a breeding project... cut it to crystal, dawg, and try to use for bonafide medical purpouses only... it won't take a full bowl for do-it-yourself-home-surgery (note : I neither recommend nor am I responsible for lettuce-whacked individuals carving themselves or others to bits. In fact, I'm responsible for nothing.)

在口服 药物 - 瑞斯龙 (药片/ 药片) 我首先建议任何服用β-阻断剂和SSRI的人不要服用Risperidone. 这也是我第一次使用抗精神病药物, 如此地狱般的副作用可能会随着重复使用和耐受而消失. 我买了60粒1毫克的药, 我决定把1毫克的药丸放下门. 我没有想到这块小小的粉笔会给我带来什么. 在第一小时内, 完全没有感觉. 现在我注意到一种安静的感觉, 我尽快赶到录音室. 我到达那里的时候, 效果已经开始严重了. 这是一种无声无息的,平静的动感. 现在我仍然怀疑, 归咎于睡眠不足. 我很难集中注意力听他说的话. 我只是在着他, 完全意识到我的荒谬状态. 我现在意识到我的眼睛在物理上是不协调的:我很难看任何东西, 我们打电话给另一个制作人. 我告诉他们我已经采取了什么,他们只是嘲笑我的脸. 现在我完全无法清晰地思考, 我迫切需要一杯饮料, 我从工作室走到走廊里, 我还在走廊里. 但好像我的通用翻译器已经自行关闭了. 我注意到一切都变得安静,我意识到我会昏倒,然后摔倒在地板上. 我打开眼睛,我跪下了. 这位制片人正在着技术废话, 显然完全无视我的昏迷. 我躺在他的脚下思考情况; 我认为这一定是贝塔阻断剂的抗高血压效应和里斯帕德尔的初始降血压效应的结合. 也许这解释了看起来像老板的眼睛. 在行走时失调. 上午11点10分 - - 废了录音. 我现在感觉很糟糕,我只想回家. 我对自己说, "他们给精神病患者吃这种东西. 我只会躺下,然后再忍受下去. 我完全失去了食欲. 我决定第二天再试一次, 只是为了检查它不是异常. 摄入后几秒钟我意识到我是个子,并试图吐没有效果. 一旦进入血液, 恐慌发作就会发生, 接着又一个, 接着又一个. 我只能想到我今天会有多糟糕. 然后我意识到我今天晚上有工作计划. 这是一个愚蠢的混蛋. 这种药物可以让你做好工作吗? 我怀疑这一点! 在服用贝塔阻断剂和抗抑郁药时, 时间表: 26024

Jan 22, 2004. erowid.org/exp/26024 DOSE: 80 mg oral Pharms - Propranolol (pill / tablet) 100 mg oral Pharms - Sertraline (pill / tablet) 1.0 mg oral Pharms - Risperidone (pill / tablet) I would like to start by advising anyone who takes beta-blockers and SSRI's not to take Risperidone. However, this is my first time with an anti-psychotic drug, and so the hellish side-effects may subside with repeated use and tolerance. I obtained 60 1 mg pills, and had already read through the side effects but thought nothing of them. 9:00am - After taking my daily dose of Propranolol and Lustral, I decided to put a 1mg pill down the hatch. Little did I know of what this tiny fragment of chalk had in store for me. Feeling nothing whatsoever for the first hour. 10:00am - I am now noticing a sedate but not sleepy feeling ensuing. Late for college and a session in the recording studio, I hurry down as quick as possible. By the time I get there the effects are coming on heavy. It is a silently peaceful spasticated feeling. At this time I am still skeptical and put it down to lack of sleep. 10:30am - Sitting in the recording studio with my fellow collaborator, I am having extreme difficulty concentrating on what he's saying. To me it's just 'bla blallab bla bal. balba balbb bla', and Im staring at him with this fixed expression completely aware of my ridiculous state. I am now aware that that my eyes are physically out of sync: I have trouble looking at anything and I am physically cross-eyed. 10:45am - Trying desperately to make head or tail of this song we are producing, we call in another producer. I tell them what I've taken and they just laugh in my face. By this stage I cannot think straight at all and I have real bad cotton-mouth. I desperately need a drink, and I am getting hot and cold flushes all over. I stumble out of the studio into the hallway to get some air. 11:00am - I am still monging in the hall. The producer comes out and starts talking technical bollox to me, but it's as if my universal translator has just switched itself off. I notice everything goes quiet and I realize I'm going to faint, before falling to the floor. I open my eyes and I'm on my knees. The producer is sting babbling technical shite, obviously completely oblivious to my fainting. Im laying there at his feet pondering the situation; I figure it must be the combination of the beta-blockers' anti-hypertensive effect and the intitial blood pressure lowering effect of the Risperdal combined. Maybe that explains looking boss-eyed. Miss-co-ordination when walking. 11:10am - Sod the recording session. I feel so fucked up now I just want to go home. The effects are at max, 'and they give this shit to mental patients' I think to myself. I will just go lay down and ride it out. Lost my appetite completely. Stupid fuck that I am, I decide to give it one more go the next day, just to check it wasn't an anomaly. A few seconds after ingestion I realize what a cunt I am, and try to regurgitate to no effect. Once in the blood, a panic attack ensues, followed by another, followed by another. All I can think is how shit a day I'm going to have. Then I realize I'm scheduled for work this evening. What a stupid prick. However, it made the work pass without bore, so could be a good drug to go to work on? I doubt it! The moral here is, think twice before taking anti-psychotics whilst taking beta-blockers and anti-depressants.

我试了一次UR-144, 我开始变得非常高兴, 我跑去院子里拿一个球, 我们必须看起来有点奇怪在院子里扔这个篮球在凌晨2点. 这实际上是非常有趣的. 在身体效应减弱后, 我经历了偏执的经历. 我认为我会被抓住的肯定在这样一个高的状态. 但这只持续了一秒钟, 这确实让我对UR-144保持警. 我从来没有感觉到一个大麻素很喜欢它. 这是一种最强烈的桑, 完全没有石头. 现在我们要做些什么? 我有一个朋友可以以合理的价格和质量获得这些大麻素. 我曾经尝试过几次使用大麻素, 而且还使用了许多合法的草药混合物. 一个管道装满了未知数量的大麻混合物. 看起来足够两次. 这样一来, 几乎只剩下一半的粉末. 我开始从机器中吸取一点,但我很惊,当我几乎完成后,它开始大量吸烟, 我相信一些树脂已经开始吸烟以及由于过器的增加热量. 我立即知道我会遇到一场艰难的旅程, 我可能在较高剂量范围的某个地方消耗, 没有耐受性. 我也许有点欺骗了自己, 自动认为这会是坏事; 最终我把这些感觉带到了自己身上. 接下来发生的事情并不一定是不愉快的, 这就好像我吃了大量的纯种草. 我开始,抽,移动, 这也是我对大麻素的常见问题. 咖啡因也能提升我的体力. 我常常跑步或炼以平静下来. 这一次, 没有任何简单的炼方式, 没有任何器械, 我试着做伸展, , 俯卧, 坐姿, 总的来说, 在凌晨四点, 在一平方英尺的空间里炼, 如果我停下来, 震会回来, 但不会像以前那么严重. 身体上的伤害很烦人, 但心理上的伤害更糟. 我不能理解任何东西任何轻松,即使试图. 我无法完成大学水平的数学方程, 除了失去思维能力外, 我还有许多奇怪的想法, 我认为这些化学物质无法进入我的身体, 所以它们正在破坏我的,肝脏,骨和肌肉. 它们在我的身体中被困在不应该存在的地方. 这是一个令人不安的想法, 另一个有趣的想法是, 我们正在观看一段视频, 但一旦我停止笑, 视频开始让我愤怒和焦虑. 我想要看其他视频,听音乐, 这相当类似于酸性旅行中的某些时刻, 房间变成了一片色彩和城堡的奇迹之地, 一个美丽的红色和绿色的平凡平板. 门,例如,成为一个巨大的草地. 基本上它看起来像是旧电影中的色画. 这发生在我的视力变得"失控", 尽管如此,我还是很惊,因为即使在高剂量的酸中, 这只持续了几秒钟. 这种经历让我感到非常丧, 我从来没有吸过很多年大麻. 只有一个或两个会议与多个大碗高品质的草可以比较这个单击的经验. 整体而言, 很不愉快, 因为身体强烈地要求我起床做点什么. 我看起来和感觉像一个傲慢的瓜几个小时, 后果明显,有些HPPD仍然存在. 很有趣的是,我可以很好地回忆起发生了什么. 这使我不再想使用大麻素, 甚至可能暂时停止使用真正的大麻. 在几次高剂量尝试之后, 旅行太过艰苦, 无法放松. 现在我唯一能使用它们的方法是作为我炼的动机. 其他:

Confused and Anxious JWH-210 and& UR-144 by anonymous Citation:   anonymous. "Confused and Anxious: An Experience with JWH-210 and& UR-144 (exp95403)". Erowid.org . Aug 3, 2015. erowid.org/exp/95403 DOSE: vaporized JWH-210 (powder / crystals) vaporized UR-144 (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 165 lb I tried one hit of UR-144, with a low tolerance. I proceeded to get exceptionally high, and could not control my euphoria. I ran to get a ball to play with in the yard, and immediately began throwing it (hard) at my friend. We must have looked a little strange throwing this basketball around in the yard at 2 in the morning. It was actually very fun. After the physical effects wore off, I had a paranoid experience. I thought that I would get caught for sure in such a high state. But this lasted only a second, so overall this experience was awesome. It did make me wary of UR-144. I had never felt a cannabinoid quite like it. It is like the strongest sativa you could imagine, with absolutely no stone. -------------------------- A friend had access to these cannabinoids for a reasonable price and quality. I had some experience using cannabinoids, as I had tried them a few times and furthermore used many of the legal herbal blends available. A pipe was loaded with an unknown amount of the cannabinoid mixture. It looked like enough for 2 hits. A hit was taken which left about half of the powder visibly left. I began to take a hit off the apparatus but was surprised when it began smoking heavily with yellow smoke after I was almost finished. I believe that some of the built of resin had begun to smoke as well due to the increasing heat of the filter. I immediately knew that I was in for a ride and that it may well be unpleasant. I probably consumed somewhere in the higher dose range experience, with no tolerance. However, I probably somewhat tricked myself by automatically assuming it would be bad; ultimately I brought these feelings upon myself. What ensued was not necessarily unpleasant, but was an extremely intense stoned feeling. It was as if I had eaten a huge amount of pure sativa hash. I began to shake, twitch, move, and in general could not sit still. This is a common recurring theme of my cannabinoid experiences. It amps me up physically similarly to caffeine. Often when this happens, I will just go for a run or work out to calm down. This time, there was no easy way to exercise, with no apparatuses, so instead I tried to do stretches, yoga, pushups, sit ups, and in general probably looked quite strange at 4:00 in the morning exercising in a space of 1 square foot. If I stopped, the shaking would return, but not as bad as it was before. The physical effects were annoying, but the mental effects were in a way even worse. I could not comprehend anything with any ease whatsoever, even when trying. I could not do college level math equations which I have a decent level of experience with, both because of a lack of concentration, and an extremely diminished memory. Besides the loss of my mental capabilities, I also had many weird and trippy thoughts that may or may not have been logical. One of the harsher ones was that I thought that the chemicals had no where to go in my body, so they were destroying my kidneys, liver, bones, and muscles. They were getting caught in places in my body where they shouldn't be. This was an uncalming thought, although to be honest I really didn't care at the time. Another interesting thought was that we were watching a video, which at first was very funny, and I was laughing very hard. But once I stopped laughing, the video began to make me angry and feel anxious. I wanted to watch other videos and listen to music, and in some way be stimulated. This was quite akin to some moments during an acid trip where a change of setting is necessary for whatever reason. At one point during the experience, I completely zoned out and the room became a wonderland of colours and castles, a beautiful bucolic pallet of light reds and greens. The door, for example, became a giant pasture of grass. Basically it looked like a matte painting from old movies. This happened when my vision became 'unfocused' and I zoned out. It still surprised be, as I don't get many visuals at all even on high doses of acid. This only lasted for a few seconds. This experience was very overwhelming, and I exclaimed multiple times that the hit had thoroughly destroyed me. I had not been that high in many years of smoking weed. Only one or two sessions with multiple big bong bowls of high grade weed could compare to this single hit experience. It was overall unpleasant because of the strong physical urges it gave me to get up and do something. I looked and felt like an insolent fool for some hours, and probably was to some extent. The after effects are noticeable and some HPPD remains. It is interesting that I can so well recall what happened. This made me not want to do cannabinoids any more, and may even stop with real weed for a good time. The cannabinoids do not seem to sit well with me after a couple tries of high doses. The trip is too overpowering and it is impossible to chill out. The only way I would use them now is as a motivator for me to work out.

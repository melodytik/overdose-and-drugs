I went through 2 grams in one sitting before tossing it and going to sleep. Though friends who tried NEP thought it was hexen at first--again the SSRI blocking the purported serotonin activity here is probably what's causing it to not be remarkable for me. Of course this is subjective and I'm unsure one can be aware one's IQ is tanking and brain cells are being lost. For what it's worth, it does not seem to hinder performance at work and does not result in behavioral or psychological changes that are noticeable to close relatives.

Nov 28, 2019. erowid.org/exp/113827 DOSE: T+ 0:00 100 ug buccal ETH-LAD (blotter / tab) T+ 0:00 7 - 12 mg insufflated 5-MeO-AMT (powder / crystals) T+ 48:00 200 ug buccal ETH-LAD (blotter / tab) T+ 48:00 1 line insufflated 5-MeO-AMT (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 170 lb It was a random though to experiment with the combination of these substances, initially I wasn’t really sure what to expect. One tab of ETH-LAD (100ug) bucally, and a small line of Alpha O up the nose, administered at the same time. Looking back at the trip it’s sort of hard for me to remember exactly what happened, although I do remember intensely tripping. I do remember intensely tripping. What comes back to me is that I remember seeing a flower of life oval in the dark room along with closed eye visuals running toward me and cycling and spiraling almost in a ketamine dissociative like fashion sort of “summoners skull” like in some ways lol. The CEVs were not as intense as DMT or 5-meo-dmt but there was some color to it but a lot less intricate, not the focal point of the trip. Not hypnotizing but mesmerizing CEVs! I decided to redose 48 hours after. With 2 tabs of ETH-LAD and about 1 and a half of the original dose of alpha-o. Much to my surprise this one was close to equal in intensity and the come down was a bit easier on the body. I remember finding the visuals to be very intriguing. This was the type of trip that produced a heady mindset afterward, Very profound and though provoking. This year it has came to me that the difference in effects from substances is a new found understanding or a new found perception. Both very helpful in seeing my true self and approaching social interaction with a new sense of confident. This trip was a great way to put a cap on my year of Psychonautics and a great source of inspiration for my music production. I have been producing for about 8 years now on and off. Some people have thought I had a unique angle toward design.

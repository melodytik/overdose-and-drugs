我有史以来的第一个兴奋剂 我从来没有真正感兴趣过兴奋剂, 我经常阅读很多经验报告, 我发现了乙烯酸又名乙烯, 它看起来很安全, 我订购了一些,并试过它到达的同一天. 唯一的问题是剂量, 因为我找不到关于口服剂量的太多信息, 只是知道供应商提供50毫克颗粒和其他和注入剂量应该是25 100毫克所以我决定给50毫克口服剂量去. 时间表: 我在我的毫克量表上加重50毫克的剂量, 并将其包裹在纸巾中. 我把它放在一旁, 没有发生任何事情,所以我真的试了一下. 时钟显示下午3点,我降落了组织,这是午餐后2小时. 我还在办公室工作, 在那一刻,我的心情是中立的. 我知道会发生一些事情, 但当时没有什么, 我确信它不会那么难, 现在开始了. 我心情开始好转, 轻微的刺激开始起作用. 在接下来的20分钟里, 第二剂可能是因为效果越来越明显而开始起作用. 这就是我第一次使用兴奋剂, 感觉很好, 尽管如此, 我觉得我还没有达到目标, 由于这似乎是相对较高的剂量, 我认为口服可能不如吸鼻子那么有效, 所以我决定吸鼻子更多, 并更深入地探索这种化合物. 我用信用卡了一条小线, 但它并没有像2C那样糟糕, 因为疼痛在几分钟内就消失了. 我决定再吸入20毫克. 我不想在这个实验中进一步. 饮用水却让我感到极其清爽. T+2:15 时间回家了. 我离开了大楼,当我走出去我开始感觉更好. 所有微小的快乐,如风,新鲜的空气,耳机里的音乐, 持续了1个半小时 (所以很快,因为它是几个小时内所有剂量的混合,单剂量的峰值肯定会持续更长时间). 我觉得自己很自信,很冷静,很社交,很适合使用, 我回家和女朋友聊天. 我几乎无法停止说话. 音乐听起来比平常好一点. 我把音量放大了, 不像我放低迷幻药的音量. 下来是平稳而缓慢的, 因为没有太多的时间从最后一剂. 我一点也不饿, 却记得我已经吃了6个小时了, 我终于吃了一顿体面的饭. 我觉得大约在那个时候睡着了. 睡觉很差,第二天我很累. 另一方面, 我也期待它会很棒, 我没有测量我的心率在峰值但我猜它可能是100bpm而我的静止心率是50bpm. 我还在高峰时感到轻微不安, 我在下药时没有再服用的冲动, 我在第二天的情绪也很好, 总之,我会把乙化留给特殊场合, 经过两周的时间, 我再次服用乙胺, 我们喝酒! 其他: 发布时间:2017年5月21日

May 21, 2017. erowid.org/exp/103115 DOSE: T+ 0:00 50 mg oral Ethylphenidate (powder / crystals) T+ 0:45 50 mg oral Ethylphenidate (powder / crystals) T+ 1:55 25 mg insufflated Ethylphenidate (powder / crystals) T+ 2:05 20 mg insufflated Ethylphenidate (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 75 kg My First Stimulant Ever Stimulants were never something I was really interested in, however, despite that I decided to try cocaine sometime. I read a lot of experience reports regularly and I’ve discovered ethylphenidate aka ethylcaine and was astonished by the comparisons to cocaine. It looks quite safe as its relative methylphenidate is a widely used prescription drug. I ordered some and tried it the same day it arrived. The only issue was the dosing because I couldn't find much information about oral dosing, just knew vendors offer 50 mg pellets among others and the insufflated dose is supposed to be 25 – 100 mg so I decided to give the 50 mg oral dose a go. Timeline: T-0:40 – I weighted 50 mg dose on my milligram scale and wrapped it into a tissue. I put it away and tried a test dose of a few milligrams firstly. T+0:00 – Nothing happened so I gave it a real try. The clock showed 3 PM and I parachuted the tissue, it was 2 hours after a lunch. I was still sitting in my office at work but I already finished all the work and was prepared to go home whenever I wanted to. My mood was neutral at that moment. T+0:45 – Still nothing. I knew something would happen but as there were nothing at that time I was sure it wouldn’t be as hard so I weighted another 50 mg and swallowed a second bomb. T+0:55 – Here it comes. My mood started to lift as well as a mild stimulation started to kick in. The effect were building over the next 20 minutes. T+1:45 – The second dose probably started to kick in as the effects were being more pronounced. This was my first euphoric stimulant and there was a feeling of well-being present but nothing I call euphoria. However, I would hardly call it disappointment as I felt great anyway. T+1:55 – I had already got above 100 mg in my system but I felt I didn’t hit the target yet and as this seemed to be relatively high dose I thought the oral ingestion may not be as effective as snorting so I decided to snort some more and explore this compound deeper. I had scaled a small line and prepared it with a credit card and snorted a half to one nostril and the second half to the other. It was burning quite a lot but it wasn’t as bad as 2Cs because the pain diminished within a few minutes. T+2:05 – Not much more happened so I decided to snort another 20 mg. That was 150 mg in sum and enough for the day as I didn’t want to go any farther in this experiment. My appetite was completely suppressed but drinking water felt extremely refreshing. T+2:15 – Time to go home. I left the building and when I went out I started feeling even better. All the tiny joys such as wind, fresh air, music in earphones amplified the feeling of well-being. About this time I was peaking and it lasted hour and a half (so shortly because it was a mix of all the doses over the hours, a peak of a single dose would certainly last longer). I FELT CONFIDENT, VERY CALM AND VERY PROSOCIAL, VERY USABLE AND YET HIGH. T+3:15 – I arrived home and talked with my girlfriend. I was SO MUCH talkative and almost couldn’t stop it. Music sounded a little better than usual. I turned the volume up unlike with psychedelics where I turn it down. T+3:45 – Started coming down. The come down was smooth and slow because not much time passed from the last dose. I was not hungry at all but I remembered I did not eat for 6 hours which is a huge gap for me so I ate some biscuits. T+5:15 – I didn’t feel the effects anymore and finally ate a decent meal. T+8:30 – I felt asleep approximately at that time. The sleep was poor and I was tired the next day because of it. I was expecting it to be a lot different but on the other hand I was expecting it to be great and that was fulfilled. I didn’t measure my heart rate at the peak but I guess it could be about 100 bpm while my resting heart rate is about 50 bpm. I also had a feeling of slight unease for about 10 minutes when peaking which was probably just a hypertension. I got no urge to redose when coming down and my mood was still positive when the effects faded off. My mood was also positive the next day but I was tired due to the poor sleep. All in all, I will save ethylphenidate for a special occasions because while it’s so lovely it also disrupts a sleep which is a feature I hate. Now, after more than two weeks, I did ethylphenidate again which helped me to finish this writing. Cheers!

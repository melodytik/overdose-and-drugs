我在七月的一次修炼会上遇到了一对有趣的夫妇. 他们给我写下了他们第一次使用这种物质的经历. 虽然我不能保证它的真实性, 他们都似乎是真诚的. 40毫克,合成的TMA-2 晚上八点 40毫克. 在水中溶解. 典型的甲胺不好吃. 8:20 轻微的胃动荡. 是因为我在PIHKAL的TMA-2旅行报告中读到"短暂的肠"吗? 八点四十分加一个. 紧张,兴奋的能量. 眼睛看起来很光亮. 我和K一直在四处走动. 很难坐下来超过几分钟. 我们之间的对话流动非常快. 九点加两个. 9:20加上三个. 现在我们可以看到. 我最记得的是那种充满活力,不停的对话. 我们谈了,谈了,谈了. 虽然有点像MDMA的同情性, 开启心灵的东西,当然,但在不同的方式比MDMA. 这可能是非常有效的治疗. 我把视力集中在一个特定的方向上, 它们与2CB的视觉效果非常相似, 但色彩不如它 - - 变形的表面,光亮的表面上闪的"热"图案, 照明棒, 像往常一样, 提供了视觉享受, 凯的裸体身体感觉充电和刺我的. 这次倒闭是很长的. 这种东西在9个多小时内仍然有效. 医生给我们开了二, 帮助我们在9小时后睡觉. 事实上,我们需要两次瓦利治疗才能休息. 性行为是可能的,但高潮仍然是无法达到的 (类似于MDMA). 醒来后感觉很好. 但这可能是因为瓦利安和睡眠不足. 总的来说,我们与TMA-2的第一次经验是非常有益的. 这种物质似乎可以增强清晰的智力交流, 视觉效果是不引人注目的, 可以引起人们的兴趣. 在高峰期, 准备10个多小时的骑行. 我和K都经历了的胃, 但我的也感觉像即将发生的腹在几个场合 (从来没有实现). 这次旅行无疑值得. 其他: TMA-2 (112):未知背景 (20),第一次 (2),一般 (1)

Sep 8, 2000. erowid.org/exp/2830 DOSE: 40 mg oral TMA-2 (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 175 lb I met an interesting couple at a retreat in July. They gave me a write-up of their first experience with this substance. While I cannot vouch for its veracity, they both seemed genuine and honest. 40 mg., synthesized TMA-2 8 p.m. 40 mg. TMA-2 was dissolved in lemonade. Typical phenethylamine bad taste. 8:20 Mild stomach agitation. Was it real, or was it because I read about 'brief intestinal cramps' in a TMA-2 trip report in PIHKAL? 8:45 Plus one. Nervous, agitated energy. Eyes feeling glossy. K and I keep pacing around. Hard to sit down for more than a few minutes. Conversation between us flowing very quickly. 9:00 Plus two. 9:20 Plus three. TMA-2 takes hold. The thing I remember the most was the energized, nonstop conversation. We talked and talked and talked. There was a bit of the empathic buzz of MDMA, but tempered a nice, unattached (in the Buddhist sense) lucidity. Heart-opening stuff, for sure, but in a different way than MDMA. This could be very effective in therapy. When I directed my vision in a concentrated manner the visuals appeared. They were very similar to 2CB visuals, but not as colorful -- morphing surfaces, shimmery 'heat' patterning on light-colored surfaces, some peripheral sparkling. Glowsticks, as always, provided a visual treat, as did a glowing Frisbee and fireflies leaving blurry light trails against the darkness. K's naked flesh feels electrically-charged and tingly against mine. The comedown was long. This stuff is still working at 9+ hours. Benzodiazepenes were just what the doctor ordered to facilitate sleep at 9 hours. In fact, it took 2 administrations of Valium before we were able to get some rest. Sex was possible, but orgasm remained just out of reach (similar to MDMA). Woke up feeling fine. Slight foggy hangover, but that could be due to Valium and lack of sufficient sleep. Overall, our first experience with TMA-2 was very rewarding. This substance seems to enhance clear, intellectual communication and is also physically energizing without being enervating. Visuals are unobtrusive but can be summoned with concentration, and can be interesting. During the peak it gets much more visually stimulating. Prepare for a 10+ hour ride. K and I both experienced fluttery stomachs, but mine also felt like imminent diarrhea on a few occasions (which never materialized). The mild body load was certainly well worth the trip.

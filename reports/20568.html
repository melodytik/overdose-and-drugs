我在网上搜索了更多关于草植物的信息, 我发现这个主题的数据很少. 我有一个朋友在网上买了一些子种子. 我同意做一个保姆, 蒂姆试图吸烟种子. 我说服他开始用很低的剂量,大约3种种子. 我想试试抽几支,等待一个小时左右,然后可能再抽3支. 这种种子很小, 经过一个小时, 没有明显的效果, 再次,没有什么值得注意的. 这结束了今晚的实验. 在接下来的尝试中, 经过大约45分钟, 他又吸了8支. 他说有一些微小的"woozy"效应, 但没有什么实质性的. 他今晚辞职了. 最后一个实验比较激进. 他一次抽了十几个种子. 半小时后, 他又抽了大约20个种子. 他声称"迷幻"效应已经回来, 他终于抽了100多个种子. 事实上,他吸了剩下的烟. 他对种子没有任何显著反应. 我很惊这剂量不足. 这种种子的化物含量明显高于叶子. 我没有提交这份报告来建议你从100多个种子开始. 我建议您使用我们为找到适当剂量而采取的谨慎方法. 我希望将来能有更多关于这家工厂的信息. 尼格尔 (278):小组 (2-9) (17),一般 (1)

Jun 3, 2003. erowid.org/exp/20568 DOSE: 100 seeds smoked Hyoscyamus niger BODY WEIGHT: 180 lb I have searched the net far and wide for more information on the Henbane plant, especially dosage information. I have found very little data on the subject. Some time ago, a friend of mine purchased some henbane seeds from an online vendor. I agreed to be a sitter while Tim tried smoking the seeds. I convinced him to start with a very low dose, about 3 seeds. I figured try smoking a few, wait an hour or so, then possibly smoke 3 more. The seeds are very small, and Tim was not certain that he got a hit. After an hour went by, there were no noticable effects, so he smoked 3 more seeds. Again, nothing of note. This concluded the experiment for the evening. On the next attempt, Tim smoked about 6 seeds at once. After about 45 minutes, he smoked about 8 more. He said there was a very slight 'woozy' effect, but nothing substantial. He quit for the evening. The last experiment was quite a bit more aggressive. He smoked about a dozen seeds at once. After about a half hour, he smoked roughly 20 more seeds. He claimed that the 'woozy' effect had returned, but he was nothing even close to a 'trip'. He ended up smoking well over 100 seeds that evening. In fact, he smoked all that was left. He did not have any significant reaction to the seeds. After reading up on Henbane, I am very surprised that this was not a sufficient dose. The seeds apparently are higher in alkaloid content than the leaves. I AM NOT SUBMITTING THIS REPORT TO SUGGEST THAT YOU START OFF WITH OVER 100 SEEDS. BEING THAT TROPANE ALKALOIDS CAN BE VERY DANGEROUS, I SUGGEST THAT YOU USE THE CAUTIOUS APPROACH THAT WE DID TO FIND THE PROPER DOSAGE. I am hoping that there will be more information about this plant available in the future.

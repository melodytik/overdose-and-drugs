This made me curious so I bought a few pills from a friend. I took one and within fifteen minutes I was feeling bad. I smoked a few puffs off a joint, hoping it would make my stomach feel better, but it didn't work. I became very nauseous and vomited repeatedly with dry heaves. Also I kept having small bowel movements. When I closed my eyes it felt like everything was spinning and I couldn't stand it for long. There was a nervous feeling in my arms and legs. It felt like energy was trying to spray out of my hands, and I had a bad feeling all over. My mind felt cloudy. The experience was pretty rough. Finally I was able to sleep and I was so grateful. Twelve hours after dosing I was able to finish a bowl of soup. It's now twenty-four hours after dosing and I feel alright but I'm still kind of drained. I read up on possible side effects beforehand, but I never expected such a strong reaction to the drug. By the way, I did dream more than usual but they weren't good dreams.

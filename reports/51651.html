我对Dilantin/phenytoin的体验与一般的原剂师所期望的完全不同. 我必须因医疗原因服用这种药物. 这是我的故事... 我从小就把自己看作是深入了解内因质的, 我在15岁左右开始出现部分发作, 我甚至不知道有这样的东西! 我只是在这些事件中知道有什么不对. 这就像一个坏的甲胺/上腺素警报. 2005年7月, 我处于一个非常可怕的状态, 临床抑郁, 由于未经诊断的病情, 我的头脑一团糟, 我在家里遭受了一次非常严重的, 这是我第一次意识到发生了什么. 我患有. 在急诊室里, 他们首先给我注射了大量的迪兰丁, 对于我来说,这是令人惊叹的,我的第一个月, 在那个时候,我被处方为每天200mg (分两剂). 发作后的第一两周非常不舒服, 我感觉完全幸福, 仿佛我有一个开/关快乐开关! 感觉就像我的大脑被温柔地抱在一个温暖的奢华毛皮挂的吊床里. 过量服用也会对认知产生衰弱影响,警觉水平受损,双视强度高,思维困难. 现在我的血值为0.5,是治疗范围0.25的两倍. 医生很惊他们没有伤害我的肝脏. 你必须了解到2005年7月17日之前我生活中发生了什么. 我在生命中处于极低的状态, 危险的抑郁和不断的发作. 我不知道自己在发作, 我觉得自己正在崩, 然后! 我开始了新的生活. 在医院里醒来, 感觉就像我想象的双极症患者所经历的. 尽管如此, 我有一个更为创伤的经历. 这真是太棒了! 抑郁症在那天消失了, 当然我也像其他人一样有负面想法, 但它们只是漂浮而去. 我想知道如果它是Dilantin? 现在在两到六个月的时间里, 我每天服用200毫克/两次, 我觉得自己是个新人. 我从来没有控制过自己的思想, 我觉得自己已经回到了正轨, 虽然我有很多天服用这种剂量, 我会在句子中间停下来, 忘记自己在说什么, 有时我觉得自己像个尸! 经过几个月的不良副作用后, 我被转换为一种新的药物Trileptal, 我认为迪兰丁是一种很好的诺特罗普药, 有些副作用可能太多了. 布鲁格曼西亚是危险的! 我认为这可能是我多年前开始抓住这些人的原因. 所以,大家要保持安全... 迈克D,来自加利福尼亚州 已使用的证券 药品 - 素 (261):健康问题 (27),抑郁症 (15),健康益处 (32),不适用 (38)

Emotional Renewal but Bad Side Effects Phenytoin (Dilantin) by MikeD Citation:   MikeD. "Emotional Renewal but Bad Side Effects: An Experience with Phenytoin (Dilantin) (exp51651)". Erowid.org . Feb 5, 2007. erowid.org/exp/51651 DOSE: 200 mg oral Pharms - Phenytoin (daily) BODY WEIGHT: 130 lb My experience with Dilantin/phenytoin was quite different than what the average enthogenist can expect. I have to take this drug for medical reasons. This is my story... I had considered myself to be deep into enthobotony since a young age, and had many growth experiences. At some point I started experiencing partial seizures around the age of fifteen, and I didn’t know it. I didn’t even know there was such a thing! I just knew something was wrong during those episodes. Kind of like a bad phenylethylamine/adrenaline alert. Well, July 2005 I was in a really horrible place, clinical depression, seizing and not knowing it. My mind was in a wreck from my undiagnosed condition, and the seventeenth of that month I snapped. I suffered a really bad Grand-mal seizure that day at home, then another in the ER. It was the first time I realized what was going on. I have epilepsy. First thing they did in the ER was pump me full of injectable Dilantin, so much so it was at toxic levels in my blood for the three following weeks. It was amazing to me, my first month I experience a complete rebirth in my psyche. I was prescribed 200mg/day (in two doses) at that time. The first week or two after the seizures where discomforting, but I was all overdosed on dilantin. I was feeling totally blissed, as if I had an on/off pleasure switch! There where many moments, where it literally felt like my brain was being gently cradled in a warm luxurious fur lined hammock. The overdose also caused debilitating effects on cognition, impaired alertness level, strong double vision, and difficulties thinking. At that point my blood level read 0.5, double the 0.25 therapy range. My doctor was surprised they didn’t damage my liver. To understand how Dilantin effected my emotional state at that time you have to understand, what had been happening in my life up to July 17th, 2005. I was at an extremely low point in my life, dangerously depressed and seizing constantly. I didn’t know that I was seizing, and the depression was a symptom. I felt as if I was falling apart, I basically was down and out. Then Bam! I enter a new life. Have a mid life crisis and wake up in the hospital on the other side of the spectrum. It felt like, what I would imagine bi-polar sufferers go through. All though, I had a more traumatic experience. It was amazing because I stayed up there! All the depression went away that day and hasn’t returned. Sure I get negative thoughts like everyone else, but they just float away. I wonder if it’s the dilantin? So now between the two and six month period, I was taking 200mg/twice daily and my blood levels where at the therapeutic range. I was feeling like a new man. I had not had control over my thoughts before like dilantin has given me. I was feeling very “on track”, really back in the natural cycle. I had many days though at this dose, when I still found it hard to cognitively function. Thoughts would come slow, I would find it really hard to focus, would stop in the middle of a sentence and forget what I was talking about, and some times just feel like a zombie! After months of bad side effects I was switched to a new drug Trileptal, which I think might have more promise as a nootropic. I think dilantin was a good nootropic, only because it was a great mood-elevator. Some of the side effects can be too much, though. P.S.-Brugmansia is dangerous! I think that might be the reason I started seizing those many years ago. So, stay safe everybody...

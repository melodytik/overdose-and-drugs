中文译文 致幻剂

首先我应该说这份报告主要是为那些有过一些迷幻体验的人准备的. 我真的没有时间深入探讨每一个洞察力. 下午2点 我通过一张信用卡吸入2毫克的DOI. 然后我去做一些快速的差事. 下午三点 我回来了. 第一个警报? 可能有点. 感觉就像一种普通的甲胺的早期发作, 也许有点安非他林感觉, 我猜这是可以预料到的. 下午四点 没有太大的区别. 现在有相当的身体声. 感觉像是安非他胺/乙胺. 我指的是安非他命, 因为能量流动在我的整个身体. 我指的是"乙胺",因为至少在这个时候, 这显然不是某些高阶精神药物 (LSD,,4-aco-mipt) 可以产生的神能. 整体而言,这对身体和心灵来说是非常轻松的. 在这个时候,我称之为一个加一个半. 还没有"迷幻". 下午四点半 好吧,这里是视觉. 首先我注意到墙上的颜色有所变化. 色和其他色彩似乎更加突出. 有些颜色会变化并流血到其他颜色. 我检查了痕迹, 发现它们不是典型的"长模糊"痕迹, 下午五点 我觉得有必要打扫我的公寓. 清理这些东西给了我满足感和一种释放能量的方式. 我必须说,如果我服用20毫克的阿德拉尔我会有更多的类能量. 在这个方面,DOI实际上比2C-I更顺. 下午五点半 我玩吉他一段时间, 我发现是增强非常漂亮. 通常在迷幻药时, 我会想出新的和原创的东西来演奏, 有时差异是非常惊人的. 无论如何, DOI, 既是迷幻药, 也是安非他林, 下午六点 现在我注意到地毯上有一些变化. 其他视觉效果难以解释. 它们是微妙的,但美丽的. 我相对平静的心理状态 (考虑到我在) 结合相对平静的视觉障碍状态 - - 与高剂量的LSD,相反, 我记得DOI对于解决当时我生活中的物流/情境问题很有用. 然而,我不记得这究竟是什么. 晚上6点15分 我下楼的烦和精神分裂的邻居来看看我是否想做任何事情. 我告诉他这是一个糟糕的时机. 我打开"辛普森一家"和刚刚来过的朋友一起抽烟. 吸食大麻显著改变了这种体验. 既然我通常不喜欢吸胺,我通常不喜欢吸胺,我并不惊. 基本上它把一个有趣的,清醒的,幽默的旅程变成了更为混乱的,内向的东西. 我只是后悔抽了一点烟, 等待它消逝. 晚上七点 我的朋友不得不离开一会儿, 我的烦和精神分裂的邻居回来了, 这一次带来了一些非常的毒头看起来我从来没有见过的家伙. 这家伙问我吸烟他,我说'不'. 然后他问我是否有什么东西要卖,我说,'不.'然后我给我的邻居一个讨厌的目光,把他们从我的门口. 接下来的几个小时我都看着电视. 直到我们... 晚上九点 我的人带着一大堆优质的酒和三盒鞭子来了! 没必要说,接下来的几个小时都是在打灯泡. 当然,我们一直在抽烟, 喝着最好的毛伊瓦伊香. 我也喝了一些酒, 并且吃了10毫克的瓦, 我可能会吃更多的, 但这就是我剩下的. 晚上十点 效果没有变化. 仍然在一个强大的++. 虽然头脑很清晰, 我可以很好地与人们互动, 晚上11点 没有变化. 我出去抽烟,看夜空. 我看到了一些看似彗星的星星在夜空中飞翔, 树顶在距离树木至少10英尺的地方发光. 偶尔看到的火虫总是引起我的兴趣. 在凌晨12点 没有变化. 现在是中等++. 上午两点 坐在沙发上看电视,喝酒. 睡觉似乎是不可能的,但我很少在凌晨3点之前入睡. 上午三点半 我吃了12毫克的黑激素. 虽然我的身体仍然有点紧张, 所以我抽了一大碗,然后躺在床上. 在这段时间里, 我得到了整个旅程中最好的视觉效果. 我在天花板上形成了半径图案, 这让我想起了我在2C-1上看到的东西. 差异在于我的天花板变成了来自太空的场景, 这些生物图案似乎是从这个空间投射出来的. 我睡了四个小时, 才醒来. 至少我睡了! 在... 第二天凌晨11点半 仍然感觉轻微刺激, 但绝对没有. 到了下午, 在... 什么结论? 首先我应该说我认为2毫克是低剂量的DOI. 我认为舒尔金低估了这一点. 我也知道很多朋友曾经用更高剂量的化学物质试验过, 然而, 似乎不止4毫克需要24小时的旅行. 不幸的是,4毫克似乎是人们需要的剂量, 我听说有些人用更高剂量进入完全陌生和不可识别的世界. 我觉得DOI适合作为一个"入门级"的旅行, 然而它似乎变得非常强烈和持久的两倍,或更高. 剂量高于2mg可延长作用时间至50小时左右. 根据我的同伴们所说的, 我在几次活动中观察到. 开始时间在1小时到+++到3小时到++之间. 我怀疑在更高的剂量它得更快. 一个2毫克的剂量持续了大约与LSD一样长时间, 降落也可能会持续更长时间. 总而言之,这是一种相当愉快的化学物质. 如果有人喜欢梅斯卡林和2C-x化学物质他们可能会喜欢这个. 考虑到它的安非他命结构和作用时间, 这种化学物质似乎也提供了积极的推动力或动力, 这并不是一件坏事. 然而,有强迫症或严重躁狂/抑郁症的人可能希望避免这种情况. 和平 已使用的文件: DOI (259):其他 (28),一般 (1)

Oct 21, 2005. erowid.org/exp/46861 DOSE: T+ 0:00 2.0 mg oral DOI (powder / crystals) T+ 4:15 smoked Cannabis (plant material) T+ 7:00 repeated inhaled Nitrous Oxide (gas) T+ 7:00 repeated smoked Cannabis (plant material) T+ 9:00 smoked Tobacco - Cigarettes T+ 13:30 12 mg oral Melatonin (pill / tablet) T+ 13:30 1 bowl smoked Cannabis (plant material) BODY WEIGHT: 150 lb First I should say this report is mostly intended for people with some psychedelic experience. I don't really have time to delve into each and every insight. 2:00pm I ingest 2mg of DOI by licking it off a credit card. Then I go on a few quick errands. 3:00pm Back from my errands. First alerts? Maybe slightly. Feels like very early onset of a generic phenethlamine, maybe a bit amphetamine feeling but not pushy at all. I guess this was to be expected. 4:00pm Not much difference. Got a decent body buzz going on now. Feels amphetamine/phenethylamine. I mean 'amphetamine' because the energy flows throughout my whole body. I mean 'phenethylamine' because, at least at this point, this is clearly not the God-Energy that certain higher order psyhedelics (LSD, mushrooms, 4-aco-mipt) can produce. Overall it feels very easy on the body and very easy on the mind. At this point I'd call it a plus one and a half. Not yet 'psychedelic'. 4:30pm Okay, here come the visuals. First I noticed colors shifting on the walls. Orange and other pastel colors seem accentuated. Some of the colors will change and bleed into other colors. I checked for tracers, and noticed they were not typical 'long-blur' tracers, but more like a delayed snapshot tracer, if that makes any sense. 5:00pm I felt compelled to clean my apartment. It was a bit dirty and getting things clean gave me satisfaction and a way to expell my energy. I must say though, had I taken 20mg of Adderall I would have had a lot more amphetamine-type energy. DOI was actually smoother than 2C-I in this aspect as well. 5:30pm I played guitar for a while, which I found was enhanced very nicely. Normally on psychedelics I think of new and original things to play, and normally on amphetamines I can play with increased speed and precision. Sometimes the difference is pretty amazing. Anyway, DOI, being both psychedelic and amphetamine, captured both these qualities very well, and I had a very satisfying jam. 6:00pm Now I notice some shifting in the carpet. Other visual effects are difficult to explain. They were subtle, but beautiful. My relatively calm state of mind (considering I was tripping) combined with the relatively calm state of visual disturbance -- as opposed to high dose LSD, mushrooms, where everything in your visual field goes nuts -- allowed me to relax and enjoy the subtleties of my current trip. I remember finding DOI useful for working out logistical / situational problems that were going on in my life at the time. However I can't exactly remember what that was all about. 6:15pm My annoying and schizophrenic neighbor from downstairs comes up to 'see if I wanna do anything'. I tell him it's a bad time. I turn on The Simpsons and smoke a bowl with my friend who's just stopped by. Smoking the cannabis significantly changed the experience. Since I don't usually like to smoke on amphetamines.... and I don't usually like to smoke on phenethylamines, I'm not surprised. Basically it turned a fun, clear-headed, good humored trip into something a bit more confusing and turned inward. By no means was this something I couldn't have dealt with, so I just regretted smoking a little and waited for it to ride out. 7:00pm My buddy had to leave for a bit and my annoying and schizophrenic neighbor came back up, this time bringing up some really schetchy crack-head lookin' dude I've never seen before. This dude asked me to smoke him out, and I said 'no'. Then he asked me if I had anything to sell, and I said, 'No.' Then I gave my neighbor a nasty look and kicked them out of my doorway. So this put me in kind of a bad mood for the next couple hours, which I spent just watching TV. Until... 9:00pm My boys roll up with a bunch of quality beer and three boxes of whippits! So needless to say the next couple hours were spent hittin' the bulbs. Of course the entire time we were smoking blunts and bowls of only the finest Maui Waui x Skunk homegrown. I also drank some beer during this time, and ate 10mg valium in hopes that I would eventually be able to sleep that evening. I probably would have ate more benzos but that was all I had left. 10:00pm No change in effects. Still at a strong ++. Though very clearheaded, I could interact with people just fine and they didn't know I was tripping (I asked later to verify). 11:00pm No change. I went outside to have a cigarette and check out the night sky. I saw what looked like comets shooting across the night sky, and the dark color of the sky would pulsate. The treetops were glowing at least 10 feet away from the trees. The occasional firefly going by always caught my interest. 12:00am No change. Medium ++ now. 2:00am Sittin on my couch watching TV, drinking beers. Sleep doesn't seem like a possibility, but then again I rarely fall asleep before 3:00am anyway. 3:30am I ate 12mg melatonin. My body still feels slightly tense and a bit 'up' but my mind is pretty worn out. So I smoke a fat bowl and go lie in bed. During this period I got probably the best visuals of the whole trip. Aztec-looking creatures formed radial patterns on my ceiling, which reminded me very much of what I've seen on 2C-I. The difference was that my ceiling turned into a kind of scene from space, and these creature-patterns seemed to be projected from this space. Soon after that the melatonin made me pass out, and I got about 4 hours of restless sleep before waking up. At least I slept! .... 11:30am, The Next Day Still feeling slightly stimulated, but definitely not tripping. This wore off by mid-afternoon. .... Conclusion? Well, first I should say I consider 2mg to be a low dose of DOI. I think Shulgin lowballed this one. I also know plenty of friends who have experimented with this chemical in doses much higher.... up to 15mg, and have not suffered any obvious health problems. However, it seems that imore than 4mg is asking for a full 24 hour trip. Unfortunately, it seems like 4mg is the kind of dose people need for a full +++ experience. I have heard of people entering completely alien and unrecognizable worlds on higher doses. My feeling is that DOI is suitable as an 'entry-level' trip at low doses, like 2mg. However it appears to get very intense and long-lasting at double that, or higher. Doses above 2mg extend the length of action up to 50 hours or so. This is all based on what my companions have told me, and I've observed on a few occasions. Onset varies between 1 hour to +++ to 3 hours to ++. I suspect at a higher dose it kicks in quicker. A 2mg dose lasted about as long as LSD, except with a longer comeup. The comedown might also last a wee bit longer. In summary.... a pretty pleasant chemical. If someone likes mescaline and 2C-x chemicals they might love this. It was surprisingly easy on the body considering it's amphetamine structure and length of action. This chemical also seems to provide a positive push, or motivation, to get things done. This is not a bad thing.

Not All the Way MPT & Mullein by nowimhere Citation:   nowimhere. "Not All the Way: An Experience with MPT & Mullein (exp113672)". Erowid.org . Oct 21, 2019. erowid.org/exp/113672 DOSE: 40 mg vaporized MPT (powder / crystals) smoked Mullein BODY WEIGHT: 170 lb For context, when purchasing my last batch of 5 MeO DMT, I wanted to add a few other things to the cart. And did some investigating. And a trip report was enough to make me hit the "add to cart" button. I did it in a different way. Basically the same way I smoke 5 MeO DMT (sandwhiched in mullein leaves). But with a twist. I weighed out two 15 mgs and one 10 mg. I also bought two brand new pipes, to facilitate this process. I put each dose in each pipe as I would with a single hit. Reason I did this was I read in a subreddit that some people where having issues vapurising the whole thing. But I knew this particular method would work. Had very mild pre trip anxiety as I didn't know exactly how this would play out. Had very mild pre trip anxiety as I didn't know exactly how this would play out. T 00:00 - took the first 15 mg hit. Waited a little bit. And could notice things were visually changing around me. Kinda like the onset of a acid or mushroom trip. Nothing like the trip report... So I knew it was time to take the second 15mg hit from pipe number two. Things started changing more. No geometric patterns tho, which was interesting... The visuals were pretty cool tho. I would breathe, and the room would breathe with me. I decided to take the third hit from pipe number 3.... Now, the visuals were already intense after the second hit... But they just intensified even more. I was looking at this sock monkey doll I have which is next to another doll. And both dolls appeared to change faces, maniacal and back to angelic. It was interesting thats for sure. T:00:30 - I was getting tired of looking at that one side of the room so I changed positions and decided to look at the other wall, which is usually behind me. There is only a mandala on that wall. So it was less visually distracting. The mandala turned almost "3-D" . Was cool. Also, this whole time I found myself getting horney so I just decided to appease myself and rub one out. No looking at porn or anything. Just thinking about a girl I like personally, and find attractive. Anyways, took a little while to "release" but when I did... Something interesting happened. For a couple of seconds at least, I somewhat whited out. It was "almost" non dualistic in nature. T:01:00 - visual effects pretty much all gone (zero). Still had a pretty good "high" going tho. T:02:00 - Mental high still going strong. T:03:00 - Mental high still there, but less. One thing I will say is that I intend to up my dose the next time for this. Probably 50, or even 60mgs. Same method. And most importantly, I can see how this substance CAN be used to acheive a breakthrough experience. If it actually will or not... Will yet be determined. But overall, was enjoyable. Also, interesting how the "peak" experience "came" after I did. That was like the 40min mark and I was staring at the ceiling. I said "almost white out" cause the tiles on my ceiling are white, and all of reality whited out, except for the crosses of the tiles. Like seeing the energetic essense of it. But as quickly as it came, it left. And I feel like that's what this substance was trying to do, this whole time.

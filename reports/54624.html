我每天服用200毫克的塞特拉林和300毫克的布洛普治疗抑郁症. 我读过抗抑郁药可以减轻LSD等迷幻药的效果. 我认为一个小旅行比没有旅行更好,对吧? 起初我只吃了一片, 因为我对精神活性物质很敏感. 我和一个经验丰富的精神探险家朋友R在我的公寓里. 我有点紧张, 因为我以前在急诊室遇到过不良药物相互作用, 但我很有信心我的一天不会在医院结束. 我和R看电视等我感觉到一些东西. 我确实感到很奇怪. 感觉有点像身体高从低剂量的DXM,几乎没有精神/情感的阴谋. 我的子变得很硬和疼痛, 我不能正确集中我的眼睛. 我没有任何视觉或听觉幻觉. 我觉得我的身体充满了声, 仿佛它很焦虑, 准备飞往外太空, 我在第一个药后4到5小时服用了第二片, 这不是. 另一件有点有趣的事:我不能品尝食物. 我的大脑收到的信息都是一样的, 我猜这是一种共感, 像听到颜色或其他什么. 我花了大部分的'旅程'躺着看电视, 我没有睡觉. 总体而言,这是一个巨大的失望, 随后是24小时的头痛. 如果我想试着停药几天, 这通常不值得. 其他: LSD (2),药物 - - 塞特拉林 (88),药物 - - 布普罗 (87):宿醉 / 几天后 (46),组合 (3),小组 (2-9) (17)

Jun 14, 2007. erowid.org/exp/54624 DOSE: T+ 0:00 200 mg oral Pharms - Sertraline (daily) T+ 0:00 300 mg oral Pharms - Bupropion (daily) T+ 0:00 1 hit oral LSD (blotter / tab) T+ 4:30 1 hit oral LSD (blotter / tab) BODY WEIGHT: 235 lb I take 200 mg sertraline and 300 mg bupropion daily for depression. I had read that anti-depressants can lessen the effects of psychedelics such as LSD. I figured that a little trip is better than no trip at all, right? I took just one tab at first, because I am sensitive in general to psychoactive substances. I was with a very experienced psychonaut friend, R, just hanging out in my apartment. I was a bit nervous because I've landed in the ER with bad drug interactions before and it sucks. But I felt pretty confident that my day wouldn't end in a hospital. R and I watched tv while I waited to feel something. I did feel pretty weird. It felt a little like the body high from a low dose of DXM, with almost none of the mental/emotional intrigue. My neck got very stiff and sore, and I couldn't focus my eyes properly. I had no visual or auditory hallucinations of any kind. The most noticeable aspect was a kind of buzzing feeling in my whole body, like it was antsy and ready to blast off to outer space but couldn't quite make it off the ground. I took a second tab about 4-5 hours after the first one just to see if it was a matter of dosage. It wasn't. One thing that was mildly interesting: I couldn't taste food. My brain was receiving the information all the same, but I was experiencing it in a way other than tasting, I don't know what exactly. I guess that's a kind of synesthesia, like hearing colors or whatever. I spent most of the 'trip' laying around watching tv and trying to sleep off the neck and jaw stiffness. I couldn't sleep though. Overall a huge disappointment followed by a massive 24-hour headache. I will have to plan very carefully if I want to try to go off the meds for a few days so I can attempt to trip. It's not usually worth it.

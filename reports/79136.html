中文译文 抗胆碱能类

我之前做过几次, 结果却没有很令人满意 (听觉幻觉和记忆丧失). 我试过它之前我已经服用了15-20个25毫克的药丸. 我决定要做一个非常生动的体验, 最后我又拿了20张, 我很难专注于周围发生的事情. (大约6:45) 在近似. 吃完10分钟后, 胃开始疼痛, 这是一种不愉快的恶心感, 导致全身不适和过热感. (从大约7点到7点半) 我开始有熟悉的听觉幻觉, 试图在网上与我的朋友交谈, 我决定向朋友们说再见 (这也许是因为一些小视觉幻觉使得键盘看起来几乎是外星字母). 我看到一些非常奇怪的东西, 比如我床上的影子人爬行 (看起来像戴着帽子的男人, 她很少问问题,当我回答时,我的声音听起来很大,听觉幻觉让我相信我处于不同于当时的位置. 我看到我爸爸进入房间,直接进入浴室,并留在那里约两三分钟, 他然后出现,手里拿着打火机问这是在做什么. 我说对不起, 没有进一步回复. (八点钟?) 忘记如何退出我试图玩10分钟的游戏. 这就是所有事情的落点. 没有人知道. 我看着我的床, 在床单的折叠之间, 我非常害怕, 因为这一切似乎如此真实, 我试图离开我的房间, 很自然地,我像一个 解释说我的房间里有蜘蛛,我不知道该怎么办. 很遗憾的是,她愚蠢地相信我,跟着我跑出房间, 她打电话给我父亲, 他很快就来了, 我以为自己被咬了, (完全不确定,可能是9:30) 我父亲说我需要去医院, 在去医院的路上, 我不断地从现实中消失, (9:??) 在 我到医院时以为自己在洛杉矶某个陌生的机场. 我开始回忆起实际发生的事情. 我等待医生来看我被一系列问题所困扰, 目的是确定我服用了什么,为什么, 随着任何谈话需要极大的努力, 因为我忘记了对我说的任何东西, (完全不确定) 我一直坐在房间里,仍然看到类似的东西 蜘蛛爬行 (他们更像几乎看不见的线程似乎像毛发爬行沿着我的毯子和衣服). 我做了尿液检查和血液检查 (我还记得那次血液检查, 一个相当大的针头用一个弹针进入了我的静脉, (还不确定,可能迟到了) 我意识到自己是如何造成这一切的, 因为我对一些显然不真实的东西做出了不好的反应, 医生和护士带着一杯充满粘性黑色液体的塑杯和一个漂亮的小曲吸管进入房间, 我被迫喝下整个杯子, 这可能是我一生中最糟糕的事情. 我已经到盆地大约三次 (我的记忆仍然相当不清楚). 总的来说这次旅程并不是很愉快, 可能是因为我摄入了大量的二胺, 虽然希望不是和我一样多. 时间表: 79136 双胺 (109):不好的旅行 (6),健康问题 (27),困难的经历 (5),小组 (2-9) (17)

Driving Insane Diphenhydramine by Drilled Citation:   Drilled. "Driving Insane: An Experience with Diphenhydramine (exp79136)". Erowid.org . Apr 3, 2012. erowid.org/exp/79136 DOSE: T+ 0:00 500 mg oral Diphenhydramine (pill / tablet) T+ 0:20 150 mg oral Diphenhydramine (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 125 lb One night a few months ago I had decided to try Benadryl again; I had done it a few times before without very satisfying results (minor auditory hallucinations and loss of memory). When I tried it before I had taken around 15-20 25mg pills. I decided that I wanted to have a very vivid experience and ended up taking 20 then 6 more about fifteen to twenty minutes later to make sure it was memorable. **note, the times are probably EXTREMELY off as I was having a very hard time concentrating on what was happening around me.** (~6:45) At aprox. 10 minutes after ingestion my stomach began to ache as it had before when I took Benadryl a few weeks earlier. It is an unpleasant nauseating feeling that causes full body discomfort and a feeling of overheating. (from about 7:00-7:30) I begin to have the familiar auditory hallucinations while attempting to talk to my friends online, this proves to be very difficult to do as the hallucinations COMPLETELY cause you to lose focus and sense of where you are. Typing had become a very difficult task and I decided to say my goodbyes to friends (which probably ended up being along the lines of 'fi hasdx tha hjen' due to minor visual hallucinations that made the keys appear to be almost alien letters). (7:30-8:45) I had been seeing very strange things such as shadow people on my bunk bed crawling around (looking like men with hoods or a 'hunter' from the game Left 4 Dead) on my blankets. Starting at 7:45 or so my sister entered my room and chatted with me, sitting on my dresser and watching over me (I thought this was normal, as she is somewhat of a parent to me) she only asks questions rarely and when I answer my voice seems very loud, and the auditory hallucinations have me believe that I am in a different place that I actually was at the time. She had sat there for what seemed like thirty or so minutes when I see my dad enter the room, head straight into the bathroom and stay there for about two or three minutes, he then emerged and had a lighter in his hand asking what this was doing there. I said sorry and did not respond further. *(mind you this was all hallucinated and VERY real seeming)* (8:00?) I get up to pee after having been sitting on the computer staring at a blank screen forgetting how to exit out of a game I had tried to play for about 10 minutes. This is where it all goes down the shitter. (8:25?) As I exit the bathroom,(having a difficult time walking) I look at my bed and see in between the folds of the sheets spiders emerge, accompanied by larger spiders. I am VERY scared because it all seemed so incredibly real that I was actually wondering why this had to happen to me when I took the Benadryl. I attempt to exit my room only to have my door handle COVERED with baby spiders. Naturally, I sqeauled like a schoolgirl and called out to my sister (where she actually was), explaining that there were spiders in my room and I didn't know what to do. Sadly, she foolishly believed me and followed me as I ran outside of my room almost in tears from fear. She called my father who quickly came, he was not believing what I seeing (obviously). I thought I had been bitten and I actually SAW redness on my foot from the alleged 'bite'. (not sure at all, maybe 9:30) My father said I needed to go to the hospital and on the way there I thought I was going to the mall with a few of my friends. I kept fading in and out of reality, still seeing spiders on the back of the headrest in front of me. (9:??) I arrived at the hospital thinking I was in some strange airport in L.A. I began to fade back to what was actually happening. Waiting for the doctor to see me I was barraged with a series of questions that were used to determine what I had taken and why, and if I had attempted suicide with the diphenhydramine. Following ANY conversation took extreme effort as I forgot anything that was said to me, or that I had said quickly after it had been spoken. (not sure at all) I had been sitting in the room, STILL seeing things that resembled spiders crawling all along (they were more like nearly invisible threads of what seemed like hair crawling along my blanket and clothing). I was given a urine test as well as a blood test (I still remember the blood test, a rather large needle entering my vein with a pop and having to release my fist into a more relaxed position to allow the blood to fill the multiple glass vials). (still not sure, probably late) At this point I had nearly completely sobered up, realized the situation I am in and how I had caused this all to happen by reacting badly to something that was obviously not real, but seemed so realistic and vivid, including actual bite feelings from the spiders. The doctor and nurse entered the room with a styrofoam cup filled with a viscous black fluid and a nice little bendy straw, and I was handed a plastic basin. I was forced to drink the entire cup, which is still probably the worst thing I have ever had to endure in my life. By the time all the charcoal drink had, at some point, entered my body I had thrown up into the basin about three times (my memory is still rather unclear). All in all this wasn't a very enjoyable trip, probably due to the sheer amount of diphenhydramine I ingested, but I see it as something that people should experience at least once in their lives. Hopefully not with the same amount that I had, though.

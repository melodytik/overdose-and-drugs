Preparation of doses: (Skip this part if you just want to read the trip-reports or the conclusion) I obtained this compound from a reputable company offering proton NMR spectra, and I am therefore very confident that I ingested the above stated compound. For the 5, 10 and 15 mg experiences, the doses were measured out as follows: 40 +/- 2 mg of the freebase of 5-MeO-DPT where weighed out using a jewelry scale which is accurate to 1 mg. Due to poor solubility of the freebase in water, an excess of lemon juice was added to a 500 ml beaker (about 50 ml of juice). So really, what I was consuming was the citrate salt of 5-MeO-DPT. The 5-MeO-DPT freebase was added to the juice and allowed to dissolve. Once dissolved, tap water was added to the beaker until there was a total of 400 +/- 10 ml of liquid in the beaker. The 5mg, 10mg, and 15mg doses in the reports below were measured out by measuring off 50 +/- 1 ml, 100 +/- 1 ml and 150 +/- 1 ml, respectively, using a graduated cylinder. Additionally there was a certain mental stimulation present, which is also present in compounds such as LSD (and most other psychedelics). While the euphoria, optimism and increased energy where a very common feature, there was still room for malaise. The euphoria and energy were not amphetamine-like and certainly not as intense as, for instance, MDMA. It was also not anorexic in the least, I always had a good appetite. The emotional space was more of a euphoria with a psychedelic flavour, and it was very pleasant. Tactile sensations were also enhanced greatly, with my skin feeling much softer and touch feeling better in general. This, together with the euphoria, definitely gives this compound some entactogenic qualities. In terms of sexuality, this compound definitely did not interfere with any such actions. Everything was still working just as well, if not better, than sober, and as with most psychedelics, touch and sensation were greatly increased, making the sexual aspect enjoyable and workable overall. However, unlike say 5-MeO-MIPT, this compound does not seem to be an aphrodisiac inherently. One can steer the experience in that direction if one chooses to, but it doesn’t come as naturally as with other compounds such as 5-MeO-MIPT or even psilocybin. This may be due to the fact that mental imagery is not particularly vivid with this compound and most of the focus seems to be on the physical and intellectual aspect. So overall, not the best aphrodisiac, but ok if nothing else is available. In terms of spirituality, 5-MeO-DPT seems to have far less to offer than say psilocybin or LSD. While insights about life and the world are certainly possible, it just generally lacks the depth of more traditional psychedelics. It does seem to enhance intellectual ability somewhat, at least at lower doses. I was able to do some mathematical work much more fluidly than usual (at the tail end of a 15 mg experience). At the 15 and 20 mg doses, the drug also had quite a dissociating character, almost like a low dose (about 4mg) of smoked 5-MeO-DMT. There also seems to be a plateau that one reaches for the positive effects. I found that at 20mg, the effects were barely stronger than at 15mg, and the positive effects where largely at the same level. What I did notice however, was a persistent and somewhat worrying decline in my short term memory and ability to focus with the 20 mg dose. This effect persisted for several hours, even after the other effects had largely worn off. This may be a cause for concern and a warning about taking this compound up to too high of a dose. I did make a full recovery by the end of the day though. Overall, this is a lovely and potentially very useful compound. The duration is about 4-6 hours.

我觉得有必要找到一种合法且在标准药物测试中无法检测到的"草药高". 网上搜索发现我父母家里有两种草药可以吸烟, 也可以口服, 这很有趣. 我以前吃过药,但没有感觉到什么...也许吸它们是值得尝试的. 其他吸烟者的结果都很平, 我把房间里的CD播放器装满了一些保罗·奥肯福德和BT的ESCM, 然后打开我的等离子灯. 我把根的囊拆开, 我打破了我的房间的窗户,并点亮了. 喉的背部已经在第一次吸入后严重燃烧. 我认为,看着瓶子的瓦勒里安药丸已经来了. 检查后发现这种草药补充剂不仅仅含有花,木质混凝土,头,黑色根,激情花和果. 哈哈哈 我怀疑草是使烟雾燃烧如此严重的原料. 尽管如此,我还是吸了整个囊. 我开始吸烟. 现在好了很多. 让我在喉留下一种令人愉快的薄荷味. 我在床上坐下,拿着一袋小饼干, 戴上耳机. 音乐听起来很好. 真的非常好. 我看着绿色的电流绕着我的等离子灯, 我开始想象液体虹灯是跟着音乐跳舞的. 我是不是被了? 没有. 我很高兴,感兴趣, 半小时后我关掉了等离子灯和CD播放器, 这绝对不是一个激烈的经历, 任何伸展的想象力, 我可能会试着吸纯香, 我昨天订购的奥阿克萨克萨沙维亚应该在不到一周的时间里到达这里. 我非常渴望发现萨尔维亚夫人有什么东西可以提供. 时间表: 7412 猫 (68), 大 (48): 单独 (16), 准备/食谱 (30), 一般 (1)

Smoking Herbal Sleep-aids Valerian & Catnip by phaze3 Citation:   phaze3. "Smoking Herbal Sleep-aids: An Experience with Valerian & Catnip (exp7412)". Erowid.org . Jun 3, 2003. erowid.org/exp/7412 DOSE: 1 capsl smoked Valerian (ground / crushed) 1 capsl smoked Catnip (ground / crushed) BODY WEIGHT: 150 lb After testing positive for marijuana a few weeks earlier and facing all the expected parental bullshit for the offense, i felt compelled to find an 'herbal high' that was both legal and undetectable in standard drug tests. A quick online search revealed that two of the the herbal sleep-aids my parents had around the house could be smoked, as well as taken orally; Valerian and Catnip. Interesting. I'd taken the pills before, but felt nothing...perhaps smoking them was worth a try. The posted results of others who smoked the substances were mediocre, but my parents were sleeping soundly and I was in the mood to experiment. I loaded the CD player in my room with some Paul Oakenfold and BT's ESCM, then flipped on my plasma lamp. I pulled the capusle of ground Valerian root apart and emptied half of the green powder into my pipe. I cracked the window to my room and lit up. The back of my throat was already burning badly after the first inhalation. 'Holy shit!' i thought, and looked at the bottle the Valerian pill had come from. It was actually labeled 'Valerian Combination,' and inspection of the back label revealed that the herbal supplement contained much more than Valerian...Hops Flowers, Wood Betony, Scullcap, Black Cohosh Root, Passion Flower, and Cayenne Fruit. Hmm. I was unfamiliar with most of these herbs, and suspected the Cayenne was the ingredient that made the smoke burn so badly. I managed to smoke the entire capsule anyway. I then emptied the contents of a catnip capsule into my pipe and began to smoke it. Ahh, much better. The catnip smoke was much easier to inhale, and left a pleasant minty feeling in my throat. After I'd finished smoking both the Valerian (Combination) and the Catnip I settled down on my bed with a bag of mini pretzels and slipped on my headphones. The music sounded good. Really good. I watched the green swirls of electricity flow through my plasma lamp as I munched the pretzels. And I began to imagine that the liquid neon light was dancing to the music. Was I stoned? Not really. But I felt happy, interested, and relaxed. After i turned off the plasma lamp and my CD player about 30 minutes later, I drifted off to sleep easily, and slept soundly the rest of the night. It was definately not an intense experience by any stretch of the imagination, yet still enjoyable and relaxing. I may try smoking pure Valerian, as I presume the effects would be stronger and the smoke more tolerable. The Oaxacan Salvia I ordered yesterday should be here in a little less than a week. I'm quite eager to discover what Lady Salvia has to offer.

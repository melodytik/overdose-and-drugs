我在澳大利亚偏远地区的采矿部门工作, 因此,当我们试验这种改造的马拉胡安尼拉时, 在我工作中实行毒品检测之前, 我曾经是水性大麻的吸烟者, 我朋友们之前也曾订购过马拉胡安尼拉, 我认为, 您购买的常用药品 (最好) 可以比作吸食低品质大麻叶. 内容通常是花头和叶子材料. 我联系了另一州的一家幼儿园, 经过长时间的信函和劝说, 他们同意种植西伯利亚狮子并为我收获. 经过近八个月的收到作物报告后, 我被告知他们对他们开发的最终产品感到满意. 接着我们收到了大约16司的马拉胡安花. 标签是"仅供研究使用". 鲜花的头非常柔软 (甚至通常的刺是柔软的) 和有一个轻微但愉快的香气. 它们的颜色也比我们见过的任何其他马拉胡安拉人更白. 几乎均的淡黄色. 我们每人抽了一根, 效果是立即的:一个镇静/高兴的感觉持续了大约一个小时. 每个人都同意他们感觉更有社交性. 也有一些轻微的笑, 但没有偏执或食. 烟雾是光滑的和美味的, 在连续抽4~6根时, 我不建议在使用过多的药物后开车. 现在, 幼儿园已经解决了生长过程, 他们卖给我每16克4美元. 考虑到最初的投资, 这也许有助于增加人们最喜欢的吸烟草的供应, 但这种特殊产品是大麻的非常好和有效的替代品. 我建议你花点时间研究那些为医疗行业提供服务的托儿所. 在我们这个案例中, 其他: 利昂纳斯西比里克斯 (277):培养/合成 (31),一般 (1),小组 (2-9) (17)

Aug 24, 2005. erowid.org/exp/39370 DOSE: smoked Leonurus sibiricus (dried) BODY WEIGHT: 265 lb I work within the Mining Sector in Outback Australia and we are subject to rigorous (planned and random) drug testing. Therefore we had very clean systems when we trialed this modified Marahuanilla and we were extremely pleased with the results. Having been an experienced smoker of hydro cannabis prior to the introduction of drug testing at my job, I am always looking at alternatives. Some of my friends had ordered Marahuanilla before and we were always disappointed and in most cases we were only slightly affected. In my opinion, the usual stuff you buy can (at best) be likened to smoking low-grade cannabis leaf. The content was usually flower heads and leaf material. I contacted a Nursery in another state that specializes in supplying plant products to the Medical Industry. After much correspondence and cajoling, they agreed to grow Leonurus Sibiricus and harvest it for me. Yes, I had to pay them to research the subject. :( After nearly eight months of receiving their crop reports, I was advised they were satisfied with the final product they had developed. We then received approx 16 ounces of nothing but Marahuanilla flower heads. It was labeled “Research Material Only”. The flower heads were very soft (even the usual prickles were soft) and there was a slight but pleasant aroma. It was also far paler than any other Marahuanilla we had seen. Almost uniformly pale yellow. We smoked a cone each through a chilled water vessel. The effects were immediate: a sedative/euphoric feeling which lasted about an hour. Music was brighter and everyone agreed they felt more sociable. Also some slight attacks of the giggles but no paranoia or munchies. The smoke is smooth and palatable but nothing like the taste associated with quality hydro cannabis. When smoking 4~6 cones in succession, there is a loss of fine motor skills and a strong lethargy. I don’t recommend driving after a heavy session of this product. Now that the Nursery has sorted out the growing process, they are selling the product to me for $4 per 16 grams. A very good return, considering the initial investment. I can’t endorse the Marahuanilla that is normally available via the Internet or through Head Shops. It may be useful for extending the supply of one's favourite smoking herbs but that’s about it. But this particular product is a very good and potent alternative to cannabis. I recommend spending some time researching Nurseries that service the Medical Industry. In our case the gamble, money and long wait have reaped great dividends.

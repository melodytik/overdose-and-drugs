中文译文 兴奋剂

每次大约60毫克被消耗在一个药丸. 这通常会让人全身感到高兴, 认知功能显著提高. 每次这样做, 之后的恶心变得更加明显. 到了第五次,我只感到恶心. (剂量间隔时间为4 - 14天). 再给药只会增加恶心. 副作用:昏迷时恶心,深呼吸"疼痛",经常去洗手间,失眠和阴茎长度显著减少. (上述所有点只适用于经验的持续时间). 在第三次体验中, 每次心跳都能听到微弱的哨声. 我认为没有任何的, 并与MPA的刺激作用. 只是更大声. 我不知道为什么我不担心, 我还会偶尔听到轻微运动后的哨声. 两周后, 我开始觉得任何运动都会让我昏迷, 心跳也变得不规律. 我终于决定拨打111 (英国非紧急医疗咨询号码). 听到我的症状后, 他们告诉我立即去医院, 医生听我的心声,检查我的血压. 他看起来有点担心, 检查结果后, 我不得不为细菌感染 (不是药物测试) 取尿样. 接下来的几天里, 搬家是很困难的, 由于心脏不正常, 我不得不再做心电图和血液检查. 医生说他可以听到每一个节拍的流量声. 我目前正在等待我的结果, 没有. 我刚才想到我给出的60毫克的数量仅仅是用二分之三填满100毫克的5-HTP囊来衡量. 我现在意识到它们可能有不同的分子质量, 所以这些数字可能不准确. 其他: 甲基胺 (545):回顾 / 摘要 (11),旅行后的问题 (8),健康问题 (27),未知背景 (20)

The Effects and Dangers I Found Methiopropamine by 808828808 Citation:   808828808. "The Effects and Dangers I Found: An Experience with Methiopropamine (exp103699)". Erowid.org . Feb 12, 2017. erowid.org/exp/103699 DOSE: oral Methiopropamine (capsule) BODY WEIGHT: 75 kg Not much is known about methiopropamine, and yet I was stupid enough to give it a try about 5 times. Each time, roughly 60mg was consumed in a pill. The effects were usually an intense euphoria all over the body, and a remarkable increase in cognitive function. Each time I did it, the nausea afterwards became more pronounced. By the fifth time, I only got nausea. (4-14 days between dosages). Re-dosing only added to the nausea. Side effects: nausea on comedown, deep breaths 'ache', very regular bathroom visits, insomnia and a dramatic decrease in penis length. (All aforementioned points last only for the duration of the experience). During the third experience, I could hear a faint whistling with each heartbeat. I thought nothing of it and associated it with MPA's stimulant effects. The fourth and fifth time, I experienced it again, only louder. I have no idea why I wasn't more worried or why I continued using it. After flushing the rest, I would still occasionally hear the whistling after light exercise. Two weeks after the final dose, I started to feel lethargic with any exercise and my heartbeat became irregular. I finally decided to call 111 (The English non-emergency health advice number). After hearing of my symptoms, they told me to get to hospital immediately and get someone else to drive me. The doctor listened to my heart and checked my blood pressure. He looked a bit worried and sent me to have an ECG trace. After the results were checked, I had to do a urine sample for a bacterial infection (Not a drug test). During the next few days, I had no energy and my heart was working very hard. Moving was difficult and I just felt plain awful. I then had to have another ECG and a blood test at a different location as my heart was not functioning normally. The doctor said he could hear a flow murmur with every beat. I am currently awaiting my results, but in light of my troubling experience . Side note: I just had a thought that my 60mg figure I put was only measured by 2/3rds filling an empty 100mg 5-HTP capsule. I now realise they may have different molecular masses so the figures may be inaccurate.

我写这份报告是为了让人们知道使用Salvia Divinorum与其他物质结合的心理安全 (我认为单独使用Salvia Divinorum是没有问题的). 是我一个可爱的经验, 但最近我有问题与组合. 我使用了叙利亚取物 (3克干种子相当量) 与非常非常少量的Salvia Divinorum (几片) 结合起来. 我有类似于5-Meo-Dmt的压倒性效应. 我不明白为什么会有这种感觉. 对于我来说,这是非常不舒服的. 没有任何"迷幻"效果, 只产生了可怕的压倒性. 我已经做了40多次Salvia和从来没有发生过这样的事情. 这一直是个很棒的体验. 没有人曾经对我这样做过 (这也是一个非常非常小的数量). 后来我试着用尼基尔 (完全没有想到它) 的沙维亚. 我使用尼基尔让我睡觉,但我根本没有生病. 我吃了2大匙的最佳选择仿制尼基尔, 我再次抽了两片, 因为你永远不会太小心, 连5秒钟也没有过, 一种压倒性的恐惧感再次降临在我身上 (类似于5-Meo-Dmt). 这持续了30-45分钟. 我一直深呼吸, 试图不惊慌. 我还在服用尼基尔或吸之前吸了7支马尔博罗红烟. 我不知道这是否使情况变得更糟). 几天后 (我没有吸毒,吸烟,喝酒或做任何事情),我突然感觉到完全压倒性的感觉 (类似于5-Meo-Dmt或7g Psilocybe Mushrooms与3g叙利亚Rue的警报),我试图不恐慌, 包括我的"自我"在内的"正常世界"正在解体. 我感到极度恐惧 (比以前更严重), 这持续了四到五个小时. 我可以处理大量的化学品, 但这是疯狂的. 只是为了记录我也已经了300多次, 从来没有发生在我之前组合 (萨尔维亚单独没有任何负面, 现在每天18个小时 (除了睡5-6个小时) 不停地发生, 我醒来时希望一切结束, 现在 (我写这篇文章的时候) 随着"时代"的推移, 我还禁食了5天10小时, 30小时不喝水, 睡眠时间只有1-5小时, 禁食和脱水, 飞机毁都发生在这个"时间"里, 但从来没有想过这会引起令人丧的5Dmt感觉, 叙利亚的Rue和Salvia的组合 (只是更压倒性的) 使我认为它必须是组合. 我听到声之前, 发生了压倒性. 每次都准时到场. 这可能只是电气污染. 我对此感到困惑. 无论你做什么, 只要非常小心地将沙维亚与其他物质混合在一起 (尼基尔, 叙利亚, 科莱乌斯植物, 或大量的Ciggeretes, 我只是想让人们保持他们的心理安全, 压力,禁食,脱水,睡眠不足,电场我肯定会影响Salvia Divinorum. 你不需要担心的是萨尔维亚·迪维诺勒姆. 这是组合. 重复组合. 在我看来,萨尔维亚是个安全而伟大的人. 非常小心组合. 我绝对不打算吓人或让萨尔维亚看起来不好, 我列出了所有可能导致这种反应的因素. 组合可能会永久性地改变大脑的化学结构, 像我说的非常非常小心. 我希望知道是否有其他人因为组合或是因为最近的事件而产生了这种反应. 其他: 萨尔维亚神 (44),叙利亚路 (45),DXM (22),伪 (215):回顾 / 摘要 (11),旅行后的问题 (8),组合 (3),单独 (16)

Avoid Mixing Salvia Divinorum, Syrian Rue, and/or Tobacco, DXM & Pseudoephedrine by Be Careful Citation:   Be Careful. "Avoid Mixing: An Experience with Salvia Divinorum, Syrian Rue, and/or Tobacco, DXM & Pseudoephedrine (exp9530)". Erowid.org . Mar 8, 2004. erowid.org/exp/9530 DOSE: 3.0 g oral Syrian Rue (extract) smoked Salvia divinorum (dried) smoked Tobacco - Cigarettes oral DXM oral Pseudoephedrine smoked Salvia divinorum BODY WEIGHT: 145 lb I am writing this report for the Mental Safety of People using Salvia Divinorum in COMBINATION with other substances (I see NO PROBLEM with Salvia Divinorum ALONE). Salvia Divinorum is a Lovely Experience to me, but recently I have had problems with COMBINATIONS. I used Syrian Rue extract (3g dried seed Equivalent) in combination with a VERY VERY small amount of Salvia Divinorum (a couple flakes). I had Overwhelming effects similar to 5-Meo-Dmt. I could not understand why it felt this way. To me it was quite uncomfortable. It lasted longer (35-45 minutes) then the usual 3-5 minutes WITHOUT ANY 'Psychedelic' effect and only produced fearful overwhelmingness. I have done Salvia over 40 times and havn't EVER had this happen before. It's always been a great experience. I am used to high amounts and none ever did this (this was also a VERY VERY small amount) to me. Then I tried Salvia Divinorum with Nyquil (not at all thinking about it). I used the Nyquil to get me to sleep, but I was not sick at all. I took 2 tbsp of Best Choice immitation Nyquil and for some reason decided to try Salvia Divinorum. I smoked Two flakes again cause you can never be too cautious, and held it in for no more then 1 second (which I think was pathetic, but I did it to make sure I didn't regret it). Not even 5 seconds after that an Overwhelming fearful sensation came over me again (Similair to 5-Meo-Dmt). It lasted 30-45 minutes. I kept having to take deep breaths and try not to panic. Eventually I came down (just for the record I also smoked 7 Marlboro red ciggeretes within 2 hrs time span 30 minutes BEFORE I took Nyquil or smoked the Salvia. I don't know if this made it worse or not). Several days later (in which I have done no Drugs or smoked any ciggerets and drank no alcohol or done anything) out of nowhere I felt the EXACT overwhelming sensations (similair to 5-Meo-Dmt or the ALARM of 7g Psilocybe Mushrooms with 3g Syrian Rue) and I tried not to panic, but this time 'Reality' was shifting and the 'normal World' including my 'Ego' was dissolving (around Public or by yourself this is not normally great). I felt extreme fear (more then before) and felt extremely horrible. It lasted 4-5 hours. I can handle ALOT of Chemicals but this was crazy. Just for the record I also have tripped over 300 times and have NEVER EVER had have this happen to me BEFORE THE COMBINATIONS (Salvia ALONE DOES NOTHING NEGATIVE and I mean NOTHING or at least for me). The overwhelming 'flashbacks' have been happening for 3 days now 18 hours a day (except when sleeping 5-6 hours) non stop and has been litterly hell. I wake up hopeing for it to be over and only to find out it's still there hounding me. It's now (as I write this) gradually decreasing with 'TIME' and am feeling WAY better. Also for the record I fasted 5 days and 10 hours, drank no water for 30 hrs, slept only 1-5 hours durring the fast and dehydration, and the damn airplane crashes happened all durring this 'time' and I was also upset by all the recent happenings (not understanding how people can be so cruel) BEFORE the Overwhelmings effects happened but in no way did I think it would cause an Overwhelming 5-meo-Dmt sensation because of all this (who knows maybe lots of people are feeling this overwhelmingness because of all the event recently and maybe that's all). The fact that it felt JUST like the Immitation NYQUIL and Salvia combination and the Syrian Rue and Salvia combination (only MORE overwhelming) made me think it HAD to have been the COMBINATIONS. One ODD thing though, I actually kept hearing buzzing sounds BEFORE the overwhelmingness happened. EVERY time on the dot. It could have been electo pollution alone though. I am so confused about this. Whatever anyone does, just be VERY careful in MIXING Salvia with other SUBSTANCES (Nyquil, Syrian Rue, Coleus Plants, or lots of Ciggeretes, Electrical Fields and who knows how many others). I just want people to keep their Psychology safe and not have to experience this. Stress, Fasting, Dehydration, Sleep Deprivation, Electrical Fields I am sure affect Salvia Divinorum. It's NOT THE SALVIA DIVINORUM ALONE that you have to worry about. It's the COMBINATIONS. Repeat COMBINATIONS. Salvia ALONE appears Safe and GREAT to me. Be VERY carefull with combinations. In NO WAY do I intend to scare people or make Salvia look bad but I would hate for someone to screw their mind up and end up freaking or something and end up giving Salvia a bad name because of NOT being cautious. I listed everything that COULD have been responsible for this reaction. Combinations COULD alter brain chemestry permanently or at least for a while (hopefully not for the rest of my life). As I said be very very careful. I would like to know if other people have had these reactions because of combinations or maybe because of the events lately.

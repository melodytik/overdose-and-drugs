美国 由于已知2C药物和吸气问题, 与口服剂量相比, 我开始服用的剂量很低. 我不得不将20毫克的剂量视觉分成两部分. 我实际的目标是评估IAP作为潜在的ADD药物, 因为我读过其他报道, T=4分钟, 鼻腔膜的轻微烧伤, 但与MDA或2cb的"迷幻火"的尖咬合相比, 鼻腔有点流水, 但比T=0之前更清晰. 时间=10分钟,轻微的身体感觉,包括,没有太明显的东西. 灼热开始减弱/排水. 非常轻微但明显的情绪升高. T=15分钟,燃烧几乎完全消失,非常短暂的血压高峰期和心跳大约在T=12分钟,但已经消失了. 我现在可以感觉到它,但仍然相当温和和好. 仍然感觉满意,宁静. 想知道我应该计划撞击第二半剂量. 显然达到某种精神活性值. 我在我的头脑和思想中感觉到它. T=40分钟,头部和身体的移动与其他低剂量类药物相似. 时间=1小时,一些影响仍可见,但主要冲击已经结束,轻微的身体声. 时间增加了,但只有10毫克. 时间等于1小时5分钟, 这次烧得更多但不那么明显, 鼻腔清理. 这东西有着独特的"新车"气味. 我刚刚花了最后一个小时快乐地阅读一篇科学文章. 到目前为止, 我不能说吸气是有趣的这种药物. 打喷再次. 时间=1小时30分钟,燃烧都消失了. 感觉很集中,专注和放松,但有轻微的部紧张. 快乐,宁静,发光. 不特别刺激. 我今天晚些时候有电话采访, 这感觉好像事情应该顺利. 低剂量药对社会有轻微的滑作用. 我不知道我服用的巴斯帕是否有任何明显的影响, 总的来说我感觉很好. 盟友 其他: IAP (280):一般 (1),一个人 (16)

I had to visually split a 20mg dose. My goal is actually to assess IAP as a potential ADD med because of reading other reports that TV and such were 'more engrossing' after taking low doses of IAP and that at higher doses some aspects of social interaction might be enhanced. T = 4min, on going mild burning of the sinus membrains but not bad compared to the sharp bite of MDA or the 'psychedelic fire ants' of 2cb. sinuses are a little runny but more cleared up compared to before T = 0. a bit sneezy but was sneezy before so hard to say if it is related. T = 10min, slight somatic sensations consisting of tingle, nothing too obvious. burning starting to abate/drain. very slight but noticeable mood elevation. content. T = 15min, burning almost completely gone, very transitory period of BP spike and heart palpitation at about T = 12min but already gone. I *can* feel it now but still rather mild and nice. still feelings of contentment, serenity. wondering when I should plan on bumping the second half of the dose. thinking about T = 1hr. still growing in effect tho so we will see then. T = 25min distinctly hit somekind of psychoactive threshold. I feel and sense it in my head and thoughts. T = 40min, head and body shift not dissimilar to other low dose phen drugs. still mild, not unlike ritalin but much less euphoric/giddy. T = 1hr, some effects still noticeable but primary rush is over. slight body buzz. Time to bump it up but 10 mg. T = 1h5min, more burning but less noticeable this time, clear sinuses. this stuff as a distinct 'new car' smell. yuck. swallowing the gel cap it came in. I just spent the last hour happily reading a science article. so far giving low doses a thumbs up for ADD treatment effectiveness. I can't say insufflation is fun with this drug. sneezy again. much drainage. T = 1h30min, burning all gone. feel very centered, focused and relaxed but with slight neck tension. happy, serene, glowing. not particularly stimulated. slightly floaty and tingly. rather similar to ritalin but yeah not as giddy/euphoric. over all good head space. I have a telephone interview later today for an article I am writing for some mag. This feels like things should go well. Just what I was looking for, the mild social lubricant effects of low dose phen drugs. :) I donno if the buspar I take had any obvious impact but figured I would mention it just in case. Over all I feel great.

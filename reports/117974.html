Dec 19, 2024. erowid.org/exp/117974 DOSE: 1.5 - 3.0 mg oral Bromazolam BODY WEIGHT: 150 lb The below tests were run in isolation of any other substances. I have no benzodiazepine tolerance, although I have previously taken pharmaceutical lorazepam 1mg and clonazepam 0.25mg, both to good effect. My most recent benzodiazepine use before the below trials was pharmaceutical clonazepam 0.25mg about 1-2 months prior. 1.5 mg, taken on empty stomach: Onset @ 30 mins. Over the next 15-20 minutes the effects build, and I notice myself off balance. For example, my phone rang and I quickly sat up from the chair I was sitting in and was surprised that I missed my first step and almost stumbled. I noticed in my headspace a mild slowing effect, such that when I would turn my head my vision felt as if it was lagging behind my head movements. I also noticed mild gross motor miscoordination. I attempted to slowly walk a straight line, toe-to-toe, and was actually struggling to do so. I went to bed 70 minutes after dosing and fell asleep very quickly. I slept about 10 hours and was very groggy and slow to wake the next morning. Anxiolytic effects were present upon waking and trailed off about 4-5 hours later. 3.0 mg, taken on empty stomach, two weeks later: Onset @ 30 mins. Over the next 15-20 minutes, I noticed a quicker change in headspace compared to before marking the transition from sobriety to effects. There was a similar headspace as described previously, where it felt my vision would lag behind my head movements. I felt during this experience, my motor coordination was less impacted compared to before, however the headspace was so tiring, that I actually started to develop what felt to be about 10% of an exhaustion headache. I went to bed 80 minutes after dosing and fell asleep within 5-10 minutes. I proceeded to sleep about 11-12 hours, and woke up very groggy and still tired, not wanting to get out of bed. I had to drink some caffeinated tea to start my day, which is highly unusual for me, as I strongly dislike caffeine and try to avoid it at all costs. Anxiolytic effects were present upon waking to a stronger degree then previously and trailed off about 5-6 hours later. Summary: I don't really like Bromazolam. In terms of sleep, I think 3mg melatonin is much superior. With melatonin, the onset is quicker (20 mins) and it doesn't leave me feeling residual effects the next day. Bromazolam was too interfering with my pre-sleep consciousness, and the next day produced substantial grogginess and cognitive slowing that interfered with my morning routine. There was however residual anxiolysis the next morning which was unexpectedly pleasant. Both of my bromazolam experiences felt similar to alcohol in a pill. It didn't feel euphoric at all, and unlike alcohol there was profound tiredness, and no real sense of disinhibition or improvement in mood. Similar to alcohol, I would strongly recommend against driving after taking this material. I don't feel any desire to continue trials of bromazolam. I think the abuse potential for this substance is low to minimal.

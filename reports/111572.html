Admittedly this is some of the the dopest dope we have ever tried, especially considering the enormous stimulant tolerance we had built up. But the comedown can be quite dangerous (both to your gastro-intestinal system and kidneys, and to the real people you meet during the psychosis which you might mistake for shadow people and murder).

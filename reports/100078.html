制剂 新鲜的露天草被加工成3种制剂 发酵茶 - 250g新干的碎叶被加入到 750ml 的温水中, 放在一个密封的瓶子里, 在阳光下放入 2 天, 过, 然后喝. 在混合器中混合500克新鲜草药和2升净化水. 结果是一种厚厚的绿色泡状液体. 在一个小中将液体沸,取出火并让它10分钟. 液体被压缩并返回低温蒸发. 结果是黑色,厚厚的粘性树脂. 一半加在干燥的叶子上,然后完全干燥. 另一半则分开保存, 让它们完全干燥. 用另一批新鲜草药重复提取,这次从前一批中添加了干燥的叶子,在制剂中留下了更多的水分,让它一夜浸泡,然后在50°C的烤箱中蒸发,直到完全干燥. 原始提取物 - - 1.6g的树脂从第一批的强化叶子提取物制备被置到干燥和保存作为原始提取物. 实验1:强化叶子 (收获后3天) 强化叶子被装入水管中, 一次吸入, 保持在肺部30秒, 然后重复. 第一次吸入 - 材料重量为0.23g. 烟是严厉的,但没有尝到所有不愉快. 两分钟后没有发现任何效果. 第二次吸入 - 材料的重量为0.24g. 这一次用火点燃材料. 在第一次吸入后4分钟开始. 一种"沉重的头"的感觉非常类似于Salvia Divinorum,尽管较轻微. 脉率略微升高 (+ 20 BPM). 也注意到体温升高的感觉. 第三次吸入 - 材料重量为0.29g. 用火点火,每次吸入一次. 在第二次吸入后4分钟开始. 重头的感觉变得稍微强烈. 时间感也稍微增强. 没有观察到视觉,开放或关闭的眼睛. 效果在30分钟内消失. 这些效应与Salvia Divinorum的1 - 2级效应非常相似,尽管较轻微,并且没有空间扭曲和认知效应. 可能需要快速连续的多次冲击才能获得可视剂量,但由于烟雾的强度,这可能是不可能的. 实验2:发酵茶 (收获后5天) 实验开始于饮用一杯 Matricaria 香茶在空腹15分钟的时间内作为CYP1A2抑制剂. 发酵茶是立即喝的250毫升的批量. 茶的味道非常强烈和相当苦. 第一个杯子 - - 喝了10分钟. 在茶叶完成后,用水管吸入0.07g的干叶. 同样体温感觉和高脉的实验1被注意到. 在此期间, 在视角看到白光闪. 没有观察到闭眼可视. 第二杯 - 在第一个杯后8分钟,在7分钟的时间内饮用. 开始有轻微的恶心. 抽出0.08g的干叶. 吸烟材料似乎会稍微增加欢乐感. 第三杯 - 在第二杯后10分钟内饮用. 抽出0.08g的干叶. 在吸烟后, 听觉幻觉以耳和声音扭曲的形式存在. 人们更喜欢有纹理的物品. 触觉也变得更加强烈. 运动功能略有受损. 恶心已经变得更强. 在喝完第三杯后1小时内效果会减弱. 实验3:原始提取物 (收获后7天) 作为CYP1A2抑制剂,试验开始于15分钟内喝一杯Matricaria香茶. 然后将1. 6g的原始提取物保持在舌下30分钟,然后丢弃. 使用这种方法没有发现任何效应. 由于它在发酵茶后两天才被消耗, 效力不太可能会减弱, 也许一个标准化的提取物会更有效. 结论 使用大量植物材料获得了相当强烈的效果. 最有效的制剂是发酵茶,尽管与更强大的物质相比, 视觉效果相对较低. 其他: 发布时间:2020年6月18日 Tagetes lucida (917):未知背景 (20),准备/食谱 (30),回顾/总结 (11)

Jun 18, 2020. erowid.org/exp/100078 DOSE: oral Tagetes lucida (tea) smoked Tagetes lucida (extract) sublingual Tagetes lucida (extract) oral Chamomile (tea) BODY WEIGHT: 55 kg Preparations Fresh aboveground herbage was processed into 3 preparations – Fermented Tea - 250g of freshly dried, crushed leaves were added to 750ml of warm water in a tightly stoppered jar and left in full sun for 2 days, strained, then drunk. Fortified Leaf - 500g fresh herbage and 2l of purified water mixed in blender. Result was a thick green foamy liquid. Liquid brought to the boil in a small saucepan, taken off heat and left to steep for 10 minutes. Liquid was strained and returned to a low heat to evaporate. Result was a black, thick, sticky resin. Half added to dried leaves and left to dry completely. Other half was kept separate and likewise left to dry completely. Extraction was repeated with another batch of fresh herbage, this time dried leaves from the previous batch were added with slightly more moisture left in the preparation, left to soak overnight, then evaporated in an oven at 50°C until completely dry. Crude Extract - 1.6g of resin from first the batch of the preparation of the fortified leaf extract was set aside to dry and saved as a crude extract. Experiment 1: Fortified Leaf (3 days after harvesting) Fortified leaf was loaded into a water pipe and smoked in one inhalation, held in the lungs for 30 seconds then repeated. First inhalation - Weight of material is 0.23g. Smoke was harsh, but did not taste all that unpleasant. No effects noticed after 2 minutes. Second Inhalation - Weight of material is 0.24g. This time a torch lighter was used to ignite the material. Began 4 minutes after first inhalation. A ‘heavy headed’ sensation very similar to that of Salvia Divinorum was noted, although much milder. Pulse rate is slightly elevated (+20 BPM). Also noticed a feeling of increased body heat. Third inhalation - Weight of material is 0.29g. Ignited with a torch lighter as per second inhalation. Began 4 minutes after second inhalation. ‘Heavy headed’ sensation has become slightly stronger. Sense of time is also slightly increased. No visuals, open or closed eye were observed. Effects subsided within 30 minutes. The effects were very reminiscent of the level 1 - 2 effects of Salvia Divinorum, although much milder and with the absence of space distortion and cognitive effects. Numerous hits in quick succession may be required to obtain a visionary dose, although this may not be possible due to the harshness of the smoke. Experiment 2: Fermented Tea (5 days after harvesting) Experiment began with drinking one cup of Matricaria chamomilla tea over the course of 15 minutes on an empty stomach as a CYP1A2 inhibitor. Fermented tea was drunk immediately after in 250ml batches. The taste of the tea was very strong and quite bitter. First Cup - Drank over a 10 minute period. 0.07g of dried leaf was smoked through a water pipe after finishing the tea. The same body heat sensation and elevated pulse as experiment 1 are noted. A slight euphoria is also present. Flashes of white light are seen in the corner of vision. No closed eye visuals are observed. Second Cup - Drank over a 7 minute period 8 minutes after the first cup. Slight nausea is beginning. 0.08g of dried leaf is smoked. The smoked material seems to increase the euphoria slightly. Third Cup - Drank over a 10 minute period 10 minutes after the second cup. 0.08g of dried leaf is smoked. Once again an increase in euphoria noticed after the leaf is smoked. Auditory hallucinations are present in the form of ringing in the ears and distortion of sounds. An increased appreciation of textured objects noted. Sensation of touch is also amplified. Motor function is slightly impaired. Nausea has become stronger. Effects subsided within 1 hour of finishing the third cup. Experiment 3: Crude Extract (7 days after harvesting) Experiment began with drinking one cup of Matricaria chamomilla tea over the course of 15 minutes as a CYP1A2 inhibitor. 1.6g of crude extract was then held sublingually for 30 minutes then discarded. No effects were noticed using this method. It is unlikely that potency had faded since it was consumed only two days after the fermented tea, which still had much potency. Perhaps a standardised extract would be more effective. Conclusion Quite strong effects were obtained using large quantities of plant material. The most effective preparation was the fermented tea although the visionary results were rather muted in comparison to more potent substances.

使用的MAOI是茶形式的3gRue;仙人掌的数量是6' (3' dia). 这次旅行的强度相当于4个微点, 持续时间不少于12小时. 准备: 在咖啡机中将3gm的Peganum Harmala粉碎成粉末,然后在6gm的 Askorbic 酸/100ml水中10分钟. 然后加入蜂蜜, 最后在冰箱里冷却. 通过切除内核和棘 (留下皮) 清理直径为3'的Trichocereus Pachanoi. 这些碎片被冷了一夜,然后在混合器中碎,加上一些酸性水 (~200mL,pH=3). 液体收集,固体返回混合器,再用更多的水进行另一轮. 这一过程总共进行了三次, 最后的固体残骸大小惊人. 液体/泡量为1000毫升. 这被冷,然后解以破碎泡. 十一点五十分 - - 喝茶. 这真是太糟糕了! 这味道比仙人掌汁还要糟糕. 12:00 - 喝四杯250毫升的仙人掌汁中的第一杯. 12:10 - 喝第二杯. 在我做这件事之前, 我已经感觉到了第一杯. 是的! 这很快. 12:30 - - 第三杯. 由于胀/充满的感觉, 我真的只喝了三分之四. 我之前也感觉到了, 但是我感觉到头部发生了很大的变化, 一点钟 - - 瞳孔检查,我们有明显的扩张. 现在我只能说''了. 这就是旅程开始的地方, 我停止了笔记. 虽然这次的到来很愉快, 这高原是宽,广和持久的. 经验的质量是无可否认的:它是真实的,有机的,聪明的. 视觉和声音是同样诱人的. 身体上, 我感觉很棒, 许多时候我意识到我的眼皮已经闭了, 我们去海看日落,一切都是真实的. 经过十多年的迷幻药使用, 这种东西很像2CB和2CT7,只是更好. 也许我只是让自己享受它更多的自然来源. 这时我意识到我已经下降了一级. 镜子显示扩张有所减弱, 但效应仍然明显. 事实上,我的笔记反映出在... 十二点 - 我写下了"强"和在... 1:30 - 我写下了'音乐'音乐让我全身心动 (DI.fm: Chillout) 三点钟 - - 漂浮美丽的入睡. 我从来没有感到恶心, 下次我会把液体煮成一杯. 没有高血压或脉问题. 我从来没有感觉到情绪或精神失衡, 但我的妻子做了 (她没有MAOI服用6'. 她'无法舒服',并发表这样的评论:'一个人必须有非常强大的思想来享受这,'和'我只是不准备面对自己完全像这个仙人掌要求我.'我很感激我们在不同的时间倒在这里,因为她清醒时更有趣的周围. 公平地说,我有相当多的经验与各种类型的幻觉药物在高剂量, 除此之外,我认为如果她做完整的剂量事情会变成伟大的她. 总而言之, 佩德罗+鲁对我来说非常合适. 我无法想象自己能从旅行中得到更多, 9'或12'可能是可以的, 但18'+MAOI肯定会涉及相当多的扭动. 6'+MAOI对于我第一次来说绝对足够. 其他:

Aug 24, 2004. erowid.org/exp/26855 DOSE: T+ 0:00 3.0 g oral Syrian Rue (extract) T+ 0:10 6 in oral Cacti - T. pachanoi (extract) BODY WEIGHT: 200 lb The MAOI used was 3g Rue in tea form; the amount of cactus was 6' (3' dia). The resultant trip was equal in intensity to perhaps 4 microdots and had a duration of no less than 12 hours. prep: 3gm of Peganum Harmala were ground to powder in a coffee grinder and simmered for 10 minutes in a solution of 6gm ascorbic acid/100mL water. The mixture was strained then honey was added, and finally it was left to chill in the refrigerator. 6' of Trichocereus Pachanoi 3' in diameter was cleaned by removing the inner core and spines only (cuticle remained). These chunks were frozen overnight and then chopped in a blender along with a little acidified water (~200mL,pH=3). This was strained, the liquid collected and the solids returned to the blender for another round with more water. This was done three times total and at the end the solid remains were of a surprisingly small size. The liquid/froth was in the amount of 1000mL. This was frozen and then thawed to break the bubbles. exp: 11:50 - Drink rue tea. Yuck! This is much worse tasting than cactus juice. 12:00 - Drink first of four 250mL cups of cactus juice. 12:10 - Drink second cup. Before I do this, I am already feeling the first cup. Yeah! It's that quick. 12:30 - Third cup. Had to wait awhile on that one due to the bloated/full feeling. 12:40 - Final cup. I really only drank 3/4 of this one. 12:50 - *snap* I was feeling it before, but I felt a major head change here, 10 minutes after my final cup. 1:00 - Pupil check and we have distinct dilation. 1:20 - Here my notes simply say 'BOOM.' The ramp up was dramatic. This is where the trip began and I stopped making notes. The come-up was delightful yet short lived. The plateau was broad, expansive and long lasting. The quality of the experience was undeniable: it was authentic, organic, intelligent. Sight and sound were equally seductive. Bodily, I felt great, although I would not describe it as orgasmic or even ecstatic. Many times I realized that my eyelids had fluttered closed and I was just wrapped up in a feeling. We went to the beach for the sunset and everything was so true. After 10+ years of psychedelic use, I have found a new favorite. This stuff is very much like 2CB and 2CT7 only better. Maybe I just allowed myself to enjoy it more due to the natural source. 9:00 - This is where I realized that I had stepped down a notch. The mirror showed that the dilation had diminished somewhat, but the effects were still pronounced. In fact my notes reflect that at... 12:00 - I wrote down 'STRONG' and at... 1:30 - I wrote down 'music' The music had me all wound up (DI.fm: Chillout) 3:00 - Drift beautifully into sleep. I never felt nauseous although there was some intestinal gas, however. Next time I will boil the liquid down to just one cup. There were no high blood pressure or pulse problems. I never felt emotionally or mentally out of balance, but my wife did (she took 6' without MAOI). She 'couldn't get comfortable' and made remarks like, 'A person must need a really strong mind to enjoy this,' and 'I'm just not ready to face myself as totally as this cactus is asking me to.' I am thankful that we tripped at different times on this because she was a lot more fun to be around when she was sober. To be fair, I have considerable experience with a variety of psychedelics at high doses, while she has only used ecstasy and LSD at low doses. Besides, I think if she had done the full dose things would have turned out great for her. In conclusion, Pedro + Rue works very well for me. I cannot imagine ever wanting more out of a trip, but I know there is further to go. 9' or 12' would probably be ok, but 18' + MAOI would most certainly involve quite a bit of squirming. 6' + MAOI was _definitely plenty_ for my first time.

Long Euphoric High Baclofen by Nicole Citation:   Nicole . "Long Euphoric High: An Experience with Baclofen (exp92444)". Erowid.org . May 20, 2020. erowid.org/exp/92444 DOSE: 80 mg oral Pharms - Baclofen repeated oral Pharms - Baclofen BODY WEIGHT: 132 lb [Erowid Note: The dose described in this report is very high, potentially beyond Erowid's 'heavy' range, and could pose serious health risks or result in unwanted, extreme effects. Sometimes extremely high doses reported are errors rather than actual doses used.] I've been taking the muscle relaxer Baclofen off and on now for 2 years. I found that taking more then the recommended dose will without a doubt create a very long euphoric high. If I take four 20mg at 9am my high will last until I pass out. When I say pass out, I mean it! It's almost like narcolepsy. I'll fall asleep anywhere. This medication isn't a controlled substance so it's super easy to get from my doc, and it's relatively cheap. A few months back I took four 20mg baclofen around noonish, then went on a walk with my husband. By the time I got back home, I was feeling very sleepy, and fell asleep for about 20 minutes. When I woke up I couldn't remember if I had taken the medication so I took another 4, and this is when the sh*t hit the fan. It was around 2 or 3 pm and I began feeling extremely, extremely wasted. I had no control over what I was saying or doing. My husband was a witness to it all and even videotaped it so I could see the next day. I was yelling out randomly, clapping my hands and refused to sit down. But I was being nice, like I was in the best mood ever (says my hubs). Then, during this episode I took another 4 pills. 1 hour later is a blur, and can only remember flashes of things. I refused to wear my clothing, I wouldn't sit down. I was rambling on and on things that made no sense. Then sometime around 10pm I began vomiting. It didn't matter where I was in the house I puked wherever I was stationed. Every 10 minutes I was throwing up nothing but bile. This I remember clearly. Every time I woke up after passing out, I would attempt to find my bedroom door and had absolutely no luck. I watched the video my husband shot the next day and It showed me convulsing with no control of my arms and hands. Almost like a seizure. The hangover was intense. Everything smelled and tasted chemically and nasty. I have to say this was one of the scariest things that has ever happened to me. How close I came to death, and I know this because of the videos that were shot that night. Never again have I taken this drug!!! I wouldn't risk it. But it does create the longest euphoric high out of anything I ever ingested or smoked. The only real issue with this drug is that it fucks with my memory, and if I forgot I had already taken it then take more then it could very well kill me.

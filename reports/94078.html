背景: 自从我上大学以来, 我喜欢很多LSD,可卡因,甲基,和MDMA使用在我的俱乐部的日子, 我已经结婚了, 进入了我的职业生涯, 也就是成年, 我一直坚持定期的RC (大约每年一次). 我看过浴盐的热潮, 在路易斯安那州很快被禁止, 我大部分的RC体验都倾向于令人丧或迷幻般的, 比如5MeOXXX或2CT7, 复合物:在一个标有DMC的小拉链袋里. 色 (不是白色),有结晶光泽和强烈的化学气味的块状粉末. 在英国的研究化学公司购买, 邮寄到达8天. 付出了大约48美元的运费. 我没有检验内容或纯度, 它看起来不像我在网上找到的几张照片, 我把90毫克放在一行上, 没有直接的效果,而且它会燃烧,所以也许是微小的声. 我担心没有看到任何关于火灾的报道. 0:05 - 感觉到一些肯定,但它仍然是轻. 我的鼻子有点麻木, 它不再燃烧,所以也许是DMC. 鼻子滴水,但没有过度. 闻起来很熟悉,我敢打我以前已经被卖给了其他东西. 0:15 - 确定的声,但比一般刺激更少的冲动或高兴. 如果这是可卡因,我会想要我的钱回来,但我已经看到东西切割如此糟糕它是这个弱所以仍然有趣基于合法性和价格. 决定使用50毫克的线. 一点钟 - 它在15-20分钟的标志上达到顶峰, 只有更少的兴奋剂效果, 显然不容易模拟高剂量可乐的效果. 对于的程度来说比可卡因更有紧张感, 嘴巴干燥,下巴也能工作,我希望我有口香糖或仍然吸烟. 我真的无法引导能量, 我很不安, 无法集中精力, 现在看来我已经减轻了一些, 根据我读到的报道, 这似乎很短, 这么长时间没有做任何有意义的事情, 2:30 - 没有完全降低, 但高兴感大多已经消失了所以很可能很快会重新服用. 每次服用一小时左右都保持稳定. 实际上,它只能让人感觉愉快, 缓慢的光滑出现和消失似乎与DMC报告一致. 结论:假设这是DMC, 更多的挑逗 没有. 降落是平稳而缓慢,所以不是一个问题,就像想要一个更强大的声和确定当 我已经达到顶峰,因为没有忙,而是一个温柔的来吧. 如果人们试图追逐好可乐的效果, 对于那些能够享受它所提供的自我控制的人来说, 我没有像其他化合物一样专注或清晰地完成任何有成效的事情, 这有点令人失望. 妻子不介意我的实验当我敲出蜂蜜-do清单;) [报告的剂量: "250毫克多个小时"] 其他: 发布时间:2020年5月24日 丁甲 (552):一般 (1),第一次 (2),未知背景 (20)

I enjoyed a lot of LSD, coke, meth, and MDMA use in my clubbing days, but it's been years since I've even seen them. As I've gotten married and into my career AKA 'adulthood', I've quit things that could wreck that, being content with the occasional safe buzz. Since then I've stuck to the periodic RC (as in once a year or so). I watched the Bath Salt craze which was banned quickly here in Louisiana and saw bad things come from it so don't miss it, but have to admit I partook of the easy access to stimulants as I have bad ADD and have always had an affinity towards them. Most of my RC experiences have leaned towards the underwhelming or psychedelic'ish like 5-meo-xxx or 2CT7 and last too long or just aren't fun so I'm usually once and done, just for the experience or for something different. Compound: Came in small zip bag labeled DMC. Tannish (not white), clumpy powder with a crystal sheen and strong chemical smell. Bought from UK research chem co. and arrived by mail in 8 days. Paid about $48US with shipping for 3g. I have no verification of contents or purity and it doesn't really look like the few pics I've found online which looked more fine and white, so take this for what it's worth. 0:00 - I laid out about 90mg in a line and snorted. No immediate effects and it burns so maybe slight buzz from that sensation. Worried that I saw no reports of burning. 0:05 - Feel something for sure, but it's still light. My nose is a little numb and it isn't burning anymore so maybe it is DMC. Nose dripping, but not excessive. Smells familiar and I bet I've been sold this before as something else. 0:15 - Definite buzz, but less rush or euphoria than just general stimulation. If this was coke I'd want my money back, but I've seen stuff cut so bad it was this weak so still interesting based on legality* and price. Decided to insulfate a 50mg line. 1:00 - It peaked at the 15-20 min mark and has held pretty steady since then so tried a 110mg line. 1:20 - Hasn't really improved the euphoria only a little more stimulant effect so aparently won't be easy to simulate effects of good coke with higher doses. At this point feeling more tension (jaw, neck, breathing, chest, etc...) for the level of buzz than coke so have to make myself avoid the lure of redosing too soon. Mouth dry and jaw is working and I wish I had gum or still smoked cigs. I can't really direct the energy and I'm fidgity and unfocused with trouble finding enjoyable tasks. 2:00 - Seems like I've come down some, but still feeling effects obviously. This seems short based on reports I've read so again casts doubt on the contents. Haven't really done anything productive this whole time and just feeling the buzz. 2:30 - Haven't come down totally, but euphoria is mostly gone so probably redose soon. Stays pretty steady over an hour or so per dose. Can't really get beyond a good buzz i.e. similar to having done something good about 5 hours ago. Slow smooth come on and fade seem in line with DMC reports. Conclusion: Assuming this is DMC it's a mixed experience. Kind of teases at more . The come down is smooth and slow so isn't an issue as much as wanting a stronger buzz and identifying when I've peaked since there isn't a rush so much as a gentle come on. I could see this being dangerous if people tried to chase the effects of good coke as DMC supposedly has lighter, but similar negative cardio effects. This could be interesting for those with self control who can enjoy what it offers. Has somewhat of a more distracted feel and I'm not as focused or clear headed for getting anything productive done as I can be with other compounds which is a bit of a letdown.

我在上周五的晚上遇到了一个让你学会区分艰难的夜晚和糟糕的夜晚. 和女朋友分手,这是一个艰难的夜晚. 糟糕的夜晚是你在一个聚会上和一群朋友一起试图入, 其中一个是你着的漂亮女孩, 我试图进入这个新朋友群. 认识他们,结交新朋友,扩大约会群. 所以我做了正常的派对. 一些白俄罗斯人,几杯朗姆酒,和一些酒. 然后我走出去,我们打了几次管道. 五或六次的某种类型的他们称之为"奶酪". 我估计大约15分钟后我的世界开始旋转. 事情开始变得越来越艰难, 然后我的世界消失了. 我只能讲述我脑子里发生了什么奇怪的思维过程, 我躺在椅子上昏倒了, 我已经死了,或者至少我认为我正在经历死亡. 我最害怕的就是死亡... 更确切地说是未知世界. 我用双手抓住眼镜, 拉开我的脸, 尖叫一个长的喉尖叫, 我又一次昏迷了. 我记得我在走廊里着阿曼达, 我几乎可以看到我的眼切断了我的视线的顶部和底部. 艾曼达问道:"这是吗?" 我在摇头说"不"时着说. 接下来,我们还有一些熟悉的面孔. 带来大麻的人,比尔,走进大厅,向内倾斜. 他问:"这是恐慌发作吗?" 我然点了点头,然后又走了. 我在车库里醒来. 现在我的幻象一定已经开始了, 因为我真正记得的是求比尔不要离开. 我还记得混凝土地板和单一的灯. 我只是希望比尔能阻止我再次陷入可怕的无意识和奇怪的幻觉. 但我又没有了. 这就是我开始记住我的想法的时候. 时间在猛烈地上升下降. 前进和后退. 我一直在祈求慢慢的时刻来临. 他们是我逃脱的机会. 我以为我已经死了. 我认为这才是人生的真正意义. 过去和未来不存在. 只是你目前被困在其中的那一刻. 一个最慢的时刻,当那个时刻结束时,一切都会重新开始, 我知道那一刻,这就是生活的运作方式... 就像一个巨大的子连接在尽头,当你终于达到你最慢的时刻, 最糟糕的部分是, 快速的部分就像是你知道或永远都会知道的一切. 你知道当你回到现在时刻, 你会忘记它. 我清楚地记得我的一个视觉. 现在我确切地知道这一切究竟是什么. 我在想到这个时,自己大声喊着. 我想要坚持这一想法. 然后我在浴室里. 那里的情况变得非常奇怪. 我记得我要求和阿曼达说话. 我认为她是我的前女友, 我记得那天晚上我遇到一个叫艾尔的家伙. 我一直在谈论电视节目, 我觉得我对他有点敏感, 我只记得他和我谈论电视节目House和Nip/Tuck, 我试图写下一些东西, 我知道,当我写给他,但我只是需要有人来帮助我. 我又一次滑倒... 回到了时间变化. 我记得我在思考生活中的相似之处--生命和死亡. 黑色和白色的 快乐和痛苦. 我感觉到一个令人痛苦的痛苦. 像一点点的光穿过我的身体. 我认为吐. 我记得那天晚上至少有三次吐. 隔天我口中或喉中没有吐味道,所以我不认为我做了, 在浴室里... 坐在柜台上... 我的头发湿了. 我自己的反射. 这就是我! 再次消失了. 通过时间滑动... 回到新的时刻. 等待一个. 一下时间. 艾米尔,浴室... 为什么我比其他地方更多地在那里. 这些很重要,回到那里. 回去浴室吧! 的上帝该死的回到那里! 这是一个很好的机会. 接下来我知道我在救护车里被束, 那个可怕的氧气面具. 让我... 我们不会脱下面具. 医生说:"你已经吐了两次唾. 我不会...再也不会,我设法说出口. 我记得救护车的侧面, 和黑色的 我还记得我被送到医院的床上, 我称一名护士为胖子, 他们让我喝木炭. 后来我离开了, 记得很少. 医生告诉我,从我表现的方式他们认为我有一些X或酸在我的系统. 我也记不起我吃过哪一个. 也许有人把一些自己和饮料被交换了... 也许我拿起一个人用来酒的玻璃杯和得到一些剩余. 也许是因为草被加上了, 我只是反应不好. 我知道我非常激动. 很有侵略性,但我知道不伤害任何人. 艾曼达曾经向我提供躺在她的床上. 我拒绝了因为我知道如果我进去我会尝试做一些愚蠢的事情. 我一直在进攻. 现在回想起来, 这可能是偏执最基本的形式. 我还记得我很抱歉,我很抱歉举办了这个聚会, 我发现我手里有钱包, 却失去了手机,钥匙,眼镜,鞋子和右. 接下来的早上我坐出租车回阿曼达的家去拿我的东西. 她害怕我. 我去拥抱她,她不知不觉地从我身边跳走. 我是一个漫长的夜晚, 和我生命中的第一个坏夜晚. 其他: 酒精 (61),大麻 (1),未知 (120):组合 (3),困难经历 (5),不愉快的旅行 (6),火车毁和旅程灾难 (7),过量服用 (29),大群体 (10+) (19)

Apr 30, 2007. erowid.org/exp/60827 DOSE: 6 glasses oral Alcohol (liquid) 1 bowl smoked Cannabis (plant material) BODY WEIGHT: 150 lb I had one of those nights last Friday that teaches you to distinguish between a hard night and bad night. Breaking up with a girlfriend, that’s a hard night. A bad night is one where you’re at a party with a group of friends you’re trying to break into, one of which is the attractive girl you’ve had your eye on, when your world starts to spin you black out and wake up in the hospital. I was trying to break into this new group of friends. You know … get to know them ... make some new friends, expand my dating pool. So I do the normal party thing. Some White Russians, a couple of shots of rum, and a few beers. Then I head outside and we hit the pipe a few times. Five maybe 6 hits of some type of pot they were calling “cheese”. I’d estimate that about fifteen minutes later my world starts to spin. Things start moving as tough they’re out of a bad drug movie. And then my world disappeared. This is when I can only recount what strange thought processes went through my head and what I think happened. I passed out on the chair and was having a nightmare. I was dead or at least thought that I was experiencing death. My penultimate fear in the world is death... more exactly the unknown beyond it. This scares me out of my passed out state and I grab my glasses with both hands … pull them off my face and scream a long guttural scream as though trying to press through some incredible pain. I black out again…. I recall being in the hallway crouched down with Amanda, the girl who was having the party. This part is really hazy and my field of vision is almost as though I can see my eyelids cutting off the top and bottom of my sight. “Was that … that epilepsy?” Amanda asked. I mumbled incoherently as I shook my head no. Then one of the other few familiar faces from the party comes by. The person, who brought the weed, Bill, walks into the hall and leans in. “Was it a Panic attack?” He asks. I blearily nod yes and I’m gone again. I come to in the garage. By this time though my visions must have started because all I really remember is begging Bill not to leave. I remember the concrete floor and the single overhead light. I just wanted bill to keep me from slipping back into the terrifying lack of consciousness and bizarre visions I was having. But I was gone again. This is when I start to remember my thoughts. Time was violently ramping up and down. Forward and backward. I was begging the slow moments to come. They were my chance to escape. I thought I was dead. I thought that this was what life was really all about. That it just started over and that the past and future don’t exist. Just the moment that you’re currently trapped in. The slowest moment, and when that moment would end it would all start again and start at a rapid pace until you got to your next moment which was slower then the last. I knew at that moment it was how life worked … like a giant slinky connected at the ends and when you finally reached your slowest moment the moment when you really die you start the whole thing over again. The worst part about the fast parts was that it was like falling through everything you knew or ever will know. And you know that by the time you get back to your current moment you’ll forget it. There’s one line from my visions that I remember clearly. “And now I know exactly what it’s really all about. I screamed it to myself as the thought raced by. I wanted to hold onto that thought. And then I was in the bathroom. Things in there get really strange. I remember asking to talk to Amanda. I think I thought she was an ex-girlfriend of mine named Tammy. I remember talking to a guy I met there that night named Air. I kept talking about TV shows and I think I was getting kind of touchy feely with him, which is when I think I thought he was a different friend of mine named Paul, who happens to be gay. I only remember talking abut the TV shows House and Nip/Tuck with him … I think I was trying to ask for a doctor. I tried to write something down, but it was totally incomprehensible. I knew it while I wrote it to but I just need somebody to help me. I slipped again... back to the time shifts. I remember thinking about the parallels in life … Life and Death. Black and white. Pleasure and pain. I felt an excruciating pain. Like a pinpoint of light boring through my body. I thought about vomit. At least three times that night I remember vomit going through my head. The next day I didn’t have that puke taste in my mouth or throat so I don’t think I did, but I’d swear I did. Back in the bathroom … sitting on the counter… my hair is wet. My reflection. That’s me! Gone again. Slipping through time… getting back to the new moment. Wait. Hold on a second. Amir, the bathroom … why have I been there more then anywhere else. Those are important get back there. GET BACK TO THE BATHROOM! GOD FUCKING DAMNIT GET BACK THERE! AHHHHHHHHH! Next thing I know I’m spitting at an EMT as I’m being restrained in an ambulance. That god awful oxygen mask. Get that off me … “We’re not taking that mask off. You’ve already spit on us twice,” said the male EMT. “I won’t ... not again,” I manage to blabber out. I can remember the side of the ambulance but that’s it. And black. I remember being moved to the hospital bed and calling a nurse fat and then shoving the woman who was giving me an IV away. They made me drink charcoal. After that I was gone and remember very little. The doctors told me that from the way I was acting they think I had some X or Acid in my system. I don’t remember taking either. Maybe someone dropped some for themselves and drinks got switched … maybe I picked up a shot glass someone used to spike a drink and got some leftover. Maybe the weed was laced and I just reacted badly. I know I was VERY agitated. Very aggressive, but I knew not to hurt anyone. Amanda at one point offered or me to lie down in her bed. I refused because I knew if I went in there I’d try and do something stupid. I was on the offensive the whole time. Trying to fight off paranoia, this in retrospect is probably the most basic form of paranoia. I also remember being very apologetic, I was sorry for running the party It felt like every bad drug trip you’ve seen in a movie. When I finally was lucid at the hospital I realized that I had my wallet, but was missing my phone, keys, glasses, shoes and my right sock. After they discharged me the next morning I took a cab back to Amanda’s house to get my things. She was terrified of me. I went to hug her any she skittishly jumped away from me. I was a long night, and the first BAD night of my life.

其他: 这两起事件发生在今年早些时候. 我只想说这发生在2001年秋天, 我从朋友那里听说他们买了一些片, 后来我决定去朋友家看看这一切是怎么回事. 他把一些小红石放在上面. 对于我来说,这是一个新鲜事, 味道和气味更像是油漆而不是花, 经过一段时间, 我知道这不是我典型的草高, 这有点麻木,但我仍然很清醒. 我们吸了大部分碗 (片一直在吸整个会议,最终覆盖了所有的草和内管). 我把剩余的片覆盖的草放在一个袋子里 (我认为它只值几次), 当我们完成后, 我们讨论了我们的感受. 他认为这更像是用石头死, 我同意. 我很快就在回家的路上,感觉很好. 我走进车库, 打鼓, 感觉超现实, 我记得那一天的情况, 但没有其他重大事件. 几天后,我另一个朋友来了, 我把小片草加上一些小片我能从我的片子出来. 我把它放在碗上, 非常激烈. 我们吸了那个碗, 我相信还有一两个, 我确实可以在第一碗里品尝片, 即使在接下来的两个,有点微弱. 没有 我们是非常高和相当放松, 我醒来时, 感觉所有的血都冲到我的头上, 我走进浴室,看着镜子, 我很糟糕, 发生了一些偏执的感觉, 但它不是主要的, 我们睡觉了,一切都很好. 是大麻的好补充, 在我的生活中有两个非常有趣的日子. 其他: 大麻 (1),红岩 (156):小组 (2-9) (17),回顾 / 摘要 (11),组合 (3)

In the Tub Red Rock & Cannabis by Spaced Experiences Citation:   Spaced Experiences. "In the Tub: An Experience with Red Rock & Cannabis (exp15148)". Erowid.org . Jun 28, 2007. erowid.org/exp/15148 DOSE: repeated smoked Red Rock repeated smoked Cannabis (plant material) BODY WEIGHT: 210 lb These are two linked events that happened earlier this year. I want to say that this happened during the fall of 2001, but it could have happened a little earlier or later. Anyway, I had been hearing from a few friends that they had bought some opium and described it as 'weed times ten'. I was skeptical at first but after a while I decided to go to my friend's house and see what it was all about. So I packed a bowl of decent weed into my pipe, and on top he set a few small red rocks. It was new to me, since my only experiences had been mainly weed, and alcohol a few times. We went outside to smoke it, and it tasted/smelled a little more like paint than flowers, but I can see how people think it is flowery. After a while I knew it wasn’t my typical weed high, which I’ve experienced oh so many times in the past few years. This was a little more numbing, yet I was still quite clear headed. We smoked most of the bowl (the opium stayed smoking the whole session, eventually covered all of the weed and inner pipe). I put the remaining opium covered weed in a baggy (I assume it was only a few hits worth) when we were done and we discussed how we felt. He thought it was more of a physical stoning than anything else and I agree. Soon I was on my way home and felt good. I went into my garage and played drums and it was a surreal feeling I had, I did not feel like I was moving as fast as I was. That’s pretty much all I can recall of that day, but nothing else major occurred. Appox. a few days later a different friend of mine came over and we packed a bowl of much better weed. On top of this bowl I put the small opium weed piece/s plus some small opiate matter I had been able to scrape out of my piece. I put this on top of the bowl, and it was quite intense. We smoked that bowl, and I believe one or two more, and there was a profound difference. I could really taste the opium in the first bowl, even through the next two, a bit weakly. We went in a hot tub and it was one of my best experiences with mind-altering substances. W We were very high and quite relaxed, a bit from the tub of course, and substantially from the weed and opium. After an hour or two we got out and went inside, and when I got up I felt all of the blood rushed to my head, or a sensation like that. I went into the bathroom and looked in the mirror, and I was very fucked up, as I have felt only when smoking much larger amounts of weed. Some feelings of paranoia occurred but it was not major it was just something I remember. We went to sleep and all was good. The Opium was a very good addition to the cannabis, and made for two very fun days in my life.

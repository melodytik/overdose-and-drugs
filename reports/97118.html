I made sure that the spaces I would be in were safer for someone who might be very high. Sharp and otherwise dangerous things got put away. Couches and beds were ready for falling onto. I set out the following: * paper towels (for nose blowing and cleaning) * a large bowl (for vomit) * cold water (for drinking) * an energy drink (I often get very fatigued on psychadelics) * notepad and pen I talked my sitter through what I want them to do: * Stop me from doing anything dangerous. * Help keep me calm if I had a difficult or frightening trip. * Make sure that I was in the recovery position if I needed to sleep within an hour or so of taking the cebil snuff. ## The Trip ## == T - 00:01 == * I divided 0.25 grams of self-prepared cebil snuff into 5 roughly equal lines. (Snuff recipe follows.) == T + 00:00 == * Insufflated ~0.05g of snuff as a test. * There was mild burning in my sinuses, roughly equivalent to having eaten a bit too much wasabi. * My sinuses started to run. I tilted my head forward to keep the snuff from running down my throat. == T + 00:01 == * Insufflated the remaining 0.20g * Unpleasant, but not as bad as feared. Glad that I ground the powder as finely as I could. * Sinus drip increased. Some made it's way into my mouth. It was quite bitter. * Fought the urge to blow my nose. == T + 00:02 == * Nausea sets in, as does the initial light high. == T + 00:03 == * High and nausea coming on strong. * Vigourously blew nose several times. == T + 00:05 == * Nausea just keeps on coming. * Feel very tired and a bit weak. * Disoriented. Notes at this point in time are messy and full of spelling errors. * Move from chair to couch. * Attempted to watch a comedy that I've seen many times – was far too much stimulation and had to shut it off. * Sensitivity to everything significantly ramped up. == T + 00:07 == * Start seeing strong patterns behind closed eyes. * Patterns look like circular ripples in water, of the kind that happen when a stone is dropped into calm water * The ripples significantly overlap in a regular grid pattern. * Distorted faces blink in and out in the intersections of the ripples. * I can hear chanting in the background that verges on becoming chaotic chattering. * By this time, I am soaked in sweat. The nausea is becoming unbearable and I get pretty worried that I've got another few hours of this coming. == T + 00:09 == * Nausea has abated slightly * I try listening to familiar music. I'm able to pick out a tremendous amount of detail that I'd never noted before. * Would be having a good time except for the nausea and sweating. * Ripple hallucinations fade. I look out the window at trees lit up with full sunlight, their leaves rustling in the breeze. It's very enjoyable to watch. I'd be blissed out, except for the again-rising urge to vomit. * Try closing my eyes again. The after-images last for what seems a long time and distort as they fade. == T + 00:14 == * Nausea suddenly peaks and I dash for the bathroom. * After two deep purges, I start to feel better. Nausea and the sweats fade to manageable levels. * The vomit in the bowl is fascinating to look at. * Feeling very tired and shaky, but otherwise quite in control. Retreat to my bed. * Closed eye hallucinations taper off quickly from this point. * I spent the next 15 minutes lying down and listening to music. Continue to have the experience of being able to pick out much more than I've been able to before. == T + 00:30 == * Drank energy drink. Caffeine seemed to stave of some of the fatigue. * Vaporized a small bowl marijuana to fight off the nausea. == T + 01:15 == * Largely down. The smell was both nutty and unpleasantly beany. * I let the beans cool for a few minutes. * The beans weighed 1.3 grams after toasting. (N.B. The accuracy of my scale is somewhat suspect. Take weights with a grain of salt.) * I shelled the beans using a sharp knife and my fingers, and discarded the skins. I spread the mix thinly over the bottom of the pan. This was a finicky process, as the paste stuck to everything. * I cooked the mix over medium heat until it was a dark brown color and very dry. * Before the cooked paste cooled, I scraped it off the bottom of the pan and set it aside to cool. * Once cool, it was fairly hard and stiff. * I ground the dark chips of cebil bean/sodium carbonate mixture in the mortar and pestle. * Total weight at the end was 1 gram.

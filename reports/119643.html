Intro: I haven’t posted many of my experiences, but there’s not many out there on legitimate 2C-EF. As it seems previously when it was around, it was found to be other 2C-x compounds. Background: I’ve tried a vast number of compounds and psychedelics over time. Ranging from tryptamines to phenethylamines, dissociatives, classical psychedelics and RCs. As for 2C-x compounds, I’ve tried 2C-B, 2C-C, 2C-D, 2C-E, 2C-I, 2C-P, and 2C-B-FLY in the past. The 2C-EF is a white powder. I’ve been excited and prepared to try it since I saw there was a new batch and it was confirmed to be 2C-EF. I made sure I had no responsibilities for the day, and with a mostly empty stomach and nothing else in my system I felt ready. +0:00 (4:45pm): Dosed 16mg of powder orally with some water. +0:15 (5pm): Waiting for the onset, and not worried. I tried to put any kind of expectations aside in order to try and give a honest report. I have a positive mindset and comfortable setting with plenty to keep me occupied. I have a positive mindset and comfortable setting with plenty to keep me occupied. +1:00: (+) Think it has onset, my body feels heavier but still light. +2:00: (++) Started to notice some vasoconstriction, that’s one sensation I don’t like too much when it occurs. I’m also more energetic and in a good mood. I’m just pacing a bit and looking around. Visuals have started to set in. Patterns, colors and textures start to pop out more, the visual effects are more noticeable. Bright lights are much more intense than normal. Things in my field of view shift, move, and vibrate, especially when fixated or staring at something. Certain objects and patterns make these effects more pronounced. My body’s thermoregulation is definitely off in a sense. +2:30: It’s not too hard to record my thoughts. Although when typing; the distortion, enriched colors, and letters moving in place on the screen captivates my attention. +3:33: (+++). All the physical and visual effects from the onset into the peak increase in intensity. It’s like two symphonies or orchestras harmonizing, and gradually coming to a crescendo. My headspace is still positive, but there is no denying I’m immersed in this experience. My thinking is definitely disorganized. I don’t want to be quick to draw comparisons, but it’s not as light or euphoric like 2C-B. The body load feels deeper and more ingrained, closer to 2C-E, but it is still unique in its own way. What’s in my field of vision is vibrant. There’s a general flow of things visually; if I took a little higher of a dose, I think there would be more encompassing visuals. Most ridged shapes and text still keep their defining lines, but still somewhat warp and blend. I’m infatuated with every little detail that stands out. +4:37: The peak continues. What’s occurring is mostly what I just described. I also noticed there’s a sense of time dilation. +7:30: Physical sensations have become more “drawn out”, they are not as intense but still noticeable. Visuals are also starting to ramp down. I think I’m starting to hit the come down. +10:31: Some physical effects are barely lingering like a little vasoconstriction or tense muscles. I have come down though. Overall it was an enjoyable experience. Conclusion & Final Thoughts: Overall I enjoyed my first encounter with 2C-EF and what it had to offer. Like I mentioned, I feel it is closer to 2C-E but unique in its own way. Coming up, I noticed myself smiling and laughing a little bit more at things. There’s some calm before the very lovely storm. At 16mg the body load wasn’t too overwhelming personally but was still all-encompassing, I didn’t have too much nausea. The visuals were vibrant, especially the OEVS. I had a good and positive headspace throughout, some deep thinking in the peak but not too much. I’d personally like to revisit 2C-EF sometime again.

虽然我出生在摩门教,但我内心深处已经植根了许多道德观, 为了更好地判断那些使用大麻娱乐的人, 我首先要解释THC对我的影响, 并将莲花与这种经历进行比较. 听到一个好酒的声音, 我就了. 接下来又花了大约3分钟, 我记得一些事情是因为我写下来, 总的来说,我发现这种经历令人厌恶. 我当时把它比作感冒. 我的皮肤过度敏感, 视力模糊, 头脑扭曲, 记忆力也很差. 我觉得自己发热而出汗, 晚上留给我一个不好的味道植物, 但我印象深刻它的效力, 某种程度上, 我被迫回忆起一个老朋友的莲花经历. 我非常好奇. 如果其他东西可以像这样, 有更多的'启发性'效果, 几天后,我的订单出货, 一周后, 我的蓝莲花在邮寄中到达. 一整司. 很漂亮的东西. 很棒的蓝色,黄色,绿色. 我打开我自己到我当地的 headshop, 拿起一个小管,渴望看到发生了什么. 我吸了一整碗 (虽然它们是小的,粗的命中) 的植物. 由于新的金属管道和未经验的肺, 尽管如此,我有很好的数量在... 没有. 没有任何事情发生. 我说服我的女朋友也去尝试, 我们了两杯茶, 每杯茶用了大约2克他的东西. 我们每人抽了一碗半. 我们都没有感觉到什么! 我非常丧. 我向他祝愿好运, 告诉他如果他感觉到什么, 就打电话给我. 我几乎没有在街区的尽头, 当我的电话响了. 没有人知道. 他把它和大麻混合在一起. 我回收了我的鲜花, 也买了一棵我不太感兴趣的植物, 的 现在我明白了. 我在两次击球后就被石头击中了, 因为我是一个轻量级, 我被击中了3个波. 第一次发生在吸烟后3分钟左右, 我的视力改变,我的听力改变,我的所有感知都有点... 不同. 大约10分钟后, 世界是平静的,我被爱. 十分钟后,我已经了. 我不想移动,我不想多说话,但思考是相当愉快的. 经过30-45分钟, 第三层的效果就消失了, 我更能体验到我的快乐,反思状态. 另一个45分钟, 开始消失. 在4个小时的时间里, 视力:通过大麻, 我看到了一个世界. 我很拙, 几乎好像喝醉了. 莲花把边缘从那,并扭曲了它. 我看起来像是一只. 你知道的,看到一百万的东西一次? 但一旦我习惯了, 景色变得更加愉快, 声音:大麻让我听到沉默的声音, 伤害我的耳朵和大脑. 像我想象的一样强烈的宿醉 随着莲花的加入, 事情变得更加清晰, 我还没有用它来享受音乐, 思想与记忆:在中,如果我说出一句话, 我可以告诉你我小时候坐的校车是黄色的, 我很清楚黄色是什么, 我能想到房间里有十种黄色的物品, 我知道自己在说什么, 但我刚说完的话, 似乎远不如我即将要说的话. 这使得我不想回头. 让我对自己更加诚实, 不用担心我的想法或言语与我已经想到或说过的东西相符. 谎言不能建立在谎言之上. 触摸:这是最令人印象深刻的事情, 草使我的皮肤几乎疼痛, 莲花,好吧,我只能想象什么E感觉,并认为莲花是可取的! 我可以感觉到我的每一个皮肤细胞, 我的女朋友把她的指甲拖在我的手臂上感觉像一个温暖的,感官的火. 她的轻轻的爱抚就像做爱一样. 许多人告诉我, 没有性爱的亲密关系是你想要的. 亲近一个人, 或许抱着他或被他抱着. 我没有. 我是一个炉. 然而,直到第二次, 我还是设法制了这种欲望, 我只能解释这一部分, 第二次,我说服了我的女朋友再试一次. 这不是很难,我似乎有很多乐趣... 她又开始触摸我, 让我感觉自己是个女人. 我有心灵的存在, 从来没有真正离开, 离开床, ,这是一个 ,这是一个 你有没有见过一只火热的猫? ,这是一个 我不知道如果我称之为一种性爱药,我不想做爱... 但一切都很好,我想要快乐. 这就是我关于莲花的报道. 另一个Ereport提到的. ! 没有! 我几乎忘记了我的最重要的建议. 我发现莲花也是一种催眠剂. 虽然我很高,但很快之后我感觉到了拉力,这不是坏部分. 莲花不会让我直到我很好地休息. 对于我来说,这就像16个小时,如果我有一个漫长的一周,或6如果我有足够的休息一周. 我已经错过了一整天的工作, 即使我能成功地唤醒自己, 飞向前. 已使用的文件: 莲花/百合花 - - 花 (Nymphaea nouchali var caerulea) (105):回顾 / 摘要 (11),组合 (3),小组 (2-9) (17)

The Enhancer Blue Lotus & Cannabis by Oni Citation:   Oni. "The Enhancer: An Experience with Blue Lotus & Cannabis (exp32697)". Erowid.org . Nov 1, 2005. erowid.org/exp/32697 DOSE: smoked Lotus/Lily - Nymphaea nouchali var caerulea (dried) smoked Cannabis (plant material) BODY WEIGHT: 145 lb Born Mormon, many morals have been imbedded deep within me, even after being away from the 'religion' for so long. However, in an effort to better judge those who use Marijuana recreationally, I decided I must take a few steps in their shoes, and hesitantly tried cannabis for the first time about a month ago. This report is about Lotus, but in an effort to relate my experiences in a more understandable way, I shall first explain how THC affected me, and compare lotus with that experience. With one big hit, of what I was told was 'goooood stuuuuuuff Man!' from a nice bong, I was toast. It took about 2 minutes for it to catch up to me, and about another 3 before I was overwhelmed. I remember things only because I wrote some down, and was careful to describe how I felt to my friend, so that he could relay the scene back to me when I was sober. All in all, I found the experience distastful. I compared it at the time to having the flu. My skin was oversensetive, my vision blurred and strobing, my mind twisted, and memory shot. I was lethargic, and thought I was sweating with fever, though I'm pretty sure now that I wasn't. The night left me with a bad taste for the plant, but I was impressed with its potency, and the overall effects of the drug. Somehow, I was made to recall an old friend's Lotus experience. I was very curious. If something else could be like this, and have more 'enlightening' effects, as he described, I wanted to give it a whirl. Not a few days later, my order was out, and a week from then, my Blue Lotus arrived in the mail. A whole ounce. Beautiful stuff. Wonderfull blues, yellows, greens. I shot myself to my local headshop, and picked up a little pipe, eager to see what happened. I smoked an entire bowl (though they were small, sloppy hits) of the plant. It was harsh, primarily thanks to a new metal pipe and inexperienced lungs. Nonetheless, I got a good amount in... nothing. Not a darn thing happened. That night I convinced my girlfriend, who was even less experienced than I, to try as well. We made two mug fulls of tea, each using about 2 grams of his stuff. About 20-30 minutes after consumption, we each smoked a bowl and a half. NEITHER OF US FELT A THING! I was quite upset. The next day I gave the bag to a pot smoking friend, and wished him the best of luck, telling him to call me if HE felt anything. I'd barely made it around the end of the block when my phone rang. 'Whoa! This stuff's AWESOME!' He mixed it 50/50 with cannabis. I made the reclaiming of my flowers, as well as a small purchase of the plant I was less interested in, and combined the two 50/50 as well. Wow. Now I get it. I was stoned after two hits, since I'm a lightweight, and was quite impressed. I was hit in 3 waves. The first happened about 3 minutes after smoking, and was a mild disorientation. My sight changed, my hearing changed, all of my perception was a little... different. About 10 minutes later, the euporia hits. The world is calm, and I am loved. About 10 minutes after that I'm stoned. I don't want to move, I don't want to talk any more than I need to, but thinking is quite pleasant. After about 30-45 minutes the third level wears off, and I'm more able to experience my happy, reflecting state. Another 45 minutes, and that begins to fade. At the end of 4 hours, I'm at baseline. Sight: With cannabis, I saw the world strobed. My vision was blurred, and I was clumsy, almost as though quite drunk. Lotus took the edge off that, and twisted it around. It was like I had a fly's eyes. Ya know, seeing a million things at once? It was disorienting at first, but once I got used to it, it made sights more pleasant, and intriguing. Sound: Cannabis left me with muted, muffled sounds, that hurt my ears and brain. Much as I'd imagine a fierce hangover. With Lotus added in, things were crisper, and not quite as overpowering. I have yet to enjoy music with this, but I can't wait. Thought and memory: With pot, if I'd speak a sentence, by the time I got to the end of it, I'd forgot what the beginning was. I could tell you the schoolbus I rode as a child was yellow, and knew well what yellow was, and could think of 10 items in the room that were yellow, but I had no idea why you brought up the color yellow. With lotus, I know what I'm saying, but what I've finished saying seems, somehow, far less important than what I'm about to say. It keeps me from backtracking in my thoughts. It allows me to be more honest with myself, without worrying about how my thoughts or words coincide with what I've already thought or said. Lies can't build off of lies. Touch: This is the most impressive thing, though I fear this may just be for me, as none of my friends have reported this. Pot left my skin aching almost, and uncomfortable to wear. Lotus, well, I can only imagine whae E feels like, and think Lotus to be preferable! I could feel every cell in my skin with crystal clarity. My girlfriend dragging her nails on my arm felt like a warm, sensuous fire. Her lighter caress was like sex. Many people have told me sexless intimacy is what you want with lotus. To be near someone, maybe to hold them or be held. Not me. I was a furnace. I managed to stave off this desire until my second time, however, which I'll only explain this part of, because the rest is much the same as the first. The second time, I convinced my girlfriend to try it with me again. This wasn't tough, I seemed to have so much fun... As we lay in bed, watching half baked, while we were fully baked, she began to touch me again. I had the presence of mind, which never really left, to get off the bed, move behind her, and see how she reacted to touch. Wow... Wow... Have you ever seen a cat in heat? Wow... I dunno if I'd call this an aphrodesiac, I didn't WANT sex... But everything feels SO good, I wanted pleasure. So, this is my report on Lotus. I've enjoyed it several times now, and it's perfect for a lazy sunday, as another Ereport had mentioned. OH! My most important advice, I almost forgot. I find Lotus is also a sleep inducer. Not while I'm high, but soon afterwards I feel the tug, that's not the bad part. Lotus won't let me up until I am WELL rested. For me, that's like 16 hours, if I've had a long week, or 6 if I've gotten enough rest throught the week. I've missed an ENTIRE day of work thanks to this. Even if I can manage to get myself awake, good luck getting out of bed, much less functioning. PLAN AHEAD.

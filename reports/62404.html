中文译文 阿片类

纳尔特雷克森是一种纯粹的阿片类受体对抗剂,在阿片类/阿片类成者中使用纳尔特雷克森会导致急性戒断症状. 我决定使用一些纳尔特雷克森来停止氧康丁的作用, 因为它曾经引起过一些失眠. 我认为没有退出会发生因为我不是一个经常使用阿片类药物. 我使用了大约12.5毫克的剂量. 纳尔特雷克森药片是50毫克. 这是一个坏主意. 药物注射15分钟后我开始感到奇怪和焦虑. 我决定躺在床上,但这没有帮助. 我感到很奇怪, 半小时后, 我在镜子里看了看, 我的尖的眼睛变得更大 (可能是正常的). 我感到非常虚弱和生病. 我试图睡觉,但在第一小时没有成功. 之后我睡了几个小时, 但是在一个非常奇怪的状态, 总之,我的实验并没有成功. 虽然12.5毫克确实完全逆转了4小时前55毫克的氧化的效果. 其他: 纳尔特 (338):困难的经历 (5),健康问题 (27),组合 (3),未知背景 (20)

Sep 14, 2009. erowid.org/exp/62404 DOSE: T+ 0:00 55 mg Oxycodone T+ 4:00 12.5 mg Naltrexone BODY WEIGHT: 65 kg Naltrexon is a pure opiate-receptor antagonist and the use of naltrexon in opiate/opioid addicted individuals will lead to acute withdrawl symptoms. After spending the night on 55 mg Oxycontin (oxycodone) I decided to use some naltrexon to stop the effects of oxycontin, since it had caused some insomnia in the past. I figured no withdrawl would occur since I'm not a regular user of opioids. I used about 12,5 mgs as a dose. Naltrexon pills are 50 mgs. It happened to be a bad idea. 15 minutes after administration I started to feel weird and anxious. I decided to lay in bed but this didn't help. I felt really strange and out of my body. After 30 minutes I looked in the mirror and my pinpointed eyes had turned bigger (probably normal). I felt really weak and sick. I tried to sleep but didn't succeed in the first hour. After that I slept some hours, but in a really weird state and with a lot of sweating. To conclude, my experiment was not a success. Though 12,5 mgs did seem to completely reverse the effects of 55 mg oxycodone 4 hours earlier.

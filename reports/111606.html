关于我的一些事情: 我曾经有过一些迷幻体验, 一次吸过, 吸了很多大麻,做了一些phenibut, 和其他轻微的事情,所以我不是最有经验的心理探险家, 这次旅程是我历史上最激烈,最具挑战性的旅程. 我,M,M的女朋友和A. 星期六晚上, 我们都在朋友家. 我和A决定我们想走一下,M不能因为他要晚些时候去接女朋友. 我有一些150μg的酸抹布躺在附近, 但我们必须在早上起来所以我们做半个抹布. 我们感觉很好,很平静. 没有任何幻觉 atm,这是预期的. 我去找他的女朋友,我们一起出去玩,打包管道. 我们吸了大麻, LSD的强度有点高, 接近幻觉程度, 经过T+1:30我们记得M还有一些Changa躺在那里. 我们之前也抽过几次, 结果大约有15到20分钟, 相当激烈, 这真是一个坏 (好) 的想法. 我把几个粒的Changa在管道, 只是足以让一个清醒的人,看到一些奇怪的东西, 我点燃了管子,当效果不像我习惯的那样立即起作用时, 我没有再给药, 因为我确信它最终会起作用, 我在吸烟15分钟后, 发现地板开始倾斜. 无论我在哪里看,地板都倾斜在20度左右. 这就是我注意到我球! 我朋友抓住我的手,说:"哥哥,你必须在镜子里看看自己. 我们经常在跌倒时这样做. 我看着自己的脸, 开始笑. 笑声变成了无法控制的笑声, 红色变得更加强烈, 然后突然出现了"" 我回来了, 我在镜子里看着自己的惊脸. 或至少有一些颜色和形状类似于我的脸. 现在我走路的情况非常好. 我朋友告诉我不可能看到我在,直到我看着他或打开我的嘴. 我的视力非常奇怪. 这就像是通过不完全透明的东西来观察. 没有什么是真实的, 感觉就像是通过电视屏幕看到世界. 有时我不确定我能否抓住东西, 感觉好像我的手会碰到屏幕或其他东西. 世界仍然倾斜,一切仍然是形状和颜色. 有时有些可怕,很难保持好心情. 虽然很有趣,但也很有挑战性. 现在我一定是超级有创意的. 我拿起吉他,看着弦, 令人惊的是它们看起来很熟悉. 我试图触摸它们时, 它们就开始扭动, 有时,第七个字符串会出现, 只是把我弄得糟糕,所以我放弃了. 如果它不让我玩它,那么我不会尝试. 我听说这之后, 其他人走了出去, 接下来我记得我突然站在外面说:"! 我们在外面! 这有点可怕,这种"远程传输"发生了几次, 接下来的几个小时很平淡 (我认为它们非常繁忙, 现在我仍然有很好的视觉幻觉. 像之前提到的, 这大约是4个小时后, 我还在! 我躺在床上, 吉他在我的脑海里轻轻地哭泣着, 没有你通常会有一个令人讨厌的歌在你的脑海里,我可以听到它. 这是一个我在晚上早些时候遇到的问题, 有时它会比我说话的人大声, 我起床3次关掉音乐, 才意识到没有音乐, 我使用了一些相当低的剂量,我感谢上帝小心,因为如果这是一个星期五晚上, 这将100%导致糟糕的旅程. 这是一次非常艰难的旅程, 但它持续了2-3个小时, 强度很高, 我很惊地发现自己还活着. 虽然我觉得自己有点慢, 时间表: 111606 发布时间:2018年2月17日

Feb 17, 2018. erowid.org/exp/111606 DOSE: T+ 0:00 75 ug oral LSD (blotter / tab) T+ 0:00 smoked Cannabis (plant material) T+ 1:30 smoked Changa BODY WEIGHT: 65 kg Things about me: I have a few psychedelic experiences under the buckle, done shrooms once, LSD once before, and changa a few times. Smoked a lot of weed, done some phenibut, and other minor things, so I am not the most experienced psychonaut, but I would classify me, as at least a little experienced. This trip was easily the most intense and challenging I have ever experienced. Characters: Me, M, M's gf, and A. It was a saturday night, and we were all hanging out a friend (M)'s house. A and I decided we wanted to trip a little, M couldn't cause he was picking up his girl later. M had some 150µg acid blotters laying around, but we had to get up in the morning so we did half a blotter each. After about an hour the acid had pretty much kicked fully in, and we were feeling nice and peaceful. Not any hallucinations atm, which was to expect. M left to get his gf, and we hung out and packed the pipe. We smoked the weed, and it potentiated the LSD a little bit, close to a hallucinatory extent but still not very intense, this was fine though, everything was going as planned. After about T+1:30 we remembered M still had some changa lying around. We have smoked it a few times before, and it has led to about 15-20 minutes of some pretty intense shit, so we thought “hey let’s trip a little bit of balls and party on afterwards”... Oh boy was that a bad (good) idea. I put a few grains of the changa in the pipe, just enough to get a sober man, seeing some weird shit and think some thoughts, we agreed that it was probably about 0.02g. I lit up the pipe, and got a little disappointed when the effects didn’t kick in immediately like I’m used to. I didn’t redose though, as I was pretty sure it would kick in eventually and I was right. About 15 minutes after I smoke the changa I notice the floor starts slanting. Anywhere I look, the floor slanting upwards in an about 20 degree angle. That is where I notice “oh fuck guys I’m tripping balls!” My friend takes me by the hand and says “dude you gotta look yourself in the mirror”. We often do this while tripping. As I look at my face I start giggling. The giggling turns into uncontrollable laughter, as my face begins looking unfamiliar and gets a red glow. The red intensifies, as the face in the mirror turns into a red siluet. Then suddenly *poof*. I’m back and I’m looking at my own face of amazement in the mirror. Or at least some colors and shapes resembling my face. At this point I am walking surprisingly well. My friend told me it was impossible to see I was tripping, until I looked at him or opened my mouth. My sight was very weird. Kind of like looking through something that isn’t quite transparent. Nothing feels real, it feels like the world is seen through a tv screen. Sometimes I wasn’t sure if I could grab things, it felt like my hands would just hit the screen or something. The world is still slanting and everything is still shapes and colors. It was kind of scary at times, and hard to keep a good mood. It was fun but challenging. Suddenly I think “I’ll play some guitar I must be super creative right now”. When I pick up the guitar, and look at the strings, they surprisingly look pretty familiar. When I try to put my fingers on them though, they start twisting, and avoiding me. Sometimes, a 7th string would appear and just screw with me, so I gave up. Fuck that guitar, if it wouldn’t let me play it, then I wouldn’t try. I was told that after this, the others went outside, and I followed them pretty normally. The next thing I remember is suddenly standing outside and going “Wow shit! We’re outside!” and everyone starts laughing. This was kind of scary, and this “teleportation” happened a couple of times, but I (fascinatingly) didn’t bad trip. The next few hours were pretty uneventful (I think they were very eventful, but not much to talk about) and at about T+5 I go to bed. At this point I still have pretty good visual hallucinations. As earlier mentioned the changa should have lasted about 20 minutes. This was about 4 hours later, and I was still tripping! While I lay in my bed, I have “while my guitar gently weeps” in my head and it keeps on playing. Not as you would normally have an annoying song in your head, I could literally hear it. This was a problem I had encountered earlier in the evening, and sometimes it had been louder than the people speaking to me which was really annoying, but not as annoying as this. I got up 3 times to turn off the music, only to realise that there was no music playing and it was all in my head. I used some pretty low doses, and I thank god for being careful, cause if this was a friday evening, and I didn’t have to get up in the morning, I could easily have doubled the dose. This would 100% have resulted in a bad trip. It was a very challenging trip, especially because of the duration. Changa should last about 20 minutes or so, but it lasted about 2-3 hours with very high intensity, before I even noticed it starting to wear off. The next morning, I was surprisingly fresh and sane. That being said I could easily feel that I was a little slow, but I felt more normal than I feel like I should.

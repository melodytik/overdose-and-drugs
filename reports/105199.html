我买了五克的狮子尾花. 我唯一的吸毒方式是口服或使用我的火山蒸发器, 我开始蒸发一小部分, 大约一花, 这足够一个气球. 蒸汽是愉快的,虽然有点厚, 我感觉有点头, 但没有更多. 我把更多的花放进了火山中, 经过五个气球, 我确实获得了令人愉快的醉酒, 麻的体力高和一个非常温和的头部高. 简单地说,头部高包括一个无事件的温和放松的状态剥夺了大麻的的效果 (没有加速的想法,没有超感觉,没有时间扭曲或增加幽默感等). 另一方面, 身体的高是相当美味和愉快非常像大麻. 总体而言, 感觉有点不合标准, 我感到很惊,但也感到失望. 我使用舒尔金尺度称之为a++. 我拿起剩下的东西, 大概4到3克, 开始茶:我让材料浸在热水中, 茶叶有色和黄色, 味道很苦, 经过大约20分钟的时间, 我开始感觉到一点 (头 + 放松 + 模糊的温暖), 持续了大约一个小时. 实际上只有一个绝对的+点. 尽管如此, 另外,我还没有提到一个效果, 每个病例都有明显的轻微情绪提升 (没有安慰剂). 这几乎就是全部. 这种材料是真实的, 但在我看来不令人印象深刻. 但它没有大麻不能做的,更好, 特别是因为与大麻不同, 如果只有这件事我可以使用, 但除此之外,我没有什么强烈的推动力去做野蛮的Daga. 这种植物是非常漂亮的. 我正在考虑把它添加到我的花园里! 莱昂诺蒂斯·莱昂诺鲁斯 (119):一般 (1),第一次 (2),单身 (16)

Apr 12, 2018. erowid.org/exp/105199 DOSE: repeated vaporized Leonotis leonurus (flowers) 3.5 g oral Leonotis leonurus (tea) BODY WEIGHT: 69 kg I bought five grams of Lion’s tail flowers. My only methods of drug intake are either orally or using my volcano vaporizer as I don’t smoke. I started by vaporizing a small amount, about a pinch of flowers, with the volcano temperature set to five and a half. That was enough for one balloon. The vapor was pleasant, although a bit thick, and reminded me of potpourri. I felt a bit of light headedness but not much more. I then packed some more flowers in the volcano valve which was enough for several balloons until exhaustion of the material. After maybe five balloons, I indeed achieved a pleasant intoxication quite reminiscent of that of cannabis. There was the body high of cannabis and a very mellow version of the head high. Simply put, the head high consisted of a state of uneventful gentle relaxation deprived of the trippy effects of cannabis (no accelerated ideation, no hyperesthesia, no time warp or increased sense of humour etc). On the other hand, the body high was quite luscious and pleasurable very much like cannabis. However as a whole it felt a bit subpar and required several balloons to achieve less than what I get with a single balloon of cannabis. I felt pleasantly surprised yet underwhelmed. I would call this a ++ using the Shulgin scale. Consequently, feeling a bit frustrated, I took the rest of my stuff -probably four to three grams- and proceeded to making a tea : I let the material soak in hot non-boiling water for maybe twenty minutes and proceeded to drinking it. The tea had an orange and yellow colour with a very bitter taste that I counteracted by eating half a lemon as I drank it. About twenty minutes later I started to feel a bit of a buzz (light headedness + relaxation + fuzzy warmth) which lasted for maybe an hour or so. It was never as strong as what I had previously achieved with the vaporizer, so really only a definite +. However I should mention that my stomach wasn’t empty back then. Also, an effect I didn’t mention so far is that in every case there was a definite gentle mood lift (no placebo). And that’s pretty much it. This material is for real but unimpressive in my opinion. It’s certainly reminiscent of cannabis, no doubt about that, but it does nothing that cannabis can’t do and better, notably because unlike cannabis it feels like I need a constant influx of the stuff to remain high. It would be fine to use if that were the only thing I had access to. But other than that, I don’t really have a strong push to go on with the wild dagga. Last but not least, the live plant is quite pretty and ornamental. I’m considering adding it to my garden !

中文译文 致幻剂

这是我第一次真正体验灵分子. 我以前吸过, 只有几次可视化, 我也曾几次服用过LSD. 英语不是我的母语. 第一个旅行报告. 我把我在网上订购的Harmine FB袋子, 溶解在汁中200毫克. 经过一个小时左右, 我又服用了150毫克的哈尔, 我把160毫克的DMT溶解在汁中, 喝了, 味道像吐, 我洗了个冷淋浴, 因为我想要感觉清洁和好像我洗完后, 随着效应的出现, 恶心也随之增加. 我干掉了,然后走进我的房间. 我坐在床上, 觉得自己变得精神分裂或其他原因, 因为我觉得有东西在看着我, 这很奇怪. 我感到吐在我的喉上,所以我吐了一个巨大的红. 我把吐从嘴上和鼻子上擦掉, 让我不发的是我反复告诉自己一切都会好起来的, 我躺在床上,放弃自我,让自己死去. 旅程开始了. 我躺下,戴上耳机, 播放我的心理舞蹈歌单. 精神惚是令人难以置信的酷听当. 我躺在床上, 面朝下, 视觉和音乐在我的眼前漂亮地流动. 我觉得她/他来自印度. 现在回想起来, 我相信是维什努或是希瓦. 我只是在体验,只是在观察发生了什么. 当视觉开始下降时, 时钟大约是凌晨1点30分, 随着视觉效果变得不那么强烈, 经历的焦点从外在的体验转向内在的体验. 我意识到自己已经死亡了. 我不再是那个进入经历的人, 我只是这样, 但这是一个无聊的解释, 我的意识已经脱离了我们所说的思想, 我的思想仍在思考, 我们是我们的自我, 但我们的自我是一个幻觉, 所以我们什么都不是, 在这个状态中, 没有善恶, 没有欲望, 我唯一能看到的是我们没有任何地方可以去,永远没有最终的目的地, 在任何其他时刻之前或之后都会有一段时间. 因此,所有的都是永恒的和无限的. 这意味着你已经做了一切, 你一直是每个人, 你将永远这样继续, 你是一切. 当你已经完成了一切, 如果继续没有意义, 为什么要继续? 那么不要继续吗? 你不能不继续. 我在床上坐着的时候, 思想的轨道走向了这一点, 走向了这样的方向: 一切都是无的对立面, 随着我接近无所思之时, 我自动驾驶, 把头放在枕头上, 就好像宇宙本身告诉我那样. 因为我不认同我的情感和思想, 没有自我, 我的思想和意识是免费的混乱和虚幻的自我. 我的头脑就像一台计算机, 只能集中精力解决问题. 它一再试图解决如何停止运行的问题. 永恒意味着一切都已经发生, 但解决方案从来没有被发现, 所以没有解决方案. 没有解决方案的问题是问题吗? 因为问题和解决方案是相互形成的. 所以它们实际上是两种相同的东西. 就像我和其他人互相创造, 就像光与黑暗相互创造, 佛陀也明白这一点. 我们都在浪费时间, 有些人把时间浪费在冥想上,有些人把时间浪费在玩游戏上,有些人把时间浪费在看电影上, 我们是业力,也就是行动. 我们是自己做的,我们不能什么都不做. 什么都不做就是做. 没有坏事,也没有好事. 这一切只是一个持续的经历. 我就是. 你是佛陀,你只需要意识到这一点. 这种虚无/万物的体验持续了大约4个小时, 这是我有史以来最深刻的迷幻体验. 谢谢您的阅读. 和阴. 时间表: 112734 发布时间:2019年1月27日 药水 (822),哈马林 (314),DMT (18):一般 (1),第一次 (2),实体/生物 (37),神秘经验 (9),独自 (16)

Yin and Yang is One Pharmahuasca (Harmaline & DMT) by yinyang Citation:   yinyang. "Yin and Yang is One: An Experience with Pharmahuasca (Harmaline & DMT) (exp112734)". Erowid.org . Jan 27, 2019. erowid.org/exp/112734 DOSE: T+ 0:00 200 mg oral Harmine (freebase) T+ 1:00 150 mg oral Harmine (freebase) T+ 2:00 160 mg oral DMT BODY WEIGHT: 65 kg This was my first real experience with the spirit molecule. Previously I had smoked it and got nothing more than a few visuals, probably due to not smoking properly. I have also done LSD a couple of times. English is not my first language. First trip report. I took the bag with harmine fb that I ordered online and dissolved 200 mg in lemon juice. After about an hour or so I took about 150 mg harmine more, because I didn't feel much effects. After 1 more hour it’s midnight and the effects of harmine is quite strong at this point, so I dissolved 160 mg of DMT in lemon juice and drank, it tasted like vomit and might have been one of the most disgusting things I have ever drank. I took a cold shower because I wanted to feel clean and nice as I do after a shower, I felt the effects coming on now and were getting more and more scared. As the effects came on, so did the nausea. I dried off and went into my room. I sat on the bed and thought I was becoming schizophrenic or something because I felt like something watched me and my brain started to interpret sounds with meaning, like any sound has a meaning connected to it so every sound is like an external thought. Weird. I felt vomit coming up my throat so I puked a huge red puke. I wiped the vomit off my mouth and nose and what kept me from freaking out was that I repeatedly told myself that everything is going to be fine and I tried to allow everything to happen. I lay back in my bed, I let the ego go, I let myself die. The journey begins. I lay back and put on my earphones, and play my psytrance playlist. Psytrance is unbelievably cool to listen to while tripping. I lay on my stomach facing down in my bed and the visuals was beautifully flowing together with the music before my eyes. At the peak there was a blue person floating and dancing in front of me, I felt that she/he was from India. At the moment I did not know who it was but now thinking back on it, I believe it was Vishnu or Shiva. When the visuals were intense I was only experiencing, only observing whatever happened. When the visuals started declining clock was about 1.30 am so had been tripping for maybe an hour. And as the visuals were getting less intense, the experience changed focus from outward experience to inward experience. When the focus changed I realized I had an ego death. I was no longer the person I was coming into the experience, I simply was, but that is a boring explanation so we will go with a more exciting one. My awareness had detached from what we call mind, my mind was still thinking but I was not affected by it because I was not there. We are our ego but our ego is an illusion so we are nothing but nothing is everything because how could everything exist without nothing and nothing without everything. In this state there was no good nor bad, there was no desire, there was no attachments/caring about anything. And the only thing I could see was that we don’t have anywhere to get, there is never a final destination, because every ending is a new beginning and vice versa. There will always be a moment before or after any other moment. The ALL is therefore eternal and infinite. Which means that you have done everything, you have been everyone and you will continue like this forever, you are everything. There is no meaning going on when you have already done everything. Why go on if there is no meaning in going on? Then don’t go on? You can’t not go on. When I realized interdependent opposites (Dark-Light, Life-Death etc) there was a moment when I sat in my bed my train of thought comes towards that realization and it went something like this: Everything is the opposite of nothing and they make each other, everything is nothing. And as I approach the thought nothing I went on autopilot and put my head in a pillow so that I would see nothing. As if the universe itself told me that. And because I was not identifying with my emotions and thoughts, there was no ego and my mind and awareness was free from the confusion and illusory of the ego. My mind was now like a computer that has one focus solving problems. It repeatedly tried to solve the problem of how to stop going on. And the problem of not going on can’t be solved, because eternity means that everything has happened but the solution to that has never been found so there can’t be a solution. Is a problem without a solution a problem? No, because problem and solution make each other. So they are really 2 things of the same. Just like me and other make each other, just like light and dark make each other, there is not one without the other and they are fundamentally one. The chinese daoists understood this a long time ago, and the buddha understood this. What we are all doing is wasting time because there is nothing else to do. Some waste their time on meditating, some waste their time on playing games and some waste their time watching a movie, it doesn’t really matter how we waste our time, it is only up to you what you want to waste it on. We are karma which means action. We are our doing, we can’t do nothing. Doing nothing is doing. There is no bad, and no good. It is all just a continuing experience. I AM. You are the buddha you just have to realize it. This experience of nothingness/everythingness continued for about 4 hours until my ego slowly started crawling back in. This was the deepest I have ever come in the psychedelic experience. Thanks for reading. Yin and Yang.

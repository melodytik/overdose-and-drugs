Jan 8, 2007. erowid.org/exp/58504 DOSE: T+ 0:00 250 mg oral Pharms - Propylhexedrine (capsule) T+ 3:00 4.0 mg oral Pharmaceuticals BODY WEIGHT: 155 lb After first reading about propylhexedrine, I was curious, but also somewhat hesitant. From what I had read, it definitely seemed to have potential as a stimulant, yet there are many reports of people experiencing moderate peripheral stimulation. It seems increased blood pressure leading to throbbing headaches isn’t all that rare and certainly an unpleasant side effect. However, curiosity eventually got the best of me and I gave it a try. I bought an inhaler at a local pharmacy. I pulled off the top and took out the cotton. Dividing it into two, I stuffed it into two gelcaps. The following was written as throughout the experience. 4:17 Took half the cotton in a gelcap. Feel normal, relaxed, its been a laid back day. Blood pressure (Bp) 125/71 pulse 74. Normally it would probably be lower, but I’m slightly anxious just having swallowed something I’ve never tried before. Just a regular Saturday. I intend to write a final paper for one of my classes. My stomach is empty. 4:30 Slight stimulation seems to be noticed. Could be placebo. Feeling slightly more focused and motivated. 4:48 Definitely stimulated. Mild going towards moderate it seems. Second half swallowed. Feel energetic, want to get things done. 5:00 Bp 132/64 pulse 64. I feel clearheaded, but not as focused as I wish I was. Full of energy. 5:15 A general sense of well being is felt, but not much euphoria. Hopefully its still yet to have peeked.

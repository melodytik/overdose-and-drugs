美国 第一次使用阿雅瓦斯卡 背景:在复活节星期日前的满月期间, 我们带来了一个八个人的大帐作为我们的仪式室. 帐的顶部是开放的,四面都有窗户, 我们都带来了独立的吊床躺在经历. 我们还带着一个桶来清洗. 我很紧张, 因为我很清楚这将是一次非常激烈的体验. 我吸了几次DMT, 这有助于熟悉这种物质, 我也一直在遵循阿雅哈瓦斯卡饮食前两天, 我用3小时的时间洗了三次, 然后把三种液体结合在一起, 我们把每一个酒分成四分之一. 这使得一个剂量留下来,以防一个使用者吐或需要第二剂量. 我们的计划是先喝葡萄酒, 晚上7点左右, 我们决定喝葡萄酒. 我们三个人在此之前大约6个小时没有吃东西. 我一次性吞下了我的份量. 我立刻感觉到意识的变化, 我感觉到我的身体充满了能量, 我认为葡萄酒的味道并不坏. 几乎像咖啡一样有强烈的木质味道. 十五分钟后我可以肯定地说, 葡萄树本身是非常迷幻. 颜色有点不同,更加鲜, 整身感觉很好, 眼睛闭着看起来很轻, 最重要的是, 没有恶心. 我的朋友在喝了葡萄酒后不久就开始抗拒清洗. 他喝了大约20分钟, 喝完葡萄酒后, 我们都进了帐, 我觉得这将是一个非常强烈的酒. 我已经清洗过的朋友, 他声称在帐外看到一个女人的影子. 我和我哥哥确实处于另一种意识状态, 我朋友决定不参加这次的炼, 因为他已经得很厉害, 我同意他应该在这个会议上坚持使用葡萄藤. 时间大约是7点40分, 我当时非常紧张. 坐在那儿,手里拿着, 感觉就像吸DMT之前的感觉一样. 我知道我必须鼓起勇气, 我知道我面前的物质世界将被更外来的事物所取代, 我知道我即将被推入自己的英雄之旅, 我喝下了大部分的杯子,剩下大约八分之一. 这是一个可怕的味道. 这是一件非常痛苦的事情. 它们使我的嘴和喉充满了苦的粘液. 我立刻觉得自己要吐了. 我用我的全部力量试图把它住, 我用桶跑到房间的另一边, 开始清洗. 我能感觉到从我内心深处的吐. 我从来没有这样吐过. 听起来非常,持续了5分钟. 吃完之后,我感到很丧, 我坐了几分钟,双腿交叉,头下. 这时我开始听到熟悉的DMT声. 听起来像是外星人, 每个地方都充满了振动. 我再次感到恶心,回到我的桶. 我开始再次吐, 但这次来自我的清洗的声音和令人惊叹的低音. 噪音让我想起了"矩阵"中的那个场景, 这是一个奇怪的低音, 通过我回响. 我把头放在水桶里, 闭上眼睛, 开始看到实体. 第一个是女性. 她伸手摸我的脸,给了我一个额头上的吻. 在这个时候,我知道DMT在短时间内被吸收到我的体内, 眼睛闭着, 我处在一个变形的几何碎形的空间里. 它们的颜色是深色的印地和红色. 无处不在有各种各样的实体从这些几何图案中出来. 这些实体看起来像神和女神. 他们是人形, 但看起来更先进和外星人. 他们从男性转变为女性, 它们给我赠送的礼物看起来像是多层生日蛋糕, 我听到一个声音说:"看看! 看看这个! 我是特伦斯·麦肯纳的粉丝, 听过他的机器精灵之旅很多次, 我注意到我心灵的边缘有一位外星女性穿着医生服装, 在剪贴板上写下东西, 她不像那些给我礼物的实体, 她没有变成任何东西. 她看起来非常真实. 这位外星人似乎是我在吸烟DMT之前遇到的实体之一, 我躺在一张桌子上, 很快我又开始大量清洗. 每次我清洗之前, 我都会听到一个声音告诉我吐, 我很难专注于这些幻象, 因为峰值非常强烈. 我求它过去, 主要是因为它在身体和情感上是如此不舒服. 我知道我不能记住很多部分, 整个经历中, 那些相同的实体一直在寻找我, 一旦峰值开始淡化, 洞察力开始涌入. 我意识到这种经历是如此不舒服的唯一原因是因为我的自我试图不放手. 我的自我正在死亡, 它试图欺骗我, 帮助我了解我生活中许多负面因素是因为我自尊心在生命早期围绕着我筑起了墙壁来保护我. 这让我意识到这些墙壁一直把我隔离离生活中唯一真正有实质的东西, 我一直把人们推开, 每次我拥抱我生命中最亲近的人, 一股强烈的狂喜能量涌入我的身体. 它解构了我认为我是的一切, 只是留下了我真正的爱心. 我听到一些脚步在我后面的帐外. 听起来像是两脚的声音, 一步两步. 这让我很害怕,所以我甚至没有看看它是什么. 哥哥告诉我他听到了脚步声, 抬头看看是什么. 他说他看到一个穿着西装的影子, 当它注意到他意识到它, 它走了, 消失了. 他告诉我,我的下巴了下来, 因为我听到的脚步声听起来非常真实. 旅行结束后大约2小时喝了chacruna. 他遇到了阿雅华斯卡母亲, 她最初是树, 她告诉他,这就是植物如何看待世界,以及它们如何与人类沟通. 他还联系了一位外星人, 告诉他人类的生命是由他的物种播种的. 他问他能不能叫它父亲, 生物变得非常高兴, 它的脸变成了父亲的脸, 它告诉他它会在任何时候为他提供帮助. 我的朋友也从葡萄树上获得了许多洞察力. 这让他看出所有让他绝望的负面思维模式. 他还说它给他带来了大麻叶的幻象, 这次经历非常艰难, 有时候我觉得自己无法应对. 我知道这些洞察力对我来说无价, 我之前曾经经历过很多迷幻之旅, 但没有一个能真正与阿雅华斯卡的力量相比. 这就像有智慧引导你通过它, 现在我知道它有多么能真正治愈. 我也无法理解DMT是如何如此迅速地被吸收到我的体内, 已使用的证券 香 (Banisteriopsis caapi) (169种),白 (Psychotria viridis) (170种),华斯卡组合 (Huasca Combo) (269种),亚华斯卡 (Ayahuasca) (8):第一次 (2),组合 (3),实体/生物 (37种),小组 (2-9) (17)

Oct 25, 2020. erowid.org/exp/105945 DOSE: T+ 0:00 56 g oral Banisteriopsis caapi (tea) T+ 40:00 56 g oral Psychotria viridis (tea) BODY WEIGHT: 190 lb First Experience With Ayahuasca Setting: Camping on the full moon before Easter Sunday in an isolated location out in the desert with my brother and a friend. We had brought a large eight person tent to serve as our ceremonial room. The tent had an open top with windows on all sides which made outside viewing possible. We had all brought freestanding hammocks to lie on while going through the experience. We had also each brought a bucket to purge into. Set: I was pretty nervous because I had a pretty good idea that this was going to be a fairly intense experience. I have smoked DMT a few times which helped with getting a familiarity with the substance but I also knew that this experience would be much different and last much longer. I had also been following the Ayahauasca diet for the previous 2 days, which mainly consisted of me just eating salads and green smoothies. Dose: A few days prior to the trip I had brewed 227 grams of Banisteropsis Caapi Cielo and 227 grams of Psychotria Viridis separately from one another. I did three washes 3 hours apart from one another and then combined the three liquids at the end to reduce each substance to about a pint worth of liquid. We separated each pint jar into fourths. This left one dose in case one of use threw up or wanted a second dose. Our plan was to drink the vine first, and then after about 30 minutes to drink the chacruna. It was around 7:00 pm when we decided to drink the vine. All three of us had not eaten for about 6 hours prior to this. I gulped down my portion all at once. I instantly felt a change in consciousness after downing the small amount. I felt energy rushing through my body, and at the same time, a strange peace of mind. The taste of the vine wasn’t bad at all in my opinion. Almost coffee like with a strong wood like flavor. Fifteen minutes after that I could definitely tell that the vine itself was very psychedelic. Colors were a bit different and more vibrant, my whole body felt strangely good, I was receiving mild closed eye visuals, and best of all, no nausea. My friend was fighting off the purge soon after drinking the vine. He eventually succumbed to it about 20 minutes after drinking and threw up. We all went into the tent about 25 minutes after drinking the vine to get ready for the chacruna. At this point I had a feeling that this was going to be a very strong brew. My friend who had already purged was already in a full blown trip from just the vine. He claimed to see a shadow person in the female form right outside the tent. Me and my brother were definitely in another state of consciousness but not to that extent. My friend decided not to take part in the chacruna because he was already tripping pretty hard and he didn’t think it was possible for him to hold down the DMT containing brew. I agreed that he should probably just stick with the vine for this session. It was about 7:40 when my brother and I decided to chug down the chacruna. I was very nervous at this point. Sitting there with the chacruna in my hand had the same feeling I would get right before smoking DMT. I knew I would have to muster up the courage, I knew that the material world in front of me would be replaced with something much more alien, I knew I was about to be thrusted into my own hero’s journey, and most of all, I knew it was going to be some serious work. I downed most of the cup, leaving maybe about an eighth of it. It was horrible tasting. It was gritty and very very bitter. It coated my mouth and throat with a gritty and bitter slime. I instantly felt like I was going to vomit. I tried with all my strength to hold it in but I knew it was futile. After about 30 seconds with the brew in my stomach, I ran to the other side of the room with my bucket and began to purge. I could feel the throw up coming from the depths of my being. I’ve never thrown up like this before. It was very loud and lasted a good 5 minutes. Once I was finished I was bummed that I wasted my chacruna brew. I sat cross legged with my head down for a few minutes. That’s when I started to hear the familiar DMT buzz. It sounded like something not of this world, very alien. Everything had this vibrating buzz. I felt nauseas once again and went back to my bucket. I began throwing up once again, but this time the noises coming from my purge had and amazing bass to it. The noise reminded me of the scene in the Matrix after Neo swallowed the pill and that metallic liquid was going down his throat. It was a strange bass that echoed through me. With my head in my bucket and my eyes shut I began to see entities. The first one was a female. She reached for my face and gave me a kiss on my forehead. At this point I knew the DMT somehow absorbed into my system in that short period of time before throwing it up. With my eyes closed I was in a space of morphing geometric fractals. The color scheme was of dark indigos and reds. There were entities everywhere coming out of these geometric patterns. The entities looked like gods and goddesses. They were humanoid but looked far more advanced and alien like. They were morphing from male to female, never really staying the same. They were handing me morphing gifts that looked similar to multiple tiered birthday cakes made out of impossible geometric patterns. I heard a voice saying “Look at this! Look at this!”. As a side note, I am a big fan of Terence Mckenna and have heard his Machine Elf trip plenty of times so I’m not sure if this was coming from that or if it was independent of that. As this was going on, I noticed in the periphery of my psyche an alien woman in what seemed to be doctor attire, writing things down on a clipboard as if she was taking notes of what was going on. She was not like the entities giving me presents, she was not morphing into anything. She looked very real. This alien woman seemed to be one of the entities I had encountered on my prior smoked DMT experience in which I was lying on a table surrounded by these high tech alien beings which seemed to be studying me. Soon after this I began to purge heavily once again. Every time before I would purge I heard a voice telling me to throw up and that it would make things better. It was difficult for me to pay attention to the visions because the peak was so intense. I was begging for it to pass, mainly because it was so uncomfortable physically and emotionally. I know there is a lot of parts I cannot remember because the intensity of the experience. Throughout the entire experience I had these same entities reaching for me and interacting with me. Once the peak started to mellow out, the insights started to rush in. I realized the only reason this experience was so uncomfortable was because of my ego trying to not let go. My ego was dying and it was trying to trick me into thinking I was dying. It helped me understand how many negative aspects of my life were due to the fact that my ego had put up walls around me trying to protect me early on in life. It showed me that these walls have been cutting me off from the only thing in life that really has any substance which is love. I had been pushing people away as a defense mechanism. I had visions of me hugging the people close to me in my life and every time it showed that, a powerful surge of ecstatic energy would rush through my body. It deconstructed everything I thought I was and just left the loving core of which I really am. Near the end of the trip I heard footsteps coming up behind me outside the tent. At first I thought it was an animal outside the tent but I then noticed the sound was bipedal, as in two steps at a time. It freaked me out so I didn’t even look at what it was. Once we all came down my brother told me near the end of the trip he heard the footsteps and looked up to see what it was. He said he saw a shadow person in what appeared to be a suit looking at all three of us in the tent. When it noticed that he was aware of it, it walked away and disappeared. When he told me that my jaw dropped because the footsteps I heard sounded so incredibly real. The trip was over about 2 hours after drinking the chacruna. My brother also had a life altering experience in which he met mother ayahuasca which appeared first as a tree but then morphed into an old Mexican woman and gave him many insights. She told him that this is how plants see the world and how they are able to communicate with mankind. He also contacted an alien being which told him that human life was seeded by his species. He asked if he could call it father and the being became very pleased and its face morphed into a fatherly looking face with a beard and glasses smiling with joy. It told him that it would be there for him anytime he needed him. My friend also had many insights just from the vines. It showed him all of his negative thought patterns that were causing him despair. He also said it showed him a vision of a pot leaf and showed him that many of his problems came from abusing cannabis. This experience was very difficult but oh so worth it. There were points where I didn’t think I could handle it. The insights I got were priceless to me and I know they will stay with me for the rest of my life. I have had many psychedelic trips before this but none of them can really compare to the power of Ayahuasca. It is like there is an intelligence guiding you through it, almost as if it was a therapy session. Now I know how truly healing it can be. I also can’t get over the fact of how the DMT absorbed into my system so quickly, it doesn’t really make sense.

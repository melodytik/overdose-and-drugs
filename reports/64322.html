中文译文 大麻素

曾经有一段时间, 我享受着种植自己"非常特别"的室内花园的奢. 我常常在剪下多余的叶子, 为了节省空间和增加产量, 很长一段时间, 我吸烟的叶子 (使用烟斗) 和享受的自由, 没有支付它. 很棒, 但有时我想要在没有酒的地方吹, 我有个聪明的想法把叶子放进汁机里, 我有一台非常好的果汁机可以从任何东西中提取果汁. 我把几把新鲜的叶子放进机器中, 结果产生了很少的液体 (也许是半司液体), 液体是非常深绿色和永久污染我的白色果汁机. 我加入了足够多的叶子肉 (从抽汁机的过器) 制成一个多汁的糊,然后在一些纸上形成一个'蛋糕'或'棒'. 在几天的时间里, 那里的气味仍然像剪草一样. 结果的"Hillbilly Hash"很容易以同样的方式吸烟,并且产生相当于吸烟相同数量的正确的芽的高. 我还能品尝到草的味道, 这是我的食谱,我坚持它! 其他: 大麻 - 大麻 (93):制备/食谱 (30),不适用 (38)

Sep 25, 2009. erowid.org/exp/64322 DOSE: repeated smoked Cannabis - Hash (tar / resin) BODY WEIGHT: 175 lb There was a time when I enjoyed the simple luxury of growing my own 'very special' indoor garden. Like many people who have such an indoor garden, I had lots and lots of excess leaves which I was constantly pruning off as I continuously topped the plants (to conserve space and increase yield) and trimmed off big top leaves so that light could reach branches at the bottoms of the plants. For a long while, I smoked the leaves (using a bong) and enjoyed the freedom of toking up without having to pay for it. This was great but sometimes I wanted to puff in places where having a bong was impractical so I looked for a way to process the leaves into something more potent and more palatable. After trying a few different things with varying results, I got the bright idea to put the leaves in a juicer to extract what I could. I have a very good juice machine that can extract juice from about anything. I fed several handfuls of fresh leaves into the machine which yielded a very small amount (maybe 1/2 fluid oz) of nearly black liquid that smelled very similar to grass clippings (the type that stick to the bottom of your mower). The liquid was very dark green and permanently stained my white juicer. I added enough leaf pulp (from the strainer of the juice extractor) to make a juicy paste, then formed the paste into a 'cake' or 'bar' on some wax paper. Over a few days, the little bar dried and shrunk somewhat. It still smelled like grass clippings. The resulting bar of 'Hillbilly Hash' was easy to smoke in the same method as hash and produced an equivalent high to smoking the same amount of decent buds. When smoking Hillbilly Hash, I can still taste the grass clipping flavor but it is actually pretty pleasant and is far and away better that smoking plain leaves. That's my recipe and I'm sticking to it!

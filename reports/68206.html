尤其因为我没有预料到它会发生. 我从来不知道阿尔布特罗尔可以增强大麻中THC的作用. 晚上开始了令人惊叹的吸烟, 它们是种植在某种液态肥料中的无人种子. 它们是我吃过的最好的中烟, 我只吸烟. 无论如何,我在抽烟的时候, 在旋转水时, 我抽了一口到三口. 我很长一段时间没事了,大约2个小时过去了, 这使我超级,大约在那个时候我认为阿尔布特罗尔开始与大麻反应, 我和我的朋友离开了我们的朋友宿舍走到我们这只是走廊. 当我走到门外, 然后我昏倒了,一秒钟后醒来. 然后我又做了一遍,躺下了更长时间,然后站起来又跌了下来. 我终于有了力量跑到宿舍, 走到柜台, 我呼吸很沉重, 汗水流. 我觉得自己好像在发作, 我终于平静下来了, 我一直以为自己会忘记呼吸, 我记得当我昏倒时的感觉是, 我觉得我的灵魂离开我的身体, 我觉得自己忘记了自己是谁, 不停地告诉自己自己是谁. 我终于完全安静下来, 睡着了. 对于我来说,这是我一生中最疯狂,最可怕的事件. 我很高兴它已经消退了, 我建议从不使用阿尔布特罗尔与大麻. 这不是任何人都应该经历的. 其他: 大麻 (1), 药物 - 阿尔布醇 (241):小组 (2-9) (17), 卫生问题 (27), 组合 (3)

Aug 17, 2011. erowid.org/exp/68206 DOSE: 1.5 hits inhaled Pharms - Albuterol repeated smoked Cannabis BODY WEIGHT: 120 lb My experience was a crazy one especially because I didn't see it coming. I never knew before that albuterol can heighten the effects of THC in cannabis. The night started of awesome smoking some extremely good mids. They were dro seeds grown in some liguidy manure. They were the best mids I have ever had and I smoke only dro. Anyway while I was smoking I took maybe 1 all the way to 3 puffs during the rotation of a hooka. I was fine for a long time about 2 hours had gone by before I smoked 2 js and 1 blunt (not a cigarello a full blunt). This made me super stoned and about that time I think the albuterol started to react with the cannabis and made me start to trip. not like a mushroom trip but made me literally freak. My friend and I left our friends dorm to walk to ours which was just down the hall. when I got outside the door to walk, I felt a wierd rush go through me and it made me freak even more. Then I fainted and woke up a second later. Then I did it again and stayed down longer then got up and fell again. Then I finally got it together and was able to run to the dorm and get to the counter and prop myself up. I was breathing heavily and was sweating. I felt like I was having a seizure and was just flippin out. I finally settled down but was still messed up. I kept thinking that I was going to forget to breathe but never did. When I remember what I felt when I was fainting was that I felt like my soul was leaving my body and then when I woke up my soul was falling back in. It was crazy then after I settled down I felt like I was forgetting who I am and kept telling myself who I was in my head. Eventually I was able to completely settle and I went to sleep. It was single-handedly the craziest and scariest event in my life. I was so relieved when it subsided and I could control myself again. I suggest to never use albuterol with cannabis. This is nothing that anyone should want to go through.

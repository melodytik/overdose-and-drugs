我的报告不是关于一个单一的狂喜使用. 我在使用时从未想到的长期不良影响. 狂喜是一种很有趣的药物, 如果我只是偶尔服用. 这是我一生中最美好的经历. 这真是太好玩了, 幸运的是,我遇到了一个不好的旅行, 我有恐慌发作, 我以为我会死在上面. 我祈祷我会好,如果我是我永远不会再次跌倒只要我活着. 在那之后,我一段时间没有. 我开始和一个喜欢E的男朋友约会. 我看到他们总是有多乐趣, 尽管我很紧张, 我开始每周三次, 有时在整个冬天四次. "01年 - "02年5月) 我可能从未有过这么多的乐趣在我的整个生活. 遗憾的是,这有很多后果. 我只会担心短期的后果, 不知是否会有不好的后果, 首先,我的免疫系统完全下降了. 我知道每个人都会生病, 但我真的很健康, 我开始倒的时候,总是有什么东西让我气,无论是支气管炎,流感,感冒还是最糟糕的单一性肺炎. 我还没有恢复到最好的健康状态, 似乎不够糟糕, 心理上的副作用也很严重. 我开始意识到当我没有倒时, 我会一直紧张. 我认为它最终会消失,但它没有. 然后效应变得更糟. 我现在很奇怪. 我认为最奇怪的事情. 我经常感到紧张和偏执. 我总是想着死亡,担心会发生什么坏事. 如果这还不够糟糕, 我变成了一个虚弱症患者. 我几乎总是丧. 我觉得我再也没有任何期待了. 我不喜欢像以前那样出去. 为了补充一下我的男朋友总是与他的朋友一起,自从我辞职以来,我总是被遗弃. 他总是试图让我这样做, 因为我们紧密地结合在一起. 我真的想,但我只是害怕. 我觉得自己一整天都很糟糕,而且我总是很累. 我一直都觉得自己不适合. 我还有一个永久性失眠症. 我可以免费睡觉. 也许这就是我慢性疲劳的原因. 我甚至无法长时间睡觉. 我每晚只能睡3-4个小时. 然后我醒来了,我不能再睡着了. 在我睡觉的短暂时间里, 我不断地紧紧着我的下巴. 我知道这是因为每次醒来时我的下巴都会点击和卡住, 我觉得我只是在我的睡眠倒. 我也经常做梦. 我刚开始戒掉, 就会在睡觉之前出现一些奇怪的情况. 我会半睡半醒, 我的脑海会开始思考一些奇怪的东西, 我会觉得自己在躺着时一直在旋转, 我会试着开眼睛, 尽管如此,这是唯一一个长期效应消失了. 唯一好处在于我的短期记忆似乎还不错. 如果我意识到在狂喜中度过如此美好的时光会导致永久的伤害, 狗我可能根本就不会这样做. 因为即使是我在狂喜中所经历的一切, 最烦人的,最恶心的事情是我太想念了. 在我醉之前, 我经常喝酒和大麻. 现在我讨厌酒精和大麻是好的, 即使它不能竞争. 六个月过去了, 我仍然记得那段美好的时光. 如果我出去喝酒, 我唯一喜欢做的事情就是呆在家里. 有时我觉得我想要把一切搞,然后再一次倒. 我猜你只活一次,对. 但我不能这样做, 因为从长远来看, 一晚的乐趣绝对不值得一子的压力,疾病,抑郁和焦虑. 我认为在某种程度上我慢慢好转,或者也许不是. 也许我只是在学会应对抑郁和焦虑. 我正在考虑试试佐洛夫特或普罗扎克, 但现在我害怕服用药物. 我害怕不好的副作用. 关于E,我只能说这么多. 只是记住不要太, 因为E对我确实有可怕的长期影响. 如果我不得不重新做我永远不会采取它因为一旦我采取了它没有像它再一次. 我很想念它, 但我必须学会处理它, 因为自从我成为E-head以来, 截止日期:19447年 MDMA (3):各种 (28), 失去魔力 (34), 旅行后的问题 (8), 抑郁 (15), 和习惯 (10), 回顾 / 总结 (11)

The Anxious Decline MDMA (Ecstasy) by Blondie Citation:   Blondie. "The Anxious Decline: An Experience with MDMA (Ecstasy) (exp19447)". Erowid.org . Sep 28, 2005. erowid.org/exp/19447 DOSE: repeated oral MDMA BODY WEIGHT: 120 lb My report isn't about a single ecstacy use. Its about my using ecstacy over a whole period of time causing bad long term effects which I never thought of while I was using. Ecstacy is a really fun drug if I take it only once in a while. When I first tried ecstacy when I was 15 (about 4 years ago) and it was the best experience of my life. It was so fun that I wanted to do it over and over again. I tried it again 1 week later and fortunately for me I had a bad trip which caused me to be scared and not want to trip ever again. I had a panic attack and I thought I was going to die while I was on it. I prayed that I would be all right and if I was I would never trip again as long as I lived. I didnt trip for a while after that. Then about 3 years later I started hanging out with a bunch of people that trip every day and I started going out with a guy who really loved E in that group. I saw how much fun they always had and even though I was really nervous I ended up taking an E pill. It was alot of fun and after that I began tripping all the time about 3 times a week sometimes 4 over the course of a whole winter (Dec. '01 - May'02). I probably never had as much fun before in my whole life. Unfortunately this had a lot of consequences. See, the whole time I would trip I would only worry about the short term effects and if I was gonna have a bad trip or not, but never did it cross my mind any of the long term damage I could do. First of all my immune system just lowered completely. Of course I know everyone gets sick but I was really healthy and I hadn't been sick since the 8th grade. When I started tripping I was always coming down with something whether it was bronchitis, the flu a cold and the worst Mono. I still am not back to my best health although I'm trying to get back there. And as if being sick all the time wasn't bad enough there were the mental side effects as well. When I started slowing down around April, May I began to realize that when I wasn't tripping I would just constantly be nervous all the time. I figured it would eventually go away but it hasn't. Then the effects got worse. I'm so weird now. I think of the wierdest things. I'm constantly nervous and paranoid. I'm always thinking and worrying about death and if something bad is going to happen to me. And if thats not bad enough I turned myself into a hypochondriac. I'm pretty much always depressed. I feel like I never have anything to look forward to anymore. I dont like going out like I used to. To top it off my boyfriends always tripping with his friends and since I quit I'm always the left out one. He's always trying to get me to do it because we bonded so much on it. I really want to but I'm just scared to. I feel like shit 24/7 and I'm constantly tired and fatigued. I feel just 'out of it' all the time. To top it off I have permanant insomnia. I can get to sleep for nothing. Maybe thats the reason for my chronic fatigue. I can't even sleep for long periods of time. I can only sleep for 3-4 hours a night. Then I wake up and I can't fall back asleep. During that little period of time I am sleeping I am constantly clenching my jaw. I know this because my jaw clicks and is stuck everytime I wake up and I also bite the insides of my cheeks too. I feel like I just tripped in my sleep. I also get nightmares alot too. When I first stopped tripping I would get weird things before I would go to sleep. I would be in a half asleep/half awake state and my mind would just start to think of weird things and I would feel like I was spinning around and around while lying down and I would try to open my eyes but I couldn't for a few minutes. This is the only long term effect that went away tho. The only good thing is that my short term memory seems all right. If I realized that having such a good time on ecstacy was going to result in permanant damage and I would have to live my life like this I definitely would not have done it as much. Shit I probably wouldn't have done it at all. Because even all the shyt I got put through on ecstacy the most annoying and nastiest thing of all is that I miss it so much. Before I tripped on ecstacy alot I would have a blast on beer and weed. Now I hate alcohol and weed is ok even though it can't compete. Even now like 6 months later I still can't forget the great times I had on it and if I go out to try to drink I practically fall asleep cuz it's so boring now. The only thing I like to do is just stay home. Sometimes I feel like just fuckin everything and just tripping again. I guess you only live once right. But I can't do that cuz in the long run it's just not worth it. A night of fun definitely is not worth a lifetime of stress, sickness, depression and anxiety. I guess in a way I'm slowly getting better, or maybe not. Maybe I'm just learning to deal with depression and anxiety. I was thinking of trying zoloft or prozac but I'm scared to take medications now. I'm scared of the bad side effects. (another weird thing about me). Well thats about all I have to say about E. Just remember try not to trip so much because E really did have horrible long term effects for me. If I had to do over I would never have taken it cuz once I took it there was nothing like it ever again. I miss it alot but I got to learn to deal with out it cuz my life sux ever since I became an E-head.

现在,我觉得有必要详细介绍PMA的化合物. 似乎Paramethoxy-amphetamine在这里得到了不好的名字. 似乎每个人都对PMA感到不安, 现在的MDMA通常可以服用几剂, 按重量计算,MDMA在生理上比PMA更弱. ,我们应该得到报告吗? 很抱歉,我只是试图解释在消费之前了解你的化学物质. 现在作为一个年轻人, 大约17或18我在一个秘密的三明治店工作. 我有一位稍微年长的同事因购买各种药物而获得养老金, 曾经有一年春天, 某个同事购买了绿色三, 他说同事需要帮忙, 比如我替他替换星期五晚上的班, 我同意在规定他提供我一个Mitz或两个. 没有了. 我在吃药之前研究了这些药的成分. 我的研究表明它们是PMA, 更多的研究表明PMA的一些副作用是说话和集中注意力 (如甲基). 我认为PMA与甲基胺相似 (只有类似于MDMA的一些轻微的迷幻效应). 现在就开始吧. 我相信这是一个星期二. 我没有上学,因为我在演讲团队. 我醒来时已经喝了一片PMA. 我抵达学校,乘坐公共汽车参加演讲比赛. 随着公共汽车的起飞, 我对每个人的言论过分警,对公共汽车的加速过分警,对颜色的差异过分警,对时间的侮辱. 旅程持续了很长时间, 很不寻常, 即使在我的系统中, 我们走近目的地, 我仍然清楚地知道自己已经醉了. 然而,由于我处于一个平静的开放环境中, 我不认为我被化合物的效应过度影响了... 实际上在许多方面我会说效果在适当的剂量相当类似于Ritalin, 我甚至还做了一些不经意间的演讲. 我和一个同事演出了莎士比亚的"西撒"的场景. 显然我们把它撕裂了. 我听到了每一个字. 这一天拖了很长时间, 但最终我和我的同事获得了第一名在州的莎士比亚演出. 这一天很长,我在回家的路上昏迷了. 我睡得很好,做了愉快的梦. 我对PMA没有抱怨, 只要你知道它是一种特殊的安非他命. 也许这是最好的结束方式. 其他: 不良/可疑的狂喜 (567):多种 (28),第一次 (2)

Sorry I was just trying to expound on knowing your chemicals before you consume them. Now as a young man, around 17 or 18 I worked at an undisclosed sandwich shop. One of my slightly older co-workers had a pension for acquiring various pills, preferentially ecstasy. Now once upon a time, in the spring of the year said co-worker acquired what was distributed as green Mitsubishis that were sold as ecstasy (MDMA). Said co-worker required a favor, something like me covering his shift on Friday night so he could bang Suzzie Sluton. I agreed under the provision he provide me with a Mitz or Two. Yada yada. Being the ever vigil chemically minded genius psychonuat, I researched the composition of said pills before I consumed them. My research indicated that they were PMA and not to be taken lightly in hot dancy situations. A little more research indicated that some of the side effects of PMA were talkativity and focus (like meth). Now being both familiar with MDMA and Amphetamine, I would state that PMA is far more similar to methamphetamine (only with some mild psychedelic effects similar to MDMA). Going right along. I believe it was a Tuesday. I got to skip school because I was on the speech team. When I woke up I consumed a tab of PMA. I arrived at school and boarded the bus to the speech competition. As the bus took off preliminary effects ensued. I was overly aware of everyone's speech, overly aware of the acceleration of the bus, overly aware of the difference of colors, and insulted by time. The trip dragged on, unusual to note, even with a amphetamine analog in my system I dosed of for a major duration of the trip, awakening only as the plubians I was traveling with began to awake an jabber like south American monkeys. As we approached our destination I shoke off some cob webs. I was still overtly aware that I was intoxicated. However since I was in a calm open environment I don't believe I was overly taxed by the effects of the compound ... Actually in many ways I would say the effects at appropriate doses are quite similar to Ritalin, but what do I know. Eventaully I gave some in-prompto speeches. And eventually Me and a partner performed a scene from Shakespear's Ceaser. Apparently we tore it up. I got every word perfectly. The day dragged but eventually me and my associate got first place in state for our rendition of shakespeare. The day being long, I drifted off on the way home. I slept well with pleasant dreams. I have no complaints about PMA as long as you understand it is a peculiar Amphetamine. Well maybe that's the best way to end it.

中文译文 兴奋剂

我在大学的第一年是一个有趣的经历. 我在大学的第一年开始上瑞塔林和其他兴奋剂. 但我的故事始于高中. 我已经使用可卡因 (可卡因) 了大约2年了. 我在高中的最后一年开始吸. 在HS中,我用了大约5次,大约一周内做了一克. 可乐给了我一个很棒的感觉, 我很社交,乐观, 我从来没有上, 因为我没有钱买大笔的东西. 毕业后我很好, 两个月没有吸鼻子, 感觉还好. 但突然间, 我发现我在工作时遇到了一个不时使用可卡因的同事. 我和他成为了真正的好朋友,一起去健身房,一起聚会, 我朋友住在离我们工作非常近的地方, 实际上是5秒的步行路程, 这使得我们在工作中更容易吸毒. 所以我们了很多钱. 当我们感到疲时, 我们会上楼再做一行. 这种疯狂的循环持续了整个夏天, 我完全停止使用可卡因. 很难停止, 但因为我没有钱, 完全没有联系, 我花了大约两周的时间才恢复100%的清洁, 没有冲动或任何东西. 喝红牛酒有助于我有冲动. 我对兴奋剂的热爱使我在大学尝试了许多新事物. 例如,我相信每个人都听说过阿德拉尔和里塔林. 我开始在大学里偶尔使用阿德拉尔和瑞塔林. 我只使用它们当我需要学习或写论文. 他们很棒! 这让我立刻感到有动力. 对于这些东西来说, 无论如何,我开始更喜欢他们当我投资大量的药丸非常便宜. 我用25美元买了120粒20毫克的药丸! 这已经失控了. 每个药的街头价格是1美元,甚至可能是0.50美元. 我开始每天使用它们. 我开始是吞下药,但很快我发现如果把药粉碎和吸入,效果会更快. 每天我都会在上课前醒来, 吸入一粒20毫克的药, 课后同样的事情, 吸另一个然后继续我的一天. 我真的很喜欢上课和学习, 里塔林成为我的"廉价大学可乐", 因为我买不起真正的可乐, 在20MG之后的任何事情都会让我感到奇怪, 经过大约一个月的使用, 我感冒了,没有什么严重的,但它让我在床上几天. 无论如何,当我生病的时候我没有吸任何东西. 我从每天平均使用100毫克 (5片) 变成了无用. 这成为一个大问题,第二天的冷火我感觉到退出我的股! 我吐,有时感到疯狂, 我很糟糕,我是一个混乱. 我把剩余的药丸扔掉了,大约30粒. 我在家度过了一个星期的假期, 我知道如果我告诉她或她抓住我, 她会杀了我, 这对我有很大的帮助. 我仍然使用瑞塔林, 因为我很容易获得它, 我可以连续几个星期不喝任何东西, 我也限制了我的使用为学校工作 (每天1片) 或如果我在聚会,我想保持晚上我会做2 - 3片最大. 其他: 可卡因 (13), 药品 - 甲基基 (114):不适用 (38), 宿醉 / 几天后 (46), 和习惯性 (10), 回顾 / 摘要 (11)

My 1st year of college marks the beginning of my addiction to ritalin, and many other stimulants. But, my story starts in High school. I had been using cocaine (coke) on and off for about 2 years. I started coke in high school, senior year. In HS I used it about 5 times total, doing a gram in about a week. Coke gave me an awesome feeling, I was very social, upbeat, and I always had fun on it. I could never get addicted because I didn't have money to pay for any large amounts. Anyway, after leaving High school I was fine, I went about two months without snorting a thing, and I felt fine. But all of a sudden, I discovered someone at work who used coke casually. Me and him became real good friends, goin to the gym together, partying together, and we also did ALOT of coke together. My friend actually lived Extremely close to our work, literally a 5 second walk, which made it easier for us to do coke at work. We both worked full time and late shifts, so we made a lot of money. We would use our money to buy on average one 8-Ball twice a week (1 gram a day, or 7 grams a week, total $350 a week). We would be running back and forth like crazy at work, and when we felt tired, we would just go upstairs and do another line. This crazy cycle lasted the entire summer, and I wasted about $1400 on the stuff in 2 months. When the summer was over, I stopped using coke completely. It was pretty hard to stop, but since I had no money and absolutly NO connections, I had no choice. It took me about 2 weeks to be back to 100% clean, no urges or anything. Drinking Redbull helps when I have urges. My love for stimulants made me try a number of new things in college. For instance, I'm sure everyone has heard of Adderall, and Ritalin. Well I began using adderall and ritalin in college on rare occasions. I only used them when I needed to study or write a paper. They were awesome! It made me feel instantly motivated. School work was a breeze on these things! Anyway, I began to grow a bigger liking to them when I invested in a Large amount of pills for VERY cheap. I bought 120 20MG pills for $25!! That's out of control. Street price is a dollar a pill, maybe even .50. So with my new lump sum of 'Smart Pills' I began using them everyday. I started off by swallowing the pills, but soon thereafter I noticed that it would work faster if I crushed them and snorted them. I loved the rush, everyday I would wake up before class, snort a 20mg pill, and then be on my way. After class the same thing, snort another then go about my day. I really enjoyed going to class and learning while I was high on these pills. The Ritalin became my 'Cheap College Coke' seeing as I couldn't afford real coke, and it gave almost identical results. Anything after 20 MGs just makes me feel weird, almost in a trance but I am super energized, just like yay. This was not a bad habit until I finally hit rock bottom after about 1 month of use. I had caught a cold, nothing to serious but it kept me in bed for a couple of days. Anyway, when I was sick I didn't snort ANYTHING. I went from using an average of 100mgs a day (5 pills) to nothing. This became a big problem, the second day of going cold turkey I felt the withdrawal kicking my ass! I was throwing up, feeling insane at some times, lost and confused about everything, depressed etc. It was terrible, I was a mess. I flushed the remaining pills, about 30. I spent a week at my aunt's house over break to keep myself away from the pills, and it worked. My aunt is SUPER anti-drug, so I knew if I told her or if she caught me she would kill me, so I couldn't do anything while I was there. This helped me a lot. Since then I have been a lot better, I still use ritalin since I have easy access to it, but I know I am not addicted. I can go weeks on end with out anything, and I have. I also curbed my usage for School work only (1 pill a day) or if I am partying and I wanna stay up late I'll do 2 - 3 pills max.

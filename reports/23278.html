我在7点左右到达我家, 我在7点45分左右回家. 需要一些心理诱导的失败, 他生产了米饼覆在5-MeO-AMT. T+0:00 - 发生饼的消费 (8:00 pm) T+0:30 - 异常的旅行感觉,伴随着恶心. (表哥大约一个小时后吐) T+1:30 - 影子已经获得了类似生命的特征,随意移动. 任何能反射光线的物品都会出现颜色, 我们找到了一个CD盒, 我们把它带走了. 那些物体在移动, 留下蛇形的痕迹, 我们看电影"星期五"时, 颜色的色调不再有意义, 很快我们就开始玩'Gran Turismo 3'. 由于某种原因, 我表哥能够控制游戏中的车辆, 游戏中的天空非常蓝, 草地非常绿. 逻辑上我们得出结论, 这款游戏与"马里奥兄弟"有着相似的特点. 电视上的像素很大, 房子里的窗移动了,电影中的窗也移动了. 需要新的环境, 我们换了房间. 收藏了我们所"发现"的一切 (全息图,饮料,食物,睡袋等等), 我们就像子一样, 找东西然后堆起来. 电脑太复杂了,我们只想像野一样敲击键盘. 我们说话不合时宜, 但我们完全能理解对方. 在这个"现实"中的一切都有意义, 我们基本上是同一个人, 我们都知道对方的想法. 我们会随机想象相同的东西 (我们都在同一时间看到指的CEV). 我们在这一点上遇到了"我们与他们"的理论. 这是一幅令人困惑的木棍图画, 分隔着一个垂直线, 一个木棍图没有标签 (我猜那应该是我们), 这能够回答我们可能会想到的每一个问题. 我们想连接一个NES来玩"马里奥兄弟". 但这项任务太艰巨了,所以我们问'我们应该连接NES吗? 我们查阅了这些数字,并从逻辑上得出结论,NES是"他们",我们是"我们",两者之间存在着一个障碍,即现实的门. 任何不是""的东西 (物体,人,动物) 都是"他们",任何"我们"的东西. 在过去的四个半小时里, 这种情况只能持续一段时间, 我们的脸上着紧张, 我们摇摇着, 黑光的视觉效果超出预期, 我们继续画画,然后开始把许多'在黑暗中发光'的星星扔到各处. 我表哥没有注意到自己倒了一杯酒. 但一切都仍然相当疯狂. 我们听到了贝克的新CD"海变". 这也是旅程中最不舒服的部分. 我们很累,但我们不能睡觉. 在接下来的3个小时里, 笑声和视觉效果都慢慢消失了. 在此期间发生的事情是模糊的. 我们发现我们已经把东西扔到各个地方, 这正是从"拉斯维加斯的恐惧与厌恶"中出来的. 我们意识到我们所说的没有多大意义. 我们基本上已经恢复正常. 后一天的效果并不是很严厉. 我们没有感觉不好或任何,只是疲. 但下次我们计划在白天进行.

Apr 28, 2003. erowid.org/exp/23278 DOSE: 6.0 mg oral 5-MeO-AMT (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 210 lb My cousin arrived at my house about 7 or so, and I came home at about 7:45. In need of some psychadelically induced failure he produced a rice crispie treat covered in 5-MeO-AMT. T+0:00 - Consumption of rice crispie occurs (8:00 pm) T+0:30 - Unusual feeling of the oncoming trip, accompanied by nausea. (cousin vomited about an hour later) T+1:30 - Shadows had gained life-like characteristics, moving as they pleased. Colors soon appeared on everything that was able to reflect light, which could only be rationalized as what we called RGB (TV talk for red green blue). We found a CD case, which looked like a hologram. This stayed with us for the entire trip, we just kind of walked around with it. T+3:00 - This is where things got way out of control. Objects were moving and leaving snake like trails with rainbow effects going through out and around them. While we were watching the movie 'friday', color hues stopped making sense and soon just started jumbling together in a watery fashion, after that the whole TV seemed to be underwater. Soon we began to play 'Gran Turismo 3'. For some reason my cousin was able to control the vehicles in the game but he had no clue what was happening. The sky in the game was incredibly blue, and the grass incredibly green. Logically we came to the conclusion the game had similar characteristics as that of 'Mario Bros'. Pixels in the TV were huge, and seemed to be quite distorted. Curtains in the house moved, and so did the curtains in movies. T+5:00 - The need for new environment made us change rooms. After accumulating everything that we had 'found' (The hologram, drinks, food, a sleeping bag, etc.) we went to another room that had a black light, and a lot of glowing shit. We were acting like monkeys, just finding things and putting them in piles. The computer was too complex to handle and we just wanted to pound on the keyboard like wild apes. We babbled incoherently and yet we were totally able to understand eachother. Everything in this 'reality' made sense, regardless of relevance or logic. We were basically the same person, we each knew what the other person was thinking. We would visualize the same things at random (We both had CEVs of a thumbtack at the exact same time). We at this point came accross the 'us versus them theory'. This was a drawing of confusing stick figures seperated by a vertical line, one stick figure was not labeled (I'm guessing that was supposed to be us) and the other was labeled 'them'. This was able to answer every question we could possibly concieve. For example: We wanted to hook up an NES to play Mario Bros. But the task was too daunting, so we asked 'Should we hook up the NES?'. We consulted the stick figures and we logically came to the conclusion that the NES was 'them' and we were 'us', and that there was a barrier inbetween that was the threshold of reality. Anything that wasn't tripping (Objects, People, Animals) was 'them', anything that was.....well it was 'us'. T+6:00 - Uncontrollable laughter and babble has been occurring for the past four and a half hours, literally without pause. This could only continue for so long before insanity would insue. Our faces were sore from tension, and we were rocking back and forth babbling incoherently while everything around us went totally insane. Black light visuals were off the charts, trails were as long as the mind was capable to produce, while being full of the whole color spectrum. We continued to draw and then proceeded in throwing many 'glow-in-the-dark' stars everywhere. My cousin spilled a drink all over himself without noticing. T+8:00 - Noticeable comedown had commenced, but everything was still quite insane. We listened to the new Beck CD 'Sea Change'. This was also the most uncomfortable part of the trip. We were tired, yet we couldn't sleep. The laughter slowly trailed away over the next 3 hours as did the significant visuals. What happened during this time is hazy. But when we did realize what was going on we noticed that we had thrown things everywhere, and had insane drawings scattered all over the room. This was straight out of 'Fear and Loathing in Las Vegas'. It was during this time that we realized how little sense we had been making. T+19:30 - We are basically back to normal. The day after effects aren't very harsh. We don't feel bad or anything, just tired. This is definitely something that will be repeated, but next time we plan on doing it in the daytime.

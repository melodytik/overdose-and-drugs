我是一名16岁,重达130磅的男性, 我已经使用了大量大麻,,甲基,DXM,氧化,LSA (早晨的光) 和二胺. 我在朋友家的环境中以积极的心态进入夜晚. 时间线是这样的: 我到达朋友的家,发现他有一份Strattera (阿托莫克西丁) 的处方. 在网上做了一些研究, 我决定为社会做出贡献, 我在空腹中服用一粒40毫克的药丸. 时间+45分钟:我开始感到头部和四肢有轻微的刺痛. 我决定这还不够,再服用40毫克的药. 我开始感觉到第二个和第一个药丸的完整效果. 这完全不同于我通常从甲基中感受到的快速冲动感. 我觉得身体完全平静/放松. 伴随着这种放松, 我发现Strattera似乎有助于我的思维过程. 首先, 我和朋友讨论政治, 我可以从自己的角度和朋友的角度思考这些想法. 思想和观点从我的脑海里流向我的嘴里. 最美妙的是,一旦我们谈完, 我的心灵和身体恢复了非常放松的状态. 我决定吃最后一个40毫克的药丸. 时间+125分钟:生活非常顺利. 我的身体的放松达到低氧化水平. 闭上眼睛, 专注于身体的感觉, 像以前一样,我的头脑像以前一样敏. 我知道我现在能够比以前更清晰地思考, 在我们35分钟的谈话中, 我说了25分钟, 朋友们提醒我说话的速度太快了, 我相信这是一个顶峰. 时间+165分钟:我开始下降. 时间+280分钟:我的感觉与时间+45分钟一样. 我已经回到了基线水平. 只有因为我一直在思考我的心灵/身体状态, 第二天:感觉有点疲, 没有其他不愉快的副作用. 总的来说,我度过了一个非常愉快的时光. 这种物质对于平凡的学日来说是理想的, 因为它会增加注意力, 我明白为什么会给有ADD的孩子开这药, 因为它会减慢一切, 我绝对会考虑再做一次. 我相信成的可能性是因为它是一种愉快的感觉, 其他: 药品 - 阿托莫克西 (316):一般 (1),第一次 (2),小组 (2-9) (17)

Not What I Expected Atomoxetine (Strattera) by GBVsoldier Citation:   GBVsoldier. "Not What I Expected: An Experience with Atomoxetine (Strattera) (exp61063)". Erowid.org . Sep 3, 2008. erowid.org/exp/61063 DOSE: T+ 0:00 40 mg oral Pharms - Atomoxetine (pill / tablet) T+ 0:45 40 mg oral Pharms - Atomoxetine (pill / tablet) T+ 1:30 40 mg oral Pharms - Atomoxetine (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 130 lb I am a 16 year old 130 pound male in reasonably good shape. To give a little background on my experiences, I’ve done copious amounts of marijuana, salvia, methylphenidate, DXM, oxycodone, LSA (morning glory), and diphenhydramine. I went into the night with a positive mindset and environment at my friend’s house. The timeline went something like this: T = 0: I arrive at my friend’s house and learn that he has a prescription to Strattera (Atomoxetine). After doing a little research online, I could not find much information. I then decide to contribute to society and try the Strattera. I take one 40 mg pill on an empty stomach. T + 45 minutes: I start to feel a slight tingling sensation on my head and extremities. I decide that this is not enough and take an additional 40 mg pill. T + 90 minutes: I start to feel the full effects of the second and first pills combined. It is completely unlike the speedy rush feelings I generally feel from the methylphenidate. It’s more of a marijuana body high, in that I feel a complete body calm/relaxation. Combined with this relaxation was an incredibly alert state of mind. I found that the Strattera appeared to be helping my thinking process. At first, my friends and I discussed politics, and I was able to think about the ideas from both mine and my friends’ perspective. The thoughts and points flowed articulately from my mind to my mouth. And the beautiful part was that as soon as we were done talking, my mind and body went back to a very relaxed state. Excited by how well it is going, I decide to take one last 40 mg pill. T + 125 minutes: Life is going very well. The relaxation of my body is reaching low oxycodone levels. Closing my eyes and just focusing on the feeling of my body is incredibly enjoyable. Just as before, my mind is as sharp as ever. Knowing that I am able to think more clearly than before, I purposely start a religious debate with my friend’s girlfriend. In the 35 minutes that we talk, I talk for about 25 minutes, but I barely notice the passage of time. My friends alert me to the fact that I’m talking incredibly fast, which surprised me because I was consciously trying to talk slowly. I would believe that this was the peak. T + 165 minutes: I start to come down. T + 280 minutes: I feel as I did at T + 45 minutes. T + 345 minutes: I have come back down to baseline levels. The decent was smooth and pleasant, and only very evident because I was constantly thinking about my state of mind/body. The next day: I feel mildly tired, but most likely because I stayed up late. No other unpleasant side affects are present. Overall, I had a very enjoyable time. This is the kind of substance that would be ideal for a mundane school day, as it does increase focus with fun side effects. I can definitely see why this would be prescribed to kids with ADD, as it slows down everything while keeping the mind sharp. I would definitely consider doing it again. I believe the addiction potential is there because it is a pleasant feeling that I already, a day later, wish to experience again.

我曾经在俱乐部中使用纯粹的MDMA, 有人告诉我4-fluoro也会有类似的效果. 我们有一种水晶化/粉状的4-氨, 这将是我第一次... 我们开始时每人服用125毫克, 味道很糟糕, 很难从嘴里清洗出来. 这次进场非常顺利, 同时与陌生人交谈也变得更加容易. 效果与MDMA非常相似, 经过一个小时 (T+1) 后, 我在舞台上做出自己的动作, 仿佛音乐控制着我的行为. 很容易停下来放松, 但我绝对不想这样做. 我没有张或反射, 但我很少有, 因为我用我的肌肉力量跳舞和我的嘴笑:D我的瞳孔扩大, 但不像在MDMA和我的嘴一直干燥. 关于4-fluoro最奇怪的事情是完全控制大脑. 没有心理扭曲的感觉, 所以完全是身体的事情. 在T+3的时候, 我决定把剩下的部分吃起来更好, 从那一刻起, 我一直在跳舞, 直到聚会结束. 我们觉得如果有节奏的话, 我们可以再跳舞2-3小时. 很遗憾没有任何后派对,所以我们去休息在家里. 我失去了说话的意愿, 我听不了15秒钟的对话, 但这可能是因为我从来没有经历过衰退或血清素缺乏症. 虽然感觉很奇怪, 但并没有让我感到不愉快, 我们一整天都在抽烟, 直到我们睡着了 (T+21) 睡得很好. 后果与正常的宿醉非常相似, 嘴巴干燥是一个常见的因素, 我没有食欲直到T+17. 毕竟这是一个非常放松的一天, 感谢良好的朋友在同样的心态. 很难说哪些后果是由4-fluo造成的, 我的朋友告诉我,可以连续4天使用fluo, 这是一个很好的替代品! 与MDMA相比: 慢慢上升,当它开始工作时没有"". 完全清醒,没有爱和拥抱的感觉. 我还没有经历过MDMA或"自杀星期二"的尬事件. 根据我的第一次经历, 我会说, 很好的MDMA替代品, 其他: 4-氨 (276):一般 (1), 俱乐部/酒吧 (25)

Oct 8, 2007. erowid.org/exp/66229 DOSE: T+ 0:00 125 mg oral 4-Fluoroamphetamine (powder / crystals) T+ 3:00 125 mg oral 4-Fluoroamphetamine (powder / crystals) T+ 5:00 repeated smoked Cannabis BODY WEIGHT: 85 kg There was a techno party in a far away city, some friends invited me to come dance with them :) I had a few experiences with pure MDMA while clubbing and I was told 4-fluoro would have similar effects. We had a crystallized/powdered form of 4-fluoro amphetamine in small 250mg bags. This would be my first time... We took about 125mg each at the start (T=0) and went directly to the party. The taste was terrible and very hard to wash out of your mouth. The coming up was very smooth and easy... The beat started to appeal more and more, while at the same time speaking with strangers went easier. The effects were a lot like with MDMA, except there was no 'kick' or a sudden feeling of the drug starting to work. After an hour (T+1) the 4-fluoro was doing its job... I was making my moves on the dancefloor and it was like the music was controlling my actions. It was easy to stand still and relax, but there was no way I wanted to do that. I had no jaw tension or chew reflexes, but I rarely have that while im on MDMA because I use my muscle power to dance and my mouth to smile :D My pupils were dilated but not as much as while on MDMA and my mouth was constantly dry. The strangest thing about 4-fluoro was the complete control over the brain. There was no feeling of mental distortion, so it was completely a body thing. At T+3 I decided to take the rest of my portion to party even harder, so another 125mg of 4-fluoro went down my throat. From that moment on I was dancing my legs off until the end of the party. That end was way too soon for us (T+5), we had the feeling we could dance 2-3 hours more if there was a beat. Too bad there weren't any afterparty's so we went chilling out in a home. There we smoked some joints and from that moment on I was completely disconnected to reality, I lost my will to talk and I couldnt listen to a conversation for more than 15 seconds before I lost it. For me it was not really pleasant compared to MDMA, but that's probably because I never had a comedown or serotonin shortage yet. It felt weird but not unpleasant since there were still beats going on in my head. We skipped sleep and kept on smoking joints the whole day till we fell asleep (T+21) and slept like roses. The after effects were quite similar to a normal hangover, a kinda uneasy and lame feeling with goosebumps and waves of cold. The dry mouth was a constant factor and I had no appetite till about T+17. After all it was a very relaxing day, thanks to good friends in the same state of mind. Its hard to say which of the after effects were caused by the 4-fluo and which by sleep deprivation and cannabis. My friends told me its possible to use 4fluo days in a row without loss of effect or body functions... Its a great alternative to MDMA! Compared to MDMA: Slow coming up, no 'kick' when it starts working. Completely sober, no loving&hugging feeling. I cant say much about the aftereffects because I haven't had a MDMA comedown or 'suicide tuesday' yet :) From my first experience I'd say, good MDMA alternative, I'll do this more often!

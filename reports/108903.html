Jul 25, 2016. erowid.org/exp/108903 DOSE: 4 ml oral 1,4-Butanediol (liquid) BODY WEIGHT: 320 lb I have been very interested in GBL, BDO and GHB, because the drug sounded very euphoric and like a super nice body akin to alcohol but clean feeling and just overall a great time. So I sourced some 99percent butanediol and took 2 ml first day with some food and a few cups of coffee. I felt very relaxed little euphoric and a bit tipsy. Next day I up dosage to 3ml this was more euphoric and relaxing but after a few hours I found it hard to stay awake..... now here is where the dosage can mean life or death. I took 4ml and headed to a local park, I felt really good, pretty euphoric and just was very connected to people I talked to. Kind if like that feeling from MDMA but not as strong... I was talking to a girl on the park bench and this was about an hour after I took the 4ml. Well I passed out, I have no clue how long I was out I was asked by a neighbor who said he slapped me and could not awake me. Other people poured water in me. Nothing worked. When I awoke I was surrounded by a few police and an ambulance. They asked what I take I said nothing and after about 30 minutes when I arrived at hospital I felt about 90 percent good.... So this drug has an extremely small margin for error. Just one ml more than usual and I completely passed out. If I had not been awoken by the dude I could have died lying in the grass at the park. Also if I was at home alone and say I dosed a hour or two before bed I am pretty sure I would not have woke up. If I am ever to try this substance again I believe I would just do the synthesis to get pure GHB which is much more euphoric which was what I was chasing. By upping my dose I only caused myself to overdose. 1 to maybe 1 and a half ml is the most I would ever dose again and always buy reagent grade pure.

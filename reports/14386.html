(对于一个星期六的早晨来说,这是一个意想不到的惊喜)! 我们立即改变了早餐的计划, 想知道自从前一天晚上就禁食了, 我拿出我的口袋秤 (迪林10克) 给自己切出250毫克,给我妻子切出200毫克 (大约为我们两人的体重2.85毫克/公斤). 上午11点 在口下. 它的味道和MDMA相似, 这味道有毒. 我们坐在舒适的沙发上, 放一些温和的音乐, 打开岩灯. 上午11:20 (T+0:20) 非常出汗的手掌. 虽然速度更快, 但其余情况与MDMA相同. 上午11:30 (T+0:30) 眼睛的瞳孔大大扩张, 没有人否认我们是高. 视觉效果与使用MDMA的高潮非常相似. 虽然光源变得更亮, 边缘却有点模糊. 鲜的颜色变得更加生动. 我们的岩灯看起来非常非常酷. 不幸的是,视觉效果就在这里停下来. 在整个体验中, 视觉变化不再出现. 上午11:40 (T+0:40) 有一个身体声. 呈现方式类似于MDMA的身体声, 如果有人能描述MDMA的身体声, 仿佛快乐的波穿透了皮肤, 深入肉身, 如果我必须扩大它, 我会说声是25%的愉快作为什么是MDMA. 我们感觉有点冷汗, 虽然我们感觉有点冷, 简而言之,在一个小时后, 在40分钟的时间里, 没有人会对此感到惊. 下午12点 (T+1点) 尼斯塔格姆斯开始起作用, 非常好看的 紧的效果比高剂量的麻醉药还要糟糕. 非常令人不快. 有一些同理心的特质, 但它们似乎更注重自我. 我很难在治疗中使用这种方法. 这很容易反映,但很难沟通. 虽然我不想说话, 但我觉得我和妻子更亲近, 没有任何起床或做任何事情的愿望, 非常愉快. 这就是旅程停滞点. 下午2:30 (T+4:30) 非常缓慢的下来. 轻微的头痛开始. 我们注意到我们一整天没有吃东西, 非常饿了. 我们有后照到晚上8点左右 (T+10点). 结论: 这是一个有趣的研究化学物质! 虽然它本身有其享受之处, 但与MDMA相比, 我会说至少值得尝试一次,但我不会再试一次. 虽然MBDB与MDMA有许多相似之处, 我发现很难将其归类为MDMA类型. 任何期望像MDMA这样的体验的人都可能会非常失望. 是的,经验是相似的, 但只有副作用几乎相同 (阴囊, 身体负荷, 紧握). 所有的乐趣属性MDMA (身体声,同情,温和的视觉,声音和气味) 是要么非常减少或根本没有... 时间表: 14386 MBDB (190):一般 (1),第一次 (2),小组 (2-9) (17)

Not a Substitute for MDMA MBDB by Jackburbee Citation:   Jackburbee. "Not a Substitute for MDMA: An Experience with MBDB (exp14386)". Erowid.org . May 2, 2002. erowid.org/exp/14386 DOSE: 250 mg oral MBDB (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 88 kg Because of its claimed similarities to MDMA, I had been curious about MBDB for quite a while. I consider myself to be moderately experienced, particularly considering research chems and MDMA. Many people might have the same questions I had, so I will attempt to run a qualitative comparison between it and MDMA. The express delivery package with 1g of MBDB arrived this morning at 10:30am while I was making coffee. (Unexpected surprise for a Saturday morning)! We immediately changed our plans for breakfast and figure that since we had been fasting since the night before and had nothing planned for the rest of they day, this would be perfect. I then pulled out my pocket scale (Deering 10g) and cut 250mg for myself and 200mg for my wife (which doses at approximately 2.85mg/kg of body weight for the both of us). 11:00am Down the hatch. It tastes similar to MDMA, but even more 'chemical'. It tastes poisonous. We sit on our comfy couch, put on some soft music and turn on the lava lamp. 11:20am (T+0:20) Very sweaty palms. Quicker but otherwise same as MDMA. 11:30am (T+0:30) Pupils highly dilated, first effects become very obvious. There is no denying that we are high. Visuals are strikingly similar to the come up of an MDMA high. Lights sources become brighter and yet get a little hazy around the edges. Bright colors become more alive. Our lava lamp looks very very cool. Unfortunately, this is where the visuals stop. There are no more visual alterations throughout the entire experience. 11:40am (T+0:40) There's a body buzz. Similar in presentation to the MDMA body buzz, but not nearly as enjoyable. If one can describe the MDMA body buzz as if ripples of joy were penetrating the skin and sinking deep into the flesh, then MBDB seems to have a warm fuzzy feeling only a few millimeters below the skin. If I had to scale it, I would say the buzz is 25% as enjoyable as what it is on MDMA. There is a bit of an unpleasant cold sweat going on, we are cold, but sweating a bit under the arms and at the temples. Simply put, MDMA gives you a swift kick in the ass after an hour. MBDB grows on you over the period of 40 minutes. MBDB will not surprise anyone. 12:00pm (T+1:00) Nystagmus kicks in, pretty similar to a high dose of MDMA. Very nice. Jaw clenching kicks in, worse than a high dose of MDMA. Very unpleasant. There are some empathetic qualities, but they seem to be a lot more introspective. I would find it very hard to use this under therapy. Its easy to reflect, but hard to communicate. There is just no desire to talk, but I feel closer to my wife just stretched out in the same couch with our legs interlocked. There is no desire to get up or do anything except stay put, relax, and trip. Very enjoyable. This is where the trip plateaued. 2:30pm (T+4:30) Very slow come down. Mild headache kicks in. We notice that we are extremely hungry not having eaten all day. We had an afterglow until about 8:00pm (T+10:00). Conclusion: MBDB is a fun research chemical! It has an enjoyability of its own, but nowhere near as fun as MDMA, and seems to have a much lower potential for abuse. I would say its worth trying out at least once if you can, but I wouldn't go out of my way to get some again. MBDB shares many similarities with MDMA, but it absolutely does not work as a substitute. I find it hard to classify it as an MDMA analogue. Anyone expecting an MDMA like experience will probably be very disappointed. Yes, the experience is similar, but only the side effects are virtually identical (nystagmus, body load, clenching). All of the fun properties of MDMA (body buzz, empathy, mild visuals, sounds and smells) are either very diminished or simply not there...

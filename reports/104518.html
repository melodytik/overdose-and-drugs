Very, very good communication tool, very warm, nice. Only downside was I drank HUGE quantities of water, and I think I would like to monitor my temperature and heart rate next time, just to see how it compares to baseline. I would never advise using without lots of drinking water nearby. We did drink some beer afterwards, and it seemed cool, no negative effects. Very mellow the next day. I think we became much closer as a result, and both of us are eager to try again, but we are really conservative, so probably every two months or so. I bet this would save relationships and friendships in trouble. I am much closer now to the friends and my wife than before, as we talked out all kinds of issues. Therapists will be using this in the future, I am sure. Not very many reports on this stuff out there, so I am making my contribution.

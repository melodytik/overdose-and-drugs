所以... 我不是一个吸毒者. 我从来没有试过任何非法药物用于娱乐目的, 也许我永远不会 (虽然我必须承认, 我很好奇精神活性药物). 尽管我坐在电脑前, 打字你正在读的东西, 我觉得即使在这个无名,无聊的生活中做这件事也很有趣... 我现在心情很好,我很高兴今天放学了 (有各种原因),太阳照着,天气似乎很好 (虽然我觉得有点热). 我正坐在电脑前, 正如我所说的, 我是一个人. 我可以听到我在楼下打电话,在房子里散步,在她这样做时聊天. 这不是我想要的兴奋... 我刚刚从一个小时前喝过的可口可乐经典中醒来. 我现在正在吃一口糖果: 我吃了一小口, 甜美,丰富的味道迅速通过我的舌头. 我的思想告诉我,我渴望更多. 为了满足我的渴望, 我现在意识到这块糖果有点老了 (它是在2007年复活节期间购买的), 现在我吃了一口, 巧克力和核桃的味道让我的嘴充满了. 我喜欢它的味道. 现在是凌晨11点55分, 这绝对是美味的. 奶油和软在中心, 质感有点颗粒或粉. 现在是凌晨11点57分, 我吃了最后一块糖果. 巧克力的后果 (对我来说, 巧克力的大部分或全部都是如此) 只是渴望更多的巧克力. 味道似乎释放了相当大的幸福 (也许内啡因?) 我感觉有点精力充, 我只是想要更多. 这不是世界上最常见的精神活性物质吗? 如果是,我绝对不会感到惊. 其他: 巧克力 (182):一般 (1),一个人 (16)

Jun 28, 2007. erowid.org/exp/63200 DOSE: oral Caffeine (liquid) 12 g oral Chocolate (edible / food) BODY WEIGHT: 130 lb So.... I'm not exactly a drug user. I've never tried any illicit drugs for recreational purposes, and I probably never will (though I must admit, I am quite curious about psychoactives). And yet as I sit here at my computer, typing what you are reading, I feel happy that I've decided to post this. I find even this something fun to do in this anonymous, boring life of mine... I'm in a generally good mood right now, I'm feeling happy that I got a day off from school today (for various reasons), the sun is shining, and the weather seems to be quite nice (though a bit to hot for my liking). I am sitting here, at my computer, as I have said, in the second floor loft of my Connecticut home. I am alone. I can hear my Mom on the phone downstairs, pacing around the house and chatting while she does so.. Not the excitement I would have liked.... It is right now 11:50 AM, and I'm freshly awake from a can of Coca-Cola Classic I had about an hour earlier. I am now taking a bite of the fudge: I eat a small morsel, and the sweet, rich taste goes down quickly through my tongue. My mind is telling me that I crave more. As to fulfill my craving, I scrape out a bit more out and eat it. I now realize that the fudge is a bit old (It was purchased during Easter season of 2007), and hard, though it is still sweet and chocolaty. I now take a large bite, and the taste of chocolate and walnuts overwhelms my mouth. I savor the taste. It is now 11:55 AM, and I am taking yet another bite of the chocolate. It is absolutely delicious. Creamy and soft in the center, the texture is somewhat grainy or starchy. At what is now 11:57 AM, I eat the last bit of the fudge. The aftereffect of the chocolate (As is with most or all chocolate, for me) is simply a craving for more. The taste seems to release a considerable amount of happiness (Perhaps endorphins?) within me. I feel a bit more energetic, and yet at the same time relaxed. I simply want more. Is this not the most common psychoactive substance in the world.

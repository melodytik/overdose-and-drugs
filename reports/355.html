我终于找到了. 如果有人遇到同样的问题, 我有个小建议: 所以它可能一看就不像化学品. 只是仔细阅读标签. 我和我的朋友T决定出三克左右. 没有太多或任何东西, 只是足以看看食谱是否起作用. 在蒸发了大部分水之后, 我们得到了大约2g. 我们整晚都在抽烟. 非常有趣. 味道非常接近烟. 实际上我认为它是相同的. 它们的效果有点不同. 我不是一个速度疯子, 我仍然记得, 什么是好的感觉. 甲基是没有接近的地方. 这种高兴感与麻醉相比是没有的. 感觉很强烈,有些头轻. 我感觉非常好整个晚上. 我们用了3个小时就把它擦掉了. 这种东西让我很困难,但它让我整晚都在寻找更多. 我没有感觉到强烈的衰落,只是高原开始消退, 但总的来说是一个很好的体验. 其他: 甲 (12):一般 (1),制备/配方 (30),培养/合成 (31),小组 (2-9) (17)

"Cat Ain't That Bad: An Experience with Methcathinone (exp355)". Erowid.org . Sep 11, 2000. erowid.org/exp/355 BODY WEIGHT: 140 lb Well, after doing some carefull research on Methcathione, I decided that I would go ahead and try to make some. The recipe's all over the 'net right now and let me tell ya, it works. The most annoying part of the whole experience was trying to find the potassium permanganate. The faq decribed where I might find it and I went to most of those places a few times. Finally I found it. If anyone out there is having the same problem I did here's a bit of advice: the bottles are usually pretty damn big and placed in boxes for selling. So it may not even look like a chem at first glance. Just read the labels carefully. My friend T and I set out to make about three grams. Not too much or anything, just enought to see if the recipe worked. After evaporating off most of the water, we had what looked like about two g's. All night long we smoked this stuff. Very fun. The taste is very close to crank smoke. I actually think it is identical. The effects are a little different. I'm not a speed freak (anymore), I grew somewhat out of it after high school. I still remember though, what good meth feels like. Methcathinone comes nowhere close. The euphoria is just not there compared to meth. There was a strong sense of energy with some light headedness. I felt pretty damn good all night. We polished off what we had in like three hours. The stuff got me spinnin' pretty hard, but it kept me fiendin' for more all night. I didn't feel a strong comedown, it's just that the plateau began to wear off and I would miss it so bad. But, all in all a good experience.

有一天晚上我有个冲动, 冲动在一些DPT上硬. 除了没有"好的"方式来处理这个问题, 吸入超过50毫克完全搞了我的鼻子几天之后,不能吸烟,因为它是HCL和我不能做一个自由基础,因为我只有100毫克剩下. 口服是个浪费,除非你有一些菜种子, 我最近为此目的而获得的口服DPT-hausca将是去除一个小问题 我希望在五个小时后睡觉, 而在一个小时前, 这种方法需要10个小时, 足以让我睡觉. 解释这一切的目的是为了表明我没有选择, 我开始设置我的旅行区 (音乐,毯子,我的两个美丽的狗). 我去所试图和狗. 我把20毫升的水放进一杯, 我用一个塑料测量注射器吸取了DPT水, 躺在床上, 我躺在那里几分钟,所以它可以浸泡,想自己'你在干什么? 塞研究化学品到你的股,你该死的疯狂"这不是一个美好的记忆,但它不是那么糟糕. 我的逻辑大脑知道医生已经这样做了几十年, 我坐在舒适的座位上等待效果. 现在我已经有了一个圆, 在我完成它 (30秒) 之前, 我已经疯狂地了. 五分钟之内, 我已经达到了顶峰, 波浪的高潮快乐波浪穿过我的身体间歇性地当我来了但在高峰期间我甚至不知道我有一个身体. 如此令人叹为观止, 如此不同于我尝试过的任何其他迷幻药物, 如此立体和沉浸式, 我能记住的许多幻象中, 却有成千上万的我无法记住的; 墙上有着你从未见过的最详细的纹理, 我意识到这是我的自我试图制我的灵魂在我的身体, 我把我的手臂开,并打开我的灵魂墙. 墙面变成一个巨大的二维平面, 我开始将手臂移动进去, 墙壁被移除,我可以看到一个无限的移动美丽的颜色我见过. 我变成了一个女灵, 漂流到一个黑暗的房间里, 我安慰自己说:"没事,没事,别太担心. 我没有感到痛苦, 我相信我正在纠正它. 后来我也从上帝手中收到了最伟大的智慧书, 与数以百万计的人,上帝,书和一切同时成为一体. 我无法想象一个更精神强烈的经历 (但可能没有,我无法想象). 现在我可以在现实世界中运行. 我走到房子前面,看着街灯, 灯光的光环几乎完全阻碍了我的视力, 我可以在几条街道外追赶一辆高速警察, 我似乎能够准确地说出所有车辆的位置, 两个多小时, 我在房子里漫步, 看着一切都变成了蛇或美洲虎或其他外来动物, 我猜这可能是哈马拉的影响, 也许是因为我在追求阿雅华斯卡风格的体验, 我还没有尝试过亚华斯卡, 现在我只能轻微倒, 在床上躺了几分钟后, 没有任何化学援助就睡着了. 我很难处理给药的方法, 只有全身高潮, 旅行是荒谬的强烈, 没有鼻子烧伤或讨厌的滴滴, 我鼓励其他冒险者尝试这种组合. 现在, 这是不可思议的.

Feb 11, 2002. erowid.org/exp/7827 DOSE: T+ 0:00 1 tsp oral Syrian Rue (ground / crushed) T+ 0:45 100 mg rectal DPT (liquid) T+ 1:05 smoked Cannabis (plant material) BODY WEIGHT: 80 kg One night I got an urge, the urge to trip hard on some DPT. All of my previous experiments with DPT had gone quite well, except for the problem of having no 'good' way take it. Snorting more then 50mg completely fucks my nose for days afterward, can't smoke it cause it's the HCL and I couldn't do a freebasing because I only had 100mg left. Orally is a waste, unless you've got some rue seeds, which I recently obtained for this purpose and oral DPT-hausca would have been the way to go except for one little problem... I wanted to go to sleep in about five hours and I'd had a medium sized meal about an hour ago. That method would had taken 10 hours wear off enough for me to sleep. Now the point of explaining all this is to show that I didn't have a choice, I had to do the unthinkable... 11pm - I crushed up and swallowed a teaspoon of rue seeds and then began to setup my tripping area (music, blankets, my two gorgeous dogs). I go to the toilet to try and to shit. 11:45pm - I put about 20ml of water into a shot glass and stir in 100mg of DPT, about 50% dissolves. I sucked the DPT-water up with a plastic measuring syringe, laid down on the bed and then squirted up me bum. I laid there for a couple of minutes so it could soak in, thinking to myself 'what the fuck are you doing? Stuffing research chemicals up your arse, your fucking crazy' it's not a nice memory but it's not that bad really. The logical part of my brain knows that docs have been doing this for decades and it's actually a very effective method. 12pm - I sit in my comfortable seat and await the effects. 12:05pm - Nothing yet so I have a cone and by the time I've finished it (30 seconds) I am tripping madly. I am overwhelmed as in five minutes I have reached the peak which is ridiculously intense, I mean FUCK! Waves of orgasmic pleasure surge through my body intermittently while I was coming up but during the peak I didn't even know I had a body. I was in a hyperspace trance for something like 45minutes, so many visions, so breath-taking, so unlike any other psychedelic I've tried, so 3 dimensional and immersive, an unbelievable amount of content... fucking absolutely amazing. Of the many visions I can remember, there were 1000's I can't; I was confronted with giant walls all around me, all moving in as if to crush me. There was the most detailed texture you'll ever see on the walls, it was millions of images of my own head, all their lips moved very quick but I couldn't hear them. I realised that this was my ego trying to contain my spirit inside my body and that I needed to embrace the wall without fear and brake through it - it was the only way, it was a test. I throw my arms out wide and opened my soul to the wall. The wall flattered out to a huge single 2D plain, I began to move my arms in, slowly removing the skin between me and infinity as I do so. The walls are removed and I can see an infinity of the move beautiful colors I've ever seen. I became a female spirit and drifted to a dark room where my body was tried up in a chair and it's head was in a mad fit (sorta like in Jacobs ladder). I comforted my struggling psyche 'It's ok, everything's ok, don't worry so much'. At no time was it scary, I wasn't feeling my pain, I was confident I was correcting it. Later I also received the ultimate book of wisdom from the hand of god and became one with millions of people and god and the book and everything at the same time. I can't imagine a more spiritually toned or intense experience (but there not probably is, I just can't imagine it). 1pm - I am slowly descending from the peak now and can function in the physical world. I walk out to the front of my house and look at the street lights, WOW! The aura from the lights almost completely obstructs my vision like super thick fog. I can here a high-speed police chase a couple of streets away, I seem to be able to tell EXACTLY where all the cars are even though that's more then a mile away, it's so quiet except for those cars, I decide that is why and go inside because it's pretty cold out there. Over 2 hours I wonder around the house looking at everything turn into snakes or jaguars or other exotic animals that may or may not exist something. I guess that's the harmala influence but maybe it's just because I was exacting a ayahuasca-style experience and it was, minus the bodyload you hear about from ayahuasca but then again. I haven't tried ayahuasca yet and therefore can't properly compare it to this experience. 4:30pm - I'm only tripping lightly now and end up falling asleep without any chemical assistance after only a few minutes laying in bed. It was difficult for me to deal with the method of administering the dose but in the end I'm very glad I did it that way. The only bodyload was an all body orgasm, the trip was ridiculously intense, no nose burn or nasty drip and I was able to sleep only 5 hours after dosing. Great stuff that DPT but be ready, it took a lot of mental effort and strength to go with the flow and not freak out... approach with caution but I would encourage other adventures to try this combo. It's incredible.

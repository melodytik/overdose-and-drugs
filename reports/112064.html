这份报告将会很快, 因为我认为这种物质是激励剂的底部. 我已经吃了两个小时了. 开始出现. 我很,很快就会变得不安. 现在我已经走了很长的路. 仍然是真正的粘性. 这种能量基本上迫使我移动. 我有工作要做,所以我必须满足于我的腿摇超快. 我突然感到极度焦虑. 无论如何我都有一般的焦虑, 非常紧张,有点偏执. 焦虑已经到了我不能再专注于工作的地步. 我把它放下,试着用YouTube视频来放松. 我很快地喝了一瓶水, 希望能减轻焦虑. 如果有什么, 担心和偏执已经得到稍微更糟. 我决定开车, 希望这能让我平静下来. 我相信你已经听过很多次, 无论你是吸着迷幻药,因为视觉问题而看不见,还是吸着兴奋剂而认为自己是世界上最好的司机, 没有人知道. 焦虑情绪越来越严重. 另外,我不能专注. 你会认为一种类药物会有所帮助, 我无法摆脱震动和偏执. 现在我正处于恐慌发作的边缘. 幸运的是,我大部分时间都带着一些埃蒂索拉姆, (再次,一个糟糕的想法. 吸取我的愚蠢经验) 只是吞下药似乎有点安慰剂效应. 我转身开车回家. 我已经回家了. 现在我已经离开公路了. 艾提索拉姆已经开始起作用. 现在我可以控制焦虑, 我还在摇摇脚,不管怎样. 即使用了埃蒂索拉姆, 我似乎仍然有一些外周刺激. 虽然我没有特别想到什么. 幸运的是,这足以让我昏倒. 我第二天早上醒来时感觉很好, 总的来说,我会说这药物是可怕的. 我有大约一个小时的体面专注, 但这是积极的结束. 只有它能让我在高潮期间感到焦虑, 如果我没有服用一些Etizolam我几乎肯定会有恐慌发作. 这种药物基本上是3,4-CTMP (又称无用), 我会更好地把半克咖啡因. 4F-MPH的情况更糟. 时间表: 112064 发布时间:2018年7月2日

Jul 2, 2018. erowid.org/exp/112064 DOSE: T+ 0:00 13 mg oral 4-Fluoromethylphenidate (capsule) T+ 3:15 2 mg oral Etizolam (pill / tablet) T+ 9:00 1 mg oral Etizolam (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 150 lb This report is going to be a quick one as I think this substance is a bottom of the barrel stimulant. T+0:00 – Eat ~13mg I put in a gel capsule. I haven’t eaten in about 2 hours. T+0:35 – Starting to come up. I’m very clammy and quickly becoming restless. T+1:00 – I’d say I’m all the way up at this point. Still real clammy. The energy is basically forcing me to move around. I have work to do though, so I’m going to have to be content with shaking my leg super-fast. T+1:30 – I’m extremely anxious all of a sudden. I have general anxiety anyways, but this is clearly the work of the 4F-MPH. Very on edge with a bit of paranoia. T+2:00 – The anxiety has gotten to the point where I can’t really focus on my work anymore. I put it away and try to relax with a YouTube video. I quickly chug an entire water bottle hoping that it may take the edge off. T+3:00 – No dice. If anything, the worrying and paranoia has gotten slightly worse. I decide to go for a drive in hopes that maybe it will calm me down. (NOTE: I’m sure you’ve heard it a million times before but don’t drive high. Whether you’re on a psychedelic and can’t see because of the visuals or on a stimulant and think you’re the best driver in the world it isn’t fair to others. Don’t be stupid.) T+3:10 – The anxiety just keeps getting worse. Plus, I can’t focus for shit. You would think that a drug in the -phenidate family would help with that but I can’t get past the shaking and paranoia. I can’t get past the shaking and paranoia. T+3:15 – I’m on the verge of having a panic attack. Thankfully I keep some etizolam on me most of the time, so I take 2mg. (Once again, a bad idea. Learn from my stupidity.) Just swallowing the pill seemed to have a bit of a placebo effect. I turn around and start the drive home. T+3:30 – I’m home. Still super anxious, but I feel a bit better now that I’m off the road. T+3:50 – The etizolam has kicked in. The anxiety is manageable now and I’m not clammy any more. I’m still shaking my leg a bit but that’s whatever. T+7:00 – Even with the etizolam I still seem to have quite a bit of peripheral stimulation. My thoughts are still racing a bit as well, although not about anything in particular. T+9:00 – I take another 1mg of etizolam and get in bed. Thankfully it’s enough to knock me out. I wake up the next morning feeling fine, if a bit hazy from the benzos. So overall, I would say this drug is awful. I got about an hour of decent focus but that’s where the positives end. It’s the only stimulant to give me anxiety during the high, and it gave it to me in droves. If I hadn’t taken some etizolam I would almost certainly have had a panic attack. This drug is essentially 3,4-CTMP (aka useless) but with the added benefit of thinking that I was going to die the entire time I was on it. I’d be better off railing half a gram of caffeine. 4F-MPH is worse.

自从我五岁起就开始喝咖啡和茶. 爸爸给我一些咖啡, 但用牛奶一点. 我想要喝茶,因为我的父母喝了很多茶,所以他们也给了我茶. 我每天早上都喝咖啡, 我还会偷偷走进厨房, 我每天喝几杯咖啡和几杯茶. 我每天至少喝6杯咖啡, 再加上茶和咖啡因含有苏打水, 我每天喝大约两到五杯咖啡和五到八杯茶. 我有时会喝含有瓜拉纳和人参的苏打水, 大多数时候,如果你来到我的办公室, 我会一直在桌子上喝咖啡和茶, 我会交替喝这两种饮料, 这听起来很极端, 这是一种微妙的成, 开始缓慢, 我慢慢增加了咖啡因的摄入量. 为了满足咖啡因需求, 我不再喝过咖啡. 导致头痛,紧张,失眠 (我只睡3个小时左右, 如果睡着了就会每隔0点半到1点醒来) 和攻击性行为. 偏头痛药在此时没有效果, 因为它也含有咖啡因. 我完全理解我的成, 当我试图戒掉咖啡因的所有来源冷火. 我经历了我一生中最严重的戒断. 现在我偶尔抽烟, 却不觉得上或戒烟. 但咖啡因确实给我带来了严重的戒断:头痛,紧张,情绪不佳,攻击性行为,缺乏集中力,恶心,吐,焦虑,无法控制的肌肉运动, 五天后我再也忍不住了, 早上又喝了一杯咖啡, 现在我只喝了一两杯咖啡和几杯茶. 茶对我来说比咖啡更容易上 没有. 无论如何,这是一个警告,小心. 其他: 咖啡因 (11),咖啡 (173):一般 (1),回顾 / 总结 (11),成和习惯性 (10),各种 (28)

Dec 14, 2016. erowid.org/exp/72146 DOSE: oral Coffee (daily) oral Tea (daily) BODY WEIGHT: 125 lb I've been drinking coffee and tea since the age of 5 years old. Before going to elementary school, my dad would give me part of his espresso coffee, but strongly diluted with milk. I would want to drink tea, as my parent drank a lot of tea, so they gave me some tea also. Ever since, I drink coffee every morning and several cups of tea day. I would also sneak in the kitchen and make myself cups of instant coffee around the age of 7. When I hit high school, I got to several cups of coffee a day and several cups of tea. But when I hit college I was drinking about 6 cups of coffee minimum per day, with tea and caffeine sodas on top of that, but my college years, I drank mostly coffee. A couple month ago, I was drinking about 2-5 cups of coffee and up to 5 to 8 cups of tea a day. Sometime I drink sodas with guarana and ginseng, tea made with ginseng and guarana or added some gingseng and guarana to my teas and sometime coffee. Most of the time if you came in my work office, I would have a coffee and a tea at all time on my table and I would drink the two alternating them, this sounds extreme and it is, but I didn't noticed at the time, that was just the way I was for several years and stayed focus at work. This is a subtile addiction, it started slowly and I slowly raised the amount of caffeine I drink. At one point, I was drinking a full cup of espresso ( about 4 espressos in one cup ) just to feed my caffeine need, I wasn't drinking filter coffee anymore. Up to recently, I hit a point when I was always on caffeine intoxication ( overdose ), which creates hard migraines, nervousness, insomnia ( I only slept something like 3 hours and would wake up every 0h30 -1h00 if I fell asleep ) and aggressive behaviors. Migraine medication doesn't work at this point, since it contains caffeine also. I totally understood my addiction when I try to quit caffeine of all source cold turkey. I had the most severe withdrawal of my life. I quit cigarettes several times for long periods of times and now I smoke on occasion without feeling addicted to it or withdrawal. But caffeine did give me severe withdrawals; headache, nervousness, bad mood, aggressive behaviors, lack of concentration, nausea, puking, anxiety, un-controlled muscle movement, running around in circles looking for something I don't have a clue what I am looking for ( my brain just wants caffeine ) and just yelling at people for no reason. After 5 days I couldn't take it, I got back to my cup of coffee in the morning, now I'm trying to cut down, but I don't think I'll ever quit my sweet caffeine completely. Right now I got down to 1 to 2 cups of coffee and several cups of tea. The tea somehow is more addictive to me then coffee . Anyways, this is a warning, watch out.

让我从一开始开始. 警告说这将是一个长... 我是个吸毒者多年来, 我已经做了一切,大麻,胺,海洛因,可口可乐,,,,,安普,酸,2c-b,狂喜,安比恩,月光,刺,你把它. 我甚至做过一些罕见的快乐药液, 我没有遇到过一个处方我没有比3千美元的妓女更强烈地操了. 我是个酒鬼, 过去两年, 我从海洛因戒断后的几个月里, 吸了可卡因, 胺, 吸了几把药, 补充了氧康丁, 由于不断的骚扰或是因为他们开始偷我的钱, 我选择了酒精作为一种合法的, 我爱上了她. 酒精是合法的,每个人都这样做,你可以在公共场合喝酒,你没有经销商, 一瓶优质伏特加永远不会超过35美元, 快速推进16个月 偶尔会有和瓦,或偶尔的狂喜药片, 然后,我摔了膝盖. 不仅仅是一个破裂,我把它分成两半,撕裂了带, 一个正常的人会放弃酒... 我开始吸食阿片类药物, 每天喝到1/4/750毫升/五份的强烈酒, 医生断绝了我, 开始依赖酒精. 我每天喝酒的几个月后, 我开始昏迷, 肆虐, 破坏财产, 我的神经受到了打击,我几乎看不见,我的酸反流是如此糟糕当我闻到伏特加我的胃爆发像纳帕姆. 所有都是没有希望的. 我再次被判处沉迷生活. 那些愉快的日子已经过去了, 朋友们也走了, 我决定戒酒,但由于我很危险地依赖酒精, 我的梦想是,仍然是享受醉酒狂欢没有该死的副作用... 我去看了医生,开始戒毒. 我离开佐治亚州前往新泽西州, 我的症状都消失了, 我的朋友Librium正在帮助我. 然而,当我停止了排毒,我的Librium消失了,恐慌发作几乎每天. ,还有失眠. 医生拒绝给我睡眠药或恐慌药, 他想让我尝试呼吸. 医生们并不是瓜, 他们知道最轻微到中度的疼痛,焦虑和失眠都可以用安慰剂或心灵转移物质治愈. 他们知道有药物可以治疗这种疾病, 如果能得到药物, 他们会忍受痛苦或转向其他药物, 所以,我没有浪费时间在打一瓶灰快于你可以说'该死的30美元. 四个月后, 像排毒前一样深入. 我解雇了我的医生. 我还记得我作为一个有抱负的子时代的瓦和克萨克斯, 我也知道在网上可以找到它们, 我不得不选择一个或另一个, 我知道克萨克斯更强,所以就去买了100粒1毫克的药. 我常常喝酒, 只是我不喜欢喝酒. 每当我试图一个体面的声, 我要么昏或被搞和困惑. 它杀死了我的恐慌发作, 和几个马提尼使安比恩感觉像阿司匹林. 尽管有缺点, 饮酒对我来说更令人愉快. 我开始喝酒, 一个月后, 我决定要休息一下, 我喝了40毫克, 在梦境中. 我很放松,充满活力和快乐. 我没有想吸毒或喝酒, 睡得像婴儿一样, 这是一种高兴感,与Xanax不同,而且更柔和. 虽然不那么强大, 但这是件好事. 我正在享受一个清醒的早晨. 我可以看到, 我的反流已经消失了, 我感到放松, 不担心, 因为我有母亲的小助手和我在一起. 时间表: 48759 药品 - 迪亚泽巴姆 (115),药品 - 阿尔普拉佐拉姆 (98), 酒精 (61):回顾 / 摘要 (11), 保健益处 (32), 和习惯性 (10), 不适用 (38)

Warning this is going to be long... I've been a drug user for years, constantly teetering between depression and addiction. I've done everything, pot, ketamine, heroin, coke, absinthe, benzo, meth, amps, acid, 2c-b, ecstasy, ambien, lunesta, barbs you name it. I've even done rare happy potions like mescaline or talwin. I have not met a prescription I haven’t fucked harder then a 3 grand prostitute. I've been an alcoholic for the past two years, following a heavy heroin addiction. The months after my withdrawal from heroin saw me shooting cocaine, ketamine, popping handfuls of pills, supplementing with oxycontin, and generally doing anything and everything to stay high. I eventually ran out of money, and lost my drug contacts through either constant badgering or because they started to rip me off. I settled with alcohol as a legal and readily available choice for a high. I fell in love. Alcohol was legal, everyone did it, you could drink in public, you had no dealer, and a bottle of premium vodka is never over 35 bucks, that beats a daily coke/heroin habit by far. Fast forward 16 months. Other then the occasional blow and valium combo, or the occasional ecstasy tablet, I'd pretty much been drug free, just boozing my way to victory. Then, I broke my knee. Not just a break, I split it in half, tore a ligament, and to top it off, I was drunk when it happened. A normal person would have given up the hooch... I start binging on opiates and was up to a quart/750ml/fifth a day of hard liquor, 4-6 mg ov dilaudid iv'd, 10-50 mg of percocet orally and I was so fucked up I was little more then a pile of fat on an easy chair. The doc cut me off, and started to rely on just booze. After a few more months of daily drinking, I started to hallucinate if I didn’t get my fix. I started to black out, go on rampages, destroying property, beating the shit out of anyone in my way, and acting like a complete moron. My nerves were shot, I could barely see, and my acid reflux was so bad when I smelled vodka my stomach erupted like napalm. All was hopeless. I was damned to a life of addiction, once more. The fun days were gone, the friends were gone, and I was alone, in complete addiction. I decided to quit, but since I was DANGEROUSLY dependant on alcohol, I had to find something to get off the hooch. My dream was and still is to enjoy drunken binges without the fucking side effects... I saw a doctor, and started a detox. I left my state of Georgia for new jersey to visit my girlfriend, and detoxed for 17 days. My symptoms were all gone, and my friend Librium was helping me out. However, when I stopped the detox, and my Librium went out, panic attacks became almost daily. Oh, and insomnia. My doc refused me sleep meds or panic atacks meds for fear of addiction. He wanted me to try 'breathing'. Docters aren't stupid, they know that most mild to moderate pain, anxiety and sleeplessness can be cured with placebo or mind-over matter. Former Drug abusers/users refuse mind over matter, knowing full well there are meds for it, and know if they can get them, they'll suffer or retreat to other drugs until they get them. So, I wasted no time in slamming a bottle of grey goose quicker then you can say 'fuck the 30 bucks'. And, 4 months later was in just as deep as before the detox. I fired my doctor. I remembered valium and remembered xanax from my days as an aspiring gonzo, and knew that either could be used for insomnia, alcohol withdrawal and anxiety. I also knew that they could be found online, if you know where to look, and trust me, I did. I had to pick one or the other, and could find little comparison on the two online. I knew xanax was stronger, so just went for it and ordered 100 1 mg pills. It worked for a few days but had no euphoria, so often I'd drink instead. And whenever I tried to cop a decent buzz, I'd either black out or be to fucked up and confused. It killed my panic attacks, and a few with a martinis made ambien feel like aspirin. However, it didn't supplement my drinking because drinking was more pleasurable, despite it's downsides. After my supply ran out, I resumed drinking even more. A month later, I decided I had to take a break from the hooch, so I decided to try valium this time, and finally, it worked. I popped 40 mg, and was in dream land. I was relaxed, buzzed and happy. I had no desire for drugs or alcohol, slept like a baby and was finally cured for the night. It is euphoric unlike xanax, and much smoother. Not as strong, but that's a GOOD thing. I'm enjoying a sober morning. I can see, my reflux is gone and I feel relaxed and not worried because I have mother's little helper with me.

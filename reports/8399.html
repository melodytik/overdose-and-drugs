你必须使用面包师的巧克力和可可. 吃一磅的雪茄会让你吐,感觉很糟糕. 如果您正确地使用, 并且使用过多的巧克力, 这不适合热量意识或非巧克力爱好者. 这是12司的最强烈的可可你将有史以来品尝. 这是一个烟雾,黑暗和苦甜的. 这里是如何做到这一点: 让自己一些热牛奶 - 约8司. 让它煮沸后, 停下来, 加入足够多的可可粉, 形成深色, 丰富的, 红棕色. 现在用糖和溶液. 接下来,加入大约2司的奶油. 这应该让颜色变得更浅. 添加可可,直到它再次变暗. 现在加热,使它非常热 - 不要让它再次沸. 现在,把大约2司的面包师巧克力和你想要的糖混合进去. 这应该会产生非常深的棕色,有点,甜的混乱. 慢慢地喝下, 半小时或一个小时的时间里, 别急着做. 我认为吃过多的巧克力会让人感到恶心, 我通常在30分钟后开始感到放松,舒适和快乐. 这种感觉会在接下来的一个小时内积累起来, 我不确定这是因为巧克力中的化物, 不管它是什么,我喜欢它. 坐下来放松心情,阅读报纸,和朋友聊天,打电邮,这都是很好的时机. 这不是令人惊叹的自然高潮, 这只是一种美好感觉, 来自一些有味道和有趣的东西. 快乐的可可制作. 其他: 巧克力 (182):一个人 (16),一般 (1)

Psychoactive Cocoa Chocolate by Sids Citation:   Sids. "Psychoactive Cocoa: An Experience with Chocolate (exp8399)". Erowid.org . Jul 18, 2002. erowid.org/exp/8399 DOSE: 12 oz oral Chocolate BODY WEIGHT: 165 lb Well, most of you are going to laugh at this, but it is all true - Psychoactive chocolate drinks? You have to use bakers chocolate and cocoa. Eating a pound of Hershey's will make you puke and feel shitty. However - Hershey's COCOA POWDER and BAKING CHOCOLATE can give you a langourous sense of relaxation and well-being if you do it right, and in a rather excessive quantity. This is not for the calorie conscious or the non-lover of chocolate. This is 12oz of the strongest cocoa you will ever taste. It is smokey, dark and bittersweet. Here's how to do it: Make yourself some hot milk - about 8 oz. After you bring it to a boil, stop, and stir in enough cocoa powder to create a deep, rich, reddish brown color. Now saturate the solution with sugar. Next, add about 2 oz of cream. This should make the color much lighter. Add cocoa until it gets darker again. Now, heat it so that it is quite hot - don't let it boil again. Now, stir in about 2oz of bakers chocolate and as much sugar as you want. This should make a very dark brown, somewhat soupy, sweet mess. Drink it slowly - sip it over the course of a half-hour, maybe an hour. Don't rush it. This prevents the unpleasant, sweaty feeling of nausea that I associate with eating 'too much' chocolate (as if there is a such thing!). I usually begin to feel relaxed and comfortable and happy after about 30 minutes. This feeling builds over the next hour and then slowly fades. I'm not sure if this is because of the alkaloids in the chocolate, or if it is due to the MASSIVE endorphin release that is supposedly happening when you eat something sweet and tasty. Whatever it is, I enjoy it. It is a good time to just sit down and chill out, read the paper, talk to friends, type a friendly e-mail. It isn't a mind-blowing natural high, and it is not an entheogen by and stretch. It's just a good feeling that comes from something palatable and fun to make. Happy Cocoa-making.

4乙胺 (粉末/晶体) 一个 烟草 - 烟草 在当时服用的物质:4-CEC,少量酒精 (1/3小香杯) 其他物质:过去一年每天服用900毫克的德帕科特 (双极性障碍) 我是空腹的,最后一顿饭是大约5个小时前给药. 背景: 这一天开始像往常一样. 在学校度过大部分时间, 没有太多的工作, 总体来说, 这一天真的很轻松. 我在晚上10点左右从生日聚会回家, 我回忆起我最近从一个合法的RC化学品供应商那里买了一包含有几种不同的化学品. 由于没有人在家, 网上的所有信息都表明效果很短, 在包装中我有0.2g的4-CEC. 因为我不知道这种化学物质究竟是做什么, 因为它是包装中的奖金, 除了大麻和酒精之外,我对任何药物没有任何经验. 我决定在网上查看一些论坛. 用户报告说,这些影响是可以忽略不计的,他们中的大多数人服用了100-200毫克的剂量. 我决定服用比大多数人报告服用的剂量更低的剂量, 因此我把200毫克分成三堆, 按一下66毫克的重量,做出一条细细的线, 一次性地嗅到它. 我认为它是相当光滑的,它没有燃烧,因为每个人都报告它燃烧 22:11 - 我的鼻子,眼皮后面的某个地方,以及整个脸的右侧都感到令人烦但可以忍受的疼痛. 我会把它描述为一个很大的压力和轻微的烧伤. 没有什么太难. 虽然我现在还没有意识到这一点, 我却经历了轻微的刺激. 我在房子里走来走去, 试图摆脱烦人的痛苦. 我还记得有用户说粉末通过鼻子进入喉时有可怕的味道, 22:20 效果已经达到顶峰, 燃烧已经消失了. 我能感觉到粉末准备排泄我的鼻子和进入我的喉. 背后可以感觉到一种强烈的苦味, 但回头看, 22:22 - - 我口中有一口最苦的东西. 这与我吃过的任何东西都无法比拟 (我小时候也曾尝试自己的狗) 或想象. 我试着喝可乐, 但很快意识到这不会有帮助. 因为如果它在我的鼻子里烧得这么厉害, 味道也这么糟糕, 我不得不说吐的需求不是很大, 如果我试着,我可能会忍受. 22:25-6 我吐完就马上冲去洗嘴. 这就是峰值的时间. 我感觉处于异界的水平 (请注意, 我没有与其他刺激物质的比较, 我做的一切都非常缓慢, 并且比平常更加小心. 这绝对是我刷牙最愉快的时刻. 我开始吐出牙膏时, 我觉得我的脸跟着牙膏伸展. 我觉得这很有趣, 但不是以笑/愚蠢的方式, 我在刷牙的时候, 擦脸, 打电话. 现在,我迫不及待地想和别人说话. 我看着键盘, 通常是白色的, 我注意到颜色跳动和变化, 我最惊的是我对此的看法完全正常. 看到颜色变化, 我简单地在脑海中把它作为一种效应, 我对时间的感知非常奇怪. 时间似乎过得很快, 但同时它也过得很慢 (一秒钟感觉像一个小时, 但是一个非常快速和快速的小时, 如果这有任何意义) 22:30-35 - - 现在的高峰期已经结束. 我放上我最喜欢的说唱歌手的唱片. 音乐听起来不错, 但不像在大麻上听起来那么精神错乱. 听起来更清晰, 但在某种程度上, 我真的想和人们聊天, 但是我记得有人在报道关于兴奋剂的愚蠢报道, 如果真的有显著的效果, 我觉得我不能不大声指出. 幸运的是我有自己的房子 (或者不,如果我有话的人我可能不会和自己说话). 我也跟着音乐跳舞, 我确实受到刺激. 22:40 - 几乎完全结束. 由于我感觉到它正在消失, 我决定用最后的高分来卸载洗碗机. 我做得非常快 (没有菜破碎, 协调也没有受到影响), 实际上,不管怎么说,我一直在对自己微笑,我心情很好. 22:48 - - 实际上已经结束了. 我抽了一支香烟. 现在心情还不错. 心理上需要/渴望重新服用. 如果我不小心, 我可能会沉迷于它, 我可能会在那时入睡, 这是一个很大的优点, 00:40 - 奇怪的感觉,好像高回来,可能是因为滴水已经通过胃再吸收 晚上好 总的来说,这是一个令人惊叹的经历. 我可以想象自己和几个朋友一起再试一次, 我们都在喝,聊天,玩, 我几乎不可能在聚会上服用它, 尤其是因为它需要如此多的重剂量. 我不喜欢被人们称为'那个君子'. 对于负面的副作用, 我不得不说没有除了红眼,可怕的滴水和烧伤. 我很惊地发现没有血管收缩的迹象, 也没有动, 其他: 4-乙 (734):单独 (16),第一次 (2)

Apr 25, 2016. erowid.org/exp/108163 DOSE: T+ 0:00 900 mg oral Pharms - Divalproex (daily) T+ 0:00 66 mg insufflated 4-Chloroethcathinone (powder / crystals) T+ 0:38 1 smoked Tobacco - Cigarettes BODY WEIGHT: 60 kg Substance taken at the time: 4-CEC, negligible amount of alcohol (1/3 of a small champagne glass) Other substances: 900mg of depakote daily for the past year (Bipolar disorder) I was on an empty stomach, last meal was about 5 hours before dosing. Background: The day started out as usual. Spent most of it at school, didnt have much work and wasnt especially tired. After coming back home I had to get ready for a short meet-up for a friends birthday, and overall had a really relaxed day. I was back home from the birthday at about 10 pm, in a honestly shit mood as the party sucked but at the same time it was a good friend and I didnt want to leave early. When I came inside I reminded myself I recently picked up a package containing a few different chemicals from a legal rc chem vendor. Since no one was home and all information online suggested the effects are rather short, I decided to give it a go. In the package I had .2 g of 4-CEC. As I had no clue what this chemical does exactly, because it came as a bonus in the package, and because I have zero experience with any drugs aside from weed and alcohol I have zero experience with any drugs aside from weed and alcohol , I decided to check it out online on a few forums. Users reported that the effects were negligible to none, and most of them had taken doses in the range of 100-200 mg. As I'm usually cautious when it comes to messing with drugs, I decided to take a dose lower than what most people reported taking, so I split the 200 mg into three piles, put one of the piles on a weight and weighed about 66mg out. 22:10 - weigh out the 66 mg, made a nice thin line and sniffed it all in one go. I thought it was pretty smooth, it didnt burn as everyone else reported it burning 22:11 - My nose, somewhere behind my eyelids and the whole right side of my face are in annoying but bearable pain. I'd describe it as a big pressure with mild burning. Nothing too hard. 22:13 effects are slowly coming on, my senses slowly sharpen and even though I'm at this point unaware of it, I was experiencing slight stimulation. I was walking around the house emptyminded, trying to 'walk off' the annoying pain. I also remembered users reporting an awful taste when the powder goes through the nose into the throat so to prepare I poured myself some coke and went to my room, and set some music on. 22:20 Effects are halfway to the peak, burning is gone. I can feel the powder getting ready to evacuate my nose and enter my throat. A strong bitter taste can be sensed in the back, but looking back I had no idea what was going to come next 22:22-3 huge gulp of the most bitter thing I have ever tasted enters my mouth. It's seriously incomparable to anything I've ever eaten ( and I've tried licking my own shit as a kid) or imagined. I try to drink coke but I quickly realize that's not going to help. I decided I'll just puke and spit out the powder so it doesnt go to my stomach (or at least not in its entirety) because if it burned that bad in my nose and tasted that awful it has to be in some way even worse for the stomach. I have to say that the need to puke wasnt massive, I was able to take my scarf and hoodie off before. I probably couldve held it in if I tried. 22:25-6 I finish puking and immedietly rush to wash my mouth. This is when the peak set in. My senses were on otherworldly levels (note that I have no comparison with other stimulating substances, my only comparison is with weed and alcohol both of which dull the senses in comparison with this) while washing my teeth I was never that gentle and focused. I was doing everything pretty slowly, and with much more care than usual. It was by far the most enjoyable time I've washed my teeth. When I began to spit out the toothpaste, I felt as if my face was following and stretching out with the toothpaste. My face as a whole felt as if it were warping, which I found funny, but not in a giggly/stupid way as I would find any perception change on weed. As I finished washing my teeth, I wiped my face and went on my phone. The urge to talk to someone was unbearable at this point. As I looked at the keyboard, which is normally predominantly white, I noticed colours pulsating and changing, mostly into reds, pinks, purples and at one point green. What surprised me most was that I was completly normal in the way I perceived this. My mind was in a really analytical mode, much more than usual, and as I saw the colour changes I simply noted it off in my brain as one of the effects without paying much attention to it. And on the last note, my perception of time was really weird. Time seemed to go by really fast, but at the same time it was going real slow (a second felt like an hour but a reaaaaly speedy and quick hour if that makes any sense) 22:30-35 - the peak is over by now. I put on a vinyl of my favorite rapper on. Music sounds good, but not psychodelic like it does on weed. It sounds much clearer, but in a way it is fuzzed out (I couldnt pay attention to the lyrics much). I really wanted to talk to people, but I remembered people report writing stupid shit on stimulants, so instead I put my phone out and had small monologs in my head about the high, noting every aspect, thinking what the effects on brain chemistry might be etc etc. If there was a really prominent effect I felt I couldnt help but note it out out loud. Thank god I had the house to myself (or not, if I had somone to talk to I probably wouldnt talk to myself). At the same time I was dancing a bit to the music, but the essential motivation behind that was just to move. I was definetly stimulated. 22:40 - almost completly over. Since I sensed it dissapearing I decided to use the last bits of the high to unload the dishwasher. I did so suprisingly quick (and no dishes broken ergo coordination was not impaired) and was in a really positive mood. Actually, scratch that, I was constantly grinning to myself, I was in an amazing mood. 22:48- effects practically over. Smoked a cigarette. Still in a great mood. Big mental need/craving for a redose. I could see myself getting addicted to it if I wasnt cautious, since it lasts so short ( 30 minutes) but is so fun, and most importantly legal. 23:00- back to normal. I couldve probably fallen asleep then, which is a great plus as many other stimulants cause insomnia. 00:40 - strange feeling, as if the high was coming back on, may be because the drip has been reabsorbed through the stomach 1:00- goodnight Overall I have to say it was an amazing experience. I could see myself trying this again with a couple of friends over with us all taking it, talking and just goofing around, but it has zero party-drug potential for me. The short duration, Ungodly drip, and irritating burn make it almost impossible for me to take it in a party setting, especially because it would require so many redoses. Also the incredibly devilish bloodshot red eyes are a clear indicator of being high and I dont like to be known as 'that one junkie'. As for negative side effects, I have to say there were none aside from the red eyes., horrible drip and burn. I was surprised to see no vasconstriction signs, nor bruxism which I was expecting from reading trip reports of stimulant drugs.

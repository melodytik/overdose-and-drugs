其他: 我认为这些关于纳尔特的故事非常重要. 我的人生有点不同. 我已经服用了多年氧康丁和迪劳, 我每天服用150毫克Dilaudid (或相当量的氧) 持续了大约6个月. 我预订了一次去非洲的去除毒素之旅, 我没有其他选择, 只是试图快速排毒自己. 医生建议我可以在戒片5天后服用纳尔特, 药剂师说我先清洁10天. 我在周六早上8点醒来 (航班定于周二早上). 我立即服用了一种镇静剂 (克洛纳扎), 经过10分钟的时间, 我不能从沙发上移动. 我在客厅的沙发旁的地板上吐着. 我几乎无法转过头来确保吐不会落在我身上, 尽管如此,我几乎没有窒息. 我从来没有这样吐过. 只是不断地来来而去, 我无法呼吸. 我很确定自己会死于自己的吐. 一个朋友来找我, 发现我在沙发上吐了大约10分钟到1小时后. 他们设法帮助我上楼到卧室和洗手间. 我坐在所尽我所能, 排尿我的股洞大约15分钟然后坐了另一个小时反复做同样的事情. 我一直在经历所有其他典型的戒断症状! 我不知怎么的清理好了, 我认为我的朋友帮了忙, 他把一大桶吐放在床边, 问我是否需要去医院 - 我拒绝了. 他离开了. 我仍然在床上,在一个妄想,错过吐桶几次. 晚上6点左右, 我求有人来, 他们给了我一个晚上的正常剂量. 我设法让它保持低! 这足以让最糟糕的戒断痛苦遥无期. 半片药的效果持续了几天, 慢慢地减弱, 我错过了飞往非洲的航班. 我在一个月后清洁了, 走到大陆的另一边的灌木 (一间有热水浴和游泳池的小屋). 在纳尔特克松附近小心! 时间表: 72039

Jul 21, 2010. erowid.org/exp/72039 DOSE: T+ 0:00 Pharms - Clonazepam T+ 0:30 0.5 tablets oral Naltrexone (pill / tablet) T+ 10:00 150 mg Hydromorphone BODY WEIGHT: 210 lb I think that these stories about Naltrexone are very important. Mine is a little different. I had been taking oxycontin and dilaudid for years, but I had been struggling to get off them almost from the start. I had been taking either 150 mg Dilaudid (or the equivalent amount of oxy) daily for about 6 months solid. I had booked a trip to Africa to detox and I could not get a legitimate prescription for that amount to carry over the border. My supplier was running low, so I had no choice but to try to rapid detox myself. The doctor suggested that I could squeeze by taking the Naltrexone after being off opiates for 5 days, the pharmacist told me 10 days clean first. After ZERO days clean, I woke at 8 am Saturday morning (flight scheduled for Tuesday morning). I immediately took a tranquilizer (clonazapam) and after waiting 1/2 hour, I took 1/2 of a Naltrexone tablet. After 10 minutes the floor fell out of my existance. I could not move from the couch. I puked and puked actual projectile vomiting, right on the floor beside my couch in my living room. I barely was capable of turning my head to make sure the vomit did not land on me or choke me. Never-the-less, I almost did choke. I had never puked like that before. It just kept coming and coming and I could not get a breath in edgewise. I was pretty sure that I would die from choking on my own puke. A friend came over and an found me on the couch puking again about 10 minutes to 1 hour later. Somehow they managed to help me upstairs to the bedroom and washroom area. I sat on the toilet as best as I could and PEED out my arse-hole for about 15 minutes then sat for another hour itermitently doing the same thing. All the while I was experiencing all the other typical withdrawl symptoms TIMES 10! I somehow got cleaned up, I think my friend helped, and into the next room, which was the bedroom. He put a big puke bucket by the side of the bed, asked if I needed to get to the hospitol - I declined. He left. I remained in bed, in a delerium, missing the puke bucket a few times more. At about 6 pm, I begged someone to come over who had some dilaudid and they did. They gave me my regular dose for an evening. I managed to keep it down! It was enough to keep the worst of the withdrawl pain at bay. The 1/2 pill's effects lasted a couple of days; slowly tapering off and allowing the opiates to have more and more effect. I missed my flight to Africa. I got clean a month later by heading to the bush on the other side of the continent (a cabin with a jacuzzi and a swimming pool). Watch yourself around Naltrexone!

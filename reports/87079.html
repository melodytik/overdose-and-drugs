T+1小时 - 600mg 我是一个阿片类人. 我已经戒掉了阿片类药物8年了, 但却接受了心脏手术, 我发现自己处于阿片类药物戒断后急性戒断抑郁症+开放心脏抑郁症+自然倾向于抑郁. 在阅读后服用了1200毫克这种药物, 我发现它有点乱. 就像LSD一样. 心脏跳,感觉好像在我的喉里. 我对光线,噪音和人们过度敏感. 感觉头但紧张, 像在草我已经超越了许多月. 紧张使我表现得很温和, 任何我遇到的东西都太激烈了, 我失去我的皮肤, 电视上的新闻穿透了我. 快速而惰, 过度而悲伤, 情绪加剧, 这种药物让我无处不在,我无法放松. 我口中有一种奇怪的味道, 肚子感到空虚, 但食物感觉异常, 我很脆弱,就像一个全身露面的演员. 我在浴室里跳着我的iPod跳着一些轻松的舞蹈. 听音乐真的很好, 但我更喜欢模糊的歌词, 这种药物是刺激的, 没有镇静. 五小时后,我服用了5毫克的瓦 (我不能耐受), 我不需要戴任何面具, 能完全无拘无束, 我和家人在一起. 我不认为这有助于或阿片类药物戒断, 它确实可以完全治愈神经疼痛. 我仍然在手术切口周围感到灼热的疼痛, 除了这种药物之外, 作为一种反作用, 让我有点头痛. 药物的效果逐渐减弱, 睡在床上做了片般的梦想. 我喜欢被隔离,接地, 感觉没有疼痛和温暖的脊椎, 其他: 发布时间:2017年1月11日 药物 - - 加巴丁 (183): 单独 (16), 抑郁 (15), 困难经历 (5), 一般 (1)

Jan 11, 2017. erowid.org/exp/87079 DOSE: T+ 0:00 600 mg oral Pharms - Gabapentin T+ 1:00 600 mg oral Pharms - Gabapentin BODY WEIGHT: 130 lb Dosage: T+0 – 600mg; T+1hr - 600mg I am an opiate person. Have been off opiates for 8 years but had open heart surgery and got on oxy/hydros for 7 weeks. After withdrawals found myself in opiate post acute withdrawal depression + post open heart depression + a natural inclination to get depressed. Took 1200 mg of this drug after reading up on it and expecting a weak benzoish + antidepressant effect. Instead I find it trippy & dissociative. Like LSD. Heart pounds and feels like it’s in my throat. I’m oversensitive to light and noise and people. Feel lightheaded but nervous, like being on weed which I’ve outgrown many moons ago. The nervousness makes me behave mellow but I am anything but mellow inside. Everything I encounter is way too intense, I lose my skin, the news on the tv pierces through me. Speedy yet lethargic, hyper yet sad, emotions are heightened yet I’m numb and cotton woolly. This drug puts me all over the map and I can’t relax into it. With a weird taste in the mouth I’m thirsty yet don’t feel like drinking anything. Stomach feels empty but food feels alien, and I have to stuff some down. I am all vulnerable like a method actor fully exposed. Got some relief dancing to my ipod in the bathroom. Listening to music is really nice but I preferred obscure lyrics, like German pop rock because it feels better not to understand words 100%, ethereal and somewhat sad intimations being preferable to the obvious and explicit. This drug is stimulative, not sedating in the least. After 5 hours I took 5 mg of valium (Im not benzo tolerant) to take the edge off, to come down. Better be around someone I am really comfortable with and don’t have to put up any kind of mask and can be totally disinhibited around, which I wasn’t. I was with family. I would not consider this as an aid in benzo or opiate withdrawal because of the mindfuck, speediness and dry mouth syndrome. It does cure nerve pain completely. I still have searing pain around my surgery incision which nothing touches except this drug. As a karmic counterbalance it gives me a slight headache. The end effects, when the drug is wearing off, are best. Got into bed and had waking dreams of opiate quality – finally... I like to be insulated, grounded, feeling no pain and warm in my spine, and this drug does not do any of those things.

(我从其余的人中选择了两个, 我加入了一个传道人,第三) 什么种族的男人 可能会有人居住. 他们离开,混. 没有任何敌意. 或野蛮的Lotophagi设计 反对我们的朋友, 但提供给他们的口味 莲花的果实,人们所希望的. 一旦品尝,没有欲望感觉他来 有消息回来,或寻求他的国家更多, 但宁愿食莲花 和Lotophagi,和放弃 所有的想法家. - 荷马,奥德赛 - 第一个实验 3克干鲜花,盖在凝上 背景 - 我在服用维科丁药的5天, - 没有其他常用的药物/草药补充剂. 我先测量了3克的干花, 用咖啡机粉碎, 3克装满90大小的囊. 上午11:30 (t+0:00) - 服用9个囊,加上2杯萨克 (30分). 我还喝了一些果汁和一些无花果牛顿, 因为这是我今天的第一餐. 我通常避免酒精, 但其他报告我读到声称它强化了莲花. 我开始感到疲倦, 这不是一个不愉快的疲劳, 但它也不是有趣的 - - 像一个满足的昏迷. 我躺在床上,闭上眼睛,坐在那里. 我发现自己太了,甚至无法做白日梦. 晚上12:45 (t+1:15) - - 在这个时候我睡着了. 晚上6点45分 (t+7点15分) - - 我醒了. 我没有食欲,但我自己吃晚饭. 我仍然感到惰的满足和我的情绪被抑制. 晚餐期间发生了几件事情, 通常会让我感到一丝悲伤, 但当时我似乎同样免疫悲伤和幸福. 虽然Prozak从来没有让我感到惰. 实验二 7克干花,浸泡在80毫升的金科酒 (30分) 中2小时. 背景 - 同一个主体,在实验1后2周 我过了萨克和莲花混合物, 并丢弃了固体物质. 得到的溶液是绿色和非常苦. 我把它和汁混合在一起, 喝起来很好. 我注意到一个轻微的石头和放松. 柔软的音乐非常好听. 我觉得要躺下,一旦我这样做了很快就睡着了. 我通常很难入睡, 我从一个令人愉快的梦中醒来, 感觉很正常, 但有点惊, 这很奇怪. 后来的思考 我不能说我对莲花的热情和乌利塞斯的同伴一样. 虽然我确实感受到明显的效应, 镇静剂/催眠药通常不是我的茶杯因为我是一个很温和的人开始. 另外,任何想要使用镇静剂/催眠药的人, 都有更多有效的选择. 我能说的唯一好处是它似乎是一种相当有效的睡眠辅助. 莲花/百合花 - - 花 (Nymphaea nouchali var caerulea) (105):一个人 (16),一般 (1)

Apr 8, 2001. erowid.org/exp/4549 DOSE: 7.0 g oral Lotus/Lily - Nymphaea nouchali var caerulea (flowers) BODY WEIGHT: 160 lb I order’d forth my people to inquire (Two I selected from the rest, with whom I join’d an herald, third) what race of men Might there inhabit. They, departing, mix’d With the Lotophagi; nor hostile aught Or savage the Lotophagi devised Against our friends, but offer’d to their taste The lotus; of which fruit what man soe’er Once tasted, no desire felt he to come With tidings back, or seek his country more, But rather wish’d to feed on lotus still With the Lotophagi, and to renounce All thoughts of home. - Homer, The Odyssey - Experiment 1 3 grams dried flowers, gelcapped w/ 2 shots of Sake Background - I was on a 5-day prescription of vicodin that ended 3 days ago. - No other medications / herbal supplements regularly used. I first measured 3 grams of dried flowers, powdered it using a coffee grinder and packed it into gelcaps. 3 grams filled 9 0-sized capsules. 11:30 AM (t+ 0:00) - 9 capsules taken, with 2 shots of Sake (30 proof). I also had some fruit juice and a couple fig newtons, as this is my first meal of the day. I normally avoid alcohol, but the other reports I've read claimed that it potentiates the lotus. 12:00 PM (t+ 0:30) - I began to feel tired and slightly zoned out. It was not an unpleasant tiredness, but it wasn't interesting either - like a contented lethargy. I got on my bed, closed my eyes and just sat there. In a while I found myself feeling too lazy to even daydream. 12:45 PM (t+ 1:15) - I fell asleep around this time. 6:45 PM (t+ 7:15) - I awoke. I had no appetite but made myself eat dinner. I still felt lazily content and my emotions were dampened. Several things happened during dinner that would normally have made me feel a tinge of sadness, yet at the time I seemed equally immune to sadness and happiness. The state of mind reminded me of Prozac (I've taken Prozac several times after MDMA), although Prozak never made me feel as downright lazy. Experiment 2 7 grams dried flowers, steeped in ~80 ml Ginko Sake (30 proof) for 2 hours. Background - Same subject, ~2 weeks following Experiment 1 10:00 pm (t+ 0:00) I filtered the Sake/Lotus mixture and discarded the solid material. The solution obtained was green and very bitter. It was quite drinkable once I mixed it with a can of orange soda. 10:20 pm (t+ 0:20) I noticed a mild stoning and a relaxation. Soft music was very nice. I felt like laying down and fell asleep very quickly once I did so. This surprised me because I normally have a hard time falling alseep and my regular bedtime is around 2 AM. 7:30 am (t+ 9:10) I awoke from a pleasant dream feeling normal, but was somewhat surprised – the dream I had was an instance of a recurring dream that I hadn’t had since I was 8 or 9 and had completely forgotten about. Strange. Afterthoughts I can't really say I'm as enthusiastic about the Lotus as Ulysses' companions were. While I did feel unmistakable effects, they were fairly mild, uninteresting and entirely unenlightening. Sedatives/Hypnotics generally aren’t my cup of tea since I'm a pretty mello guy to begin with. Plus anyone looking to go down the sedative/hypnotic road has numerous more effective options. About the only good thing I can say about it is that it seems to act as a fairly effective sleep aid.

中文译文 兴奋剂

我已经服用了很多药物, 唯一能涉及到这次旅行报告的药物是6-APB, 我有过很多的"卷",只是从来没有真正的MDMA. 我订购了一克半的4-MEC, 价格15美元. 我知道这不是最好的'狂喜替代品'但我真的想要毒品, 我读了几天,听到好东西和坏东西. 我认为只要它不仅仅是一种兴奋剂 (具有额外的'狂喜品质), 我知道它感觉像MDMA和可卡因之间的某个地方, 这应该在充气时燃烧很多 (这很糟糕,因为它是如此方便的ROA)...这就是我所知道的一切. 我几乎立即打开它. 它是如此轻微的白色晶体的外观. 像海盐一样的碎片, 很容易用信用卡来压制. 我测量了50毫克, 通过左鼻孔吸入. 烧得相当严重, 但没有什么是我以前没有感觉到的. 我感觉到10分钟的效果, 轻微提升情绪, 这还不够. 在左鼻孔吸入100毫克. 五秒钟过去了,令人难以置信的灼热的疼痛穿过我的鼻子和大脑的左侧. 后来我意识到疼痛是如此严重, 因为左鼻孔是我用来吸毒的鼻孔, 因为它通常比右鼻更清晰. 我决定我喜欢这种药物. 确实刺激但没有压倒到这样的程度, 没有,它确实有类似"狂喜"的特质. 如果它持续更长时间, 我肯定会买一堆, 在街上卖, ,不能把它卖成可乐,因为它烧得太厉害了. 我决定我需要一瓶葡萄酒. 我做了一个50毫克的囊,吃了,走到街对面的BevMo (lol是对面的街),买了酒,回家洗. 时间+2小时 完全下降,等待囊开始起作用, 我洗手,做一个炸弹的股三明治. 只是因为我完成了萨米我觉得它开始,然而我可以告诉这是一个弱剂量,我不确定为什么. 时间+3小时 仍然感觉到剂量, 口服+门组合肯定会持续更长时间. 这就是我右鼻孔的位置, 我决定尝试右鼻孔. 吸了100毫克, 烧了很多, 在这之后,我失去了跟踪. 在接下来的5个小时里, 我大概在右鼻孔里注射了5-6条100毫克的药物, 我也在喝葡萄酒这段时间所以我有点忘记了狗. 我对这种药物的看法是... 是的,我喜欢它,但它值得鼻子烧吗? 可能. 我很高兴我买了它并尝试了, 我会尽我所能用其他细节来描述这种感觉... 快乐,刺激, 非常轻微的视觉变化. 典型的身体感觉:热,然后冷等. 适度的触觉/感官增强. 张紧握. 没有抑制我的食欲,在实验开始时我很饿,在两次剂量后仍然想吃东西. 在一天的吸毒后, 总的来说,除了持续时间和鼻子灼热, 我对再给药的抑制很低,通常可能会持续整晚再给药. 我知道我在服用药物之前会遇到什么, 我不关心, 4-MEC与6-APB没有相比. 后者在任何可想象的方面都更好, 希望这能帮助任何考虑尝试这项工作的人! 其他:

I've had my share of 'rolls', just never had real MDMA. I ordered a gram and a half of 4-MEC for $15. I knew it wasn't going to be the best 'alternative to ecstasy' but I really wanted drugs and I was broke as shit so I settled for some 4 methylethcathinone. I read up on it for a few days, heard good shit and bad shit. Mostly bad but I figured as long as it was more than just a stimulant (having additional 'ecstasy qualities) then I would probably enjoy it. I understood that it felt like somewhere in between MDMA and cocaine and had a pretty short duration. It was supposed to burn a lot upon insufflation (which sucks because it's such a convenient ROA)...and that's about all I knew about it. I opened it almost immediately. It was ever so slightly off white crystally in appearance. Shards were like sea salt but more rectangular rather than square. Pretty easy to crush up with a credit card. T+0 I measured out ~50mg and snorted it up my left nostril. Pretty bad burn but nothing I hadn't felt before. I felt effects withing 10 minutes, slight mood lift and I could tell my consciousness was slightly different in general. Not enough. T+45 Snorted 100mg up left nostril. 5 seconds pass and and incredible burning pain goes all through my nose and the left side of my brain. I later realized the pain was so bad because the left nostril is the one I use to snort most all my drugs since it's usually more clear than the right one. After 10-15 minutes the high was very nice, I had decided I liked this drug. definitely stimulating but not overpowering to the point where it feels like I'm on just a straight upper. No, there are definite 'ecstasy' like qualities about it. In fact if it lasted longer I would definitely buy a bunch and sell it on the street, but the duration is a real turn off and no one would come back to buy more. Lol, can't sell it as coke + because it burns so fucking bad. ANYWAY I came down within an hour and decided I needed a bottle of wine. T+90 I made a 50mg capsule and ate it, went across the street to BevMo (lol yeah across the street), got wine, went home and took a shower. T+2hours Totally come down, waiting for capsule to kick in, I plug ~50mg up my butt. I wash my hands and make a BOMB ass sandwich. Just as I'm finishing up the sammy I feel it start to kick in, however I can tell it's a weak dose and I'm not sure why. T+3hours Still feeling the dose, the oral+anal combo definitely lasts longer. This is where my right nostril cleared up and I decided to give that side a go. Sniffed ~100mg, burned a lot but only in my nose and throat, not through my brain :). After this I lost track. Over the next 5 hours I probably did 5-6 lines of ~100mg all through the right nostril.. trip info doesn't really matter because the info I previously gave is sufficient. I was also drinking wine during this time so I kinda lost track of shit. My take on this drug is... Yes I do like it, but is it worth the nose burn? Maybe. I am glad I bought it and tried it, I just probably wont buy it again. I'll try to describe the high as best I can with other details and shit...Euphoric, stimulating, however doesn't last nearly long enough. Very slight visual changes. Typical body sensations: Hot then cold etc. Moderate tactile/sensual enhancements. Jaw clenching. Did not suppress my appetite, I was hungry at the beginning of the experiment and still wanted to eat after two doses. Little to no hangover after one day of getting high. Overall it's a cool high, fun drug except for the duration and nose burning. I have low inhibitions about re-dosing and am usually likely to just keep redosing for the whole night. I knew what I was getting into before I took the drug and I kinda didn't care, I just needed something other than weed and alcohol. 4-MEC does not compare to 6-APB. The latter is better in every way imaginable except for the price. Hope this helps anyone considering trying this one!

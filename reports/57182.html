每个人都在使用这些药物时, 我不得不亲自尝试, 因为A) 与MDMA相比, 它们价格便宜得可笑, 我决定开始服用大约30毫克mCPP和50毫克meOPP的囊, 经过一个小时, 我感到脚轻,有点头. 经过一个半小时的时间, 如果我盲目地用药丸服用, 我可能不会知道有什么区别, 我甚至敢说mCPP (和meOPP) 实际上比MDA更好地模仿MDMA. 我觉得自己很舒服, 但这种感觉是波浪般的, 偶尔会伴随着麻木和失调的感觉, 我认为这是因为剂量很小, 一个小时后, 基本上就像麻醉药一样, 但没有紧, 我甚至没有感到恶心, 我认为恶心的报道有点夸大. 这绝对比MDMA更微妙, 正如我之前所说, 欣喜往往会波而来--平静下来, 如果我达到适当的剂量, 这将令人惊的感觉像MDMA, 我感觉非常同情, 甚至还有一种轻微的视觉成分, 基本上很像MDA (显著的标记物和颜色增强). 我没有失去食欲. 我不能吃太多,但我吃了. 经过四到五个小时, 我做了一个小小的点大约20或30mgs的mCPP/meOPP, 这些药物燃烧着一千个太阳的火. 经过一分钟的疼痛, 它实际上很快就开始起作用, 我已经开始下来了,所以这并没有让我感到高兴. 我的身体感觉很好, 但我的大脑有点过度刺激, 我觉得有点压倒. 即使在这个时候,对血压或体温没有明显的影响. 我觉得很奇怪在这个时候去称之为一天,去了躺在床上一点. 我觉得一旦我缩在毯子里就会感觉更好. 我觉得床铺在这一点上, 和毯子 (加上Tempurpedic床) 感觉很好. 我很快就入睡了. 之后我不会感到丧. 我只是觉得疲不堪. 即使在今天,当我在打字的时候, 我有头痛, 但从来没有感觉到",我很!" 这种东西令人惊地好. 这对我来说是MDMA的绝佳替代品, 这不是一种我可以在一次旅行结束时继续服用更多的药物以感受到效果的药物 - - 就像MDMA一样, 我认为mCPP和meOPP对延长这种体验的友好程度不如MDMA. 如果我服用太多,我会感到很奇怪. 时间表:57182

Nov 5, 2006. erowid.org/exp/57182 DOSE: T+ 0:00 50 mg oral MeOPP (capsule) T+ 0:00 30 mg oral mCPP (capsule) T+ 1:00 30 mg oral mCPP (capsule) T+ 1:00 50 mg oral MeOPP (capsule) T+ 5:00 15 mg insufflated MeOPP (powder / crystals) T+ 5:00 15 mg insufflated mCPP (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 140 lb Everyone seems to be polarized at opposite ends of the spectrum on these drugs. Either they love them to the point of sounding like they’re advertising for a legal high online store, or they absolutely loathe them to the point of calling the drugs dirty, poisonous stimulants. I had to try them myself because A.) they're ridiculously cheap compared to MDMA, and B.) I'll try anything once, if there's at least a few documented cases of positive experiences, as long as they're remotely safe and relatively unpoisonous. ,) I decided to take a capsule of approximately 30mg of mCPP and 50mg of meOPP to start, just to see how my body would react to it. After about an hour I started to feel like I was coming up on a pill of ecstasy. I felt light of foot, and a tad dizzy. After about an hour and a half, I felt a pretty decent euphoria that was not unlike an MDMA high. Not quite there, but if I had taken it blindly in a pill, I might have not known the difference, and have thought it was probably a small amount of MDMA mixed with MDA. I'd even venture to say that mCPP (and meOPP) is actually a better imitation of MDMA than MDA is. At this point, I was feeling pleasant, but it was in waves, mixed with occasional feelings of lethargy and dysphoria, which I attributed to the smallish dose, so I upped the dose and took another capsule of mCPP/meOPP with the same dosage as before. After an hour, it hit pretty well. It was basically just like MDMA, but no jaw clenching, and my pupils were relatively normal. I didn't even feel nauseous at any point, except mildly during the come-up. I think reports of nausea are a bit overstated. It's definitely more subtle than MDMA, and like I said before, the euphoria tends to come in waves -- settling down, then building back up again. If a proper dose is reached for me, it will feel surprisingly like MDMA, enhanced tactile sensations and all. I felt very empathetic, but not to the same degree as MDMA. There's even a mild visual component, which basically is a lot like MDA in magnitude (significant tracers and color enhancement). And, the weirdest effect of all -- for me, there was no loss of appetite. I couldn't eat too much, but I did eat. After about four or five hours, I decided to give insufflation a try. I went ahead and made a small dot of about 20 or 30mgs of mCPP/meOPP, and railed it up. These drugs burn with fire of a thousand fucking suns. After a minute of pain, it actually kicked in pretty fast and increased the effects, mostly the visual component. I had already been coming down, so this didn't do much in the way of euphoria. My body felt pretty good, but my brain was slightly over stimulated, and I couldn't focus on anything. I felt a bit overwhelmed. Even at this point, there was no appreciable effect on blood pressure or body temperature. I felt weird enough at this point to go and call it a day, and went and laid in bed for a bit. I felt better once I was curled up in the covers. I felt floored at this point, and the blankets (plus the Tempurpedic mattress) felt so good. The comedown from these drugs is relatively painless, and I was able to get to sleep pretty quickly. I don't feel depressed afterwards. I just feel tired and worn out. Even today as I type this, the following evening, I'm experiencing absolutely no depression whatsoever. I have a headache, but I never once felt the 'woe is me!' that everyone gets the day after MDMA. This stuff is surprisingly good. It's a fantastic alternative, for me, to MDMA that is milder on the body while still retaining a good deal of the 'rolling experience'. It's not a drug where I can keep taking more and more at the end of the trip to feel effects -- like MDMA, I'll just feel more side effects the longer I keep taking the drug in one contiguous session of usage. Furthermore, I think mCPP and meOPP are less friendly for prolonging the experience this way than MDMA is. I end up feeling plain weird if I take too much.

美国 我在使用重量的领域有经验. 许多LSD实验在600-1000ug的,在1.5-6克, 甚至使用高剂量的DXM, 我读过某些准备可以鼓励一个做一些愚蠢的事情. 两小时内迅速摄入2.5-5克, 便于我从体中获得想要的东西. 我不理解为什么我会错误地实现一个疯狂而激烈的旅行. 我有大约. 在4小时的时间内,第一天3克. 然后我喝了一口维里迪斯. 我在大学写了一篇关于电影"梦想的安葬"的论文. 我几乎已经完成了,四十分钟后,我的思想很快地移动,然后显然是视觉成分, 这对我来说非常有趣. 这种药似乎有麻醉作用, 也许是由于触觉幻觉使我感觉高或无身体. 我把论文打印出来,然后地走出了大楼. 我又到了自己的住处, 经过大约6克维里迪斯, 我完全结束了. 我的自我很抗拒, 我出去散步, 这只持续了20分钟. 在旅行后的两个小时内出现了平面线. 虽然我体内含有3克RUE (随着时间的推移而摄入), 但它却在不断地减弱, 这使我在一个小时内进入了我整个晚上所渴望的领域. 它们非常像, 带有令人惊叹的熟悉感, 视觉依赖于我们对现实的解释, 这使得它们比任何其他视觉都更具吸引力. 随着夜晚的推移, 视觉变得更加激烈, 后来我睡着了,觉得我违反了规则, 否认他们的效果. 第二天 然后不断增加0.1克的剂量, 直到我体内有2.8克的病毒. 我错误地使用了已经浸泡超过20个小时的左侧. 两个小时,然后浸泡几小时. 我在一个晚上获得了最多的约18克 (我没有理由理解这些剂量的含义). 这与3.5-5克的一样强烈, 但视觉的复杂性令人震惊, 这剂量与前一晚的毛抑制相结合, 这实际上是为什么它是 soooooooooo强烈. 如果使用不当, 就可能导致轻微的街头,高血压,轻微的DMT影响, 由于我身体对MAO阻断剂的敏感性, 并且在2天内逐渐服用RUE, 我吃了这种后一个小时内, 我变得非常紧张, 一个巨大的oppppsssssss.... 通过我的意识粗. 随后,一个不动的动机似乎开始控制. 我经历了这种疯狂, 在一个实体的帮助下, 一切都被混沌的矩阵所包围, 认知的空白对我来说变得显而易见, 每一个都是同步填充, 没有语言,没有身体, 摧毁了我, 虽然我可能会哭了几天, 其他: 叙利亚街道 (45), 精神病病毒 (170): 单独 (16), 多日经验 (13), 实体/生物 (37), 组合 (3)

Sep 12, 2005. erowid.org/exp/41602 DOSE: T+ 0:00 3.0 g oral Syrian Rue T+ 0:30 0.5 g oral Psychotria viridis (tea) T+ 1:15 7.0 g oral Psychotria viridis (tea) T+ 2:00 oral Psychotria viridis (tea) BODY WEIGHT: 155 lb I am experienced in the realms of heavy usage. Many lsd experiments at 600-1000 ug's, shrooms at 1.5-6 grams, Even the use of high dose DXM, but nothing prepared me for this. I've read of certain preparations that could encourage one to do something stupid. I'd like to set the records straight by saying that a rapid intake of 2.5-5 grams of rue, over a period of two hours, will facilitate what I want out of my viridis. Unless I mess up by using some bad viridis, I can’t see how I can go wrong with the goal of attaining an insane and intense trip. I had approx. 3 grams rue the first day over a period of 4 hours. Then I had a sip of viridis. I then went to type a paper at the college about the movie 'requiem for a dream' for a psych. class. I was almost finished when forty minutes after this gradual ingestion, (after Rue ingestion), my thoughts were moving quite rapidly and then an obviously visual component arrived due to the single .5 gram viridis-tea-sip I had drank 30 min. after the rue, prior to going to school. This was very interesting to me. The rue seemed to have an anesthetic effect that was provided perhaps by the dissociating tactile hallucinations that made me feel as high or bodyless as ever. So I finished my paper, printed it and tripped my way out of the building. I then arrived at my abode and had another 6-8 grams worth of viridis. After about 6 grams of viridis, I was totally finished. My Ego was resistant, and I went outside for a walk to deal with the intensity. This however only lasted for about 20 min. Then a sort of a flatline occurred within the 2 hours after the trip. It became obvious to me that although 3 grams of RUE (ingested over time), was present in my system, it was perpetually waning and struggling to hold on to the effects of the viridis. so I had another sip of the viridis for scientific purposes only. This perpetuated me within one hour into the realm I had desired all night. It was very much like mushrooms with a stunning hint of familiarity/light and depth to the visions. The visions abide by our interpretation of reality and this is what made them more involved than any other. The visuals got more intense as the night wore on, yet I still knew it could have been far more interesting with the same consituents and a more regimented maoi dosage. I fell asleep later and felt that I had broken the rules, and denied their eeffect. DAY 2 I had 2 grams Rue over a period of 1.5 hour after work, and then perpetually by .1 grams added to the dosage until I had about 2.8 grams in me of viridis. I mistakenly used leftoveres which had been steeped for over 20 hours + More viridis low-boiled for apprx. 2 more hours and then steeped for a few. SO I got the most out of about 18 grams in one night (I had no grounds by which to understand the implications of these dosages). This was as intense as 3.5-5 grams of mushrooms, but with a stunning complexity to the visions that in fact last far longer than mushrooms because of their depth and there level of integration with the psyche. This dose was coupled with the mao-inhibition of the night before, this is actually why it was soooooooooooo intense. Maoi's are the spirit of this concoction, and if used incorrectly one could find themselves with a minor Rue trip, hypertension, only a slight DMT infuence, or a favorably DMT-soaked rollercoaster. In this case I experienced the latter, because of my body's suceptibility to MAOI's, and the gradual dosage of RUE over a period of 2 days. Within one hour of my consumption of this viridis I became extremely nervous, as if I had taken more shrooms than were possible for my tolerance level. A giant ooppppsssssss.... coarsed through my consciousnesss. Then the un-moved mover seemed to take control. I lived through this madness with the help of an entity that fused time with causal happenings as it pertained to my cognition. Everything was enveloped in the matrix of chaos as it roped around the finite form of the spiral. The voids of cognition then became apparent to me, and every one was synchronistically filled, as disturbing as it might have been, I knew my course of action, and I followed 'IT'. It without language, without body, destroyed me, and left me with 4 days of recovery to get my vision back to normal. It was sooooooooooooooo worth it, although I could have cried for days at the mere sight/sound/smell/tactile of the real world.

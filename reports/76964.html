I took them and froze them and put them into an ice chest and didn't eat them until we got home. Luckily it was just me trippin balls after that. About an hour into the trip I started to feel really dreamy and tired like a twilight trip and everything was vibrating and shaky feeling. I started feeling nausea rizing so I took some liquid phenagran or promethazine and it made me puke even more. I would love to try amanitas again someday but for now I am going to try to keep my head in school.

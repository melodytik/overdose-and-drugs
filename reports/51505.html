在2006年3月10日晚上9点30分左右, 我在一杯茶中摄入了5毫克阿米特里普提林, 的茶让我的舌头感到麻木. 我在舌头上感到麻木的感觉, 这也与服用阿米素后出现的恶心有关. 我在舌头上感到麻木, 药物缓解疼痛, 并引起恶心, 这可能是对药物的胆反应. 吃完半小时, 我感到疲, 睡着了. 醒来时,我感到轻微的恶心,焦虑和其他与阿米素相关的症状. 在整个一天中, 患者也出现了焦虑和白日梦. 我觉得这种药物会加重我的抑郁症, 因为焦虑表明低血糖反应和白日梦可能表明抑制REM和相关的梦境状态. 艾米特里普提林的缓解疼痛的副作用与我的舌头麻木和短暂的恶心波有关. 我目前所经历的只是白日梦, 这可能是一种轻微的幻觉. 我也无法识别朋友在电话中的声音, 经过四个小时的清醒, 我从阿米的大部分副作用中恢复过来, 消极的副作用 (醒来时昏昏欲睡和短暂的恶心,随后有6小时的焦虑和12小时的白日梦) 往往超过了积极的副作用 (缓解疼痛和镇静). 对于我经历的镇静时间 (7小时的不稳定睡眠),12小时的白日梦,6小时的焦虑,以及昏昏欲睡的波浪 (包括心动症), 总的来说,我觉得在有头部创伤,边缘性人格障碍,抑郁症,焦虑症和低血糖症相关的失眠症的人群中,阿米素只会加剧抑郁症和焦虑症的症状. 然而,它是一种伟大的镇静剂. 我经历的时间表: 身体状况:有轻度创伤性脑损伤史. 心理社会状况评分为10分之4.5 10 = 完美 36小时的清醒,感觉疯狂,有一个繁忙的一天. 第1天: 晚上9点30分 - - 摄入了5毫克的阿米. 晚上11点半 - - 在上网一小时后退休睡觉. 第二天: 清晨6点半 - - 醒来; 在床上. 上午9点 - - 在淋浴时出现焦虑发作, 通常我可以把门放回去. 焦虑和白日梦的出现和消失. 午后1:30 - - 焦虑似乎已经消退. 在接下来的8个小时里, 晚上9点30分 - - 没有焦虑, 上午1点半 - - 退休睡觉; 在一小时内入睡. 心理社会状况评分: 第三天: 早上8点半 - - 醒来,洗,吃早餐. 晚上7点30分 - 心理社会状况评分: 其他: 药物 - 阿米特里普提林 (165):抑郁症 (15),宿醉/几天后 (46),多日经验 (13),一般 (1),单独使用 (16)

7 Hours of Sleep Amitriptyline by Sageb1 Citation:   Sageb1. "7 Hours of Sleep: An Experience with Amitriptyline (exp51505)". Erowid.org . May 30, 2008. erowid.org/exp/51505 DOSE: 5.0 mg oral Pharms - Amitriptyline (pill / tablet) 1 cup oral Tea (tea) BODY WEIGHT: 45 kg On or around 9:30 PM March 10, 2006, I consumed 5 mg of Amitriptyline in a cup of tea with approximately 0.250 gram of Chinese green tea (less than 30 mg caffeine). The amitriptyline-laced tea made my tongue feel numb. That numb feeling on my tongue from the amitriptyline-laced tea is similar to the numbness encountered with ibuprofen. This is also causally associated with the nausea experienced after taking amitriptyline. Thus the numbness on my tongue, the drug's pain relief and subsequent nausea indicate to me that, like ibuprofen, amitriptyline also suppresses pain. This is probably a cholinergic reaction to the medication. Within 30 minutes of ingestion, I felt tired and went to sleep. On waking I experienced light nausea, anxiety and other symptoms associated with amitriptyline. Ataxia (lack of coordination in walking) was also present throughout the day with anxiety and daydreams. I feel that this medication would worsen my depression, since the anxiety indicates a hypoglycemic reaction and the daydreams indicate probably suppression of REM and its associated dream state. Amitriptyline's pain-relieving side effect is associated with both the numbness on my tongue and the brief waves of nausea. All I have experienced so far are daydreams, which may be a mild form of hallucinations. I also didn't recognize my friend's voice on the phone, and my social skills are temporarily absent. After four hours awake, I'd recovered from most of the side effects of amitriptyline, except for occasional daydreams. The negative side effects (grogginess and brief nausea on waking, followed by 6 hours of anxiety and 12 hours of daydreams) tend to outweigh the positive side effects (pain relief and sedation. For the length of sedation I experienced (7 hours of fitful sleep), the 12 hours of daydreams, 6 hours of anxiety, and the waves of grogginess (including ataxia) does not make amitriptyline a great sleeping aid. Overall I feel that in cases of persons with a history of head trauma, borderline personality disorder, dysthymia (depression), anxiety and insomnia associated with hypoglycemia, amitriptyline only worsens the symptoms of depression and anxiety. However, it is a great sedative. Timeline of my experience: Physical condition: history of mild traumatic brain injury. Rating of psychosocial condition: 4.5 out of 10. 10 = perfect 36 hours awake, feeling manic, had a busy day. Day 1: 9:30 PM - ingested 5 milligrams of amitriptyline. 11:30 PM - retired to bed after spending an hour on the Internet. Day 2: 6:30 AM - awoke; stayed in bed. 9:00 AM - had anxiety attack in shower due to the shower door coming off the rail holding it in place. Usually I can put the door back on without incident. 9:30 AM - 1:30 PM: anxiety and daydreams come and go in waves. 1:30 PM - anxiety seems to subside. Daydreams come and go for the next 8 hours 9:30 PM - no anxiety, and daydreams have subsided. 1:30 AM - retire to bed; fell asleep within one hour.

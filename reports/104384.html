In pain and terrified of my heart stopping I crept into bed and again what should have been a 5-hour long night only felt like 20 seconds. It was the strangest of sensations. 4:30 AM - When I gave up on attempting to sleep and realised I still couldn't remember anything that just happened I quickly got my dad to take me to hospital. On the drive there my heart was becoming weaker and weaker to the point where my vision was greying over and my entire body was going numb. When in hospital I slowly recovered my memories but I will forever be haunted with this odd eye problem of extreme fields of view. I fucked with the drug and it fucked me in return.

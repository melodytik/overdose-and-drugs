Aug 28, 2015. erowid.org/exp/106459 DOSE: 60 mg vaporized 3-MeO-PCMo (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 65 kg As a US resident, I (note: 'i', 'he', 'her', 'it', 'them' should be thrown out of our vocabulary to propel us toward a common understanding and world peace, but will remain in this report many reasons), ahem.. as a US resident I am irritated that I don't have access to my beloved dissociatives 3-MeO-PCP and Methoxethamine (MXE), given these are legal here and the recent (2014 going into 2015) EU wide ban should not in theory impact access to these substances. I can dig deeper and find these gems still, but for the sake of my race I will go ahead with this report on this very new substance, 3-Meo-PCMo. A gram of 3-MeO-PCMo arrived in the mailbox of my northeast home. With very little literature on this substance I advise NOBODY to try this yet but some will—I did—and hopefully this report will help in someone in some form or fashion. A clear crystal about 60 mg in mass was thrown into a clean glass pipe of 2mm thickness. Between my lungs and this 'novel dissociative' was a sterilized piece of steel wool (a damn brillo pad!) and a flame was ignite beneath the crystal until vaporization took affect in the form of solid turning to liquid and gas. At 29 years old and weighing ~60-65kg, this male inhaled the nice tasting vapor and held for 5-10 seconds before exhaling and repeating the ritual. After about 3 lungfuls out I felt the higher functions of my brain activated (this tidbit being completely subjective) and a nice relaxation take hold. For those with dissociative experience, this felt like the come-up of MXE, K, diphenidine, 3-Meo-PCP etc.. A few minutes later, with nothing to write home about, more lungfuls were inhaled and this feeling of dissociation and relaxation became stronger and rolled into a giddy and calming wave that only left me wishing I had a line of MXE to inhale. More tokes were taken of this substance throughout the hours, with hits of tobacco and weed here stringed along. No adverse effects, no trouble sleeping, nothing novel to report. If the recent EU wide ban (lol, fuck the War on Drugs) impacts you, then you have a decent substance here. I would agree with the subjective effects and potency of other reports I've read online being accurate. I'm specifically talking about those saying that this is like a much weaker 3-MeO-PCP. Stay safe!

实验 我曾听说过一些叫做昏倒液, 或化水合物, 然后我找到了一个化学品供应商, 我查了一下, 发现它与酸盐有着相同的作用, 所以我决定尝试一下. 第一个实验 网站上说这种药物被用作鱼类镇静剂, 由于我还没有听说过有人尝试过, 我为了安全而在凝中摄入0.25克, 等了20分钟, 我再服用0.25克, 等了20分钟, 仍然没有任何结果. 我只是觉得有点昏昏欲睡, 不知道是因为甲醇,还是因为现在是凌晨2点, 第二个实验 时间是凌晨12点. 我在一个凝囊中摄入1克butanol, 等待20分钟. 我觉得有点昏昏欲睡,所以我知道有一些潜力. 我再摄入一克物质,等待20分钟. 好吧,现在我们开始有所作为. 我们现在在T+40. 我不一定觉得'昏昏欲睡', 我可以肯定看到,有几杯酒,有人会因为这些东西而昏倒. 我还注意到我的唾液味道像湖水, 在这两次实验中, 我不断检查我的心率, 我也患有糖尿病, 血糖保持正常. 我注意到,即使服用低剂量药物, 我不得不不断地纠正字型错误, 尤其是我打算输入"f"的地方. 无论如何,我觉得这件事有潜力. 和平 #小心 # 时间表: 7798 丁醇 (850) 单独 (16),第一次使用 (2)

Jul 30, 2003. erowid.org/exp/7798 DOSE: T+ 0:00 1 g oral Chlorobutanol (capsule) T+ 0:20 1 g oral Chlorobutanol (capsule) BODY WEIGHT: 145 lb Chloral Experiment I had heard about these things called knock out drops, or chloral hydrate, supposed to be like date rape drugs (knock-you-out type of substance). Then I found this chemical supplier who had chlorbutanol or 1,1,1-Trichloro-2-methyl-2-propanol hemihydrate in the HCl salt form. So, I looked it up and found out that it was along the same lines as chloral hydrate, only it resulted in less GI irritation. So I decided to try it out. First Experiment The website said that this is used as a fish tranquilizer and that a good human dose was any where from 0.25 to 1.25 gms. Since I haven't heard of anyone trying it, I ingested 0.25 gms in a gelcap to be safe...waited 20 minutes...nothing (this stuff is supposed to take effect within 15 to 20 minutes). So I took another 0.25 gms...waited another 20 minutes...still nothing. I just feel a little drowsy but I can't tell if its from the chlorbutanol or the fact that it's 2:00 in the morning, so I go to sleep disappointed. Second Experiment It was 12:00 in the morning. I ingested 1 gram of chlorbutanol in a gelcap and wait 20 minutes. I feel a little drowsy, so I know there's some potential. I ingest an additional gram of the substance and wait 20 minutes. Okay, now we're getting somewhere. We're at T + 40 now. I don't feel necessarily 'drowsy,' but more of a 'spacey,' 'out of it' feeling. I can definitely see how, with a few drinks, someone could pass out on this stuff. Also, one thing I noticed is that my saliva tastes like lake water or that I've been in the pool too long and drank too much pool water. During both of these experiment I've constantly checked my heart bpm which stayed normal. I'm also diabetic: blood sugars remained normal. One things I noticed however is, even at low doses, I had to think for a second to remember what I was doing. It's now T + 1:30 as I write this and I have had to constantly correct typos, especially 'r's where I meant to type 'f's. Anyway I feel that there is potential with this.

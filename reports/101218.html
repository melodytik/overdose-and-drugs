购买了一瓶皮卡米隆粉,20克, 一段时间前被冷藏到使用和检查剂量后. 最近试过350毫克, 这就是我可以塞进一个'00'囊. 我在互联网上发现的最高剂量是每天三次100毫克. 两小时后, 完全没有感觉到什么, 这次又没有任何效果. 第二天重复实验,结果相同. 这两次对心率和血压都没有影响. 报告说有降血压效果, 但我没有注意到任何. 长期使用应该会增加脑血流量. 我不确定如何检测, 但每天会服用350毫克, 已使用的证券 皮卡米隆 (322):第一次 (2), 不知背景 (20)

Nov 22, 2017. erowid.org/exp/101218 DOSE: T+ 0:00 350 mg oral Picamilon (capsule) T+ 2:00 350 mg oral Picamilon (capsule) BODY WEIGHT: 233 lb Bought a bottle of Picamilon powder, 20 G, some time ago which was refrigerated until use and after checking out dosages. Recently tried 350 mg which is what I could stuff into a '00' capsule. The highest dosage I noted on the internet was 100 mg 3 times a day. After 2 hours hadn't noticed anything at all and took another 350 mg. Again no effect. Repeated the experiment the next day with the same results. No effect on heart rate or blood pressure either time. There is a blood pressure lowering effect reported but I didn't note any. Longer term use should produce an increase in cerebral blood flow. Not sure how to test for that but will take 350 mg once a day till my order runs out.

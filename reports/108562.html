我是一个经验丰富的物质使用者, 没有做任何负载 (除了玛丽简). 我一直在寻找类似于MDMA的东西, 这项任务比我希望的要困难得多. 希望他们会把它合法化. 我从一个著名的英国供应商那里买了50毫克的5-MeO-DiBF颗粒. 我在一天中吃了很正常的东西,最后一顿饭前大约4小时服用1片. 有一个相当大的潜水艇. 我有强烈的迷幻感觉,奇怪的想法和大量的冲动. 我很喜欢这样的小笑话. 虽然有些犹不决, 但还是很开心. 我吸了一小片麦克西德龙 (最近才试过, 给我能量, 让我很聊, 我有点性冲动, 不会描述它去哪里了. 我觉得有点无趣, 我觉得要睡觉, 但令人遗憾的是, 我在电脑上玩了一会儿, 测试了我的认知能力, 和其他兴奋剂一样, 只是失去了注意力, 我开始试图睡觉, 现在是6.5小时, 仍然清醒, 总结:可能是剂量不够高, 我可能会试着服用2颗颗粒,然后再写一篇评论. 或试着与其他东西混合. 我有一些MDAI在路上. 我不认为我会再买它们. 或至少不是. 我不认为我短暂的乐趣爆发值得错过所有的睡眠. 1p-LSD更有趣. 时间表: 108562 发布时间:2016年5月29日 5-MeO-DiBF (701):一般 (1),未知背景 (20)

May 29, 2016. erowid.org/exp/108562 DOSE: T+ 0:00 50 mg oral 5-MeO-DiBF (pill / tablet) T+ 1:00 1 line insufflated Mexedrone (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 83 kg I'm an experienced user of substances, not done anything loads (except Mary Jane). I've been searching for something close to MDMA, a task more difficult than I'd hoped. Hopefully they will legalise it :) I bought 50mg 5-MeO-DiBF pellets off of a well-known UK vendor. Decided to take after a long day of hard worth, I had eaten quite normal in the day, last food before taking 1 tablet approx 4 hours before. Had a decent size sub. Came on quite fast at +30 min I had strong psychedelic feelings, strange thoughts and a good amount of rush. I enjoyed this quite a lot, having little giggles to myself. Walking back and forth between 'situations' being a bit indecisive, but still having fun. At about +1h I could feel it wearing off, I snorted a small line of mexedrone (only recently tried, gives energy and makes me very chatty, but not much psychological effects or feelings) to try giving it a boost, didn't do much. At +1.5h I got a bit of a sex drive rush and won't describe where that went. After this I was a bit bored of the effects, they were uninteresting and I felt like going to sleep, but sadly the stimulant effect had not worn off. I played on my computer for a bit and played some computer games. Tested my cognitive skills and as with other stimulants just lost focus, but everything else was fine. +4h and I start trying to sleep, now currently + 6.5h and still awake, wishing I had a joint to help send me off. In conclusion: It could be that I didn't dose high enough, or that the full effects do really only last a very short time. I may try taking 2 pellets and write a further review. Or try mixing with something else. I've got some MDAI on the way. I don't think I'd buy them again for the price. Or probably not anyway. I don't think the short burst of fun I had was worth missing all this sleep. 1p-LSD is much more fun.

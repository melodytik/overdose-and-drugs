背景:我的家. 背景: 18岁,体重140磅. 我已经使用了LSA (MG和HBWR),DXM,Acacia Confusa (多次),20xSalvia (多次) 和弱的阿曼尼塔. 目标:我想试试DMT与1种Datura Stramonium. 材料:黄色的Banisteriopsis Caapi葡萄,一颗Datura Stramonium的种子,以及粉状的Acacia Confusa树皮. 准备工作 我煮了两大匙B. 在1°C的水中浸泡3分钟,从火中取出,浸泡10分钟. 我再煮了两次, 总共3°C的茶 (最后一次煮沸的茶比前两次要轻, 因为它们是珀色的). 然后我测量了7g的A. 混,封装2g,并将其余5g混合在一些花生中. 旅行前我禁食了大约四个小时 (只吃早餐和午餐). 影响 下午四点,喝了一杯. 卡帕的 晚上4点45分喝了两杯 卡皮和吃了一颗大图拉斯特拉莫尼奥的种子. (请注意,我前一天尝试了1种Datura Stramonium,所以我可能在旅行时体内有2种种子的化学物质). 晚上5点15分吃7g A. 没有人知道. 大约30分钟后, 我觉得我在某个地方除了我的身体整个时间除了我打开眼睛. 我再次注意到,时间与此无关. 每当我试图记住刚刚发生的事情, 我都无法做到, 这些视觉比仅仅DMT和B所产生的更为现实和包罗万象. 卡帕的 触觉幻觉也与仅使用DMT产生的幻觉明显不同. 而与低剂量萨尔维亚引起的刺痛感更为密切相关. 我也注意到, 添加达图拉的幻象比单独使用DMT的幻象更容易让我感到恐惧. 我常常发现自己跳跃着, 视觉也与萨尔维亚非常相似. 我记得在Salvia期间, 墙上的虫子或其他东西使一切变得模糊, 我记得这次旅行的主要记忆是某个男人 (他很大,看起来不像真人) 在地牢里跑向我, 用他的手动肉机攻击我, 这让我想起了"战争之神"的封面. 我还记得被外星人放在浴里检查. 我第一次体验到这种情况是很奇怪的, 没有人知道. 整个旅程, 我得好像在发作, 整个旅程, 我得好像在发作, 我也能在我的脑海中保持一个愿景如果我集中足够努力. 随着旅程的结束,我地滚到我的床上. 起初我无法入睡,但最终睡觉来了. 我醒来时,我还像以前那样. 起初我以为这只是一个梦, 床上的毯子形成了奇怪的形状, 我沉入了床的深处, 值得一提的是我觉得任何动作都会让我吐. 幸运的是, 因为苦难喜欢陪伴, 我只是在想象. 终于我起身跑到卫生间吐. 我很难动了. 我无法吐,所以我回到床上. 走路让我感觉好一点,所以我认为看电视可能更有帮助. 我从坏男孩开始. 我看到21跳街上映,所以我看了. 我一直在进步,所以我决定吃我早些时候做的烤面包和子. 我认为黄油有助于旅行, 因为它肯定有助于我的DMT旅行更快. 我躺在沙发上看着房间的移动,变形和变化. 我在一个惚. 我一直在抽. 甚至电视上的角色也以一种不真实的,不真实的方式移动. 这是一种令人愉快的感觉, 我不知道这会持续多久, 另一方面, 身体的高是相当愉快, 后来我注意到我的眼睛非常干燥. (与其他粘膜一起) 我觉得自己很疲, 我肯定会在未来再次尝试这种组合, 但我不确定我是否会很快做到这一点. 其他: 华斯卡酒 (268),混 (664),香 (169),达图拉 (15):一个人 (16),实体/生物 (37),组合 (3)

Apr 19, 2017. erowid.org/exp/106168 DOSE: T+ 0:00 oral Banisteriopsis caapi (tea) T+ 0:45 oral Banisteriopsis caapi (tea) T+ 0:45 1 seed oral Datura T+ 1:15 7 g oral Acacia confusa BODY WEIGHT: 140 lb Setting: My home. Background: 18 yo, 140 lb. I have used LSA (MG and HBWR), DXM, Acacia Confusa (multiple times), 20x Salvia (multiple times), and weak Amanita Muscaria. Goal: I wanted to try DMT with 1 seed of Datura Stramonium. Ingredients: Yellow Banisteriopsis Caapi vine, 1 seed of Datura Stramonium, and powdered Acacia Confusa Bark. PREPARATION I boiled 2 tbsp. of B. Caapi bark in 1 C of water for 3 minutes, removed from heat, and steeped for 10 minutes. I re-boiled the same Caapi two more times for a total of 3 C of tea (the last boil produced a much lighter brew than the first two, which were a solid amber). I then measured 7g of A. Confusa, encapsulated 2g, and mixed the other 5g into some peanut butter. I fasted for about four hours before the trip (ate only breakfast and a small lunch). EFFECTS 4:00 pm Drank 1 C B. Caapi. 4:45 pm Drank 2 C B. Caapi and ate 1 seed of Datura Stramonium. (Note that I tried 1 seed of Datura Stramonium the day before, so I could have had the chemicals of 2 seeds in my system at the time of the trip). 5:15 pm Ate 7g A. Confusa. Approximately 30 mins later the trip began. I felt like I was somewhere other than my body the whole time except when I opened my eyes. I noticed that again, time was irrelevant. Whenever I tried to remember what had just happened I was unable to because each successive vision replaced the previous. The visions were notably more realistic and engulfing than those produced by just DMT and B. Caapi. The tactile hallucinations were also markedly different than those produced by only DMT. They were much less euphoric, and more closely related to the prickly feeling brought about by a low dose of Salvia. I also noticed that I was much more easily scared by the visions produced with the addition of Datura as opposed to those produced by DMT alone. I often found myself jumping as an insanely realistic vision jumped at me. The visions were also closely similar to Salvia. I remember visions where everything was fuzzy because of bugs on the wall or something during Salvia, and this happened with this combination of DMT too. The main thing I remember from this trip is some guy (who was very big and didn’t look like a real person) ran at me in this dungeon and attacked me with his manual meat tenderizer (but it was much larger than a normal manual meat tenderizer—it was probably as tall as me. It reminded me of something off God of War video game covers). I also remember being put in a tub and inspected by aliens. This memory just came to me, but it’s pretty weird that I experienced this for the first time through the use of Datura (because IV DMT often produced this vision, according to Dr. Strassman). Throughout the trip I was twitching like I was having a seizure, but I was able to control it with careful, difficult concentration. Throughout the trip I was twitching like I was having a seizure, but I was able to control it with careful, difficult concentration. I was also able to hold a vision in my mind if I concentrated hard enough. As the trip was winding down I painstakingly rolled into my bed. I was unable to sleep at first, but eventually sleep came. I awoke some time later tripping just as hard as before. At first I thought it was just a dream, but when I opened my eyes it certainly wasn’t. The covers were making weird shapes in my bed, I was sinking deep into the bed, and I had no sense of up or down. It is also worth noting that I felt like any movement would cause me to throw up. Thankfully, since misery loves company, I was sharing this experience with the other people in my bed (there was no one in the bed with me. I was imagining them). Eventually I got up and ran to the bathroom to throw up. EVERYTHING was distorted, and I could barely move. I was unable to throw up, so I went back to bed. Walking had helped me feel a little better, so I thought watching some TV might help even more. I started with Bad Boys. After it was over I saw 21 Jump Street was on, so I watched that. I was constantly improving, so I decided I would eat the toast and orange I made earlier. After finishing this food I was coming up on another trip (I believe butter helps facilitate a trip, as it definitely has helped my DMT trips come up more quickly). I lay down into the couch and watched the room move, morph, and change. I was in a trance. I was twitching constantly. Even the characters on TV were moving in a choppy, non-lifelike manner. Everything was again distorted, but it was quite an enjoyable feeling in a weird way. On one hand I didn’t know how long this would last and I questioned if I had hurt myself. On the other hand, the body high was fairly enjoyable, and the visuals were amazing. Later I noticed that my eyes were very dry. (Along with…other mucous membranes). The next morning I woke up tired, but experienced no after-effects. I will definitely try this combination again in the future, but I’m not sure that I’ll do it any time soon—It was very intense, and I’m a busy person any way.

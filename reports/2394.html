晚上九点零五分 刚刚吞下了一支含有110毫克BZP,60毫克TFMPP和25毫克mCPP的凝. 我从上次使用的BZP和TFMPP剂量大幅下降, 因为我正在添加一个新的化合物, 皮佩拉辛的问题在于, 关于剂量和效果, 似乎存在各种各样的意见. 人们推的剂量有很大差异,例如BZP, 我看到了50-100mg, 对于一些人来说, 皮佩拉辛是MDMA之后最好的药物. 另一些人则报告焦虑增加,恐慌反应,恶心,头痛,还有两天的宿醉. 我还觉得很多关于piperazines的信息是故意误导的, 一些消息来源描述mCPP增加了类似piperazineMDMA尾酒的"身体感觉"方面. 我们将看到它与BZP/TFMPP组合相比如何. 晚上9点35分 现在感觉到一些明确. 类似于我上次感受到的刺激. 我认为这在很大程度上是精神病学的. 我选择服用2.5g的GHB和吸一点大麻. 我开始感觉到胸部有点温暖, 我确实认为有点不同, 在游戏中太早了. 晚上9点50分 绝对可以接收... 我开始得到一些视觉的开始, 和一些身体的感觉. 截至目前为止, 感觉与上周的感觉相当相似. 身体的感觉有所不同 - - 但我还不太确定是怎么回事. 我觉得如果我专注于它, 它可能会变成恶心... 但很容易忽略. 这类似于MDMA引起的胃恶心, 我从来没有过 MDMA, 所以这可能是类似的. 到目前为止,这似乎很不错. 晚上10点15分 胃部的不适转变为轻微的恶心, 问题在于不要去想它... 这似乎主要是心理上引起的恶心. 忽视它会使它消散. 没有什么值得写的. 吸食大麻肯定会增加这种效果. 晚上11点05分 现在T+2h... 这很好. 类似于上次... 稍微温和一些, 似乎mCPP为这次体验带来了一个新的层面. 一种温暖的冲感. 这看起来比我的最后一个实验少一点迷幻 - 剂量? 似乎有更多的身体负荷. 晚上11点35分 时间+2.5小时... 我现在应该达到最高血水平. 这种影响似乎不再增加了. 我的,那起飞是有点粗的... 我觉得mCPP不喜欢我,或者25毫克太多了... 有轻微的,但非常明显的恶心, 现在,我感觉很好. 这与我仅使用BZP和TFMPP的实验有所不同. 没有任何我可以把我的手指. 总的来说,mCPP似乎还没有添加任何积极的东西. 据报道, 我很确定我做得太过分了. 我知道我很愚蠢, 在尝试组合之前, 我很幸运, 很开心. 这一次,我没有那么幸运... 虽然现在起飞已经在我身后, 我感觉很好. 后一个词 在最后一次入场之后,事情变得更糟. 我开始感觉几乎好一会儿... 然后一个可怕的恶心的波浪来了我,我去躺下. 接下来的几个小时都是这样度过的, 在短暂的"好吧,也许我会开始感觉好"和漫长的沉浸在极度有毒的感觉之间. 强烈的恶心, 从来没有导致吐. 感觉我的血压非常高. 头痛是什么? 毒性精神病包括恐慌感和极端偏执, 这种感觉是我这次真正毒害了自己, 尿液留是非常不舒服的 - - 我会觉得要尿得很厉害, 头痛是什么? 虽然我的呼吸似乎正常, 这是一种非常可怕的感觉, 这可能是我一生中最糟糕的一夜. 我从3岁左右开始, 首先感觉到有毒精神病, 让大家知道我还活着 (我当时非常感激这一事实), 并描述发生了什么. 我在网上呆了一个多小时, 躺在床上,我开始感到非常寒冷. 我仍然感到很恶心. 我在早上6点左右睡着了, 只有在7点半左右再次醒来. 我留在床上,并很快就睡着了. 我在下午三点半左右醒来, 轻微但持续的头痛与感冒的身体感觉相结合. 我直到晚上8点才起床, 躺在沙发上看电视3个小时. 我头痛和子硬, 现在凌晨1点, 硬还在. 发生了什么事? 我是否大规模过量服用mCPP (或其组合) 是有害的? 我认为这两者都有点. 我是否有任何真正的身体危险, 还是恐慌反应? 我不确定. 我认为高剂量可能会有风险. 我可以说,我很可能会把mCPP无限期地排除在外. 事实上,尽管我的第一次经验非常积极, 我认为我需要一段时间才能探索任何piperazines, 后一个后一个 我终于有了两天的宿醉, 几天的残留恶心感觉. 无需说我发现自己完全对皮佩拉家族失望. 人们,没有人试图把一些美妙的MDMA替代品包裹在这些... 这些化学物质是讨厌的. 我最终把它们都扔进了所, 时间表: 2394 BZP (101),TFMPP (100),mCPP (102),GHB (25):单独使用 (16),健康问题 (27),困难经历 (5),组合使用 (3)

A Trip to ToxiCity BZP, TFMPP, mCPP & GHB by Murple Citation:   Murple. "A Trip to ToxiCity: An Experience with BZP, TFMPP, mCPP & GHB (exp2394)". Erowid.org . Jul 13, 2000. erowid.org/exp/2394 DOSE: T+ 0:00 110 mg oral BZP (capsule) T+ 0:00 60 mg oral TFMPP (capsule) T+ 0:00 25 mg oral mCPP (capsule) T+ 0:30 2.5 mg oral GHB (liquid) BODY WEIGHT: 170 lb 9:05PM Just swallowed a gelcap containing 110mg of BZP, 60mg of TFMPP, and 25mg of mCPP. I significantly dropped the dosages of BZP and TFMPP from what I used the last time, since I am adding in a new compound, mCPP. The problem with piperazines is that there seems to be a wide range of opinions regarding dosage and effects. Dosages people recommend vary widely, for example with BZP, I've seen '50-100mg' and I've seen '500-600mg'... and effects described are also very inconsistent. To some people, piperazines are the next best thing to MDMA. Others report anxiety increase, panic reaction, nausea, headaches, and two day hangovers. I also get the impression that a lot of the information about piperazines is deliberately misleading, with bias running strongly in both directions. mCPP is described by some sources as adding the 'body sensations' aspect of the piperazine MDMA-like cocktail. We'll see how it compares to the BZP/TFMPP combo. 9:35PM Feeling something definate now. Similar to the come-on I felt last time. Kind of like a phenethylamine come-on but with a little more anxiety, which I think is largely psychosomatic. I opt to take 2.5g of GHB and smoke a little cannabis. I'm starting to feel a little bit of that warmth in my chest that I felt last time. I do think there is something a little different, but I can't quite tell yet. Too early in the game. 9:50PM Definately picking up... I'm starting to get some beginnings of visuals, and some body sensations. So far, it feels pretty similar to what I did last week. The body sensations are somehow different - but I'm not quite sure how, yet. Some mild stomach discomfort, like I felt transiently last time - I think if I focused on it, it could become nausea... but its very easy to ignore. It is similar to MDMA induced stomach queasiness that some people get, and which I can occasionally get from higher doses. I've never thrown up from MDMA however, so this will probably be similar in that regards. So far, this seems pretty nice. 10:15PM The stomach discomfort developed into some mild nausea, but this passed. The trick is not to think about it... it seems to be largely a psychologically induced nausea. Thinking about it can make it get significantly worse, ignoring it makes it dissipate. Not worth writing about any further. Smoking some cannabis is definately enhancing the effects. 11:05PM T+2h now... This is nice. Similar to last time... a little milder, I think, due to lower BZP and TFMPP dosages. The mCPP does seem to have introduced a new dimension to the experience. A warm flushing sensation. This seems a little less psychedelic that my my last experiment - dosage? Seems to be a little more body load. 11:35PM T+2.5h... I should be at peak plasma levels now. The effects don't really seem to be increasing anymore. My, that take off was a little rough... I think that either mCPP does not like me, or 25mg was too much... or, of course, it could all have been in my head. There was mild, but very noticable nausea, combined with a general feeling best described as 'Ugh...' for about an hour. Now, I feel pretty nice. It is a little different, bodywise, than my experiment using just BZP and TFMPP. Not anything I can put my finger on. Overall, the mCPP doesnt seem to have added anything positive that I've noticed yet. A rough take-off seems to have been its primary contribution. I'm fairly certain that I overdid it. With the wisdom of hindsight, I realize that I was stupid to not sample these things separately before trying combinations. With BZP and TFMPP, I was lucky and had a great time. This time, I wasn't quite so lucky... although now that the take-off is behind me, I'm feeling pretty good. Afterword Well, things took a turn for the worse after the last entry. I began to feel almost good for a while... then a wave of horrible nausea came over me, and I went to lay down. The next few hours were spent this way, alternating between brief periods of 'OK maybe I'm going to start feeling OK' and long plunges into extremely toxic feelings. Intense nausea, which never resulted in vomiting. The sensation that my blood pressure was very high. Headache. Toxic psychosis consisting of panic feelings and extreme paranoia, the feeling that I'd really poisoned myself this time and was about to die. Very uncomfortable urinary retention - I would feel like I had to pee really bad, would stumble to the toilet, and stand there unsuccessfully - feeling worse from the effort of having walked. Headache. A feeling that my lungs were producing too much phlegm, although my breathing seemed OK. It was a very horrible feeling, and the fact that time seemed to slow to a crawl didn't help a whole lot. This was in fact probably one of the most hellish nights of my life. Starting around 3, I began to feel first the toxic psychosis and soon after some of the nausea lifting. I was able to get back to my computer and get on the chat room I had been on, to let everyone know I was still alive (a fact I was *very* thankful for at that point!) and to describe what had happened. I stayed online for a little over an hour at which point I decided to lay down again, maybe even try to sleep. While laying down, I began to feel very cold. I still felt quite nauseous. I drifted off to sleep around 6AM, only to awaken again around 7:30AM. I stayed in bed, and fell back asleep soon after. I woke up around 3:30PM with a terrible hangover. Mild but persistent headache combined with a flu like body feeling. This lasted through most of the day, and I didn't get out of bed until 8PM, only to lay on the couch for 3 hours watching TV. After this, most of the worst hangover symptoms had passed, leaving me with a mild headache and a stiffness in my neck, which is still there now at 1AM. What happened? Did I massively overdose, or is mCPP (or the combination) noxious by nature? I think a little of both. Was I in any real physical danger or was it mostly a panic reaction? I'm not sure. Somehow I think that a higher dose could have been risky. I can say that I will most likely leave mCPP out of the equation indefinately. In fact, despite my very positive first experience, I think it will be a while before I explore any piperazines, or anything else outside the tryptamine and phenethylamine families for that matter. After-Afterword I ended up having a 2 day hangover, with residual icky feelings for several days. Needless to say I found myself entirely disillusioned with the piperazine family. Folks, nobody is trying to keep some wonderful MDMA replacement under wraps with these... these chemicals are nasty. I ended up dumping all of them down my toilet, and can only say 'Good Riddance!!!'

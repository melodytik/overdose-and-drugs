阿利斯卡林第二次试验 我决定做一些调整, 确保我的胃是空的, 没有酒精或任何额外的东西, 我保证在5-6个小时内不吃东西 (上次可能是超油的食物让我感觉这么糟糕, 实际上,这轮比第一轮更多了. 剂量如下: 15毫克和30分钟后增加10毫克. 在大约+2-2.5小时后, 我又服用了10毫克, 旅行似乎花了更少的时间开始, 这一次, 随着散步和狗和淋浴 (+45分钟) 我开始感觉到一个奇怪的的感觉在我的身体, 但没有视觉. 我躺在房间里, 关灯等待, 我发现我几乎没有得到任何CEV,感觉很快就会很难阅读,我注意到在半黑暗的房间里有很多运动,我打开灯. 我可以看到墙上挂着一个被, 楼层再次看起来很, 但没有像上次那样改变颜色. 我的身体感觉很好,所以我决定再服用. 后来我又上网试着阅读. 我手机的屏幕变得很深, 字母要么沉入几英寸, 剧本从正常的剧本不断变化, 变成任何东西, 哥特式,漫画,泡,报纸等等. 很难读出四维字体的变化! 我决定放下手机冥想, 我开始看到很奇怪的CEV,用我的眼睛 (手,毯子等等) 似乎他们可以投射约一英尺左右,这意味着如果有任何视觉12'左右远,我不能看到CEV (开放的眼睛),因为我会看到别的东西,在一个黑暗的房间 (灯光使它几乎不可能看到CEV开放的眼睛). 这些奇怪的颜色光斑不断缩小,形成更大的斑点 (大约相同的原始大小), 然后现在的微小不同颜色的斑点会脱落,分散和消失, 它会形成无生命物体的微观图像, 我开始沉思, 不知为什么, 我之前看过的一篇文章突然想起了这一事实. 这是一篇非常奇怪的论文, 解释了一些类似于死亡/重生的人在高剂量LSD时的感受. 这篇论文, 写成某些人之间的虚构对话 讨论了某种种族是如何从其他星系来到地球的 我最后一次重新服用了. 我觉得好像我听到一些声音告诉我某些事情让我思考论文. 我开始感到有些偏执, 也许其中的部分可能是真实的, 现在大约是凌晨四点. 我试着阅读,但我正在阅读的书的主题对我来说太令人陶醉了 (基本上是如何在不服用迷幻药时实现自我死亡),我开始感觉到第三和第四剂量开始起作用,书中的字母开始做同样的事情 我放下了书,走出去抽一支, 这让我感觉很好, 仿佛我在从AL中吸到大药, 我进去吃了一碗麦片, 看着地板像水池一样波动, 然后回楼下. 我仍然有大量的头脑空间所以我开始阅读另一本书... 这是一个坏主意! 我选择了一个关于阿兹特克人的故事, 根据他们的个人历史, 我开始在脑海中想象一艘火箭飞船落在一座山上,然后人们出来 (因为我看到的早期文章,我认为这可能是我的头脑试图合理化它),认为这是一个洞穴,然后基本上生活和死亡作为一个牧师,农民,国王,战士,等等一千年左右,直到科尔特斯到达 这本书的主题是 (阿兹特克人做了什么以及他们如何做到了科尔特斯之前. 我得到的这些心理图像并不是视觉图像 (虽然我得到了很多),而是感觉到某些东西, 我在这次旅行中有很大的头脑空间, 我用再剂量延长了它. 几个小时后醒来, 感觉好像没有睡过. 其他: 艾利斯卡林 (573):单独 (16),一般 (1)

I made sure not to eat for around 5-6 hours (last time it might have been extra greasy food that made me feel so bad, who knows?) and start with a smaller dose, and increased it gradually over time instead of taking a lot at once. I actually ended up taking more this round then I did the first time. Dosage was as follows: 15 mg and 30 minutes later +10mg. About +2-2.5 hours I took 10 mg again and then 20-30 minutes after that took an additional 10. The trip seemed to take a little less time to kick in, this time after the walk with the dog and a shower (+45 min) I was starting to feel a weird tingling feeling throughout my body, but no visuals. After I got my room situated I lay back with the lights off waiting, and playing a game on my phone, and checking shroomery posts. About +2 hours I noticed I was hardly getting any CEV, it felt like any minute now it would be very hard to read, andI was noticing a lot of movement in the semi-dark room I turned the light on. I could see a quilt hanging on the wall fluttering like in a breeze, but obviously there wasn't one. The floor tiles looks really fluffy again, but not changing colors like last time. My body felt good so I decided to dose again. After that I went back online and tried to read. The screen on my phone gained a weird depth/bulge to it where letters were either sunken in a few inches or bulging out a bit. Some were right next to each other, and the script kept changing from normal, to just about anything, Gothic, comic, bubbles, newsprint, etc. The scripts kept rolling over it in waves and it got really hard to read 4 dimensional font changing print! I decided to put my phone down and meditate, with the light on but pointed down so it was dim. I began to see very weird CEV, and with something over my eyes (hands, blanket etc.) It seemed they could be projected about a foot or so, meaning that if there was anything visual 12' or so away, I couldn't see the CEV (with open eyes) because I would be seeing something else, in a darkened room (lights make it almost impossible to see CEV with open eyes). These were weird looking blobs of colored light that kept shrinking and forming larger blobs (which were about the same original size) and then the now tiny different colored blobs would pop off and scatter and vanish and it would happen again. It would form slight pictures of inanimate objects like it was trying to show me something unfamiliar looking. I began to meditate, and for some reason an essay I had seen earlier popped into my head as a fact. It was a very weird paper, explaining somewhat like the death/rebirth people feel while on high doses of LSD. This paper, written as a fictional dialogue between certain people discussed how certain races came to earth from other galaxies and reasons how and why we are here, angels, really off the wall stuff, but written as if true. Right before this point, I had redosed the last time. I feel as if I was hearing voices telling me certain things making me think about the essay. Nothing was vocalized but I began to feel kind of paranoid that maybe parts of it could be true, so I began to read a book to see if I could read. It was now about 4 am. I tried to read, but the subject matter of the book I was reading was way too heady for me (basically how to achieve ego death while not on psychedelics) and I began to feel the third and fourth doses kick in, the letters in the book began doing the same thing as before, although a bit subdued on the depth changes so it was easier to read (although some letters looked as though I could fall in them) I put the book down and went out to smoke a blunt which made me feel really good, as if I was getting really high from the AL but the weed was a little buzz I was getting, to keep me relaxed for the ride. I went inside and ate a bowl of cereal, and watched the floor wave around like water in a pond, then went back downstairs. I still had a tremendous amount of headspace so I started reading another book... Bad idea! I picked one on the history of Aztecs, which from their personal history says that they were the last of the earths children to leave a cave in a mountain. I began to get mental imagery of a rocketship falling into a mountain and then people coming out (because of the earlier essay I had seen, I think it might have been my mind trying to rationalize it), thinking it was a cave, and then basically living and dying as a priest, farmer, king, warrior, etc for a thousand years or so, until Cortes arrived, which was the subject matter of the book (what the Aztecs did and how they did it up until Cortes. These mental images I was getting wasn't as much visuals although I did get lots) as they were feeling certain things and seeing pictures in the book of objects the Aztecs used, and knowing how it was used. I had a massive headspace with this trip, and I lengthened it quite a bit with the redosing. I eventually fell asleep around +12 hours and woke up a few hours later feeling like I hadn't slept.

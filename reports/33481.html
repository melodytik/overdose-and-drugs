最近我被邀请参加一个涉及喝阿雅瓦斯卡的巫师周末. 我在星期五晚上到达那里, 团队共享了一顿晚餐, 这一群人是男女的混合群体,年龄在31至60岁之间,经验不尽相同,从没有经验到非常有经验. 我们将在最后一天晚上喝阿雅华斯卡. 我决定喝完整杯, 对于我来说,这是一个非常强大的剂量, 于是我走到前面, 喝了一杯, 这让我很烦. 如果您想要更多,请到前面补充剂量 (但只要您能坐起来). 我等了一会儿,然后又从另一个巫师那里得到了小剂量. 惊喜,惊喜,我又把它扔回了. 于是我又坐下. 我着自己的脸,这很好. 后来我突然走到了超越倒我的水平. 我一直在重复"我投降了,我投降了" (向阿雅瓦斯卡) 我求阿雅瓦斯卡的灵魂降低我的水平, 但她说她不能, 我没有意识到, 我在呼吸过度, 吸酒的巫师不得不从他的阿雅瓦斯卡惚下来, 我觉得我不知道自己是谁,也不知道别人是谁, 我从来没有感到这么高兴的烟在我的脸和肺. 香味丰富,味道美味 另一个巫师帮我站起来, 带我到所, 我闭上眼睛和阿雅瓦斯卡进行了几次对话. 她告诉我我在私人生活中的错误, 打了我几次在脸上, 因为我没有每天向她祷告, 而表现出她的不尊重. 她帮助我摆脱了我对前男友的负面情绪, 说:"阿雅华斯卡是克莱尔, 她向我展示了我脑海中的物理门, 我开始重新升级到下一个强度水平, 没有喝过亚华斯卡的巫师不得不走过来, 这花了比第一次更长的时间和更多的努力. 我躺在地板上听着"伊卡洛斯". 两人把我带到草上, 我立刻被疯狂的视觉干扰. 我回到房间摔倒在地板上, 艾哈瓦斯卡告诉我, 沙曼们离开我去睡觉是不重要的, 因为过去,现在,未来都是一个, 我花了剩下的夜晚躺在地板上, 观察总是存在的能量, 我在黎明时刻地走到所, 感觉到真实的悲伤, 我只能喝香茶到第二天的午餐, 因为我的味觉无法处理水的化学味道. 第二天, 另外两位参与者给我一个大大的拥抱, 这是一个伟大的经历. 时间表: 33481 精神病病 (170):大群 (10+) (19),导游/护理 (39),实体/生物 (37),神秘经验 (9),困难经验 (5),一般 (1)

Jul 8, 2004. erowid.org/exp/33481 DOSE: T+ 0:00 1 cup oral Ayahuasca (tea) T+ 1:00 0.5 cups oral Ayahuasca (tea) BODY WEIGHT: 50 kg Recently I was invited along to a Shamanism weekend that involved the drinking of Ayahuasca. I got there on Friday night feeling very dispirited about the decision to break up with my boyfriend. The group had a communal dinner and got to know one another. The group was a mixed one, both men and women, aged between 31 to 60 (?), with a variety of experience of such matters, from none at all to being very experienced. The Shamans who were leading the session announced that rather than waiting til the fourth weekend to drink Ayahuasca we would be drinking it on the last night. I decided to drink un completo or full cup which was what I drank in Peru. For me this is a very strong dose but within my capacity. So I went to the front and had a full cup which I threw back up five seconds later. After a while I was only getting very mild effects which was really annoying me. One of the Shamans had said to wait an hour and a half and if you want more come to the front for a top up dose(but only if you can sit up by this point). So I waited a while and then got another small dose from the other Shaman.surprise surprise I chucked it back up again. So I sat down again.Then things EXPLODED!. I was tripping my face off which was fine. Then all of a sudden I went to the level beyond tripping my face off. I kept on repeating 'I surrender, I surrender' (to Ayahuasca) whilst shaking uncontrollably from the cold. I appealed to the spirit of Ayahuasca to bring me down a level but she said that she couldn't and that I had to stay at that level all night. I didn't realise it but I was hyperventitilating, the Shaman who had been imbibing had to come down from his Ayahuasca trance to blow sacred tobacco in my face. Immediately this calmed things down for me and I felt that I didn't know who I was or or who anyone else was but knew that I was in England and that I felt safe and warm and loved. I have never felt so happy about getting smoke in my face and lungs. It smelt rich and tasted delicious The other Shaman helped me up and walked me to the toilet where Ayahusaca made love to me bringing me to orgasm and back to the room again. I closed my eyes and had several conversations with Mama Ayahuasca. She showed me where I was going wrong in my personal life and slapped me hard several times on the cheek for showing her disrepect but not praying to her daily. Then she helped me get rid of all the negative feelings i've been harbouring towards my ex-boyfriend of late and said 'Ayahuasca is Claire and Claire is Ayahuasca' and 'one is all and all is one'. She showed me physical doors in my mind which she got me to open to deal with past traumas. I started drifting back up to the next level of intensity so the Shaman who had not been drinking Ayahuasca had to come over and blow more tobacco in my face to ground me again. This took a lot longer and more effort than it had done the first time round. I lay on the floor listening to the Icaros.The session slowly ended and I managed to mumble the words toilet at the two Shamans whilst they were leaving. Between the two of them they got me to the lawn where I was immediately distracted by the mad visuals. I went back to the room crashed to the floor, Ayahuasca told me that it did not matter if the Shamans left me to go to bed as past, present, future were all one and that energetically they would always be present there. I spent the rest of the night lying in a huddle on the floor observing the energy that is always present but filtered out by the brain flying round the room. At dawn-break I stumbled off to the toilet and did the necessary feeling a real sense of sadness that things were returning to normal and that Ayahuasca was leaving my body. I could only drink Chamomile tea up til lunch the next day as my sense of taste couldn't handle the chemical taste of water. Two of the other participants gave me a big hug the next day and asked whether I was feeling delicate?. All together it was a great experience.

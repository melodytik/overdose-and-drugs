中文译文 兴奋剂

相当健康 几年来经常吸 曾经吸烟, 约7个月前通过尼古丁贴片 (5个月) 戒烟. 吃很多甘糖果, 这对血压不好. 想尝试一种精神药物. 准备时间: 将大约2g的树茎 (ma huang, ephedra vulgaris) 放入冷水中,慢慢加热至沸点,放沸10分钟,冷却20分钟. 我很快就喝了茶. 杯子里有棕色的粉末,我吞下了. 叶绿茶的味道非常好,不需要任何糖. 这是我喝过的最美味的草药茶之一. 时间+15分钟:我的视力看起来更准确,颜色也很好. 事实上没有真正的变化, 我觉得自己有点愚蠢. 虽然它声称能让思想变得快速清晰, 我感到轻微的幸福. 我开始用自己收获的果实制作葡萄汁. 呼吸比以往任何时候都容易, 时间过得很快, 我感觉很好. 时间+2小时:我已经喝完了葡萄汁了. 我觉得有能量可以消耗. 我抽了一根很小的MJ棒. 这并没有改变我的心态, 我抽了一根更大的大麻, 我想散步,但我不能因为我在等朋友. 我开始担心自己的血压, 因为我吃子很重, 每天吃5克. 我感觉到心脏有什么奇怪的东西. 我的心跳有点加快了. 我感觉心脏有点怪异, 但我想这与大麻偏执有关. 时间+4小时/4小时30分:此时, 心理效应如注意力集中和快乐正在消失, 而身体能量的感觉没有改变. 我开始感到有点疲倦. 然后,我的朋友来了,我们在花园里工作了一个小时. 我停止了关心我的心,我没有感到疲劳的工作. 时间+6小时:我感到疲倦. 我在心脏区域有轻微的疼痛. 我有点担心. 我只是想拿走一些东西. 精神活性药常常会因吸烟的强烈渴望而消失. 随着时间的推移, 于是我抽了一支烟, 晚上我在电脑上工作,然后看电影. 在心脏区域有不愉快的感觉. 我没有抑郁或悲观的心态, 我吸了草和野, 十个多小时后, 我很快就入睡了. 第二天早上我早起,感觉很好. 我认为对心脏的影响开始于精神影响1小时后, 这是一个愉快的实验. 虽然效果很轻微, 但持续时间长. 我会再试一次, 但用较低的剂量. 很明显毒剂量不大于有效剂量, 而试图从中获得巨大的高能很容易导致严重的心脏问题. 其他: 单独 (16),健康问题 (27),第一次 (2),一般 (1)

Tea Time Ephedra vulgaris, Cannabis, Lobelia, & Lactuca - L. canadensis by Unknown Citation:   Unknown. "Tea Time: An Experience with Ephedra vulgaris, Cannabis, Lobelia, & Lactuca - L. canadensis (exp36618)". Erowid.org . May 8, 2007. erowid.org/exp/36618 DOSE: T+ 0:00 2.0 g oral Ephedra sinica (tea) T+ 2:00 smoked Cannabis (plant material) T+ 2:30 1 cig. smoked Cannabis (plant material) T+ 6:00 1 cig. smoked Lobelia spp. (plant material) T+ 10:00 smoked Cannabis (plant material) T+ 10:00 smoked Lactuca - L. canadensis (plant material) BODY WEIGHT: 65 kg me : male 40 years 65 kg rather good health regular pot smoker for several years former heavy tobacco smoker, quit around 7 months ago with the help of nicotine patches (for 5 months). Eating a lot of licorice candies, which are bad for blood pressure. mindset : interested in trying a psychoactive product. preparation : About 2 g of ephedra stems (ma huang, ephedra vulgaris) into cold water slowly heated to boiling point, let boiling for 10 minutes, cooled down for 20 minutes. T : I drank the tea quickly. There was a brown powder in my cup, I swallowed it to. Ephedra tea had a very good taste and didnt need any sugar. It's one of the best tasting herbal tea I ever drank. T + 15mn : My vision seemed a little more accurate, colors were fine. In fact there was no real change but I was more concentrated on my senses. T+30 mn : I was feeling a little stupid. When I tried cocaine one year ago, it drove me to a stupid state of mind too, although the claims it makes thoughts fast and clear. I was feeling mildly happy. I started to make grapevine juice with the fruits I collected from my grapevine plant. Breathing was easier than ever, and very pleasant. T + 1 h : Time passed quick, I was deeply concentrated on my manual task. I was feeling fine. T + 2 h : I finished with the grapevine juice. I was feeling like having energy to spend. I smoked a very little MJ stick. It didnt change my state of mind, except I was more likely to laugh. T + 2 h 30 : I smoked a bigger joint with the same result. I'd like to take a walk, but I couldn't cause I was waiting for a friend. I started to worry a bit about my blood pressure because I'm a 'heavy' (5 g / day) licorice eater. T + 3 h : I felt something weird with my heart. My heart was pounding at a slightly accelerated rate. I was feeling something weird with my heart, but I guess it had something to do with pot paranoia. T + 4h / 4h 30 : At this time, the mental effects like concentration and happyness were fading, while the feeling of having a physical energy didn't change. I started to feel a little tired. Then, my friend arrived and we worked in the garden for an hour. I stopped to care about my heart and I didn't feel tired while working. T + 6 h : I was feeling tired. I got a slight ache in the heart area. I was a bit worried about it. I was 'wanting to take something'. Since I quit smoking, psychoactives often ended by the need, sometimes very strong, to smoke tobacco. As time has passed, it was replaced by a slighter need to take some psychoactive substance. So I smoked a cigarette of Lobelia inflata dried leaves. I spent the evening working on my computer and then watched a movie. There were unpleasant feelings in the heart area. I had no depression or even pessimistic state of mind when the effects were fading away. I smoked pot mixed with wild lettuce more than 10 hours after having drunk the ephedra and fell asleep very quickly. Next morning I woke up early, feeling fine. I think that the effects on the heart started 1 hour after the mental effects, a bit like with caffeine, and lasted longer. It was a pleasant experiment. The effects were mild, but long lasting. I'll try it again, but with a lower dose. It's obvious that the toxic dose is not a lot larger than the effective dose and that trying to get a massive high from ephedra could lead very easily to a major heart problem.

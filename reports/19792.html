吗啡 2C-B 其他 (粉末/晶体) 药品 - 迪亚泽帕姆 我注射15毫克的吗啡和30毫克的安非他命, 现在是晚上10点,大家都来了. 我口服了2粒药,回想起来,我会说它们很可能是甲基 [其实没有像Mdxa一样的头部空间,但我的所有感官都显然得到了增强, 我从来没有经历过与今晚的感受相似的东西], 但它们也可能只是Mdma, 我永远不会确定. 在早上12点. 效果是非常缓慢和微弱的, 所以我口服一半的药丸, 我的朋友提到他想去得到一些股,所以我们去脱衣舞俱乐部. 我们享受着脱衣舞者一段时间,他告诉我我们需要在3点前回来,得分! ~12:30终于效果真的开始起作用了. 触觉增强,下巴像疯子一样声, 瞳孔扩大. 我们离开俱乐部,回到家里. 我在厨房里和另一个朋友聊天, 他告诉我他必须在几个小时内回家,所以我继续给他礼物 - 20毫克2cb. 我们聊了一会儿, 谈其他朋友有多么的"e-tarded", 我们说他们早就应该长大了, 但这可能只是因为我们真的很厌倦. 到了凌晨2点,我们开车送生日男孩回家, 每个人都得到2cb. 显然我们的司机将等待服用他的剂量, 我们到达生日男孩的家,把他送下来. 我从来没有感受到过的最好的快乐感开始包围我. 我爬进后座,躺下, 几乎高潮因为我感觉很好. 我们回到了脱衣舞俱乐部, 接了那个同意和我们一起回家的脱衣舞者; 我们决定在我的状态下, 最好我留在车里, 他离开了一分钟,然后回来,告诉我门是锁的. 这就是那个想法... 我们空手回家. 我真的不太记得实际发生的事情. 我只是记得那种强烈的高兴情绪. 由于2cb的"波斯地毯覆盖", 我的视野非常扭曲和模糊, 我在接下来的一个小时里, 我记得躺在床上, 和我最好的朋友和他的女朋友聊天. 他提到了我带来的胺, 所以我们去了厨房, 没有太有趣,我真的没有注意到它. 我发现自己太刺激了, 无法下尿, 于是我让某人为我了一杯饮料, 不幸的是,当时没有任何帮助. 我们回到后面的房间, 聊了一些, 讨论生活和人际关系. 我告诉他我要去做什么, 我走进了浴室, 给我注射了50毫克的胺. 快速前进大约5-10分钟. 凯他胺的打击. 我倒在地板上,因为我突然失去自我. 我只能记得发生了什么. 我记得我的视野被扩大到我可以看到至少270度的周围; 我记得我的视野变得,看到一切都倾斜向上,向下,和侧面; 我也记得从我的身体外看到自己, 感觉好像有好几个小时我处于这种状态, 在某个时候我开始抓住现实, 找到类似于基线的东西, 时间是凌晨4点半, 凯他胺的效果已经减弱, 我几乎不能与任何人互动, 听力非常扭曲, 我坐下来接受一位朋友的按摩. 几次我了一下, 几乎又"掉下来". 之前的高兴情绪仍然存在. 终于我回到后面的房间与我最好的朋友聊天, 和谁还在后面. 现在,我们有一个半大小的小组在后面与我们. 显然我在K-land的时候完全了, 没有人真正意识到在迷幻药物中失去自我是可能的, 为了保持我的高兴状态, 我吸了最后1/4的药. 几个人步行到我家, 距离6个街区不远, 我又吸了20毫克, 完全恢复了我的高兴状态. 我注意到如果我睡着了 (我的身体在这个时候已经非常疲), 我在眼睛上放了一些发光棒, 几次惚到接近高潮, 我朋友和他的女朋友告诉我他们会在一个小时后离开, 她给我10毫克的瓦来帮助我睡觉, 另外,我终于可以说出这次的情况了. 于是我们又回我家去买一些. 我又吸了一口20毫克. 每当我转移眼睛时, 视力就会产生反响, 剩下的时间我只能着看电影, 我决定回家, 在那里我迅速吸入2.5毫克的瓦, 昏倒. Skip ahead to 10pm, everyone has arrived. I take 2 pills orally, which, looking back, I'd say its highly possible they were methylone [there really wasnt an mdxa-like headspace to it, but all my senses were definately enhanced, and everyone who took them said that the pills were weak; exactly what I'd expect from methylone, although I still haven't knowingly tried methylone, so I can't say for sure; and out my past mdxa+2cb trips (3 mda+2cb, 2 mdma+2cb, and 1 mda+mdma+2cb), I've never experienced anything even remotely similar to what I felt this night], but they also could've just been mdma, I'll never know for sure. 12am. The effects are comming on really slow and weak, so I take half a pill orally and absorb the other half sublingually (total of 3 pills so far). My friend mentions that he wants to go get some ass, so we head off to the strip club. We enjoy the strippers for awhile and he tells me we need to come back before 3; score! ~12:30am Finally the effects are really starting to kick in. Enhanced sense of touch, jaw chattering like crazy, and dialated pupils. We leave the club and head back to the house. When we get back, I stay in the kitchen to chat with another friend, who's birthday was the previous day. He tells me he's gotta head home in a few hours so I go ahead and give him his present - 20mg of 2cb. We chat for awhile about how e-tarded the rest of our friends are, with their glowsticks, kandy bracelets, and pacifiers. We say that they should've grown out of this long ago, but it could just be cuz we're really jaded. 2am, we head out to give birthday boy a ride home, and everyone gets some 2cb. Obviously our driver will wait to take his dose, but I promptly snort about 20mg. 2:45am, We get to birthday boy's house and drop him off. By this time, the 2cb is really starting to kick in, and the best euphoria I've ever felt in my life begins to encompass me. I crawl into the back seat and lie down, practically orgasming because of how good I'm feeling. We return to the strip club to pick up the stripper who agreed to go home with us; we decide in my state, it is best I remain in the car, so my friend heads off to go inside. He is gone for a minute, then returns, telling me that the doors are locked. There goes that idea... We return to the house, empty handed. Now around this time, I honestly can't remember much that actually happened. I just remember the intense euphoria. My visual field is really distorted and foggy, due to the 2cb 'persian carpet overlay', and the orgasmic feeling takes up most of my focus. I spend a large portion of the next hour just lying around, moaning and enjoying. I do remember lying on the bed, chatting with my best friend and his girl. He mentions the ketamine I brought along, so we head to the kitchen and dry out 1/2cc, and I promptly snort up half. Not too interesting, I dont really notice much from it. Somewhere around this time, I realize I'm too stimulated and tripping too hard to piss, so I have somebody mix me up a drink, to exploit alcohol's CNS Depressant/Diahretic properties so I can piss. Unfortunately it didnt help any at the time. We head back into the back room and chat some more, discussing life and relationships. After about 15 minutes, (should be around 3:30-4am by now) I tell him what I'm gonna go do so that he knows whats going on. I head into the bathroom and IM 50mg of ketamine. fast forward about 5-10 minutes. the ketamine hits. I drop to the floor, as I suddenly obtain ego loss. I can only remember bits and pieces of what happened. I remember my visual field being expanded to where i can see at least 270 degrees around; I remember my visual field becomming skewered, seeing everthing tilted up, down, and angled to the side; I also remember seeing myself from outside my body, and at one point I saw my image multiplied. It feels like hours that I'm in this state, and at one point I begin grasping for reality, to find something resembling baseline, so that I could have something to hold on to and anchor myself to reality. 4:30am, enough of the ketamine wore off so that I could control my body again. I can hardly interact with anyone else tho; my hearing is very distorted, echoing massively, and my vision is swamped by the 'carpet' overlaying itself on everything in the darkness. I sit down and get a massage from a friend. Several times I go limp and almost 'fall' out of my body again. The euphoria from before is still present. eventually I head back into the back room to chat with my best friend again, and whoever else is back there. By this time, we've got a semi-large group back there with us. One person tells me that I scared the shit out of everyone earlier; apparantly I went totally limp and spasmed while I was out in k-land. Nobody else there was really aware that ego loss was possible on psychedelics, but with k its very possible; nobody was prepared for me going down like I did, since I only informed 2 people before I IM'd the k. 4:30am, I snort the last 1/4 pill to try to maintain my euphoric state. 6am or so, a few of us head to my house on foot, a short journey of 6 blocks, to get some more 2cb. I snort another 20mg and return completely to my euphoric state. I notice that if I sort of nod off (my body is extremely tired by this point), the pleasure is increased, so I snort 15mg of morphine to encourage nodding off. I lay some glow sticks over my eyes and trance out to a near-orgasm several times, but unfortunately shake myself out of it before getting too deep into it. 7am, my friend and his girl tell me they're leaving in an hour, and she gives me 10mg of valium to help me sleep, which I hang on to until later. Also, I was finally able to piss about this time :) 11am, i get talked into doing more 2cb, so again we head back to my house and to get some. I snort up yet another 20mg. My vision begins 'echoing' when it kicks in, leaving multiple images of everything as tracers, every time I shift my eyes. The rest of the day is spent lounging around watching movies, my jaw still shaking, and I am still intensely euphoric. 3:30pm, I decide to head home, where I promptly snort 2.5mg valium and pass out.

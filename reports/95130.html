我和我的两个朋友决定我们要做一些莫莉粉我最近得到了. 在我的朋友们来之前, 我试图权衡和分担莫利. 我试着把它分成三条等长的线. 我试着把它分成三条等长的线. 这也没有太好... 我在纸上了一些粉, 试图对其进行称量, 结果大约是0.3/4. 我把它拿下来重新衡量. 它的重量是0.3所以我试图增加更多的得到0.4-5. 假设它不超过0.6 (我会说它很可能是0.5 - - 6在回顾). 我把它分成三条相同的线, 我看了看囊, 它们大多是均的, L的囊比其他两个囊少一点, 在我去接我的朋友之前, 我吃了补充剂. 我服用1000毫克维生素C,一些'免疫增强剂' (含有各种抗氧化剂),多种维生素和鱼油. 我吃了所有这些在空腹 (本身不是一个好主意, 因为我很容易有胃病). 我以前都服用过这些药, 这是在他摄入莫利之前的45分钟. 我带着我的朋友回家, 吃了莫利囊. 我们在等待来的时候吸了一碗大麻. 这是我们所有人第三次带着莫莉, 通常我需要一个小时才能开始感觉到它. 我们在楼上我的房间里, 通常是相当温暖所以我有风扇和开放的窗户. 约在30点左右, F 起床去所, 说他头很轻, 已经很高了. 他的声音听起来有点担心. 我告诉F要冷静下来, 这可能是他最高的, 我确实开始感受到效应, 我从以前的经历中得到了我认为的高峰效应. 我非常享受自己,我们都在进行很棒的对话. 我母亲打电话给我, 要求我为她找一些东西. 她要求我查看月份, 所以我不得不将月份的名称转换为数字, 我可能在搞了, 但总的来说没问题, 我不认为她怀疑我搞了. 虽然我做这件事时, 视觉效果很轻, 我相信在1:30左右, 效果开始变得非常严重. 我越来越高兴, 眼睛也开始抽, 我还在享受自己, 而这种高兴情绪也越来越强. 我开始闭上眼睛试图缓解它. 我几乎躺在沙发上, 不太注意朋友们在说什么, 我告诉他我感觉很好, 但有点太高了, 我认为是大约1:45我的情况变得非常糟糕. 几乎所有的高兴感都消失了, 我感觉自己变得非常热和出汗. 几乎所有的高兴感都消失了, 我感觉自己变得非常热和出汗. 房间里风扇开着,窗户开着, 但房间对我来说仍然很热. 我离开房间试着冷静下来, 我感觉到我的额头非常热, 非常出汗. 我知道如果在狂喜中过热, 坏事会发生, 我回到房间里告诉我的朋友我感觉不好. 我告诉他我不确定, 但如果情况恶化, 医院的朋友们立刻担心, 我说我不确定, 他们开始试图在网上查看是否有致命剂量的MDMA. 我说这是好的,给我一分钟,我再次离开房间试图冷却. 我走了出去一分钟得到一些凉爽的空气. 我仍然感觉很糟糕, 但在这一点上肯定是可控的, 然后突然间所有的不好的感觉完全消失了. 这一切都发生在5到10分钟内. 奇怪的是,我真的不再觉得自己在服用狂喜药, 我感觉有点快, 但远远不如以前, 我告诉我的朋友我感觉好了很多, 他们告诉我这很奇怪, 但很好他们担心我. 我为吓他们而道歉, 但他们说这不是什么大不了的事. 随着时间的推移, 我慢慢开始获得更多的高兴感和'迷幻'效应, 直到我再次感觉良好, 整体而言,这场比赛并没有太糟糕, 我不确定是什么引起了我的不良反应,可能是过多的莫利 (很可能,即使我觉得我只有一点多的F),莫利中的东西 (这是我从来没有从一个新人得到的东西,虽然这似乎是最不太可能的解释),房间太热 (尽管我的朋友没有任何问题),我服用的新"免疫增强剂" 或许是其他原因. 我肯定不会说我度过了一个糟糕的时光, 考虑到它的重量可能接近0.6和我得到的最,我可以有在0.18到0.21的范围内. 我只有135磅重,所以这可能是3毫克/公斤的剂量. 我本不应该有太多的耐受性, 因为我最后一次服用它是3周前. 其他: 发布时间:2022年11月21日 MDMA (3),维生素/补充剂 (231):组合 (3),一般 (1),小组 (2-9) (17)

Even this did not work out too well... I scooped some molly powder onto a paper and attempted to weigh it, it was coming out to around .3/.4 so I added a little more. It went up to .6 but that didn't seem right so I took it off and reweighed it. It was weighing .3 again so I tried to add more to get .4-.5. After fiddling with it for 10 more minutes, unsuccessfully, I decided to just use the whole pile I had scooped out, assuming it would be no more than .6 (I would say it was most likely .5 - .6 in retrospect). I divided it up into 3 equal lines and put each line in a capsule. When I looked at the capsules they were mostly even, the one for L had slightly less than the other two and mine had slightly more than F (so I had the most, but it didn't seem like it was by very much). Before I leave to pick my friends up I take my supplements. I take 1000mg Vitamin C, a few 'immune booster' pills (contain various antioxidants), a multivitamin, and fish oil. I took all of these on an empty stomach (not a good idea in itself, as I am prone to having upset stomachs). I had taken all these before except I added one new 'immune booster' pill. This was about 45 minutes before ingesting the molly. So I pick up my friends and we go back to my house and ingest the molly capsules. We vaped a small bowl of weed while waiting for the molly to come on. This was all of ours third time taking molly, and usually for me it takes around an hour for me to start feeling it. We were rolling in my room upstairs which is usually pretty warm so I had the fan on and windows open. At around :30 F gets up to go to the bathroom and says he is very light headed and already very high. The tone in his voice sounded actually kind of worried. I tell F to calm down and that this is probably as high as he is going to be, so he might as well enjoy it. I am definitely starting to feel effects but not as strong as F seemed to be. At around :50 I was definitely getting what I would consider peak effects from my previous experiences. I was greatly enjoying myself and all of us were having great conversations. At 1:15 or so my mother called me and asked me to look up some stuff for her. I was able to talk with her fine, but she was asking me to look up months, so I had to convert the month names to numbers which got slightly confusing. The task was kind of difficult at this point and I was probably messing up on that, but it was generally OK and I don't think she suspected I was messed up or anything. However, while I was doing this I was getting very light visuals, not unlike what I see on LSD. I believe it was around 1:30 when the effects started getting very heavy. I was getting more and more euphoric and I was getting very heavy nystagmus (eye twitches) and it was very hard to concentrate on looking at something due to this. At this point I was still enjoying myself and the euphoria was still getting stronger. At some point close to this the nystagmus got to be too much for me and I started closing my eyes to try to relieve it. I was half laying down on the couch and not really concentrating on what my friends were talking about, but still at this point I was feeling very good. My friend asked me how I was feeling and I told him that I was feeling good but a little too high and that it was getting uncomfortable. I believe it was around 1:45 that all at once my condition became very bad. Almost all euphoria was gone and replaced by pretty intense dysphoria. My heart was pounding quite fast and I could feel myself getting very hot and sweaty. Almost all euphoria was gone and replaced by pretty intense dysphoria. My heart was pounding quite fast and I could feel myself getting very hot and sweaty. The fan was on and window open in the room we were in, but the room was still very hot to me. I left the room for a bit to try and cool down, I felt my forehead and it was very hot and very sweaty. I knew that bad things can happen if you overheat on ecstasy and at this point I was feeling like it was possible that I could throw up or pass out. I went back in the room and told my friends I was not feeling very well. F asked if I was going to throw up and I told him I wasn't sure but that they might have to call the hospital if I got worse. Once I said hospital my friends immediately got concerned and asked if I had perhaps taken too much. I said I was unsure and they started attempting to see online if there was a lethal dose of MDMA. I said it was OK and to just give me a minute and I again left the room to attempt to cool down. It was noticeably cooler outside my room and I walked outside for a minute to get some cool air. I still felt quite bad but definitely manageable at this point so I walk back upstairs into my room and lay down on the couch. Then all at once all the bad feelings completely stopped. All of this happened in about 5 - 10 minutes. The odd part was that I honestly no longer felt like I was on ecstasy at all at this point, I felt almost completely sober. I felt slightly speedy, but nowhere near what it was before but I had no euphoria, dysphoria, or any of the 'psychedelic' effects of ecstasy. I told my friends that I was feeling much better but that I didn't even really feel high any more. They told me that was quite odd, but good as they were worried about me. I apologized for freaking them out, but they said it wasn't a big deal. As time went on I slowly started to gain more euphoria and 'psychedelic' effects until I was feeling good again but nowhere near as high as I was when I was getting the nystagmus. Overall the roll was not bad at all, but a tad too intense for me. I am not sure what caused my bad reaction, it may have been either too much molly (most likely, even though I feel I only had slightly more than F), something in the molly (this was stuff I hadn't gotten before from a new guy, although this seems like the most unlikely explanation), the room being too hot (although my friends weren't having any problems), the new 'immune booster' I took, the fact that I took supplements on an empty stomach or perhaps something else entirely. I certainly wouldn't say I had a bad time, but I am definitely going to err more on the safe side with my doses next time. Considering it may have weighed close to .6 and that I got the most, I could had had somewhere in the range of .18 to .21. I only weigh 135lb so that it was probably a 3 mg/kg dose. I should not have had much tolerance either as the last time I had taken it was 3 weeks previous.

我用半杯干燥的西尼卡, 用三杯水和两大匙的汁, 了三小时. 使用咖啡过器将Ephedra过,剩余的液体 (约60毫升) 放置在冰箱中12小时. 第二天,它被消耗: 我在禁食12个小时后喝茶. 味道几乎无法忍受, 但几根口香糖有所帮助. 下午12点:第一种效应被发现. 我的手指尖很冷,但我的手掌是出汗的. 也可以注意到动. 我试图在课堂上做课. 我每分钟写大约100个字, 但每秒钟都能想到下一个1000个字. 在手和腿部局部化的过度活动是最突出的. 下午1点:我的午餐时间. 虽然我已经禁食了14个小时, 现在,我已经有了更好的情绪, 我预计这会持续更长时间, 情绪升高和局部过度活跃的同样的效应也存在,但减少了. 晚上2点:一切效果都消失了, 总的来说,这是一个很好的体验. 我认为这种效果会更好地发挥作用. 发布时间:2019年7月10日 青 (243):准备/食谱 (30),第一次 (2),学校 (35)

Jul 10, 2019. erowid.org/exp/108089 DOSE: 0.5 cups oral Ephedra sinica (tea) BODY WEIGHT: 210 lb I took about a half cup of dried Ephedra Sinica and simmered it for three hours with three cups of water and two tablespoons worth of lemon juice. The Ephedra was strained off using a coffee filter and the remaining liquid (about 60 ml) was placed in the refrigerator for 12 hours. The following day, it was consumed: 11:30 am: I consume the tea after fasting for about 12 hours. The taste is near unbearable, but a few sticks of gum help. 12:00 pm: First effects noticed. The tips of my fingers are cold, yet my palms are sweaty. Jitters are also noticed. 12:30 pm: I am attempting to do in-class work. It is near impossible because I write about 100 words per minute, but I think of the next 1000 words every second. Hyperactivity localized to the hands and legs is most prominent. 1:00 pm: My lunch time. All hunger (now about 14 hours of fasting) is gone, but my energy levels are soaring. My mood also seems to be improved for the better, as I am more sociable and friendly. 1:30 pm: I expected this to last longer, but I already feel a comedown on my energy levels. Same effects of increased mood and localized hyperactivity are present, but decreased. 2:00 pm: All effects gone, slightly tired in a physical and mental way. Overall, quite a good experience. Next time, I will consume the tea before I workout, and I presume that the effects will be used in a much better way.

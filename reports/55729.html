第一次注射50μg的IM几乎没有效果,一些耳. 我躺下, 经历了心脏的减速, 一些缓慢的OEV视觉效果和CEV,大约25分钟后开始. 激发了,并照顾它... ,-) 高潮是长期的, 不像DXM是反高潮. 吃了一些东西之后. 时间扩展, 视觉上像一个沉重的DXM旅行. 在大约1个半小时后, 似乎所有的效果都消失了, 在第二次尝试时,我IM'ed100μg. 这还没有发展到很大程度. 我想注射另一个100μg,所以我做了. "好吧,这足够了接下来的四个小时左右... "在大部分情况下,这似乎是非常催眠 - 我在摇摆与音乐,感觉非常轻重. 打字很难. 我相信我可以着天空,而没有看到云. 将会让大家知道 Mk-801是非常邪恶的 我只能把这种情况描述为"邪恶的尸效应" - - 我的身体一直活着, 在过去的24小时里, 我记忆中有些"洞". 我几乎没有睡觉醒来,然后去购物,写了一份名单在我的手上,现在我不能读它,只是胡说八道.. 我后来做了一个400μg的剂量 (IM'ed) - - 它做了什么? 我只是不记得任何东西吗? 我看了一部DVD, 不记得有没有看过. 我不喜欢这种经历. 这很可怕,就像雅各布的梯子一样可怕. 我正在看着液体, 认真考虑把它倒出来. 虽然最初的感觉有些高兴, 但脑腔切除术患者往往也很开心. 这里有一点我没有提到. 尤其是在试图睡觉时, 这种经历与其他类似经历不同, 它不是"随机的", 事实上,当我专注于它时, 我以前有过这样的经历, 但mk-801版本很不同. 首先,这些声音的口音,音色和格局显然不是我的. 它们似乎都是用英语写的, 不同的声音似乎谈论着不同的东西, 但它们大部分都是无意义的. 有些听起来像是收音机的评论, 有些只是重复的无意识的声音, 在某个时候, 声音似乎有一些视觉成分, 但它非常模糊和模糊, 让我补充一下,这是一百分之百的失调. 另一方面,我在其他药物中经历过类似的经历时, 所有声音都是男性的, 没有口音. 我实际上把"头部的收音机"与LSD联系起来, 我从来不认为它只是一种幻觉 - 这似乎很像心灵感知. 凯他胺心灵感应的经历完全不同 - - 就像爱上了我, 没有视觉效果, 所以对话没有意义. 我有物小鼠与我在胺旅行中交流 - - 有时我可以转换成小鼠的观点, 感觉到小鼠的思想, 我保护它,爱它,反之亦然 (她的名字是弗洛拉:-)). 不知如何, 胺增加了我们的联系. 然而,DXM的精神体验总是非常奇怪和超现实. 我唯一真正的半心灵感应体验, 是两个人之间信息传递, 我试图教一个朋友化学, 信息只是从头脑流向头脑, 几小时之内, 他掌握了大量的化学知识, 很奇怪,令人震惊, 但很好 - - 这不可能发生在一个陌生人身上. 这部MK-801就像恐怖小说中的地狱电话. 虽然它比K或DXM的伪心理效果更明显,更强大, 听到有人低声,然后你把电话下来? - - 想象一下你房间里有一台无线电,你不能关掉它,也不能离开它. 这就是它是什么样的. 无论如何,我想说MK-801绝对不是娱乐性的. 记忆力受损,以及严重的精神损伤, 这似乎是一个非常强大的PSI放大器几乎没有娱乐价值. 即使是微小的mk-801也不能很好地溶解 - - 还有可见的颗粒物, 它是用蒸水溶解的, 不应该有任何杂质, 我已经完成了这个化合物. 我确实试过200微克的口服剂量, 但这次我意识到精神障碍, 并且对字体错误更加谨慎, 我打算服用安比恩来睡觉 - - 有证据表明醇降低了NAN效应. 这一切的目的是因为我真的买不起胺 或许1毫克的价格和200毫克的胺一样. 我希望500ug能成为一种体验, 我的头还在,奇怪的不是正确! 已使用的证券 迪佐基尔平 (330): 单独使用 (16),第一次使用 (2)

Sep 1, 2006. erowid.org/exp/55729 DOSE: repeated IM Dizocilpine The first shot of 50µg IM had little effect, some tinnitus. I lay down and experienced a definite slowing of the heart, part of K-programming. Some slow strobing OEV visual effects and also CEV, onset in about 25min. Got aroused and took care of it.. ,-) Orgasm is prolonged, unlike DXM which is counterorgastic. Ate some stuff afterwards.. Time dilation, visually like a heavy DXM trip. All effects seemed to be non-existant after about an hour and a half, took some Ambien and went to sleep. On the second try I IM'ed 100µg. This is still not developing into much. I thought of injecting another 100µg and so I did. 'Okay, that's enough for the next four hours or so..' For the most part it just seems very hypnotic - I'm swaying with the music, feeling very light weighted. Typing is difficult. I believe I could stare at the sky and not see the clouds. Will keep you all posted.. Mk-801 is VERY wicked.. I could only describe this as the 'evil zombie effect' - my body keeps going but the brain isn't there. There are some 'holes' in my memory in the last 24hrs.. I woke up after almost no sleep and went shopping, wrote a list on my hand and now I can't read it, just nonsense.. I later did a 400µg dose (IM'ed)- did it do anything..? Or do I just not remember anything from it? I watched a dvd, and couldn't remember if I've ever seen it before. I do NOT like this experience. It's creepy, like jacob's ladder creepy.. I'm looking at the liquid and am seriously considering pouring it out.. I mean, the initial effect was somewhat euphoric, but people with lobotomies are often happy as well. Here's a bit I didn't get to mention. When trying to sleep, the ear-ringing became particularly annoying. Unlike other such experiences, it's not 'random', so it was impossible to tune out. In fact, when I focus on it, there are voices. I've had this type of experience before, but the mk-801 version is quite different. For one, the voices have accents, tones and patterns that are clearly not mine.. They all seem to be in English, but then again, I'm tuning out what I don't understand, so I'm not sure what that's worth. Furthermore, the different voices seem to talk about different things, yet all of them are mostly meaningless. Some resemble radio like commentary, some are just repetitive mindless subvocalizations, like people talking to themselves. At one point, the sounds seemed to have some visual component to them, but it was very fuzzy and indistinct, more like an idea than a picture. Let me add that this was 100% dysphoric. On the other hand, when I've had this type of experience before on other drugs, the voices were all male and accentless. I actually associate the 'radio in the head' bit with LSD and I never considered it anything but a hallucination - when I've had too much and I want it to stop, in my bedroom in the dark, etc.. This, however, seemed VERY MUCH like telepathy. The ketamine telepathy experience is totally different - it's like being in love so much I just think the same things. The mk-801 is like having a television going in the next room without visuals, so the dialogue doesn't make sense. I have pet mice which I communicate with in Ketamine trips - I can at times switch into mouse point-of-view, feel the mouse's mind, and urge my little friend to do this or to do that, and watch it happen.. There is a very strong feeling of love with this - the mouse is my friend, I protect her, love her and vice versa (her name is Flora :-)). Somehow Ketamine increases our bond. DXM psychic experiences however, are always VERY weird and surreal. The only quasi-telepathic experience I've really had, had to do with information transfer between two people both on low doses. I was trying to teach a friend chemistry, and the info was just flooding from mind to mind, like the rest of the world was not there. In a few hours' time, he grokked a huge amount of chemistry, that had taken me months to learn. It was odd and overwhelming, but GOOD - there's no way this could happen with a stranger. The mk-801 was like a horror novel phone call from hell.. It was MUCH more distinct and powerful than the pseudo-psi effects of K or DXM, but it was also very WRONG. Ever had the phone ringing and you pick it up and there's no one there, but you listen and listen and there's this whispering and then you slam down the phone.? - Well, imagine that was a radio in your room you couldn't turn it off or get away from. That's what it was like. In any case, I'd like to say that mk-801 is definitely NOT recreational. There is memory impairment, and significant mental impairment, kinda like valium - it just makes me 'dumb'. For the most part, it seems to be a VERY powerful psi amplifier with almost no recreational value. Also, even the tiniest amount of mk-801 doesn't dissolve very well - there is still visible particulate matter, which might be dust or might be not.. It was dissolved using distilled water, there shouldn't be any impurities, but there are. I'm done with this compound.. I did try a 200µg sublingual dose, but all I really did was zone out and stare, with a strange heavy feeling in the chest. I'm writing this report under the effect (forgive me for being scatterbrained), but this time I'm aware of the mental impairment and being more careful about typos, at least I hope. I plan on taking ambien to sleep - there is evidence that benzo's reduce the NAN effect. The whole purpose of this was that I can't really afford ketamine.. With mk-801 (+), maybe 1mg costs the same as 200mg of Ketamine. I had hoped that maybe 500ug would be an experience, but I'm just not going to go there. Geez, my head is still ringing, weird not rightness!

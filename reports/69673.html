这不是我第一次吸入oxymorphone (opana ER) 也可能不会是最后一次. 这是我唯一滥用过的止痛药, 我是个大麻吸烟者, 我写这篇文章是因为缺乏关于这个令人着迷的物质的其他报道让我失望. 它的效果比oxycontin强2至3倍,比吗啡强4倍. 我不确定这是否是真的, 因为我从来没有尝试过任何一种. 每次我吸入oxymorphone, 我都会感到极度恶心, 有一次我了15次, 我意识到我通常服用的剂量与其他使用者相比是无价的, 虽然负面影响 (恶心,头,偶尔的吐和) 确实很糟糕,但我发现积极影响 (极度高兴,强烈的身体高潮,幸福感,抑制的丧失,以及与他人有强烈联系的能力) 是值得的. 今天晚上我了一片20毫克的药片, 尽可能多地放下时间, 我会先吃一顿好吃的饭, 因为我发现这样做的效果会加倍. 另一件值得注意的是,如果口服,粉碎,并与水混合后服用, 效果只会减半, 5分钟 - - 已经有轻微的效果 10分钟 - - 感觉熟悉的轻体感觉以及一点高兴感. 15分钟 - - 像之前一样,只放大,手明显. 尽管我只是坐在那个可怕的办公椅上, 我似乎无法停止微笑. 我觉得现在是个好时机播放Pink Floyd的歌曲"舒适的麻木", 我也开始感到,但这并不困扰我. 现在我记得为什么我这么喜欢这种药物. 现在的很严重, 主要在我的脸上, 我感觉有些恶心, 但喝牛奶对我有很大帮助. 我可以说这种高兴感更强烈, 几乎感觉就像它占据了我身体的每一寸. 我感觉比以往任何时候都更好, 而音乐只会放大这种体验. 我有一个非常强烈的冲动告诉某人我爱他们. 我希望这种感觉永远持续下去, 我喜欢这种药物. 75分钟 - - 我决定去厨房买更多的牛奶, 闭上我的眼睛是很好的感觉. 我认为它的影响正在减弱, 开始让人烦. 我认为我会躺下几分钟,享受我的音乐. 105分钟-我开始回到现实生活中, 这种药物的另一大优点是上升速度很快, 而下降速度很好,很慢, 感觉好像在大气层中漂浮之后, 120分钟-几乎所有的效果都消失了, 对于我来说,它只会让我感到疲倦,让我沉入一个美好的深度睡眠, 总的来说,我认为这是一种很好的药物. 2009年6月19日发布

Jun 19, 2009. erowid.org/exp/69673 DOSE: 7 mg insufflated Pharms - Oxymorphone (ground / crushed) BODY WEIGHT: 120 lb This is not my first time insufflating oxymorphone (opana ER) and will very likely not be my last. This is the only painkiller I’ve ever abused so I really don’t have anything to compare it to. I’m an avid marijuana smoker and have experimented with a few other drugs including cocaine and Adderall. I’m writing this because the lack of other reports on this fascinating substance disappointed me. Oxymorphone is extremely potent and is said to be about 2-3 times stronger than oxycontin and about 4 times stronger than morphine. I’m not sure if this is true or not because I’ve never tried either of these. Every time I have snorted oxymorphone I have experienced extreme nausea and once vomited about 15 times until my stomach was completely empty. I realize that the dosage I usually take is NOTHING compared to that of other users but I find that about 5mg suits me just fine. Although the negative effects (nausea, dizziness, the occasional vomiting, and itchiness) really suck, I find the positive effects (extreme euphoria, strong body high, sense of wellbeing, loss of inhibitions, and the ability to feel a strong connection with others) make it worthwhile. So, tonight I crushed up a 20mg tablet, after getting off as much of the time release shit on it as possible, and snorted a 7 mg line. I make sure to eat a nice big meal first as I find that doing that doubles the effects. Another thing to note is that it’s only half as effective if taken orally, crushed and taken as soon as it’s mixed with water because it WILL GEL UP when it comes into contact with a liquid. 5 minutes- already slightly feeling the effects 10 minutes- feeling the familiar light body sensations as well as a bit of euphoria. 15 minutes- same as before only amplified, hands are noticeably shaking. 30 minutes- vision seems different for some reason as it always does, I’m extremely comfortable even though I’m just sitting on my crappy desk chair. I can’t seem to stop smiling. I think it’s a good time to put on the song Comfortably Numb by Pink Floyd, as it always enhances the experience for me. I’m also starting to get itchy, but that doesn’t bother me. 45 minutes- I am a cloud, my shoulders feel as though they are sinking, and I now remember why I love this drug so much. The itchiness is bad now, mostly on my face, but I still could care less. I’m experiencing slight nausea, but I find that drinking milk, as I am, helps a lot. Having tried cocaine on numerous occasions I can say that the euphoria is much stronger and almost feels as if it inhabits every inch of my body. 60 minutes- I feel incredible, better than ever, and the music only amplifies the experience. I have a very strong urge to tell someone I love them. I wish this feeling would last forever and I can certainly understand how someone can easily become an addict. I love this drug. 75 minutes- I decided to venture into the kitchen to get more milk and I’m now burping and hiccupping which is what usually happens before I puke. Closing my eyes feels very nice. I think the effects are starting to lessen, unfortunately. The itching is getting annoying now. I think I’m going to lay down for a few minutes and enjoy my music. 105 minutes- I’m starting to step back into reality after a very pleasant and relaxing high. Another great thing about this drug is that the come up is fairly quick, while the comedown is nice and slow, almost rounded. It feels as though I’m coming back down to earth after floating around in atmosphere. 120 minutes- nearly all of the effects are gone, but I still feel nice and relaxed. Some people say that they have a hard time sleeping after using oxymorphone, for me it just makes me tired and lets me drift into a nice deep sleep, so I’ll be going to bed in a few minutes. Overall, I think this is a great drug.

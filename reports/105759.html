我第一次尝试时, 是作为一个. 由于我将20毫克混合在0.5毫克水中, 重要的是水量. 这需要更多的东西来感受任何东西. 我试过很多次, 增加剂量, 最多大概是50毫克, 我终于尝试使用2-3毫克水和50-75毫克, 虽然它没有像好的氧康丁那样强大, 但它仍然有所期望的和冷静,快乐的感觉. 我真的很喜欢使用它. 我确实在不同的尝试中吃过和吸过. 吃东西有影响, 但不是我想要的. 然而,当子和凝囊混合时, 效果远远是最好的. 几乎值得一个oxy,与一些脱离附加 (但不是很强). 没有铁路. 这味道太糟糕了, 我几乎在尝试后吐了. 我感觉到了效果, 但它不值得在我的嘴里味道. 从来没有. 已使用的证券 2015年11月5日发布 MT-45 (676):回顾 / 摘要 (11),未知背景 (20)

Mild but Fun MT-45 by YES Citation:   YES. "Mild but Fun: An Experience with MT-45 (exp105759)". Erowid.org . Nov 5, 2015. erowid.org/exp/105759 DOSE: MT-45 BODY WEIGHT: 145 lb I acquired 1 gram. I had no real ideas about it besides what receptor it bound to (mu negative). A classic opioid it seemed. I have had experiences with vicodin/ oxy’s before (as in chew up a bit too many for my own good). The first time I tried it I did it as a suppository. It did not have the desired effects because I mixed about 20mg into .5 mg water. The amount of water was crucial. It needs to be much more to feel anything. I tried it many other times upping the dosage with the max probably being about 50mg in around .5-1 mg water. I finally tried using 2-3 mg water with 50-75 mg and it was amazing. Not the strength as chewing some good ole oxycontin but still, it had the desired itches and chilled, happy feeling. I really enjoyed it after using it correctly. I did eat it and snort it in different attempts. Eating had some effects, but not what I was looking for. HOWEVER, when mixing the suppository and a gel capsule, the effects were by far the best. Almost worthy of an oxy, with some disassociation attached (but not very strong). DO NOT RAIL. It tastes so bad I almost vomited multiple times after trying. I felt the effects, but it was not worth the taste in my mouth. Never again.

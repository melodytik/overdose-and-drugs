Mar 2, 2019. erowid.org/exp/112961 DOSE: 35 mg oral 4-MeO-MiPT (capsule) BODY WEIGHT: 180 lb ==Previous tolerance Took a microdose of 8mcg ALD-52 on the previous day, and 5mcg two days prior to that. I don't feel that this affected my tolerance. ==Mindset going into the experience In good spirits, things going well in my personal and professional lives. ==Previous psychadelic/empathogen experiences LSD, ALD-52, 1P-LSD, AL-LAD Psilocybin, 4-ACO-DMT, 4-HO-MET, 4-HO-MIPT, 4-ACO-MET, 4-ACO-MIPT, 4-MEO-MIPT DMT, MET MDMA, MDA ==Daily vitamins & supplements taken (may affect the experience) Men's Multivitamin, Vitamin D, Lion's Mane Mushroom Extract, Tongkat Ali, Sunflower Lecithin, Pygeum, Spray-on Magnesium ==Goal of this experience I had previously taken a 20mg dose of 4-MEO-MIPT, but didn't experience much in terms of psychadelic effects. I wanted to see if a higher dose would produce more or different effects. To experiment with the sexuality enhancing effects. ==Why am I submitting this experience I don't see many experience reports on this novel tryptamine, so I thought it would be worthwhile to contribute. T - 2:30 (8:00 AM) Ate a light breakfast. I had been wanting to try a higher of dose of 4-MEO-MIPT for a while, and was eager to give it a go. T + 0:00 (10:30 AM) Weighed out 35mg of 4-MEO-MIPT, put it into a gelcap, and ingested it. The 4-MEO-MIPT was a white powder, sourced from a well-known online vendor. I go sit in front of my computer and read some articles. T + 0:20 (10:50 AM) I am beginning to feel a strong tryptamine body high. My body feels warm and heavy. T + 0:40 (11:10 AM) I am experiencing some euphoria. It's not the in-your-face laughing euphoria you might get on 4-HO-MET or other tryptamines, but just a subtle feeling of a good mood. I'm not experiencing any OEVs. I feel somewhat "breathless". It's reminiscent of the feeling I get on other tryptamines. I spend 5 minutes with a blindfold on attempting to draw out CEVs. Nothing significant materializes. After I take the blindfold off, my vision seems to be blurry for a minute or two afterwards. There is an erotic push at this point and I masturbate to orgasm. T + 1:10 (11:40 AM) I am starting to feel pretty hungry. This is an effect I noticed on my previous 20mg experience with 4-MEO-MIPT. On other tryptamines (such as 4-HO-MET), I typically become hungry only around 3-4 hours into the experience, since it's usually been ~6 hours since I last ate. In those instances, I know that I am hungry, and I do feel hungry, but the idea of eating sounds somewhat revolting--my appetite would be suppressed. In both of my experiences on 4-MEO-MIPT so far, I seemed to have an enhanced appetite. In both of my experiences on 4-MEO-MIPT so far, I seemed to have an enhanced appetite. After getting up to use the restroom, I notice some parts of my vision seeming to blur with my eyes open. T + 1:30 (12:00 PM) I go into my bedroom and lay on the bed for 20 minutes with a blindfold and noise-blocking headphones on. I definitely experience some degree of auditory hallucinations. There is a strobing hum effect which I often also hear on LSD. I am able to imagine a wide variety of alien sounds and music in my head, but I wasn't quite hearing it in my ears, like I would have on 4-HO-MIPT. I was definitely able to hear sounds "in my head" with greater detail than I normally would sober though. My CEVs seem to be building towards something, but they never really quite get there. There is definitely an increase in visual activity, but it's just indistinct dark neon purplish looking stuff. It seems to be coalescing into a very fast moving and intricate looking visual, but it never turns into anything distinct. I feel as if I am going to fall asleep as I lay there. I experience some minor tremors in my legs, but nothing more than 1 quick tremor in certain spots. I experience some compulsion to clench my jaw. I'm not experiencing much of a "headspace"; my thoughts feel pretty clear, as they normally would while sober. I am feeling very hungry after my closed eye journey, so I order some food. T + 1:50 (12:20 PM) I try listening to some psytrance with a blindfold on for 20 minutes. It seems like the CEVs are increased and move with the music, but no distinct visuals emerge. I experience some minor changes in felt bodily form, as one normally does on most hallucinogens. It was very mild. I got the sensation of moving through space at one point, but it was very fleeting. There are moments where I wander off into a vague psychedelic headspace. I very quickly return to a normal train of thought. It feels as if my analysis enhancement is improved. I feel pangs of empathy thinking about certain subjects, which is reminiscent of MDMA's empathogenic effects, but not anywhere near as strong (but it felt familiar, just like MDMA). I am able to get some detailed yet fleeting images in my mind's eye. My food that I ordered arrive. I decide to watch some TV while I eat, and try doing a few nitrous chargers to see if that has any synergy. T + 3:10 (1:40 PM) I do 10-20 nitrous chargers, trying a maximum of 4 at a time. I don't find the effects to be any different than doing nitrous by itself. I spend a while watching TV and eating my food. I feel pretty stuffed at one point, but 10 minutes later I am able to finish the rest of my food. I'm not feeling much of a body high or headspace at this point anymore. T + 3:30 (2:00 PM) I have a slight headache at this point. I'm unsure if it's from the 4-MEO-MIPT or from the nitrous, or other causes. T + 5:00 (3:30 PM) I feel back to baseline at this point, headache is gone. ==Summary The body high and euphoria definitely seemed to be slightly increased in intensity compared to 20mg. This stuff definitely increases your appetite. There's very little body load on this substance, even with the increased dose. Jaw clenching was the strongest effect I noticed, and it reminded me of MDMA. I believe this stuff has the potential to produce strong audio hallucinations at a higher dose, similar to 4-HO-MIPT, but they didn't quite reach that point yet. I'd say they were on par with audio hallucinations I normally get (with some concentration) from 200 micrograms of ALD-52. (of course, I could be wrong) The visuals were practically non-existent. I felt very teased by the CEVs, as it kept feeling like I was going to see something. The only consistent and significant OEV I noticed was blurred vision. (possibly nystagmus?) My pupils were never dilated at any point, and I checked several times during the experience. There seems to be a capability for a minor headspace, which reminded me most of MDMA, but with less intensity. As my thoughts would wander, I would "snap back" to a normal train of thought within 30 seconds to a minute. Overall, it didn't seem to have much consistent effect on my thought process at all. Didn't experience any time dilation/compression effects. ==Next steps I want to try this at 50mg to see if any visuals can be produced from this substance on its own. There is another experience report online at 50mg, claiming to get visuals, so I am curious. I want to try this substance in combination with 4-HO-MIPT, hopefully to produce the effects of a stronger and more consistent erotic push, more visuals, and a clearer headspace than one would normally get on 4-HO-MIPT. Possibly a reduced body load as well. Although I'd be happy with just increased eroticism without any increase in the challenging headspace.

我从12岁起就开始服用抗抑郁或抗焦虑药物 (感谢母亲). 我尝试过一些药物:帕克西尔,塞莱克萨,佐洛夫特,塞雷基尔,普罗扎克,阿提万,韦尔布特林等. 我总是服用阿提万来帮助我入睡, 为了让我在MRI前昏倒, 我被给了2毫克的阿提万, 我开始觉得有点高, 首先我服用了3毫克. 我感觉比吸大麻时更高. 我感到高兴, 整个身体都. 我感觉自己完全幸福, 我写了许多关于人性和和平的诗歌. 我跳舞着音乐, 感觉到音乐在我的周围振动. 我把一瓶水倒在我的头上, 只是为了看我是否还能感觉到什么 (我几乎没有感觉到什么!). 我喜欢这项工作, 唯一的问题是我已经发展出了很高的耐受性. 也许是因为我习惯了服用药物,也许不是, 我怀疑我是否能够再次感受到这种美妙的药物. 我很快发现我需要不断增加剂量, 我会非常昏昏欲睡 (因为它是一种抗焦虑药). 尽管我觉得清醒了, 但它仍然在我的系统, 有时我看起来高或'缓慢' 周围的人. 美国 药物 - 洛拉泽帕姆 (79):成和习惯性 (10),回顾 / 摘要 (11),不适用 (38)

May 13, 2008. erowid.org/exp/57834 DOSE: repeated Pharms - Lorazepam BODY WEIGHT: 145 lb Ever since I was 12 years old I have been on some kind of anti depressant or anti anxiety medication (thanks to my mother). To name a few, I have tried: paxil, celexa, zoloft, cerequil, prozac, ativan, wellbutrin etc. I always took ativan to help me go to sleep and thought it didn't do anything. One time, to knock me out before an MRI, I was given 2 mg ativan whereas I usually took .5mg. I started to feel a little high, and decided to try it recreationally. At first I took 3 mg. I felt a better high than I had ever from smoking pot. I felt euphoric, and my entire body was tingling. I felt completely happy and like I understood the world. I wrote pages and pages of poetry about human nature and peace. I was dancing to music and feeling it vibrating all around me. I dumped a bottle of water over my head just to see if I could still feel anything (and I barely did!). I loved it and did this for a while. The only thing is I've developed a high tolerance. Maybe because I'm so used to taking medication, or maybe not, but now if I even take 6mg I only get tired. I doubt I will ever be able to feel that wonderful on the drug again. Pretty quickly I find that I needed to keep increasing my doses, which is a huge pain. I will be very sleepy (as it is an anti-anxiety med). Even when I feel sober, it does stay in my system, and I sometimes appear high or 'slow' to others around me.

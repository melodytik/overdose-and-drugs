我个人对尼古丁蒸发的体验非常积极. 首先,我一直塞住的鼻已经清除, 我觉得这是因为我体内的氧气水平更高. 我使用的雾化剂 (阿尔布特罗尔) 效果类似, 我感受到更强的视力敏. 这种情况发生在我停药后开始服用SSRI时. 我越来越关注自己的环境. 我可以环顾周围, 看到我需要做的事情, 没有SSRI能够做到这一点. 之前的SSRI让我渴望酒精. 酒精增加了一些缺少的大脑化学物质, 使我能够感受到愉快的情绪. 这种需要已经消失了. 我有能力成为一个快乐的人. 尼古丁使我的食欲下降, 让我比以往任何时候都满足于少量食物. 由于多年大量饮酒, 我变得非常肥胖, 无论如何饮食或运动都不能让我摆脱肥胖. 现在, 体重开始减轻. 这也帮助我专注于学习, 提高注意力和记忆力. 我一直是个需要更高剂量的东西产生效果的人, 我可以喝一口或十口,我希望. 总之, 尼古丁, 纯粹的尼古丁, 蒸汽而不是吸烟, 我已经使用了两周了, 完全没有成或习惯的迹象. 没有生理上的需要或渴望, 只有有意识的愿望在我选择的时候使用它. 已使用的证券 发布时间:2019年3月30日 尼古丁 (383):健康益处 (32),治疗意图或结果 (49),提高表现 (50),烂的经验 (4),回顾 / 摘要 (11),不适用 (38)

I Have Been Using It for Two Weeks Nicotine by Finula Citation:   Finula. "I Have Been Using It for Two Weeks: An Experience with Nicotine (exp104133)". Erowid.org . Mar 30, 2019. erowid.org/exp/104133 DOSE: vaporized Nicotine BODY WEIGHT: 280 lb My personal experience with vaporized nicotine it extremely positive. First of all, my continually stuffed up sinuses have cleared and my allergies seem to be reduced. I have a feeling that I attribute to greater levels of oxygen in my system. It is similar to what I get from using a nebulizer (albuterol), used to help asthma. I experience greater visual acuity. This is something that happens when I start taking SSRI's after a period of discontinuance. More nerves are firing, I become more aware of and interested in my environment. For the first time in more years than I can count, I can look around me and see things that I need to do, and actually do things. This is something that no SSRI has been able to achieve. Previously the SSRIs made me crave alcohol. This was not addictive, but the alcohol added something lacking to the brain chemistry that made it possible for me to feel pleasant emotions. That need is gone. I am capable of being happy. The nicotine is decreasing my appetite, and causing me to be satisfied with smaller amounts of food than was ever the case previously. I had become morbidly obese from years of heavy alcohol consumption, and no amount of diet or exercise would rid me of it. Now, the weight is starting to come off. It has also helped me focus on my studies, increasing attention and retention. Dosage: I have been using nicotine liquid with a concentration of 2.4 mg per milliliter. I have always been a person who required a higher dose of anything to have effect, but I simply find the higher concentration more cost effective. I can have one puff or ten, as I wish. In sum, nicotine, pure nicotine vaped and not smoked, seems to be the wonder drug to shame all others. I have been using it for two weeks now, and there is absolutely no sign of addiction or habituation. There is no physiological need or craving, just the conscious desire to use it when I choose.

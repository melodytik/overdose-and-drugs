我喜欢茶的味道. 没有. 它和蜂蜜一起好吃. 经过2个月的每日3杯茶后, 我决定尝试一次性服用相同的剂量. 我和朋友一起煮茶 (茶6克,两者用0.5升水). 我们让它们在低温上加热 (最多是沸) 大约2小时. 这种液体几乎都是黑色的. 看起来就像杯子里的咖啡. 我们等了一会儿我们的饮料冷却下来 (加入蜂蜜之前是冷的) 有成为茶上的东西看起来像汽油在水上. 我们认为这是喝茶的最佳时机. 味道非常 (非常) 强烈, 我们等了一会儿, 咖啡因像炸弹一样激起, 我认为还有其他的感觉. 因为当我吃1500毫克咖啡因时, 只是茶的能量提升. 这种感觉有点沉醉, 但不像酒精. 似乎我落入了我所关注的一切, 我问我的朋友是否也感觉到了,他感觉到了. 我们第二天也做了同样的事情. 同样的感受和所有. 降落使我们maby更多的浪费,但大约相同. 我们决定不再喝茶, 笑着说'是的, 我们已经上了'. 我们坐了一会儿后都想要茶. 我们什么都做不了. 身体没有任何能量. 我们仍然远离茶的剩余的一天. 朋友打电话给我说,他觉得他必须喝茶. 我也有同样的感受. 我们认为喝两杯茶不会造成严重的成, 但如果我每天喝同样数量的茶, 可能 茶会导致上吗? 没有. 很抱歉我的英语不好 (如果有很多的话)... 其他: 茶 (447):一般 (1), 和习惯性 (10), 小组 (2-9) (17)

Feb 23, 2018. erowid.org/exp/65098 DOSE: 6 g oral Tea (tea) BODY WEIGHT: 65 kg I like the taste of tea. Mmm. Its good with honey. After 2 months of daily 3 tea cups I decided to try the same dose at 1 time. I and my friend made tea (6 grams of tea and 0.5 liter of water to both). We let them heat on low temperature (allmost boiling) for about 2 hours. The liquid was allmost black. Looked allmost like coffee in a glass cup. We waited for a while for our drinks to cool down (added honey before was cool) there become something on the tea that looked like gasoline on water. We thought that it was the best time to drink the teas. Taste was very (VERY) strong and lasted in mouth for long time. After both had finished the teas we waited a while and then suddenly: like a bomb the caffeine came on and we both was hyper. I think there was an other feeling with it too. Because when I eat 1500 mg of caffeine it still doesn't give me the same feeling. Just the energic boost of the tea. The feeling was bit intoxicated but not like alcohol. It was like I fell inside everything that I focused on, and snapped right back to normal world. I asked that does my friend feel it too, he felt it. We did the same at next day too. Same feelings and all. The comeing down made us maby more wasted but about the same. The day after that day we decided not to ''do tea'' anymore and laughed that ''yea we got addicted to it''. After we had sat a while both wanted tea. We could do nothing. No energy in the body at all. We still stayed away from the tea the rest of the day. Next morning my friend called me and said that it feels like he must get tea. I felt the same. At 3rd day after ''tea abuse'' we had energy to go play basketball so we think that at 2 high tea doses it doesnt cause a bad addiction but what about if I drink the same amount daily for a month!? Might tea cause an addiction??! Ps. Sorry my bad english (if there is lots of it)...

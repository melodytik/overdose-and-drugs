我有时使用瓦索普林来看看它是否会提高"思维清晰度". 但我发现思想清晰度保持不变或减少. 但是记忆似乎更容易保留, 如果我需要快速学习一堆事实, 无论如何, 我昨晚服用了4次瓦索普林, 我做了很多事情, 感觉很好, 所以我想 - - , 我慢慢地吞下了一些葡萄酒和一些伏特加, 我觉得很, 这是两杯酒后不寻常的, 胃部的感觉有点不舒服, 我处于一个不熟悉的思维空间. 一种不适和内在的紧张感, 我的血压略微升高. 我喝了大约四杯水, 为了确保没有宿醉, 减少副作用, 我喝了大约两杯酒和四杯水, 却没有冲动去尿, 我突然意识到在服用抗尿剂瓦索普林时, 不应该喝这么多液体. 由于不舒服和奇怪的头痛, 我睡不着好3个小时, 只有在服用瓦索普林药后12个小时, 我才能够尿尿, 现在我感觉好得多了. 我无法想象我整晚在里面有这么多液体, 我今天早上有轻微的头痛, 下次我会更认真地对待关于瓦索普林是抗尿剂 (不要喝太多) 的警告. 时间表: 10787 血管压素 (224), 酒精 (61): 背景不明 (20), 组合 (3)

Uncomfortable Evening Vasopressin & Alcohol by Fred Citation:   Fred. "Uncomfortable Evening: An Experience with Vasopressin & Alcohol (exp10787)". Erowid.org . Nov 24, 2001. erowid.org/exp/10787 DOSE: 4 shots insufflated Vasopressin 2.5 shots oral Alcohol BODY WEIGHT: 180 lb I have used Vasopressin on occasion to see if it will enhance 'clarity of thought'. It definitely has some effects, but I have found thought clarity staying the same or decreasing. However, memories seem to be easier to retain, which is great if I need to learn a bunch of facts, fast. Anyway, I took 4 puffs of Vasopressin last night - to see if it would motivate me or increase my organization while packing for a move. I got a lot done, and was feeling generally good, and so I thought - what the heck, I haven't had a drink of alcohol for over 2 months, might as well relax. So at T+2h after taking the Vasopressin, I ingested (slowly) some port with some vodka - probably about 2-3 shots-worth in all. I was feeling very tipsy, which is unusual after 2 drinks, but surprisingly clear-minded. The stomach feeling was a bit uncomfortable, and I was in an unfamiliar mindspace. A certain discomfort and inner tension, much like 'coming up' on a tryptamine or phenethylamine was present. My blood pressure was slightly elevated. It was then that I realized I was drinking lots of water - I had about 4 full glasses, which I always do when I drink alcohol, to ensure no hangover and fewer negative effects. So, I had consumed about 2 glasses of port/vodka and 4 glasses of water, yet I had no urge to pee -- I couldn't pee, my bladder was empty. Suddenly it struck me that it was NOT wise to drink so many liquids while taking Vasopressin - an anti-dieuretic. I couldn't sleep for a good 3 hours due to discomfort and a strange headache, and when I finally fell asleep and woke up this morning, I still didn't have to pee. It wasn't until about 12 hours later from taking the Vasopressin that I was finally able to pee and get some liquid (and processed alcohol, perhaps?) out of my system. I still feel slightly inebriated (which is crazy!) but I'm feeling much better now. I can't imagine having all those liquids in me all night, not going anywhere. I have a slight headache this morning, and I will take the warnings about Vasopressin being an anti-dieuretic (and not drinking too much) more seriously next time.

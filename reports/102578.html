T+0:00 - 将3至5毫克的5-美欧米普特混合到一杯水中. 为了避免这种情况, 我转向溶解剂量在水中的方法, 它和4-aco-DMT一起工作, 所以它应该对moxy有效. 我开始喝几口. 几乎立即我感觉到一个"第一警报", 这不是一个很明显的效果, 它可能是安慰剂, 这种熟悉的感觉让我放松. t+0:15 - 大约五分之一的水已经被饮. 我觉得自己充满活力, 但它与5米米特完全不同. 这种刺激不是令人不舒服的安非他命般的动, 有一些张力, 但不是很强. 我决定玩Dota2, t+0:50 - 三分之一已经消耗. 能有强烈的能量感, 虽然我的感知似乎微妙地改变了以一种无法识别的方式. 我通常比正常人打得更好. 一些短语会脱而出, 像是突出显示的, 这种效果会消失,当我看到突出显示的短语时, 一组新的短语会被突出显示. t+1:12 - 我开始感到强烈的冲动,类似于MDMA. 肌肉有点紧张, 但最重要的是我想要跳舞! 这让我感到很惊, 这是一款令人难以置信的游戏, 一款关于做你想做的事情的游戏. 我能感觉到键盘的振动在桌子,地板和我的脚上响起. 我的感官得到了显著的增强. 我可以感觉到空气, 我可以听到我邻居家的对话. 感觉一切都活着! 微妙的视觉图像出现, 我在精神上感到平静, 但在身体上我迫切需要做点什么. 我不知道它是什么,但我知道我想做它. 我知道我想做什么,我想跳舞! 另一个克皮拉塞坦的门下, 因为它是关于时间我采取它. 我坐在风扇前, 倒水在我的头和背部保持凉爽. 直到现在,我一直在享受,但这有点糟糕. 虽然我仍然需要一个风扇来感觉凉爽. 我从来没有经历过这种效果,所以我只能假设这是第二剂piracetam的结果. 皮拉的预剂量似乎很好. 旅行期间的提升是一个大不-不. 我走了一步,进入大自然. 晚些时候再见,旅行报告! 这种感觉完全消失了, 取而代之的是一种平滑, 迷幻的感觉. 很难描述; 酸的部分不是视觉,不是动的情感, 而是与环境融为一体的清晰和宁静. 在视觉上, 几乎感觉一切都是通过倾斜转移镜头看到的. 一切都是相同的尺寸,没有扭曲, 像之前的研究人员所说, 我在一个深沉的迷幻头脑空间, 我的漂亮的颜色在哪里? 我也意识到我经历过的过热很可能是因为我在电脑房间里度过时间 - - 它被用作加密货币的矿山, 身体负荷是不寻常的,有时是愉快的,有时不是. 效果大多已经消退,留下一个令人愉快的后光. 闭上眼睛会产生微弱的静态视觉效果, 我感到疲倦. 总结:整体来说, 这种组合是令人愉快的. 它增强了5-meo-mipt的三重方面, 虽然它不是我最喜欢的物质 (4-aco-dmt永远会保持这个称号) 但它肯定是最喜欢的, 但我必须警告人们不要在旅行期间服用piracetam - - 我服用了低剂量的5-meo-mipt, 保持安全,并学习像我这样的白痴. 其他: 5-MeO-MIPT (287),皮拉塞坦 (95):健康问题 (27),提高表现 (50),组合 (3),单独使用 (16)

Sensory and Thermal Overload 5-MeO-MIPT & Piracetam by psychosomaticdragon Citation:   psychosomaticdragon. "Sensory and Thermal Overload: An Experience with 5-MeO-MIPT & Piracetam (exp102578)". Jul 13, 2014. erowid.org/exp/102578 DOSE: T+ 0:00 4 mg oral 5-MeO-MIPT (powder / crystals) T+ 3:50 1 g oral Piracetam (capsule) BODY WEIGHT: 78 kg Due to the lack of reports on combining psychedelics with racetams, i decided to do this trip report. All entries are written as they happen. Important notes: Everyone responds to psychedelics differently. In my case, i've found that psychedelics last unusually long on me, and take a lot longer to kick in compared to others. A tab of LSD will affect me for a good 16 hours, and DOC lasts for days. As such, don't expect 5-meo-mipt to last as long for you as it does for me. Set and Setting I live a comfortable life. While i'm currently unemployed, some smart saving in previous years has left me with a bit of cash to experiment with some novel psychedelics. I've had a bag of 5-meo-mipt sitting in my stash for some time, and my previous experiments with it have been threshold doses, around 1~2mg. There's no particular reason i'm taking this, other than it feels like the right time to do so. For the past 3 days i've been taking 4g of piracetam a day, along with 2g of choline bitartrate. Again, no particular reason other than to see what happens. T+0:00 - Between 3 to 5 milligrams of 5-meo-mipt were mixed into a cup of water. My prior experience of this compound make me wary of the speedy rush during the come-up. To avoid this, i've switched to the method of dissolving the dose in water, then slowly sipping it until the desired effects are achieved. It's worked well with 4-aco-DMT, so it should work for moxy. I start with a few little sips. Almost immediately i feel a 'first alert', something i get with all psychedelics. It's not a very discernable effect, and it may be placebo, but there's a warm feeling creeping over my body. This familiar feeling keeps me relaxed. t+0:15 - About a fifth of the water has been sipped. I feel energetic, and focused. But it's very different to 5-meo-mipt on it's own. The stimulation isn't the uncomfortable amphetamine-like jitter, it's pure energy. There's some jaw tension, but it's not strong. I decide to play a bit of Dota 2 to give me something to focus on. t+0:50 - A third has been consumed. There's a strong sense of energy, and incredible focus. No empathogenic or psychedelic effects are noticeable yet, although my perception seems subtly altered in an unidentifiable way. Most noticeable was my performance in dota; i was generally playing much better than normal. t+0:55 - An unusual distortion i've noticed is that when reading text, certain phrases stand out, like they're highlighted or in a brighter font colour. This effect disappears when i look at the highlited phrase, and a new set of phrases becomes highlighted. t+1:12 - I start to feel a strong rush, similar to MDMA. There is slight muscle tension, but more than anything i feel like dancing! i put on some psytrance, and mellow out while playing minecraft. It strikes me how incredible the game is, a game about doing what you feel like doing. I can feel the vibrations from the keyboard echo through the table, into the floor, and up through my feet. My senses are enhanced considerably. t+3:40 - everything is so enhanced! i can feel the air, i can hear conversations in my neighbors house. Everything feels so alive! subtle visuals are appearing, and disappearing as soon as i see them. I feel at peace mentally, yet physically i'm urging to do something. I don't know what it is but i know i want to do it. t+3:50 - i figured out what i want to do, i want to dance! Another gram of piracetam down the hatch, because it's about time i took it. t+4:00 - My heat regulation seems all screwy. I'm sitting in front of a fan, pouring water on my head and back to keep cool. Up until now i was having a blast, but this is a bit crappy. t+4:05 - the heat problem seems mostly solved, although i still need a fan to feel cool. I've never had this effect before, so i can only assume it's a result of the second piracetam dose. Pre-dosing piracetam seems fine. Boosting during the trip is a big no-no. t+4:40 - I go for a walk, to get into nature. I'll see you later trip report! t+5:38 - the mdma feeling is completely gone, and replaced with a smooth, psychedelic feeling. It's hard to describe; it's the part of acid that's not the visuals, not the rolling emotions, but the clarity and peace of being one with your environment. Visually it almost feels like everything is seen through a tilt-shift lens. Everything is the same size, no distortions, and yet it all appears so tiny. As a previous researcher put it, it's insultingly non-visual. I'm in a deep psychedelic headspace, where are my pretty colours? I've also realized the overheating i experienced was likely due to spending time in my computer room - it's being used as a crypto-currency mine, and the room is unusually warm most of the time. The body load is unusual, sometimes pleasant, sometimes not. t+6:25 - The effects have mostly worn off, leaving me with a pleasant afterglow. Closing my eyes produces faint, static-like visuals, but nothing to really write about. t+7:00 - Back to baseline. I feel tired. Summary: The combination was overall pleasant. It enhanced the trippy aspects of 5-meo-mipt, without increasing the body load. While it's certainly not my favorite substance (4-aco-dmt will always hold that title) it's certainly a favorite, even better than MDMA in my eyes. But i have to warn people not to take piracetam during the trip - I was on a low dose of 5-meo-mipt, and the overheating was intense. Stay safe, and learn from idiots like me.

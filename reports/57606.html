我曾经可以获得相当多的迪兰丁药, 我真的很想上, 我最终服用了大量的迪兰丁 (大概从600毫克到1500毫克不等, 这种药物的效果非常奇怪, 我的记忆有点模糊. 我记得最惊人的效果是声音与感觉之间的难以形容的联系. 我认为这可能是共感, 但我没有在网上找到任何关于这种副作用的PDR副本中读到任何东西. 但我记得在服用药物后的前36-48小时内, 影响会在几天内慢慢消失. 起初的症状是昏昏欲睡,轻微的困惑 (我记得头非常模糊,很难清晰地思考),以及协调失常. 听觉/感官效应会在稍后出现, 但它们会比其他效应持续更长时间. 虽然这种体验并不愉快 (有时会让人感到不安), 我完全不推它用于娱乐用途, 我没有注意到任何认知上的改善. 其他: 2007年11月19日发布

Nov 19, 2007. erowid.org/exp/57606 DOSE: 1000 mg oral Pharms - Phenytoin BODY WEIGHT: 150 lb I used to have access to quite a bit of Dilantin because my father suffered from seizures but didn't take his medication like he should (mainly because of its interaction with alcohol). But, anyway, when I would run out of weed and anything else and I realllly wanted to get high I wound up taking very large doses of Dilantin (Varying from perhaps 600 up to 1500mg It's been awhile it is hard to remember). The effects of this drug were very strange and my memory is kind of hazy. The most startling effect that I remember was an indescribable connection between sounds and feeling. I suppose this is synesthesia, but I haven't read anything about that side-effect in any of the PDR copies I've found on the web. But I remember it being extremely pronounced for the first 36-48 hours after ingesting the drugs. The effects would slowly wear off over the course of a few days. The initial effects are sleepiness, mild confusion (I remember feeling extremely fuzzy headed, it was hard to think clearly), and loss of coordination. The auditory/sensory effects would kick in a little while later, but they would last much longer than the other effects. While the experience wasn't very pleasant (sometimes the synesthesia was kind of unnerving) I tried it a few times. I don't recommend it at all for recreational use, and I didn't notice any cognitive improvements.

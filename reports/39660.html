中文译文 致幻剂

这一切始于2004年11月中旬. 我决定和我的朋友尼尔一起去他家喝一些酒. 尼尔给我提供了一些大麻, 但我拒绝了, 因为我正在做毒品测试. 尼尔抽了几碗大麻, 我喝了五杯酒. 庆祝活动结束后, 我们回到学校, 我一直喝醉,直到我上了回家的巴士. 我的朋友约翰问我是否想在学校后在他的家里休息. 我在他的家下车. 我知道他有各种各样的药物. 我吸了60毫克的阿德拉尔来试图让我清醒, 我打电话给他们, 约翰告诉我他的哥哥在地下室种植了一棵'天使号角'植物. 据说,这会让你三天. 我们下楼. 我吃了三个叶子,约翰吃了两个. 我们放上一部电影等待旅程开始. 电影开始一小时后, 我开始感到很累, 几乎好像要睡着了. 我们站了起来,停止了电影,试图吃点东西. 我注意到我的运动能力受到严重影响, 我去所打尿. 我看了一下镜子, 看到我的瞳孔非常大. 我走出去告诉约翰关于我的学生的令人兴奋的消息, 他看着我,开始笑. 我们上去他的房间玩电子游戏. 我试图与汽车竞赛, 但我一直撞到墙壁和其他汽车. 在某个时候他的妹妹走进房间. 我尽我所能向她介绍自己. 很快之后, 我昏迷了. 我朋友克里斯,他不吸毒,刚刚下班,来到约翰的家, 现在所有的记忆都是克里斯告诉我的, 因为我不记得. 约翰的母亲回家,问我是否想吃点东西. 我很快就以惊慌的态度说不. 我一直叫他的母亲 虽然约翰的姓名不是安德森. 我们决定我太糟糕了在约翰的房子所以我们出去和克里斯的车挂. 我告诉约翰和克里斯,我要在森林里找我的储物柜. 他们建议我不要去, 因为我们不在学校. 在这个时候,我记得我的幻觉. 我在我的学校, 每个人都穿着白色衣服, 这所学校是黑白的. 我记得我从幻觉中看到的最常见的人是我的朋友德鲁, 克里斯告诉我,当我们站在他的车周围时, 我试图在父亲的车里换油, 我们都坐上了他的车, 走向商场的路上, 当我在抚摸皮座椅时, 显然,约翰的邻居之一在观察我在他们的后院游荡后, 打电话给警察. 警方把我带到了警察局. 我还记得我坐在警察车的后座, 警方打电话给我的父母, 他们来到了警察局. 我几乎被一辆车撞了. 我的父母带我回家. 我不记得回家的车程. 我记得我回家时, 我告诉他我必须在树林里尿尿,走下门廊的楼梯大约五步,然后转身回来. 我父亲问我为什么没有去所, 我母亲在电话里疯狂地试图从克里斯那里获取我服用的药物. 我记得我看着柜看到堆的谷物盒. 我也在疯狂地与那些不在场的人交谈, 从地板上起狗毛, 终于我着说'天使的号角'. 我父亲在大学读了一本关于布鲁格曼西亚和其他幻觉植物的书, 我不太记得去医院的路程. 我试图与父母沟通, 忘记了我曾服用过任何药物. 我记得在我说了这么多话之后, 我到达医院时, 约翰的父母是医生. 我躺在医院的床上, 我以为我在约翰的父母的卧室里打个小睡. 医生问我那天有没有服用药物, 我说没有. 我父母告诉我,我在向他们背诵一些东西,比如如何在微积分中解决相关的速率问题以及葡萄糖的化学结构. 医院的窗上写着我的微积分笔记, 我还告诉父亲,我的血液是虹红色的, 医生最终给我注射了葡萄糖和一种化学物质来抵消布鲁格曼西亚. 我停止了幻觉,醒来时觉得自己正要拔掉智. 我在医院和母亲在一起整晚, 说一些愚蠢的话, 比如说, '我需要在森林里去所'. 在从医院回家的路上, 幻觉持续了整整一天. 道路标志的字母不断地重新排列和改变. 终于在那晚, 幻觉几乎完全消失了. 总而言之,布鲁格曼西亚搞了我的头脑. 我知道我永远不会再这样做了. 时间表: 布鲁格曼西亚 (84):火车毁和旅行灾难 (7),宿醉 / 几天后 (46),艰难的经历 (5),小组 (2-9) (17)

Went to Jail and the Hospital Brugmansia by JC Citation:   JC. "Went to Jail and the Hospital: An Experience with Brugmansia (exp39660)". Erowid.org . Aug 20, 2006. erowid.org/exp/39660 DOSE: 3 leaves oral Brugmansia (fresh) BODY WEIGHT: 150 lb It all started in mid November 2004. Being in study period with nothing to do, I decided to leave with my friend Neil to drink some beers at his house. Neil offered me some marijuana but I declined seeing as that I was being drug tested. When we arrived at his house, Neil smoked a few bowls of his weed and I drank about five beers. We returned to school after the festivities were over and commenced to go to last period. I remained drunk until I got on the bus to go home. My friend John asked me if I wanted to chill at his house after school. I got off the bus at his house. While at his house, I learned he had an assortment of various drugs available. I snorted 60mg of Adderall to try and sober me up so that I could call and tell my parents my plans for the evening. After I called them, John told me that his brother was growing an 'Angels Trumpet' plant in the basement. Supposedly, it would make you trip for three days. We went down to the basement. I ate three leaves, John ate about two. We put on a movie waiting for the trip to start. About one hour into the movie, I started to feel very tired, almost as if I were to fall asleep. We stood up and stopped the movie and attempted to get something to eat. I noticed that my motor skills were severely affected as I would reach out to touch the door and it would take me several attempts before actually reaching the knob. I went to the bathroom to piss. I looked in the mirror and saw that my pupils were very dilated. I went out to tell John the exciting news about my pupils and he looked at me and started laughing. We went up to his room to play video games. I tried to race the car but I kept crashing into walls and other cars. At one point his sister came into the room. Seeing as I had never met her before, I introduced myself to her in the best of my ability. Soon after this, I blacked out. My friend Chris, who does not use drugs, had just gotten out of work and came over to John's house to hang out. All memories from here on are told to me by Chris because I do not remember. John's mother came home and asked me if I wanted something to eat. I quickly said no in a panicked way. I kept calling his mother Mrs. Anderson even though John's last name is not Anderson. We decided that I was too fucked up to be at John's house so we went out and hung around Chris' car. I told John and Chris that I was going to look for my locker in the woods. They advised me against it seeing as we were not at school. At this point, I remember my hallucination. I was at my school, everyone had pale white clothes on, and pale grey skin. The school was black and white. My friend Drew, who used to be the drummer in my band, was the prevalent person that I remember from this hallucination. Chris told me that as we were standing around his car, I was attempting to change the oil in my father's car which was not there. We all got in his car, which was really there, and decided to go to the mall. On the way to the mall, while I was caressing the leather seats, we were stopped by the police. Apparently, one of John's neighbors had called the cops after observing me wandering in their back yard in a delusional fashion. The cops brought me down to the station. I somewhat remember being in the back of the police car and trying to stick my finger through the metal grating. The police called my parents and they came down to the station. While they were there, I almost wandered into the road and got hit by a car. My parents brought me home. I don't remember the ride home. I do remember when I got home, my father punched me in the face and was trying to get out of me what drugs I had taken. I told him I had to take a piss in the woods, walked down the porch stairs about five steps, and turned around and came back in. My father asked why I hadn't gone to the bathroom and I got confrontational with him saying that I did. My mother was on the phone frantically trying to get from Chris what drugs I had taken. I remember looking over at the cabinet and seeing stacks and stacks of cereal boxes. I was also talking to people who weren't there in a frantic manner, and picking pieces of dog hair off the floor and playing with them and putting them back down. Eventually I muttered 'angel's trumpet'. My father, having read a book on brugmansia and other hallucinogenic plants in college, decided that I needed to be brought to the hospital. I don't remember much of the ride to the hospital. I was trying to communicate with my parents, having forgotten once again that I had taken any drugs. I remember that after everything I had said, my mother would say things like 'What the fuck?'. When I arrived at the hospital, I was hallucinating wildly. John's parents are doctors. While I was laying on the hospital bed, I thought I was in John's parents' bedroom taking a nap. The doctors were asking me if I had taken any drugs that day, I said no. My parents told me I was reciting things to them such as how to solve related rate problems in calculus and the chemical structure for glucose. I remember I was hallucinating that my calculus notes were written on the hospital curtain because of the strange patterns that were there. I also told my father that my blood was neon red because of the heart monitor on my finger. The doctors finally injected me with glucose and a chemical to counteract the brugmansia. I stopped hallucinating and woke up in the bed thinking I was getting my wisdom teeth taken out. I spent all night in the hospital with my mother saying foolish things such as, 'I need to go to the bathroom in the woods.' The hallucinations continued throughout the morning on the way home from the hospital. The road signs' letters kept rearranging and changing. Eventually that night, the hallucinations finally diminished almost completely. In conclusion, Brugmansia fucked with my head. I know I'll never do it again.

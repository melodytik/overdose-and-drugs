我们收到了1克AH-7921 (CAS编号41804-96-0) 来自一家知名的欧洲供应商. 这是一种细的晶体粉末, 它被溶解成40毫升水和10毫升汁的溶液,并在水浴中加热,因为它不会单独溶解. 使用口服注射器 (每毫升20毫克) 进行舌下注射,并每次在舌下保持10 - 15分钟. T - 1:15 过敏测试~5mg 血压读数为B的124: 88,脉冲率为113和P的105:65 (脉冲率误差). 经历了一些头. 在给药前B的脚受伤,这有所缓解. 经历了温暖,警觉,温和的刺激和清晰的思想的感觉. 我们感觉不像其他阿片类药物那么"愚蠢". 每个服用20毫克. 开始和胰岛素释放,并持续到晚上剩下的时间. 我们感到放松,有一些高兴和温暖的片"光". 每个服用20毫克. 经历了更大的疼痛缓解 - - B不再感觉到脚的疼痛,P不再感觉到肩膀受伤. 这种情绪也增加了. 眼睛有点缩, 有轻微到中度刺激的感觉. 有时还会感到恶心. 每个服用10毫克. 血压为115:78,心率为90. 我们都吃了三明治. T + 5:30 剂量为10mg (P) 和20mg (B). 虽然P感到有些动荡, 但仍然有阿片类药物和肌肉放松. 每个服用另一个20mg. 给出最后的20毫克. 我们都经历了一些手,B经历了一些抽 - - 经过一些阅读,这是相当常见的尾部的旅行. 最终的血压读数为:B 121:76 脉率为 100,P 124:62 脉率为 86. 总的来说,这是一次很好的体验. 这种化学物质本身味道不好, 整个晚上我们都感受到内心的温暖和高兴, 我们发现与他人交往是容易而愉快的. 我们还想跳舞,这是不寻常的. 虽然这可能是由于对我们所希望的剂量升高. 然而,由于缺乏关于该化合物的信息,这是一种推的方法. 吸了一碗大麻, 让许多已经消失的效果回来. 我是一个很容易生气的人, 不幸的是,在某些部分, 如果你像我一样, 容易生气, 也许在平静的环境中, 没有大声的音乐. 其他: 2013年8月4日发布 AH-7921 (595):一般 (1),第一次 (2),小组 (2-9) (17)

Aug 4, 2013. erowid.org/exp/100939 DOSE: 160 mg sublingual AH-7921 (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 130 lb I (P) and a friend (B) have low tolerance to opiates, with previous experience using Oxycodone, Dihydrocodeine, Codeine and Fentanyl. Setting was a party with a small number of friends. We received 1 gram of AH-7921 (CAS number 41804-96-0) from a reputable european vendor. This was a fine crystalline powder with some larger crystals in it. It was dissolved into a solution of 40ml water and 10ml of lemon juice and heated in a water bath, as it would not dissolve alone. This was dosed using an oral syringe (20mg per ml) sublingually and held under the tongue for 10-15 minutes each time. T - 1:15 Allergy test ~5mg T 0:00 Dosed 40mg. Blood pressure readings were 124:88 for B with a pulse rate of 113 and 105:65 for P (pulse rate error). T + 0:40 Experienced some light-headedness. T + 0:55 Mild pain relief - B had an injured foot before dosing, this was alleviated somewhat. T + 1:10 Experienced feelings of warmth, alertness, mild stimulation and clarity of thought. We felt less 'dumb' than on other opiates. T + 1:25 Dosed another 20mg each. T + 2:15 Itching and histamine release began and continued for the rest of the night. We felt relaxed, with some euphoria and a warm opiate 'glow'. T + 2:55 Dosed another 20mg each. T + 3:15 Greater pain relief was experienced - B could no longer feel the pain in his foot and P was no longer feeling a shoulder injury. Feelings of euphoria were increased. We had some pupil constriction by this point, and feelings of mild to moderate stimulation. There were also occasional feelings of nausea. T + 4:05 Dosed another 10mg each. T + 4:30 B's blood pressure readings were 128:91 with a pulse rate of 103. P's were 115:78 with a pulse rate of 90. T + 4:50 Sandwiches were consumed by both of us. T + 5:30 Dosed 10mg (P) and 20mg (B). P was experiencing some agitation, but still with opiate glow and muscle relaxation. T + 7:35 Dosed another 20mg each. T + 9:05 Dosed a final 20mg. T + 11:20 Both of us experienced some hand tremors, and B experienced some twitching - after some reading, this is fairly common at the tail end of a trip. Final blood pressure readings were: B 121:76 with a pulse rate of 100, and P 124:62 with a pulse rate of 86. Overall the experience was a good one. The chemical itself tastes foul, and is a little difficult to keep under the tongue. Throughout the evening we felt inner warmth and euphoria, mental clarity and muscular relaxation. We found socialising with others easy and pleasant. We also felt the urge to dance a lot, which was unusual. It was not as intense as we expected, though this may have been due to titrating up towards our desired doses. However, this is a recommended method due to the lack of information available about this compound. Smoking a bowl of marijuana brought back a lot of the effects after they had faded. I'm a pretty irritable person, and unfortunately during some parts this was magnified. So if you're like me, and get pissed off easy, maybe do this in a calm environment, without loud music.

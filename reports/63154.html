最近,我的朋友一直在遇到一个叫Beaver的男人, 他以20美元的价格出售, 现在,我已经有了我的公平的分数的药丸爆发,从氧化到水化到安比恩到安非他命, 我曾经在上学之夜, 吃过4毫克布诺芬, 好吧,有一点. 我告诉我的朋友我很嗜睡, 他走了, 我也做了. 我第二天早上醒来时仍然感觉到药物. 我试图站起来, 发现游泳更容易. 我根本没有在一起. 我告诉爸爸我生病了, 决定可以睡觉, 没有子. 这是一个循环重复的剩余的一天, 间歇的吐和睡眠. 真的是一件很棒的事情. 我认为这是一个简单的过量. 于是,当我的朋友在第一节给我一片2毫克的药片时, 半小时后, 我开始感觉到药物. 我也开始感到类似的头. 开始后一个半小时, 我还很难坐起来. 谈话需要太多的努力, 我曾经去所吐过几次, 我老师说我看起来不太好,没有和她争论. 我要求离开去健康房间,在那里我躺下了好四十分钟. 奇怪的是,躺下,我没有感觉到任何问题,只是止痛药. 我试图站起来, 却完全下坡, 我在吸食后大约两小时回到课堂上, 坐在沙发上试着做数学. 我很明显是生病, 经验丰富的人可以很容易地看到我在某种东西. 午餐钟响了,我地走出去坐在阳光下. 我意识到最好的办法就是打电话给, 吃完3个半小时后, 我终于回家了, 我上楼,躺下,然后睡觉了. 接下来的21个小时, 我开始了躺下,打电话,看电视, 睡觉的循环. 任何垂直的运动都会让我的头, 我记得我试图去拿起背包,然后去所吐. 奇怪的是,当我腰时,它消失了. 我会站起来, 冲动会超越我, 只有水,通常不愉快, 并没有改善的迹象. 我可以在第二天早上站起来, 我感觉到36个小时的标志, 同之前的时间一致. 我认为我对药物的耐受性很低. 我之前过量服用过药, 在午餐前吐了8次. 但布普伦诺芬的奇特之处在于, 躺下似乎是治疗疾病的方法. 我真的没有能力,躺在床上. 我相信我身上有一些东西不符合这些东西, 我不打算再次使用它. 他从来没有像我这样被倒过, 虽然他在回家的路上从我的车窗里了出来. 其他: 药品 - - 布普伦诺芬 (265):一般 (1),健康问题 (27),宿醉/几天后 (46),多天经历 (13),困难经历 (5),各种 (28)

Fool Me Once, Shame On You Buprenorphine by Bagels Citation:   Bagels. "Fool Me Once, Shame On You: An Experience with Buprenorphine (exp63154)". Erowid.org . Oct 10, 2010. erowid.org/exp/63154 DOSE: 4 mg sublingual Pharms - Buprenorphine (pill / tablet) 2 mg sublingual Pharms - Buprenorphine (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 130 lb So lately, my friend has been meeting this man we'll call Beaver, and buying buprenorphines off of him. He sells them at 20 bucks for 8 mg and 5 for 2. Now, I've had my fair share of pill popping, from oxycodone to hydrocodone to ambien to amphetamines, and I swear to you, that buprenorphine is by far the strongest and longest lasting painkiller I have found to date. I took 4 mg of buprenorphine, under the tongue, on a school night, once before, and felt just fine. Well, for a bit. Within the hour I was feeling very floaty, a feeling akin to vertigo, and telling my friend I was sleepy, he left and I did just that. I woke up the next morning still feeling the drug. I attempted to stand, and found out that swimming was much easier. I was not together at all. I told my dad I was sick and decided I could sleep the drug off and go to school in a few hours. No dice. This was a cycle repeated for the rest of the day, with interludes of vomiting and sleep. Great stuff, really. I figured it was a matter of simple overdose. So, when my friend presented me with a 2 mg tablet in first period, I said what the hell, and went with it. 50 minutes later I began to feel the drug. It was the run of the mill pain killer effect, but I began to get the same kind of dizziness. An hour and a half from the start, and I was having trouble simply sitting up. Talking required too much effort, and I felt too awful to attempt it. I went to the bathroom once or twice to try and throw up, but to no avail. My teacher said I didn't look too good, and didn't argue with her. I requested leave to go the health room, where I laid down for a good forty minutes. The strange thing is, laying down, I felt no problems, just the painkilling. When I tried to stand up, it went completely down hill and I felt incredibly nauseous. I went back to class approximately 2 hours after ingestion, where I sat on the couch and tried to do math. I was obviously sick, and an experienced person could have easily seen I was on something. The lunch bell rang, and I ambled outside to sit in the sun. I realised that the best thing I could do at that point was to call my mom and go home, which I did promptly. 3 and half hours after ingestion, I finally get home after a great bout of carsickness which ended anticlimatically, ie, without vomiting. I went upstairs, laid down, and went to sleep. For the next twenty one hours, I began a cycle of laying down, making phone calls, watching tv, and falling asleep. Any vertical movement caused my head to spin and the nausea to come on. I recall trying to go get my back pack, and going to the bathroom to throw up. The strange thing is, when I bent down, it went away. So I would stand up and the urge would overcome me, and bingo bango, vomit. Nothing but water, generally unpleasant, and not a sign of improvement. I could stand up the next morning, around 24-27 hours after ingestion. I did not feel right until the 36 hour mark, which concurred with the previous time. I suppose that, when all has been said and done, I have a very low tolerance for pills. I've overdosed on percocet before and threw up 8 times before lunch. But buprenorphine is strange in the fact that lying down seems to be a solution to the sickness. I was literally incapacitated and bedridden. I believe that there is something with me that does not agree with the stuff, and I don't plan on taking it again. My friend has built himself a tolerance up to 8, and he's never been floored like I was, although he threw up out of my car window on a ride home.

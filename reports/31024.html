我经常使用大麻, 也经常喝咖啡因. 我在某个时候买了一些"精力薄荷",其中包括瓜拉纳,高果糖玉米糖和红色染料. 我会一次吃7到10粒, 总体效果是素刺激, 喝一杯不太强的咖啡. 我决定看看是否可以从吸入中获得任何效果. 我从来没有吸过可卡因, 通过鼻子血管吸收某种物质, 允许通过血脑屏障进行更大的和. (THC 是一个血管收缩剂吗?) 瓜拉纳的吸入: 我用这个假设粉碎了这些颗粒, 足以制造出一条轨道, 我把一行分成两行, 我应该说我在过去的12小时里没有吃东西, 我吸了第一行,它像地狱一样燃烧 (像肉一样), 然后留下了刺痛和微微的甜味. 我的鼻子开始流,所以我走了第二行. 房间变得非常安静,和身体的感觉发生了前十到十五分钟. 没有耳,我记得. 我在完成第二行之后的10分钟, 感觉到了硬, 这是非常不寻常的. 我失去了我想记录的其余微妙效应的轨迹. 最后在最初的45分钟内, 现在,我知道我在哪里. 这就好像我视觉的风玻璃有一块石头飞到它和破裂连续性. 虽然我从周边仍然可以看得很好. 我不知道我的视力是否会恢复正常. 没必要说我没有触摸那些瓜拉纳药丸几个星期之后. 我还想知道是怎么发生的,是什么原因造成的. 其他: 瓜拉纳 (117):一个人 (16),一般 (1)

Superstimulative Effects Guarana by Josh Citation:   Josh. "Superstimulative Effects: An Experience with Guarana (exp31024)". Erowid.org . Feb 20, 2004. erowid.org/exp/31024 DOSE: 2 lines insufflated Guarana (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 210 lb Background: I am a regular cannabis user and also regularly consume caffeine. At one point I acquired some 'energy mints', which contained among other things guarana, high-fructose corn syrup, and a red dye. These were small hard pellets, cinnamon flavored, and I would eat about 7-10 at a time with the general effect of xanthine stimulation, about a cup of not-too-strong coffee. After a few weeks of eating these off ann on, I decided to see what if any effect could be gained from insufflation. I have never used cocaine, but have insufflated THC with the following result time and again: a substantially different feeling of onset and effect when snorted. My self-educated guess suggested that absorbtion of a substance through blood vessels in the nose allowes for a greater saturation through the blood-brain barrier. (Is THC a vasoconstrictor?) Insufflation of Guarana: Armed with this hypothesis, I crushed these pellets, enough to make one rail, approximately the normal dose of 7-10. I divided the one line into two and then took some deep breaths and relaxed myself. I should say I had not eaten in the previous 12 hours, and neither had any cannabis nor caffeine. I snorted the first line and it burned like hell (as cinnamon will do), then remained tingly and slightly sweet-smelling. My nose began to run so I took the second line. Within seconds there was a noticable effect, with the room becoming very quiet, and the feeling of bodily buzzing occured for the first ten to fifteen minutes. No tinnitus that I can recall. The peak was about 10 minutes after finishing the second line, when I felt a stiffness, or what felt like a hand grabbing the back of my neck and head. This was very unusual. This tense feeling was uncomfortable, and I lost track of the rest of the subtle effects I was hoping to catalogue. Finally within the first 45 minutes it had subsided, as had most of the other effects. However, about an hour later I was driving around town when my vision became disrupted. It was as if the windshield of my vision had a stone fly into it and crack the continuity. There was a definite spot where visual acuity was distorted although I could still see perfectly well from the periphery. This was a VERY unexpected and unpleasent occurance, as I did not know if my vision would ever return to normal. Needless to say I didn't touch those guarana pills for a few weeks after that. My normal vision returned later that day, but I am still curious as to how or what would cause this to happen.

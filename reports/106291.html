Jun 10, 2026. erowid.org/exp/106291 DOSE: 400 mg oral Pharms - Tramadol (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 195 lb Hello all. To start off, I would like to go through a brief history of my drug use. I was completely anti-drug up until I reached about 15 years old, at which point I started smoking marijuana. This stuck as the only drug I would use, how's see as the years passed I would start using others. These included cocaine, air duster, many types of opiates and opioids, LSD, mushrooms, 2CI, mescaline, synthetic cannibis, and many others I cannot seem to recall. The biggest issue of all of these seemed to be my addiction to opiates. I started out taking one or two 5mg tabs of Vicodin as needed for pain and such, however this quickly escalated to 10 or 20 tabs a day. I would get them from my mothers stash or from friends on the street. After a long time of destructive tendencies, I decided to check myself into rehab and get clean. This happened and I was off of drugs completely. However I went to my family doctor for back pain that I've had since I was very little and asked him to give me something that I could not get high off of and that was not addictive. He gave me tramadol. Silly man. I was taking the normal dose as prescribed, two 50mg tabs every 8 hours or so, not to exceed 400mgs a day. After doing some research I found that one could definitely get high off of this substance and started trying to do so myself. I started taking 4 tabs, 6 tabs, then eventually even 8 tabs (400mg) at a time. I would get a script that was supposed to last a month and it would be gone in 6 or 7 days. The. I would wait a while and fill it again and again and again. It was a vicious cycle. The events following happened to me in November of 2014. I was home from college for thanksgiving break and woke up in the morning to take my morning dose of tramadol (400mgs or 8 tabs). After taking the pills, I ate some cereal for breakfast and went upstairs to let my dogs and talk with my mom. Now... Here is what I remember. I was talking to my mom when all of the sudden I was on my back, searing pain in my shoulders, being held down by many people. Thinking this was a dream, I kept trying to wake up as well as screaming 'MOOOOM!!!' at the top of my lungs for help. No matter what I did I could not seem to wake up. Well, it turned out it was not a dream. What happened was after I went up to talk to my mom, I had a terrible seizure and was unconscious for a few minutes. I was unresponsive and not breathing, so they called an ambulance. I had a terrible seizure and was unconscious for a few minutes. I was unresponsive and not breathing, so they called an ambulance. The medics arrived and tried to help but I was batting them away. They had said that if I didn't stop they were going to call the cops... Which they did. The cops came and were wrestling with me to keep me restrained. This is when I came to. There were 6 officers holding me down, with 1 sitting on each of my arms which accounted for the pain in my arms as they were dislocated due to the pressure. This wrestling match ensued for a good 10 minutes until I was sedated and they loaded me into the ambulance. I then again lost consciousness. I woke up the next day in the hospital hooked up to machines and drips. I was told what had happened and was completely shocked. I have never had a bad experience with drugs in the past. After another day or two in the ICU, I was released. I then was arrested 2 months later for ELEVEN FELONIOUS COUNTS of battery on an officer and battery on public officials. Eleven counts... They carried a max of 27.5 years in prison. I am still going through the courts to see what is going to happen. I have never again used tramadol and have since remained clean from all substances apart from nicotine and caffeine. I hope this report stops even one person from taking this devilish substance.

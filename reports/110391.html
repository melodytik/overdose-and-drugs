Although the report is included in the collection, the substance might be something other than the author believed it to be.] Many attempts with different doses. Biggest attempt was 120mg dose of MET freebase, loaded in a bowl, smoked with tobacco from a pipe. Took a clean hit, then attempted to heat up the sticky brown oily stuff that formed to smoke it. Regardless, the effects were slim to none. MET freebase does not do anything by itself when smoked. If it is anything like DMT with respect to tolerance, then my attempts at getting anything out of this drug were a complete failure, and it is not a good drug. However, if there is a cross tolerance with MET-OH, then this might be the problem. (MET-OH was very good orally. I took 10mg with water 2 days before these attempts.) The MET freebase was provided by a reputable vendor, and was in the form of a moderately brown powder. This is only what is 100% discernible from my attempts. However, I have personally completely lost faith in this drug, and will never procure it again, as I believe it is a fail. There are other drugs that will provide much better effects.

Feb 13, 2017. erowid.org/exp/109971 DOSE: T+ 0:00 5 mg insufflated N-Ethylhexedrone (powder / crystals) T+ 0:15 15 mg insufflated N-Ethylhexedrone (powder / crystals) T+ 0:00 20 mg insufflated N-Ethylhexedrone (powder / crystals) T+ 0:00 20 mg insufflated N-Ethylhexedrone (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 155 lb I have experience with Cannabis, RX opiates, RX stimulants, LSD, Mushrooms, Xanax, Coke once that was probably mostly cut, and 4-ho-met. I need to preface this by saying that stimulants do not seem to have a very pronounced effect on me, even at medium to high doses. I also have never experienced what I would consider a “rush” from any stimulant. I received a 1 gram sample of Hexen from a trusted source, and the crystals seemed consistent with what they should look like. I started off measuring 20 mg with a scale accurate ~2mg. I then split it into 4 equal piles and insufflated 5 mg. Slight burn but nothing worse than Adderall. I waited 15 minutes and the effects were very minimal, similar to caffeine when I had no tolerance. I then insufflated the remaining 15 mg over the next 30 minutes. The effects are definitely present similar to 20 mg of Adderall. I received no rush and no euphoria. My focus is definitely increased and I clean my room and work on homework for the next two hours. I decide to weigh out another 20 mg, and proceed to insufflate it in 2 lines. The effects are increased and similar to what I had felt earlier. Again, no rush and no euphoria. I then insufflate another 20 mg about 35 minutes after the last dose. Effects are very prevalent and I definitely have a lot of energy. A friend stopped into my room and I have a little difficulty conversing fluently, but nothing noticeable to my friend. In summary: I had purchased this drug because I had read reports that it was similar to coke, and I wanted to achieve a rush and euphoria people claim to get from stimulants. In the past the highest dose of any stimulant I had taken was 70 mg of IR Adderall, and this was disappointing with no euphoria. Of the 3 stimulants I have taken in the past (Adderall, vyvanse, and concerta, I don’t count the coke bcz it had no effects), adderall and vyvanse caused FREQUENT urination, I'm talking 5 times an hour, and difficulty urinating. With the 70 mg of Adderall dose I had taken it was so bad that it ruined the entire experience. I would pee a tiny amount, leave the bathroom, and automatically feel the need to pee again. With Hexen there was definitely frequent urination, but it was manageable and not that uncomfortable. My appetite was suppressed, but I still managed to eat a grilled cheese and some fries, so it is definitely possible to eat on this substance. I had planned to use this on nights when I go out to drink, but I don’t really see that much of a potential for this with me. The comedown was very minimal and I hardly even noticed it. I would say that the increased focus similar to Adderall lasted for 5 hours after ingestion of my last dose, and sleep was possible 7 hours after. I plan to try a higher dose in the future, but for now I can see this as a replacement for Adderall and similar study aids in doses around 20 mg. Please take my experience with a grain of salt, because all stimulants seem to not have a strong effect on me, and there are reports of people taking 20 mg and feeling effects similar to coke.

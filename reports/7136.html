[Erowid的注意事项: 大多数Coricidin含有CPM (胺酸),高剂量可能是危险的. 看看 品牌警告 更多信息] 我们决定和她的和小兄弟姐妹一起去沃尔玛购买一双鞋. 我们走到药房, 发现如同往常一样, 我们决定我偷一些. 所以我拿起3个盒子,她也这样做了. 我们开始偷走它们. 我们回家,每个人都拿了一盒. 我们将其余部分分成两半. 星期六我又拿了一个盒子. 星期天我醒来时, 仍然感觉很强烈. 我真的不记得我们如何获得更多, 我们去了当地的公园, 任何东西都在移动, 我看起来半疯狂,我的瞳孔大约是硬币大小. 现在,我的大脑中存在一种化学不平衡, 所以抑郁症总是存在. 我开始感到丧,所以当我回家时,我又拿了一个盒子. 我在地板上躺着听Godsmack的Voodoo, 通常我不会听. 我觉得我必须休息几天. 我母亲带我和我哥哥去沃尔玛, 我偷了两个盒子的科里西丁. 我在回家的时候吃了一杯. 我很确定自己会死. 我打电话给我的女朋友, 哭着, 流着汗, 不知道自己在做什么. 我告诉了她,她告诉我这将是好的. 只是不再忍受了. 我向说晚安,因为她要睡觉了. 我们发生了争. 我很生气, 拿走了另一盒. 我再次打电话给她, 她开始哭泣和尖叫, 她把她的母亲给我打电话,她安慰我. 第二天我醒来时,我仍然非常糟糕. 我今天早上去看了我的精神病医生. 后来我回到家, 开始感到非常丧. 我拿着剃须刀砍伤了自己的手腕和前臂12次, 我走到车库,看到我大出血,赶快把我送到医院. 我把所有的药物都吐出来了. 接着他们把我送到精神病院. 我在那里呆了7天. 我的血压高了4天. 我每天睡15-16个小时, 我今天刚出来, 我试图向自己承诺不再服用它们... 我可能会再次接受他们. 对于任何休使用Coricidin的人来说, 我不会骚扰你, 我希望我已经. 其他: 非尼拉胺 (164),DXM (22):各种 (28),旅行后的问题 (8),抑郁症 (15),过量服用 (29),火车事故和旅行灾难 (7),艰难的经历 (5)

Hell from Coricidin DXM (Coricidin with CPM) by Alex S. Citation:   Alex S.. "Hell from Coricidin: An Experience with DXM (Coricidin with CPM) (exp7136)". Erowid.org . Nov 18, 2002. erowid.org/exp/7136 DOSE: oral DXM oral Chlorpheniramine Maleate BODY WEIGHT: 135 lb [Erowid Note: Most Coricidin contains CPM (Chlorpheniramine Maleate) which can be dangerous in high doses. See DXM Brand Warnings for more info.] Well,it all started on a Friday afternoon at my girlfriends house.We decided that we would go with her mom and younger siblings to wal-mart to buy a pair of shoes. We wandered over to the pharmacy and noticed as we always do, the Coricidin Cough & Cold Tablets. We decided that I would steal some. So I picked up 3 boxes as she did also. We proceeded to steal them. We got home and took 1 box each. We then split the rest evenly. On saturday I took another box. Then on Sunday when I woke up I still felt it pretty strong. I dont really remember how we managed to get more but on that Sunday I took a box at about 11:00 a.m. We went to a local park and man was I hammered. Everything was moving even when I was standing still. I looked half-crazy and my pupils were about the size of pennies. Now, I have a chemical imbalance in my brain known as bipolar disorder. So depression is always around. I started feeling depressed so when I got home I took another box. Bad Idea....I stayed up half the night laying on the floor listening to Voodoo by Godsmack, which I ordinarily wouldnt be listening to. The next day when I woke up I knew I had to chill out for a couple of days. My mother drove me and my brother to wal-mart to buy a fish for him. I stole 2 more boxes of Coricidin. I took one when we got home. I was pretty sure that i was going to die. I called my girlfriend on the phone crying,sweating horribly,and not knowing what I was doing. I told her about it and she told me that it would be ok. Just not to take anymore. I told my mom good night,because she was going to sleep. We got into an argument. I then got really mad and took the other box. I called her again and she started crying and screaming because an overdose was in question we thought. She put her mom on the phone and she comforted me. The next day I woke up and I was still really fucked up. I went to see my psychiatrist in the morning. Then when I got home I started feeling really depressed. I took a razorblade and slashed my wrist and forearm 12 times...blood galore, dripping all over the floor. My mom came out to the garage and saw me bleeding pretty bad and rushed me to the hospital. I spit it all out about all the pills. They then committed me to a mental hospital. I spent 7 days in there. My blood pressure was high for 4 days. I slept for about 15-16 hours a day for the 1st 3 days. I just got out today and I'm trying to promise myself not to take them anymore...but who knows what will happen? I may take them again. For any recreational Coricidin users out there..I'm not going to harass you about it..just be careful. I wish i would have been.

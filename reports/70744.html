几天前,在4月20日星期日的满月之夜, 我经历了一件奇怪的事, 我和我的两个同伴喝了20克缓慢造的caapi, 20分钟后,一个含有250毫克非常纯净的DMT的"00"凝被取出. 我对使用DMT提取物有点担心, 我之前用过两次, 终于有一个机会出现了. 对于我来说, 这种经历花了很长时间才发生, 我朋友们很快就陷入了一种深沉的惚状态. 我朋友有一位印第安人视他, 几小时后, 我突然陷入了深沉的惚状态. 实际上就像一个开关被打开了. 我意识到这一点30秒后, 我感觉到一种强烈的,轻微威胁的电感入我的大脑, 然而,这比本应的要难得多. 我总是得到一个漂亮的清洁. 我从来没有清洗过. 几次我成功地吃了太多的, 我感到很恶心, 但实际上无法清洗. 卡皮和叙利亚路似乎是强烈的冲突 - - 他们不喜欢对方. 经历不断积累, 这就是恐怖的感觉表现出来的地方. 这很奇怪. 我认为自己是一个经验丰富的迷幻冒险家, 没有什么明显的产生这些感觉, 经验绝对是强烈的, 我也在此次旅行中更了解叙利亚街道的个性. 有时我的意识似乎升高到接近绝地级别. 叙利亚是一种非常强大的植物, 我听说caapi被称为"光". 叙利亚街是'黑暗的', 我不是说这么糟糕, 卡皮的"光"也在那里, 而DMT是作为一种提取物而不是原始的植物添加剂, 没有真正的指导 我没有参考框架或导航方式. 现在我知道我在哪里. 音乐是一个很好的盟友, 在达到高峰时,每个人都处于非常深的惚状态, 并意识到一种声, 这种声音或振动是所有物质和能量的振荡... 总的来说,这次经历持续了5个半小时, 是我最奇怪,最有趣的经历之一. 我个人对这次旅行的结论是: - 我不会使用叙利亚酒在未来喝亚华斯卡. 它本身就是一种强大的植物, 这只是我的看法. 我绝对不建议将其与Caapi结合起来. - 提取不是最好的方式. 在这种情况下, 吸收原始,纯化化学物质与造植物并以这种方式饮用它们的体验有很大不同. 造真实的阿雅华斯卡植物是我唯一的选择. 其他: 2009年8月9日发布 DMT (18),叙利亚路 (45),巴尼斯特里奥普西斯caapi (169),华斯卡组合 (269):小组 (2-9) (17),组合 (3),一般 (1)

One Strange Dream Huasca Combo (B. caapi, Syrian Rue, & DMT) by Sam Citation:   Sam. "One Strange Dream: An Experience with Huasca Combo (B. caapi, Syrian Rue, & DMT) (exp70744)". Erowid.org . Aug 9, 2009. erowid.org/exp/70744 DOSE: T+ 0:00 20 g oral Banisteriopsis caapi (tea) T+ 0:00 1 g oral Syrian Rue (extract) T+ 0:20 250 mg oral DMT (extract) BODY WEIGHT: 72 kg I had a strange experience a few nights ago, on Sunday April 20th on the night of the full moon, which I thought I would share with anyone who might be interested to hear such tales... Me and my two companions drank 20g of slow brewed caapi, taken as a shot, following 1g of 4x Syrian Rue extract taken in a '00' gel cap. 20 minutes later a '00' gel cap of 250mg of very pure DMT extracted from Mimosa was taken. I was a little apprehensive about using DMT extract again, I had used it twice before, but is much more keen on brewing with the plants the way nature intended, . However, this had been planned for a long time, and an opportunity had finally presented itself. For me, the experience took a very long time to come on which itself was unusual. My friends were in quite a deep trance quite rapidly. One of my friends had an Indian looking down on him, which he said was providing some guidance. Then, quite suddenly, around a few hours in, I was in a deep trance. It was literally like a switch had been flipped. 30 seconds after I was aware of this I felt this very powerful, slightly menacing electrical feeling creeping into my brain, and I knew it was time to purge. However, this was a lot harder than it should have been. With caapi I always get a nice clean purge. With Syrian rue, I never purge. The few times I managed to take too much rue, I felt very nauseous but was actually unable to purge. The caapi and the Syrian rue seemed to be clashing strongly - they DID NOT like each other. I failed to purge much at all, and the experience kept on building, and this is where feelings of terror made themselves known. This was strange. I considers myself quite an experienced psychedelic adventurer, and there was nothing obvious producing these feelings, the experience was definitely intense but it was like a more basic, primal, animalistic part of my brain was screaming 'FLIGHT OR FIGHT', the foundations of the ego were being shaken, and simply overriding the more advanced parts of the brain. Also during this trip I got to know the personality of the Syrian Rue a bit better. At times my awareness did seem heightened to near Jedi levels. Syrian Rue is a very powerful plant, and not one that should be taken lightly. I have heard caapi referred to as 'the light'. Well Syrian Rue is 'DARK', and I don't mean this in a bad way, but I felt this SO vividly. The 'light' of the caapi was also there, but much further off, while the rue was enveloping me, much closer. The fact that the caapi and Syrian Rue were clashing, and the DMT was taken as an extract, not as the raw, admixture plant, meant the experience HAD NO REAL GUIDANCE There was a little, but for a good hour or more the experience resembled the chaotic intensity of a strong smoked DMT experience, I had no frame of reference or way of navigating. Music proved a good ally, and kept the experience moving. While peaking, everyone was in a very deep trance, and became aware of a humming, a bit like ommmmmmmmmm... Could the DMT act like a cosmic tuning fork, and this sound or vibration is the oscillation of all matter and energy... it was strange. All in all, the experience lasted a good 5/6 hours, and was one of my strangest, and most interesting experiences. My personal conclusions from the trip: - I shall not be using Syrian rue when drinking ayahuasca in the future. It is a powerful plant in its own right, and belongs to a different part of the world, and should not be combined with ayahuasca. Just my opinion. I certainly wouldn't recommend combining it with Caapi. - Extract is not the way to go. In this case taking a raw, purified chemical is very much a different experience to brewing the plants and drinking them that way. Au natural, brewing the authentic ayahuasca plants properly is the only way to go for me.

这份报告是我大约一年前在EPT上参加的两次旅行的汇编. 我会尽可能地保持它们与原始相近. 原标题:EPT - 温暖和温柔, 野生色彩. 所以我昨天通过一个DAB装置, 免费吸入70毫克EPT. 烟雾是相当光滑的 (尽管仍然很粗,像DMT). 我把大部分的旅程都花在闭着眼睛听爵士乐. 这使得这次旅行是有史以来最美好的旅行! 音乐是天堂般的, 视觉是空气般的, 流动的和碎形, 但对我来说最突出的是颜色! 我基本上看到了所有的颜色. 它们的色调最柔和, 配合最独特. 我闭上眼睛看着红,然后,一个最奇特的绿色爆发,然后一个爆发柔软的色,然后一个爆发温暖的紫色. 这种颜色真是太棒了! 另一件值得注意的事情是我的身体感觉非常棒. 我感觉到这令人难以置信的温暖. 我是最舒适和放松我在很长一段时间. 对于我来说,三胺使得质感感觉非常柔软, 感觉就像我在一个由云组成的床上, 物体有着泰迪熊的纹理. 我也觉得自己的头脑很清晰. 我从来没有觉得自己在精神上倒. 总的来说,10/10初学者胺,下次会吸100毫克. 我认为增加剂量可能会带来我读过的那些强烈的科幻视觉效果. 我还剩下110毫克的EPT, 接下来发生的是严重的混乱和相当奇怪的身体负荷. 70毫克感觉像一个温暖的毯子, 但这个剂量感觉太温暖和不舒服的刺痛. 这让我想起了DIPT的身体高. 视觉效果仍然令人失望, 似乎EPT不是一种非常可视的物质, 音乐也很好听, 但在接下来的两个半小时里, 与气相结合也没有增加视觉方面. 我决定不想听音乐, 在旅行的大部分时间里, 这种化学物质确实有内省力, 总的来说,这次旅行持续了2.5个小时. 大量的EPT基本上是缺少的, 在适当的剂量下, 精神上是我服用过的最清晰的迷幻药. 视觉效果让人想起DPT, 虽然70毫克的剂量很好, 适合抱, 时间表: 119451 发布时间:2026年3月11日 EPT (776):一个人 (16),回顾 / 摘要 (11),一般 (1)

Mild, Yet Warm, Gentle and Beautiful EPT by Shiratakipoodles From Reddit Collection Citation:   Shiratakipoodles. "Mild, Yet Warm, Gentle and Beautiful: An Experience with EPT (exp119451)". Erowid.org . Mar 11, 2026. erowid.org/exp/119451 DOSE: 70 - 110 mg vaporized EPT inhaled Nitrous Oxide BODY WEIGHT: 64 kg This report is a compilation of two trips I partook on EPT, about a year ago. I'll try to keep them as close to original as possible. *** Original title: EPT - warm and gentle, wild colours. So I vaped 70mg EPT freebased yesterday, through a dab rig. Worked flawlessly, and the smoke was pretty smooth (still harsh tho, like DMT). I then spent the majority of the trip listening to some jazz with my eyes closed. This resulted in the loveliest trip ever! The music was heavenly, the visuals were ethereal, flowing and fractal, not many defined shapes, just suggestions of shapes. But the outstanding thing for me was the colours! I saw basically all the colours. And they were in the softest hues, and in the most unique combinations. I'd have my eyes closed seeing velvetty red, then, a burst of the most peculiar shade green, then a burst soft orange, then a burst of warm violet. Amazing colours! Another notable thing was how amazing my body felt. I felt this overwhelming warmth all over. I was the most comfortable and relaxed I've been in a long time. Tryptamines for me make textures feel very soft in general, but EPT is the softest for sure. Felt like I was on a bed made of cloud and objects had the texture of a teddy bear. I also felt very clear headed. At no point did I feel like I was tripping per se mentally. Overall, 10/10 beginner tryptamine, will smoke 100mg next time. *** So, since my last trip at 70mg, which was visually kinda disappointing but had amazing body high, clear head and wonderful colours, I concluded that upping the dose might bring out those intense sci-fi visuals I read about. I had some 110mg left of my EPT, so I freebased and vaped it. What followed was intense confusion and quite a weird body load. 70mg felt like warm blanket, but this dose felt way too warm and uncomfortably prickly. Reminded me of the DiPT body high. Visuals were quite disappointing still, ethereal and vague. Seems like EPT is not a very visual substance, and more of a body high sensation. Music was okay but for the next 2,5 hours I was restless and couldn't find a comfortable spot. Combining with nitrous did not increase the visual aspect either. I then decided that I don't wanna listen to music and spent most of the trip in silence in deep introspection, which proved quite fruitful. This chemical definitely has the introspective push, but there's better substances for that imo. Overall the trip lasted 2.5ish hours. High dose EPT is mostly lacking, there's better tryptamines out there. *** EPT has a charm, it's very nice and comfy at the appropriate dose, and mentally the clearest psychedelic I've taken. The visuals are reminiscent of coming down from DPT, but very mild and vague at both doses I tried. It's wonderful at 70mg and would be amazing for cuddling, but is lacking depth or intricateness in my experience.

(每个人的合理容量的想法都会有所不同,但我的意思是我可以说话,直线行走等等) 我真的很高兴尝试Methoxetamine, 因为我读过K用户的其他报告, 时间是凌晨3点 我刚刚从演出回来, 几个人在我们的公寓里从自己的夜晚回家, 时间是凌晨3点半 决定与一个不那么有经验的吸毒者,女性. 很长一段时间没有发生什么,可能30分钟? 时间是凌晨4点 剂量 50mg 我决定再给药, 因为它似乎没有任何作用, 在这个点上,时间已经扩展到我可以准确的点. 我知道我再服用了几次, 因为我是个瓜, 我把它推得太远了. 我不确定我服用了多少, 因为我把很多药倒在床上. 这是一件好事. 可能是早上7点 我几乎无法控制嘴巴和大脑之间的交流. 我知道周围发生了什么, 却没有办法向任何人传达我想说的话, 我会说它类似于拥有3秒记忆. 你知道你想做什么,然后你立刻忘记了. 我很困惑,试图找出你为什么困惑. 这让你更加困惑. 这不是一个有趣的经历. 我预计在最初的""之后, 让我越来越难以应对日常任务, 这与"k"不同, 因为它没有让我感觉到太空旅行沉浸在一个温暖的蜂蜜浴中, 从大约? 上午9点到下午4点左右 很难说话,心跳加快,手脚明显. 感觉好像我在脑海中跳过了一些画面, 仍然很难走路. 现在是最可怕的部分: 这种药物不像胺, 胺可以让我在半小时到一个小时内恢复正常. 我被毁了大约24小时. 两天之内, 几乎没有睡觉. 走路是可能的, 但躺下一段时间后我的平衡被摧毁. 语音仍然模糊. 完全不了解单词/数字/方程等, 任何语言的东西几乎是不可能理解的. 我不为此感到自豪, 我绝对不认为这是一个好主意. 晚上八点到十一点我感到非常丧和困惑. 我和女朋友在电话里深情地聊天, 结论 我强烈建议任何试验这种由于其与胺的关系非常小心. 我相信在适当的环境下, 这可能是一个很好的体验, 我绝对不会再试用甲基胺, 其他: 甲索克西坦 (527):小组 (2-9) (17),宿醉/几天后 (46),困难经历 (5)

Warning From a K-User Methoxetamine by jiffyam Citation:   jiffyam. "Warning From a K-User: An Experience with Methoxetamine (exp90919)". Erowid.org . May 13, 2011. erowid.org/exp/90919 DOSE: T+ 0:00 1 line insufflated Methoxetamine (powder / crystals) T+ 0:30 1 line insufflated Methoxetamine (powder / crystals) T+ 0:00 repeated insufflated Methoxetamine (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 150 lb To start, I would consider myself an experienced k user, and it is by far my favourite drug, although I do like shrooms and pills. I have been using k fairly regularly for a few years now but I've tried to cut down as my tolerance has gotten to a point where I can comfortably do 500mg lines and go out and function within reasonable capacity. (everyones idea of reasonable capacity will be different, but I mean I can talk, walk in straight lines etc etc.) I was really excited about trying Methoxetamine as I'd read other reports from k users saying it's got a hint of the k aspect to it but it is in itself a very unique drug. Time 3:00am I'd just gotten back from a gig in and a few people were in our flat having come home from their own nights out, I had drunk a bit throughout the night but wouldn't consider myself in any way intoxicated. Time 3:30am Dose: roughly 50mg insufflated Decided with a less experienced drug user, female. Nothing much happened for a long time maybe 30 minutes? Time 4:00am Dose 50mg Decided to redose as it didnt appear to be doing anything, maybe slight slurred speech and noticeably slower responses to questions but that was about it. At this point, time got dilated past a point I can be accurate. I know I redosed several more times because, tbh, I'm an idiot and I like to experiment to see how far I can push a substance. I pushed it WAY too far. I can't be sure how much I dosed at this point because I spilled alot of it onto my bed. Smooth move. Maybe 7:00am The effects are similar to ketamine in the sense that I have virtually no control over the communication between my mouth and my brain. I knew what was going on around me, but had no way at all of conveying what I wanted to say to anybody despite knowing EXACTLY the words I wanted to say, I was unable to say them. I'd say it was similar to having about a 3second memory. You knew what you wanted to do, then you forgot it instantly. Got confused and tried to work out why you were confused. Which made you more confused. Not a fun experience. I had expected the larger doses I knew I had done after the initial 'fuzz' to put me into a hole, much like with ketamine, but it didn't. It just made me more and more unable to cope with everyday tasks whilst simultaneously knowing exactly what I wanted to do. This is unlike k in the sense that it doesn't give me that space travel sinking into a warm bath of honey feeling that I so much love from ketamine. From about? 9:00am till about 4:00pm Very difficult to talk, increased heartbeat, noticeable shaking in the hands and feet. Felt almost as if I was skipping frames in my head as for every step I took it felt like I'd missed one. Still difficult to walk. Now for the scary part: This stuff isn't like ketamine in the sense that, with ketamine, I can return to near normality within half hour to an hour. I was ruined for about 24 hours. I had extremely intense closed eye visuals for the whole time, was unable to sleep for the best part of 2 days. Walking was possible but after lying down for a while my balance was destroyed. Speech still slurred. A complete lack of understand of words/numbers/equations etc. anything that was remotely lingual was virtually impossibly to comprehend. Now I'd say this is probably because I did way too large a dose, I'm not proud of it and I definitely don't think it was a good idea. I found the experience genuinely frighting and was EXTREMELY depressed and confused in the later part of the evening, 8p-11pm. I had a very heartfelt chat with my girlfriend on the phone and tried to get to sleep, which I found virtually impossible due to the onslaught of CEV's. CONCLUSION I would strongly advise that anyone experimenting this due to its relationship with ketamine be VERY careful. I'm sure under the right circumstance it could be an ALRIGHT experience, but in my view ketamine is a much more friendly (for myself) drug. And I will most certainly NOT be trying Methoxetamine again, and will steer well clear of any more research chems.

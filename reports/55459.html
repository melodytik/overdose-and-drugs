我开始服用静音, 为了了解我的梦想, 我在一周内, 服用了半茶匙粉状三次. 我在清晨空腹饮用, 等待几个小时吃饭. 在此期间, 我会对某事或某人进行冥想, 我读过一些关于科利乌斯梦幻的报道, 我在烤箱中在300F中干了几片叶子, 我抽了一片中等大小的叶子, 我立即感觉到一个轻微的声, 我坐在花园里, 五分钟左右, 我无法主观地看待自己的想法, 听力也增强了, 附近街道的声音也让我很生气. 在半小时内, 我很失望没有得到更多的烟草, 晚上10点半左右, 我吸了一根大约1/8的1/2茶匙粉状. 经历了非常轻微的头, 在晚上11点左右入睡. 从那时起, 我睡得很轻, 偶尔, 直到凌晨3点半左右. 在凌晨四点睡觉, 经历了一次非常生动, 强烈的体验. 在早上7点半左右醒来, 一切都仍然是完全难忘的, 现在,早上10点43分, 我还能回忆起故事的每一段, 一切都非常强烈. 梦本身是有意义的,有趣的,有时是非常可怕的,也是预言性的. 我打算对这两种物质进行进一步的广泛实验, 已使用的证券 科莱斯 (168),西莱内 (386):单独 (16),组合 (3)

Oct 10, 2006. erowid.org/exp/55459 DOSE: T+ 0:00 oral Silene capensis (tea) T+ 0:00 1 leaf smoked Coleus (dried) T+ 0:30 3 leaves smoked Coleus (dried) T+ 4:45 0.0625 tsp insufflated Silene capensis (ground / crushed) BODY WEIGHT: 120 lb I began taking Silene capensis in order to gain insight into my dreams, whom I havent heard from in months. Over a week I took an infusion of 1/2 teaspoon of powdered silene capensis 3 times. I mixed this with 1/2 cup warm water and agitated, drinking in early morning on an empty stomach, and waiting several hours to eat. During this time I would meditate on something or someone to allow me an experience, but I acquired no visionary dreams, or any dreams at all. I read a few reports of the oneiric aspects of coleus, so I decided to try it as well. I dried several leaves in the oven at 300F for about 20 minutes. Around 5:45 pm I smoked a medium size leaf, holding it in for about 15 seconds. I immediately experienced a mild buzzing, also in my eyes. I sat in my garden and for about 5 minutes I was utterly captivated by the play of light and shadow caused by the movement of the trees. I was unable to view my thoughts subjectively, and the darted around semi-uncontrollably. My hearing was also increased, and the sounds of the nearby street irritated me greatly. The effects disappeared in about a half-hour. I was disappointed at not being given more, and I foolishly proceeded to smoke three more leaves, to no effect. At about 10:30 pm, I snorted a line of approximately 1/8 of a 1/2 teaspoon of powdered silene capensis. Experienced very slight dizziness, and fell asleep at around 11 pm. I slept lightly and sporadically from then until about 3:30 am. Went back to sleep at 4:00 am, and recieved an extremely vidid, powerful experience. Woke up around 7:30 am, and proceeded to rapidly jot down the dream. Everything was still completely memorable, down to the most minute detail. Right now, at 10:43 am, I can still recall every segment of the narrative, every color, everything spoken, and every emotion it illicited from me. Everything was very strong. The dream itself was involving, intriguing, at times very frightening, and prophetic. I plan on extensively experimenting further with both substances, combined and seperate.

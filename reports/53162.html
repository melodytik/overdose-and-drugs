我在我的家乡遇到了一些酸, 我的生日还有几个月, 我曾经尝试过几次, 也许在一个半月内, 现在回想起来可能有点过分. 无论如何,我的生日来了,去了,我决定在生日后的周末和一个朋友在一起. 他和我在一个美好的四月天, 而我的未婚夫决定不这样做. 我们决定从室内开始, 我用了2.5次,而他用了2次. 我的药物包括和甲基马, 我已经有幻觉和听到东西, 由于我的精神疾病, 我能逃脱我至少一天的声音的精神监狱, 这对我来说是个巨大的解脱. 我还应该提一下, 我有很多研究化学经验, 我已经服用了8种这种药物, 我休息了一个月, 没有对我的生日经历有任何宽容. 我一直想要改变生活的经历, 但是在我自己的条件下, 好吧,30分钟到一个小时是当它在好. 视觉效果很好, 在服用LSD30分钟后, 特别是在碗之后. 我想出去,所以我起身去拿鞋. 我觉得自己站得太快了, 因为突然间一切变白了, 为了尽量减少伤害, 每次我站过这个点, 这就是发生的事情. 在T+1:15: 我躺在地上. 我无法站起来或移动. 有什么不对. 我再也看不到任何人或任何东西. 我没有听到任何人或任何东西. 我真的没有了. 这一定是六级的!!! 很快,情况从糟糕变得非常糟糕. 我开始失去意识, 我觉得如果失去意识, 我就是死去的, 因为我不能过量服用2.5次, 两个人不知道该怎么做. 我告诉我的女儿打电话给救护车, 因为失去意识对我来说不对, 我没有提及视觉效果, 视觉效果比沙尔维亚更强烈, 我实际上处于另一个世界, 我主要是看到我和我的未婚夫很多,与小叶漂浮在背景这是我的生活的图片,接下来是死亡. 在这个时候,我不能说话. 我只能吐出一堆毫无意义的术语. 我会把单词切成两半, 医护人员无法从我身上得到任何必要的信息,除了说,'你吃了多少?'我说2或3个,他们直截了当地称我为骗子,说'你的意思是6,7,8,10!?! 你没有花2个小时就像这个朋友! 他们不敢相信眼前所看到的. 我一直在失去意识, 每次都影响着我的旅行. 我失去了时间的概念, 然后在医院里我躺在那里,在自己的世界. 我听到医生说:"已经太晚了,我们已经失去了他". 他们开始断开连接到我的线路. 我开始向家人和女儿喊出最后一句话, 握住她的手... 然后我看到死亡, 它是完全和完整的虚无. 没有意识的想法,没有感觉,没有什么. 我开始停止呼吸, 经历死亡. 在这段时间里, 我停止了呼吸, 短暂的, 我被拒绝,然后被送回我的身体, 任何最后的想法都消失了. 我醒来时听到的是'你一定会成功!' 这句话来自护士握住我的左手,而我的女朋友则在我的右边. 我醒来时发现我身边有各种各样的线路和监控设备. 我问我离开了多长时间......我的急诊报告显示我在那里待了五个半小时, 我25岁了, 没有孩子的想法真的很糟糕. 我看到了我错过的一切. 我看到了自己的葬礼, 我从来没有经历过这么强烈的东西, 我18-19岁时, 喜欢LSD, 我寄这封信的唯一原因是因为我和另一个朋友经历了一些糟糕的时刻... 但我们都有一个共同点, 我们每个人经常使用2C在6个月的时间内, 我几乎没有回来,他几乎死于脑出血. 我不认为LSD是这么深刻的旅程的主要因素, 我认为2C的舞台, 大多是2CI. 其他: 2007年6月19日发布 LSD (2),药物 - (91):单独 (16),实体/生物 (37),火车残骸和旅程灾难 (7),组合 (3)

Jun 19, 2007. erowid.org/exp/53162 DOSE: T+ 0:00 oral Pharms - Lithium (daily) T+ 0:00 oral Pharmaceuticals (daily) T+ 0:00 2.5 hits oral LSD (blotter / tab) T+ 0:30 1 bowl smoked Cannabis (plant material) BODY WEIGHT: 160 lb I had come across a bit of acid in my hometown and my birthday was a few months away so I snatched some before it was gone about two months prior. I had tried it a few times.... maybe 10 or so trips within a month and a half or so. Maybe a bit excessive looking back on it. Anyway, my birthday came and went and I decided to hang with a friend the weekend after my birthday. Me and him were tripping this fine day in April and my fiance decided not to. It was a beautiful day out and we decided to start indoors and go outside once things got 'colorful'. I took 2.5 hits of blotter, and he had 2 hits. My medications include lithium and methimazole which is a Thyroid pill for hyperthyroidism. I already hallucinate and hear things due to my mental illness which is either Post Traumatic Stress Disorder or Schizoaffective Disorder. However when I trip I'm able to escape the mental prison of voices that I have for at least a day which is great relief to me. I should also mention that I've had many Research Chemical experiences and I think that played into today. Back to the trip, I had taken as many as 8 of this batch with wonderful results about a month prior. I took a month break from tripping to have no tolerance for my birthday experience. I've always wanted a life changing experience, but on my own terms and I now know why these experiences come when I least expect them because they change me. Ok, 30 min. to an hour is when it kicked in good. Visuals were good, and we had started toking on a bowl 30 min. after taking the LSD. At the one hour mark things had started to get crazy, especially after the bowl. I wanted to go outside so I got up to go get my shoes. As I was walking I thought I had stood up to fast because all of a sudden everything turned white and sound was so distorted and slowed down I thought I was passing out. So my first instinct was to drop to the ground to minimize injury. Every time I stood up past this point, that is what happened. T+1:15: I was lying out on the floor. I couldn't get up or move. Something was wrong. I could no longer see anyone or anything. I could not hear anyone or anything. I was literally gone. This must've been a level 6!!! Quickly things went from bad, to really really bad. I started to lose consciousness, which didn't make sense. I felt if I lost consciousness I was then dead because I can't overdose off of 2.5 hits, besides I tried 8 about a month prior. The two people with me didn't know what to do. I told my girl to call an ambulance because losing consciousness didn't seem right to me and if I was going to be bad off I wanted to be in the hospital where I felt safe and my family could be called to see me in the event of death. I haven't mentioned the visuals because that's all anybody ever talks about. The visuals were more intense than salvia, I was literally in another world, seeing, touching, feeling another world. I mostly saw me and my fiancé a lot, with little pot leafs floating in the background this was my picture of life and next was death. At this point I couldn't talk. All I could do was spit out a bunch of jargon that made no sense. I would cut to words in half and combine them to make new words. The paramedics couldn't get any necessary info out of me other than, 'How many did you take?' I said 2 or 3 and they flat out called me a liar and said 'You mean 6,7,8, 10!?! You didn't take 2 to get like this pal!!!' They couldn't believe what they were seeing. At this point I thought I was on the edge of death, I kept going in and out of consciousness and it effected my trip each time. I lost track of time because I couldn't see the world around me. And then in the hospital I lay there, in my own world. And I heard the Dr. say, 'It's too late, we've lost him.' They start disconnecting the lines attached to me. I begin to cry out my last words to my family and my girl, as I hold her hand... but I can't see anything that's really going on or hear anything that's going on. And then I saw death, it was total and complete nothingness. No conscious thought, no feeling, no nothing. And then I proceeded to stop breathing as I went through death. During this time where I stopped breathing, it was brief, I made it to heaven and God gave me requirements in order to get my life back. I was denied and then I was sent back to my body which was then discarded in a dump or where ever they keep masses of dead people. And that's where any final thought dissipated. At this point my fiancé must've realized I wasn't breathing, I woke up to 'You're going to make it!' This came out of the nurse holding my left hand and my girl was on my right. I woke up to find all kinds of lines attached to me, and monitoring equipment. I asked how long I was gone for..... my ER report showed I had been there for five and a half hours plus the half hour at home made for a 6 hour experience on the deep end. It was a complete life altering experience, I'm 25 years old and the thought of never having kids really sucked. I saw everything I'd miss out on. I saw my funeral, and everyone who attended. I've never experienced anything this intense and I've eaten 16 hits before. When I was 18-19 years old, I liked LSD over everything and it was abundant. The one reason I sent this in was because me and another friend had some bad times... his on ecstasy. But we both had one thing in common, we each used 2C's frequently for a 6 month period prior to both of us landing in the hospital. I almost didn't come back, he almost died from bleeding in the brain. I don't think the LSD was the main factor in such a profound trip, I think the 2C's set the stage, mostly 2CI.

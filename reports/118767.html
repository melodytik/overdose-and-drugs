In these situations I begin to withdraw from the methadone, in place of the latter I obtained a kratom extract known as 7-Hydroxy-Mitragynine. Upon sublingual administration of five tablets, I consumed roughly 118mg of the kratom extract. I have found that my withdrawal symptoms from the methadone slightly subsided, along with a burst of energy that is accompanied by a sense of motivation and or euphoria. Since the solubility of the 7-Hydroxy-Mitragynine is sparsely different from the commonly found ground kratom leaves, I was able to consume the products without any aftertaste or unpleasant texture.

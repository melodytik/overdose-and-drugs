I experienced mild and mostly enjoyable effects. I felt light and floaty, I had a lot of energy and my thoughts seemed to be moving all around, for lack of a better description, plus dry mouth. I enjoyed my test tea so much, that I decided to do a larger dose as soon as I could. Love her and perhaps she'll imbue you with some of her power, treat her poorly and she may kill you.

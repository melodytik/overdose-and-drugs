背景: 几天前订购了一些I-theanine. 刚收到它并决定在09:18开始我的工作变更以实验目的. T+0:00 - 将~600mgs混合在玻璃杯中,加上一些水,然后喝. 非常奇怪的味道. 甜蜜的内涵,但相当恶心. 感觉平静,有点空虚,但可能只是安慰剂. 开始兴奋. 根据我读到的情况, 开始时间应该至少是30分钟, 我感觉右手指的尖端在打电话时声. 已经专注于工作了一段时间, 我觉得自己很正常. 也许有点放松,我的眼睛有点垂落. 现在我已经从椅子上站起来了. 肌肉放松明显, 但不是很重. 我没有注意到任何效果. 可能是因为我已经习惯了身体的轻微放松. 没有真正思考就喝了一杯能量饮料, 所以在体验中添加了75毫克咖啡因, 自上次更新以来,除了工作后感觉良好之外,没有其他影响. 我通常很累. 结论: 对于我来说, 这种经历就像一种轻微的二类药物, 我不容易感到压力或焦虑, 我相信,如果服用这种L-Theanine 我认为压力/焦虑可能是有益的. 为了实验/娱乐用途, 已使用的文件: 发布时间:2020年9月29日 工作场所 (51),组合 (3),第一次 (2)

Sep 29, 2020. erowid.org/exp/114807 DOSE: T+ 0:00 600 mg oral Theanine (powder / crystals) T+ 2:45 75 mg oral Caffeine (liquid) BODY WEIGHT: 60 kg Background: Ordered some l-theanine a couple of days ago. Just recieved it and decided to take it at the beginning of my work shift at 09:18 for experimental purposes. T+0:00 - Mixed ~600mgs in a glass with a little water and drank it. Very weird taste. Sweet undertones but pretty gross. T+0:10 - Feeling calm and a little spacey, but probably just placebo at this point. Getting excited. T+0:17 - From what I've read the onset should be AT LEAST 30 minutes, but I'm pretty sure that something is going on. I feel the tip of my right thumb buzzing as I'm typing this on my phone. T+1:20 - Been focused on work for a bit so haven't been able to think about any potential effects. I feel completely normal though. Maybe a little more relaxed and my eyes are a bit droopy. T+1:28 - Got up from my chair now after sitting down since ingestion. Muscle relaxation is definitely apparent, but not heavy. T+2:45 - Been walking around doing work for a while. Not really noticing any effects. Possibly because I've gotten used to the slight body relaxation. Had an energy drink without really thinking about it so 75mg of caffeine is being added onto the experience, but I'm guessing it will have a negligible effect. T+7:50 - No other effects has been noted since the last update except feeling alright after work. I'm usually very tired. Conclusion: The experience for me was like a light version of a benzodiazepine like alprazolam. I don't suffer from a lot of stress or anxiety so benzos have never really done much for me in normal doses. I'm sure that taking this amount of L-Theanine if I had stress/anxiety could be beneficial. For experimental/recreational use; not so much.

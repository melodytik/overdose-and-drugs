我从来不喜欢在没有任何信息的情况下尝试任何药物. 我觉得这是一件我会非常喜欢的事情. 我不是在寻找什么让我'旅行', 只是为了放松, 可能是冥想的目的. 我读到你可以把部分根在冰箱里的冷水中, 我填满了一个平均12-16司的水瓶, 放下大约2-3'的根, 接下来的早晨, 我把黄色的液体倒入另一个容器, 取出根块, 扔掉, 把液体放回冰箱. 我在那晚喝了整个混合物, 喝了大约10-15分钟. 味道并没有那么糟糕 (正如我预期的那样). 经过大约30-40分钟的时间, 我注意到自己开始失去了先进的灵巧. (我再也不能玩电子游戏了) 我的注意力不断地从电子游戏上转移. 于是我走进我的卧室, 冥想一下. 这也让我有点昏昏欲睡. 经过一两个小时的温柔, 这就是我有史以来最激烈的梦想开始的地方. 这绝对令人难以置信! 我的梦想是如此生动和真实的. 我曾经在夜里醒来, 我意识到这一切都只是一个梦. 我重新入睡, 能够从我停下来的位置继续做梦. 我醒来第二天感觉清爽, 非常快乐. 我不确定这是否巧合, 这一次,将大约4-5'的根再次浸泡在冷水中. 我忘了那天把根从水里取出来, 直到晚上晚些时候才能把它取出来. 液体比前一晚稍微黄色一些. 我把根碎片取下来, 再次喝下12-16司的液体. 我最好的描述就是你第一次喝了几杯酒. 虽然我没有喝醉,但我感觉很放松, 我不会在等待它到来的时候进行这项工作. 我只是放松和平静自己, 它将滑上我非常愉快. 我发现最好的剂量是4-6'根浸泡在水中, 无论我想要多久 (我已经把它留在3天内没有负面影响) 似乎在第二天之后得到最大的效果. 人们可能会用它做各种各样的事情, 让梦想变得更令人难以置信,更难忘,更生动. 有时我从梦中醒来, 想知道是不是真的. 另一个关于这个根的令人惊叹的事情是,在梦中,一旦我意识到它实际上是一个梦. 我开始能够控制某些方面. 不同于其他梦想, 我只是被带走了. 能够控制/操纵自己的梦想, 极大地增加了我对梦想的享受. 经过一些研究, 我发现一些来源建议不要从这种根中提取提取物, 我没有尝试过这两种方法, 其他: 卡拉姆斯 (106),梦想 (85):回顾 / 总结 (11),一个人 (16)

The Dream Juice Calamus by HippieJason Citation:   HippieJason. "The Dream Juice: An Experience with Calamus (exp44156)". Erowid.org . Mar 7, 2007. erowid.org/exp/44156 DOSE: 5 in oral Calamus (liquid) BODY WEIGHT: 220 lb I have never been one to just go and try every drug out there without any information first. So after studying up quite a bit on Calamus Root, it seemed something that I would greatly enjoy. I wasn't looking for anything to give me a 'trip', merely something for relaxing, possibly meditation purposes. I read that you could steep portions of the root in cold water in your fridge overnight, so i decided this is how I would try it first. I filled an average 12-16oz water bottle and dropped approx 2-3' of the root in and let it sit overnight. The next morning I poured the yellow-ish liquid into another container and removed the root pieces and threw them away, placing the liquid back in the fridge. That night, I drank the entire mixture, sipping on it over the course of around 10-15 minutes. It didn't have a bad taste (as I had expected it to). After approx 30-40 minutes I felt this warm blanket of relaxiveness begin to cover me. It was a very enjoyable, mellow experience, and I noticed I began to lose advanced dexterity. (I couldn't play video games any more) And my focus kept drifting away from the video game. So I went into my bedroom and meditated a bit. It was rather late in the evening, and it also made me rather sleepy. After about 1-2 hours of mellowness, I drifted off. This is where some of the most intense dreams I have ever had started. It was absolutly incredible! My dreams were SOOOO vivid and lifelike. I woke up once through the night, after I had forced myself awake from my dream. At that point, I realized that the whole thing had been a dream. I went back to sleep and was able to pick up where I had left off, and resumed dreaming. I woke up the next day feeling refreshed, and very happy. I wasn't sure if this was a coincidence, so I tested it again the next night. This time with approx 4-5' of the root steeped again in cold water overnight. However, I forgot to take the root out of the water that day, and wasn't able to remove it until later on that night. The liquid was slightly a bit more yellow than the previous night. It still tasted fine, and I removed the root pieces and again drank down the 12-16 oz of liquid. Same time frame, same feelings. . . the best way I can try to describe it is when you have your first couple of alcoholic drinks. Not to the point where I'm drunk, but feeling very relaxed, and almost euphoric. I do not go into this EXPECTING and WAITING for it to arrive. I just relax and calm myself, and it will slide up on me very pleasantly. After various experiments with dosage, I have found the best amount to be between 4-6' of root steeped in water for however long I want (I have left it in up to 3 days without a negative affect) It seems to get the most out of it after the second day. . . so I'm not sure if it does any good to let it sit longer, I had just forgotten I left it in there. People may use this for a variety of purposes, but I have found my purpose for it. The 'Dream Juice' making dreams more incredible, more rememberable, and more vivid. Sometimes I have woken up from a dream wondering if it was in fact real or not. Another awesome thing about this root, is that in the dreams, once I realized it actually WAS a dream. . I began to be able to control certain aspects over it. As opposed to other dreams where I'm just kind of 'Taken along for a ride'. And being able to control / manipulate my dreams has greatly increased my enjoyment of such dreams. After some research, I found a few sources advising not to develop an extract from this root, and to NOT steep it in hot water. I have not tried either of these methods, and do not see a reason to.

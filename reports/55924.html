最近在我们的城镇开设了一个氧气吧. 我和朋友在商场上走来走去的时候, 我们决定去试试, 所以一周后又回来了. 我咨询了一位曾经去过氧气酒吧的朋友, 她说她确实注意到了一些不同之处, 我们在星期五晚上去, 我的期望相当高. 他建议我们只做半个小时, 因为一整小时对初学者来说可能是'相当激烈'的. 我决定制作一种叫做"智慧人"的风味, 像老人有时穿的, 我躺下深呼吸. 我们坐在那里安静地. 首先我感到有点疲, 很快就显然没有什么特别的事情会发生. 我感到失望, 渐渐变得有些生气, 想起我花了8美元让香气喷入鼻子. 我们的45分钟终于结束了, 我们都没有注意到任何真正的效果, 除了希瑟说它确实让她更性欲. 尽管如此, 由于她总是高兴, 结论: 氧气没有做任何重要的事情. 提供了一个很好的思考环境. 我无法想象氧气会被用来娱乐. 时间表: 55924 氧气 (187):一般 (1),第一次 (2),俱乐部/酒吧 (25)

A Lame Evening Oxygen by Day Tripper Citation:   Day Tripper. "A Lame Evening: An Experience with Oxygen (exp55924)". Erowid.org . May 4, 2008. erowid.org/exp/55924 DOSE: inhaled Oxygen (gas) BODY WEIGHT: 145 lb Recently, an oxygen bar opened up in our town. Me and my friends noticed it one evening while walking up and down the mall. We resolved to try it out, so we went back a week later. I consulted a friend who had already gone to the oxygen bar, and she said that she had definitely noticed something different, and described it as a legal high. We went on Friday night, and my expectations where reasonably high. The owner came over with oxygen menus, and he advised us to only do a half hour, since a whole hour could be 'pretty intense' for a beginner. I decided to do a flavor called 'wise man' which promised to open my inner mind, while my friends both chose an aphrodisiac flavor. They put an oxygen tube in my nose, like old people sometimes wear, and I laid back breathing deeply. This more or less killed our conversation, and we sat there quietly. At first I felt a little bit tired, but that’s because it was a Friday night after a long week. It soon became clear that nothing extraordinary was going to happen. I was disappointed, and gradually became a little pissed as I thought of how I paid 8 bucks to get scented air blown up my nose. Eventually our 45 minutes where up, and we left feeling like we had wasted the first part of the evening. None of us noticed any real effects, except Heather who said that it did make her hornier. However this is up for debate, since she is always horny. CONCLUSION: The oxygen didn’t do anything significant. The oxygen, bar however, was a quiet place and provided a nice setting for thought. I cannot imagine oxygen being used recreationally.

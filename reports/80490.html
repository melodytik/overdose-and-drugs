中文译文 兴奋剂

所有剂量都是近似的. 最近对M1/B1进行了三次试验, 同样对Etaquanol和JWH073进行了短期试验. 尽管我尝试过的大部分都相当令人失望, 唯一的例外是M1和JWH073. 几年前, 我曾在不同的场合使用过速度和LSD, --- 没有 复活的事件. 我昨天第一次尝试了一种MDPV. 关于这种化合物的广泛阅读在事件发生前几天让我对它的影响有了各种各样的看法, 我非常坚定地决定不做这件事. 午餐后, 我吸了大约10毫克的药, 我几乎没有感觉到它击中, 但在30分钟后注意到轻微刺激. 很快就消灭了我对工作的渴望. 尽管我预先决定不再服用, 在T+0030我吸入另一个10毫克, 我向自己保证不再再服用. 我只想写一些关于各种话题的论证性文章, 在第一次重剂后, 我很快就明显受到刺激. 现在我可以更容易地评估效应. 在我的书中最接近的相似之处是安非他命. 它们之间的主要区别在于身体感不存在, 有些相似的感觉虽然不那么愉快. 也没有任何改善心情或高兴的感觉. 没有紧张. 我发现这种经历到目前为止还没有那么有价值. 对于精神状态,最好的描述是清醒和完全清醒的感觉在最初阶段 (6-7小时) 其他效应最好是分散的. 我发现自己无法确定自己的情绪. 我心情依然非常集中, 让人烦的疏远. 这不是一个有用的情绪为我的目的, 和这种化合物的效果不是特别适合我或我的偏好. 身体上的影响到目前为止很少. 食欲消退,体温和汗水略微升高, --- 没有 在T+0130时, 我又吸了10毫克, 我没有. 我试图通过打开3.5%半升酒来提升我的情绪, 在接下来的2.5小时里, 我慢慢地再喝两杯. 我几乎没有注意到酒带来的任何影响, 任何情绪提升是可以忽略的, 酒精的其他常见影响也是如此. 我没有太多的热情. 现在让我们换个角度... 我可能在写作时喜欢假对话, 但最终, 在第二次检查中, 原因很多, 在+0900我注意到一个发育头痛. 我很想吃一些. 需要很短的时间才能失去兴趣, 最后四分之一的小餐开始必须被迫下来. 很快之后, 我想要喝一杯的维生素和矿物质, 我需要很长时间才能找到维生素. 我忽略了唯一一个地方,我一直保持他们. 我忘了我需要的是维生素. 当我不知道自己在寻找什么时, 我只是在一些非常愚蠢的地方随机搜索, 这是一场梦. 我终于找到了维生素, 把它放进杯子里, 离开厨房,很快就忘了我有一杯准备喝的维生素. 想象一下我很惊, 我开始注意到其他记忆障碍的迹象... 我认为我最近的饮食表明我几乎完全下来了. 然而, 6 小时后 (T+1500) 我仍然清醒. 我看电影时, 无法理解故事情节, 脑子里总是游荡. 血压升高和脉升高, 在我的头骨内部有轻微的压力. 有点焦虑, 但仍然在这个中心, 独立的心情. 仍然清醒. 迄今为止, 已经不可能睡觉. 此外, 在身体中轻微的硬感. 总的来说, 这次下降被认为是非常温和的, 尽管它对我的记忆产生了奇怪的影响, 慢慢地衰退,所有症状都被认为是轻微的. 没有问题仍然像其他刺激器. 而是让人沉睡不前. 相比安非他命, 这似乎是非常温和的确在经历的影响. 没有抑郁症, 因为情绪大多是被鞭打出来的. 现在回想起来, 记忆力差异似乎已经很糟糕了. 由于MDPV缺乏娱乐价值, 我决定不再这样做. 现在我又饿了,又吃了. 我很快就失去了兴趣. 观看更多的电影, 但就像以前一样, 在T+1900时, 我陷入半清醒的睡眠, 醒来后,我等了很长时间才吃早餐. 整天没有排便, 持续的血压升高/心跳加快/头骨内部的安全感/在不活动时容易陷入沙发锁. 我的身体有点硬. 我今天经常想到药物, 想象它的气味几次. 我有时会想再服用药物, 我吹了鼻子,发现我的鼻子是红的. 我缺乏积极做任何事的动力. 电影中的情节仍然很难追踪, 仍有很多回滚. 这种药物的积极之处在于, 它的降低感觉非常温和, 而且它似乎是一种强大的社会滑剂. 消极的部分是它剥夺了我的灵魂, 我不喜欢在36个小时内服用的物质, 至少我认为如此. 心理和情感问题仍然存在. 没有碗运动. 这一次我睡觉了. 在T+4800我再次醒来. 记忆和情绪似乎恢复正常, 这种药物对我来说完全没有价值, 其他: 2010年1月24日发布 MDPV (377):一般 (1),第一次 (2),宿醉 / 几天后 (46),多天经验 (13),困难经验 (5),独自 (16)

Jan 24, 2010. erowid.org/exp/80490 DOSE: T+ 0:00 10 mg insufflated MDPV (powder / crystals) T+ 0:30 10 mg insufflated MDPV (powder / crystals) T+ 1:30 10 mg insufflated MDPV (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 67 kg All dosages are approximates. Previous drug experiences: Longterm cannabisuser, I Recently did three trials with M1 / B1 each, also similar short-term tests with Etaquanol and JWH073. Discovering the online RC market has been interesting, tho most of what I've tried have been rather dissapointing, only exception being M1 and to some extent JWH073. Years back, I did one-time trials with speed & LSD at separate occasions, and also slipped into a completely unintended week of morphine use. --- The Event; Having taken no drugs for a week or so, yesterday I tried some MDPV for the first time. Extensive reading about this compound in the days leading up to the event had left me with a very diverse array of opinions concerning it's effects, as well as more than one cautionary tale about it's addictive properties and possible neurotoxic effects. I was very decided not to go haywire on this. After a light meal at lunch, I snorted about 10mg's worth, and proceeded to sit down to do some work. I hardly felt it hitting, but after 30 minutes noticed slight stimulation. It didn't feel very good though, and soon killed all my desire to work. Despite my pre-determination not to redose, at T+0030 I snort another 10mg, to see if this will add anything further to the compound. I promise myself not to redose anymore. After this, I still hate my work, and all I want to do is to write argumentative texts about various topics, which is what I wind up doing. Following redose #1 it wasn't long before I became clearly stimulated. Now I could asses the effects more easily. Closest resemblance in my book would be amphetamine. Major differences between them thus far would be that bodily chills isn't present here, some similar though much less pleasurable feelings. Neither is there any sense of improved mood or euphoria. Less jitters. I find the experience less rewarding so far. As for mental state, the best description would be a feeling of clarity and complete awakenness during the initial stage, (6-7 hours) other effects are diffuse at best. Upon introspection, I found myself unable to determine my mood. With slight pendular motions between happyness and sadness, my mood still felt very centered and annoyingly detached. This is not a useful mood for my purposes, and the effects of this compound isn't particularily accomodating to me or my preferences. Physically, effects are few so far. killed apetite, slight rise in body temperature and perspiration but negligable compared to M1 or B1. --- At T+0130 I snort another 10mg's in vain hopes of experiencing something worthwhile with this compound. I don't. At T+0700 I try to brighten my mood and fend of thoughts of MDPV-redose by opening a 3.5% half litre beer. Over the next 2.5 hours, I slowly drink another two. I hardly notice any effects from the beer, except for some minor circulatory changes I commonly experience at the earliest stage of alcohol intoxication. Any mood enhancement is negligable, so is other common effects of alcohol. I drink without much enthusiasm. Now let´s switch perspectives... I may enjoy my pseudo-conversations while writing, but ultimately and on second inspection I wind up trashing almost everything I write between +0130 and +0900. The reasons are many and not very interesting. At +0900 I notice a developing headache. Around the same time, I become very hungry and eat a little. It takes a short time before I loose interest, and the last quarter of what was a small meal to start with has to be forced down. Shortly after, I want to drink a fizz w. vitamins & minerals and start to notice how weird I feel. It takes me forever to find my vitamins. I ignore the only place I ever keep them. I forget that vitamins is what I'm looking for. When I don't know what I'm looking for, I just search at random in very stupid places, hoping that what I find will jog my memory. Cross reference nightmare. Finally I find the vitamins, and put one in a glass, first w/o water, but I catch on soon enough. Leave the kitchen and soon forget that I have a glass of vitamins ready to drink. Imagine my surprise upon entering the kitchen again a while later. I start noticing other signs of memory impairment.. . At this time, I assumed that my recent meal indicated I had come down almost entirely. However, 6 hours later (T+1500) I was still awake. During this interval, I watched movies without being able to follow the storyline, my mind constantly wandering. Elevated blood pressure and pulse noted, slight pressure on the inside of my skull. Slightly anxious, but still in this centered, detached mood. Still wide awake. So far, it would have been impossible to sleep. Furthermore... Mild feelings of stiffness in the body. All in all, the comedown was perceived as very gentle despite the weird effects on my memory, which I really didn't care that much about. Slow and gradual decline, all symptoms perceicved as mild. No problems remaining still like on some other stims. Rather, it had a couchlocking effect. In comparison with Amphetamine, this seems very mild indeed while experiencing the effects.. No depression since feelings are largely whiped out. Thus no bothers about memory defects either, which in retrospect seems pretty fucked up. It is around this time I decide that, because MDPV lacks recreational merit I won't be doing it anymore. By now I'm hungry and eat again.. As before, I quickly loose interest. Watch a little more of my movie, but as before I have to rewind all the time. At T+1900 I drift off into some semi-wakefull sleep, and wake up again at T+2400. Upon waking, I wait a long time before having breakfast. No bowel movements at all throughout the day, though I eat as normal. Continued feelings of elevated bloodpressure / rapid heartbeat / preassure on inside of skull. / Easy to get couchlock when inactive. Slight Stiffness in my body. I've thought about the drug often today, and has imagined it's smell several times. At those times, I've felt the urge to redose but did not act on it. I Blew my nose and found my snot was red. I lack the motivation to do anything actively. Still very hard to follow the plots in movies, still have to rewind a lot. The positive thing about this drug is that it's comedown feels really gentle, also it seems to be a strong social lubricant. The negative thing is that it robs me of my soul and feels very addictive. When I crave a substance I didn't enjoy taking @ +36 hours, at least that's what I assume. Mental & emotional issues remain. No bowl movement. I go to sleep around this time. At T+4800 I wake up again. I feel better now, bowel movement restored, memory and emotions seems back to normal, although lingering aftereffects may be impossible to assess. This drug was completely worthless to me, it had no recreational merits and felt mostly like one longlasting side effect.

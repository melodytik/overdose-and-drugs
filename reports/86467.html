德索西皮普拉德罗尔,大麻,酒和NBOMe-美斯卡林组合旅行报告 我是一个20多岁的男性. 我已经服用了很多胺和胺. 我之前服用了2DPMP3或4次, 达到6毫克. 我已经试验了NBOMe-mescaline注射到大约50毫克, 上午9点 我已经醒了14个小时了. 只是吸入了3毫克的脱氧皮普拉德, 因为我必须保持清醒, 完成任务. 上午9点06分 这对我来说是令人惊的,但我觉得更清醒,更清醒,更不那么疲倦. 上午9点10分 我认为我可以入睡如果我试着. 下午1点 在飞行4个小时后不再感到疲倦. 没有高兴感. 我每隔几个小时抽一点大麻. 我睡了大约下午5点到5点半, 然后被唤醒, 现在要去露营... 晚上7点左右 首先是一袋重量测试袋里有90毫克的NBOMe-mescaline. 我把40毫克的NBOMe放在舌头下面. 在此之后吸了一些大麻. 只是一个碗,但这次湿. 我开始想:我真是个瓜! 我正在服用一种几乎未知的物质, 我没有睡觉,睡得太久了, 我把它和2-DPMP混合在一起. 这是多么愚蠢. 我希望我没有这样做. 但是我做到了,所以让我们希望最好的,? 经过30分钟的时间, 我感觉很好. 不同的,看起来很奇怪,像一个新土地是我周围. 晚上7点45分 (大约) 我把另一个20毫克涂在我的舌头下面, 让它吸收. 在晚上8点15分左右, 吸入了最后的30毫克, 感觉很好. 这种情绪让人想起MDMA, 这不是酒. ~晚上八点半 我看着日落,远处的暴风雨云. 观看湖水中的复杂波, 视觉部分已经开始. 我记得我对自己说,我看到的是美丽的. 我感觉很好. 晚上10点是完全发育, 喝酒之前这甚至开始, 整天都在抽大麻. 在晚上11点 这可能是峰值 ~凌晨2点,我去散步. 这是一个满月. 我现在有点醉了. 试图关注NBOMe-mescaline的作用, 在这次旅行中记下心中的笔记. 眼睛闭着, 构成不同于我习惯的2C类型迷幻药物. 视觉不太清晰,很模糊, 但有色彩闪, 就像一幅抽象画在我眼前被创作. 这不是经典的 kaleidoscope 图案类型的东西. 开眼睛... 在湖边看着山上的房子. 这很美. 灯光从它们的源头跳转到我视野中的不同点. 这是一些很好的,很好的东西. 但它们不是像DPT那样的重量级的追踪器. 追踪器跟随它们的源头, 但它们似乎也会跳转, 这种东西有潜力成为伟大, 真的. 上午四点 我还在. 胃口很好,但我一天中没有吃太多. 旅行总共花了大约10个小时, 但在12-13个小时后, 然后它就消失了. 没有恶心. 我没有注意到任何身体负荷. 必须睡觉... 在回家的路上睡在车里. 睡了大约8个小时. 第二天感觉很好. 在服用NBOMe-mescaline后的24小时内, 还有一些令人愉快的视觉效果, 这只是一次. 我几乎称之为"回忆", 这是一种有趣的化合物, 值得进一步研究. Jun 9, 2011. erowid.org/exp/86467 DOSE: T+ 0:00 3 mg insufflated Desoxypipradrol T+ 0:00 repeated smoked Cannabis T+ 8:30 3 mg insufflated Desoxypipradrol T+ 10:00 40 mg sublingual Mescaline-NBOMe (powder / crystals) T+ 0:00 Alcohol - Beer/Wine T+ 10:45 20 mg sublingual Mescaline-NBOMe (powder / crystals) T+ 11:15 30 mg insufflated Mescaline-NBOMe (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 120 lb desoxypipradrol, marijuana, beer, & NBOMe-mescaline combo trip report I'm a mid-20's male. I've done many tryptamines and phenethylamines. I've done 2-DPMP about 3 or 4 times before up to about 6mg. I've experimented with NBOMe-mescaline intranasally up to about 50mg, with little to no effects so far. 9:00 AM I've been awake for 14 hours. Just snorted ~3mg desoxypipradrol because I have to stay awake and get stuff done. 9:06 AM it's surprising to me, but I feel more awake and alert and less tired. 9:10 AM I think I could fall asleep if I tried. 1:00 PM Not feeling tired anymore after 4 hours from lift off. No euphoria. I smoke some weed (schwag) every few hours. I went to sleep about 5:00-5:30 PM, then was awakened and almost immediately snorted another ~3mg of 2-DPMP. Going camping now... 7:00 PM (approx.) Starting with a weighed out bag with 90mg of NBOMe-mescaline in it. I put about 40mg of the NBOMe under the tongue. And some weed is smoked after that. Just a bowl, but dank this time. I start thinking: What a fucking fool I am! I'm taking a virtually unknown substance at a dosage only reported once (first hand) before. That after I've had no sleep and I've been up way too long, and I'm mixing it with 2-DPMP. How fucking foolish. I wish I hadn't done that. But I did, so let's hope for the best, eh? After about 30 minutes, I'm feeling pretty good. Different, things look strange, like a new land is all around me. 7:45 PM (approx.) I put another maybe 20mg smeared around under my tongue and left to absorb. About 8:15 PM the final ~30mg was snorted, feeling very good. Some euphoria reminiscent of MDMA, talking is increased. It's not the beer. ~8:30 PM I'm looking at the sun set, and storm clouds far away. Looking at intricate ripples in the lake water, colored by the sunset. The visual stuff has started. I remember saying to myself that what I see is beautiful. I'm feeling pretty good. at 10:00 PM it's fully developed, drinking beer before this even started, but I'm not really drunk or not very drunk if I am drunk. Been smoking pot (schwag) too, all day actually. At 11:00 PM this is probably the peak ~2:00 AM, I go walking. It's a full moon. I'm kind of drunk now. Trying to pay attention to the effects of the NBOMe-mescaline, taking mental notes on this trip. There's brilliant visual stuff with eyes closed, of a different composition than I'm used to with 2C- type psychedelic drugs. The visions are not very clear, pretty muddy, but there are flashes of color and awesome looking stuff, hard to describe. Like an abstract painting being created before my eyes. This is not the classic kaleidoscope patterning type stuff though. With eyes open... looking across a lake at houses on the hill. It's beautiful. Lights are jumping around from their source to various points in my field of vision. This is some nice, nice stuff. Tracers follow most things that move, but they are not heavy tracers like with DPT. The tracers follow their source, but they seem to jump around as well, especially if I move my gaze to another location. This stuff's got potential for greatness, seriously. 4:00 AM I'm tripping still. Appetite was good, but I hadn't eaten much during the day. The total trip was around 10 hours, but I was still feeling little bits of it at 12-13 hours from when I dosed the first bit of it. Then it's gone. No nausea. No body load that I noticed at all. Must sleep... Falling asleep in car on the way home. Slept about 8 hours. Feeling fine the next day. About 24 hours after taking the NBOMe-mescaline, there are still some nice visuals that come rather suddenly and then leave within minutes. This only happened once. I would almost call these 'flashbacks', but I don't believe in such fairy tales and have never experienced a 'flashback'. This is an interesting compound and it deserves more research.

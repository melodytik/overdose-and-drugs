The first time I took it (only 2 mgs!) I got into my car and was talking to a friend on the phone. My destination was my boyfriend's restaurant which was a straight drive from my house no turns to worry about...yet suddenly I was confused, high, disoriented, had no idea if I'd passed the restaurant or not, or where I even was. It was hard to drive since it seemed like my car was huge and couldn't fit between the lines. Like I said before had had NO warning that I shouldn't drive on the stuff.

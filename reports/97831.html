Sometimes extremely high doses reported are errors rather than actual doses used.] I have dealt with many experiences with these three substances, Methylone, Mephedrone, and 4-Mec to the point where they don't react to my body as a substance, but more of a stimulant. Repeated use of all three chemicals for a total of 3 years with non-use varying from a time being 1 day- 15 days in that whole timespan has probably had a significant effect on my body and mind. I may be excluding/missing information, but this is because it's irrelevant or 'common-knowledge' already known about these drugs. Mephedrone. My first experience with drone was very euphoric, very uplifting and it was very apparent that it was necessary to redose. With constant use for several months (obviously there were days of recovery), I've realized that snorting mephedrone made me feel normal, and not being on this substance made me 'fucked up'. Plateauing (not getting any higher) was fairly easy, after about 5 hours of use due to seratonin depletion. I would always have extreme vascular constriction. I stopped mephedrone because of nasal congestion to the point where i was blowing my nose for probably twenty minutes just to do another rail which in fact was very unsuccessful numerous amounts of times due to no airway in my nasal passage. in my experience, Mephedrone has not shown up on the standard 5 panel drug test. Methylone. Now this was, is my favorite of all three. After talking to my provider about my fuckupness in the nose, he offered methylone as a substitute. I could be totally wrong but, at least the mephedrone I was acquiring had no effect when swallowed, only insufflated. Methylone became the miracle because it gave my nose time to recover. The most methylone I've taken in a single dose is 1000mg swallowed, and 500mg insufflated, leading to, being drenched in sweat, dry heaving leading to having a chemical film of foam on my tongue. Plateauing wouldn't occur until 12 hours after my first dose, but I could still get that kick, that I strived for, even though I knew it weren't possible to get back to the level of shudder vision when taking that initial 400mg dose. In my experience, Methylone has not shown on the standard 5-panel drug test, but on expensive hometests, there's the possibility of testing positive for MDMA & Methamphetamine. 4-Mec. I have only acquired 4-mec a couple of times. The first time it was due to no supply of Methylone so 4-mec was in substitution. At first, I eased into this new drug and from a 50mg bump, I was confused whether to expect more a lone or drone high. It didn't take much at first, only 50m-150mg for the first few doses, but I had heart palpitations and extreme vascular constriction so I took a day break. Every dose after this, vascular constriction was apparent, but started to go back to normal, and there were no heart palpitations . The thing is, I plateaued extra fast and there was no euphoria unlike methylone and mephedrone. I was chasing my tail like crazy missing that initial high, where I would be swallowing grams and snorting 500mg to feel 'More awake' since I tend to find myself awake for 72 hours with no real energy, only fueled by straight speed. I have only felt like puking once from these higher doses, but never managed to chuck. 4-mec in my experience shows up as an amphetamine on a standard 5-panel drug test. This is a crude overview of my experience with these three chemicals, but I'd just like to keep you all informed.

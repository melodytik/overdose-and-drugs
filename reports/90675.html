中文译文 兴奋剂

我在下午5点吸了第一颗. 我几乎没有感觉到它,所以我认为它不像我以前得到的其他东西那么强大. 我本该买梅菲德龙, 然而我的经销商说他们跑出来给了我一克MDPV. 我第一次打开袋子的时候, 很奇怪, 效果很早就开始起作用;我想那天自中午以来我没吃过,这可能有所作为. 只有10分钟后, 我已经开始牙, 必须去加油站买一包口香糖. 经过30分钟到一个小时的不同效果, 这持续了大约两个小时, 现在我觉得我吸了毒, 开始下来, 我很累,想睡觉,所以这种不安的感觉让我很烦. 那天晚上我没有睡十分钟. 我很难专注于任何事情, 因为我的脑子不停跑, 每当我开始舒服的时候, 这种可卡因/可卡因感觉持续了6个小时. 现在我已经醒了几天了, 但幻觉还没有开始. 我发现没有什么能让这种感觉消失. 我喝了一把螺丝刀来缓和我的心跳, 一个小时的疲劳, 随后我服用了3毫克克洛尼平和许多非处方的睡眠辅助药. 我还在身体里工作, 但我的大脑却想睡觉, 我觉得如果我服用更低的剂量这会不会这么糟糕, 现在我知道晚上不应该服用太多, 这似乎像是狂欢或白天大剂量的药物. 我应该早点看一下这些帖子, 25毫克是足够的, 但我们都从错误中学习. 我希望下一次的经历更好. 其他: 单独生活 (16), 艰难经历 (5), 第一次 (2)

Jul 28, 2011. erowid.org/exp/90675 DOSE: insufflated MDPV (powder / crystals) oral Alcohol - Hard 3 mg oral Pharms - Clonazepam (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 125 lb I snorted the first bump of the gram I had gotten at 5:00 pm. I hardly felt it so I figured it was not as potent as other things I had gotten before. I was supposed to get Mephedrone, however my dealer said they ran out and gave me a gram of the MDPV. When I first opened the bag it was strange because it was not very like powder at all, much denser. But I got my scupula and took out enough to do a line, which turned into three because the effects started kicking in very early; I suppose I hadn’t eaten that day since noon which might have had a part. After only 10 minutes I had already started to grind my teeth and had to make a trip to the gas station to get a pack of gum. After about 30 minutes to an hour different effects came in, it felt like I was rolling. This lasted for about two hours, the euphoria feeling that is, then the adverse effects of the drug began. It now felt like I had smoked crack and was coming down, but it didn’t end. The restless feelings became irritating because I was tired and wanted to go to sleep. I did not get 10 minutes of sleep that night. It was terribly difficult to focus on anything because my mind would not stop racing, and I couldn’t concentrate on anything for more than a minute. I got terrible twitches every time I would begin to get comfortable. The coke/crack feeling lasted for about 6 hours. Now I have been awake for a couple days, but hallucinations have not started yet. I have found that nothing will make the feeling go away. I drank a screwdriver to begin with to mellow down my racing heart, I was tired for an hour, and then all effects of the alcohol were gone. I then took 3mg of klonipins as well as many over the counter sleep aids. This did not help either for then I was still wired in my body but my brain wanted to sleep, which lead to a night of tossing and turning in my bed. I feel that if I had taken a lower dose this would not have been so bad, I know now that a little is a lot, and it is much more effective and longer lasting than cocaine. It was worth the experience, now I know to not take as much late in the evening, it seems like a rave or day drug at high quantities. I should have looked at these posts before, 25mg is plenty and I took four times as much, not thinking. But we all learn from mistakes. I hope the next experience is better.

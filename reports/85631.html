美国 我被处方Lamictal为Effexor XR引起的躁狂发作. 我不认为我是双极性, 我只是认为高剂量的Effexor,225毫克, 我说话的速度很快, 睡眠不足, 精力充, 易怒, 拉米克塔尔使我的情绪稳定. 我对自己说的话没有过. 我打电话给我叔叔, 尖叫他因为他不在我的生活中. 我告诉我的同学们要闭嘴. 我和我的同事因为一件小事而发生争, 在Lamictal时, 我非常逼人. 我知道另一位服用拉米克塔尔的人非常逼人,经常发誓, 两周后, 我不得不停止使用Lamictal, 现在我正在服用Seroquel XR以及Effexor XR. 与Lamictal相比,Seroquel XR有助于缓解我的情绪不稳定, Seroquel的唯一主要副作用是昏昏欲睡,在一个月内消失了. Feb 23, 2011. erowid.org/exp/85631 DOSE: 100 mg oral Pharms - Lamotrigine (daily) 225 mg oral Pharms - Venlafaxine (daily) BODY WEIGHT: 178 lb I was prescribed Lamictal for manic episodes caused by Effexor XR. I do not believe I am bipolar, I just think the high dose of Effexor, 225mg, put me in the episodes. During the episodes, I spoke very fast, had little sleep, major energy, irritability, walked out of school, and spent a lot of money on unnecessary things. I also stole from stores, which was nothing like me. The Lamictal stabilized my mood. However, I had no filter in what I said. I called my uncle on the phone and screamed at him for not being in my life. I told people in my classes to shut the f*** up. I got into a fight over a petty thing with my co-worker, almost costing me my job. On Lamictal, I was extremely aggressive. I know another person that takes Lamictal who is very aggressive, swears frequently, and has an extremely short temper. Two weeks later, I had to stop Lamictal due to a rash associated with the drug. Now I am on Seroquel XR as well as Effexor XR. Seroquel XR helps with my unstable mood and has little side effects compared to Lamictal. The only major side effect of Seroquel is drowsiness, which went away in a month.

中文译文 致幻剂

我听说过很多关于这个化合物的好话. 从90年代的狂喜药替代品, 到作为一种嗜好药, 我听说它是舒尔金最喜欢的, 像往常一样,我认真地记录下来. 我听说它与LSD和MDMA相似, 我发现最相关的化合物是MDMA,MDA,LSD,2C-I,在某种程度上,2C-E以及25I-NBOMe和25B-NBOMe. 在我服用含有20毫克2C-B的囊之前, 我知道要寻找哪种味道. 粉末的颜色是浅灰棕色的, 味道很像纯粹的甲. 没有任何异常,如果有的话, 它的味道稍微比其父脊椎更甜. 我在这个短暂的,即兴的,可能不准确的试验之后, 我感觉到我的身体有点温暖. 现在很难看出任何其他影响. 我认为这是第一次警报. 我在伸展时感觉到一些温暖的刺痛, 但可能是安慰剂. 也许触觉也略有增加, 0:50 - 我感觉有点昏迷, 发现自己打哈欠. 我通常会在乙胺和4-AcO-DMT的出现时这样做, 它没有三胺的感觉, 提醒我我在2C-I, 2C-E, 和2C-T-2的时间. 感觉就像一种胺, 味道就像一种胺. 我做了一个快速的十分钟的午睡, 我以前只做过2C-I. 1:00 - 我睡了一会儿, 睡了一会儿之后, 我写下了以下内容: '我已经经历了一小时了, 的意思是什么? 是的 清醒的头脑? 没有. 我应该没有清醒的头脑? 我不知道. 有没有更明亮的颜色? 很难说. 我眼睛的瞳孔甚至没有扩张. 但当我站起来,移动时, 突然开始撞击. 我从来没有经历过这样的经历. 在5分钟之内从相对清醒到+到++的感觉是值得注意的. 现在的颜色和灯光更亮了. 现在有追踪器. 现在我非常温暖和刺痛. 1:10 - 我回想起之前我做过25B-NBOMe的时候, 我在之前的报告中也提出了2C-I和25I-NBOMe之间的相似性, 在许多方面,我会说这是真的... 2C-B对音乐的作用是我发现的化合物中最有趣的事情之一. 对于我来说,它就像LSD或MDMA, 低音更强大,更重,更深入地触动了我. 听到这首歌的每一段, 就像LSD一样, 我可以感觉到声音, 这仍然是经历的核心主题. 随着音乐的发展,我注意到时间扩展也开始增加. 1:30 - 效果无疑是独特的. 它与2C-I有很大的相似之处, 这种情况似乎会持续到永远. 但不同的是,整体来说,回归速度更快. 它也比2C-I更热, 没有恶心. 他们的进步也更顺利. 2C-I需要到此时才开始显示2C-B在30分钟前的位置. 显然是典型的胺感觉. 让我感到头,不安,身体...然而,有一种奇怪的,稍微金属般的平滑感, 对于我来说,LSD是最常见的, 而在迷幻曲中出现的酸合成器的声音让我想起了这种感觉... 我可以清楚地感觉到手指尖下的键盘在振动. 这对我来说也是新鲜事. 我发现自己在按低音的节奏打字. 我记得在25B-NBOMe上打字, 发现自己发现了另一个相似之处... 我发现音乐有时会让我觉得太多了. 我明白为什么舒尔金喜欢这个合并. 他和安说他们欣赏的化合物是"不太石化". 这种化合物, 舒尔金的最爱, 非常适合这个法案. 这是一个非常清晰的头脑. 我发现自己有思想,有时很空虚, 几乎是以一个基本的主题的方式, 首先想到的是2,5-dimethoxy-phenethylamine母分子的多少变化已经被创造出来, 有机化学如何提炼原始产品, 这些思想和我的思想有多深? 我发现自己躺着玩弄着手中的任何物体的跟踪器. 在这种情况下,它是一支电子烟, 我挥舞着它, 发现了美丽的长彩虹色的痕迹. 追踪器至少延迟了3秒, 终于追上了静止物体. 虽然这种化合物可以在社交环境中使用,但它在这些环境中,内省和内向. 三点 - 淋浴时间. 这证明是完全有利于经验. 我获得了许多洞察力, 并以第三人称,独立的角度思考我生活中的许多问题. 分析了它们. 他们分开了. 在某些方面甚至有点精神, 在这样做时, 洗过我的皮肤的水再次证实了这种化合物是多么的触觉. 我甚至可以在淋浴室墙壁的凝结液中形成的水滴中看到模式. 随着墙壁的水流下来, 在那里也形成了模式! 模式在此时很强大, 并且在任何地方呈现出来, 树枝对我来说并不像在LSD或DMT中那样突出, 我看到的东西好像在振动. 4:30 - 峰值开始减弱. 开始时大约是+++, 但在接下来的一个小时内, 虽然身体的刺痛开始消失, 颜色的增强和痕迹仍然存在, 在这座建筑中也能找到幽默. 音乐也开始失去它的魔力. +++ 到 ++ 6:30 - - 经验的95%都在此结束. 只有很少部分的旅程仍然. + 基线. 我还记得听到Spongle在这个化合物的下降, 听到的,感觉到的,感知到的, 颜色和纹理与声音一起爆发. 总体评论: 但也许这是一件好事. 无论如何,这完全是值得的. 我可以在晚上5点服用这种药物, 没有问题. 在一天的工作结束时, 或周末下午, 音乐节? 当然! 狂欢节? 为什么不! 和朋友一起玩,或者在水酒吧? 这也很酷. 峰值持续了大约3个小时, 下降的速度和上升一样快... 我希望我能再坚持一个小时. 这将是很好的. 考虑到一切, 我有丰富的经验... 2C-B可以把很多事情弄得很简单. 但我觉得如果有人把这种体验当作是LSD或MDMA的替代品, 另一方面,我认为几乎不可能忽视使这种化合物独一无二的原因. 这就是你的脸! 它向你展示它能做什么, 并不隐太多. 但它在起伏和下降方面都很温和, 最重要的是, 没有后的MDMA忧郁. 没有头痛. 没有什么. 这里是干净的, 里面和外面, 就像LSD一样. 其他: 发布时间:2018年6月9日 2C-B (52):令人兴奋的经历 (4),第一次 (2),未知背景 (20)

Its the Bs Knees 2C-B by MrMoran Citation:   MrMoran. "Its the Bs Knees: An Experience with 2C-B (exp106241)". Erowid.org . Jun 9, 2018. erowid.org/exp/106241 DOSE: 20 mg oral 2C-B (capsule) BODY WEIGHT: 160 lb I have heard many great things about this compound. Everything from it being an ecstasy replacement in the 1990s, to it being an aphrodisiac, to it helping supplement MDMA psychotherapy sessions. I had heard it was Shulgin's favorite, and I think I can understand why it was. As usual, I took diligent notes. Most of all, I have heard it being described as similar to LSD and MDMA, but different from either. Compounds most relevant I found to this review were things such as MDMA, MDA, LSD, 2C-I, and to some extent, 2C-E, as well as 25I-NBOMe and 25B-NBOMe. 0:00 - Before I take the capsule containing 20mg of 2C-B, I sample a small taste of the compound. Having tasted pure phenethylamine powder before, I know what taste to look for. The powder, a lighter grey-brown, and fairly clumpy, tasted very similar to pure phenethylamine hydrochloride. Nothing abnormal, if anything, it had a slightly sweeter taste than its parent backbone. I proceed to take the capsule after this short, impromptu, likely inaccurate litmus test. 0:30 - There was a slight warmth that had spread throughout my body. It was hard to discern any effects otherwise at this point. I'd consider it the first alert in a sense. There were some warm tingles here and there, first felt in my hands when I stretched, but it could be placebo. Maybe a slight increase in tactile sensations too, but again, hard to really tell at this point. +/- 0:50 - I feel slightly lethargic, and find myself yawning. I tend to do this on phenethylamine come-ups as well as 4-AcO-DMT, but this was unmistakably different for 4-AcO-DMT. It had none of the feelings of the tryptamine, and reminded me of times I was on 2C-I, 2C-E, and 2C-T-2. So far, so good - feels like a phenethylamine, tasted like a phenethylamine. I took a quick ten minute nap on the come-up, which I had only ever done with 2C-I before. +/- to + 1:00 - As I was laying down, after my nap, I wrote down the following: 'I am now an hour into the experience and I am STILL not sure if I am feeling it. Tingly? Yes. Clear-headed? Certainly. Am I supposed to not be clear-headed? I don't know. Are colors brighter? Tough to say. My pupils aren't even dilated at this point.' But yet... when I stood up, and moved around, it suddenly started to hit. It was weird, as I have never really experienced that before. Just the sensation of going from being relatively sober to a + to ++ within the matter of 5 minutes was something worth noting. Now colors and lights are brighter. Now there are tracers. Now I am very warm and tingly. 1:10 - I recalled a time earlier when I had done 25B-NBOMe, and noted its effect on music. I hypothesized about a similarity between 2C-I and 25I-NBOMe as well in a previous report, in terms of their visuals, if the same held true between 25B and 2C-B. In many ways, I would say it was true... what 2C-B does for music is among one of the most interesting things about the compound that I found. To me, it was like LSD or MDMA, but uniquely different. Vocals were crisp and clear, bass was stronger and heavier and hit me more deeply. Hearing every part of the song clearly like LSD does, and feeling the groves of MDA/MDMA. Synaesthesia was also present around this time, where I could feel sounds, and this remained a central theme to the experience. As all of that was happening with music, I noticed that time dilation also starts to increase. ++ 1:30 - Effects are unmistakably unique. It shares a lot of similarity with 2C-I, where the comeup seems to take FOREVER the comeup seems to take FOREVER , but it is different in that the comeup is faster overall. It is also warmer than 2C-I, without nausea present. The comeup was smoother as well. 2C-I took until this point to begin to show where 2C-B already was 30 minutes ago. Unmistakably, the classic phenethylamine feeling. The one that gives me that giddiness, restlessness, body buzz... and yet there's a weird, slightly metallic smooth feeling, which reminds me of the trippier elements, which I can only name as 'crook-shankedness' or 'twerkiness.' (I should note that this is a word I have arrived to independent of the already existing slang word, 'twerk'). These are most prevalent on LSD for me, and the sound of an acid synthesizer featured in psychedelic trance songs reminds me of the feeling... and yet, these are brought out in this particular phenethylamine! As I was typing with the bass from my speakers eminating, I could feel very clearly and distinctly the keyboard beneath my fingertips vibrating. This, also, was new to me. As such, with me discovering this as well, I found myself typing to the beat of the bass. I recall typing on 25B-NBOMe, and found myself finding another similarity... how good it felt to actually type things. However, I noted that at some points, music felt like it was just TOO much. ++ to +++ 2:00 - I see why Shulgin likes this compound. He and Ann would say they appreciate compounds that are 'not too stoning.' This compound, Shulgin's favorite, fits the bill very much. It is very clear-headed. I found myself thoughtful, spacey sometimes, and very insightful. My thoughts would find themselves on tangents upon tangents, almost in a fractaline sort of way, with an overarching theme. Topics first thought about were how many variations of the 2,5-dimethoxy-phenethylamine parent molecule have been created, all with uniquely rich and interesting effects. How organic chemistry can refine crude products, and create beautifully pure experiences. How deep do those valleys of thought and my mind go? 2:30 - I find myself reclining and playing with the tracers of whatever objects are fixated in my hands. In this case, it was an electronic cigarette, and the silvery top to it was reflecting the sunshine slightly. I waved it around, and noticed beautifully long rainbowy tracers. There was at least a 3 second delay for when the tracers finally caught up with a the still object. While this compound can be used in social settings, it is teetering on those settings and being introspective and introverted. 3:00 - Shower time. This proved to be wholly beneficial for the experience. I gained many insights, and thought about many problems in my life in a sort of 3rd-person, detached perspective. Analyzed them. Picked them apart. It was even lightly spiritual in some ways, but not nearly in the way that Shrooms or LSD are. And while doing so, the water washing over my skin reaffirmed how tactile this compound really was. I could even see patterns in the droplets that formed in the condensation on the walls of the shower. As water ran down the walls, patterns had formed in that too! Patterning was strong at this point, and presented itself everywhere, and the patterns could only be described as a mix between geometric and organic in shape. Tree branches didn't stand out for me like they do on LSD or DMT, but they were a minor point of interest. Things in my vision felt as though they were vibrating. +++ 4:30 - Peak begins to wane. Starts at about a solid +++, but over the course of the next hour wanes to a ++. While the body tingles began to fade around here, color enhancement and tracers remain, as well as insightfulness. Good humor could be found in this compound, too. Music also began to lose some of its magic around this point. +++ to ++ 6:30 - This is when the experience would be about 95% over. Very few parts of the trip remain. + to Baseline. I recall listening to Shpongle on the comedown of this compound, and it still seemed pretty groovy. There was a lot of play between with what was heard and felt and perceived. Colors and textures popped with sounds. Overall commentary: It was a bit shorter than I would prefer in terms of overall experience, but maybe that's a good thing. It is wholly worthwhile regardless. It is the kind of compound I can take at 5pm and be fine to sleep around midnight or 1am. No problem. At the end of a day's work, or maybe an afternoon on the weekend, it is a solid choice. Music festivals? Sure! Raves? Why not! Kicking it with friends, or maybe a hookah bar? That'd be cool too. The peak lasted about 3 hours, and comes down as quickly as it comes up... I wish I would have lasted just another hour or so longer though. That would have been nice. All things considered, I had a royally rich experience... 2C-B blows a lot of things out of the water. But yet, I feel like if one were to go into this experience thinking of it as a replacement for LSD or MDMA, they might be missing what really makes this compound unique. On the other hand, I think it's practically impossible to ignore what makes this compound unique. It is in your face about it! It shows you what it does, and doesn't hold much back. But it's gentle in its comeup and comedown, and best of all, there is no hangover from it. No post-MDMA blues. No headache. Nothing. It is clean, in and out, like LSD.

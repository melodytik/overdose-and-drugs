"Infinitely More: An Experience with Huasca Combo (Syrian Rue & Acacia confusa) (exp105756)". Erowid.org . Dec 24, 2021. erowid.org/exp/105756 DOSE: T+ 0:00 2 g oral Syrian Rue (capsule) T+ 3:30 6 g oral Acacia confusa (ground / crushed) BODY WEIGHT: 140 lb Setting: My home. I fasted the whole day after having a small breakfast. Background: 18yo, 137lb. I have used MG, DXM, HBWR, Acacia Confusa (multiple times), and weak Amanita Muscaria. Goal: I wanted to experience a strong DMT trip. I was in a happy, carefree mindset I was in a happy, carefree mindset as I had just finished loads of homework on Saturday :). Ingredients: Ground Syrian Rue seeds and powdered Acacia Confusa Bark. PREPARATION I ground 2g of Syrian Rue seeds using my mortar and pestle, and encapsulated them. I then measured 6g of A. Confusa, encapsulated 3g, and mixed the other 3g into some peanut butter. EFFECTS 4:20 pm I swallowed the Syrian Rue. Shortly afterwards I ate a large piece of homemade toast with butter. 7:50 pm I took all the Acacia Confusa powder. I was still barely even feeling the S. Rue, but I just couldn’t wait any longer—I needed some sleep tonight. 9:00 pm approx. I had experienced satisfying although not intense OEV and CEV, and I assumed the trip was nearly peaking—but it was a very weak peak so I went downstairs and ate another slice of homemade bread with butter, an orange, and some orange juice. Somewhere around 9:30-9:40 pm I was just finishing up my snack when the trip hit me like a train. I felt the “heavy” DMT effects so strongly that I could not move out of the chair I was sitting in—I couldn’t even reposition my arms or legs to a more comfortable position. All of a sudden the cabinets just took on a more saturated color, and EVERYTHING became trippy. I was in awe as I watched the kitchen do whatever it did. I still can’t explain much of what happened, but I do remember that the green countertops we have disappeared and became a galaxy of stars. I also experienced very vivid CEV, and felt like I was communicating with some type of DMT beings—they had faces like the masked voodoo guy from Scooby Doo. Additionally, the body load was extreme as always. My arms still felt like they were moving behind my back at odd angles and clapping down onto honeycombs, and sometimes it felt like they would stick together for a second, or like they were weighted down with heavy sugar cubes (don’t ask me why). My whole body felt the effects, but I usually enjoy those arm effects the most. Honestly I can hardly even remember what it was like, but I know it is amazing and weird. I do remember that I was marveling about this feeling I would get in my hand—kind of like I was sliding a ring on and off my finger…I guess that’s the best way to describe it in terms of this world. Probably 45 minutes later I finally noticed that the trip was a little less strong, so I decided to try walking around. My movements were robotic, and I remember looking at my hands as I went to pick up my plate: they flopped down onto our island like I wasn’t controlling them, and I almost felt like I wasn’t in my body at all—unfortunately this mild OOBE feeling quickly subsided. Of course nothing looked like my house, and a few times I almost jumped out of my skin when I felt like people were watching me and then thought I saw them in the shadows. I went upstairs and started to head to bed around this time, but decided to listen to a little music first to see if the trip would continue longer…Nope, it was still slowly fading. I took this time to write down a few thoughts to help me remember this amazing experience: 'I’m gonna forget it all until next time, but it is infinitely more than you can even begin to imagine. There is stuff—and none of it is describable with words but it’s all so real. And then your body moves and it’s so weird, but everything feels natural like I’ve been here a hundred times—like I’m just a returning friend. It is impossibly easy to get frozen in time and just do nothing. Will I remember any of this? Will I remember any of this? ????' Unfortunately I don’t remember much of how it felt or looked, but I remember being utterly amazed and confused and speechless. The comedown was in full fledge around 11 pm. I felt very uncomfortable and kind of jittery, so I just put on some soothing music and went to bed. I got about six hours of sleep (woke up at 5:00 am for school), and I felt tired but decent. This time I do notice one weird sensation that’s lingering: my face started to feel like it was sneering on my left side, but when I look in the mirror it isn’t. It’s pretty annoying and kind of scary, but I can’t attribute it to the DMT necessarily because I first noticed this on Tuesday, and I tripped Sunday night—I went the whole Monday without noticing anything…I also can’t find any other reports of this happening. I assume the face thing is unrelated.

作为一个多年来使用过的可代因药物, 起初,我有一份15毫克的处方, 我开始沉迷,在课堂上把它们扔回学校, 总体上我喜欢它们带来的平静放松的感觉. 我意识到自己是个上, 停止经常服用, 当15毫克的剂量停止工作时, 我能够让我的医生开出30毫克的药物. (菲奥里纳尔) 它们很有趣, 确实给我带来很大的打击, 因为我对药物没有初始耐受性, 我一下子只能服用一粒药, 效果很好, 有一天晚上,我感到无聊. 我整天都睡着了,感觉还挺好. 我看到了我的可代因瓶, 我打开一个药丸,然后扔掉它. 我知道我不能把它全部吸进去. (我以前曾吸过毒品, 但从来没有吸过毒品) 我用牙签在桌子上分离了一些小的粉末, 我倾斜了一下, 吸了一小堆. 它已经燃烧了. 我没有咳,也没有打喷, 只是感觉像鼻子上有点水. 大约5分钟后, 我感觉放松了. 我认为',这真的很好... 我认为由于它没有经过我的肝脏, 而是直接进入我的血液, 感觉还很好, 我下身, 吃了一堆小东西. 我觉得有趣,我想做更多. 我等待着它开始起作用. 像高中时的激烈经历一样, 我的身体真的很糟糕, 我试着走路,我有点像在. 我还独自一人在我的房间里,时间是晚上十点半. 我最初的剂量大约是10.15左右. 愚蠢地, 虽然这是我从来没有吃过的最糟糕的东西 (我已经喝了一段时间了, 这个人做到了. 我不再感到如此放松和温和. 我只是不再有感觉了. 我和一些人聊天, 无法打字. 房间有点扭曲了. 我开始想知道到底发生了什么事, 我开始感到恐慌,我的心跳如同一样. 我不知道自己想哭还是想自杀. 很像我吸了太多大麻, 经历了一次糟糕的旅行, 我设法让自己平静下来, 但每隔几分钟, 我不知道我吃了多少. 这不是很多,不超过1/4的药丸. 半个小时, 我不停地感到恐慌, 我感觉到自己很冷, 我不知道发生了什么事, 发誓再也不会吸. 我之前曾多次服用过这种药, 整体情况在一段时间内仍有轻微的扭曲. 我几乎看到了新的视角. 形状变得不同,但颜色保持不变. 这不像幻觉剂,更像是醉酒. 皮肤开始, 就像我服用代代因时通常会一样. 晚上11点半,除了和,大部分都停止了. 我从来没有做过这样的事. 我已经得到了严重的身体像我喝醉了,但我从来没有疯了. 我没有服用其他药物, 我确实早些时候在晚上6点半左右服用了泰伦醇, 我不知道为什么发生了除了我吸了它 没有. 在它平息后, 我想要吸更多. 我发誓我再也不会这样做. 发布时间:2018年6月28日

Jun 28, 2018. erowid.org/exp/23394 DOSE: T+ 0:00 oral Acetaminophen T+ 3:30 insufflated Butalbital T+ 3:30 insufflated Codeine T+ 3:30 insufflated Caffeine T+ 3:30 insufflated Aspirin BODY WEIGHT: 135 lb As a codeine user for years, I had some fun with it. Originally, I had a 15mg prescription for migraine headaches. I became addicted, tossed them back at school during classes and generally loved the mellowed-out relaxed feeling I got off them. I realized I was addicted and stopped taking them so often, but still enjoyed them once in a while as a way to get messed. When a 15mg dose stopped working, I was able to get my doctor to prescribe a 30mg one. (Fiorinal) They were fun, really hit me hard as I have no initial tolerance for drugs, it usually takes a while and they were, to me, a double dose. I had only ever taken one pill at a time and they did the trick well, I got really relaxed and usually ended up falling asleep peacefully. One night, I got bored. I had spent the day asleep and felt pretty ok. I saw my bottle of codeine and decided to try snorting it, see if it would be any different. I broke open a pill and dumped it out. It seemed like so much powder to me I knew I couldn't snort it all. (I have done drugs before but have never snorted anything in my life). I used a toothpick to seperate some small mounds of powder on my desk, maybe 1/4 to 1/5 of the pill in total. I leaned over and snorted a small pile. It burned. I didn't cough, didn't sneeze, just felt like I had some water up my nose. About 5 minutes later, I got a relaxed feeling. I thought 'Hey, that worked really nicely... Better than a whole pill.' I figured since it didn't go through my liver and such first, and instead went straight into my bloodstream that it was more effective. Feeling pretty ok still, I leaned over and took in another small pile. It no longer burned in my nose, I found it kind of fun and I wanted to do more. I waited for that to kick in. It came over me like a wave, it was almost like some of the more intense experiences I had in high school, my body was really fucked up like I was drunk and I was just stuck on one thought. I tried walking, I kind of went zig-zag like. I was still alone in my room and the time was 10.30 pm. My initial dose had been around 10.15 or so. Stupidly, although it was the most fucked I had ever gotten off the shit (It had been a while since I'd taken it so maybe that did it) I snorted some more. That one did it. I stopped feeling so relaxed and mellow. I just stopped feeling. I was chatting to some people, couldn't type. The room got a little distorted. I started wondering what the hell was going on and I stumbled into the bathroom. I started freaking out and my heart was going off like a jackhammer. I didn't know if I wanted to cry or kill myself. It was much like when I had smoked too much weed and had a bad trip, couldn't escape and wanted to die. I managed to calm myself down but every few minutes I'd start panicking and I thought my heart was going to explode. I don't know how much I took. It wasn't very much at all, not more than 1/4 of a pill. For half an hour, I kept freaking out periodically, but gradually it got less and less. I got very cold and felt like I was coming down off it. I wondered what the fuck had happened and vowed never to snort it again. Of all the times I had taken it before, I never had this happen to me. Everything was still slightly distorted for a while. It was almost like I was seeing things in a new light. Shapes became different but colors remained the same. It's not like a hallucinogen, more like being drunk. My skin started to itch all over like it does normally when I take codeine. My 11.30pm, most of it had stopped except for the itching and some chills. Of all the times I've taken it, nothing like this has ever happened. I have gotten severe body fucks like I was drunk, but I never freaked out. I'm not on any other pills, I did take a tylenol earlier around 6.30 pm, but I've had many Tylenol-2's before so I don't think it had anything to do with it. I don't know why it happened except for the fact that I snorted it . Right after it subsided, I wanted to snort more. I swore I wouldn't do that again.

我对植物 (尤其是精神活性植物) 感兴趣. 我了解了科勒乌斯, 我买了一棵大约1英尺高的植物, 我基本上剪掉了整个植物 (大约60到70片叶子加上茎). 我在厨房的水槽里冲洗了叶子和茎, 经过彻底的混合 (切割) 我有一个黑色的液体与一个薄层的泡在顶部. 我在塑料杯的底部了几个洞, 用棉花覆盖了底部, 我很快就喝完了, 坐在房子里的一个隔离房间里. 关闭窗户和灯光. 以下是我在效果过程中记录的笔记: 在东部时间下午4点喝了液体. 效果开始于下午4点10分 微妙的 我感觉到一种在我的脸肌肉. 我觉得自己很安全. 下午4点20分:听力似乎从耳朵跳到耳朵,来回. 呼吸加速,但还是平静. 周围的一切都在微妙地扭曲和移动. 不是很多,但有些. 肩膀和手臂有轻微的紧张. 下午4点40分:我觉得睡觉很累, 但不想睡觉. 像是在清醒的梦境状态. 我睡着了, 梦见了我一生中最真实的梦想:在巴西雨林里游泳, 这是一个假觉醒. 忽然陷入城市战争, 眼睛的视力是模糊的. 我看着房间的周围, 似乎一切都是小方块. 在半小时内, 效果会消失. 我在那天早些时候吸烟, 但在实验期间我没有吸烟. 我一直有经验说,在使用幻觉药物时吸烟会使药物的效力减弱,我开始比如果不吸烟会更快地减肥. 我在许多不同的幻觉药物上试验了这个理论, 发现我吸烟时, 旅行并不像戒烟时那样长或强烈 (我最近戒烟了). 时间表: 46787 科勒乌斯 (168):第一次 (2),独自 (16)

Downing the Black Liquid Coleus by C. Skeleton Citation:   C. Skeleton. "Downing the Black Liquid: An Experience with Coleus (exp46787)". Erowid.org . Oct 10, 2005. erowid.org/exp/46787 DOSE: 65 leaves oral Coleus (extract) BODY WEIGHT: 220 lb This past summer I got seriously interested in plants (especially those which are psychoactive). I learned about coleus and wanted to try it out. I bought a plant about 1 ft. tall for $2.50. I clipped basically the entire plant (nearly 60 or 70 leaves plus stems). I rinsed the leaves and stems in the kitchen sink and placed them in a blender along with precisly 1 pint of water. After thoroughly blending (chop) I had a blackish liquid with with a thin layer of foam on the top. I cut holes in the bottom of a plastic cup and covered the bottom with cotton balls to filter the remaining plant matter so I would just have the black liquid. I drank it down quite fast and sat in an isolated room in the house. Windows closed and lights out. Here are the notes I recorded during the effects: Drank liquid at 4:00 pm EST. Effects begin at 4:10 pm Subtle. I feel a sort of humming in the cheek-muscles of my face. I feel safe and secure. 4:20 pm: Seems as though my hearing is jumping from ear to ear; back and forth. Accelerated breathing but calm. 4:30 pm: A subtle warping and movement in all things around me. Not much but some. Slight tightness in shoulders and arms. 4:40 pm: I feel sleepy but not like I want to sleep. Like being in a lucid dream state. From 4:45 to 6:30 pm I fell asleep and had some of the most realistic dreams of my life: On safari in Brazilian rainforest hacking out a trail through the jungle with a machete. False awakenings. Suddenly thrown into urban warfare running from building to building carrying an AK-47. When, finally, I awake for real at 6:30 pm my vision is hazy and blurred. I look around the room and everything appears to be made of small square blocks. Within a half hour the effects fade. Earlier in the day I smoked cigarettes but abstained for the duration of my experiment. It has always been my experience that smoking cigarettes (tobacco) during the use of hallucinogenic drugs causes the drugs power to weaken and I begin coming down sooner than I would if I hadn't smoked. I have experimented with this theory on many different hallucinogens and found that when I smoked the trip was not as long or as powerful as it was when I abstained from smoking (I recently quit smoking).

- IAP (印尼胺) 的运行总结 - 我最近有机会和一些朋友一起品尝这款新鲜的零食, 我读过最初的报道和赞扬, 这不是你父亲的麻醉药, 然而,它是一个非常有趣的和全新的药物. 虽然不一定按这个顺序. 对于80公斤的男性,30毫克的效果绝对不够,50-60毫克的剂量范围也非常温和和柔软,但效果远远大于30或40毫克. 75毫克的效果在高峰期处于不舒服的状态,焦虑的刺激音调并没有增加较低水平的愉悦感. 我认为我会让别人把剂量提高. 我发现我最喜欢的化学物质是60毫克. 对于我来说,它的效果最像AET. 一些微妙的精神刺激,清晰的刺激, 很难分类, 因为没有其他类似的东西. 很容易出现在公共场合, 社交, 谈, 你在某种物质上的事实很容易被忽视. 另外值得一提的是, 我同伴也注意到了这一点, 评论说他的性欲和潜伏本能开始高. 非常清洁和清晰的感觉物质,刺激没有太多的身体负荷或副作用. 这种化合物在与GHB结合时才真正闪耀, 没有任何疑问, 这是我尝试过的最好的G组合之一. 我怀疑酒精也会有同样的愉快感, 我和我的同伴都认为, 单独来说, IAP 感觉不太令人兴奋, 肯定是重复按! 我还没有尝试使用大麻或其他物质, 动力学是有趣的, 一个长的坡道到峰值1.5-2小时与2-3小时的高原之后一个非常长的清醒刺激尾巴. 总的来说,效果似乎持续10-12小时. 很好让你在聚会上保持清醒. 除了非常低剂量的实验外, 我需要环境辅助才能在夜晚结束时入睡. 这种材料似乎没有任何与它相关的崩像典型的MD. 尽管整晚没有睡觉, 恢复似乎很好, 我通常很敏感于医学课后的后果, 这不是问题. 一个有趣的工具, 看到别人对此有什么看法会很有趣. 其他:

Oct 26, 2003. erowid.org/exp/27688 DOSE: oral IAP (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 80 kg - IAP (Indanylamphetamine) runup summary - I recently had the opportunity to sample this novel morsel and put it to the road test with some friends. I had read the initial glowing reports and praises, so had hopes but kinda figured the first reports would be highly subjective and somewhat inaccurate. Well one thing is for sure, This is not your Father's MDMA, no big rushing love gusher here... However it's a pretty interesting and completely novel drug. So far I’ve tried oral dosages of 30,40,50,60,75mg (though not necessarily in that order). For 80kg male the effects at 30mg level were definitely not enough for full flavor, 50-60mg dosage range was also very mild natured and soft but much more of an effect than 30 or 40mg. At 75mg the effects bordered on uncomfortable during the peak, with an anxious over stimulation tone that did not add much to the pleasantness found at lower levels. Think I'll let someone else push the dose higher. So far I’ve found that my sweet spot is 60mg for this chemical. It's effects seem most like AET to me. A little bit of everything in there, a slight psychedelic sparkle, clear stimulation, a little bit of serotonin mood lifting, but all on micro scale. It's quite difficult to catagorize because there isn’t anything else quite like it. Very easy to be in public situations, sociable, talkative, almost like a designer mood than ecstacy type experience for me. The fact that you're on a substance can easily be lost in the background. Also deserving mention was that the substance made me pretty horny. My fellow traveler also noted this and commented that his libido and prowling instincts kicked into high gear. Very clean and clear feeling substance, stimulation without too much body load or side effect. In combination with GHB is where this compound really shines, once again the yin/yang phenomena at play. The G provides a *very* nice euphoria synergy with IAP, no question about it, one of the best G combinations I have tried. I suspect alcohol will be similarly pleasant, although probably much less euphoric than G in combination. My fellow traveler and I both commented that by itself IAP wasn’t all that exciting in terms of feel good potential, but with G... Repeat button for sure! I have yet to try with cannabis or other substances, but I suspect it will combine well with the relaxers in general. The kinetics are interesting, a long ramp up to peak 1.5-2 hours with a 2-3 hour plateau followed by a very long wakeful stimulation tail. In total the effects seem to last 10-12 hours. Good for staying awake all night at party. For all but the very low dose experiments I required ambien assistance to achieve sleep at the end of the night. The material does not seem to have any crash associated with it like typical MD's. Aside from the general wear and tear of staying up all night without sleep, recovery seemed A-OK with no mood depression or lag behind in the following days. I'm typically very sensitive to the after effects in the days following MD class experience, and this was no problem. All and all a mixed bag and interesting tool for the connoisseur treasure chest... Will be fun to see what others think of it.

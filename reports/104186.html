我已经读了bk-2C-B. 我认为它是2C-B的新"法律"类型. 政府无法阻止我跌倒. 这就是毒品法! 诚实地说, 我对此并不感兴趣. 我以前曾经使用过真正的2C-B和其他迷幻药物, 主要是LSD, 我曾经尝试过和DMT, 设置: 我朋友加入了一个乐队, 我们的朋友们去看她在我们城市的首次演出. 演出结束后, 她邀请我们回到乐队成员之一的公寓, 我们开车穿过半个城市到达那里. 这是一个相当繁忙的聚会, 很多药物在周围. 麻醉药,药丸,可卡因和大量的大麻. 我在两天前服用了MDMA粉末, 我仍然感到丧, 我只是喝了几杯酒, 不同的人群开始混在一起, 过了一会儿我的朋友告诉我, 我知道它一定是bk-2C-B所以我问我的朋友带我去他知道这可能是我唯一的机会尝试bk-2C-B现实上我永远不会去买下一个合法高网站. 经验: 我找到了那个男人, 问他有多少, 他很高兴免费提供一些. 他从口袋里拿出一个满是白色粉的袋子. 它被标记为研究化学品. 贝塔基-2,5-二甲-4-甲胺,不适用于人类食用 他试图说服我把炸弹,但我只是想要一个小线. 我拿起一块十块钱, 吸了一根肥胖的迷幻药. 我估计我服用了50毫克, 仅仅是标准剂量 (~80毫克) 之下的. 经过几分钟, 鼻孔后部出现了尖的疼痛, 我开始感到大脑中的声. 仿佛有一只或一只黄蜂进入了我的脑海, 虽然这非常不舒服, 但并没有太困扰我, 因为我以前在2C-B经历过这种情况. 终于我脑海中的声和喉中的药物变得太多了. 我是快速的上来. 这太快了. 我闭上眼睛时, 看到鲜的色彩散落在我的视线上. 我开始感到压倒,所以我不得不坐在外面一会儿. 我的朋友给我带来了一些水, 我在花园里吐,因为声使它消退,我感到奇怪地清醒. 我重新加入了这个党, 像烟花一样, 我的高潮迅速停滞, 我抽了一些大麻, 买了一片随机的狂欢药 (我没有服用), 接下来,我给自己提出了一个二分法:要么接受Bk-2C-B是个废话, 我很愚蠢地选择后者. 这个人很乐意提供更多, 因为他以15英的价格买了1克. 我甚至试图给他钱,他拒绝了. 我把大量的粉末 (大约100毫克) 放入香烟纸中, 我没有感觉到什么. 我等了一个小时. 我仍然没有感觉到什么. 至少我没有付钱. 我和朋友决定去买披萨, 然后坐出租车回家. 到了我上床的时候, 已经是凌晨四点左右了, 我睡着了. 我突然发现自己在我的房间里醒了. 我感到恐惧的感觉. 我觉得房间里有人在说话, 我开始流汗, 凯尔特人十字架的碎片吞了我的视力, 我感到最奇怪的感觉, 最终我意识到这种事情已经发生了, 这使得这次旅行更容易承受. 我已经控制住了自己, 喝了一些水来平静下来. 我不知道这持续了多久, 我对时间的思考能力消失了, 我在第二天醒来时感觉很累. 我睡了大概3到4个小时. 幸运的是第二天我没有工作, 我对这种物质的结论是它没有什么特别的. 除了持续时间,它与常规的2C-B并没有太大不同 (正常: 3-4小时,Bk: 8-10小时). 我保证它将在明年或更长时间内被禁止,我不太烦. 我只会坚持正常的2C-B. 或许明年我会写一篇关于Bk-2C-E的报道, 时间表: 104186 bk-2C-B (618):第一次 (2),一般 (1),各种 (28)

Stick to Real 2C-B BK-2C-B by Volvic Citation:   Volvic. "Stick to Real 2C-B: An Experience with BK-2C-B (exp104186)". Erowid.org . Nov 2, 2015. erowid.org/exp/104186 DOSE: 1 line insufflated bk-2C-B (powder / crystals) smoked Cannabis oral bk-2C-B (powder / crystals) Alcohol BODY WEIGHT: 160 lb I had read up on bk-2C-B. My understanding of it was a new “legal” analogue of 2C-B. One oxygen atom extra and the government can't stop me from tripping. That's drug laws for you! It's being sold on legal high sites here in the UK and to be perfectly honest, I wasn't really interested in it. I have previous experience with real 2C-B and other psychedelics mainly LSD and I have tried Mushrooms and DMT once so I'm not new to the psychedelic experience. The setting: My friend had joined a band and our group of friends went to see her play her first gig in our city. After the gig she invited us back to the flat of one of the band members for an after-party. We drove halfway across the city to get there. It was quite a busy party with a lot of drugs going around. MDMA, pills, cocaine and a lot of cannabis. I had taken MDMA powder 2 days prior and I was still feeling depressed from the comedown so I wasn't having any of that, cocaine I'm not interested in. So I was fine just having a few beers and a couple of joints. The different crowds started mixing and after a while my friend told me that a guy at the party had some “legal high 2C-B shit”. I knew it must have been bk-2C-B so I asked my friend to take me to him as I knew this might be my only ever opportunity to try bk-2C-B as realistically I'm never going to bother buying off a legal high site. The experience: When I found the guy I asked him for a bit and he was more than happy to provide some for free. He pulled a baggie out of his pocket filled to the brim with white powder. It was labelled as if it was a research chemical. “Beta-Ketone-2,5-dimethoxy-4-bromophenethylamine, Not for human consumption”. He tried to convince me to take a bomb of it but I just wanted a small line. I grabbed a tenner and snorted a fat line of psychedelic goodness. I estimate I took 50mg of it which is just below a standard dose (~80mg) I was well aware that you need much more of Bk-2C-B than normal 2C-B which I only usually take about 15mg of. After a few minutes a sharp pain erupted in the back of my nostril and the pain dripped into the back of my throat. This pain grew exponentially and I began to get a buzzing sensation in my brain. As if a fly or wasp had got inside my head and was flying about. Although this was extremely uncomfortable, it didn't bother me too much as I have experienced this before on 2C-B. Eventually the buzzing in my head and painful drug in my throat became too much, however. I was coming up fast. Too fast. Upon closing my eyes, bright vibrant colours splattered across my vision. I was becoming overwhelmed so I had to sit outside for a bit. My friend got me some water and sat with me for a bit. I vomited in the garden from the buzzing which caused it to subside and I felt strangely sober. I rejoined the party and was well aware that I was not tripping. Like a firework, my high had plateaued extremely quickly and fizzled into sobriety. I chilled out for a bit smoked some weed, bought a random ecstasy tablet (which I did not take) and had a laugh at some maniac who was out of his face on MDMA dancing like he was in an Ibiza super club. At this point I presented myself with a dichotomy: Either accept that Bk-2C-B was shit and just leave it or ask for another bomb. Stupidly, I went with the latter. The guy was more than happy to provide more as he had bought 1g of the stuff for £15. I even tried to offer him money which he refused. This time I'd learnt my lesson and I dumped a large portion of the powder (~100mg) into a cigarette paper and swallowed the bomb. I felt nothing. I waited an hour. I still felt nothing. “Ah well, at least I didn't pay.” I thought to myself. My friends and I decided to hit the boost and we went to get pizza and jumped in a taxi home. By the the time I got into my bed it was about 4am, about an hour and a half since I'd taken the bomb. I drifted off to sleep. Suddenly I found myself awake in my room. A feeling of terror shrouded me. I thought there were people in my room talking, I started sweating uncontrollably and my vision was shaking to the point where focusing on anything was impossible. Celtic cross fractals engulfed my vision and I felt the strangest sensation as if I was becoming separated from my own head. Eventually I realised that this shit had kicked in which made the trip more bearable. I was able to grasp control of myself and drink some water to calm me down. I'm not sure how long this went on for, my ability to contemplate time disappeared although it was bright by the time I drifted off to sleep. I awoke the next day feeling exhausted. I had slept maybe 3-4 hours. Thankfully I wasn't working the next day so I was able to get a can of coca-cola and a joint to help me recover. My conclusion about this substance is that it's nothing special. It's not too different from regular 2C-B apart from duration (Normal: 3-4 hours, Bk: 8 -10 hours). I guarantee it'll be banned in the next year or so and I'm not too fussed. I'll just stick to regular 2C-B. Maybe next year I'll be writing a report on Bk-2C-E, if there is such a thing!

这是一个神奇的礼物! (可提供8,12和24英寸的零件) 永远不要独自做 圣 (Salvia divinorum),阿曼尼塔斯 (Amanitas) 和西尼卡 (Ephedra sinica) 爆炸的星 "永远不要一个人去做:与Salvia divinorum,Amanitas和Ephedra sinica (exp19916) 的经验". 我买了一克萨尔维亚10X. 我在凌晨1点半左右服用了一克飞10X. 我没有感觉到任何东西, 持续了大约一个半小时, 为了在旅途中不睡觉, 其他人都在睡觉. 我走出去,装满了我的水, 用吹火机抽了萨尔维亚. 马上, 效果是可见的, 在第一碗之后,我很,还没有倒. 我进了大约5分钟,然后走出另一个碗. 我点燃了我的碗,我记得我的吹火和我的水对我说话. 我记得我的水看起来像它正在扩展到三角形, 我醒来了,然后说''. 我觉得自己是谈话的局外人. 我记得我觉得自己被困在一个位置, 我似乎在另一个维度. 我试图转身,但我不能. 我记得我试图移动自己, 我做到了, 我感到痛苦. 我觉得我把我的手通过我的甲板和砍掉我的一个手指. 我摸到了我认为是我被砍断的手指. 我开始担心一点,但我没有看到我的手指. 我知道我没有砍断我的手指, 我放下烟斗, 走进去, 拿着着的烟火. 似乎一切都在重复. 我再次进入另一个维度. 我终于醒来, 吹灭了吹火, 关闭了门, 我觉得手抓住我,阻止我进入. 我倒在后门,然后翻了起来. 我必须小心自己的行为, 否则他们会限制我. 我可能会猜测我的感受是精神病发作是什么样的. 我把烟斗和吹火机放在一边, 打开一些音乐,躺下. 我闭上眼睛时看到了轻微的视觉效果. 半小时后, 我已经接近基线了. 我的手指还疼. 第二天,我意识到有个大的泡, 我不确定发生了什么,但我认为我的手指可能触摸了热水碗或其他东西. 总之,Salvia 10X是一种非常有趣的实验物质, 很容易伤害自己, 不知道自己在做什么, 我很高兴自己没有受到严重伤害, 我完全没有为此做好准备, 穆亚黄还禁止我睡到凌晨6点左右. 截止日期:1996年 圣 (44):组合 (3),火车残骸和旅程灾难 (7),单独 (16)

Never Do It Alone Salvia divinorum, Amanitas & Ephedra sinica by ImplodedStar Citation:   ImplodedStar. "Never Do It Alone: An Experience with Salvia divinorum, Amanitas & Ephedra sinica (exp19916)". Oct 5, 2005. erowid.org/exp/19916 DOSE: T+ 0:00 1.0 g Amanitas (extract - 10x) T+ 1:30 0.25 tsp Ephedra sinica (dried) T+ 0:00 2 bowls smoked Salvia divinorum (extract - 10x) BODY WEIGHT: 280 lb Having tried regular Salvia divinorum without the trip I had heard so much about, I bought one Gram of Salvia 10X. At about 1:30 AM, I took one gram of Fly Agaric 10X. I felt nothing, and continued to feel nothing for about an hour and a half, so I decided to try my Salvia 10X. Before that, I took a pinch of the Mua Huang 5x foliage so I would not fall asleep during the trip. Everyone else was sleeping. I went outside, loaded my water bong and smoked the Salvia with a blowtorch. Immediately, the effects where noticable, similar to Marijuana, so much that I wondered if I was smoking Marijuana instead of Salvia. After the first bowl, I was pretty buzzed and hadn't really tripped yet. I went inside for about 5 minutes, then went out for another bowl. I lit my bowl and I remember my Blow Torch and my Waterbong talking to me. I remember my waterbong looking like it was expanding to a triangular shape, and it was kind of red. I came to, and I said 'woah'. I felt like I was an outsider to the conversation. Then, I remember feeling like I was trapped in a position, and it was impossible to move. I was in an alternate dimension it seemed. I tried to turn arround but I could not. Then, I remember trying to shift myself, and I did, and I felt pain. I thought I put my hand through my deck and chopped off one of my fingers. I touched what I thought was my severed finger on the deck. I began to worry a little, but I did not see my finger. I was relieved to know that I had not severed my finger. Then I put my bong down and went to go inside, holding my still lit blow torch. As I got close to the door, I was locked in a position again and everything seemed to be repeating. I slipped into the alternate dimension again. Eventually, I came to, and blew out the blow torch, turned the valve off, and grabbed my waterbong. I felt like hands where grabbing me, preventing me from going inside. I stumbled into the back door, and went upside. I still felt like hands where grabbing me, and I had to be careful what I did, or they would restrict me. I would probably guess what I felt is what a psychotic episode is like. I put my bong and blowtorch away and turned on some music, and laid down. I had mild visuals when I closed my eyes. I was beginning to come down, and after about a half an hour, I was relatively close to baseline. My finger still hurt. The next day, I realised there was a large blister where I thought it went through the deck. I am not sure exactly what happened, but I think my finger probably touched the hot water bong bowl or something. In conclusion, Salvia 10X is a very interesting substance to experiment with, but I must stress the importance of a sitter from this experience. It is too easy to injure yourself not knowing what you are doing otherwise, especially if you are holding a lit blow torch in one hand. I am glad that I did not injure myself very badly, however the blister on my finger will be a reminder to have a sitter present next time. I was totally unprepared for the experience, although it was enjoyable to take a break from reality. The Mua Huang also prevented me from going to sleep until about 6 AM.

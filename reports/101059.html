Jun 4, 2026. erowid.org/exp/101059 DOSE: T+ 0:00 60 mg oral Pharms - Baclofen (pill / tablet) T+ 1:00 50 mg oral Pharms - Baclofen (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 170 lb A Partially Enjoyable Experience I got ahold of 10mg Baclofen tabs, and did some research online to figure out if there was any sort of recreational value in them. Upon figuring out that other people had found success in taking recreational doses of the drug, I decided to experiment for myself. The night before I was planning to take the drug, I took a small test dose to make sure that I had no allergic reaction or the like to it. It began around 7 a.m. I took 60mg of Baclofen. I had eaten a light breakfast and was looking forward to testing out a new drug. The onset of the drug was quite mellow, with the first effects being felt around 30 minutes after ingestion. I felt a slight lightheadedness that intensified whenever I stood up. Though the feeling of being lightheaded is generally annoying and nauseating to me, on Baclofen it was different. It was enjoyable. It was about 15 minutes later that I noticed I was talking quite a bit more that I ever do. I am generally a reserved, quiet person, but the first dose of Baclofen had me chatting up my roommate like I never had before. An hour after ingestion, with the effects of the drug slowly becoming more and more pronounced, I took a second dose of 50mg. When the second dose began to take effect, I began to realize just how powerful Baclofen can be. At 10 a.m., 3 hours after the first dose, I reached what I figured out later was the peak. My head felt like it was in a fog. Whenever everything got quiet, there was still white noise in the background that sounded to me like the high pitched frequency whine of an old television. My roommate and I had spent the better part of the morning watching videos on the internet and such, and I found that my mood was bordering on giddy. Everything we watched or talked about had me laughing, often uncontrollably. After a short while, my roommate left, not to be back for hours. Around this time, I stumbled across an effect that I had never read about in my research of this drug: I had incredible energy. I stalked around the apartment, cleaning, dancing to music, washing dishes, and any other activity that would expend my energy. All the while, I found that I was in a wonderful mood, and had an increasingly positive outlook on life. Then, about 6 hours in, it began to change. Then, about 6 hours in, it began to change. For lack of a better way of putting it, I felt 'too high.' After the initial surge of energy and euphoria subsided, I found that the body and head high was still very much in effect, and only growing stronger. While this may sound pleasant, with the absence of the energy and euphoria, all that was left was dizziness, slight nausea, and a sort of 'drunken effect,' which had me stumbling around, to finally sit and wonder when I would come down. At this point I felt ready for the trip to be over as it had started to become uncomfortable. It wasn't until 12 p.m. (noon) the next day that I finally began to come down--31 hours after I had taken the first dose. Never before had I taken anything that kept me that incapacitated for so long. Even more surprising is the fact that my high never decreased in intensity after the euphoria wore off. I woke up just as dizzy and nauseous as when I fell asleep. I woke up just as dizzy and nauseous as when I fell asleep. I refused to smoke any cannabis as my friend had offered me a bowl, and it was simply due to being so out of it on Baclofen. By the end of the next day, I had fully recovered. Retrospect: Baclofen was indeed a surprising drug. I expected to just be very relaxed, and came away from the experience realizing that I had most likely taken too much given the fact that I had no tolerance to Baclofen. The come-up and outset of the peak are quite wonderful and indeed something different, but the extended amount of time spent wishing that I would just sober up makes this drug just not worth it to me--at least not at 11 times the prescribed dose.

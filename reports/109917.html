这是我找不到用处的药物之一. 我要求药剂师推一些催眠药, 他能做的最好的是一些1毫克的阿尔普拉佐拉姆药片, 草和15毫克的布斯皮龙药片, 他向我保证, 尽管巴斯皮龙是一种抗焦虑药, 我在第一天晚上服用了5毫克,感觉不太好. 我终于累了, 在此之后不久就睡了. 我没有睡眠问题, 晚上11点睡觉对我来说并不罕见. 第二天晚上我服用了7.5毫克. 酒吧有一条线分离出这笔钱. 我觉得有点疲, 但在一个小时后我平静下来, 今天,三小时前我决定最后一次尝试, 我感到疲,好像我刚刚度过了一天很长的一天, 轻微的压力,一些光敏感性. 这种疲劳与我服用低剂量抗胆固醇或镇静剂的感觉相似. 虽然我变得更加冷静,但我并没有变得更加冷漠, 这些话没有流动. 我觉得我已经过度疲了, 写这篇文章很复杂, 我希望这份报告不会在所有地方, 但如果是, 它显示药物如何影响我. 总而言之, 甚至第一代抗组胺药也比这更有趣. 我可以睡觉,但不值得. 已使用的证券 药品 - 布司皮龙 (155): 后期 / 总结 (11), 不适用 (38)

Apr 29, 2020. erowid.org/exp/109917 DOSE: 5 - 15 mg oral Pharms - Buspirone BODY WEIGHT: 180 lb This is one of the few drugs, that I cant find use for. I asked my pharmacist to recommend me some hypnotics since doxylamine and diphehydramine are not available in my country. The best he could do was some 1mg alprazolam pills, valerian and 15mg dividose buspirone tablets, that closely resemble these 2mg Xanax bars. I bought everything that was offered, and he assured me that even though buspirone is an anxiolytic its prescribed off label for sleep. I took 5mg the first night and did not feel much. By 11PM (3 hours after ingestion) I finally got tired, and fell asleep shortly after that. I dont have major trouble sleeping, and falling asleep by 11PM is nothing unusual for me. The second night I took 7.5mg. The bar had a line made for seperating exactly that amount. I suppose I felt a little exhausted but calm after an hour, but nothing major. Today, three hours ago I decided to give it one last try and take a single dose of 15mg. I feel exhausted, as if I just had a very long day, with a hint of nausea and the type of headache I get when I'm exhausted at the end of a day. A slight pressure, some light sensitivity. The tiredness is similar to the foggy feeling I get from a low dose of an anticholinergic or sedating antihistamine. Even though I am calmer, I am not more apathetic which is unlike any other anxiolytic. The words arent flowing. I feel like I am overexhausted from a long day and cant function anymore. Writing this is complicated even though words usually flow and make more sense. I hope this report isnt all over the place, but if it is, it shows how the drug affects me. In conclusion it makes me tired in a bad way. Even first generation antihistamines are more enjoyable than this. I can go to sleep, but its not worth it.

我决定去订购一些. 我犹了一下, 因为对2FMA没有太多的信息. 需要注意的是,我对2-FMA的意图并不是获得一个令人惊叹的聚会兴奋剂, 我过去使用的相关药物:甲基酸盐,乙基酸盐,胺,咖啡因. 我试着在我的手指上涂抹2-FMA, 检测是否有过敏, 这是一种柔软的白色粉末, 它的味道和气味像一样. 没有不愉快,但这就是我注意到的. + 00:10 一种轻微的刺激感, 没有面对面的冲动, + 00:20 吸入另一个15mg + 00:30 第二次剂量后,明显的刺激效应. 情绪稍微升高, 但没有什么高兴感. 我最能比较的感觉是高峰咖啡因, 没有任何偏执, 不舒服的紧张. 这是一个非常干净,功能性的刺激. 整体上是一个愉快的情绪, 和舒适的刺激水平. 在公共场合也能表现得更好. 没有令人不舒服的,口腔过度干燥或过度出汗 (所有这些都是我在使用德克斯胺时经历的). + 00:55 ~ 20mg 充气. + 01:30 摄入50mg后,显然产生了刺激作用. 没有什么高兴的, 我确实注意到口腔干燥和出汗增加, 但没有不舒服的或偏执, + 01:35 另外一个15mg的输入. 总的来说,我还是很积极的. 只有增加了负面影响 (出汗,口腔干燥,心跳跳动), 而刺激并没有大幅增加. 总共注入了65mg的2-FMA. 这似乎缺少许多刺激剂的负面方面, 如过度出汗,口腔干燥,肌肉不舒服和不安的感觉. 所以很难说2-FMA是否完全抑制了我的饥饿感. 由于这种化学物质的性质和我的意图, 虽然这并不糟糕,但当这种化学物质没有像一些兴奋剂那样提供大''时, 吸入一块块的白粉是不必要的. 注意:我在睡前服用了一剂二 (6小时每次使用2-FMA),所以我不确定2-FMA是否会阻碍睡眠,我也不确定2-FMA的效果持续时间. 时间表: 101804 二甲胺 (668):第一次 (2),单独 (16)

Sep 15, 2014. erowid.org/exp/101804 DOSE: T+ 0:00 15 mg insufflated 2-Fluoromethamphetamine (powder / crystals) T+ 0:20 15 mg insufflated 2-Fluoromethamphetamine (powder / crystals) T+ 0:55 20 mg insufflated 2-Fluoromethamphetamine (powder / crystals) T+ 1:35 15 mg insufflated 2-Fluoromethamphetamine (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 165 lb After hearing some second-hand positive experiences about 2-FMA, I decided to go ahead and order some. I was hesitant because there isn't a lot of info out there on 2-FMA. To note, my intent with 2-FMA was not to get a kick-in-the-ass party stimulant, but rather a functional psychostimulant such as dextroamphetamine (adderall). My past related use: Methylphenidate, Ethylphenidate, Dextroamphetamine, Caffeine. I tried a dusting of 2-FMA on my finger to test for any allergy, and decided to titrate my dose. T: 00:00 weighed 15mg of 2-FMA out on a scale. It's a soft white powder that forms clumps. Insufflated with a slight burn for ~30 seconds, and it smelled/tasted like rubber. Not unpleasant, but that's what I noted. + 00:10 A slight feeling of stimulation, no in-your-face rush, no euphoria. + 00:20 Insufflated another 15mg + 00:30 After the second dose, there was a clear stimulation effect. A slight elevation of mood but nothing euphoric. The best I can compare the feeling is to the peak of a caffeine buzz, without any paranoia, uncomfortable jitters. A very clean, functional stimulation. + 00:50 An overall pleasant mood, and a comfortable level of stimulation. Increased sociability and chattiness, but certainly able to function in a public setting. No uncomfortable bruxism, overly dry mouth, or excessive sweating (all which I experience on dextroamphetamine). + 00:55 ~20mg insufflated. + 01:30 After the 50mg total ingested, there is a clear stimulation effect. Nothing euphoric but mood is certainly positive. At this higher dose I do notice a slightly dry mouth and increased sweating, but no uncomfortable jitters or paranoia sometimes experienced with adderall/methylphenidate. + 01:35 An additional 15mg insufflated. + 02:20 Still a definite stimulation and overall positive state of mine. At this point I would say the only effects seen with the last dose was an increase of the negative effects (sweating, dry mouth, pulsatile heart beats) with no huge increase in stimulation. Conclusion: A total of 65mg of 2-FMA was insufflated. 2-FMA is a great functional stimulant that seems to lack many negative aspects of stimulants such as excessive sweating, dry mouth, bruxism, and a sense of jittery discomfort. I ate prior to use so it is hard to say whether 2-FMA fully suppressed my hunger or not. Due to the nature of this chemical and my intentions, I would take ~50mg orally to be used as a functional psychostimulant in a work/study setting. While not terrible, insufflation of a clumpy white powder was unnecessary when this chemical did not provide a big 'kick' like some stimulants. NOTE: I took a dose of benzodiazepine before bed (6 hrs s/p 2-FMA use), so I am unsure whether or not 2-FMA would hinder sleep, nor am I certain on the duration of 2-FMA's effects. I hope more people share their experience as chemical seems to have a better cost/benefit ratio than Rx psychostimulants out there!

我用药治疗与酸性反流有关的恶心. (是的,这是我得到的症状,而不是胃烧). 我通常在早上开始恶心, 起初,它 (phenergan) 是伟大的. 事实上,这是非常愉快的. 我会在醒来时服用它, 感到恶心, 我可以开始我的一天感觉很好. 然而,几天后, 在夜间出现不安腿综合征. 人们嘲笑它, 但我可以告诉你, 我不能睡觉. 我几乎不能在床上. 这种情况发生在午夜左右,持续到凌晨6点左右. 在网上花了很多晚上寻找RLS以及该怎么办. 我花了一点时间才知道是费纳根. 医生同意并为恶心开了康巴津药, 为了让人觉得睡觉/愉快, 医生允许我这样做, 试图消除恶心, 另一个可能导致RLS的原因是茶. 我在服用时感觉很好, 但如果喝了太多天, 当我停止时, 几天后我开始退缩, 我不知道为什么其他人会患上RLS, 我从来没有听说过有人承认轻度片戒断可能是原因, 科代因和氧康丁是一样的故事. 让我的系统重新开始工作 (令人讨厌的部分). 我已经注意到一个明显的因果关系超过4年. 偶尔使用上述物质对我自己, 那么你就会陷入诱惑, 不管是什么药物, 时间表: 82201 发布时间:2021年6月26日 药品 - 普罗米他辛 (336): 宿醉/几天后 (46), 医疗用途 (47), 不适用 (38)

Jun 26, 2021. erowid.org/exp/82201 DOSE: 25 mg oral Pharms - Promethazine oral Poppies - Opium (tea) BODY WEIGHT: 130 lb I take phenergan for nausea related to acid reflux. (yes, that's the symptom I get, not heartburn). My nausea usually began in the morning, apparently as a result of laying down all night. At first, it (phenergan) was great. Quite pleasant, in fact. I would take it on awakening, feeling nausea, and it would quell the nausea and make me a bit snoozy and, well, just pleasant. Then I could start my day feeling pretty good. However, after several days the downside kicked in. Restless leg syndrome at night. People make fun of it, but I can tell you that restless leg syndrome is a nightmare. I CANNOT sleep. I pretty much can't even stay in bed. It came on around midnight and kept up until around 6am. Spent a lot of nights online looking up RLS and what to do about it. It took a bit to figure out it was the phenergan. The Dr. agreed and prescribed compazine instead for the nausea, but I can't say that it is nearly as effective. It's tempting to take the phenergan for the sleepy/pleasant feeling, but it's just not worth it in the long run. So, I kind of go back and forth between them, as the Dr. allows, trying to get rid of the nausea, yet trying to avoid the RLS. fyi: another thing that can bring on RLS is coming down off poppy tea. I feel great while I'm having it, but if I sip it too many days, when I stop, after several days I start to withdraw and I can't sleep, I can get RLS, can be really sick to my stomach, and then there's the diahrea when the intestines kick in again. I don't know why some other people get RLS, and I've never heard anyone admit that mild opiate use withdrawal might be a cause, but, really, who's going to tell their Dr. that? Codeine and Oxycontin are the same story. It's all about shutting my system down for a while (the pleasant part), then having my system kick back in (the icky part). I have noted a distinct cause and effect over more than 4 yrs. of off and on occasional usage of the above substances for myself, just trying to feel decent. Of course, then the temptation is to keep taking whichever substance to put off the downside, then you're really in trouble.

我有抑郁症,焦虑,社会焦虑,强迫症. 我已经尝试了许多医生给我的药物. 有一天,医生给我一盒9毫克的EMSAM贴片. 我尝试了几个月, 每天一次, 没有明显的改善, 我决定每天戴上4个, 在剂量低于10 mg/ 天时,EMSAM是一种选择性MAO- B抑制剂. 我也想实现MAO-A抑制......因为理论上,MAO-A抑制会提高血清素水平, 我用酒洗手,然后连续地贴上, 现在我的手臂上有4个贴片. -00 戴上贴片 心跳加快,动力稍微增加. 我一整天都不那么焦虑,也不那么丧. 我有更多的精神活力, 音乐听起来有点好一些. - 8个小时 我开始感到头,头,非常疲. 我决定上床睡觉. 我躺在床上半睡了几个小时,然后起来. 我看着镜子, 眼睛里满是血, 每次我站起来, 我几乎会昏倒. 我相信这种低血压是由于EMSAM增加氧化血管扩张效应. 我觉得自己是极度脱水. 我有点不安. 整晚我躺在床上试图睡觉, 这种令人刺的失眠伴随着极低的血压和昏昏欲睡, 模糊的思维, 这种感觉可能被描述为'醉酒,醉酒和一整天外出工作的极度疲'. 现在是早上7点, 我正在脱掉所有贴片. 我意识到自己是个瓜......我甚至吃了大量的胺食物. 我整天都睡着了,整晚都抽. 其他: 药物 - 素 (228):医疗用途 (47),治疗意图或结果 (49),一般 (1),未知背景 (20)

Erowid.org . Jul 17, 2009. erowid.org/exp/78057 DOSE: 12 transdermal Pharms - Selegeline (patch) BODY WEIGHT: 150 lb I have depression, anxiety, social anxiety, OCD. I have tried many medicines the doctors have given me. One day my doctor gave me a box of EMSAM patches, 9 mg each. I tried them for a couple months, one a day, and I felt nothing. No improvement was evident, I felt just as tired and anxious as always. So I decided to put 4 on each day for 3 days, amounting to 12 total patches. EMSAM is a selective MAO-B inhibitor at doses below 10 mg/a day. I wanted to achieve MAO-A inhibition as well.....because theoretically, MAO-A inhibition would raise serotonin levels, which should help with my anxiety and OCD. I washed my arms with rubbing alcohol, then put on the patches, consecutively, one next to the other on my arm. So now (presently) there are 4 patches on my arm. -00 put on patches --5 minutes : slight stimulation, faster heartbeat, slightly increased motivation. I felt less anxious throughout the day, and less depressed. I had slightly more mental energy, and a brighter outlook on life. Music sounded somewhat better. -8 hours I started getting dizzy and lightheaded, very tired. I decided to go to bed. I lay in bed half sleeping for a couple hours, then got up. I looked in the mirror, my eyes were extremely bloodshot, my brother asked me if I had smoked weed. I walked around in a daze, I was super tired, every time I stood up I almost fainted. I believe this low blood pressure is due to the Nitric Oxide vasodilation increasing effects of EMSAM. I felt as if I was extremely dehydrated. I was a little agitated. The whole night, I laid in bed trying to sleep because my body was so tired, but I couldn't get to sleep. This ironic insomnia was accompanied by extreme low blood pressure and drowsiness, hazy thinking, and mildly improved mood. It could be described as a 'mixture of a booze hangover, a stonedness, and extreme exhaustion from working outside all day.' I havent slept in 3 nights now, it is 7 o clock in the morning, and I am taking off all the patches. I realized I was kind of stupid.....I even ate tons of food with tyramine. I just slept all day, and twitched all night.

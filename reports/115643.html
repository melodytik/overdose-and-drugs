Got myself the one narcotic that a specific source was selling : desmethyltramadol. I would like to specify what I think it truly is in terms of potential. The product is essentially the same as tramadol, but at a different dosage and without some of the stimulating side effects of tramadol. It has more to do with a regular narcotic. Feelings are indeed a lot less seroto-like, they are practically nothing like a serotonin agent IMO. What I get is some buzz, some nausea, some nod and ...a lot of overtime on the toilet seat. Looks like product I already know. But in terms of euphoria ? Quite a bit below morphine and other synthetics. I was a little disappointed. Even in sublingual or straight up powder form, it's not the most hardcore product. I went straight to normal doses (didn't bother trying out a half first) and then up to 100mg but I didn't feel nearly as buzzed as with the morph. Haven't tried other ROAs but it seems hard to smoke a product that requires high amounts, it's not practical. I intend to keep the rest as an "alternative" in case I get hurt and feel like analgesics are needed. I mean it does give opioid effects, but the intensity is less than those of products that are considered 1st tier. Also I found out that it assists sleep .

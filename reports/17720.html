我只能尝试一次4-MAR, 通过合成1.5g的产品, 产量远低于预期. 在耐受性日常使用甲胺的人群中, 4-MAR 与甲胺相比, 在使用甲胺的持续时间内消除了使用甲胺的欲望. 思想的清晰度是更有序的,少躁狂的性质. 我发现麻会让人变得很躁狂, 除了其其他的心理活动, 麻醉药的激励性作用也没有. 现在,4-mar比甲更有效的是提供一种高兴的体感. 4-MAR会让你感觉很舒服, 但它不会让你感觉像在超市里买一样, 你会觉得自己在家里, 由于4-MAR的非躁狂,非激励性质,它更适合于研究小组,或朋友之间的互动,思想交流. 它不适合调整项目,或出去完成一些你通常不会被迫做的事情. 这种药物不像甲一样, 而4-mar似乎更像是一种,非常清醒,刺激的药物, 它缺乏一些使得甲基变得如此有趣的化学物质的特殊特性. 4-MAR在日常使用中也可能具有这些持久特性,但从未以这种方式进行过测试. 如果我有足够的化学物质来制造甲基或4马合成器, 我会选择每次都制造甲基合成器, 虽然试用4-MAR对我有很大的兴趣, 我个人把甲基放在与其他药物分开的日常使用类别中, 因此我可能不是关于4-mar/甲基比较的最佳信息来源, 至于甲基替代品,没有. 它不应该被看作是甲基的替代品, 它是一种不同的化学物质, 时间表: 17720 4- 甲基胺诺雷克斯 (56):小组 (2-9) (17),回顾 / 摘要 (11),一般 (1)

Sep 25, 2002. erowid.org/exp/17720 DOSE: 4-Methylaminorex BODY WEIGHT: 200 lb I was only able to try 4-MAR once, and obtained from a sysntesis 1.5g of product, yields were much lower then expected. 4-MAR when compared to methamphetamine, in tolerant daily methamphetamine users, took away the desire to use meth during the duration of it's use. The clarity of thought achieved was of a more ordered, less manic nature. I find meth makes people very manic in addition to its other psycological activity, this was not felt. The motivational get up an go part of meth is also absent. Now what 4-mar does do better then meth is provide a euphoric body sensation. 4-MAR makes you feel very comfortable in the situation you are in, but not the feeling like meth gives you where you are in the supermarket, and you feel so at home that you could just sit on the floor, and it would be fine, a comfortable feeling like MDMA, but without the social lubrication. The 1g was consumed over 2 days with a grou of 4, so no long term sleep deprivation with 4-mar psycology is available; however, due to 4-MAR's non-manic, non-motivational nature it is more suited to a study group, or an interaction among friends, an exchange of ideas. It is not suited to tweaking on projects, or going out and about to accomlish something, that you normally wouldn't be compelled to do. In such 4-mar does not seem like much of a daily use drug, as meth does. Meth also has the ability to allow you to change patterns in your life directly just by thinking about them and acting on the idea; whereas, 4-mar seems like more of a strait, very clairvoyent, stimulant, it lacks some of the special characteristics that makes meth such an interesting chemical. 4-MAR may also have these enduring characteristics with daily use, but it has never been tested that way. If I had all the chems to do a meth or a 4-mar synth I would choose to do the meth synth every time for personal use. Although it was of great interest to me to try 4-MAR, it is not a drug I would put at the top of a psychonauts to do list, but if you have a pretty full resume then why not. Personally I put meth in a daily use category seperate from other drugs, so I may not be the best source of information regarding a 4-mar/meth comparison, but I have experienced MANY differnt chemicals, and in the stimulant class, without hallucinations, or an entheogenic experience the 4-MAR is the way to go. As far as a meth substitute, nope. It really shouldn't be veiwed as a meth sub, it is a differnt chemical, which is also a stimulant, but it sure isn't meth.

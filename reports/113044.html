Seeing My Essence LSD & S-Ketamine by sweatypie Citation:   sweatypie. "Seeing My Essence: An Experience with LSD & S-Ketamine (exp113044)". Erowid.org . Apr 8, 2019. erowid.org/exp/113044 DOSE: T+ 0:00 75 ug sublingual LSD T+ 4:00 2 bumps insufflated S-Ketamine (powder / crystals) What follows is my first experience with combining LSD and ketamine. I have snorted ketamine many times before, I have been way down the k-hole many times, and it's one of my favorite drugs. I have never taken a small dose of ketamine before this, always aiming for the k-hole or close to it. I also have many experiences with LSD, both on lighter and heavier doses (ranging from what's assumed to be 4-200µg). For this experience, I took around 75µg LSD sublingually at around 11am. At 3pm I decided I wanted to intensify the experience and gain a more dissconnected perspective on LSD, so I took two small bumps (which I would estimate to around 10mg) of s-isomer ketamine up my nose and laid down. I reasoned that a small dose should be enough for at least something to happen, and I didn't want to be nauseous from a big dose for the rest of my LSD-trip. I then experienced things I've never experienced on any other substance alone. All in all it lasted for about 30 minutes. For one, I had an experience which could be described as malplaced astral projection. I saw a dog as from the view of 5 inches below the floor. I saw its tongue from below and I saw it lay down above me. It looked incredibly real and now I like to play with the thought that I really experienced actual astral projection, even if I'm sceptical of it usually. I also spontaneously tried to find out what I am/the universe is at my/its essence, and I peeled of layers of concepts and even dimensions, and I saw myself/the universe in a 2-dimensional space as a dancing point, dancing along a line and twirling at the end. That movement that I saw is the same movement I always see warping the patterns on the walls and ceilings and other surfaces while on LSD. I think I did see something essential in myself, as a kind of mental pacing that can be seen as the reason why an ADHD-diagnosis describes my behaviour and feelings well. During this ketamine-trip, I was completely out of it, I not only saw the 2-dimensional world, I was as 2-dimensional as it, I had no 3-dimensional body and no concept of the world around me. So in a sense it was like the k-hole, except nothing like it, because like the k-hole I was not in my body or in this world anymore, but it was not the usual dark, hard, sterile environment that ketamine always is for me. Rather, it was very soft, colorful and lively, the way LSD is. This experience is the most magnificent thing I have experienced in a long time, and I will definitely keep mixing these two substances. I think powerful things can come of it.

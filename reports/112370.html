My first full on IV experience with this drug was harrowing, and at the same time beautiful and one of my most profound experiences but I can't help but feel a good part of the reason I lived through it was luck. I haven't since dared that dose of 500 micrograms. It was the most intense experience I've ever had, and wish I could experience it again without the worry of what I might be putting my body and brain through at that level of receptor overload. I will say though, that in terms of comedown this drug has been nothing but overnurturing. Personally, I experience no ill repute with this drug.

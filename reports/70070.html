In fact, it was absolutely terrifying, and still haunts my thoughts almost daily, more than a month later. I was hooking up with a friend of mine, and I asked if he had any poppers around, which we had used together before many times. He said he didn't have any, but offered me this other stuff in a silver spray can, which I know now (after a little post-experience research) to be ethyl chloride. He showed me how to spray it onto a tshirt and inhale the vapors, it reminded me of the cans of compressed air. Anyways, the first time I only did a normal hit and it just made me light headed and drunk feeling, but really good. A couple minutes later I tried spraying a bunch of it and inhaling it through my mouth. I slipped off into what felt like a deeper state of consciousness. I was totally worked up, shaking, freaking out, my friend calmed me down, and I felt pretty much fine after a minute or two, except for the memory of the experience, which is somehow both very vivid, yet impossible to describe. The experience really shook me to the core, and I'm sure I will never forget it. I'll never use it again, but I'd tell my friends to never use more than the normal small amount.

我曾经使用过其他类毒素和胺类药物, 并且对这种新药的强度感到震惊. 通常我有时间, 但在这次旅行我甚至不能读, 更不用说写. 我有两粒药, 每粒至少含有10毫克. 我把一个囊清空, 测试它在水中的溶解性, 我蒸发了水,把10毫克切成两堆, 吸了一口, 等待效果, 经过5分钟的安慰剂水平轻微的精神活动, 我躺下, 感觉到从头部开始的能量波在我的身体中流动. 我喜欢这种感觉,所以我把第二个囊的内容溶解在水中, 现在我有一些过敏药我可以享受森林. 经过短暂的时间, 一切都同时发生, 极端, 比我预期的要强得多, 这很像是DXM和DMT的混合. 我无法直行, 双臂摇, 我在可怕的德克萨斯噪音音乐下狂热跳舞, 然后准备走路. 我可以很容易地表现得清醒, 尽管幻觉非常强烈. 这些幻觉并不完全像4-HO-MET或其他胺类药物, 它们移动很快, 它们几乎完全像技术高超的数字艺术家复制典型的迷幻视觉, 结构移动和重叠, 石岩在我的天花板上生长. 我感到恶心,嘴里有强烈的塑料-金属-化学味道, 我可以正常行走一段时间, 但随机我会腰, 陷入醉, 一旦我从我服用药物的谷仓进入我的房子, 我停下来在浴室里尿尿, 一件事导致另一个, 我走到森林, 那里我有典型的迷幻体验, 我听着好听的音乐, 在烂的山谷中漫步, 我感到非常不稳定, 我和自己说话,用手做了很大的手势. 由于我跳过, 避开水池, 经历过程中我会变得非常虚弱, 腿部会摇, 我会感到头部急转, 我会坐在各种阳光浴,风景如画的地方思考和听音乐. 阳光照射着我的身体, 音乐是美丽的 想起那些悲剧和美丽的白日梦. 药物对我的心理效应是非常明显的, 我极度地感受每一种情绪, 我脑海中最深刻的想法是,任何可能发生的事情都已经以类似的方式发生, 虽然我一分钟害怕, 但也让我发笑. 我只是坐在长上感觉和平与美丽大约半个小时. 睡觉是很难的, 我很疲, 身体也很累. 我在休息之前服用了3粒50毫克的DPH药, 我睡到第二天午餐, 总的来说,这次旅行可以总结为令人陶醉,精神模仿,高兴,有时性感,和强大的. 我绝对学会了我在等待药物袭击时的耐心. 时间表: 116347 发布时间:2022年5月11日 乙 (23),4-PrO-DMT (902):单独 (16),组合 (3),第一次使用 (2)

May 11, 2022. erowid.org/exp/116347 DOSE: 5 mg insufflated 4-PrO-DMT (powder / crystals) 5 mg insufflated 4-PrO-DMT (powder / crystals) 10 mg insufflated 4-PrO-DMT (powder / crystals) 45 mg oral Ephedrine 150 mg oral Diphenhydramine (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 125 lb I had experience in other psilocin prodrugs and tryptamines among many other substances and was still shocked at the strength of this new drug. Normally I have time-stamps, but during this trip I couldn't even read, let alone write. I had 2 pills that each contained at least 10mg. I emptied one capsule and tested its solubility in water, to see if insufflating could be a good ROA. It was fairly soluble in room temp water, I evaporated the water and cut the 10mg into 2 piles, I snorted one and waited for effects, after maybe 5 minutes of placebo level mild psychoactivity, I figured I should take the other line, right after I did so the first dose must have taken effect because as soon as I sat back up I got a strong headrush. I laid back and felt waves of energy starting at my head flow down my body. I liked the feeling so I dissolved the 2nd capsules contents into some water and swallowed 45mg ephedrine with it. Now that I had some allergy medicine I could enjoy the woods. After a short while it all hit at once and it was extreme, much stronger than I expected, thinking it would be equipotent to 4-HO-MET. It was very similar to a mix of a DXM come-up and DMT hitting. I couldn't walk straight and my arms were swaying like they were only attached and not under my control. I danced wildly to scary Texas noise music, and then got ready to walk. At this point the initial rush had calmed down and I could act sober very easily, despite the very strong hallucinations. These hallucinations were not exactly like 4-HO-MET or other tryptamines, it was almost delirious, CEVs were close to DMT but darker and mostly shades of red and deep blue tones. They moved quickly and resembled facial structure. OEVs were almost exactly like how skilled digital artists replicate typical psychedelic visuals, which until now I always thought of as inaccurate. Textures moved and overlapped, and stalagmites grew on my ceiling. I was nauseous and had a strong plastic-metallic-chemical taste in my mouth, I had the feeling of crystalline chemicals rushing through my body. I could walk normally for a while but randomly I would hunch and fall into a drunken stupor, but never once actually falling down. Once I had made it into my house from my barn where I took the drug, I stopped to take a leak in my bathroom, one thing led to another and I discovered that this drug extremely amplified orgasms, and it seems the orgasm amplified the drug. I left to make my way to the woods, where I had the typical psychedelic experience complete with being even more anxious around strangers. I listened to great music and wandered around the lush valley nearing sundown in a psychedelic haze. I was frenetic and felt very unstable and jittery. I was talking to myself and making big gestures with my hands. Gravity and momentum were amplified as I hopped fences and avoided puddles. Throughout the experience I would get really weak and my legs would wobble, I'd feel a head-rush and like I lost a lot of blood, but a few seconds later I'd be back. I would sit and think and listen to music in various sun soaked, scenic spots. The sun hitting my body was euphoric. Music was beautiful. Thinking about tragic and beautiful daydreams. The psychotomimetic aspect of the drug was very apparent, I felt every emotion in an extreme way and had a few irrational thoughts. The biggest thought that dominated my head was that everything that could happen has already happened before in a similar way, modern tools and archetypes are analogous to those 100, 1000 years ago etc. When I made it back on the road I was startled by a motion sensor that emitted a painful high frequency, though I was scared for a minute it also sent me into a laughing fit. I just sat on a bench feeling peace and beauty for about half an hour. Once I made it home and showered, I was very slightly starting to stabilize, but sleep was difficult to achieve, I was exhausted and burned out. I took 3 50mg DPH pills before I was able to rest, and I slept in till lunch the next day, but didn't feel refreshed. Overall the trip can be summarized as intoxicating, psychotomimetic, euphoric, at times erotic, and powerful. I absolutely learned my lesson in being patient when waiting for a drug to hit.

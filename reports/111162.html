我去看了医生, 感冒的症状和得到一个巨大的注射器充满了清晰的液体注射到我的肌. 完全没有疼痛, 我甚至不知道它已经完成了, 但它了一会儿, 没有什么大不了的. 我从检查室走进大厅拿起我的文件时, 已经注意到我的心率有了显著的增加, 现在回想起来, 我很兴奋, 我已经相当睡眠被剥夺了从生病的转整晚连续几晚. 眼下皮肤已经开始变黑了, 那天晚上离开医生的办公室后, 我试着睡一会儿, 我很疲,但不能坐着 我无法平静自己的思想, 甚至无法入睡. 我终于整晚不睡觉, 眼睛凸着在网上浏览, 我也经常口渴, 嘴巴很容易干燥, 黎明轻松穿过窗的裂, 我觉得像垃圾. 我觉得自己是个好人. 我终于在一天之内靠着快餐生存下来, 晚些时候我吃了一大块披萨, 里面装满了配料, 喝了两升苏打水. 第二个失眠的夜晚就在我面前, 同样的浪漫冲浪, 同样的黎明入我的眼睛几个小时后, 到了第三天晚上, 我看起来像个尸. 我还生病, 肺部和鼻出现严重的二次感染. 精神敏似乎来来去去 ~70个小时后, 我仍然清醒但几乎没有功能. 精神敏似乎来来去去 只有在第三天晚些时候 (或是第四天?) 我才成功地昏倒, 如果这听起来很熟悉的话, 效果非常相似, 我醒来后又醒了24小时, 然后我的睡眠周期开始恢复正常, 每次我睡了4个小时后醒来 (我仍然不能睡8个整整小时), 我觉得眼睛里有碎玻璃, 似乎我仍然非常缺乏睡眠. 这种东西应该减少我的鼻炎症, 我还被处方给了普雷德尼松和一些可代因, 我认为我有睡眠的部分原因是因为可代因. 我也得到了很多, 现在我希望普雷尼松会减轻我从那瓶中感受到的迷你戒断. 德克萨是一种毒药. 我在感冒时, 几天都没有睡觉, 医疗方面也没什么好处. 时间表: 111162 德克萨米他 (920):单独使用 (16),宿醉/几天后 (46),医疗用途 (47)

Oct 27, 2017. erowid.org/exp/111162 DOSE: IM Dexamethasone (liquid) repeated vaporized Nicotine oral Codeine oral Pharms - Prednisone (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 150 lb Went to the doctor for flu-like symptoms and got a huge syringe full of a clear liquid injected into my butt muscle. It was completely painless, I didn't even know it was done by the time it was over, but it itched after a while, and then felt kind of bruised for a couple of days. No big deal. By the time I got out of the exam room and into the lobby to pick up my paperwork I already noticed a significant increase in my heart rate, and what felt like a more focused sense of mental acuity. In retrospect, I was amped, but in a way I didn't really recognize at the time. I was already fairly sleep deprived from being sick with the tossing and turning all night for a couple of nights in a row. The skin under my eyes had already started to darken by the time I took the shot. Later that evening after leaving the doctor's office I tried to take a nap because I was exhausted, but couldn't sit still I was exhausted, but couldn't sit still and couldn't calm my thoughts enough to even come close to falling asleep. I wound up staying awake all night surfing the Internet with my eyes bulging and constantly reaching for my nicotine vaporizer. I was constantly thirsty as well, with my mouth going dry easily between the effects of this crap and the propylene glycol from the vaporizer. Dawn eased through the cracks in the blinds and I felt like garbage. I was kind of sore from laying down all night and was hungry but couldn't bring myself to go make something to eat. I wound up subsisting off of fast food for the day, and not much of it. Later that night I ate most of a large pizza loaded with toppings and downed a 2 liter of soda along with it. Then the second night of insomnia was ahead of me, same bugged-out surfing session, same ass crack of dawn penetrating my eyes hours later, and more fast food throughout the day. I had trouble picking up after myself, and by the time the third night rolled around I looked like a zombie. I was still sick, with serious secondary infections setting in to my lungs and sinuses. ~70 hours after this shot I am still wide awake but barely functional. The mental acuity seemed to come and go ~70 hours after this shot I am still wide awake but barely functional. The mental acuity seemed to come and go , and it wasn't until late in the 3rd (or was it the 4th?) day that I managed to pass out hard, but only for a couple of hours. If any of this is sounding familiar to anyone, yes, the effects are remarkably similar, except I felt how tired I was and couldn't do anything about it. After waking up from that initial coma, I was again awake for another 24 hours, and then my sleep cycle started to normalize, but not without feeling extremely drained. It has been about a week since I had that shot and every time I wake up from a four hour nap (I am still unable to sleep 8 full hours) I feel like I have broken glass in my eyes and as if I am still extremely sleep deprived. This stuff was supposed to reduce the inflammation in my sinuses so they could drain. I was also prescribed prednisone and some codeine, and I think part of the reason I have had what sleep I have had is because of the codeine. I was given a lot, too, and now I'm hoping the prednisone will take the edge off of the mini-withdrawal I will feel from that bottle. Dexamethasone is poison. It kept me awake for days while I had the flu and didn't do anything for me as far as treatment goes as far as I can tell.

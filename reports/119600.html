Mar 3, 2026. erowid.org/exp/119600 DOSE: 8 mg oral Flubromazepam (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 60 kg This took place in May of 2025. I bought ten 8 mg blue flubromazepam pills from a Dutch research chemical website. The pills looked fine; I decided to take one around midday to see what would happen. The next thing I remember is waking up in my bed. I didn't know it at the time, but I had blacked out for 3 days since the first dose. At this time I didn't even remember ever buying or having the substance. I wake up; the first notice I see is that all my belongings are scattered throughout my parents house, and that my parents are furious at me. I didn't know why at the time but was able to piece two and two together and just told them I smoked too much weed. Turns out, on day 2 I apparently met up with a friend of mine to go party, the friend told me later that I could barely talk or walk normally. Mind you, I was like this for 3 days. Please never do this substance. You have a good chance of just blacking out for days like I did.

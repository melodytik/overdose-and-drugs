美国 几天前我第一次尝试了茶. 我相信这些花叫做波斯金色. 它们的尺寸从一个大核桃到一个网球大小. 我很惊他们有多大. 它们的颜色从浅色奶油到深棕色/黑色. 较浅色的豆更柔软,看起来更新鲜,而较深色的豆更干燥 (可能是干烧掉的?) 并且很容易碎裂. 我用了七个大来酒. 经过咖啡机, 我得到了大约10克粉. 我把两杯半杯水煮沸,把粉末倒进去,然后立即把从火焰上取下来让酒"". 我把茶了10到12分钟, 我用一个金属器将液与泥分离. 我用子的背面把所有的液体都挤干了. 时间是下午五点, 茶叶在接下来的十分钟内被吸入, 下次我会试着用蜂蜜或糖来装饰它. 我在20分钟的时间开始感觉到一些效应. 他们以一种温暖的身体放松的形式出现, 精神放松, 我看着电视, 非常满足于坐在那里, 只是享受温暖的冲动. 我在1个半月前使用了30个片, 在一个小时后, 效应的强度增加到大约两次5毫克的冲击. 我很放松, 身体充满热和高兴. 非常好! 我确实经历了一些轻微的胃, 但没有鼻. 强度似乎在三小时时段平衡在相同的2-3percocet高. 我在3个半小时的时间里有点难以排尿. 必须站在那儿一两分钟的时间, 才开始流动! 晚上9点 (T+4) 情况还很顺利. 但我可以说,我开始下来了. 我觉得很累, 于是我爬进去, 只有我无法入睡! 我在半清醒的梦中翻转了一整晚. 我也像个混蛋一样四处. 听觉上的有点令人愉快. 我不知道那天晚上我是否真的睡着了. 幸运的是,第二天我没有什么计划, 我躺在沙发上跌倒在内心的整天和睡觉像一个婴儿. 后果: 如果您喜欢片/止痛药, 身体上的不适很少, 除了一点胃和极端,但令人愉快的. 我还有一段时间出汗, 我建议在下午早些时候喝茶, 下次我打算尝试10-12个, 这将相当于约15克的粉末. 我认为这将是一个梦! 其他: - (43): 单独 (16), 准备/食谱 (30), 健康问题 (27), 第一次 (2), 一般 (1)

Erowid.org . Jun 25, 2001. erowid.org/exp/7522 DOSE: 10 g oral Poppies - Opium (tea) BODY WEIGHT: 190 lb A couple of nights ago I tried my first batch of poppy tea. The poppies were called Persian Gold Variety I believe. They ranged in size from a large walnut to about the size of a tennis ball. I was surprised at how big they were. They ranged in color from a light creme to dark brown/black. The lighter colored pods were more pliable and seemed fresher, while, the darker ones were much drier (possibly burned from kiln drying?) and easily crumbled. I used 7 large pods for the brew. After passing them through the cofee grinder I had about 10 grams of poppy powder. I brought 2 1/2 cups of water to a boil, dumped the powder in and immediately removed the pot from the flame to let the brew 'steep'. I let the tea steep for 10-12 minutes occassionally stirring the mixture with a wooden spoon. I then passed the brew through a metal strainer seperating the liquid from the poppy sludge. I made sure to squueze the sludge dry of all the liquid I could with the back of the spoon. The time was 5:00PM and the tea was imbibed over the next ten minutes, the taste was pretty nasty but bearable. Next time I'll try dress it up with some honey or sugar. I first started to feel some effects at the 20 minute point. They came in the form of a nice warm body relaxation an mental realxation typcal of opiates. I was watching T.V. and was extremely content to just sit there and do nothing but enjoy the warm flush. (NOTE: I am a person with little or no opiate tolerance I did use 30 percocets for achillies tendon surgery about 1 1/2 months ago, but, nothing since then.) At about the one hour point the effects grew in intensity to about two 5mg percocts. I was very relaxed with that all over warm body buzz and euphoria. Very nice! I did experience some slight stomach cramping but no naseau. The intensity seemed to level off at the three hour point at that same 2-3 percocet high. I had a little difficulty urinating at the 3 1/2 hr, point. Had to stand there for a minute or two longer before things started to flow! Things were still going quite nicely at 9:00PM (T+4). But, I could tell I was beginning to come down. I stayed up until about 11:00pm (T+6) and was pretty much baseline and feeling pretty washed out and tired so I climbed into be thinking I would drift off with no problems. Only I couldn't fall asleep! I tossed and turned all night in and out of half awake dreams. I was also ithcing all over like a bastard. The itching acually was sort of enjoyable in a weird way. I don't know if I acually ever fell asleep that night. Luckily I had no plans the next day because I felt very tired and unmotivated for the majority of it. I layed on the couch falling in and out of conciussness the whole day and slept like a baby that night. Aftermath: This was a very enjoyable experience if you like the opiate/painkiller high. There was very little physical discomfort except for a little stomach cramping and extreme , but, enjoyable itchiness. I also had sweaty plams for a while which wasn't even a discomfort. I would reccommend drinking the tea early in the afternoon or even in the morning due to the difficuly getting to sleep. Next time I plan on trying 10-12 pods which would be equivelant to about 15 grams of the powder. I think it will be quite a dream!

Oct 29, 2021. erowid.org/exp/115868 DOSE: 50 mg insufflated MXiPr (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 70 kg I had just received 500mg of MxiPr from a well-known research chemicals website and I was very eager to try it, alone with my cat, for science’s sake. I weighed 50mg of the white chalky powder, which is quite a strong dose . I was at the time trying to get at the core bottom of my mind, hence the heavy dose (which is not recommendable for a first try !). I ate my dinner (pasta) and proceeded to insufflate the powder I previously grounded and arranged in two small lines. It was about 20h30, I guess. T+5min : I was comfortably laying on my sofa, listening to a popular lofi radio, and I felt my nose and mouth becoming numb, as I felt the drop making its way through my body. I rapidly lost all consciousness of time and almost of conception of space, I didn’t know where I was anymore. My mind made up quite a variety of scenarios, it was like some sort of thought whirlwind about my life and more generally about the meaning of life, the place I was in, the body I occupy and couldn’t feel anymore (my extremities were totally numb, I remember petting my cat and it was really a weird unusual feeling to touch him). It was really exhausting It was really exhausting to be honest, my brain was on overspeed for a long moment. I remember to have vomited, because I had felt something going through my throat with the characteristic noise vomit makes, but it was absolutely painless. I cried for help a little, and for my mom. I succeeded to make it to my bed, which is quite incredible considering the total loss of motor control I experienced, I guess I was on my legs and hands ? I was sobering up though, and I watched my phone, it said 21:30. So the peak lasted for almost an hour. I was then in and out of sleep, petting my cat and feeling that something unusual was on him, it was not just his warm fur. I finally turned up the lights around 2:00 in the morning and saw that my cat was covered with some vomit, which made me have concerns about the state of my apartment. I succeeded to get up, which was quite hard given that my motor control was not entirely back, and saw that my living room floor was covered in vomit. Which sucks a bit if you want my opinion on it. I suffered no after effects, except for the dehydration and the associated headache linked to the vomiting, nothing that a healthy breakfast couldn’t break. If I have to make conclusions after this train-wreck of a first time, I’d say that you shouldn’t try an unknown substance with a heavy dose. Start lightly and make your way up if you feel like it (and put your poor cat out of the way, please).

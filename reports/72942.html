[Erowid的注意事项: 由于阿片类药物使用可能导致显著的耐受性 (需要更高的剂量来产生相同的效果),因此首次使用者使用的剂量明显小于常用者使用的剂量. 根据别人服用的剂量来选择自己的剂量可能是极其危险的. 第四次苏博试验 由于我从未见过有人用SHOOTING (IV) suboxone写报告, 我去看了suboxone的网站, 它实际上说,如果你在过去几天使用了任何阿片类药物, 基本上我读到了,因为我不会有问题. 我每天服用8毫克, 我不再使用毒品, 所以我决定服用大约1/4的药丸, 我担心ID会有问题, 因为suboxone仍然是一种片. 我担心的原因是它说当一个人用舌下服用suboxone时, nalaxone永远不会进入血液, 我开始担心,也许这给了我一些硬心跳,因为在约7秒后,我真的很热了大约4秒,然后我的心开始跳动,大约15秒后它消失了,我很感激没有问题. 我寻找常见的"疾病"的迹象, 没有. 我会说,一分钟后,我可以感觉到我从服用8毫克补充剂中得到的积极情绪转变. 但如果我只服用4毫克. 我用了更少的时间,而且通过静脉注射的路线更快, 想想一下 如果我真的很热, 实际上可以感觉到汗水从我的皮肤上出来, 我知道这与纳拉进入血液和大脑有关. 我觉得它有点像被布雷诺芬附着在受体上, 就像那拉克松说你不应该在这里. 我不是一个真正的激进者, 我也是你的朋友,一个对手. 我认为有两个人同时在那里,所有这些事情. 希望这是一些建议. 时间表: 72942 发布时间:2018年9月28日 纳洛 (339),药物 - 布普伦诺芬 (265):一般 (1),第一次 (2),未知背景 (20)

Overdoses of opiates can be fatal.] The **IV** Suboxone Trial Since I havent seen anyone write a report in SHOOTING (IV) suboxone, I decided to look into if the nalaxone would send me into withdrawl like I've read. Well I went to the suboxone web site and it actualy said that if youve used any opiate like heroin meth or morphine in the last few days then when one shoots up the suboxone they'll get withdrawls. So basically I read that as I wont have a problem. I take 8mg every day and I dont use dope anymore so I decided to take about a little more than 1/4th of the pill and crush and add water..then stir..then cotton...then suck up...then shoot. I was worried that id have a problem though because suboxone is still an opiate. The reason I worry is that it says that when one takes the suboxone sublingually that the nalaxone never gets to the blood stream because it cannot be absorbed when taken correctly. Well about 7 seconds after I shot the 1/4th of the pill I felt as if I got hot for a second...I started to worry and maybe that gave me some kind of hard heart beat because after about 7seconds from shooting I got real hot for about 4 seconds then my heart started pounding and it went away after about 15 seconds and I was thankful for no problems. I looked for the usual signs of the 'sickness' and there were none. Id say about a minute after the pounding stoped I could feel the posative mood shift I get from taking 8mg sub. But as if I had only taken 4mg. Which makes sense because I used less and it was a fast route via IV so I actually dont think this is a good idea to shoot. Think about it. If I got real hot n actually could feel the sweat coming out of my skin off of 1/4th an 8mg pill then I dont think shooting 8mgs would be good. I know for a fact it has to do with the nalaxone entering the blood and to the brain. I think its kind of wierded out by the buprenorphine attaching to the receptors right as the nalaxone is sayin hey ur not supposed to be here I was here first n the the bup. Says wait I'm not really a true agonist I'm also your friend an antagonist.. I think there was a problem there between the 2 being there at the same time and all that shit. Hope this is some advice.

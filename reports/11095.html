我在一个俱乐部和我的好朋友给了我一个酸的命中, 我很担心,但我知道我会和他度过愉快的时光. 我们跳舞了,我和一个可爱的女孩亲吻了,我们回家的路上去买了一些食物. 我必须在第二天早上上班,所以我想慢慢下来,这样我就可以睡觉. 我朋友告诉我他听说服用尼亚会使其效果几乎停止. 他有错误的信息! 我服用了200毫克的维生素,10分钟后, 一种灼热的疼痛开始在我的沟,然后我的下,我的部,我的脚... 我的朋友已经回家了, 和我的室友睡着了, 我跑到浴室, 在镜子里看不出自己. 我的脸是甜菜红色和胀我的眼睛 - - 我不得不抓出我的联系,因为我的眼球感觉就像有人倒了液体火在他们. 我跳进冷水淋浴里试着冷静下来. 我真的认为我可能会死,所以我喊叫我的室友醒来帮助我. 想确保自己不是在幻觉. 但当她跑进来时,她也尖叫着. 我的脚和手是明亮的紫色, 紫色的条纹跑到我的腿和手臂. 我的眼睛几乎胀了. 整個皮肤都被拉開了, 我也感到震惊, 于是我开始抽, 发, 淋浴使情况变得更加糟糕! 我试图让她打电话给急诊室问我是否可以进来. 我无法想象这种反应是由于酸, 因为我6小时前已经服用了它. 我知道这与尼雅有关. 我的室友很害怕, 几乎打电话给911但我没有保险, 于是我打电话给自己, 回答电话的人笑了笑, 我坐了两个小时, 在很多毯子下抽, 喝水, 医生说这只是对尼雅的过敏反应, 他称我的病情为"冲洗" - - 哈! 我实际上害怕自己会永久变形. 你不会相信我的样子. 我发现每天100%的尼雅剂量只有20毫克! 我服用的剂量是正常剂量的10倍! 我从来没有如此害怕. 其他: 亚酸 (2), 维生素 - 尼亚 (230):小组 (2-9) (17),健康问题 (27), 艰难经历 (5)

Dec 6, 2001. erowid.org/exp/11095 DOSE: T+ 0:00 1 hit oral LSD (liquid) T+ 6:00 200 mg oral Vitamins - Niacin (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 120 lb I was at a club and my good friend gave me a hit of acid, which I hadn't done in over a year. I was apprehensive, but took it knowing I'd have a fun time with him. So we danced and I made out with a cute girl, and we went to get some food on the way home. I had to be up for work the next morning, so I wanted to come down easy so i could sleep. My friend told me he heard that taking Niacin makes the effects virtually stop. He had the WRONG information!!! I took 200mg of the vitamin, and 10 minutes later, my whole body felt like it was on fire. A searing pain started in my crotch, then my armpits, my buttocks, my feet... all my hot points--and spread quickly. My friend had gone home, and my roommate was asleep, and I was freaking out. I ran to the bathroom and couldn't recognize myself in the mirror. My face was beet red and swelling up around my eyes--I had to claw out my contacts because my eyeballs felt literally like someone had poured liquid fire into them. By this point I was screaming, and I jumped into a cold shower to try to cool off. I really thought i might be dying, so I screamed for my roommate to wake up and help me. I was tripping very hard at this point and wanted to make sure I wasn't hallucinating. But when she came running in, she screamed too. My feet and hands were bright purple, with streaks of purple running up my legs and arms. And my eyes were almost swollen shut. All my skin was stretched and cracking. By the way, I was in shock too, so I was convulsing and shivering and chattering my teeth uncontrollably. The shower had made the condition much worse! I tried to have her call the ER to ask if I should come in. I couldn't imagine the reaction being from the acid, since I had taken it 6 hours before. I knew it had to do with the Niacin. My roommate was so scared she almost called 911, but I had no insurance and didn't want anyone to know I took drugs. So I called myself, while I could barely walk or talk. The man that answered kind of laughed at me and told me to just wait it out. I had to sit for 2 hours convulsing under lots of blankets, drinking water, tripping out. The doctor said it was just an allergic reaction to Niacin, which is very common. He called my condition 'flushing'--HA! I literally was afraid that I was going to be permanently disfigured. You wouldn't believe what I looked like. So the next morning, I looked at my multivitamin, and saw that 100% daily dose of Niacin is only 20 mg! I took 10 times the normal dose! I have never been so scared in my life.

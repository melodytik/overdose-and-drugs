我已经使用了很长一段时间的特拉马多尔和可代因. 我每天服用一次,总是在晚上,睡前几个小时. 我认为自己是个阿片类药物成者, 但由于价格和可用性, 我通常只能使用可代因和特拉马多尔. 我不经常同时服用两种药物, 我开始每天晚上服用可代因. 我爱上了这种药物. 效果非常快,令人难以置信, 通常很快就消失了. 我在寻找替代药物时遇到了特拉马多尔. 我开始每天晚上服用特拉马多尔, 药的高与可代因不同. 它们发作速度较慢,强度较低 (尽管在正确剂量下可以产生非常强烈的声), 而且持续时间更长. 我决定提交这份报告, 我喜欢这两种药物, 两种都有效, 我注意到从一种药物转换到另一种药物对我造成了一些问题. 我注意到当我从代因转换为特拉马多尔时, 我感觉不到疼痛的缓解, 感觉不好, 我真的没有得到任何特拉马多尔. 但是在第二或第三个晚上, 胺开始正常地在我的身体中起作用. 我会坚持使用它, 直到我的供应耗尽, 或者我对药物产生过多的耐受. 我最喜欢的时间是在前几周. 这不是很多,也许100或150毫克一晚安抚我的疼痛. 在大部分时间里,我感觉正常. 我开始每天服用200-300毫克或更多的药物, 药物开始影响我的生活. 我觉得自己的睡眠时间变得不规律. 我在白天变成了一个尸. 我总是很累,很气. 我有非常生动的梦想让我感到比睡觉时更疲. 我不喜欢用特拉马多尔达到这一点, 转向可代因是另一个故事. 我可以在第一天晚上服用代代因. 我没有感觉到什么, 但至少我没有感觉到拉马多尔. 虽然每天在服用前, 我都会感觉到特拉马多尔的停药, 退款对我来说是非常尬的. 身体上的不舒服, 情绪的波动也很大. 我有时会感到非常悲伤, 也非常快乐, 我总是发现自己在进行很多内心对话, 这段时间对我来说很艰难, 这几乎是一个神圣的时刻. 在此期间, 我感受到的情绪, 尤其是当我在收音机上听到一首歌时, 这让我想起了我生命中最快乐的时光. 我甚至发现自己偶尔会哭泣, 但这种状态并没有持续很长时间. 我每天晚上都服用代因, 没有任何问题. 在最初的几周里, 我发现自己感觉很活, 很多时候在一天中很有运动能力 (我很有运动能力...或者试图做到). 这可能是为什么我更喜欢代代因而不是特拉马多尔. 因为白天我感觉更好. 我认为这与我在服用可代因后的睡眠质量有关, 但随着耐受性的发展, 代代因很快就占据了我的生活. 编码因似乎更容易形成习惯,耐受性更快发展. 几周后,我发现我的身体和精神需要剂量... 如果我不按常规时间服用剂量, 我手指和关节变得硬. 我讨厌这种感觉. 我知道是时候再换药了. 现在我知道我需要改变药物, 每当我处于一个宽容程度的阶段, 我的生活就开始崩. 我相信我的家人和朋友也受到影响, 我从一种药物转换到另一种药物后的几天总是最好的, 有时我几乎感觉正常, 我只是希望我把片和阿片类药物作为一种娱乐性药物. 那些日子里他们比较有趣. 现在,它们只是一种生活方式. 其他: 药物 - (149), 代代因 (14):各种 (28), 旅行后的问题 (8), 和习惯性 (10), 回顾 / 总结 (11)

The Long Struggle Tramadol & Codeine by Aliasdck Citation:   Aliasdck. "The Long Struggle: An Experience with Tramadol & Codeine (exp46965)". Erowid.org . Jan 17, 2008. erowid.org/exp/46965 DOSE: repeated oral Pharms - Tramadol (pill / tablet) repeated oral Codeine (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 180 lb I’ve been using tramadol and codeine for a long time now. I take either drug once a day, always in the evening, several hours before bed time. I consider myself an addict to opiates and have had love affairs with other opiates such as hydrocodone and morphine. But due to prices and availability, I am usually confined to codeine and tramadol. I don’t take both drugs at the same time, or switch back and forth very often due to some reasons I will get to later on in this article. To summarize, I started taking codeine every night. I fell in love with this drug. The effects come on very fast, were incredible, and usually faded away rather quickly. My supply of codeine was dwindling, and I can across tramadol in my search for a substitute. I began using tramadol every night instead of codeine, because like codeine, it would take away my pain and also cause a very nice high. Tramadol's high was different from codeine. It came on slower, was less intense (though you can get very intense buzz off of the right dose), and lasted much longer. I decided to submit this report because I’ve discovered some things about these two drugs. I love both drugs, and both are effective, though codeine does help with pain more than tramadol does. I’ve noticed that switching from one drug over to the other causes some problems for me. However, I notice that when I switch from codeine to tramadol, my first few nights with tramadol are not very comfortable. Instead of feeling pain relief and a sense of well being, I tend to feel the effects of withdrawal from the codeine more severely. I don’t really get anything out of tramadol. But, by the second or third night, tramadol begins to act normally in my body. By this time, I am well settled with tramadol and will stick with it until my supply runs out, or I build up too much tolerance to the drug. My best time with tramadol is within the first few weeks. It doesn’t take very much, maybe 100 or 150mg a night to soothe my pain. For the most part, I feel normal during the day. But once my tolerance builds up, and I start taking around 200 to 300mg a day or more, and the drug begins to take its toll on my life. My sleeping schedules become irregular. I become a zombie during the day. I am always tired and very grumpy. When I do finally sleep I have very vivid dreams that leave me feeling more exhausted then when I feel asleep. I hate it when I get to this point with tramadol, so this is when I decide to switch back to codeine. Switching back to codeine is another story all together. I can take codeine the first night without a problem. I don’t really feel much from taking the codeine, but at least I don’t feel the withdrawals from tramadol. Every day before dosing, though, I feel the withdrawals from tramadol and these withdrawals usually last a few days. The withdrawals, for me, are very awkward. Aside from the physical discomfort, I get wide swings in my emotions. I can feel very sad at times, and also very happy, and I always find myself doing a lot of internal dialogue in which I work out a lot of problems in my life. This time is hard for me, but part of me likes it. It’s almost a sacred time. During this time, I feel emotions that I don’t get the chance to feel very often, and they are usually very amplified. Especially the feeling I get when I hear a song on the radio and it reminds me of a very happy time in my life. I even find myself crying from time to time, which is very unusual for me. But this state doesn’t last very long. After 3 or 4 days it’s gone, and I am taking codeine every night without a problem. During the first few weeks it’s great. The side effects are minimal, and I find myself feeling very alive and many times very athletic during the day (I am athletic... or try to be). This is probably why I like codeine more than tramadol. Because I feel better during the day. I think it has to do with the quality of sleep I am getting after a dose of codeine as opposed to a dose of tramadol. But, as tolerance develops, codeine soon takes over my life. Codeine seems to be more habit forming and tolerance develops quicker. After several weeks, I find my body and mind needing the dose... and if I don’t take my dose at the usual time I can become quite irritated. My fingers and joints become stiff. I hate that feeling. Once my tolerance to codeine gets really bad, the side effects just outweigh the positives, and then I know it’s time to switch again. Whenever I am at a point where my tolerance has built up, my life begins to fall apart. I’m sure my family and friends are affected, and I’m sure they can tell something is wrong with me. The best times are always after a few days after I make the switch from one drug to the other, sometimes I almost feel normal, almost like I’m not a drug addict. I just wish I had kept opiates and opioids as a recreational drug. They were much more fun back in those days. Now, they are simply just a way of life.

经过大量的研究, 我决定自己做好准备. 我在东欧吃了Amanita Muscaria但没有经历任何影响. 我把它们生吃了. 我在2022年5月8日在西北太平洋挑选了这些阿曼尼塔鱼. 另一个未成熟的帽子也长得这么长. 我在烤箱中在210度干了几个小时, 第一个完全开花的帽子在一个小时后几乎没有任何效果. 第二个,部分开花的帽子产生非常强烈的效果仅仅20分钟左右. 效果是如此强烈, 我觉得我的心在我的脑海里跳着古老的鼓, 我觉得自己正走在时间的边缘, 不受时间的线性约束, 我开始感到有点疲倦. 这就是我的记忆消失的地方. 我刚刚做了一个令人难以置信的清醒梦. 我把地球想象成一个圆形, 所以为了让这些人互相理解, 他们不得不彼此信任. 那些彼此信任的人成长和进步, 他们进步, 而其他人则退步. 这是一个令人难以置信的简化人类的历史, 随着他们的进步,他们的语言,爱和彼此的理解也随之增长. 这种物质只能被定义为魔法或药物. 我不能把它归类为其他任何东西. 睡觉后,我仍然感觉到一个深深的放松和和平躺在床上. 我在卧室里试图更换遥控器的电池. 除此之外,没有什么不寻常的在我的房子里. 我建议在使用这种药物时非常小心,请服用比我小的剂量. 我觉得对待我的方式很幸运. 时间表: 116362 发布时间:2022年5月23日 阿曼尼塔斯 - (71),梦想 (85):一般 (1),一个人 (16)

May 23, 2022. erowid.org/exp/116362 DOSE: 2 caps oral Amanitas - A. pantherina (tea) BODY WEIGHT: 210 lb After much research on Amanita Pantherina I decided to prepare and dose myself. I ate Amanita Muscaria in Eastern Europe but experienced no effects. I ate them raw. I picked these Amanita Pantherinas on the 8th of May 2022 in the Pacific Northwest. One cap fully bloomed at 3 inches and another premature cap at the same length. I partially dried the mushrooms in the oven at 210 degrees for a couple hours then proceeded to chop them up, and boil them for an hour. The first fully bloomed cap produced little to no effects after an hour of ingestion. The second, partially bloomed cap produced very intense effects after only 20 minutes or so. The effects were coming on so strong that I induced vomiting. I felt as though my heart was beating in my head to the beat of an ancient drum, I mean this in the most pleasant way. I felt as if I was walking next to time, not bound by the linear constraints of it, I had a benzo, opiate, alcohol like feeling of relaxing and peace with a feeling of excitement and joy at the same time. I was dancing around telling my friends of my experience when I started to feel a little tired. This is where my memory fades. I wake up at exactly 6AM having just had an incredible, lucid dream. I envisioned the earth as a circle with humans walking about the surface of this circle. Language was broken down into nothingness so in order for these humans to understand each other, they had to TRUST each other. The ones that trusted one another grew and advanced, they progressed, while the others regressed. It was an incredibly simplified history of man, or so it seems. As they progressed so did their language, love and understanding of one another. This substance can only be defined as magical, or medicinal. I cannot classify it as anything else. Upon waking I still felt a deep relaxation and peace as I lied in my bed. There was some food strewn about and batteries (I was trying to replace the batteries of my remote control in my bedroom). Besides that, nothing unusual was afoot in my house. I advice extreme caution when using this medicine, please take a smaller dose than I did. I feel I got lucky with the way the mushroom treated me.

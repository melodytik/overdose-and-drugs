我正在第二次尝试戒掉Cymbalta, 我不得不去看精神科医生, 不幸的是,他帮助我摆脱它还没有工作. 如果我试图停止,会发生什么? 让我们看看. 我不能开车, 因为当我转过头, 我每隔30秒到1分钟就会发生脑震荡. 每隔几分钟就会出现明显的鼻 面部动和肌肉. 梦,电击 (大脑) 不断地发生 这是一个可怕的判决. 短期记忆力不佳 情绪突发会在几分钟内从1到10升高, 极度衰弱的恶心和腹 流,虚弱,头与极度焦虑相结合 我第一次试图摆脱它, 我逐渐超过两个月. 我在最后一剂药后的三周内, 仍然非常受影响. 我几乎失去了我的工作 (医生级职位), 我花了两个月的时间试图摆脱它. 我试着在早上拿起衣服穿上衣服, 感觉就像是,,,抓住墙,,拿起衫,,恶心,坐下. 然后我会和工作里的人说话,我会有,想说',别介意我的阴影,没什么可担心的' 为了缓解戒断症候群的症状, 我重新开始服用. 我第二次试图戒烟时, 精神科医生给我服用了普罗扎克, 对于6周的低剂量Prozac+低剂量Cymbalta的想法是,当我取消两者时, 现在我已经停药17天了, 精神病医生还给我每8小时服用0.5毫克克洛纳泽, 我每天至少喝两杯酒, 每天服用两杯维科丁来减轻症状, 这种药物有助于缓解运动问题 (减轻腹),并使焦虑问题变得更容易忍受. 我认为如果第一次,真的很糟糕的戒断期没有发生, 我现在可能不会注意到这些迹象, 我现在不觉得这一切都很生气, 我只是想知道如果它将是17天或170天之前大脑zaps走好 我每天服用Cymbalta已经有6个月了, 现在这已经成为一种笑话了, 大多数时候我只是感到奇怪的头. 我不知道它是否是"永久的"很多. 这种感觉比起让我去寻找抗抑郁药的抑郁更为模糊和可怕. 时间表:58611 杜洛西丁 (395),药品 - 西丁 (80):各种 (28),成和习惯性 (10),健康问题 (27),回顾 / 总结 (11)

Withdrawal Hell Duloxetine (Cymbalta) by Pea Citation:   Pea. "Withdrawal Hell: An Experience with Duloxetine (Cymbalta) (exp58611)". Erowid.org . Jan 6, 2007. erowid.org/exp/58611 DOSE: oral Duloxetine (pill / tablet) oral Pharms - Fluoxetine (capsule) I'm on attempt #2 to get off Cymbalta and have had to go to a psychiatrist to help me get off it. Unfortunately his help in getting me off it hasn't worked yet either. What happens when I try to stop? Let's see. I couldn't drive because when I turn my head I get a 'brain shock' that could cause dizzyness, distraction, etc. I would have a brain shock literally every 30 seconds to 1 minute apart. Obvious nystagmus every few minutes Twitchy facial sensations and muscle spasms in face. Nightmares, electric shock (brain zaps) constantly Terrible judgement Poor short term memory Emotional freakouts that wind up from 1- 10 within minutes and then dissappear if i recognize them and intentionally bottle them up. Extrememly debilitating nausea and diarrhea drooling, weakness, dizzy coupled with extreme anxiety The first time I tried to get off it, I tapered over two months. Three weeks after the last dose, I was still extremely affected. I almost lost my job (doctor-level position), I almost quit my job. I had about two months of hell trying to get off it. So I'd be trying to pick up my clothes to get dressed in the morning and it was like zap zap zap grip wall zap pick up shirt zap zap nausea zap sit back down zap. Then I'd be talking to someone at work and i'd have the zaps and want to say 'oh just dont mind my nystagmus, nothing to worry about' I started taking it again to relieve the symptoms of the withdrawal / 'discontinuation syndrome'. On my second attempt to quit it, the shrink put me on Prozac, a drug with a longer half life than Cymbalta (which like effexor has a short half life and SNRI effects). The idea of Prozac low dose + Cymbalta low dose for 6 weeks was that when I withdrew both, the Prozac would still taper off more slowly with its active metabolites. Now I'm up to day 17 after stopping the drugs and I'm still experiencing the brain shocks. The psychiatrist put also put me on 0.5 mg clonazepam every 8 hrs and that helps but does not fully stop the symptoms: It doesn't cover it up but it helps. The first time when I was trying to get off Cymbalta on my own, I was drinking at least 2 drinks a day and taking a couple vicodin a day to reduce the symptoms, but it was still barely tolerable. The hydrocodone helped with the motility issues (reduced diarrhea) and made anxiety issues more tolerable. I think that if the first, really BAD withdrawal period hadn't happened, I probably wouldn't be as keyed into noticing the signs now that they're blunted by Prozac, but when I get a brain zap now, I just acknowledge the little monkey on my back rather than freak out and almost vomit. I don't feel that pissed off about it all right now, I just wonder if it's going to be 17 days or 170 days before the brain zaps go away for good I think i might donate some money to some organization this year that lobbies for more disclosure to patients about this shit because my dr who first prescribed it to me said 'Really? It causes vertigo in you? I am on it too and I get that too--I wonder what's up with that.'. Followup: I'm up to about 6 moths since i was taking Cymbalta every day and i still have weird effects like those described above. It has gotten to be kind of a joke now and mostly I just get the weird vertigo zaps. I wonder if it is 'permanent' a lot. It is a very distinct 'zap' and it feels way more sketchy and scary than the depression that caused me to go looking for an antidepressant.

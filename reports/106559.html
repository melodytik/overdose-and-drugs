虽然我偶尔会服用2-5毫克的阿尔普拉佐拉姆, 我也尝试过flunitrazepam,diazepam和clonazepam这些药物也让我度过了美好的时光. 周一 (3天前) 我很高兴看到我已经得到了10毫升瓶1毫克/毫升的流布罗马胺液和90粒克隆纳胺药片 (每片为0.5-.7毫克). 开始服用一片药, 25分钟后再服用, 我记得在那之后也摄入了大约0.5毫升的流布罗马胺液体 (使用0.25毫升的细分管管). 所以总共1毫克克隆纳佐拉姆和0.5毫克弗鲁布罗马佐拉姆 (问问题,我知道). 无论如何,第二天我醒来26个小时后, 发现流马胺液体完全空了. 我相信我倒了它,因为如果9.5毫克的黄确实被摄入, 我相信我仍然会睡着或更不连贯. 无论如何,因为我的抑制降低我开始爆破药片像糖果. 我给了一些朋友,也许总共有20个, 在星期二和星期三之间, 我要么摔倒在地板上, 我父亲发现我躺在厨房的地板上, 我朋友的有咖啡店, 她说我在店外吃咖啡,吃冰淋, 发短信和滑板, 她说一辆车差点撞到我,不得不踩车. 我确实模糊地回忆起一辆汽车非常紧密地向我敲响喇, 这是一条45英里/小时的道路. 我还记得我打破了一堆玻璃,踩在上面, 我昨天从地毯上清理了很多碎玻璃和血. 我今天醒来没有长板,出去找了一会儿,然后放弃了. 我两周前以190美元的价格购买了这个长板, 所以是的,它已经消失了. 我给我的一个朋友一片太多了, 整天都昏迷了, 我不会推这些子给任何人, 除非你已经有12毫克的阿尔普拉佐拉姆或类似的疯狂习惯 (我很认真,没有夸张). 这些会搞你的生活. 其他: 克罗纳佐拉姆 (686),弗卢布罗马佐拉姆 (690):一般 (1),第一次 (2),组合 (3),困难的经历 (5),不好的旅行 (6),火车残骸和旅行灾难 (7),独自 (16)

Jul 25, 2015. erowid.org/exp/106559 DOSE: T+ 0:00 0.5 mg oral Clonazolam (pill / tablet) T+ 0:25 0.5 mg oral Clonazolam (pill / tablet) T+ 1:00 0.5 mg oral Flubromazolam (liquid) T+ 27:00 repeated oral Clonazolam (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 180 lb Male, 180 lbs, no current benzo tolerance before this but occasionally I occasionally take 2-5mg of alprazolam and remember most of the night. I have also tried flunitrazepam, diazepam and clonazepam and have good times with these substances. So on Monday (3 days ago) I was excited to see I'd gotten my 10ml bottle 1mg/ml flubromazolam liquid and 90 count tablets of clonazolam (.5-.7mg each). Started with one tablet, took another 25 minutes later and that's when things got blurry. I remember ingesting maybe .5ml of the flubromazolam liquid after that as well (using a .25ml segmented pipette). So a total of 1mg clonazolam and .5mg flubromazolam (ASKING FOR TROUBLE, I KNOW). Anyways, I wake up the next day about 26 hours later to find the flubromazolam liquid completely empty. I believe I spilled it because if 9.5mg if flubromazolam was actually ingested I believe I would have still been asleep or much less coherent. Anyways, because of my lowered inhibitions I start popping the tablets like candy. I gave some to friends, maybe a total of 20 but even that's a maximum overstatement. Between tuesday and wednesday I either dropped on the floor, or consumed over 75 pellets of clonazolam. During one of the nights, my step dad found me on the kitchen floor, covered in bed sheets with a big mess everywhere including food all over the ground. Using my longboard as transportation, my friends mom who owns a coffee/ice cream shop said I stopped by and was outside the shop eating a latte, ice cream, texting and longboarding at the same time looking super fucked up. She said a car almost hit me and had to slam on the brakes. I do vaguely remember recall a car honking at me very closely and I'm lucky I'm still alive. That was a 45mph road. I also vaguely remember breaking a bunch of glass, stepping on it and leaving blood stains all over the floor where I was stepping around in my room (I had absolutely no pain so I didn't give a fuck at the time). I cleaned a lot of broken glass and blood off of the carpet yesterday. I woke up today without a longboard, went out looking for it for a while and gave up. I had just bought this longboard two weeks ago for $190 and just got new bearings for it in the mail today. So yeah that's gone. One of my friends I gave 1 tablet too blacked out for the entire day and missed his date with his girlfriend. I wouldn't recommend these benzos to anyone unless you already have like a 12mg a day alprazolam habit or something crazy like that (I'm serious, no exaggeration). These will fuck up your life.

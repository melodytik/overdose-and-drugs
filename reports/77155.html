我是一名住在澳大利亚的16岁男孩. 我患有脊椎退化, 所以我决定找些止痛药, 两个月前在苏格兰尝试DMT, 葡萄 没有. 我在网上搜索了一下, 发现我家后花园的一棵树, 这是我的经验. 我已经吃了7到8个小时, 在学校里喝了一瓶Rikodeine, 在飞机上坐了25个小时之后, 里科代因每10毫升含有20毫克以下的二代因, 但由于它还含有, 我建议你在早上服用大量的铁丸 (大量的可以让你大便, 铁丸有相反的效果, 我喝了半瓶以上的药物, 100毫克以上的二代, 这时我想到一个巨型子和干汁混合在一起. 我坐在沙发上,在我的家的后面与我所有的通常的装备 (管道,,等等) 我坐在那里得到高看着有大葡萄树覆盖它,并将大约两倍高的它,你不能看到它,在所有,住在另一边的人有他们的房子在另一端一个完整的街区,所以你很少听到他们. 时间是下午四点半, 我很想知道,但这是一个估计时间. 下午四点半. 吸了我的第一个巨型强混合干燥的树液,这是一个非常甜蜜的烟. 我得到了通常的延迟 (我发现管道需要1或2分钟来击中我) 然后通过石头无法移动的感觉. 时间下午4:35. 我感觉越来越好, 直到我全身充满了极度的快乐. 想想你有史以来最好的高潮, 乘以100万, 你仍然没有接近. 这真是太棒了. 下午四点四十分. 现在我可以控制自己的身体, 我可以开眼睛, 环顾四周, 我周围看了看,一切都很奇怪,然后我听到我身后的邻居说他们要来从我这里买药,那个住在那里的家伙和一个女人,他们都在这个二维世界里, 另一个问题是,有一个单身男子住在那里. 在下午4点42分 我起身走进厨房,喝了一杯清爽的泡水,然后看了看窗外,令我惊的是,树还在那里,但现在有四角洞点在上面,就像一个格子,虽然我的邻居和这个女人比房子大,仍然从一边到另一边移动 (不行走) 而只是从一边到另一边悬浮). 我走回外面和我的满杯水,并留下屏幕门打开,而不是转向右沙发我去躺在草地上. 然后我闭上我的眼睛,当我打开他们回来我仍然坐在沙发上与我手中的烟斗. 没有杯子的水和屏幕门关闭. 没有脏的盘子,甚至没有脏的玻璃. 我一直坐在沙发上. 下午四点五十分 我继续使用大麻了一段时间, 晚上7点左右, 这是我一生中最奇怪的经历之一. 我从来没有经历过类似这样的事情. 我不确定我是否会重现这种情况, 自那以后我没有使用大麻或任何合法的药物, 我希望你们能从中得到一些想法. 其他: 单独使用 (16),组合使用 (3),首次使用 (2)

Oct 20, 2013. erowid.org/exp/77155 DOSE: T+ 0:00 100 mg Pharms - Dihydrocodeine (liquid) T+ 7:00 1 bowl smoked Cannabis T+ 7:00 1 bowl smoked Acacia BODY WEIGHT: 80 kg I am a 16 year old male living in Australia. I have spinal degeneration so I decided to find something to numb the pain, it just happened to be in the form of a cough medicine (Rikodeine) containing 'dihydrocodeine'. I also like the odd cone and became interested in DMT two months ago when I was in Scotland after trying Salvia divinorum . After some searching on the internet I realised a tree in my back garden looks remarkably like an acacia from a picture I found so I decided to put some on top of a jumbo cone and see what happens. This is my experience. I had not eaten for 7 or 8 hours and had been sipping on a bottle of Rikodeine while at school, after the 25 hours of sitting on a plane my back was in dire need of a chiropractic attention but I couldn't get an appointment for a few days so I figured self medication was the way. Rikodeine contains just under 20mg dihydrocodeine per 10ml but because it also contains sorbitol I recommend an iron pill the morning you are taking much of this stuff (lots of sorbitol can give you the shits, iron pills have the opposite effect so they kind of cancel each other out). Over the course of these 7 or 8 hours I had consumed just over half the bottle, just over 100mg dihydrocodeine so I was feeling very happy and no pain at all. This is when I thought of having a jumbo cone mixed with dried sap. I went and sat on the sofa out the back of my house with all my usual gear (pipe, pot, etc.) where I sit to get high looks over to the fence which has big vines covering it and going about twice as high as it, you can't see through it at all, the people who live on the other side have their house at the other end of a full block, so you rarely hear them either. It was 4:30pm in the afternoon and I was pretty base line by 7pm. I was pretty out of it but this is an estimate for the timing. 4:30pm. Smoked my first jumbo cone of strong pot mixed with dried sap, it was a very sweet smoke. I got the usual delayed pot buzz (I find pipes take a 1 or 2 minutes to hit me) then going through to the stoned unable to move feeling. 4:35pm. At the point where the feeling would normally stop increasing didn't occur, I just kept feeling better and better until I had this complete and utter euphoria all over my body. Think of the best orgasm you have ever had, times it by a million and you're still nowhere fucking close. It was completely amazing. 4:40pm. My utter euphoria feeling was still very much with me but I had some form of control over my body now, I could actually open my eyes and look around; which is when things started getting weird. I looked around and everything was very odd and then I heard my neighbours who live behind me clear as day saying they were going to come and buy some drugs from me, there was the guy who lives there and a women, they were both in this 2 dimensional world just moving from side to side of the garden, with no facial expressions or even facial movement but I could hear them talking. Another thing is there is a single man who lives there. 4:42pm. I got up and walked/staggered through to the kitchen to get a drink and after having a refreshing glass of bubbly water I looked out the window and to my surprise the hedge was still there but there were now square holes dotted all over it like a lattice and though the squares my neighbour and this women were bigger than the house and still moving from side to side (not walking but just hovering from side to side). I walked back outside with my full glass of water and left the screen door open, instead of turning right to the sofa I went and lay on the grass. Then I closed my eyes and when I opened them back up I was still sitting on the sofa with my pipe in my hand. No glass of water and the screen door was shut. There were no dirty dishes, not even a dirty glass. I had been sitting on the sofa all along. 4:50pm. I continued on with my pot high for a while with it coming to a complete end at about 7pm, nothing else like that happened again. This had been one of the weirdest experiences of my life. I had never experienced anything remotely like this before. I'm not sure if I'll try to re-create it, I haven't used pot or any legal drugs since; it happened just over a week ago and has changed my life. This has been my first experience report, I hope you enjoyed reading it and have gotten some ideas from it.

感觉冷静和放松 莲花/百合花 - 尼姆菲亚·努查利·瓦尔·卡鲁莱亚和尼姆菲亚·阿尔巴 富士曼 "冷静和放松:与莲花/百合花的经验 - 花花 var caerulea & Nymphaea alba (exp43858) ". 在口服 莲花/百合花 - 尼姆菲亚·诺卡利·瓦尔·卡鲁莱亚 (茶) 一朵花 在口服 莲花/百合花 - 白 (茶) 我从一位好朋友那里听说了莲花, 他说抽莲花让他快乐 然后我订购了半司的N. caerulea - 蓝百合花和1司的白莲花 (Nymphaea alba) 没有任何效果 (也许有点放松), 我尝试了茶. 我把一大匙蓝花和一朵干的白莲花放进一个茶具中. 煮了一些水,把东西放进去,让它休息15分钟. 我第一次喝了一口是可怕的,所以我把一些蜂蜜和一把棕糖添加到茶中, 完成后大约15分钟, 我开始感到困惑. 我感到轻松和温暖. 但这些效应并没有很强, 我觉得很累,所以躺在床上15分钟, 我玩了一些电脑游戏, 我花了一个小时为迪伦唱歌, 享受音乐. 我仍然觉得放松和温和但然后去了海不过... 我在车里感觉疲不堪, 很安静. 后来我玩了一些高尔夫球. 我通常会在愤怒和诅咒中大喊大叫,但这一切都没有发生在我一轮. 我只是过于冷静和放松. 莲花是一种美丽的草药, 我认为它有点像达米亚娜,但我喝达米亚娜因为它的美味. 我会更多地使用它, 也许会用一些酒精来增加效果. 和平与爱 时间表: 43858 2007年4月24日发布 莲花/百合花 - 花 (Nymphaea nouchali var caerulea) (105), 莲花/百合花 - 花 (Nymphaea alba) (414):各种 (28),一般 (1)

Apr 24, 2007. erowid.org/exp/43858 DOSE: 1 Tbsp oral Lotus/Lily - Nymphaea nouchali var caerulea (tea) 1 flower oral Lotus/Lily - Nymphaea alba (tea) BODY WEIGHT: 160 lb I heard about lotus from a good friend who said that smoking lotus made him happy I then orderd 1/2 ounze of N. caerulea - Blue Lily Flower and 1 ounze of White Lotus (Nymphaea alba) I first smoked a bowl and a 1/2 of the blue lilly with no effects ( maybe a little relaxation) the smoke was harsh. I then tried the tea. I placed a large Tble spoon of blue lilly and one dried flower of White lotus in a tea holder. Boiled some water and put the stuff in and let it rest for about 15 minutes. My first sip was horrible so I then added some honey and a handfull of brown sugar to the tea because of its taste, took 15-30 minutes to drink. About 15 minutes after I finished I started to feel zoned out. I felt relaxed and warm. But these effects were not strong and nothing from a buzz came to me. I felt tired so laid in bed for 15 minutes and called my friend. Then feeling really mellow I played some video games and blasted Bob Dylan. This was great I spent about a hour singing to Dylan and just enjoying music. I still felt relaxed and mellow but then went to the beach nontheless ... my 'Buzz' (I wouldnt call it that) wore off in the car and the rest of the day I felt tired and zoned out and quiet. I later played some golf. I have a bad temper and usually will scream swing and club in anger and curse but none of this happend at all during my round. I was just too chilled and relaxed. In the end Lotus is a somewhat nice herb but I'm debating if its worth the price and taste... In my opinion its kinda like damiana but I drink damiana cause of its nice taste.

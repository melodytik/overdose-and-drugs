在黑暗中养了4个月的21英寸成熟的Bridgesii. 茶很顺利地下来了. 在花园里花了两个小时看鸟, 享受户外的美景, 在旅行开始时躺在吊床上. 从吊床上看了一只绿鸟在树上. 景观设计师来了, 在邻居的院子里做了一些工作. 这真是太骚了! 所有的鸟儿看起来很担心,尤其是绿鸟. 为了躲避喧,我不得不进去. 一切都在融化. 我几乎看不清. 我不得不躺在床上. 这只狗来检查我. 起狗,把她放在床上. 她躺在我旁边, 没有动, 除了我的手臂一遍又一遍. 感觉和平和安全与狗在那里. 我被分解到一个非常基本的水平. 视力是模糊的 我真的无法用语言表达. 我意识到我唯一能做的就是躺在那里让狗我的手臂. 时间是无限的. 分钟变成了小时. 时间飞逝. 我一直在床上无动于衷, 一整天都过去了. 然后是夜晚. 在床上醒整晚. 终于感觉到一种极端的平静和接受, 太阳升起,我出去走在后院. 情况开始变得更加正常. 但仍然是. 在那个半途状态下,直到下午1点左右. 然后变得极度疲. 必须睡觉. 睡得很深. 第二天早上醒来时感觉很好. 专注于自己的工作. 没有了. 活着的人. 充满了能量. 连续一个星期的感觉. 然后慢慢恢复正常. 布里奇西是非常强大的. 这让我震惊. 我将永远尊重它. 其他: 发布时间:2023年9月30日 单独 (16),一般 (1)

Sep 30, 2023. erowid.org/exp/117296 DOSE: 21 in oral Cacti - T. bridgesii (tea) BODY WEIGHT: 205 lb 21 Inches Bridgesii Rocked Me Drank tea made from 21" of mature Bridgesii that was aged in darkness for 4 months. The tea went down smooth. Spent about 2 hours in the backyard watching birds and enjoying the outdoors while the cactus built up. Ended up laying down in the hammock as the trip kicked in. Watched a green bird in a tree from the hammock. Landscapers showed up and did work in the neighbor's yard. What a ruckus! The birds all looked worried, especially the green bird. Had to go inside to escape the ruckus. Everything was melting. Could barely see straight. Had to lay down in the bed. The dog came over to check on me. Picked the dog up and put her on the bed. She laid down next to me and didn't move, except for licking my arm over and over again. Felt peaceful and safe with the dog there. I was broken down to a very basic level. Vision was blurry. Couldn't really formulate words. Came to the realization that all I could do was lay there and let the dog lick my arm. Time was immeasurable. Minutes were hours. Hours flew by. I remained wiped out, nonverbal, motionless on the bed. The whole day passed by. Then it was night. Laid awake in bed all night. Eventually came to feel an extreme level of peacefulness and acceptance of just being. The sun rose and I went outside and walked around the backyard. Things were starting to feel more normal. But was still tripping. Stayed in that halfway state until about 1pm. Then became extremely tired. Had to sleep. Slept a deep sleep. Woke up the next morning feeling terrific. Focused. Clear. Alive. Full of energy. Felt that way for about a week straight. Then slowly normalcy returned. Bridgesii is powerful. It rocked me. I will respect it forever.

May 23, 2026. erowid.org/exp/48977 DOSE: 100 - 500 mg oral Diethylcathinone BODY WEIGHT: 170 lb Synthesis of N-Diethylcathinone Many years ago while still an undergraduate chemistry major I stumbled upon a most unusual chemical. At the time I had no knowlege of cathinone neither did any of my associates. For some reason I had begun to explore the chemistry of the amphetamine family, everything from the lowly meth to its more exotic cousins the various methoxy substituted amphetamines. The synthesis of these materials was becoming somewhat problimatic, due to the risk in obtaining the necessary precursers. Several of my associates had recently been visited by DEA. I thought it would be wise to move into yet unexplored areas of chemistry or at least into unfamiliar realms for DEA. So began my search for new amphetamine-like substances. During my literiture search I ran across phenyl-1-propanone-2-diethylamine. It was somewhat simular in structure to the amphetamines and was noted to have an appetite suppressing effect. Therefore I decided to look further into its synthesis which turned out to be straightforward. One need only treat propiophenone with bromine followed by diethylamine. Upon work up the free amine was isolated as the hydrochloride. The effects of the compound proved to be somewhat different from amphetamine. The initial rush was very simular to speed, but the overall effect was much shorter lived. However, upon repeated dosages this shortcoming was overcome. Many began to liken its effects to coke. It even acquire a street reference as 'synthetic Cocaine'. Which turned out to be very fortunate for me. A number of years later, while in grad school, I decided to see if it would be possible to improve upon this most likable little molecule. It seemed that reduction of the ketone function would improve upon the substance's only arguable shortcoming. This of course is a simple matter easily accomplished by any of a number of standard synthetic methods. I will say that the resulting compound is quite a different beast. Over the years I've used this as a problem on my undergraduate organic exams and have seen a number of creative approaches.

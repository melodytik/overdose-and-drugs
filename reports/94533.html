作为我慢性焦虑的辅助药物, 医生给我开了一种名为Skelaxin或Metaxalone的药物. 它的效果很好;它尽其所能地完成了它的工作,然而由于我倾向于滥用我没有大麻或其他任何药物的处方,我开始尝试找到滥用它的方式只是时间问题. 由于相对缺乏有关该主题的信息, 我想分享我的知识. 这完全是我自己的经验, 我发现它是最令人愉快的补充其他娱乐性药物, 特别是大麻. 一种深深的无意识的放松感, 运动控制中的小差异, 嗜睡, 我最喜欢的药物消费方法是将800毫克大片分成一碗大麻, 我发现这种药最好与其他抑郁药结合使用, 值得注意的例外是其他常用的肌肉松剂 (卡里索普罗多尔, 环素等) 在我的经验中, 微小的片或大麻的组合产生一个理想的体验. 我在这种药物的影响下不喝酒. 我总是确保我有大约12小时的时间, 我不建议你在上班/上学前服用, 因为我的表现总是大大降低. 我从来没有感觉到任何类似于过量服用的东西, 对于我来说,一个安全的剂量似乎是1600mg-2000mg左右. 这是一种奇妙,有趣的物质, 给我带来了大量的新体验, 让我能够与同龄人交往, 要小心,好玩,保持安全. 其他:

Jul 10, 2013. erowid.org/exp/94533 DOSE: Pharms - Metaxalone (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 135 lb In my early teens I was prescribed a drug commonly known as Skelaxin, or Metaxalone, as a secondary treatment for the chronic anxiety I suffer. It worked fairly well; it did its job to the best of its ability, however due to my tendency to abuse the medications I was prescribed in the absence of marijuana or any of the various other drugs I used, it was only a matter of time before I began to attempt to find a way to abuse it. Over the years I have become experienced in the high caused by this drug, and due to the relative lack of information on the subject, I figured I would share my knowledge. This is purely my own experiences, I am no doctor etc. Metaxalone doesn't get me extremely high, the effects are very, very, subtle and I have found that it is most pleasant complementing other recreational drugs, marijuana in particular. When used in isolation, the effects are almost unnoticeable at first, a profound sense of unconscious relaxation, a small discrepancy in motor controls, sleepiness, and a noticing of minute details are all fairly standard, as is a somewhat hazy memory. My preferred method of consuming the drug is to break up a sizable amount of the 800mg pill over a bowl of cannabis sativa and smoking it, but parachuting, insufflation, and popping are all viable options. I find that the pill is best used in combination with other depressants, a notable exception being other commonly prescribed muscle relaxants (Carisoprodol, Cyclobenzaprine etc) which has the nasty tendency to produce unpleasant side effects, turning me into a, well, a zombie for an extended period of time. In my experience, combinations of a minor opiate or marijuana produce an ideal experience. I don't drink alcohol while under the influence of this drug. I always make sure I have roughly a 12 hour block of time freed up, as I typically fall asleep after the trip plateaus. I do not advise taking this before you go to work/school as my performance is always drastically reduced. I have never felt anything resembling an overdose, but as with any pharmaceutical, I use caution. A safe dose for me seems to be about 1600mg-2000mg. This is a wonderful, interesting substance that opens up a plethora of new experiences for me in standard recreational drugs, or induces a period of euphoric relaxation. It allows me to be sober enough to interact with my peers, while allowing me to enjoy myself on a more personal level. Be careful, have fun, stay safe.

我在7月下旬在亚利桑那州南部 (海拔约4000英尺) 附近收集了一棵白色花朵,蓝色和绿色的多彩叶状刺的种子. 这是我在州里唯一见过的物种, 我注意到它们在从图森到巴塔哥尼亚的各个地方开花, 我有这样的印象, 西南地区所有的鱼种类都有相似的化学成分. 我把种子放在阴影下干燥, 经过一台干净的咖啡机, 如果我给它留下一个更粗的纹理, 草药学家迈克尔·摩尔建议在吸烟前将种子分解, 我和一个朋友用不到一个茶匙的东西装满了我的chillum (管道), 然后把火焰放在里面. 虽然喉不痛, 但我们一段时间没有感觉到任何东西. 我们加载了2个碗, 在效果显现之前, 这可能是25分钟的时间. 一种轻微的头痛感, 只是在头部的一个特定部位, 并没有痛苦甚至很烦人 (我注意到这一点, 影响至少持续了一个小时, 也许更多, 我没有跟踪. 我确实觉得吸烟对你来说是不好的, 但我不认为它比大麻或烟草更糟. 虽然有些效应令人愉快, 但它们很温和. 我不觉得这是一种有远见的药物, 我可能会再试一次, 它在某些地区是很常见的, 其他: 阿格蒙特种类 (500) :一般 (1),第一次 (2),小组 (2-9) (17)

Smoking the Seed Argemone spp. (Prickly Poppy) by Goathead Citation:   Goathead. "Smoking the Seed: An Experience with Argemone spp. (Prickly Poppy) (exp36028)". Erowid.org . Dec 30, 2009. erowid.org/exp/36028 DOSE: 1.5 bowls smoked Argemone spp. (plant material) BODY WEIGHT: 145 lb I gathered the seeds of a white flowered, blue and green variegated leaf prickly poppy near a wash in southern arizona (elevation~4000 ft.) in late july. This is the only species I've seen in the state, but I haven't keyed it out. I've noticed them flowering from in locations ranging from Tucson down to Patagonia from june all the way to the present mid-august. I'm under the impression that all of the argemone species in the southwest have similar chemical constituents. After drying the seed in the shade, I ran it through a clean coffee grinder, and turned it into a fine powder. It would have been easier to smoke if I had left it a courser texture. In the section on Prickly Poppy in one of his medicinal plant guides to the southwest, the herbalist Michael Moore recommends breaking up the seeds before smoking them, to prevent them from popping in your face. One evening at home with nothing to do, a friend and I loaded my chillum (pipe) with less than a teaspoon, and put the flame to it. It smoked smooth, didn't hurt the throat, but we didn't feel anything for a while. We loaded up 2 more bowls and smoked them before the effects were pronounced. This was a period of maybe 25 minutes. The effects we noticed and were able to articulate were: a mild stoned feeling, similar to marijuana; a feeling of a slight headache which was only in one distinct part of the head, and was not painful or even really annoying (I noticed this, my friend didn't get a headache); a feeling in the lungs of having smoked too much- just a general harshness I guess. The effects were noticeable for at least an hour, maybe more, I didn't keep track. I did get the feeling that smoking this stuff isn't good for you, but I don't think its much worse than pot or tobacco. While some effects were pleasurable, they were pretty mild. I don't get the impression that this is a visionary medicine (entheogen), and for that reason I lost interest. I'll probably try it again, but not for a little while. It is quite common in some areas and does have an effect on the conscious mind.

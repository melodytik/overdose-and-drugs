我最好的朋友和另外两个朋友决定喝点狂喜药. 我从来没有服用过狂喜药, 所以我不知道药物应该是什么样子, 我最好的朋友说我们得到的药丸是三重的, 这意味着一个药丸实际上包含3个莫莉药丸. 我终于拿到了一颗药丸, 感到紧张, 因为那个'药丸'很小, 暗绿色, 形状像立方体. 我从来没有见过这样的东西. 我看到我的朋友每人服用1个, 我自己也做了2个 (因为我对药物有很高的耐受性, 我们开车,在去河边的路上,吸烟的景色. 我们在那里抽了4到5支烟, 我在第一小时内没有感觉到药丸的任何效果. 我们决定停车,停车, 起四个更多的块. 我仍然只是感觉高的草,没有感觉的莫利还. 我开始担心我需要做更多的事情,所以我在12点左右再服用一粒药. 我最好的朋友和我必须在12在她的房子,当我们到达那里我们把我们的东西在里面和决定散步. 我开始感觉到莫利的作用. 起初,我觉得自己真的活着, 我最好的朋友和我在街上散步, 有一个死电话,没有草和一包香烟. 我们决定回到她的房子,坐在后门廊. 我开始感到恶心. 我们只走了45分钟, 却以为已经走了好几个小时! 我对时间的感知完全消耗, 我喝了一杯甘酒, 忽然变得很冷. 我在一个毯子里看电视了大约10分钟. 我问朋友现在可以出去吗? 我心灵从一个想法跳到另一个想法, 但我的大脑无法产生话语. 我坐在那里,奇怪的安静,后悔我刚刚抽烟. 我觉得我的肚子就像一个子里放了蝶. 我觉得我的头完全空虚,我的腿麻木,我的整个身体看起来紫色. 我几乎吓坏了,但只是把它归结为幻觉. 我需要吐或大便. 我觉得我必须要下,所以我进去洗手间, 我的大脑根本不能正常工作, 告诉我的东西从我的股里出来. 所以,我想我必须吐. 我花了35分钟的时间试图吐, 我走在她的后院,只是试图吐和它在我的喉,但只是不会出来. 这就是我开始疯狂的时候...坏. 到了凌晨3点我开始有呼吸问题. 无论我多么平静,或多么多次让自己放心, 我每隔一段时间就会深呼吸, 这只会让我更加担心. 我打电话给, 让她来接我 (我和我最好的朋友住在三条街相隔的地方). 她到达并立即知道我在使用药物. 她让我进入浴, 然后我能够让自己吐. 我了5到10次,然后我真的无法呼吸. 我带我下楼测血压. 她说我的心率是每分钟229次. 我被送往医院, 在那里, 我得到了Zofran治疗恶心和Ativan, 他们做了毒品测试, 令人惊的是, 我和都知道这不可能是对的,我每天抽大麻5年. 现在我已经有点平静了, 我检测出大麻,狂喜药,可卡因和海洛因均为阳性. 我从来没有吸过海洛因, 我知道大麻和狂喜会出现, 但可卡因和海洛因? 没有告诉他们其中还有海洛因和可卡因. 让我想要并且向自己和家人发誓, 我永远不会再吸毒. 没有任何其他信息 发布时间:2016年6月11日 MDMA (3),坏/可疑的狂喜 (567):第一次 (2),组合 (3),健康问题 (27),小组 (2-9) (17)

Jun 11, 2016. erowid.org/exp/108641 DOSE: 2 tablets oral Bad/Suspect Ecstasy repeated smoked Tobacco - Cigarettes repeated smoked Cannabis BODY WEIGHT: 180 lb It was a very warm May night and my best friend, along with 2 other friends, decided to do some ecstasy. I've never done ecstasy before, so I didn't know what the pills were supposed to look like, or how much to take. My best friend said the pills we got were triple stacks, which meant that one pill actually contained 3 molly pills total. When I finally had one in my hand I felt nervous, as the 'pill' was small, dark green, and cube-like. I had never seen something like this before, ever. I saw my friends each take 1 and I took it upon myself to do 2 (because I tend to have a high tolerance for drugs-even if I have never done them-due to past drug abuse issues) around 10PM. We were driving, on the way to the river, to smoke by the view. We smoke 4 or 5 blunts there and I was pretty high. I did not feel any effects of the pill within the first hour. Around 11 PM, we are on our way back to my side of town and decide to pull over, park, and roll up 4 more blunts. I still only felt high off the weed, no feeling of the molly yet. I was starting to get worried I needed to do more, so I took one more pill around 12AM. My best friend and I had to be at her house at 12 and when we got there we set our shit inside and decided to take a walk. Probably at 1230AM was when I began feeling effects of the molly. At first, I just felt really alive - like I was in touch with my inner soul. My best friend and I were literally walking around, chain-smoking cigarettes with the filter dipped in molly powder. With a dead phone, no weed and a pack of cigarettes. We decided to go back to her house and sit on the back porch. On the walk home, I began feeling extremely nauseous. When I finally got into the house, I checked the clock and it was only 12:49AM...we had only been walking for 45 minutes which we thought we had been out for hours! My perception for time was totally depleted and my heart was pounding and racing. I chugged a small cup of Gatorade and suddenly became freezing. I cuddled up in a blanket and watched TV for about 10 minutes. Then, I felt too hot for the blanket and ask my friend if we can go outside now. At 1AM was the beginning of the peak of my high, as my mind was jumping from thought to thought but my brain just couldn't produce the words to talk. I sat there, oddly quiet, regretting all the cigarettes I had just smoked. My stomach felt like someone released a cage full of butterflies in there. My head felt completely empty, my legs were numb, and my whole body looked purple. I almost freaked out, but just chalked it up to a hallucination. 2AM and I NEED to puke or shit. I feel like I have to shit so I go inside to the bathroom and to my surprise, I cannot feel any body part under my bellybutton. I am trying to push shit out, heart rate increasing, anxiety induced, and my brain literally cannot function well enough to tell my shit to come out of my asshole. So, I just figured I have to puke. Spending 35 minutes trying to vomit, I go back outside thinking fresh air may help me out. I walk around her backyard, just trying to throw up and its in the back of my throat but just won't come up. This is when I start to freak...bad. 3 o'clock in the morning I began to have issues breathing. No matter how calm I told myself to be or how many times I reassured myself I could breathe, I couldn't. Every now and then I could get a huge deep breath, and that only made me worry more. Very panicy, I call my mom to come pick me up (me and my best friend live 3 streets over from each other). She arrives and immediately knows I'm on drugs. She gets me into the bathtub, and I'm then able to get myself to puke. I throw up 5-10 times, then I really couldn't breathe. My mom took me downstairs and took my blood pressure. She said my heart rate was 229 BPM. I was then rushed to the hospital and there, I was given Zofran for nausea and Ativan, a muscle relaxer. They did a drug test and surprisingly, it was negative for everything EVEN MARIJUANA. My mom and I both knew that couldn't be right, I've been smoking weed everyday for 5 years. So, once I am a bit calmer we are discharged from the hospital. My mom then went to the store and bought a 10-panel drug test. I tested positive for marijuana, ecstasy, cocaine, and heroin. I have never done heroin and I did cocaine once a few years ago. I knew the marijuana and ecstasy would show up, but cocaine and heroin? That's messed up someone would sell that to someone and NOT tell them there's heroin and coke in it as well. This experience has made me quit a long 6-year cigarette habit and has also made me want to AND swear to myself and family that I will NEVER do any type of drug EVER again.

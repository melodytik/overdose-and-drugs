星期一晚上11点半左右, 我和爸爸决定吸入一些马尔特. 我从来没有尝试过DMT, 我没有任何药物或处方. 我以前的精神药物使用经验是:四合一,四合一,四合一,EPT,和. 晚上之前我吸入了10毫克的4-ho-mipt. 我在天平上称重25毫克, 放入我的Yocan Magneto蒸汽化器, 坐在后面的甲板上, 我一次长时间地打了, 持续了15秒, 在最初的迹象中, 几何图案覆盖了一切. 我开始觉得很沉重,感觉越来越强烈,后来我意识到我没有为任何超级激烈的事情做好准备,因为我的狗一直在嗅着我的腿,试图在我闭着眼睛的时候爬上我的膝盖,这让我很烦. 我真的只是想要安静, 没有人来干扰我. CEV变得非常明显, 但没有很清楚像4-ho-met或EPT. 我决定站起来,回到里面,坐在沙发上用毯子,因为突然,我觉得我是在南极洲,开始. 我突然觉得自己就像在南极洲,开始. 没有. 随着我走路, 一切都在变化. 我爸爸喝了25毫克,但说没有任何效果. 似乎事情不会变得比我更激烈. 我决定再加上25毫克, 看看能不能得到更清晰的CEV. 接着我开始倒下, 看起来就像一百万只孔雀羽毛被吸进我的视野中部的黑洞, 随后它变成了一个不断变化的3D世界. 我开始看到明亮的光线在我左边和右边出现, 于是我父亲来问我一些问题, 洗衣机和干机有泡的效果, 喜欢非常棕色而不是白色. 我很高兴, 我和爸爸决定在YouTube上观看关于DMT旅行报告的视频, 现在看来一切都很轻松, 只有几何图案. 几何图案持续了晚上剩下的时间, 这很酷. 但这似乎是每一次迷幻药都会发生在我身上的事情. 他们通常会留在这里,直到我睡着了. 我很可能会在未来服用60或70毫克, 我从来没有尝试过DMT, 其他: 发布时间:2019年7月17日 MALT (855) : 家庭 (41), 一般 (1), 小组 (2-9) (17)

Vibrant Potential MALT by Greg Citation:   Greg. "Vibrant Potential: An Experience with MALT (exp113350)". Erowid.org . Jul 17, 2019. erowid.org/exp/113350 DOSE: T+ 0:00 25 mg vaporized MALT T+ 0:25 25 mg vaporized MALT BODY WEIGHT: 140 lb It was a Monday night around 11:30 PM and my dad and I decided to vape some MALT. I have never tried DMT and heard MALT was closely related. I am not on any form of medication or prescriptions. My previous experiences with psychedelics are: 4-ho-met,4-aco-met,4-ho-mipt, EPT, and Mushrooms. The night before I had insufflated 10mg of 4-ho-mipt. T-0:10 – I weighed out 25mg on a scale and put it in my Yocan Magneto vaporizer and sat down on the back deck and breathed in and out heavily to calm myself down cause I had a lot of anxiety due to this being something new. T+0:00 – I took one long hit and held it in for about 15 seconds and instantly started feeling a change in headspace. It was decently slow coming on, about 2 min in the first signs were geometric patterns overlaying everything. T+0:05 – I started feeling heavy and it was getting more intense, I then came to the realization that I wasn’t prepared for anything super intense because my dogs kept sniffing my leg and trying to get on my lap while my eyes were closed and it was bugging me a lot. I really just wanted quietness and nothing messing with me. T+0:10 – CEV were getting very obvious, but weren’t very clear like 4-ho-met or EPT. I decide to get up and go back inside and sit on the couch with a blanket because all of a sudden I feel like I am in the Antarctica and start shivering all of a sudden I feel like I am in the Antarctica and start shivering . Everything was sorta shifting as I walked. My dad loads up 25mg and takes a hit but says he has no effects. T+0:20 – Things didn’t seem like they were going to get anymore intense than they were for me. T+0:25 – I decide to go ahead and load another 25mg and see if I can get clearer CEV. We went into the garage where it was warmer and After taking a few hits, things instantly went up a level and I started falling backwards as what looked like a million peacock feathers were getting sucked into a black hole in the middle of my vision with my eyes closed. T+0:30 – It then transformed into this 3D world with a constantly moving landscape. I started seeing bright light appear to my left and right and it was getting clearer. But then my dad came over and started asking me questions and so I opened my eyes. The washer and dryer had like a bubbling effect and seemed to be a different color. Like very tan instead of white. I felt very euphoric and my dad and I decided to watch YouTube videos on DMT trip reports for the rest of the night. T+1:00 – things seem to be pretty light now with weak CEV and there were only geometric patterns everywhere still. T+3:00 – the geometric patterns lasted just about the rest of the night which was pretty cool. But it seems to be a thing that happens to me with every psychedelic. They usually stay around until I sleep. T+4:00 – I go to sleep without any issues. In the end I think MALT is a winner and I’ll most likely do 60mg or 70mg in the future to see how intense it can get. I have never tried DMT so I can’t say if this was familiar or not, but it was different for sure.

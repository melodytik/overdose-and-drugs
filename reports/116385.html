我从来没有吃过铁草 (Sideritis syriaca), 我不需要任何医疗原因的药物 (祝), 我个人不使用娱乐性药物, 包括乙醇. 咖啡因和香对我没有影响, 除了曾经的止痛药, 我甚至没有跑步者的高潮. 我刚刚在一个春天的晚上跑完, 坐在我的厨房里聊天, 我忘记它是用来帮助睡眠的, 我肚子很空,除了跑步后喝的水. 铁草是像热水中的茶一样造的, 我没有添加任何东西. 我喝了大约一个茶袋的价值, 味道有点草,有点像香. 我没有想到它会有什么作用, 然后我突然感到非常平静和平静. 这很奇怪,因为我觉得这很突然, 也不是我心情的结果. 我通常是个神经 (不是一种疾病,只是个性), 感觉就像听冷静复杂的音乐,或者当我在一个复杂但不紧张的项目上工作, 不是快乐或高兴,只是稳定. 我没有昏昏欲睡, 当一个小的家庭事情需要一些快速的反应, 我反应迅速, 和社会更容易和较少的神经比平时. 我睡得很好, 我从那时起就有过几次, 并且它再也没有那么强烈的影响了. 我猜在前端处于平静的环境增加了效果. 已使用的证券 发布时间:2022年11月8日 不同 (136):第一次 (2),单身 (16)

Nov 8, 2022. erowid.org/exp/116385 DOSE: oral Various (tea) BODY WEIGHT: 63 kg I’d never had ironwort (Sideritis syriaca) before but a friend from Greece sent me some (there it’s used as a mild sleep aid, like coffee in reverse) so I gave it a try. Some background: I don’t need drugs for any medical reasons (fingers crossed) and I don’t personally use recreational drugs, including ethanol. I’m also not affected by caffeine or chamomile, so aside from painkillers once, and sometimes the l-theanine (I guess) in tea, having my mood or energy affected at all by something I take in is a real novelty for me. I don’t even get runner’s highs. I’d just finished a run on a spring evening and was sitting around chatting in my kitchen, and decided to try some ironwort. I’d forgotten that it was used as a sleep aid and I just wanted to drink it for the taste/novelty. My stomach was pretty empty except for some water I’d had just after running. Ironwort is brewed like tea in hot water and I didn’t add anything to it. I drank around one teabag’s worth in maybe ¾ liter of water. The taste is kind of grassy, somewhat like chamomile. It didn’t do anything for about fifteen minutes (and I wasn’t expecting it to do anything) and then I suddenly felt very calm and composed. It was weird because it felt really abrupt and also not an immediate outgrowth of my mood. I’m normally a bundle of nerves (not a disorder or anything, just a personality) but that went away, like the snow in a snowglobe settling out. It was the same feeling as listening to chill complex music or when I'm working on a complicated but not stressful project and it’s going great. Not happiness or a high, just steadiness. I wasn’t drowsy – if anything, I was slightly more energetic than before. I was still stone cold sober and would have felt totally fine driving, and when a minor household thing came up that needed some speedy reaction, I reacted fast, and with more social ease and less nerves than usual. I slept great and still felt a little calmer than usual in the morning. I’ve had it a few times since then, and it hasn’t had quite that strong of an effect again. My guess is that being in a calm setting on the front end added to the effect.

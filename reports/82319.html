我需要一些消灭表演焦虑的东西, 乐队的其他成员以前曾经上过舞台, 在演唱会前一周, 我会短暂地感到焦虑, 感觉好像我的心脏在跳, 星期三 (演出当天,我们在20点30分演出) 我从容器里取了两片400毫克的药片, 打算拿一片, 我注意到我非常平静, 并没有恐慌. 虽然我们忙着设置我从空酒吧订购了340毫升的酒... 后来 我在18点左右又点了一杯酒, 人们开始挤满酒吧,我还相对平静. 我觉得我有点不耐烦或某种东西. 我没有感到紧张, 晚上8点左右, 我又服用了400毫克的L-theanine药片. 但每个人都决定我们应该等到更多的人填满酒吧. 我得到另一个酒. 我不想让人们等太久. 我没有经历过太多的焦虑! 我们终于决定上台了. 我没有害怕. 非常冷静. 虽然,我必须说当我开始唱歌我感觉有点紧张. 尽管如此,情况并没有太糟糕. 结论: 对于焦虑而言L-氨酸确实有所帮助. 我在演出前感受到的焦虑 (即便只是短暂的几秒钟) 只是坐在办公室里想着演出, 这并没有让我昏昏欲睡. 我觉得这也许会平息我的个性 (以及我的感知能力) 也许我只是没有喝足够的酒 我发现很难回答这个问题:"那么, 你的演出是怎么回事?" 我不知道是L-Theanine使我的感知变得模糊了, 我不是说我的视力是模糊的或任何东西...它只是我不能告诉你如果这是一个伟大的节目或一个糟糕的节目! 我不知道! 我猜,在舞台前没有很多人, 也与此有关. 所以很难预测演出的结果? 无论如何,L-神氨酸的工作;-) 时间表: 82319 发布时间:2020年3月13日 酒/葡萄酒 (199):第一次 (2),提高表现 (50),组合 (3),俱乐部/酒吧 (25)

Mar 13, 2020. erowid.org/exp/82319 DOSE: T+ 0:00 800 mg oral Theanine T+ 0:00 340 ml oral Alcohol - Beer/Wine T+ 2:00 750 ml oral Alcohol - Beer/Wine T+ 4:30 750 ml oral Alcohol - Beer/Wine BODY WEIGHT: 66 kg I needed something to kill performance anxiety, as we had our debut gig in a few days. The rest of the band have been on stage before, but this would be my first time. The week prior to the gig, I would get these short bursts of anxiety, that would feel like my heart was being zapped, whenever I would THINK about the event that was to come. Wednesday (the day of the gig, we play at 20:30) On the day of the gig, at about 16:00 I took two 400mg tablets from the container, meaning to take 1 and put the other one in my pocket to take later, but I absentmindedly took both - thats 800mg L-Theanine. By the time we went to the pub to do the sound check and stuff I noticed that I was very calm and was NOT panicking at all. While we were busy setting up I ordered a (340ml) beer from the empty bar... Later At around 18:00 I ordered another beer, this time a quart (750ml). People were actually starting to fill the pub, I was still relatively calm.. But I could tell in the way I conversed with a friend that I was somewhat impatient or something..I didnt feel NERVOUS per se but I was totally relaxed and calm. At around 20:00 I took another 400mg L-Theanine tablet. 20:30 rolled passed, but everyone decides that we should wait till more people filled the pub. I get another quart of beer. 21:30 I actually WANT to get playing now, I dont want to let the people wait too long. I dont experience alot of anxiety! 21:50 We finally decide to go on stage. I am not scared. Very calm. Though, I must say when I started singing I DID feel a bit nervous. It wasn't too bad at all though. Conclusion: The L-Theanine really DID help with anxiety. The amount of anxiety (even if just short bursts of a few seconds) I experienced before the gig, just sitting in the office and thinking of the gig, WAS WAY more than I actually experienced AT the actual gig. It did NOT make me drowsy. One thing though, I think it might have calmed my personality (and my sense of perception) as well...or maybe I just didnt drink enough beer...but I wasnt VERY entertaining...very chill..(well kinda). After the gig, I found it REALLY hard to answer the question: 'So, how did your gig go??' I dont know if it was the L-Theanine that blunted my perception, or if it was just how it is with these things, but it all just kinda felt like a blur. I'm not saying my vision was blurred or anything..it's just I CANNOT TELL you if it was a GREAT show or a SUCKY show! I dont know! I suppose the fact that there weren't alot of people in front of the stage also had something to do with that? There weren't a lot of people to cheer (or boo!) so I suppose it's hard to gauge the outcome of the gig?

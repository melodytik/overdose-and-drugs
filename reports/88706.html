Apr 17, 2018. erowid.org/exp/88706 DOSE: 3.7 g oral Mushrooms (dried) 2 tablets oral MDE (pill / tablet) repeated smoked Tobacco - Cigarettes BODY WEIGHT: 115 lb This past weekend I had an amazingly psychedelic trip. Dosed approximately 3.7 grams of mushrooms and later on in the trip consumed 4 pumas (mde pills). Ate the mushrooms with 2 of my closest friends, let's call them C and S. C did 3 grams of shrooms and 2 pumas while S did 1.5 hits of LSD and also 2 pumas. After consuming the shrooms and acid we just chilled watching planet earth while listening to some electro house. We then began watching Pink Floyd: Live at Pompeii. A much easier movie to watch than the Book of Eli while tripping(lol). My visuals are very shroomy, lots of flowing patterns and I feel good, other than feeling very nauseous. I was only focusing on how sick I felt and it was bringing down the spirit of my trip but after a joint is smoked it becomes more of a background feeling. This is where the night gets interesting. Me and C decide to double-drop pumas, as S had already taken some. After I swallow the pills, oddly my nausea disappears. Around 40 minutes later We start to feel really good, and I notice colors became extremely vibrant, everything almost looking like liquid. the wall I'm looking at is waving up and down while vibrant patterns flow out of the wavyness. We feel as though we are in a very colorful dream but are fully aware. It is certainly one of the best feelings I've had. We feel as though we are in a very colorful dream but are fully aware. It is certainly one of the best feelings I've had. While watching pulse it felt as though I was at the concert. At one point C points out that the drummer in the movie is drumming abnormally fast and thinks something is wrong with the movie. S says it seems normal. I then looked at the time of the video and the seconds are changing way faster than a second. Then the song high hopes came on and the bells in the beginning were happening much more often than they would at a normal tempo. After looking at my alarm clock and watching it flash every second it was also flashing way faster than every second. Time seemed to be faster than normal, and when we would focus on watching the clock it would slow down as we try to understand. This completely blew our minds, and I still don't understand how that can happen. WE continued to chill and smoke cigarettes until passing out/resting (sleeping wasn't the easiest). This was definitely one of the most magical drug combinations and trips of my life. The next day I had a wonderful afterglow and when weed was smoked I got a very pleasant high. During the trip I thought of each of my close friends and why I loved them and then I would get this amazing rush of warmth and energy throughout my whole body starting at my heart. Wonderful experience, Thanks for reading.

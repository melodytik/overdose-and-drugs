(0:00) - John weighs out a 10mg dose. One thing that was consistent in most threads regarding 4-FMA was that this stuff shouldn't be snorted and should instead be taken orally. The back of my head is tingling and I feel waves of euphoria washing over me. John wants to go chill out on the back porch. Walking from my bedroom to the back porch is when I feel the most intense effects. (03:45) - I'm talking non-stop. I have a strong desire to be surrounded by people. This would be a great party drug. I eat another 20mgs. (05:45) - I still feel some light tingling in my head but waves of euphoria are pretty much gone. (06:45) - I can't keep my legs still. Every so often the my legs just twitch uncontrollably. Even though the high has pretty much worn off I'm still in a really great mood. (07:45) - I hit the sack. There's a blur between my dreams and reality. Even though I'm pretty much sleeping at this point my hands in real life keep mimicking the things my hands are doing in the dream. I keep having this dream that I'm using my phone and I'll wake up with my hand in midair like I'm actually trying to use the phone. I don't sleep for more than 15 or 20 minutes at a time. Every time my hands start mimicking the dream I wake up. And I'm still having a hard time keeping my legs still. The twitching is worse but only seems to be affecting the lower half of my body. I can barely keep my eyes open at work, but I manage to get through my day. I doubt the crash was related to the drug. The lack of food was what caused it. This stuff completely destroys your appetite. After work at about 7pm I realized it had been almost 36 hours since I'd eaten. I force down some chicken nuggets. And I mean it when I say force. I tried 4-FA before using 4-FMA and was pretty disappointed. - I've had a few experiences with 4-FMA since writing this experience report and I have to say that this stuff seems to be way more potent when taken orally. Don't snort this shit. It burns like hell and produces minimal results. - I've found that dosing 100mgs and then redosing an additional 20-40mgs once the initial dose begins to kick in produces the best effects. Its all about finding the sweet spot.

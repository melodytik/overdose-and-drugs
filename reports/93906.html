中文译文 兴奋剂

我最近做了一项类似于提取DMT的实验, 我拿了一把贝特尔坚果, 放在一个大里. 我用了大约一升水. 我加入了一杯普通. 在低温煮6个小时后, 我将混合物透在一些伊斯兰布上. 我把混合物放回炉子上再用几个小时. 我慢慢地加入一些碳酸, 直到它不再泡. 我注意到上面有一层微小的油性, 所以我加了一点, 我让混合物煮成糊, 降低了热量, 然后我用厨房的子把它出来, 我用足够的量来遮盖厨房刀的尖端 (相当于吸或大麻所需的量) 我把它放进嘴里, 我把它放在那里一分钟左右. 声有点像一个强烈的咖啡,清晰的思想. 实际上并不是像安非他命或更强烈的药物. 大约20分钟后, 声被轻微的恶心和头所取代, 在45分钟之内, 无论如何,这是一个很好的小实验. 其他: 贝特尔坚果 (92):一般 (1),制备/食谱 (30),单独 (16)

Dec 2, 2011. erowid.org/exp/93906 DOSE: 30 mg sublingual Betel Nut (extract) BODY WEIGHT: 174 lb Just recently I tried an experiment, somewhat similar to extracting DMT but using more benign chemicals. I got a good handful of betel nuts and put them in a large pot. I would have used about a liter of water. I added about a cupful of normal vinegar. After simmering on a low heat for about 6 hours I strained the mixture through some muslin cloth. I put the mixture back on the stove for another couple of hours. When there was about a centimeter of water left I slowly added some sodium bicarbonate until it no longer fizzed. I noticed there was a slight oily layer on top so I added a dash of vinegar until it was gone. I let the mixture boil down to a paste, lowering the heat some more so it didn't burn. Then I scraped the paste using a kitchen spatula and put it into a bag. Using enough to cover the end of a kitchen knife (somewhat similar to the amount you would use in smoking opium or hash) I put it in my mouth with a similar amount of sodium bicarbonate. I held it there for a minute or so. The buzz was somewhat like a strong coffee, with a clear mind. Not really anything like an amphetamine or stronger substances. About 20 minutes later the buzz was replaced with a slight nausea and dizziness, nothing to worry about. Within 45 minutes the experience was completely gone. Well, it was a nice little experiment anyway.

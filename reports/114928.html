中文译文 兴奋剂

我在网上购买了1克2C-E和2.5克4-FMA. 这家RC供应商一直都能现承诺. 背景 在类兴奋剂中, 我以前尝试过2-FA,2-FMA,3-FPM和4F-MPH. 我也对 speed (安非他胺) 有很多经验. 我对这些化合物感兴趣, 我从来没有觉得有必要再做一次. 设置和设置 起床时间大约是上午11点. 在白天正在做一个项目. 开始喝酒的时间是14点左右. 晚上八点左右我吃了一顿大餐. 这是一个美好的一天. 我大约在22:00结束了这项工作. 我决定试试4FMA, 看看它是否能产生某种高兴感, 没有. 我本周早些时候做了一点速度. 我不使用药物或其他补充剂. 我通常选择低剂量, 因为我觉得确定适当剂量的兴奋剂比过量更好. 经验 为了测试这不是错误的其他化合物, 接下来的1小时20分钟我感觉有点温暖, 不确定是否与剂量有关. 我可能会感觉到一点点触觉增强, 我决定尝试在"常见"范围内的剂量, 我在一个囊中体重74毫克. 我决定把2C-E的袋子放在4FMA的同一个地方. 意外服用74毫克2C-E可能会很糟糕. 我已经很担心自己可能犯了错. 我将2C-E移动到其他迷幻药存储的地方. 现在我正在听着一些科技音乐, 截至目前为止, 感觉还没有改变. 可能是警报,不确定. 皮有点 感觉有点温暖,可能比起起始值有点精力. 感觉稍微"起身",但仍然有感觉我现在可以上床睡觉很好. 实际上我觉得有点饿了. 我考虑吃东西,但决定不吃. 如果没有什么改变,我打算很快就去睡觉. 我没有去睡觉,而是用一个68毫克的囊重新服用. T +02:16: 感觉比重新服用前更清醒, 没有高兴感. 自从午夜以来, 我可能再喝了大约4瓶酒. 自从醒来以来,我喝了12杯酒. 我决定去睡觉,感觉很累. 睡觉后的十分钟内就睡着了. 我醒来要去所, 腹. 我不确定是否与该化合物有关, 我醒来时仍然有点疲. 我觉得酒精和周内睡眠不足可能比4-FMA更为重要. 没有头痛,但我的头有点沉重. 尽管如此,这似乎将是一个富有成效的一天. 总结,见解 我对4-FMA的第一次体验并不太令人惊叹. 我不完全确定我是否达到值效应, 我觉得这种化合物对我来说可能与MDMA类似, 在第二次或第三次再给药时 (一次服用约500毫克) 没有产生任何附加效果, 但确实让我一周都不稳定, 我真的希望得到一个反应在这个剂量. 我可能会再试一次, 如果它确实产生反应, 我应该指出, 服用兴奋剂 (或精神药物) 后睡觉是不常见的. 我只是想指出, 服用相同剂量的4-FMA对其他人有不同的效果. 尤其是咖啡因, 在过去的14年里, 我可以在一天中消耗1-1.5克咖啡因, 没有感到紧张, 我也在许多场合睡过了LSD的成长阶段. 我发现自己醒来时会倒, 然后往往倒在床上继续睡觉. 然而最近一次使用18毫克的2-FMA让我保持12个小时的清醒. 经过阅读其他4FMA报告后, 确实表明每种物质对每个人的影响都不同. 时间表: 114928 4 - 甲胺 (580):第一次使用 (2),组合 (3),单独使用 (16)

Nov 17, 2020. erowid.org/exp/114928 DOSE: T+ 0:00 9 mg oral 4-Fluoromethamphetamine (capsule) T+ 1:20 74 mg oral 4-Fluoromethamphetamine (capsule) T+ 3:05 68 mg oral 4-Fluoromethamphetamine (capsule) T+ 0:00 repeated oral Alcohol - Beer/Wine BODY WEIGHT: 78 kg I had purchased 1 gram of 2C-E and 2.5 grams of 4-FMA from an online RC vendor. So far this RC vendor always delivered what was promised. Background In the fluorinated class of stimulants I have previously tried 2-FA, 2-FMA, 3-FPM and 4F-MPH. I also have a lot of experience with speed (amphetamine). I am interested in these compounds, to see if they can be useful as stimulant on some occasions. However, I notice I only try them a few times and never really feel the need to do them again. Set and setting Woke up around 11:00 in the morning. Was working on a project during the day. Started drinking beers at around 14:00. I had a large dinner at around 20:00. It was a nice day. I quit working on the project at about 22:00. Around 22:30 I decide I would try the 4-FMA to see if it produces any euphoria and get some experience in writing a report that might be useful to others . I had done a little bit of speed earlier this week. I do not use medicine or other supplements. I usually go for low doses, as I feel determining the right dose of a stimulant is better than just overdoing it. Experience T -01:20: Took 9 mg in a capsule to test if this isn't mistakenly some other compound with a way lower threshold. The coming 1 hour and 20 minutes I feel a little bit warm, not sure if it was related to the dose. I might be feeling a very slight bit of tactile enhancement, not sure though. I decide to try a dose in the "common" range as noted on psychonautwiki. I weigh out 74 mg in a capsule. As I'm weighing out I decide it's probably not a good idea to keep the baggy of 2C-E in the same spot as the 4-FMA. Accidentally dosing 74 mg of 2C-E would be bad. I was already paranoid that I might had mistaken myself. I move the 2C-E to the spot where the other psychedelics are stored. T +00:00 (also midnight, coincidentally): Took the 74 mg capsule after feeling this is not mistakenly also 2C-E, which I purchased at the same time. At this time I'm listening to some techno music, drinking beers and reading a bit online. T +00:18: Not yet really feeling any different so far. T +00:31: Maybe an alert, not sure. T +00:49: Bit of goosebumps T +01:00: Feel a bit warm, perhaps slightly energetic than baseline. T +01:15: Feel just slightly "up", but still have the felling I could go to bed now and sleep just fine. T +01:28: I actually feel a bit hungry. I contemplate eating but decide not to. If nothing changes planning on heading to bed soon. T +01:45: Instead of going to bed I redose 68 mg in a capsule. T +02:16: Feel a little more awake than before redosing, no euphoria. I've probably drank about 4 more beers (0.25-0.3L bottles) since midnight. Probably around 12 beers since waking up. T +02:42: I decide to go to bed, feeling tired. I sleep within 10 minutes after getting to bed. T +05:00: I woke up to go to the toilet, I had diarrhea. Not sure if related to the compound or if I ate too much products with lactose (I am lactose intolerant). T +11:00: I wake up still a bit tired. It wasn't the best sleep but I think the alcohol and perhaps too little sleep during the week are more likely to blame than the 4-FMA. T +12:00: Feel slight dehydrated in an amphetamine way, different than the dehyrated feeling after just alcohol. No headache, but my head feels a bit heavy. It feels like it's going to be a productive day though. Summary, insights My first experience with 4-FMA was not very spectacular. On the Shulgin scale it is a (+/-), I'm not entirely sure if I reached threshold effects or if that was placebo. I feel this compound might be similar to MDMA for me, where low dosages hardly do anything at all (<80 mg). Then there is a sweet spot with the good stuff (100-140 mg), and dosing way too much where it does not produce any added effects when redosing the second or third time (~500 mg in one sitting), but does leave me shaky for a week and increases my HPPD symptoms. I really expected to get a response at this dose. I will probably try this again, but it might be a few months before I get to it. If it does produce a response I will do my best to write a report, since little is documented about this compound. Finally, I should note it's neither common nor uncommon for me to sleep after taking stimulants (or psychedelics for that matter). I just want to state this in order to indicate that taking the same dose of 4-FMA with work out differently for others. Especially caffeine I have seem to have developed a huge tolerance to the past 14 years. I can consume 1-1.5 grams of caffeine during the day without feeling jittery and can be seen falling asleep within 30 minutes after taking a 300 mg caffeine capsule. I have also on numerous occasions slept through the come-up phase of LSD. Finding myself to wake up tripping, then usually stumbling to bed and continue sleeping. However, a recent experience with 18 mg of 2-FMA left me awake for 12 hours. After reading up on other 4-FMA reports, it really goes to show every substance affects everyone differently.

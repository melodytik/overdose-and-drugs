*** 通过酸盐在一个Soxlet提取器提取 *** [Erowid注:一个人不应该试图进行类提取,除非先了解所需化学品和设备的安全处理程序. 我吃了大约118克的维罗拉西奥多拉树皮. 我用600毫升的乙进行了索克斯莱特提取. 118克大整块树皮被放进一个玻璃手指甲, 底部有棉球. 热板设置为500摄氏度. 它大约每20分钟抽出一次. 我让它运行了大约5个小时, 相当于大约15个洗与蒸乙在Soxhlet. 在索克斯莱特提取完成后,提取物的颜色是深红色,略有色. 我把提取物放在一个盘子里干燥, 放在一个热板上, 温度为125摄氏度, 我将干燥材料溶解在50毫升的异醇中, 然后加入100毫升的水和150毫克的酸. 在混合了几分钟后,pH值约为2.5. 在一个分离道中,我用50毫升的烯去除了提取物3次. 第一次使用烯的脂肪是深红色的. 第三个是明确的. 所有的烯都被丢弃了. 在脱脂过程完成后, 我简单地把提取物干了. 一旦干燥, 很难把它从干燥盘上擦掉. 在把它从盘子上擦掉之后, 我称重了它. 结果是195毫克干燥的晶体粉末, *** 活动测试 *** 我测试了10毫克鼻腔内. 几乎没有被检测到的效果. 经过30分钟的时间, 一种轻微的遥远感觉开始消失. 没有其他影响. 这与我2毫克的5-MeO-DMT剂量相似. 后来我测试了30毫克鼻子内 (15毫克在每个鼻孔). 几乎在吸入物质后, 鼻腔中出现了轻微的烧伤. 效果在10分钟后开始. 我再次感受到一种异常遥远的感觉,一种梦幻般的感觉, 在30分钟后, 我感到非常不同寻常. 而不是DMT, 而是Amanita pantherina. 虽然Virola theiodora据说含有5-MeO-DMT,DMT和bufotenine,但这显然只是一个5-MeO-DMT的经验. 如果有任何DMT或布福存在, 它是如此之小, 没有明显的影响. 没有增加色彩效应,几何图案,化或旋转效应,而这两种效应在布福和DMT中是常见的. 我经历了非常温和的视觉效果, 眼前的景象有点像烟花, 东西看起来有点模糊或遥远. 我心灵非常放松, 但没有像阿曼蒂塔那样醉酒的感觉. 几乎像是用石头打死的人. 没有恶心或其他副作用. 我周围的一切看起来很奇怪, 这部分体验与LSD相似. 我觉得自己几乎完全脱离了环境. 我还注意到我可以清楚地听到远处的声音. 根据感觉到的效果, 提取物可能有20%的5-MeO-DMT (约6毫克的5-MeO-DMT). 在这种剂量水平下, 5-MeO-DMT 对于冥想或作为社交药物非常有用. 在这次旅行中, 像是5-MeO-DMT关闭了我所有的情绪, 我发现很容易娱乐人们在它的影响下. 我觉得自己很有趣,很容易让别人笑. 我注意到我讲笑话的时候, 椅子好像在笑. 这不是幻觉,只是意识到椅子上的轮形成了一个笑脸. 我周围的一切看起来不熟悉. 这就像我第一次看到一切一样. 这种效果与LSD相似. *** 关于提取的注意事项 *** 下次我尝试这种提取, 我会先试着把树皮撕碎. 这可能有助于提高产量. 如果你想用去除脂肪, 另一次我尝试了这个. 我用酒精提取,将其缩到50毫升, 加入100毫升水,150毫克酸, 这样就产生了厚厚的乳液, 为了分解乳液, 我不得不使水性层变成50%的酒精. 酒精可以吸收很多垃圾. 即使使使水性层是50%的酒精, 需要大约6个脱脂来去除所有的脂肪. 通过乙提取似乎是更好的方法. 在脱脂阶段 (我将干提取物溶解在50毫升酒精和150毫升水中), 我只需要用烯脱脂3次, *** 总结 *** 维罗拉草是一种廉价的天然5-MeO-DMT来源. 它的含量很少, 尽管我使用索克斯莱特抽机多小时, 在118克 (118,000毫克) 的树皮中, 我只能提取大约195毫克的脱脂物质, 这大约是0.03%的5-MeO-DMT产量. 但由于5-MeO-DMT是如此强大, 活性大约在5毫克鼻内, 这样一剂量大约是2美元 (对于一个轻微的剂量). 这一点也不错. 这是一种非常独特的迷幻药物. 尽管名字相似, 5-MeO-DMT 绝对不像DMT. 双氧化物 (甚至纯的自由基布丁) 的效果与DMT相比,更接近于5-MeO-DMT. 五米奥-德是很好的冥想辅助剂, 让你感觉与周围环境的联系断开. 它具有与LSD非常相似的视角改变效果, 使事物看起来不同,不熟悉, 这是5-MeO-DMT实验中最有趣的部分. 除非使用非常高剂量, 否则这种体验缺乏视觉效应. 其他: 病毒种类 (319) : 不适用 (38), 培养/合成 (31), 制备/配方 (30), 一般 (1)

Acetone Extract of Virola Theiodora Virola Theiodora by Baron Citation:   Baron. "Acetone Extract of Virola Theiodora: An Experience with Virola Theiodora (exp67329)". Erowid.org . Dec 1, 2011. erowid.org/exp/67329 DOSE: 30 mg insufflated Virola spp. (extract) BODY WEIGHT: 250 lb *** Extracting With Acetone In A Soxhlet Extractor *** [ Erowid Note: One should not attempt to perform alkaloid extractions without first having an understanding of safe handling procedures for the chemicals and equipment required.] I had about 118 grams of Virola Theiodora bark. I did a Soxhlet extraction using 600 ml of acetone. 118 grams of large whole pieces of bark were placed in a glass thimble with cotton balls on the bottom. The hotplate was set to 500 C. It siphoned about once every 20 minutes. I let it run for about 5 hours amounting to about 15 washes with distilled acetone in the Soxhlet. After Soxhlet extraction was complete, the color of the extract was dark red with a slight orange tint. I dried the extract in a dish on a hotplate at 125 C with plenty of forced air overnight. I dissolved the dried material in 50 ml of isopropyl alcohol and then added 100 ml of water and 150 mg of citric acid. After mixing for a few minutes, the pH was about 2.5. In a separatory funnel I defatted the extract 3 times each using 50 ml of xylene. The first defat with xylene was dark red. The third was clear. All the xylene was discarded. After the defatting process was complete I simply dried the extract. Once dry, it was pretty hard to scrape it off the drying dish. After scraping it off the dish, I weighed it. It came to 195 mg of dry crystalline powder that was red with a hint of orange. *** Activity Tests *** I tested 10 mg intra-nasally. It produced barely detectable effects. There was a slight distant feeling felt after about 30 minutes, and then it started to fade. There were no other effects. This is similar to a 2 mg dose of 5-MeO-DMT in me. Later I tested 30 mg intra-nasally (15 mg in each nostril). There was a very slight burn in the nasal passages almost immediately after snuffing the material. The effects started after about 10 minutes. Again, I felt an unusual distant feeling, sort of a dreamy feeling, reminiscent of dried Amanita pantherina capsules. After about 30 minutes it peaked. I felt really unusual. The effects of 5-MeO-DMT are more like Amanita pantherina than DMT. Although Virola theiodora is said to contain 5-MeO-DMT, DMT, and bufotenine, this is clearly a 5-MeO-DMT experience only. If there is any DMT or bufotenine present, it is so small as to have no noticeable effects. There were no increased color effects, geometric patterns, melting or swirling effects which are common with bufotenine and DMT. I experienced very mild visual effects that were similar to Amanita pantherina’s visual effects. There were mild sparkly firework-like visuals near the edge of my view and things looked a little cloudy or distant. I felt extremely relaxed mentally, but there was no drunken feeling like there is with Amanita pantherina. There was a slight bit of mental confusion, almost a stoning effect. There was no nausea or other side effects felt. Everything in my environment looked odd, not different, just somehow unfamiliar. This part of the experience is similar to LSD. Unlike most other psychedelics I felt almost completely detached from my environment. I also noticed that I could hear distant sounds very clearly. Judging by the effects felt, the extract is maybe 20% 5-MeO-DMT (about 6 mg 5-MeO-DMT). In me, at this dosage level, 5-MeO-DMT is very useful for meditation or as a social drug. During this trip, I felt emotionally withdrawn. As if the 5-MeO-DMT turned off all of my emotions, except my sense of humor. I found it easy to entertain people under its effects. I felt comical and could easily make other people laugh. I noticed how my chair looked like it was smiling as I was telling jokes. This was not a hallucination, just a realization that the contours on the chair made a smiley face. Everything around me looked unfamiliar. It was like I was looking at everything for the first time. Again, this effect is similar to LSD. *** Notes About Extraction *** The next time I try this extraction I’ll try to shred the bark first. This might help improve the yield a little. You want to avoid extracting with alcohol if you’re going to defat with xylene. On another occasion I tried this. I extracted with alcohol, concentrated it down to 50 ml, added 100 ml of water, 150 mg of citric acid, and tried to defat with xylene in a separatory funnel. This produced thick emulsions that would not break down. I had to make the aqueous layer 50% alcohol in order to break down the emulsions. Alcohol pulls a lot of junk. Even after making the aqueous layer 50% alcohol, it took about 6 defats to remove all of the fats. Extracting with acetone seems to be the better route. It pulls less junk; and during the defatting stage (where I dissolved the dried acetone extract in 50 ml alcohol and 150 ml water), I only needed to defat 3 times with xylene, and I never had problems with emulsions. *** Summary *** Virola theiodora is a cheap natural source for 5-MeO-DMT. It doesn’t contain much on a gram for mg basis. Even when using a Soxhlet extractor for many hours, my yield was very small! From the 118 grams (118,000 mg) of bark, I only managed to extract about 195 mg of defatted material, and of that only about 39 mg was actually 5-MeO-DMT. That’s a yield of about 0.03 % 5-MeO-DMT. But because 5-MeO-DMT is so potent, active at about 5 mg intra-nasally, the yield in terms of doses is close to 8 mild doses. The 118 grams cost me only $16, so that comes to approximately $2 a dose (for a mild dose). That’s not bad at all. 5-MeO-DMT is a very unique psychedelic. Despite the name similarities, 5-MeO-DMT is absolutely nothing like DMT. Dibufotenoxide (or even plain freebase bufotenine) is closer in effects to DMT than 5-MeO-DMT is. 5-MeO-DMT is great as a meditation aid in mild doses. It has a stoning effect that relaxed the mind and makes you feel disconnected from your environment. It has perspective alteration effects very similar to LSD causing things to look different, unfamiliar, as though you are seeing them for the first time. This is the most interesting element of the 5-MeO-DMT experience. Unlike DMT, the experience lacks visual effects unless very high doses are used.

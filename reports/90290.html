剩下的是我最喜欢的灵性方法:祈祷. 您可能会问,如果我更喜欢祷告, 我们应该从他们那里获得我们所能获得的智慧和友, 并为其他宗教的人们提供我们必须给予的爱的服务和勇敢的真理. 无论如何, 对我来说, 祷告是我所知道的最有价值的宗教实践. 我使用的第一种方法是最正式的. 我找到了一个安静的地方, 在我永恒的父面前谦卑地跪下, 经历了这样的经历:几年前, 我曾经非常丧, 我有一个好朋友带我吃午饭, 他问我回家后会不会做些什么. 我说当然. 然后他挑战我回家,跪下, 求上帝告诉我他爱我,让我感受到他的爱. 我同意这样做. 于是我回家祈祷了一两分钟, 什么也没发生. 尽管如此,我毫不怀疑,当他认为合适的时候,我会得到答案,所以我继续日常工作. 那天晚上,我父亲突然来访了, 他直接走到钢琴前, 开始给我演奏一首他刚刚创作的绝对美丽的乐曲. 虽然音乐是我最大的世上激情, 听到他演奏, 我意识到这是上帝回答我的祷告的个人方式. 我确实感觉到上帝的爱充满了我的灵魂! 我使用的第二种祷告方式是非正式的祷告. 我在日常生活中注意到生活中的祝福, 或是思考我看到或听到的东西, 这对你来说可能很奇怪, 无论如何,有一天我在思考为什么圣经教导我们特别向父祷告, 我向上帝说,我真希望和她有同样的关系, 答案却是出乎意料而令人愉快的. 我突然感觉到圣灵充满了我的心,这些话像纯粹的智慧一样流入我的脑海:"你的天母也想念你,她期待着在天国拥抱你. 不像哈哈那很有趣;但笑的纯粹的快乐. 这真是太棒了! 第三种方法是不同的, 我不经常这样做. 基本上是这样的:当我太累或有其他原因使得自己软弱,甚至无法表达自己的想法时,我只能让我的心向上帝倾诉自己的渴望. 我在医疗监督下服用了一年重量级镇静剂, 我倒在地板上, 身体和精神都在痛苦中. 我想要祈祷,但我不能把话语放在一起. 我只是在痛苦中哭泣, 希望得到某种解脱. 我的情绪平静下来, 我的身体恢复了力量. 我站了起来,震惊,但非常放松. 我惊奇地想到耶稣基督听到了我的哀声,并因同情而承担了我的痛苦. 我感受到深深的感激, 我可以继续我的生意,履行我的责任,好像一整天都没有什么不对! 接下来的几天是充满挑战的, 其他: 发布时间:2016年12月19日 活动 (129):成与习惯 (10),实体/生物 (37),回顾/总结 (11),孤独 (16)

"Communing With Deity: An Experience with Prayer (exp90290)". Erowid.org . Dec 19, 2016. erowid.org/exp/90290 BODY WEIGHT: 175 lb I am the sort of person who enjoys and seeks wholesome spiritual experiences because of the richness it brings to life. I have used many entheogens and practiced prayer, Vipassana meditation and shamanic journeying. I have decided to leave the entheogens behind seeing as I am a “Mormon” and the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints encourages sobriety. I confess though, sometimes I feel the allure of strange plants calling, if you know what I mean. I continue to practice Vipassana meditation, but I don't do shamanic journeying with regularity. That leaves my preferred method of spirituality: prayer. You may ask why, if I prefer prayer, do I practice other methods of enlightenment at all. Well, the Church teaches that all religions have good in them and that we ought to receive what wisdom and fellowship we can from them and offer people of other religions what loving service and courageous truth we have to give in return. Anyway for me, prayer has been the most rewarding religious practice I have ever known. There are basically three ways that I pray; and I would like to share with you one experience with each method in hopes that they will inspire you and help you find additional happiness in your quest. The first method I use is the most formal. I find a quiet place, kneel humbly before my Eternal Father, and pour out my heart and speak my mind in the name of His Son, Jesus Christ. So here is the experience: there was a time a few years ago when I was extremely depressed and slacking off on all my responsibilities. A good friend of mine took me to lunch probably to talk some sense into me. After listening to me complain, he asked if I would do something when I got home. I said sure. He then challenged me to go home, get on my knees, and ask God to tell me He loves me, to let me feel His love. I agreed to do this. So I went home and said the prayer and sat there for a minute or two and nothing happened. Nevertheless, I did not doubt that I would get an answer when He saw fit, so I went about my daily business. That night, my father unexpectedly dropped by for a visit, which he never did. I showed him in and he went straight to the piano and began to play me an absolutely beautiful piece of music which he had just composed. Although music is my greatest earthly passion, my dad had never done this before. As he played, I realized this was God's very personal way of answering my prayer. Indeed I felt the love of God fill my soul! The second method of prayer I employ is the informal sort. As I go about my daily business I take notice of the blessings of this life or contemplate something I have seen or heard and I make comments to God as if He were right there just hanging out like a pal. Perhaps this seems strange to you, but it is quite normal for me. Anyway, one day I was contemplating why the scriptures teach us to pray specifically to the Father even though the Church has always taught that we also have a glorified Eternal Mother as well. I commented to God that I really wish I could have the same sort of relationship with Her as I have with Him and I asked why we are not taught to pray to Her at this time. Well, God did not see fit to explain Himself to me, but the answer I did get was quite unexpected and delightful. I suddenly felt the Spirit fill my heart and these words flowed like pure intelligence into my mind: “Your Heavenly Mother misses you too, and She looks forward to embracing you in the Kingdom of Heaven.” After being startled for a moment, I began to laugh. Not like ha ha that's funny; but a laugh of pure joy. It was awesome! The third method is different and I don't do it often. It is basically this: when I am too tired or otherwise weakened to even formulate thoughts, I just let my heart pour out its yearnings to my God. So here is what happened: I was going through withdrawals from a major tranquilizer that I had taken under medical supervision for a year. Three days into the withdrawal my mind just scrambled and I fell to the floor literally writhing in agony both physical and mental. I wanted to pray but I could not put words together. I simply cried out in distress hoping for some sort of relief. This went on for a short while when very suddenly my mind re-organized, my emotions settled, and my body received its strength. I stood up, shocked, but greatly relieved. As I marveled the clear and distinct impression came to my mind that Jesus Christ had heard my cry and had taken upon Himself my suffering out of compassion. I was moved with deep gratitude and I expressed my thanks. I was able to go about my business and fulfill my responsibilities as if nothing was wrong all that day! The following days were challenging but nothing I couldn't handle.

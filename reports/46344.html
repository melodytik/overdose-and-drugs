中文译文 大麻素

介绍: 今晚我和我的室友已经没有芽了, 我曾为一个我经常参加的论坛记录过这段经历, 有人建议我也在这里提交报告. 我当时体内没有其他药物, 除了在准备大麻时喝的少量咖啡因. 我不能代表我的室友谈论这个话题, 然而 - - 他正在不断变化的情绪改变处方为他的双极倾向. 他的身材和我的身材相似. 我们的原料和工具: - 节省,叶子,剪切,茎,和/或摇. 在我们的案例中, 这大约是半司的茎残余从大多是中等质量的大麻芽. - 足够的摩擦酒精完全覆盖植物材料. 瓶中的唯一成分是异醇和水. 我们的情况是,一瓶16司的91%异醇足够了. - 一个石头混合酒精和植物材料. -至少有两个咖啡过器过酒精和植物物质混合物. - - 一个杯子来捕捉过液体, 一个光滑的玻璃或陶表面让它干燥, 一个风扇来吹走由此产生的烟雾, 确保你在通风的地方 - - 温暖的摩擦酒精可以释放一些令人印象深刻的烟雾. - 一把剃须刀或X-acto类型的刀, 用于从蒸发表面除哈希. 我们使用塑料身份证, 但剃须刀可能会更好. - - 卷纸或其他可吸烟的材料, 准备:在外出购买了91%的异醇摩擦酒后, 我们回家, 磨碎了大约半司的剩余树干, 倒入石头瓶中. 然后加入酒,直到磨的茎被彻底浸泡,上面有一层大约一英寸的额外酒. 我们使用了16司瓶的三分之一. 子被封闭,然后大约三分钟大力摇动,然后打开,然后通过两种咖啡过器将其内容倒入测量杯中. 过后大约有一半到三分之二杯绿色/黄色半透明液体. 这被倒在一个大 (10'直径),圆形,平面的陶板上,放置在一个充满水的深. 我们不想将火焰直接应用到盘子上, 因为害怕酒精点燃. 而是把水煮得温和一点, 放置一个风扇, 过后的液体在约50分钟的时间内蒸发,在陶板的顶部形成了一层薄而粘的深绿色涂层. 这种树脂是用火把加热从板上松开的, 并用塑料学生身份证的边缘 (唯一可用的工具) 除. 然后用一张翻两折的卷纸擦除身份证两边的树脂. 纸上有厚厚的绿黑树脂被从纸上撕下来, 成球, 每个球大小就像一个大针头. 球被一个接一个地放进管子里, 我从一开始就不认为这会起作用, 整个过程非常简单 - - 下次我唯一要做出的改变是使用更多的材料, 我使用了所有剩余的植物物质, 对于蒸发表面来说,该板工作得很好,但使/树脂的擦除有点困难. 蒸发过的液体在这样一个大面积使它很快地消失 (酒精蒸发不到一个小时),但结果在盘子上很薄. 这使得削变得更加困难, 尤其是因为形成的树脂非常粘, 尽管有这些问题,我们还是取得了不错的成绩. 结果:我先抽烟, 因为我的室友不愿意抽烟, 我了第一个球,并吸了它. 需要几次拍摄, 我才知道如何在每次拍摄中, 吸入足够的烟雾. 管的碳水化合物必须被塞到每次打击的尽头,以吸引足够的烟雾. 烟雾本身是厚的, 但缺乏典型的味道和香味的芽. 个人而言, 我不认为烟味比我们用来擦身而过的卷纸还要好. 这是一个有点在严厉的一面与大多数相比,但绝不是无法忍受的. 每个球持续了两到四次. 我发现自己的心情有所改变. 我没有生病,这令人鼓舞,所以我又滚了一颗球,这次更大, 我清楚地记得一个沉重的头, 持续了几分钟. 我注意到这种高潮与吸烟的高潮有很大不同. 几乎完全集中在脑海中 - - 我没有感觉到我从芽开始的正常体质, 缺乏身体的兴奋使得它有点迷失方向. 我觉得身体是清醒的, 我觉得自己没有被吸毒, 但当我起床做某事, 在三小时的时间里, 我抽了四个球, 现在已经有两个小时了, 自从我吸了最后一支, 诚实地说,这份报告写起来好像是一子, 我总是被小事分散注意力. 我还必须重读段落, 确保我打字后的段落有意义 - - 有些段落没有. 我同居的同伴, 只是几个月来一直在吸大麻, 在吸了几颗球后, 头部急剧打击他很难. 他有很多神经能量,开始步行,说一些我不太懂的东西, 站起来,走到厨房,停在门口,环顾一下,硬起来,然后说'...水!' 接下来的几个小时里, 他似乎也很困惑, 也许是因为他用纸比我用滚球更多. 在任何情况下,哈希奇对他有深刻的影响. 结论:我很惊这个方法能如此有效. 如果我能得到很多廉价,低质量的草, 我们用从盘子上取的树脂出了六个球. 但考虑到我们开始时只有半司的茎, 我认为这是一个成功. 我会推给任何有足够幸运的人, 这是一个简单的实验, 其他: 大麻 - 大麻 (93):一般 (1), 准备/食谱 (30), 小组 (2-9) (17)

Little Balls of Joy Cannabis - Hash by Jacobo Citation:   Jacobo. "Little Balls of Joy: An Experience with Cannabis - Hash (exp46344)". Erowid.org . Dec 23, 2008. erowid.org/exp/46344 DOSE: 4 smoked Cannabis - Hash (extract) BODY WEIGHT: 210 lb Introduction: Tonight my roommate and I were out of bud, so we decided to try to do an cannabinoid extraction of some leftover stems using rubbing alcohol (isopropanol) as the extracting solvent. I documented the experience for a forum I frequent, and it was suggested that I submit the report here as well. At the time, I had no other drugs in my system except a small amount of caffeine from the Mountain Dew I drank throughout the preparation of the hash. I can't speak for my roommate on this topic, however - he's on an ever-changing battery of mood-altering prescriptions for his bipolar tendencies. He is comparable in body size to me. Our ingredients and tools: -Spare bud, leafs, trimmings, stems, and/or shake. In our case, this was about 1/2 an ounce of stems leftover from mostly middle-of-the-road quality cannabis buds. -Enough rubbing alcohol to completely coat the plant material. The only ingredients in the bottle were isopropanol and water. In our case, one 16 oz. bottle of 91% isopropanol was more than enough. -A mason jar to mix the alcohol and plant material. -At least two coffee filters to filter the alcohol/plant matter mix. -A cup to catch the filtered liquid, a smooth glass or ceramic surface for it to dry on, a fan to blow the resulting fumes away, and a double boiler of some sort to prevent fire. Make sure you're in a ventilated area - warm rubbing alcohol can let off some impressive fumes. -A razor blade or X-acto type of knife for scraping the hash off the evaporation surface. We used a plastic ID card, but a razor blade would have probably worked much better. -Rolling papers or some other smokable material on which to apply the hash. Preparation: After going out and buying some 91% isopropanol rubbing alcohol, we came home and ground what I would estimate to be half an ounce of leftover stems and poured the result into a mason jar. Then rubbing alcohol was added until the ground-up stems were thoroughly soaked with a layer of about an inch of extra rubbing alcohol on top. We used about a third of the 16 oz. bottle. The jar was sealed and shaken vigorously for about three minutes before it was opened and the contents poured through two coffee filters into a measuring cup. There was about one-half to two-thirds of a cup of green/yellow semi-transparent liquid after the filtering. This was poured onto a large (10' in diameter), circular, flat, ceramic plate which was placed in a deep baking pan filled with water. The pan was placed over the stove burner and served as a makeshift double-boiler - we didn't want to apply the flame directly to the plate for fear of the alcohol igniting. Instead, the water was brought to a gentle boil and a fan was placed to blow all the fumes and steam away from the area. The filtered liquid evaporated over the course of about 50 minutes and formed a thin, sticky, dark green coating over the top of the ceramic plate. This resin was loosened from the plate by applying heat with a lighter and scraped using the edge of a plastic student ID card (the only tool available). Then the resin was wiped off both sides of the edge of the ID using a piece of rolling paper folded in half. The areas of the paper with thick coatings of green-black resin were ripped from the rest of the paper and rolled into balls, each the size of a large pinhead. The balls were placed one at a time into a pipe and smoked. Observations: I honestly didn't think this was going to work in the first place, but I figured it was worth a try. The whole procedure was pretty simple - the only change I'd make next time is to use more material to start with and to evaporate the filtered liquid over a smaller area than the large ceramic plate. I used all the leftover plant matter I had available, so the materials issue couldn't be helped. As for the evaporation surface, the plate worked decently but made scraping the hash/resin somewhat difficult. Evaporating the filtered liquid over such a large area made it go quickly (it took less than an hour for the alcohol to evaporate), but the result was spread very thinly across the plate. This made the scraping bit harder, especially because the resin that formed was really sticky and held fast to smooth surfaces. In spite of those problems, we still got a decent result. Results: I smoked it first, as my roommate was leery of scraping and smoking something we mixed together on a whim that resembled dried green paste. I rolled the first ball and smoked it. It took a couple hits before I figured out how to get enough smoke into the pipe each hit. The little balls of resin and paper seemed to smolder more than they burned, and the carb of the pipe had to be plugged until the very end of each hit to draw enough smoke. The smoke itself was thick, but lacked the typical taste and scent of bud. My roommate compared the taste of it to 'burning plastic...sort of.' Personally, I didn't think the smoke had much of a taste beyond that of the rolling paper we used to wipe the ID's clean. It was a little on the harsh side compared to most bud, but by no means unbearable. Each ball lasted for two to four hits. Within a minute after I finished smoking the first ball, I noticed a change in my state of mind. I wasn't getting sick, which was encouraging, so I rolled another ball, larger this time, and smoked it as well. At that point, my thoughts got a little jumbled, but I distinctly remember a heavy head rush that lasted for a couple minutes. Once that was over, I noticed that the high was considerably different from the high gotten from smoking bud. It was almost entirely centralized in the mind - I didn't feel the normal body high that I do from bud, but my line of thought was obviously altered. The lack of a body high made it a bit disorienting. My body felt sober, but my mind felt heavily intoxicated. It led to a few weird times when I wouldn't *feel* stoned, but when I got up to do something, like getting a glass of water, I'd have to pause and remind myself what I was doing. Over the course of three hours, I smoked about four balls in all, with about equal time between each. It's now about two hours since I smoked the last one, and I'm still far from sober. To be honest, this report feels like it's taking forever to write, and I keep getting distracted by small things. I also have to reread paragraphs to make sure they make sense after I type them - a few of them haven't. My roommate, who has been smoking weed with regularity for only a couple months, felt the effects more strongly. After smoking a couple of the balls, the head rush hit him hard. He had a lot of nervous energy and started pacing and saying things that didn't make particularly much sense to me, though to be fair, I was high as well. At one point, he entered the kitchen then left it, sat down on the couch for a moment, got up, walked to the kitchen, paused at the entrance, looked around for a moment, stiffened up, and said 'uhh...water!' before finally grabbing his cup and filling it up. He did a lot of strange little things like that for the next couple hours. He also seemed rather confused and didn't look like he was enjoying himself as much as I did. Also, the taste of the smoke got to him much more, possibly because he used more paper than I did when rolling the balls. In any case, the hash had a profound effect on him. Conclusions: I was surprised this worked so well. It might be worth repeating if I can get my hands on a lot of cheap, low-quality weed. In this case, we were able to roll six balls from the resin we scraped from the plate. But considering we started with nothing but a half an ounce of stems, I'd consider this a success. I'd recommend this to anyone luck enough to have extra weed around - especially schwag, trimmings, stems, and/or shake - and some time to kill. It's a simple experiment, and somewhat crude, but the results are definitely interesting.

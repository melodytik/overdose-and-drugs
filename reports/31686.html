我知道如果我继续这样做会失去什么. 故事的开始是这样: 我在1月份在监狱里遇到了一个女孩,我叫她"Babycakes". 她住在我家附近的一个小镇, 宝贝蛋糕告诉我她会让我高第一天我们在一起当她出狱. 我很满意,自去年8月以来我没有做过. 我们从我的城镇去她的城镇, 大约在途中她拿出了"泡"并把它交给了我. 我知道常规,点燃火,把它放在毒品下,滚动泡几次...把嘴放进去,慢慢吸入高. 我不确定我吃了多少次,但我知道整个晚上直到10点我感觉像我回到成的日子, 像每个人都说的,我感到高兴,我很口, 车上没有无线电, 完全没有音乐, 所以整个行程, 我觉得自己会发疯! 没有音乐当我 tweakin? 这怎么可能呢? 通常情况下,这不会是这样一个大问题, 但那时与我一起在车上的其他三个人, 后来我的身体拒绝了液体或食物的想法. 我得到了我的香烟,每隔15分钟就会点燃另一个. 每次我吸毒, 我都想起8月份的经历. 我试图睡觉的时候, 胃里有一种恶心的感觉, 因为我做了太多, 我认为这次一定会更大,更好,更有趣. 也许我甚至可以在这个过程中减掉几磅. 因为我在签署保证金, 我必须在那天晚上10点回家. 我忘记了我的男朋友, 他是我生命中唯一一个让我为自己感到高兴的人, 他表示这"不适合他". 我和他做了一些计划, 去和在明尼阿波利斯, 计划是让Babycakes带我...在Twack World,我们不清楚地思考事物, 我告诉他我会在20分钟内给他打电话,20分钟变成3小时. 整个时间,被了我的股和我旋转如此之快. 紧紧,骨疼痛, 两侧感觉好像要裂开, 我们在路上两次停下来, 让我们最好的朋友点灯, 吸入高, 距离我家大约一英里, 我们又停下来, 我感觉到我的身体在, 在我的无法控制的下巴, 心跳比我以前感觉到的更快. 打电话说话, 走到我家的路上, 谈话对我来说没有意义. 人们跟我不一样, 尤其是清醒的人. 问我为什么没有打电话告诉他们那天晚上我不去溜冰. ,是的! 我现在记得,,这就是我能说的. 我无法坐在那里和他们进行某种高度激动的对话, 我让步了,说我很抱歉,离开我的避风港,我的房间. 现在,我想要另一个命中...他们让我生气,我觉得我的高正在消失. 她把我放下后约10分钟就打电话给我, 着我说了些东西, 我的男朋友没有接电话, 这显然表明他对我生气. 这可以理解,但我是否关心? 没有. 我写信,唱歌,写诗,看音电视,听我的音乐. 假装睡着了, 不然我父母会下楼来抓住我. 我听到的每一个噪音都是"他们"在地板上的每一个声,都是他们试图听我在做什么. 闭上眼睛几乎是不可能的, 我看着那天早上的日出, 整天都感到胃病, 麻醉的气息, 我的手,我的手臂,我的脸,我的喉,我的鼻子,我的舌头,我的头. 我认为它在任何地方. 我刷牙,吸喉液,口香糖,喝水,没有什么帮助. 我在床旁放一个垃圾桶, 我手指在连续四小时写作后疼痛, 没有休息, 今天是星期二,我的痛得很严重, 周一开始成为大家的正常生活, 我心情很糟糕. 我的身体很累,我的思想也很累, 我预约了上学时间8点, 到了十点半, 我已经通过了测试, 我整天都在打扫,所有东西. 我洗手间,我父母的洗手间,客厅,把一切都洗干净,清理地板, 到了五点半, 我已经和男朋友在聊天了. 这次谈话带来了一些不好的感觉. 我和他心里有些不对. 他知道,昨晚他知道我在做什么. 他感到失望, 我心里不敢告诉他, 羞愧我在我的生活中采取了转折, 这让我每次被困几个月. 我应该知道我该怎么做. 药物激发了我大脑中的某种东西, 晚上渐渐晚了,我开始看到我的后院里有东西在移动, 我伤害了我的男朋友的感情, 因为他说的每一句话, 我都误解了, 现在是什么正常人,会破坏她生命中最好的关系? 这就是爱,因为我已经几天没睡觉,也没吃饭, 于是到了11点半, 我就这样做了. 我的身体一直想醒来,我的子疼痛我不能舒服和的气息慢慢消失和我的高. 我今天早上8点半醒来, 希望我能感觉更好, 不仅在身体上, 过去的48小时是模糊的,我的舌头是尖的,我几乎失去了我不能失去的爱. 我还能看到自己在两个方向上滑动. 如果我按照身体的意愿去做, 那就是地狱......每天不停地撞击那个泡, 不睡觉, 不吃饭, 不关心任何人, 我身体想要走的路是充满心痛和痛苦, 谎言和欺骗, 另一条道路是希望和爱的道路. 我可以获得的幸福只要我远离我认为对我有益的东西, 对于我来说,麻醉药就像水一样, 经过这次旅程, 我看到我的梦想和目标, 愿望和野心都消失了, 我曾经为之付出努力的自尊心如同光线一样消失, 今天我必须依靠我自己, 我可以承认,我的名字是K, 我是一个吸毒者/酒精. 其他: 甲基 (37): 关系 (44), 和习惯性 (10), 其他 (28)

Aug 23, 2006. erowid.org/exp/31686 DOSE: repeated smoked Methamphetamine (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 120 lb Once again, I have tried meth. This time though, it has made me realize I’m a totally different person when I’m on it and what I can lost if I keep doing it. Here’s how the story starts While I was in jail back in January, I met a girl there I'll call 'Babycakes' She lives in a town close to mine and Sunday we hung out finally. Babycakes had told me she'd get me high the first day we hung out when she got outta jail. I was down with it, I hadn’t done it since last August. We were takin a road trip from my town to her town, about halfway there she got out the 'bubble' and passed it back to me. I knew the routine, light the torch, hold it under the dope and roll the bubble a few times...put mouth to it and slowly suck in the high. I'm not sure how many hits I took, alls I know that the whole night up until 10:00 I was feeling like I had back in the addiction days, after I had been up for a week and it must've been almost instantly that I cheered up from my mood earlier that day. Like everyone says, a euphoric feeling came over me, I was talkative, same old shit I had been back in August. The car didn't have a radio and absolutely no music, so the whole ride, I heard a torch, an occasional window being rolled down for someone smoking a cigarette. I felt like I was going to freak out! No music when I’m tweakin? How could this be? Normally, it wouldn’t have been such a big deal but the three others in the car at the time with me, were makin a big deal about it also. I had smoked up my 1/2 pack of cigarettes within 45 minutes and I needed more. *6 more miles and we're at a store* That flew by in about a millisecond for me, my mouth was dry so a Pepsi and Mountain Dew did the trick for a few minutes. But then my body rejected the thought of liquid, or food. I got my cigarettes and every few 15 minutes another would be lit. As for the bubble of dope, it kept coming my way, every time I took that hit I remembered my experience back in August. The meth breath, the heavy smell of it pouring out of my pores as I try and sleep, the sick feeling in my stomach when I’ve done far too much but still I keep doing it. That was me, but I dismissed those thoughts and thought this time will definitely be bigger, better and fun. Maybe I could even lose a few pounds in the process of all this. Since I was out on signature bond, I had to be home by 10:00 that night. While I was with Babycakes and my newfound friends I forgot about my boyfriend, the one person in this life that makes me happy for who I am and is really against smokin ice. Its 'not for him' he says. I had made plans with him, to go and hang out in Minneapolis which is an hour drive from where I live. The plan was for Babycakes to bring me....well in Twack World we don't think things through clearly and think in a legitimate way. I told him I'd call him in twenty minutes, twenty minutes turned into 3 hours. The whole time, getting ripped off my ass and I was spinning so hard. Jaws clenched, my temples hurt, my sides felt like they were splitting yet I kept taking hits from the bubble. On the way to my house, we stopped 2 times on the country roads to light up our best buddy, inhaled the high and kept our upbeat attitudes. About a mile from my house, we pulled over again and literally smoked up the Blazer we were in. It looked like somebody was hotboxin in there and alls I could taste was dope, gum and cigarettes, my body felt shaky, gum smacking with my uncontrollable jaws, heart beating faster than I’ve ever felt before. Talking on the phone on the rest of the way to my house felt like nothing I’ve felt before. Conversations weren't making sense to me. People weren't on the same level as me, especially the sober ones. As I stepped in my house, my parents had a sit down with me asking why I hadn't called to tell them I wasn't going to Figure Skating that night. Oh yah! I remember now, oops was all I could say. I couldn’t sit there and have some sort of highly agitated conversation with them, there’s no way my mind would sit still for that long. I gave in, said I was sorry and left down to my haven, my room. About this time, I wanted another hit...they pissed me off and it felt like my high was wearing off. Babycakes called me about 10 minutes after she dropped me off and mumbled shit to me, I mumbled back and that was it, I said call me tomorrow. My boyfriend wasn’t answering his phone which was an obvious pointer that he was more than pissed at me. Understandable but did I care? Hell no. All night, my eyes were BIG and BULGED...I wrote letters all night, songs, poems, watched MUTED tv and listened to MY music. Around 4:30 AM there was nothing else to do, so I 'fake slept' just in case my parents decided to come downstairs to catch me doin something. Every noise I heard was 'them' Every creak in the floor, was them trying to listen to what I was doing. Closing my eyes was almost impossible, my fluttering and twitches kept them open, wide open. I watched the sunrise that morning, all the while feeling sick to my stomach, the meth breath, I smell it everywhere. My hands, my arms, my face, my throat, my nose, my tongue, my head. It was anywhere I thought it was. I brushed my teeth, sucked on throat drops, chewed gum, drank water, nothing helped. By this time, I felt so sick that I kept a waste basket next to my bed just in case it was time to dry heave. My fingers hurt after writing for four hours straight, no break, my neck muscles strained to the maximum extent and the jaws were a totally different story. It is Tuesday today, my temples hurt so badly its almost difficult to close my mouth. As Monday progressed into normal life for everyone, I was in a miserable state of mind. My body was tired, my mind wasn’t, oh no, I was up by 7:30 getting my laundry ready to be washed. By 8 AM I had made an appointment to go into school and take two tests so I could finally finish a course. Half my laundry was done by 9:30 and by 10:30 I had passed the tests and was going home to clean. I cleaned all day, every thing. My bathroom ,my parents bathroom, living room, dusted everything, cleaned floors, and the vacuum seemed to be my second best friend, next to the BUBBLE. This proceeded until 5, and by 5:30 I was talking to my boyfriend. There was some sort of bad vibe going on with this conversation. Something wasn’t right, in my mind and his. He knew, last night he knew what the hell I was doing. He was disappointed and in my heart I couldn’t bring myself to tell him because I was ashamed. Ashamed I took a turn in my life that keeps me stuck for months at a time. In and out of treatment, group homes and Juvenile Detention Centers I should know what the hell I should do. But I don’t think twice, the drug stimulates something in my brain that says MORE MORE MORE and ignores the consequences. The night progressed and I started seeing things out in my backyard move that really, weren’t supposed to move! **Am I going crazy?** I need sleep, but I cant. I hurt my boyfriends feelings cuz everything he said, I took way wrong, almost like he was cutting into me, I assumed, I had a sharp tongue and my mission last night was to make him break down. Now what sane person, would sabotage the best relationship of her life? This is love and because I haven’t slept or eaten for a few days, I’am going to make my life even worse. He said, 'Go to bed, baby girl just get some sleep and holla at me in the morning' So by 11:30 I did just that. My body kept wanting to wake up, my neck hurt I couldn’t get comfortable and the meth breath was slowly fading and so was my high. I awoke this morning at 8:30 wishing I felt better, not just physically but emotionally and mentally. The past 48 hours have been a blur, my tongue was sharp and to this I almost lost a love I cannot lose. Not only that, but I can see myself sliding in two directions. If I go the way my body wants to go, its to hell....hitting that bubble non stop, everyday, not sleeping, not eating, not caring for anybody not even myself...the only thing I care for is that hit and I can get that, then life is good. The way my body wants to go is filled with heartache and pain, lies and deceit, doing anything for anybody just as long as I don’t feel the pain I feel today. The other path is a road of hope and love. Happiness that I can achieve if I just stay away from what I think is good for me, a false claim to happiness and life. Meth for me is like water for others, it quenches my thirst. To all the drugs I’ve ever done in my life, through it all I’ve had people to stick by me, who love me unconditionally....and after this trip, I see my dreams and goals, aspirations and ambition fade away, the self esteem I once worked my ass off for is fading like a light and that glow I once had in my eyes and face, is now gone, bare, gaunt and empty. Today I have to depend on myself, and only myself for my own life. I can admit that yeah, my name is K and I’m a drug addict/alcoholic.

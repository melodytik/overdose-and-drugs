作为一个简单的背景信息, 我目前在高中初三, 我在六点之前回家, 虽然我有工作 (通常是六点半夜), 这导致我购买阿德拉尔. 现在,我不是瓜,我做了我的家庭作业在Adderall之前, 毕竟,我确实打算在生物化学专业. 我花了很长时间才找到我惊喜的连接. 但最终,我找到了我以前认识的患有严重多动症的人, 他每天被处方330毫克的XR阿德罗尔, 我从他那里买了几个,但在第一次购买时只收到一个,因为他忘记了其他. 当天晚些时候我意识到这个囊变得粘着,我决定现在 (中午左右) 服用它. 在半小时到一个小时内, 整个房间似乎比平时更明亮. 我真的很喜欢这种感觉. 接下来是三小时的排练. 我开始真正感受到刺激的效果. 我的脑子开始跑步. 我觉得它能够做任何事, 这才是我真正能描述的唯一方式. 这一切都会有意义. 我不相信我曾经经历过. 我的身体很疲 (前几天晚上我也没有睡太多, 事实上,这让我想起了我的大脑一旦发生启示时会进入的状态. 那些想法是如此迅速,自然地发生, 在即兴游戏中我是一个神. 我能创造出最烂,最令人笑的剧情, 我还在一天中不断检查自己的脉, [Erowid的注意事项: 驾驶时喝醉,倒,或极度缺乏睡眠是危险和不负责任的, 因为它危害了其他人. 不要这样做! 我开始感到头痛. 我不确定这是因为压力还是因为下降, 但它不是太严重, 尽管如此,在5点左右开车回家还是很有趣. 我忘记我应该在路的右边开车, 因为我的大脑太忙于考虑其他问题, 甚至无法专注于开车. 幸运的是,我又学会了如何开车, 我每分钟都在说一百万个单词, 我喜欢我现在的状态, 但回想起来, 我认为这只是开始的, 我喜欢能够集中精力, 我在这个阶段唯一的负面经历是口, 我决定上网,做一些作业,和朋友聊天, 不管是星期五晚上. 在这里似乎没有太大的变化. 我有过几次美妙的对话 (还有一次深入的对话), 我真的很惊,我感到高兴. 我8个月的女朋友打电话给我, 告诉我我显然醉了, 我开始质疑我自己的心理思维方式, 我真的能听到一个声音在我的头告诉我我不需要她. 我开始担心. 无论如何我确实选择了她, 这将是我最后一次娱乐性吸毒. 我甚至答应从来没有再服用非处方药. 我不确定偏执和可能的精神病是否与药物有关, 现在我不能睡觉也不能吃饭. 我不想去, 而是我不想去, 我觉得即使我抓住一些东西吃, 尽管完全没有欲望, 我不会能够实际上吃, 我真的开始失去了连贯性. 我说话的每个人都认为我一定是醉了, 因为错误的打字和词的滥用, 我仍然喜欢这种感觉, 我只能坐下来担心我能用我的时间做什么. 我开始打哈欠, 意识到15小时后, 我去洗手间,在镜子里, 看到自己的表情, 我感到震惊. 我看到很多以前从未见过的东西. 由于某种原因, 我用手尽可能大张地开嘴, 虽然这看起来很奇怪, 我终于明白, 我只不过是我当时看到的骨头,肉和皮肤. 只是因为无法入睡而在早上6点半醒来, 从这里开始,事情真的开始下坡了. 我开始有精神崩. 我生活中的一切都在走向错误, 我不能做任何事情来阻止它. 我只是想再次感到快乐. 我不能再服用阿德拉尔, 因为我做了承诺, 我觉得我真的想死, 我从来没有经历过严重的抑郁或自杀念头, 因此这一定与毒品有关. 很多的偏执. 很多压力和失败的感觉. 下午3点,我现在的位置. 尽管过去100多个小时没有吃东西, 已使用的数据: 胺类药物 (6):一般 (1),抑郁症 (15),宿醉/几天后 (46),多日经验 (13),第一次使用 (2),各种 (28)

Jan 31, 2008. erowid.org/exp/47681 DOSE: 30 mg oral Amphetamines (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 151 lb As a little background information I am currently in my junior year of High School and am a Full IB Diploma Canidate and at the top of my class. Now with IB we are given easily 6 hours of homework a night, trying to suceed at homework when I don't get home until 6 while maintaining a job (6-midnight usually) it become extremely difficult to fit it in at all even with the inability to sleep. And that led to my purchase of Adderall. Now, I'm not stupid, I did my homework on Adderall before taking it, finding out everything I could. After all, I do plan on majoring in BioChemistry. It took me a while before I could find a hook-up to my great surprise. But eventually, I found someone with Severe ADHD that I had known prior and whom was prescribed 3 30mg XR adderall daily and he only took 2 per day. I bought a few off of him but only received one at the first purchase as he forgot the others. Later on in the day I realized this capsule was becoming sticky and I decided I should take it now (around noon). Within a half hour to and hour everything shifted just slightly. The entire room seemed to be brighter than usual. Everything seemed so much more vibrant and alive than it usually would and I really enjoyed the feeling I was having. Now I head to last period, and hour and a half of IB Theatre then followed by a 3 hour Rehearsal. By the time class has started and gotten underway fairly well I began to really feel the stimulant effects. My mind began racing. I felt as if it was capable of anything and had somehow been placed upon a higher level. That is the only way I can really describe it. Things would make sense. Soon after I began feeling another odd sensation that I don't beleive I have ever experienced prior. My body was physically tired (I hadn't slept much the few nights prior either nor eaten in 2 days) and I seemed exhausted, however my brain wasn't tired in the least. In fact it was reminescent of the state my brain goes into once an epiphany occurs. The thoughts came so quickly and so naturally that I was on a higher level. During Improv Games I was a God. I would be able to create the most brilliant and hillarious skits off of the top of my head and all around everything was making so much more sense than usual. I also continually checked my pulse throughout the day and I realized it was generally lower than normal. [Erowid Note: Driving while intoxicated, tripping, or extremely sleep deprived is dangerous and irresponsible because it endangers other people. Don't do it! ] About 4:30-5:00ish I began experiencing a headache. I'm not sure if this was from stress or from coming down, but it wasn't too severe so I payed it no mind. Driving home around 5:00 was quite fun however. I had forgotten that I was supposed to be driving on the right hand side of the road because my brain was too busy contemplating other issues to even concentrate on driving. Luckily, I figured out how to drive again and made it home safely. The entire time I'm home (the rest of the night) I can't stop speaking a million words per minute and my thoughts no longer become coherent to everyone else. I love the state I am in, however in retrospect I think it was actually just the beggining of coming down, and I love being able to concentrate so little and still grasping everything, as if it wasn't required for me to pay attention to anything and still understand it all. The only negative I experienced during this phase was strong cotton mouth and my mouth tasted horribly. At about 9 o'clock (I finally escape the 3 hour phone conversation I had just had) I decide I'll get online and do some homework and chat with friends and what not, despite it is a Friday night. Nothing seemed to change too much here. I had a few wonderful conversations (and a few deep ones) and one that I was coming up with the most pure and beautiful of thoughts I had ever heard before. I was truly amazing myself and I felt euphoric. 12:00 My Girlfriend of 8 months calls me and can tell I'm obviously inebrieated and flat out tells me I have to choose between her or drugs. I start questioning my own psychological mindset here, I could LITERALLY hear a voice in my head telling me that I didn't need her. She was taking away what happiness I had etc. and I began to become worried. However I did choose her and this will be the last drug I ever take recreationally. I even promised to never take another OTC med again, ever. I'm not certain if the paranoia and possible psychosis where drug-related or not but I am going to assume so. 1:00 AM Cotton mouth is still in effect and I can't sleep nor eat. It's not even so much the fact that I don't want to, it's me not wanting to to the extent of not being able to. I felt as if even if I where to grab something to eat, despite complete lack of desire, I wouldn't be able to actually eat it, that I was too full as it was. I begin really losing coherency here. Everyone I am talking to has the idea that I must be drunk because of the mistypings and misuse of words, like where and were confused and there and their confused etc. and I would tell a 5 minute story over the period of an hour due to all of the tangents I would go off on. I still loved the feeling and never wanted it to end. 5:00 AM None of my friends are any longer awake and I have nothing to do but sit and worry about what I could be doing with my time. I then take a yawn and realize that finally, 15 hours later, I might be able to catch some sleep. I go to the restroom and in the mirror I shock myself by the look on my face. I see so much wear that was never there before. For some reason I open my mouth as wide as I can with my hands and I realize I am human. Although this may seem odd, I finally understood that I am nothing more than the bones, flesh, and skin that I saw right then. Only to be awoken at the hour of 6:30 AM from inability to sleep but still completely full of stamina and energy. Things really go downhill from here. Between 10:00 AM and 1:00 PM I begin to have a literal break-down. Everything in my life is going wrong and I can't do anything to stop it. I just want to feel happy again. I can't take another Adderall because of the promise I had made and I felt like literally dying, not that I was going to, but that it would be nice to die, and I knew I couldn't. On this note I should mention I am always easy-going and generally pleasant and have never gone through serious depression before nor suicidal thoughts and therefore it must have been drug-related. Loads of paranoia. Loads and loads of stress and feelings of failure at life. 3:00 PM This is where I am now. I am just now writing this experience and I am JUST now starting to feel somewhat normal again and have an inkling of an appetite despite lack of food for the past 100+ hours.

我一直在互联网上寻找不同的联系人, 现在看来,我的毒品经历并不是很惊人的. 我不经常吸毒, 只是发现这个话题非常有趣. 我是某个信息板/聊天室的成员, 我遇到了一位欧洲化学家, 他曾经合成了一种我从未听说过的化合物. 我们开始私下聊天, 不同于公共场合, 他表示这种化学物质与MDMA类似, 但效果持续时间较长, 没有崩. 我询问购买情况,我们最终达成协议,允许我得到一个样本 (500毫克) 的化合物. 告诉我大约50毫克是个不错的剂量, 不是帕特或基诺的, 但一个不太知名 (在我看来,更好的) 在南街叫吉姆的地方. 现在,我已经有了足够的时间. 我点了一份奶酪牛排,然后和朋友们一起上楼坐下. 这时,该化合物的特殊性变得明显. 结果非常温和. 没有什么会让我头痛, 但非常高兴和好. 感觉比MDMA清洁得多, 我成为一个很好的人. 我没有在社交场合常常感到的紧张. 我告诉故事在一个更好和更有趣的时尚比以往任何时候. 对于我来说这很奇怪, 这不是MDMA给人的"我爱每个人"的全力推动, 如果我能用一句话来描述我的心态, 离开吉姆的,我回家,我们公司的两个离开点在其他地方,与三个朋友. 我妹妹在玩"泽尔达:风之", 评论这款游戏有多"可爱". 接着我们一起讲述了她如何用刀子刺伤我哥哥的眼睛, (这个故事比表面上听起来有趣得多,我保证. 很快,所有人都走了. 我在T+6小时睡着了, 有轻微的视觉现象直到我失去意识. 总的来说,我不得不把IAP列为我做过的最好的药物之一, 其他: IAP (280):多种 (28),第一次 (2)

Mr. Nice Guy IAP by FN Citation:   FN. "Mr. Nice Guy: An Experience with IAP (exp24668)". Erowid.org . Jul 10, 2003. erowid.org/exp/24668 DOSE: 50 mg oral IAP (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 185 lb I have, for a while, been scouring the internet for different contacts in my endless search for knowledge and experience with research chemicals. As it stands, my drug experiences are not spectacular. I don't really do drugs that often, I just happen to find the subject incredibly interesting. I am a member of a certain message board/chat room that happens to deal exclusively with information regarding various research chemicals. While chatting, I came into contact with a European chemist who happened to have synthesized a somewhat large amount of a compound I had never heard of. We moved off into private chat, where, unlike in public, discussion of less than legal activities was not prohibited. He represented the chemical as being in the vein of MDMA, but with a longer duration of effects and no crash. I inquired about purchase, and we eventually worked out a deal that allowed me to get a sample (500 mg) of the compound sent to me. As I was told that about 50 mg would be a nice dose, I ingested that amount, letting all present know what was going on, and went with my friends to a cheesesteak place in downtown philadelphia. Not Pat's or Geno's, but a less known (and in my opinion, better) place on South Street called Jim's. When we walked in, the effects had hit me fairly hard. I ordered a cheesesteak and went to the upstairs seating with the my friends. This is when the rather peculiar character of the compound became noticeable. The effects were very mild. Nothing that would bonk me on the head, but very euphoric and nice. Much cleaner feeling than MDMA, with far less jaw clenching and no real speed push to it. Under the influence of this substance, however, I became a really nice and entertaining guy. My wit was expanded, and there was none of the usuall nervousness I feel in social situations. My trademark calousness was not at all present, and I told stories in a better and more entertaining fashion then ever before. This is strange for me, as I am usually a bit introverted, and unintentionally (or intentionally) mean to many. This was not the full force 'I love everyone' push that MDMA gives, which is why it took me off guard. If I could use a sentence to describe my state of mind, it would have to be 'It's all good.' Leaving Jim's, I went home, two of our company departing for points elsewhere, with three friends. My sister was playing Zelda: The Wind Waker, and commenting on how 'cute' the game was. In tandem, we then told the story of how she threw a knife at my brother's eye, to hilarious uproars from everyone else. (That story is a lot funnier than it sounds on the surface, I promise.) A short while later, everyone left. I fell asleep at about T+6 hours, with mild visuals present until I lost conciousness. Overall, I'd have to rank IAP up there as one of the best drugs I've ever done, and if I could somehow procure some more, I would, gladly, scoop it up.

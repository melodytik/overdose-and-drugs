May 12, 2015. erowid.org/exp/104851 DOSE: alpha-PHP I have been using this chem for a while, as have many others. Due to a lack of knowledge, the decision to collect and research this chemical became quite appealing. The dosages are comparable to the previous a-PVP; yet due to its addictive nature, redosing makes it hard to set a solid dosage. Around the 30-40mg mark seems to be solid for people who are not habitual stimulant users. The consensus among those who have tried similar drugs (MDPV/a-PVP) is that a-PHP is much less straining on the body, with a cleaner high; albeit a less euphoric one. The tachycardia and paranoia has been reduced by quite a bit, at least when compared to a-PVP. Bellringers have been achieved through vaporization; although at least for me, this was not done by one single massive dose, rather it seemed the accumulation of repetitive redosing. Friends who never tried MDPV or a-PVP say its very similar to coke, and through researching past analogues and other similar stimulants, it wouldn't be that far fetched to state that this chem is also an NDRI. Vomiting has occurred to some people, although it was mainly to people who had not done a stimulant in a while, which from past stories also occurred with a-PVP. Its lacking the sweet taste that the original a-PVP had, and the technique involved with the vaporization ROA is a bit complex, which when done wrong releases a very unpleasant taste. When vaporized correctly, the taste isn't bad at all. I have tested some ROAs: sublingual, intranasal, and vaporization. I have also hotrailed, and smoked some in a joint (friend wanted to try). Intranasal seems to be a solid roa, with minimal burn, and less desire to redose. It also lasts a while, internet and personal findings seem to be around 2-8 hours, although for most the euphoria is usually dwindling down around the two hour mark; vaporization probably an hour, as re dosing and lack of weighing out the dosage or timing have not been made yet; partly do to tolerance and other drugs making it irrelevant. The remaining hours are more likely after effects; lingering stimulation, albeit without the euphoria. It is an addicting drug, although it seems to be easier to put down than past iterations. It's a nice chem, not as hard hitting, both euphoria and comedown wise. A more refined a-PVP, although that’s only an opinion, as real research has yet to be done on this chem.

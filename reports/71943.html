我很少吸, 之前只有5次. 我不是毒品专家, 我只是阅读了大量的信息, 我从朋友和互联网上听说, 达米安娜和猫可以诱发一种有趣的"合法高", 我在50克的袋子里找到了大米安娜,猫草和其他草药, 每袋约3.50加元. 这真是太棒了! 我跑回家,试着用大米亚娜茶, 却没有什么感觉. 事实上,我第一次使用这种药物时, 感觉不太好. 我昨天的最后一次尝试却完全不同. 我认为第一次我真的没有使用足够的草药来做茶. 我父母有一个小的1杯铁茶, 顶部有一个片, 我决定试试用一匙大米和一匙猫. 互联网上关于这两种药物的剂量信息非常有限, 这比我第一次使用的要多得多. 我还把它浸在沸水中, 整整10分钟. 我认为, 溶解叶子中的药用物质的时间越长, 我第一次泡茶只有3分钟. 我在茶中用了一杯沸水. 后来我加入了一些蜂蜜, 喝下来很快. 这并不是一个不好吃的组合, 所以我喝了,我在客厅,所以我决定玩个小小的钢琴. 经过15分钟左右的时间, 我感觉到我的身体有点麻木,我的腿和手臂感觉更加重, 我下楼试着在电脑上听一些音乐, 这是一个美丽的,清晰的晚上,鸟儿唱歌,所有的好东西. 我只是完全放松了. 让我澄清一下, 这不是同样高草, 这不是一个迷幻, 你不会好奇的狗, 你不会所有或任何东西. 这就像平静地忘记所有的压力. 我喜欢, 有时我很容易压力 (比如现在考试时间). 整个峰值持续了大约45分钟. 后来我又喝了一杯同样的剂量,只是为了玩, 我觉得很累,所以决定早睡,比平常早上一个小时睡觉. 没有时间睡觉. 我认为这正是达米安娜的特点. 这种饮料是一种强大的梦想增强剂. 整个晚上, 我做了生动的梦, 这就像在电视上翻频道, 我醒了,在醒来和打字30分钟后, 我仍然记得一些梦. 我绝对会推给那些想放松或整夜做梦的人. 显然要适度,看看我的体重, 最好的部分是,这一切都是合法的! 我的父母发现了茶,我只是说这是合法的,比大麻更好. 他们不能争辩,所以我甚至让他们吃了一些哈哈! 如果有人感兴趣的话, 这是一个很好的地方. 让我们来看看. - 一个茶匙. 达米安娜的叶子,干燥. - 一个茶匙. 猫草的叶子,干燥 一杯沸水 把叶子放在一个茶球或类似的. 确保茶叶有足够的空间漂浮. 我认为一个小的茶球也不会很好. 用液体蜂蜜加甜. 这是一个很好的地方,我们可以看到它. 2011年8月7日发布 达米安娜 (107),猫 (68):单独 (16),准备/食谱 (30),第一次 (2),一般 (1)

An Excellent Relaxation and Dreamscape Damiana & Catnip by the_bassist Citation:   the_bassist. "An Excellent Relaxation and Dreamscape: An Experience with Damiana & Catnip (exp71943)". Erowid.org . Aug 7, 2011. erowid.org/exp/71943 DOSE: 2 Tbsp oral Damiana (tea) 2 Tbsp oral Catnip (tea) BODY WEIGHT: 129 lb I will start by saying that I have smoked pot very infrequently, only about 5 times previously. I am no expert on drugs, I just read a ton of information and have a stoner friend who is a wealth of info. I heard from my friend and on the internet that Damiana and Catnip can induce an interesting 'legal high' which I was curious to look into. I went to my local health food store and sure enough, they have a huge section with big ziploc bags full of these herbs. I found the Damiana and Catnip and other herbs in bags of 50g for about $3.50 CAD a bag. What a deal! I ran home and tried making some of the tea out of the damiana and I did not really feel anything. In fact the first two times I was playing around with the stuff I felt little effects. However my last try yesterday was a completely different story. I think the first times I hadn't really used enough of the herbs to make the tea. My parents have a small 1-cup iron teapot with a sieve piece that fits in the top and the leaves just soak through the sieve. It was a pretty large screen so I decided I would try 1 Tbsp of Damiana and mix it with 1 Tbsp of catnip. There is very limited information on the internet about the dosages of these two so I decided I would benchmark it here. This is a lot more than I used the first time. Also, I steeped this in rapid-boiling water for 10 entire minutes. I figured the longer it had to dissolve the actual medicinal properties from the leaves the more effective. I only steeped the tea for about 3 minutes the first time. I used 1 cup of boiling water in the tea. After this I added just a bit of honey to the mix and drank it down relatively fast. It's not a bad-tasting combination, it's actually quite subtle. So I drank that down and my mom was in the living room so I decided just to fool around playing piano a little bit. After about 15 minutes I stood up and I knew it had hit me. I was feeling slightly numb all over, my legs and arms were feeling heavier and I just felt generally relaxed. I went downstairs and tried listening to some tunes on the computer but it was the wrong setting so I went outside and sat on my deck. It was a beautiful, clear evening out, birds singing, all the good stuff. I was just completely relaxed. Let me just clarify this is not an identical high to weed, it's not a psychadelic, you won't be inquisitive of shit and you won't be all giddy or anything. This is like just being calm and forgetting about all your stresses. I love it, I tend to stress easily at times (like exam time right now). The whole peak lasted about 45 minutes. I had another mug with the same dosage afterwards just for kicks but it didn't seem as strong, probably because I was still feeling the effects of the last dosage. As I came down I became sleepy so I decided to call it an early night and went to bed about an hour earlier than normal. It took no time to sleep. This is where the second part of the drink stands out.(I believe it's the Damiana in particular). This drink is a powerful dream enhancer. All night, I had vivid dreams, like little bits of big dreams. It was like flipping through the channels on TV and it was VERY entertaining. I woke up, and 30 minutes after waking up and typing this I can still remember a few of the dreams. I would definitely reccommend this to someone who feels like relaxing or having a neat dreamscape all night. Obviously take things into moderation, look at my body weight and estimate that with the dosage I took. The best part is, this is all legal! My parents found the teas and I just said it's legal and it's better than pot. They couldn't argue so I even let them have some haha! Here's the recipe I used if anyone is interested: ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ -1 Tbsp. Damiana leaves, dried. -1 Tbsp. Catnip leaves, dried. -1 cup boiling water Place the leaves in a tea ball or similar. Make sure the tea leaves have room to float around a bit. I think a small teaball will not work as well. Steep 10 minutes and sweeten with liquid honey to taste. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Exp Year: 2008 ExpID: 71943 Gender: Male Age at time of experience: Not Given Published: Aug 7, 2011 Views: Not Supported [ View PDF (to print) ] [ View LaTeX (for geeks) ] [ Swap Dark/Light ] Damiana (107), Catnip (68) : Alone (16), Preparation / Recipes (30), First Times (2), General (1) COPYRIGHTS: All reports copyright Erowid.

I had been planning this trip for a couple of weeks, but the decision to trip last night was made just earlier that day. I had tripped on DXM one week before, and 4-HO-MET two weeks before, so it was quite soon to be trying something psychedelic again. But, from everything I had researched, I was just barely in the clear to have a solid trip again with no significant tolerance. Because of how uncommon this substance is, I had a hard time knowing exactly how potent my product was, and how much to take. I decided to start with one tablet, and if after 30-45 minutes I wasn’t feeling what I wanted to feel, I would take more. At 10:45 pm, I took the first tablet and sat down next to my open window, enjoying the ambience of the light rain that was coming down. I sat in relative silence, and closed my eyes in an attempt to meditate. I wanted to be as calm and as grounded as possible before things started to get weird. My intention was simply to see what this substance had to show me, so I was open to just about anything. And soon enough, I started to feel it. It began with an electric current running through my body. My head felt lighter, and my feet felt like they were discharging this current into the floor. I stood up and walked around my room for a second, looking for any visual distortions. Nothing quite yet, but I was definitely starting to feel something. I grabbed my room's throw blanket and wrapped it around myself, as I fought through some light anxiety and shivers. It was mostly a halo around certain objects, and a shimmering, shifting effect. I decided that it wasn’t quite strong enough, so I grabbed the container again and took another half of a tablet. I put on some music at this point, and then decided to take the other half of the tablet, which brought me to a total of 2 tablets or 16mg of 4-PrO-DMT. The album I had picked out for this first leg of the trip was “I Robot” by The Alan Parsons Project. I had found this album recently and had enjoyed a couple of full listen-throughs sober. I began seeing more movement and breathing in the floor, ceiling, and walls. I was starting to see faces in certain patterned objects, and I went out into my kitchen to try the pineapple that I had prepared earlier.

In the Torus Field for 10,000 Years MXIPr, 2-Fluorodeschloroketamine, Beer & DMT by Didgital Citation:   Didgital. "In the Torus Field for 10,000 Years: An Experience with MXIPr, 2-Fluorodeschloroketamine, Beer & DMT (exp115661)". Erowid.org . Sep 1, 2021. erowid.org/exp/115661 DOSE: T+ 0:00 repeated insufflated 2-Fluorodeschloroketamine (powder / crystals) T+ 0:00 repeated oral Alcohol - Beer/Wine T+ 2:00 50 mg insufflated MXiPr (powder / crystals) T+ 0:00 vaporized DMT BODY WEIGHT: 145 lb This combination was by far one of the most psychedelic experiences of my life. It all started when a buddy of mine called me, very emotional as he had just broken up with his girlfriend. He said he wanted to get high and asked me if I had any Ketamine. "Sorta" I replied. "Come over". +0:00 He brought a 6 pack of beers with him, and I explained to him I had 2-Fluoro-Deschloroketamine, where the chlorine atom was replaced with a fluorine atom. Since I knew he was in bad state, and I didn't want him in a hole in my living room, we did small bumps over a few hours, probably totaling 150mg each. We'd also drank the 6 pack. We were wobbly, but we never got too far out, which was my plan. I walked his wobbly butt to his house, and wobbled back to my own. +2:00 I got home, and for some reason, I just had this urge to go deeper. Alas my 2F-DCK was all gone. But there was that 50mg MXIPr sample someone had given me. I knew 50mg was a pretty hefty dose, but I also didn't want 2 underwhelming trips with it. My friend had told me to eat it, but I I snorted it. +2:15 I could feel reality getting really really weird and I went to lay on my Yoga mat. I brought my DMT vape cart with. +????? I can't tell you how I got there, but I can describe where I was and how I returned. I basically became aware that I was a single point of consciousness In the center of a massive toroid structure. With trillions of colored lines that were constantly moving. I could see in all directions simultaneously. I spent easily 10,000 years in a state of complete nirvana before remembering a fragment of my original existence. As soon as I had a memory of it, I started to slide down one of the millions of colored lines of the toroid. As I moved out of the center of the toroid, I watched all the other lines moving in a different way and they started turning into these waveforms and then into water. Which I realized was the ocean. I could hear it as well. Then I remembered someone I love (Ally!), and then I remembered who I was, and that I can control my body with my mind so I opened my eyes to find myself in my little house 1000s of miles away. I've never had such an experience and I've had several breakthrough experiences with 5-MeO-DMT. I feel like I left a part of myself in that place, so I hopefully can go back. I almost felt like I was a different person, than the one who entered the field. I don't recall actually hitting the DMT vape pen, but it was empty, and it wasn't empty before so I must have hit the DMT at the peak of my MXIPr. I called my friend who is a psychedelic therapist, I was freaking out telling him that I don't want to remain on earth anymore and that I discovered the true universe. He told me I was probably undergoing "Disassociative Induced Psychosis" and that I should calm down, and that everything is going to be ok, and that I'll be the same person I was in a few days. Well it took a few days but he was right. I eventually became my normal self. However the first 2 days after, I would practice half ass holotropic breathing, and I could feel reality escaping me, but I would stop every time, fearful that if I made it back to that space, I would make the choice not to return. Many months have passed and I still think about that space, but I'm pretty sure I have a few more things to experience before I finally die and go back there.

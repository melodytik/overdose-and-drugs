这是两个不同的实验的结合. 一个在另一个晚上完成. 这两种药的剂量都相同. 2 克的 花 10克的 4个相当大的 (大麻) 在之前45分钟就摄入了. 在最后一分钟, 落入牛肉中. 在后一个半小时后, 我开始感觉到在一个小时后开始起作用. 在吸食大麻之前, 效果很温和 (大麻对我来说总是增强原体). 在那之后,一切变得非常迷幻. 任何地方都形成了色彩, 形成一个巨大的螺旋 (包含整个宇宙的螺旋). 我在第一天晚上有更多的视觉. 在第二天晚上是我突破: 我闭上眼睛,开始集中精力. 忽然,我不再在自己的身体里. 我没有在地球上. 我已经进入了灵界. 那里的黑暗太大,我看不到任何东西,或者我看不到. 我只能感觉到远处的另一个灵魂 (我进入灵界的目的是与亲人联系). 我觉得它 (灵魂) 没有任何颜色, 随着我接近它, 我开始感受到爱. 到了那里, 我便融入其中. 我们不再是两个单独的灵魂, 我们都是一个. 这种感觉是如此强烈. 这是一种纯粹的爱情. 这是我有史以来最好的感觉. 无法形容这是一种多么美好的感觉. 我也注意到我和我身边的另一个灵魂没有任何想法. 我们是纯能量. 没有任何想法,只是一种能量 (爱) 的感觉. 不幸的是,这并没有持续很长时间 (不超过30秒). 接下来我知道,我回到我的身体. 我也能达到一种更深层次的意识. 在这次经历之前, 现在我知道这要比这更深. 在这种更深层次的意识中, 我被显示出了我心灵的更大一部分. 但我也意识到还有更多. 我不确定这种是否能让我深入. 也许与的结合将会有所作为. 时间表: 82 叙利亚 (45), - G. spectabilis (118):未知背景 (20), 组合 (3), 一般 (1)

Sep 10, 2000. erowid.org/exp/82 DOSE: T+ 0:00 2.0 g oral Syrian Rue T+ 0:45 10 g oral Mushrooms - G. spectabilis This is a combination of two separate experiments. One done one night before the other. Both with the same dosage. 2 grams of Peganum harmala 10 grams of Gymnopilus spectabilis 4 decent size roaches (marijuana) The Peganum h. was ingested 45 minutes before the mushrooms. The mushrooms were crushed as much as possible, and then at the last minute, dropped into beef stew. The roaches were smoked one and a half hours after the mushrooms. I began to feel the mushrooms taking effect after about an hour. The effects were mild, until the weed was smoked (marijuana always enhances entheogens for me). After that, everything became very psychedelic. There were colors forming everywere, and the patterns could definitely be noticed. If I concentrated enough, I could put all the patterns together, to form one inmense spiral (the spiral that contains the whole universe in it). I had more visuals on the first night. On the second night was when I broke through: I closed my eyes and began to concentrate. All the sudden I was no longer in my body. I was not anywere on Earth. I had entered the spirit realm. It was either too dark to see anything, or I just couldn't see. I could only feel another spirit in the distance (the purpose of me going into the spirit realm, was to make contact with a loved one). I could not feel that it (the spirit) had any color to it, it was very transparant tho. As I approached it, I began to feel love. When I reached it, I merged with it. We were no longer two separate spirits, we were both one. The feeling was so intence. It was a feeling of pure love. It was the best feeling I have ever felt. Words can't ever describe how good it felt. I also noticed that I, or the other spirit I was with didn't have any thoughts. We were pure energy. There was no thoughts, it was all just a feeling of energy (love). Unfortunately this didn't last very long (not longer than thirty seconds). The next thing I knew, I was back in my body. I was also able to reach a deeper consiousness. Before this experience, I believed that there were only three. Now I know it goes much deeper than that. In this deeper consciousness I was shown a much larger part of my mind. But I also realized that there is much more. I am not sure if this mushroom will allow me to go deeper. Perhaps a combination with Salvia will do the trick.

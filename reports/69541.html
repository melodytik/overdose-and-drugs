I have tried over forty five psychoactive substances and hope to find many more. Benzodiazepines are a new interest of mine. I love the relaxation they bring and the way they can turn any amount of stress to a sudden, calming stop. Until my Dalmane experience I have only tried 3 of these wonderful drugs. Xanax, Valium and Klonopin; I loved all 3. The Story My Girlfriend, A, and I just got home from work. My girl was suffering from bad cramps and went to her dad for help. I was sitting on the couch waiting for her to return. She came back with some kind of prescription bottle. I asked what she got and she said she didn't know. I looked at the bottle and read it read '30mg FLURAZEPAM Generic for: Dalmane'. I didn't know what this was but was sure almost positive it was a Benzo, hence the 'pam' ending. It was, I was syched out. I opened the bottle and there was only one. I wasn't about to take the last pill when my girl needed it, even if it was super rare. I got lucky, she said she would take a flexiril instead and that I could have it. Man I was happy. Shortly before I took it without hesitation my best friend came over, B. I popped the pill, chewing the capsule to break it open. I am with people I love and in the comfort of my Girls house, basically home. No other drugs. 0:15 I start to feel lowered inhibitions already. 0:30 Still just lowered inhibitions, slight stoney body high 1:00 Starting to stumble mildly, feeling totally relaxed, strong body high. I have trouble speaking clearly. 1:30 Really high, stumbling alot. I feel great but I have to go home. B gives me a ride and we chill in my room for a while. He says that I look and am acting really fucked up. 2:00 After trying to play Vampire Night on the PS2, I couldn't control it for shit, B takes off. I am still really high and now extremly tired. I feel extremely stress free and tranquil. I start to watch TV. 7:00 I wake up with my TV playing commercials, I feel so great still. Not high but good. This sense of well being stayed for the entire day. Looking back I think my dose was a little high. I hope this will give a somewhat good overview my Flurazepam experience. If I came across this rare drug again I would like to find a 15mg capsule and hopefully not pass out to feel the whole experience through. Peace and be safe all.

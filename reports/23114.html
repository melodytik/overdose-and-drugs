GABA (氨酸) 是一个广泛研究的,虽然不太了解的神经递质,主要在大脑深处的边缘系统中发现. 科学家说它是一种"抑制性"化学物质, 药物包括酒精,二 (Xanax,Valium) 和巴比酸盐 (Phenobarbital,Sodium Pentothal) 以不同的方式显著增强这种传递器. 无论如何,我们稍后再谈科学. 现在,我会描述GABA本身的一些实验. GABA作为食补充剂销售,主要是作为性能增强剂. 虽然GABA是一种抑制剂,但它刺激人体生长激素 (HGH) 的产生. 坦率地说,我认为服用GABA药片是没有用处的. 他们不会让我高, 他们可能不会让我更大. 这种无效的原因是它被消化道广泛分解,而未被分解的东西难以穿越血脑屏障. 我想知道GABA是否有任何娱乐或治疗价值通过其他途径的管理, 因为似乎更直接的途径如上皮吸收 (这是和Salvia quids工作的途径) 可能产生可测量的效果. 我在不同的条件下进行了几次实验. 我试过的药物注射方法是:吸气 (吸气),下舌吸收 (我刚才描述的),以及吸入蒸发的GABA. 我做了这三项实验, 完全清醒, 结合酒精, 和结合阿尔普拉佐兰 (Xanax). 每个单独的实验都在不同的日期进行. 我必须强调, 我在药理学,神经生物学和生物化学方面有着广泛的培训 (也就是说, 尽管如此, 以下是发生的事情. 材料:PURE实验室反应剂等级GABA. 这不是你在GNC买的, 它含有其他化学物质,缓冲剂和结合剂. 没有人知道. 吸气: 4 条线,每条的重量为 175 mg,被吸入. 相对较小的不适, 和一个独特但温和的味道. 很小的效果, 但一个明显的愿望去睡觉出现. 蒸发: 1000 mg 被蒸发并吸入. 烟雾非常温和. 没有发现任何副作用. 最有可能的是,GABA被热量破坏了, 需要注意的是,蒸发与阿尔普拉佐兰或酒精并无任何影响. 语言下: 1000 mg 被放置在舌头下. 五分钟后,整个剂量已经被吸收. 大约两分钟后, 一种非常明显的昏迷发展, 这次测试重复了三次 (不同日期), 每次我都会感觉到同样的感觉. 含有1.5毫克的阿尔普拉佐兰 吸入:在吸入另一个500mg的GABA前60分钟,空腹服用了1.5mg的阿尔普拉佐拉姆 (Xanax). 我注意到协调有进一步的障碍 (虽然没有什么戏剧性的). 这似乎在25分钟内消失了. 我记忆力有缺陷, 后悔没有记笔记本. 我清楚地回忆起一个强烈的头和镇静的感觉之前全克已经吸收, 这持续了大约15分钟 (我认为) 含有2.4司的酒精 在空腹中喝了4个1. 5司的Skyy伏特加 (见"科学可以很有趣") 30分钟前. 500毫克似乎有点影响了我的协调, 持续了大约10分钟. 在吸收后非常明显的效果. 我感觉有些头, 协调也很不正常, 简而言之,我觉得我又喝了四杯伏特加. 结论: 我认为吸食GABA是无用的. 另外,我认为吸入它只会产生边际效应. 然而, 通过舌下吸收, 虽然这些效应 (虽然很明显) 是可以忽略的. 最强烈的效应来自于Xanax和酒精. 让我们再看一下科学, 我认为你至少对突触传递 (神经与神经之间发生的化学交流) 有所了解. GABA 结合的受体蛋白可以被化. 这意味着像卡纳克斯 (Xanax),托巴比他 (Pentobarbital) 和酒精 (Alcohol) 这样的药物与GABA结合于同一个受体, 想象一下GABA受体是个关闭的门. 我们可能会说, 药物解锁了门锁, 我们不能打开门只是通过解锁死锁而不是主锁,反之亦然. 如果没有GABA, 在没有全激动剂的情况下,GABA的活性仅轻微. 两者结合起来, 打开大门. 这可能部分解释了我在清醒时经历的GABA相对缺乏精神活性. 结合起来,GABA和其他药物协同作用, 显著增强了我已经感受到的效果. 我指的是GABA蛋白质的开放. 这允许原子进入神经, 这实际上是我们所说的药物影响下减慢你的速度. 简而言之,GABA确实, 缺乏更好的方式来说, 我怀疑我很快就会把更多的GABA放进我的身体, 因为它不值得麻烦, 我还没有看到任何关于GABA的研究. 一个明智的经验规则是远离, 尤其是考虑到大多数人可以接触到的GABA是不纯净的. 这份报告仅用于信息和教育目的. Sublingual, Vaporised and Insufflated GABA by Blue skies Citation:   Blue skies. "Sublingual, Vaporised and Insufflated: An Experience with GABA (exp23114)". Erowid.org . Feb 6, 2006. erowid.org/exp/23114 DOSE: 500 mg insufflated GABA (powder / crystals) 1000 mg sublingual GABA (powder / crystals) 1000 mg smoked GABA (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 170 lb GABA (gamma-amino-butyric acid) is an extensively studied, though poorly understood neurotransmitter found primarily in the limbic system deep inside the brain. Scientists say that it is an 'inhibitory' chemical, which essentially means it can act as a Central Nervous System Depressent. Drugs including alcohol, benzodiazepines (Xanax, Valium), and barbiturates (Phenobarbital, Sodium Pentothal) dramatically potentiate this transmitter in different ways. In any case, we'll come back to science later. For now, I'll describe some experiments with GABA itself. GABA is sold as a dietary supplement, mainly as a performance enhancer. Although GABA is inhibitory, it stimulates the production of HGH, or Human Growth Hormone. Frankly, I think it's quite useless to take GABA tablets for any reason. They won't get me high, and they probably don't make me any bigger. The reason for this ineffectiveness is that it is extensively broken down by the GI tract, and that which is not broken down has difficulty crossing the blood/brain barrier. After thinking about this, I wanted to find out if GABA has any recreational or therapeutic value by other routes of administration, as it would seem that a more direct route like epithelial absorption (this is route by which dip and Salvia quids work) may yield measurable effects. I conducted several experiments under different conditions. The methods of administration I tried were: insufflation (snorting), sublingual absorption (what I just described), and inhalation of vaporized GABA. I did all three experiments completely sober, in combination with alcohol, and in combination with alprazolam (Xanax). Each individual experiment was performed on a different day. Before I continue, I must emphasize that I have extensive training in Pharmacology, Neurobiology, and Biochemistry (ie, working on graduate degrees) and it was still quite stupid for me to use myself as a Guinea Pig. That being said, here's what happened. Material: PURE Laboratory Reagent Grade GABA. This is NOT what you buy at GNC, which contains other chemicals, buffers, and binders. SOBER: Insufflation: 4 lines, each weighing out to 175mg, were snorted. Relatively little discomfort, and a distinct but mild taste. Very negligible effects, but a distinct desire to go to bed emerged. Vaporization: 1000mg were vaporized and inhaled. The smoke was very mild. No effects whatsoever were noted. Most likely, the GABA was destroyed by the heat, but I'd have to do Gas Chromatography to confirm this. Note that vaporization had no effects in combination with Alprazolam or alcohol either. Sublingual: 1000mg were placed under the tongue. After 5 minutes, the entire amount had been absorbed. Roughly two minutes later, a very pronounced lethargy developed along with disturbances in balance in coordination. This test was repeated 3 times (on different days), and each time I felt the same thing. WITH 1.5 MG OF ALPRAZOLAM Insufflated: 1.5 mg of alprazolam (Xanax) had been taken on an empty stomach 60 minutes prior to blowing another 500mg of GABA. After this was done, I noticed further disturbances in coordination (though nothing dramatic). This seemed to subside in 25 minutes. Sublingual: I have lapses in memory and regret not keeping a notebook. I distinctly recall a strong feeling of dizziness and sedation before the full gram had been absorbed, and this continued for about 15 minutes (I think) before I decided to call it a night and go to bed. WITH 2.4 OZ OF ALCOHOL Insufflated: 4 1.5oz shots of Skyy vodka were consumed on an empty stomach (see, science CAN be fun) 30 minutes prior. After which time, 500mg seemed to upset my coordination a little bit, much like it did on the Xanax. Lasted about 10 minutes. Sublingual: VERY pronounced effects after absorption. Extreme feelings of vertigo, coordination was quite disturbed, and I felt a little nausea. In short, it felt like I had taken another 4 shots of Vodka. CONCLUSIONS: I believe smoking GABA is useless. Furthermore, I think snorting it had only marginal effects. Sublingual absorption, however, really seemed to make a big difference. I will say, though, while sober, these effects (though quite distinct) were pretty negligible. The strongest effects came with Xanax and alcohol. Let's look at a little more science, as I think it puts this in perspective. I'll assume that you're at least vaguely familiar with synaptic transmission (the chemical communication that occurs from nerve to nerve). The receptor protein to which GABA binds can be allosterically agonized. In English, this means that drugs like Xanax, Pentobarbital, and Alcohol bind to the same receptor that GABA does, but they bind to different places on that same receptor. Think of the GABA receptor as a dead-bolted door. We might say that Xanax unlocks the deadbolt, and GABA unlocks the lock on the doorknob. We can't open the door just by unlocking the deadbolt and not the main lock, and vice versa. Xanax, alcohol, and barbiturates are all useless in the absense of GABA. GABA in the absense of allosteric agonists is only mildly active. A combination of the two swings the door wide open. This may partially explain the relative lack of psychoactive effects I experienced with GABA while sober. In combination, GABA and the other drugs worked synergistically to considerably potentiate the effects I was already feeling. When I say the door swings open, I mean the GABA proteins open. This allows Chlorine atoms to pass into the nerve, and this is actually what slows you down under the influence of the drugs we've talked about. In short, GABA definitely, for lack of a better way to put it, makes me drunker, but only under very specific circumstances. I doubt I'll be putting any more GABA into my body soon as it's not worth the trouble, and I've seen NO studies that indicate doing so is safe. Hell, I haven't seen any human studies on GABA administered in these ways. A smart rule of thumb is to stay away, especially considering the GABA most people have access to is impure.

What happened after that was a prime example of irresponsible drug use. A 7 day binge that involved eyeballing doses of powerful chemicals, dosing at work, and just reckless drug use in general. I don’t recommend anyone trying this, but since these are relatively new chemicals I thought I would write up a report. It all started with the diphenidine. I call it the crack of dissociates. It is super fiendish, and was the only one I touched for the first 4 days. I shared a little with 3 friends, we can call them L, B, and A (maybe 200mg) but I smoked the other 800mg or so myself. Not really smoking but vaping, I used an oil burner, and just vaped it off of foil. I just kept smoking it from the time I got off work at 10 a.m. Until the time I went to bed at around 10p.m. It makes me feel super numb and fucked up almost like a speed ball with DXM, but I can walk and talk just fine, unless I smoked enough to go into a hole. I did it one time, and I couldn't see anything just hear weird sounds, and felt like I was spinning in circles and flying upwards really really fast, I decided I was either dead or had went completely insane. I would watch movies and TV on it most of the time. I could watch a movie I had seen 1000 times and it would seem like something completely new and magical. Fear and Loathing in Las Vegas was absolutely amazing, and so was Futurama. At the end of day 4 I ran out of the diphinine and tried the DCK. I took about 25mg and it felt a lot like DXM, but way more euphoric, clear headed and psychedelic. I decided to snort a tiny bump of the 3-meo-pce (8-10mg) about 2 hours in and shit got weird. I laid down, closed my eyes and ended up in this wave of euphoria. I completely lost my self, didn't feel my body anymore, and was just floating in this black river with chance the rapper in the background. After that I decided I really liked the 3-meo-pce and did a bunch of it for the next 2 days about 50mg total. It makes me feel really manic and stimulated, almost crazy but not quite, and it is super trippy, not many visuals with my eyes open but it gets cool when I close them. I did small doses of DCK (15-20mg) every night to bring down the 3-meo-pce stimulation and put myself in a more relaxed state to sleep. And I ended up giving B quite a bit of DCK because he liked it. Yesterday I took the last 100mg or so of the DCK and converted it to DCKrack I just used baking soda and water just like making crack with coke, and that shit was crazy. A tiny little piece would get me so fucked up. I would take a hit and I would feel it go down, but it took such a small amount that I didn't even blow any smoke out, and then bam my entire body is numb, I feel like a cloud, and I walk like they did in the ether scene in fear and loathing. I did that most of the day yesterday, and shared some with L, B, and A out at the lake, when we ran out Me, L, and B all did a tiny line of 3-meo-pce (probably around 15mg) and we all went home. I finished the DCKrack, holed myself and then passed out. This morning I woke up and had about 40-45 mg of 3-meo-pce left. I snorted about 5mg before work, just to help with my hangover, and snorted another 5mg an hour after I got there just as a stimulant. Ended up having a shitty day, a trash bag tore open and I had to clean up about 80lbs of wet rice and pico by hand, so when I got home I rolled up a 20 stuck it in the bag and snorted my last 30mg of the 3meo-pce all at once. Shit got weird. I was completely numb, Had double vision so bad I couldn’t even watch TV, without closing one eye, and my body felt so amazing!! My speech and my movement were definitely impaired, but not terribly. I could still function. It has been 11 hours since I did that, and I still feel extremely intoxicated, and very manic as I type this. I’m out of drugs. Except for cannabis and nicotine, but that is probably for the best. I need some time to recover.

我一直在读到的普遍观点是, 麻醉药不是可以替代MDMA的药物, 我不得不不同意这些观点. 晚上5点半, 清晰的日程安排, 足够的时间来单独探索这篇文章, 这味道像苦酒. 我准备好了自己的环境, 折叠衣服,放香,选择音乐. 没有超过15分钟后, 我开始注意到MBDB的效果. 很难辨认出它的影响. 没有像MDMA那样大波来袭, 也没有像2C-I那样地到我身边. 显著的变化是我每件事都显得无关紧要. 没有抵抗这种感觉, 我把剩下的衣服扔到衣柜里, 在45分钟的时间里, 效应开始变得更加明显. 笑容的这种愚蠢倾向的背后, 是悲伤和孤独的感觉, 这是一种沉默的,绝望的感觉, 这很快就过去了. 虽然巴赫的音乐有了新的风格, 我很容易分心. 我突然觉得自己很说话. 我使用66毫克. 现在已经过去了一个小时, 我开始希望我能等到我的女朋友和我一起做这件事. 我开始拨打电话. "K"没有回答. "S"也没有回答. 幸运的是"E"很快回答了. 现在我们正在谈话, 我觉得有必要向他道歉 我说话的时候,我的狗走到我身边,把头放在我的膝盖上,期待着我. 我立即知道这意味着什么. 这只狗要尿的很难. 我看了一眼录像机,显示7点. 她最后一次在外面只有三个小时前. 我绝对不想把自己置于这些孩子和父母的中间, 我说再见我的朋友,关上电话,带上狗,然后走到田野. 我很高兴我们这样做了. 我家附近有一片大片田地, 里面覆盖着草,和野花. 距离城内有一座小山, 开口处是一片年轻的森林, 在狗做好后, 我冒险进去. 我从未有过如此幸运的经历. 风在树上的叶子中吹过来, 我灵魂中的某种东西引起了注意. 叶子的声音仿佛经过精心策划, 这种情绪的涌动让我震惊. 我转眼看周围的树木, 对于美丽的看法是压倒性的. 我觉得自己被召唤,被求回家. "大自然母亲"这个词有了新的含义. 没有应对这种经历的机制, 我只能不经意地自语, 人类的影响,战争,污染,资源消耗,物种灭绝速度, 自然的力量是无情的, 永远耐心. 这就是我学到的. 这只狗跑来跑去,在森林里玩. 完全在这个时刻. 我们坐在一起,一起玩,一起共享经验, 已经开始黑了. 感觉到一阵寒冷,我决定回家. 在我到达时, 录像机显示9点. 我急忙打电话给我的女朋友"N". 她回答了,并责备我没有等待她. 她说她将在半小时内回家. 她到达的时候, 我已经开始感觉到MBDB的效果. 我开始注意到一些奇怪的幻觉, 主要是CEV, 这些幻觉与大量MDA所观察到的幻觉有着相同的性质; 视觉是强烈由暗示和想象所支配的. 我和'N'谈了下一个小时. 到了11点,我们决定睡觉. 睡眠不是很容易的, 但它是. 我发现自己在床上感到不安, 我认为我有点多余的能量. 一个3毫克的黑激素药片处理了它. 我觉得自己很开心. 结论: 没有人知道. 经过这次经历, 我必须说我更喜欢MBDB而不是MDMA. 这种情绪让我感到无力, 让我感到失控. 我发现这种感觉是不舒服的,有点不真诚. 虽然MBDB没有像MDMA那样强烈, 尽管如此,MBDB对我来说比MDMA更丰富,更有价值. 我将MDMA与MBDB比作糖与蜂蜜的对比. 还有显著的MBDB效应,协调不良,醉. 我发现这使得一些任务变得困难, 但这不是严重的损害. 我希望很快就会有更多的数据来研究MBDB对神经毒性的潜力, 我觉得MBDB在对有责任心的灵魂旅行者的内心探索以及心理治疗中发挥了宝贵的作用. 其他: MBDB (190):第一次 (2),烂的经历 (4),自然/户外 (23),人际关系 (44),一般 (1),独自 (16)

Aug 27, 2003. erowid.org/exp/26415 DOSE: T+ 0:00 167 mg oral MBDB (powder / crystals) T+ 1:20 66 mg oral MBDB (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 150 lb The common sentiment I've been reading has been that MBDB is not a viable subsitute to MDMA, nor is it worth the trouble to procure and imbibe. I have to disagree with these points of view. At 5:30 one evening, having a clear schedule and some time to explore this material one-on-one, I weighed out 167mg MBDB and swallowed it in a cup of water with four drops of a relaxing herbal tincture. It tasted like bitter rootbeer. I set about to prepare my environment for whatever the experience might have in store. Folding laundry, setting out incense, selecting music. Not more than fifteen minutes later, I'm beginning to notice the effects of the MBDB. There was a certain impact to it that was difficult to recognize. It didn't come in a sweeping wave as MDMA does, nor did it creep up on me as with 2C-I. The most distinguishable change was this profound unimportance that seemed to come over everything I was doing. Not resisting this feeling, I dumped the rest of the unfolded laundry in the closet and went to sit down. At the 45 minute mark, effects were starting to become more pronounced. Underneath this sort of dopey tendancy to smile was a feeling of mourning and lonliness, as though I'd lost something very important to me. It was a muted, desperate feeling, and it worried me to discover it. It soon passed. Bach's music had taken on a new edge, communicative and powerful, but I found myself unable to pay close attention to it. I was easily distracted. Suddenly, I began feeling very chatty. I boost with 66mg. A little bit over an hour has passed now, and I'm starting to wish I'd waited until my girlfriend and I could do this together. Having no one to talk to, I start dialing the telephone. 'K' does not answer. 'S' does not answer either. 'E', fortunately, answers promptly. As we're talking, I feel compelled to apologize to him for an argument we'd had a year in the past. Some time into our conversation, my dog comes over to me and puts her head on my lap, looking up at me expectantly. I know immediately what this means. This dog has to pee really bad. I glance at the VCR, which reads 7:00. She was last outside just three hours ago. I desperately don't want to put myself in the middle of the children and parents who populate these streets while in this eye-wiggling, jaw-clenching state, but this has to be done. I say goodbye to my friend, hang up the telephone, leash up the dog, and head to the field. I am glad we did. Near my home is a large field covered in grass, sage, and wildflowers. Some distance inside is a hill which has a mouth to a young forest that extends all the way to city limits. After the dog has done her business, I venture inside. Inside the forest was where I was struck by some of the most intense emotions I've ever been so fortunate to experience. As the wind moved through the leaves on the trees, something in my spirit came to attention. It felt as though the sound of the leaves was carefully orchestrated, anything but random; a song with an idea attached to it. This flood of feeling is causing me to shake. I turn my eyes to the trees surrounding, and I am blown away. The perceptions of beauty are overwhelming. I feel as though I am being called -- pleaded with -- to come home. The term 'Mother Nature' takes on new meaning. Having no coping mechanism for this type of experience, I'm reduced to muttering somewhat incoherently about how glorious this natural earth truly is. The Human Impact, the wars, the pollution, the consumption of resources, the rate of extinction, all of these things are temporary, and they too will pass. Nature's force is unrelenting, and eternally patient. This is what I learned. The dog is running all around me bounding and leaping, playing in the woods. Entirely in the moment. We sat together, played together, communed together in that experience long enough to notice changes in the sky. It was getting dark. Feeling a chill, I decided to head back to the house. The VCR reads 9:00 when I arrive. I scramble to the telephone to contact my girlfriend, 'N'. She answers, and reprimands me in good humour for having not waited for her. She says she will be home within half an hour. By the time she arrived I was beginning to feel the effects of the MBDB taper. I began to notice some peculiar hallucinations, prominently CEV, but some startling OEV's as well. The hallucinations have the same nature as those observed on hefty doses of MDA; visuals that are strongly governed by suggestion and imagination. 'N' and I spoke for the next hour. At 11:00, we decide to get some sleep. Sleep did not come easily, but it did come. I found myself feeling restless in bed, tossing and turning. I suppose I had a bit of excess energy. A 3mg melatonin tablet took care of it. The next morning I awoke feeling refreshed, contented. Conclusion: ----------- After this experience, I must say I prefer MBDB to MDMA. MDMA carries me along with this feeling of delerium, a euphoria that is so overpowering that it feels I'm spinning out of control. I find that feeling to be uncomfortable, somewhat insincere. MBDB, while not as forceful as MDMA, opened up my heart to the expected feelings of empathy, compassion, and community. Something about MBDB, though, was much thicker, richer and more rewarding to me than MDMA ever was. I would compare MDMA to MBDB as I would compare maple syrup to honey. There were also the notable MBDB effects, malcoordination, 'drunkheadedness'. I found this made some tasks difficult, but it was not a severe impairment. I hope soon there will be more data to be researched about MBDB's potential for neurotoxicity, serotonin depletion, etc. I feel MBDB has a valuable role in the intro-explorations of responsible entheovoyagers, as well as in psychotherapy.

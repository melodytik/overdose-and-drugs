我很无聊,所以决定尝试吸一支2:1的BZP/TFMPP组合. 我知道它是HCL而不是FB, 但我决定给它一个机会, 我测试了一小部分 (可能是5-7毫克的预混合组合), 我打包了四分之一碗水和15毫克的组合. 我慢慢地吸入,直到所有的粉末都沸在上, 我把火焰放在组合上. 很好比说PCP或K吸烟 (我对这两种都不介意). 呼气后,我注意到一点点的不同,可能只是精神上的. 我再加上1/4碗和15-20毫克的组合, 可能是多或少我从来没有权衡的东西,只是在小试验剂量. 接下来又有两次大抽, 等待结果. 我现在感觉很困惑. 我有更多的冲动, 绝对不仅仅是大麻但不是一个巨大的脑海中爆炸的差异, 现在我感觉比我刚刚了更轻松. 吸烟后只有四到五分钟. 肺部似乎受损了, 虽然没有疼痛, 我能感觉到我的血液在我的大脑中动, 我的脉似乎是抽动相当强. 我开始很努力地把空间,所有的一切和一切都没有那么好. 开始变得像头痛一样. 我不知道我是否应该再吸烟, 这可能会增加门头痛到真正的头痛. 我知道如果我吸足够多的烟, 这会减轻麻给我的麻烦, 并且肯定会增加一些东西, 也许在更高的剂量会变得更好,但我不认为我会麻烦,这东西必须是非常糟糕的肺部. 除了AMT,你认为它闻起来很糟糕,正常,当它燃烧时, 我认为我会坚持吸烟5meoDMT和PCP, BZP (101),TFMPP (100):组合 (3),单独使用 (16)

Oct 27, 2004. erowid.org/exp/14942 DOSE: 0.5 bowls smoked Cannabis (plant material) 20 mg smoked BZP (powder / crystals) 10 mg smoked TFMPP (powder / crystals) BODY WEIGHT: 140 lb Well I was bored so I decided to try smoking the 2:1combo of BZP/TFMPP. I knew it was hcl and not fb but I decided to give it a shot seeing as how most hcls are still effective when smoked just not as good as freebase. I tested a small hit (maybe 5-7mg of the premixed combo) to see what it would taste like, not bad. So I pack a 1/4 bowl of dank and about 15mg of the combo. I inhale slowly until all the powder boils down onto the pot and I keep the flame over the combo. I hold it in, noticable, but nothing bad, much better than say PCP or K smoked (and I dont mind either of them). After exhaling I notice a little bit of a difference, could be just psychosomatic. I load another 1/4 bowl and another 15-20mg of combo, could be more or less I've never weighed the stuff, just went on small trial doses. Took 2 more big pulls and waited to see what would happen. I feel very spaced out right now. I have a little more of a rush, definitely more than just pot but not a huge mind blowing difference, just enough. I feel lighter now than I would if I was just stoned. It's only been about 4-5min after smoking. My lungs feel like I did some damage, not painful but very tight and I feel slightly short of breathe. I can feel my blood pumping thru my brain like I could easily check my pulse from my head. My pulse seemed to be pumping quite strong. I'm starting to space out pretty hard, all and all nothing all that great. It's starting to turn into a headache feeling. I dont know if I should smoke any more, it would probably just increase the threshold headache to an actual headache. I know that with PCP if I only smoke enough for a threshold effect I'll get a headache, but I dont think this is a simular case. It takes away some of the lethargy that pot gives me and and definitely added something, trouble focusing eyes, a slight headache, a real spaced out feeling, nothing worth while. Perhaps at a higher dosage it would turn out to be a lot better but I don't think I'll bother, this stuff has got to be really bad for your lungs. As far as smoking chems go none of seem that bad except AMT, you think it smells vile normal, when it burns, that smell sticks eewww. I think I'll stick to smoking 5meoDMT and PCP as far as smoking chems are concerned, they're the only chems that produce the best high when smoked.

美国 我从一个朋友那里收到了25片克隆, 我和一个朋友去买了, 我们和他的妹妹去商场做指甲. 我和朋友在去商场的路上, 我们每次服用新药时都会感受到正常的"药物高兴", 笑声,兴奋, 我们开始聊天, 开始很容易想到一些事情, 放松10分钟后, 我们的听力也不是很好. 我们在沙发上坐了25分钟, 我们都站了起来, 极端的头的感觉打我们正确的脸, 走路是个挑战,我们发现自己每隔几分钟就停下来坐下来放松. 我们又遇到了两把皮革按摩椅, 我们又坐下聊了15分钟. 其他3个孩子走过来说我不喜欢的东西 (我也注意到我比平时更具侵略性), 我注意到地板在,就像水床一样, 服用这些药后一个小时, 我们都走无目的, 几乎像尸. 他们的脸上没有情绪,嘴巴开,眼睛半闭, 我们终于停在美甲店对面的长椅上. 我几乎没有醒来, 我了一眼我的朋友, 他的头被吊下, 触摸他的胸部, 我不禁笑了, 我用剩下的力量和精力打他, 他像在课堂上睡着一样摇. (这是因为一个孩子走过来试图和他说话, 那时我真的觉得自己是个吸毒者) 两个女孩走出沙龙, 很惊我们是多么混乱. 我很惊,因为我几乎不能说话,看到,移动,或听到任何东西. 我们决定在回家的路上完全放松. 五分钟后, 我们都昏倒了, 没有意识到发生了什么. 我朋友的妹妹说我们都在和他们交谈, 眼睛对光非常敏感, 所以我们不得不保护眼睛. 我们回家后抽了一点大麻, 大约20分钟后, 我们的夜晚结束了. 我们在午夜左右昏倒,直到第二天早上11点左右才醒来. 这一天早上很糟糕. 我感觉好像前一天已经炼了12个小时, 这次旅行最糟糕的部分是第二天. 其他: 药品 - 克隆丁 (344):小组 (2-9) (17),宿醉 / 几天后 (46),第一次 (2)

Jun 14, 2006. erowid.org/exp/42480 DOSE: 1.2 mg oral Pharms - Clonidine (pill / tablet) smoked Cannabis (plant material) BODY WEIGHT: 190 lb I received 25 tablets of Clonidine from a friend, in payback of $25 he owed me. I and a friend went to pick it up, and we went with his sister to the mall to get her nails (??) done. On the way to the mall, me and my friend both dropped 2 of these tablets each. Within 20 minutes, we started to feel the normal 'drug euphoria' we get anytime we take a new drug, the giggling, the excitement, and all that good stuff...However, within 40 minutes of taking the pills, we started to actually feel some extreme side effects. We sat in a Bose store, listening to some music they had playing. We began to talk, and things really started to come easily to the mind at first, then after about 10 minutes of relaxing, we both felt as if we had smoked an ounce of pot, and were really burnt out. Things were visually moving slow, speech was beginning to get slurred, and our hearing wasn’t too great. We snapped out of it after about 25 minutes sitting on that couch, and we decided to make our way to the nail salon to meet up with his sister. As soon as we stood up, an extreme sense of vertigo smacked us right in the face, and we both stood staggering. Walking was a challenge, and we found ourselves stopping every few minutes to sit and relax. Again, we came across 2 leather massage chairs, and again, we sat talking for about 15 more minutes. 3 other kids walked by and said something I didn’t like (I also noticed I was more aggressive than usual) so this was the only thing that motivated me to move, as my friend sat and watched. Waiting for him, I noticed the floor wiggling, as a water bed would, and the lights seemed very dim for a mall. Now, about an hour after taking these pills, the effects were devastating. Both of us were walking aimlessly, almost zombie like. Emotionless faces, mouths hanging open, and eyes half shut, if not all the way shut some of the time. We stopped finally across from the nail salon, on a bench. I was barely awake, and I glanced at my friend, whose head was hung down, chin touching his chest, and a stream of drool falling from his lip, to his lap. I couldn’t help but laugh at this sight, but I also worried a little bit. I whacked him with whatever strength and energy I had left, and he jolted like he was sleeping in the middle of class. (this was after a kid walked by and tried talking to him, and his mother scolded him away. I really felt like a druggie then) The two girls walked out of the salon and were surprised at how messed up we were. I was just as surprised, as I could barely talk, see, move, or hear anything. On the way out of the mall, we both took 2 more tablets, and decided to totally chill the ride home. 5 minutes into the car ride home, we were both knocked out cold, unaware to anything that was going on. My friends sister said we were both talking to them, and we were conscious half the time, but I don’t remember anything of the sort. When we arrived home, almost 2 hours after taking the first dose, our eyes were so sensitive to light we had to shield them walking into the house. We smoked a little pot after getting home, and about 20 minutes later, our night was over. We passed out, at around midnight, and didn’t wake up until 11 or so the next morning. The morning was pretty awful. I felt as if I had worked out 12 hours the day before, and my head felt like it was a log in a lumber mill. The day after is certainly the worst part about this trip.

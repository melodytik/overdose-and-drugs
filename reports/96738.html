每日补充剂的一部分是含有Eleuthero根和Gotu Kola的三重甘产品. 几年来一直没有问题. 这一次添加了5毫克的维诺. 消化不良和轻微的焦虑开始在摄入后2小时. 这有点不愉快. 类似于尼亚的冲洗. 毕竟,维诺是一种血管扩展剂. 很难集中注意力. (奇怪的东西应该帮助集中注意力虽然它可能是焦虑) 效果大大减少后4小时. 我打算再试一次, 但没有三重产品, 其他: 葡萄 (162):一般 (1), 背景不明 (20)

Jun 2, 2013. erowid.org/exp/96738 DOSE: 5 mg oral Vinpocetine BODY WEIGHT: 180 lb Normal morning. Green protein shake. 1 cup of coffee. Part of daily supplements is a Triple Genseng product that contains Eleuthero Root and Gotu Kola. Never a problem for several years. Added 5mg of Vinpocetine this time. Onset of Digestive distress and slight anxiety begin 2 hours after ingestion. Slightly unpleasant. Flushing similar to Niacin. Vinpocetine is a vasodiliator after all. Hard to concentrate. (odd for something that is supposed to aid concentration although it could have been the anxiety) Effect greatly diminished after 4 hours. I intend to try this again but without the the Triple genseng product and with a full stomach to validate its effectiveness.

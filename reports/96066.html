黄素 (粉末/晶体) 我从网上购买了1克黄素HBr. 试用剂量从100mg到500mg, 溶于一杯水中的物质. 它的味道令人厌恶的苦. 效果非常快,比如说5分钟, 虽然我能获得短暂的快乐, 但没有明显的高兴. 有些轻微的迷幻效应也存在,包括CEV和灯光. 效果持续大约一个小时, 然后很快结束. 然而我的经历都伴随着一种非常不舒服的失调和普遍的不适感, 我在每一次用草甘的经历后都感到非常丧, 甚至想自杀. 这真的是那么糟糕. 这种性障碍持续数小时, 值得注意的是,最大500mg剂量后也出现了尿,因此这可能为作用机制提供线索. 我会说这种衰退与MDMA衰退或酒后狂醉相当, 在高剂量时也出现了一些恶心. 总体而言, 这是一种极其糟糕的娱乐性物质. 避免使用. 其他: 黄素 (487):回顾 / 摘要 (11),未知背景 (20)

Erowid.org . Dec 6, 2015. erowid.org/exp/96066 DOSE: oral Glaucine (powder / crystals) I acquired 1 gram of Glaucine HBr from an online source. Doses were tried ranging from 100mg to 500mg, with the substance dissolved in a glass of water. It tastes disgustingly bitter. Effects come on extremely quickly, say 5 minutes and resemble a very weak opiate high with a stimulant edge. I was able to achieve a fair if fleeting buzz, but no overt euphoria. Some mild psychedelic effects are also present, consisting of CEV's and lights. Effects last about an hour and then quickly end. However my experiences were all followed by a very uncomfortable dysphoria and general ill feeling, worsening as the dose gets higher. I seriously felt utterly depressed, suicidal even, after each of my experiences with glaucine. It really is that bad. This dysphoria persists for hours, lasting well into the morning when taken the night before. Interestingly urinary retention was also experienced after the largest 500-mg dose, so this may provide a clue to the mechanism of action. I would say the comedown is genuinely on par with an MDMA comedown or alcohol hangover, which is very strange to say the effects are so mild. Some nausea was present at the higher doses also. Overall an extremely poor recreational substance. Avoid.

中文译文 致幻剂

在两天前, 我从来没有听说过花让人脚. 两位朋友带着六朵长号花来到我的公寓. 我怀疑他们会做什么. 我和我的室友在三杯水中煮了这六朵花, 我们把它均地分成四杯, 我们坐在客厅,在不到十分钟的时间里喝完尾酒. 时间是晚上8点左右. 只有五分钟之后, 我的室友是第一个提到感觉. 他也一直在喝酒. 然后我开始感到奇怪. 我觉得呼吸很短, 眼睛也无法集中. 我试图移动它们时, 动作似乎迟延. 我注意到每个人的皮肤色调似乎比平常更红. 我只能记住一些碎片, 它们发生的顺序不清楚. 我们四个人从那时起就能够讲述我们每一个经历, 但我将不得不回忆我的经历的夜晚似乎是. 我记得看着我的朋友的脸, 几乎没有认出他们是谁. 他们是明亮的红色与沉没的黑色眼. 我试图说话,但不能,好像我试图说话后呼出所有的空气从我的肺. 我感觉到我的手指, 知道我在抽烟, 只是意识到那里什么都没有. 我认为它消失后剩下的就是粉状残留物. 我真的相信我已经发现了如何让香烟消失, 我试图向我的朋友解释发生了什么事, 我还记得有一次幻觉, 它有数百条腿, 像在液体中行走一样移动. 我还看到几百只在厨房地板上吞烟. 晚上早些时候,我的室友感觉不好, 走进他的房间睡觉. 撞到几面墙,最后撞到桌子,然后撞到我旁边的地板. 这让我从半睡中醒来. 我注意到他的头正在出血,他似乎失去了理智. 他表现得像一个梦游者. 我越看他,他变得越红. 然后我开始幻觉到他的眼上有亮白的瘤. 我担心他的安全, 但觉得无能为力. 我又沉睡了. 我再次被大声唤醒. 我室友在公寓里奇怪地走来走去, 摸着东西, 他倒在一个风扇上, 似乎在试图控制它时摔. 我有一个朋友把它从他的路上, 但他仍然在毁灭的道路上. 他伸出手来触摸我的旋转机, 摔到桌子上, 担心他的安全,当然也担心我的财产安全, 他坐了一会儿,然后立刻起来. 我发现他站在壁里面向内. 他告诉我第二天,他记得他在衣柜里试图找到浴室镜子背后的房间,他没有认出作为一个镜子,在其中他看到自己的反射,他认为我,玩笑他. 我在房间里的同伴似乎是最受影响的. 我还有两个朋友,其中一个 (我们叫他J) 记得很少. 另一个 (V) 声称整晚的思维相对清晰, 没有人能记住的事件. 他声称,在某个时候,J抓住了一堆木制的子, 把它们放在里说他正在做面食. 然后我告诉他他必须把他们分开. 我把它们打破了,扔在地上. 然后我们都做了小碗的谷物和吃了它们. 我和我的室友似乎试图把几天前拆掉的书架放起来. 我们应该把它放在开放的柜子上. 很快它又撞到了我们的头上, 我们用它扎了几分钟, 似乎是把他的子穿上和脱下, 虽然我记得不太清楚, 我和我的室友试图讨论发生了什么事. 我们发现我们反复忘记我们在谈论什么, 我们发现许多事情都不合适, 我的牙刷是在我的相机包. 在厨房的水槽里. 果汁和谷物倒在厨房的地板上, 客厅的地毯, 和一张桌子. 惊的是,除了额头上的伤口和墙上的血迹, 经历非常有趣, 我再也不会碰这些东西了. 时间表: 63118 2007年6月6日发布 布鲁格曼西亚 (84):艰难的经历 (5),第一次 (2),小组 (2-9) (17)

Utter Delirium Brugmansia by Anonymous Citation:   Anonymous. "Utter Delirium: An Experience with Brugmansia (exp63118)". Erowid.org . Jun 6, 2007. erowid.org/exp/63118 DOSE: 1.5 flowers oral Brugmansia (tea) BODY WEIGHT: 125 lb Before two nights ago, I had never heard of any flower that made someone trip. Two of my friends came to my apartment with six trumpet shaped flowers. I was skeptical that they would do anything. The two friends, my roommate and I, boiled all six flowers in about three cups of water and strained it. We divided it evenly into four glasses and mixed with juice. We sat in the living room and finished our cocktails in under ten minutes. It was about 8:00 pm. After only five minutes, my roommate was the first to mention feeling something. He had also been drinking beer. Then I began to feel strange. I felt a shortness of breath and was unable to focus my eyes on anything. When I would move them, the motion seemed delayed. I noticed that everyone’s skin tone seemed much redder than usual. From this point on, I could only remember bits and pieces, and the order in which they happened was unclear. The four of us have since been able to recount each of our experiences to piece the evening together into a somewhat coherent timeline of events. But I will have to recount my experience of the night as it seemed to be. I remember looking at the faces of my friends and having little to zero recognition of who they were. They were bright red with sunken black eye sockets. I tried to speak but was unable, as though I was attempting to speak after exhaling all of the air from my lungs. At one point, I brought my fingers to my mouth to take what should have been the final drag of my cigarette, which I felt in my fingers and understood I had been smoking, only to realize there was nothing there. In its place, I felt a powdery residue, which I believed was all that remained after it had disappeared. I genuinely believed that I had discovered how to make cigarettes disappear and this made perfect sense to me. I attempted to explain what had happened to my friends but was unable to get the point across. I also remember a hallucination of a nickel-sized spikey bug that appeared to be a combination caterpillar and sea urchin. It had hundreds of legs and moved as if walking in fluid. I also saw hundreds of ants devouring a cigarette butt on the kitchen floor. Early in the evening, my roommate, who was not feeling well, went to lie down in his room. He came out in what appeared to be a drunken stupor, crashed into several walls, and finally a table and then the floor next to me. This awoke me from the half-sleep I was in. I noticed his head was bleeding and he appeared to be out of his mind. His behavior resembled that of someone who was sleepwalking. The more I looked at him, the redder he became. Then I started hallucinating that there were bright white growths on his eyelids. I was concerned for his safety but felt unable to do anything about it. I drifted back to sleep. I was awoken again by loud noises. My roommate was travelling strangely around the apartment touching things and staring at them in confusion. He tripped on a fan and appeared to wrestle it while trying to gain physical control of it. One of my friends got it out of his way but he was still on a path of destruction. He reached out to touch my turntables and fell into the table they were on, scattering the records propped up against it. Concerned for his safety and, admittedly the safety of my belongings, I asked him to sit down and directed him to the couch. He sat for a moment and got right back up. I found him standing in the closet facing inward. He told me the next day that he remembered that he was in the closet trying to find the room behind the bathroom mirror, which he did not recognize as a mirror, and in which he saw his own reflection which he perceived as me, playing a joke on him. Of the four of us, my roommate seemed to be affected the most. Of my other two friends, one of them (we'll call him J) remembers very little. The other (V) claims to have been thinking relatively clearly the entire night, aside from not recognizing me. V related a large amount of occurrences that no one else could remember. He claims that at one point, J grabbed a stack of wooden skewers and put them in a pot saying that he was making pasta. Then I told him that he had to break them up. I broke them in half and dropped them on the floor. Then we both made bowls of cereal and ate them. My roommate and I apparently tried to put up a shelf that we had taken down days earlier. We supposedly put it up by resting it on open cabinets. It soon came crashing back down on our heads and we struggled with it for a couple minutes before putting it aside. J was apparently putting his socks on and off repeatedly and pressing his toes into a puddle of juice on the floor. By about 1 am, we were all able to communicate with each other although I cannot remember it very well. J and V left and my roommate and I tried to discuss what had happened. We found that we repeatedly forgot what we were talking about and decided it was best to go to sleep and hope that our eyesight and brain function would be back to normal in the morning. The next morning, we found many things out of place which none of us could explain. My toothbrush was in my camera bag. The toilet paper dispenser core was in the kitchen sink. There was juice and cereal spilled on the kitchen floor, the living room carpet, and a table. Surprisingly, there was little evidence of my roommate the bulldozer, besides the gash in his forehead, and blood on the walls. The experience was incredibly interesting, but overall very unpleasant. I will never touch the stuff again.

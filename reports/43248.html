我听说水是有趣的, 因为它给你一个很好的, 松散的感觉, 我决定自己也应该买几瓶. 我被访问我的朋友,我问如果我可以有一个,他同意. 我在30分钟后服用了第一个药物, 几乎立即感觉到一些效果. 在前5到10分钟. 我感到头,松, 一切都听起来有点沉默. 我很快就开始感到头, 灯光让我很烦,所以我们把它们关掉了,我开始恶心. 我感觉非常热, 我的脸非常,非常粘. 我在床上躺着, 脸正好在通风口上. 我发现这有助于我的恶心. 我开始感到非常疲. 我朋友一直在看着这一切, 他当然试图让我保持清醒, 我不能走路,所以他帮助我的外面认为如果我有一个香烟,它可能会唤醒我一些. 没有人会这样想! 两次抽烟,我立刻开始干燥激烈地抽. 我一整天都没有吃东西,所以我的胃酸很糟糕. 这是可怕的! 吐后,症状开始有所减弱. 我决定再抽一支烟, 当然这是个愚蠢的举动. 所有的症状都回来了, 持续了大约半小时. 经历过这种经历之后, 我不想再尝试那些我还不知道效果的药物. 我认为我学到了教训. 其他: 水 (111):小组 (2-9) (17),不好的旅行 (6),导游/保姆 (39),健康问题 (27),第一次 (2)

Apr 11, 2007. erowid.org/exp/43248 DOSE: T+ 0:00 20 mg oral Hydrocodone (pill / tablet) T+ 0:30 40 mg oral Hydrocodone (pill / tablet) BODY WEIGHT: 230 lb I'd been told that hydrocodone was fun to take because it gave you a nice, loose feeling that typically lasted a pretty good amout of time. So, when my friend was perscribed a 2 bottles of 60 pills each I decided that I should ask for a few. I was visited my friend and I asked If I could have one and he agreed. I took the first one without much effect after about 30 min. I then took 2 more and began, almost intstantly to feel some results. For about the first 5-10 min. I felt giddy, looser, and everything sounded somewhat muffled. Soon after that I started to become quite dizzy so I layed down. The lights were bothering me so we turned them off and the my nausea set in. I got very hot and my face was very, very clammy. I was so hot and disoriented that I turned up the air conditioning to about 60-55 degrees and layed on the floor with my face positioned directly over the air vent. I found that helped my nausea some. It had been 1-2 hours after original dosage and I became very tired. My friend had been watching all of this and so he of course tried to keep me awake because we both knew that if I was to sleep that there was chance of me going into a comotose state. I couldn't walk so he helped my outside thinking that if I had a cigarette that it might wake me up some. DO NOT think that! Two hits of the cigarette and I instantly started to dry heave intensively. I hadn't had anything to eat all day so I was wretching stomach acid. It was horrible! After throwing up the symptoms became to fade somewhat. After about another hour they were mostly gone so I decided to have another cigarette, of course that was a dumb move. All the symptoms came back and that lasted about half an hour. After that experience I don't want to ever dabble with perscription that I don't know the effects of yet. I guess I learned my lesson.
